# 1. MEANING OF PARLIAMENTARY COMMITTEES


Parliament deals with **large volume of legislative, financial and oversight work** → detailed examination by full House not possible.


Therefore **Parliamentary Committees assist Parliament** in:

- detailed scrutiny of **legislation**
- examination of **government expenditure**
- investigation of **issues and policies**
- ensuring **executive accountability**

They **examine issues in detail and submit reports to Parliament**.


---


# 2. CONSTITUTIONAL POSITION


| Feature                 | Details                                           |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Mention in Constitution | Yes (at various places)                           |
| Composition / functions | **NOT specified**                                 |
| Governed by             | **Rules of Procedure of Lok Sabha & Rajya Sabha** |


📌 **Exam Trap:** Parliamentary committees are **not detailed in Constitution**.


---


# 3. DEFINITION OF PARLIAMENTARY COMMITTEE


A body is a Parliamentary Committee only if:


1️⃣ **Appointed/elected by House OR nominated by Speaker/Chairman**


2️⃣ Works **under direction of Speaker/Chairman**


3️⃣ **Reports submitted to House or Presiding Officer**


4️⃣ Secretariat provided by **Lok Sabha / Rajya Sabha**


📌 **Consultative Committees are NOT parliamentary committees** (fail above conditions).


---


# 4. CLASSIFICATION OF PARLIAMENTARY COMMITTEES


## (A) STANDING COMMITTEES


Permanent committees.

- constituted **every year**
- function **continuously**

## (B) AD HOC COMMITTEES


Temporary committees.

- created **for specific purpose**
- dissolved after **task completion**

# PUBLIC ACCOUNTS COMMITTEE (PAC)


## Origin


Established **1921** under **Government of India Act 1919**.


---


## Composition


| House       | Members |
| ----------- | ------- |
| Lok Sabha   | 15      |
| Rajya Sabha | 7       |
| Total       | **22**  |


Election → **Proportional Representation by Single Transferable Vote (STV)**


Term → **1 year**


Minister → **Cannot be member**


---


## Chairman


Appointed by **Speaker of Lok Sabha**


Convention:


| Period      | Chairman       |
| ----------- | -------------- |
| Before 1967 | Ruling Party   |
| After 1967  | **Opposition** |


Purpose → independent scrutiny.


---


## Role of CAG


PAC examines **CAG audit reports**.


CAG submits **3 reports to President**:


1️⃣ Audit Report on **Appropriation Accounts**


2️⃣ Audit Report on **Finance Accounts**


3️⃣ Audit Report on **Public Undertakings**


CAG acts as **“Guide, Friend and Philosopher of PAC.”**


---


## Functions


PAC examines:

- **Appropriation Accounts**
- **Finance Accounts**
- accounts of autonomous bodies audited by **CAG**
- audit reports on **government receipts**
- **excess expenditure**
- accounts of **state corporations**

### Meaning


| Account                | Meaning                          |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Appropriation Accounts | Actual vs sanctioned expenditure |
| Finance Accounts       | Annual receipts & expenditure    |


---


## Objective


Checks:

- legality of expenditure
- financial propriety
- efficiency

Detects:

- waste
- corruption
- extravagance
- nugatory expenditure

---


## Limitations


PAC cannot:

- examine **policy matters**
- interfere in **administration**
- **disallow expenditure**
- issue **executive orders**

Work is **post-mortem**.


Recommendations → **advisory only**.


---


# ESTIMATES COMMITTEE


## Origin


First constituted **1950**.


Recommended by **Finance Minister John Mathai**.


---


## Composition


| Feature     | Detail |
| ----------- | ------ |
| Members     | **30** |
| Lok Sabha   | 30     |
| Rajya Sabha | **0**  |


Election → **STV**


Term → **1 year**


Minister → **Cannot be member**


---


## Chairman


Appointed by **Speaker**.


Convention → **Ruling party member**.


---


## Objective


Examines **budget estimates**.


Suggests:

- economy
- efficiency
- administrative reforms

Hence called:


**“Continuous Economy Committee.”**


---


## Functions


1️⃣ Suggest **economies in expenditure**


2️⃣ Suggest **alternative policies**


3️⃣ Check **proper utilisation of funds**


4️⃣ Suggest **budget presentation format**


---


## Special Feature


Examines **selected ministries annually**.


All ministries covered **by rotation**.


---


## Limitations

- Cannot question **policy approved by Parliament**
- Only **selected ministries examined**
- No **CAG assistance**
- Recommendations **advisory**

---


# COMMITTEE ON PUBLIC UNDERTAKINGS (COPU)


## Origin


Established **1964**.


Recommended by **Krishna Menon Committee**.


---


## Composition


| House       | Members |
| ----------- | ------- |
| Lok Sabha   | 15      |
| Rajya Sabha | 7       |
| Total       | **22**  |


Earlier → 15 members (10 LS + 5 RS)


Changed **1974**.


Election → **STV**


Term → **1 year**


---


## Chairman


Appointed by **Speaker**.


Must be **Lok Sabha member**.


---


## Functions


Examines:

- reports of **public sector enterprises**
- **CAG reports on PSUs**
- management of PSUs

Ensures:

- commercial efficiency
- sound business principles
- autonomy of PSUs

---


## Matters NOT examined

- Government policy
- Day-to-day administration
- PSUs under special statutes

---


## Limitations

- Only **10–12 PSUs/year**
- **Post-mortem work**
- Members not **technical experts**
- Recommendations **non-binding**

# DEPARTMENTALLY RELATED STANDING COMMITTEES (DRSC)


## Establishment


| Year | Committees |
| ---- | ---------- |
| 1993 | 17         |
| 2004 | +7         |


Total = **24 committees**.


---


## Objective


Strengthen **parliamentary control over executive**.


Examine:

- policies
- bills
- budgets

---


## Composition


| Feature     | Detail |
| ----------- | ------ |
| Members     | **31** |
| Lok Sabha   | 21     |
| Rajya Sabha | 10     |


Members nominated by:


| House       | Authority |
| ----------- | --------- |
| Lok Sabha   | Speaker   |
| Rajya Sabha | Chairman  |


Minister → **cannot be member**


Term → **1 year**


---


## Control of Committees


| House       | Committees |
| ----------- | ---------- |
| Lok Sabha   | 16         |
| Rajya Sabha | 8          |


---


# FUNCTIONS OF DRSCs


1️⃣ Examine **Demands for Grants**

- before voting in Lok Sabha
- cannot propose **cut motions**

2️⃣ Examine **Bills**


3️⃣ Examine **Annual Reports**


4️⃣ Examine **Long-term policy documents**


---


# LIMITATIONS OF DRSCs


Cannot examine:

- day-to-day administration
- matters examined by other committees

Recommendations → **advisory only**.


---


# ADVANTAGES OF COMMITTEE SYSTEM


1️⃣ Less **party politics**


2️⃣ Flexible procedures


3️⃣ Greater **executive accountability**


4️⃣ Better **financial scrutiny**


5️⃣ More **participation of MPs**


6️⃣ Consultation with **experts & stakeholders**


7️⃣ Greater role for **Opposition & Rajya Sabha**
