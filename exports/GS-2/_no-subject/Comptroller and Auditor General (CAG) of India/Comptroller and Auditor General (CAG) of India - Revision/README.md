# 1. Constitutional Position


| Aspect                   | Details                                                            |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Constitutional Provision | **Article 148**                                                    |
| Office                   | **Comptroller and Auditor General (CAG) of India**                 |
| Head of                  | **Indian Audit and Accounts Department**                           |
| Key Role                 | **Guardian of Public Purse**                                       |
| Controls                 | Entire financial system at **Union + State level**                 |
| Ensures                  | Financial administration follows Constitution + Parliamentary laws |


### Dr. B.R. Ambedkar’s Statement

> “The most important Officer under the Constitution of India.”

### Bulwarks of Democracy

1. Supreme Court
2. Election Commission
3. UPSC
4. CAG

# 2. Appointment, Term & Removal


| Feature                   | Details                                     |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| Appointed by              | **President of India**                      |
| Method                    | Warrant under President’s hand and seal     |
| Oath administered by      | President                                   |
| Term                      | **6 years or 65 years** (whichever earlier) |
| Resignation               | Addressed to President                      |
| Removal                   | Same manner as **Supreme Court Judge**      |
| Grounds of Removal        | **Proved misbehaviour or incapacity**       |
| Parliamentary Requirement | Special majority in both Houses             |


---


# 3. Oath of CAG


CAG swears to:

- Bear true faith to Constitution
- Uphold sovereignty & integrity of India
- Perform duties without fear or favour
- Uphold Constitution and laws

---


# 4. Independence of CAG


| Safeguard                   | Provision                                                     |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Security of tenure          | Cannot be removed easily                                      |
| Pleasure doctrine           | Does NOT hold office during President’s pleasure              |
| No future office            | Cannot hold govt office after retirement                      |
| Salary                      | Determined by Parliament                                      |
| Salary parity               | Equal to Supreme Court Judge                                  |
| Salary protection           | Cannot be altered to disadvantage after appointment           |
| Administrative independence | IAAD staff conditions fixed by President after consulting CAG |
| Expenses                    | Charged on **Consolidated Fund of India**                     |
| Parliamentary vote          | Expenses NOT votable                                          |
| Executive control           | No minister responsible for CAG                               |


### Important Point


➡ Expenses charged on Consolidated Fund = **No vote in Parliament**

# 6. Duties & Powers of CAG


## Constitutional Basis


### Article 149


Parliament determines duties and powers.


### Important Law


**CAG (Duties, Powers and Conditions of Service) Act, 1971**


### 1976 Amendment


➡ Separation of **accounts from audit** in Central Government.


### Called


**Departmentalisation of Accounts**


Meaning:

- Separate departments maintain Central accounts
- CAG mainly performs audit

---


# 7. Functions of CAG


## A. Audit Functions


| Audit Area          | Details                                         |
| ------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Consolidated Fund   | India, States, UTs with legislature             |
| Contingency Fund    | Union + States                                  |
| Public Account      | Union + States                                  |
| Govt departments    | Trading, manufacturing, P&L, balance sheet etc. |
| Revenue audit       | Assessment, collection & allocation of revenue  |
| Govt bodies         | Substantially financed bodies                   |
| Govt companies      | Audit functions                                 |
| Public corporations | As per law                                      |
| Public debt         | Debt, sinking fund, deposits etc.               |
| Local bodies        | On request of President/Governor                |


---


## B. Advisory Function


### Article 150


CAG advises President regarding:


➡ Form in which Union & State accounts should be maintained.


---


## C. Certification Function


### Article 279


CAG certifies:


➡ Net proceeds of taxes and duties


### Net Proceeds


Tax revenue – Cost of collection


### Important Point


➡ CAG certificate is **final**


---


## D. Reporting Function


| Government | Report submitted to | Finally laid before |
| ---------- | ------------------- | ------------------- |
| Union      | President           | Parliament          |
| State      | Governor            | State Legislature   |


---


## E. PAC Relationship


CAG acts as:

> “Guide, Friend and Philosopher” of PAC

PAC examines CAG reports.


---


## F. Compilation of Accounts


| Before 1976                             | After 1976                        |
| --------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| CAG maintained Central + State accounts | CAG maintains only State accounts |

# 11. Types of Audit

### Purpose


Checks whether expenditure:

- Was legally available
- Used for authorised purpose
- Followed rules

### Nature


Mandatory


---


## 2. Propriety Audit


### Purpose


Checks:

- Wisdom
- Economy

### Nature


Discretionary

# 12. Limitation of CAG


## Secret Service Expenditure


CAG cannot demand detailed records.


Must accept:


➡ Certificate from competent authority.


---


# 13. Comptroller vs Auditor Role


## In India


CAG functions mainly as:


➡ **Auditor General**


Reason:

- No control over withdrawal from Consolidated Fund
- Departments can issue cheques directly
- Audit occurs after expenditure

---


# India vs Britain


| India                          | Britain                  |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------ |
| Mainly Auditor                 | Comptroller + Auditor    |
| No prior control on withdrawal | Prior approval necessary |

# 14. CAG & Public Corporations


## Category 1 — Direct Audit by CAG


Examples:

- Damodar Valley Corporation
- ONGC
- Air India
- Indian Airlines

---


## Category 2 — Private Audit + CAG Oversight


Private auditors audit, but CAG may conduct supplementary audit.


Examples:

- Central Warehousing Corporation
- Industrial Finance Corporation

---


## Category 3 — Entirely Private Audit


CAG has no role.


Examples:

- LIC
- RBI
- SBI
- FCI

# 17. Appleby’s Criticism of CAG

1. Public Administration in India (1953)
2. Reexamination of India’s Administrative System (1956)

---


## Criticisms


| Point                         | Explanation                         |
| ----------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| Colonial legacy               | Audit system inherited from British |
| Repressive effect             | Creates fear among administrators   |
| Parliament overvalues audit   | Excessive dependence on audit       |
| Auditors lack admin expertise | Not trained administrators          |
| Narrow function               | Audit has limited usefulness        |
| Department knows better       | Deputy Secretary understands more   |


### Appleby even suggested:


➡ Abolition of CAG office.
