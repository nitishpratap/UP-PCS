
# Authoritative Text of the Constitution in Hindi Language


## 1. Background

- **Originally**, the **Constitution of India** did **not contain any provision regarding an authoritative Hindi text**.
- The Constitution adopted by the **Constituent Assembly on 26 November 1949** was **only in English language**.

To address this issue, a constitutional amendment was later made.


---


# 58th Constitutional Amendment Act, 1987


### Key Provision

- The **58th Constitutional Amendment Act, 1987** introduced provisions regarding the **authoritative text of the Constitution in Hindi**.

### Important Change

- It inserted **Article 394-A** in the Constitution.

### Location in Constitution

- Inserted in **Part XXII**.

### Part XXII Title


**Short Title, Commencement, Authoritative Text in Hindi and Repeals**


---


# Article 394-A: Authoritative Text of the Constitution in Hindi


Article 394-A provides for publication of an authoritative Hindi text of the Constitution.


## 1. Publication of Hindi Translation


The **President of India shall cause to be published under his authority**:


### (a) Translation of the Constitution in Hindi

- The Constitution shall be translated into **Hindi language**.
- Necessary **modifications may be made** to bring the translation in conformity with:
    - language
    - style
    - terminology used in **authoritative Hindi texts of Central Acts**.
- All **constitutional amendments made before the publication** must also be incorporated in the translation.

### (b) Translation of Constitutional Amendments

- Every **amendment of the Constitution made in English** shall also be **translated into Hindi**.

---


## 2. Same Meaning as Original English Text

- The Hindi translation of:
    - the Constitution
    - every amendment
- **shall have the same meaning as the original English text**.

### If any difficulty arises

- The **President shall cause the Hindi text to be revised appropriately**.

---


## 3. Authoritative Text in Hindi

- The Hindi translation of:
    - the Constitution
    - its amendments
- **shall be deemed to be the authoritative text in Hindi for all purposes**.

This means it becomes **legally valid and authentic** in Hindi.


---


# Reasons for the 58th Constitutional Amendment (1987)


The amendment was introduced due to several reasons:


## 1. Constitution Originally in English

- The Constitution adopted on **26 November 1949** was **in English language**.

---


## 2. Hindi Translation Published in 1950

- A **Hindi translation of the Constitution** was published in **1950**.
- It was **signed by members of the Constituent Assembly**.
- It was published **under the authority of the President of the Constituent Assembly**.

However:

- This version was **not declared an authoritative legal text**.

---


## 3. Demand for an Authoritative Hindi Text


There was a **general demand** for:

- an **authoritative Hindi version of the Constitution**
- incorporating **all subsequent constitutional amendments**.

---


## 4. Need in Legal Processes


It became **necessary for legal purposes** because:

- Hindi is widely used in **administration and judiciary in India**
- Courts and legal institutions required **an official Hindi text**.

Thus, an authoritative Hindi version was required to **facilitate legal usage**.


---


## 5. Standardisation of Language


Another objective was to ensure that the Hindi translation:

- conforms with the **Hindi translation published in 1950**
- matches the **language, style and terminology used in Central Acts in Hindi**.

---


## 6. Empowering the President


The amendment aimed to **empower the President of India** to:

- publish the **Hindi translation of the Constitution**
- publish **Hindi translations of constitutional amendments**

under his authority.


---


# Implementation of Article 394-A


In accordance with **Article 394-A**:

- The **President published the authoritative Hindi text of the Constitution**
- It was published in the **Gazette of India**.

# Part XXII of the Constitution


Title:


**Short Title, Commencement, Authoritative Text in Hindi and Repeals**


Originally this Part contained **three Articles only**:


| Article         | Subject      |
| --------------- | ------------ |
| **Article 393** | Short title  |
| **Article 394** | Commencement |
| **Article 395** | Repeals      |

| Article Added     | Amendment                                            |

# Important Notes (From Laxmikanth References)


### 1. Amendment Bill

- The **56th Constitutional Amendment Bill, 1987** was introduced.
- After passing through Parliament and receiving Presidential assent, it became the **58th Constitutional Amendment Act, 1987**.

---


### 2. Hindi Translation of 1950


The Hindi translation published in **1950** was done:

- according to a **resolution adopted by the Constituent Assembly**.
