
# 1. INTRODUCTION


## NCRWC (National Commission to Review the Working of the Constitution)


| Feature          | Details                                                       |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Full Name        | National Commission to Review the Working of the Constitution |
| Established      | 2000                                                          |
| By               | Government of India                                           |
| Chairman         | **M.N. Venkatachaliah** (Former Chief Justice of India)       |
| Members          | 11-member Commission                                          |
| Report Submitted | 2002                                                          |
| Nature           | Advisory and recommendatory                                   |
| Objective        | Review working of Constitution after 50 years                 |


---


# 2. TERMS OF REFERENCE


The Commission was asked to examine:

- Whether constitutional provisions are capable of meeting:
    - efficient governance
    - smooth administration
    - socio-economic development
    - needs of modern India

## Important Limitation


Commission could recommend changes:


✅ Within framework of:

- Parliamentary democracy
- Basic structure doctrine

❌ Could NOT:

- Rewrite Constitution
- Disturb basic features/basic structure

---


# 3. IMPORTANT CLARIFICATION


The Commission stated:

- It was reviewing **working** of Constitution
- Not rewriting Constitution
- Recommendations were:
    - advisory
    - non-binding

Final power remained with:

- Parliament
