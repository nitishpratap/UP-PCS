# PART 1 — INTRODUCTION & ESTABLISHMENT


## Meaning of Parliamentary Forums


Parliamentary Forums are **informal institutional platforms within Parliament** created to enable **Members of Parliament (MPs) to interact with experts, ministers, and officials** on specific public policy issues.


They help MPs:

- discuss **important national issues**
- understand **ground realities**
- obtain **technical knowledge**
- improve **policy discussion in Parliament**

These forums **do not exercise legislative powers** but act as **knowledge-sharing bodies**.


---


## Establishment of Parliamentary Forums


The **first Parliamentary Forum** was created in **2005**.


### First Forum

- **Parliamentary Forum on Water Conservation and Management**
- Established in **2005**

Later **seven more forums** were created.


### Total Parliamentary Forums


Currently **8 Parliamentary Forums** exist.

## Special Historical Note


On **12 May 2005**, the **Speaker of Lok Sabha Somnath Chatterjee** announced the creation of the first forum.


Purpose:

- To allow MPs to discuss **water issues in a structured way**
- To help MPs raise water issues **effectively in Parliament**

The forum was officially constituted on:


**12 August 2005**

# PART 2 — OBJECTIVES OF PARLIAMENTARY FORUMS


The forums were created with **three main objectives**.


---


## Objective 1 — Platform for Interaction


The forums provide a platform for MPs to interact with:

- concerned **Union Ministers**
- **experts**
- **officials of nodal ministries**

Purpose:

- focused discussions on **critical national issues**
- result-oriented dialogue
- speeding up **implementation of policies**

---


## Objective 2 — Sensitisation and Knowledge Enhancement


The forums aim to:

- make MPs aware of **key policy concerns**
- provide **latest information and technical know-how**
- understand **ground-level realities**

Sources of expertise:

- Indian experts
- international experts
- technical specialists

This helps MPs:

- raise issues effectively in the **Parliament**
- raise issues in **Departmentally Related Standing Committees (DRSCs)**

---


## Objective 3 — Creation of Knowledge Database


The forums also build a **database on important issues**.


Information sources include:

- ministries
- NGOs
- newspapers
- United Nations
- internet
- research studies

This information is circulated among MPs so they can:

- participate meaningfully in discussions
- seek clarifications from experts and officials

---


# IMPORTANT RULE (VERY IMPORTANT FOR EXAMS)


Parliamentary Forums:


❗ **Cannot interfere with Departmentally Related Standing Committees**


Meaning:

- They **cannot encroach upon the jurisdiction** of Parliamentary Committees.
- They are **discussion platforms**, not decision-making bodies.

---


# PART 3 — COMPOSITION OF PARLIAMENTARY FORUMS


## President of Forums


The **Speaker of Lok Sabha** is the **ex-officio President** of all Parliamentary Forums.


### Exception


For the **Parliamentary Forum on Population and Public Health**:

- **Chairman of Rajya Sabha** → Ex-officio President
- **Speaker of Lok Sabha** → Ex-officio Co-President

---


## Vice Presidents of Forums


The following are **ex-officio Vice Presidents**:

- Deputy Chairman of Rajya Sabha
- Deputy Speaker of Lok Sabha
- Concerned Ministers
- Chairmen of Departmentally Related Standing Committees

---


## Membership Size


Each Parliamentary Forum consists of:


Maximum **31 members**


This number **excludes**:

- President
- Co-President
- Vice-Presidents

---


### Distribution of Members


| House       | Maximum Members |
| ----------- | --------------- |
| Lok Sabha   | 21              |
| Rajya Sabha | 10              |


---


## Selection of Members


Members are **nominated by**:

- Speaker of Lok Sabha
- Chairman of Rajya Sabha

Selection criteria:

- leaders of political parties
- nominees of political parties
- MPs with **special knowledge or interest** in the subject

---


## Tenure of Members


The duration of membership is:


**Co-terminus with their membership in Parliament**


Meaning:

- if the MP's parliamentary membership ends,
- their forum membership also ends.

---


## Resignation from Forum


A member may resign by writing to:

- **Speaker of Lok Sabha** or
- **Chairman of Rajya Sabha**

---


## Member-Convener


Each forum has a **Member-Convener**.


The Convener is appointed by:


**President of the Forum**


Role of Convener:

- conduct meetings
- organise programmes
- coordinate forum activities

Convener works **in consultation with the President**.


---


## Meetings of the Forums


Meetings are held:

- **during sessions of Parliament**
- **whenever necessary**

---


# PART 4 — ADMINISTRATIVE SUPPORT


The **Secretary-General of Lok Sabha** acts as:


**Secretary to all Parliamentary Forums**


Responsibilities include:

- administrative coordination
