
# 📜 THE CONSTITUTION OF INDIA – COMPLETE STRUCTURE (PART I–XXII)

- Adpted on November 26, 1949, and enacted on January 26, 1950. Originally, it featured 395 Articles divided into 22 Parts and 8 Schedules.
- Indian Constitution has undergone 106 amendments.
- Currently  of 25 Parts, 12 Schedules, and 448 Articles As of Dec 2024.

### 📘 ALL 12 SCHEDULES


The Constitution of India contains **12 Schedules**, which act as appendices providing additional details, structural lists, and classifications to support various articles. Originally, there were 8 schedules; four more were added through constitutional amendments. They cover topics ranging from administrative, salary, and oath details to the division of powers, language, and anti-defection laws


| Schedule | Content                             |
| -------- | ----------------------------------- |
| 1st      | States & UTs                        |
| 2nd      | Salaries & allowances               |
| 3rd      | Oaths                               |
| 4th      | Rajya Sabha seats                   |
| 5th      | Scheduled Areas                     |
| 6th      | Tribal Areas                        |
| 7th      | Union/State/Concurrent List         |
| 8th      | 22 Languages                        |
| 9th      | Laws protected from judicial review |
| 10th     | Anti-Defection                      |
| 11th     | Panchayats (29 subjects)            |
| 12th     | Municipalities (18 subjects)        |
