| --------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |

| Amendment             | **7th Constitutional Amendment Act, 1956**     |
| Inserted Article      | **Article 350-B**                              |
| Constitutional Part   | **Part XVII – Official Language**              |
| Chapter               | **Chapter IV – Special Directives**            |


---


# 2. Article 350-B : Constitutional Provisions


## Appointment

- Special Officer for Linguistic Minorities appointed by the **President of India**.

## Duties

- Investigates all matters relating to:
    - Constitutional safeguards for linguistic minorities.

## Reporting Mechanism


Special Officer submits reports to:


**President → Parliament + Concerned State Governments**


Process:

1. Reports submitted to President.
2. President places reports before Parliament.
3. Copies sent to concerned State Governments.

---


# 3. Constitutional Gap (Very Important Prelims Area)


## Constitution DOES NOT specify:

- Qualifications
- Tenure
- Salary & allowances
- Service conditions
- Removal procedure

⚠️ Only **appointment + duties** are constitutionally mentioned.


### Prelims Trap


If asked which are mentioned in Constitution:


✅ Appointment & duties


❌ Tenure / salary / qualifications / removal

# 9. Role of Commissioner


## Main Responsibilities


### Investigates

- Non-implementation of safeguards for linguistic minorities.

### Receives Complaints From

- Individuals
- Groups
- Associations
- Organisations

### Raises Issues At

- Highest political level
- Administrative level
- State Governments
- UT Administrations

### Remedial Function

- Recommends corrective measures.

⚠️ Commissioner only:

- Investigates
- Reports
- Recommends

❌ Does NOT directly enforce safeguards.

# 16. Linguistic Minority : Meaning


## Definition


A group whose:

> Mother tongue differs from majority language of the State or part of State.

---


## Important Principle


⚠️ Linguistic minorities are determined **STATE-WISE**, not nationally.


---


## Examples


| State         | Majority Language | Linguistic Minority     |
| ------------- | ----------------- | ----------------------- |
| Tamil Nadu    | Tamil             | Hindi speakers          |
| Uttar Pradesh | Hindi             | Urdu speakers           |
| Karnataka     | Kannada           | Telugu/Marathi speakers |
