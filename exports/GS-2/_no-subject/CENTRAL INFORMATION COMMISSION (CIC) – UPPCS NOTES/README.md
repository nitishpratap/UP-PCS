
# 1. Central Information Commission (CIC)


### Establishment

- The **Central Information Commission (CIC)** was established by the **Central Government in 2005**.
- It was created through an **Official Gazette Notification**.
- It was constituted under the **Right to Information Act, 2005**.

### Nature of the Body

- It is **NOT a constitutional body**.
- It is a **statutory body** created by an Act of Parliament (RTI Act, 2005).

### Nature and Role


CIC is a **high-powered independent body** which:

- Looks into **complaints regarding access to information**
- Decides **appeals under RTI**
- Ensures implementation of **Right to Information**

### Jurisdiction


The CIC deals with complaints and appeals related to:

- Offices under **Central Government**
- **Financial institutions**
- **Public Sector Undertakings**
- Authorities under **Union Territories**

---


# 2. Composition of Central Information Commission


The Commission consists of:


| Position                             | Number           |
| ------------------------------------ | ---------------- |
| Chief Information Commissioner (CIC) | 1                |
| Information Commissioners (ICs)      | Not more than 10 |


Thus:


**Maximum members = 11**


---


# 3. Appointment of CIC and ICs


They are appointed by:


👉 **President of India**


On recommendation of a **selection committee**.


### Selection Committee


| Member                                 | Role        |
| -------------------------------------- | ----------- |
| Prime Minister                         | Chairperson |
| Leader of Opposition in Lok Sabha      | Member      |
| Union Cabinet Minister nominated by PM | Member      |


---


### Special Provision (Important for Exams)


If **Leader of Opposition is not recognized**:

- **Leader of the largest opposition party in Lok Sabha** becomes member of the committee.

---


# 4. Qualifications


The CIC and Information Commissioners should be:


Persons of **eminence in public life** with knowledge and experience in:

- Law
- Science and Technology
- Social Service
- Management
- Journalism
- Mass Media
- Administration
- Governance

---


# 5. Disqualifications


They **cannot be**:

- Member of Parliament
- Member of State Legislature
- Holding **office of profit**
- Connected with **political party**
- Carrying on **business**
- Pursuing **profession**

---


# 6. Tenure


| Post                           | Tenure            |
| ------------------------------ | ----------------- |
| Chief Information Commissioner | 5 years OR age 65 |
| Information Commissioner       | 5 years OR age 65 |


Whichever occurs **earlier**.


---


### Reappointment

- **Reappointment is NOT allowed.**

However:

- An **Information Commissioner can become Chief Information Commissioner**

But:

- Total tenure **cannot exceed 5 years**.

---


# 7. Removal of CIC and ICs


They can be removed by the **President of India**.


---


## Removal without Supreme Court Inquiry


The President may remove them if:

1. **Adjudged insolvent**
2. **Convicted of an offence involving moral turpitude**
3. Engages in **paid employment outside office**
4. **Unfit due to infirmity of mind or body**
5. **Financial or other interest affecting official functions**

---


## Removal with Supreme Court Inquiry


Grounds:

- **Proved misbehaviour**
- **Incapacity**

Procedure:

1. President refers matter to **Supreme Court**
2. Supreme Court conducts **inquiry**
3. If charges proven → advises President
4. President removes the Commissioner

---


# 8. Misbehaviour (Important Detail)


A person is deemed guilty of **misbehaviour** if he:

- Is **interested in any contract or agreement with Central Government**
- Gains **profit from such contract**
- Participates in **benefits arising from such contract**

Except:

- When holding shares **as a member of a company with others**

---


# 9. Salary and Service Conditions


| Post                           | Equivalent to               |
| ------------------------------ | --------------------------- |
| Chief Information Commissioner | Chief Election Commissioner |
| Information Commissioner       | Election Commissioner       |


### Important Rule


Their salary and conditions **cannot be varied to their disadvantage after appointment**.


---


# 10. Powers and Functions of CIC


The **Central Information Commission** performs several functions under RTI Act.


---


# 1. Receive Complaints


The Commission can receive complaints from any person if:


### (a) No Public Information Officer

- Person cannot submit RTI because **PIO not appointed**

### (b) Refusal of Information

- Requested information **denied**

### (c) No Response

- Applicant **did not receive reply within time limit**

### (d) Unreasonable Fee

- Fees charged for information are **unreasonable**

### (e) Incorrect Information

- Information provided is:
    - incomplete
    - misleading
    - false

### (f) Other RTI-related matters


Any issue related to **obtaining information**.


---


# 2. Power to Order Inquiry


CIC can order **inquiry into any matter** if reasonable grounds exist.


This is called:


👉 **Suo-moto power**


---


# 3. Powers of Civil Court


While conducting inquiry, CIC has powers similar to a **civil court**.


These include:

1. **Summoning persons**
2. **Enforcing attendance**
3. **Compelling evidence**
4. **Producing documents**
5. **Discovery and inspection of documents**
6. **Receiving evidence on affidavit**
7. **Requisitioning public records**
8. **Issuing summons for witnesses**
9. Any other prescribed matter

---


# 4. Access to Records


During inquiry:

- CIC can **examine any record** under control of public authority.

Important rule:


👉 **No record can be withheld from CIC.**


---


# 5. Ensuring Compliance of RTI


CIC can ensure that public authorities comply with RTI Act.


It can order:


### Administrative actions

- Provide **information in specific form**
- **Appoint Public Information Officer**
- **Publish categories of information**

### Institutional reforms

- Improve **record management**
- Improve **record maintenance**
- Improve **record destruction procedures**

### Capacity building

- Increase **training for officials on RTI**

### Monitoring

- Seek **annual report on RTI compliance**

### Compensation

- Order compensation to applicant for **loss suffered**

### Penalty

- Impose **penalty on officials**

### Rejection

- Reject the application if not valid

---


# 6. Penalty Provision (Very Important for Prelims)


Penalty on **Public Information Officer (PIO)**:

- **₹250 per day**

Maximum penalty:


👉 **₹25,000**


Additionally:

- CIC can recommend **disciplinary action** against the official.

---


# 7. Annual Report


CIC prepares:


👉 **Annual report on implementation of RTI Act**


The report is submitted to:


👉 **Central Government**


Then:

- Government places it before **both Houses of Parliament**.

---


# 8. Recommendation Power


If a public authority violates RTI Act:

- CIC can **recommend steps to ensure compliance**.
