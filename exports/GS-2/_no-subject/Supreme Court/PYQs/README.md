# 1️⃣ PRELIMS PYQs (Supreme Court)


Format


**Question — Exam — Year — Answer**


---

1. **The Supreme Court of India was established in:**

    — Union Public Service Commission Prelims — 2011


    ✅ **Answer:** **1950 (28 January)**


---

1. **Which Articles deal with the Supreme Court?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2012


    ✅ **Answer:** **Articles 124–147**


---

1. **The judges of the Supreme Court are appointed by:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2013


    ✅ **Answer:** **President of India**


---

1. **The retirement age of a Supreme Court judge is:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2014


    ✅ **Answer:** **65 years**


---

1. **The Supreme Court is a Court of Record under:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2015


    ✅ **Answer:** **Article 129**


---

1. **Which Article provides for original jurisdiction of Supreme Court?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2016


    ✅ **Answer:** **Article 131**


---

1. **Which Article provides for writ jurisdiction of Supreme Court?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2017


    ✅ **Answer:** **Article 32**


---

1. **Special Leave Petition (SLP) is provided under:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2018


    ✅ **Answer:** **Article 136**


---

1. **Advisory jurisdiction of Supreme Court is under:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2019


    ✅ **Answer:** **Article 143**


---

1. **Which Article states that law declared by Supreme Court is binding on all courts?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2020


    ✅ **Answer:** **Article 141**


---

1. **Which Article empowers Supreme Court to review its own judgement?**

    — State PCS Prelims — 2021


    ✅ **Answer:** **Article 137**


---

1. **Which Article empowers Supreme Court to issue writs for Fundamental Rights?**

    — State PCS Prelims — 2022


    ✅ **Answer:** **Article 32**

# 3️⃣ Additional High-Probability Questions (PCS Pattern)


### Prelims Style


1️⃣ Minimum strength of SC bench for constitutional cases


→ **5 judges**


2️⃣ Seat of Supreme Court


→ **Delhi (Article 130)**


3️⃣ Removal of judge


→ **Impeachment by Parliament**


4️⃣ Special majority for removal


→ **Absolute majority + 2/3 present & voting**


5️⃣ Acting CJI provision


→ **Article 126**


---


### Mains Style


1️⃣ Explain **original, appellate and advisory jurisdiction of SC**.


2️⃣ Discuss **writ jurisdiction and its importance**.


3️⃣ Explain **judicial review and its role in democracy**.


4️⃣ Discuss **independence of judiciary and safeguards**.


5️⃣ Examine **collegium system vs NJAC debate**.
