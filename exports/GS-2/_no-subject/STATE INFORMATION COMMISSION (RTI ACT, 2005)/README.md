
# STATE INFORMATION COMMISSION (RTI ACT, 2005)


## PART 1 — CONCEPT, COMPOSITION & APPOINTMENT


## 1. Background


The **Right to Information Act, 2005** provides for the creation of two types of Information Commissions:

1. **Central Information Commission (CIC)** – at the Union level
2. **State Information Commission (SIC)** – at the State level

Every state government has established a **State Information Commission through an Official Gazette notification**.


The **State Information Commission is a high-powered independent body**.


### Main Role


The Commission:

- Looks into **complaints and appeals related to RTI requests**
- Ensures **implementation of RTI Act in the state**
- Provides citizens a **second appeal mechanism**

It deals with complaints related to:

- State government offices
- State financial institutions
- State public sector undertakings
- Other authorities under the **state government**

---


# 2. Composition of State Information Commission


The Commission consists of:

1. **State Chief Information Commissioner (SCIC)**
2. **Not more than 10 State Information Commissioners (SICs)**

However:

- The exact number of commissioners **varies from state to state** depending on workload.

---


# 3. Appointment


### Appointing Authority


The **Governor of the State** appoints:

- State Chief Information Commissioner
- State Information Commissioners

### Recommendation Committee


Appointments are made on the recommendation of a **three-member committee**:

1. **Chief Minister** — Chairperson
2. **Leader of Opposition in Legislative Assembly**
3. **State Cabinet Minister nominated by the Chief Minister**

### Special Situation


If the **Leader of Opposition is not officially recognized**, then:

- **Leader of the largest opposition party in the Assembly** is treated as the Leader of Opposition.

---


# 4. Qualifications


The members of the Commission should be **persons of eminence in public life**.


They should have **wide knowledge and experience** in areas such as:

- Law
- Science and Technology
- Social Service
- Management
- Journalism
- Mass Media
- Administration
- Governance

---


# 5. Disqualifications


The Chief Information Commissioner and Information Commissioners **cannot**:

- Be **Member of Parliament**
- Be **Member of State Legislature**
- Hold **any office of profit**
- Be connected with **any political party**
- Carry on **any business**
- Pursue **any profession**

This ensures **independence and neutrality of the Commission**.


---


# 6. Tenure


The **State Chief Information Commissioner** and **State Information Commissioners** hold office for:


**5 years OR until the age of 65 years**


(whichever is earlier)


They **are not eligible for reappointment**.


### Important Note


A **State Information Commissioner can later become State Chief Information Commissioner**.


However:


Total tenure **cannot exceed 5 years combined**.


Example:


| Position | Tenure          |
| -------- | --------------- |
| SIC      | 3 years         |
| SCIC     | Maximum 2 years |


---


# 7. Salary and Service Conditions


### Salary Comparison


| Position                             | Salary Equivalent            |
| ------------------------------------ | ---------------------------- |
| State Chief Information Commissioner | Election Commissioner        |
| State Information Commissioner       | Chief Secretary of the State |


Important rule:


Their **salary, allowances and service conditions cannot be varied to their disadvantage during service**.


This ensures **institutional independence**.


---


# PART 2 — REMOVAL, POWERS & FUNCTIONS


---


# 8. Removal of State Information Commissioner


The **Governor can remove the State Chief Information Commissioner or any State Information Commissioner**.


### Removal Grounds


The Governor can remove them if they:

1. Are **adjudged insolvent**
2. Are **convicted of an offence involving moral turpitude**
3. Engage in **paid employment outside official duties**
4. Are **unfit due to infirmity of mind or body**
5. Acquire **financial or other interests affecting their functions**

---


# 9. Removal for Misbehaviour or Incapacity


In cases of:

- **Proved misbehaviour**
- **Incapacity**

The procedure is different.


### Process

1. Governor **refers the matter to the Supreme Court**
2. Supreme Court conducts an **enquiry**
3. If the Court **upholds the charges**
4. Governor can **remove the Commissioner**

This process ensures **judicial protection of independence**.


---


# 10. Meaning of Misbehaviour (Important for Exams)


A Commissioner is deemed guilty of **misbehaviour** if:


He is:

- Concerned or interested in **any contract or agreement with the state government**
- Participates in **profit from such contracts**
- Receives **benefit or emolument from such agreements**

Exception:


Allowed only if he is **a member of an incorporated company like other shareholders**.


---


# 11. Powers and Functions of the State Information Commission


The Commission has several powers under the RTI Act.


---


# (1) Handling Complaints


The Commission receives complaints from people who:

1. Cannot submit RTI request due to **non-appointment of Public Information Officer**
2. Were **refused information**
3. Did not receive reply **within prescribed time**
4. Believe **fees charged are unreasonable**
5. Believe **information provided is false or misleading**
6. Have **any other problem related to obtaining information**

---


# (2) Suo Motu Inquiry


The Commission can **order an inquiry on its own (suo-motu)** if there are **reasonable grounds**.


This means it can act **without a formal complaint**.


---


# (3) Powers of a Civil Court


During inquiry, the Commission enjoys powers of a **Civil Court**.


These include:

1. **Summoning and enforcing attendance**
2. Compelling persons to give **oral or written evidence**
3. **Discovery and inspection of documents**
4. **Receiving evidence on affidavit**
5. **Requisitioning public records from courts or offices**
6. **Issuing summons for witnesses or documents**

---


# (4) Access to Public Records


During inquiry:


The Commission may **examine any public record under control of a public authority**.


Important rule:


**No public authority can withhold records from the Commission.**


This ensures **transparency and accountability**.


---


# (5) Power to Enforce Compliance


The Commission can ensure compliance with its decisions.


It may order the public authority to:

1. Provide **information in specific form**
2. **Appoint Public Information Officer**
3. **Publish information categories**
4. Change **record management practices**
5. Improve **RTI training for officials**
6. Submit **annual compliance report**
7. **Compensate applicant** for losses
8. **Impose penalties**
9. **Reject application**

---


# 12. Penalty Provision


The Commission can impose penalty on **Public Information Officer (PIO)**.


Penalty rate:


**₹250 per day**


Maximum limit:


**₹25,000**


It can also **recommend disciplinary action** against the officer.


---


# 13. Annual Report


The State Information Commission submits an **annual report to the State Government** regarding:

- Implementation of RTI Act
- Performance of public authorities

The **State Government places this report before the State Legislature**.


---


# 14. Advisory Role


If a public authority **fails to comply with RTI Act**, the Commission can:

- Recommend **corrective steps**
- Suggest measures to **improve compliance**
