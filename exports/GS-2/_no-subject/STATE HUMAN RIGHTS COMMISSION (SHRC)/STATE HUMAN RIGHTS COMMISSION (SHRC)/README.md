
_(Protection of Human Rights Act, 1993)_


---


# 1. LEGAL BASIS


| Aspect        | Details                                               |
| ------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Governing Law | **Protection of Human Rights Act, 1993**              |
| Amendment     | **2006 Amendment**                                    |
| Provides For  | **NHRC + SHRC + Human Rights Courts**                 |
| Objective     | Protection & promotion of human rights at State level |


---


# 2. ESTABLISHMENT OF SHRC


| Aspect         | Details                                   |
| -------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| Established By | **State Government**                      |
| Mode           | Through **Official Gazette Notification** |


### States having SHRC


Assam, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Bihar, Chhattisgarh, Gujarat, Goa, Himachal Pradesh, Jammu & Kashmir, Kerala, Karnataka, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Manipur, Odisha, Punjab, Rajasthan, Tamil Nadu, Uttar Pradesh, West Bengal, Jharkhand, Sikkim, Uttarakhand, Haryana, Tripura.


---


# 3. JURISDICTION OF SHRC


| Can Inquire Into                                  | Cannot Inquire Into                         |
| ------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| Matters related to **State List (List II)**       | Matters already under inquiry of NHRC       |
| Matters related to **Concurrent List (List III)** | Matters before another statutory commission |


### Important

- Jurisdiction derived from **Seventh Schedule**.
- SHRC deals only with **human rights violations** connected to state/concurrent subjects.

---


# 4. COMPOSITION


### Multi-member Body → Total = **3 Members**


| Post        | Qualification                                                                                                   |
| ----------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Chairperson | Retired Chief Justice of High Court                                                                             |
| Member      | Serving/Retired High Court Judge                                                                                |
| Member      | District Judge (minimum 7 years experience) **OR** person having knowledge/practical experience in human rights |


### 2006 Amendment

- Members reduced from **5 → 3**
- Eligibility conditions modified.

---


# 5. APPOINTMENT


| Aspect               | Details                               |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Appointing Authority | **Governor**                          |
| Basis                | Recommendation of Selection Committee |


## Selection Committee


| Member                                       | Role        |
| -------------------------------------------- | ----------- |
| Chief Minister                               | Chairperson |
| Speaker of Legislative Assembly              | Member      |
| State Home Minister                          | Member      |
| Leader of Opposition in Legislative Assembly | Member      |


### If State has Legislative Council


Additional Members:

- Chairman of Legislative Council
- Leader of Opposition in Legislative Council

### Important


For appointment of:

- Sitting High Court Judge
- Sitting District Judge

➡ **Consultation with Chief Justice of High Court mandatory**.


---


# 6. TENURE


| Aspect        | Details                     |
| ------------- | --------------------------- |
| Term          | 5 years OR 70 years of age  |
| Rule          | Whichever earlier           |
| Reappointment | Allowed for another 5 years |
| Maximum Age   | 70 years                    |


---


# 7. POST-TENURE RESTRICTION


After tenure, Chairperson/Members:


❌ Cannot accept employment under:

- Central Government
- State Government

### Objective


Ensure:

- Independence
- Autonomy
- Impartiality

---


# 8. REMOVAL


| Aspect       | Details       |
| ------------ | ------------- |
| Appointed By | Governor      |
| Removed By   | **President** |


### Removal Procedure


Similar to **NHRC Members**.


---


## Grounds for Removal


President may remove if member:

1. Adjudged insolvent
2. Engages in paid employment outside office duties
3. Infirmity of mind/body
4. Declared unsound mind by court
5. Convicted & sentenced to imprisonment

---


## Removal for Misbehaviour/Incapacity


### Procedure

1. President refers matter to Supreme Court
2. Supreme Court conducts inquiry
3. SC upholds charges
4. President removes member

---


# 9. SALARY & SERVICE CONDITIONS


| Aspect        | Details                                            |
| ------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Determined By | State Government                                   |
| Protection    | Cannot be varied to disadvantage after appointment |


### Purpose


Maintain:

- Independence
- Neutrality
- Functional autonomy

---


# 10. FUNCTIONS OF SHRC


## A. Inquiry into Human Rights Violations


Commission may inquire into:

1. Violation of human rights
2. Negligence by public servant in preventing violation

### Mode of Inquiry

- Suo motu
- Petition/complaint
- Court direction

---


## B. Intervention in Court Proceedings


May intervene in cases involving:

- Alleged human rights violations

---


## C. Jail & Detention Centre Inspection


Purpose:

- Study prison conditions
- Recommend improvements

---


## D. Review of Safeguards


Reviews:

- Constitutional safeguards
- Legal safeguards

Recommends:

- Better implementation measures

---


## E. Review Factors Inhibiting Human Rights


Includes:

- Terrorism
- Other obstructive factors

Suggests remedial measures.


---


## F. Research Promotion

- Conducts human rights research
- Encourages academic/institutional research

---


## G. Human Rights Awareness


Promotes:

- Human rights literacy
- Awareness of safeguards

---


## H. Encouraging NGOs


Supports NGOs working in:

- Human rights protection

---


## I. Other Necessary Functions


Can perform functions necessary for promotion of human rights.


---


# 11. WORKING & POWERS


## Procedural Power

- Can regulate its own procedure.

---


## Powers of Civil Court


During inquiry, SHRC has powers of Civil Court:


✔ Summon witnesses


✔ Examine under oath


✔ Receive evidence


✔ Demand documents


### Important

- Proceedings are **judicial in nature**.

---


## Power to Seek Information


Can seek reports/information from:

- State Government
- Subordinate authorities

---


# 12. LIMITATION ON INQUIRY


| Aspect      | Rule                                  |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Time Limit  | Complaint must be filed within 1 year |
| Older Cases | Cannot investigate                    |


---


# 13. ACTIONS AFTER INQUIRY


## 1. Recommend Compensation


May recommend:

- Compensation
- Damages to victim

---


## 2. Recommend Prosecution


May recommend:

- Prosecution of guilty public servant
- Departmental/disciplinary action

---


## 3. Recommend Interim Relief


Can suggest:

- Immediate interim relief to victim

---


## 4. Approach Constitutional Courts


May approach:

- Supreme Court
- High Court

For:

- Directions
- Orders
- Writs

---


# 14. NATURE OF SHRC POWERS ⚠️


## Recommendatory Nature


### SHRC Cannot:


❌ Punish violators


❌ Enforce compensation directly


❌ Issue binding orders


---


## Recommendations Not Binding


Government is not legally bound to accept recommendations.


### But:


Government must inform SHRC within **1 month** regarding:

- Action taken
- Reasons for rejection

---

# MAINS VALUE-ADDED POINTS ✍️


## Importance of SHRC

- Protects citizens from state excesses
- Strengthens constitutionalism & rule of law
- Improves prison administration
- Enhances accountability of public servants
- Promotes human rights culture

---


## Limitations of SHRC

- Recommendatory powers only
- Dependence on state government
- Delay in appointments
- Resource/manpower shortage
- Low implementation of recommendations

---


# ONE-LINE EXAM TRAPS ⚠️

- SHRC established by **State Government**, not Constitution.
- Chairperson must be **retired Chief Justice of HC**, not Supreme Court Judge.
- Appointed by **Governor**, removed by **President**.
- SHRC jurisdiction → **State + Concurrent List only**.
- SHRC has **Civil Court powers**, but recommendations are **non-binding**.
- Complaints older than **1 year** cannot be investigated.
- Human Rights Courts established with concurrence of **Chief Justice of HC**.
