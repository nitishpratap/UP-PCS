# National Commission for Scheduled Tribes (NCST)


---


# 1. National Commission for Scheduled Tribes (NCST)


The **National Commission for Scheduled Tribes (NCST)** is a **constitutional body** established to safeguard the interests of **Scheduled Tribes (STs)**.

- It is **directly established by Article 338-A of the Constitution**.
- The commission monitors the **constitutional and legal safeguards provided to Scheduled Tribes**.

📌 **Constitutional Basis**


| Feature              | Details                                                       |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Article              | **Article 338-A**                                             |
| Part of Constitution | **Part XVI – Special Provisions relating to Certain Classes** |
| Nature               | **Constitutional Body**                                       |


---


# 2. Background: Creation of the Commission


Originally, there was a **combined commission for SCs and STs**.


### 65th Constitutional Amendment Act, 1990

- Created **National Commission for SCs and STs**.
- Established under **Article 338**.
- Objective:
    - Monitor safeguards provided for **Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes**.

### Reasons for Creating a Separate Commission for STs


Scheduled Tribes have **distinct characteristics and problems**, such as:

- Geographical isolation
- Cultural differences
- Forest dependence
- Land alienation
- Development displacement

Therefore, it was felt that **ST issues require focused attention**.


---


# 3. Creation of Ministry of Tribal Affairs (1999)


In **1999**, the Government of India created a separate ministry:


### Ministry of Tribal Affairs


Purpose:

- Focus specifically on **tribal welfare and development**.
- Coordinate activities relating to **Scheduled Tribes**.

Reason:

- It was **not administratively feasible** for the **Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment** to manage both **SC and ST issues simultaneously**.

📌 Note


| Ministry                                 | Responsibility   |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------- |
| Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment | Scheduled Castes |
| Ministry of Tribal Affairs               | Scheduled Tribes |


---


# 4. 89th Constitutional Amendment Act, 2003


To ensure better protection for STs:


The **89th Constitutional Amendment Act, 2003**:

- **Bifurcated** the National Commission for SCs and STs.
- Created a **separate commission for STs**.

### Constitutional Changes


| Change       | Details  |
| ------------ | -------- |
| Article 338  | Amended  |
| Article 338A | Inserted |


Article **338A** created the **National Commission for Scheduled Tribes**.


📌 **Effective Date**


The Act came into force on **19 February 2004**.


---


# 5. Establishment of NCST


The **National Commission for Scheduled Tribes** came into existence in:


➡ **2004**


---


# 6. Composition of the Commission


The NCST consists of:

1. Chairperson
2. Vice-Chairperson
3. Three other Members

Total Members = **5**


---


# 7. Appointment


Members of the Commission are:

- **Appointed by the President of India**
- By **warrant under his hand and seal**

---


# 8. Tenure and Conditions of Service

- Determined by the **President of India**.
- According to rules:

📌 **Tenure = 3 years**


---


# 9. Functions of the National Commission for STs


The functions are mainly related to **safeguarding tribal rights and development**.


### 1. Investigation and Monitoring


The Commission investigates and monitors:

- All matters related to **constitutional safeguards**
- All matters related to **legal safeguards for STs**

---


### 2. Inquiry into Complaints


The Commission can:

- Inquire into **specific complaints**
- Related to **deprivation of rights and safeguards of STs**

---


### 3. Participation in Development Planning


The Commission:

- Participates in **planning of socio-economic development of STs**
- Advises on development policies

---


### 4. Evaluation of Development Progress


The Commission evaluates the **progress of development programs** for STs under:

- Union Government
- State Governments

---


### 5. Submission of Reports


The Commission submits reports:

- **Annually**
- Or **whenever required**

These reports are submitted to the **President of India**.


---


### 6. Recommendations


The Commission recommends:


Measures for:

- Effective implementation of safeguards
- Protection of STs
- Welfare of STs
- Socio-economic development

---


### 7. Additional Functions Assigned by President


The President may assign additional functions related to:

- Protection
- Welfare
- Development
- Advancement of STs

---


# 10. Additional Functions (Specified in 2005)


In **2005**, the President assigned additional functions to the Commission.


These are provided under:


**National Commission for the Scheduled Tribes (Specification of Other Functions) Rules, 2005**


---


## Important Additional Functions


### 1. Forest Produce Rights


Measures regarding:

- Ownership rights of **minor forest produce**
- For STs living in forest areas.

---


### 2. Tribal Rights over Natural Resources


Measures to safeguard rights over:

- Mineral resources
- Water resources
- Other natural resources.

---


### 3. Tribal Livelihood Development


Measures for:

- Improving livelihood strategies
- Tribal economic development.

---


### 4. Rehabilitation of Displaced Tribals


Measures to improve:

- Relief and rehabilitation
- For STs displaced by **development projects**.

---


### 5. Prevent Land Alienation


Measures to:

- Prevent tribal land alienation
- Rehabilitate tribals who lost their land.

---


### 6. Forest Protection and Social Afforestation


Measures to:

- Increase tribal participation in forest protection
- Promote social afforestation.

---


### 7. Implementation of PESA Act, 1996


Ensure full implementation of:


📌 **Panchayats (Extension to the Scheduled Areas) Act, 1996**


---


### 8. Elimination of Shifting Cultivation


Measures to reduce and eventually eliminate:


➡ **Shifting cultivation**


Reasons:

- Causes environmental degradation
- Leads to tribal economic instability.

---


# 11. Reports of the Commission


The Commission submits reports to the **President of India**.


### Types of Reports

1. **Annual Report**
2. **Special Reports (whenever required)**

---


# 12. Procedure After Report Submission


### Role of the President


The President:

1. Places the report before **Parliament**.
2. Submits a **memorandum** explaining:
    - Action taken on recommendations.
    - Reasons for **non-acceptance of recommendations**.

---


# 13. Reports Related to States


If the report concerns a **state government**:


The President sends the report to:


➡ **Governor of the State**


---


### Role of the Governor


The Governor:

- Places the report before the **State Legislature**
- Along with a memorandum explaining:
    - Action taken
    - Reasons for rejection of recommendations.

---


# 14. Powers of the Commission


The Commission has powers similar to a **Civil Court**.


It can regulate **its own procedure**.


---


## Civil Court Powers


While investigating complaints, the Commission can:


### 1. Summon Persons

- Summon and enforce attendance of any person from **any part of India**.

---


### 2. Examine Witnesses

- Examine individuals **on oath**.

---


### 3. Demand Documents

- Require **discovery and production of documents**.

---


### 4. Receive Evidence

- Accept **evidence on affidavits**.

---


### 5. Requisition Public Records

- Obtain records from:
    - Courts
    - Government offices

---


### 6. Issue Summons

- Summon witnesses
- Summon documents.

---


### 7. Other Powers


Any other power **determined by the President**.


---


# 15. Consultation with the Commission


Both:

- **Central Government**
- **State Governments**

must **consult the Commission** on **major policy matters affecting Scheduled Tribes**.
