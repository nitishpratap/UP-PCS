| Effective From       | **19 February 2004**                                          |
| Part of Constitution | **Part XVI – Special Provisions relating to Certain Classes** |
| Purpose              | Safeguards interests of **Scheduled Tribes (STs)**            |


---


# 2. Historical Background


## Earlier Arrangement


Initially there was a **combined commission for SCs & STs**.


| Amendment                                   | Provision                                                       |
| ------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **65th Constitutional Amendment Act, 1990** | Created National Commission for SCs & STs under **Article 338** |


---


## Why Separate Commission for STs?


STs have unique issues:

- Geographical isolation
- Cultural distinctiveness
- Forest dependence
- Land alienation
- Displacement due to development projects

➡ Hence separate focused body required.


---


# 3. Ministry of Tribal Affairs (1999)


| Ministry                                 | Responsibility   |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------- |
| Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment | Scheduled Castes |
| Ministry of Tribal Affairs               | Scheduled Tribes |


### Reason for Separate Ministry


Managing SC & ST issues together became administratively difficult.


---


# 4. 89th Constitutional Amendment Act, 2003


## Major Changes


| Provision    | Change   |
| ------------ | -------- |
| Article 338  | Amended  |
| Article 338A | Inserted |


➡ Created separate:


# **National Commission for Scheduled Tribes (NCST)**

# 6. Appointment & Tenure


| Feature              | Details                        |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Appointing Authority | **President of India**         |
| Method               | By warrant under hand and seal |
| Tenure               | **3 years**                    |
| Service Conditions   | Determined by President        |


---


# 7. Functions of NCST


## A. Investigation & Monitoring

- Investigates constitutional safeguards for STs
- Monitors legal safeguards for STs

---


## B. Inquiry into Complaints


Can investigate complaints regarding:

- Deprivation of rights
- Violation of safeguards of STs

---


## C. Socio-Economic Development

- Participates in planning process
- Advises on ST development policies

---


## D. Evaluation Function


Evaluates development progress under:

- Union Government
- State Governments

---


## E. Reporting Function


Submits reports to President:

- Annually
- Special reports whenever necessary

---


## F. Recommendation Function


Recommends measures for:

- Protection
- Welfare
- Development
- Effective implementation of safeguards

---


## G. Additional Functions


President may assign additional functions relating to:

- Protection
- Welfare
- Advancement
- Development of STs

---


# 8. Additional Functions (2005 Rules)


## National Commission for Scheduled Tribes (Specification of Other Functions) Rules, 2005


### Important Additional Functions


| Area                 | Function                                                |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| Minor Forest Produce | Ownership rights for forest tribes                      |
| Natural Resources    | Safeguard rights over minerals, water & resources       |
| Livelihood           | Improve tribal livelihood strategies                    |
| Rehabilitation       | Relief & rehabilitation of displaced tribals            |
| Land Alienation      | Prevent tribal land loss & rehabilitate affected tribes |
| Forest Protection    | Increase tribal participation & social afforestation    |
| PESA Act, 1996       | Ensure implementation                                   |
| Shifting Cultivation | Reduce & eliminate shifting cultivation                 |


---


# 9. Reports of Commission


## Types

1. Annual Reports
2. Special Reports

---


# 10. Procedure After Submission of Report


## Role of President


President places report before Parliament along with memorandum containing:

- Action taken on recommendations
- Reasons for non-acceptance

---


## Reports Relating to States


President forwards report to Governor.


### Governor:

- Places report before State Legislature
- Explains:
    - Action taken
    - Reasons for rejection/non-acceptance

---


# 11. Powers of NCST


## Status


➡ Powers of a **Civil Court**


➡ Can regulate its own procedure


---


## Civil Court Powers


| Power                  | Details                    |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------- |
| Summon Persons         | From any part of India     |
| Examine on Oath        | Witness examination        |
| Discovery of Documents | Demand production          |
| Evidence on Affidavit  | Accept affidavits          |
| Requisition Records    | From courts/offices        |
| Issue Summons          | Witnesses & documents      |
| Other Powers           | As determined by President |


---


# 12. Consultation with NCST


Both:

- Central Government
- State Governments

must consult NCST on:


➡ Major policy matters affecting Scheduled Tribes.
