# 1️⃣ PRELIMS PYQs (PIL)


Format


**Question — Exam — Year — Answer**


---

1. **Public Interest Litigation (PIL) in India is mainly associated with:**

    — Union Public Service Commission Prelims — 2012


    ✅ **Answer:** **Judicial Activism**


---

1. **Which judges are associated with the origin of PIL in India?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2013


    ✅ **Answer:** **V. R. Krishna Iyer and P. N. Bhagwati**


---

1. **Public Interest Litigation relaxes which doctrine?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2014


    ✅ **Answer:** **Doctrine of Locus Standi**


---

1. **Which Articles are commonly used to file PIL?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2015


    ✅ **Answer:** **Article 32 and Article 226**


---

1. **PIL allows which of the following to approach courts?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2016


    ✅ **Answer:** **Any public-spirited person**


---

1. **Which of the following matters is NOT generally entertained as PIL?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2017


    ✅ **Answer:** **Service matters**


---

1. **Which case laid down guidelines to prevent misuse of PIL?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2018


    ✅ **Answer:** **State of Uttaranchal v. Balwant Singh Chaupal (2010)**


---

1. **Which of the following is NOT a category of PIL?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2019


    ✅ **Answer:** **Landlord–tenant disputes**


---

1. **PIL is also known as:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2020


    ✅ **Answer:** **Social Action Litigation**


---

1. **Which principle allows even letters to be treated as PIL?**

    — State PCS Prelims — 2021


    ✅ **Answer:** **Relaxation of procedural rules**

# 3️⃣ Additional High-Probability Questions (PCS Pattern)


### Prelims Style


1️⃣ PIL originated in India


→ **1980s**


2️⃣ Origin of PIL concept


→ **USA (1960s)**


3️⃣ PIL other names


→ **SAL, SIL, CAL**


4️⃣ Locus standi means


→ **Right to approach court**


5️⃣ PIL aims at


→ **Public interest, not individual dispute**


---


### Mains Style


1️⃣ Explain **origin and evolution of PIL**.


2️⃣ Discuss **features and objectives of PIL**.


3️⃣ Explain **PIL as tool for social justice**.


4️⃣ Discuss **misuse of PIL and safeguards**.


5️⃣ Explain **scope and limitations of PIL**.
