# 1. ESTABLISHMENT & LEGAL STATUS


| Topic                          | Fact                                                          |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Established                    | **1963**                                                      |
| Created by                     | Resolution of Ministry of Home Affairs                        |
| Present Administrative Control | **DoPT**, Ministry of Personnel, Public Grievances & Pensions |
| Nature                         | **Non-Statutory Body**                                        |
| Legal Powers Derived From      | **Delhi Special Police Establishment Act (DSPE Act), 1946**   |
| Parent Earlier Agency          | **Special Police Establishment (SPE), 1941**                  |
| SPE Purpose                    | Vigilance & corruption in wartime procurement                 |
| Recommendation for CBI         | **Santhanam Committee (1962–64)**                             |
| Type                           | Main investigative agency of Central Government               |

# 14. PRIOR PERMISSION ISSUE — SECTION 6A DSPE ACT


## Earlier Provision


CBI required prior Central Government approval before investigating:


➡ Officers of Joint Secretary rank & above.


---


## Supreme Court Judgment (6 May 2014)


### SC struck down:


➡ **Section 6A of DSPE Act**


### Ground


Violation of:


➡ **Article 14 (Equality before Law)**


---


## Important Observations

- Corruption = enemy of nation
- No special protection for senior bureaucrats
- Rule of Law applies equally

# 21. VERY IMPORTANT ONE-LINERS

- CBI is **not a constitutional body**.
- CBI is **not a statutory body**.
- CBI derives powers from **DSPE Act, 1946**.
- CBI works under **DoPT**.
- CBI acts as **NCB of Interpol in India**.
- CVC supervises CBI in corruption cases under PC Act.
- Director gets fixed tenure of **2 years**.
- Section 6A DSPE Act struck down in **2014**.
- CBI Academy located at **Ghaziabad**.
- Santhanam Committee recommended establishment of CBI.
