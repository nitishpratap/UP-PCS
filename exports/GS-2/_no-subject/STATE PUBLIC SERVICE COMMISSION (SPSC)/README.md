
# STATE PUBLIC SERVICE COMMISSION (SPSC)


## Constitutional Provisions

- **Articles Covered:** **315 – 323**
- **Part:** **Part XIV – Services under the Union and the States**

These articles deal with:

- Composition
- Appointment
- Removal
- Functions
- Independence
- Reports of Public Service Commission

### Key Principle


Just like **UPSC at the Centre**, every state has its own **State Public Service Commission (SPSC)**.


---


# 1. COMPOSITION OF STATE PUBLIC SERVICE COMMISSION


### Structure


SPSC consists of:

- **Chairman**
- **Other Members**

### Appointment

- **Appointed by:** **Governor of the State**

### Number of Members

- **Not fixed by Constitution**
- Decided by **Governor**

### Qualification


The Constitution **does not prescribe specific qualifications**.


However:


At least **50% of members must have held office for 10 years** under:

- Government of India

    or

- State Government

### Conditions of Service

- Determined by the **Governor**

---


# 2. TENURE OF MEMBERS


### Term of Office


Members hold office for:

- **6 years**

    OR

- **Until age of 62 years**

Whichever occurs **earlier**.


### Comparison with UPSC


| Commission | Retirement Age |
| ---------- | -------------- |
| UPSC       | 65 years       |
| SPSC       | 62 years       |


### Resignation


Chairman or member can resign by writing to:


➡ **Governor**


---


# 3. ACTING CHAIRMAN


The **Governor appoints an Acting Chairman** when:


1️⃣ Chairman post becomes **vacant**


2️⃣ Chairman **cannot perform duties** due to:

- Absence
- Illness
- Any other reason

### Duration


Acting chairman serves until:

- New chairman assumes office

    OR

- Existing chairman resumes duties

---


# 4. REMOVAL OF SPSC MEMBERS


Important **UPPCS Prelims fact**


Even though the **Governor appoints them**, they are **removed only by the President**.


This ensures **independence**.


---


## Grounds of Removal


The **President can remove** a member if:


### 1. Insolvency


If the member is declared **bankrupt**


### 2. Paid Employment


If he engages in **paid employment outside official duties**


### 3. Infirmity


If the President believes he is **unfit due to mental or physical infirmity**


---


## Removal for Misbehaviour


If removal is due to **misbehaviour**:


1️⃣ President refers the case to **Supreme Court**


2️⃣ Supreme Court conducts **enquiry**


3️⃣ If SC recommends removal → President removes the member


### Important Point


Advice of **Supreme Court is binding on President**.


---


### Suspension During Enquiry


While enquiry is ongoing:


➡ **Governor can suspend the member**


until the final decision of the President.


---


# 5. MEANING OF MISBEHAVIOUR


A member is guilty of **misbehaviour** if he:


### 1. Has Interest in Government Contract


Directly or indirectly involved in:

- Contract
- Agreement with Government

### 2. Gains Profit from Government Contract


If he:

- Participates in profit
- Gains benefit

except as:

- Member of an **incorporated company**
- With other shareholders

---


# 6. INDEPENDENCE OF SPSC


The Constitution provides several safeguards.


## 1. Security of Tenure


Members can be removed only:

- By **President**
- On **specific constitutional grounds**

---


## 2. Service Conditions Cannot Be Changed


After appointment:


Service conditions **cannot be altered to their disadvantage**.


---


## 3. Expenses Charged on Consolidated Fund


Expenses of SPSC are charged on:


➡ **Consolidated Fund of the State**


Includes:

- Salaries
- Allowances
- Pensions

Important effect:

- **State legislature cannot vote on them**

This ensures financial independence.


---


## 4. Post-Retirement Restrictions (Chairman)


After leaving office:


Chairman of SPSC can become:

- Chairman of UPSC
- Member of UPSC
- Chairman of another SPSC

But **cannot hold any other government job**.


---


## 5. Post-Retirement Restrictions (Member)


After leaving office, a **member of SPSC** can become:

- Chairman or member of **UPSC**
- Chairman of **same SPSC**
- Chairman of **another SPSC**

But cannot hold **any other government job**.


---


## 6. No Reappointment


Chairman or member:


❌ Cannot be reappointed to **same office**


Meaning:

- **No second term**

---


# 7. FUNCTIONS OF SPSC


SPSC performs **similar functions for state services** as UPSC does for central services.


---


## 1. Conducts Examinations


SPSC conducts exams for recruitment to:


➡ **State Civil Services**


Example:

- UPPCS
- State Engineering Services
- State Judicial Services (in consultation)

---


## 2. Consultation in Personnel Matters


Government must consult SPSC regarding:


### (1) Recruitment Methods


How recruitment should be conducted for:

- Civil services
- Civil posts

---


### (2) Appointment Principles


Principles for:

- Appointments
- Promotions
- Transfers between services

---


### (3) Suitability of Candidates


Consultation required for:

- Appointments
- Promotions
- Transfers
- Deputation

Departments recommend candidates and SPSC **ratifies them**.


---


### (4) Disciplinary Matters


For civil servants serving the state government.


Includes penalties like:

- **Censure**
- **Withholding increments**
- **Withholding promotion**
- **Recovery of financial loss**
- **Reduction in rank (demotion)**
- **Compulsory retirement**
- **Removal from service**
- **Dismissal from service**

Important difference:


| Removal                              | Dismissal                               |
| ------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------- |
| Future government employment allowed | Future government employment prohibited |


---


### (5) Legal Expense Claims


If a civil servant:

- Defends legal proceedings
- For acts done during official duty

SPSC is consulted regarding **reimbursement**.


---


### (6) Pension for Injury


Consultation for:

- Pension claims
- Injuries during government service

---


### (7) Any Other Personnel Matters


State government may consult SPSC on other issues.


---


# Supreme Court Judgement (Important for Mains)


If government **fails to consult SPSC**:


➡ Decision **does not become invalid**


Meaning:


Consultation with SPSC is:


**Directory, not mandatory**


Also:


Selection by SPSC **does not give candidate automatic right to job**.


Government must still act **fairly and without arbitrariness**.


---


# 8. EXTENSION OF FUNCTIONS OF SPSC


The **State Legislature** can expand the functions of the SPSC.


### Constitutional Provision


**Article 321**


### Meaning


The legislature can assign **additional functions** to the SPSC regarding:

- Services of the state
- Personnel system of local authorities
- Public institutions

### Example


State Legislature may bring recruitment of employees of:

- Municipal corporations
- Local bodies
- Public corporations

under the **jurisdiction of SPSC**.


Thus, SPSC jurisdiction **can be extended by law**.


---


# 9. ANNUAL REPORT OF SPSC


Every year:

- **SPSC submits its report to the Governor**

### After that


The **Governor lays the report before the State Legislature**.


This report must include:

1. **Work done by SPSC**
2. **Cases where government rejected SPSC advice**
3. **Reasons for rejection**

### Important Point


The governor presents:

- **Report of SPSC**
- **Memorandum explaining rejection of advice**

before **both Houses of the State Legislature**.


---


# 10. LIMITATIONS OF SPSC


There are certain matters where **consultation with SPSC is not required**.


---


## 1. Reservation Matters


SPSC is **not consulted** while making reservations for:

- **Backward Classes**

---


## 2. SC/ST Claims


Consultation not required while considering:

- Claims of **Scheduled Castes**
- Claims of **Scheduled Tribes**

in government appointments.


---


## 3. Exemption by Governor


The **Governor can exclude posts or services** from SPSC consultation.


### How?


By making **regulations**.


These are known as:


### **SPSC (Exemption from Consultation) Regulations**


---


### Legislative Control


All such regulations must be placed before:


➡ **State Legislature**


for **at least 14 days**.


The legislature can:

- **Amend**
- **Repeal**

these regulations.


---


# 11. ROLE OF SPSC


The Constitution considers SPSC as:


## **“Watchdog of Merit System”**


Meaning:


It ensures that **recruitment is based on merit**.


---


## Core Role


SPSC mainly handles:

- Recruitment to state services
- Advice on promotions
- Advice on disciplinary matters

---


## What SPSC Does NOT Handle


These functions are performed by the **Department of Personnel** or **General Administration Department (GAD)**.


SPSC does not deal with:

- Classification of services
- Pay structure
- Service conditions
- Cadre management
- Training

Thus:


| Institution             | Role                 |
| ----------------------- | -------------------- |
| SPSC                    | Recruitment agency   |
| Department of Personnel | Personnel management |


---


# 12. NATURE OF SPSC ADVICE


Important **UPPCS concept**


Advice of SPSC is:


### **Advisory, not binding**


Meaning:


State government can:

- Accept
- Reject

the recommendations.


---


### Safeguard


Government must explain to **State Legislature** if it rejects advice.


This ensures **accountability**.


---


# 13. STATE VIGILANCE COMMISSION ISSUE


In **1964**, the **State Vigilance Commission (SVC)** was created.


This affected the role of SPSC in disciplinary matters.


### Why?


Both bodies may be consulted regarding:

- Disciplinary action against civil servants

### Problem


They may give **conflicting advice**.


### Which body prevails?


SPSC has an **edge** because:

- It is a **constitutional body**

Whereas SVC is generally a **statutory or executive body**.


---


# 14. ROLE IN JUDICIAL SERVICES


The **Governor consults SPSC** while framing rules for:


➡ **Appointment to judicial services**


However, this excludes:


❌ **District Judges**


Because district judges are governed by **Articles 233–234**.


---


### Consultation Required With


Two bodies must be consulted:

1. **State Public Service Commission**
2. **High Court of the State**

---


# 15. JOINT STATE PUBLIC SERVICE COMMISSION (JSPSC)


The Constitution allows creation of a **Joint Public Service Commission**.


---


## Constitutional Provision


**Article 315**


---


## Creation


Unlike UPSC and SPSC, JSPSC is **not directly created by the Constitution**.


It is created by:


➡ **Act of Parliament**


after request by **State Legislatures concerned**.


---


### Nature


| Commission | Nature         |
| ---------- | -------------- |
| UPSC       | Constitutional |
| SPSC       | Constitutional |
| JSPSC      | Statutory      |


---


### Example


After **creation of Haryana in 1966**, Punjab and Haryana briefly had a **Joint Public Service Commission**.


---


# 16. APPOINTMENT IN JSPSC


Chairman and members are appointed by:


➡ **President of India**


---


# 17. TENURE OF JSPSC MEMBERS


Members hold office for:

- **6 years**

    OR

- **Age of 62 years**

Whichever is earlier.


---


# 18. RESIGNATION


Members resign by writing to:


➡ **President**


---


# 19. REMOVAL


Members of JSPSC are:

- **Suspended**
- **Removed**

by the **President**.


---


# 20. NUMBER OF MEMBERS


The **President determines**:

- Number of members
- Conditions of service

---


# 21. REPORT OF JSPSC


JSPSC submits its **annual report** to:


➡ Governors of all concerned states.


Each governor then presents the report to:


➡ **State Legislature**


---


# 22. UPSC SERVING STATES


UPSC can also assist states.


### Condition

- Request by **Governor**
- Approval by **President**

Thus UPSC may perform functions of **SPSC for a state**.


---


# 23. HISTORICAL BACKGROUND


### Government of India Act 1919


Provision for creation of **Public Service Commission**.


---


### 1926


First **Central Public Service Commission** was established.


Purpose:


Recruit **civil servants**.


---


### Government of India Act 1935


Provided for:

1. **Federal Public Service Commission**
2. **Provincial Public Service Commission**
3. **Joint Public Service Commission**

These later evolved into:

- UPSC
- SPSC
- JSPSC

# 26. IMPORTANT SUPREME COURT RULING


A **blind university professor** was appointed as member of SPSC.


Challenge was made saying he had **infirmity of body**.


### Supreme Court held:


Blindness **cannot automatically disqualify** a person.


Thus the appointment was **valid**.

# 28. ONE-LINE PRELIMS REVISION

- Articles **315–323** deal with **Public Service Commissions**.
- SPSC members are **appointed by Governor but removed by President**.
- SPSC is called **Watchdog of Merit System**.
- Advice of SPSC is **advisory, not binding**.
- Expenses charged on **Consolidated Fund of State**.
- **Half members must have 10 years government service**.
- **Governor appoints acting chairman**.
- **State legislature can extend functions** of SPSC.
- SPSC not consulted for **reservation matters**.
- JSPSC is **statutory body created by Parliament**.
