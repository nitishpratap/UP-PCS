# 1️⃣ PRELIMS PYQs (Judicial Activism & Restraint)


Format


**Question — Exam — Year — Answer**


---

1. **The term “Judicial Activism” was first coined by:**

    — Union Public Service Commission Prelims — 2013


    ✅ **Answer:** **Arthur Schlesinger Jr.**


---

1. **Judicial Activism in India is closely associated with the rise of:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2014


    ✅ **Answer:** **Public Interest Litigation (PIL)**


---

1. **Which of the following judges is associated with judicial activism in India?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2015


    ✅ **Answer:** **P. N. Bhagwati**


---

1. **Judicial restraint implies:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2016


    ✅ **Answer:** **Judiciary limiting interference in executive and legislative matters**


---

1. **Which concept is opposite to judicial activism?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2017


    ✅ **Answer:** **Judicial Restraint**


---

1. **Relaxation of locus standi is related to:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2018


    ✅ **Answer:** **Judicial Activism**


---

1. **Which of the following is NOT a feature of judicial restraint?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2019


    ✅ **Answer:** **Interference in policy matters**


---

1. **Judicial activism mainly aims at:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2020


    ✅ **Answer:** **Protection of Fundamental Rights**


---

1. **Which concept promotes minimal judicial interference?**

    — State PCS Prelims — 2021


    ✅ **Answer:** **Judicial Restraint**


---

1. **Public Interest Litigation allows:**

    — State PCS Prelims — 2022


    ✅ **Answer:** **Any public-spirited person to approach courts**

# 3️⃣ Additional High-Probability Questions (PCS Pattern)


### Prelims Style


1️⃣ Judicial activism started in India


→ **Mid-1970s**


2️⃣ Key judges of activism


→ **V.R. Krishna Iyer, P.N. Bhagwati**


3️⃣ Opposite of activism


→ **Judicial restraint**


4️⃣ Locus standi means


→ **Right to approach court**


5️⃣ Judicial activism is also called


→ **Judicial dynamism**


---


### Mains Style


1️⃣ Explain **meaning and origin of judicial activism**.


2️⃣ Discuss **causes of judicial activism in India**.


3️⃣ Explain **judicial activism and PIL relationship**.


4️⃣ Critically examine **judicial activism vs judicial overreach**.


5️⃣ Discuss **importance of judicial restraint in democracy**.
