
# Fundamental Rights (Articles 12–35)


| Article | Provision                                 | Clauses                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ------- | ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **12**  | Definition of State                       | —                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **13**  | Laws inconsistent with Fundamental Rights | (1) Pre-constitutional laws void if inconsistent with FR; 
(2) State cannot m’;ake laws violating FR; 
(3) Defines “law” including ordinances, orders, by-laws, rules, regulations, and customs.
(4) Constitutional amendments not invalid under Art 13 |

# Right to Equality (Articles 14–18)


| Article | Provision                                    | Clauses                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ------- | -------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **14**  | Equality before law                          | State shall not deny equality before law or equal protection of laws                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **15**  | Prohibition of discrimination                | (1) No discrimination by State on religion, race, caste, sex, place of birth; (2) Equal access to public places; (3) Special provisions for women & children; (4) Special provisions for SEBC/SC/ST; (5) Reservation in educational institutions including private except minority; (6) Up to 10% reservation for EWS                                    |
| **16**  | Equality of opportunity in public employment | (1) Equality of opportunity in employment; (2) No discrimination in public employment; (3) Parliament may prescribe residence requirement; (4) Reservation for backward classes; (4A) Promotion reservation for SC/ST; (4B) Carry forward backlog vacancies; (5) Religious institutions may prefer their religion members; (6) Up to 10% EWS reservation |
| **17**  | Abolition of Untouchability                  | Untouchability abolished and punishable by law                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **18**  | Abolition of titles                          | (1) State cannot confer titles except military/academic; (2) Citizens cannot accept foreign titles; (3) Foreigners holding office cannot accept foreign titles without President’s consent; (4) Office holders cannot accept foreign gifts/titles without President’s consent                                                                            |

# Right to Freedom (Articles 19–22)


| Article | Provision                               | Clauses                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ------- | --------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **19**  | Protection of six freedoms              | (1)(a) Freedom of speech and expression; (1)(b) Peaceful assembly; (1)(c) Form associations/unions; (1)(d) Move freely in India; (1)(e) Reside and settle anywhere; (1)(g) Practice profession or business                                                                                                              |
| **20**  | Protection in criminal cases            | (1) No ex-post-facto punishment; (2) No double jeopardy; (3) No self-incrimination                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **21**  | Protection of life and personal liberty | No person shall be deprived of life or personal liberty except according to procedure established by law                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **21A** | Right to Education                      | Free and compulsory education for children aged 6–14 years                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **22**  | Protection against arrest and detention | (1) Right to know grounds and consult lawyer; (2) Produced before magistrate within 24 hours; (3) Exception for enemy aliens and preventive detention; (4) Preventive detention limited without advisory board; (5) Grounds must be communicated; (6) Public interest exception; (7) Parliament may prescribe procedure |

# Right Against Exploitation (Articles 23–24)


| Article | Provision                                          | Clauses                                                                                      |
| ------- | -------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **23**  | Prohibition of human trafficking and forced labour | (1) Begar, trafficking and forced labour prohibited; (2) State may impose compulsory service |
| **24**  | Prohibition of child labour                        | Children below 14 prohibited from working in factories, mines or hazardous work              |

# Right to Freedom of Religion (Articles 25–28)


| Article | Provision                             | Clauses                                                                                                                           |
| ------- | ------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **25**  | Freedom of religion                   | Freedom of conscience and right to profess, practice and propagate religion                                                       |
| **26**  | Manage religious affairs              | Religious denominations may manage religious affairs                                                                              |
| **27**  | No tax for promotion of religion      | No person compelled to pay taxes for promotion of religion                                                                        |
| **28**  | Religious instruction in institutions | (1) No religious instruction in state-funded institutions; (2) Allowed in endowment institutions; (3) Attendance cannot be forced |

# Cultural and Educational Rights (Articles 29–30)


| Article | Provision                         | Clauses                                                                                                                        |
| ------- | --------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **29**  | Protection of minority interests  | (1) Right to conserve language, script and culture; (2) No discrimination in admission to educational institutions             |

# Right to Constitutional Remedies (Articles 32–35)


| Article | Provision                             | Clauses                                                                                                                                            |
| ------- | ------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **32**  | Constitutional remedies               | (1) Right to move Supreme Court; (2) SC may issue writs; (3) Parliament may empower other courts; (4) Right cannot be suspended except as provided |
| **33**  | Modify Fundamental Rights             | Parliament may restrict FR for armed forces, police etc.                                                                                           |
| **34**  | Martial law restrictions              | FR may be restricted during martial law                                                                                                            |
| **35**  | Parliament to implement FR provisions | Parliament empowered to make laws to give effect to certain FR provisions                                                                          |

# Directive Principles of State Policy (Articles 36–51)


| Article | Provision                                   | Clauses                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ------- | ------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **36**  | Definition of State for DPSP                | Same meaning as Article 12                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **37**  | Application of DPSP                         | DPSP not enforceable by courts but fundamental in governance                                                                                                                                                            |
| **38**  | Social order for welfare                    | (1) Promote welfare through social justice; (2) Reduce inequalities in income, status, opportunities                                                                                                                    |
| **39**  | Principles of policy                        | (a) Adequate livelihood for citizens; (b) Resources distributed for common good; (c) Prevent concentration of wealth; (d) Equal pay for equal work; (e) Protect workers’ health; (f) Protect children from exploitation |
| **39A** | Equal justice and legal aid                 | Provide free legal aid and equal access to justice                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **40**  | Organisation of village panchayats          | Establish self-government at village level                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **41**  | Right to work, education, public assistance | Provide assistance in unemployment, old age, sickness etc. within economic capacity                                                                                                                                     |
| **42**  | Humane working conditions                   | Just and humane working conditions and maternity relief                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **43**  | Living wage                                 | Living wage and decent working conditions for workers                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **43A** | Workers participation in management         | Workers participation in management of industries                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **43B** | Promotion of cooperative societies          | Encourage voluntary cooperative societies                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **44**  | Uniform Civil Code                          | State shall endeavour to secure UCC for citizens                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **45**  | Early childhood care                        | Early childhood care and education for children below 6 years                                                                                                                                                           |
| **46**  | Promotion of weaker sections                | Promote educational and economic interests of SC/ST and weaker sections                                                                                                                                                 |
| **47**  | Public health and nutrition                 | Raise level of nutrition, standard of living and improve public health                                                                                                                                                  |
| **48**  | Agriculture and animal husbandry            | Organize agriculture scientifically and prohibit cow slaughter                                                                                                                                                          |
| **48A** | Protection of environment                   | Protect and improve environment and forests                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **49**  | Protection of monuments                     | Protect monuments and places of national importance                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **50**  | Separation of judiciary                     | Separate judiciary from executive                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **51**  | International peace                         | (a) Promote peace; (b) Maintain honourable relations; (c) Respect international law; (d) Encourage arbitration                                                                                                          |
