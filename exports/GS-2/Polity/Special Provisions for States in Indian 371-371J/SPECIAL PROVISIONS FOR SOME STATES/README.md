
## Articles 371 – 371J (Part XXI)


### Constitutional Location


**Part XXI — “Temporary, Transitional and Special Provisions”**


**Articles Covered:** **371 → 371J**


**States Covered (12)**


Maharashtra | Gujarat | Nagaland | Assam | Manipur | Andhra Pradesh | Telangana | Sikkim | Mizoram | Arunachal Pradesh | Goa | Karnataka


---


# WHY SPECIAL PROVISIONS WERE CREATED


Added **later via Constitutional Amendments**.


### Main Objectives

- Development of **backward regions**
- Protection of **tribal culture & resources**
- Handling **law & order disturbances**
- Protection of **local population interests**
- Addressing issues from **state reorganisation**

---


# ARTICLE 371


## Maharashtra & Gujarat


**President → assigns special responsibility to Governors**


### Governor’s Responsibilities


**1. Development Boards**


| Maharashtra         | Gujarat         |
| ------------------- | --------------- |
| Vidarbha            | Saurashtra      |
| Marathwada          | Kutch           |
| Rest of Maharashtra | Rest of Gujarat |


---


**2. Annual Report**


Report on working of development boards must be **placed before State Legislative Assembly every year**.


---


**3. Equitable Allocation of Funds**


Fair distribution of **development expenditure** among regions.


---


**4. Equal Opportunities**


Governor ensures:

- Technical education
- Vocational training
- Employment opportunities in state services

---


# ARTICLE 371A


## Nagaland


Provides **extensive autonomy**.


### Parliamentary Laws NOT automatically applicable unless **Nagaland Assembly approves**

1. Religious/social practices of Nagas
2. Naga customary law
3. Civil & criminal justice based on customary law
4. Ownership & transfer of land and resources

→ Protects **tribal traditions + land rights**


---


## Governor’s Special Responsibility


Area: **Law & Order**


Conditions:

- Applicable during **internal disturbances**
- Governor consults **Council of Ministers**
- May exercise **individual judgment**
- **Governor’s decision final**

Ends when **President decides**.


---


## Control of Central Funds


Governor ensures:


Central funds for specific purposes **must not be diverted**.


---


# REGIONAL COUNCIL FOR TUENSANG


**35-member Regional Council**


Governor frames rules regarding:

- Composition
- Selection method
- Qualifications
- Tenure
- Salaries & allowances
- Procedure of business
- Staff appointment
- Service conditions

---


# SPECIAL ADMINISTRATION OF TUENSANG DISTRICT


Duration: **10 years from formation of Nagaland (extendable)**


### Key Provisions


**1. Governor administers district directly**


---


**2. Fund Allocation**


Governor ensures equitable distribution between:

- Tuensang
- Rest of Nagaland

---


**3. Applicability of State Laws**


Nagaland laws apply **only if Governor directs** based on **Regional Council recommendation**.


---


**4. Governor’s Regulation Power**


Governor may make regulations for:

- Peace
- Progress
- Good governance

Regulations may **amend or repeal**:

- State laws
- Acts of Parliament

---


**5. Minister for Tuensang Affairs**


One minister must represent **Tuensang district**.


---


**6. Final Authority**


Governor’s decision **final in Tuensang matters**.


---


**7. Election of MLAs**


Members from Tuensang are **not directly elected**.


They are **chosen by Regional Council**.


---


# ARTICLE 371B


## Assam


**President may create a special committee** within **Assam Legislative Assembly**.


### Composition

- Members elected from **Tribal Areas**
- Other members specified by **President**

---


### Tribal Areas (Sixth Schedule)

1. North Cachar Hills
2. Karbi Anglong
3. Bodoland Territorial Areas District

---


### Objective

- Protect **tribal interests**
- Ensure **legislative participation of tribal representatives**

---


# ARTICLE 371C


## Manipur


Special provisions for **Hill Areas**.


---


### Hill Areas Committee


President creates committee of **MLAs elected from Hill Areas**.


**Hill Areas = areas declared by President**


---


### Governor’s Special Responsibility


Ensures proper functioning of **Hill Areas Committee**.


---


### Governor’s Reporting Duty


Annual report to **President of India** on **administration of hill areas**.


---


### Power of Centre


Central Government may **direct Manipur Government** regarding **hill area administration**.


---


# ARTICLE 371D


## Andhra Pradesh & Telangana


Originally for **Andhra Pradesh**.


Extended to **Telangana (2014)** through **AP Reorganisation Act 2014**.


---


## Objective


Ensure **equitable opportunities in**

- Education
- Public employment

for people from **different regions of state**.


---


## Powers of President


### 1. Equal Opportunities


Ensure fair access to:

- Public employment
- Educational institutions

---


### 2. Creation of Local Cadres


State civil posts organised into **local cadres**.


Purpose:

- Balanced regional employment.

---


### 3. Recruitment Rules


President may regulate **direct recruitment to local cadres**.


---


### 4. Local Areas for Education


Certain areas designated as **local areas for institutions**.


→ Enables **regional reservation**.


---


### 5. Reservation / Preference


President determines reservation in:

- Civil posts
- Educational admissions

---


# ADMINISTRATIVE TRIBUNAL (Article 371D)


President may establish **Administrative Tribunal**.


### Jurisdiction


Handles disputes related to:

- Appointment
- Allotment
- Promotion
- Service matters

---


### Court Jurisdiction

- **Outside jurisdiction of High Court**
- Only **Supreme Court can hear appeals**

---


### Abolition


President may **abolish tribunal** if unnecessary.


---


# ARTICLE 371E


## Central University in Andhra Pradesh


Parliament empowered to establish a **Central University in Andhra Pradesh**.


---


# ARTICLE 371F


## Sikkim


Inserted by **36th Constitutional Amendment Act, 1975**


(Sikkim became a **full state**)


---


### Key Provisions


**1. Assembly Strength**


Minimum **30 MLAs**


---


**2. Lok Sabha Representation**

- **1 Lok Sabha seat**
- Entire state = **one constituency**

---


**3. Protection of Population Sections**


Parliament decides:

- Seat allocation for specific communities
- Constituencies reserved for those groups

Purpose: **balanced ethnic representation**


---


**4. Governor’s Special Responsibility**


Areas:

- Maintain **peace**
- Ensure **equitable socio-economic development**

Governor acts:

- In **discretion**
- Subject to **President’s directions**

---


**5. Extension of Laws**


President may extend any Indian law to Sikkim with **modifications or restrictions**.


---


# ARTICLE 371G


## Mizoram


Protects **Mizo customary practices**.


Parliamentary laws NOT applicable unless **State Assembly approves** for:

1. Religious/social practices
2. Customary law
3. Civil & criminal justice via customary law
4. Ownership/transfer of land

(Similar to **Article 371A — Nagaland**)


---


### Assembly Strength


Minimum **40 members**


---


# ARTICLE 371H


## Arunachal Pradesh


### Governor’s Special Responsibility


Area: **Law and Order**

- Consults Council of Ministers
- May use **individual judgment**
- **Governor’s decision final**

Ends when **President directs**.


---


### Assembly Strength


Minimum **30 members**


---


# ARTICLE 371I


## Goa


Only provision:


**Goa Legislative Assembly → minimum 30 members**


---


# ARTICLE 371J


## Karnataka


Inserted by **98th Constitutional Amendment Act, 2012**


Applies to **Hyderabad-Karnataka Region**.


---


### Objective

- Develop **most backward region**
- Reduce **regional imbalance**
- Promote **inclusive growth**

---


### Governor’s Responsibilities


**1. Development Board**


Separate board for **Hyderabad-Karnataka region**.


---


**2. Annual Report**


Report submitted to **State Legislative Assembly**.


---


**3. Equitable Fund Allocation**


Fair distribution of **development funds**.


---


**4. Reservation in Education**


Seats reserved in:

- Educational institutions
- Vocational training institutes

---


**5. Reservation in Government Jobs**


State government posts reserved for **local residents**.


---


### Hyderabad-Karnataka Region Districts

1. Gulbarga
2. Bidar
3. Raichur
4. Koppal
5. Yadgir
6. Bellary
