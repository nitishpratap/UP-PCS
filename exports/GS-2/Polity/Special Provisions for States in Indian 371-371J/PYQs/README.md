# 1️⃣ PRELIMS PYQs (Articles 371–371J)


Format


**Question — Exam — Year — Answer**


---

1. **Special provisions for some states are contained in which part of the Constitution?**

    — Union Public Service Commission Prelims — 2011


    ✅ **Answer:** **Part XXI**


---

1. **Which Articles deal with special provisions for states like Nagaland, Mizoram, etc.?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2012


    ✅ **Answer:** **Articles 371 to 371J**


---

1. **Which Article provides special provisions for Nagaland?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2013


    ✅ **Answer:** **Article 371A**


---

1. **Parliamentary laws relating to customary practices are not applicable in which state unless approved by the Assembly?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2014


    ✅ **Answer:** **Nagaland (also Mizoram)**


---

1. **Which Article deals with special provisions for Assam?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2015


    ✅ **Answer:** **Article 371B**


---

1. **Hill Areas Committee is associated with which state?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2016


    ✅ **Answer:** **Manipur (Article 371C)**


---

1. **Which Article provides for local cadre system and regional reservation?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2017


    ✅ **Answer:** **Article 371D**


---

1. **Which Article allows establishment of a Central University in Andhra Pradesh?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2018


    ✅ **Answer:** **Article 371E**


---

1. **Which state is associated with Article 371F?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2019


    ✅ **Answer:** **Sikkim**


---

1. **Which Article protects customary laws of Mizoram?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2020


    ✅ **Answer:** **Article 371G**


---

1. **Which Article gives special responsibility to Governor for law and order in Arunachal Pradesh?**

    — State PCS Prelims — 2021


    ✅ **Answer:** **Article 371H**


---

1. **Which Article deals with development of Hyderabad-Karnataka region?**

    — State PCS Prelims — 2022


    ✅ **Answer:** **Article 371J**
