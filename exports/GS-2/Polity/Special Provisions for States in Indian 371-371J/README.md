- Development of **backward regions**

# SPECIAL PROVISIONS FOR SOME STATES (Articles 371–371J)


## Constitutional Location

- These provisions are contained in **Part XXI of the Constitution**.
- Part XXI is titled:

**“Temporary, Transitional and Special Provisions.”**


### Articles Covered


Articles **371 to 371J**


### States Covered (12 States)


Special provisions exist for:

1. Maharashtra
2. Gujarat
3. Nagaland
4. Assam
5. Manipur
6. Andhra Pradesh
7. Telangana
8. Sikkim
9. Mizoram
10. Arunachal Pradesh
11. Goa
12. Karnataka

---


# WHY WERE SPECIAL PROVISIONS CREATED?


The Constitution originally **did not contain these provisions**.


They were added later through **constitutional amendments**.


### Reasons

1. To meet the **aspirations of backward regions**
2. To **protect tribal cultural and economic interests**
3. To deal with **disturbed law and order situations**
4. To protect **local population interests**
5. To address issues arising due to **reorganisation of states**

---


# ARTICLE 371


## Special Provisions for Maharashtra and Gujarat


The **President can assign special responsibilities to the Governors** of Maharashtra and Gujarat.


### Governor’s Special Responsibilities


### 1. Establishment of Development Boards


Separate development boards must be created.


**For Maharashtra**

- Vidarbha
- Marathwada
- Rest of Maharashtra

**For Gujarat**

- Saurashtra
- Kutch
- Rest of Gujarat

---


### 2. Annual Report to State Legislature

- A **report on working of these boards** must be
- **Presented every year before the State Legislative Assembly**.

---


### 3. Equitable Allocation of Funds


Funds must be **fairly distributed for development expenditure** among the above regions.


---


### 4. Equal Development Opportunities


The Governor ensures:

- Adequate **technical education facilities**
- **Vocational training**
- **Employment opportunities in state services**

for people of those regions.


---


# ARTICLE 371A


## Special Provisions for Nagaland


Article **371A** provides extensive autonomy to Nagaland.


### Parliamentary Laws Not Applicable Automatically


Acts of Parliament **do NOT apply to Nagaland** regarding the following matters **unless the State Legislative Assembly approves**.


These include:

1. Religious or social practices of the Nagas
2. Naga customary law and procedure
3. Administration of civil and criminal justice based on customary law
4. Ownership and transfer of land and its resources

This protects **Naga traditions and tribal rights**.


---


## Special Responsibility of the Governor


The Governor has special responsibility for **law and order**.


### Important Points

- Applicable **while internal disturbances exist**.
- Governor acts **after consulting the Council of Ministers**.
- But he may exercise **individual judgment**.
- **Governor’s decision is final**.

This special power ends when **the President decides so**.


---


## Control Over Central Funds


The Governor must ensure:

- Money provided by the **Central Government for specific purposes**
- Is included **in the demand for grant for that purpose**
- And **not diverted elsewhere**.

---


# REGIONAL COUNCIL FOR TUENSANG DISTRICT


A **Regional Council of 35 members** must be created.


### Governor Frames Rules for

1. Composition of the council
2. Selection of members
3. Qualifications of members
4. Tenure
5. Salaries and allowances
6. Procedure of business
7. Appointment of staff
8. Service conditions
9. Other matters for proper functioning

---


# SPECIAL ADMINISTRATION OF TUENSANG DISTRICT


For **10 years from the formation of Nagaland** (or longer if Governor recommends):


### 1. Administration by Governor


The **Governor administers Tuensang district directly**.


---


### 2. Allocation of Funds


Governor ensures **equitable distribution of Central funds** between:

- Tuensang district
- Rest of Nagaland.

---


### 3. Applicability of State Laws


Acts of Nagaland Legislature **do not apply to Tuensang** unless:

- Governor directs it
- On recommendation of the **regional council**.

---


### 4. Governor Can Make Regulations


Governor can make regulations for:

- Peace
- Progress
- Good governance

These regulations may:

- **Amend**
- **Repeal**

any law including **Acts of Parliament** applicable to that district.


---


### 5. Minister for Tuensang Affairs

- One minister in the state government
- Must be from **members representing Tuensang district**.

---


### 6. Final Authority


Governor has the **final decision on all matters related to Tuensang district**.


---


### 7. Election of MLAs


Members from Tuensang district are:

- **Not directly elected by people**
- **Chosen by the Regional Council**.

# ARTICLE 371B


# Special Provisions for Assam


### Constitutional Provision


Article **371B** empowers the **President of India** to create a special committee within the **Assam Legislative Assembly**.


---


## Composition of the Committee


The committee consists of:

1. Members **elected from Tribal Areas of Assam**
2. Such **other members as the President specifies**.

---


## Tribal Areas of Assam


The **Tribal Areas** referred to here are those mentioned in the **Sixth Schedule of the Constitution**.


These include:

1. **North Cachar Hills District**
2. **Karbi Anglong District**
3. **Bodoland Territorial Areas District**

---


## Purpose


The objective of this committee is:

- To ensure **adequate representation of tribal interests**
- To allow **tribal members to participate in legislative matters affecting them**

---


# ARTICLE 371C


# Special Provisions for Manipur


Article **371C** gives special provisions for the **Hill Areas of Manipur**.


---


## Creation of Hill Areas Committee


The **President is empowered** to create a committee of the **Manipur Legislative Assembly** consisting of:

- Members **elected from Hill Areas**.

---


## Definition of Hill Areas


The term **“Hill Areas”** means:


Areas declared as Hill Areas by the **President through an order**.


---


## Special Responsibilities of the Governor


The President may direct that:


The **Governor of Manipur** shall have **special responsibility** to ensure:

- Proper functioning of the **Hill Areas Committee**.

---


## Governor’s Reporting Duty


The Governor must:

- Submit an **annual report to the President**
- Regarding **administration of the Hill Areas**.

---


## Power of Central Government


The **Central Government** can give directions to the **State Government of Manipur** regarding:

- Administration of the **Hill Areas**.

---


# ARTICLE 371D


# Special Provisions for Andhra Pradesh & Telangana


Articles **371D and 371E** were originally created for **Andhra Pradesh**.


Later, **Article 371D was extended to Telangana in 2014** through the **Andhra Pradesh Reorganisation Act, 2014**.


---


# OBJECTIVE


The aim of Article **371D** is to ensure:

- **Equitable opportunities**
- **Fair access to education**
- **Fair access to government employment**

for people from **different regions of the state**.


---


# POWERS OF THE PRESIDENT


The **President of India** is empowered to ensure equal opportunities through several mechanisms.


---


## 1. Equal Opportunities in Employment and Education


The President may provide for:

- **Equitable opportunities**
- **Equitable facilities**

for people belonging to **different parts of the state** in matters of:

- Public employment
- Education.

Different provisions may be made for **different regions of the state**.


---


## 2. Creation of Local Cadres


The President may require the **State Government to organise civil posts into local cadres**.


This helps ensure:

- **Regional representation**
- **Balanced employment distribution**.

---


## 3. Direct Recruitment to Local Cadres


The President can provide rules regarding:

- **Direct recruitment to civil posts**
- In **specific local cadres**.

---


## 4. Local Area for Educational Institutions


The President may specify:

- Certain areas as **local areas for educational institutions**.

This allows:

- **Regional reservation in admissions**.

---


## 5. Reservation / Preference


The President can determine:

- **Extent of reservation or preference**

for people belonging to certain regions in:

1. Recruitment to civil posts
2. Admission in educational institutions.

---


# ADMINISTRATIVE TRIBUNAL


The President may also establish an **Administrative Tribunal** in the state.


### Purpose


To deal with disputes relating to:

1. Appointment
2. Allotment
3. Promotion
4. Other service matters

for **civil posts in the state**.


---


## Jurisdiction of Tribunal


The tribunal operates:

- **Outside the jurisdiction of the State High Court**.

Meaning:

- High Court cannot hear cases falling under the tribunal.

---


## Jurisdiction of Courts


No court except the **Supreme Court** can exercise jurisdiction in such matters.


---


## Abolition of Tribunal


The **President may abolish the tribunal** if he believes:

- Its continued existence is **not necessary**.

# ARTICLE 371F


# Special Provisions for Sikkim


### Background


The **36th Constitutional Amendment Act, 1975** made **Sikkim a full-fledged state of the Indian Union**.


At the same time, **Article 371F** was inserted to provide **special provisions for Sikkim**.


---


# Key Provisions


## 1. Size of Legislative Assembly


The **Sikkim Legislative Assembly must consist of not less than 30 members**.


---


## 2. Representation in Lok Sabha

- Sikkim has **1 seat in the Lok Sabha**.
- The entire state forms **one Parliamentary constituency**.

---


## 3. Protection of Different Sections of Population


To protect the rights and interests of various communities, **Parliament is empowered to decide**:

1. Number of seats in the Legislative Assembly to be filled by **candidates belonging to particular sections of population**.
2. **Delimitation of constituencies** from which only candidates belonging to those sections can contest.

This ensures **balanced political representation of different ethnic groups**.


---


## 4. Special Responsibility of Governor


The **Governor of Sikkim** has special responsibility for:

1. Maintaining **peace in the state**
2. Ensuring **equitable social and economic advancement** of all sections of the population.

While performing this responsibility:

- The Governor **acts in his discretion**
- But **subject to directions issued by the President**.

---


## 5. Extension of Laws to Sikkim


The **President can extend any law applicable in other Indian states to Sikkim**.


However:

- It may be applied with **restrictions or modifications**.

---


# ARTICLE 371G


# Special Provisions for Mizoram


Article **371G** protects the **customary laws and social practices of Mizos**.


---


# Parliamentary Laws Not Automatically Applicable


Acts of Parliament relating to the following matters **will not apply to Mizoram unless the State Legislative Assembly decides**:

1. Religious or social practices of the Mizos
2. Mizo customary law and procedure
3. Administration of civil and criminal justice based on customary law
4. Ownership and transfer of land.

This is very similar to the provision for **Nagaland (Article 371A)**.


---


# Legislative Assembly Strength


The **Mizoram Legislative Assembly must consist of not less than 40 members**.


---


# ARTICLE 371H


# Special Provisions for Arunachal Pradesh


Article **371H** provides special provisions for **Arunachal Pradesh**.


---


# Special Responsibility of the Governor

Important points:

- Governor acts **after consulting the Council of Ministers**.
- However, he may exercise **individual judgement**.
- **Governor’s decision is final**.

---


## End of Special Responsibility


This special power will **cease when the President directs so**.


---


## Legislative Assembly Strength


The **Arunachal Pradesh Legislative Assembly must consist of not less than 30 members**.

# ARTICLE 371J


# Special Provisions for Karnataka


Article **371J** deals with the **Hyderabad-Karnataka region**.


This article was inserted by the:


**98th Constitutional Amendment Act, 2012**.


---


# Objective


To:

- Accelerate development of the **most backward region of Karnataka**
- Reduce **regional disparities**
- Promote **inclusive growth**.

---


# Powers of the President


The President may give special responsibilities to the **Governor of Karnataka**.


---


# Governor’s Special Responsibilities


## 1. Establishment of Development Board


A **separate development board** must be created for the **Hyderabad-Karnataka region**.


---


## 2. Annual Report


A **report on the working of the development board** must be presented **every year before the State Legislative Assembly**.


---


## 3. Equitable Allocation of Funds


Funds for development expenditure must be **distributed fairly across the region**.


---


## 4. Reservation in Educational Institutions


Seats must be reserved in:

- Educational institutions
- Vocational training institutions

## 5. Reservation in State Government Jobs


Reservation must be provided in **state government posts** for **people belonging to that region**.


---


# Hyderabad-Karnataka Region Includes


Six backward districts of **Northern Karnataka**:

1. Gulbarga
2. Bidar
3. Raichur
4. Koppal
5. Yadgir
6. Bellary
