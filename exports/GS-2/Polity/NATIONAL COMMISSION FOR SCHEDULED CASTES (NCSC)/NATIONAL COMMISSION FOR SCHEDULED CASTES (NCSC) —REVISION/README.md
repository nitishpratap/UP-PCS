| Part           | Part XVI                                        |

### Important Prelims Distinction


| Commission  | Type           |
| ----------- | -------------- |
| NCSC        | Constitutional |
| NCST        | Constitutional |
| NCW         | Statutory      |
| NCM         | Statutory      |
| NCBC (1993) | Statutory      |
| NHRC        | Statutory      |
| NCPCR       | Statutory      |


### Core Point

- **Only bodies directly created by Constitution = Constitutional Bodies**
- Others created through Parliamentary Acts = Statutory Bodies

# 2. EVOLUTION / TIMELINE OF NCSC


## Original Constitution


### Article 338 initially provided:

- **Special Officer for SCs & STs**
- Title:
    - **Commissioner for Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes**
- Function:
    - Investigate safeguards
    - Report to President

---


## 1978


Government created:

- **Non-statutory multi-member Commission for SCs & STs**

Important:

- Commissioner system continued simultaneously

---


## 1987


Commission renamed as:


### National Commission for SCs and STs


Role:

- Advisory body on:
    - Policy matters
    - Development of SCs/STs

---


## 65th Constitutional Amendment Act, 1990


### Major Change


Replaced:

- Single Special Officer

With:

- Multi-member constitutional commission

### Features

- Constitutional status granted
- Replaced:
    - Commissioner system
    - 1987 Commission

### Effective From

- **12 March 1992**

---


## 89th Constitutional Amendment Act, 2003


### Bifurcation of Commission


Created:


| Commission | Article      |
| ---------- | ------------ |
| NCSC       | Article 338  |
| NCST       | Article 338A |


### Effective From

- **19 February 2004**

# 4. FUNCTIONS OF NCSC


## A. Investigation & Monitoring

- Investigates safeguards for SCs
- Monitors implementation
- Evaluates effectiveness

---


## B. Inquiry into Complaints


Investigates complaints regarding:

- Deprivation of rights
- Denial of safeguards

---


## C. Developmental Role

- Participates in socio-economic planning
- Advises Union & States
- Evaluates development progress

---


## D. Reporting Function


Submits reports to President:

- Annual reports
- Special reports

---


## E. Recommendation Function


Recommends measures for:

- Protection
- Welfare
- Socio-economic development
- Better safeguard implementation

---


## F. Additional Functions


Performs functions assigned by President regarding:

- Protection
- Welfare
- Advancement of SCs

---


# 5. REPORTS OF THE COMMISSION


## Submitted To

- President of India

## Types

1. Annual Report
2. Special Reports

---


## Action by President


President:

1. Places report before Parliament
2. Presents memorandum containing:
    - Action taken
    - Reasons for rejecting recommendations

---


## State-Related Reports


If report concerns state:


| Step | Action                                   |
| ---- | ---------------------------------------- |
| 1    | President sends report to Governor       |
| 2    | Governor places before State Legislature |
| 3    | Memorandum presented                     |


Memorandum includes:

- Action taken
- Reasons for non-acceptance

---


# 6. POWERS OF NCSC


## Procedural Power

- Can regulate its own procedure

---


# Civil Court Powers


During investigation/inquiry, NCSC has powers of Civil Court.


## Powers Include


### 1. Summoning Persons

- Summon from any part of India
- Enforce attendance
- Examine under oath

---


### 2. Production of Documents

- Discovery & production of documents

---


### 3. Evidence on Affidavit

- Can accept affidavit evidence

---


### 4. Requisition of Public Records

- From court/government office

---


### 5. Summons for Witnesses

- Examination of witnesses/documents

---


### 6. Other Powers

- Additional powers by President

---

### Major policy matters affecting SCs
