# NATIONAL COMMISSION FOR SCHEDULED CASTES (NCSC)


## 1. Nature of the Body

- The **National Commission for Scheduled Castes (NCSC)** is a **constitutional body**.
- It is established **directly by the Constitution under Article 338**.

### Constitutional Position

- **Article:** 338
- **Part:** Part XVI
- **Part Title:** _Special Provisions Relating to Certain Classes_

### Important Distinction (Very Important for Prelims)


| Commission                                                | Type                |
| --------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------- |
| National Commission for Scheduled Castes                  | Constitutional body |
| National Commission for Women (1992)                      | Statutory           |
| National Commission for Minorities (1993)                 | Statutory           |
| National Commission for Backward Classes (1993)           | Statutory           |
| National Human Rights Commission (1993)                   | Statutory           |
| National Commission for Protection of Child Rights (2007) | Statutory           |


**Key Point:**

- **Only NCSC is created directly by the Constitution**.
- Others are created **by Acts of Parliament**.

---


# 2. Evolution of the Commission


This topic is **very important for UPPCS Prelims** because questions often come from **amendments and timeline**.


### 1. Original Constitutional Provision


Originally, **Article 338** provided for:

- Appointment of a **Special Officer for SCs and STs**.
- This officer investigated **constitutional safeguards for SCs and STs**.
- He reported **directly to the President**.

### Title of the Officer

- **Commissioner for Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes**

### Functions

- Investigate safeguards
- Report to President

---


### 2. 1978 Development


The Government set up:

- A **non-statutory multi-member Commission** for SCs and STs.

Important point:

- The **Office of Commissioner for SCs and STs continued to exist simultaneously**.

---


### 3. 1987 Change


Government modified the Commission through another **Resolution**.


The Commission was renamed:


**National Commission for SCs and STs**


Purpose:

- To advise the government on:
    - Policy matters
    - Development levels of SCs and STs

---


### 4. 65th Constitutional Amendment Act, 1990


This amendment made a **major change**.


It:

- Replaced the **single Special Officer** with a
- **High-level multi-member National Commission for SCs and STs**.

Important points:

- This became a **constitutional body**.
- It replaced:
    - Commissioner for SCs and STs
    - Commission created in 1987

**Implementation Date:**

- **12 March 1992**

---


### 5. 89th Constitutional Amendment Act, 2003


Another important reform.


This amendment **bifurcated the commission**.


It created two separate bodies:


1️⃣ **National Commission for Scheduled Castes (NCSC)** – Article 338


2️⃣ **National Commission for Scheduled Tribes (NCST)** – Article 338A


**Implementation Date:**

- **19 February 2004**

---


### Final Structure after 2004


| Commission                               | Article      |
| ---------------------------------------- | ------------ |
| National Commission for Scheduled Castes | Article 338  |
| National Commission for Scheduled Tribes | Article 338A |


---


# 3. Composition of the Commission


The **National Commission for Scheduled Castes** consists of:

1. **Chairperson**
2. **Vice-Chairperson**
3. **Three other members**

Total members = **5**


### Appointment

- Appointed by the **President of India**.
- Appointment is made **by warrant under his hand and seal**.

### Service Conditions

- Determined by the **President**.

### Tenure

- **3 years** (as per rules).

---


# 4. Functions of the Commission


The functions of the NCSC are **very important for Prelims and Mains**.


### 1. Investigation and Monitoring


The Commission:

- Investigates all matters related to **constitutional and legal safeguards for SCs**.
- Monitors their implementation.
- Evaluates their effectiveness.

---


### 2. Inquiry into Complaints


The Commission:

- Inquires into **specific complaints** regarding:
    - deprivation of rights
    - denial of safeguards of SCs.

---


### 3. Participation in Development Planning


The Commission:

- Participates in planning for **socio-economic development of SCs**.
- Advises the **Union and State Governments**.
- Evaluates development progress.

---


### 4. Reporting Function


The Commission:

- Submits **reports to the President**.
- Reports may be:
    - **Annual reports**
    - **Special reports when necessary**.

---


### 5. Recommendation Function


The Commission recommends measures to:

- Improve implementation of safeguards
- Promote **protection**
- Improve **welfare**
- Support **socio-economic development of SCs**.

---


### 6. Additional Functions


The Commission may perform **additional functions assigned by the President** relating to:

- Protection
- Welfare
- Development
- Advancement of SCs.

# 5. REPORT OF THE COMMISSION


The **National Commission for Scheduled Castes** submits reports regarding the implementation of safeguards for Scheduled Castes.


### Annual Report


The Commission:

- Presents **an annual report to the President of India**.

### Special Reports


The Commission may also submit **reports at any time** if it considers necessary.


---


## Action by the President


After receiving the report:

1. The **President places the report before Parliament**.
2. Along with the report, a **memorandum** is presented.

### The memorandum explains:

- **Action taken** on the recommendations of the Commission.
- **Reasons for non-acceptance** of any recommendations.

This ensures **accountability of the government**.


---


## Reports Related to States


If the Commission’s report concerns a **state government**:

1. The **President forwards the report to the Governor of that state**.
2. The **Governor places the report before the State Legislature**.
3. A **memorandum is also presented** explaining:
    - Action taken by the state government.
    - Reasons for not accepting any recommendations.

This mechanism ensures **parliamentary and legislative oversight**.


---


# 6. POWERS OF THE COMMISSION


The Commission has important **investigative powers**.


### Power to Regulate Its Procedure


The Commission has the authority to:

- **Regulate its own procedure** while functioning.

---


## Civil Court Powers


While investigating matters or inquiring into complaints, the Commission has **powers of a civil court**.


This means it can legally enforce certain procedures similar to courts.


### These powers include:


### 1. Summoning Individuals


The Commission can:

- **Summon any person from any part of India**
- **Enforce attendance**
- **Examine them under oath**

---


### 2. Production of Documents


The Commission can:

- Require **discovery and production of documents**.

---


### 3. Evidence on Affidavits


The Commission can:

- Receive **evidence through affidavits**.

---


### 4. Requisition of Public Records


The Commission can:

- Ask for **public records from any court or government office**.

---


### 5. Summons for Witnesses


The Commission can:

- Issue summons for the **examination of witnesses and documents**.

---


### 6. Additional Powers


The Commission may exercise **other powers as determined by the President**.


These powers ensure the Commission can **effectively investigate violations of safeguards**.


---


# 7. CONSULTATION WITH GOVERNMENTS


Both levels of government must consult the Commission.


### Mandatory Consultation


The following governments must consult the Commission:

- **Central Government**
- **State Governments**

Consultation is required on:

> **All major policy matters affecting the Scheduled Castes**

This ensures that policies consider **SC welfare and constitutional safeguards**.


---


# 8. ROLE REGARDING OTHER COMMUNITIES


The Commission also performs similar functions regarding:

1. **Other Backward Classes (OBCs)**
2. **Anglo-Indian Community**

This means it investigates matters relating to **constitutional and legal safeguards** of these communities and reports to the President.


---


# 9. ARTICLE 338 – CLAUSE 10 (IMPORTANT)


Article 338 contains an important provision.


### Clause 10 of Article 338


It states that:


References to **Scheduled Castes** in Article 338 may also include:

1. **Other Backward Classes (OBCs)**

    (as specified by the President after receiving the report of a commission under Article 340)

2. **Anglo-Indian Community**

This allows the Commission to **extend certain functions beyond SCs when specified**.

# 13. PRELIMS TRAP POINTS (VERY IMPORTANT)


1. NCSC is a **constitutional body**, not statutory.


2. It was **separated from NCST by the 89th Constitutional Amendment (2003)**.


3. The commission reports to the **President**, not Parliament directly.


4. Reports are **laid before Parliament by the President**.


5. The commission has **powers of a civil court** during investigations.


6. Governments must **consult the commission on major SC policy matters**.
