# PRELIMS PYQs (with Answers)


Format


**Question — Exam — Year — Answer**


---

1. **Cabinet Committees in India are:**

    — Union Public Service Commission Prelims — 2011


    ✅ **Answer:** **Extra-constitutional bodies**


---

1. **Cabinet Committees are constituted under:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2013


    ✅ **Answer:** **Rules of Business of Government of India**


---

1. **Cabinet Committees are formed by:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2015


    ✅ **Answer:** **Prime Minister**


---

1. **Which committee is popularly known as the “Super Cabinet”?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2016


    ✅ **Answer:** **Cabinet Committee on Political Affairs**


---

1. **Which committee deals with high-level appointments in the Government of India?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2017


    ✅ **Answer:** **Appointments Committee of the Cabinet (ACC)**


---

1. **Which Cabinet Committee coordinates government economic policies?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2018


    ✅ **Answer:** **Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs**


---

1. **Cabinet Committee responsible for scheduling government business in Parliament:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2019


    ✅ **Answer:** **Cabinet Committee on Parliamentary Affairs**


---

1. **Cabinet Committees are generally composed of:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2020


    ✅ **Answer:** **3–8 ministers**


---

1. **Which of the following bodies were abolished in 2014 to speed up decision making?**

    — State PCS Prelims — 2021


    ✅ **Answer:** **Groups of Ministers (GoMs) and Empowered Groups of Ministers (EGoMs)**


---

1. **Groups of Ministers (GoMs) are:**

    — State PCS Prelims — 2022


    ✅ **Answer:** **Ad hoc bodies formed for specific issues**

    ✅ **Answer:** **Yes**
