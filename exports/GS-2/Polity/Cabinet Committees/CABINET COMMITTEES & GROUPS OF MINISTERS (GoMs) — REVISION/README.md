# CABINET COMMITTEES


## Meaning


Cabinet Committees = **smaller groups of ministers formed from Council of Ministers** to assist Cabinet in efficient functioning.


### Functions

- Policy formulation
- Coordination among ministries
- Decision making
- Reducing Cabinet workload
- Detailed examination of issues

---


# CONSTITUTIONAL STATUS


| Aspect                    | Status                |
| ------------------------- | --------------------- |
| Mentioned in Constitution | ❌ No                  |
| Nature                    | Extra-constitutional  |
| Created under             | Rules of Business     |
| Basis                     | Executive arrangement |


### Core Prelims Fact


➡ Cabinet Committees are **NOT constitutional bodies**.


---


# TYPES OF CABINET COMMITTEES


| Type                | Nature    | Purpose                  |
| ------------------- | --------- | ------------------------ |
| Standing Committees | Permanent | Regular policy matters   |
| Ad Hoc Committees   | Temporary | Specific issues/problems |


### Examples


### Standing

- Cabinet Committee on Political Affairs
- Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs

### Ad Hoc

- Emergency Committee during 1962 Chinese aggression

---


# KEY FEATURES OF CABINET COMMITTEES


## 1. Extra-Constitutional Bodies

- Not mentioned in Constitution
- Created under Rules of Business

---


## 2. Formed by Prime Minister


PM decides:

- Number
- Name
- Composition
- Functions

---


## 3. Membership


Usually:


➡ **3–8 ministers**


May include:

- Cabinet Ministers
- Ministers of State
- Non-cabinet ministers (sometimes)

---


## 4. Composition


Includes:

- Concerned subject ministers
- Senior coordinating ministers

---


## 5. Chairmanship


Usually chaired by:

- Prime Minister

Sometimes chaired by:

- Home Minister
- Finance Minister

### Important Rule


➡ If PM is a member → PM presides over committee.


---


## 6. Role in Decision Making


Cabinet Committees:

- Examine issues in detail
- Prepare proposals
- Sometimes take final decisions

BUT:


➡ Cabinet can review committee decisions.


---


## 7. Administrative Importance


Cabinet Committees help in:

- Division of labour
- Delegation of authority
- Faster governance
- Inter-ministerial coordination
- Reducing Cabinet burden

---

# GROUPS OF MINISTERS (GoMs)


---


# Meaning


GoMs = groups constituted by Government to examine specific issues requiring inter-ministerial coordination.


---


# PURPOSE

- Examine policy issues
- Coordination among ministries
- Recommend solutions to Cabinet
- Resolve complex matters

---


# NATURE OF GoMs


| Feature     | Nature                |
| ----------- | --------------------- |
| Type        | Ad hoc                |
| Duration    | Temporary             |
| Purpose     | Specific issue        |
| Dissolution | After task completion |


Members usually = concerned ministers.


---


# IMPORTANCE OF GoMs


Served as:

- Coordination mechanism
- Policy resolution platform
- Inter-ministerial consultation body

---

# EMPOWERED GROUPS OF MINISTERS (EGoMs)


## Meaning


GoMs with decision-making powers.


### Difference


| GoM            | EGoM                        |
| -------------- | --------------------------- |
| Recommendatory | Decision-making             |
| Advise Cabinet | Decide on behalf of Cabinet |


---

### 1. Too Many GoMs

- Clear mandate
- Fixed timelines
