
Urban Local Government = **Local Self-Government for Urban Areas**.


Constitutional Status → **74th Constitutional Amendment Act, 1992**.


---


# 1. URBAN LOCAL GOVERNMENT : BASICS


| Topic                     | Details                                              |
| ------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| Meaning                   | Governance of urban areas by elected representatives |
| Constitutional Position   | Local Government in **State List (7th Schedule)**    |
| Responsibility            | Primarily State Governments                          |
| Constitutional Status     | 74th Constitutional Amendment Act, 1992              |
| Effective From            | **1 June 1993**                                      |
| Constitutional Part Added | **Part IX-A**                                        |
| Articles                  | **243P – 243ZG**                                     |
| Schedule Added            | **Twelfth Schedule**                                 |
| Municipal Functions       | **18 Functions**                                     |

# 3. EVOLUTION / HISTORY OF URBAN LOCAL GOVERNMENT


| Year    | Event                                           | Importance                                       |
| ------- | ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| 1687–88 | First Municipal Corporation at Madras           | Beginning of municipal administration            |
| 1726    | Municipal corporations at Bombay & Calcutta     | Expansion                                        |
| 1870    | Lord Mayo’s Resolution                          | Financial decentralisation                       |
| 1882    | Lord Ripon’s Resolution                         | Magna Carta of Local Self-Government             |
| 1907    | Royal Commission on Decentralisation (Hobhouse) | Report submitted in 1909                         |
| 1919    | GOI Act 1919                                    | Local Self-Government became transferred subject |
| 1924    | Cantonments Act                                 | Regulated cantonment boards                      |
| 1935    | GOI Act 1935                                    | Local self-government became provincial subject  |


### Important Personalities


| Personality                  | Importance                               |
| ---------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| Lord Ripon                   | Father of Local Self Government in India |
| Lord Ripon Resolution (1882) | Magna Carta of Local Self Government     |

# 6. 74th CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT ACT, 1992


## Objectives

- Strengthen Urban Local Governments
- Democratic decentralisation
- Improve urban governance
- Local planning & development
- Public participation

## Constitutional Features


| Feature              | Details                                 |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| Part Added           | Part IX-A                               |
| Articles             | 243P – 243ZG                            |
| Schedule Added       | Twelfth Schedule                        |
| Subjects in Schedule | 18                                      |
| Nature               | Constitutional status to municipalities |


---


# 7. TYPES OF MUNICIPALITIES (Art. 243Q)


| Municipality          | Area                              |
| --------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Nagar Panchayat       | Transitional area (Rural → Urban) |
| Municipal Council     | Smaller urban area                |
| Municipal Corporation | Larger urban area                 |


## Determination of Urban Area by Governor Based On:

- Population
- Density
- Revenue
- Non-agricultural employment
- Economic importance
- Other factors

---


# 8. COMPOSITION OF MUNICIPALITIES (Art. 243R)


## Election

- Members directly elected
- Area divided into **Wards**
- Members called **Ward Councillors**

## Chairperson


Method decided by State Legislature.


## Representation Allowed

- Experts (No voting rights)
- MPs
- MLAs
- RS/LC Members registered as voters
- Committee chairpersons

---


# 9. WARD COMMITTEES (Art. 243S)


| Feature     | Details                                 |
| ----------- | --------------------------------------- |
| Mandatory   | Municipalities with population ≥ 3 lakh |
| Composition | Decided by State Legislature            |
| Purpose     | Local participation & governance        |


States may create additional committees also.


---


# 10. RESERVATION IN MUNICIPALITIES (Art. 243T)


| Reservation                | Provision                   |
| -------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| SC/ST                      | In proportion to population |
| Women                      | Minimum 1/3 seats           |
| Women Reservation Includes | SC/ST women                 |
| Chairperson Reservation    | SC/ST/Women                 |
| OBC Reservation            | At discretion of State      |


---


# 11. DURATION OF MUNICIPALITIES (Art. 243U)


| Provision                  | Details                                            |
| -------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Normal Term                | 5 years                                            |
| Election Timing            | Before expiry or within 6 months after dissolution |
| Exception                  | No election if remaining term < 6 months           |
| Reconstituted Municipality | Continues only remaining term                      |


---


# 12. DISQUALIFICATION (Art. 243V)


Disqualification based on:

- State legislative election laws
- State laws

## Minimum Age


👉 **21 years**


(Though legislative age generally 25 years)


## Authority


Determined by State Legislature.


---


# 13. STATE ELECTION COMMISSION (Art. 243ZA)


## Functions

- Electoral rolls
- Conduct municipal elections

## Importance


Ensures:

- Free & fair elections
- Independence from state government

---


# 14. POWERS & FUNCTIONS OF MUNICIPALITIES (Art. 243W)


State Legislature gives powers for functioning as institutions of self-government.


## Main Areas


### 1. Preparation of Plans

- Economic development
- Social justice

### 2. Implementation of Schemes


Related to Twelfth Schedule subjects.


---


# 15. MUNICIPAL FINANCES (Art. 243X)


## Sources


### A. Tax Revenue

- Property tax (**Most Important**)
- Entertainment tax
- Advertisement tax
- Professional tax
- Water tax
- Lighting tax
- Market tax
- Toll
- Octroi (abolished in many states)

### Cesses

- Library cess
- Education cess
- Beggary cess

---


### B. Non-Tax Revenue

- Rent
- Fees
- Fines
- Royalties
- Interest
- User charges

Examples:

- Water charges
- Sewerage charges
- Sanitation charges

---


### C. Grants


From:

- Central Government
- State Government

---


### D. Devolution


Based on State Finance Commission recommendations.


---


### E. Loans


Need State Government approval.


---


# 16. STATE FINANCE COMMISSION (Art. 243Y)


Constituted every:


👉 **5 years**


## Functions

- Review municipal finances
- Recommend revenue distribution
- Recommend taxes assignment
- Recommend grants-in-aid
- Improve financial health

Governor places report before State Legislature with Action Taken Report.


---


# 17. AUDIT OF MUNICIPAL ACCOUNTS (Art. 243Z)


State Legislature makes laws regarding:

- Maintenance of accounts
- Audit

Purpose:

- Accountability
- Transparency

---


# 18. APPLICATION & EXEMPTIONS


## Application to UTs (Art. 243ZB)


President may apply provisions with modifications.


## Exempted Areas (Art. 243ZC)

- Scheduled Areas
- Tribal Areas

Also does not affect:


👉 Darjeeling Gorkha Hill Council


---


# 19. DISTRICT PLANNING COMMITTEE – DPC (Art. 243ZD)


## Purpose


Prepare district development plan.


## Functions

- Consolidate Panchayat & Municipality plans
- Prepare draft district development plan

## Composition


Determined by State Legislature.


## Important Rule


👉 At least **4/5 members elected**


From:

- District Panchayats
- Municipalities

## Representation Basis


According to rural–urban population ratio.


---


# 20. METROPOLITAN PLANNING COMMITTEE – MPC (Art. 243ZE)


## Metropolitan Area


Population:


👉 **10 lakh or more**


Includes:

- Two or more municipalities/panchayats

## Function


Prepare metropolitan development plan.


## Important Rule


👉 At least **2/3 members elected**


From:

- Municipalities
- Panchayats

---


# 21. BAR TO COURT INTERFERENCE (Art. 243ZG)


Courts cannot interfere in:

- Delimitation
- Seat allocation

Election disputes only through:


👉 Election petitions

# 24. MUNICIPAL CORPORATION


## Setup

- States → State Legislature Act
- UTs → Parliament Act

## Authorities


| Authority              | Role                |
| ---------------------- | ------------------- |
| Council                | Legislative wing    |
| Standing Committees    | Specialized work    |
| Municipal Commissioner | Executive authority |


## Mayor

- Ceremonial head
- Usually 1-year renewable term

## Municipal Commissioner

- Chief Executive Authority
- Appointed by State Government
- Usually IAS officer

---

# 28. CANTONMENT BOARD


## Purpose


Civilian administration in military areas.


## Law


👉 Cantonments Act, 2006


(Replaced Cantonments Act, 1924)


## Administrative Control


👉 Ministry of Defence


## Composition

- Partly elected
- Partly nominated

## President


Military officer commanding station.


## Executive Officer


Appointed by:


👉 President of India


---
