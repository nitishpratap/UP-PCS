# 1. PRELIMS PYQs (State Council of Ministers)


Format


**Question — Exam — Year — Answer**


---

1. **The Council of Ministers in a state is collectively responsible to:**

    — Union Public Service Commission Prelims — 2012


    ✅ **Answer:** **Legislative Assembly**


---

1. **Which Article provides for the Council of Ministers to aid and advise the Governor?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2014


    ✅ **Answer:** **Article 163**


---

1. **Which Article deals with appointment and tenure of state ministers?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2015


    ✅ **Answer:** **Article 164**


---

1. **The total number of ministers in a state cannot exceed:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2017


    ✅ **Answer:** **15% of total strength of Legislative Assembly**


---

1. **Minimum number of ministers in a state council of ministers:**

    — State PCS Prelims — 2018


    ✅ **Answer:** **12**


---

1. **The 91st Constitutional Amendment Act (2003) deals with:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2019


    ✅ **Answer:** **Limitation on size of Council of Ministers and anti-defection provisions**


---

1. **Ministers hold office during the pleasure of:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2020


    ✅ **Answer:** **Governor**


---

1. **Which Article deals with conduct of business of state government?**

    — State PCS Prelims — 2021


    ✅ **Answer:** **Article 166**


---

1. **Rights of ministers in the state legislature are provided under:**

    — State PCS Prelims — 2022


    ✅ **Answer:** **Article 177**


---

1. **A minister who is not a member of state legislature must become member within:**

    — UPSC / PCS Prelims — Multiple years


    ✅ **Answer:** **6 months**

# 3. Additional High-Probability Questions (PCS Pattern)


### Prelims Style


1️⃣ Maximum size of state council of ministers


→ **15% of Legislative Assembly**


2️⃣ Minimum size


→ **12 ministers**


3️⃣ Amendment limiting size of ministry


→ **91st Constitutional Amendment Act (2003)**


4️⃣ Article related to duties of Chief Minister


→ **Article 167**


5️⃣ Ministers can speak in legislature under


→ **Article 177**


---


### Mains Style


1️⃣ Explain the **composition of Council of Ministers in states**.


2️⃣ Discuss **collective responsibility of ministers**.


3️⃣ Explain **individual responsibility of ministers**.


4️⃣ Distinguish between **Council of Ministers and Cabinet**.


5️⃣ Discuss **role of Cabinet Committees in state governance**.
