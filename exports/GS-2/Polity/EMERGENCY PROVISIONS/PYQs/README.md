# PRELIMS PYQs (with Answers)


Format


**Question — Exam — Year — Answer**


---

1. **Emergency provisions in the Constitution are contained in which Part?**

    — Union Public Service Commission Prelims — 2011


    ✅ **Answer:** **Part XVIII**


---

1. **Which Article deals with National Emergency?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2013


    ✅ **Answer:** **Article 352**


---

1. **Which Article deals with President’s Rule in states?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2015


    ✅ **Answer:** **Article 356**


---

1. **Financial Emergency is declared under which Article?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2016


    ✅ **Answer:** **Article 360**


---

1. **The term “internal disturbance” was replaced by “armed rebellion” by which amendment?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2017


    ✅ **Answer:** **44th Constitutional Amendment Act (1978)**


---

1. **Which Fundamental Rights cannot be suspended even during Emergency?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2018


    ✅ **Answer:** **Article 20 and Article 21**


---

1. **Article 19 is automatically suspended during which emergency?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2019


    ✅ **Answer:** **External emergency (war or external aggression)**


---

1. **Approval of National Emergency requires:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2020


    ✅ **Answer:** **Special majority of Parliament**


---

1. **Maximum duration of President’s Rule in a state is:**

    — State PCS Prelims — 2021


    ✅ **Answer:** **3 years**


---

1. **Financial Emergency has been declared in India:**

    — State PCS Prelims — 2022


    ✅ **Answer:** **Never**

# Additional PRELIMS Questions (Frequently Asked)


These are **very common in PCS exams**.


---

    ✅ **Answer:** **352–360**


---

    ✅ **Answer:** **1 month**


---

1. **Duration of National Emergency after approval:**

    ✅ **Answer:** **6 months**


---

1. **Grounds for National Emergency:**

    ✅ **Answer:**

- War
- External aggression
- Armed rebellion

---

1. **First National Emergency declared in India:**

    ✅ **Answer:** **1962 (China war)**


---

1. **How many times National Emergency has been declared in India?**

    ✅ **Answer:** **3 times**


---

1. **Which article suspends Article 19 automatically?**

    ✅ **Answer:** **Article 358**


---

1. **Which article allows suspension of enforcement of Fundamental Rights?**

    ✅ **Answer:** **Article 359**
