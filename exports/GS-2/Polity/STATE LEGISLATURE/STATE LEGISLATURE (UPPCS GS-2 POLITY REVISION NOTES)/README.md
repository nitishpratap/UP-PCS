# 1. ORGANISATION OF STATE LEGISLATURE


There is **no uniform structure** across states.


## Types of State Legislature


### 1️⃣ Unicameral Legislature


```plain text
Governor + Legislative Assembly (Vidhan Sabha)
```


Most Indian states follow this system.


---


### 2️⃣ Bicameral Legislature


```plain text
Governor
   │
   ├── Legislative Assembly (Vidhan Sabha) → Lower House
   └── Legislative Council (Vidhan Parishad) → Upper House
```


---

## Criticism of Legislative Council


Raised in **Constituent Assembly debates**


Main criticisms:

- Not truly **representative of people**
- **Delays legislation**
- **Costly institution**

Therefore Constitution allowed **flexibility to create/abolish**.

# CREATION & ABOLITION OF LEGISLATIVE COUNCIL


## Article 169


Deals with **creation or abolition of Legislative Council**.


### Procedure


Step 1 → **State Legislative Assembly Resolution**


Special Majority required:


```plain text
Majority of total membership
+
2/3 of members present and voting
```


Step 2 → **Parliament passes law**


Important facts:


| Feature                      | Detail              |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Amendment under Article 368? | ❌ No                |
| Type of majority             | **Simple majority** |


---


## Examples


### Andhra Pradesh


| Event     | Year |
| --------- | ---- |

| Revived   | 2007 |


### Tamil Nadu

| --------------------------- | ---- |
| Abolished                   | 1986 |
| Resolution to revive        | 2010 |
| Resolution to abolish again | 2011 |

# LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY (VIDHAN SABHA)


## Nature

- **Directly elected**
- Based on **Universal Adult Franchise**

---


## Strength


| Limit   | Members |
| ------- | ------- |
| Maximum | **500** |
| Minimum | **60**  |


---


## Special Minimum Strength (Exceptions)


| State             | Minimum Members |
| ----------------- | --------------- |
| Arunachal Pradesh | 30              |
| Sikkim            | 30              |
| Goa               | 30              |
| Mizoram           | 40              |
| Nagaland          | 46              |


Special fact:

- Some members in **Sikkim & Nagaland** are **indirectly elected**.

# NOMINATED MEMBER


Governor may nominate:


```plain text
1 Anglo-Indian member
```


Condition:


If **Anglo-Indian community not adequately represented**.


### Duration


Originally → **10 years (till 1960)**


Extended multiple times.


| Amendment                   | Extension              |
| --------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| **95th Amendment Act 2009** | Extended till **2020** |


(Note: Later removed by **104th Amendment**, beyond this text.)

# TERRITORIAL CONSTITUENCIES


State divided into **territorial constituencies**.


Principle followed:


```plain text
Equal Representation
```


Meaning:


```plain text
Population / Seats ratio
should be uniform
```


Population based on **last published census**.


---


# DELIMITATION


After each census:


Two adjustments:


1️⃣ Total Assembly seats


2️⃣ Territorial constituencies


This process = **Delimitation**

# FREEZE ON SEATS


## 42nd Amendment Act (1976)


```plain text
Seat readjustment frozen till 2000
```


Reason → **Encourage population control**


---


## 84th Amendment Act (2001)


Freeze extended till **2026**


---


## 87th Amendment Act (2003)


Delimitation based on:


```plain text
2001 Census
```


But **without increasing total seats**.


---


# RESERVATION OF SEATS


Reserved for:

- **Scheduled Castes (SC)**
- **Scheduled Tribes (ST)**

Basis:


```plain text
SC/ST Population
÷
Total Population
=
Reserved Seat Ratio
```


---


## Duration


Originally:


```plain text
10 years (till 1960)
```


Extended repeatedly.


| Amendment                 | Extension              |
| ------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| **95th Amendment (2009)** | Extended till **2020** |

# LEGISLATIVE COUNCIL (VIDHAN PARISHAD)


## Nature


Members are **indirectly elected**.


---


## Strength


| Rule    | Members                      |
| ------- | ---------------------------- |
| Maximum | **1/3 of Assembly strength** |
| Minimum | **40 members**               |


Actual strength determined by **Parliament**.


---


# COMPOSITION OF COUNCIL


Members come from **5 sources**


| Source                       | Fraction            |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Local bodies                 | **1/3**             |
| Graduates                    | **1/12**            |
| Teachers                     | **1/12**            |
| Legislative Assembly members | **1/3**             |
| Nominated by Governor        | **Remaining (1/6)** |


---


## Governor's Nominees


Governor nominates **1/6 members**.


From fields:

- Literature
- Science
- Art
- Cooperative movement
- Social service

---


## Election Method


```plain text
Proportional Representation
+
Single Transferable Vote
```


---


### Exam Fact


```plain text
5/6 elected
1/6 nominated
```


---


### Judicial Review


Governor's nominations:


```plain text
Cannot be challenged in court
```


---


### Parliament Power


Parliament can **change composition of council**, but **no law passed yet**.

# 3. DURATION OF HOUSES


| House                | Nature    |
| -------------------- | --------- |
| Legislative Assembly | Temporary |
| Legislative Council  | Permanent |


---


# LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY TERM


### Nature


Like **Lok Sabha**.


---


### Normal Term


```plain text
5 years
```


Starts from:


```plain text
Date of first meeting after elections
```


After 5 years:


```plain text
Automatic dissolution
```


---


### Early Dissolution


Governor may dissolve before 5 years.


Reasons:

- Loss of majority
- Political instability
- Fresh elections

---


### Extension During National Emergency


Under **Article 352**


Parliament may extend term:


```plain text
1 year at a time
```


Limit:


```plain text
Maximum 6 months after emergency ends
```


---


# LEGISLATIVE COUNCIL TERM


Council is:


```plain text
Permanent body
```


Cannot be dissolved.


---


## Retirement System


```plain text
1/3 members retire every 2 years
```


Member term:


```plain text
6 years
```


Retiring members **can be re-elected unlimited times**.

Person must:

- Be **elector in Assembly constituency**
- **Resident of state** (for nomination)

Person must:


```plain text
Be elector in Assembly constituency
```

Candidate must belong to **SC/ST**.

```plain text
SC/ST can contest general seats
```

# DISQUALIFICATIONS


Sources:


1️⃣ Constitution


2️⃣ Representation of People Act 1951


3️⃣ Tenth Schedule (Defection)


---


## Constitutional Disqualifications


A person is disqualified if:


### Office of Profit


Holds office under **Union or State Government**


Exceptions:

- Minister
- Offices exempted by legislature

---


### Unsound Mind


Declared by **court**


---


### Insolvent


If **undischarged insolvent**


---


### Foreign Citizenship


If:

- Not citizen of India
- Acquired foreign citizenship
- Allegiance to foreign state

---


### Disqualified by Parliament


Under law made by Parliament.


---


# DISQUALIFICATIONS UNDER RPA 1951


Includes:


### Election Offences


Guilty of **corrupt practices**


---


### Criminal Conviction


If sentenced:


```plain text
2 years or more imprisonment
```


Exception:


```plain text
Preventive detention does not disqualify
```


---


### Election Expense Violation


Failure to submit **expense accounts**


---


### Government Contracts


Interest in **government contracts**


---


### Government Corporation Office


Director/agent in corporation with:


```plain text
25% or more government shares
```


---


### Dismissal from Govt Service


For:

- Corruption
- Disloyalty

---


### Promoting Enmity


Conviction for:

- Communal hatred
- Bribery

---


### Social Crimes


Punished for:

- Untouchability
- Dowry
- Sati

---

```

```

## Oath Includes


Member promises to:

- Bear true faith to Constitution
- Uphold sovereignty and integrity
- Discharge duties faithfully

## If Oath Not Taken


Member cannot:

- Vote
- Participate
- Enjoy privileges

```plain text
₹500 per day
```


Applies when:

- Oath not taken
- Disqualified
- Prohibited by law

# 6. VACATION OF SEATS


Seat becomes vacant in several situations.


---


## Double Membership


Person **cannot be member of both houses**.


Must choose one seat.


Otherwise:


```plain text
One seat automatically vacant
```


---


## Disqualification


If member becomes disqualified.


Seat becomes vacant.


---


## Resignation


| House                | Resignation To |
| -------------------- | -------------- |
| Legislative Assembly | Speaker        |
| Legislative Council  | Chairman       |


Seat vacates **after acceptance**.


Speaker/Chairman may reject if:


```plain text
Not voluntary or genuine
```


---


## Absence


If member absent:


```plain text
60 days without permission
```


House may declare seat vacant.


---


## Other Cases


Seat becomes vacant if:

- Election declared void by court
- Member expelled by House
- Member elected **President or Vice-President**
- Member appointed **Governor**

# SPEAKER OF LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY


## Election


Speaker is:


```plain text
Elected by Legislative Assembly
```


From:


```plain text
Among its own members
```


---


## Tenure


Normally remains in office during:


```plain text
Life of Assembly
```


---


## Vacates Office When


1️⃣ Ceases to be Assembly member


2️⃣ Resigns to **Deputy Speaker**


3️⃣ Removed by Assembly resolution


Removal requirements:


```plain text
Majority of all then members
+
14 days advance notice
```


---


# POWERS OF SPEAKER


### Maintaining Order


Ensures:

- Order
- Discipline
- Decorum

Decision **final in Assembly**.


---


### Final Interpreter


Interprets:

- Constitution
- Rules of procedure
- Legislative precedents

---


### Quorum


If quorum absent:


Speaker may:


```plain text
Adjourn Assembly
or
Suspend meeting
```


---


### Casting Vote


Speaker:


```plain text
Does not vote initially
```


But in case of tie:


```plain text
Exercises casting vote
```
