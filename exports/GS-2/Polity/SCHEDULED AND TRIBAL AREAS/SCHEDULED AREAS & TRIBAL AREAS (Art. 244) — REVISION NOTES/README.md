# CONSTITUTIONAL FRAMEWORK


| Article / Schedule | Subject                                         |
| ------------------ | ----------------------------------------------- |
| **Art. 244**       | Administration of Scheduled & Tribal Areas      |
| **Art. 244A**      | Creation of Autonomous State within Assam       |
| **Art. 339**       | Union control over Scheduled Areas & ST welfare |
| **Fifth Schedule** | Scheduled Areas                                 |
| **Sixth Schedule** | Tribal Areas (NE States)                        |


---


# FIFTH SCHEDULE vs SIXTH SCHEDULE


| Feature           | Fifth Schedule          | Sixth Schedule                     |
| ----------------- | ----------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Area              | Scheduled Areas         | Tribal Areas                       |
| Applicable States | 10 states               | 4 NE states                        |
| Region            | Rest of India           | Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura, Mizoram |
| Autonomy          | Limited                 | High autonomy                      |
| Main Institution  | Tribes Advisory Council | District & Regional Councils       |
| Law-making Power  | Governor regulations    | Autonomous councils                |
| Judicial Setup    | State courts            | Village courts                     |
| Focus             | Welfare + Protection    | Self-governance                    |


---


# WHY SPECIAL PROVISIONS FOR TRIBAL AREAS


### Reasons

- Social & economic backwardness
- Distinct tribal culture/customs
- Protection of tribal land/resources
- Need for administrative protection
- Need for self-governance

### Constitutional Principle


➡️ **Protective Discrimination**


Used in:

- Scheduled Areas
- Reservation
- Tribal autonomy

# FIFTH SCHEDULE — SCHEDULED AREAS


## Applicability


Applicable to all states **except**:

1. Assam
2. Meghalaya
3. Tripura
4. Mizoram

These four come under **Sixth Schedule**.

# FEATURES OF FIFTH SCHEDULE


## 1. Declaration of Scheduled Areas


### Authority → President


President can:

- Declare Scheduled Area
- Increase area
- Decrease area
- Alter boundaries
- Rescind status
- Redesignate area

➡️ Done after consultation with Governor.


---


## 2. Executive Powers


| Authority        | Power                                      |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| State Govt       | Executive power extends to Scheduled Areas |
| Governor         | Special responsibility                     |
| President/Centre | Can issue directions to states             |


### Governor must:

- Submit annual report to President
- Submit report whenever demanded

📌 Governor reports to **President**, NOT State Legislature.


---


## 3. Tribes Advisory Council (TAC)


### Mandatory in states having Scheduled Areas.


### Purpose


Advise on welfare & advancement of STs.


### Composition


| Feature           | Details             |
| ----------------- | ------------------- |
| Total Members     | 20                  |
| ST Representation | 3/4 must be ST MLAs |


### Important


States having STs but no Scheduled Areas may also have TAC if directed by President.


---


## 4. Governor’s Legislative Powers


Governor may direct that:

- Parliamentary law may not apply
- State law may not apply
- Law may apply with modifications

### Governor can make regulations for:


### Land Protection

- Restrict tribal land transfer
- Prohibit transfer between tribal & non-tribal

### Land Allotment

- Regulate land allotment

### Money Lending

- Regulate money-lending activities

### Governor’s regulations can:

- Amend laws
- Repeal laws

BUT → require **President’s assent**.


📌 Strongest Governor powers exist here.


---


# COMMISSION ON SCHEDULED AREAS


## Constitutional Provision


President may appoint commission to examine:

- Administration of Scheduled Areas
- Welfare of STs

### Appointment

- Any time
- Mandatory after 10 years from Constitution commencement

# SIXTH SCHEDULE — TRIBAL AREAS


## Applicable States (4)

1. Assam
2. Meghalaya
3. Tripura
4. Mizoram

These areas are called **Tribal Areas**.


---


# OBJECTIVE OF SIXTH SCHEDULE


Provide:

- Autonomy
- Self-government
- Cultural protection
- Tribal identity preservation

---


# FEATURES OF SIXTH SCHEDULE


# 1. Autonomous Districts


Tribal areas divided into Autonomous Districts.


### Important

- Have self-governance institutions
- Still under executive authority of state government

---


# 2. Powers of Governor


Governor may:

- Increase/decrease district area
- Alter boundaries
- Change names
- Reorganize districts
- Create autonomous regions

---


# 3. Autonomous Regions


If multiple tribes live in one district:


➡️ Governor may create Autonomous Regions.


### Structure


State → Autonomous District → Autonomous Region


---


# 4. District Councils


Each Autonomous District has District Council.


## Composition


| Feature               | Details         |
| --------------------- | --------------- |
| Total Members         | 30              |
| Elected               | 26              |
| Nominated by Governor | 4               |
| Basis                 | Adult Franchise |


## Tenure


| Member    | Tenure               |
| --------- | -------------------- |
| Elected   | 5 years              |
| Nominated | Pleasure of Governor |


---


# 5. Regional Councils


Each Autonomous Region has Regional Council.


Functions:

- Administration
- Legislative powers
- Governance functions

---


# 6. LEGISLATIVE POWERS OF COUNCILS


Councils can make laws on:

- Land
- Forests
- Canal water
- Shifting cultivation
- Village administration
- Marriage
- Divorce
- Inheritance
- Social customs

### Condition


➡️ Laws require Governor’s assent.


---


# 7. JUDICIAL POWERS


Councils may establish:

- Village courts
- Village councils

### Jurisdiction

- Civil disputes among tribes
- Criminal disputes among tribes
- Appeals from village courts

➡️ Governor determines HC jurisdiction.


---


# 8. EXECUTIVE / DEVELOPMENT FUNCTIONS


Councils manage:

- Primary schools
- Roads
- Markets
- Ferries
- Fisheries
- Dispensaries

May regulate:

- Money-lending
- Trading by non-tribals

➡️ Requires Governor approval.


---


# 9. FINANCIAL POWERS


Councils may:

- Assess land revenue
- Collect taxes

---


# 10. APPLICABILITY OF LAWS


| State                     | Authority regarding applicability                            |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Assam                     | Governor decides applicability of Parliamentary + State laws |
| Meghalaya/Tripura/Mizoram | President → Parliamentary laws; Governor → State laws        |


### Laws may:

- Not apply
- Apply with modifications/exceptions

---


# 11. COMMISSION ON AUTONOMOUS DISTRICTS


Governor may appoint commission to examine:

- Administration
- Governance issues
- Development problems

Governor may dissolve councils based on report.


---

# ARTICLE 244A — AUTONOMOUS STATE WITHIN ASSAM


### Parliament may create:

- Autonomous State within Assam
- Legislature
- Council of Ministers
- Both

### Purpose


For tribal areas of Assam.


📌 Linked with Bodoland concept.


---


# ARTICLE 339 — UNION CONTROL


## Clause 1


President may appoint commission regarding:

- Scheduled Areas administration
- ST welfare

## Clause 2


Union can issue directions to states regarding:

- ST welfare
- Scheduled Area administration

➡️ Shows Central supervisory control.
