
# Union Executive (President)


| Article | One-line Text                                                                                                                        |
| ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 52      | There shall be a President of India.                                                                                                 |
| 53      | The executive power of the Union shall be vested in the President and exercised according to the Constitution.                       |
| 54      | The President shall be elected by an electoral college of elected MPs and elected MLAs of States and UTs with legislatures.          |
| 55      | The election of the President shall be conducted according to the system of proportional representation by single transferable vote. |
| 56      | The President shall hold office for a term of five years from the date of entering office.                                           |
| 57      | A person who has held office as President shall be eligible for re-election.                                                         |
| 58      | A person shall be eligible for election as President if he is a citizen of India and at least 35 years of age.                       |
| 60      | The President shall take an oath to preserve, protect and defend the Constitution.                                                   |
| 61      | The President may be impeached for violation of the Constitution.                                                                    |
| 72      | The President shall have the power to grant pardons, reprieves, respites or remissions of punishment.                                |

# Vice President


| Article | One-line Text                                                                           |
| ------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 63      | There shall be a Vice-President of India.                                               |
| 64      | The Vice-President shall be ex-officio Chairman of the Council of States (Rajya Sabha). |
| 65      | The Vice-President shall act as President during vacancy or absence of the President.   |
| 66      | The Vice-President shall be elected by members of both Houses of Parliament.            |
| 67      | The Vice-President shall hold office for a term of five years.                          |
| 69      | The Vice-President shall take oath before entering office.                              |

# Council of Ministers


| Article | One-line Text                                                                                                                           |
| ------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 74      | There shall be a Council of Ministers with the Prime Minister to aid and advise the President.                                          |
| 75      | The Prime Minister shall be appointed by the President and the Council of Ministers shall be collectively responsible to the Lok Sabha. |
| 77      | All executive actions of the Government of India shall be expressed in the name of the President.                                       |
| 78      | The Prime Minister shall communicate decisions of the Council of Ministers to the President.                                            |

# Attorney General

| ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |

# Parliament


| Article | One-line Text                                                                                                          |
| ------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 79      | There shall be a Parliament for the Union consisting of the President, Rajya Sabha and Lok Sabha.                      |
| 80      | The Council of States (Rajya Sabha) shall consist of representatives of States and nominated members.                  |
| 81      | The House of the People (Lok Sabha) shall consist of members chosen by direct election.                                |
| 83      | The Rajya Sabha is a permanent body and Lok Sabha shall continue for five years unless dissolved earlier.              |
| 84      | A person shall be qualified for Parliament membership if he is a citizen of India and meets prescribed qualifications. |
| 85      | The President shall summon and prorogue the Houses of Parliament.                                                      |
| 86      | The President may address either House of Parliament and send messages to them.                                        |


---


# Parliamentary Officers


| Article | One-line Text                                                                                          |
| ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 93      | The House of the People shall choose two members to be Speaker and Deputy Speaker.                     |
| 94      | The Speaker or Deputy Speaker may resign or be removed by resolution of the House.                     |
| 97      | Salaries and allowances of the Speaker, Deputy Speaker and Chairman shall be determined by Parliament. |


---


# Parliamentary Procedure


| Article | One-line Text                                                                                    |
| ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 100     | All questions in Parliament shall be decided by majority of votes of members present and voting. |
| 105     | Members of Parliament shall enjoy freedom of speech and certain parliamentary privileges.        |
| 107     | A Bill may originate in either House of Parliament and must be passed by both Houses.            |
| 108     | The President may summon a joint sitting of both Houses to resolve disagreement on a bill.       |
| 109     | Money Bills shall be introduced only in the Lok Sabha and follow special procedure.              |
| 110     | A Money Bill deals exclusively with taxation, borrowing, Consolidated Fund and related matters.  |
| 111     | A Bill becomes law only after the President gives assent.                                        |

# Financial Matters


| Article | One-line Text                                                                                   |
| ------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 112     | The President shall cause the Annual Financial Statement (Budget) to be laid before Parliament. |
| 113     | Estimates of expenditure shall be submitted as demands for grants to Lok Sabha.                 |
| 114     | No money shall be withdrawn from the Consolidated Fund except by Appropriation made by law.     |
| 115     | Parliament may make supplementary or additional grants during the financial year.               |
| 116     | Parliament may grant Vote on Account or exceptional grants.                                     |

# Supreme Court


| Article | One-line Text                                                                                    |
| ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 124     | There shall be a Supreme Court of India consisting of the Chief Justice and other judges.        |
| 125     | Salaries and allowances of Supreme Court judges shall be determined by Parliament.               |
| 126     | The President may appoint an Acting Chief Justice of India.                                      |
| 127     | The Chief Justice may appoint ad-hoc judges of the Supreme Court with approval of the President. |
| 128     | Retired judges may sit and act as judges of the Supreme Court with consent.                      |


---


# Jurisdiction of Supreme Court


| Article | One-line Text                                                                                                    |
| ------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 129     | The Supreme Court shall be a Court of Record with power to punish for contempt.                                  |
| 130     | The Supreme Court shall sit in Delhi or other place decided by the Chief Justice with approval of the President. |
| 131     | The Supreme Court shall have original jurisdiction in disputes between Union and States.                         |
| 132     | Appeals to the Supreme Court shall lie from High Courts in constitutional matters.                               |
| 133     | Appeals shall lie to the Supreme Court in civil cases as provided by law.                                        |
| 134     | Appeals shall lie to the Supreme Court in criminal cases under specified conditions.                             |


---


# Special Powers


| Article | One-line Text                                                                                |
| ------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 136     | The Supreme Court may grant special leave to appeal from any judgment of any court in India. |
| 137     | The Supreme Court shall have power to review its own judgments.                              |
| 141     | The law declared by the Supreme Court shall be binding on all courts in India.               |
| 142     | The Supreme Court may pass any decree necessary to do complete justice.                      |
| 143     | The President may refer questions of law to the Supreme Court for advisory opinion.          |

# CAG


| Article | One-line Text                                                                                        |
| ------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 148     | There shall be a Comptroller and Auditor General of India appointed by the President.                |
| 149     | The CAG shall perform duties relating to accounts of the Union and States.                           |
| 150     | Accounts of Union and States shall be kept in the form prescribed by the President on advice of CAG. |

# Governor


| Article | One-line Text                                                                        |
| ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 153     | There shall be a Governor for each State.                                            |
| 154     | The executive power of the State shall be vested in the Governor.                    |
| 155     | The Governor shall be appointed by the President by warrant under his hand and seal. |
| 156     | The Governor shall hold office during the pleasure of the President.                 |
| 157     | A Governor must be a citizen of India and at least 35 years of age.                  |
| 158     | The Governor shall not hold any office of profit while in office.                    |
| 159     | The Governor shall take oath to preserve, protect and defend the Constitution.       |
| 161     | The Governor shall have power to grant pardons in State matters.                     |
