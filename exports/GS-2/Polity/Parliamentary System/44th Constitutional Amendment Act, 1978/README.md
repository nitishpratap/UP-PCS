
## 44th Constitutional Amendment Act, 1978


The **44th Constitutional Amendment Act** was passed by the **Janata Party government** mainly to **reverse many changes made by the 42nd Amendment** and **restore democratic safeguards after the Emergency (1975–77)**.


---


### 1. Right to Property Removed as Fundamental Right

- **Article 31** (Right to Property) was **removed from Fundamental Rights**.
- It was made a **legal right under Article 300A**.

➡ Citizens can still own property, but it is **not a Fundamental Right anymore**.


---


### 2. Protection Against Misuse of Emergency


Changes were made in **Article 352**.


Important safeguards added:

- **“Internal disturbance” replaced with “Armed rebellion.”**
- Emergency declaration now requires **written advice of the Cabinet**.
- Parliament must approve Emergency within **1 month**.

---


### 3. Lok Sabha and State Assembly Tenure Restored


The 42nd Amendment increased tenure to **6 years**.


44th Amendment restored it back to:

- **5 years** for **Article 83**
- **5 years** for **Article 172**

---


### 4. Protection of Fundamental Rights During Emergency


The amendment ensured that certain rights **cannot be suspended even during Emergency**.


Rights protected:

- **Article 20**
- **Article 21**

➡ Even during Emergency, **Right to Life and Personal Liberty cannot be suspended**.


---


### 5. Safeguards for Preventive Detention


New protections added:

- Advisory board must review detention cases.
- Maximum detention without review **reduced from 3 months → 2 months**.

---


### 6. President’s Power Clarified


Under **Article 74**:

- President can **once send advice back for reconsideration**.
- After reconsideration, President must accept the advice.

---


### 7. Strengthening Civil Liberties


Many provisions that limited **judicial review** in the 42nd Amendment were removed.


This restored the authority of:

- **Supreme Court of India**
- **High Courts**

---


✅ **UPPCS / UPSC Prelims Quick Points**


Remember **“PAPER”**:

- **P** – Property removed from Fundamental Rights
- **A** – Armed Rebellion replaced Internal Disturbance
- **P** – Protection of Articles **20 & 21** during Emergency
- **E** – Emergency safeguards added
- **R** – Restoration of **5-year tenure**
