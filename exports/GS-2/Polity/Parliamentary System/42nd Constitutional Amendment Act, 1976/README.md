
**42nd Constitutional Amendment Act, 1976**


(Also called the **“Mini Constitution”** because it made very large changes to the Constitution.)


### 1. Preamble Changed


Three new words were added to the Preamble:

- **Socialist**
- **Secular**
- **Integrity**

So the Preamble became:


**“Sovereign Socialist Secular Democratic Republic”**


---


### 2. Fundamental Duties Added

- **Part IVA** was added to the Constitution.
- **Article 51A** introduced **10 Fundamental Duties** for citizens.

Example duties:

- Respect the Constitution and national symbols
- Protect environment
- Promote harmony
- Safeguard public property

---


### 3. Directive Principles Strengthened


DPSPs were given **more importance over Fundamental Rights** in some cases.


New DPSPs added:

- **Article 39A** – Free legal aid
- **Article 43A** – Worker participation in management
- **Article 48A** – Protection of environment

---


### 4. Parliament’s Power Increased

- Parliament’s power to amend the Constitution was strengthened.
- **Judicial review of constitutional amendments was limited**.

---


### 5. Tenure of Lok Sabha and State Assemblies Increased

- Tenure increased from **5 years → 6 years**.

    (Later reversed by **44th Amendment**.)


---


### 6. Tribunal System Introduced

- **Part XIV-A** added.
- Created **Administrative Tribunals** for service matters.

---


### 7. Curbed Power of Judiciary

- Reduced powers of **Supreme Court and High Courts**.
- Some constitutional amendments were made **non-justiciable**.

---


### 8. Changes in Centre–State Relations

- Made the **Central Government stronger** compared to states.

---


### 9. Added Subjects to Concurrent List


Subjects moved from **State List → Concurrent List**:

- Education
- Forests
- Weights & Measures
- Protection of wild animals and birds

---


### 10. Constitutional Amendment During Emergency


This amendment was passed during **The Emergency in India** under the government of **Indira Gandhi**.


---

Remember **“SIFET”**:

- **S** – Socialist added
- **I** – Integrity added
- **F** – Fundamental Duties added
- **E** – Emergency period amendment
- **T** – Tribunals introduced
