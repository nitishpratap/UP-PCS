# 1. MEANING OF PARLIAMENTARY FORUMS


**Parliamentary Forums = Informal institutional platforms in Parliament where MPs interact with experts, ministers, and officials on important policy issues.**


### Purpose

- Discuss **important national issues**
- Understand **ground realities**
- Gain **technical knowledge**
- Improve **quality of parliamentary debate**

### Key Feature


❗ **Forums do NOT have legislative powers**


They function only as:


**Knowledge-sharing & discussion platforms**

# 4. OBJECTIVES OF PARLIAMENTARY FORUMS


### Objective 1 — Interaction Platform


MPs interact with:

- Union Ministers
- Experts
- Officials of nodal ministries

Purpose:

- Discuss critical policy issues
- Improve policy implementation

---


### Objective 2 — Sensitisation & Knowledge


Forums help MPs understand:

- Policy challenges
- Technical knowledge
- Ground realities

Sources of expertise:

- Indian experts
- International experts
- Technical specialists

MPs use this knowledge in:

- **Parliament debates**
- **Departmentally Related Standing Committees**

---


### Objective 3 — Knowledge Database


Forums maintain information database using:

- Ministries
- NGOs
- Newspapers
- United Nations reports
- Internet
- Research studies

Purpose:

- Enable **informed parliamentary discussion**

---


# IMPORTANT RULE (EXAM FACT)


❗ Parliamentary Forums **cannot interfere with Departmentally Related Standing Committees (DRSCs)**


Meaning:

- Forums = discussion bodies
- Committees = decision/oversight bodies

---


# 5. COMPOSITION OF PARLIAMENTARY FORUMS


## President


Normally:


**Speaker of Lok Sabha**


### Exception


Forum on **Population & Public Health**


| Position     | Authority                   |
| ------------ | --------------------------- |
| President    | **Chairman of Rajya Sabha** |
| Co-President | **Speaker of Lok Sabha**    |


---


## Vice Presidents (Ex-Officio)

- Deputy Chairman of Rajya Sabha
- Deputy Speaker of Lok Sabha
- Concerned Ministers
- Chairpersons of DRSCs

---


# 7. NOMINATION OF MEMBERS


Members nominated by:

- **Speaker of Lok Sabha**
- **Chairman of Rajya Sabha**

Criteria:

- Party leaders
- Party nominees
- MPs with **special knowledge or interest**

---


# 8. TENURE OF MEMBERS


Membership duration:


**Co-terminus with Parliamentary membership**


Meaning:


If MP membership ends → forum membership ends.


---


# 9. RESIGNATION


Members resign by writing to:

- **Speaker of Lok Sabha** OR
- **Chairman of Rajya Sabha**

---


# 10. MEMBER-CONVENER


Each forum appoints **Member-Convener**


Appointed by:


**President of Forum**


Role:

- Organise meetings
- Conduct programmes
- Coordinate activities

Works **in consultation with President**


---


# 11. MEETINGS


Meetings held:

- **During Parliament sessions**
- **Whenever required**

---


# 12. ADMINISTRATIVE SUPPORT


Secretary of Forums:


**Secretary-General of Lok Sabha**


Functions:

- Administrative coordination
- Meeting organisation
- Documentation
- Circulation of reports

# 14. FUNCTIONS OF INDIVIDUAL FORUMS


---


# 1️⃣ Forum on Water Conservation & Management (2005)


### Focus


Water resource management


### Functions

- Identify **water conservation problems**
- Recommend solutions to **government**
- Encourage MPs to promote **water conservation in constituencies**
- Conduct **seminars and workshops**
- Promote **efficient water management**

---


# 2️⃣ Forum on Youth (2006)


### Focus


Youth empowerment


### Functions

- Discuss strategies for **youth development**
- Promote youth as **human capital**
- Interact with **youth organisations and leaders**
- Improve **Parliament outreach to youth**
- Consult **national & international experts**

---


# 3️⃣ Forum on Children (2006)


### Focus


Child welfare


### Functions

- Promote **child-centred policies**
- Exchange ideas through **seminars and workshops**
- Facilitate interaction with **NGOs, media, corporate sector**
- Engage with **UNICEF**
- Undertake programmes for **child development**

---


# 4️⃣ Forum on Population & Public Health (2006)


### Focus


Population stabilisation & health


### Functions

- Strategies for **population control**
- Public health policy discussions
- Awareness programmes for **citizens & communities**
- Interaction with global bodies:
- **WHO**
- **UNFPA**

---


# 5️⃣ Forum on Global Warming & Climate Change (2008)


### Focus


Environmental sustainability


### Functions

- Identify climate change issues
- Recommend policy responses
- Interaction with scientists & institutions
- Organise **seminars/workshops**
- Promote **public awareness**

---


# 6️⃣ Forum on Disaster Management (2011)


### Focus


Disaster risk reduction


### Functions

- Identify disaster-related problems
- Recommend mitigation measures
- Interaction with disaster experts
- Conduct awareness programmes
- Promote **disaster preparedness**

---


# 7️⃣ Forum on Artisans & Craftspeople (2013)


### Focus


Protection of traditional crafts


### Functions

- Highlight problems of artisans
- Promote preservation of traditional crafts
- Exchange ideas through seminars
- Interaction with organisations:
- **Khadi & Village Industries Commission (KVIC)**
- **Coir Board**
- **CAPART**

Purpose:


Promote **artisan livelihoods**


---


# 8️⃣ Forum on Millennium Development Goals (2013)


### Focus


Monitoring global development targets


### Functions

- Review progress on **MDGs**
- Exchange ideas and best practices
- Interaction with **NGOs & civil society**
- Consultation with **UN agencies**
