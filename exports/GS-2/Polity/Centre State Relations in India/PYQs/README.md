# PRELIMS PYQs (with Answers)


Format


**Question — Exam — Year — Answer**


---

1. **The distribution of powers between the Centre and the States in India is based on which schedule of the Constitution?**

    — Union Public Service Commission Prelims — 2011


    ✅ **Answer:** Seventh Schedule


---

1. **Which Article deals with the establishment of the Inter-State Council?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2013


    ✅ **Answer:** Article 263


---

1. **Which Article empowers Parliament to legislate on State List in national interest if Rajya Sabha passes a resolution?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2015


    ✅ **Answer:** Article 249


---

1. **Residuary powers in the Indian Constitution belong to:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2016


    ✅ **Answer:** Parliament


---

1. **Which amendment transferred education from State List to Concurrent List?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2017


    ✅ **Answer:** 42nd Constitutional Amendment Act (1976)


---

1. **Which body recommends distribution of tax revenue between Centre and States?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2018


    ✅ **Answer:** Finance Commission


---

1. **The Finance Commission is constituted under which Article?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2019


    ✅ **Answer:** Article 280


---

1. **Which Article imposes duty on the Union to protect states against external aggression and internal disturbance?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2020


    ✅ **Answer:** Article 355


---

1. **Which service belongs to the All-India Services category?**

    — State PCS Prelims — 2021


    ✅ **Answer:** Indian Administrative Service (IAS)


---

1. **During National Emergency Parliament can legislate on State List under which Article?**

    — State PCS Prelims — 2022


    ✅ **Answer:** Article 250
