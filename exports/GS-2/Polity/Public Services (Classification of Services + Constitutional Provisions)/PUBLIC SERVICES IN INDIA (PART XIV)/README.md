# ALL-INDIA SERVICES (AIS)


## Meaning

- Common to **Union + States**
- Officers serve both governments by rotation
- Link between Centre & States
- Hold top administrative posts

---


# AIS STRUCTURE


| Aspect      | Provision      |
| ----------- | -------------- |
| Recruitment | UPSC           |
| Training    | Central Govt   |
| Cadre       | State cadres   |
| Deputation  | Centre ↔ State |
| System      | Tenure system  |


### Important


Even though divided into state cadres → AIS remains **one national service**:

- Uniform pay
- Same status
- Common rights
- Common service rules

| ------------ | ----------------- |
| State Govt   | Immediate control |
| Central Govt | Ultimate control  |


### Important

- Disciplinary action → **Only Central Govt**
- Salary & pension → **Paid by States**

# ARTICLE 310 — DOCTRINE OF PLEASURE


\text{Civil Servants hold office during the pleasure of President/Governor}


## Core Principle


Civil servants hold office during pleasure of:

- President (Union)
- Governor (State)

---


# APPLIES TO


## President’s Pleasure

- Defence services
- Union civil services
- AIS
- Military/civil posts under Union

## Governor’s Pleasure

- State civil services
- Civil posts under State

---


# EXCEPTION TO DOCTRINE OF PLEASURE


Compensation possible if:

1. Post abolished before contract expiry
2. Removal not due to misconduct

### Condition


Applicable only to new entrants.


---


# MEANING OF CIVIL POST


Civil-side administrative employment (not military).


---


# ARTICLE 311 — SAFEGUARDS TO CIVIL SERVANTS


## Purpose


Protection against arbitrary:

- Dismissal
- Removal
- Reduction in rank

Acts as limitation on Art. 310.


---


# TWO SAFEGUARDS


## 1️⃣ No dismissal/removal by lower authority


Must not be lower than appointing authority.


---


## 2️⃣ Mandatory Inquiry


Before:

- Dismissal
- Removal
- Reduction in rank

Employee must:

- Know charges
- Get reasonable opportunity of hearing

---


# ARTICLE 311 APPLIES TO

- Union civil services
- State civil services
- AIS
- Civil posts

---


# DOES NOT APPLY TO


❌ Defence services


❌ Military posts


---


# REASONABLE OPPORTUNITY INCLUDES

- Knowledge of charges
- Cross-examination
- Producing witnesses
- Inquiry report copy
- Representation opportunity

---


# REDUCTION IN RANK


Penalty-based demotion.


---


# DISMISSAL vs REMOVAL


| Dismissal               | Removal                    |
| ----------------------- | -------------------------- |
| Future disqualification | No future disqualification |


---


# EXCEPTIONS TO INQUIRY RULE


## 1️⃣ Criminal conviction


No inquiry needed.


## 2️⃣ Inquiry impracticable


Reasons must be recorded in writing.


## 3️⃣ Security of State


President/Governor satisfied inquiry not desirable.

1. Inquiry stage

❌ Second opportunity removed.
