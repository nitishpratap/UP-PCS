# 1. PRELIMS PYQs (Governor)


Format


**Question — Exam — Year — Answer**


---

1. **The Governor of a state is appointed by:**

    — Union Public Service Commission Prelims — 2011


    ✅ **Answer:** **President of India**


---

1. **Minimum age required to become Governor:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2013


    ✅ **Answer:** **35 years**


---

1. **Articles related to Governor are contained in:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2014


    ✅ **Answer:** **Articles 153–167**


---

1. **A person can be Governor of two or more states under which amendment?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2015


    ✅ **Answer:** **7th Constitutional Amendment Act, 1956**


---

1. **Governor holds office during the:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2016


    ✅ **Answer:** **Pleasure of the President**


---

1. **Who administers oath to the Governor?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2017


    ✅ **Answer:** **Chief Justice of the High Court**


---

1. **Governor’s pardoning power is mentioned under:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2018


    ✅ **Answer:** **Article 161**


---

1. **Governor can promulgate ordinances under:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2019


    ✅ **Answer:** **Article 213**


---

1. **Governor appoints the State Election Commissioner but removal is done by:**

    — State PCS Prelims — 2020


    ✅ **Answer:** **Same manner as High Court Judge**


---

1. **Which of the following is NOT a qualification for Governor?**

    — State PCS Prelims — 2021


    ✅ **Answer:** **Must be member of Parliament (incorrect)**

# 3. Additional High-Probability Questions (PCS Pattern)


### Prelims Style


1️⃣ Qualification of Governor


→ **Citizen of India + 35 years**


2️⃣ Salary of Governor determined by


→ **Parliament**


3️⃣ Governor oath under


→ **Article 159**


4️⃣ Ordinance validity


→ **6 weeks after legislature reassembles**


5️⃣ Governor cannot pardon


→ **Death sentence**


---


### Mains Style


1️⃣ Explain **appointment and qualifications of Governor**.


2️⃣ Discuss **powers and functions of Governor**.


3️⃣ Explain **Governor’s veto powers**.


4️⃣ Discuss **Governor’s role during constitutional crisis**.


5️⃣ Examine **Governor–Chief Minister relationship**.
