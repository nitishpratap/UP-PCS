# 1. STATE EXECUTIVE – BASIC STRUCTURE


**Part VI of Constitution**


| Feature           | Details                                 |
| ----------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| Articles          | **153 – 167**                           |
| System            | **Parliamentary system at State level** |
| Nominal Executive | **Governor**                            |
| Real Executive    | **Council of Ministers headed by CM**   |


### Composition of State Executive

1. Governor
2. Chief Minister
3. Council of Ministers
4. Advocate General

❗ **No Vice-Governor (unlike Vice-President at Centre)**


---


# 2. GOVERNOR – CONSTITUTIONAL POSITION


| Feature    | Explanation                                 |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| Status     | **Chief executive head of State**           |
| Nature     | **Nominal / Titular / Constitutional head** |
| Real power | **Council of Ministers led by CM**          |
| Model      | Similar to **President at Union level**     |


---


# 3. DUAL ROLE OF GOVERNOR


| Role                    | Explanation                                       |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| **Constitutional Head** | Works on **aid & advice of Council of Ministers** |
| **Agent of Centre**     | Represents **Union Government in State**          |


➡ Important for **Indian federal structure**


---


# 4. ONE GOVERNOR FOR MULTIPLE STATES


| Provision | Details                                               |
| --------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Amendment | **7th Constitutional Amendment Act, 1956**            |
| Provision | Same person can be **Governor of two or more states** |


Used frequently for **additional charge**.


---


# 5. APPOINTMENT OF GOVERNOR


| Feature     | Provision                                    |
| ----------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Article     | **155**                                      |
| Appointment | **President appoints**                       |
| Method      | **Warrant under hand and seal of President** |
| Nature      | Governor is **nominee of Union Government**  |


---


# 6. SUPREME COURT VIEW (1979)


Governor is:


✔ **Independent constitutional authority**


❌ **Not an employee of Central Government**


---


# 7. WHY GOVERNOR IS NOT ELECTED


The **Constituent Assembly rejected direct election**.


### Reasons

1. Against **parliamentary system**
2. **Conflict with CM**
3. Governor is **nominal head**
4. Election becomes **political contest**
5. Governor may become **partisan**
6. Encourages **regionalism**
7. Centre loses **control over states**
8. **Dual leadership conflict**
9. CM may install **loyal candidate**

---


# 8. MODELS CONSIDERED


| Model              | System                       | Decision   |
| ------------------ | ---------------------------- | ---------- |
| **American Model** | Governor elected             | ❌ Rejected |
| **Canadian Model** | Governor appointed by Centre | ✔ Adopted  |


India follows **Canadian model**.


---


# 9. QUALIFICATIONS OF GOVERNOR


**Article 157**


A person must:

1. **Citizen of India**
2. **Minimum age: 35 years**

---


# 10. CONVENTIONS (UNWRITTEN RULES)


### 1. Outsider Governor


Governor **should not belong to the same state**


Purpose → avoid **local politics**


---


### 2. Consultation with Chief Minister


President should **consult CM before appointment**


⚠ Often violated.


---


# 11. CONDITIONS OF GOVERNOR OFFICE


**Article 158**


| Condition              | Provision                     |
| ---------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| Legislature membership | Cannot be MP or MLA           |
| If appointed           | Seat automatically vacated    |
| Office of profit       | Cannot hold                   |
| Residence              | **Raj Bhavan (rent free)**    |
| Salary                 | Determined by **Parliament**  |
| Salary reduction       | **Not allowed during tenure** |


---


### Salary Revision


| Year              | Amount               |
| ----------------- | -------------------- |
| Earlier           | ₹36,000              |
| **2008 revision** | **₹1.10 lakh/month** |


---


### Governor of Multiple States


Salary **shared by states** (decided by President).


---


# 12. PRIVILEGES & IMMUNITIES


Same as **President**


| Immunity             | Explanation                    |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Official acts        | Cannot be legally questioned   |
| Criminal proceedings | **Not allowed during tenure**  |
| Arrest               | Not possible                   |
| Civil cases          | Only after **2 months notice** |


---


# 13. OATH OF GOVERNOR


**Article 159**


Governor swears to:

1. **Faithfully execute office**
2. **Preserve, protect & defend Constitution**
3. **Serve people of state**

| Administered by |                                 |
| --------------- | ------------------------------- |
| Primary         | **Chief Justice of High Court** |
| Alternative     | Senior-most HC judge            |


---


# 14. TERM OF GOVERNOR


**Article 156**


| Feature  | Details                   |
| -------- | ------------------------- |
| Tenure   | **5 years**               |
| Reality  | **Not fixed**             |
| Doctrine | **Pleasure of President** |


President can remove **anytime**.


---


### Resignation


Governor resigns to **President**.


---


### No Security of Tenure


SC:


President **not required to give reasons**.


---


# 15. POLITICAL CONTROVERSIES


Change of government often leads to **removal of Governors**.


Examples:


| Year | Event                                                        |
| ---- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1989 | V.P. Singh govt asked Congress-appointed Governors to resign |
| 1991 | P.V. Narasimha Rao removed **14 Governors**                  |


---


# 16. TRANSFER & REAPPOINTMENT


President may:


✔ Transfer Governor


✔ Reappoint Governor


---


# 17. CONTINUATION AFTER TERM


Governor continues **until successor takes charge**


Purpose → avoid **constitutional vacuum**


---


# 18. VACANCY IN OFFICE


If Governor dies / office vacant:


President may appoint **temporary arrangement**


Example:


✔ **Chief Justice of HC** may discharge functions.

# POWERS OF GOVERNOR


Governor powers similar to **President**, but:


❌ No **diplomatic powers**


❌ No **military powers**


❌ No **emergency powers** (except recommending **Article 356**)


---


# CLASSIFICATION OF POWERS

1. Executive
2. Legislative
3. Financial
4. Judicial

---


# 1. EXECUTIVE POWERS


### Administrative Powers


| Power                | Explanation                        |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Executive actions    | Done in **Governor's name**        |
| Authentication rules | Governor decides procedure         |
| Business rules       | Allocation of work among ministers |


---


### Appointment Powers


| Appointment                 | By Governor     |
| --------------------------- | --------------- |
| Chief Minister              | Yes             |
| Ministers                   | On advice of CM |
| Advocate General            | Yes             |
| State Election Commissioner | Yes             |
| State PSC Chairman/Members  | Yes             |
| District Judges             | Yes             |


---


### Special Appointment


**Tribal Welfare Minister**


States:

- Chhattisgarh
- Jharkhand
- Madhya Pradesh
- Odisha

Amendment:


✔ **94th Amendment (2006)**


Removed **Bihar**.


---


### Information Powers


Governor can ask CM:

- Information about **administration**
- **Legislative proposals**

---


### Collective Responsibility


Governor can require CM:


➡ Place individual minister decisions before **Council of Ministers**


---


### President’s Rule


Governor can recommend:


✔ **Article 356**


During this period → Governor acts as **agent of President**.


---


### Chancellor of Universities


Usually Governor is:


✔ **Chancellor of State Universities**


Functions:

- Appoint Vice-Chancellors
- University supervision

---


# 2. LEGISLATIVE POWERS


Governor is **part of State Legislature**


Legislature =


Governor + Assembly + Council (if any)


---


### Legislature Control


| Power             | Explanation          |
| ----------------- | -------------------- |
| Summon            | Call session         |
| Prorogue          | End session          |
| Dissolve Assembly | Usually on CM advice |


---


### Special Address


Governor addresses legislature:

1. First session after **general elections**
2. First session **each year**

---


### Message Power


Governor can send **messages to legislature**.


---


### Presiding Officer Appointment


If posts vacant:


| House    | Situation                         |
| -------- | --------------------------------- |
| Assembly | Speaker + Deputy Speaker absent   |
| Council  | Chairman + Deputy Chairman absent |


Governor appoints presiding member.


---


### Nomination Power


Governor nominates:


| Institution         | Number          |
| ------------------- | --------------- |
| Legislative Council | **1/6 members** |


Fields:

- Literature
- Science
- Art
- Social service
- Cooperative movement

---


### Anglo-Indian Nomination


Governor could nominate **1 Anglo-Indian member**.


❌ Removed by **104th Constitutional Amendment (2020)**.


---


### Disqualification Decision


Governor decides disqualification of MLAs


BUT:


✔ Based on **Election Commission opinion**


---


# ASSENT TO BILLS (ARTICLE 200)


Governor options:


| Option   | Result            |
| -------- | ----------------- |
| Assent   | Bill becomes law  |
| Withhold | Bill rejected     |
| Return   | Reconsideration   |
| Reserve  | Sent to President |


---


### Mandatory Reservation


When bill **endangers High Court powers**


---


### Optional Reservation


Governor may reserve if bill:

- Against Constitution
- Against DPSP
- Against national interest
- Of grave importance
- Property acquisition (Art 31A)

---


# ORDINANCE POWER


**Article 213**


Governor can issue **ordinance when legislature not in session**.


Conditions:

- Immediate action needed
- Governor satisfaction

---


### Validity


Ordinance must be approved within:


✔ **6 weeks of legislature reassembly**


---


### Governor May


✔ Withdraw ordinance anytime.


---


### PRESIDENT'S INSTRUCTIONS REQUIRED IF

1. Bill requires **Presidential sanction**
2. Bill should be **reserved for President**
3. Law invalid without **President's assent**

---


# FINANCIAL POWERS


| Power                    | Explanation                                          |
| ------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| Budget                   | Annual Financial Statement placed before legislature |
| Money Bill               | Requires **Governor recommendation**                 |
| Demand for Grants        | Only on Governor recommendation                      |
| Contingency Fund         | Governor can advance funds                           |
| State Finance Commission | Constituted every **5 years**                        |


---


# JUDICIAL POWERS


### Clemency Power (Article 161)


Governor may grant:

1. Pardon
2. Reprieve
3. Respite
4. Remission
5. Suspension
6. Commutation

For **offences against State laws**


---


### Important Limitation


Governor:


❌ **Cannot pardon death sentence**


But can:


✔ Suspend


✔ Commute


✔ Remit death sentence.


Only **President can pardon death sentence**.


---


### Judicial Appointments


| Position          | Appointment               |
| ----------------- | ------------------------- |
| High Court judges | Governor consulted        |
| District Judges   | Governor appoints with HC |
| Judicial service  | Governor appoints         |


---


# VETO POWERS OF GOVERNOR


| Veto            | Meaning           |
| --------------- | ----------------- |
| Absolute veto   | Withhold assent   |
| Suspensive veto | Return bill       |
| Pocket veto     | No action         |
| Reserved veto   | Send to President |


---


# MONEY BILL VETO


Governor options:

1. Assent
2. Withhold
3. Reserve for President

❌ Cannot return money bill.


---


# PRESIDENT VS GOVERNOR VETO (TRAP)


| Feature           | President | Governor |
| ----------------- | --------- | -------- |
| Return money bill | ❌         | ❌        |
| Reserve bill      | ❌         | ✔        |
| Pocket veto       | ✔         | ✔        |


---


# DISCRETIONARY POWERS OF GOVERNOR


Two types:

1. **Constitutional discretion**
2. **Situational discretion**

---


# CONSTITUTIONAL DISCRETION


Governor may:

- Reserve bill for President
- Recommend **President’s Rule**
- Act as **UT Administrator**
- Decide **royalty to tribal councils**
- Seek information from CM

---


# SITUATIONAL DISCRETION


Occurs in political situations:


| Situation           | Action            |
| ------------------- | ----------------- |
| Hung Assembly       | Appoint CM        |
| CM death            | Select successor  |
| Govt loses majority | Dismiss ministry  |
| No stable govt      | Dissolve assembly |
