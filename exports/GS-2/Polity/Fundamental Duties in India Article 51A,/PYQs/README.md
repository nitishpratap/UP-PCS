# PRELIMS PYQs (with Answers)


Format:


**Question — Exam — Year — Answer**


---

1. **Fundamental Duties were added to the Constitution by which Constitutional Amendment?**

    — Union Public Service Commission Prelims — 2011


    ✅ **Answer:** 42nd Constitutional Amendment Act, 1976


---

1. **Fundamental Duties are mentioned in which part of the Constitution?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2013


    ✅ **Answer:** Part IVA


---

1. **Fundamental Duties are contained in which Article of the Constitution?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2015


    ✅ **Answer:** Article 51A


---

1. **Which Constitutional Amendment added the 11th Fundamental Duty?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2017


    ✅ **Answer:** 86th Constitutional Amendment Act, 2002


---

1. **The concept of Fundamental Duties in India was borrowed from which Constitution?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2016


    ✅ **Answer:** USSR (Soviet Union)


---

1. **Which committee recommended the inclusion of Fundamental Duties?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2018


    ✅ **Answer:** Swaran Singh Committee


---

1. **Fundamental Duties apply to:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2019


    ✅ **Answer:** Citizens only


---

1. **Fundamental Duties in the Indian Constitution are:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2020


    ✅ **Answer:** Non-justiciable


---

1. **Which of the following is a Fundamental Duty?**

    — State PCS Prelims — 2021


    ✅ **Answer:** To safeguard public property


---

1. **Which Fundamental Duty was added by the 86th Amendment?**

    — State PCS Prelims — 2022


    ✅ **Answer:** Duty of parents/guardians to provide education to children (6–14 years)

    ✅ **Answer:** Article 51A(g)

1. **Which Fundamental Duty refers to developing scientific temper?**

    ✅ **Answer:** Article 51A(h)

    ✅ **Answer:** Article 51A(a)
