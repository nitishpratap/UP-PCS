# PRELIMS PYQs (with Answers)


Format


**Question — Exam — Year — Answer**


---

1. **The President of India is elected by:**

    — Union Public Service Commission Prelims — 2011


    ✅ **Answer:** Electoral College of elected MPs and elected MLAs


---

1. **Which method is used for election of the President of India?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2013


    ✅ **Answer:** Proportional Representation by **Single Transferable Vote**


---

1. **The oath of the President is administered by:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2015


    ✅ **Answer:** **Chief Justice of India**


---

1. **The term of office of the President of India is:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2016


    ✅ **Answer:** **5 years**


---

1. **The President of India can be removed from office by:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2017


    ✅ **Answer:** **Impeachment by Parliament**


---

1. **Which Article deals with impeachment of the President?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2018


    ✅ **Answer:** **Article 61**


---

1. **Which Article gives the President power to grant pardons?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2019


    ✅ **Answer:** **Article 72**


---

1. **Which Article provides ordinance-making power to the President?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2020


    ✅ **Answer:** **Article 123**


---

1. **The President can return which bill for reconsideration?**

    — State PCS Prelims — 2021


    ✅ **Answer:** **Ordinary Bill (not Money Bill)**


---

1. **Maximum life of an ordinance issued by the President is:**

    — State PCS Prelims — 2022


    ✅ **Answer:** **6 months + 6 weeks**

# Additional PRELIMS Questions (Frequently Asked)


These are **very common in PCS exams**.


---

1. **Minimum age required to become President:**

    ✅ **Answer:** **35 years**


---

1. **President must be qualified to be elected as:**

    ✅ **Answer:** **Member of Lok Sabha**


---

1. **Electoral College includes:**

    ✅ **Answer:**

- Elected MPs
- Elected MLAs
- Elected MLAs of Delhi & Puducherry

---

1. **Who decides disputes regarding Presidential election?**

    ✅ **Answer:** **Supreme Court**


---

1. **Only President elected twice in India:**

    ✅ **Answer:** **Dr Rajendra Prasad**


---

1. **President can seek advice from Supreme Court under:**

    ✅ **Answer:** **Article 143**


---

1. **President’s veto power is contained in:**

    ✅ **Answer:** **Article 111**


---

1. **Executive power of Union is vested in:**

    ✅ **Answer:** **President (Article 53)**
