### Nature of DPSP

- **Non-justiciable** (cannot be enforced by courts)
- **Fundamental in governance** (Article 37)
- Provide **moral and political obligations** to the State

---


### Meaning of “State”


As per **Article 36** (same as Part III):


Includes:

- Central Government
- State Governments
- Parliament
- State Legislatures
- Local authorities
- Other public authorities

### Article 31C


Inserted by **25th Amendment (1971)**

- Laws implementing **Article 39(b) & (c)** cannot be challenged under:
    - Article 14
    - Article 19
- 39(b) : The State should ensure that **natural and economic resources of the country are distributed in a way that benefits everyone**, not just a few individuals or companies.
- 39(c) : The State should ensure that the **economic system does not allow wealth and production power to concentrate in a few hands**, which could harm society.

# Concept Notes (For Mains)


---


# Meaning of DPSP


Directive Principles are **constitutional guidelines for the State** to achieve:

- **Social justice**
- **Economic justice**
- **Political justice**

They aim to establish a **Welfare State** in India.


---


# Objectives of DPSP


### 1. Establish Welfare State


Shift from **Police State → Welfare State**


| Welfare State      | Police State         |
| ------------------ | -------------------- |
| Focus on welfare   | Focus on law & order |
| Social justice     | Limited state role   |
| Economic democracy | Individualism        |


---


### 2. Achieve Goals of Preamble


DPSP expand ideals of:

- Justice
- Liberty
- Equality
- Fraternity

---


### 3. Promote Economic Democracy


According to **Dr B.R. Ambedkar**, DPSP aim to establish **economic democracy**.

# Conflict Between Fundamental Rights and DPSP


---


### Champakam Dorairajan Case (1951)


Court held:


**FR > DPSP**


Led to **1st Constitutional Amendment (1951)**.


---


### Golaknath Case (1967)


Parliament **cannot amend Fundamental Rights**.


---


### 25th Amendment (1971)


Inserted **Article 31C**.


Protection to laws implementing:

- Article **39(b)**
- Article **39(c)**

---


### Kesavananda Bharati (1973)

- Introduced **Basic Structure Doctrine**
- Judicial review protected.

---


### Minerva Mills Case (1980)


Court declared:

> FR and DPSP are **two wheels of a chariot**.

Balance between them is part of **Basic Structure**.


---


# Distinction Between FR and DPSP


| Fundamental Rights   | Directive Principles        |
| -------------------- | --------------------------- |
| Justiciable          | Non-justiciable             |
| Negative obligations | Positive obligations        |
| Political democracy  | Social & economic democracy |
| Individual welfare   | Community welfare           |
