# 1. Parliamentary Executive in India


| Feature           | Explanation                                       |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| System            | **Parliamentary System (British model)**          |
| Nominal Executive | **President of India**                            |
| Real Executive    | **Council of Ministers headed by Prime Minister** |
| Principle         | Executive is responsible to **Lok Sabha**         |


➡ **Council of Ministers exercises real executive power in India**


---


# 2. Constitutional Framework


| Article    | Provision                                        |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| **Art 74** | Council of Ministers to aid & advise President   |
| **Art 75** | Appointment, tenure, responsibility of ministers |
| **Art 77** | Conduct of Government business                   |
| **Art 78** | Duties of Prime Minister                         |
| **Art 88** | Rights of ministers in Parliament                |


---


# 3. Article 74 — Council of Ministers to Aid and Advise President


### Provision

- Council of Ministers **with PM as head** aids and advises President.

### Binding Nature of Advice


President **must act according to advice**.


### Reconsideration Provision


President may:


➡ **Ask Council of Ministers to reconsider advice**


After reconsideration:


➡ **President must accept advice**


### Judicial Review

- **Ministerial advice cannot be questioned in court**

Purpose:

- Confidentiality
- Executive independence

---


# 4. Amendments Related to Article 74


| Amendment                   | Provision                                                    |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **42nd Amendment Act 1976** | Made advice of Council of Ministers **binding on President** |
| **44th Amendment Act 1978** | President can **ask reconsideration once**                   |


---


# 5. Article 75 — Other Provisions Related to Ministers


Covers:

- Appointment
- Tenure
- Responsibility
- Oath
- Qualification
- Salary

---


## 5.1 Appointment of Ministers


| Post                | Appointment Authority                      |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| **Prime Minister**  | Appointed by President                     |
| **Other Ministers** | Appointed by President **on advice of PM** |


➡ Real power of selection lies with **Prime Minister**


---


## 5.2 Maximum Size of Ministry


| Provision | Detail                                     |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Limit     | **15% of total strength of Lok Sabha**     |
| Amendment | **91st Constitutional Amendment Act 2003** |


Purpose:

- Prevent jumbo ministries
- Reduce political corruption

---


## 5.3 Disqualification due to Defection


Under **91st Amendment (2003)**:


MP disqualified under **Anti-Defection Law**:


➡ **Cannot become minister**


---


## 5.4 Tenure of Ministers


Ministers hold office:


➡ **During pleasure of the President**


But practically:


➡ President acts on **PM’s advice**


Therefore:


➡ **PM controls tenure of ministers**


---


## 5.5 Collective Responsibility


| Provision                                                  | Article           |
| ---------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| Council of Ministers collectively responsible to Lok Sabha | **Article 75(3)** |


Meaning:


➡ All ministers act as **one unit**


If **No-Confidence Motion passes**:


➡ Entire ministry **must resign**


---


## 5.6 Oath of Ministers


Administered by:


➡ **President of India**


Two types:


| Oath                | Meaning                                 |
| ------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| **Oath of Office**  | Loyalty to Constitution & faithful duty |
| **Oath of Secrecy** | Confidentiality of government matters   |


---


## 5.7 Membership Requirement


Minister must become **MP within 6 months**.


Otherwise:


➡ **Ceases to be minister**


---


## 5.8 Salary and Allowances


Determined by:


➡ **Parliament**


Law:


➡ **Salaries and Allowances of Ministers Act, 1952**


---


# 6. Article 77 — Conduct of Government Business


### Key Provisions


| Provision         | Explanation                             |
| ----------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| Executive actions | Taken **in name of President**          |
| Authentication    | Orders authenticated according to rules |
| Rules of business | Made by **President**                   |


Rules cover:

- Transaction of government business
- Allocation of work among ministers

---


# 7. Article 78 — Duties of Prime Minister


PM acts as **link between President and Council of Ministers**


### Duties


| Duty                | Description                                                        |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Inform President    | Decisions of Council of Ministers                                  |
| Provide information | Administration & legislative proposals                             |
| Refer matters       | Decisions of individual ministers to Council if President requires |


---


# 8. Article 88 — Rights of Ministers in Parliament


Every minister has right to:

- Speak in **Lok Sabha**
- Speak in **Rajya Sabha**
- Participate in **Joint Sitting**
- Participate in **Parliamentary Committees**

But:


➡ **Voting allowed only in House where he is member**


Example:


Rajya Sabha minister:


✔ Can speak in Lok Sabha


❌ Cannot vote there


---


# 9. Nature of Ministerial Advice


Advice under **Article 74** is:


✔ Binding


✔ Confidential


✔ Not subject to judicial inquiry


---


# 10. Supreme Court Judgments


### 1971 Judgment


Court held:


Even after **Lok Sabha dissolution**:


➡ Council of Ministers **continues**


Reason:


President **cannot function without advice**


---


### 1974 Judgment


Court clarified:


“**Satisfaction of President**” actually means:


➡ **Satisfaction of Council of Ministers**


---


# 11. Appointment of Ministers


### Process


1️⃣ President appoints **Prime Minister**


2️⃣ PM advises appointment of **other ministers**


➡ PM has **real authority**


---


### Non-Member as Minister


A person **not MP** can become minister.


Condition:


➡ Must become MP within **6 months**


---


# 12. Deputy Prime Minister (Important Case)


Case:


**Devi Lal Deputy PM case (1990)**


Issue:


Constitution **does not mention Deputy PM**


### Supreme Court Decision


Court held:

- Deputy PM title is **descriptive**
- No additional constitutional powers

Thus:


✔ Appointment **valid**


---

# 14. Types of Ministerial Responsibility


| Type                      | Basis                   |
| ------------------------- | ----------------------- |
| Collective Responsibility | Article 75(3)           |
| Individual Responsibility | Pleasure of President   |
| No Legal Responsibility   | Constitutional practice |


---


# 14.1 Collective Responsibility


Meaning:


➡ Entire Council responsible for government actions


Principle:


**“They sink or swim together.”**


### Consequences


If **No Confidence Motion passes**:


➡ Entire ministry resigns


Even ministers from **Rajya Sabha**


---


### Alternative Option


Instead of resigning:


Council may advise President to:


➡ **Dissolve Lok Sabha**


President may refuse if:


➡ Government clearly lost majority


---


### Cabinet Solidarity


All ministers must support decisions:

- Inside Parliament
- Outside Parliament

If disagreement:


➡ Minister must **resign**


---


### Historical Resignations


| Leader                 | Reason                     |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------- |
| **Dr B. R. Ambedkar**  | Hindu Code Bill (1953)     |
| **C. D. Deshmukh**     | State Reorganisation issue |
| **Arif Mohammad Khan** | Muslim Women Act 1986      |


---


# 14.2 Individual Responsibility


Ministers hold office:


➡ **During pleasure of President**


In practice:


➡ Controlled by **Prime Minister**


PM can:

- Ask minister to resign
- Advise President to dismiss minister

Purpose:

- Maintain discipline
- Enforce collective responsibility

---


### Ambedkar’s Observation


Collective responsibility works because:


➡ **PM can appoint and dismiss ministers**


---


# 14.3 No Legal Responsibility


### British System


King’s orders must be **countersigned by minister**


Minister is **legally responsible**


Principle:


**“The King can do no wrong.”**


---


### Indian System


No legal responsibility because:

- President’s orders **do not need countersignature**
- Courts **cannot examine ministerial advice**

Thus:


Ministers are **politically responsible**, not legally responsible.

# 15. Composition of Council of Ministers


| Category               | Description                 |
| ---------------------- | --------------------------- |
| **Cabinet Ministers**  | Top policy-making ministers |
| **Ministers of State** | Second category ministers   |
| **Deputy Ministers**   | Assist senior ministers     |


Head:


➡ **Prime Minister**


---


# 15.1 Cabinet Ministers


Characteristics:

- Head major ministries
- Attend Cabinet meetings
- Take key policy decisions

Examples:

- Home
- Defence
- Finance
- External Affairs

---


# 15.2 Ministers of State


Two types:


### MOS with Independent Charge

- Head ministry independently
- Similar powers as Cabinet minister
- Usually **do not attend Cabinet meetings**

---


### MOS Attached to Cabinet Minister

- Assist Cabinet minister
- Handle departmental work

---


# 15.3 Deputy Ministers


Characteristics:

- Assist ministers
- No independent department
- Perform parliamentary duties

➡ Do **not attend Cabinet meetings**


---


# 15.4 Parliamentary Secretaries


Role:

- Assist ministers in parliamentary work
- No executive authority

Since:


➡ **1967 rarely used**


---


# 15.5 Deputy Prime Minister


Features:

- **Not mentioned in Constitution**
- Created for **political reasons**
- No special powers

---


# 16. Council of Ministers vs Cabinet


| Feature     | Council of Ministers | Cabinet                          |
| ----------- | -------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Size        | Large (60–70)        | Small (15–20)                    |
| Composition | All ministers        | Only Cabinet ministers           |
| Meetings    | Rarely meets         | Meets frequently                 |
| Role        | Implement decisions  | Policy making                    |
| Status      | Constitutional body  | Constitutional mention (Art 352) |


---


# 17. Role of Cabinet


Cabinet is **real decision-making body**


Functions:

- Policy formulation
- Supreme executive authority
- Administrative coordination
- Advisory body to President
- Crisis management
- Legislative planning
- Financial control
- Key appointments
- Foreign policy decisions

# 19. Cabinet Dictatorship


Scholar:


➡ **Ramsay Muir**


Description:


➡ Cabinet system resembles **“Dictatorship of the Cabinet”**


Reason:

# 20. Kitchen Cabinet


Also called:


➡ **Inner Cabinet**


Composition:

- PM
- 2–4 trusted ministers
- Close advisors/friends

---


### Advantages

- Quick decisions
- Greater secrecy
- Frequent meetings

### Disadvantages

- Weakens official cabinet
- Outsiders influence decisions

Example:


➡ Powerful during **Indira Gandhi era**
