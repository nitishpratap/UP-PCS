# 1️⃣ PRELIMS PYQs (Vice-President)


Format


**Question — Exam — Year — Answer**


---

1. **The Vice-President of India is elected by:**

    — Union Public Service Commission Prelims — 2011


    ✅ **Answer:** **Members of both Houses of Parliament (elected + nominated)**


---

1. **Which Article deals with election of Vice-President?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2013


    ✅ **Answer:** **Article 66**


---

1. **The Vice-President is ex-officio Chairman of:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2014


    ✅ **Answer:** **Rajya Sabha**


---

1. **Minimum age required to become Vice-President:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2015


    ✅ **Answer:** **35 years**


---

1. **Vice-President is removed by:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2016


    ✅ **Answer:** **Resolution of Rajya Sabha + agreement of Lok Sabha**


---

1. **Notice period required for removal of Vice-President:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2017


    ✅ **Answer:** **14 days**


---

1. **Election disputes of Vice-President are decided by:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2018


    ✅ **Answer:** **Supreme Court of India**


---

1. **Vice-President acts as President for a maximum period of:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2019


    ✅ **Answer:** **6 months**


---

1. **Vice-President is elected by which method?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2020


    ✅ **Answer:** **Proportional Representation by Single Transferable Vote (STV)**


---

1. **Who administers oath to the Vice-President?**

    — State PCS Prelims — 2021


    ✅ **Answer:** **President of India**
