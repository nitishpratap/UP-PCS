
---


## 1. Constitutional Position

- The **Vice-President of India** holds the **second highest constitutional office**.
- Ranked **next to the President** in the **Warrant of Precedence**.
- Office inspired by the **Vice-President of the United States**, but functions differ.

---


# 2. Election of Vice-President


### Nature of Election

- **Indirect election**

### Electoral College


Consists of **all members of Parliament**:

- Elected members of **Lok Sabha**
- Nominated members of **Lok Sabha**
- Elected members of **Rajya Sabha**
- Nominated members of **Rajya Sabha**

Thus **both elected and nominated MPs vote**.


---


# 3. Difference: Election of President vs Vice-President


| Feature | President        | Vice-President          |
| ------- | ---------------- | ----------------------- |
| MPs     | Only elected MPs | Elected + nominated MPs |
| MLAs    | Participate      | Do not participate      |
| Nature  | Federal election | Parliamentary election  |


### Explanation (Dr. B. R. Ambedkar)

- **President represents Union + States → MLAs participate**
- **Vice-President mainly presides over Rajya Sabha → MPs only**

---


# 4. Method of Election


Election is conducted by:

- **Proportional Representation System**
- **Single Transferable Vote**
- **Secret Ballot**

Purpose → ensures **fair political representation in Parliament**.


---


# 5. Qualifications


A candidate must:

1. Be a **citizen of India**
2. Be **at least 35 years old**
3. Be **qualified for election as a member of Rajya Sabha**
4. **Not hold office of profit** under:
    - Union Government
    - State Government
    - Local authority
    - Public authority

---


# 6. Office of Profit Exceptions


The following **are not considered office of profit**:

- President
- Vice-President
- Governor
- Union Ministers
- State Ministers

---


# 7. Nomination Requirements


To contest the election:

- **20 proposers**
- **20 seconders**

(All must be **MPs**)


### Security Deposit

- **₹15,000 deposited in RBI**

Deposit may be **forfeited if required votes not secured**.


---


# 8. Oath or Affirmation


Before assuming office, the Vice-President takes an oath to:

- Bear **true faith and allegiance to the Constitution**
- **Faithfully discharge duties**

### Administered by

- **President of India**
- or **person appointed by President**

---


# 9. Conditions of Office


### 1. Not a Member of Legislature


Vice-President **cannot be a member of**:

- Parliament
- State Legislature

If an MP becomes Vice-President → **seat automatically vacates**.


### 2. No Office of Profit


Cannot hold **any other office of profit**.


---


# 10. Term of Office

- **5 years** from date of entering office.

### Key Points

- May **resign anytime** (to President)
- Can be **removed before term**
- **Eligible for re-election any number of times**

### Continuity Rule


Vice-President continues **until successor assumes office**.


---


# 11. Removal of Vice-President


Vice-President **is NOT impeached**.


### Removal Procedure

1. **Resolution passed in Rajya Sabha**
2. Must be passed by **Absolute Majority**

    (majority of total membership)

3. **Lok Sabha must agree to it**

### Notice Requirement

- **14 days advance notice required**

### Important Feature

- **No grounds specified in Constitution** for removal.

---


# 12. Vacancy in Office


Vacancy may occur due to:

- Expiry of term
- Resignation
- Removal
- Death
- Disqualification
- Election declared void by Supreme Court

---


# 13. Filling the Vacancy


### 1. Vacancy due to Expiry


Election held **before expiry of term**.


### 2. Vacancy due to Death / Resignation / Removal


Election held **as soon as possible**.


New Vice-President gets **full 5-year term** from date of office.


---


# 14. Powers and Functions of Vice-President


The functions of the Vice-President are **two-fold**.


---


# 1. Ex-Officio Chairman of Rajya Sabha


The **primary function** of the Vice-President is to act as the **Chairman of the Rajya Sabha**.


### Powers

- Presides over **Rajya Sabha proceedings**
- Maintains **order and discipline**
- Allows **debates and discussions**
- Decides **points of order**
- Supervises **functioning of the House**

### Voting Power

- **No vote in the first instance**
- Can cast a **casting vote in case of tie**

### Comparison

- Similar to the **Speaker of Lok Sabha**, but presides over **Rajya Sabha**.

---


# 2. Acting President of India


The Vice-President **acts as President** in case of vacancy in the office of President.


### Vacancy may arise due to:

- Death
- Resignation
- Removal
- Otherwise (invalid election etc.)

### Duration


Vice-President can act as President for **maximum 6 months**.


Within **6 months a new President must be elected**.


---


# 3. When President is Temporarily Unable to Work


Vice-President **discharges the functions of President** if the President is unable to perform duties due to:

- Illness
- Absence
- Any other reason

Vice-President performs the duties **until the President resumes office**.


---


# 4. When Vice-President Acts as President


During this period:

- Vice-President **does not perform Rajya Sabha Chairman duties**

Instead:


➡ **Deputy Chairman of Rajya Sabha presides over the House**.


---


# 15. Indian vs American Vice-President


| Feature    | India                          | USA                |
| ---------- | ------------------------------ | ------------------ |
| Succession | Acts as President temporarily  | Becomes President  |
| Duration   | Until new President elected    | For remaining term |
| Role       | Mainly Chairman of Rajya Sabha | Executive role     |


Thus:

- **India → temporary acting role**
- **USA → full Presidential succession**

---


# 16. Importance of Vice-President


The Constitution assigns **limited executive powers** to the Vice-President.


Some scholars describe the office as:


**“His Superfluous Highness.”**


However, the position ensures:

- **Continuity of government**
- **Stability of constitutional system**
- **Smooth functioning of Parliament**

---


# 17. Emoluments (Salary & Allowances)


The Constitution **does not fix a salary for the Vice-President** in that capacity.


Instead he receives salary as:


➡ **Chairman of Rajya Sabha**


### Salary


In **2008**, salary increased from:


**₹40,000 → ₹1.25 lakh per month**


### Other Facilities


Vice-President receives:

- Daily allowance
- Free furnished residence
- Medical facilities
- Travel allowances
- Staff and other privileges

---


# Salary When Acting as President


When acting as President:

- Vice-President **does not receive Rajya Sabha Chairman salary**

Instead receives:


➡ **Salary and allowances of the President of India**.

# 20. Constitutional Amendment Related to VP Election


Originally:


Vice-President election was conducted through a **joint sitting of Parliament**.


This was changed by:


**11th Constitutional Amendment Act, 1961**


Now:


➡ Election conducted through **separate voting by MPs**.
