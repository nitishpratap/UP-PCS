
# Election Commission of India (ECI)


(Article 324)


---


# 1. Constitutional Status


### Establishment

- The **Election Commission of India (ECI)** is a **permanent and independent constitutional body**.
- Established **directly by the Constitution of India**.

### Constitutional Provision

- **Article 324** provides for the **Election Commission**.

### Powers under Article 324


The Election Commission has the power of:

- **Superintendence**
- **Direction**
- **Control**

of elections to:

1. Parliament
2. State Legislatures
3. President of India
4. Vice-President of India

### Nature of Body

- It is an **All-India body**.
- Works for **both Central Government and State Governments**.

### Important Limitation


ECI **does NOT conduct elections for:**

- Panchayats
- Municipalities

These elections are conducted by the **State Election Commission**.


### Constitutional Provision for State Election Commission

- Created through **73rd Constitutional Amendment Act (1992)**
- and **74th Constitutional Amendment Act (1992)**.

---


# 2. Composition of Election Commission


According to **Article 324**, the composition is determined as follows.


### Members


The Election Commission consists of:

1. **Chief Election Commissioner (CEC)**
2. **Other Election Commissioners (ECs)** as determined by the President.

### Appointment

- Appointed by the **President of India**.

### Chairman

- When multiple election commissioners are appointed:
    - **CEC acts as Chairman** of the Commission.

### Regional Commissioners

- The **President may appoint Regional Commissioners**
- Appointment is made **after consultation with the Election Commission**.

### Service Conditions

- Determined by the **President of India**.

---


# 3. Evolution of the Election Commission


### 1950 – 1989

- Election Commission functioned as a **single-member body**.
- Only **Chief Election Commissioner** existed.

### 16 October 1989

- **Two Election Commissioners were appointed**.

Reason:

- Voting age reduced from **21 years → 18 years**.

### Constitutional Amendment Responsible

- **61st Constitutional Amendment Act, 1988**

### January 1990

- The two additional posts were **abolished**.
- Commission again became **single-member body**.

### October 1993

- Two Election Commissioners were **reappointed**.

### Present Structure

- **Three-member body**
    - 1 Chief Election Commissioner
    - 2 Election Commissioners

---


# 4. Status of Election Commissioners


### Equality of Members

- **CEC and Election Commissioners have equal powers**.

### Salary and Allowances

- Equal salary and perquisites.
- Similar to **Judge of Supreme Court**.

Example (historical reference):

- In **2009**, salary of Supreme Court judge was **₹90,000 per month**.

### Decision Making


If disagreement occurs:

- Decision taken by **majority**.

---


# 5. Tenure of Office


Election Commissioners hold office for:

- **6 years**

OR

- Until **65 years of age**

Whichever occurs earlier.


### Resignation

- Can resign anytime.

### Removal

- Can be removed **before expiry of term**.

(Procedure explained in independence section)


---


# 6. Independence of Election Commission


To ensure **independence and impartial functioning**, the Constitution provides safeguards.


---


## 1. Security of Tenure (CEC)


The **Chief Election Commissioner**:

- Cannot be removed easily.
- Removal procedure is **same as Supreme Court Judge**.

### Removal Procedure


CEC can be removed by:

- **President**
- After resolution passed by **both Houses of Parliament**
- With **Special Majority**

### Grounds of Removal

1. **Proved Misbehaviour**
2. **Incapacity**

Important point:


CEC **does NOT hold office at the pleasure of the President**.


---


## 2. Service Conditions Protection

- Service conditions **cannot be varied to the disadvantage** of CEC after appointment.

---


## 3. Protection of Other Commissioners


Election Commissioners and Regional Commissioners:

- Can be removed **only on recommendation of the CEC**.

---


# 7. Weaknesses in Independence (Important for Exams)


Despite safeguards, the Constitution has **some shortcomings**.


### 1. No Qualifications Specified


The Constitution **does not specify**:

- Educational qualification
- Legal qualification
- Administrative qualification
- Judicial qualification

for Election Commissioners.


---


### 2. Term Not Specified in Constitution

- The Constitution **does not specify tenure** of Election Commissioners.
- It is determined by **law or executive decision**.

---


### 3. Post-Retirement Jobs Allowed

- Constitution **does not prohibit future government appointments** for retired Election Commissioners.

This may affect independence.


---

Powers can be classified into **three categories**.


### 1. Administrative Powers


### 2. Advisory Powers


### 3. Quasi-Judicial Powers

# 9. Detailed Powers and Functions


### 1. Delimitation of Constituencies

- Determines **territorial areas of electoral constituencies**.

Based on:

- **Delimitation Commission Act** passed by Parliament.

Delimitation Acts:

- 1952
- 1962
- 1972
- 2002

---


### 2. Preparation of Electoral Rolls


Responsibilities:

- Prepare electoral rolls
- Revise them periodically
- Register all eligible voters.

---


### 3. Conduct of Elections


The Commission:

- Announces **election dates**
- Declares **election schedule**
- Scrutinizes **nomination papers**

---


### 4. Recognition of Political Parties


ECI:

- Grants recognition to political parties.
- Allots **election symbols**.

---


### 5. Settlement of Disputes


ECI acts as a **court-like authority** in disputes regarding:

- Recognition of political parties
- Allotment of election symbols

This is a **quasi-judicial function**.


---


### 6. Inquiry Officers


ECI can:

- Appoint officers to investigate **electoral disputes**.

---


### 7. Model Code of Conduct


ECI determines:

- **Code of conduct** for political parties and candidates during elections.

---


### 8. Media Time Allocation


ECI prepares **roster for publicity** of political party policies on:

- Radio
- Television

during elections.


---


### 9. Advice to President


ECI advises the **President** regarding:

- Disqualification of **Members of Parliament**.

---


### 10. Advice to Governor


ECI advises the **Governor** regarding:

- Disqualification of **Members of State Legislature**.

---


### 11. Cancellation of Polls


ECI can cancel polls in cases of:

- Booth capturing
- Rigging
- Violence
- Other irregularities

---


### 12. Election Staff


ECI may request:

- **President**
- **Governor**

to provide **staff for election work**.


---


### 13. Supervision of Elections


ECI supervises **entire election machinery** to ensure:

- Free elections
- Fair elections

---


### 14. Elections During President's Rule


ECI advises the **President** whether:

- Elections can be held in a state under **President's Rule**.

This helps in deciding **extension of emergency after one year**.


---


### 15. Registration of Political Parties


ECI registers political parties and grants them:

- **National Party status**
- **State Party status**

based on **poll performance**.


---


# 10. Election Administration Machinery


The Election Commission **does not conduct elections alone**.


It works through a **large administrative machinery** at **central, state and district levels**.


---


# 1. Deputy Election Commissioners

- Appointed by **Election Commission of India**.
- Usually **drawn from civil services**.
- Work under a **tenure system**.

### Role


They assist the Election Commission in:

- Planning elections
- Managing election logistics
- Coordinating with states
- Supervising electoral operations.

---


# 2. Secretariat of Election Commission


The Election Commission has a **secretariat**.


Officials include:

- Secretaries
- Joint Secretaries
- Deputy Secretaries
- Under Secretaries

### Function


They assist in:

- Administrative work
- Election planning
- Legal matters
- Communication with states.

---


# 3. State Level Election Machinery


At the **state level**, ECI is assisted by the:


### Chief Electoral Officer (CEO)

- Appointed by the **Chief Election Commissioner**
- Appointment is done **in consultation with the State Government**.

### Responsibilities


The CEO:

- Supervises election work in the state
- Coordinates between **state government and Election Commission**
- Ensures electoral rolls are prepared properly
- Supervises polling arrangements.

---


# 4. District Level Machinery


At district level elections are managed by:


### District Returning Officer

- Usually the **District Collector (District Magistrate)**.

### Responsibilities

- Conduct elections in the district
- Coordinate with Election Commission
- Supervise election staff
- Manage polling stations.

---


# 5. Constituency Level Machinery


### Returning Officer (RO)

- Appointed for **each constituency**.

Responsibilities:

- Accept nomination papers
- Scrutinize nominations
- Conduct polling
- Declare election results.

---


# 6. Polling Booth Level


### Presiding Officer

- Appointed for **each polling booth**.

Responsibilities:

- Conduct voting
- Ensure fair voting process
- Maintain order at polling booth
- Seal ballot/EVM after voting.

---


# Election Administrative Hierarchy (Very Important)


```plain text
Election Commission of India
        ↓
Deputy Election Commissioners
        ↓
Chief Electoral Officer (State Level)
        ↓
District Collector / District Returning Officer
        ↓
Returning Officer (Constituency)
        ↓
Presiding Officer (Polling Booth)
```

# 11. Vision of Election Commission


The Election Commission aims to become an **institution of excellence**.


### Vision


To enhance:

- Active engagement
- Participation of citizens
- Strengthening electoral democracy

both:

- In India
- Globally.

---


# 12. Mission of Election Commission


The mission of ECI is to:

- Maintain **independence**
- Maintain **integrity**
- Maintain **autonomy**

while conducting elections.


### Key Goals


Ensure:

- Accessibility
- Inclusiveness
- Ethical participation

by stakeholders.


### Objective


Conduct elections that are:

- Free
- Fair
- Transparent

so that people **trust the democratic system**.
