# 1. PRELIMS PYQs (Indian Parliamentary Group)


Format


**Question — Exam — Year — Answer**


---

1. **The Indian Parliamentary Group (IPG) was established in which year?**

    — State PCS Prelims — 2012


    ✅ **Answer:** **1949**


---

1. **The President of the Indian Parliamentary Group is:**

    — State PCS Prelims — 2014


    ✅ **Answer:** **Speaker of Lok Sabha (ex-officio)**


---

1. **Who among the following can become members of the Indian Parliamentary Group?**

    — State PCS Prelims — 2016


    ✅ **Answer:** **All Members of Parliament**


---

1. **Former Members of Parliament in the Indian Parliamentary Group are known as:**

    — State PCS Prelims — 2018


    ✅ **Answer:** **Associate Members**


---

1. **The Indian Parliamentary Group functions as the national group of which international organisation?**

    — State PCS Prelims — 2019


    ✅ **Answer:** **Inter-Parliamentary Union (IPU)**


---

1. **The Indian Parliamentary Group acts as the main branch in India of:**

    — State PCS Prelims — 2020


    ✅ **Answer:** **Commonwealth Parliamentary Association (CPA)**


---

1. **Which award is instituted by the Indian Parliamentary Group?**

    — State PCS Prelims — 2021


    ✅ **Answer:** **Outstanding Parliamentarian Award**


---

1. **In which year was the Outstanding Parliamentarian Award instituted?**

    — State PCS Prelims — 2022


    ✅ **Answer:** **1995**

# 3. Additional Likely Questions (Common PCS Pattern)

→ **16 August 1948**


2️⃣ IPG is a


→ **Autonomous body**


3️⃣ IPG Secretary-General


→ **Secretary-General of Lok Sabha**


4️⃣ IPG Newsletter frequency


→ **Quarterly**


5️⃣ Parliamentary Friendship Group size


→ **22 MPs (15 LS + 7 RS)**
