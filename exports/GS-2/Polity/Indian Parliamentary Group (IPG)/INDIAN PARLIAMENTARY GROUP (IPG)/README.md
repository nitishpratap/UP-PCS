# 1. RATIONALE / BACKGROUND OF IPG


Explained by **M.N. Kaul & S.L. Shakdher**


### Core Idea


Growing **global interdependence** requires **cooperation among national parliaments** to strengthen democracy and address international issues.


---


### Need for IPG


| Purpose                               | Explanation                                                               |
| ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Promote Inter-Parliamentary Relations | National parliaments must maintain links with foreign parliaments         |
| Protect Democracy                     | Legislators across nations cooperate to safeguard democratic institutions |
| Address Global Problems               | Security, economic crises, development, peace, stability                  |
| Exchange of Ideas                     | Sharing parliamentary practices and experiences                           |
| Interaction of Parliamentary Systems  | Older vs younger parliaments, different institutional systems             |
| Frank Discussions                     | Parliamentary dialogue more open than diplomatic conferences              |
| Learning from Parliamentary Problems  | Issues faced in one parliament may emerge elsewhere                       |


---


### Mechanism for Parliamentary Diplomacy


India maintains relations through:

- Exchange of **parliamentary delegations**
- **Goodwill missions**
- **Correspondence**
- **Exchange of parliamentary documents**

These activities are coordinated through **Indian Parliamentary Group (IPG)**.


---


### International Role of IPG


IPG acts as:


1️⃣ **National Group of Inter-Parliamentary Union (IPU)**


2️⃣ **India Branch of Commonwealth Parliamentary Association (CPA)**


---


# 2. COMPOSITION OF IPG


| Feature      | Detail                                                   |
| ------------ | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| Nature       | **Autonomous body**                                      |
| Formation    | **1949**                                                 |
| Legal Origin | Motion adopted by **Constituent Assembly (Legislative)** |
| Motion Date  | **16 August 1948**                                       |


---


## Membership


### Regular Members


All **Members of Parliament**

- Lok Sabha MPs
- Rajya Sabha MPs

---


### Associate Members


Eligible:

- **Former Members of Parliament**

Rights:

- Limited rights compared to MPs.

Restrictions:


Associate members **cannot**

1. Represent India in **IPU meetings**
2. Represent India in **CPA conferences**
3. Receive certain **travel concessions of CPA branches**

# 5. FUNCTIONS OF IPG


---


## 1. Bridge Between Parliaments


Acts as a **link between Indian Parliament and foreign parliaments** through:

- Delegations
- Goodwill missions
- Correspondence
- Document exchange

---


## 2. Role in International Parliamentary Organisations


IPG represents India as:


1️⃣ **National Group of Inter-Parliamentary Union (IPU)**


2️⃣ **Main Branch of Commonwealth Parliamentary Association (CPA)**


---


## 3. Addresses by Foreign Leaders


IPG organises addresses by:

- Visiting **Heads of State**
- Visiting **Heads of Government**
- Eminent personalities

---


## 4. Seminars and Symposia


Organised on **current parliamentary issues**


Types:

- National seminars
- International seminars

---


## 5. Assistance to MPs Visiting Abroad


Members receive:


Letters of introduction to:

- Secretaries of **IPU National Groups**
- Secretaries of **CPA branches**

Indian diplomatic missions are informed to assist visiting MPs.


---


## 6. Eligibility for Parliamentary Delegations


MPs can join foreign parliamentary delegations only if they:


➡ Have been **IPG members for at least 6 months**


---


## 7. Information Dissemination


IPG publishes:


### **IPG Newsletter**


| Feature   | Detail                      |
| --------- | --------------------------- |
| Frequency | **Quarterly**               |
| Sent to   | Members & associate members |
| Purpose   | Inform about IPG activities |


---


## 8. Outstanding Parliamentarian Award


| Feature        | Detail                                |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Award          | **Outstanding Parliamentarian Award** |
| Instituted     | **1995**                              |
| Selection      | Committee of **5 persons**            |
| Constituted by | **Speaker of Lok Sabha**              |


Committee invites and finalises nominations.


---


## 9. Parliamentary Friendship Groups


Established to promote **bilateral parliamentary relations**.


### Objectives

- Political cooperation
- Social contacts
- Cultural exchanges
- Parliamentary information exchange

---


### Composition of Friendship Groups


| House       | MPs        |
| ----------- | ---------- |
| Lok Sabha   | **15**     |
| Rajya Sabha | **7**      |
| Total       | **22 MPs** |


Representation based on **party strength in both Houses**.


---


### Leadership


Appointed by **Speaker of Lok Sabha**

- President
- Vice President (Lok Sabha)
- Vice President (Rajya Sabha)

# 6. INTER-PARLIAMENTARY UNION (IPU)


### Nature


**International organisation of parliaments of sovereign states**


---


### Membership

- **153 national parliaments**

---


### Objectives of IPU

1. Promote **peace and cooperation among peoples**
2. Strengthen **representative institutions**
3. Exchange parliamentary experiences
4. Improve democratic institutions
5. Discuss international issues of common concern

---


### Activities


IPU promotes:

- Parliamentary contacts
- Coordination among legislatures
- Exchange of parliamentary practices
- Discussion on international issues
- Parliamentary reforms
- Strengthening global institutions

---


### Advantages for Indian MPs


IPG membership allows MPs to:

- Establish contacts with foreign parliamentarians
- Study political reforms abroad
- Meet foreign MPs during visits
- Participate in **Inter-Parliamentary Conferences**

---


### Representation of India in IPU


Indian MPs have served as:

- Committee office bearers
- Rapporteurs
- Drafting committee chairpersons

Helps India present its **views on global issues**.


---


# 7. COMMONWEALTH PARLIAMENTARY ASSOCIATION (CPA)


### Nature


Association of **Commonwealth parliamentarians**


---


### Membership


| Category         | Number                                                         |
| ---------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Parliamentarians | ~ **17,000**                                                   |
| Legislatures     | **175 national, state, provincial & territorial legislatures** |


---


### Objectives of CPA

1. Promote understanding of:
    - Constitutional systems
    - Legislative systems
    - Economic systems
    - Social systems
    - Cultural systems
2. Strengthen **parliamentary democracy in the Commonwealth**
3. Create an informed parliamentary community
4. Promote democratic governance
5. Encourage cooperation among legislatures

---


# Advantages of IPG Membership (CPA Related)


---


### 1. Conferences and Seminars


Members can attend:

- Plenary conferences
- Regional conferences
- Seminars
- Parliamentary exchange programmes

Provided by **Parliamentary Information & Reference Centre (CPA Secretariat)**

- Parliamentary matters

- Commonwealth affairs

---


### 4. Introductions Abroad


CPA branches help MPs visiting other Commonwealth countries by arranging:

- Introductions
- Meetings with parliamentarians

---


### 5. Parliamentary Facilities


Members visiting Commonwealth legislatures receive:

- Parliamentary courtesies
- Access to debates
- Interaction with local MPs

---


### 6. Study Tours


Some CPA branches organise:

- Study tours
- Institutional visits

Purpose:

- Compare political and procedural developments.
