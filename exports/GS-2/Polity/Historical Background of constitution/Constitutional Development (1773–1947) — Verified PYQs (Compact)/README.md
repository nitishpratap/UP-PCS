# PRELIMS PYQs (with Answers)


Format


**Question — Exam — Year — Answer**


---

1. **Which Act first attempted to regulate the affairs of the East India Company in India?**

    — Union Public Service Commission Prelims — 2010


    ✅ **Answer:** **Regulating Act, 1773**


---

1. **Who was the first Governor-General of Bengal?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2012


    ✅ **Answer:** **Warren Hastings**


---

1. **Which Act established the system of dual control in India?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2013


    ✅ **Answer:** **Pitt’s India Act, 1784**


---

1. **Under which Act was the Governor-General of Bengal designated as Governor-General of India?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2014


    ✅ **Answer:** **Charter Act, 1833**


---

1. **Open competition for civil services in India was introduced by:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2015


    ✅ **Answer:** **Charter Act, 1853**


---

1. **The office of Secretary of State for India was created by:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2016


    ✅ **Answer:** **Government of India Act, 1858**


---

1. **Separate electorates for Muslims were introduced by:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2017


    ✅ **Answer:** **Indian Councils Act, 1909 (Morley–Minto Reforms)**


---

1. **Dyarchy in provinces was introduced by:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2018


    ✅ **Answer:** **Government of India Act, 1919**


---

1. **Provincial autonomy was introduced by:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2019


    ✅ **Answer:** **Government of India Act, 1935**


---

1. **India and Pakistan were created as two dominions under:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2020


    ✅ **Answer:** **Indian Independence Act, 1947**
