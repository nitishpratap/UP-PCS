# 1️⃣ ARRIVAL OF BRITISH IN INDIA


| Event                      | Year     | Key Facts                                                         |
| -------------------------- | -------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| East India Company Charter | **1600** | Granted by **Queen Elizabeth I** → Exclusive trade rights in East |
| Battle of Buxar            | **1764** | British defeated **Mughal + Nawab of Bengal + Nawab of Awadh**    |
| Diwani Rights              | **1765** | Granted by **Shah Alam II**                                       |


### Diwani Rights


Authority to:

- **Collect revenue**
- Administer **civil justice**

Regions:

- Bengal
- Bihar
- Orissa

Result


➡ Company transformed from **trading body → territorial power**

**1858 – 1947**

# 5️⃣ COMPANY RULE (1773–1858)


Key Constitutional Acts


| Act              | Year     | Key Feature                  |
| ---------------- | -------- | ---------------------------- |
| Regulating Act   | **1773** | Parliamentary control begins |

| Charter Act      | **1833** | Centralisation               |
| Charter Act      | **1853** | Civil service exams          |


---


# 6️⃣ REGULATING ACT, 1773


### Significance

- First **British Parliamentary control over Company**
- Recognised **political role of Company**
- Beginning of **central administration**

### Key Provisions


| Provision                  | Details                                   |
| -------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| Governor-General of Bengal | Created                                   |
| First GG                   | **Lord Warren Hastings**                  |
| Executive Council          | **4 members**                             |
| Presidencies               | **Bombay & Madras subordinate to Bengal** |


### Supreme Court (1774)


Structure:


| Chief Justice | Judges |
| ------------- | ------ |
| 1             | 3      |


First Chief Justice


**Sir Elijah Impey**


### Restrictions on Company Officials


Prohibited:

- Private trade
- Accepting gifts
- Accepting bribes

### Parliamentary Control


Court of Directors must report on:

- Civil affairs
- Military affairs
- Revenue

---


# 7️⃣ ACT OF SETTLEMENT (1781)


Also called


➡ **Amending Act of 1781**


Purpose


➡ Remove defects of **Regulating Act 1773**


Key Point


➡ Supreme Court jurisdiction **limited mainly to Calcutta**


---


# 8️⃣ PITT’S INDIA ACT, 1784


Created **Dual Government (Double Control)**


| Authority          | Function           |
| ------------------ | ------------------ |
| Court of Directors | Commercial affairs |
| Board of Control   | Political affairs  |


Board of Control supervised:

- Civil administration
- Military administration
- Revenue

### Significance

- Company territories called **“British Possessions in India”**
- British Government gained **supreme control**

---


# 9️⃣ CHARTER ACT, 1793


Key provisions:

- Company monopoly extended **20 years**
- Company must pay **£5 lakh annually** to British government
- Senior officials **cannot leave India without permission**

---


# 🔟 CHARTER ACT, 1813


Major Features


| Provision             | Details                                |
| --------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| Trade monopoly ended  | Except **Tea & China trade**           |
| Missionaries allowed  | Entry for **propagating Christianity** |
| Education grant       | **₹1 lakh per year**                   |
| Company rule extended | **20 years**                           |


---


# 11️⃣ CHARTER ACT, 1833


Major step toward **administrative centralisation**


### Governor-General of India


| Before                     | After                         |
| -------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| Governor-General of Bengal | **Governor-General of India** |


First GG of India


**Lord William Bentinck**


### Legislative Centralisation

- Bombay & Madras **lost legislative powers**
- Power centralized with **Governor-General**

### Other Provisions

- Laws renamed **Regulations → Acts**
- Company **stopped commercial activities**
- Indians **not to be barred from public services** (not implemented)

---


# 12️⃣ CHARTER ACT, 1853


Last Charter Act.


### Key Features


| Provision            | Details                                                |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| Separation of powers | Legislative & Executive separated                      |
| Legislative council  | Expanded with **6 legislative members**                |
| Mini-Parliament      | Legislative council functioned like British Parliament |
| Civil services       | Recruitment through **open competitive exam**          |
| Committee            | **Macaulay Committee (1854)**                          |


### Representation introduced


Members from:

- Bengal
- Bombay
- Madras
- Agra

### Charter without time limit


Earlier charters renewed **every 20 years**


➡ 1853 Act removed time limit


Meaning


➡ Company rule could end anytime


---


# 13️⃣ GOVERNMENT OF INDIA ACT, 1858


Also called


**Act for the Good Government of India**


Passed after **Revolt of 1857**


### Key Provisions


| Provision         | Details                     |
| ----------------- | --------------------------- |
| Transfer of power | Company → **British Crown** |
| Governor-General  | Became **Viceroy**          |
| First Viceroy     | **Lord Canning**            |
| Dual Government   | Abolished                   |


Bodies abolished:

- Board of Control
- Court of Directors

### Secretary of State for India


Features:

- Member of **British Cabinet**
- Responsible to **British Parliament**
- Controlled Indian administration

### Council of India

- **15 members**
- Advisory body

### Secretary of State-in-Council


Corporate body


Can **sue and be sued**


---


# 14️⃣ INDIAN COUNCILS ACT, 1861


Major constitutional landmark.


### Features


| Provision           | Details                                              |
| ------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| Indians in councils | Viceroy could nominate Indians                       |
| Examples            | Raja of Benaras, Maharaja of Patiala, Sir Dinkar Rao |
| Decentralisation    | Legislative powers restored to **Bombay & Madras**   |


### Provincial Councils


| Province | Year |
| -------- | ---- |
| Bengal   | 1862 |
| NWFP     | 1866 |
| Punjab   | 1897 |


### Portfolio System


Introduced by


**Lord Canning (1859)**


Meaning


➡ Ministers responsible for departments


Origin of **Cabinet system**


### Ordinance Power


Viceroy could issue ordinances


Validity


**6 months**


Influenced


➡ **Article 123**


---


# 15️⃣ INDIAN COUNCILS ACT, 1892


### Features


| Provision             | Details                      |
| --------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Expansion of councils | Additional members increased |
| Official majority     | Retained                     |
| Budget                | Discussion allowed           |


But


❌ **No voting on budget**


Budget introduced in India


**1860**


### Indirect Elections


Members nominated based on recommendations from:

- Municipalities
- District boards
- Universities
- Chambers of commerce
- Zamindars

Important


Term **“Election” not used**


---


# 16️⃣ INDIAN COUNCILS ACT, 1909


(Morley–Minto Reforms)


| Person      | Role               |
| ----------- | ------------------ |
| Lord Morley | Secretary of State |
| Lord Minto  | Viceroy            |


### Major Features


| Provision             | Details              |
| --------------------- | -------------------- |
| Central council size  | **16 → 60 members**  |
| Official majority     | Retained at centre   |
| Non-official majority | Allowed in provinces |


### Legislative Powers


Members could:

- Ask supplementary questions
- Move budget resolutions
- Debate policies

### Indians in Executive Council


First Indian


**Satyendra Prasad Sinha**


Portfolio


**Law Member**


### Separate Electorates (VERY IMPORTANT)


Introduced for:


➡ **Muslims**


Impact


➡ Beginning of **communal politics**


Lord Minto called


**Father of Communal Electorate**

# GOVERNMENT OF INDIA ACT, 1919


(Montagu–Chelmsford Reforms)


Policy declared


**20 Aug 1917**


Objective


➡ Gradual responsible government


Came into force


**1921**


---


### Major Features


### Division of Subjects

- Central subjects
- Provincial subjects

---


### Dyarchy in Provinces


| Category    | Examples                                   |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Transferred | Education, Public health, Local government |
| Reserved    | Police, Finance, Revenue                   |


Meaning


**Dyarchy = Dual Government**


Origin


Greek **Di-arche**


Outcome


➡ Failed system


---


### Bicameral Legislature


First time in India


| House                | Type        |
| -------------------- | ----------- |
| Council of State     | Upper House |
| Legislative Assembly | Lower House |


---


### Other Features

- **3 Indians** in Viceroy’s Executive Council
- Separate electorates extended to
    - Sikhs
    - Anglo-Indians
    - Europeans
    - Indian Christians
- Limited franchise (property, tax, education)
- **Public Service Commission provision**

Result


➡ **Central Public Service Commission (1926)**


Recommended by


**Lee Commission (1923–24)**

Slogan

- **321 Sections**
- **10 Schedules**

---


### Major Features


### All-India Federation

- British provinces
- Princely states

| List       | Subjects |
| ---------- | -------- |

| Concurrent | 36       |


Residuary powers

Dyarchy **abolished in provinces**


Implemented


**1937**


Ended


**1939**


---


### Dyarchy at Centre


Introduced but **never implemented**

**15 Aug 1947**


---
