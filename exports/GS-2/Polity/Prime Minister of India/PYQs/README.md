# 1️⃣ PRELIMS PYQs (Prime Minister)


Format


**Question — Exam — Year — Answer**


---

1. **The Prime Minister of India is appointed by:**

    — Union Public Service Commission Prelims — 2011


    ✅ **Answer:** **President of India**


---

1. **Which Article deals with appointment of Prime Minister?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2013


    ✅ **Answer:** **Article 75**


---

1. **Council of Ministers is collectively responsible to:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2014


    ✅ **Answer:** **Lok Sabha**


---

1. **A person who is not a member of Parliament can remain Prime Minister for a maximum period of:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2015


    ✅ **Answer:** **6 months**


---

1. **Which Article defines the duties of Prime Minister towards the President?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2016


    ✅ **Answer:** **Article 78**


---

1. **The Prime Minister is the head of:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2017


    ✅ **Answer:** **Council of Ministers**


---

1. **Prime Minister is the link between:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2018


    ✅ **Answer:** **President and Council of Ministers**


---

1. **If the Prime Minister resigns, what happens to the Council of Ministers?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2019


    ✅ **Answer:** **Entire Council of Ministers resigns**


---

1. **Which Article provides that President shall act on advice of Council of Ministers?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2020


    ✅ **Answer:** **Article 74**


---

1. **Prime Minister can be a member of:**

    — State PCS Prelims — 2021


    ✅ **Answer:** **Either Lok Sabha or Rajya Sabha**

# 3️⃣ Additional High-Probability Questions (PCS Pattern)

→ **Article 75**


2️⃣ Duties of PM toward President


→ **Article 78**


3️⃣ Real executive of India


→ **Prime Minister**


4️⃣ PM oath administered by


→ **President**


5️⃣ Collective responsibility of ministers


→ **Lok Sabha**

1️⃣ Explain **powers and functions of Prime Minister**.


2️⃣ Discuss **PM as head of Council of Ministers**.


3️⃣ Explain **PM’s role in Parliament**.


4️⃣ Examine **PM–President relationship**.


5️⃣ Discuss **Prime Ministerial government vs Cabinet government**.
