# 1. POSITION OF PRIME MINISTER IN INDIAN POLITY


### Parliamentary System


India follows **Parliamentary Form of Government**.


| Authority      | Type                            | Role               |
| -------------- | ------------------------------- | ------------------ |
| President      | **Nominal Executive (De Jure)** | Head of State      |
| Prime Minister | **Real Executive (De Facto)**   | Head of Government |


**Meaning**


| Term                   | Meaning                           |
| ---------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| **De Jure Executive**  | Constitutional / formal authority |
| **De Facto Executive** | Actual authority exercising power |


✔ **President → Head of State**


✔ **Prime Minister → Head of Government**


➡ **Prime Minister is the most powerful functionary in Indian political system.**


**Dr B. R. Ambedkar**

> If any functionary under our Constitution is to be compared with the US President, it is the **Prime Minister and not the President.**

---


# 2. APPOINTMENT OF PRIME MINISTER


### Constitutional Provision


**Article 75**

> Prime Minister shall be appointed by the President.

However:


⚠ Constitution **does not specify detailed procedure**


➡ **Constitutional conventions followed**


---


## Normal Situation (Clear Majority)


When **one party has majority in Lok Sabha**


➡ President appoints **leader of majority party** as Prime Minister.


Example


| PM            | Year       |
| ------------- | ---------- |
| Narendra Modi | 2014, 2019 |


---


## Hung Parliament (No Clear Majority)


When **no party has majority**


➡ President uses **discretion**


Usually appoints:


➡ Leader of **largest party / coalition**


Then asks PM to:


➡ **Prove majority in Lok Sabha**


Usually through **Confidence Vote within about one month**


---


### Example


| Event        | Detail                           |
| ------------ | -------------------------------- |
| Year         | 1979                             |
| President    | Neelam Sanjiva Reddy             |
| PM Appointed | Charan Singh                     |
| Reason       | Fall of Morarji Desai government |


---


## Special Situation: Sudden Death of PM


Example


| Event     | Detail                     |
| --------- | -------------------------- |
| Year      | 1984                       |
| PM        | Indira Gandhi assassinated |
| President | Zail Singh                 |
| Appointed | Rajiv Gandhi               |


⚠ This **bypassed caretaker PM convention**


Later:


✔ Congress Parliamentary Party **elected Rajiv Gandhi unanimously**


---


## When Ruling Party Elects New Leader


If ruling party elects new leader after PM death/resignation


➡ President **must appoint that person**


❗ President **has no discretion**


---


# Majority Proof Rule (Important Case)


### Delhi High Court Judgment (1980)


Constitution **does NOT require majority proof before appointment**


✔ Correct sequence:

1. President appoints PM
2. PM proves majority in Lok Sabha **within reasonable time**

---


### Examples


| Prime Minister      | Year |
| ------------------- | ---- |
| Charan Singh        | 1979 |
| V. P. Singh         | 1989 |
| Chandra Shekhar     | 1990 |
| P. V. Narasimha Rao | 1991 |
| A. B. Vajpayee      | 1996 |
| H. D. Deve Gowda    | 1996 |
| I. K. Gujral        | 1997 |
| A. B. Vajpayee      | 1998 |


---


# PM WITHOUT BEING MEMBER OF PARLIAMENT


### Supreme Court Judgment (1997)


A person **not member of Parliament can be appointed PM**.


Condition:


➡ Must become member of **either House within 6 months**


Otherwise:


➡ **Ceases to be Prime Minister**


---


# PRIME MINISTER FROM RAJYA SABHA


Indian Constitution allows PM from **either House**.


Examples


| Prime Minister          | House       |
| ----------------------- | ----------- |
| Indira Gandhi (1966)    | Rajya Sabha |
| H. D. Deve Gowda (1996) | Rajya Sabha |
| Manmohan Singh (2004)   | Rajya Sabha |


---


### Comparison with UK


| Country | Rule                                        |
| ------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| UK      | PM must be from **House of Commons**        |
| India   | PM can be from **Lok Sabha or Rajya Sabha** |

# 3. OATH, TERM AND SALARY


---


# OATH


Before entering office


➡ **President administers oath**


Two oaths:


1️⃣ **Oath of Office**


2️⃣ **Oath of Secrecy**


---


## Oath of Office


PM swears:

1. True faith and allegiance to **Constitution**
2. Uphold **sovereignty and integrity of India**
3. Faithfully discharge duties
4. Do justice **without fear or favour**

---


## Oath of Secrecy


PM promises:


➡ Not to reveal **confidential matters**


Except when required for **official duties**


---


# TERM OF PRIME MINISTER


Constitution **does not fix tenure**.


PM holds office:


➡ **During pleasure of President**


But in practice:


➡ Depends on **majority support in Lok Sabha**


If majority lost:

- PM resigns

    OR

- President dismisses PM

---


# SALARY AND ALLOWANCES


Determined by **Parliament**


Prime Minister receives:

- Salary of **Member of Parliament**
- **Sumptuary allowance**
- **Free official residence**
- **Travelling allowance**
- **Medical facilities**

Example


2001


Sumptuary allowance increased


₹1500 → ₹3000


---


# 4. POWERS AND FUNCTIONS OF PRIME MINISTER


Four major areas:


1️⃣ Powers relating to **Council of Ministers**


2️⃣ Powers relating to **President**


3️⃣ Powers relating to **Parliament**


4️⃣ **Other powers and functions**


---


# I. POWERS IN RELATION TO COUNCIL OF MINISTERS


PM is **Head of Council of Ministers**


---


## 1. Appointment of Ministers


PM **recommends ministers**


➡ President appoints ministers **on PM's advice**


Thus:


✔ PM determines **composition of Council of Ministers**


---


## 2. Allocation of Portfolios


PM:

- Allocates ministries
- Can reshuffle portfolios anytime

Examples


Finance / Defence / Home / External Affairs


---


## 3. Removal of Ministers


PM may:

- Ask minister to **resign**

    OR

- Advise President to **dismiss minister**

Reasons

- Policy differences
- Misconduct
- Political crisis

---


## 4. Chairman of Council Meetings


PM:

- Presides over meetings
- Controls agenda
- Influences decisions

---


## 5. Coordination Role


PM:

- Guides
- Directs
- Controls
- Coordinates

activities of all ministers.


Ensures **policy unity + administrative coordination**


---


## 6. Collapse of Council of Ministers


If **Prime Minister resigns**


➡ Entire **Council of Ministers collapses**


Reason


PM is **head of Council of Ministers**


But


If **any other minister resigns**


➡ Only that minister’s office becomes vacant


---


# II. POWERS IN RELATION TO PRESIDENT


PM acts as **principal link between President and Council of Ministers**


---


## 1. Channel of Communication


PM communicates to President:

- Decisions of Council of Ministers
- Administration of Union
- Legislative proposals

---


## 2. Providing Information


President may request information regarding

- Administration
- Legislative proposals

PM **must provide it**


---


## 3. Placing Matters Before Council


If President requires


PM must place before Council of Ministers:


➡ Decisions taken by an **individual minister**


not yet considered by cabinet


---


## 4. Advice on Key Appointments


PM advises President on appointment of:

- **Attorney General of India**
- **CAG**
- **UPSC Chairman & Members**
- **Election Commissioners**
- **Finance Commission Chairman & Members**

---


# III. POWERS IN RELATION TO PARLIAMENT


PM is **Leader of Lok Sabha**


---


## 1. Summoning and Proroguing Parliament


PM advises President regarding:

- Summoning sessions
- Proroguing sessions

---


## 2. Dissolution of Lok Sabha


PM may recommend:


➡ **Dissolution of Lok Sabha**


before completion of **5-year term**


---


## 3. Announcement of Government Policies


PM:

- Announces government policies
- Represents government in debates

---


# IV. OTHER POWERS AND FUNCTIONS


---


## Chairman of Important Bodies


PM chairs:

- **Planning Commission** (now **NITI Aayog**)
- **National Development Council**
- **National Integration Council**
- **Inter-State Council**
- **National Water Resources Council**

---


## Foreign Policy Role


PM plays major role in

- Diplomacy
- Strategic relations
- International negotiations

---


## Chief Spokesperson


PM explains government policies to:

- Parliament
- Public
- Media

---


## Crisis Manager


During crises:

- War
- Economic crisis
- National emergency

PM acts as **national crisis manager**


---


## Leader of Nation


PM:

- Interacts with society
- Receives grievances
- Provides national leadership

---


## Leader of Ruling Party


PM leads **party in power**


Influences:

- Government policy
- Parliamentary strategy
- Party decisions

---


## Political Head of Civil Services


PM is considered **political head of civil services**


---

# PRIME MINISTERIAL GOVERNMENT


Due to increasing powers:


Cabinet Government → **Prime Ministerial Government**


**R. H. Crossman**

> Post-war period transformed cabinet government into prime ministerial government.

---


**Humphrey Berkely**

> Parliamentary democracy weakened due to super-ministerial powers of PM.

This observation also applies to **India**.

# RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN PRESIDENT AND PRIME MINISTER


---


# ARTICLE 74


Council of Ministers with PM at head


➡ **Aid and advise President**


President must **act according to advice**


But:


President may ask **reconsideration once**


After reconsideration:


➡ President **must accept advice**


---


# ARTICLE 75


Deals with:


| Provision      | Details                                |
| -------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| Appointment    | PM appointed by President              |
| Ministers      | Appointed on advice of PM              |
| Tenure         | Pleasure of President                  |
| Responsibility | Collective responsibility to Lok Sabha |


---


## Collective Responsibility


If **Lok Sabha passes No-Confidence Motion**


➡ Entire **Council of Ministers resigns**


---


# ARTICLE 78


Duties of PM towards President


PM must:

1. Communicate Cabinet decisions
2. Provide information
3. Submit matters to Council when required
