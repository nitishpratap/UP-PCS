# Special Officer for Linguistic Minorities (UPPCS Notes)


---


## 1. Background


Originally, the **Constitution of India did not contain any provision** regarding a **Special Officer for Linguistic Minorities**.


Later developments led to its creation.


### States Reorganisation Commission (1953–1955)

- Recommended creating a mechanism to **protect linguistic minorities** in India.
- Based on this recommendation, the Constitution was amended.

### Constitutional Amendment

- **7th Constitutional Amendment Act, 1956**
- Inserted **Article 350-B** into the Constitution.

### Location in Constitution

- **Part XVII** – Official Language
- **Chapter IV – Special Directives**

Thus:


| Provision                 | Detail                                         |
| ------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Constitutional Article    | **Article 350-B**                              |
| Inserted by               | **7th Constitutional Amendment Act, 1956**     |
| Commission recommendation | **States Reorganisation Commission (1953-55)** |
| Part of Constitution      | **Part XVII – Official Language**              |
| Chapter                   | **Chapter IV – Special Directives**            |


---


# 2. Constitutional Provisions (Article 350-B)


Article 350-B provides for the creation and functioning of the **Special Officer for Linguistic Minorities**.


### Key Provisions


### 1. Appointment

- There shall be a **Special Officer for Linguistic Minorities**.
- The officer is **appointed by the President of India**.

### 2. Duty


The Special Officer shall:

- Investigate all matters relating to **constitutional safeguards for linguistic minorities**.

### 3. Reporting


The officer must:

1. Submit reports to the **President of India** at intervals directed by the President.
2. The **President lays these reports before Parliament**.
3. The reports are also sent to the **concerned State Governments**.

---


# 3. Important Constitutional Gap


The Constitution **does NOT specify** the following for the Special Officer:

- Qualifications
- Tenure
- Salary and allowances
- Service conditions
- Procedure for removal

These aspects are decided **administratively by the government**.


⚠️ **UPPCS Prelims Trap:**


Questions may ask _which of the following are specified in the Constitution regarding the Special Officer_.


Correct answer: **Only appointment and duties are specified.**


---


# 4. Commissioner for Linguistic Minorities


To implement **Article 350-B**, the government created an office.


### Establishment

- **1957** – Office of the **Special Officer for Linguistic Minorities** was created.

### Official Designation


The Special Officer is known as:


**Commissioner for Linguistic Minorities**

The Commissioner:

- Examines grievances arising from **non-implementation of safeguards for linguistic minorities**.
- Receives complaints from:
    - Individuals
    - Groups
    - Associations
    - Organisations belonging to linguistic minorities.

### Level of Intervention


The Commissioner raises issues at:

- **Highest political level**

The Commissioner **recommends corrective measures** to resolve grievances.

- Language use

- Administrative language use
- Protection of minority languages

The Commissioner submits reports to the **President of India** regarding:

These reports are later:

- Presented before **Parliament**
- Sent to the **concerned state governments**

---


### 3. Monitoring Implementation


The Commissioner monitors the implementation of safeguards using multiple methods:

- Questionnaires
- Field visits
- Conferences
- Seminars
- Meetings
- Review mechanisms

This monitoring ensures that **states are complying with constitutional protections**.

# 16. Meaning of Linguistic Minority


A **linguistic minority** refers to:

> A group of people whose **mother tongue is different from the majority language of the state or part of the state**.

### Important Feature


Linguistic minorities are determined **state-wise**, not nationally.


Example:


| State         | Majority Language | Linguistic Minority Example |
| ------------- | ----------------- | --------------------------- |
| Tamil Nadu    | Tamil             | Hindi speakers              |
| Uttar Pradesh | Hindi             | Urdu speakers               |
| Karnataka     | Kannada           | Telugu or Marathi speakers  |


Thus a language may be:

- **Majority language in one state**
- **Minority language in another state**
