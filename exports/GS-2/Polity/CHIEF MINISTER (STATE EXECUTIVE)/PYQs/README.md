# 1. PRELIMS PYQs (Chief Minister)


Format


**Question — Exam — Year — Answer**


---

1. **The Chief Minister of a state is appointed by:**

    — Union Public Service Commission Prelims — 2011


    ✅ **Answer:** **Governor**


---

1. **Which Article of the Constitution deals with appointment of Chief Minister?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2013


    ✅ **Answer:** **Article 164**


---

1. **A person who is not a member of the state legislature can be Chief Minister for a maximum period of:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2014


    ✅ **Answer:** **6 months**


---

1. **Council of Ministers in a state is collectively responsible to:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2015


    ✅ **Answer:** **Legislative Assembly**


---

1. **The Chief Minister acts as the link between:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2016


    ✅ **Answer:** **Governor and Council of Ministers**


---

1. **Which Article defines the duties of Chief Minister towards the Governor?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2017


    ✅ **Answer:** **Article 167**


---

1. **The Chief Minister advises the Governor regarding:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2018


    ✅ **Answer:** **Appointment of ministers**


---

1. **In case of a hung assembly, who appoints the Chief Minister?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2019


    ✅ **Answer:** **Governor (using discretion)**


---

1. **If the Chief Minister resigns, what happens to the Council of Ministers?**

    — State PCS Prelims — 2020


    ✅ **Answer:** **Entire Council of Ministers dissolves**


---

1. **The salary and allowances of the Chief Minister are determined by:**

    — State PCS Prelims — 2021


    ✅ **Answer:** **State Legislature**

# 3. Additional High-Probability Questions (PCS Pattern)


### Prelims Style


1️⃣ Chief Minister appointed under


→ **Article 164**


2️⃣ Duties of CM toward Governor


→ **Article 167**


3️⃣ Collective responsibility of Council of Ministers


→ **Legislative Assembly**


4️⃣ CM oath administered by


→ **Governor**


5️⃣ CM may belong to


→ **Legislative Assembly or Legislative Council**


---


### Mains Style


1️⃣ Explain **powers and functions of Chief Minister**.


2️⃣ Discuss **CM as head of Council of Ministers**.


3️⃣ Explain **CM’s role in state legislature**.


4️⃣ Examine **CM as link between Governor and Council of Ministers**.


5️⃣ Discuss **CM as real executive authority in state administration**.
