# 1️⃣ PRELIMS PYQs (Judicial Review)


Format


**Question — Exam — Year — Answer**


---

1. **Judicial Review in India means:**

    — Union Public Service Commission Prelims — 2011


    ✅ **Answer:** **Power of courts to examine constitutionality of laws and executive actions**


---

1. **The doctrine of Judicial Review originated in:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2013


    ✅ **Answer:** **USA (Marbury v. Madison, 1803)**


---

1. **Which Article declares laws inconsistent with Fundamental Rights as void?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2014


    ✅ **Answer:** **Article 13**


---

1. **Which Article provides for enforcement of Fundamental Rights by Supreme Court?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2015


    ✅ **Answer:** **Article 32**


---

1. **Which Article empowers High Courts to issue writs?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2016


    ✅ **Answer:** **Article 226**


---

1. **Judicial Review is part of:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2017


    ✅ **Answer:** **Basic Structure of the Constitution**


---

1. **Which case established the Basic Structure Doctrine?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2018


    ✅ **Answer:** **Kesavananda Bharati Case (1973)**


---

1. **Which case declared NJAC unconstitutional?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2019


    ✅ **Answer:** **NJAC Case (2015)**


---

1. **Which Article deals with Ninth Schedule protection?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2020


    ✅ **Answer:** **Article 31B**


---

1. **Cut-off date for Ninth Schedule judicial review is:**

    — State PCS Prelims — 2021


    ✅ **Answer:** **24 April 1973**
