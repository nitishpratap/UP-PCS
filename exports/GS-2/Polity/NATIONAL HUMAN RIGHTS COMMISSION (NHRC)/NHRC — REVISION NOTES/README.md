# 1. NHRC : INTRODUCTION


| Topic                     | Details                                  |
| ------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| Nature                    | **Statutory Body**                       |
| Established Under         | **Protection of Human Rights Act, 1993** |
| Amendment                 | **Human Rights (Amendment) Act, 2006**   |
| Headquarters              | **New Delhi**                            |
| Role                      | Watchdog of Human Rights in India        |
| Nature of Recommendations | **Recommendatory / Non-binding**         |


---


# 2. HUMAN RIGHTS : MEANING


Human Rights include rights related to:

- Life
- Liberty
- Equality
- Dignity of Individual

These rights must be:


✔ Guaranteed by Constitution OR


✔ Contained in International Covenants


✔ Enforceable by Indian Courts


---


# 3. INTERNATIONAL COVENANTS


| Covenant        | Details                                                      |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| ICCPR           | International Covenant on Civil & Political Rights           |
| ICESCR          | International Covenant on Economic, Social & Cultural Rights |
| Adopted by UNGA | 16 Dec 1966                                                  |
| India Acceded   | 10 April 1979                                                |


---


# 4. OBJECTIVES OF NHRC

- Strengthen human rights institutions
- Independent investigation of violations
- Complement existing human rights protection mechanisms
- Promote and protect human rights

# 7. TENURE


| Provision       | Details                       |
| --------------- | ----------------------------- |
| Tenure          | 5 Years OR 70 years age       |
| Reappointment   | Allowed subject to conditions |
| Post-retirement | No further govt employment    |


Purpose:

- Independence
- Impartiality
- Autonomy

---


# 8. REMOVAL OF CHAIRPERSON/MEMBERS


## A. Removal WITHOUT SC Inquiry


President can remove if:

- Insolvent
- Paid employment outside duties
- Infirmity of mind/body
- Unsound mind declared by court
- Convicted & imprisoned

---


## B. Removal WITH SC Inquiry


Grounds:

- Proved Misbehaviour
- Incapacity

Procedure:


President → Reference to SC → Inquiry → Removal


---


# 9. SALARY & SERVICE CONDITIONS

- Determined by Central Government
- Cannot be altered to disadvantage after appointment

Purpose:


✔ Independence of Commission


---


# 10. FUNCTIONS OF NHRC


## A. Inquiry into Human Rights Violations


Can inquire:

- Suo motu
- Petition by victim/person
- Court direction

Against:

- Human rights violation
- Negligence by public servant

---


## B. Intervention in Court Proceedings


Allowed only with court permission.


---


## C. Inspection of Jails & Detention Centres


Purpose:

- Study prison conditions
- Recommend reforms
- Ensure humane treatment

---


## D. Review of Safeguards


Reviews:

- Constitutional safeguards
- Legal safeguards

Recommends effective implementation.


---


## E. Review Factors Inhibiting Human Rights


Studies impact of:

- Terrorism
- Insurgency
- Violence

Suggests remedial measures.


---


## F. Study of International Instruments


Examines treaties/conventions and suggests implementation measures.


---


## G. Research Promotion


Promotes:

- Human rights research
- Policy development

---


## H. Human Rights Literacy


Promotes awareness through:

- Publications
- Education
- Training
- Media

---


## I. Encouragement to NGOs


Supports NGOs working in human rights field.


---


## J. Other Necessary Functions


Residual powers for promotion of human rights.


---


# 11. WORKING OF NHRC


| Topic                 | Details                     |
| --------------------- | --------------------------- |
| Headquarters          | New Delhi                   |
| Regional Offices      | Can establish anywhere      |
| Procedure             | Regulates its own procedure |
| Nature of Proceedings | Judicial Character          |


---


## Powers of Civil Court


NHRC can:

- Summon witnesses
- Receive evidence
- Demand documents
- Requisition public records

---


## Investigation Machinery


NHRC can use:

- Own investigation staff
- Central govt officers
- State govt officers
- Investigation agencies
- NGO assistance

---


# 12. TIME LIMIT FOR INQUIRY


⏳ NHRC cannot investigate cases after:


➡ 1 year from date of violation


---


# 13. ACTIONS AFTER INQUIRY


NHRC may recommend:

- Compensation/damages
- Prosecution of guilty officials
- Departmental action
- Interim relief

May also approach:

- Supreme Court
- High Courts

for writs/orders/directions.


---


# 14. NATURE OF NHRC


| Aspect                      | Status         |
| --------------------------- | -------------- |
| Punishing Power             | ❌ No           |
| Compensation Awarding Power | ❌ No           |
| Recommendations Binding?    | ❌ No           |
| Nature                      | Recommendatory |


---


## Government Response Time


| Case                    | Time     |
| ----------------------- | -------- |
| General Recommendations | 1 Month  |
| Armed Forces Cases      | 3 Months |


---


# 15. LIMITATION REGARDING ARMED FORCES


NHRC has limited jurisdiction.


Cannot directly investigate.


Procedure:

1. Seek report from Central Govt
2. Examine report
3. Make recommendations

---

# 25. ONE-LINE CONCLUSION


NHRC acts as India’s premier statutory watchdog for protection and promotion of human rights, but its effectiveness is constrained by its recommendatory nature and limited enforcement powers.
