
# Meaning of Pressure Groups


A **Pressure Group** is an organised group of people formed to **promote and protect common interests** by influencing government policies.

- Origin of term: **USA**
- Also called:
    - Interest Groups
    - Vested Groups

### Definition


Pressure groups attempt to bring changes in:

- Public policy
- Government decisions
- Administrative actions

by exerting pressure on the government.


They act as a **link between government and citizens/members**.


---


# Features of Pressure Groups


## 1. Organised Groups

- Formed by people having common interests.

## 2. Non-Political Nature

- Do not contest elections.
- Do not seek political power directly.

## 3. Specific Objectives

- Concerned with specific interests/issues.
- Activities limited to promotion and protection of member interests.

## 4. Influence Government


Influence:

- Policy-making
- Policy implementation

## 5. Use Legal Methods


Methods include:

- Lobbying
- Petitioning
- Publicity
- Propaganda
- Correspondence
- Public debates
- Contacting legislators

## 6. Sometimes Use Illegal Methods


Some groups may resort to:

- Strikes
- Violent activities
- Corruption

Effects:

- Damage public interest
- Affect administrative integrity

---


# Difference Between Political Parties and Pressure Groups


| Basis     | Political Parties       | Pressure Groups           |
| --------- | ----------------------- | ------------------------- |
| Objective | Capture political power | Influence government      |
| Elections | Contest elections       | Do not contest elections  |
| Scope     | Broad national issues   | Specific interests/issues |
| Nature    | Political               | Non-political             |
| Aim       | Form government         | Influence policies        |


---


# Techniques Used by Pressure Groups


According to **Odegard**, pressure groups use 3 major techniques:


---


## 1. Electioneering


### Meaning


Trying to place favourable persons in public office.


### Objective


Ensure policymakers support group interests.


---


## 2. Lobbying


### Meaning


Persuading public officials to adopt favourable policies.


### Methods

- Meetings
- Negotiations
- Memorandums
- Discussions

---


## 3. Propagandising


### Meaning


Influencing public opinion to indirectly influence government.


### Methods

- Media campaigns
- Public speeches
- Awareness campaigns
- Publications

---

# 11. Anomic Groups


## Meaning


According to Almond and Powell:


These are spontaneous groups that emerge suddenly through:

- Riots
- Demonstrations
- Assassinations
- Violent protests

These arise because:

- Government ignores particularistic demands.
- Groups feel alienated from political system.

---


## Major Examples

1. Naxalite Groups
2. Jammu and Kashmir Liberation Front (JKLF)
3. United Liberation Front of Assam (ULFA)
4. Dal Khalsa

# Role/Functions of Pressure Groups


## Positive Role

1. Promote public participation
2. Act as link between people and government
3. Protect interests of weaker sections
4. Provide specialised information to government
5. Strengthen democracy

---


## Negative Role

1. Promote sectional interests
2. Can encourage corruption
3. May use violent methods
4. Obstruct national interest
5. Increase social divisions (caste/religion/language)

---


# Pressure Groups vs Political Parties (Important Mains Point)

| ------------------- | ------------------- |
| Interest-oriented   | Power-oriented      |
| Specific issues     | Broad issues        |
| Informal role       | Constitutional role |
| No direct power     | Form government     |
| Influence decisions | Take decisions      |

# PYQ/EXAM ORIENTED ANALYTICAL POINTS


## Why Pressure Groups are Weak in India?

1. Illiteracy
2. Poverty
3. Lack of political consciousness
4. Traditional society
5. Dominance of political parties
6. Lack of financial resources

---


## Why Business Groups are Most Powerful?

1. Financial strength
2. Organised structure
3. Access to policymakers
4. Influence through funding/lobbying

---


# Mains Enrichment Points


## Pressure Groups are Essential Because:

- Democracy is not only about elections.
- They provide representation to specialised interests.
- They deepen participatory democracy.

## But Excessive Pressure Group Politics Can:

- Encourage identity politics
- Lead to policy capture
- Distort public welfare priorities
