# PRELIMS PYQs (with Answers)


Format


**Question — Exam — Year — Answer**


---

1. **Which Article of the Constitution deals with Parliament of India?**

    — Union Public Service Commission Prelims — 2011


    ✅ **Answer:** **Articles 79–122**


---

1. **Parliament of India consists of:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2012


    ✅ **Answer:** **President + Rajya Sabha + Lok Sabha**


---

1. **Maximum strength of Rajya Sabha is:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2013


    ✅ **Answer:** **250 members**


---

1. **How many members of Rajya Sabha are nominated by the President?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2014


    ✅ **Answer:** **12 members**


---

1. **Maximum strength of Lok Sabha as per Constitution:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2015


    ✅ **Answer:** **552 members**


---

1. **Which system is used for election of Rajya Sabha members?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2016


    ✅ **Answer:** **Proportional Representation by Single Transferable Vote (STV)**


---

1. **Minimum age to become a member of Lok Sabha:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2017


    ✅ **Answer:** **25 years**


---

1. **Minimum age to become a member of Rajya Sabha:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2018


    ✅ **Answer:** **30 years**


---

1. **Quorum in Parliament is:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2019


    ✅ **Answer:** **1/10 of total membership**


---

1. **Which House of Parliament is permanent?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2020


    ✅ **Answer:** **Rajya Sabha**

# Additional PRELIMS Questions (Very Common)


These are **frequently asked in PCS exams**.


---

1. **Term of Lok Sabha:**

    ✅ **Answer:** **5 years**


---

1. **Term of Rajya Sabha members:**

    ✅ **Answer:** **6 years**


---

1. **One-third members of Rajya Sabha retire every:**

    ✅ **Answer:** **2 years**


---

1. **Voting age in India reduced from 21 to 18 by:**

    ✅ **Answer:** **61st Constitutional Amendment Act (1988)**


---

1. **Election system used for Lok Sabha:**

    ✅ **Answer:** **First Past the Post (FPTP)**


---

1. **Money Bills are defined under:**

    ✅ **Answer:** **Article 110**


---

1. **Joint sitting of Parliament is provided under:**

    ✅ **Answer:** **Article 108**


---

1. **Speaker decides whether a bill is Money Bill:**

    ✅ **Answer:** **Yes**


---

1. **Rajya Sabha representation of states based on:**

    ✅ **Answer:** **Population**


---

1. **Rajya Sabha seat allocation given in:**

    ✅ **Answer:** **Fourth Schedule of Constitution**
