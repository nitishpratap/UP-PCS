| Constitutional Provision | **Articles 79–122 (Part V)**                     |
| System                   | **Parliamentary Government (Westminster Model)** |

| Country | Legislature Structure                                  |

# 12. Territorial Constituencies


Country divided into **single member constituencies**


Each constituency → **1 MP**


Principles:


1️⃣ Seats allocated to states proportional to population


2️⃣ Population-seat ratio uniform inside state


Exception → states below **6 million population**


Population defined as → **latest published census**


---


# 13. Delimitation (Readjustment)


Occurs after **each census**


Two tasks:

1. Seat allocation among states
2. Territorial constituency boundaries

Authority → **Parliament**

# 15. Reservation of Seats (SC/ST)


| Basis | Population proportion |
| ----- | --------------------- |


Features

- No **separate electorate**
- All voters vote
- SC/ST can contest general seats
- General candidates cannot contest reserved seats

Repeated extensions

**95th Amendment (2009) → extended till 2020**

# ELECTORAL SYSTEM


---


# 16. Territorial Representation System


Also called:


**First Past the Post System (FPTP)**


| Feature       | Meaning             |
| ------------- | ------------------- |
| Constituency  | Single member       |
| Winner        | Highest votes       |
| Majority type | **Simple majority** |


Limitation → minorities may not be represented.


---


# 17. Proportional Representation (PR)


Types:

1. Single Transferable Vote
2. List System

India uses **STV for**

- Rajya Sabha
- Legislative Council
- President
- Vice President

---


# 18. Why PR not used for Lok Sabha


1️⃣ Complex system


2️⃣ Could cause **political instability**


Additional demerits:

- Expensive
- Weak voter-representative link
- Encourages group politics
- No by-elections

# 21. Qualifications


| Requirement          | Details                    |
| -------------------- | -------------------------- |
| Citizenship          | Indian citizen             |
| Oath                 | Allegiance to Constitution |
| Minimum Age          | LS **25**, RS **30**       |
| Other qualifications | Prescribed by Parliament   |


---


### Additional (RPA 1951)

1. Must be **elector in parliamentary constituency**
2. SC seat → SC candidate
3. ST seat → ST candidate

Rajya Sabha rule change:


Candidate need not be elector of that state


Upheld by **Supreme Court 2006**


---


# 22. Disqualifications


### Constitutional

1. Office of profit
2. Unsound mind
3. Undischarged insolvent
4. Foreign citizenship
5. Disqualified under parliamentary law

---


### RPA 1951


Disqualification if:

- Election offences
- Corrupt practices
- Imprisonment **≥ 2 years**
- Failure to submit election expenses
- Govt contract interest
- Director in govt corporation
- Dismissed for corruption
- Promoting enmity
- Social crimes (dowry, sati, untouchability)

Preventive detention → **not disqualification**


---


### Authority deciding disqualification


**President**


But must act on **Election Commission advice**


---


# 23. Anti-Defection (Tenth Schedule)


Disqualification if:

1. Voluntarily gives up party membership
2. Votes against whip
3. Independent joins party
4. Nominated member joins party after **6 months**

Authority:


| House       | Authority |
| ----------- | --------- |
| Lok Sabha   | Speaker   |
| Rajya Sabha | Chairman  |


SC judgement **1992 → judicial review allowed**


---


# 24. Vacating Seats


Seat vacant if:


1️⃣ Double membership


2️⃣ Disqualification


3️⃣ Resignation


4️⃣ Absence **60 days**


5️⃣ Election declared void


6️⃣ Expelled


7️⃣ Becomes President / Vice President


8️⃣ Becomes Governor


---

# SPEAKER OF LOK SABHA


---


# 28. Election


Elected by **Lok Sabha members**


Date fixed by **President**


---


# 29. Tenure


Normally → life of Lok Sabha


Vacates if:

1. Ceases MP
2. Resigns to Deputy Speaker
3. Removed by resolution

---


# 30. Removal


Requires:

- **Absolute majority**
- **14 days notice**

During motion:


Speaker

- cannot preside
- can vote first
- no casting vote

---


# 31. Important Provision


After Lok Sabha dissolution:


**Speaker continues until new LS meets**


---


# 32. Major Powers of Speaker


1️⃣ Maintain order


2️⃣ Interpret rules


3️⃣ Ensure quorum


4️⃣ Casting vote


5️⃣ Preside joint sitting


6️⃣ Secret sitting permission


7️⃣ Money Bill certification


8️⃣ Anti-defection decision


9️⃣ Committee chairman


10️⃣ Appoint committee heads


---


### Committees chaired by Speaker

- Business Advisory Committee
- Rules Committee
- General Purpose Committee

---


# 33. Independence of Speaker


Ensured by:

- Security of tenure
- Salary charged on **Consolidated Fund**
- Conduct discussed only by **substantive motion**
- Courts cannot interfere in procedural decisions
- Votes only in tie

Rank → **7th in Order of Precedence**


---


# 34. Deputy Speaker


| Feature     | Details                            |
| ----------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Elected by  | Lok Sabha                          |
| Resignation | Speaker                            |
| Removal     | Absolute majority + 14 days notice |


Convention since **11th Lok Sabha**


Speaker → ruling party


Deputy Speaker → opposition


---


# 35. Panel of Chairpersons


Speaker nominates **up to 10**


They preside when:


Speaker + Deputy Speaker absent


---


# 36. Speaker Pro Tem


Appointed by **President**


Usually **senior most member**


Functions:

1. Administer oath
2. Preside first sitting
3. Conduct Speaker election

# 38. Deputy Chairman

Removal:

- 14 days notice

# 39. Panel of Vice Chairpersons


Nominated by Chairman


Preside when Chairman + Deputy Chairman absent

- requires **50 members**
- allowed only in **Lok Sabha**

# JOINT SITTING


Article **108**


Called by **President**


Presiding order

1. Speaker
2. Deputy Speaker
3. Deputy Chairman RS

Not allowed for:

- Money Bills
- Constitutional Amendment Bills

### Historical Joint Sittings

2. **Banking Service Commission Bill 1978**

---


# MONEY BILL


Defined → **Article 110**


Includes:

- taxation
- borrowing
- Consolidated Fund
- Contingency Fund
- appropriation

Certification → **Speaker**


---


### Procedure


Introduced → **Lok Sabha only**


Requires → **President recommendation**


Rajya Sabha:

- discuss
- recommend amendments
- must return within **14 days**

If not returned → **deemed passed**


President **cannot return Money Bill**


---
