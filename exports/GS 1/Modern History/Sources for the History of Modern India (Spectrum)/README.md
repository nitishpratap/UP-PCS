
# A. CHAPTER NOTES


# Introduction


The study of **Modern Indian History** is based on a wide variety of sources:

1. **Archival Materials**
2. **Biographies, Memoirs & Travel Accounts**
3. **Newspapers & Journals**
4. **Oral Evidence**
5. **Creative Literature**
6. **Paintings**

These sources help historians reconstruct:

- Political developments
- Administrative evolution
- Economic conditions
- Social transformations
- Cultural changes
- Growth of nationalism

---


# 1. ARCHIVAL MATERIALS


## Meaning


Archives = Official records preserved systematically.


They are the **most authentic and reliable source** for Modern Indian History.
