
## 1. Concept of Change in Nature

- **Change is a fundamental law of nature.**
- Earth’s environment is dynamic and continuously changing.

### Types of Changes


| Type of Change     | Description                                | Example                         |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------- |
| **Gradual change** | Slow processes occurring over long periods | Evolution of landforms          |
| **Sudden change**  | Rapid and destructive events               | Earthquakes, volcanic eruptions |
| **Local change**   | Limited spatial extent                     | Tornadoes, thunderstorms        |
| **Global change**  | Affect large areas or the whole Earth      | Global warming, ozone depletion |


**Important Insight (UPPCS Mains):**

- From **nature’s perspective → changes are value-neutral**.
- From **human perspective → changes may be good or bad**.

Examples:


| Desirable Change                        | Undesirable Change |
| --------------------------------------- | ------------------ |
| Change of seasons                       | Earthquakes        |
| Ripening of crops                       | Floods             |
| River deposition forming fertile plains | Cyclones           |


These **undesirable extreme events → become disasters**.


---


# 2. Disaster: Definition


### Standard Definition


A **disaster** is:

- An **undesirable catastrophic event**
- Caused by **natural or human forces**
- Occurs **suddenly with little warning**
- Leads to:
    - Loss of **life**
    - Damage to **property**
    - Disruption of **social and economic systems**

📌 Key point for exams:

> A disaster occurs **when the magnitude of damage exceeds the capacity of the affected community to cope.**

---


# 3. Hazard vs Disaster (VERY IMPORTANT)


| Hazard                         | Disaster                         |
| ------------------------------ | -------------------------------- |
| A **potential threat**         | A **real destructive event**     |
| May or may not cause damage    | Causes **actual loss**           |
| Natural condition              | Human impact determines disaster |
| Example: Earthquake fault zone | Earthquake destroying a city     |


### Example


```plain text
Earthquake (Hazard)
        ↓
Population exposure
        ↓
Weak buildings
        ↓
Mass destruction
        ↓
DISASTER
```

| Term                   | Meaning                                       |
| ---------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |

```plain text

EARTHQUAKE
```

| **L waves (Surface waves)** | Move along surface     | Most destructive |

# 1. Earthquake: Concept


### Definition


An **earthquake** is the **sudden shaking of the Earth's crust caused by the release of accumulated energy along faults or plate boundaries**.


This energy travels in the form of **seismic waves**.


---


# 2. Internal Structure Related to Earthquakes


Earthquakes mainly occur in the **lithosphere** where tectonic plates interact.


| Layer         | Characteristics       | Relation to Earthquakes    |
| ------------- | --------------------- | -------------------------- |
| Lithosphere   | Rigid outer layer     | Earthquakes originate here |
| Asthenosphere | Semi-molten layer     | Plates move over it        |
| Core          | Dense metallic region | Indirect effect            |


---


# 3. Key Terms in Earthquake


| Term                   | Meaning                                            |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| **Focus (Hypocentre)** | Point inside the Earth where earthquake originates |
| **Epicentre**          | Point on Earth's surface above focus               |
| **Fault**              | Fracture in rock where movement occurs             |
| **Seismic waves**      | Waves of energy released during earthquakes        |


---

```plain text
      Epicentre

          │
          │
```

# 4. Causes of Earthquakes


Earthquakes occur due to **three major causes**.


---


## (A) Tectonic Earthquakes (Most Common)


Caused by **movement of tectonic plates**.


### Mechanism


```plain text
Plate movement
      ↓
Stress accumulation
      ↓
Rock fracture along fault
      ↓
Sudden energy release
      ↓
EARTHQUAKE
```


Examples

- Himalayan earthquakes
- San Andreas Fault earthquakes

---


## (B) Volcanic Earthquakes


Caused by **movement of magma during volcanic eruptions**.


Example regions

- Japan
- Indonesia
- Pacific Ring of Fire

These earthquakes are usually **less destructive** than tectonic earthquakes.


---


## (C) Human-Induced Earthquakes


Also called **Artificial Seismicity**.


Causes:


| Cause                        | Example             |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Reservoir induced seismicity | Large dams          |
| Mining                       | Rock collapse       |
| Nuclear explosions           | Underground testing |


Example

- **Koyna earthquake (1967, Maharashtra)** linked to dam reservoir.

---


# 5. Types of Seismic Waves


Seismic waves are divided into **two main groups**.


---


## (A) Body Waves


Travel through **Earth’s interior**.


### 1. Primary Waves (P Waves)


Characteristics:

- Fastest seismic waves
- Travel through **solids, liquids, gases**
- First waves recorded by seismographs
- Cause **compression and expansion**

---


### 2. Secondary Waves (S Waves)


Characteristics:

- Slower than P waves
- Travel only through **solids**
- Cannot pass through liquids
- Cause **side-to-side movement**

---


## (B) Surface Waves


Travel along **Earth’s surface**.


### Characteristics

- Slowest waves
- Most destructive
- Cause **maximum damage**

Examples

- Love waves
- Rayleigh waves

---


# 6. Measurement of Earthquakes


Two scales are used.


---


## (A) Richter Scale


Measures **magnitude (energy released)**.


Characteristics:

- Logarithmic scale
- Each increase of 1 magnitude = **10 times stronger shaking**

Example


| Magnitude | Impact   |
| --------- | -------- |
| 3–4       | Minor    |
| 5–6       | Moderate |
| 7+        | Major    |


---


## (B) Mercalli Scale


Measures **intensity (damage experienced)**.


Range

- I → Not felt
- XII → Total destruction

# 2. Causes of Volcanic Eruptions


Volcanoes occur mainly due to **tectonic activity**.


### Main Causes

1. **Plate divergence**
    - Plates move apart
    - Magma rises through cracks
2. **Plate convergence (subduction)**
    - Oceanic plate sinks under continental plate
    - Melting produces magma
3. **Hotspots**
    - Rising mantle plumes
    - Example: Hawaii islands

# 8. Causes of Landslides


## Natural Causes


| Cause             | Explanation                        |
| ----------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Heavy rainfall    | Water reduces friction             |
| Earthquakes       | Ground shaking destabilizes slopes |
| Weathering        | Weakens rocks                      |
| Volcanic activity | Triggers slope collapse            |


---


## Human Causes


| Activity           | Result                   |
| ------------------ | ------------------------ |
| Deforestation      | Soil erosion             |
| Road cutting       | Weak slope structure     |
| Mining             | Rock instability         |
| Urban construction | Increased load on slopes |


---


# 9. Landslide Process


```plain text
Steep slope
      ↓
Heavy rainfall
      ↓
Soil saturation
      ↓
Loss of cohesion
      ↓
LANDSLIDE
```

| **Rotational slide**    | Curved sliding surface                  |
| **Translational slide** | Movement along flat surface             |

# 3. TSUNAMI


## Definition


A **tsunami** is a series of **large sea waves caused by underwater earthquakes, volcanic eruptions, or landslides**.


---


## Causes

- Undersea earthquakes (most common)
- Volcanic eruptions
- Landslides

---


## Process


```plain text
Underwater earthquake
        ↓
Seafloor displacement
        ↓
Energy transfer to water
        ↓
Waves travel at high speed
        ↓
Huge waves near coast
        ↓
TSUNAMI
```


---


## Example

- **2004 Indian Ocean Tsunami**
    - Affected: India, Sri Lanka, Indonesia
    - Severe impact in **Andaman & Nicobar Islands**

---


## Impacts

- Coastal destruction
- Loss of life
- Salinisation of soil

---


## Mitigation

- Early warning systems
- Coastal zoning
- Mangrove protection

# 4. DROUGHT


## Definition


A **drought** is a **prolonged period of deficient rainfall leading to water scarcity**.


---


## Types of Drought


| Type               | Description             |
| ------------------ | ----------------------- |
| **Meteorological** | Less rainfall           |
| **Agricultural**   | Soil moisture deficit   |
| **Hydrological**   | Reduced water in rivers |
| **Socio-economic** | Impact on people        |


---


## Causes

- Monsoon failure
- Climate variability
- Overuse of groundwater

---


## Drought-Prone Areas in India


| Region           | Characteristics    |
| ---------------- | ------------------ |
| Rajasthan        | Arid climate       |
| Bundelkhand (UP) | Water scarcity     |
| Deccan Plateau   | Irregular rainfall |


---


## Drought in Uttar Pradesh

- **Bundelkhand region** is most affected
- Causes:
    - Poor rainfall
    - Hard rock terrain
    - Low irrigation

---


## Impacts

- Crop failure
- Migration
- Water scarcity
- Livestock loss

---


## Drought Management

- Rainwater harvesting
- Irrigation development
- Drought-resistant crops

---
