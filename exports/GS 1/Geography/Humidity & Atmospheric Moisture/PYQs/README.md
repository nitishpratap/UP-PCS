# PRELIMS PYQs (with Answers)


Format


**Question — Exam — Year — Answer**


---

1. **Relative humidity is defined as:**

    — Union Public Service Commission Prelims — 2011


    ✅ **Answer:** Ratio of **actual moisture present to maximum moisture capacity of air × 100**


---

1. **The temperature at which air becomes saturated is called:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2013


    ✅ **Answer:** **Dew Point**


---

1. **Which process converts water vapour into liquid water?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2015


    ✅ **Answer:** **Condensation**


---

1. **Which instrument is used to measure humidity?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2016


    ✅ **Answer:** **Hygrometer**


---

1. **Fog differs from mist mainly in terms of:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2017


    ✅ **Answer:** **Visibility**


---

1. **Which clouds are associated with thunderstorms?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2018


    ✅ **Answer:** **Cumulonimbus clouds**


---

1. **Which cloud type occurs at the highest altitude?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2019


    ✅ **Answer:** **Cirrus**


---

1. **Rainfall caused by mountains blocking moist winds is called:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2020


    ✅ **Answer:** **Orographic rainfall**


---

1. **Rainfall caused by heating and rising air currents is called:**

    — State PCS Prelims — 2021


    ✅ **Answer:** **Convectional rainfall**


---

1. **Frozen raindrops falling to the ground are called:**

    — State PCS Prelims — 2022


    ✅ **Answer:** **Sleet**


---


# Additional PRELIMS Questions (Very Common)


These are **frequently asked in PCS exams**.


---

1. **Absolute humidity is measured in:**

    ✅ **Answer:** **g/m³**


---

1. **When relative humidity becomes 100%, air becomes:**

    ✅ **Answer:** **Saturated**


---

1. **Clouds are formed due to:**

    ✅ **Answer:** **Condensation of water vapour**


---

1. **Hailstorms are usually formed in:**

    ✅ **Answer:** **Cumulonimbus clouds**


---

1. **Smog is a mixture of:**

    ✅ **Answer:** **Smoke + Fog**


---

1. **Rain shadow region occurs on:**

    ✅ **Answer:** **Leeward side of mountains**


---

1. **Highest rainfall in India occurs at:**

    ✅ **Answer:** **Mawsynram / Cherrapunji**


---

1. **Hydrological cycle refers to:**

    ✅ **Answer:** **Continuous circulation of water between land, ocean and atmosphere**
