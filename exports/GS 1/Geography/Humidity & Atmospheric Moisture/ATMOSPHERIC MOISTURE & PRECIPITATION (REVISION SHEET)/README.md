# 1. 🌫️ Atmospheric Moisture


## Definition

- **Atmospheric Moisture = Water vapour present in atmosphere**

---


## Sources (Prelims Direct)

- **Evaporation** → Oceans, rivers
- **Transpiration** → Plants
- **Sublimation** → Ice → vapour

---


## Hydrological Cycle (VERY IMPORTANT FLOW)


```plain text
Solar Energy
     ↓
Evaporation + Transpiration
     ↓
Water Vapour
     ↓
Condensation
     ↓
Cloud Formation
     ↓
Precipitation
     ↓
Runoff → Ocean
```


👉 Continuous exchange between **Ocean–Atmosphere–Land**


---


## Forms of Water


| Form   | Example      |
| ------ | ------------ |
| Gas    | Water vapour |
| Liquid | Clouds, rain |
| Solid  | Snow, ice    |


---


# 2. 💧 Humidity


## Definition

- **Humidity = Amount of water vapour in air**

---


## Types (VERY IMPORTANT)


### Absolute Humidity

- **Mass of water vapour per unit volume**
- Unit → **g/m³**

👉 Features:

- ↓ with altitude
- High near **equator & oceans**

---


### Relative Humidity (FREQUENT PYQ)

- % of actual moisture vs max capacity

```plain text
RH = (Actual / Maximum) × 100
```


👉 Key Concept:

- **Warm air → holds more moisture**

---


## Distribution (Prelims)


| Region        | Humidity |
| ------------- | -------- |
| Oceans/coasts | High     |
| Interior      | Low      |
| Deserts       | Very low |


---


## India Examples

- **High RH** → Kerala, Assam, WB
- **Low RH** → Rajasthan, Bundelkhand, W. UP

---


# 3. 🌡️ Saturation & Dew Point


## Saturated Air

- Air cannot hold more moisture

    👉 **RH = 100%**


---


## Dew Point

- Temperature at which air becomes saturated

    👉 **Condensation begins**


📌 Example: Dew on crops in UP winters


---


# 4. 🔥 Evaporation


## Definition

- Liquid → Vapour due to heat

---


## Factors


| Factor       | Effect        |
| ------------ | ------------- |
| Temperature  | ↑ evaporation |
| Wind         | ↑ evaporation |
| Humidity     | ↓ evaporation |
| Surface area | ↑ evaporation |


---


## India Example

- **Thar Desert, Gujarat → highest evaporation**

---


# 5. ☁️ Condensation


## Definition

- Vapour → Liquid due to cooling

---


## Conditions

1. **Saturation (dew point reached)**
2. **Condensation nuclei** (dust, smoke, salt)

👉 Called **Hygroscopic nuclei**


---


## Process Flow


```plain text
Cooling of Air
     ↓
Dew Point
     ↓
Condensation
     ↓
Droplets
     ↓
Cloud / Fog / Dew
```


---


# 6. 🌾 Forms of Condensation


## Near Surface vs Upper Air


| Surface | Atmosphere |
| ------- | ---------- |
| Dew     | Clouds     |
| Frost   | Clouds     |
| Fog     | Clouds     |
| Mist    | Clouds     |


---


## Dew

- Water droplets on cold surface

👉 Conditions:

- Clear sky
- Calm winds
- High humidity

📍 India: North India winters


---


## Frost

- Ice crystals (Temp ≤ 0°C)

📍 Regions: Himalayas


⚠️ Crop damage (Potato, wheat)


---


## Fog (IMPORTANT)

- Cloud near ground
- Visibility < 1 km

📍 India: Indo-Gangetic Plain


---


## Mist


| Feature    | Fog   | Mist  |
| ---------- | ----- | ----- |
| Visibility | <1 km | >1 km |
| Density    | Dense | Light |


---


## Smog

- **Fog + Smoke**

📍 India: Delhi, Kanpur, Lucknow


---


# 7. ☁️ Clouds


## Definition

- Mass of water droplets/ice crystals in atmosphere

---


## Formation Flow


```plain text
Air rises
     ↓
Cooling
     ↓
Dew Point
     ↓
Condensation
     ↓
Clouds
```


---


## Characteristics

- Float due to air currents
- Shape depends on wind
- Source of precipitation

---


# 8. 🌩️ Cloud Classification


## Major Types (MEMORY: CCNS)


| Type    | Feature      |
| ------- | ------------ |
| Cirrus  | Feather-like |
| Cumulus | Cotton-like  |
| Stratus | Layered      |
| Nimbus  | Rain clouds  |


---


## Height Classification


| Level    | Height       | Type         |
| -------- | ------------ | ------------ |
| High     | 6000–12000 m | Cirrus       |
| Middle   | 2000–6000 m  | Alto clouds  |
| Low      | 0–2000 m     | Stratus      |
| Vertical | up to 12 km  | Cumulonimbus |


---


## Important Cloud (VERY IMP)


### Cumulonimbus

- Thunderstorms
- Lightning, hail
- Heavy rainfall

---


## India Example

- **Western Disturbances → Cirrus + Nimbus clouds**

---


# 9. 🌧️ Precipitation


## Definition

- Moisture falling from atmosphere to earth

---


## Formation Flow


```plain text
Evaporation
     ↓
Condensation
     ↓
Clouds
     ↓
Droplet growth
     ↓
Precipitation
```


---


## Forms


| Type  | Description  |
| ----- | ------------ |
| Rain  | Liquid       |
| Snow  | Ice crystals |
| Sleet | Frozen rain  |
| Hail  | Ice balls    |


---


## Snowfall Areas (India)

- Himalayas, Ladakh, J&K

---


## Hail (IMPORTANT)

- Formed in **Cumulonimbus**
- Concentric ice layers

⚠️ Damages crops (UP, Punjab)


---


# 10. 🌦️ Types of Rainfall


## 1. Convectional Rainfall


```plain text
Heating → Air rises → Cooling → Rain
```


👉 Features:

- Heavy, short duration

    📍 Amazon, Congo


---


## 2. Orographic Rainfall (VERY IMPORTANT)


```plain text
Moist air → Mountain → Rise → Rain
```


| Side     | Feature           |
| -------- | ----------------- |
| Windward | Heavy rain        |
| Leeward  | Dry (Rain shadow) |


📍 India:

- Western Ghats
- Meghalaya (Mawsynram)

---


## 3. Cyclonic Rainfall

- Due to **air mass convergence**

📍 India:

- Monsoon depressions
- Western disturbances
