# 1. PRELIMS PYQs


Format


**Question — Exam — Year — Answer**


---

1. **The hydrological cycle refers to:**

    — Union Public Service Commission Prelims — 2011


    ✅ **Answer:** **Continuous circulation of water between oceans, atmosphere and land**


---

1. **Approximately what percentage of Earth's water is contained in oceans?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2013


    ✅ **Answer:** **About 97%**


---

1. **Which is the largest ocean on Earth?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2015


    ✅ **Answer:** **Pacific Ocean**


---

1. **Which ocean trench is the deepest on Earth?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2016


    ✅ **Answer:** **Mariana Trench**


---

1. **The continental shelf is important mainly because it:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2017


    ✅ **Answer:** **Contains rich fishing grounds and petroleum resources**


---

1. **Which feature connects the continental shelf to the deep ocean basin?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2018


    ✅ **Answer:** **Continental slope**


---

1. **Which part of the ocean floor is known as the smoothest region on Earth?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2019


    ✅ **Answer:** **Abyssal plains**


---

1. **The thermocline refers to:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2020


    ✅ **Answer:** **Layer of rapid temperature decrease with depth**


---

1. **Which feature is a flat-topped seamount?**

    — State PCS Prelims — 2021


    ✅ **Answer:** **Guyot**


---

1. **Lakshadweep Islands are examples of:**

    — State PCS Prelims — 2022


    ✅ **Answer:** **Coral atolls**

# 3. Additional High-Probability Questions (PCS Pattern)


### Prelims Style


1️⃣ Warmest ocean water occurs near


→ **Equator**


2️⃣ Deep ocean temperature


→ **0–3°C**


3️⃣ Depth of continental shelf


→ **0–200 m**


4️⃣ Depth of abyssal plains


→ **3000–6000 m**


5️⃣ Major source of ocean heat


→ **Solar radiation**


---


### Mains Style


1️⃣ Explain the **hydrological cycle with diagram**.


2️⃣ Describe the **major divisions of ocean floor**.


3️⃣ Explain **mid-ocean ridges and seafloor spreading**.


4️⃣ Discuss **horizontal distribution of ocean temperature**.


5️⃣ Explain **thermocline and vertical temperature distribution**.
