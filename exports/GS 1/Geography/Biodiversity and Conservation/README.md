
# 1. Biodiversity (Definition & Meaning)


### Definition


**Biodiversity = Variety + Variability of life forms on Earth**


It includes:

- **Plants**
- **Animals**
- **Microorganisms**
- **Genes**
- **Ecosystems**

### Standard Definition

> **Biodiversity refers to the variety of organisms in a specific geographic region and the variability among living organisms.**

---


### Word Origin


| Word      | Meaning |
| --------- | ------- |
| Bio       | Life    |
| Diversity | Variety |


➡ Therefore **Biodiversity = Variety of Life**


---


### What Biodiversity Includes


1️⃣ Variety of **species**


2️⃣ Variety of **genes** within species


3️⃣ Variety of **ecosystems**


---


### Important Fact (Exam Point)

- Biodiversity evolved through **millions of years of evolution**
- **Most species that ever lived are now extinct**

➡ Approx **99% species that lived on Earth are extinct**


---


# 2. Global Biodiversity Patterns


### Uneven Distribution


Biodiversity is **not evenly distributed across Earth**.


### Pattern


| Region    | Biodiversity |
| --------- | ------------ |
| Tropics   | Very High    |
| Temperate | Moderate     |
| Polar     | Very Low     |


---


### Reason


Main factors:

- **Solar Energy**
- **Water Availability**
- **Climate**

---


### Example


| Region            | Biodiversity   |
| ----------------- | -------------- |
| Amazon rainforest | Extremely high |
| Congo Basin       | Very high      |
| Tropical Asia     | Very high      |


---


### Important Concept


**Tropical forests contain the highest biodiversity on Earth.**


Reason:

- Warm climate
- High rainfall
- Long growing season

---


### Example from India


India's biodiversity hotspots:

- **Western Ghats**
- **Eastern Himalayas**
- **Indo-Burma region**
- **Sundaland (Andaman & Nicobar)**

---


# 3. Levels of Biodiversity


Biodiversity exists at **three levels**.


```plain text
BIODIVERSITY
     ↓
1 Genetic Diversity
2 Species Diversity
3 Ecosystem Diversity
```


---


# 3.1 Genetic Diversity


### Definition


Genetic diversity refers to:

> **Variation of genes within a species.**

---


### Example


Humans belong to the same species but differ in:

- Height
- Skin colour
- Hair
- Physical appearance

➡ These differences occur due to **genetic diversity**


---


### Importance


Genetic diversity ensures:

- **Healthy breeding population**
- **Adaptation to environment**
- **Disease resistance**

---


### Indian Example


Rice varieties in India:

- Basmati
- IR varieties
- Indigenous rice varieties

India once had **>30,000 rice varieties**.


---


### Uttar Pradesh Example


UP grows multiple varieties of:

- Wheat
- Paddy
- Sugarcane

These show **genetic diversity in crops**.


---


# 3.2 Species Diversity


### Definition

> Species diversity refers to the **number of species in a given region.**

---


### Measurement


Species diversity is measured using:


1️⃣ **Species richness**


2️⃣ **Species abundance**


---


### Species Rich Areas


Areas with large number of species are called:


### Biodiversity Hotspots


Example:

- Amazon rainforest
- Western Ghats

---


### Important Exam Fact


Tropical forests occupy only:


**~7% of Earth's surface**


But contain:


**More than 50% of global species**


---


# 3.3 Ecosystem Diversity


### Definition

> Ecosystem diversity refers to the variety of ecosystems in a region.

---


### Ecosystem Types


Examples:

- Forest ecosystem
- Desert ecosystem
- Grassland ecosystem
- Wetlands
- Marine ecosystem

---


### Key Concept


Different ecosystems have:

- Different species
- Different ecological processes
- Different environmental conditions

---


### Ecosystem Boundary


Ecosystem boundaries are:


⚠ **Not sharply defined**


They are **complex and overlapping**.


---


# 4. Role of Biodiversity in Ecosystem


Biodiversity plays an essential role in maintaining ecosystem balance.


---


### Ecosystem Functions


Species perform important roles:


| Function           | Role             |
| ------------------ | ---------------- |
| Energy capture     | Plants           |
| Decomposition      | Bacteria & fungi |
| Nutrient cycling   | Soil organisms   |
| Climate regulation | Forests          |
| Water cycle        | Vegetation       |


---


### Ecosystem Stability


Important principle:


```plain text
Higher biodiversity
        ↓
Greater ecosystem stability
```


---


### Why?


Because ecosystems with many species:

- Adapt better to changes
- Survive environmental stress
- Maintain ecological balance

---

| -------------- | -------------- |

- Rice

- Millets

---

# 7. Loss of Biodiversity


### Meaning

> **Loss of biodiversity refers to the decline in the number of species, genetic diversity, and ecosystems on Earth.**

This includes:

- **Species extinction**
- **Habitat destruction**
- **Decline in population of organisms**

---


## Major Reason (NCERT Insight)


Since the last few decades:


```plain text
Population Growth
        ↓
Increased Resource Consumption
        ↓
Habitat Destruction
        ↓
Species Extinction
```


---


# Important Exam Fact


Tropical regions:

- Occupy **~25% of Earth’s surface**
- Contain **~75% of human population**

➡ This leads to heavy pressure on natural ecosystems.


---


# Key Observation


**Tropical rainforests contain nearly 50% of Earth's species.**


Therefore:


```plain text
Deforestation in tropical forests
        ↓
Massive biodiversity loss
```


---


# Example (India)


Major deforestation zones:

- **Western Ghats**
- **Central India forests**
- **North-East forests**

---


# Example (Uttar Pradesh)


Biodiversity pressure areas:

- **Terai region**
- **Shivalik foothills**
- **Dudhwa landscape**

Important species affected:

- Tiger
- Swamp deer
- Rhinoceros (historically)

---


# 8. Causes of Biodiversity Loss


## 1 Habitat Destruction


Most important cause.


Examples:

- Deforestation
- Urbanization
- Mining
- Agriculture expansion

---


### Cause–Effect Chain


```plain text
Forest Clearing
      ↓
Loss of Habitat
      ↓
Species Migration or Death
      ↓
Extinction
```


---


### Indian Example


Amazon-like biodiversity in India exists in:

- **Western Ghats**
- **North-East India**

Both threatened by **deforestation and infrastructure projects**.


---


# 2 Over-exploitation of Resources


Overuse of natural resources for human needs.


Examples:

- Timber extraction
- Wildlife hunting
- Overfishing

---


### Example


Animals hunted for:


| Animal     | Reason |
| ---------- | ------ |
| Tiger      | Skin   |
| Elephant   | Ivory  |
| Rhinoceros | Horn   |
| Crocodile  | Skin   |


---


### Poaching Impact


Poaching has caused species to become:

- **Endangered**
- **Critically endangered**

---


# 3 Natural Calamities


Natural disasters also affect biodiversity.


Examples:

- Earthquakes
- Floods
- Volcanic eruptions
- Forest fires
- Droughts

---


### Effect


```plain text
Natural Disaster
      ↓
Habitat Damage
      ↓
Flora & Fauna Loss
      ↓
Regional Biodiversity Change
```


---


# 4 Pollution


Chemicals damage ecosystems.


Examples:

- Pesticides
- Hydrocarbons
- Heavy metals

These destroy:

- Sensitive species
- Aquatic organisms
- Soil microorganisms

---


### Example


Pollution in rivers affects:

- Fish
- Amphibians
- Aquatic plants

---


# 5 Invasive Species (Exotic Species)


### Definition

> Species introduced outside their natural habitat that harm native species.

---


### Examples


| Exotic Species | Impact              |
| -------------- | ------------------- |
| Lantana        | Damages forests     |
| Parthenium     | Harmful weed        |
| Water hyacinth | Blocks water bodies |


---


### Indian Example


**Lantana camara** has spread widely in Indian forests.


It reduces biodiversity.


---

- Meat

### Example

# 9. IUCN Classification of Threatened Species


### Organization


**IUCN = International Union for Conservation of Nature**


It classifies species for conservation.


---


### Red List


IUCN publishes:


### **Red List of Threatened Species**


This list shows species at risk of extinction.


---


# Categories of Threatened Species


---


## 1 Endangered Species


### Definition

> Species that are **in danger of extinction**.

Example species:

- Red Panda
- Tiger
- Asian Elephant

---


### Indian Examples

- Bengal Tiger
- Indian Elephant
- Great Indian Bustard

---


### Uttar Pradesh Example


Protected species in UP:

- Tiger (Dudhwa)
- Swamp Deer (Barasingha)

---


## 2 Vulnerable Species


### Definition

> Species that are **likely to become endangered in the near future** if threats continue.

---


### Reason

- Rapid population decline
- Habitat loss

---


### Example


Some forest species and rare plants.


---


## 3 Rare Species


### Definition

> Species with **small populations** and **limited geographical distribution**.

They may become endangered if threats increase.


---


### Characteristics

- Small population
- Restricted habitat
- Sensitive to disturbance

---


# Example from India


Rare plant species found in:

- Western Ghats
- Eastern Himalayas

---


# Example from NCERT Image


Rare grass species found in:

- **Agasthyamalai peak (India)**

---

# 11. Biodiversity Conservation


### Definition

> Biodiversity conservation means **protecting, preserving, and managing biodiversity to prevent extinction of species.**

---


# Two Major Methods of Conservation


```plain text
Biodiversity Conservation
        ↓
1 In-situ Conservation
2 Ex-situ Conservation
```


---


# 12. In-situ Conservation


### Meaning

> **In-situ conservation means protecting species in their natural habitat.**

This is the **most effective method**.


---


### Examples

- National Parks
- Wildlife Sanctuaries
- Biosphere Reserves

---


## 1. National Parks


### Definition


Protected areas where:

- Wildlife
- Ecosystems
- Natural habitat

are protected.


Human activities are **restricted**.


---


### Indian Examples


| National Park | State       |
| ------------- | ----------- |
| Jim Corbett   | Uttarakhand |
| Kaziranga     | Assam       |
| Gir           | Gujarat     |
| Sundarbans    | West Bengal |


---


### Uttar Pradesh Example


| National Park            | Location        |
| ------------------------ | --------------- |
| **Dudhwa National Park** | Lakhimpur Kheri |


Important species:

- Tiger
- Rhinoceros
- Swamp deer (Barasingha)

---

- Gene banks

### Examples

| ---------------- | --------------------------------- |

| Seed Bank        | Crop gene banks                   |
