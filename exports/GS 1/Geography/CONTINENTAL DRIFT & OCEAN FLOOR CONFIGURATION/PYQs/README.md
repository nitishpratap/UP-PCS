
# PRELIMS PYQs (with Answers)


Format:


**Question — Exam — Year — Answer**


---

1. **Who proposed the Continental Drift Theory?**

    — Union Public Service Commission Prelims — 2010


    ✅ **Answer:** Alfred Wegener


---

1. **Pangaea refers to:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2012


    ✅ **Answer:** A supercontinent that existed about 200 million years ago


---

1. **Panthalassa was:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2013


    ✅ **Answer:** The superocean surrounding Pangaea


---

1. **Which fossil is considered important evidence for continental drift?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2014


    ✅ **Answer:** Mesosaurus


---

1. **Tillite deposits indicate:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2015


    ✅ **Answer:** Glacial origin of sediments


---

1. **Which is the longest mountain chain on Earth?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2016


    ✅ **Answer:** Mid-Ocean Ridge system


---

1. **Abyssal plains are:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2017


    ✅ **Answer:** Flat deep ocean floor areas between continental margins and mid-ocean ridges


---

1. **Pacific Ring of Fire is associated with:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2018


    ✅ **Answer:** Intense volcanic and earthquake activity


---

1. **Which continents formed Gondwanaland?**

    — State PCS Prelims — 2019


    ✅ **Answer:** South America, Africa, India, Australia, Antarctica


---

1. **The deepest ocean trench in the world is:**

    — State PCS Prelims — 2020


    ✅ **Answer:** Mariana Trench
