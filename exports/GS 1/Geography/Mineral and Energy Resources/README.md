
# PART 1 — BASIC CONCEPTS OF MINERALS


## 1. Mineral: Definition


**Mineral** → A **natural substance** of **organic or inorganic origin** having **definite chemical and physical properties**.


### Key Characteristics of Minerals


1️⃣ **Uneven distribution** across the earth


2️⃣ **Inverse relationship between quality and quantity**

- High quality → less quantity
- Low quality → more quantity

3️⃣ **Exhaustible resources**

- Form over **millions of years**
- Cannot be replenished quickly

👉 Therefore **mineral conservation is necessary**.


---

## 2.1 Metallic Minerals

# PART 2 — DISTRIBUTION OF MINERALS IN INDIA


### Important Concept for Prelims


Most minerals occur in **Peninsular Plateau** because it contains **old crystalline rocks**.


### Important observation


👉 **North Indian plains have very few minerals**


Reason:

- Plains consist of **alluvial deposits**
- Minerals are mostly found in **igneous & metamorphic rocks**

# 2. PETROLEUM (Liquid Gold)


### Definition


**Petroleum** is a mixture of **hydrocarbons in liquid and gaseous form** found in sedimentary rocks.


It is called **“Liquid Gold”** because:

- It is **scarce**
- Has **diverse industrial uses**

---


## Uses of Petroleum


| Sector                 | Uses                          |
| ---------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| Transport              | Petrol, diesel, aviation fuel |
| Industry               | Lubricants                    |
| Petrochemical industry | Plastic, synthetic fibre      |
| Agriculture            | Fertilisers                   |
| Medicine               | Vaseline, cosmetics           |


---


# Geological Occurrence


Petroleum is mainly found in **sedimentary rocks of tertiary period**.

# PART 3 — NON-CONVENTIONAL ENERGY


### Definition


Energy sources that are:

- Renewable
- Environment friendly
- Sustainable

Examples:

- Solar energy
- Wind energy
- Tidal energy
- Geothermal energy
- Bio energy

---


# 1. Solar Energy


Solar energy is produced using **photovoltaic cells**.


Two main technologies:


| Technology               | Description                       |
| ------------------------ | --------------------------------- |
| Photovoltaic cells       | Convert sunlight into electricity |
| Solar thermal technology | Uses heat energy                  |


---


### Advantages

- Renewable
- Pollution free
- Easy to construct

Solar energy plants are:

- **7% more efficient than coal plants**
- **10% more efficient than nuclear plants**

---


### Regions with High Solar Potential


Best areas:

- Rajasthan
- Gujarat

Reason:

- Clear skies
- High solar radiation

---


# 2. Wind Energy


Wind turbines convert **kinetic energy of wind → electricity**.


Wind energy sources include:

- Trade winds
- Westerlies
- Monsoon winds
- Sea breeze & land breeze

---


### Major Wind Energy States


| State       | Reason        |
| ----------- | ------------- |
| Gujarat     | Coastal winds |

| Karnataka   | Plateau winds |


---


### Important Wind Farms

---


# 3. Tidal Energy


Source:

- **Ocean tides and waves**

- **West coast of India**

---


# 4. Geothermal Energy

Sources:

- Hot springs
- Geysers

# 5. Bio Energy

- Agricultural waste

- Industrial waste

---

👉 **Okhla (Delhi)**


Benefits:

# PART 4 — CONSERVATION OF MINERAL RESOURCES


Mineral conservation is important because minerals are **exhaustible resources**.


---


# Methods of Conservation


### 1️⃣ Use renewable energy


Replace fossil fuels with:

- Solar
- Wind
- Geothermal
- Tidal

---


### 2️⃣ Recycling of metals


Use scrap metals especially:

- Copper
- Lead
- Zinc

---


### 3️⃣ Use substitutes


Example:

- Aluminium instead of copper

---


### 4️⃣ Reduce export of rare minerals


So that **domestic industries have long-term supply**.


---


# CAUSE-EFFECT CHAIN (UPSC MAINS)


```plain text
Rapid industrialization
        ↓
Higher mineral extraction
        ↓
Resource depletion
        ↓
Environmental degradation
        ↓
Need for conservation
```
