# 1. Concept of Transport


### Definition


**Transport** = Service for the **movement of people, goods and services** from one place to another using different carriers.


✔ It uses

- humans
- animals
- vehicles
- ships
- aircraft

Transport occurs through:

- **Land**
- **Water**
- **Air**
- **Pipelines**

---


## Why Transport is Important


### Core Idea


Resources, industries and markets **are not located at the same place**.


Therefore transport connects:


```plain text
Production Areas  →  Markets
Raw Materials     →  Industries
People            →  Economic Opportunities
```


### Importance

1. **Trade and commerce**
2. **Economic development**
3. **National integration**
4. **Defense logistics**
5. **Urbanisation and industrialisation**

Example

- Coal from **Jharkhand → power plants in UP**
- Wheat from **Punjab → rest of India**

---


# 2. Transport Network


### Definition


A **transport network** is formed when:


```plain text
Nodes (places)
   ↓
Connected by
   ↓
Links (routes)
```


Example


```plain text
Delhi (node)
   |
NH-44
   |
Agra (node)
```

# 3. Modes of Transport


| Mode          | Characteristics | Best For              |
| ------------- | --------------- | --------------------- |
| **Road**      | Flexible        | Short distances       |
| **Railways**  | Heavy load      | Long distance freight |
| **Waterways** | Cheapest        | Bulk cargo            |
| **Airways**   | Fastest         | High-value goods      |
| **Pipelines** | Continuous flow | Liquids & gases       |


In a **well managed system these complement each other.**


Example


```plain text
Coal Mine → Rail → Port → Ship → Foreign Market
```

# 16. Pipelines


## Definition


Pipelines transport **liquids and gases continuously through pipes**.


Transported materials:

- petroleum
- natural gas
- water
- liquid coal
- milk (New Zealand example)

---


## Advantages of Pipelines

1. Continuous transport
2. Safe and reliable
3. Low operational cost
4. No traffic congestion

---


## Major Pipeline Networks


### USA


Large pipeline networks connect:


```plain text
Oil wells (Gulf of Mexico)
→ Refineries
→ Industrial cities
```


Example


**Big Inch Pipeline**

- Carries petroleum from **Texas oil fields to northeastern USA**.

About **17% of freight transport in USA occurs through pipelines**.


---

# 19. Cyberspace and Internet


## Definition


**Cyberspace** is the **virtual electronic world created by computer networks and the Internet**.


It allows communication without physical movement.


---


## Components of Cyberspace

1. Internet
2. World Wide Web (WWW)
3. Email
4. Social networks
5. Cloud networks

---
