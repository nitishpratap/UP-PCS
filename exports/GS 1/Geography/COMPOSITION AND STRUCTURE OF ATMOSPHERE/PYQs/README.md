
# PRELIMS PYQs (with Answers)


Format:


**Question — Exam — Year — Answer**


---

1. **Which gas constitutes the largest proportion of the Earth's atmosphere?**

    — Union Public Service Commission Prelims — 2011


    ✅ **Answer:** Nitrogen (≈78%)


---

1. **Which layer of the atmosphere contains the ozone layer?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2013


    ✅ **Answer:** Stratosphere


---

1. **In which atmospheric layer do most weather phenomena occur?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2014


    ✅ **Answer:** Troposphere


---

1. **Which atmospheric layer is responsible for reflecting radio waves?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2016


    ✅ **Answer:** Ionosphere (Thermosphere)


---

1. **In which layer do meteors burn up due to friction?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2017


    ✅ **Answer:** Mesosphere


---

1. **The outermost layer of the atmosphere is:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2018


    ✅ **Answer:** Exosphere


---

1. **Which gas is mainly responsible for the greenhouse effect in the atmosphere?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2015


    ✅ **Answer:** Carbon dioxide (CO₂)


---

1. **Water vapour in the atmosphere mainly contributes to:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2019


    ✅ **Answer:** Cloud formation and precipitation


---

1. **Dust particles in the atmosphere act mainly as:**

    — State PCS Prelims — 2020


    ✅ **Answer:** Condensation nuclei for cloud formation


---

1. **The normal lapse rate in the troposphere is approximately:**

    — State PCS Prelims — 2021


    ✅ **Answer:** 6.5°C per km


---


# Additional PRELIMS Questions (Very Common)


These are **repeatedly asked across UPSC / PCS exams**.


---

1. **Up to what altitude does about 99% of the atmospheric mass lie?**

    ✅ **Answer:** Within about **32 km**


---

1. **Which atmospheric layer is considered most stable for aircraft flying?**

    ✅ **Answer:** Stratosphere


---

1. **Which gas absorbs ultraviolet radiation in the atmosphere?**

    ✅ **Answer:** Ozone (O₃)


---

1. **Which atmospheric layer shows temperature inversion due to ozone absorption?**

    ✅ **Answer:** Stratosphere
