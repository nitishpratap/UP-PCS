# 1. PRELIMS PYQs


Format


**Question — Exam — Year — Answer**


---

1. **The Himalayas were formed as a result of collision between which plates?**

    — Union Public Service Commission Prelims — 2013


    ✅ **Answer:** **Indian Plate and Eurasian Plate**


---

1. **Which of the following rivers flows through a rift valley in India?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2014


    ✅ **Answer:** **Narmada**


---

1. **Which of the following is the youngest mountain range in India?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2015


    ✅ **Answer:** **Himalayas**


---

1. **The Indo-Gangetic plain was formed mainly by the deposition of sediments brought by which rivers?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2016


    ✅ **Answer:** **Indus, Ganga and Brahmaputra**


---

1. **Bhabar region of northern plains is characterized by:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2017


    ✅ **Answer:** **Pebbles and disappearance of rivers underground**


---

1. **Which region of northern plains is marshy and waterlogged?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2018


    ✅ **Answer:** **Terai**


---

1. **Old alluvium of northern plains is known as:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2019


    ✅ **Answer:** **Bhangar**


---

1. **Which of the following is the newest alluvium deposited by rivers?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2020


    ✅ **Answer:** **Khadar**


---

1. **India’s only active volcano is located at:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2021


    ✅ **Answer:** **Barren Island**


---

1. **Which physiographic division contains the Thar Desert?**

    — State PCS Prelims — 2022


    ✅ **Answer:** **Indian Desert**

# 3. Additional High-Probability PCS Questions


These are **very common in State PCS exams**.


---


### Prelims Style


1️⃣ Which rivers flow through **rift valleys in India**?


→ **Narmada and Tapi**


2️⃣ Which physiographic division is the **oldest landmass of India**?


→ **Peninsular Plateau**


3️⃣ Which region of the plains receives **new alluvium every year**?


→ **Khadar**


4️⃣ Which Himalayan range contains **permanent snow and glaciers**?


→ **Himadri**


5️⃣ Which coastal plain is **narrower**?


→ **Western coastal plain**


---


### Mains Style


1️⃣ Explain **endogenic and exogenic geomorphic forces**.


2️⃣ Discuss **plate tectonics and the origin of Himalayas**.


3️⃣ Explain **physiographic divisions of India**.


4️⃣ Discuss **formation of Indo-Gangetic plain**.


5️⃣ Explain **Bhabar–Terai–Bhangar–Khadar sequence**.
