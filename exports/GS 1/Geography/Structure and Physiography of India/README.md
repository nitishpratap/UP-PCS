# 1. GEOMORPHIC FORCES SHAPING INDIA


## Definition


**Geomorphic processes** = Processes responsible for shaping the **Earth’s surface landforms**.


### Two Major Types


| Type                 | Meaning                                 | Examples                                |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| **Endogenic Forces** | Forces originating **inside the Earth** | Plate tectonics, volcanism, earthquakes |
| **Exogenic Forces**  | Forces acting **on Earth's surface**    | Weathering, erosion, deposition         |


### Key Concept (Exam Point)


```plain text
Endogenic forces → create relief
Exogenic forces → modify relief
```


India’s landforms are the result of **interaction between these two forces**.


---


# 2. PLATE TECTONICS AND ORIGIN OF INDIA


## Movement of Indian Plate


Originally the **Indian Plate was located south of the equator** and was part of the **Gondwana landmass**.


### Geological Evolution


```plain text
Gondwana Supercontinent
        ↓
Breakup (~200 million years ago)
        ↓
Indian Plate moves northward
        ↓
Collision with Eurasian Plate
        ↓
Formation of Himalayas
```


### Important Fact


The **Indian Plate is still moving northward**, causing:

- earthquakes
- uplift of Himalayas
- tectonic instability

# 3. GEOLOGICAL DIVISIONS OF INDIA


India’s geological structure can be divided into **three major divisions**:


| Division                            | Nature                  |
| ----------------------------------- | ----------------------- |
| **Peninsular Block**                | Ancient stable landmass |
| **Himalayas and other mountains**   | Young fold mountains    |
| **Indo-Gangetic-Brahmaputra Plain** | Recent alluvial plain   |


These divisions correspond to India’s major **physiographic features**.
