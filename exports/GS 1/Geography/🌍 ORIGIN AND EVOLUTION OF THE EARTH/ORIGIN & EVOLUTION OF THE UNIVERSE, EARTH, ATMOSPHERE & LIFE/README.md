# 1. Fundamental Questions About the Universe


Humans have always asked:

- How did the **Universe originate?**
- How did the **Solar System form?**

These questions led to several **scientific hypotheses and theories**.


---


# 2. Early Theories of the Origin of Earth


## 2.1 Nebular Hypothesis (Kant–Laplace Theory)


| Feature     | Details                                                          |
| ----------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Proposed by | **Immanuel Kant**                                                |
| Modified by | **Pierre-Simon Laplace (1796)**                                  |
| Idea        | Planets formed from a **rotating gaseous nebula** around the Sun |


### Process


```plain text
Giant rotating nebula
        ↓
Gravitational contraction
        ↓
Rotation increases
        ↓
Flattened disc forms
        ↓
Rings separate
        ↓
Condensation of rings
        ↓
Planets formed
```


### Keywords

- Nebula
- Gravitational collapse
- Condensation

---


## 2.2 Planetesimal Hypothesis


| Feature     | Details                                                 |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| Proposed by | **Chamberlin & Moulton (1900)**                         |
| Supporters  | **Sir James Jeans, Harold Jeffreys**                    |
| Core Idea   | A star passed close to the Sun pulling material from it |


### Formation Process


```plain text
Passing star near Sun
        ↓
Strong tidal forces
        ↓
Hot gaseous filament forms
        ↓
Cooling & condensation
        ↓
Planetesimals form
        ↓
Planets formed
```


### Important Term


**Planetesimal** → Small planetary bodies that combine to form planets.

# 5. Stages of the Big Bang


## Stage 1: Initial Singularity

- Entire universe compressed into a **tiny dense point**
- **Infinite density**
- **Extremely high temperature**

---


## Stage 2: Big Bang Explosion

- Occurred **~13.7 billion years ago**
- Universe began **rapid expansion**

---


## Stage 3: Formation of Matter

- Energy → **Matter**
- Expansion rate slowed gradually.

---


## Stage 4: Formation of Atoms


| Event                        | Time              |
| ---------------------------- | ----------------- |
| First atoms                  | **3 minutes**     |
| Universe becomes transparent | **300,000 years** |
| Temperature                  | ~ **4500 K**      |

# 12. Why Inner and Outer Planets Differ


### 1. Temperature


Inner region near Sun:

- Very **high temperature**
- Gases could not condense.

---


### 2. Solar Wind


Strong solar wind:

- **Blew away gases**
- Inner planets became rocky.

---


### 3. Gravity


Inner planets:

- Smaller size
- Weak gravity → cannot retain gases.

---

> 
>
> **Solar Wind**
>
> - **Solar wind** is a continuous stream of **charged particles** (mainly **electrons and protons**) that flow outward from the **Sun’s upper atmosphere (corona)**. ☀️
> - These particles travel through **space at very high speeds (about 300–800 km/s)**.
> - The wind carries the **Sun’s magnetic field** with it across the solar system.
> - When solar wind interacts with **Earth’s magnetic field**, it can cause **auroras (Northern and Southern Lights)** 🌌.
> - Strong solar wind storms can also **disturb satellites, GPS, radio communication, and power grids**.
>

# Giant Impact Hypothesis (Most Accepted)


### Idea


A **Mars-sized body (Theia)** collided with early Earth.


### Formation Process


```plain text
Early Earth
      ↓
Collision with Theia
      ↓
Huge debris cloud
      ↓
Debris orbit Earth
      ↓
Accretion of material
      ↓
Moon formed
```


Fact:


Moon formed **~4.4 billion years ago**.

# Evolution of Atmosphere


Atmosphere evolved in **three stages**.


---


## 1. Primary Atmosphere


Composition:

- Hydrogen
- Helium

Lost due to:

- Weak gravity
- Solar wind

---


## 2. Secondary Atmosphere


Formed by **volcanic outgassing**


Gases released:

- Water vapour
- Nitrogen
- Carbon dioxide
- Methane
- Ammonia

Key Process:


**Outgassing**


---


## 3. Oxygen Atmosphere


Produced by **photosynthesis**


Example:


**Cyanobacteria (Blue-green algae)**


Process:


```plain text
Photosynthesis
      ↓
Oxygen released
      ↓
Ozone layer formed
      ↓
Protection from UV radiation
```


---
