| Feature     | Details                                                          |
| ----------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Proposed by | **Immanuel Kant**                                                |
| Modified by | **Pierre-Simon Laplace (1796)**                                  |
| Idea        | Planets formed from a **rotating gaseous nebula** around the Sun |


### Process


```plain text
Giant rotating nebula
        ↓
Gravitational contraction
        ↓
Rotation increases
        ↓
Flattened disc forms
        ↓
Rings separate
        ↓
Condensation of rings
        ↓
Planets formed
```


### Keywords

- Nebula
- Gravitational collapse
- Condensation

        ↓

        ↓

        ↓

**Planetesimal** → Small planetary bodies that combine to form planets.

# 12. Why Inner and Outer Planets Differ


### 1. Temperature


Inner region near Sun:

- Very **high temperature**
- Gases could not condense.

---


### 2. Solar Wind


Strong solar wind:

- **Blew away gases**
- Inner planets became rocky.

---


### 3. Gravity


Inner planets:

- Smaller size
- Weak gravity → cannot retain gases.

---

> 
>
> **Solar Wind**
>
> - **Solar wind** is a continuous stream of **charged particles** (mainly **electrons and protons**) that flow outward from the **Sun’s upper atmosphere (corona)**. ☀️
> - These particles travel through **space at very high speeds (about 300–800 km/s)**.
> - The wind carries the **Sun’s magnetic field** with it across the solar system.
> - When solar wind interacts with **Earth’s magnetic field**, it can cause **auroras (Northern and Southern Lights)** 🌌.
> - Strong solar wind storms can also **disturb satellites, GPS, radio communication, and power grids**.

# 4. Evolution of Lithosphere


### Stage 1: Accretion of Matter


Dust and gas accumulated through **accretion process**.


```plain text
Dust + gas particles
      ↓
Collisions
      ↓
Planetesimals
      ↓
Proto-Earth
```


---


### Stage 2: Heating of the Earth


Three main sources of heat:


| Source                    | Explanation                       |
| ------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Accretion heat            | Collisions of particles           |
| Gravitational compression | Increasing pressure               |
| Radioactive decay         | Breakdown of radioactive elements |


This heat caused **melting of interior materials**.


---


### Stage 3: Differentiation of Earth


Because of melting:

- **Heavier elements sank**
- **Lighter elements rose**

Result:


| Layer  | Composition        |
| ------ | ------------------ |
| Core   | Iron and Nickel    |
| Mantle | Silicate materials |
| Crust  | Light rocks        |


This process is called **planetary differentiation**.
