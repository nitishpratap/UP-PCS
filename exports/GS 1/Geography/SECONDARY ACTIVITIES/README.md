
# 1. Secondary Activities


## Definition


**Secondary activities** are economic activities that **transform raw materials into finished or more valuable goods**.


**Key idea:**


➡ **Value Addition**


Examples


| Raw Material | Process            | Final Product |
| ------------ | ------------------ | ------------- |
| Cotton       | Spinning & weaving | Cloth         |
| Iron ore     | Smelting           | Steel         |
| Timber       | Processing         | Furniture     |


### Definition (Exam Ready)


**Secondary activities involve manufacturing, processing and construction that convert natural resources into usable products.**


Examples

- Textile industry
- Steel industry
- Automobile industry
- Cement industry

---

# Iron Extraction Process


Iron is extracted from **iron ore** in a **blast furnace**.


Process


```plain text
Iron Ore
   +
Coke (Carbon)
   +
Limestone
        ↓
Blast Furnace
        ↓
Molten Iron
        ↓
Pig Iron
        ↓
Steel
```
