# PRELIMS PYQs (with Answers)


Format


**Question — Exam — Year — Answer**


---

1. **The incoming solar radiation received by the Earth is called:**

    — Union Public Service Commission Prelims — 2013


    ✅ **Answer:** Insolation


---

1. **Which process is responsible for the blue colour of the sky?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2015


    ✅ **Answer:** Scattering of light


---

1. **Which process transfers heat vertically in the atmosphere?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2016


    ✅ **Answer:** Convection


---

1. **Horizontal transfer of heat in the atmosphere is called:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2017


    ✅ **Answer:** Advection


---

1. **Reflection of solar radiation by the Earth's surface is called:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2018


    ✅ **Answer:** Albedo


---

1. **Atmosphere is heated mainly by:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2019


    ✅ **Answer:** Terrestrial radiation


---

1. **Lines joining places of equal temperature on maps are called:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2020


    ✅ **Answer:** Isotherms


---

1. **At which date does the Earth come closest to the Sun?**

    — State PCS Prelims — 2021


    ✅ **Answer:** 3 January (Perihelion)


---

1. **At which date does the Earth reach farthest distance from the Sun?**

    — State PCS Prelims — 2022


    ✅ **Answer:** 4 July (Aphelion)


---

1. **The average environmental lapse rate is:**

    — State PCS Prelims — 2023


    ✅ **Answer:** 6.5°C per 1000 meters

# Additional PRELIMS Questions (Frequently Asked)


These are **very common in PCS exams**.


---

1. **Which type of radiation does the Earth emit?**

    ✅ **Answer:** Longwave (terrestrial radiation)


---

1. **Which radiation mainly heats the Earth's surface?**

    ✅ **Answer:** Shortwave solar radiation


---

1. **Which surfaces have high albedo?**

    ✅ **Answer:** Snow and ice


---

1. **Which surfaces have low albedo?**

    ✅ **Answer:** Oceans and forests


---

1. **Maximum insolation occurs over:**

    ✅ **Answer:** Subtropical deserts


---

1. **Which heat transfer process is dominant in the troposphere?**

    ✅ **Answer:** Convection
