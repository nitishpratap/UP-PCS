# 1. BASIC IDEA OF EARTH’S HEAT SYSTEM


### Core Principle


Earth maintains **energy balance** between incoming and outgoing radiation.


```plain text
Sun → Solar Radiation → Earth Surface
Earth Surface → Terrestrial Radiation → Atmosphere → Space
```


If incoming = outgoing → **Global temperature stability**


---


### Key Facts (Prelims)


| Concept               | Fact                  |
| --------------------- | --------------------- |
| Primary energy source | Sun                   |
| Solar radiation type  | Shortwave             |
| Earth radiation type  | Longwave              |
| Atmosphere heating    | Mainly from **below** |


---


### Important Concept


Earth receives energy but also **re-radiates energy**, preventing overheating.


---


# 2. INSOLATION (INCOMING SOLAR RADIATION)


### Definition


**Insolation = Incoming solar radiation received by Earth.**


---


### Characteristics


| Feature              | Description           |
| -------------------- | --------------------- |
| Type                 | Shortwave radiation   |
| Source               | Sun                   |
| Avg. energy received | **~1.94 cal/cm²/min** |
| Distribution         | Uneven                |


Important: **Earth receives only a tiny fraction of Sun's energy.**


---


# 3. EARTH–SUN DISTANCE & INSOLATION


Earth’s orbit is **elliptical**, causing slight variation in solar energy.


| Position | Date          | Distance        | Term           |
| -------- | ------------- | --------------- | -------------- |
| Closest  | **3 January** | ~147 million km | **Perihelion** |
| Farthest | **4 July**    | ~152 million km | **Aphelion**   |


### Important Insight


Although Earth is **closer in January**, it does **not cause summer**.


Reason:

- Land–water distribution
- Atmospheric circulation

---

| Slanting rays | Low             |


Reason:


Slanting rays spread over **larger area**.


---


### 3. Length of Day


| Season | Effect                      |

| Summer | Longer days → More heating  |
| Winter | Shorter days → Less heating |


---

# HEATING & COOLING OF ATMOSPHERE


Important concept:


**Atmosphere is heated indirectly by Earth.**


```plain text
Sun → heats land

```


---


# 1. CONDUCTION


### Definition


Heat transfer through **direct contact**.


Example:


Ground heats air touching it.


```plain text
Hot surface
↓
Air heated
↓
Heat spreads upward
```


---


### Characteristics


| Feature    | Detail                |
| ---------- | --------------------- |
| Type       | Direct contact        |
| Efficiency | Weak in atmosphere    |
| Reason     | Air is poor conductor |


---


# 2. CONVECTION


### Definition


**Vertical transfer of heat through air movement.**


---


### Process


```plain text
Surface heating
↓
Air becomes lighter
↓
Air rises
↓
Cool air descends
```


Creates **convection currents**.


---


### Occurs Mainly In


**Troposphere**


Examples:

- Thunderstorms
- Cumulonimbus clouds

---


# 3. ADVECTION


### Definition


**Horizontal transfer of heat via wind movement**


---


### Example


Hot desert winds → nearby regions.


Indian example:


**Loo Winds**


Affected states:

- Uttar Pradesh
- Rajasthan
- Haryana
- Delhi

---


### Comparison


| Process    | Direction  |
| ---------- | ---------- |
| Convection | Vertical   |

# HEAT BUDGET BREAKDOWN


From **100 units solar radiation**


### Reflected


| Source                | Units  |
| --------------------- | ------ |
| Clouds                | 27     |
| Snow & Ice            | 2      |
| Atmosphere scattering | 6      |
| Total                 | **35** |


This reflection is **Albedo**.


---


### Absorbed


| Location      | Units  |
| ------------- | ------ |

| Total         | **65** |


---


### Outgoing Radiation


| Process                   | Units |
| ------------------------- | ----- |

| Absorbed by atmosphere    | 34    |


Atmosphere sends **48 units back to space**.

# 1. BASIC IDEA OF EARTH’S HEAT SYSTEM


### Core Concept

- The **Earth receives energy from the Sun**.
- The Earth **re-radiates energy back to space**.
- Because of this **energy balance**, Earth’s temperature remains **relatively stable**.

### Important Points

- Earth is surrounded by the **Atmosphere** which supports life.
- **Solar energy** is the **primary source of energy** for Earth.

### Process Flow


```plain text
Sun → Solar Radiation → Earth Surface
```

# HEATING AND COOLING OF THE ATMOSPHERE


Important concept:


**The atmosphere is not heated directly by the Sun.**


Instead:


```plain text
Sun → heats Earth surface → Earth heats atmosphere
```


Reason:

- Solar radiation is **shortwave**
- Atmosphere absorbs **very little shortwave radiation**
- Earth emits **longwave radiation** which heats the atmosphere.

---


# 1. CONDUCTION


### Definition


**Conduction = transfer of heat through direct contact.**


Example:

- Land surface heated by Sun
- Air touching the ground gets heated

Process:


```plain text
Hot Earth surface
      ↓
Air in contact heated
      ↓
Heat transferred to upper layers
```


### Key Characteristics


| Feature       | Description                |
| ------------- | -------------------------- |
| Medium        | Direct contact             |
| Effectiveness | Very limited in atmosphere |
| Main role     | Heating lower atmosphere   |


Reason conduction is weak in air:

- Air is **poor conductor of heat**

---


# 2. CONVECTION


### Definition


**Convection = vertical transfer of heat through movement of air.**


### Process


```plain text
Surface air heated
       ↓
Becomes lighter
       ↓
Rises upward
       ↓
Cool air descends
```


This creates **convection currents**.


### Important Facts


| Feature          | Detail                        |
| ---------------- | ----------------------------- |
| Direction        | Vertical                      |
| Occurs mainly in | Troposphere                   |
| Role             | Major heat transfer mechanism |


Example:

- Afternoon cloud formation
- Thunderstorms

---


# 3. ADVECTION


### Definition


**Advection = horizontal transfer of heat through movement of air.**


Example:

- Warm winds moving from deserts to nearby areas.

### Important in Weather


| Process    | Movement   |
| ---------- | ---------- |
| Convection | Vertical   |
| Advection  | Horizontal |


### Indian Example


**Loo winds in North India**

- Occur in **summer**
- Caused by **hot air moving horizontally**
- Strong in **Uttar Pradesh, Rajasthan, Delhi, Haryana**

This example is **very important for UPPCS**.

### Concept

**Terrestrial Radiation**

| Feature | Detail             |
| ------- | ------------------ |
| Type    | Longwave radiation |

# Why Atmosphere is Heated from Below


Step-by-step process:


```plain text
Sun radiation
      ↓

      ↓

      ↓

      ↓

```

# 5. EARTH'S HEAT BUDGET


### Definition


**Heat budget = balance between incoming solar radiation and outgoing terrestrial radiation.**


Earth maintains **energy balance**.


If this balance did not exist:

- Earth would continuously heat up
- Or freeze.

---


# HEAT BUDGET BREAKDOWN


From **100 units of solar radiation**:


### Reflected back to space


| Source                                                       | Units  |
| ------------------------------------------------------------ | ------ |
| Clouds                                                       | 27     |
| Snow & Ice                                                   | 2      |
|  Scattered by atmospheric dust, aerosols, and air molecules. | 6      |
| Total reflection                                             | **35** |


This reflected radiation is called:


**Albedo**


---


### Absorbed Energy


| Location      | Units |
| ------------- | ----- |
| Atmosphere    | 14    |
| Earth Surface | 51    |


Total absorbed = **65 units**


---


# OUTGOING RADIATION


Earth emits **51 units** back.


Distribution:


| Process                   | Units |
| ------------------------- | ----- |
| Direct radiation to space | 17    |
| Absorbed by atmosphere    | 34    |


Atmosphere then radiates **48 units back to space**.


---


# Simplified Heat Budget Flow


```plain text
100 units solar radiation
      ↓
35 reflected (Albedo)
      ↓
65 absorbed
   ↓         ↓
Atmosphere   Earth surface
   14            51
```


Earth then radiates **51 units** back.

# TEMPERATURE DISTRIBUTION ON EARTH


## 1. TEMPERATURE


### Definition


**Temperature = Degree of hotness or coldness of the air.**


It represents the **amount of heat energy in the atmosphere**.


Important:

- Temperature is measured using a **thermometer**.
- Unit: **°C or °F**

---


# 2. FACTORS CONTROLLING TEMPERATURE DISTRIBUTION


Temperature on Earth is **not uniform**.


Major factors:


1️⃣ Latitude


2️⃣ Altitude


3️⃣ Distance from sea


4️⃣ Ocean currents


5️⃣ Winds


6️⃣ Slope and aspect


7️⃣ Cloud cover


---


# 3. EFFECT OF LATITUDE


### Principle


Temperature decreases from **Equator → Poles**


Reason:

- Sun rays become **slanting at higher latitudes**.

### Concept


| Region        | Solar Radiation     | Temperature |
| ------------- | ------------------- | ----------- |
| Equator       | Vertical rays       | High        |
| Mid-latitudes | Moderately slanting | Moderate    |
| Poles         | Highly slanting     | Low         |


Important fact:

- **Equatorial regions receive maximum insolation.**

---


# 4. EFFECT OF ALTITUDE


### Rule


Temperature decreases with increase in altitude.


### Environmental Lapse Rate


Average rate:


**6.5°C decrease per 1000 m**


Example:


| Location | Temperature |
| -------- | ----------- |
| Plains   | High        |
| Hills    | Low         |


Example in India:


| Place  | Altitude | Temperature |
| ------ | -------- | ----------- |
| Delhi  | Low      | Hot         |
| Shimla | High     | Cool        |


In **Uttar Pradesh**:

- Terai region slightly cooler due to **altitude + vegetation**.

---


# 5. DISTANCE FROM SEA (CONTINENTALITY)


### Land vs Water Heating


| Surface | Heating Speed |
| ------- | ------------- |
| Land    | Heats quickly |
| Water   | Heats slowly  |


Reason:

- Water has **higher heat capacity**.

---


### Result


| Region         | Temperature Variation |
| -------------- | --------------------- |
| Coastal areas  | Small variation       |
| Interior areas | Large variation       |


Examples:


| Coastal city | Interior city |
| ------------ | ------------- |
| Mumbai       | Delhi         |
| Chennai      | Nagpur        |


Delhi experiences **extreme climate**.


---


# 6. EFFECT OF OCEAN CURRENTS


Ocean currents influence coastal temperatures.


### Warm currents


Increase temperature.


Example:

- Gulf Stream warms **Western Europe**

---


### Cold currents


Decrease temperature.


Example:

- Peru Current cools **western South America**

---


# 7. EFFECT OF PREVAILING WINDS


Winds carry temperature characteristics of their origin.


Examples:


| Wind origin    | Effect  |
| -------------- | ------- |
| Polar winds    | Cooling |
| Tropical winds | Warming |


Example in India:

- **Loo winds** in North India increase temperature in summer.

Affected states:

- Uttar Pradesh
- Rajasthan
- Haryana
- Delhi

---


# 8. EFFECT OF CLOUD COVER


### Daytime


Clouds reduce incoming radiation → **cooler**


### Night


Clouds trap heat → **warmer**


Thus clouds act like a **blanket**.


---


# 9. SLOPE AND ASPECT


Direction of slope affects solar heating.


Example:


| Hemisphere          | Warmer slope |
| ------------------- | ------------ |
| Northern Hemisphere | South-facing |
| Southern Hemisphere | North-facing |


Reason:

- Receives **more direct sunlight**.

---


# 10. ISOTHERMS


### Definition


**Isotherms = lines joining places with equal temperature.**


Used in **temperature maps**.


Important features:


| Characteristic           | Explanation                |
| ------------------------ | -------------------------- |
| Run parallel to latitude | In general                 |
| Distort near land        | Due to continental effects |
| Distort near currents    | Due to ocean currents      |


---


# Example Pattern


```plain text
Equator → High temperature
      ↓
Subtropics → Moderate
      ↓
Polar regions → Low
```

# 11. GLOBAL TEMPERATURE DISTRIBUTION


### January Temperature Pattern

- Northern Hemisphere → Winter
- Southern Hemisphere → Summer

Temperature belt shifts **southward**.


Example:

- Siberia extremely cold
- Australia warm

---


### July Temperature Pattern

- Northern Hemisphere → Summer
- Southern Hemisphere → Winter

Temperature belt shifts **northward**.


Example:

- Sahara extremely hot
- Antarctica extremely cold
