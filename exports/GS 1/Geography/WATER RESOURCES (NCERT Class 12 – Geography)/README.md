
# 1. Importance of Water Resources


### Key Idea


Water is a **cyclic renewable resource** but **usable freshwater is extremely limited**.


### Global Distribution of Water


| Type of Water         | Percentage              |
| --------------------- | ----------------------- |
| Oceans (saline)       | ~97%                    |
| Freshwater            | ~3%                     |
| Accessible freshwater | **Very small fraction** |


👉 **71% of Earth's surface is covered by water**, but only a small amount is usable for humans.


---


### Why Water is Becoming a Major Global Issue


Future societies will face major challenges:

1. **Population growth**
2. **Demographic transition**
3. **Technological development**
4. **Environmental degradation**
5. **Water scarcity**

**Water Scarcity = Demand > Supply**


This can lead to:

- conflicts between **regions**
- disputes between **states**
- disputes between **countries**

Example:

- **Cauvery river dispute**
- **Indus water dispute**

---

- Storage constraints

## 2. Water Pollution


### Definition


**Water quality** refers to **purity of water free from harmful substances**.


Water becomes polluted due to:

- microorganisms
- chemicals
- industrial waste
- domestic sewage
- agricultural chemicals

When pollutants enter rivers, lakes or oceans they:

1. dissolve in water
2. remain suspended
3. seep into groundwater

This damages:

- aquatic ecosystem
- human health

---

# 17. Watershed Management


### Definition


Watershed management means:


**Efficient conservation and management of surface and groundwater within a drainage basin.**


Objectives:

- reduce runoff
- recharge groundwater
- soil conservation
- sustainable development

---


### Methods Used

- percolation tanks
- recharge wells
- check dams
- contour bunding

---


### Broad Scope


Watershed management involves:


| Resource | Conservation         |
| -------- | -------------------- |
| Land     | soil conservation    |
| Water    | groundwater recharge |
| Plants   | afforestation        |
| Animals  | ecological balance   |


---


### Importance


Watershed management ensures:

- water availability
- agricultural productivity
- ecological balance
