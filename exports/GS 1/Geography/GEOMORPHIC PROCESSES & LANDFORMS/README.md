    — Union Public Service Commission Prelims — 2012


    ✅ **Answer:** Running water

    — UPSC Prelims — 2014


    ✅ **Answer:** Youth stage

1. **Meanders are formed mainly due to:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2016


    ✅ **Answer:** Lateral erosion and deposition


---

1. **Which landform is formed when a meander loop is cut off from the main river channel?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2017


    ✅ **Answer:** Oxbow lake


---

1. **Which landform is formed at the mouth of a river where it enters the sea?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2015


    ✅ **Answer:** Delta

    — UPSC Prelims — 2018


    ✅ **Answer:** Alluvial fan

    — State PCS Prelims — 2021

# 1. GEOMORPHOLOGY : BASIC CONCEPTS


## Landform


### Definition


Small to medium-sized natural features of Earth’s surface formed by geomorphic processes.


### Examples

- Mountains
- Plateaus
- Plains
- Valleys
- Gorges
- Canyons

---


## Landscape


### Definition


A large area consisting of several related landforms.


### Example


**Himalayan Landscape**

- Mountains
- Valleys
- Rivers
- Glaciers

---


# 2. GEOMORPHIC PROCESSES


## Definition


Processes responsible for shaping and modifying Earth’s surface over geological time.


---


## Major Processes


| Process        | Meaning               |
| -------------- | --------------------- |
| Weathering     | Breakdown of rocks    |
| Erosion        | Removal of material   |
| Transportation | Movement of sediments |
| Deposition     | Settling of sediments |


---


## Geomorphic Agents


| Agent         | Major Landforms      |
| ------------- | -------------------- |
| Running water | Valleys, floodplains |
| Glacier       | U-shaped valleys     |
| Wind          | Sand dunes           |
| Groundwater   | Caves                |
| Waves         | Sea cliffs           |


---


# 3. EROSION → DEPOSITION RELATION


## Important Concept


**Deposition always follows erosion**


```plain text
Weathering
   ↓
Erosion
   ↓
Transportation
   ↓
Deposition
   ↓
New Landforms
```


### Example


```plain text
Himalayan erosion
      ↓
Sediments transported by rivers
      ↓
Deposited in Northern Plains
      ↓
Indo-Gangetic Plain formed
```


---


# 4. EVOLUTION OF LANDFORMS


Landforms continuously evolve due to:

- Climate change
- Tectonic uplift/subsidence
- Changes in geomorphic intensity

---


## Davis’ Cycle of Erosion


Proposed by William Morris Davis


```plain text
Youth → Mature → Old Age
```


### Concept


Landforms pass through stages like living organisms.


---


# 5. CONTROLS OF GEOMORPHIC PROCESSES


| Control    | Main Factors                |
| ---------- | --------------------------- |
| Structural | Rock hardness, permeability |
| Tectonic   | Uplift, folding, faulting   |
| Climatic   | Rainfall, temperature       |


---


## Structural Control


### Hard Rock

- Resistant to erosion
- Forms gorges, cliffs

### Soft Rock

- Easily eroded
- Forms broad valleys

---


## Tectonic Control


| Process    | Result    |
| ---------- | --------- |
| Uplift     | Mountains |
| Subsidence | Basins    |


### Example


Himalayas formed due to tectonic uplift.


---


## Climatic Control


| Climate | Dominant Geomorphic Agent |
| ------- | ------------------------- |
| Humid   | Running water             |
| Arid    | Wind                      |
| Cold    | Glacier                   |


---


# 6. RUNNING WATER AS GEOMORPHIC AGENT


## Importance


Most dominant geomorphic agent in humid regions.


### Important Regions

- India
- Monsoon Asia
- Uttar Pradesh

---


# 7. TYPES OF WATER FLOW


## 1. Overland Flow


Sheet-like flow over land surface.


### Causes

- Sheet erosion
- Initial channel formation

---


## 2. Channel Flow


Flow inside streams/rivers.


### Produces

- Valleys
- Floodplains
- Meanders
- Gorges

---


# 8. RIVER EROSION MECHANISMS


| Process          | Meaning                   |
| ---------------- | ------------------------- |
| Hydraulic action | Force of water            |
| Abrasion         | Scraping by sediments     |
| Attrition        | Collision among particles |
| Solution         | Chemical dissolution      |


---


# 9. STREAM GRADIENT & EROSION


```plain text
Steep slope → Fast flow → More erosion

Gentle slope → Slow flow → More deposition
```


---


# 10. STREAM EROSION SEQUENCE


```plain text
Overland Flow
      ↓
Small Channels
      ↓
Rills
      ↓
Gullies
      ↓
Valleys
```


---


# 11. RILL & GULLY EROSION


## Rills


Small narrow channels formed by runoff.


## Gullies


Deep channels formed by enlargement of rills.


---


## UPPCS Example


### Chambal Ravines

- UP–MP border
- Famous example of gully erosion

---


# 12. TYPES OF VALLEYS


| Valley Type     | Features             | Example          |
| --------------- | -------------------- | ---------------- |
| V-shaped valley | Narrow, steep        | Himalayan rivers |
| Gorge           | Deep & narrow        | Indus Gorge      |
| Canyon          | Wider top, step-like | Colorado Canyon  |


---


## Difference : Gorge vs Canyon


| Feature   | Gorge      | Canyon            |
| --------- | ---------- | ----------------- |
| Width     | Narrow     | Wider             |
| Rock Type | Hard rocks | Sedimentary rocks |
| Shape     | Steep      | Step-like         |


---


# 13. FLUVIAL CYCLE OF EROSION


## A. YOUTH STAGE


### Characteristics


| Feature     | Description               |
| ----------- | ------------------------- |
| Slope       | Steep                     |
| Flow        | Fast                      |
| Erosion     | Vertical erosion dominant |
| Valleys     | V-shaped                  |
| Floodplains | Absent                    |


---


## Landforms

- Waterfalls
- Rapids
- Gorges
- V-shaped valleys

---


## Process


```plain text
Vertical downcutting
        ↓
Deep valleys
        ↓
Waterfalls & rapids
```


---


## Indian Examples

- Alaknanda
- Bhagirathi
- Teesta

---


# B. MATURE STAGE


## Characteristics


| Feature    | Description               |
| ---------- | ------------------------- |
| Valley     | Wider                     |
| Erosion    | Lateral erosion increases |
| Streams    | Well integrated           |
| Floodplain | Begins developing         |


---


## Landforms

- Meanders
- Broader valleys
- Floodplains

---


## Process


```plain text
Reduced vertical erosion
          ↓
More lateral erosion
          ↓
Valley widening
```


---


## Examples


Middle courses of:

- Ganga
- Yamuna
- Narmada

---


# C. OLD STAGE


## Characteristics


| Feature    | Description |
| ---------- | ----------- |
| Gradient   | Gentle      |
| Flow       | Slow        |
| Deposition | Dominant    |
| Relief     | Very low    |


---


## Landforms

- Oxbow lakes
- Natural levees
- Floodplains
- Meanders

---


## Additional Features

- Marshes
- Swamps
- Lakes

---


## Example


Lower Ganga Plain


---


# 14. EROSIONAL LANDFORMS OF RIVERS


# A. POTHOLES


## Definition


Circular holes in rocky river beds formed by rotating pebbles.


---


## Formation


```plain text
Pebbles trapped
      ↓
Rotational movement
      ↓
Grinding action
      ↓
Potholes
```


---


# B. PLUNGE POOLS


## Definition


Deep pools formed at waterfall bases due to strong erosion.


---


## Formation


```plain text
Waterfall falls
      ↓
High kinetic energy
      ↓
Rock erosion
      ↓
Plunge pool
```


---


## Example


Jog Falls


---


# C. WATERFALLS


## Definition


Vertical fall in river due to sudden break in slope.


---


## Causes

- Faulting
- Hard rock layer
- River rejuvenation

---


## Major Examples


| Waterfall  | Location        |
| ---------- | --------------- |
| Jog Falls  | Karnataka       |
| Dudhsagar  | Goa             |
| Hundru     | Jharkhand       |
| Chitrakoot | UP–Chhattisgarh |


---


# D. RAPIDS


## Definition


Fast turbulent flow over irregular rocky beds.


### Causes

- Steep gradient
- Uneven river bed

---


# Difference : Waterfall vs Rapids


| Feature           | Waterfall     | Rapids        |
| ----------------- | ------------- | ------------- |
| Flow              | Vertical drop | Turbulent     |
| Height difference | Large         | Small         |
| Cause             | Sudden step   | Irregular bed |


---


# 15. DEPOSITIONAL LANDFORMS


# A. FLOODPLAINS


## Definition


Flat low-lying areas formed by river deposition during floods.


---


## Formation


```plain text
Flooding
    ↓
Sediment spread
    ↓
Alluvial deposition
    ↓
Floodplain formation
```


---


## Characteristics


| Feature   | Description |
| --------- | ----------- |
| Soil      | Alluvial    |
| Fertility | Very high   |
| Slope     | Gentle      |


---


## Example


Indo-Gangetic Plain


---


## UPPCS Focus


UP lies in middle Ganga floodplain.


### Important Cities

- Kanpur
- Prayagraj
- Varanasi

---


# B. NATURAL LEVEES


## Definition


Raised embankments formed along river banks by coarse sediment deposition.


---


## Formation


```plain text
Flood
  ↓
River overflows
  ↓
Heavy sediments deposited near banks
  ↓
Levees formed
```


---


# C. POINT BARS


## Definition


Deposits on inner bends of meanders.


---


## Key Concept


```plain text
Outer bend → erosion
Inner bend → deposition
```


---


# D. MEANDERS


## Definition


Large river bends formed due to lateral erosion and deposition.


---


## Formation


```plain text
Velocity difference
       ↓
Outer bank erosion
       ↓
Inner bank deposition
       ↓
Meanders
```


---


## Important Terms


| Term      | Meaning                 |
| --------- | ----------------------- |
| Cut bank  | Outer eroded bank       |
| Point bar | Inner depositional bank |


---


## Examples

- Ganga
- Yamuna

---


# E. OXBOW LAKES


## Definition


Crescent-shaped lakes formed by cut-off meanders.


---


## Formation


```plain text
Meander enlarges
      ↓
Neck narrows
      ↓
Flood cuts neck
      ↓
Loop separated
      ↓
Oxbow lake
```


---


# F. YAZOO STREAMS


## Definition


Tributaries flowing parallel to main river due to levee obstruction.


---


## Formation


```plain text
Levee forms
    ↓
Tributary blocked
    ↓
Flows parallel
```


---


# G. DELTAS


## Definition


Depositional landforms at river mouths.


---


## Formation


```plain text
River enters sea
       ↓
Velocity decreases
       ↓
Sediment deposition
       ↓
Distributaries form
       ↓
Delta
```


---


## Types of Deltas


| Type        | Example     |
| ----------- | ----------- |
| Arcuate     | Nile        |
| Bird’s Foot | Mississippi |
| Cuspate     | Tiber       |


---


## Important Example


Ganga-Brahmaputra Delta


Largest delta in the world.


---


# H. ALLUVIAL FANS


## Definition


Fan-shaped deposits formed where rivers emerge from mountains.


---


## Formation


```plain text
Mountain stream
      ↓
Enters plains
      ↓
Velocity drops
      ↓
Fan-shaped deposition
```


---


## Example


Himalayan foothills.


---


# I. BRAIDED CHANNELS


## Definition


Multiple interweaving channels due to excess sediment load.


---


## Causes

- Heavy sediment load
- Variable discharge
- Shallow channel

---


## Example


Brahmaputra River


---


# 16. RIVER REJUVENATION


## Definition


Renewed river erosion due to increase in gradient.


---


## Causes

- Tectonic uplift
- Sea-level fall
- Increased discharge

---


## Resulting Landforms

- River terraces
- Incised meanders
- Waterfalls

---


# 17. RIVER TERRACES


## Definition


Step-like benches formed by river rejuvenation.


---


## Formation


```plain text
Floodplain exists
       ↓
Land uplift
       ↓
River cuts downward
       ↓
Old floodplain left as terrace
```

# 21. ONE-LINER PRELIMS FACTS

- Running water = dominant geomorphic agent in humid regions.
- Deposition follows erosion.
- Rills → Gullies → Valleys.
- Youth stage = vertical erosion dominant.
- Mature stage = lateral erosion dominant.
- Old stage = deposition dominant.
- Largest delta = Ganga-Brahmaputra delta.
- Chambal ravines = gully erosion example.
- Meanders form due to lateral erosion.
- Oxbow lake = cut-off meander.

### Key Characteristics


Every landform has:


| Feature   | Explanation                                           |
| --------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Shape     | Physical form (V-shaped valley, U-shaped valley etc.) |
| Size      | Small (gully) to large (plateau)                      |
| Material  | Rock, soil, sediments                                 |
| Origin    | Formed by geomorphic processes                        |
| Evolution | Changes with time                                     |


---


## 2. Landscape


### Definition


A **landscape** is a **large tract of earth’s surface made up of several related landforms**.


Example


Himalayan Landscape

- mountains
- valleys
- glaciers
- rivers

---


## 3. Geomorphic Processes


### Definition


Processes responsible for **shaping and modifying the earth’s surface**.


They operate **very slowly over long geological time**.


### Major Processes


| Process        | Meaning               |
| -------------- | --------------------- |
| Weathering     | Breakdown of rocks    |
| Erosion        | Removal of material   |
| Transportation | Movement of sediments |
| Deposition     | Settling of sediments |


---


## 4. Geomorphic Agents


Agents that **perform geomorphic processes**.


### Major Agents


| Agent         | Examples of Landforms        |
| ------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Running Water | Valleys, gorges, floodplains |
| Groundwater   | Caves, stalactites           |
| Glaciers      | U-shaped valleys             |
| Wind          | Sand dunes                   |
| Waves         | Sea cliffs                   |


These agents **erode, transport and deposit sediments**.


---


## 5. Erosion – Deposition Relationship


### Important Concept


**Deposition follows erosion**


Cause–Effect Chain:


```plain text
Weathering
     ↓
Erosion
     ↓
Transportation
     ↓
Deposition
     ↓
Formation of new landforms
```


Example


Himalayan Rivers


```plain text
Himalayas → erosion
↓
Sediments transported
↓
Deposited in plains
↓
Formation of Indo-Gangetic Plains
```


---


## 6. Evolution of Landforms


Landforms **change continuously over time**.


### Reasons

1. Climate change
2. Vertical movements of land
3. Horizontal movements of land
4. Variation in intensity of geomorphic processes

### Important Concept


Landforms evolve like **life stages**.


```plain text
Youth
 ↓
Maturity
 ↓
Old age
```


This concept is called:


### **Cycle of Erosion**


(Proposed by **William Morris Davis**)


---


# CONTROLS OF GEOMORPHIC PROCESSES


Landform evolution depends on **three major controls**.


| Control           | Explanation                       |
| ----------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Structure         | Rock type, hardness, permeability |
| Tectonic activity | Uplift and subsidence             |
| Climate           | Rainfall, temperature, glaciers   |


---


## 1. Structural Control


Depends on **rock characteristics**


Important factors

- rock hardness
- permeability
- rock structure
- rock type

Example


Hard rocks → gorges


Soft rocks → wide valleys


---


## 2. Tectonic Control


Movement of lithospheric plates.


Examples


| Process    | Result    |
| ---------- | --------- |
| Uplift     | Mountains |
| Subsidence | Basins    |


Example


Himalayas formed due to **tectonic uplift**.


---


## 3. Climatic Control


Climate influences **erosion intensity**.


Examples


| Climate | Dominant Process      |
| ------- | --------------------- |
| Humid   | Running water erosion |
| Cold    | Glacial erosion       |
| Arid    | Wind erosion          |


---


# RUNNING WATER AS A GEOMORPHIC AGENT


In **humid regions**, running water is the **most important geomorphic agent**.


Example areas

- Monsoon Asia
- India
- Uttar Pradesh

---


## Components of Running Water


Two types of water flow:


### 1. Overland Flow


Water flowing **over land surface like a sheet**.


Causes


→ Sheet erosion


---


### 2. Channel Flow


Water flowing in **streams and rivers inside valleys**.


This produces:

- valleys
- gorges
- floodplains
- meanders

---


## River Erosion Mechanisms


| Process          | Meaning                |
| ---------------- | ---------------------- |
| Hydraulic action | Force of flowing water |
| Abrasion         | Scraping by sediments  |
| Attrition        | Particles collide      |
| Solution         | Chemical dissolution   |


---


# STREAM GRADIENT AND EROSION


### Important Relationship


```plain text
Steep slope → fast flow → erosion dominant
Gentle slope → slow flow → deposition dominant
```


---


# STREAM EROSION SEQUENCE


### Step-by-Step Evolution


```plain text
Overland flow
       ↓
Small channels
       ↓
Rills
       ↓
Gullies
       ↓
Valleys
```

# RILL AND GULLY EROSION


### Rill


Small narrow channels formed by **surface runoff**.


### Gully


Large deep channels formed by **continued erosion of rills**.


### Example (UP)

- Chambal region (UP-MP border)
- Ravines formed by gully erosion

---


# VALLEY DEVELOPMENT


Valleys develop gradually.


```plain text
Rills
 ↓
Gullies
 ↓
Deep valleys
```


Different valley types depend on:

- slope
- rock type
- erosion intensity

---


# TYPES OF VALLEYS


### 1. V-shaped Valley


Features

- steep sides
- narrow floor
- common in mountains

Example


Himalayan rivers


---


### 2. Gorge


Very **deep and narrow valley with steep sides**.


Example

- Kaveri river gorge (Tamil Nadu)
- Indus gorge (Ladakh)

(Page 3 image shows **Kaveri river gorge near Hogenakkal**)


---


### 3. Canyon


Similar to gorge but:

- **wider at top**
- **step-like sides**

Example


Colorado Canyon (USA)


---


# Differences: Gorge vs Canyon


| Feature   | Gorge       | Canyon            |
| --------- | ----------- | ----------------- |
| Width     | Narrow      | Wider             |
| Rock type | Hard rock   | Sedimentary rocks |
| Shape     | Steep sides | Step-like sides   |

_(Cycle of Erosion – William Morris Davis)_

```plain text

 ↓
Mature
 ↓
```

- slope gradient
- erosion vs deposition
- valley shape

# 2. YOUTH STAGE (EARLY STAGE)


### Key Characteristics


| Feature      | Explanation               |
| ------------ | ------------------------- |
| Slope        | Very steep                |
| River flow   | Fast                      |
| Erosion type | Vertical erosion dominant |
| Valleys      | V-shaped                  |
| Floodplain   | Absent or very narrow     |


Streams are **few and poorly integrated**.


---


### Landforms in Youth Stage

- V-shaped valleys
- Gorges
- Waterfalls
- Rapids

According to the **image on page 3**, steep valleys with narrow floors occur in this stage.


---


### Important Processes


```plain text
Vertical downcutting
↓
Deep valleys
↓
Waterfalls & rapids
```


---


### Example (India)


Himalayan rivers:

- Alaknanda
- Bhagirathi
- Teesta

These rivers show **strong vertical erosion**.


---


# 3. MATURE STAGE


### Key Characteristics


| Feature           | Explanation               |
| ----------------- | ------------------------- |
| River integration | Well developed            |
| Valley shape      | Still V-shaped but wider  |
| Floodplains       | Begin to develop          |
| Erosion type      | Lateral erosion increases |


Streams start **meandering** within valleys.


---


### Important Changes


```plain text
Reduced vertical erosion
↓
Increased lateral erosion
↓
Valley widening
```


---


### Landforms in Mature Stage

- meanders
- floodplains
- widened valleys

Waterfalls and rapids gradually **disappear**.


(Page text explains that streams flow in **meanders within broader valleys** during this stage.)


---


### Example


Middle course of rivers:

- Ganga
- Yamuna
- Narmada

---


# 4. OLD STAGE


### Key Characteristics


| Feature    | Explanation |
| ---------- | ----------- |
| Gradient   | Very gentle |
| Flow speed | Slow        |
| Erosion    | Minimal     |
| Deposition | Dominant    |


River flows across **wide floodplains**.


---


### Landforms in Old Stage

- oxbow lakes
- natural levees
- floodplains
- meanders

(Page description states rivers **meander freely over vast floodplains**.)

# VALLEYS


### Formation Process


```plain text
Overland flow
↓
Rills
↓
Gullies
↓
Valleys
```


Valleys deepen and widen over time.


---


### Types of Valleys


| Valley          | Features                  |
| --------------- | ------------------------- |
| V-shaped valley | steep slopes              |
| Gorge           | narrow deep valley        |
| Canyon          | wide at top narrow bottom |


These were discussed in Part 1.


---


# 6. POTHOLES


### Definition


Circular holes drilled into **river bed rocks** by rotating pebbles.


---


### Formation


```plain text
Pebbles trapped in depression
↓
Rotating movement by river flow
↓
Grinding of rock surface
↓
Circular holes (potholes)
```


---


### Key Points

- Common in **rocky river beds**
- Often found near **waterfalls**

---


# 7. PLUNGE POOLS


### Definition


Deep pools formed **at the base of waterfalls** due to strong erosion.


---


### Formation


```plain text
Waterfall falling from height
↓
High kinetic energy
↓
Rock erosion
↓
Deep circular basin (plunge pool)
```


---


### Example


Jog Falls (Karnataka)


---


# 8. WATERFALLS


### Definition


Vertical drop in river course due to **difference in rock hardness**.


---


### Formation Causes

1. Faulting
1. Hard rock layer
1. River rejuvenation

---


### Famous Examples


| Waterfall  | Location               |
| ---------- | ---------------------- |
| Jog Falls  | Karnataka              |
| Dudhsagar  | Goa                    |
| Hundru     | Jharkhand              |
| Chitrakoot | UP–Chhattisgarh border |


---


# 9. RAPIDS


### Definition


Sections of river with **very fast turbulent flow**.


Cause:

- steep gradient
- irregular river bed

Often occur **before waterfalls**.


---


# 10. DIFFERENCE: WATERFALL vs RAPIDS


| Feature           | Waterfall      | Rapids         |
| ----------------- | -------------- | -------------- |
| Flow              | Vertical drop  | Turbulent flow |
| Height difference | Large          | Small          |
| Formation         | Hard rock step | Irregular bed  |

# DEPOSITIONAL LANDFORMS OF RUNNING WATER (FLUVIAL LANDFORMS)

# 1. FLOODPLAINS


## Definition


A **floodplain** is a **flat low-lying area adjacent to a river channel formed by deposition of sediments during floods**.


---


## Formation Process


```plain text
River floods
↓
Water spreads over surrounding land
↓
Sediments deposited
↓
Formation of flat alluvial plain
```


---


## Key Characteristics


| Feature     | Description       |
| ----------- | ----------------- |
| Soil type   | Alluvial          |
| Slope       | Very gentle       |
| Fertility   | Extremely fertile |
| Agriculture | Highly productive |


---


## Example (India)


**Indo–Gangetic Plain**


Rivers involved:

- Ganga
- Yamuna
- Ghaghara
- Gandak

---


## Uttar Pradesh Example


UP lies in the **middle Ganga floodplain**, one of the most fertile agricultural regions in the world.


Important cities located on floodplains:

- Kanpur
- Prayagraj
- Varanasi

---


# 2. NATURAL LEVEES


## Definition


**Natural levees** are **raised embankments formed along river banks due to deposition of heavier sediments during floods**.


---


## Formation Process


```plain text
Flood occurs
↓
River overflows banks
↓
Heavy sediments deposited near banks
↓
Formation of raised ridges (levees)
```


---


## Characteristics


| Feature  | Description       |
| -------- | ----------------- |
| Location | Along river banks |
| Height   | Slightly elevated |
| Soil     | Coarse sediments  |


---


## Importance

- Protects floodplain from minor floods
- Natural embankment

---


## Example


Natural levees are common in:

- Mississippi river
- Ganga river plains

---


# 3. POINT BARS


## Definition


Sediment deposits formed **on the inner side of river bends**.


---


## Formation


```plain text
River meanders
↓
Velocity decreases at inner bend
↓
Sediments deposited
↓
Formation of point bars
```


---


## Key Concept

- Inner bend → deposition
- Outer bend → erosion

---


# 4. MEANDERS


## Definition


**Meanders are large bends in the river channel formed due to lateral erosion and deposition.**


---


## Formation Mechanism


```plain text
River flows across floodplain
↓
Velocity differences across channel
↓
Outer bank erosion
↓
Inner bank deposition
↓
Formation of bends (meanders)
```


---


## Important Terms


| Term      | Meaning                 |
| --------- | ----------------------- |
| Cut bank  | Outer eroded bank       |
| Point bar | Inner depositional bank |


---


## Example (India)

- Ganga river
- Yamuna river

These rivers show extensive meandering in **Uttar Pradesh plains**.


---


# 5. OXBOW LAKES


## Definition


A **crescent-shaped lake formed when a meander loop is cut off from the main river channel**.


---


## Formation Process


```plain text
River meanders grow larger
↓
Neck between bends narrows
↓
Flood cuts across neck
↓
Old loop separated
↓
Oxbow lake forms
```


---


## Characteristics


| Feature  | Description    |
| -------- | -------------- |
| Shape    | Crescent       |
| Location | Floodplains    |
| Water    | Often stagnant |


---


## Example (India)


Common in:

- Ganga plain
- Brahmaputra valley

---


# 6. YAZOO STREAMS


## Definition


A **tributary stream flowing parallel to the main river due to natural levees blocking direct entry**.


---


## Formation


```plain text
Natural levee forms
↓
Tributary cannot join main river
↓
Flows parallel for long distance
↓
Eventually joins downstream
```


---


## Example


Yazoo rivers occur in:

- Mississippi river basin
- Ganga floodplains

---


# 7. DELTAS


## Definition


A **delta is a depositional landform formed at the mouth of a river where it enters a sea or lake**.


---


## Formation Process


```plain text
River enters sea
↓
Velocity decreases
↓
Sediments deposited
↓
River splits into distributaries
↓
Delta formation
```


---


## Characteristics


| Feature   | Description    |
| --------- | -------------- |
| Shape     | Triangular     |
| Sediments | Alluvium       |
| Channels  | Distributaries |


---


## Types of Deltas


| Type              | Example     |
| ----------------- | ----------- |
| Arcuate delta     | Nile delta  |
| Bird’s foot delta | Mississippi |
| Cuspate delta     | Tiber       |


---


## Example in India


### Ganga–Brahmaputra Delta

- Largest delta in the world
- Located in **India and Bangladesh**

---


# 8. ALLUVIAL FANS


## Definition


Fan-shaped deposits formed when **a river emerges from mountains to plains**.


---


## Formation


```plain text
River flows in mountains
↓
Reaches plains
↓
Velocity suddenly decreases
↓
Sediments deposited in fan shape
```


---


## Example


Common along **Himalayan foothills**.


---


# 9. BRAIDED CHANNELS


## Definition


Channels divided into **multiple interweaving streams due to excessive sediment load**.


---


## Formation Causes

- high sediment load
- shallow channel
- variable discharge

---


## Example

- Brahmaputra river
- Himalayan rivers

---


# 10. RIVER REJUVENATION


## Definition


Renewed erosion in a river due to **increase in gradient or uplift of land**.


---


## Causes

1. Tectonic uplift
2. Sea level fall
3. Increased discharge

---


## Resulting Landforms

- river terraces
- incised meanders
- waterfalls

---


# 11. RIVER TERRACES


## Definition


Step-like benches along river valleys formed due to **river rejuvenation**.


---


## Formation


```plain text
River flows on floodplain
↓
Land uplift occurs
↓
River cuts downward
↓
Old floodplain left as terrace
```
