# PRELIMS PYQs (with Answers)


Format:


**Question — Exam — Year — Answer**


---

1. **Which geomorphic agent is dominant in humid regions?**

    — Union Public Service Commission Prelims — 2012


    ✅ **Answer:** Running water


---

1. **Which stage of river development is associated with V-shaped valleys?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2014


    ✅ **Answer:** Youth stage


---

1. **Meanders are formed mainly due to:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2016


    ✅ **Answer:** Lateral erosion and deposition


---

1. **Which landform is formed when a meander loop is cut off from the main river channel?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2017


    ✅ **Answer:** Oxbow lake


---

1. **Which landform is formed at the mouth of a river where it enters the sea?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2015


    ✅ **Answer:** Delta


---

1. **A fan-shaped deposit formed where a river leaves mountainous terrain is called:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2018


    ✅ **Answer:** Alluvial fan


---

1. **Which of the following forms due to deposition along river banks during floods?**

    — State PCS Prelims — 2019


    ✅ **Answer:** Natural levee


---

1. **A tributary stream flowing parallel to the main river due to natural levees is called:**

    — State PCS Prelims — 2020


    ✅ **Answer:** Yazoo stream


---

1. **Which river landform is formed by circular drilling in the river bed by pebbles?**

    — State PCS Prelims — 2021


    ✅ **Answer:** Pothole


---

1. **Which river landform is formed at the base of waterfalls due to erosion?**

    — State PCS Prelims — 2022


    ✅ **Answer:** Plunge pool

# Extra Important Questions (Frequently Asked Pattern)


These are **not always exact PYQs but examiners repeatedly frame questions like these**.


---


### High probability prelims questions


1️⃣ Which river stage forms **waterfalls and rapids**?


Answer → Youth stage


2️⃣ Which river stage forms **floodplains and oxbow lakes**?


Answer → Old stage


3️⃣ Which side of a meander experiences **erosion**?


Answer → Outer bank (cut bank)


4️⃣ Which side of a meander experiences **deposition**?


Answer → Inner bank (point bar)
