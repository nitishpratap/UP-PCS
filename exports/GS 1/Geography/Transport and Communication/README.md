
## 1. Concept of Transport and Communication


### Transport


**Definition**

- **Transport** is the movement of **goods, people, and services** from one place to another.

**Purpose**

- Connect **production centres → markets → consumers**

Examples

- Agricultural goods from **fields → markets**
- Factory goods → **urban centres**

### Communication


**Definition**

- Transmission of **ideas, information, and messages** between individuals or places.

Examples

- Internet
- Radio
- Television
- Satellite communication

### Transport + Communication together


They enable:


| Economic role      | Social role       | Strategic role    |
| ------------------ | ----------------- | ----------------- |
| Trade and industry | Cultural exchange | Defence mobility  |
| Market expansion   | Information flow  | Disaster response |

# 4. Road Transport in India


India has **one of the largest road networks in the world**.

- Total road length (2005): **33.1 lakh km**
- Carries:
    - **85% passenger traffic**
    - **70% freight traffic**

### Why roads are important

- Flexible
- Door-to-door service
- Suitable for **short distances**

# 5. Historical Development of Roads


### Ancient Period

- **Footpaths and unmetalled roads**

### Medieval Period

- **Sher Shah Suri built Shahi Road**

Route:



Today:

- **NH-1:** Delhi → Amritsar

# 16. Road Density in India


Definition:


```plain text
Road density = Length of roads per 100 sq km
```


National Average:

- **75.42 km per 100 sq km**

Variation:


| High Density    | Low Density     |
| --------------- | --------------- |
| Kerala          | Jammu & Kashmir |
| Northern plains | Rajasthan       |
| Southern states | North-East      |


Reason:


```plain text
Terrain
   ↓
Construction difficulty
   ↓
Road density
```


Plains → easy construction


Mountains → difficult

# 17. Rail Transport in India


## Importance


Indian Railways is one of the **largest railway networks in the world** and a major transport system in India.


### Functions

- Transport **passengers and freight**
- Promote **economic growth**
- Connect **different regions and cultures**

Mahatma Gandhi said railways **brought people of diverse cultures together** during the freedom struggle.


---


## History of Railways


| Year              | Development                                               |
| ----------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1853**          | First railway line: **Bombay → Thane (34 km)**            |
| Colonial period   | Railways developed mainly for **resource extraction**     |
| Post-independence | Expansion toward **national integration and development** |

# 22. Konkan Railway (Important Example)


Constructed: **1998**


Length: **760 km**


Route:


```plain text
Roha (Maharashtra) → Mangalore (Karnataka)
```


Engineering features:

- Crosses **146 rivers**
- Nearly **2000 bridges**
- **91 tunnels**

Asia’s longest railway tunnel (~6.5 km) located on this route.


States involved:

- Maharashtra
- Goa
- Karnataka

# 25. Inland Waterways


Before railways, waterways were **main transport system**.


### Present Situation

- Total navigable waterways: **14,500 km**
- Major rivers navigable: **3,700 km**

However:

- Only **2,000 km used regularly**
- Only **900 km canals navigable by mechanised vessels**

Reasons for decline


```plain text
Railway competition
+
Irrigation diversion
+
River sedimentation
```
