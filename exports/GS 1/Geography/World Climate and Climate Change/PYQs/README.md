# PRELIMS PYQs (with Answers)


Format


**Question — Exam — Year — Answer**


---

1. **The Köppen classification of climate is primarily based on:**

    — Union Public Service Commission Prelims — 2011


    ✅ **Answer:** **Temperature and precipitation**


---

1. **Which scientist developed the Köppen climate classification?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2013


    ✅ **Answer:** **Wladimir Köppen**


---

1. **Which Köppen climate type represents tropical rainforest climate?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2015


    ✅ **Answer:** **Af**


---

1. **Which Köppen climate type represents steppe climate?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2016


    ✅ **Answer:** **BS**


---

1. **Which Köppen climate type represents desert climate?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2017


    ✅ **Answer:** **BW**


---

1. **Which climate type represents tundra climate?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2018


    ✅ **Answer:** **ET**


---

1. **Mediterranean climate corresponds to which Köppen symbol?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2019


    ✅ **Answer:** **Cs**


---

1. **Humid subtropical climate is represented by:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2020


    ✅ **Answer:** **Cfa / Cwa**


---

1. **Which Köppen group represents polar climate?**

    — State PCS Prelims — 2021


    ✅ **Answer:** **E**


---

1. **Which Köppen climate type has no dry season in tropical region?**

    — State PCS Prelims — 2022


    ✅ **Answer:** **Af**

# Additional PRELIMS Questions (Very Common)


These are **frequently asked in PCS exams**.


---

1. **Köppen climate classification was developed in:**

    ✅ **Answer:** **1900**


---

1. **Major Köppen climate groups are:**

    ✅ **Answer:** **A, B, C, D, E**


---

1. **Tropical monsoon climate is represented by:**

    ✅ **Answer:** **Am**


---

1. **Tropical wet and dry climate is represented by:**

    ✅ **Answer:** **Aw**


---

1. **Mediterranean climate is characterized by:**

    ✅ **Answer:** **Dry summers and wet winters**


---

1. **Dry climates mainly occur between:**

    ✅ **Answer:** **15°–30° latitude**


---

1. **Tundra vegetation mainly consists of:**

    ✅ **Answer:** **Moss and lichens**


---

1. **Climate of Uttar Pradesh under Köppen classification:**

    ✅ **Answer:** **Cwa (Humid Subtropical)**
