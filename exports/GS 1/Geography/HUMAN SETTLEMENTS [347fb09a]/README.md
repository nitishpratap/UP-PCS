
# 1. Meaning of Human Settlement


## Definition


**Human Settlement:**


A place where **people live permanently or temporarily**.

- Houses may change
- Buildings may be rebuilt
- Functions may change
- But **settlement continues in time and space**

✔ Important Point


Some settlements are **temporary** (seasonal).


### Examples


| Type      | Example                                      |
| --------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Permanent | Village, town, city                          |
| Temporary | Nomadic camps, seasonal shepherd settlements |


---


## Why Study Human Settlements? (Important for Mains)


Human settlements reflect the **relationship between humans and environment**.


### They show:

- Population distribution
- Economic activities
- Cultural patterns
- Land use
- Urbanisation

**Mains Conclusion Line:**


Human settlements represent the **spatial organisation of human life and economic activities on Earth.**


---

# 3. Rural–Urban Dichotomy


The division between **rural and urban settlements**.


### Important Point


There is **no universal definition** for village or town.


Reason:

- Population criteria differ
- Economic structure differs

Example:

- A village in **India or China** may have more population than a **town in Europe**.

---


# 4. Difference Between Rural and Urban Settlements


| Basis                  | Rural Settlement             | Urban Settlement                |
| ---------------------- | ---------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| Main occupation        | Primary activities           | Secondary & tertiary activities |
| Examples of activities | Agriculture, fishing, mining | Industry, services, trade       |
| Population density     | Low                          | High                            |
| Infrastructure         | Limited                      | Developed                       |
| Lifestyle              | Traditional                  | Modern                          |


### Example from India


| Rural           | Urban           |
| --------------- | --------------- |
| Village in UP   | Delhi           |
| Farming village | Industrial city |


---


# 5. Suburbanisation


## Definition


**Suburbanisation:**


Movement of people from **congested urban areas to nearby suburbs**.


Reasons:

- Better quality of life
- Cleaner environment
- Lower population density

### Example


```plain text
City Center → Suburb → Daily commuting
```


Examples

- NCR region (Gurgaon, Noida)
- Mumbai suburbs

---


# 6. Criteria of Urban Settlement (India Census)


According to **Census of India 1991**, an urban settlement must have:


| Criteria              | Requirement                                     |
| --------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Population            | Minimum 5000                                    |
| Workforce             | 75% male workers in non-agricultural activities |
| Density               | At least 400 persons per sq km                  |
| Administrative status | Municipality / Corporation / Cantonment         |


(Source: NCERT page 92)

# 8. Compact (Nucleated) Settlement


### Definition


Houses are **closely built together**.


### Characteristics

- Dense settlement
- Community living
- Shared occupations

### Found in

- Fertile plains
- River valleys

### Example

- Indo-Gangetic plains
- Western UP villages

### Reason

- Fertile soil
- Water availability

---


# 9. Dispersed Settlement


### Definition


Houses are **far apart from each other**.


### Characteristics

- Scattered houses
- Surrounded by farmland

### Found in

- Hilly regions
- Forest areas

### Example

- Himalayas
- Tribal areas

(Source: NCERT page 92–93)

### 6. Cross-Shaped Settlement


Develops at **crossroads**.

# 16. Types of Urban Settlements by Size


```plain text
Urban Centres
   │
Town → City → Million City → Conurbation → Megalopolis
```


---


## 1. Town

- Smaller than city
- Limited functions
- Local importance

Example

- District towns

---


## 2. City


A city is a **large urban settlement with many economic functions**.


Characteristics

- Financial institutions
- Transport terminals
- Administrative offices

Example

- Lucknow
- Kanpur

---


## 3. Million City


City with **population > 1 million**.


Examples

- Delhi
- Mumbai
- Kolkata

---


## 4. Conurbation


Term coined by **Patrick Geddes (1915)**.


Definition


Large urban region formed by **merging of nearby cities**.


Examples

- Greater London
- Tokyo
- Chicago

### Indian Example

- Mumbai Metropolitan Region
- Delhi NCR

---


## 5. Megalopolis


Term popularised by **Jean Gottmann (1957)**.


Definition


Large region formed by **union of multiple conurbations**.


Best Example

- Boston → Washington corridor (USA)
