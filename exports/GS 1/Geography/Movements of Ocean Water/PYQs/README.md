# 1. PRELIMS PYQs (with Answers)


Format


**Question — Exam — Year — Answer**


---

1. **Ocean waves are primarily caused by:**

    — Union Public Service Commission Prelims — 2011


    ✅ **Answer:** **Wind**


---

1. **In ocean waves, water particles move in:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2013


    ✅ **Answer:** **Circular motion**


---

1. **Which of the following currents is a cold current?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2014


    ✅ **Answer:** **Peru (Humboldt) Current**


---

1. **The Gulf Stream is located in which ocean?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2015


    ✅ **Answer:** **Atlantic Ocean**


---

1. **Upwelling of ocean water is associated with:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2016


    ✅ **Answer:** **Rich fishing grounds**


---

1. **Which force deflects ocean currents on Earth?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2017


    ✅ **Answer:** **Coriolis Force**


---

1. **Spring tides occur during:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2018


    ✅ **Answer:** **New Moon and Full Moon**


---

1. **Neap tides occur when:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2019


    ✅ **Answer:** **Sun and Moon are at right angles**


---

1. **Which current warms Western Europe?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2020


    ✅ **Answer:** **Gulf Stream / North Atlantic Drift**


---

1. **Which area experiences the highest tides in the world?**

    — State PCS Prelims — 2021


    ✅ **Answer:** **Bay of Fundy**

# 3. Extra PYQs (Common PCS Pattern)


These are **very frequently asked in State PCS exams**.


---


### Prelims Style


1️⃣ Warm currents generally flow from


→ **Equator to poles**


2️⃣ Cold currents flow from


→ **Poles to equator**


3️⃣ The meeting of warm and cold currents leads to


→ **Rich fishing grounds**


4️⃣ Highest tides in India occur in


→ **Gulf of Khambhat**


5️⃣ Time gap between two high tides


→ **~12 hours 25 minutes**


---


### Mains Style


1️⃣ Explain **types of ocean water movements**.


2️⃣ Discuss **spring tides and neap tides**.


3️⃣ Explain **causes of ocean currents**.


4️⃣ Discuss **effects of ocean currents on climate**.


5️⃣ Explain **thermohaline circulation**.

# Minimal Mains Question Set (Ocean Movements)

1. **Explain the different types of ocean water movements and their causes.**
2. **Explain the mechanism of wave formation in oceans and describe the motion of water particles in waves.**
3. **Discuss the characteristics of ocean waves and the factors that determine wave size.**
4. **Explain the mechanism of tides and the forces responsible for tidal formation.**
5. **Differentiate between spring tides and neap tides with diagrams.**
6. **Discuss the factors influencing the height and occurrence of tides.**
7. **Explain the causes of ocean currents and the role of planetary winds and Coriolis force.**
8. **Differentiate between warm and cold ocean currents with suitable examples.**
9. **Discuss the impact of ocean currents on climate, fisheries, and coastal environments.**
10. **Explain the concept of upwelling and downwelling and their significance for marine ecosystems.**
11. **Describe thermohaline circulation and explain its role in global ocean circulation.**
12. **Explain the role of ocean currents and tides in navigation, fisheries, and coastal processes.**
