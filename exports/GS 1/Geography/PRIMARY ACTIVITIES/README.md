
_(Economic Geography – Human Activities)_


## 1. Economic Activities


**Definition**

- Activities performed by humans to **earn livelihood**.

### Classification of Economic Activities


| Type           | Description                     | Example             |
| -------------- | ------------------------------- | ------------------- |
| **Primary**    | Direct use of natural resources | Agriculture, mining |
| **Secondary**  | Processing raw materials        | Manufacturing       |
| **Tertiary**   | Services                        | Transport, banking  |
| **Quaternary** | Knowledge based services        | IT, research        |


**Primary Activities**

- Directly dependent on **natural environment**
- Use resources such as:
    - Land
    - Water
    - Vegetation
    - Minerals

### Examples

- Hunting & gathering
- Pastoralism
- Fishing
- Forestry
- Agriculture
- Mining

**Workers involved** → **Red Collar Workers**

# 2. HUNTING AND GATHERING


## Definition


Oldest human economic activity where people survive by **hunting animals and gathering plants**.


### Characteristics

- Primitive tools (stone, arrow)
- Very low technology
- Low productivity
- No surplus production
- Small population groups

### Regions where practiced

1. **High Latitude Regions**
    - Northern Canada
    - Northern Eurasia
    - Southern Chile
2. **Low Latitude Regions**
    - Amazon Basin
    - Tropical Africa
    - Northern Australia
    - Southeast Asia

## Definition

---


# (A) Nomadic Herding


## Definition


Pastoralists **move with livestock** in search of pasture and water.


### Features

- Primitive subsistence activity
- Animals provide:
    - Food
    - Clothing
    - Shelter
    - Transport

### Animals in Different Regions


| Region                 | Animals             |
| ---------------------- | ------------------- |
| Tropical Africa        | Cattle              |
| Sahara & Asian deserts | Camel, sheep, goats |
| Tibet & Andes          | Yak, llama          |
| Arctic                 | Reindeer            |


### Major Regions of Nomadic Herding

1. **North Africa → Central Asia → Mongolia**
2. **Eurasian Tundra**
3. **Southwest Africa & Madagascar**

## Transhumance


### Definition


Seasonal migration of livestock between **mountains and plains**.


Flow:


```plain text
Summer → Mountains
Winter → Plains
```


### India Example

- **Gujjars**
- **Bakarwals**
- **Gaddis**
- **Bhotiyas**

Region → **Himalayas**


---

Reasons:

1. Political boundaries
2. Settlement policies
3. Expansion of agriculture

# 5. SUBSISTENCE AGRICULTURE


## Definition


Agriculture where **production is mainly for local consumption**.


### Types


1️⃣ Primitive Subsistence Agriculture


2️⃣ Intensive Subsistence Agriculture


---


# (A) Primitive Subsistence Agriculture


Also called **Shifting Cultivation**


### Method


```plain text
Forest cleared
↓
Burn vegetation (slash & burn)
↓
Ash increases fertility
↓
Cultivation for 3-5 years
↓
Land abandoned
↓
New forest cleared
```


### Tools

- Stick
- Hoe

### Local Names


| Region           | Name   |
| ---------------- | ------ |
| North-East India | Jhum   |
| Central America  | Milpa  |
| Indonesia        | Ladang |

# (B) Intensive Subsistence Agriculture


## Definition


Agriculture with **high labour input on small land holdings**.


### Regions

- Monsoon Asia

Countries:

- India
- China
- Japan
- Korea

### Two Types


| Type               | Crops                  |
| ------------------ | ---------------------- |
| Wet Paddy Dominant | Rice                   |
| Non Paddy          | Wheat, barley, soybean |


### Features

- Small farms
- Family labour
- High yield per hectare
- Low labour productivity

### India Example


Rice → **Eastern India & UP Terai**

---


# Factors Affecting Mining


## Physical Factors

- Size of deposit
- Quality of ore
- Mode of occurrence

## Economic Factors

- Demand
- Technology
- Capital
- Labour
- Transport cost

---


# Methods of Mining


## 1. Surface Mining (Open Cast)


Features:

- Minerals near surface
- Cheapest method
- High output

## 2. Underground Mining (Shaft Mining)


Features:

- Deep minerals
- Vertical shafts
- Dangerous

Risks:

- Mine fires
- Gas explosions
- Flooding
