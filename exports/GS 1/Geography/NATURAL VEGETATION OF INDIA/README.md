
# 1. NATURAL VEGETATION


## Definition


**Natural Vegetation** refers to a **plant community that grows naturally without human interference** and adapts to the **local climate and soil conditions**.


Example:

- Forests
- Grasslands
- Shrubs

Natural vegetation is a plant community that has **grown naturally for a long time and adapted to climate and soil conditions**.


---


## Natural vs Planted Vegetation


| Feature           | Natural Vegetation    | Planted Vegetation     |
| ----------------- | --------------------- | ---------------------- |
| Origin            | Grows naturally       | Planted by humans      |
| Human involvement | No human intervention | Controlled cultivation |
| Biodiversity      | High                  | Low                    |
| Example           | Amazon forests        | Tea plantation         |


Example:

- **Natural vegetation:** Forests of Himalayas
- **Planted vegetation:** Tea plantations in Assam

# 4. TROPICAL EVERGREEN FORESTS


### Other Name

---


## Climatic Conditions


| Factor      | Condition  |
| ----------- | ---------- |
| Rainfall    | >200 cm    |

| Humidity    | Very high  |


---


## Distribution in India

- **North-Eastern states**
- **Andaman & Nicobar Islands**

(Shown clearly on the map on page 58 of the chapter.)


---

- Multi-layered structure
- Trees grow up to **60 meters**

### Structure of Evergreen Forest


```plain text

↑
Tall trees
↑
Middle canopy
↑
Shrubs & climbers
↑
Forest floor
```

✔ Trees **do not shed leaves simultaneously**


✔ Forest remains **green throughout the year**

# 6. BRITISH IMPACT ON INDIAN FORESTS


During the colonial period:


Major changes occurred:


1️⃣ Large-scale **commercial exploitation**


2️⃣ Forests cleared for **plantations**

- Tea
- Coffee
- Rubber

3️⃣ Oak forests replaced by **pine trees**


(for railway sleepers)


4️⃣ Timber used in **construction activities**


Hence forests changed from:


**Protection use → Commercial use**

# TROPICAL DECIDUOUS FORESTS (MONSOON FORESTS)


According to NCERT, **Tropical Deciduous Forests are the most widespread forests in India**.


---


# 1. TROPICAL DECIDUOUS FORESTS


## Definition


Forests where **trees shed leaves during the dry season** to conserve water.


### Why leaves fall?


Because of **water shortage in dry season**.


---


## Climatic Conditions


| Factor      | Condition             |
| ----------- | --------------------- |
| Rainfall    | **70–200 cm**         |
| Temperature | Warm tropical climate |
| Dry season  | Distinct dry period   |


---


## Distribution in India


Major regions:

- **Central India**
- **Northern Plains**
- **Peninsular Plateau**
- **Eastern Ghats**

Examples:

- Madhya Pradesh
- Uttar Pradesh
- Bihar
- Odisha
- Jharkhand

📌 These forests dominate large parts of **Uttar Pradesh plains**.


---

# MONTANE FORESTS (MOUNTAIN VEGETATION)


According to NCERT, **temperature decreases with increasing altitude**, which leads to **changes in natural vegetation in mountainous regions**.


---


# 1. WHY VEGETATION CHANGES WITH ALTITUDE


### Cause–Effect Chain


```plain text
Increase in altitude
        ↓
Temperature decreases
        ↓
Climate becomes colder
        ↓
Vegetation type changes
```


This phenomenon is called:


### **Altitudinal Zonation of Vegetation**


---


# 2. TYPES OF MONTANE FORESTS


| Type                     | Region        |
| ------------------------ | ------------- |
| Northern Montane Forests | Himalayas     |
| Southern Montane Forests | Western Ghats |

# 3. NORTHERN MONTANE FORESTS (HIMALAYAS)


Vegetation changes with **altitude levels**.


---


## (1) Foothills (1000–2000 m)


### Vegetation

- Subtropical forests

### Important Trees


| Tree     | Feature        |
| -------- | -------------- |
| Oak      | Hardwood       |
| Chestnut | Temperate tree |


These dominate in **Himalayan foothills**.


---


## (2) Middle Himalayas (1500–3000 m)


### Vegetation


**Coniferous forests**


---


### Important Trees


| Tree   | Use          |
| ------ | ------------ |
| Pine   | Timber       |
| Deodar | Construction |
| Cedar  | Durable wood |


📌 **Deodar** grows mainly in **Western Himalayas**.


---


## (3) Higher Himalayas (3000–4000 m)


### Vegetation

- Temperate grasslands
- Alpine forests

---


### Important Trees

- Silver fir
- Juniper
- Birch
- Rhododendron

These species occur between **3000–4000 meters altitude**.


---


## (4) Above 4000 m


### Vegetation


**Alpine pastures**


Used for **seasonal grazing**.


Tribal communities practicing **Transhumance**:


| Tribe     | Region          |
| --------- | --------------- |
| Gujjars   | Jammu & Kashmir |
| Bakarwals | Himalayas       |
| Bhotiyas  | Uttarakhand     |
