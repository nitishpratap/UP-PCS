
# 1. PRELIMS PYQs (Location of India)


**Question — Exam — Year — Answer**


---

1. **Which latitude divides India into tropical and subtropical regions?**

    — Union Public Service Commission Prelims — 2012


    ✅ **Answer:** **Tropic of Cancer (23°30′ N)**


---

1. **Indian Standard Time is based on which longitude?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2014


    ✅ **Answer:** **82°30′ E**


---

1. **The southernmost point of India (including islands) is:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2015


    ✅ **Answer:** **Indira Point**


---

1. **Which strait separates India and Sri Lanka?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2016


    ✅ **Answer:** **Palk Strait**


---

1. **Lakshadweep Islands are located in:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2017


    ✅ **Answer:** **Arabian Sea**


---

1. **Andaman and Nicobar Islands are located in:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2018


    ✅ **Answer:** **Bay of Bengal**


---

1. **Which of the following countries is not part of the Indian subcontinent?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2019


    ✅ **Answer:** **Myanmar**


---

1. **Which country shares the longest land boundary with India?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2020


    ✅ **Answer:** **Bangladesh**


---

1. **India ranks which position in the world in terms of area?**

    — State PCS Prelims — 2021


    ✅ **Answer:** **7th**


---

1. **Which line approximately divides India into tropical and subtropical climate zones?**

    — State PCS Prelims — 2022


    ✅ **Answer:** **Tropic of Cancer**

# 3. Additional Likely Questions (Common PCS Pattern)


These are **frequently asked in State PCS exams**.


---


### Prelims Style


1️⃣ Latitudinal extent of India


→ **8°4′ N – 37°6′ N**


2️⃣ Longitudinal extent of India


→ **68°7′ E – 97°25′ E**


3️⃣ Distance between two latitudes


→ **111 km**


4️⃣ Total coastline of India


→ **~7516 km**


5️⃣ Southernmost point of mainland India


→ **Kanyakumari**


---


### Mains Style


1️⃣ Explain the **concept of Indian subcontinent**.


2️⃣ Discuss the **importance of India’s central location in trade routes**.


3️⃣ Explain the **role of mountain passes in historical trade**.


4️⃣ Discuss the **importance of India’s coastline for economy**.
