
# 1. Population Composition


## Definition


**Population Composition** refers to the **structure of population based on various characteristics** such as:

- **Age**
- **Sex**
- **Place of residence**
- **Occupation**
- **Education**
- **Life expectancy**

These characteristics help in understanding:

- **Economic development**
- **Social conditions**
- **Human resource availability**
- **Planning and policy making**

### Example


India’s population composition helps policymakers determine:

- education planning
- employment planning
- healthcare planning

---


# 2. SEX COMPOSITION


## Definition


**Sex Ratio** = ratio between **number of females and males in population**


---


## Formula (Different Countries)


| Country Method | Formula                                    |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Many countries | Male Population / Female Population × 1000 |
| India          | Female Population / Male Population × 1000 |


### Indian Definition


**Sex Ratio = Number of females per 1000 males**


---


## Importance of Sex Ratio


Sex ratio indicates:

- **Status of women**
- **Gender equality**
- **Social discrimination**
- **Migration patterns**

Low sex ratio indicates:

- female foeticide
- female infanticide
- discrimination
- poor healthcare for women

---


## Important Concept


### Natural Advantage vs Social Disadvantage


Biologically:

- **Females live longer than males**
- **Higher survival rate**

But due to social issues:

- discrimination
- poor nutrition
- domestic violence

their advantage is reduced.


---


# WORLD SEX RATIO PATTERN


| Indicator               | Value                        |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| World average sex ratio | **990 females / 1000 males** |
| Highest sex ratio       | **Latvia – 1187**            |
| Lowest sex ratio        | **UAE – 468**                |


### Why UAE has low sex ratio?


Because of:


**Male labour migration**


Large number of male workers migrate to Gulf countries.


---


## Continental Pattern


### Low Sex Ratio Regions

- **Asia**
- India
- China
- Pakistan
- Afghanistan
- Saudi Arabia

Reasons:

- Gender discrimination
- Son preference

---


### High Sex Ratio Regions

- **Europe**
- Russia
- Baltic countries

Reasons:

- Higher female life expectancy
- Male deaths in wars
- Male out-migration historically

---


# 3. AGE STRUCTURE


## Definition


Age structure shows the **distribution of population among different age groups**.


It is a key indicator of:

- labour force
- dependency
- economic development

---


## Age Groups


| Age Group | Category             |
| --------- | -------------------- |
| 0–14      | Dependent population |
| 15–59     | Working population   |
| 60+       | Aged population      |


---


## Importance


### High working population (15–59)


→ Economic productivity


→ Demographic dividend


Example:


India currently has a **large working population**.


---


### High elderly population


→ High healthcare costs


→ Pension burden


Example:

- Japan
- Germany

---


### High young population


→ High birth rate


→ Future population growth


Example:

- Nigeria
- Pakistan
- Afghanistan

---


# 4. AGE–SEX PYRAMID


## Definition


A **population pyramid** represents:

- **Age structure**
- **Sex composition**

Graphically.


---


### Structure


| Side   | Represents        |
| ------ | ----------------- |
| Left   | Male population   |
| Right  | Female population |
| Bottom | Young population  |
| Top    | Old population    |

# 5. AGEING POPULATION


## Definition


**Population ageing** refers to:


Increase in proportion of **elderly population (60+)**


---


## Causes

1. Increased life expectancy
2. Improved healthcare
3. Declining birth rates

---


## Effects


| Positive              | Negative        |
| --------------------- | --------------- |
| Experienced workforce | Pension burden  |
| Skilled population    | Healthcare cost |
| Stability             | Labour shortage |


---


# 6. RURAL – URBAN COMPOSITION


## Definition


Population classified based on **place of residence**.


| Category | Characteristics        |
| -------- | ---------------------- |
| Rural    | Primary activities     |
| Urban    | Non-primary activities |


---


## Differences between Rural and Urban Areas


| Factor         | Rural       | Urban               |
| -------------- | ----------- | ------------------- |
| Occupation     | Agriculture | Industry & services |
| Density        | Low         | High                |
| Lifestyle      | Traditional | Modern              |
| Infrastructure | Limited     | Developed           |


---


# RURAL-URBAN SEX RATIO PATTERN


Important global pattern:


### Western Countries

- **More males in rural areas**
- **More females in cities**

Reason:

- mechanized farming
- women move to cities for jobs

Examples:

- USA
- Canada
- Finland

---


### Asian Countries

- **Urban areas male dominated**

Reason:

- male labour migration

Examples:

- India
- Pakistan
- Nepal

---


### Indian Context


Reasons for fewer women in cities:

- High living cost
- Housing shortage
- Safety concerns

---


# 7. LITERACY


## Definition


Literacy indicates:


**Percentage of people who can read and write with understanding**


---


### Indian Definition


In India, literacy refers to:


Population **above 7 years** who can:

- read
- write
- perform basic arithmetic

---


## Importance


Literacy indicates:

- economic development
- gender equality
- education level
- living standard

---


# 8. OCCUPATIONAL STRUCTURE


## Definition


Occupational structure shows the **distribution of working population across economic sectors**.


Working population generally includes **15–59 age group**.


---


## Economic Sectors


| Sector     | Activities                                |
| ---------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| Primary    | Agriculture, forestry, fishing, mining    |
| Secondary  | Manufacturing, industries                 |
| Tertiary   | Transport, trade, communication           |
| Quaternary | Research, information, knowledge services |


---


## Indicator of Economic Development


| Economy Type         | Occupational Pattern       |
| -------------------- | -------------------------- |
| Developing countries | High primary sector        |
| Developed countries  | High tertiary & quaternary |


---


### Example


| Country | Dominant Sector      |
| ------- | -------------------- |
| India   | Primary + tertiary   |
| USA     | Tertiary             |
| Japan   | Secondary + tertiary |
