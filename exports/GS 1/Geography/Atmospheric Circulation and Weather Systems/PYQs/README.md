# 1️⃣ PRELIMS PYQs (Atmosphere & Winds)


Format


**Question — Exam — Year — Answer**


---

1. **Wind always blows from:**

    — Union Public Service Commission Prelims — 2011


    ✅ **Answer:** **High pressure to low pressure**


---

1. **Coriolis force is caused by:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2013


    ✅ **Answer:** **Rotation of the Earth**


---

1. **Coriolis force deflects winds in Northern Hemisphere towards:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2014


    ✅ **Answer:** **Right**


---

1. **Which instrument is used to measure atmospheric pressure?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2015


    ✅ **Answer:** **Barometer**


---

1. **Lines joining places of equal atmospheric pressure are called:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2016


    ✅ **Answer:** **Isobars**


---

1. **Trade winds blow from:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2017


    ✅ **Answer:** **Subtropical high pressure to equatorial low pressure**


---

1. **Which of the following is associated with low pressure systems?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2018


    ✅ **Answer:** **Cyclones**


---

1. **Cyclones in Northern Hemisphere rotate in:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2019


    ✅ **Answer:** **Anticlockwise direction**


---

1. **Which pressure belt is also known as doldrums?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2020


    ✅ **Answer:** **Equatorial low pressure belt**


---

1. **Which wind is responsible for Western Disturbances in India?**

    — State PCS Prelims — 2021


    ✅ **Answer:** **Westerlies**

# 3️⃣ Additional High-Probability Questions (PCS Pattern)


### Prelims Style


1️⃣ Standard sea level pressure


→ **1013.25 mb**


2️⃣ Pressure decreases with altitude because


→ **Air density decreases**


3️⃣ Coriolis force at equator


→ **Zero**


4️⃣ Cyclones form beyond


→ **5° latitude**


5️⃣ Geostrophic wind flows


→ **Parallel to isobars**


---


### Mains Style


1️⃣ Explain **pressure belts of the Earth**.


2️⃣ Discuss **Coriolis force and its effects**.


3️⃣ Explain **three-cell model (Hadley, Ferrel, Polar)**.


4️⃣ Discuss **air masses and classification**.


5️⃣ Explain **types of fronts and associated weather**.
