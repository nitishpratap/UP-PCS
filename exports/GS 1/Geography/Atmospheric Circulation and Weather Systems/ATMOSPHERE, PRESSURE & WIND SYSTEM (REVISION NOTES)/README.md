# 1. ATMOSPHERIC CIRCULATION


### 🔹 Definition


**Atmospheric circulation** = Large-scale air movement redistributing heat from equator → poles


### 🔹 Flow Mechanism


```plain text
Unequal heating → Temperature difference → Pressure difference → Air movement → Wind
```


### 🔹 Importance

- Heat balance of Earth
- Moisture distribution
- Cloud formation & precipitation

---


# 2. ATMOSPHERIC PRESSURE


### 🔹 Definition


Pressure = **Weight of air column above unit area**


### 🔹 Units


| Unit | Use                    |
| ---- | ---------------------- |
| mb   | Traditional            |
| Pa   | SI                     |
| hPa  | Standard (Meteorology) |


### 🔹 Standard Pressure


```plain text
1013.25 mb / hPa
```


### 🔹 Measurement


| Instrument        | Type      |
| ----------------- | --------- |
| Mercury Barometer | Classical |
| Aneroid Barometer | Modern    |


---


# 3. VARIATION OF PRESSURE


### 🔹 With Altitude (Prelims)

- Pressure ↓ with height
- Approx: **1 mb / 10 m**

Reason → ↓ Air density


---


### 🔹 Vertical Distribution (Mains Concept)

- Rapid decrease near surface
- Non-uniform rate

⚠️ Important:


```plain text
Vertical pressure gradient > Horizontal
BUT balanced by gravity → No strong vertical winds
```


---


# 4. HORIZONTAL PRESSURE DISTRIBUTION


### 🔹 Isobars


**Isobars** = Lines joining equal pressure


📌 Interpretation:

- Close → Strong wind
- Far → Weak wind

---


# 5. PRESSURE SYSTEMS


| Type          | Feature     |
| ------------- | ----------- |
| Low Pressure  | Rising air  |
| High Pressure | Sinking air |


👉 Wind: High → Low


---


# 6. GLOBAL PRESSURE BELTS 🌍


| Latitude | Belt             | Feature        |
| -------- | ---------------- | -------------- |
| 0°       | Equatorial Low   | Rising air     |
| 30°      | Subtropical High | Descending     |
| 60°      | Subpolar Low     | Convergence    |
| 90°      | Polar High       | Cold dense air |


---


## 🔹 Key Belts


### 1. Equatorial Low (Doldrums)

- 5°N–5°S
- Rising air, heavy rainfall
- Calm winds

📍 Amazon, Congo, Indonesia


---


### 2. Subtropical High (Horse Latitudes)

- ~30°
- Dry descending air

📍 Deserts:

- Sahara
- Arabian
- Thar (👉 Important for UPPCS)

---


### 3. Subpolar Low

- ~60°
- Warm + cold air interaction
- Cyclonic storms

---


### 4. Polar High

- ~90°
- Cold dense sinking air

---


# 7. SEASONAL SHIFT OF PRESSURE BELTS


| Season      | Shift |
| ----------- | ----- |
| Summer (NH) | North |
| Winter (NH) | South |


👉 Causes **Monsoon**


---


# 8. JANUARY vs JULY PRESSURE


### ❄️ January (Winter)

- Land → High pressure (cold)
- Ocean → Low pressure

➡ Winds: Land → Ocean


---


### ☀️ July (Summer)

- Land → Low pressure (hot)
- Ocean → High pressure

➡ Winds: Ocean → Land → **Monsoon Rain**


---


# 9. INDIA / UP CONNECTION


### 🌧️ Monsoon Mechanism


```plain text
Low pressure (North India)
        +
High pressure (Indian Ocean)
        ↓
Moist winds → Rainfall
```


| Region     | Rain Source   |
| ---------- | ------------- |
| Eastern UP | Bay of Bengal |
| Western UP | Arabian Sea   |

# 🌬️ FORCES AFFECTING WIND


---


# 10. WIND


### 🔹 Definition


Wind = Horizontal movement of air (High → Low pressure)


---


## 🔹 3 Major Forces


| Force             | Role           |
| ----------------- | -------------- |
| Pressure Gradient | Initiates wind |
| Coriolis          | Deflects       |
| Friction          | Slows          |


---


# 11. PRESSURE GRADIENT FORCE (PGF)

- Rate of pressure change
- Wind ⟂ isobars

📌 Strong gradient → Strong wind


---


# 12. FRICTIONAL FORCE


| Surface | Effect        |
| ------- | ------------- |
| Land    | High friction |
| Ocean   | Low           |

- Acts up to 1–3 km
- Reduces speed & alters direction

---


# 13. CORIOLIS FORCE


### 🔹 Definition


Due to Earth’s rotation


### 🔹 Deflection


| Hemisphere | Direction |
| ---------- | --------- |
| NH         | Right     |
| SH         | Left      |


### 🔹 Variation

- 0 at equator
- Max at poles

---


### ❗ Cyclones not at Equator


```plain text
Coriolis = 0 → No rotation → No cyclone
```


---


# 14. GEOSTROPHIC WIND


Condition:


```plain text
PGF = Coriolis
```


👉 Wind flows **parallel to isobars**


👉 Occurs in upper atmosphere


---


# 15. CYCLONE vs ANTICYCLONE


| Feature      | Cyclone       | Anticyclone |
| ------------ | ------------- | ----------- |
| Pressure     | Low           | High        |
| Movement     | Converging    | Diverging   |
| NH Direction | Anticlockwise | Clockwise   |
| Weather      | Rainy         | Clear       |


---


# 16. CONVERGENCE vs DIVERGENCE


| Type        | Result              |
| ----------- | ------------------- |
| Convergence | Rising air → Rain   |
| Divergence  | Sinking air → Clear |

# 18. PLANETARY WINDS


| Wind             | Latitude |
| ---------------- | -------- |
| Trade Winds      | 0–30°    |
| Westerlies       | 30–60°   |
| Polar Easterlies | 60–90°   |


---


## 🔹 Trade Winds

- Subtropical High → Equatorial Low
- NE (NH), SE (SH)

👉 Form **SW Monsoon**


---


## 🔹 Westerlies

- 30° → 60°
- Variable

📌 Important:


👉 Bring **Western Disturbances**


---


## 🔹 Polar Easterlies

- Cold, dry winds
- Weak

# 20. ITCZ (VERY IMPORTANT)


### 🔹 Definition


Zone of trade wind convergence


### 🔹 Features

- Low pressure
- Heavy rainfall
- Thunderstorms

### 🔹 Role in India


```plain text
ITCZ shifts north → Monsoon trough → Rainfall
```

# 🌪️ AIR MASSES, FRONTS & CYCLONES


---


# 21. AIR MASS


### 🔹 Definition


Large air body with uniform temperature & humidity


---


### 🔹 Types


| Type | Meaning |
| ---- | ------- |
| c    | Dry     |
| m    | Moist   |
| T    | Warm    |
| P    | Cold    |


### 🔹 Major Types

- mT → Warm moist
- cT → Hot dry
- mP → Cold moist
- cP → Cold dry

---


# 22. FRONTS


### 🔹 Definition


Boundary between air masses


---


### 🔹 Types


| Type       | Feature         |
| ---------- | --------------- |
| Cold       | Heavy rain      |
| Warm       | Light rain      |
| Occluded   | Complex weather |
| Stationary | Cloudy          |


---


# 23. CYCLONES


### 🔹 Definition


Low pressure system with inward spiraling winds


---


## 🔹 Types


| Type      | Region  |
| --------- | ------- |
| Tropical  | 5°–20°  |
| Temperate | 30°–60° |


---


## 🔹 Tropical Cyclones


Conditions:

- Temp > 27°C
- High humidity
- Coriolis force

Structure:

- Eye
- Eyewall
- Rainbands

---


## 🔹 Mid-Latitude Cyclones

- Front-based
- Large size

👉 Cause **Western Disturbances**


---


# 24. ANTICYCLONES

- High pressure
- Clear weather
- Dry conditions
