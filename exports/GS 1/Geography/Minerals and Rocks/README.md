# 1. Elements of the Earth’s Crust


### Major Elements in Earth’s Crust


About **98% of the Earth’s crust** is composed of **8 major elements**.


| Element   | Symbol | Approx % |
| --------- | ------ | -------- |
| Oxygen    | O      | ~46%     |
| Silicon   | Si     | ~28%     |
| Aluminium | Al     | ~8%      |
| Iron      | Fe     | ~5%      |
| Calcium   | Ca     | ~3.6%    |
| Sodium    | Na     | ~2.8%    |
| Potassium | K      | ~2.6%    |
| Magnesium | Mg     | ~2.1%    |


Other elements present in smaller quantities:

- Titanium
- Hydrogen
- Phosphorus
- Sulphur
- Carbon
- Nickel

📌 **UPPCS Prelims Fact**


These 8 elements form the **major portion of lithosphere composition**.

# 2. Minerals


## Definition


A **mineral** is:

- A **naturally occurring substance**
- With **definite chemical composition**
- And **specific physical properties**
- Having **crystalline structure**

📌 Minerals are usually **formed by inorganic processes**.


Examples of **single-element minerals**

- Sulphur
- Copper
- Silver
- Gold
- Graphite

---


# 3. Source of Minerals


### Primary Source


➡ **Magma inside the Earth**


Process:


```plain text
Hot magma
     ↓ cooling
Mineral crystals form
     ↓
Solidification
     ↓
Formation of rocks
```


📌 Therefore **minerals are building blocks of rocks**.


---


# 4. Organic Mineral Resources


Some minerals originate from **organic substances**.


| Resource    | Form   |
| ----------- | ------ |
| Coal        | Solid  |
| Petroleum   | Liquid |
| Natural Gas | Gas    |


---


# 5. Rock Forming Minerals


Although thousands of minerals exist, most rocks consist mainly of **six major mineral groups**.


Examples mentioned in the chapter:

- Feldspar
- Quartz
- Pyroxene
- Amphibole
- Mica
- Olivine

📌 **Feldspar is the most abundant mineral in Earth’s crust.**


---


# 6. Physical Properties of Minerals


Minerals are identified by their **physical properties**.


---


## 1. Crystal Structure

- Minerals possess a **specific internal atomic arrangement**.
- Atoms are arranged in **geometric patterns**.

Examples

- Cubic
- Hexagonal
- Prismatic crystals

---


## 2. Cleavage


Definition:


➡ **Tendency of a mineral to break along smooth planes**


Cause:

- Due to **weak bonds between crystal planes**

Examples

- Mica → perfect cleavage
- Feldspar → two directions

If a mineral breaks irregularly → **fracture**


---


## 3. Lustre


Definition:


➡ **Appearance of a mineral when light reflects from its surface**


Types:


| Type              | Example       |
| ----------------- | ------------- |
| Metallic          | Gold          |
| Silky             | Asbestos      |
| Glassy (vitreous) | Quartz        |
| Dull              | Clay minerals |


---


## 4. Colour


Colour depends on:

- **Chemical composition**
- **Impurities**

Example:


Quartz may appear:

- White
- Red
- Yellow
- Green

because of impurities.


---


## 5. Streak


Definition:


➡ **Colour of mineral powder when rubbed on porcelain plate**


Important because:

- Streak may differ from mineral colour.

Example


| Mineral   | Colour | Streak |
| --------- | ------ | ------ |
| Malachite | Green  | Green  |
| Fluorite  | Purple | White  |


---


## 6. Transparency


Minerals classified into:


| Type        | Description                        |
| ----------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Transparent | Light passes clearly               |
| Translucent | Light passes but objects not clear |
| Opaque      | Light does not pass                |


Examples:

- Quartz → transparent
- Gypsum → translucent

---


## 7. Structure


Arrangement of crystals inside minerals.


Examples:

- Fibrous
- Granular
- Layered
- Radiating

---


## 8. Hardness


Resistance of mineral to **scratching**.


Measured by **Mohs Hardness Scale**.


### Mohs Hardness Scale


| Hardness | Mineral  |
| -------- | -------- |
| 1        | Talc     |
| 2        | Gypsum   |
| 3        | Calcite  |
| 4        | Fluorite |
| 5        | Apatite  |
| 6        | Feldspar |
| 7        | Quartz   |
| 8        | Topaz    |
| 9        | Corundum |
| 10       | Diamond  |


📌 Diamond = **Hardest natural mineral**


---


## 9. Specific Gravity


Definition:


➡ Ratio between weight of mineral and weight of equal volume of water.


Used to identify minerals.


Example:

- Metallic minerals → higher density
- Non-metallic minerals → lower density

---


# 7. Metallic and Non-Metallic Minerals


## Metallic Minerals


Contain metals.


### Types


| Type        | Example                 |
| ----------- | ----------------------- |
| Precious    | Gold, Silver, Platinum  |
| Ferrous     | Iron                    |
| Non-Ferrous | Copper, Lead, Zinc, Tin |


📌 Ferrous minerals form **base for iron and steel industry**.


---


## Non-Metallic Minerals


Do not contain metal.


Examples:

- Sulphur
- Phosphates
- Nitrates

Example mixture:

- **Cement**

---


# 8. Rocks


## Definition


A **rock** is:


➡ **An aggregate of one or more minerals**


Example:


| Rock    | Minerals                 |
| ------- | ------------------------ |
| Granite | Quartz + Feldspar + Mica |


---


### Important Rock Characteristics

- Hard or soft
- Different colours
- No fixed chemical composition

Examples:

- Granite → hard
- Soapstone → soft
- Gabbro → black
- Quartzite → milky white

---


# 9. Relationship of Rocks


Important relationships:


```plain text
Rocks → form soil
Rocks → shape landforms
Rocks → source of minerals
```


Thus geographers must study **rocks** to understand **geomorphology and soils**.


---


# 10. Classification of Rocks


Rocks are classified based on **mode of formation**.


### Three Major Types


| Rock Type   | Formation                        |
| ----------- | -------------------------------- |
| Igneous     | From magma or lava               |
| Sedimentary | From deposited sediments         |
| Metamorphic | Transformation of existing rocks |


---


# 11. Igneous Rocks


### Meaning


From Latin word **Ignis = Fire**


➡ Formed when **magma cools and solidifies**.


Therefore called **Primary Rocks**.


---


### Formation Process


```plain text
Magma
   ↓ cooling
Solidification
   ↓
Igneous Rocks
```


---


### Cooling Location


| Type      | Location     |
| --------- | ------------ |
| Intrusive | Inside Earth |
| Extrusive | Surface      |


---


### Texture


Depends on **cooling rate**


| Cooling Speed | Grain Size     |
| ------------- | -------------- |
| Slow          | Large crystals |
| Rapid         | Fine crystals  |


---


### Examples

- Granite
- Gabbro
- Basalt
- Pegmatite
- Volcanic breccia
- Tuff

---


# 12. Sedimentary Rocks


Word derived from Latin **sedimentum = settling**


---


### Formation Process


```plain text
Weathering
     ↓
Erosion
     ↓
Transportation
     ↓
Deposition
     ↓
Compaction
     ↓
Lithification
     ↓
Sedimentary Rock
```


📌 **Lithification = turning sediments into rock**


---


### Important Characteristics

- Layered structure (strata)
- Often contain **fossils**
- Formed on **Earth’s surface**

Examples

- Sandstone
- Shale
- Limestone

---


### Types of Sedimentary Rocks


| Type       | Formation             | Examples    |
| ---------- | --------------------- | ----------- |
| Mechanical | Rock fragments        | Sandstone   |
| Organic    | Plant/animal remains  | Coal, Chalk |
| Chemical   | Mineral precipitation | Halite      |


---


# 13. Metamorphic Rocks


Formed by **transformation of existing rocks**.


Causes:

- Heat
- Pressure
- Chemical reactions

Process called:


➡ **Metamorphism**


---


### Process


```plain text
Existing rock
     ↓ heat + pressure
Recrystallization
     ↓
Metamorphic Rock
```


---


### Types of Metamorphism


| Type    | Cause                             |
| ------- | --------------------------------- |
| Thermal | Heat from magma                   |
| Dynamic | Pressure during mountain building |


---


### Examples


| Original Rock | Metamorphic Rock |
| ------------- | ---------------- |
| Limestone     | Marble           |
| Sandstone     | Quartzite        |
| Shale         | Slate            |


---


# 14. Rock Cycle (Important Concept)


```plain text
Magma
  ↓
Igneous Rock
  ↓ weathering
Sediments
  ↓ compaction
Sedimentary Rock
  ↓ heat + pressure
Metamorphic Rock
  ↓ melting
Magma
```


📌 **Continuous cyclic process**.
