# PRELIMS PYQs (with Answers)


Format:


**Question — Exam — Year — Answer**


---

1. **Which element is most abundant in the Earth’s crust?**

    — Union Public Service Commission Prelims — 2011


    ✅ **Answer:** Oxygen (~46%)


---

1. **Which mineral is the most abundant in the Earth’s crust?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2013


    ✅ **Answer:** Feldspar


---

1. **Which mineral has hardness value 10 on Mohs scale?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2014


    ✅ **Answer:** Diamond


---

1. **Granite is composed mainly of which minerals?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2012


    ✅ **Answer:** Quartz, Feldspar and Mica


---

1. **Which type of rock is called the primary rock?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2015


    ✅ **Answer:** Igneous rock


---

1. **Which rock type commonly contains fossils?**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2016


    ✅ **Answer:** Sedimentary rock


---

1. **Metamorphic rocks are formed due to:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2017


    ✅ **Answer:** Heat and pressure acting on existing rocks


---

1. **Lithification refers to:**

    — UPSC Prelims — 2018


    ✅ **Answer:** Conversion of sediments into rock


---

1. **Which rock is formed from limestone after metamorphism?**

    — State PCS Prelims — 2019


    ✅ **Answer:** Marble


---

1. **Quartzite is formed from which rock?**

    — State PCS Prelims — 2020


    ✅ **Answer:** Sandstone
