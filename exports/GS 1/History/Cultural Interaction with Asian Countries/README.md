# 1. India's Early External Contacts


## Harappan Period


### Trade with Mesopotamia

- Indian traders visited Mesopotamian cities.
- Harappan seals found in Mesopotamia.
- Date: Second half of 3rd Millennium BC.

### Significance

- Earliest evidence of overseas trade.
- Shows maritime capability of Harappans.

---

### Outcomes

- Exchange of goods
- Spread of religions
- Spread of language & scripts
- Establishment of Indian settlements abroad

# A. Buddhism in Sri Lanka


## Ashokan Contribution


### Third Century BC

- Ashoka sent Buddhist missionaries to Sri Lanka.

### Evidence

- Brahmi inscriptions
- Date:
    - 2nd Century BC
    - 1st Century BC

### Impact

- Buddhism became permanently established.
- Sri Lanka emerged as a major Buddhist centre.

---


## Contribution of Sri Lanka


### Literary Contribution

- Compilation of Pali texts.
- Commentaries on Buddhist literature.

### Importance

- Many Buddhist texts preserved in Sri Lanka even after Buddhism declined in India.

---
