
# GUPTA AGE (c. 320–550 AD)


### Why Important?

- Called **"Golden Age of Ancient India"** (especially for culture, literature, science).
- Important for:
    - UPPCS Prelims
    - Ancient India Mains
    - Art & Culture
    - Society and Economy

# 1. SYSTEM OF ADMINISTRATION


## Nature of Kingship


### Gupta Kings adopted grand titles:


| Title            | Meaning           |
| ---------------- | ----------------- |
| Parameshvara     | Supreme Lord      |
| Maharajadhiraja  | King of Kings     |
| Paramabhattaraka | Supreme Sovereign |


### Features

- Monarchy was **hereditary**.
- Principle of **primogeniture (eldest son succeeds)** not firmly established.
- Succession disputes common.
- Nobles and officials often exploited these disputes.

### Religious Legitimization


**Brahmanas supported Gupta kings because of land grants.**


Flowchart:


```plain text
Land Grants to Brahmanas
          ↓
Brahmanas glorified kings
          ↓
King compared with Gods
          ↓
Royal authority strengthened
```


### Divine Association


| King             | Compared With      |
| ---------------- | ------------------ |
| Gupta King       | Vishnu (Protector) |
| Lakshmi on coins | Wife of Vishnu     |


---


# GUPTA MILITARY SYSTEM


## Important Changes


### Mauryan Army vs Gupta Army


| Maurya                | Gupta                   |
| --------------------- | ----------------------- |
| Chariots important    | Chariots declined       |
| Infantry dominant     | Cavalry dominant        |
| Horse archery limited | Horse archery important |


### Army Structure


```plain text
Standing Army
      +
Feudatory Forces
      =
Gupta Military Power
```


### Exam Point


**Horse Archery** became an important military tactic due to Central Asian influence.

# REVENUE SYSTEM


## Main Sources


### Land Revenue


Most important source.

- 1/6th to 1/4th of produce.

### Additional Burdens on Peasants


### When royal army passed:

- Food grains
- Animals

- Supplies

### For officials:


Villagers maintained:

- Royal officers
- Administrative staff

Women were now allowed to:


✔ Listen to Ramayana

✔ Listen to Puranas


✔ Worship Krishna

# NEGATIVE CHANGES

Women belonging to:

- Brahmanas

❌ No independent livelihood


❌ Limited property rights


❌ Dependence on male members


---


## Lower Varna Women


Women belonging to:

- Shudras

✔ Earn livelihood


✔ Participate in agriculture


✔ Work in domestic services


Thus they enjoyed relatively greater mobility.
