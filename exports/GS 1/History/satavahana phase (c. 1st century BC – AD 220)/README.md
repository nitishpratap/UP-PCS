### 1. introduction & historical position

- Satavahanas were the **most important native successors of the Mauryas in the Deccan and Central India**.
- In North India:
    - Mauryas → Shungas → Kanvas.
- In Deccan:
    - Mauryas → (gap of nearly 100 years) → Satavahanas.
- Generally identified with the **Andhras** mentioned in the Puranas.
- Important distinction:
    - Puranas mention **Andhra rule**.
    - Satavahana inscriptions mention **Satavahanas**, not Andhras.
- Therefore historians identify both as the same dynasty on the basis of evidence.

### exam point


| Source       | Name Used  |
| ------------ | ---------- |
| Puranas      | Andhra     |
| Inscriptions | Satavahana |

## 3. political history


### origin and early expansion

- Earliest inscriptions belong to **1st century BC**.
- Defeated the **Kanvas**.
- Established rule in Central India.

### earliest area of rule


Important fact:


❌ Not Andhra initially.


✔ First ruled in **North Maharashtra**.


Evidence:

- Earliest inscriptions.
- Earliest coins.

Main centre:

- Upper Godavari Valley.

---


### expansion


Gradually expanded over:

- Maharashtra
- Karnataka
- Andhra

---


### conflict with Shakas


Greatest rivals:

- Shakas (Western Kshatrapas)

Controlled:

- Upper Deccan
- Western India

At one stage:

- Shakas expelled Satavahanas from Maharashtra and Western India.

# gautamiputra satakarni (AD 106–130)


Most famous Satavahana ruler.


### titles

- Called himself:
    - "Ekabrahmana" (Only Brahmana)

### achievements


### against shakas

- Defeated Shakas.
- Destroyed many Kshatriya rulers.
- Ended Kshaharata line.

Main enemy:

- Nahapana.

### evidence


Near Nasik:

- 8000+ silver coins of Nahapana found.
- Re-struck by Gautamiputra.

This confirms victory.


### territorial extent


Controlled:

- Malwa
- Kathiawar
- Maharashtra
- Karnataka

Possibly exercised authority over Andhra.


### empire extent


North → Malwa


South → Karnataka


---


# vashishthiputra pulumayi (AD 130–154)


Immediate successor.


### significance


Coins and inscriptions found in Andhra.


Inference:


By mid-2nd century AD:


✔ Andhra became integral part of Satavahana kingdom.


### capital


Pratishthana (Paithan)


Location:

- Godavari Valley
- Present Aurangabad district (Maharashtra)

---


# rudradaman i (AD 130–150)


Shaka ruler of Saurashtra.


### achievements

- Defeated Satavahanas twice.

### important fact


Did not annihilate Satavahanas.


Reason:

- Matrimonial relations between both dynasties.

---


# yajna sri satakarni (AD 165–194)


Last great Satavahana king.


### achievements


Recovered:

- North Konkan
- Malwa

From Shakas.


### trade policy


Patron of:

- Trade
- Navigation
- Overseas commerce

### coin evidence


Coins found in:

- Andhra
- Maharashtra
- MP
- Gujarat

Ship depicted on coins.


### significance


Ship symbol proves:

- Maritime trade.
- Overseas commercial contacts.

---


### decline


After Yajna Sri:

- Weak rulers.
- Unable to maintain empire.

### end


Satavahana kingdom destroyed around:


### AD 220
