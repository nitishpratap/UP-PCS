## Bronze Age Urbanization in the Indus Valley


---


# 1. Introduction & Discovery of Harappan Civilization


The Harappan Civilization was the **first urban civilization of India** and belongs to the **Bronze Age**.


It is called:

- **Indus Valley Civilization (IVC)** → because it flourished around the Indus River.
- **Harappan Civilization** → because first discovered at Harappa.

# 7. Town Planning (VERY VERY IMPORTANT)


The Harappans had the **best town planning of the ancient world**.


## Features


### A. Citadel & Lower Town


Every city divided into:

1. **Citadel (Acropolis)**
    - Upper fortified area
    - Ruling class lived here
2. **Lower Town**
    - Common people lived here

---


### B. Grid Pattern


Roads intersected:

- At right angles

This formed:

- Grid system

---


### C. Drainage System


Most advanced feature.


Characteristics:

- Covered drains
- House drains connected to street drains
- Bathrooms in houses
- Wells in many houses
- Proper sanitation

➡ Harappans gave maximum importance to:

- Cleanliness
- Public health

---


### D. Burnt Bricks


Harappans extensively used:

- Baked/Burnt bricks

Unlike:

- Egypt (mainly dried bricks)

---


# 8. Great Bath (Most Famous Structure)

- Mohenjo-daro
