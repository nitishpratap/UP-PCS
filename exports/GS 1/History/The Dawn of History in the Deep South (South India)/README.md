
# 1. Introduction: Beginning of History in South India


## Indicators of Historical Age


A region enters the historical period when the following features appear:


| Feature                 | Significance           |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------- |
| Iron plough agriculture | Agricultural surplus   |
| Large rural settlements | Permanent habitation   |
| State formation         | Political organization |
| Social classes          | Stratified society     |
| Writing                 | Historical records     |
| Metal coinage           | Developed trade        |
| Written literature      | Cultural advancement   |


### South India Timeline

- These features appeared in **Tamizhakam (Tamil region)** only around **2nd Century BC**.
- Before that, South India was dominated by the **Megalithic Culture**.

---


# 2. Megalithic Culture of South India


## Meaning of Megalith


**Mega = Large**


**Lith = Stone**


➡ Megaliths = Large stone funerary structures.


### Why Important?


Megaliths are the chief archaeological source for understanding early South Indian society.


---


## Characteristics of Megalithic Culture


### Burial Practices

- Dead buried in graves surrounded by large stones.
- Graves contain:
    - Skeletons
    - Pottery
    - Iron objects
    - Weapons
    - Agricultural tools

### Belief System


Practice indicates belief in:


➡ **Life after death**


Therefore grave goods were buried with the deceased.

# 3. Regional Variations in Burial Practices


## Tamil Nadu


### Urn Burial

- Skeletons placed in large red pottery urns.
- Buried in pits.
- Often no stone circles.

---


## Krishna-Godavari Valley


### Cist/Pit Burials

- Stone circles around graves.
- More elaborate funerary structures.

---


## Comparison Table


| Feature       | Tamil Nadu | Krishna-Godavari |
| ------------- | ---------- | ---------------- |
| Burial Type   | Urn Burial | Cist Burial      |
| Stone Circles | Rare       | Common           |
| Grave Goods   | Less       | More             |
| Pottery Urns  | Common     | Less Important   |
