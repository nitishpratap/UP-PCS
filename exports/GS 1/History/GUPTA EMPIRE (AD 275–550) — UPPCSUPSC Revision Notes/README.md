# 2. HISTORICAL BACKGROUND


## Situation before Gupta Rise


### After Mauryas


```plain text
Maurya Empire Declined
          ↓
Satavahanas (Deccan)
          +
Kushans (North India)
          ↓
Both declined by mid-3rd century AD
          ↓
Political Vacuum
          ↓
Rise of Guptas (c. AD 275)
```

## Origin of Guptas


Most historians consider Guptas to be of **Vaishya origin**.


### Dharmashastra Titles


| Varna     | Title          |
| --------- | -------------- |
| Brahmana  | Sharma/Sharman |
| Kshatriya | Varman         |
| Vaishya   | Gupta          |
| Shudra    | Dasa           |


**Prelims Fact:** The title "Gupta" is traditionally associated with Vaishyas.

# 13. CAUSES OF DECLINE OF GUPTA EMPIRE


# Mains Favourite Topic


---


## 1. Huna Invasion


```plain text
Repeated Attacks
      ↓
Military Pressure
      ↓
Territorial Loss
```


---


## 2. Rise of Feudatories


Examples:

- Later Guptas (Magadha)
- Maukharis (Kanauj)
- Valabhi rulers (Gujarat)

```plain text
Feudatories Strong
         ↓
Central Authority Weak
         ↓
Political Fragmentation
```


---


## 3. Loss of Western India


Lost:

- Gujarat
- Saurashtra
- Western Malwa

### Result


Loss of trade revenue.


---


## 4. Land Grant System


```plain text
More Land Grants
        ↓
Reduced Tax Collection
        ↓
Less Revenue
        ↓
Weak Army
```


---


## 5. Decline of Foreign Trade


Evidence:


### Silk Weavers Guild Migration


AD 473


Gujarat → Malwa


Shows decline in silk demand.


---


## 6. Currency Debasement


Later Gupta rulers:


Reduced gold content in coins.


### Meaning


Economic crisis.
