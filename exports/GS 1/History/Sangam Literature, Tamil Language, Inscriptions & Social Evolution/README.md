
# 15. Sangam Age: Meaning and Background


## What is Sangam?


**Sangam = Assembly/Academy of Tamil Poets**


Traditionally believed to have been conducted under the patronage of:


➡ **Pandya Kings**


at


➡ **Madurai**


---


## Traditional Account


A later Tamil commentary (8th Century AD) claims:

- 3 Sangams existed
- Lasted 9990 years
- 8598 poets participated
- 197 Pandya kings patronized them

### Historical View


❌ Exaggerated


Historians accept only:


✔ One important Sangam assembly at Madurai.


---


# Why Sangam Literature is Important?


It is the most important source for understanding:

- Political history
- Society
- Economy
- Religion
- Trade
- Culture

of South India in the early historic period.


---

# 18. Social Evolution Reflected in Sangam Literature


One of the most important topics for UPPCS Mains.


---


## Stage 1: Pastoral Society


Earliest society described as:


✔ Cattle herding


✔ Hunting


✔ Fishing


✔ Limited agriculture


---


## Evidence


Sangam texts frequently mention:

- Cattle raids
- Tribal conflicts
- Heroic warfare

---


## Stage 2: Megalithic Background


### Archaeological Evidence


Megalithic sites contain:

- Hoes
- Sickles

but few ploughshares.


This indicates:


➡ Agriculture existed but was limited.


---


## Sangam Corroboration


Texts mention:

- Constant warfare
- Cattle wealth
- Heroic death

which matches megalithic evidence.


---


# Hero Stones (Virakkal)


## Concept


When a warrior died protecting:

- Cattle
- Tribe
- Kingdom

a memorial stone was erected.


Called:


➡ **Virakkal (Hero Stone)**


---


## Evolution


```plain text
Megalithic Stone Circles
          ↓
Memorial Tradition
          ↓
Virakkal (Hero Stones)
```


---


## Prelims Fact


**Virakkal = Hero stones erected in memory of fallen warriors.**
