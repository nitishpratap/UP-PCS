# UPPCS MAINS 10-MARKER CONCLUSION


**The period 200 BC–AD 250 represents the golden phase of ancient Indian crafts, commerce and urbanization. The expansion of guilds, flourishing Indo-Roman trade, widespread coinage and growth of manufacturing centres such as Mathura, Ujjain, Amaravati and Broach transformed India into one of the most prosperous economies of the ancient world. The decline of Kushan-Satavahana power and reduction of Roman trade in the 3rd century AD eventually led to urban contraction.**
