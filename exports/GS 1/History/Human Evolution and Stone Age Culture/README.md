# 1. Introduction: Evolution of Earth and Humans


## Age of Earth

- Earth is approximately **4600 million years old**.
- Evolution of earth’s crust passed through **4 stages**.
- The **4th stage = Quaternary Period**, divided into:
    1. **Pleistocene Epoch (Ice Age)** → 2 million BC – 12,000 BC
    2. **Holocene Epoch (Post-Ice Age)** → 12,000 BC to present

## Importance for UPPCS


Questions are frequently asked on:

- Pleistocene vs Holocene
- Ice Age
- Human evolution stages
- Stone Age chronology

---


# 2. Human Evolution


## Evolutionary Sequence


| Stage                | Period                    | Features      |
| -------------------- | ------------------------- | ------------- |
| Australopithecus     | 5.5–1.5 million years ago | Proto-human   |
| Homo habilis         | 2–1.5 million years ago   | Tool maker    |
| Homo erectus         | 1.8–1.6 million years ago | Fire user     |
| Homo sapiens         | 230,000–30,000 years ago  | Wise man      |
| Homo sapiens sapiens | From 115,000 years ago    | Modern humans |


---


# 3. Australopithecus (Southern Ape)


## Meaning

- Latin term meaning **“Southern Ape”**

## Time

- Around **5.5–1.5 million years ago**

## Features

- Bipedal (walked on two legs)
- Pot-bellied
- Small braincase:
    - Around **400 cubic cm**
- Had both:
    - Ape-like traits
    - Human-like traits

## Importance

- Last pre-human hominid
- Called **Proto-human**

### UPPCS Important Point


Australopithecus = transition stage between ape and human.


---


# 4. Homo habilis


## Meaning

- “Handy man” or “Skillful man”

## Time

- Around **2–1.5 million years ago**

## Features

- First real human
- Made stone tools
- Broke and sharpened stones
- Brain size:
    - **500–700 cubic cm**

## Importance

- First known tool maker

### PYQ Type Fact


Association of:

- Homo habilis → stone tools

---


# 5. Homo erectus


## Meaning

- “Upright man”

## Time

- Around **1.8–1.6 million years ago**

## Features

- Strong skull
- Brain size:
    - **800–1200 cubic cm**
- Used:
    - Hand axes
    - Cleavers

## Major Achievements


### 1. Discovery of Fire

- Used fire for:
    - Warmth
    - Protection
    - Cooking

### 2. Migration


Remains found in:

- Africa
- China
- South Asia
- Southeast Asia

## Importance

- First widespread human species

---


# 6. Homo sapiens


## Meaning

- “Wise man”

## Time

- Around **230,000–30,000 years ago**

## Associated With

- Neanderthal Man

## Neanderthal Features

- Short body
- Narrow forehead
- Brain size:
    - **1200–1800 cubic cm**

## Distribution


Found in:

- Germany
- Near East
- Europe

---


# 7. Homo sapiens sapiens (Modern Humans)


## Time

- Around **115,000 years ago**

## Origin

- Southern Africa

## Features

- Large forehead
- Thin bones
- Brain size:
    - **1200–2000 cubic cm**

## Achievements

- Developed advanced tools
- Better environmental adaptation
- Development of language

## Language Origin

- Earlier believed:
    - 35,000 BC
- Now believed:
    - Around **50,000 BC**

### Important Fact


Modern humans entered India:

- Through coastal migration from Africa
- Around **50,000 years ago**
- Entered from South, not North

---
