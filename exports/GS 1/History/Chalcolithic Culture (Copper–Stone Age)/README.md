# 1. Introduction to Chalcolithic Culture


## Meaning

- The word **Chalcolithic** comes from:
    - **Chalco = Copper**
    - **Lithic = Stone**
- Therefore, Chalcolithic culture means **Copper–Stone Age culture**.

## Main Feature

- People used:
    - **Copper tools**
    - Along with **stone tools**
- It marks the transition:
    - From **Neolithic Age**
    - Towards **Bronze Age / Iron Age**

## Important Distinction

- Harappans:
    - Urban civilization
    - Used **bronze extensively**
- Chalcolithic cultures:
    - Mostly **rural**
    - Used **stone + copper**
    - Less advanced technologically

# 4. Important Chalcolithic Cultures and Sites


# A. Ahar-Banas Culture (Rajasthan)


## Important Sites

- Ahar
- Gilund

## Location

- Banas Valley
- South-Eastern Rajasthan

## Time

- 2100–1500 BC

## Features


### Copper Usage

- Extensive use of copper
- Ahar originally called:
    - **Tambavati** = place of copper

### Tools Found

- Flat axes
- Bangles
- Copper sheets
- Bronze sheet

### Special Feature

- Unlike other Chalcolithic cultures:
    - Microliths almost absent
    - Stone blades rare

### Houses

- Stone houses

### Economy

- Agriculture + animal husbandry

### Importance of Gilund

- Regional centre of Ahar culture
- Burnt bricks found around 1500 BC

# 16. Decline of Chalcolithic Cultures


## Main Causes


### 1. Decline in Rainfall


Especially in:

- Western India
- Western MP

### 2. Primitive Agriculture

- Digging stick used
- No plough evidence
- No intensive farming

### 3. Limited Metal Technology

- Little bronze
- Very little iron

### 4. High Infant Mortality


Evidence:

- Many child burials

### 5. Lack of Urbanization

- Mostly rural settlements

---

# 20. Most Important One-Liners for UPPCS Revision

- Chalcolithic means Copper–Stone Age.
- Ahar culture = Banas Valley culture.
- Original name of Ahar = Tambavati.
- Gilund = regional centre of Ahar culture.
- Malwa ware = richest Chalcolithic pottery.
- Jorwe = most important Chalcolithic culture of Maharashtra.
- Inamgaon = fortified settlement with moat.
- Daimabad = largest Jorwe site.
- Ganeshwar supplied copper to Harappans.
- Black-and-Red Ware widely used from 2000 BC.
- OCP = Ochre-Coloured Pottery.
- Largest copper hoard = Gungeria (MP).
- Chalcolithic cultures mostly rural.
- Mother goddess worship prevalent.
- Bull cult found in Malwa and Rajasthan.
- Rice important in eastern India.
- Wheat and barley important in western India.
- Fish and rice were staple food in eastern India.
- Horse generally absent in Chalcolithic cultures.
- Chalcolithic phase in south India transformed into Megalithic culture.
- Chalcolithic people generally used digging sticks, not ploughs.
