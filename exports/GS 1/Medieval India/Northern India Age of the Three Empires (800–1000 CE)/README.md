
# 1. Introduction: Age of the Three Empires (800–1000 CE)


After the decline of Harsha's Empire (7th century CE), three major powers emerged:


| Empire            | Region                   |
| ----------------- | ------------------------ |
| Pala              | Eastern India            |
| Gurjara-Pratihara | Western & Northern India |
| Rashtrakuta       | Deccan                   |


### Importance

- None could establish complete control over the entire Ganga Valley like the Guptas and Harsha.
- Continuous struggle for supremacy known as **Tripartite Struggle**.
- These empires:
    - Extended agriculture.
    - Built tanks, ponds, canals.
    - Patronized art, literature and temples.
    - Provided political stability.

### Geographical Basis of Power


### North India


Control over:

- Ganga Valley
- Gujarat (ports & trade)
- Malwa
- Rajasthan

### South India


Control over:

- Krishna Delta
- Godavari Delta
- Kaveri Delta

---


# 2. Tripartite Struggle


### Meaning


Struggle among:

1. Palas
2. Pratiharas
3. Rashtrakutas

### Main Objective


Control of **Kanauj**


### Why Kanauj Important?

- Symbol of sovereignty after Harsha.
- Control of Upper Ganga Valley.
- Rich agricultural region.
- Strategic trade centre.

---


# 3. Pala Empire


## Founder: Gopala (c. 750 CE)


### Key Facts

- Elected king by local chiefs to end anarchy.
- Not from royal family.
- Father probably a soldier.
- Unified Bengal.
- Annexed Magadha (Bihar).

---


## Dharamapala (770–810 CE)


### Most Important Ruler


### Achievements

- Expanded kingdom.
- Entered Tripartite Struggle.
- Occupied Kanauj.
- Held grand darbar attended by:
    - Punjab rulers
    - Rajasthan rulers
    - Other feudatories

### Extent of Influence

- Punjab
- Malwa
- Berar
- North-west India

(Probably through suzerainty, not direct rule)


### Defeat

- Pratihara revival under Nagabhatta II.
- Defeated near Mongyr (Munger).

### Result

- Bihar and Eastern UP became contested zone.

---


## Devapala (810–850 CE approx.)


### Expansion


Conquered:

- Pragjyotishpur (Assam)
- Parts of Odisha
- Part of Nepal

### Importance


Peak of Pala power.


---


## Extent of Pala Rule


Dominated Eastern India:

- Mid 8th century → Mid 9th century

Controlled:

- Bengal
- Bihar
- Sometimes Varanasi

---


## Arab Account: Sulaiman


Visited India in 9th century.


### Observations


Called Pala kingdom:

- Ruhma/Dharma

Mentioned:

- Constant wars with Pratiharas & Rashtrakutas.
- Largest army among contemporaries.
- 50,000 elephants.
- 10,000–15,000 workers employed for army clothes.

---

### Personality

### Construction


Developed:

# 14. Indra III (915–927 CE)


Achievements:

- Restored Rashtrakuta power.
- Defeated Mahipala.
- Sacked Kanauj.

### Al-Masudi's View


Called Rashtrakuta king:

- Balhara/Vallabharaja

- Greatest king in India.

# 15. Krishna III (934–963 CE)


Last great ruler.


### Achievements


Defeated:

- Chola ruler Parantaka I (949 CE)

- Northern Chola territory.

Reached:

- Rameshwaram

Built:

- Victory pillar.
- Temple.

---


## End of Rashtrakutas


972 CE:

- Malkhed sacked and burnt.

Result:

- End of Rashtrakuta Empire.
