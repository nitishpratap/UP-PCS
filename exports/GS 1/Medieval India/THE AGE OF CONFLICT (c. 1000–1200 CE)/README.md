
# 1. Historical Background (1000–1200 CE)


## Developments in West & Central Asia


### Decline of Abbasid Caliphate

- By the **9th century**, Abbasid authority weakened.
- Rise of **Islamized Turkish military elites**.
- Turks initially:
    - Palace guards
    - Mercenary soldiers
    - Military commanders

### New Political Titles


| Title        | Meaning                     |
| ------------ | --------------------------- |
| Amir-ul-Umra | Commander of Commanders     |
| Amir         | Provincial military ruler   |
| Sultan       | Independent sovereign ruler |


### Reasons for Political Instability

- Continuous Turkish tribal invasions.
- Mercenary armies frequently changed loyalties.
- Sectarian conflicts among Muslims.
- Regional rivalries.

### Turkish Warfare


**Key Features**

- Lightning cavalry attacks.
- Rapid advance-retreat strategy.
- Surprise raids.
- Attack on isolated groups.

**Advantages**

- Superior horses.
- High mobility.
- Ability to cover vast distances.

# CAUSES OF RAJPUT DEFEAT


---


## NOT Because of Better Weapons


Indians possessed:

- Iron stirrup.
- Excellent swords.
- Deadly poisoned arrows.
- War elephants.

---


## Actual Reasons


### Political

- Disunity.
- Clan rivalries.
- No united resistance.

### Administrative

- Feudal fragmentation.
- Weak central authority.

### Military

- No permanent standing army.
- Poor coordination.
- Lack of mounted archers.
- Inferior cavalry organization.

### Strategic

- Failure to recover:
    - Kabul
    - Lahore

### Social

- Exclusive Rajput identity.
- Gap with common people.
