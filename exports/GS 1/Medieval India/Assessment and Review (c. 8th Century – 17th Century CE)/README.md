
# A. CHAPTER NOTES


## 1. Social Structure During Medieval India


### Continuity of the Caste System

- **Caste system remained the dominant social institution** throughout the medieval period.
- Continued despite:
    - Challenge posed by **Islam**
    - Decline of **Rajput political power**
    - Criticism by **Bhakti Saints** and **Nath Panthi Jogis**

### Why Caste Survived?


### Rajput Role

- Rajputs were protectors of **Dharma**
- Dharma included:
    - Maintenance of **Varna system**
    - Protection of **Varnashramadharma**

### Failure of Reform Movements

- Nath Panthi Jogis and Bhakti saints:
    - Criticized caste discrimination.
    - Could not destroy caste structure.

### Tacit Compromise


**Saints**


↓


Criticized caste in spiritual sphere


**Brahmans**


↓


Accepted Bhakti as path to salvation for all castes


Result:

- Religious equality increased.
- Social equality remained limited.

## 2. Position of Women


### Positive Developments


### Women Saints

- **Mira Bai**
- Other Bhakti traditions

Contributions:

- Opened path of Bhakti for women.
- Allowed women direct relationship with God.
- Challenged exclusive dependence on husband-centered duties.

### Negative Developments


### Overall Decline


Position of women worsened.


Major reasons:

- Expansion of **Purdah**
- Growth of patriarchal restrictions

### Hindu Women


Denied:

- Remarriage rights
- Share in father's property

### Muslim Women


Theoretically enjoyed:

- Inheritance rights
- Property rights

But:

- These rights increasingly curtailed in practice.

# 6. Mughal Ruling Class


## Composite Nobility


Objective:


Create a ruling elite consisting of:

- Muslims
- Hindus

### Success

- Rajputs integrated.

### Failure

- Marathas not fully integrated.

Reasons:

- Regional prejudice
- Social prejudice
- Marathas not recognized as traditional Kshatriyas.
- Lack of previous ruling-class status.

### Aurangzeb's Effort


Tried to recruit:

- Marathas
- Deccani nobles

Outcome:

- Deccanis integrated.
- Marathas remained outside core power structure.
