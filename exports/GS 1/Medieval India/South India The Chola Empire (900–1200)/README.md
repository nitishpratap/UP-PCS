
# 1. Background: Major South Indian Kingdoms


## Important Kingdoms (6th–8th Century)


| Kingdom   | Region             |
| --------- | ------------------ |
| Pallavas  | Tamil Nadu         |
| Pandyas   | Tamil Nadu         |
| Cheras    | Kerala             |
| Chalukyas | Maharashtra/Deccan |


### Key Facts

- Chalukya king **Pulakesin II** defeated **Harsha**, preventing expansion into Deccan.
- Pallavas and Pandyas possessed **strong navies**.
- Maintained trade, cultural and religious contacts with:
    - Southeast Asia
    - China
- Invaded and controlled parts of **Sri Lanka** for some time.

---


# 2. Rise of the Chola Empire


## Founder


### Vijayalaya Chola

- Initially a **feudatory of the Pallavas**.
- Captured **Tanjore (Thanjavur)** in **AD 850**.

### Expansion


By end of 9th century:

- Defeated **Pallavas of Kanchi (Tondaimandalam)**.
- Weakened the **Pandyas**.
- Brought most of southern Tamil region under control.

### Challenge from Rashtrakutas

- **Krishna III** defeated Cholas.
- Annexed northern Chola territory.
- Serious setback.

### Recovery

- After:
    - Death of Krishna III (965 CE)
    - Fall of Rashtrakuta Empire

Cholas re-emerged as dominant power.


---


# 3. Age of Rajaraja I and Rajendra I


## Rajaraja I (985–1014 CE)


### Military Achievements


### Against Cheras

- Destroyed Chera navy at **Trivandrum**.
- Attacked **Quilon (Kollam)**.

### Against Pandyas

- Conquered **Madurai**.
- Captured Pandyan king.

### Sri Lanka

- Invaded Sri Lanka.
- Annexed northern Sri Lanka.

### Karnataka

- Annexed northwestern Ganga territory.

### Andhra Region

- Overran **Vengi**.

### Maldives

- Conquered Maldives through naval expedition.

### Objective Behind Expansion

- Control trade routes to Southeast Asia.
- Dominate:
    - Coromandel Coast
    - Malabar Coast

---


## Rajendra I (1014–1044 CE)


### Background

- Appointed heir apparent during father's lifetime.
- Experienced in warfare and administration.

### Conquests


### Pandya & Chera Regions

- Completely overran both kingdoms.
- Incorporated them into Chola Empire.

### Sri Lanka

- Completed conquest.
- Captured:
    - Crown
    - Royal insignia
- Sri Lanka remained under Chola control for about **50 years**.

---


# 4. Temple Building & Historical Inscriptions


### Rajaraja and Rajendra's Contributions

- Built numerous:
    - Shiva temples
    - Vishnu temples

### Brihadishwara Temple

- Location: **Tanjore**
- Completed: **1010 CE**
- Most famous Chola temple.

### Historical Importance

- Cholas inscribed their achievements on temple walls.
- Provides detailed historical records.
- Major source of Chola history.

---


# 5. Rajendra's Ganga Expedition (1022 CE)


## Campaign

- Marched through **Kalinga** to **Bengal**.
- Crossed River Ganga.
- Defeated two local kings.

### Historical Significance

- Followed reverse route of **Samudragupta**.

### Title Assumed


**Gangaikondachola**

> "The Chola who conquered the Ganga"

### New Capital


**Gangaikondacholapuram**

- Near mouth of River Kaveri.

---


# 6. Chola Naval Expeditions to Southeast Asia


## Sri Vijaya Empire


### Extent


Included:

- Malay Peninsula
- Sumatra
- Java
- Nearby islands

### Importance

- Controlled trade route to China.

### Rulers

- Sailendra Dynasty
- Buddhists

### Chola-Sailendra Relations


Initially cordial.


### Example

- Sailendra ruler built Buddhist monastery at **Nagapatnam**.
- Rajendra granted village for its maintenance.

---


## Cause of Conflict


### Main Reason

- Chola desire to:
    - Remove obstacles faced by Indian traders.
    - Expand trade with China.

### Results


Conquered:

- Kadaram (Kedah)
- Several places in:
    - Malay Peninsula
    - Sumatra

### Outcome

- Chola navy became strongest in region.
- Bay of Bengal became known as:
> **"Chola Lake"**

# 8. Conflict with Later Chalukyas


## Later Chalukyas

- Successors of Rashtrakutas.
- Capital: **Kalyani**.

### Areas of Conflict

1. Vengi (Rayalaseema)
2. Tungabhadra Doab
3. Ganga territory of NW Karnataka

### Result

- No decisive victory.
- Exhausted both empires.

---


## Harsh Nature of Wars


### Chola Actions

- Sacked Kalyani and other cities.
- Massacred civilians:
    - Brahmans
    - Children

### In Pandya Region

- Established military colonies.

### Sri Lanka

- Destroyed ancient capital **Anuradhapura**.
- Treated king and queen harshly.

### Historical Assessment


These are considered:

> **"Blots on Chola history."**

## During 13th Century


Gradual decline.


### Successor States


### South India

- Pandyas
- Hoysalas

### Deccan

- Yadavas
- Kakatiyas

### Weakness

- Constant warfare among themselves.
- Sacking of cities and temples.

### End


Destroyed by:

- Delhi Sultanate rulers
- Early 14th century

# 13. Local Self-Government (Most Important Mains Topic)


## Village Assemblies


### Ur

- General village assembly.

### Sabha / Mahasabha

- Assembly in Brahmana villages (Agraharas).

---


## Agraharas


### Characteristics

- Brahman settlements.
- Mostly tax-free lands.
- High degree of autonomy.

---


## Executive Committee


### Selection

- Lottery (drawing lots)
- Rotation

### Qualifications

- Educated
- Property owners

### Tenure

- Retired every 3 years.

---


## Committees


### Functions


### Revenue Committee

- Assessment and collection of taxes.

### Law & Order Committee


### Judicial Committee


### Tank Committee


**Very Important**

- Managed irrigation water distribution.

---


## Powers of Mahasabha

- Distribute new lands.
- Exercise ownership rights.
- Raise village loans.
- Levy taxes.

### Significance


One of the finest systems of local self-government in medieval India.


### Limitation


Growth of feudalism reduced autonomy.

# 15. Dravida Temple Architecture


## Definition


Temple style dominant in South India.


### Key Feature


Multi-storeyed superstructure above sanctum.


---


## Components


### Garbhagriha

- Sanctum sanctorum.
- Main deity resides here.

### Vimana

- Tower above garbhagriha.
- Usually 5–7 storeys.

### Mandapa

- Pillared hall.
- Flat roof.
- Audience hall.

### Devadasis

- Women dedicated to temple service.
- Performed ceremonial dances.

### Pradakshina Path

- Passage around sanctum.

### Gopuram

- Monumental entrance gate.

### Courtyard

- Enclosed by high walls.

---


## Temple Functions


### Economic Role

- Revenue-free land grants.
- Received donations.

### Financial Role


Some temples:

- Lent money.
- Conducted business.

### Agricultural Role

- Dug tanks and wells.
- Constructed irrigation channels.
