# 1. Central Asia and Rise of Babur


## Political Situation in Central Asia (15th Century)


### Timur's Empire

- **Timur (Tamerlane)** reunited **Iran and Turan**.
- Empire extended from:
    - Lower Volga
    - Indus River
    - Asia Minor (Turkey)
    - Iran
    - Trans-Oxiana
    - Afghanistan
    - Part of Punjab

### Shahrukh Mirza (d. 1448)

- Grandson of Timur.
- Maintained much of Timurid Empire.
- Patron of:
    - Arts
    - Literature
- **Samarqand** and **Herat** became major cultural centres.

### Causes of Timurid Decline

- Tradition of **partitioning the empire** among heirs.
- Constant internal conflicts.

---


## New Powers in Central Asia


### Uzbeks

- Turko-Mongol tribe.
- Occupied Trans-Oxiana.
- Converted to Islam.
- Followed **Sunni Islam**.
- Led by **Shaibani Khan**.

### Safavids (Iran)

- Founded by descendants of saintly order.
- Followed **Shia Islam**.
- Persecuted Sunnis.
- Led by **Shah Ismail**.

### Ottoman Turks

- Expanding in:
    - Eastern Europe
    - Iraq
    - Iran

### Major Conflict


Three powerful empires emerged:


```plain text
Ottomans (West)
        ↓
Safavids (Iran)
        ↓
Uzbeks (Central Asia)
```


Political rivalry + Sectarian conflict (Sunni vs Shia).


---


# Babur's Early Career


## 1494

- Babur became ruler of **Farghana** at age 12.

### Attempts to Capture Samarqand

- Captured twice.
- Lost twice.

### Rise of Shaibani Khan

- Defeated Babur.
- Captured Samarqand.
- Overran Timurid kingdoms.

### 1504

- Babur conquered **Kabul**.

### Battle of Merv (1510)

- Shah Ismail defeated and killed Shaibani Khan.

### Babur's Third Attempt

- Regained Samarqand with Safavid help.
- Forced to act under Iranian generals.
- Uzbeks recovered.
- Babur expelled again.

### 1514

- Ottoman Sultan defeated Shah Ismail.
- Uzbeks became masters of Trans-Oxiana.

### Consequence


Babur abandoned dream of Central Asia and turned towards India.

# 2. Causes of Babur's Invasion of India


## Economic Causes


### Poverty of Kabul

- Kabul generated insufficient revenue.
- Could not maintain nobles and army.

### Wealth of India

- India known for:
    - Gold
    - Riches
    - Prosperity

---


## Strategic Causes

- Needed strong economic base.
- India could support future campaigns against Uzbeks.
- Fear of Uzbek attack on Kabul.

---


## Historical Claim

- Timur had annexed parts of Punjab.
- Babur considered these territories his hereditary right.

---


## Political Conditions in India


### Ibrahim Lodi (1517)

- Succeeded Sikandar Lodi.

### Problems

- Afghan nobles dissatisfied.
- Rajputs hostile.
- Daulat Khan Lodi nearly independent in Punjab.

# 3. First Battle of Panipat (20 April 1526)


## Opponents


### Babur

- Around 12,000 initially.

### Ibrahim Lodi

- Around 100,000 troops.
- 1000 elephants.

---


## Babur's Military Innovations


### Ottoman (Rumi) Method


```plain text
Carts tied together
        ↓
Breastworks between carts
        ↓
Guns positioned
        ↓
Trenches on flank
```


### Ottoman Gunners

- Ustad Ali
- Mustafa

---


## Tactics


### Tulughma System

- Flanking attacks.
- Side and rear assault.

### Combination


```plain text
Artillery
    +
Cavalry
    +
Mounted Archers
```


---


## Result

- Ibrahim Lodi killed.
- More than 15,000 soldiers killed.

---


## Significance


### Political

- End of Lodi rule.
- Delhi and Agra captured.

### Military

- Effective use of field artillery.

### Historical

- Beginning of Mughal Empire.

# PART C: SHER SHAH'S ADMINISTRATION


## Land Revenue System


### Features

- Measurement of land.
- Ray (crop schedule).
- State share = 1/3 produce.
- Patta issued.
- Cash preferred.

### Classification


```plain text
Land
│
├─ Good
├─ Middling
└─ Bad
```


---


## Military Reforms


### Dagh System

- Branding of horses.

### Chehra

- Descriptive roll of soldiers.

### Army

- 1,50,000 cavalry
- 25,000 infantry
- 5,000 elephants
- Artillery park

---


## Road Network


### Grand Trunk Road


Indus → Sonargaon


### Other Roads

- Agra–Jodhpur–Chittor
- Lahore–Multan

---


## Sarai System


### Features

- Every 2 kos (~8 km)
- Separate facilities for Hindus and Muslims

# PART D: MAINS ENRICHMENT


## Analytical Themes


### Why Babur Succeeded?

- Superior military technology.
- Effective artillery.
- Tulughma tactics.
- Weakness of Lodi state.
- Disunity among Afghans.
- Rajput-Afghan rivalry.

---


### Why Humayun Failed?

- Underestimated Sher Shah.
- Bengal misadventure.
- Weak coordination with brothers.
- Strategic mistakes at Chausa and Kanauj.
- Afghan revival under capable leadership.

---


### Why Sher Shah Important?

- Bridge between Sultanate and Mughal administration.
- Standardized revenue system.
- Improved communications.
- Strengthened internal trade.
- Administrative foundations later perfected by Akbar.
