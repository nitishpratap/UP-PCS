# FEUDALISM IN EUROPE


## Meaning


Derived from:

- Latin word: **Feudum**
- English word: **Fief**

---


## Structure


```plain text
King
 ↓
Feudal Lords
 ↓
Sub-vassals
 ↓
Peasants / Serfs
```


---


## Key Terms


### Fief


Land granted by king.


### Vassal


Person owing loyalty to superior lord.


### Aristocracy


Land-owning elite class.


---


## Features of Feudalism


### 1. Land Based Society

- Political power linked with land ownership

### 2. Hereditary Aristocracy

- Positions became hereditary

### 3. Decentralized Administration

- Local lords exercised power

### 4. Military Obligations

- Vassals provided troops
