
# 1. Historical Context (800–1200 CE)


### Key Characteristic

- Period shows both:
    - **Continuity** with earlier centuries.
    - **New socio-economic and religious developments.**

### Important Observation

- Economic, social and religious changes occur more slowly than political changes.
- Hence many earlier institutions survived.

# 2. Trade and Commerce


## Historiographical Debate


### View 1: Decline Thesis


Some historians argue:

- Decline of foreign trade.
- Decline of long-distance internal trade.
- Decline of towns.
- Rise of localism and regionalism.
- Absence of gold coins till 10th century.

### View 2: Continuity and Growth Thesis


Evidence suggests:

- Trade with West continued.
- New trading partners emerged.

### Major Trade Powers


| Period              | Trade Partner          |
| ------------------- | ---------------------- |
| After Roman decline | Byzantine Empire       |
| After Roman decline | Sassanid Empire (Iran) |
| After 7th century   | Arab Empire            |


---


## Why Trade Did Not Collapse


### Reasons

- Byzantine and Sassanid empires actively traded with India.
- Arabs expanded trade networks:
    - India
    - Southeast Asia
    - China

### Result

- Gold and silver continued entering India.
- India retained image as a wealthy land.

---


## Expansion of Agriculture and Trade


### Role of Rulers

- Wells
- Bunds

### Regions

- Bengal
- Sind
- Tamil country

### Method

- Brahmins granted rent-free lands.
- Tribes converted into settled agriculturists.

### Consequences

- Agricultural expansion.
- Strengthening of local chiefs.
- Growth of regional economies.

## Decline of Guilds in North India


### Guilds (Shreni/Sangha)


Functions:

- Trade organization.
- Lending and borrowing.
- Receiving endowments.
- Regulation of members.

### Causes of Decline

- Reduction in long-distance trade.
- Growth of local self-sufficient economies.

### Example


**Dvadasashreni**

- Earlier = Guild
- Later = Vaishya sub-caste

### Impact

- Weakening of merchant organizations.
- Decline of Jainism in North India.
