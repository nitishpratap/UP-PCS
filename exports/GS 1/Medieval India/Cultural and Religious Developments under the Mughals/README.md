
# Introduction

- Mughal period = **Second Classical Age of North India** after the **Gupta Age**.
- Marked by:
    - Flourishing of **Architecture**
    - **Painting**
    - **Literature**
    - **Music**
    - Development of **Composite Culture**
- Fusion of:
    - **Indian traditions**
    - **Turko-Iranian culture**
- Cultural centre before Mughals:
    - **Timurid Court of Samarqand**
- **Babur** consciously tried to introduce refined Central Asian cultural standards.
- Cultural development was possible because of earlier regional developments during the:
    - 14th century
    - 15th century
- Contribution came from:
    - Different regions
    - Different religions
    - Different races
- Result:
    - Emergence of a **Composite National Culture**

---


# MUGHAL ARCHITECTURE


## General Features


Mughals constructed:

- Forts
- Palaces
- Mosques
- Gates
- Public buildings
- Baolis (water tanks/wells)
- Gardens

### Distinctive Features

- Extensive use of **running water**
- Formal gardens (Charbagh tradition)
- Blend of:
    - Indian styles
    - Persian styles
    - Central Asian influences

---


## Babur's Contribution


### Gardens


Babur was extremely fond of gardens.


Built gardens around:

- Agra
- Lahore

Important surviving gardens:

- **Nishat Bagh (Kashmir)**
- **Shalimar Garden (Lahore)**
- **Pinjore Garden (Punjab foothills)**

---

### Architectural Importance


Acts as:

- Climax of Pre-Mughal architecture

Important Monuments:

## Akbar's Architecture


### Characteristics

- Massive construction activity
- Red sandstone widely used
- Blend of regional Indian styles

---


### Agra Fort


Features:

- Massive structure
- Magnificent gates
- Red sandstone

Influences from:

- Gwalior Fort
- Jodhpur Fort

---


### Fatehpur Sikri (1572–1580)


Location:

- 36 km from Agra

Built on:

- Hilltop
- Artificial lake

### Architectural Influences


### Gujarat Style


Features:

- Deep eaves
- Balconies
- Ornamental kiosks (chhatris)

### Bengal Style


Also visible in complex.


---


### Panch Mahal


Speciality:

- Built for fresh air and leisure

Features:

- Pillars from different temple traditions
- Flat roofs

---


### Rajput Palace

- Gujarat style dominant
- Probably built for Akbar's Rajput wives

---


### Persian/Central Asian Influence


Visible through:

- Glazed blue tiles
- Decorative roofing

---


### Buland Darwaza


Built to commemorate:

- Akbar's Gujarat Victory

Features:

- Lofty gateway
- Half-dome portal

### Half-Dome Portal


Origin:

- Iran

Importance:

- Became a major Mughal architectural feature later.

# Jahangir's Period


Important innovation:


### Marble Architecture


Beginning of:

- Complete marble construction

### Pietra Dura


Meaning:

- Floral decoration using semi-precious stones

# Shah Jahan: Zenith of Mughal Architecture


## Taj Mahal


Built using:

- White marble

Features:

- Pietra dura work
- Delicate marble screens
- Chhatris
- Massive dome
- Four minarets
- Formal garden setting

### Significance


Represents culmination of:

- All Mughal architectural developments

---


### Precursor of Taj Mahal


**Humayun's Tomb (Delhi)**


Features:

- Massive marble dome
- Double dome

---


### Double Dome


Purpose:

- Large outer dome
- Smaller inner dome

Advantage:

- Better proportions
- Greater monumentality

---


## Mosque Architecture under Shah Jahan


### Moti Masjid (Agra Fort)


Features:

- Entirely marble
- Elegant simplicity

### Jama Masjid (Delhi)


Features:

- Red sandstone
- Lofty gateway
- Slender minarets
- Multiple domes

- Provincial kingdoms
