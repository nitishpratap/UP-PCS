## 2. Land Revenue Systems Comparison

| Feature | Permanent Settlement (Zamindari) | Ryotwari System | Mahalwari System |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Year & Architect** | **1793** — Lord Cornwallis & Sir John Shore | **1820** — Sir Thomas Munro & Alexander Read | **1822** (Reg. VII) — Holt Mackenzie; modified 1833 by **Martin Bird** ("Father of land settlement in North India") |
| **Coverage Area** | Bengal, Bihar, Odisha, Northern Circars (Madras), Banaras (UP) | Madras, Bombay, parts of Assam, Coorg | Ganga Valley, North-West Frontier, Central Provinces, Punjab, Western UP |
| **% of British India** | **~19%** of British Indian territory | **~51%** of British Indian territory (Largest!) | **~30%** of British Indian territory |
| **Settlement Made With** | **Zamindars** recognized as hereditary absolute owners of land | **Ryots (Individual peasants)** directly | **Mahal / Village Community** collectively represented by Lambardar/Talukdar |
| **Revenue Share** | Fixed permanently; State = **10/11th**, Zamindar = **1/11th**; **Sunset Law** (1794) applied | Periodically revised (20–30 yrs); high rate (50–60% of gross produce) | Periodically revised; state claimed **66%** (later 50% under Bentinck 1833) of rental value |

## 3. Governor-Generals & Viceroys High-Yield Milestones

- **Warren Hastings (1772–1785)**:
  - Abolished Dual Government in Bengal (1772).
  - **Regulating Act of 1773**; became first **Governor-General of Bengal**.
  - Established **Supreme Court at Calcutta (1774)** (Sir Elijah Impey first Chief Justice).
  - Founded **Calcutta Madrasa (1781)**; Sir William Jones founded **Asiatic Society of Bengal (1784)**.
  - Charles Wilkins translated *Bhagavad Gita* into English (1785). Only Governor-General impeached in Britain (later acquitted).
- **Lord Cornwallis (1786–1793)**:
  - Introduced **Permanent Settlement of Bengal (1793)**.
  - Formulated **Cornwallis Code (1793)** based on separation of revenue and judicial powers.
  - Established District Thanas headed by Daroga; known as **"Father of Civil Services and Police in India"**.
  - Died at Ghazipur (Uttar Pradesh) in 1805 during second tenure; his **tomb is located at Ghazipur (UP)**.
- **Lord Wellesley (1798–1805)**:
  - Expanded empire via **Subsidiary Alliance System** (*Order of states: Hyderabad 1798 $\to$ Mysore 1799 $\to$ Tanjore 1799 $\to$ Awadh 1801 $\to$ Peshwa Baji Rao II 1802 $\to$ Bhonsle 1803 $\to$ Scindia 1804*).
  - Defeated Tipu Sultan in **Fourth Anglo-Mysore War (1799)**.
  - Founded **Fort William College (1800)** at Calcutta to train British civil servants in Indian languages.
  - Called himself the **"Bengal Tiger"**.
- **Lord Hastings (1813–1823)**:
  - **Anglo-Nepal War (1814–1816)**: Concluded by **Treaty of Sagauli (1816)** — Kumaon and Garhwal ceded to the British (**crucial UKPCS fact!**).
  - Crushed the Pindaris; defeated the Marathas in **Third Anglo-Maratha War (1817–1818)**; abolished Peshwaship.
- **Lord William Bentinck (1828–1835)**:
  - **Charter Act of 1833**: Became the **First Governor-General of India**.
  - **Abolition of Sati (Regulation XVII of 1829)** with active support of Raja Ram Mohan Roy.
  - Suppression of **Thuggee (1830)** led by **Colonel William Sleeman**.
  - **Macaulay’s Minute (1835)**: Made English the official medium of higher education in India (*Filtration Theory*).
- **Lord Dalhousie (1848–1856)**:
  - **Doctrine of Lapse** (*Satara 1848 $\to$ Jaitpur & Sambalpur 1849 $\to$ Baghat 1850 $\to$ Udaipur 1852 $\to$ Jhansi 1853 $\to$ Nagpur 1854*).
  - Annexed **Awadh in 1856** on grounds of "maladministration" based on **James Outram's report** (Nawab Wajid Ali Shah exiled).
  - **First Railway line (1853)**: Bombay to Thane (34 km).
  - **First Telegraph line (1853)**: Calcutta to Agra.
  - **Wood’s Despatch (1854)**: Magna Carta of English education in India.
  - **Post Office Act (1854)**: Uniform 2-paisa postal rate.
  - Drafted **Hindu Widows' Remarriage Act (passed 1856 as Act XV)** championed by Ishwar Chandra Vidyasagar.
- **Lord Canning (1856–1862)**:
  - Last Governor-General and **First Viceroy of India** (under **GOI Act 1858**).
  - Suppressed the **Revolt of 1857**; read Queen Victoria's Proclamation at **Allahabad Durbar on 1 Nov 1858**.
  - Introduced the **Portfolio System** (Indian Councils Act 1861); enacted **Indian Penal Code (IPC 1860)**.
- **Lord Lytton (1876–1880)**:
  - **Vernacular Press Act IX of 1878** (*Gagging Act* — aimed directly at Shishir Kumar Ghosh's *Amrita Bazar Patrika*).
  - **Arms Act of 1878** (prevented Indians from possessing weapons without license).
  - Held grand **Delhi Durbar of 1877** during severe Madras famine; proclaimed Queen Victoria as *Kaiser-i-Hind*.
  - Lowered the maximum age limit for ICS examination from **21 to 19 years**.
- **Lord Ripon (1880–1884)** (*The Good Viceroy*):
  - Repealed the infamous **Vernacular Press Act (1882)**.
  - **Local Self-Government Resolution (1882)** $\to$ **"Father of Local Self-Government in India"**.
  - **First Regular Census of India (1881)** (conducted under Census Commissioner W.W. Plowden).
  - **First Factory Act (1881)** (restricted child labour below 7 years).
  - Appointed **Hunter Commission on Education (1882)**.
  - **Ilbert Bill Controversy (1883)**: Proposed allowing Indian judges to try European offenders; triggered racist Anglo-Indian agitation.
- **Lord Curzon (1899–1905)**:
  - **Partition of Bengal**: Announced on **20 July 1905**, came into effect on **16 October 1905** (day observed as *National Mourning & Raksha Bandhan*).
  - **Ancient Monuments Preservation Act (1904)**; established Archaeological Survey of India (ASI) under Sir John Marshall.
  - **Indian Universities Act (1904)** (based on Thomas Raleigh Commission 1902).
  - Formed **Police Commission (1902)** under Sir Andrew Fraser.

## 8. Master Chronological Ladder of the Freedom Movement (1905–1947)

*Memorize this unbroken chain — tests 2 to 4 questions in EVERY UPPSC / UKPCS Prelims paper!*

1. **1905 (16 Oct)**: **Partition of Bengal** comes into effect $\to$ Swadeshi Movement peaks.
2. **1906 (30 Dec)**: **All-India Muslim League** founded at Dacca by Nawab Salimullah & Aga Khan.
3. **1907 (Dec)**: **Surat Split** between Moderates and Extremists.
4. **1908**: Khudiram Bose hanged (Muzaffarpur conspiracy); Tilak sentenced to 6 years at Mandalay.
5. **1909**: **Morley-Minto Reforms (Indian Councils Act 1909)** introduces separate electorates for Muslims.
6. **1911 (Dec)**: **Delhi Durbar**: Annulment of Partition of Bengal; Capital shifted from Calcutta to Delhi.
7. **1913**: **Ghadar Party** formed in San Francisco by **Lala Har Dayal** and **Sohan Singh Bhakna**.
8. **1914**: **Komagata Maru** incident (steamer turned back from Vancouver; Budge Budge firing).
9. **1915 (9 Jan)**: **Mahatma Gandhi returns to India** from South Africa (*Pravasi Bharatiya Divas*).
10. **1916 (Apr / Sep)**: **Home Rule League** launched by **Bal Gangadhar Tilak** (Apr, Pune) and **Annie Besant** (Sep, Madras); **Lucknow Pact (Dec 1916)**.
11. **1917**: **Champaran Satyagraha** (First Civil Disobedience).
12. **1918**: **Ahmedabad Mill Strike** (First hunger strike) & **Kheda Satyagraha** (First non-cooperation).
13. **1919 (Mar)**: **Rowlatt Act** (*Black Act* — detention without trial).
14. **1919 (13 Apr)**: **Jallianwala Bagh Massacre** (Amritsar, General Dyer firing; Tagore renounces Knighthood).
15. **1919 (Dec)**: **Montagu-Chelmsford Reforms (GOI Act 1919)** introduces Dyarchy in Provinces.
16. **1920 (1 Aug)**: **Non-Cooperation Movement launched**; Bal Gangadhar Tilak passes away.
17. **1922 (4 Feb)**: **Chauri Chaura Incident** (Gorakhpur, UP; 22 policemen burnt alive in police station) $\to$ Gandhi calls off NCM on **12 Feb 1922** at Bardoli.
18. **1923 (1 Jan)**: **Swaraj Party** formed by **Chitta Ranjan Das** (President) and **Motilal Nehru** (Secretary).
19. **1924 (Oct)**: **Hindustan Republican Association (HRA)** formed at Kanpur by Sachindra Sanyal, Ram Prasad Bismil, and Jogesh Chatterjee.
20. **1925 (9 Aug)**: **Kakori Train Action** (Kakori near Lucknow; 8-Down train looted; Bismil, Ashfaqulla Khan, Roshan Singh, Rajendra Lahiri hanged).
21. **1927 (Nov)**: **Simon Commission appointed** (All-white, 7 MPs; arrived in Bombay in **Feb 1928** $\to$ "Simon Go Back").
22. **1928 (Aug)**: **Nehru Report** (Chaired by Motilal Nehru; demanded Dominion Status, rejected separate electorates).
23. **1928 (Sep)**: **HSRA** (Hindustan Socialist Republican Association) founded at Feroz Shah Kotla, Delhi under **Chandrashekhar Azad** & **Bhagat Singh**.
24. **1928 (17 Dec)**: Saunders assassinated at Lahore to avenge the death of Lala Lajpat Rai.
25. **1929 (8 Apr)**: **Central Legislative Assembly Bombing** by Bhagat Singh and Batukeshwar Dutt ("To make the deaf hear").
26. **1929 (13 Sep)**: **Jatin Das dies** after 63-day hunger strike in Lahore Central Jail.
27. **1929 (Dec)**: **Lahore Congress Session** $\to$ Purna Swaraj adopted.
28. **1930 (26 Jan)**: First Independence Day celebrated nationwide.
29. **1930 (12 Mar – 6 Apr)**: **Dandi March** (Sabarmati to Dandi, 241 miles, 78 followers; broken salt law on 6 April) $\to$ **Civil Disobedience Movement (CDM)** begins.
30. **1930 (Nov) – 1931 (Jan)**: **First Round Table Conference** (London; Congress boycotted).
31. **1931 (5 Mar)**: **Gandhi-Irwin Pact** (Delhi Pact; suspension of CDM, Congress agreed to attend 2nd RTC).
32. **1931 (23 Mar)**: **Bhagat Singh, Sukhdev, and Rajguru executed** at Lahore Central Jail.
33. **1931 (Sep – Dec)**: **Second Round Table Conference** (Gandhi attended as sole Congress representative; deadlock on minority seats).
34. **1932 (16 Aug)**: **Communal Award** announced by British PM Ramsay MacDonald (separate electorates for Depressed Classes).
35. **1932 (24 Sep)**: **Poona Pact** signed between Dr. B.R. Ambedkar and Madan Mohan Malaviya (on behalf of fasting Gandhi in Yerwada jail; abandoned separate electorates, increased reserved seats from 71 to 148).
36. **1932 (Nov – Dec)**: **Third Round Table Conference** (Congress boycotted).
37. **1935 (Aug)**: **Government of India Act 1935** (Abolished Provincial Dyarchy, introduced Provincial Autonomy; All-India Federation proposed).
38. **1937 (Feb)**: Provincial Elections held; Congress formed ministries in 8 provinces.
39. **1939 (22 Oct)**: Congress ministries **resign en masse** in protest against Viceroy Linlithgow declaring India at war without consent $\to$ Muslim League celebrates **"Day of Deliverance"** on **22 Dec 1939**.
40. **1940 (Mar)**: **Lahore Resolution** of Muslim League (demanding independent Muslim states).
41. **1940 (8 Aug)**: **August Offer** by Viceroy Linlithgow (Dominion status post-war, veto to minorities; rejected).
42. **1940 (Oct)**: **Individual Satyagraha** launched (*Delhi Chalo* Satyagraha; **1st: Vinoba Bhave, 2nd: Jawaharlal Nehru, 3rd: Brahma Dutt**).
43. **1942 (Mar)**: **Cripps Mission** arrives (Stafford Cripps; post-dated cheque on a crashing bank).
44. **1942 (8 Aug)**: **Quit India Resolution** passed at Gowalia Tank Maidan (August Kranti Maidan), Bombay; Gandhi's call: **"Do or Die"**.
45. **1942 (9 Aug)**: **Operation Zero Hour**: British arrest all top leaders; underground movement led by Aruna Asaf Ali, Jayaprakash Narayan, Ram Manohar Lohia, and Usha Mehta (Secret Congress Radio); parallel governments formed (**Ballia** under Chittu Pandey, **Tamluk** Jatiya Sarkar, **Satara** Prati Sarkar under Nana Patil).
46. **1944**: **C.R. Formula** (Rajagopalachari Formula for Congress-League cooperation).
47. **1945 (Jun)**: **Wavell Plan & Simla Conference** (Breakdown over League's demand to nominate all Muslim members).
48. **1945 (Nov)**: **INA Trials (Red Fort Trials)**: Bhulabhai Desai, Tej Bahadur Sapru, K.N. Katju, and J.L. Nehru defend Prem Sahgal, Gurbaksh Dhillon, and Shah Nawaz Khan.
49. **1946 (18 Feb)**: **Royal Indian Navy (RIN) Mutiny** on HMIS Talwar in Bombay.
50. **1946 (24 Mar)**: **Cabinet Mission** arrives (Lord Pethick-Lawrence, Sir Stafford Cripps, A.V. Alexander; rejected Pakistan; proposed Constituent Assembly).
51. **1946 (16 Aug)**: Muslim League launches **"Direct Action Day"** (Great Calcutta Killings).
52. **1946 (2 Sep)**: **Interim Government** sworn in under Jawaharlal Nehru (Muslim League joined later on 26 Oct).
53. **1946 (9 Dec)**: **First meeting of the Constituent Assembly** (Dr. Sachchidananda Sinha temporary president).
54. **1947 (20 Feb)**: Clement Attlee's declaration: British will leave India before **June 1948**.
55. **1947 (3 Jun)**: **Mountbatten Plan / June 3rd Plan** (Partition plan accepted by Congress and League).
56. **1947 (18 Jul)**: **Indian Independence Act 1947** receives British Royal Assent.
57. **1947 (15 Aug)**: India attains **Independence**.

## 10. Anglo Wars & Treaties (Cause is expansion; result is annexation)

### A. Bengal turning points
| Event | Year | Result |
|---|---|---|
| **Farrukhsiyar’s farman** | **1717** | Duty-free trade (*dastak*); later private misuse fed Plassey politics |
| **Plassey** | **23 June 1757** | Clive + Mir Jafar conspiracy; puppet Nawab; **no Diwani yet** |
| **Buxar** | **22 Oct 1764** | Hector Munro beat Mir Qasim + Shuja-ud-Daula + Shah Alam II |
| **Allahabad** | **Aug 1765** | **Diwani** of Bengal, Bihar, Orissa to the Company |
| **Dual Government** | **1765–1772** | Company took revenue; Nawab kept nizamat in name; ended by **Warren Hastings (1772)** |

### B. Four Anglo-Mysore Wars
| War | Years | GG / Governor | Treaty / result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1st | 1767–69 | Verelst | **Treaty of Madras (1769)** — Haidar Ali forced a defensive alliance |
| 2nd | 1780–84 | Warren Hastings | **Porto Novo 1781** (Eyre Coote); Haidar died 1782; **Treaty of Mangalore (1784)** — mutual restitution |
| 3rd | 1790–92 | Cornwallis | Triple alliance; **Treaty of Seringapatam (1792)** — Tipu ceded **half** the kingdom |
| 4th | **1799** | Wellesley | Tipu died **4 May 1799**; Wodeyar minor under Subsidiary Alliance |

Tipu planted a Tree of Liberty, joined a Jacobin club, and sent embassies abroad.

### C. Three Anglo-Maratha Wars
| War | Years | Treaty chain / result |
|---|---|---|
| 1st | 1775–82 | **Surat 1775 → Purandhar 1776 → Wadgaon 1779 → Salbai 1782** (Mahadji Scindia mediator; 20 years’ peace) |
| 2nd | 1803–05 | **Treaty of Bassein (1802)** — Peshwa Baji Rao II accepted Subsidiary Alliance; Assaye / Laswari |
| 3rd | 1817–18 | Lord Hastings; Peshwaship abolished; Pindaris crushed; Peshwa pensioned at **Bithoor** |

### D. Nepal, Sikh, Sindh, Burma
| Conflict | Years | Treaty / result | UK / UP peg |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Anglo-Nepal** | **1814–16** | **Sugauli (1816)** — **Kumaon and Garhwal** ceded | Gillespie died at **Nalapani (Dehradun)** |
| **Amritsar** | **1809** | Ranjit Singh’s Sutlej peace with the British | Not the 1846 Lahore treaty |
| **1st Anglo-Sikh** | 1845–46 | **Treaty of Lahore (1846)** | Kashmir sold by **Treaty of Amritsar 1846** to Gulab Singh |
| **2nd Anglo-Sikh** | 1848–49 | **Punjab annexed 1849** (Dalhousie) | After Multan revolt |
| **Sindh** | **1843** | Charles Napier under **Ellenborough** | “Peccavi” tradition |
| **Burma** | 1824–26; 1852; 1885 | **Yandabo 1826**; full annexation **1885** (Dufferin) | 1st under Amherst; 2nd Dalhousie |

**Warren Hastings ≠ Lord Hastings.** Warren ended Dual Government, fought the 1st Maratha War, and faced Chait Singh (Banaras 1781). Lord Hastings fought Nepal, Pindaris, and the 3rd Maratha War.

**Ring Fence** (Warren Hastings, informal buffer) is not **Subsidiary Alliance** (Wellesley from **1798**: Hyderabad → Mysore → Tanjore → Awadh 1801 → Peshwa 1802).

**Awadh 1856** was annexed for **maladministration** (James Outram), **not** Doctrine of Lapse.
