## Must-Score Facts (High-Yield UKPCS)

1. **Statehood Incident Sequence (1994):**  
   $$\text{Khatima (1 Sep)} \longrightarrow \text{Mussoorie (2 Sep)} \longrightarrow \text{Rampur Tiraha (1–2 Oct 1994)}$$  
   *(UKPCS 2025 Q78 trapped on "Mussoorie — 3 September"; the correct date is 2 September).*
2. **Gaura Devi & Chipko (26 March 1974):** Gaura Devi presided over the *Mahila Mangal Dal* of Reni village. When loggers arrived while men were away in Chamoli for compensation hearings, Gaura Devi blocked their path, saying: *"This forest is our mother's home; shoot us before you axe our trees."*
3. **Chandi Prasad Bhatt:** Received **Ramon Magsaysay Award (1982)** and Padma Bhushan (2005) for pioneering grassroots non-violent ecological defense through Dasholi Gram Swarajya Mandal (DGSM, founded 1964).
4. **Sunderlal Bahuguna (1927–2021):** Undertook 5,000 km Trans-Himalayan foot march (Kashmir to Kohima, 1981–83); advocated tree conservation to Indira Gandhi leading to 15-year commercial felling ban above 1,000m; led Tehri Dam hunger strikes.
5. **UP Reorganisation Bill 2000 Parliamentary Dates:**
   - Lok Sabha passed: **1 August 2000**
   - Rajya Sabha passed: **10 August 2000**
   - Presidential Assent (K. R. Narayanan): **28 August 2000**
   - State inaugurated: **9 November 2000**

### Comprehensive Profiles of Ecological & Social Movements

| Movement | Year & Location | Primary Leader(s) | Specific Modus Operandi & Significance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Dola-Palki Movement** | 1930 (Garhwal) | **Jayanand Bharati** | Feudal caste tradition barred Dalit (Shilpkar) brides and grooms from riding in wedding palanquins (*Palki*). Bharati led direct action, petitions to British commissioners, and mass defiance to establish equal social dignity. |
| **Chipko Movement** | 1973 (Mandal) / 1974 (Reni) | **Chandi Prasad Bhatt**, **Gaura Devi**, **Sunderlal Bahuguna** | Resisted commercial logging by hugging trees. Led to 1980 central ban on commercial felling above 1,000m. Inspired Karnataka's *Appiko* movement (1983). |
| **Maiti Movement** | 1994 (Gwaldam, Chamoli) | **Kalyan Singh Rawat** | Cultural-ecological movement where the bride and groom plant a fruit/shade sapling in the bride's village (*Mait* = maternal home). Cared for by *Maiti sisters* (unmarried village girls). Spreads forest awareness across 5,000+ villages. |
| **Beej Bachao Andolan** | Late 1980s (Jardhargaon, Tehri) | **Vijay Jardhari**, Dhoom Singh Negi | Rejected cash-crop monoculture and chemical-intensive Green Revolution hybrids. Resurrected the **Barahnaja** (twelve complementary grains: Mandua, Jhangora, Bhatt, Rajma, Ramdana, Lobia, etc.), preserving over 200 indigenous hill bean and rice varieties. |
| **Pani Rakho Andolan** | Late 1980s (Ufrainkhal, Pauri) | **Sachidanand Bharati** | Addressed acute hill water scarcity. Built thousands of small check-trenches and absorption pits (*Chal-Khal*), recharging subterranean aquifers and reviving dead oak and rhododendron hillsides (*Gadkhark* forest). |
| **Raksha Sutra Movement** | 1994 (Bhilangna Valley, Tehri) | Suresh Bhai, local villagers | UP Forest Corporation attempted to cut marked green trees. Women and villagers tied yellow sacred threads (*Raksha Sutra*) to trees, successfully aborting the logging contracts. |
| **Jhapto-Cheeno Andolan** | 21 June 1998 (Lata village, Chamoli) | Local Gram Panchayats | Agitation against rigid forest bureaucracy that banned traditional cattle grazing and non-timber forest produce (herb collection) following Nanda Devi National Park's biosphere declaration. Villagers marched into the core park zone with livestock. |

## The 1994 Statehood Mass Movement & Timeline of Incidents

Following the 1994 reservation circular by the UP government under Mulayam Singh Yadav, massive protests erupted across all 8 hill districts, escalating into an unyielding mass struggle for separate statehood.

| Date | Incident & Location | What Happened / Factual Specifics |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1 September 1994** | **Khatima Incident (US Nagar)** | Peaceful rally of statehood protesters, including ex-servicemen, was fired upon without warning by police. **7 martyrs:** Bhagwan Singh Sirohi, Pratap Singh, Salim Ahmad, Dharmananda Bhatt, Paramjeet Singh, Ramikant, Gopi Chand. Observed as **"Black Day"** (*Kala Divas*). |
| **2 September 1994** | **Mussoorie Incident (Jhula Ghar)** | Day after Khatima, protesters marched in Mussoorie. Police opened fire at Jhula Ghar. **6 martyrs:** Belmati Chauhan, Hansa Dhanai, Dhanpat Singh, Madan Mohan Mamgain, Balbir Singh Negi, Rai Singh Bangari. DSP Uma Kant Tripathi was also killed in the chaos. |
| **Night of 1–2 Oct 1994**| **Rampur Tiraha Massacre (Muzaffarnagar)**| Thousands of Uttarakhand activists traveling in buses to attend a mega rally at Red Fort, Delhi, were intercepted at Rampur Tiraha by UP Police and PAC. Unarmed men were shot; women were molested and raped in sugarcane fields. **7 martyrs:** Ravindra Rawat, Rajesh Lakhera, Surya Prakash Thapliyal, Satyendra Chauhan, Girish Bhadri, Ashok Kumar Kashyap, Rajesh Negi. Condemned internationally. |
| **10 November 1995** | **Sri Yantra Tapu Incident (Srinagar)** | Statehood protesters fasting on Sri Yantra Tapu island in the Alaknanda river were attacked by police; **Yashodhar Benjwal** and **Rajesh Rawat** drowned/killed. |

## Roadmap to Statehood: Committees & Legislation

1. **Kaushik Committee (Jan 1994):**
   - Established by UP Government under Urban Development Minister **Ramashankar Kaushik**.
   - Submitted report in May 1994 recommending a separate state comprising 8 hill districts (Pauri, Chamoli, Tehri, Uttarkashi, Dehradun, Almora, Pithoragarh, Nainital).
   - Officially recommended **Gairsain** (Chamoli) as the permanent capital of the proposed state.
2. **Deve Gowda’s Independence Day Announcement (15 August 1996):**
   - Prime Minister H. D. Deve Gowda announced the creation of a separate Uttarakhand state from the ramparts of the Red Fort.
3. **Uttar Pradesh Reorganisation Act, 2000:**
   - Bill introduced in Parliament by Union Home Minister L. K. Advani.
   - Lok Sabha approval: **1 August 2000**.
   - Rajya Sabha approval: **10 August 2000**.
   - Presidential assent by President K. R. Narayanan: **28 August 2000**.
4. **State Inauguration (9 November 2000):**
   - State took birth as the **27th State of India** under the name **Uttaranchal** with 13 districts.
   - First interim Governor: **Surjit Singh Barnala**; First interim Chief Minister: **Nityanand Swami**.
5. **Renaming to Uttarakhand (1 January 2007):**
   - Uttaranchal (Alteration of Name) Bill, 2006 passed by Parliament.
   - Renamed **Uttarakhand** with effect from **1 January 2007**.
6. **Summer Capital Announcement (2020):**
   - CM Trivendra Singh Rawat declared **Bhararisain / Gairsain** (Chamoli) as the **Summer Capital** of Uttarakhand on 4 March 2020 (notified 8 June 2020).

## Confused Pairs & Common Traps

| Confused Pair | Critical Distinction |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Khatima vs Mussoorie Date** | Khatima firing = **1 September 1994**; Mussoorie firing = **2 September 1994** (trap distractor: 3 Sep). |
| **Black Day vs Muzaffarnagar Day** | Black Day = **1 September** (commemorates Khatima); Rampur Tiraha is **2 October** (Gandhi Jayanti). |
| **Maiti Movement vs Chipko** | Maiti = **bridal sapling planting (Kalyan Singh Rawat, 1994)**; Chipko = **tree-hugging anti-logging (Gaura Devi/Bhatt, 1974)**. |
| **Pani Rakho vs Beej Bachao** | Pani Rakho = **water harvesting via Chal-Khal (Sachidanand Bharati)**; Beej Bachao = **indigenous seeds & Barahnaja (Vijay Jardhari)**. |
| **Birth Date vs Rename Date** | State born as **Uttaranchal on 9 Nov 2000**; renamed **Uttarakhand on 1 Jan 2007**. |
| **Dehradun vs Gairsain** | Dehradun = provisional / winter capital; Bhararisain (Gairsain) = official **Summer Capital** declared in 2020. |

## UKPCS Inline & Practice Questions

**Q1 (UKPCS Prelims 2025, Q78).** Which of the following is NOT correctly matched?  
A. Khatima incident — 1 September, 1994  
B. Muzaffarnagar incident — 2 October, 1994  
C. Mussoorie incident — 3 September, 1994  
D. Black Day — 1 September, 1994  
*Answer:* **C** (Mussoorie firing occurred on 2 September 1994, not 3 September).

**Q2.** Who among the following was the founder of the unique ecological tradition called 'Maiti Movement'?  
A. Chandi Prasad Bhatt  
B. Sunderlal Bahuguna  
C. Kalyan Singh Rawat  
D. Sachidanand Bharati  
*Answer:* **C** (Kalyan Singh Rawat in 1994 at Gwaldam).

**Q3.** Who was elected as the first President of the Uttarakhand Kranti Dal (UKD) at its founding convention in Mussoorie in July 1979?  
A. Kashi Singh Airy  
B. Jaswant Singh Bisht  
C. Dr. Devi Datt Pant  
D. Indramani Badoni  
*Answer:* **C** (Dr. D. D. Pant, former VC of Kumaun University).

**Q4.** The famous environmental slogan *"Kya hain jangal ke upkar, mitti, pani aur bayar. Zinda rehne ke adhar"* was composed during the Chipko movement by:  
A. Gaura Devi  
B. Ghanshyam Raturi  
C. Girish Tiwari 'Girda'  
D. Chandi Prasad Bhatt  
*Answer:* **B** (Ghanshyam Raturi 'Shailani').

**Q5.** On which date was the Uttar Pradesh Reorganisation Bill, 2000 passed by the Lok Sabha?  
A. 1 August 2000  
B. 10 August 2000  
C. 28 August 2000  
D. 9 November 2000  
*Answer:* **A** (Passed in Lok Sabha on 1 August 2000, Rajya Sabha on 10 August, signed 28 August).
