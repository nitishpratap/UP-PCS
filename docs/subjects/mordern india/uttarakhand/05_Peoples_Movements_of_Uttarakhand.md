## 5.1 Long arc of people’s movements

- Hill society’s protests run from colonial **Coolie-Begar** and forest restrictions to twentieth-century Praja Mandal and nationalist work.
- **Chipko** and related forest movements enter standard UKPCS/UPPCS culture–environment overlap; keep leadership names for Environment / CA when asked.
- The syllabus head “people’s movements … and other related aspects” also covers the **Uttarakhand statehood** agitation.

---

## 5.2 Coolie-Begar as a people’s movement

- Forced porterage under colonial rule produced a mass hill campaign centred on Kumaon.
- Leadership memory: **Badri Dutt Pandey** and associates; success associated with **Bageshwar**.
- Full meeting-place and Parishad chronology stay in Topic 4; here remember it as the classic early people’s struggle of the hills.

---

## 5.3 Uttarakhand movement, 1994 — incident ratta

**Causes:** Demand for a separate hill state, administrative neglect narratives, and mobilisation after years of regional politics.

**Course (1994 flashpoints):**

- **Khatima incident — 1 September 1994**, remembered as **Black Day**.
- **Mussoorie incident — 2 September 1994** (firing the day after Khatima).
- **Muzaffarnagar / Rampur Tiraha — 1–2 October 1994**.

**Results:** The agitation hardens all-India attention; the separate state of Uttarakhand (then Uttaranchal) is created in **2000** (national polity / CA timeline).

---

## Must-Score Facts

1. Khatima = **1 Sep 1994** = Black Day.
2. Mussoorie = **2 Sep 1994** — not 3 September.
3. Muzaffarnagar / Rampur Tiraha = early **October 1994**.
4. Coolie-Begar is the colonial people’s struggle bridge.
5. State formed **2000** — end of the long movement arc.
