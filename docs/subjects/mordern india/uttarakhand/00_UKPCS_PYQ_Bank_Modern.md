## National Modern — keyed (2025)

### UKPCS Prelims 2025, Q61 — AITUC / trade unions

Consider the following statements:

1. All India Trade Union Congress was founded in 1920.
2. Lala Lajpat Rai was elected its first President.
3. All India Trade Union Federation was formed by S. A. Dange in 1929.

A. Only 1 and 2  
B. Only 1 and 3  
C. Only 2 and 3  
D. Only 2  

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Logic:** The 1929 split and the name of the breakaway federation carry the error.

**Ans: A — Only 1 and 2 (Series B provisional key).** AITUC was founded in 1920 with Lala Lajpat Rai as first president. Statement 3 is wrong because the moderate group led by **N. M. Joshi** formed the All India Trade Union Federation in 1929, not S. A. Dange. Home: [Home Rule & Labour Politics](../12_Home_Rule_and_Labour_Politics.md).

</details>

### UKPCS Prelims 2025, Q72 — 1857 statements

Consider the following statements:

1. Syed Ahmad Khan wrote 'Causes of the Indian Revolt'.
2. Mangal Pande was hanged on March 29, 1857.
3. Hazrat Mahal led the revolt at Lucknow.
4. Nana Saheb led the revolt at Bareilly.

A. Only 1 and 2  
B. Only 2 and 3  
C. Only 2, 3 and 4  
D. Only 1, 2 and 3  

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Logic:** Nana Saheb led Kanpur and not Bareilly, so any option containing statement 4 can be dropped.

**Ans: D — Only 1, 2 and 3 (Series B provisional key).** Statement 4 is wrong (Bareilly = Khan Bahadur Khan). Home: [Revolt of 1857](../05_Revolt_of_1857.md).

</details>

</details>
