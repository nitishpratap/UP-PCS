## Consolidated — 24 Must-Score Facts

1. **1857 — Kalu Mahara (Kalu Singh Mehra):** First hill freedom-fighter memory; secret org **Krantiveer** in Kali Kumaon (Bisung / Lohaghat); attacked British barracks at **Chandmari**; linked in tradition to Wajid Ali Shah’s invitation.
2. **Almora Debating Club (1870)** and **Almora Akhbar (1871)** open the vernacular political press; after the Akhbar was banned, **Badridatt Pandey** founded weekly *Shakti* on **15 October 1918**.
3. **Garhwal Hitkarini / Garhwal Union (1901)** was founded by **Tara Dutt Gairola** (*Garhwal Samachar* 1902, *Garhwali* 1905).
4. **Kumaon Parishad** founded **Sept 1916** at **Majjkhali (Almora)** by B. D. Pandey, G. B. Pant, Hargovind Pant, Indralal Shah; **merged into INC in 1926** (6th session, Ganiyadholi / Ranikhet, Mukandi Lal).
5. Landmark Parishad sessions: **1st Almora 1917** (Jay Datt Joshi) → **2nd Haldwani 1918** (Tara Dutt Gairola) → **3rd Kotdwar 1919** → **4th Kashipur 1920** (Hargovind Pant; Non-Cooperation + Coolie-Begar deadline) → **5th Tanakpur 1923** → **6th merger 1926**.
6. **Home Rule League, Dehradun (1918)** was founded by **Swami Vicharanand Saraswati** — not Dayanand (UKPCS 2025).
7. **Badridatt Pandey met Gandhi at Calcutta (Dec 1918)** to brief him on Coolie-Begar — not at Kausani.
8. **Coolie-Begar — Cause:** Forced unpaid labour, porterage, and rations for touring officials (*Coolie-Begar / Utar / Burdash*).
9. **Coolie-Begar — Course:** On **13–14 January 1921** (Uttarayani) at **Bageshwar** on the Saryu, ~40,000 peasants led by **Badridatt Pandey, Hargovind Pant, Chiranji Lal** swore to end begar and flung revenue registers into the river.
10. **Coolie-Begar — Result:** System collapses without bloodshed; Gandhi called it a **“Bloodless Revolution”**; Badridatt earns the title **“Kumaon Kesari”**.
11. **Gandhi in Kumaon (14 June–2 July 1929):** Stayed **12 days** at Kausani (later Anashakti Ashram); wrote the intro to *Anashakti Yoga*; called Kausani the **“Switzerland of India”**. Garhwal tour followed **16–24 Oct 1929**.
12. **Peshawar Incident — Cause/Course:** On **23 April 1930**, during CDM at Qissa Khwani, Captain Ricketts ordered **2/18 Royal Garhwal Rifles** to fire on unarmed Khudai Khidmatgars; Havildar Major **Chandra Singh Garhwali** ordered **cease fire**.
13. **Peshawar — Result:** Court-martial at Abbottabad; **Barrister Mukandi Lal** defended; Chandra Singh got rigorous imprisonment (saved from the gallows).
14. **Dandi March (1930)** Uttarakhand satyagrahis among the 78: **Jyotiram Kandpal, Bhairav Dutt Joshi, Gorakh Veer (Khadag Bahadur)**.
15. **Dola-Palki (1930):** **Jayanand Bharati** led the struggle for palanquin rights for Dalit (Shilpkar) wedding parties.
16. **Quit India — Sult firing (5 Sept 1942):** SDM Johnson’s police fire at **Khumaan, Sult (Almora)**; martyrs include **Ganga Ram, Kheema Dev, Churamani, Bahadur Singh**; Gandhi named Sult the **“Bardoli of Kumaon”**.
17. **Deghat firing (18 Aug 1942, Almora):** **Hari Krishna** and **Heera Mani** martyred; Chanauda Gandhi Ashram raided **10 Aug 1942**.
18. Do not swap **Kashipur 1920 (4th Parishad)** with **Majjkhali / Almora 1916 (founding)**.
19. Do not swap **Bardoli of Kumaon (Sult)** with **Bardoli of Garhwal (Gujaddoo)**.
20. Do not swap **Peshawar refusal (Garhwali troops)** with **Tilari firing (Tehri troops)**.
21. Do not swap **Calcutta 1918 (Coolie-Begar briefing)** with **Kausani 1929 (Anashakti stay)**.
22. Do not swap **Almora Akhbar (1871)** with ***Shakti* (15 Oct 1918)**.
23. Hold the hill freedom spine: **1857 Kalu Mahara → press/Parishad → Coolie-Begar 1921 → Gandhi 1929 → Peshawar 1930 → Dandi/Dola-Palki → Quit India Sult 1942**.
24. Coolie-Begar and Sult both need Cause → Course → Result memory; Peshawar is a refusal-to-fire card, not a massacre-by-hill-troops card.

## The Six Landmark Sessions of Kumaon Parishad (1916–1926)

| Session | Year | Venue | President | Key Resolutions & Significance |
| :---: | :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Founding** | Sept 1916 | Majjkhali (Almora) | — | Founded by B. D. Pandey, G. B. Pant, Hargovind Pant, Indralal Shah. |
| **1st** | Sept 1917 | **Almora** (अल्मोड़ा) | **Jay Datt Joshi** | Addressed hill forest rights, education, and local self-rule. |
| **2nd** | Dec 1918 | **Haldwani** | **Tara Dutt Gairola** | Focused on Coolie-Begar abolition and foodgrain scarcity during WWI. |
| **3rd** | Oct 1919 | **Kotdwar (Garhwal)** | **Rai (राई) Bahadur Badri Datt Joshi** | Cemented Hindu–Muslim communal amity; endorsed Montagu-Chelmsford (मांटेग्यू-चेम्सफोर्ड) reforms. |
| **4th** | Dec 1920 | **Kashipur** | **Hargovind Pant** | **Historic session:** Formally endorsed Mahatma Gandhi’s Non-Cooperation movement; declared deadline to end Coolie-Begar. |
| **5th** | Dec 1923 | **Tanakpur** | **Badri Datt Pandey** | Addressed forest policy, Van Panchayats, and peasant indebtedness. |
| **6th** | Dec 1926 | **Ganiyadholi (Ranikhet)**| **Barrister Mukandi Lal** (लैंसडाउन) | **Merger Session:** Voted to merge the Kumaon Parishad formally into the Indian National Congress. |

### 1. Coolie-Begar Abolition (13–14 January 1921)
- **The System:** Village households were compelled to provide uncompensated manual labour and luggage porterage for touring British officials, police, and surveyors.
- **The Culmination:** On Makar Sankranti (मकर संक्रांति) (Uttarayani), over 40,000 peasants gathered at Bageshwar temple. Badridatt Pandey, Hargovind Pant, and Lala Chiranji Lal administered a solemn oath by the holy water of the Saryu.
- **The Act:** Village headmen tore and threw all the village *Begar Registers* into the confluence of the Saryu and Gomti rivers. Deputy Commissioner Dyas looked on helplessly as the system collapsed permanently without a drop of blood spilled.

### 2. The Peshawar Incident (23 April 1930)
- **Context:** Civil Disobedience (सविनय अवज्ञा) Movement in Peshawar (North-West Frontier Province).
- **The Commander:** **Veer Chandra Singh Garhwali** (born 25 Dec 1891 in Meusora village, Pauri (पौड़ी) Garhwal), serving as Havildar Major in 2/18 Royal Garhwal Rifles.
- **The Defiance:** British officer Captain Ricketts ordered the platoon to shoot into the peaceful Muslim Khudai Khidmatgar demonstrators. Chandra Singh ordered his men: *"Garhwaliyo, Cease Fire! Do not shoot unarmed brethren."*
- **Legal Defense:** Garhwali troops were arrested and court-martialed at Abbottabad; celebrated nationalist lawyer **Barrister Mukandi Lal** represented them in court, saving Chandra Singh from the gallows (sentenced to 14 years rigorous imprisonment).

### 3. Mahatma Gandhi’s Kumaon–Garhwal Journey (1929)
- **Kumaon Visit (14 June – 2 July 1929):** Gandhi arrived via Bareilly and Kathgodam, visited Nainital (नैनीताल), Bhowali, Almora, Bageshwar, and Kausani.
- **Kausani Ashram:** Gandhi spent 12 days at the Dak Bungalow (now *Anashakti Ashram*) in Kausani, spellbound by the Himalayan panorama; wrote the introduction to his seminal treatise *Anashakti Yoga (योग)* and penned: *"In these hills, Nature's hospitality and beauty outrival anything that I have seen in Switzerland."*
- **Garhwal Tour (16–24 October 1929):** Accompanied by Kasturba Gandhi and Jawaharlal Nehru (जवाहरलाल नेहरू), visited Haridwar (हरिद्वार), Rishikesh (ऋषिकेश), Srinagar, Kotdwar, and Pauri.

### 4. Dandi March (1930) Uttarakhand Stalwarts
Out of 78 chosen satyagrahis who marched 240 miles with Gandhi from Sabarmati (साबरमती) to Dandi:
1. **Jyotiram Kandpal:** Teacher and nationalist organizer from Sult (Almora); later established Gandhi Ashram at Chanauda (1937).
2. **Bhairav Dutt Joshi:** Prominent Congress activist from Almora.
3. **Gorakh Veer (Khadag Bahadur Singh):** Valiant youth fighter from Dehradun.

### 5. The Quit India (भारत छोड़ो) Movement (1942) in the Hills
- **Sult Firing (5 September 1942):** British police under SDM Johnson opened fire on a peaceful crowd at Khumaan in Sult. Ganga Ram and his brother Kheema Dev died on the spot; Churamani and Bahadur Singh succumbed to injuries. Gandhi designated Sult as **"Kumaon ki Bardoli"**.
- **Deghat Firing (18 August 1942):** British forces opened fire in Deghat (Almora); Hari Krishna (कृष्णा) and Heera Mani martyred.
- **Almora Town Demonstrations:** Shanti Lal Trivedi's Gandhi Ashram at Chanauda was raided and sealed by British police on 10 August 1942.

## Confused Pairs & Common Traps

| Confused Pair | Critical Distinction |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Kashipur (1920) vs Almora (1916)** | Almora = **Founding** of Kumaon Parishad (1916); Kashipur = **4th Session** (1920, Hargovind Pant). |
| **Calcutta (1918) vs Kausani (1929)** | Calcutta = B. D. Pandey met Gandhi regarding Coolie-Begar; Kausani = Gandhi stayed 12 days writing *Anashakti Yoga*. |
| **Swami Vicharanand vs Swami Dayanand** | Swami Vicharanand Saraswati = **Home Rule Dehradun (1918)**; Swami Dayanand Saraswati = Arya Samaj founder (died 1883). |
| **Almora Akhbar vs Shakti** | Almora Akhbar = started 1871, banned 1918; Shakti = started 15 Oct 1918 by Badridatt Pandey. |
| **Bardoli of Kumaon vs Bardoli of Garhwal** | Bardoli of Kumaon = **Sult (Almora)**; Bardoli of Garhwal = **Gujaddoo (Garhwal)**. |
| **Peshawar Refusal vs Tilari Kand** | Peshawar = Garhwal Rifles refused to fire on citizens; Tilari = Royal troops fired on unarmed Tehri peasants. |

## UKPCS Inline & Practice Questions

**Q1 (UKPCS Prelims 2025, Q76).** Who founded a branch of the ‘Home Rule League’ at Dehradun in 1918?  
A. Swami Satyadeva  
B. Swami Dayanand Saraswati  
C. Swami Vicharanand Saraswati  
D. Tara Dutt Gairola  
*Answer:* **C** (Swami Vicharanand Saraswati).

**Q2 (UKPCS Prelims 2025, Q77).** In 1918, at which place did Badridatt Pandey meet Mahatma Gandhi and inform him about the Coolie-Begar system?  
A. Delhi  
B. Calcutta  
C. Lucknow (लखनऊ)  
D. Bombay  
*Answer:* **B** (Calcutta Congress session window in Dec 1918).

**Q3 (UKPCS Prelims 2025, Q80).** At which place was the fourth conference of ‘Kumaon Parishad’ held?  
A. Kotdwar  
B. Haldwani  
C. Kashipur  
D. Almora  
*Answer:* **C** (Kashipur in Dec 1920 under Hargovind Pant).

**Q4 (UKPCS Prelims 2025, Q87).** Who established the ‘Garhwal Union’ and ‘Garhwal Hitkarini Sabha (सभा)’?  
A. Ramsharan Raturi  
B. Dharmanand Gairola  
C. Asha Ram Nautiyal  
D. Tara Dutt Gairola  
*Answer:* **D** (Tara Dutt Gairola in 1901).

**Q5.** Which region of Uttarakhand was named by Mahatma Gandhi as the 'Bardoli of Kumaon'?  
A. Bageshwar  
B. Sult (Almora)  
C. Chamoli (चमोली)  
D. Devprayag (देवप्रयाग)  
*Answer:* **B** (Sult region, commemorating the 5 September 1942 Khumaan firing).

**Q6.** Who was the brave commander of 2/18 Royal Garhwal Rifles who refused to fire upon unarmed Pathan satyagrahis at Peshawar in 1930?  
A. Kalu Mahara  
B. Govind Ballabh Pant  
C. Veer Chandra Singh Garhwali  
D. Jayanand Bharati  
*Answer:* **C** (Veer Chandra Singh Garhwali).
