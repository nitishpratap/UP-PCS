### K. Mountbatten Plan (3 June 1947) — Provisions Checklist

1. British India to be partitioned into **two dominions**: India and Pakistan  
2. Early transfer of power (**15 Aug 1947**, not wait till June 1948)  
3. Provincial assemblies / referendums decide accession (Punjab, Bengal partition logic; NWFP referendum memory; Sylhet memory)  
4. Boundary Commission to demarcate borders (**Radcliffe**)  
5. Princely states free to join either dominion (paramountcy lapse context)  
6. Constituent Assemblies continue for each dominion  
7. Governor-General for each dominion (Mountbatten initially GG of India)  

**Not the same as:** Cabinet Mission’s united Groups plan (1946).

### L. Indian Independence Act 1947 — Provisions Checklist

1. Creates two independent **dominions** — India and Pakistan — from **15 Aug 1947**  
2. British Parliament Act date: **18 July 1947**  
3. Each dominion’s Constituent Assembly becomes legislature  
4. Office of Secretary of State for India abolished (end of old India Office control)  
5. British paramountcy over princely states **lapses**  
6. Title of Emperor of India dropped for British Crown  
7. Division of civil/military services, assets, and territories authorised  

**Date trap:** Act = **18 Jul 1947**; Independence Day = **15 Aug 1947**.

## 14.2 Congress Socialist Party

### Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Congress Socialist Party (CSP)** | Socialist group **inside Congress**, organised **1934** |
| **Acharya Narendra Dev / Jayaprakash Narayan** | Key CSP leaders |

### Causes

1. After CDM suspension, socialists feared Congress would drift to constitutionalism only.
2. Nasik Jail discussions forged a socialist programme inside the national movement.
3. CSP rejected both pure Gandhian anti-class politics and CPI’s early anti-Congress line.
4. They wanted agrarian/labour demands inside anti-imperial struggle.
5. Leaders sought organised left pressure without immediately leaving Congress.

### Course — Step by Step

1. **1932–33:** Nasik Jail discussions among JP, Masani, Ashok Mehta, and others.
2. **May 1934:** Patna socialist conference (Narendra Dev stream).
3. **October 1934:** All-India CSP at **Bombay** (not January).
4. CSP works as a Congress socialist caucus.
5. **Nehru never formally joins** despite socialist rhetoric.
6. CSP activists later energise labour/kisan work and Quit India underground streams.
7. After independence, socialist streams exit toward separate parties.
8. UPPCS tests month trap + Nehru non-membership (**2024 Q131**).

### Congress Socialist Party — How It Works

- CSP = **inside Congress**, **1934**, leaders **JP / Narendra Dev**.
- **Neither** statement in 2024 Q131: not January; Nehru did not join.
- Distinct from Forward Bloc (Bose, 1939) and CPI.
- Trap: "CSP formed January 1934 by Nehru." Double false.
- Trap: "CSP = Communist Party." Different organisation.
- Trap: "CSP opposed Quit India." Many CSP leaders were active in QI underground.

> **Exam note:** **2024 Q131 = Neither 1 nor 2**.

### Causes

1. Post-war Britain wanted an orderly exit.
2. Simla failure showed need for a larger constitutional plan.
3. Congress–League polarisation after elections required a framework.
4. International pressure favoured decolonisation.
5. Mission sought unity with autonomy, avoiding immediate partition.

### Course — Step by Step

1. Mission announced/arrives in **1946** (announcement season early 1946; work March–May).

3. Constituent Assembly to draft constitution.

5. League accepts then **withdraws acceptance** after disputes (esp. Nehru’s remarks on plan flexibility).
6. Congress moves toward Constituent Assembly/Interim Government path.
7. League launches **Direct Action (16 Aug 1946)**.

- Year: **1946**; members: **Pethick-Lawrence, Stafford Cripps, A.V. Alexander**.
- Rejected a fully sovereign Pakistan initially; proposed grouping instead.
- **2018 Q17**: League withdrew acceptance (**A true**); League later joined Interim Govt (**R true**); but R is **not** the explanation of A → **B**.

- Trap: "League never accepted Cabinet Mission even briefly." It accepted then withdrew.
- Trap: "Joining Interim Government caused the withdrawal." Withdrawal preceded later tactical entry.
- Trap: "Cabinet Mission = Cripps Mission 1942." Different year/purpose.

### Causes

2. Communal violence made a single centre unworkable in British eyes.
3. Congress reluctantly accepted partition as price of immediate freedom.
4. League demanded Pakistan.

### Course — Step by Step

4. Plan provides for partition, referendums/boundary procedures, and dominion status.
5. Princely states to choose accession.
6. Indian Independence Bill follows.
7. Boundary Commission (Radcliffe) works late and hastily.
8. Transfer occurs **15 August 1947** amid Partition violence.

### Mountbatten Plan — How It Works

- Date lock: **3 June 1947**.
- Content: **partition + two dominions + early transfer**.

- Trap: "Mountbatten Plan rejected partition." It accepted partition.
- Trap: "Independence Act was passed on 3 June." Act is **18 July 1947**.

> **Exam note:** 3 June Plan → Independence Act (18 Jul) → 15 Aug transfer.

## 14.15 Indian Independence Act 1947

### Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Indian Independence Act 1947** | British Parliament Act (**18 July 1947**) creating two independent dominions |
| **15 August 1947** | Date of transfer of power / Independence |

### Indian Independence Act — How It Works

- Act date: **18 July 1947**; Independence Day: **15 August 1947** — do not swap.
- Creates **India** and **Pakistan** as dominions.
- Ends British paramountcy over princely states (accession politics follow).
- Provides for division of services/assets and constituent powers.
- Trap: "Independence Act passed on 15 August." Passed **18 July**.
- Trap: "Act created a republic immediately." Dominions first; Republic of India from **26 Jan 1950**.
- Trap: "Act = Mountbatten Plan itself." Plan is political; Act is legal statute.
- Trap: "Paramountcy continued forever." Act ends paramountcy framework.

> **Exam note:** Legal date **18 Jul** vs celebration date **15 Aug**.

### Causes

1. Mountbatten Plan needed statutory backing.
2. British Parliament had to authorise dominion creation.
3. Timetable for 15 Aug required a rapid Act.
4. Partition mechanics needed legal cover.
5. End of Company/Crown Raj structures required statute.

### Course — Step by Step

1. 3 June Plan accepted by major parties.
2. Independence Bill introduced in British Parliament.
3. Act receives assent **18 July 1947**.
4. Boundary/administrative partition work races ahead.
5. Power transferred **15 August 1947**.
6. Two dominions begin with Constituent Assemblies as legislatures.
7. Princely accession negotiations intensify.
8. Constitutional republic phase comes later (1950).

### Results

| Result | Why it matters |
|--------|----------------|
| Legal independence | Statute of exit |
| Two dominions | Partition legalised |
| End of paramountcy | Princely integration era |

### Exam Facts (raata)

### Independence Act — Provisions (checklist)

1. Two dominions from **15 Aug 1947**  
2. Act passed **18 July 1947** (date ≠ Independence Day)  
3. Each Constituent Assembly = dominion legislature  
4. Secretary of State for India office ends  
5. British paramountcy over princely states **lapses**  
6. Division of assets, services, and armed forces authorised  
7. “Emperor of India” title dropped

- Independence: **15 August 1947**
- Two dominions
- Not yet Republic (1950)
