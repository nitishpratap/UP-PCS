## 6.1 Land reforms

**Land reforms** aim to change agrarian relations so that tillers gain security, fair rent and, where policy provides, ownership.

- **Zamindari / intermediary abolition** removed rent-collecting intermediaries between the State and the cultivator in the early post-Independence wave.
- **Tenancy reform** has three classic pillars: **regulation of rent**, **security of tenure**, and **conferment of ownership on tenants**.
- All three tenancy pillars appear together in the keyed UPPCS multi-statement stem.
- **Land ceilings** limit how much land one household may hold and aim to redistribute surplus.
- **Consolidation of holdings** reduces fragmentation by regrouping scattered plots.
- **Bhoodan / Gramdan** were voluntary donation movements — complementary, not a substitute for statutory reform.
- Uneven State implementation left informal tenancy, benami holdings and title gaps in many regions.
- Clear titles and digitised land records support credit access and investment incentives.

## 6.2 MSP, procurement, APMC and e-NAM

**Minimum Support Price (MSP)** is a policy floor announced to protect farmers from distress sale for mandated crops.

- The **Commission for Agricultural Costs and Prices (CACP)** **recommends** MSP.
- The **Union Cabinet** **announces** MSP — CACP does not itself notify the final price.
- Mandated MSP coverage in teaching is **22 crops** (cereals, pulses, oilseeds and selected commercial crops).
- Sugarcane uses **Fair and Remunerative Price (FRP)**, not the cereal MSP schedule.
- Budget 2018–19 teaching targets MSP at about **1.5×** all-India production cost for mandated crops.
- **FCI** and State agencies procure for the central pool and run buffer-stock / PDS logistics.
- **APMC** yards regulate local mandi trade under State laws; market fees and trader licences are classic features.
- **e-NAM** adds an electronic national market layer on top of mandi infrastructure.
- Fertilizer and food subsidies support the production and consumption sides of food security (NFSA neighbour below).

## 6.3 Schemes — PM-KISAN, insurance, soil, solar

**Central farm schemes** support income, risk cover, soil health and clean agri-energy.

### PM-KISAN

- **PM-KISAN** provides income support to eligible landholding farmer families.
- Teaching transfer is **₹6,000 per year** in three instalments in the standard scheme design.
- UKPCS keys **24 February 2019** (Gorakhpur launch) as “came into effect.”
- First-instalment benefit reckoning from **1 December 2018** is a different date trap — do not swap the two.

### Risk, soil and solar

- **PMFBY (Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana)** is the flagship crop-insurance scheme against yield loss from notified perils.
- **Soil Health Card** guides balanced nutrient use from soil-test results.
- **PM-KUSUM** supports solar pumps and solarisation of agri feeders to cut diesel dependence and daytime power stress.
- **PM-AASHA** supports price support / deficiency payments for oilseeds and pulses through designated agencies in teaching.
- Scheme names change with Budgets; keep the purpose of each flagship, not only the acronym.

## 6.4 NFSA, NFSM and food-security dimensions

**Food security** means people can obtain adequate, safe and nutritious food.

- Teaching dimensions here: **availability**, **accessibility** and **affordability**.
- **Accountability** is a governance word — it is **not** a food-security dimension in the keyed UPPCS stem.

### National Food Security Act (NFSA)

- NFSA provides a legal entitlement to subsidised foodgrains for eligible households.
- Coverage ceilings in the Act: up to **75% of the rural** and **50% of the urban** population.
- The Act has a special focus on **nutritional support to women and children**.
- The **eldest woman** of the household aged **18 years or above** is the head of the household for ration-card purposes.
- Deemed commencement teaching date is **5 July 2013** — not 2010.
- Antyodaya and priority households receive different grain entitlements in the statutory schedule.

### NFSM and related missions

- **NFSM (National Food Security Mission)** aims to raise production of rice, wheat, pulses and coarse cereals through area and productivity gains.
- Buffer stocks, PDS and FCI logistics convert production into physical availability (Topic 6.2 neighbour).

## 6.5 Colour revolutions and institutions

**Colour / sectoral revolutions** tag breakthroughs in production of specific commodities.

- The **Green Revolution** raised cereal yields through HYV seeds, irrigation and fertiliser — face **M.S. Swaminathan**; Norman Borlaug is the global breeding face.
- Green Revolution costs include groundwater stress, monoculture and millet/pulse neglect in teaching.
- The **White Revolution / Operation Flood** raised milk production through dairy cooperatives — face **Verghese Kurien** and NDDB / Amul.
- Do not swap Swaminathan (cereals / Green) with Kurien (milk / White).
- **Blue Revolution** = fisheries; **Yellow Revolution** = oilseeds; **Golden Revolution** = horticulture (+ honey in many keys).
- **Grey Revolution** = fertiliser; **Black Revolution** = petroleum; **Silver** = eggs; **Pink** = onion / prawn / meat variants in coaching keys.
- **Evergreen Revolution** stresses sustainable productivity — Swaminathan’s later tag.
- **Rainbow Revolution** means integrated multi-sector agri progress in teaching.

### Institutions

- **ICAR** leads agricultural research; **IARI (Pusa)** is the flagship institute.
- **FCI** manages procurement, storage and PDS offtake for foodgrains.
- **L.K. Jha** is associated with social control on banks in the keyed match list — not milk or Green Revolution.

## Common Traps

* NFSA: **75/50**, women–children focus, eldest woman head; date **5 July 2013** not 2010.
* Food-security dimensions ≠ **Accountability**.
* PM-KISAN: launch **24 Feb 2019**; benefits from **1 Dec 2018**.
* NFSM **2007–08** = rice + wheat + pulses; NFSA is the **2013** rights Act.
* Swaminathan ≠ Kurien; Green ≠ White.
* Tenancy reforms = **all three** (rent, tenure, ownership).
* CACP recommends; Cabinet decides; FCI procures/stores/distributes.
* Colour revolutions: Blue = fish, Yellow = oilseeds, Golden = horticulture/honey teaching.
