# Topic 25 – Census and Demographics (UPPCS & UKPCS Ratta)

> **Priority S** | Both commissions reliably ask 4-6 questions on Census 2011. Until Census 2027 is published, these figures are frozen.

---

## 1. India - Key Census 2011 Metrics
* **Total Population:** ~121 Crore (Rural: 68.8%, Urban: 31.2%)
* **Decadal Growth Rate:** 17.7% (Meghalaya highest at 27.9%, Nagaland negative at -0.6%)
* **Density:** 382 persons / sq. km (Bihar highest: 1106, Arunachal lowest: 17)
* **Sex Ratio:** 943 (Kerala highest: 1084, Haryana lowest: 879)
* **Child Sex Ratio (0-6 yrs):** 919 (Mizoram/Meghalaya high, Haryana lowest: 834)
* **Literacy Rate:** 74.04% (Male: 82.14%, Female: 65.46%)
  - **Highest:** Kerala (94%), Lakshadweep (91.8%), Mizoram (91.3%)
  - **Lowest:** Bihar (61.8%), Arunachal Pradesh (65.4%), Rajasthan (66.1%)
* **Scheduled Castes (SC):** 16.6% (Highest absolute: UP, Highest %: Punjab)
* **Scheduled Tribes (ST):** 8.6% (Highest absolute: MP, Highest %: Lakshadweep/Mizoram)

## 2. Uttar Pradesh Census 2011 (Highlights)
* **Population:** 19.98 Crore (16.5% of India - Rank 1)
* **Density:** 829 
* **Sex Ratio:** 912 (Jaunpur 1024 > Azamgarh 1019 > Deoria 1017)
* **Child Sex Ratio:** 902 (Balrampur 950, Baghpat 841)
* **Literacy:** 67.7% (Gautam Buddha Nagar 80.1%, Shrawasti 46.7%)
* **Million Plus Cities:** Kanpur, Lucknow, Ghaziabad, Agra, Varanasi, Meerut, Prayagraj.

## 3. Uttarakhand Census 2011 (Highlights)
* **Population:** ~1.00 Crore 
* **Decadal Growth:** 18.81% (US Nagar highest 33.4%, Pauri & Almora negative growth ghost villages)
* **Density:** 189 (Haridwar 801 > US Nagar 649 > Dehradun 549. Lowest: Uttarkashi 41)
* **Sex Ratio:** 963 (Almora 1142 > Rudraprayag 1114 > Pauri 1103. Lowest: Haridwar 880)
* **Child Sex Ratio:** 890 (Almora 922, Pithoragarh 816)
* **Literacy:** 78.82% (Dehradun highest 84.25%, US Nagar lowest 73.10%)
* **SC/ST Population:** SC is 18.8% (Bageshwar highest %); ST is 2.9% (US Nagar highest %).

**Q3.** Which district of Uttarakhand reported negative population growth in the 2011 Census?
A. Almora
B. Pauri Garhwal
C. Both A and B
D. Uttarkashi

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Q4.** According to Census 2011, which state has the highest percentage of Scheduled Caste (SC) population relative to its total population?
A. Uttar Pradesh
B. Punjab
C. Himachal Pradesh
D. West Bengal

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>
**Ans: B.** While UP has the highest absolute number of Scheduled Castes, Punjab has the highest percentage (31.9%).
