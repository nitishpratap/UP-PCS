## N.5 Power: Thermal, Hydro, Nuclear, Renewables

**Thermal:** coal/gas/oil heat → steam. Dominates Indian electricity; flyash; air pollution vs solar.
**Hydro:** needs head + flow. **Shivanasamudra** = hydel (KA / Cauvery). Himalayan potential huge; E-UP/Bihar limited because ranges to the north lie in **Nepal** (2025 Q31). Geothermal: **Puga (Ladakh)** — not solar-derived.

## Common Traps — Don't Fall For These

1. **Jharkhand + Karnataka always lead iron** — FALSE for 2021–22 framing. **Odisha + Chhattisgarh** (2022 Q2).
2. **Bailadila = Odisha** — FALSE. **Chhattisgarh**. Gurumahisani = Odisha; Noamundi = Jharkhand; Kudremukh = Karnataka.
3. **MALCO = Chennai** — FALSE. **Mettur, Tamil Nadu** (2022 Q52). HINDALCO = Renukoot (UP); BALCO = Korba.
4. **Maolong = Arunachal** — FALSE. **Meghalaya** (2025 Q9).
5. **Singrauli NTPC = northern Chhattisgarh** — FALSE. **MP–UP**; it **is** NTPC’s oldest unit (2021 Q89).
6. **Kashagan = Kuwait** — FALSE. **Kazakhstan** (2024 Q78). Ruhr = **Germany**, not Britain.
7. **CNG / natural gas = butane** — FALSE. **Methane**. LPG is the propane/butane mix.
8. **Black Revolution = oilseeds** — FALSE. Oilseeds = **Yellow**; Black = **petroleum**.
9. **Ahmedabad = largest cotton mill centre** — FALSE. In the cotton **belt** (R true); traditionally **Mumbai** is largest (2020 Q58).
10. **Shivanasamudra = nuclear** — FALSE. **Hydel**; Kalpakkam = nuclear (2023 Q58).
11. **ISA Secretariat = New Delhi / Paris** — FALSE. **Gurugram**. Member count is **not 38**.
12. **Nuclear / geothermal = stored solar** — FALSE. Wind and biomass are solar-linked (2022 Q139).
13. **Copper city = Chitradurga; Mn city = Bhilwara** — FALSE (2020 Q64). Ballari = iron; Katni = bauxite (as keyed).
14. **Diu is not the first 100% solar UT** — FALSE. It **is**. First solar airport = **Cochin**, not Ahmedabad.
15. **UP = Bhilai-class steel state / no coal at all** — FALSE. Locks = **Sonbhadra** energy, **non-coking** Singrauli coal, **Mathura** refinery, **Narora** nuclear, **Shankargarh** silica. Firozabad = **glass**, Moradabad = **brass**.
16. **Pegu Yoma = tin** — FALSE. **Mineral oil** (2022 Q70). Tin = Kinta / Bangka.
