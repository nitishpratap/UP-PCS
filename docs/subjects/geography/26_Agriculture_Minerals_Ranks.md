## 3. Global Mineral / Agriculture Leaders
*(Often tested as match-the-following)*
* **Wheat & Rice:** China > India 
* **Sugarcane:** Brazil > India
* **Milk:** India > USA
* **Cotton (Production):** China > India > USA
* **Coal (Production):** China > India > USA
* **Iron Ore (Reserves):** Australia > Brazil > Russia
* **Iron Ore (Production):** Australia > Brazil > China (India 4th)
* **Gold (Production):** China > Australia > Russia
* **Uranium (Production):** Kazakhstan > Canada > Namibia
* **Copper (Production):** Chile > Peru > Congo
* **Silver (Production):** Mexico > China > Peru
