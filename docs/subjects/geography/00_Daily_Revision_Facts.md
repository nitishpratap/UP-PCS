## 3. Panch Prayag & Indian River Drainage System

### A. The Panch Prayag of Uttarakhand (Order along Alaknanda River)
*Memorize downstream order from source towards plains:*
1. **Vishnuprayag**: **Alaknanda** $+$ **Dhauliganga** (Chamoli)
2. **Nandaprayag**: **Alaknanda** $+$ **Nandakini** (Chamoli)
3. **Karnaprayag**: **Alaknanda** $+$ **Pindar** (Chamoli)
4. **Rudraprayag**: **Alaknanda** $+$ **Mandakini** (Rudraprayag)
5. **Devprayag**: **Alaknanda** $+$ **Bhagirathi** $\to$ Official **GANGA** begins here! (Tehri Garhwal)

```
       Badrinath (Satopanth)
               │
           Alaknanda
               │ ◄─── Dhauliganga (Vishnuprayag)
               │
               │ ◄─── Nandakini (Nandaprayag)
               │
               │ ◄─── Pindar (Karnaprayag)
               │
               │ ◄─── Mandakini (Rudraprayag)  [from Kedarnath]
               │
               │ ◄─── Bhagirathi (Devprayag)   [from Gangotri / Gaumukh]
               ▼
             GANGA
```

### B. Ganga River Tributaries (Left vs Right Bank)
- **Left Bank Tributaries (West to East)**:
  1. **Ramganga** (originates in Dudhatoli range, Pauri Garhwal; flows through Jim Corbett NP; meets Ganga at Kannauj)
  2. **Gomati** (originates in plains at **Gomat Taal / Fulhar Lake, Pilibhit, UP** — *only Himalayan/Ganga tributary originating in the plains!*; Lucknow & Jaunpur on its banks; joins Ganga near Saidpur, Ghazipur)
  3. **Ghaghra / Karnali** (originates from Mapchachungo glacier near Mansarovar; joined by Sarda/Kali; **largest tributary by water volume**; joins Ganga at Chhapra, Bihar)
  4. **Gandak** (originates in Nepal Himalayas; joins Ganga at Sonpur / Hajipur, Bihar)
  5. **Kosi** (*Sorrow of Bihar*; 7 streams / Saptakoshi; notorious for course-shifting; joins at Kursela, Katihar)
  6. **Mahananda** (originates in Darjeeling hills; **last left-bank tributary of Ganga in India**).
- **Right Bank Tributaries**:
  1. **Yamuna** (longest tributary, 1376 km; Yamunotri glacier, Bandarpunch peak; joins at Prayagraj Triveni Sangam; its tributaries: Chambal, Sind, Betwa, Ken, Tons, Hindon)
  2. **Son** (originates from **Amarkantak Plateau, MP**; flows north-eastwards through Sonbhadra UP; joins Ganga near Danapur/Patna, Bihar)
  3. **Punpun** (originates in Palamu, Jharkhand; joins Ganga at Fatuha near Patna).

### C. Peninsular Drainage: West-Flowing vs East-Flowing
- **West-Flowing (into Arabian Sea — form Estuaries, NO deltas)**:
  - **Narmada** (1312 km): Amarkantak plateau (MP); flows through **Rift Valley** between Vindhyas (North) and Satpuras (South); forms **Dhuandhar Falls** at Marble Rocks (Bhedaghat, Jabalpur); Sardar Sarovar Dam.
  - **Tapi / Tapti** (724 km): Multai (Betul district, MP); flows in rift valley south of Satpuras; Ukai Dam & Kakrapar Project (Gujarat).
  - **Sabarmati**: Dhebar Lake (Aravalli Range, Udaipur); flows through Ahmedabad and Gandhinagar into Gulf of Khambhat.
  - **Mahi**: Vindhyan hills (Dhar, MP); **CUTS THE TROPIC OF CANCER TWICE!**
  - **Luni**: Originates near Ajmer (Aravallis) as Sagarmati; **ends in the marshy Rann of Kutch (Inland drainage, never reaches the sea)**; sweet water up to Balotra, then saline.
  - **Sharavati**: Shimoga (Karnataka); forms famous **Jog Falls (Gersoppa / Mahatma Gandhi Falls)**.
  - **Periyar & Bharathappuzha (Ponnani)**: Kerala.
- **East-Flowing (into Bay of Bengal — form large Deltas)**:
  - **Mahanadi** (851 km): Sihawa hills (Dhamtari, Chhattisgarh); flows through Odisha; Hirakud Dam.
  - **Godavari** (1465 km — **Dakshin Ganga / Vriddha Ganga**): Trimbakeshwar (Nashik, Maharashtra); **longest river in Peninsular India**; Tributaries: Pravara, Manjra, Penganga, Wainganga, Wardha, **Pranhita** (largest), Indravati, Sabari.
  - **Krishna** (1401 km): Mahabaleshwar (Maharashtra); Tributaries: Koyna, Ghataprabha, Malaprabha, Bhima, **Tungabhadra** (largest), Musi (Hyderabad on its banks), Munneru.
  - **Kaveri** (800 km — **Ganga of the South**): Talakaveri (Brahmagiri Range, Coorg, Karnataka); flows year-round (perennial due to SW & NE monsoons); creates river islands: **Srirangapatna, Shivanasamudra, and Srirangam**; Tributaries: Hemavati, Harangi, Shimsha, Arkavathi (left); Lakshmantirtha, Kabini, Bhavani, Noyyal, Amaravati (right).

## 6. World Physical Geography: Winds, Currents & Grasslands

### A. Local Winds (Hot vs Cold — High Frequency Match Questions)
- **Hot Winds**:
  - **Loo**: Northern Indian Plains (scorching summer westerly wind).
  - **Foehn**: Alps (Europe - Switzerland); dry warm wind; acts as **"snow eater"** helping grapes ripen.
  - **Chinook**: Rocky Mountains (USA / Canada); warm dry wind descending eastern slopes; **"snow eater"**.
  - **Sirocco**: Sahara Desert across Mediterranean into Italy/Spain; carries red dust causing **"Blood Rain"**.
  - **Harmattan**: Sahara towards Guinea coast (West Africa); pleasant dry wind known as **"The Doctor"**.
  - **Khamsin**: Egypt (hot, dry, sandy spring wind).
  - **Brickfielder**: Southern Australia (hot summer desert wind).
  - **Zonda**: Eastern slopes of Andes (Argentina).
  - **Simoom**: Arabian and Syrian deserts (suffocating dust-laden hot storm).
- **Cold Winds**:
  - **Mistral**: Massif Central / Alps through Rhone Valley into Mediterranean (France).
  - **Bora**: Dinaric Alps down to Adriatic Sea (Italy, Balkans).
  - **Blizzard**: Canada, Northern USA, Siberia (blinding snowstorm).
  - **Pampero**: Pampas of Argentina and Uruguay (cold violent wind from south).
  - **Purga / Buran**: Tundra / Siberia & Central Asia.

### B. Ocean Currents (Cold vs Warm Cheat-Sheet)
*Quick rule: Currents moving FROM Poles TO Equator are COLD; moving FROM Equator TO Poles are WARM.*

- **Atlantic Ocean**:
  - **Cold**: **Canary Current** (North Africa), **Labrador Current** (Canada/Greenland), **Benguela Current** (South-West Africa), **Falkland Current** (South America).
  - **Warm**: **Gulf Stream**, **North Atlantic Drift**, **Florida Current**, **Brazil Current**.
  - *(Meeting of Gulf Stream & Labrador Current off Newfoundland creates Grand Banks — dense fog & world's premier fishing ground!)*
- **Pacific Ocean**:
  - **Cold**: **California Current**, **Oyashio (Kuril) Current**, **Peru / Humboldt Current** (South America), **West Wind Drift**.
  - **Warm**: **Kuroshio (Japan) Current**, **North Pacific Drift**, **East Australian Current**.
- **Indian Ocean**:
  - **Cold**: **West Australian Current**.
  - **Warm**: **Mozambique Current**, **Agulhas Current**, **Leeuwin Current**.
