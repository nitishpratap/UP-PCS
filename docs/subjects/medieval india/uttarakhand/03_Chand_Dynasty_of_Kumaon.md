## Must-Score Facts

1. Somchand founded the Chand house of Kumaon.
2. Champawat → Almora capital shift (Kalyan Chand / 1563 tradition).
3. Char Aal = Karki + Bora + Taragi + Chaudhary.
4. Chand ≠ Chandela of Khajuraho.
5. Chand ≠ Parmar of Garhwal.
6. Gorkha conquest ends Chand rule (modern UK folder for Gorkha admin).

---

## 3.1 Rise after the Katyuris

### Causes / setting

- As Katyuri power fragments, Kali-Kumaon needs a new consolidating house.
- Local fort control and clan military service create the base for Chand kingship.

### Course

- The **Chands** establish themselves in **Kali-Kumaon**.
- Traditional genealogies push Somchand very early; historians usually place secure Chand rule from about the **11th century** after Katyuri decline.
- For Prelims, remember **Somchand = founder** and **Champawat = first seat**.
- Somchand is credited with appointing fort guards from four clans later remembered as **Char Aal**.

### Results

- Champawat becomes the early Chand capital landscape (Baleshwar and related temples mark the old seat).
- The house later expands across Kumaon and fights long border wars with Garhwal.
- The dynasty lasts into the late 18th century until **Gorkha** conquest.

---

## 3.2 Capitals and expansion

- Early political centre: **Champawat** (Rajbunga / Kali-Kumaon).
- **Kalyan Chand** shifts the capital to **Almora** in the mid-16th century (**1563** tradition; Alam Nagar / Rajapur memory in notes).
- Chands expand across Kumaon valleys and keep a tense frontier with Parmar Garhwal.
- Do not treat Almora as the first capital — Champawat comes first.

---

## 3.3 Administration ratta — Char Aal

**Somchand | four fort guards | Char Aal**

- Somchand is credited with appointing fort guards from four clans: **Karki, Bora, Taragi/Tadagi, and Chaudhary**.
- Together they are called **Char Aal**.
- UKPCS 2025 asked both the clan list and the collective name in one multi-statement stem.
- Dropping either the four names or the label **Char Aal** loses the mark.
- Do not confuse Char Aal with **Char Dham**.

---

## 3.4 End of Chand rule

- Standard lists place the end of Chand independence with **Gorkha** conquest about **1790**.
- Full Gorkha administration, Coolie-Begar, and British takeover cards sit in the Modern UK folder.
- Keep only the end-date pointer here so the medieval house does not spill into colonial detail.

## Common Traps

1. **Char Aal = Char Dham** — different ideas.
2. **Somchand founded Garhwal Parmars** — that is Kanakpal.
3. **Almora was always the first capital** — Champawat first.
4. **Chand = Chandela** — wrong region and temples.
5. **Gorkha founding of Chand rule** — Gorkha conquest ends it.
