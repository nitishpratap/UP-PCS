## 3.1 Rise after the Katyuris

- As Katyuri power fragments, the **Chands** establish themselves in **Kali-Kumaon**.
- Traditional genealogies push Somchand very early; historians usually place secure Chand rule from about the **11th century** after Katyuri decline.
- For Prelims, remember **Somchand = founder** and **Champawat = first seat**.

---

## 3.2 Capitals and expansion

- Early political centre: **Champawat** (temples such as Baleshwar mark the old capital landscape).
- **Kalyan Chand** shifts the capital to **Almora** in the mid-16th century (1563 tradition).
- Chands expand across Kumaon and fight long border wars with Garhwal.
- The dynasty lasts into the late 18th century until **Gorkha** conquest.

---

## 3.3 Administration ratta — Char Aal

**Somchand | four fort guards | Char Aal**

- Somchand is credited with appointing fort guards from four clans: **Karki, Bora, Taragi/Tadagi, and Chaudhary**.
- Together they are called **Char Aal**.
- UKPCS 2025 asked both the clan list and the collective name in one multi-statement stem.

---

## Must-Score Facts

1. Somchand founded the Chand house of Kumaon.
2. Champawat → Almora capital shift.
3. Char Aal = Karki + Bora + Taragi + Chaudhary.
4. Chand ≠ Chandela of Khajuraho.
5. Gorkha conquest ends Chand rule (modern UK folder for Gorkha admin).

## Common Traps

1. **Char Aal = Char Dham** → different ideas.
2. **Somchand founded Garhwal Parmars** → that is Kanakpal.
3. **Almora was always the first capital** → Champawat first.
4. **Chand = Chandela** → wrong region and temples.
