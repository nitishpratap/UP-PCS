# Subject Notes

Prelims-oriented notes mapped to the UP PCS / UPSC syllabus.

!!! tip "Switch subjects from the left sidebar"
    Open the **Subject Notes** tab, then expand any subject dropdown to jump between chapters without leaving the reading pane.

## How to use these notes

1. Expand a subject below (or in the left nav) and open **Syllabus** first.
2. Read numbered chapters in order (`01_…`, `02_…`).
3. After each chapter: close the page and recall 8–10 points without looking.
4. Same week: search the topic in [Mains PYQs](../mains-pyqs/index.md) and write one short answer.

!!! note "Coverage status"
    History, Geography, Environment, and Art & Culture are the densest tracks right now. **Polity** is still thin — fill chapters as you go.
