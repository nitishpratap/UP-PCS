## 16.1 Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEFCC)

**Identity:** Nodal ministry | 1985/2014 | Delhi | Category A EC | PARIVESH

- **MoEFCC** is India's **nodal ministry** for environment, forests, wildlife, and climate change policy.
- It was established as **Ministry of Environment & Forests (MoEF) in 1985**.
- It was renamed **MoEFCC in 2014** when climate change was formally added to the mandate.
- **Headquarters** is **New Delhi**.
- MoEFCC administers **EPA 1986**, **Wildlife (Protection) Act 1972**, **Forest (Conservation) Act 1980**, and **Biological Diversity Act 2002**.
- It grants **Category A Environmental Clearance** through the **Expert Appraisal Committee (EAC)**.
- The **PARIVESH portal** is MoEFCC's online system for EC applications.
- MoEFCC approves **forest land diversion** under FCA 1980.
- It coordinates India's role in **UNFCCC, CBD, and Montreal Protocol**.
- MoEFCC **does not** set **NAAQS** pollution standards — that is **CPCB's** role.
- MoEFCC **does not** publish **ISFR** — that is **FSI's** role.

> **Exam note:** MoEFCC was **not** created in 1972. **1972** = Stockholm Conference + WPA.

| Function | Body |
|----------|------|
| Category A EC | **MoEFCC** |
| Forest clearance | **MoEFCC** |
| NAAQS standards | **CPCB** |
| Category B EC | **SEIAA** |
| EC appeals | **NGT** |

## 16.2 Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) & State Pollution Control Board (SPCB)

**Identity:** Water Act 1974 | CPCB Delhi | SPCB consent | NOT EC authority

### CPCB

- **CPCB** was established in **1974** under the **Water (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act, 1974**.
- The **Air Act 1981** extended its role to air pollution control.
- **Headquarters** is **New Delhi**.
- CPCB advises the Centre, sets **National Ambient Air Quality Standards (NAAQS)**, and coordinates all **SPCBs**.
- It runs national **air and water monitoring** programmes.
- CPCB administers **EPR portals** for plastic, e-waste, battery, and tyre waste.
- CPCB **does not grant Environmental Clearance** — MoEFCC/SEIAA do.
- CPCB **does not publish ISFR** — FSI does.
- CPCB **does not prepare the Annual Groundwater Quality Report** — **CGWB** does.

### SPCB

- Every state has a **State Pollution Control Board** under the **Water Act 1974** and **Air Act 1981**.
- SPCBs issue **Consent to Establish (CTE)** and **Consent to Operate (CTO)** to industries.
- They enforce **CPCB-prescribed standards** through inspection, sampling, and penalties.
- SPCBs work under **CPCB technical guidance** and **state government** administrative control.
- **UPPCB** is Uttar Pradesh's board with HQ at **Lucknow**.
- UPPCB enforces norms on **Kanpur tanneries**, **Ghaziabad/Noida industries**, and **Ganga/Yamuna** discharges.
- SPCB **does not grant Environmental Clearance** — **SEIAA/MoEFCC** do.

| Term | Body | Purpose |
|------|------|---------|
| Consent (CTE/CTO) | **SPCB** | Pollution control for industry operation |
| Environmental Clearance | **MoEFCC/SEIAA** | Project impact assessment |
| Forest Clearance | **MoEFCC** | Forest land diversion |

## 16.3 National Green Tribunal (NGT)

**Identity:** NGT Act 2010 | Delhi HQ | Appeals | 6-month disposal target

- **NGT** was established under the **National Green Tribunal Act, 2010** and became operational in **2011**.
- **Headquarters** is **New Delhi** with regional benches at **Bhopal, Pune, Kolkata, and Chennai**.
- The **Chairperson** must be a retired **Supreme Court judge** or Chief Justice of a High Court.
- NGT hears **civil cases** with substantial environmental questions under EPA, WPA, FCA, Water Act, and Air Act.
- It hears **appeals against Environmental Clearance** decisions by MoEFCC/SEIAA.
- NGT can award **compensation** for environmental damage under the **Polluter Pays** principle.
- The Act targets disposal within **6 months**.
- NGT **does not grant EC** — it **adjudicates appeals**.
- NGT **does not set pollution standards** — CPCB does.

> **Exam note:** NGT Act = **2010**, not 1986.

## 16.4 Wildlife Institute of India (WII)

**Identity:** 1982 Dehradun | Wildlife research + training | NOT taxonomy

- **WII** was established in **1982** at **Dehradun** under **MoEFCC**.
- It is an autonomous institute for **wildlife research, science, and training**.
- WII trains **Indian Forest Service** and state forest officers.
- It helped delineate India's **10 biogeographic zones** (Rodgers & Panwar, 1988).
- WII develops **tiger census methodology** with NTCA and states.
- WII studies **protected areas**, **human-wildlife conflict**, and habitat management.
- WII **does not** maintain national plant/animal species catalogues — **BSI/ZSI** do.
- WII **does not** publish **ISFR** — **FSI** does.

---

## 16.5 Botanical Survey of India (BSI)

**Identity:** 1890 Kolkata | PLANTS | *Flora of India* | Red Data Book (plants)

- **BSI** was established in **1890** and is headquartered at **Kolkata**.
- It surveys and documents **Indian plant diversity** — flowering plants, ferns, algae, and fungi.
- BSI publishes ***Flora of India*** and state floras.
- It maintains the **Central National Herbarium** at Shibpur, Howrah.
- BSI publishes the **Red Data Book of Indian Plants**.
- BSI surveys **plants only** — **not animals**.

---

## 16.6 Zoological Survey of India (ZSI)

**Identity:** 1916 Kolkata | ANIMALS | *Fauna of India* | Red Data Book (animals)

- **ZSI** was established in **1916** and is headquartered at **Kolkata**.
- It surveys and documents **Indian animal diversity** from invertebrates to mammals.
- ZSI publishes the ***Fauna of India*** series.
- It maintains **National Zoological Collections** for species identification.
- ZSI publishes the **Red Data Book of Indian Animals**.
- ZSI supports **CITES** and **WPA Schedule** listing with scientific data.
- ZSI surveys **animals only** — **not plants**.

| Body | Est. | HQ | Kingdom |
|------|------|-----|---------|
| BSI | 1890 | Kolkata | **Plants** |
| ZSI | 1916 | Kolkata | **Animals** |
| WII | 1982 | Dehradun | Wildlife research |
| FSI | 1981 | Dehradun | Forest cover |

## 16.7 Forest Survey of India (FSI)

**Identity:** 1981 Dehradun | Biennial ISFR | Satellite forest mapping

- **FSI** was established in **1981** at **Dehradun** under **MoEFCC**.
- FSI assesses **forest cover** using satellite imagery and ground verification.
- It publishes the **India State of Forest Report (ISFR)** every **two years** — biennial, not annual.
- ISFR reports **forest cover classes**, **tree cover**, and **carbon stock** estimates.
- FSI **does not** monitor industrial air/water pollution — **CPCB/SPCB** do.
- FSI **does not** survey plant/animal species taxonomy — **BSI/ZSI** do.

| Groundwater Quality Report | **CGWB** |
| NAAQS bulletin | **CPCB** |
