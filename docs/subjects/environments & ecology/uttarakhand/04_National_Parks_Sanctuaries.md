## Consolidated — 15 Must-Score Facts (UK Protected Areas)

1. **Oldest National Park in India:** **Corbett National Park** was established in **1936** as *Hailey National Park* (later Ramganga (रामगंगा) NP in 1954, renamed Corbett in 1957); India’s first national park and the launch site of **Project Tiger (प्रोजेक्ट टाइगर) on 1 April 1973**.
2. **Largest National Park in Uttarakhand:** **Gangotri (गंगोत्री) National Park (2,390.02 sq. km)** located in **Uttarkashi** (उत्तरकाशी); covers Gaumukh (गौमुख) glacier and high-altitude alpine terrain.
3. **Smallest National Park in Uttarakhand:** **Valley of Flowers (फूलों की घाटी) National Park (87.50 sq. km)** in **Chamoli** (चमोली); discovered in 1931 by British mountaineer Frank S. Smythe; Pushpawati river flows through it.
4. **Largest Wildlife Sanctuary in Uttarakhand:** **Kedarnath (केदारनाथ) Wildlife Sanctuary (975.20 sq. km)** spread across **Chamoli and Rudraprayag (रुद्रप्रयाग)**; created in **1972** primarily to conserve the endangered Himalayan Musk Deer.
5. **Smallest Wildlife Sanctuary in Uttarakhand:** **Mussoorie (मसूरी) (Benog / Mountain Quail) Wildlife Sanctuary (10.82 sq. km)** in **Dehradun** (देहरादून); established in **1993**.
6. **Oldest Wildlife Sanctuary in Uttarakhand:** **Govind Wildlife Sanctuary (1955)** in **Uttarkashi**; covers 485.89 sq. km in the Tons (टौंस) river basin.
7. **Two Tiger Reserves of Uttarakhand:**
   - **Corbett Tiger Reserve:** Notified in **1973** (first in India).
   - **Rajaji (राजाजी) Tiger Reserve:** Notified in **2015** (48th Tiger Reserve of India; second in UK).
8. **UNESCO World Natural Heritage Sites:** **Nanda Devi (नंदा देवी) NP (1988)** and **Valley of Flowers NP (2005)** form **one composite UNESCO World Heritage (विश्व धरोहर) property** in Chamoli.
9. **Single Biosphere Reserve (जैवमंडल आरक्षित क्षेत्र):** **Nanda Devi Biosphere (नंदा देवी जैवमंडल) Reserve** was established in **1988** (core area: Nanda Devi NP; buffer: Valley of Flowers); included in UNESCO's World Network of Biosphere Reserves (जैवमंडल आरक्षित क्षेत्र) (**MAB program**) in **2004**.
10. **First Ramsar (रामसर) Wetland Site (रामसर आर्द्रभूमि स्थल) in Uttarakhand:** **Asan (आसन) Conservation Reserve (संरक्षण आरक्षित क्षेत्र) (Dehradun)** was declared Uttarakhand’s **first Ramsar site in October 2020** (at confluence of Yamuna (यमुना) and Asan rivers; winter refuge for Ruddy Shelduck).
11. **Jhilmil Jheel Conservation Reserve (Haridwar (हरिद्वार)):** Established in **2005** specifically for the conservation of the endangered **Swamp Deer (Barasingha — *Rucervus duvaucelii*)**.
12. **Musk Deer Breeding & Research Centres:**
    - **Mahroodi Musk Deer Research Centre:** Established in **1977** (in Bageshwar (बागेश्वर) / Pithoragarh (पिथौरागढ़) border).
    - **Kanchula Kharak Musk Deer Breeding Centre:** Established in **1982** near Chopta in **Chamoli district**.
    - **Askot Wildlife Sanctuary:** Established in **1986** in **Pithoragarh** (पिथौरागढ़); holds the highest density of Musk Deer.
13. **Rajaji National Park Amalgamation (1983):** Formed by amalgamating 3 contiguous wildlife sanctuaries: **Rajaji Sanctuary (1948)**, **Motichur Sanctuary (1964)**, and **Chilla Sanctuary (1977)**; named after Bharat Ratna (भारत रत्न) C. Rajagopalachari.
14. **Snow Leopard Conservation:** Centred in **Gangotri NP**, **Govind NP**, and **Nanda Devi NP**; India's first Snow Leopard Conservation Centre was set up in **Lanka, Uttarkashi**.
15. **Total Protected Areas Tally:** Uttarakhand has **6 National Parks**, **7 Wildlife Sanctuaries**, and **4 Conservation Reserves**.

## 2. Master Table: The 7 Wildlife Sanctuaries of Uttarakhand

| Wildlife Sanctuary (WLS) (वन्यजीव अभयारण्य) | Year Est. | Area ($\text{km}^2$) | District | Key Ecological Characteristics & Species Focus |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **Govind Wildlife Sanctuary** | **1955** | 485.89 | **Uttarkashi** | **Oldest wildlife sanctuary in Uttarakhand**; situated in Supin/Tons basin; Snow Leopard, Himalayan Tahr, Musk Deer, Monal. |
| **Kedarnath Wildlife Sanctuary**| **1972** | **975.20** *(Largest)* | **Chamoli & Rudraprayag** | **Largest wildlife sanctuary in Uttarakhand**; established primarily to protect the endangered **Himalayan Musk Deer (*Moschus chrysogaster*)**; Mandakini (मंदाकिनी) and Alaknanda (अलकनंदा) upper catchments. |
| **Askot Wildlife Sanctuary** | **1986** | 600.00 | **Pithoragarh** | Dedicated Musk Deer sanctuary in eastern Kumaon (कुमाऊँ) bordering Nepal and Tibet; highest documented density of Musk Deer; alpine bugyals and oak forests. |
| **Sonanadi Wildlife Sanctuary** | **1987** | 301.18 | **Pauri Garhwal** | Located north of the Ramganga reservoir; serves as the vital biological corridor connecting Corbett and Rajaji national parks; dense Sal forests; Tigers, Elephants, Cheetal. |
| **Binsar Wildlife Sanctuary** | **1988** | 47.07 | **Almora** (अल्मोड़ा) | Pristine temperate oak, rhododendron, and deodar forest atop Binsar hill (Zero Point); prominent birding sanctuary (over 200 bird species); Barking Deer, Leopard. |
| **Mussoorie (Benog) Wildlife Sanctuary**| **1993** | **10.82** *(Smallest)* | **Dehradun** | **Smallest wildlife sanctuary in Uttarakhand**; set up to conserve the critically endangered/possibly extinct **Himalayan Mountain Quail (*Ophrysia superciliosa*)**; dense Banj oak and pine woods. |
| **Nandhaur Wildlife Sanctuary**| **2012** | 269.95 | **Nainital & Champawat (चंपावत)** | Located in the Nandhaur river basin between Gola and Sharda (शारदा) rivers; part of the **Terai (तराई) Arc Landscape (TAL)**; vital elephant and tiger corridor. |

## 3. Conservation Reserves & Ramsar Site

| Protected Entity | Year | Area ($\text{km}^2$) | District | Conservation Significance & Exam Details |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **Asan Conservation Reserve** | **2005** | 4.44 | **Dehradun** | Declared **Uttarakhand's First Ramsar Site in October 2020**; situated at the confluence of Yamuna and Asan rivers; critical winter stopover on the Central Asian Flyway for migratory waterfowl (Ruddy Shelduck, Mallard, Gadwall). |

## Practice Zone: UKPCS Format Drill

**Q1.** Which is the largest National Park in Uttarakhand in terms of geographical area?  
A. Corbett National Park  
B. Nanda Devi National Park  
C. Gangotri National Park  
D. Rajaji National Park  
*Answer:* **C** (Gangotri National Park in Uttarkashi covers 2,390.02 sq. km).

**Q2.** Which of the following Wildlife Sanctuaries in Uttarakhand covers the largest geographical area?  
A. Askot Wildlife Sanctuary  
B. Kedarnath Wildlife Sanctuary  
C. Govind Wildlife Sanctuary  
D. Sonanadi Wildlife Sanctuary  
*Answer:* **B** (Kedarnath Wildlife Sanctuary covers 975.20 sq. km in Chamoli and Rudraprayag).

**Q3.** The famous 'Kanchula Kharak' centre in Chamoli district was established in 1982 for the breeding and conservation of:  
A. Snow Leopard  
B. Himalayan Musk Deer  
C. Himalayan Monal  
D. Asian Elephant  
*Answer:* **B** (Himalayan Musk Deer).

**Q4.** In which year was the 'Asan Conservation Reserve' in Dehradun designated as the first Ramsar Wetland Site of Uttarakhand?  
A. 2015  
B. 2018  
C. 2020  
D. 2022  
*Answer:* **C** (October 2020).

**Q5.** In which year was 'Project Tiger' launched in India from Corbett National Park?  
A. 1971  
B. 1972  
C. 1973  
D. 1975  
*Answer:* **C** (1 April 1973).

**Q6.** The endangered 'Swamp Deer' (Barasingha) is preserved in which of the following conservation reserves of Uttarakhand?  
A. Asan Conservation Reserve  
B. Jhilmil Jheel Conservation Reserve  
C. Pawalgarh Conservation Reserve  
D. Naina Devi Bird Reserve  
*Answer:* **B** (Jhilmil Jheel Conservation Reserve in Haridwar).
