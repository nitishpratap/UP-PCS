## 1. National Parks of Uttarakhand (Total 6)
*Sorted by Year of Establishment.*

| National Park | Year | Area (sq km) | District(s) | Key Feature / Species |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Corbett NP** | 1936 | 520.82 | Pauri & Nainital | India’s 1st NP (Hailey NP). Ramganga river flows through it. Project Tiger (1973). Patli Dun valley. |
| **Govind NP** | 1990 | 472.08 | Uttarkashi | Snow Leopard, Himalayan Monal, Musk Deer. Tons river basin. |
| **Nanda Devi NP** | 1982 | 624.60 | Chamoli | Himalayan Musk Deer, Snow Leopard. UNESCO World Heritage (1988). |
| **Valley of Flowers NP** | 1982 | **87.50** *(Smallest)* | Chamoli | Discovered by Frank S. Smythe (1931). Pushpawati river. UNESCO status (2005). |
| **Rajaji NP** | 1983 | 820.42 | Dehradun, Haridwar, Pauri | Amalgamation of Rajaji, Motichur & Chilla sanctuaries. Asian Elephant. Project Tiger (2015). |
| **Gangotri NP** | 1989 | **2390.02** *(Largest)* | Uttarkashi | Snow Leopard, Bharal. Gaumukh glacier is inside. |

## 2. Wildlife Sanctuaries of Uttarakhand (Total 7)
*Sorted by Year of Establishment.*

| Wildlife Sanctuary (WLS) | Year | Area (sq km) | District | Key Feature |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Govind WLS** | 1955 | 485.89 | Uttarkashi | Oldest sanctuary in the state. Snow leopard project (1990). |
| **Kedarnath WLS** | 1972 | **975.20** *(Largest)* | Chamoli & Rudraprayag | Established primarily for **Himalayan Musk Deer** conservation. |
| **Askot Musk Deer WLS** | 1986 | 600.00 | Pithoragarh | Highest population of Musk Deer. Bordering Nepal. |
| **Sonanadi WLS** | 1987 | 301.18 | Pauri Garhwal | Important corridor linking Corbett and Rajaji. |
| **Binsar WLS** | 1988 | 47.07 | Almora | Broad-leaved oak ranges; Zero Point. |
| **Mussoorie WLS** | 1993 | **10.82** *(Smallest)* | Dehradun | Known as Binog Mountain Quail Sanctuary. |
| **Nandhaur WLS** | 2012 | 269.95 | Nainital & Champawat | Part of Terai Arc Landscape. Tiger conservation focus. |

## 3. Conservation Reserves in Uttarakhand (Total 4)
| Conservation Reserve | Year | District | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

## Practice Zone - UKPCS Format Drill

**Q1.** Which is the largest National Park in Uttarakhand in terms of geographical area?
A. Corbett National Park
B. Nanda Devi National Park
C. Gangotri National Park
D. Rajaji National Park

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>
**Ans: C.** Gangotri National Park in Uttarkashi is the largest at 2390.02 sq km.
</details>

**Q2.** Match List-I (Wildlife Sanctuary) with List-II (District):
| List-I | List-II |
| :--- | :--- |
| A. Askot WLS | 1. Almora |
| B. Binsar WLS | 2. Dehradun |
| C. Mussoorie WLS | 3. Pithoragarh |
| D. Nandhaur WLS | 4. Nainital/Champawat |

A. A-3, B-1, C-2, D-4  
B. A-1, B-3, C-4, D-2  
C. A-3, B-2, C-1, D-4  
D. A-2, B-4, C-3, D-1  

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>
**Ans: A.** Askot is in Pithoragarh, Binsar is in Almora, Mussoorie is in Dehradun, Nandhaur is in Nainital/Champawat.
</details>

**Q3.** In which year was the Valley of Flowers declared a National Park?
A. 1936
B. 1982
C. 1988
D. 2005

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>
**Ans: B.** It became a NP in 1982 (It was listed by UNESCO in 2005).
</details>
