## Consolidated — 36 Must-Score Facts

1. **Primary** air pollutants are emitted directly (SO₂, PM, CO, NOx, lead). **Secondary** pollutants form in air (O₃, PAN, photochemical smog, acid mist).
2. **Lichens** are pollution indicators and do **not** grow well in badly polluted air.
3. India’s AQI uses **eight** pollutants (**PM10, PM2.5, NO₂, CO, O₃, SO₂, NH₃, Pb**) — **not CO₂/CH₄**. Bands: **Good (0–50)** to **Severe (401–500)**. NAQI launched **2014**.
4. **NCAP (2019)** targets non-attainment cities with about a **20–30%** particulate cut goal.
5. **London smog** is SO₂ plus smoke. **Photochemical smog** needs NOx, VOCs, and sunlight and yields **ozone + PAN**; **PAN** is a strong eye irritant.
6. **Fly ash** is a thermal-power primary pollutant usable in bricks/cement; oxides SiO₂/Al₂O₃/CaO **plus toxic metals**. **BS-VI** began **1 Apr 2020**.
7. **SAFAR** is an IITM air-quality and weather forecast system. **WAYU** units were installed in **Delhi**.
8. **CO** forms carboxyhaemoglobin (**~200–300×** affinity vs O₂). **Radon** is the top indoor air-pollutant teaching fact.
9. **BOD** measures organic pollution through microbial oxygen demand. High BOD means worse water quality.
10. **COD** is usually greater than or equal to BOD. Clean water often shows DO above about **6 mg/L** and BOD below about **3 mg/L**.
11. **Eutrophication** is nutrient (N/P) overload → algal bloom → dissolved-oxygen crash — water, not air.
12. The **Central Ground Water Board (CGWB)** issues the groundwater quality report. **CPCB** is not CGWB.
13. Classic arsenic hotspot teaching points to **Bangladesh** / Gangetic plains. **Knock-knee** = fluoride; **Black Foot** = arsenic; **Minamata** = Hg; **Itai-itai** = Cd.
14. **Damodar** = biological desert. Ganga BOD peak classically **Kanpur–Allahabad**. Leather industry = high chemical pollution.
15. Many pesticides are **not** readily biodegradable — a frequent true statement in papers.
16. Heavy-metal facts include **chromium** around Kanpur plus lead, cadmium, and mercury elsewhere. Mercury sources include **gold mining** and **coal TPPs**; **no known safe exposure level**.
17. Daytime noise standards (dB): Silence **50**, Residential **55**, Commercial **65**, Industrial **75**. **Green muffler** = tree belts for noise.
18. **Moradabad** was flagged among the world’s noisiest cities in UNEP Frontier reporting.
19. About **80%** of marine pollution comes from land. **MARPOL** governs ship-source pollution. **IMO HQ = London**. Ocean acidification harms calcareous life and corals.
20. Plastic Waste Management Rules date from **2016**. The SUP ban push is **2022**. EPR is the producer-responsibility tool. Microbeads harm marine ecosystems.
21. India ranks among the world’s largest plastic-waste generators (often taught as about **third**).
22. Indoor air pollution is driven by solid cooking fuels, tobacco smoke, poor ventilation, and **radon**.
23. Surface-water pollution includes sewage, industrial effluent, and agricultural runoff into rivers and lakes.
24. Drinking-water quality in India is commonly referenced to **IS 10500** standards.
25. Vehicular emissions: **catalytic converters** (Pt/Pd/Rh); Euro-II diesel S **≤0.05%**; India leapfrogged to **BS-VI (2020)**.
26. Coastal pollution includes oil, plastics, sewage, and industrial discharge along the shoreline belt.
27. Do not call photochemical smog a simple SO₂–smoke fog. Ozone chemistry is the key.
28. SAMEER-type apps publish AQI for public warning; they do not replace NCAP city action plans.
29. **CCAC** focuses on **methane, black carbon, HFCs** — not a G20-only initiative.
30. **Asian Brown Cloud** is especially associated with **South Asia**. Bag filter ≠ cyclone divider.
31. **Bioremediation** cleans via microbes; **Cd/Pb** not readily treated; **Oilzapper** (TERI) for oily waste.
32. **Bhopal MIC (Dec 1984)**; **Chernobyl (1986)** radioactive disaster teaching pair.
33. **SWM Rules 2016** give elaborate landfill/site criteria. **R2 Code** = responsible electronics recycling.
34. **H-CNG** reduces CO/HC/CO₂ vs CNG but does **not eliminate** CO; up to ~**1/5** H₂ by volume; not cheaper than CNG.
35. Geoengineering teaching: **cirrus thinning** and **sulphate aerosol** injection aim at cooling / offsetting warming — not cyclone control.
36. Steel industry emits **CO, CO₂, SOx, and NOx**. **Aflatoxins** = mould toxins on poorly stored grains.

## 38.1 Sources of Air Pollution

**Identity:** Point / non-point | Sectoral | Primary pollutants

- **Point sources** are chimneys, stacks, and pipes; **non-point sources** include roads, farms, and open burning.
- Major sectors: power, industry, vehicles, construction dust, biomass/stubble burning, and households.
- Primary pollutants include **SO₂, NOx, CO, PM, and VOCs**.
- UP winter peaks link stubble smoke, vehicles, industry, and Indo-Gangetic inversion.

> ** note:** Stubble smoke is a classic non-point seasonal source in north India.

---

## 38.2 Air Quality

**Identity:** NAAQS | Monitoring | Lichens | SAFAR

- **Air quality** is judged against CPCB **National Ambient Air Quality Standards (NAAQS)**.
- Key pollutants monitored: PM2.5, PM10, SO₂, NO₂, O₃, CO, NH₃, Pb, and related species.
- **Lichens** are sensitive bio-indicators of air pollution, especially SO₂ stress.
- They indicate pollution because they are **sensitive** — they do **not** grow well in polluted air.
- **SAFAR** (System of Air Quality and Weather Forecasting and Research) is run with **IITM** support for forecast products.

## 38.3 Air Quality Index (AQI)

**Identity:** CPCB 0–500 | Eight pollutants | Health categories | Sub-indices

- India’s **AQI** (CPCB) runs from **0 to 500**. **National Air Quality Index (NAQI)** launched **17 October 2014** under the “one number–one colour–one description” idea.
- Categories: **0–50 Good**, **51–100 Satisfactory**, **101–200 Moderate**, **201–300 Poor**, **301–400 Very Poor**, **401–500 Severe**.
- Eight pollutants: **PM10, PM2.5, NO₂, CO, O₃, SO₂, NH₃, Pb**. **CO₂ and CH₄ are not AQI parameters** (they are climate/GHG metrics).
- CO and O₃ use **8-hour** averages; most others use **24-hour** averages. CO is reported in **mg/m³**; NO₂/O₃ typically in **µg/m³**.
- AQI uses sub-indices for pollutants and reports the worst as the day’s AQI.
- PM2.5 and PM10 often drive winter AQI in UP NCR/plain cities.
- WHO guideline teaching (updated): annual PM2.5 **≤5 µg/m³**, 24-h PM2.5 **≤15 µg/m³**. **PM2.5** (not PM10) can enter the bloodstream; ozone can trigger asthma. Highest ozone is **not** typically in “inclement weather.”

| AQI | Category |
|-----|----------|
| 0–50 | Good |
| 51–100 | Satisfactory |
| 101–200 | Moderate |
| 201–300 | Poor |
| 301–400 | Very Poor |
| 401–500 | Severe |

> **Logic:** Higher AQI = worse air. CO₂/CH₄ ≠ AQI.

## 38.4 National Clean Air Programme (NCAP)

**Identity:** 2019 | 122 cities | 20–30% PM reduction target

- **NCAP** launched in **2019** to improve air quality in non-attainment cities.
- Covers **122 cities** with city action plans, monitoring, and source control.
- Target framework: about **20–30% reduction in PM concentrations** over the programme timeline (baseline-linked).
- UP NCAP cities include Lucknow, Kanpur, Ghaziabad, Agra, Varanasi, Meerut, Noida, Prayagraj, Moradabad, Bareilly, and others notified over time.
- Complements BS-VI fuel, dust control, and industrial CETP/stack measures.

> ** note:** NCAP = city clean-air programme, not a groundwater body.

## 38.6 Photochemical Smog

**Identity:** NOx + VOC + sunlight → O₃ / PAN | LA type | Eye irritation

- **Photochemical smog** forms when **NOx and VOCs/hydrocarbons** react in strong sunlight.
- Engine path: N₂ + O₂ → NO; then NO → NO₂; NO₂ + light → NO + O; O + O₂ → **O₃**; hydrocarbons + NOx → **PAN**.
- It always involves **ozone** as a key secondary product; **PAN**, formaldehyde, and aldehydes also appear.
- **PAN (peroxyacetyl nitrate)** is a powerful **eye irritant** (lachrymator) and damages plant chloroplasts.
- Typical of traffic-rich sunny cities (Los Angeles type); also relevant to Indian summer/pre-monsoon urban episodes.
- The brown-orange haze of photochemical smog is a **secondary** pollution state — SO₂ stack gas alone is **primary**.

## 38.7 Industrial Emissions

**Identity:** Stacks / SPM / SO₂ | Fly ash | CETP | Kanpur tanneries

- Industry emits PM, SO₂, NOx, VOCs, and process-specific toxins.
- **Fly ash** pollution is caused by **thermal power plants**.
- Fly ash is used for **bricks** and as a **Portland cement** replacement in concrete.
- Main chemical components: **SiO₂, Al₂O₃**, and sometimes **CaO**, plus toxic elements such as **lead, arsenic, and copper** — not “oxides only.”
- Control tools: ESPs, **bag filters** (finer PM, often **<~50 μm**), **cyclone separators/collectors** (coarser, **>~50 μm**), FGD, wet scrubbers, and **CETPs** for clusters.
- **Cyclone divider** is **not** a standard instrumental device name for air-pollution control.
- Mercury teaching: **gold mining** and **coal TPPs** are major sources; **no known safe level** of mercury exposure.
- UP geography: Kanpur tanneries (chromium/organics), Singrauli thermal belt, brick kilns.

## 38.8 Vehicular Emissions

**Identity:** CO / NOx / PM / VOCs | BS-VI 2020 | Urban traffic

- Vehicles emit **CO, NOx, PM, VOCs**, and CO₂; incomplete combustion and cigarette smoke classically yield **CO**.
- Lead antiknock in petrol was a major source of airborne lead toxicity.
- India moved to **BS-VI** norms nationwide from **1 April 2020**, leapfrogging BS-V after **BS-IV** from **1 April 2017**.
- Euro norms limit CO, hydrocarbons, NOx, and PM. For **Euro-II**, ultra-low sulphur diesel needs sulphur **≤0.05%**.
- **Catalytic converters** use **platinum, palladium, and rhodium** to convert CO → CO₂, NOx → N₂, and HC → CO₂ + H₂O.
- Congestion, diesel fleets, and two-wheelers drive city NO₂/PM peaks.
- Complementary tools: EV push, PUC checks, odd-even-type episodic measures, CAFE efficiency norms.
- **H-CNG** (hydrogen-enriched CNG) can cut CO/HC/CO₂ versus CNG; hydrogen up to about **one-fifth** by volume can be blended; it does **not eliminate** CO and is **not** cheaper than CNG.
- **CCAC** (Climate and Clean Air Coalition, **2012**, with UNEP) focuses on **methane, black carbon, and HFCs** — statement that it is a unique G20-only initiative is false.

> **Logic:** BS-VI cut sulphur in fuel and tightened NOx/PM limits versus BS-IV. CCAC ≠ G20 club.

## 38.9 Indoor Air Pollution

**Identity:** Biomass chulha | PM2.5 | Women/children exposure

- Indoor pollution comes from **biomass cookstoves**, kerosene, incense, and tobacco smoke.
- Cigarette smoke contains **CO, HCN, NOx, formaldehyde, acrolein, benzene**, and small amounts of **nicotine**.
- Benzene exposure sources include automobile exhaust, tobacco smoke, wood burning, varnished furniture, and polyurethane products.
- The most significant classic **indoor** pollutant gas in many stems is **radon** — colourless, odourless, radioactive, from soil/radium, linked to lung cancer.
- Rural indoor PM2.5 can exceed outdoor levels and heavily affects women and children.
- Clean cooking (LPG/PNG/electric) is the main mitigation path (Ujjwala-type coverage).
- **Asian Brown Cloud** / atmospheric brown cloud is especially noted over **South Asia** (often Jan–Mar), rich in aerosols including black carbon and sulphate.

> **Logic:** Indoor air is a household health issue, not only an outdoor AQI story. Radon ≠ SO₂ as “most important indoor.”

## 38.10 Surface Water Pollution

**Identity:** BOD / COD / DO | Sewage / industry | Eutrophication

- Surface waters are polluted by sewage, industrial effluent, and agricultural runoff.
- **BOD** measures oxygen used by microbes decomposing organic matter — a standard **aquatic pollution assay**.
- Higher BOD means worse organic pollution; **COD ≥ BOD**.
- Clean-ish river fact: **DO > ~6 mg/L**, **BOD < ~3 mg/L**. Teaching banks often call DO **<4 mg/L** highly polluted.
- **Eutrophication:** excess **N and P** → algal bloom → DO crash → fish kill. Eutrophication is **not** an air-pollution process.
- Highest Ganga **BOD** stretch is classically **Kanpur–Allahabad**. **Damodar** is the classic **biological desert** river.
- Leather-industry effluent is a top chemical pollution source for water and soil.
- UP rivers: Ganga (Kanpur stretch), Gomti (Lucknow), Yamuna (Mathura–Agra influence).

## 38.11 Groundwater Pollution

**Identity:** Arsenic / fluoride / nitrate | CGWB reports

- Groundwater contaminants include **arsenic, fluoride, nitrate, iron, salinity**, uranium, and industrial leachates.
- **Arsenic** contamination is classically associated with the **Bangladesh** / lower Gangetic basin story; first Indian notice often dated to **West Bengal, 1978**.
- Affected Indian geography teaching: West Bengal, Jharkhand, Bihar, UP, Assam, Manipur, and parts of Chhattisgarh.
- Chronic arsenic exposure links to cancers and **Black Foot** disease teaching. WHO guideline often cited as **0.01 mg/L**.
- **Fluoride** excess causes fluorosis and **knock-knee** syndrome. **Nitrate** links to blue-baby risk.
- The **Annual Groundwater Quality Report** is prepared by the **Central Ground Water Board (CGWB)** — not CPCB.

## 38.12 Drinking Water Pollution

**Identity:** Pathogens | Coliform | Fluorosis / arsenicosis | IS 10500

- Unsafe drinking water carries pathogens (diarrhoea, cholera, typhoid risk) and chemical toxins.
- Microbial safety uses **coliform / E. coli** indicators.
- Chronic chemical risks: **fluorosis** (fluoride), **arsenicosis** (arsenic), nitrate (blue-baby risk in infants).
- Treatment chain: source protection → filtration/disinfection → safe storage.

> ** note:** Microbial and chemical risks are different drinking-water failure modes.

## 38.13 Water Quality Standards

**Identity:** Water Act 1974 | BIS IS 10500 | CPCB river classes

- **Water (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act, 1974** is the core water-pollution statute.
- **BIS IS 10500** sets drinking-water specification limits.
- CPCB classifies river stretches for designated best uses (A–E style classes).
- CPCB/SPCB enforce consent, standards, and monitoring for effluent and ambient water.

> ** note:** Drinking standard (IS 10500) ≠ ambient river class alone.

## 38.14 Fertilizers and Environment

- Result: eutrophication, nitrate groundwater rise, and N₂O climate link (Topic 37).
- Indo-Gangetic wheat–rice systems are high-input geography.

> ** note:** Fertiliser pollution is mostly nutrient overload, not heavy-metal tanning.

## 38.16 Heavy Metal Pollution

**Identity:** Pb / Cd / Cr / Hg | Bioaccumulation | Kanpur Cr

- Heavy metals do not degrade; they **bioaccumulate**.
- Key facts: **Pb** (batteries/paint/antiknock petrol legacy), **Cd** (batteries/industry — **Itai-itai**), **Cr** (tanneries — Kanpur), **Hg** (**Minamata**; gold mining + coal TPP sources; **no known safe exposure level**).
- Asbestos dust → lung disease / mesothelioma teaching. Soil particles → breathing ailments. NOx → cancer risk teaching in some match lists. CO displaces oxygen in blood.
- Soil and sediment act as long-term sinks near industrial clusters.

## 38.19 Coastal Pollution

**Identity:** Land runoff | Ports / sewage | Mangrove stress

- Coastal pollution comes mainly from **land-based** sewage, industry, ports, aquaculture, and plastics.
- Damages mangroves, estuaries, turtle nesting beaches, and nearshore fisheries.
- India’s first **National Centre for Marine Biodiversity (NCMB)** is at **Jamnagar (Gujarat)** — coastal biodiversity safeguarding, not a CPCB/NGT fact.

> ** note:** Coastal pollution is mostly land-origin, not only ship dumps.

---

## 38.20 Ocean Pollution

**Identity:** MARPOL | Oil / plastic / nutrients | ~80% land-based

- About **80%** of marine pollution originates on **land**.
- **MARPOL** is the key international ship-pollution convention. **IMO** headquarters is in **London**.
- **Ocean acidification** (CO₂ → falling ocean pH) harms calcareous phytoplankton, coral reefs, and some animals with phytoplankton larvae — cloud seeding is **not** the standard concern list.
- Harmful algal blooms link to estuary nutrients, monsoon run-off, and upwelling.
- **SACEP** = South Asia Cooperative Environment Programme.
- Major issues: oil spills, nutrient dead zones, and plastic/microplastic accumulation in gyres.
- Coral stress combines warming and local pollution (cross-topic with climate/coral bleaching).

> **Logic:** Ocean pollution ≠ only mid-ocean dumping. Acidification ≠ cloud seeding.

---

## 38.21 Plastic Waste Management

**Identity:** PWM Rules 2016 | SUP ban 2022 | EPR | Microplastics

- **Plastic Waste Management Rules, 2016** (amended later) govern collection, recycling, and producer duties.
- **Single-Use Plastic (SUP)** ban notification effective **July 2022** for identified items.
- **EPR** makes producers responsible for end-of-life plastic; EPR featured early in plastic/e-waste rules from **2011**.
- India is among the world’s largest plastic-waste generators (~**3rd** in common framing).
- **Microplastics** are plastic particles **< 5 mm**. **Microbeads** in cosmetics are harmful to **marine ecosystems**.
- Polythene bags persist because they are **polymers** of ethylene, not because they are proteins.

> **Logic:** PWM/EPR = management law track; SUP ban = item-specific prohibition track.
