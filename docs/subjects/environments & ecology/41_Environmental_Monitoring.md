## 41.1 Environmental Indicators

**Identity:** PSR framework | BOD/COD/DO/AQI/dB | Bio-indicators | Ringelmann

- **Environmental indicators** turn raw data into decision signals for policy and public warning.
- **PSR model:** **Pressure** (emissions, resource use) → **State** (ambient quality) → **Response** (treatment capacity, action plans).
- Air indicators: **PM2.5, PM10, SO₂, NO₂, CO, O₃** (and AQI composite 0–500).
- Water indicators: **BOD, COD, DO, pH, coliform**. Higher BOD = more organic pollution; **COD ≥ BOD**; healthy streams need adequate **DO**.
- Noise indicator: **decibel (dB)** — logarithmic; CPCB zone limits apply.
- **Ringelmann scale (0–5)** measures **smoke plume density/opacity**, not water, fog, or noise.
- Bio-indicators: **lichens** (SO₂-sensitive air quality); aquatic macroinvertebrates for clean streams.

## 41.2 Environmental Monitoring

**Identity:** Networks | CPCB vs CGWB | SAFAR | NCAP | SPCB consents

- **Environmental monitoring** is systematic, repeated measurement of air, water, soil, noise, and related parameters against standards.
- Cycle: sample → analyse → compare with norms → report → enforce/public alert.
- **CPCB** runs national **air** and **surface-water** programmes and sets ambient/effluent standards with SPCBs.
- **NAMP** = manual air stations for long-term trends; **CAAQMS** = continuous real-time ambient air (feeds AQI boards).
- **NWMP** = National Water Quality Monitoring Programme for rivers/lakes.
- **CGWB** monitors **groundwater** levels and quality; publishes the **Annual Groundwater Quality Report** — not CPCB.
- **SAFAR** (IITM) combines air-quality monitoring with weather forecasting; inaugurated by **Dr. Harsh Vardhan** (July 2018).
- **NCAP (2019)** expands monitoring and action in non-attainment cities (including multiple UP cities).
- **SAMEER** app disseminates CPCB AQI; **PARIVESH** tracks EC compliance reporting.
- **UPPCB** issues Consent to Establish/Operate and inspects industries in Uttar Pradesh.

## 41.3 Environmental Audit

**Identity:** Compliance verification | Form V | ISO 14001 | ≠ monitoring alone

- **Environmental audit** is a systematic, documented check of whether an organisation meets environmental laws, consent conditions, and internal EMS goals.
- Monitoring **collects data**; audit **verifies compliance** and management systems using that data (and inspections).
- Under **EPA Rules, Rule 14**, specified industries file an annual **Environmental Statement (Form V)** with the SPCB — India’s statutory disclosure/audit-type tool.
- **ISO 14001** is the voluntary international standard for an **Environmental Management System (EMS)**.
- Post-EC projects submit periodic **compliance reports** (often six-monthly) — audit/compliance track linked to EIA (Topic 14).
- Green audits in institutions review energy, water, waste, and campus ecology.
- Consent to Operate renewal typically depends on monitoring results plus inspection/audit findings.

| Tool | Role |
|------|------|
| Form V statement | Annual statutory disclosure to SPCB |
| ISO 14001 | Voluntary EMS certification |
| EC compliance report | Project clearance conditions |
| SPCB inspection | Enforcement audit on ground |

> **Exam note:** Passing an AQI reading ≠ completing an environmental audit.
