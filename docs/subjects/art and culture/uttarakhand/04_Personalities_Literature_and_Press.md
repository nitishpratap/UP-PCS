### 2.2 Modern Literary Figures & National Giants

#### 1. Sumitranandan Pant (1900–1977) — *The Wordsworth of Chhayavad*
- **Birth:** 20 May 1900 in **Kausani** (Bageshwar (बागेश्वर) district; earlier Almora). Childhood name: *Gosain Datt*.
- **Pioneering Accolade:** **First Hindi author to receive the prestigious Jnanpith Award in 1968** for his poetry anthology ***Chidambara***.
- **Sahitya (साहित्य) Akademi (साहित्य अकादेमी) Award (साहित्य अकादमी) (1960):** For ***Kala Aur Burha Chand***.
- **Padma (पद्मा) Bhushan:** Conferred in **1961**.
- **Nehru (नेहरू) Peace Prize:** For ***Lokayatan*** (monumental philosophical epic).
- **Other Famous Works:** *Veena*, *Granthi*, *Pallav*, *Gunjan*, *Yugant*, *Yugvani*, *Gramya*, *Swarna Dhuli*.
- **Museum:** His ancestral house at Kausani is preserved as the **Sumitranandan Pant Pant Memorial Gallery / Museum**.

#### 2. Dr. Pitambar Datt Barthwal (1901–1944) — *Pioneer of Hindi Research*
- **Birth:** 1901 in Pali (पाली) village, Lansdowne (Pauri Garhwal).
- **Landmark Record:** **First scholar to be awarded a D.Litt. (Doctor of Letters) in Hindi literature** from Banaras Hindu University (BHU) in 1931.
- **Seminal Doctoral Thesis:** *"The Nirguna School of Hindi Poetry"* (under the supervision of Dr. Shyam Sundar Das).
- **Major Contribution:** Discovered and authenticly edited ***Gorakhbani** (गोरखबानी)* (compositions of Guru (गुरु) Gorakhnath and Nath sect).
- **Honour:** Government of Uttarakhand established the **Dr. Pitambar Datt Barthwal Hindi Academy** at Dehradun.

#### 3. Gaura Pant 'Shivani' (1923–2003) — *Queen of Hindi Fiction*
- **Birth:** 1923 in Rajkot (family origin from Almora, Kumaon). Educated at Rabindranath Tagore's Visva-Bharati, Shantiniketan.
- **National Honour:** **Padma Shri in 1982**.
- **Famous Novels & Stories:** ***Chaudah Phere***, ***Krishnakali***, ***Karier Chhima***, ***Bhairavi***, *Smritijharokha*, *Surangma*, *Aparadhini*, *Yantrik*.
- **Themes:** Vivid portrayal of Kumaoni cultural households, women's psychological resilience, and domestic social life.

#### 4. Shailesh Matiyani (1931–2001) — *Doyen of Anchalik Literature*
- **Birth:** 14 October 1931 at Barechhina (Almora). Real name: *Ramesh Singh Matiyani*.
- **Pioneering Work:** Master of regional fiction depicting the harrowing struggles of common hill folk and urban (नगरीय) pavement dwellers.
- **Magnum Opus Novels:** ***Borivali Se Boribandar Tak***, ***Kabutarkhana***, ***Hul** (हूल)*, *Kisse Rahmatullah Ke*, *Muthbhed*.
- **Honour:** The Uttarakhand State Government confers the annual **State Teacher Award as the "Shailesh Matiyani State Educational Excellence Award"**.

#### 5. Manglesh Dabral (1948–2020) — *Acclaimed Poet & Editor*
- **Birth:** Kafalpani village (Tehri Garhwal).
- **Sahitya Akademi Award (अकादमी पुरस्कार) (2000):** Awarded for his poetry collection ***Ham Jo Dekhte Hain***.
- **Famous Poetry Volumes:** ***Pahar Par Lalten*** (1981), ***Ghar Ka Rasta*** (1988), ***Awaz Bhi Ek Jagah Hai*** (2000), ***Naye Yug Mein Shatru***.
- **Prose & Travelogues:** *Ek Bar Aiowa*, *Lekhak Ki Roti*, *Kavi Ka Akelapan*.

#### 6. Ruskin Bond (born 1934) — *The Sage of Landour (Mussoorie)*
- **Residence:** Ivy Cottage, Landour, Mussoorie since 1963.
- **Sahitya Akademi Award (1992):** For English novel ***Our Trees Still Grow in Dehra***.
- **Padma Honours:** **Padma Shri (1999)** and **Padma Bhushan (2014)**.
- **Famous Works:** *The Room on the Roof* (John Llewellyn Rhys Prize 1957), *The Blue Umbrella*, *A Flight of Pigeons* (adapted into movie *Junoon* by Shyam Benegal), *Roads to Mussoorie*.

#### 7. Other Notable Literary Icons
- **Vidyasagar (विद्यासागर) Nautiyal:** Progressive leader of Tehri Praja Mandal; wrote *Bhains Ka Katya*, *Soochi Patra*, *Suraj Eka-ki Hai*.
- **Himanshu Joshi:** Acclaimed novelist from Kumaon; wrote *Kagar Ki Aag*, *Suraj Chamke Aadhi Raat*, *Tumhare Liye*.
- **Lalit Mohan Thapalyal:** Celebrated dramatist; wrote the classic Garhwali play *Prahlad*.
- **Girish Tiwari 'Girda':** People's poet and playwright; wrote *Rangdari*, *Nagare Khamosh Hain*; inspired Chipko and Uttarakhand statehood movements through protest ballads.

Uttarakhand has a glorious martial tradition with two dedicated army (सेना) regiments: the **Kumaon Regiment** (HQ at **Ranikhet**, est. 1788, shifted to Ranikhet in 1948) and the **Garhwal Rifles** (HQ at **Lansdowne**, est. 1887 by Lord Dufferin (डफ़रिन) at Kaludanda).

### 4.1 Victoria Cross (VC) Recipients (World War I)
1. **Naik Darban Singh Negi (1/39 Garhwal Rifles):**
   - Awarded the **Victoria Cross in 1914** for supreme bravery at the **Battle of Festubert (France)** on the night of 23–24 November 1914.
   - One of the first Indian soldiers ever to receive Britain's highest military decoration.
2. **Rifleman Gabar Singh Negi (2/39 Garhwal Rifles):**
   - Posthumously awarded the **Victoria Cross in 1915** for heroism at the **Battle of Neuve-Chapelle (France)** in March 1915.
   - Annual Gabar Singh Memorial Fair is celebrated in **Chamba (Tehri Garhwal)**.

### 4.2 Param Vir Chakra (PVC) Recipients
1. **Major Somnath Sharma (4 Kumaon Regiment):**
   - **First recipient of India's highest gallantry award, the Param Vir Chakra (posthumous)**.
   - Action: **3 November 1947 at Badgam (Kashmir Valley)**. Despite being heavily outnumbered and his arm in a plaster cast, he repelled tribal (आदिवासी) raiders, saving the Srinagar airfield. His immortal final transmission: *"The enemy are only 50 yards from us. We are heavily outnumbered. We are under devastating fire. I shall not withdraw an inch but will fight to the last man and our last round."*
2. **Major Shaitan Singh (13 Kumaon Regiment):**
   - Awarded the **Param Vir Chakra posthumously** for the epic **Battle of Rezang La (Chushul, Ladakh (लद्दाख))** on **18 November 1962** during the Sino-Indian War.
   - Under -30°C temperature, his company (कंपनी) of 120 Kumaoni soldiers fought to the last breath, inflicting over a thousand casualties on the advancing Chinese troops.

### 4.3 Maha Vir Chakra (MVC) & Iconic War Heroes
1. **Rifleman Jaswant Singh Rawat (4 Garhwal Rifles):**
   - Awarded the **Maha Vir Chakra (posthumously)** for his single-handed stand at the **Battle of Nuranang (Arunachal Pradesh)** on **17 November 1962**.
   - With the help of two local Monpa girls (Sela and Nura), he held off waves of Chinese soldiers for 72 hours.
   - The Indian Army still maintains his bunker as the **Jaswant Garh War Memorial** at Sela Pass, where he is revered as a guardian spirit with continuous promotions.
2. **Major Rajesh Singh Adhikari (2 Mechanized Infantry / 18 Grenadiers):**
   - Native of Nainital; posthumously awarded the **Maha Vir Chakra** during Operation Vijay (1999 Kargil (कारगिल) War) for recapturing **Tololing Ridge** at 15,000 feet.

## UKPCS Practice Drill Questions

**Q1.** Which was the first newspaper published from Uttarakhand in the year 1842?  
A. Samay Vinod  
B. The Hills  
C. Almora Akhbar  
D. Garhwali  
*Answer:* **B** (*The Hills* was launched in 1842 at Mussoorie by John Mckinnon).

**Q2.** The historic weekly newspaper *'Shakti'* was launched on the day of Vijayadashami in 1918 by Badridatt Pandey after the British administration banned which publication?  
A. Garhwal Samachar  
B. Almora Akhbar  
C. Samay Vinod  
D. Swadhin Praja  
*Answer:* **B** (Almora Akhbar was suppressed in 1918 by Deputy Commissioner Lomas).

**Q3.** Who was the first scholar to be awarded a D.Litt. degree in Hindi literature in India (1931)?  
A. Sumitranandan Pant  
B. Dr. Shiv Prasad Dabral  
C. Dr. Pitambar Datt Barthwal  
D. Harikrishna Raturi  
*Answer:* **C** (Dr. Pitambar Datt Barthwal for *"The Nirguna School of Hindi Poetry"* at BHU).

**Q4.** Who among the following is widely known by the sobriquet 'Charan' and authored the 12-volume *'Uttarakhand Ka Itihas'*?  
A. Pandit Harikrishna Raturi  
B. Dr. Shiv Prasad Dabral  
C. Badridatt Pandey  
D. Rai Bahadur Pati Ram  
*Answer:* **B** (Dr. Shiv Prasad Dabral 'Charan' of Dugadda).

**Q5.** Major Somnath Sharma, who was posthumously awarded India's first Param Vir Chakra for his supreme sacrifice in the 1947 Battle of Badgam, belonged to which regiment?  
A. 2/18 Royal Garhwal Rifles  
B. 4 Kumaon Regiment  
C. 13 Kumaon Regiment  
D. 39 Garhwal Rifles  
*Answer:* **B** (4 Kumaon Regiment).

**Q6.** The innovative environmental tradition 'Maiti Movement', wherein a newly-wed couple plants an oak/fruit sapling at their wedding, was founded in 1994 by:  
A. Sunderlal Bahuguna  
B. Chandi Prasad Bhatt  
C. Kalyan Singh Rawat  
D. Sachidanand Bharti  
*Answer:* **C** (Kalyan Singh Rawat at Gwaldam).

**Q7.** Sumitranandan Pant, who hailed from Kausani, received the prestigious Jnanpith Award in 1968 for which celebrated literary work?  
A. Pallav  
B. Kala Aur Burha Chand  
C. Chidambara  
D. Lokayatan  
*Answer:* **C** (*Chidambara* — first Jnanpith in Hindi).
