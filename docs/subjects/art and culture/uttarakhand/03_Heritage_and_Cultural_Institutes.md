## 3.1 World Heritage in Uttarakhand

- **Nanda Devi** and **Valley of Flowers** National Parks form one UNESCO World Heritage property in the Chamoli high Himalaya.
- Kaziranga and the Mountain Railways of India are World Heritage sites **outside** Uttarakhand — standard “which are in UK?” traps.
- UKPCS 2025 keyed **A and D only** (Valley of Flowers + Nanda Devi).

---

## 3.2 Living heritage — Ramman

- **Ramman** of Garhwal is UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage (**2009**).
- It is religious festival and ritual theatre honouring **Bhumiyal Devta**.
- Do not equate it with Ramlila of the plains.

---

## 3.3 Garhwal painting

- In **May 1658**, **Shyamdas and Hardas** reach Srinagar (Garhwal) in the milieu of **Prithvipati Shah** (often linked with Sulaiman Shikoh’s refuge narrative).
- They receive the court post of **Tasbirdar** (picture-maker).
- Their line leads to **Mola Ram**, the best-known Garhwal Pahari painter.
- Full political backdrop also sits on [Medieval Parmar](../../medieval%20india/uttarakhand/02_Parmar_Dynasty_of_Garhwal.md); national Pahari map sits on Painting Topic 4.

---

## 3.4 Literature personality — Pushpesh Pant

- Pushpesh Pant is a noted author linked with Uttarakhand in UKPCS framing, known especially for **food history / cuisine** writing.
- UKPCS 2025 asked which title is **not** his; Series B provisional key marks **C — Himalaya Ke Barf**.
- Lazzatnama and From the King's Table to Street Food appear as his known-type titles in the stem set.

## Must-Score Facts

1. UK WHS pair = Valley of Flowers + Nanda Devi (together).

3. Ramman = UNESCO ICH 2009, Garhwal.
4. 1658 + Shyamdas/Hardas + Tasbirdar + Prithvipati Shah → Garhwal painting.
5. Q49 Series B = **C** (Himalaya Ke Barf not his).
