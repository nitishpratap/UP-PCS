## Consolidated — 30 Must-Score Facts

1. State animal of Uttarakhand is the **Alpine Musk Deer** (*Moschus chrysogaster* / Kasturi Mriga).
2. State bird is the **Himalayan Monal** (*Lophophorus impejanus* / Danfe). State tree is **Buransh** (बुरांश) (*Rhododendron arboreum*).
3. State flower is **Brahma Kamal** (ब्रह्मकमल) (*Saussurea obvallata* / *Kaul Padma*). State butterfly is the **Common Peacock** (*Papilio bianor*, **2016**).
4. State musical instruments are **Dhol and Damau**, notified in **2015**. State song (सोंग) (**2016**) is *"Uttarakhand Devbhumi Matribhumi"* (lyrics Hemant Bisht; music Narendra Singh Negi).
5. **Nanda Devi (नंदा देवी) Raj Jat** (नंदा देवी राज जात) runs every **12 years** for about **280 km** from **Nauti** to **Homkund** (त्रिशूल).
6. A four-horned ram (*Chasingya Khadu*) leads the Raj Jat. Golden canopies called **Chhatoli** are carried by Kansuwa descendants. Women traditionally stop at **Wan**.
7. **Devidhura (देवीधुरा) Bagwal (बग्वाल)** at **Champawat** (चंपावत) (Barahi temple) is the historic stone-pelting fair on Raksha Bandhan among four *Khams*.
8. **Jauljibi (जौलजीबी) Mela** at **Pithoragarh** (पिथौरागढ़) sits at the Gori–Kali (काली) confluence; started **1914** by Gajendra Bahadur Pal.
9. **Uttarayani Mela** at **Bageshwar** (बागेश्वर) (Saryu–Gomti) on Makar Sankranti (मकर संक्रांति) is the historic Coolie-Begar immersion venue of **1921**.
10. **Purnagiri Mela** (Champawat / Tanakpur) falls in Chaitra Navratri (नवरात्रि). **Chaiti (चैती) Mela** at Kashipur (Udham Singh Nagar (ऊधम सिंह नगर)) is linked with the Buksa tribe.
11. **Bissu Mela** of Jaunsar–Bawar (Dehradun (देहरादून)) on Baisakhi (बैसाखी) features the martial **Thoda** archery dance.
12. **Syaldey–Bikhauti** (Almora (अल्मोड़ा) / Dwarahat) and **Somnath (सोमनाथ) Mela** (Masi, Almora) are Baisakhi-season Kumaon fairs.
13. **Chholiya (छोलिया)** is the Kumaon (कुमाऊँ) martial sword dance of Rajput (राजपूत) weddings with shield and sword.
14. **Langvir Nritya** (लांगवीर नृत्य) is the Garhwal (गढ़वाल) acrobatic pole dance. **Pandav Nritya** (पांडव नृत्य) is Garhwal Mahabharata ritual dance-drama.
15. **Chaunfla** is a Garhwal romantic clap-and-footwork dance without instruments. **Thandya** is a maternal-courtyard dance of newly married daughters.
16. **Jhora (झोड़ा)** and **Chanchari** are Kumaon circular community dances. **Chhapeli** is a romantic duet with handkerchief or mirror.
17. **Hurkiya Baul** (हुड़किया बौल) is Kumaon agrarian paddy song with the *Hurka* drum. **Sarau** is a Garhwal martial sword dance.
18. Instrument map: **Dhol–Damau** (state pair), **Hurka** (hourglass), **Bhankora** (straight copper trumpet), **Ransingha** (curved brass horn), **Moshakbeen** (bagpipe), **Thali–Dor** (Jagar idiophones).
19. **Radha Bahin Bhatt** received **Padma Shri 2025** for **Social Work** (Lakshmi Ashram, Kausani). **Madhuri Barthwal** received Padma Shri **2022** for folk music.
20. Pandav Nritya of Garhwal is **not** Pandavani of Chhattisgarh. Chholiya is **not** Chhapeli.
21. Devidhura is Champawat stone Bagwal. Jauljibi is Pithoragarh trade mela. Do not swap districts.
22. Nanda Raj Jat starts at **Nauti** and ends at **Homkund**, not Roopkund as the terminal shrine.
23. State flower is Brahma Kamal. State tree is Buransh. Do not reverse the two.
24. Dhol–Damau is the state instrument pair of **2015**, not Hurka alone.
25. Haridwar (हरिद्वार) Kumbh (कुंभ) and Ardh Kumbh sit on the state festival map beside hill melas, on a **12** and **6** year cycle.
26. **Gend Mela** of Pauri (पौड़ी) and **Vaikunth Chaturdashi** of Srinagar Garhwal are regional Garhwal calendar fairs.
27. Monal is also Nepal’s national bird (*Danfe*). That does not move the Uttarakhand state-bird tag.
28. Musk deer habitat memory often cites Kedarnath (केदारनाथ) and Askot sanctuaries. Brahma Kamal is offered at Kedarnath.
29. Folk dances split by zone: Garhwal (Pandav, Langvir, Chaunfla, Thandya, Sarau) versus Kumaon (Chholiya, Jhora, Chanchari, Hurkiya Baul, Chhapeli).
30. These state symbols and fairs are UKPCS Unit 1 / GK heads. They are not SNA classical dance forms.

## Must-Score Facts (High-Yield UKPCS)

1. **State Symbols Official Ratta:**
   - *State Flower:* **Brahma Kamal** (*Saussurea obvallata*), blooms July–September between 3,600 and 4,800 m. Offered at Kedarnath (केदारनाथ).
   - *State Bird:* **Himalayan Monal** (*Lophophorus impejanus*), state bird of Uttarakhand and national bird of Nepal (*Danfe*).
   - *State Animal:* **Alpine Musk Deer** (*Moschus chrysogaster*), found in Kedarnath Wildlife Sanctuary (established 1972) and Askot Wildlife Sanctuary (Pithoragarh, 1986).
   - *State Musical Instrument:* **Dhol** (notified in 2015 under recommendation of Bhandari Committee).
2. **Nanda Devi Raj Jat Yatra ("Himalayan Mahakumbh"):**
   - Frequency: Every **12 years**.
   - Route: Starts from **Nauti village** (Chamoli (चमोली)) and terminates at **Homkund** (at base of Mount Trishul (त्रिशूल)).
   - Total Distance: **280 km** on foot across 19–20 days through 19 halts.
   - Sacred Leader: A born **four-horned ram (*Chasingya Khadu*)** leads the yatra bearing the offering (*Pitolya*); leaves alone towards higher glaciers at Homkund.
   - Sacred Umbrellas: Golden canopies called **Chhatoli** carried by Kansuwa royal descendants.
   - Women are traditionally not allowed beyond **Wan village** (the last inhabited village).
3. **Devidhura Bagwal Mela (Champawat):** Held on Raksha Bandhan at the **Maa Barahi Devi Temple**. Traditionally, 4 clans (*Khams* (ख़म्स): Chamyal, Gaharwal, Walik, Lamgariya) pelted stones at each other using bamboo shields (*Chhatoli*) until blood equivalent to one human sacrifice was shed. Now played with flowers, walnuts, and pears.
4. **Padma Shri 2025 Traps (UKPCS 2025 Q53):** Smt. Radha Bahin Bhatt received the Padma Shri in 2025 for **Social Work** (associated with Sarvodaya and Lakshmi Ashram, Kausani). Do not confuse with Dr. Madhuri Barthwal who received Padma Shri in 2022 for **Art / Folk Music**.

## Folk Dances of Uttarakhand

```
                         Uttarakhand Folk Dances
                                    │
        ┌───────────────────────────┴───────────────────────────┐
        ▼                                                       ▼
[Garhwal Folk Dances]                                   [Kumaon Folk Dances]
 • Pandav Nritya (Mahabharata enactment)                 • Chholiya (Martial sword dance)
 • Langvir Nritya (Acrobatic pole dance)                 • Jhora (Circular spring dance)
 • Chaunfla (Romantic hand-clapping dance)               • Chanchari (Slow lyrical dance)
 • Thandya (Maternal courtyard dance)                    • Hurkiya Baul (Agrarian paddy song)
 • Sarau (Garhwal martial sword dance)                   • Chhapeli (Romantic duet with mirror)
```

1. **Chholiya Dance (Kumaon):** Ancient martial sword dance dating back to the Khas and Rajput warfare era. Performed by men dressed in traditional warrior tunics (*Chola* (चोल)) carrying bronze (कांस्य) shields (*Dhal*) and swords (*Talwar*) to ward off evil spirits during wedding processions. Accompanied by Dhol, Damau, and Ransingha.
2. **Pandav Nritya (पांडव नृत्य) (Garhwal):** Ritual dance-drama enacting scenes from the *Mahabharata* (महाभारत) during winter months; performers enter trance (*Pashu-Aana*) embodying characters like Bhima, Arjuna, and Draupadi.
3. **Langvir Nritya (Garhwal):** Unique acrobatic dance where a male dancer balances himself on his stomach atop a 20-foot vertical wooden pole, rotating skillfully without falling, while Dhol-Damau plays rhythmically below.
4. **Thandya Dance (Garhwal):** Performed in the home courtyards by newly married daughters visiting their maternal home (*Mait*) during *Basant Panchami* to express homesickness and maternal love.
5. **Chaunfla Dance (Garhwal):** Highly lyrical, romantic duet dance of Garhwal; unique because it requires **no musical instruments** — dancers generate rhythm entirely through rhythmic footwork and synchronous hand-clapping.
6. **Hurkiya Baul (हुड़किया बौल) (Kumaon):** Agricultural performance sung during the transplantation of summer paddy (*Ropai*); the lead singer (*Hurkiya*) plays the hourglass drum (*Hurka*) while women transplant rice in synchronized steps.

## Folk Musical Instruments of Uttarakhand

| Instrument Category | Name of Instrument | Physical Description & Performance Context |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Avanaddha** (अवनद्ध) *(Membranophone)* | **Dhol & Damau** | **Official State Musical Instrument (declared 2015)**. Dhol is a wooden barrel drum played with one stick and hand; Damau is a smaller copper bowl drum played with two thin sticks. Played by the *Auji / Dholi* masters. |
| **Avanaddha** *(Hourglass)* | **Hurka** | Small waist-drum held in hand and played with fingers; lead instrument in Kumaoni *Hurkiya Baul* (हुड़किया बौल) and ballads. |
| **Sushira** (सुषिर) *(Aerophone / Wind)* | **Bhankora** | Long, straight copper trumpet (about 3–4 feet long) blown exclusively in religious processions and *Deva-Karya*. |
| **Sushira** *(Wind / Horn)* | **Ransingha** | Curved S-shaped brass trumpet blown during martial parades, weddings, and royal entries. |
| **Sushira** *(Bagpipe)* | **Moshakbeen** (मोशकबीन) | Highland Scottish bagpipe adapted completely into Kumaoni and Garhwali wedding brass bands. |
| **Ghana** (घन) *(Idiophone)* | **Thali & Dor** | Bronze plate (*Kansi ki Thali*) struck with wooden sticks over an inverted brass jar (*Dor*) during *Jagar* (जागर) (shamanic rituals). |

## Confused Pairs & Common Traps

| Confused Pair | Critical Distinction |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Radha Bahin Bhatt vs Madhuri Barthwal** | Radha Bahin Bhatt = **2025 Padma Shri (Social Work)**; Madhuri Barthwal = **2022 Padma Shri (Folk Music)**. |
| **Pandav Nritya vs Pandavani** | Pandav Nritya = **Garhwal ritual dance**; Pandavani = **Chhattisgarh ballad theatre (Teejan Bai)**. |
| **Chholiya vs Chhapeli** | Chholiya = **Martial sword dance** (Rajput weddings); Chhapeli = **Romantic duet with handkerchief/mirror**. |
| **Devidhura vs Jauljibi** | Devidhura = **Stone-pelting Bagwal mela** (Champawat); Jauljibi = **Gori-Kali confluence trade mela** (Pithoragarh). |
| **Nanda Raj Jat Start vs End** | Starts at **Nauti village**; ends at **Homkund** (distance: 280 km, every 12 years). |
| **State Flower vs State Tree** | State Flower = **Brahma Kamal**; State Tree = **Buransh** (Rhododendron). |

## UKPCS Inline & Practice Questions

**Q1 (UKPCS Prelims 2025, Q53).** Who among the following received the Padma Shri Award in 2025 for social work from Uttarakhand?  
A. Dr. Yashwant Singh Katoch  
B. Dr. Madhuri Barthwal  
C. Smt. Radha Bahin Bhatt  
D. Shri Hugh and Colleen Gantzer (posthumously)  
*Answer:* **C** (Smt. Radha Bahin Bhatt of Lakshmi Ashram, Kausani).

**Q2.** Which instrument was officially declared the 'State Musical Instrument' of Uttarakhand in the year 2015?  
A. Hurka  
B. Bhankora  
C. Dhol and Damau  
D. Moshakbeen  
*Answer:* **C** (Dhol and Damau).

**Q3.** The famous 'Devidhura Bagwal Mela', known for its historic stone-pelting battle (*Pashan Yuddha*), is celebrated in which district?  
A. Pithoragarh  
B. Almora  
C. Champawat  
D. Bageshwar  
*Answer:* **C** (Champawat, at the temple of Maa Barahi Devi).

**Q4.** The sacred pilgrimage 'Nanda Devi Raj Jat Yatra' traverses a distance of approximately 280 km starting from Nauti village and terminating at:  
A. Roopkund  
B. Bedni Bugyal  
C. Homkund  
D. Hemkund (हेमकुंड)  
*Answer:* **C** (Homkund at the base of Mount Trishul).

**Q5.** Which unique folk dance of Garhwal features an acrobatic male performer balancing on his stomach atop a high vertical pole?  
A. Chholiya  
B. Langvir Nritya  
C. Pandav Nritya  
D. Chaunfla  
*Answer:* **B** (Langvir Nritya).
