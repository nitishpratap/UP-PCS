## 2.1 Folk dances and music

- Wedding and festival dancing centres on forms such as **Chholiya**, **Jhora**, and related circle dances.
- Garhwal ritual cycles include **Pandav** dance-drama traditions in many coaching maps.
- Folk singers and instrumentalists carry seasonal and epic repertoires; named Padma awardees must be matched to the **field** of the award.

---

## 2.2 Fairs and festivals

- **Uttarayani** at Bageshwar is a major mid-winter cultural–trading fair in Kumaon memory.
- **Nanda Devi** related processions and fairs are high-identity Garhwal–Kumaon pilgrimage culture.
- Haridwar’s bathing festivals sit on the national festival map but are also state tourism culture.

---
