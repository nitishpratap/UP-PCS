## Must-Score Facts (High-Yield UKPCS)

1. **State Symbols Official Ratta:**
   - *State Flower:* **Brahma Kamal** (*Saussurea obvallata*), blooms July–September between 3,600 and 4,800 m. Offered at Kedarnath.
   - *State Bird:* **Himalayan Monal** (*Lophophorus impejanus*), state bird of Uttarakhand and national bird of Nepal (*Danfe*).
   - *State Animal:* **Alpine Musk Deer** (*Moschus chrysogaster*), found in Kedarnath Wildlife Sanctuary (established 1972) and Askot Wildlife Sanctuary (Pithoragarh, 1986).
   - *State Musical Instrument:* **Dhol** (notified in 2015 under recommendation of Bhandari Committee).
2. **Nanda Devi Raj Jat Yatra ("Himalayan Mahakumbh"):**
   - Frequency: Every **12 years**.
   - Route: Starts from **Nauti village** (Chamoli) and terminates at **Homkund** (at base of Mount Trishul).
   - Total Distance: **280 km** on foot across 19–20 days through 19 halts.
   - Sacred Leader: A born **four-horned ram (*Chasingya Khadu*)** leads the yatra bearing the offering (*Pitolya*); leaves alone towards higher glaciers at Homkund.
   - Sacred Umbrellas: Golden canopies called **Chhatoli** carried by Kansuwa royal descendants.
   - Women are traditionally not allowed beyond **Wan village** (the last inhabited village).
3. **Devidhura Bagwal Mela (Champawat):** Held on Raksha Bandhan at the **Maa Barahi Devi Temple**. Traditionally, 4 clans (*Khams*: Chamyal, Gaharwal, Walik, Lamgariya) pelted stones at each other using bamboo shields (*Chhatoli*) until blood equivalent to one human sacrifice was shed. Now played with flowers, walnuts, and pears.
4. **Padma Shri 2025 Traps (UKPCS 2025 Q53):** Smt. Radha Bahin Bhatt received the Padma Shri in 2025 for **Social Work** (associated with Sarvodaya and Lakshmi Ashram, Kausani). Do not confuse with Dr. Madhuri Barthwal who received Padma Shri in 2022 for **Art / Folk Music**.

## Folk Dances of Uttarakhand

```
                         Uttarakhand Folk Dances
                                    │
        ┌───────────────────────────┴───────────────────────────┐
        ▼                                                       ▼
[Garhwal Folk Dances]                                   [Kumaon Folk Dances]
 • Pandav Nritya (Mahabharata enactment)                 • Chholiya (Martial sword dance)
 • Langvir Nritya (Acrobatic pole dance)                 • Jhora (Circular spring dance)
 • Chaunfla (Romantic hand-clapping dance)               • Chanchari (Slow lyrical dance)
 • Thandya (Maternal courtyard dance)                    • Hurkiya Baul (Agrarian paddy song)
 • Sarau (Garhwal martial sword dance)                   • Chhapeli (Romantic duet with mirror)
```

1. **Chholiya Dance (Kumaon):** Ancient martial sword dance dating back to the Khas and Rajput warfare era. Performed by men dressed in traditional warrior tunics (*Chola*) carrying bronze shields (*Dhal*) and swords (*Talwar*) to ward off evil spirits during wedding processions. Accompanied by Dhol, Damau, and Ransingha.
2. **Pandav Nritya (Garhwal):** Ritual dance-drama enacting scenes from the *Mahabharata* during winter months; performers enter trance (*Pashu-Aana*) embodying characters like Bhima, Arjuna, and Draupadi.
3. **Langvir Nritya (Garhwal):** Unique acrobatic dance where a male dancer balances himself on his stomach atop a 20-foot vertical wooden pole, rotating skillfully without falling, while Dhol-Damau plays rhythmically below.
4. **Thandya Dance (Garhwal):** Performed in the home courtyards by newly married daughters visiting their maternal home (*Mait*) during *Basant Panchami* to express homesickness and maternal love.
5. **Chaunfla Dance (Garhwal):** Highly lyrical, romantic duet dance of Garhwal; unique because it requires **no musical instruments** — dancers generate rhythm entirely through rhythmic footwork and synchronous hand-clapping.
6. **Hurkiya Baul (Kumaon):** Agricultural performance sung during the transplantation of summer paddy (*Ropai*); the lead singer (*Hurkiya*) plays the hourglass drum (*Hurka*) while women transplant rice in synchronized steps.

## Folk Musical Instruments of Uttarakhand

| Instrument Category | Name of Instrument | Physical Description & Performance Context |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Avanaddha** *(Membranophone)* | **Dhol & Damau** | **Official State Musical Instrument (declared 2015)**. Dhol is a wooden barrel drum played with one stick and hand; Damau is a smaller copper bowl drum played with two thin sticks. Played by the *Auji / Dholi* masters. |
| **Avanaddha** *(Hourglass)* | **Hurka** | Small waist-drum held in hand and played with fingers; lead instrument in Kumaoni *Hurkiya Baul* and ballads. |
| **Sushira** *(Aerophone / Wind)* | **Bhankora** | Long, straight copper trumpet (about 3–4 feet long) blown exclusively in religious processions and *Deva-Karya*. |
| **Sushira** *(Wind / Horn)* | **Ransingha** | Curved S-shaped brass trumpet blown during martial parades, weddings, and royal entries. |
| **Sushira** *(Bagpipe)* | **Moshakbeen** | Highland Scottish bagpipe adapted completely into Kumaoni and Garhwali wedding brass bands. |
| **Ghana** *(Idiophone)* | **Thali & Dor** | Bronze plate (*Kansi ki Thali*) struck with wooden sticks over an inverted brass jar (*Dor*) during *Jagar* (shamanic rituals). |

## Confused Pairs & Common Traps

| Confused Pair | Critical Distinction |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Radha Bahin Bhatt vs Madhuri Barthwal** | Radha Bahin Bhatt = **2025 Padma Shri (Social Work)**; Madhuri Barthwal = **2022 Padma Shri (Folk Music)**. |
| **Pandav Nritya vs Pandavani** | Pandav Nritya = **Garhwal ritual dance**; Pandavani = **Chhattisgarh ballad theatre (Teejan Bai)**. |
| **Chholiya vs Chhapeli** | Chholiya = **Martial sword dance** (Rajput weddings); Chhapeli = **Romantic duet with handkerchief/mirror**. |
| **Devidhura vs Jauljibi** | Devidhura = **Stone-pelting Bagwal mela** (Champawat); Jauljibi = **Gori-Kali confluence trade mela** (Pithoragarh). |
| **Nanda Raj Jat Start vs End** | Starts at **Nauti village**; ends at **Homkund** (distance: 280 km, every 12 years). |
| **State Flower vs State Tree** | State Flower = **Brahma Kamal**; State Tree = **Buransh** (Rhododendron). |

## UKPCS Inline & Practice Questions

**Q1 (UKPCS Prelims 2025, Q53).** Who among the following received the Padma Shri Award in 2025 for social work from Uttarakhand?  
A. Dr. Yashwant Singh Katoch  
B. Dr. Madhuri Barthwal  
C. Smt. Radha Bahin Bhatt  
D. Shri Hugh and Colleen Gantzer (posthumously)  
*Answer:* **C** (Smt. Radha Bahin Bhatt of Lakshmi Ashram, Kausani).

**Q2.** Which instrument was officially declared the 'State Musical Instrument' of Uttarakhand in the year 2015?  
A. Hurka  
B. Bhankora  
C. Dhol and Damau  
D. Moshakbeen  
*Answer:* **C** (Dhol and Damau).

**Q3.** The famous 'Devidhura Bagwal Mela', known for its historic stone-pelting battle (*Pashan Yuddha*), is celebrated in which district?  
A. Pithoragarh  
B. Almora  
C. Champawat  
D. Bageshwar  
*Answer:* **C** (Champawat, at the temple of Maa Barahi Devi).

**Q4.** The sacred pilgrimage 'Nanda Devi Raj Jat Yatra' traverses a distance of approximately 280 km starting from Nauti village and terminating at:  
A. Roopkund  
B. Bedni Bugyal  
C. Homkund  
D. Hemkund  
*Answer:* **C** (Homkund at the base of Mount Trishul).

**Q5.** Which unique folk dance of Garhwal features an acrobatic male performer balancing on his stomach atop a high vertical pole?  
A. Chholiya  
B. Langvir Nritya  
C. Pandav Nritya  
D. Chaunfla  
*Answer:* **B** (Langvir Nritya).
