## 2.1 Folk dances and music

**Identity:** Wedding sword dance · circle dances · Garhwal epic ritual drama.

- Wedding and festival dancing centres on **Chholiya**, **Jhora**, **Chanchari**, and related circle dances.
- Garhwal ritual cycles include **Pandav Nritya** dance-drama traditions in many coaching maps.
- Percussion memory runs on **hurka**, **dhol**, **damau**, and related hill ensembles.
- Folk singers and instrumentalists carry seasonal and epic repertoires; named Padma awardees must be matched to the **field** of the award.
- UKPCS usually tests **folk** forms — do not invent a new Sangeet Natak Akademi classical school for the State.

---

## 2.2 Fairs and festivals — Cause, Course, Result cards

### Uttarayani (Bageshwar)

**Cause:** Mid-winter Makar Sankranti–linked fair culture on the Kumaon trade and pilgrimage map.

**Course:** Crowds gather at **Bageshwar** for ritual bathing, trading, folk performance, and community meeting.

**Result:** Uttarayani is the standard Kumaon fair identity in UKPCS lists — place = **Bageshwar**, not a plains Kumbh city.

### Nanda Devi Raj Jat / fair cycle

**Cause:** Goddess **Nanda Devi** pilgrimage and royal / community procession memory across Garhwal–Kumaon.

**Course:** Periodic long processions and local Nanda fairs move through high villages toward sacred destinations in the coaching map.

**Result:** High-identity State pilgrimage culture. Do not collapse it into only the **Nanda Devi National Park** wildlife WHS card.

### Magh / Haridwar bathing cycle

**Cause:** Ganga pilgrimage calendar at Haridwar and related Magh gatherings in hill notes.

**Course:** Bathing festivals and local jatras pull pilgrims on the national festival map and State tourism culture together.

**Result:** Haridwar sits on both national festival teaching and UK culture GK — keep place and month traps separate from Uttarayani–Bageshwar.
