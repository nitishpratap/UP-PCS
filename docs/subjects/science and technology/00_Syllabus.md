# Science & Technology (UPPCS + UKPCS Knowledge Base)

**Desk:** [UKPCS Syllabus — Unit 5](../../ukpcs/00_Syllabus.md) 
**Ecology depth:** [Environment & Ecology](../environments%20&%20ecology/00_Syllabus.md)

## Core heads (national)

- General Science — appreciation and understanding of everyday science
- Science and Technology in India — history and contribution
- Current Affairs: awards, prizes, discoveries, inventions, Science Congresses, conferences, solar technology, applications for human welfare, health and medicines, environmental awareness, natural bioresources
- State, National and International scientific / research organisations (IUCN, WWF, IPCC, WHO, UNESCO, etc.)
- Information technology and application of computers in daily life; e-governance
- Ecology and Environment overview (detailed notes in Environment subject)
- Natural Resources — soil, water, air, forests, grasslands, wetlands, marine; renewable and non-renewable energy
- Biodiversity — threats and conservation; ethics and usage
- Environmental Crisis — air, water, soil, space pollution; laws and acts; global warming; global climate change
- Remote sensing and GIS; Weather Forecasting; Spreadsheet and Database applications
- Physical Science / Computer awareness: fundamentals of computer and information processing; Boolean Algebra; Logic Gates; networks and security; Internet and multimedia; Cloud computing; cyber law basics
- Matter and its states; acids, bases and salts; hard/soft/heavy water; batteries; fuel cells; corrosion; nuclear fission and fusion; polymers; biomolecules; medicines and healthcare; food chemistry
- Mechanics; waves; heat; light; magnetism; electricity; atomic & nuclear physics; astronomy and space; X-rays; semiconductors
- Life Science: branches; biodiversity; biotechnology; nanotechnology; vaccines; immunization; GMOs; livestock; plants and human welfare
- Scientific Glossary
