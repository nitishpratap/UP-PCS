## Consolidated — 48 Must-Score Facts

1. **Urbanisation** is the rise in the **urban share** of population and the growth of towns and cities in number and size.
2. India Census **2011** urban share ~**31.16%**; rural ~**68.84%**.
3. Urban growth comes from **natural increase** inside towns, **rural→urban migration**, and **reclassification** (census/statutory towns).
4. India’s urbanisation is heavily **migration-led** (Topic 5 neighbour).
5. Acceleration stage on the classic urbanisation curve = **second stage**.
6. A **statutory town** is notified in law (municipality, corporation, cantonment, notified town area).
7. A **census town** need not be a municipality; it must meet **all three**: population ≥**5,000**, density ≥**400**/km², ≥**75%** of **male main workers** in non-agriculture.
8. Census towns often lack full ULB services — urban by criteria, weak governance.
9. **Class I** town: population ≥ **1 lakh**.
10. **Million-plus city / UA**: population ≥ **10 lakh** (1 million).
11. **Megacity** (UN teaching): often ≥ **10 million**.
12. **Urban agglomeration (UA)** = continuous urban spread (city + outgrowths / adjoining towns).
13. **Conurbation** = merged sprawl of several towns.
14. Census 2011: **53** million-plus UAs (up from **35** in 2001).
15. Largest 2011 UAs: **Greater Mumbai > Delhi > Kolkata** band, then other metros.
16. Among religions, **Jains** have the highest urban population share.
17. Among states, **Goa** leads urban **%**; large northern States stay more rural.
18. UP urban share ~**22.3%** (2011) — below India’s 31.16%.
19. Absolute urban population leaders include **Maharashtra**; UP’s absolute urban count is still large.
20. UP million-plus teaching: **Kanpur** crossed 10 lakh in **1971**; **Lucknow** in **1981**.
21. UP 2011 UA order teaching: **Kanpur > Lucknow > Ghaziabad > Agra**.
22. Urban problems: housing shortage, **slums**, informal work, water/sanitation gaps, traffic, pollution, heat islands, weak ULB finance.
23. Slums co-occur with rapid in-migration + land scarcity + low wages when planning lags — not “inevitable forever”.
24. **Smart Cities Mission (25 June 2015, MoHUA)** — **100** cities; city-level **SPV**; ABD + pan-city ICT.
25. ABD = area-based development (retrofit / redevelopment / greenfield).
26. UP Central Smart Cities count in papers: **10** (State 2019 list is separate).
27. **AMRUT (June 2015)** — water supply, sewerage, storm water, green spaces for ~**500** cities teaching.
28. **AMRUT 2.0 (1 Oct 2021)** — water security push for statutory towns.
29. **JNNURM (2005)** is the older urban mission — do not date AMRUT as 2005.
30. **PMAY-U (25 June 2015)** — urban housing neighbour (same day as Smart Cities).

32. **HRIDAY (Jan 2015)** — **12** heritage cities; UP = **Varanasi + Mathura**.

37. Three ULB types: **Nagar Panchayat** (transitional), **Municipal Council / Nagar Palika**, **Municipal Corporation / Nagar Nigam**.

## 6.1 Urbanisation process and town classification

**Urbanisation** raises the urban share of people and expands the urban system.

### Drivers and trend

- Drivers: rural→urban migration, natural increase in towns, reclassification of villages as towns.
- India 2011: ~**31%** urban — still majority rural, but cities dominate GDP and opportunity.
- Acceleration stage on the standard curve = **Stage 2**.
- Goa leads urban %; UP stays more rural (~**22%** urban) despite large absolute urban numbers.
- **Jains** are the most urbanised major religious community.

### Statutory vs census towns

- **Statutory** = notified municipality / corporation / cantonment / notified town area.
- **Census town** = triple test (5,000; 400/km²; 75% male main workers non-agri) even without a full municipal notification.
- Census towns create a governance gap: urban density without matching ULB capacity.

### Hierarchy and UA

- Class I → million-plus → megacity (read the stem’s definition).
- **UA** stitches a city with continuous outgrowths and adjoining towns.
- 2011: **53** million-plus UAs; Mumbai–Delhi–Kolkata lead.
- UP: Kanpur (1971), Lucknow (1981); order Kanpur > Lucknow > Ghaziabad > Agra.

## 6.2 Urban problems and infrastructure

**Rapid urban growth without matching services** produces the problem set.

### Housing and slums

- Housing shortage and high rents push informal settlements.
- Slums concentrate overcrowding, tenure insecurity and service deficits.
- In-situ upgrading and affordable housing (PMAY-U neighbour) beat endless eviction cycles in teaching.

### Services and environment

- Water supply hours, sewerage gaps, solid waste and flooding follow weak drainage and concretisation.
- Air pollution, heat islands and traffic congestion are metro staples.
- Informal work dominates much urban employment — low security beside formal IT/services islands.

### Social and governance stress

- Safety, schooling and health access are uneven across wards.
- Weak property-tax effort and incomplete devolution starve ULBs of funds.
- Peri-urban sprawl grows outside clear municipal accountability.

## 6.3 Urban schemes — Smart Cities, AMRUT and neighbours

**MoHUA missions** are the programme desk for city renewal.

### Smart Cities Mission

- Launch **25 June 2015**; **100** cities; **SPV** implementation; ABD + pan-city smart solutions.
- UP papers key **10** Central Smart Cities; 2019 State Smart Cities are an add-on list.

### AMRUT / AMRUT 2.0

- **AMRUT (June 2015)** — basic urban infrastructure (water, sewerage, drains, green spaces) for a large city set (~500 teaching).
- **AMRUT 2.0 (1 Oct 2021)** — water-secure towns agenda.
- Do not call AMRUT a 2005 scheme — that is **JNNURM**.

### Other neighbours

- **PMAY-U** — urban housing (same day as Smart Cities).
- **SBM-Urban** — sanitation (2014).
- **HRIDAY** — 12 heritage cities; UP = Varanasi + Mathura.
- Keep MoHUA vs MoRD (Rurban / smart village) separate.

## 6.4 Urban governance and the 74th Amendment

**Urban local bodies** are the third-tier urban democracy.

### Constitutional frame

- **74th Amendment 1992**; force **1 June 1993**; Part **IXA**; **Twelfth Schedule — 18** subjects.
- **73rd** = rural Panchayats — never swap with 74th.
- Three types: Nagar Panchayat, Municipal Council, Municipal Corporation.
- Mayor (political) ≠ Commissioner (executive).
- Municipal elections → **State Election Commission**.

### Who is not a ordinary 74th municipality

- **Cantonment Boards** — Defence track.
- Some industrial development authorities — **243Q** exception teaching (e.g. Noida lane).

### Why governance matters for urbanisation

- Schemes cannot substitute for empowered, financed ULBs.
- Ward committees, planning and property tax decide whether Smart Cities / AMRUT outputs last.
