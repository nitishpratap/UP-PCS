## 3.1 Age structure, dependency and workforce

**Age structure** is the share of people in each age band — it shapes dependency, pyramids and the dividend window.

- Teaching bands: **0–14**, **15–59**, **60+**.
- **Youth** and **child** shares stay high where fertility is high (northern belt teaching).
- **Elderly** shares rise earlier in low-fertility southern States.
- A **population pyramid** plots age against sex; broad base = high fertility / expansive; narrow base + wide top = ageing.
- **Dependency ratio** = (young + aged) / working-age — high dependency strains earners.
- **Workforce / working population** in Census uses main and marginal worker categories.
- Occupational shift from primary toward secondary/tertiary is gradual; primary still employs a large share.
- **Demographic dividend** composition tag = large **15–59** share.
- Numbers alone do not deliver growth — skills, health and jobs must match (Economy 8).

**UPPCS (Pre) 2022**

The demographic dividend is—

A. Working population of above 60 years
B. Working population of 14 to 50 years
C. 0–6 years population
D. Working population of 15 to 59 years

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Logic:** Standard Indian teaching band for dividend is 15–59; 14–50 and 60+ are distractors.

**Ans: D.** Working population of 15 to 59 years.

</details>

## 3.2 Sex ratio and gender composition

**Sex ratio** in Indian Census teaching is **females per 1,000 males**.

### Overall sex ratio (2011)

- India: **943** (up from 933 in 2001).
- Kerala leads states (**1,084**); Haryana is the classic lowest state (**879**).
- Puducherry leads UTs; Daman & Diu is the low UT band.
- Rural SR often exceeds urban SR because cities pull more male migrants.

### Child sex ratio (0–6)

- India CSR: **919** — down from **927** in 2001.
- CSR tracks recent births and early childhood survival more sharply than overall SR.
- Haryana is the classic lowest CSR state; north-eastern hill states lead the high band.
- Never say “sex ratio improved, so CSR also improved” for 2001–2011 — overall rose, CSR fell.

### UP and UK

- UP overall SR **912**; district highs **Jaunpur / Azamgarh / Deoria**; low **Gautam Buddha Nagar**.
- UP CSR **902**; high **Balrampur**; low **Baghpat**.
- UK overall SR **963**; high **Almora**; low **Haridwar**.

## 3.3 Literacy

**Literacy** in Census 2011 counts persons aged **7 years and above** who can read and write with understanding.

### India

- Overall **74.04%**; male **82.14%**; female **65.46%**.
- Kerala leads; Bihar is the major-state floor.
- Female literacy floor among major states often keys **Rajasthan**.
- Urban > rural literacy almost everywhere in the 2011 pattern.

### Effective vs crude

- **Effective literacy** uses the 7+ denominator.
- **Crude literacy** includes under-7s in the base and looks lower.
- School enrolment is not the same statistic as Census literacy.

### UP and UK

- UP literacy ~**67.7%** — below national.
- UP lowest **female** literacy district: **Shrawasti**.
- UP high literacy district band: **Gautam Buddha Nagar**; high female literacy often **Kanpur Nagar**.
- UK literacy ~**78.8%** — above national; Dehradun leads; US Nagar / Haridwar form the trough.

## 3.4 SC, ST, religion and language

### Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes

- SC ~**16.6%**; ST ~**8.6%** of India’s 2011 population.
- **Absolute vs percentage** is the standing trap: UP leads SC **count**; Punjab leads SC **%**; MP leads ST **count**; Lakshadweep/Mizoram lead ST **%**.
- Some States/UTs report **zero SC** or **zero ST** in Census tables — learn the short lists.
- UP is SC-heavy (~**20.7%**) and ST-light (~**0.6%**), with **Sonbhadra** as the ST pocket.
- UK SC ~**18.8%**; ST ~**2.9%**; US Nagar leads ST share among major districts.

### Religion

- Hindu majority; Muslim second; Christian / Sikh / Buddhist / Jain smaller shares.
- Regional concentration matters more than all-India averages in match stems (NE Christians; Punjab Sikhs; Maharashtra Jain share teaching).
- **Jains** — most urbanised major religion in classic keys.

### Language

- Mother-tongue tables and Eighth Schedule languages appear as factual CA/PYQ items — always read the Census year in the stem.
- Do not dump a second full language chapter here; keep one-line ranks when a stem asks.

## 3.5 Rural–urban share and State / UP demography

**Rural–urban composition** is the share living in rural vs urban areas — not the full urbanisation process (Topic 6).

### India

- Rural ~**68.84%**; urban ~**31.16%** in 2011.
- Goa leads urban % among states; Himachal / Bihar sit in the low urban-% band.
- Maharashtra leads absolute urban population; UP’s absolute urban count is large even with a lower urban %.

### Uttar Pradesh card

- Most populous state; sex ratio and literacy below national averages; more rural than India.
- District extremes: Jaunpur SR high; GBN SR low; Shrawasti literacy floor; Ghaziabad urban % high; Shrawasti urban % very low.
- Million-plus UA names deepen in Topic 6; composition only notes UP’s large urban absolute stock.

### Uttarakhand card

- Sex ratio above national; literacy above national; density low (Topic 1).
- Almora SR high; Haridwar SR low; Dehradun literacy high; plains districts hold more ST/Muslim share questions.
- Five ST communities (Jaunsari, Tharu, Bhotia, Buksa, Raji) are a UK identity tag.
