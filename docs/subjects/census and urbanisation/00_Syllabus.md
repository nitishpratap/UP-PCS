- Census in India — history, methodology, Census 2011, indicators, reports and surveys
- Population growth, explosion, theories (Malthus, demographic transition, optimum population)
- Population composition — age, sex ratio, literacy, SC/ST, religion, language, workforce, dependency
- Fertility, mortality, IMR/MMR, life expectancy, National Population Policy, family welfare
- Migration (internal / international), density, distribution, population geography
- Urbanisation — towns, UA, million-plus / megacities, slums, Smart Cities / AMRUT, ULBs, challenges
- World population, UN estimates, days, books, organisations and misc one-liners
