## 1. Indian Census History & Legal Framework

| Milestone / Parameter | Year / Detail | Historical / Legal Context |
|---|---|---|
| **First Census in Modern India** | **1872** | Conducted non-synchronously under **Lord Mayo**. |
| **First Synchronous Decennial Census** | **1881** | Conducted across all India under Viceroy **Lord Ripon** (Census Commissioner: **W.C. Plowden**). |
| **The Year of the Great Divide** | **1921** | The only census decade in Indian history showing a **negative population growth rate (-0.31%)** due to 1918 Spanish Flu pandemic, plague, and severe famine. |
| **First Census of Independent India** | **1951** | Census Commissioner: **R.A. Gopalaswami**. Total population: 361 million; sex ratio: 946. |
| **Census Act, 1948** | Enacted under Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel | Permanent statutory basis; census figures are strictly confidential and inadmissible in courts as evidence. |
| **Nodal Authority** | Registrar General and Census Commissioner of India (RGCCI) | Under **Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA)**, New Delhi. |
| **2011 Census Edition** | **15th Census of India** (7th after Independence) | Motto: *"Our Census, Our Future"*; Mascot: Woman Enumerator; Commissioner: **Dr. C. Chandramouli**. |

## 4. Scheduled Castes (SC) and Scheduled Tribes (ST) Demographics

### A. Scheduled Castes (SC) — Census 2011
- Total SC Population in India: **201.4 million** (**16.6%** of total population).
- **Highest SC Population (Absolute):** **Uttar Pradesh** (41.35 million, accounts for 20.5% of total Indian SCs) > West Bengal (21.46M) > Bihar (16.57M).
- **Highest SC Percentage (% of State Population):** **Punjab** (**31.9%**) > Himachal Pradesh (25.2%) > West Bengal (23.5%) > Uttar Pradesh (20.7%).
- **States/UTs with NO Scheduled Castes:** **Nagaland, Mizoram, Lakshadweep, Andaman & Nicobar Islands**.

### B. Scheduled Tribes (ST) — Census 2011
- Total ST Population in India: **104.3 million** (**8.6%** of total population).
- **Highest ST Population (Absolute):** **Madhya Pradesh** (15.31 million, 14.7% of all Indian STs) > Maharashtra (10.51M) > Odisha (9.59M).
- **Highest ST Percentage (% of State/UT):** **Lakshadweep** (**94.8%**) and **Mizoram** (**94.4%**) > Nagaland (86.5%) > Meghalaya (86.1%).
- **States/UTs with NO Scheduled Tribes:** **Punjab, Haryana, Chandigarh, Delhi, Puducherry**.

## 7. Uttarakhand Census 2011 Highlights

- **Total Population:** **10,086,292** (~1.01 Crore, 0.83% of India).
- **Decadal Growth Rate:** **18.81%** (Plains growing rapidly: US Nagar 33.4%, Haridwar 30.6%; Hill districts showing negative growth: **Almora -1.28%**, **Pauri Garhwal -1.41%** - ghost villages phenomenon).
- **Population Density:** **189 persons/sq km** (Haridwar highest at 801; Uttarkashi lowest at 41).
- **Overall Sex Ratio:** **963 females/1000 males** (Almora highest at 1,139; Haridwar lowest at 880).
- **Child Sex Ratio (0–6 years):** **890** (Pithoragarh lowest at 816).
- **Overall Literacy Rate:** **78.82%** (Male 87.40%, Female 70.01%). Highest literacy: **Dehradun (84.25%)**; Lowest: **Haridwar (73.43%)**.
