## Must-Score Facts (High-Yield UKPCS)

1. **Uttarakhand Panchayati Raj Amendment Act, 2019:**
   - **Educational Qualification:**
     - General Candidates: Must have passed **Class 10th (High School)**.
     - SC/ST & Female Candidates: Must have passed **Class 8th** (Class 5th for SC/ST women in Gram Panchayat).
   - **Two-Child Norm:** Anyone having more than two living children after the notified cut-off date is legally disqualified from contesting elections to any tier of Panchayati Raj.
   - **Household Sanitation:** Must have a functional residential toilet.
2. **Panchayat Register Ratta (UKPCS 2025 Q134):** Gram Panchayats are statutorily required to maintain:
   - *Kutumb / Parivar Register* (Family Register)
   - *Janam-Mrityu Register* (Birth and Death Register)
   - *Anudan Register* (Grant / Financial Register)
   - *(The **Health Register** is maintained by ANMs / Primary Health Centres, NOT Panchayats).*
3. **50% Women Reservation Benchmark (2008):** In March 2008, Uttarakhand became one of the earliest states to enhance women’s reservation in all three tiers of Panchayati Raj from 33% to **50%**.
4. **Van Panchayats (Forest Councils):** Uttarakhand is the **only state in India** with statutory, democratic community forestry institutions. Established under the **Forest Grievances Committee (1921)** headed by P. Wyndham; first formalized under the **Kumaon (कुमाऊँ) Van Panchayat Rules, 1931**. Over 12,000 Van Panchayats manage community forests today.

## Rural Local Governance: Three-Tier Hierarchy

```
                      Three-Tier Panchayati Raj
                                  │
       ┌──────────────────────────┼──────────────────────────┐
       ▼                          ▼                          ▼
[Gram Panchayat]          [Kshetra Panchayat]        [Zila Panchayat]
 • Village Level           • Intermediate / Block     • District Level
 • Head: Gram Pradhan      • Head: Pramukh (95 Blocks)• Head: Zila Panchayat Adhyaksha
 • Exec: VPDO / VDO        • Exec: BDO (Block Officer)• Exec: CDO / AMA (13 Districts)
```

| Governance Tier | Administrative Unit | Political Executive | Bureaucratic / Executive Secretary |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Apex Tier** | **Zila Panchayat** (13 in UK) | **Zila Panchayat Adhyaksha** (elected indirectly by members) | **Chief Development Officer (CDO)** / Apar Mukhya Adhikari (AMA) |
| **Intermediate Tier** | **Kshetra Panchayat** (95 Blocks in UK) | **Block Pramukh** (elected indirectly by BDC members) | **Block Development Officer (BDO)** |
| **Village Tier** | **Gram Panchayat** | **Gram Pradhan** (elected directly by Gram Sabha (ग्राम सभा)) | **Gram Panchayat Vikas Adhikari (VPDO)** / VDO |

## Van Panchayats of Uttarakhand: Historical Evolution & Rules

- **Genesis:** Colonial British forest demarcation led to intense peasant unrest and forest burning (1916–1921). The British formed the **Forest Grievances Committee (1921)** under Kumaon Commissioner **P. Wyndham**.
- **The 1931 Enactment:** Based on Wyndham's report, statutory **Van Panchayats** were created in **1931** under Section 28(2) of the Indian Forest Act, 1927.
- **Van Panchayat Rules, 2005 & 2012:**
  - 9-member management committee (*Van Panchayat Prabandhakarini Samiti (समिति)*).
  - **50% reservation for women**.
  - Head of the committee is called **Van Sarpanch**.
  - Responsibilities: Preventing forest fires, rotational grazing, regulating collection of fallen dry firewood and fodder (*Ghaas*), and maintaining afforestation nurseries.

## Urban (नगरीय) Local Governance: 9 Nagar Nigams (Municipal Corporations)

Uttarakhand currently has **9 Municipal Corporations (Nagar Nigams)**, presided over by an elected Mayor (महापौर) (*Mahapaur*) and managed by a Municipal Commissioner (नगर आयुक्त) (*Mukhya Nagar Adhikari*):

1. **Dehradun** (Oldest municipal board in UK, upgraded to Corporation in 1998/2003; hosted 1st Asian Mayors' Conference in 2006).
2. **Haridwar** (हरिद्वार)
3. **Haldwani-Kathgodam** (Largest urban corporation of Kumaon)
4. **Roorkee**
5. **Kashipur** (ऊधम सिंह नगर)
6. **Rudrapur**
7. **Rishikesh** (ऋषिकेश) (created 2018)
8. **Kotdwar** (created 2018)
9. **Srinagar Garhwal** (created 2021 — first hilly municipal corporation of Garhwal)

## Confused Pairs & Common Traps

| Confused Pair | Critical Distinction |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Health Register vs Birth-Death Register** | Health register is **NOT** maintained by Panchayats; Birth-Death register **IS** maintained by Panchayats. |
| **Asian Mayors' Conference 2006 Venue** | Held at **Dehradun** (trap distractors: Mussoorie, Haridwar, Rishikesh). |
| **Women Reservation Year** | Increased to **50% in 2008** (trap distractor: 2000 or 2016). |
| **Van Panchayats Origin Year** | Conceived in **1921** (Forest Grievances Committee), legislated in **1931** (Kumaon Van Panchayat Rules). |
| **Gram Pradhan vs Block Pramukh Election** | Gram Pradhan is **directly elected**; Block Pramukh is **indirectly elected** by Kshetra Panchayat members. |

## UKPCS Inline & Practice Questions

**Q1 (UKPCS Prelims 2025, Q134).** Which record is NOT maintained by the Panchayats in Uttarakhand?  
A. Health register  
B. Family register  
C. Birth-death registration register  
D. Grant register  
*Answer:* **A** (Health register is maintained by the State Health Department).

**Q2 (UKPCS Prelims 2025, Q133).** Where was the first Asian Mayors' Conference held in Uttarakhand in 2006?  
A. Haridwar  
B. Mussoorie (मसूरी)  
C. Dehradun  
D. Rishikesh  
*Answer:* **C** (Dehradun).

**Q3.** In which year was the reservation for women in Panchayati Raj Institutions in Uttarakhand increased from 33% to 50%?  
A. 2002  
B. 2006  
C. 2008  
D. 2016  
*Answer:* **C** (March 2008 under CM B. C. Khanduri).

**Q4.** Under the Uttarakhand Panchayati Raj (Amendment) Act 2019, what is the minimum educational qualification required for a General category candidate contesting for the post of Gram Pradhan?  
A. 8th Pass  
B. 10th Pass (High School)  
C. 12th Pass (Intermediate)  
D. Graduate  
*Answer:* **B** (10th Pass for General; 8th Pass for SC/ST and Women).

**Q5.** In which year were the historic (ऐतिहासिक) 'Van Panchayats' (Forest Councils) first legally instituted in Uttarakhand?  
A. 1921  
B. 1931  
C. 1947  
D. 1973  
*Answer:* **B** (1931 under the Kumaon Van Panchayat Rules).
