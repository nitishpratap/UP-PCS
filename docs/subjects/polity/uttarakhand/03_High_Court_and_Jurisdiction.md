## 3.1 High Court sketch

**Identity:** Apex State court | seat Nainital | Part VI High Court model.

- The High Court at **Nainital** is the apex court for Uttarakhand under the Constitution.
- It exercises **original writ** jurisdiction and **appellate / supervisory** control over district and subordinate courts.
- District judiciary remains the first contact for most civil and criminal work in the hills.
- Executive capital at Dehradun does **not** move the High Court seat.

---

## 3.2 Mobile e-courts

**Identity:** Van courtroom + IT kit | remote-hill access.

- Under the **mobile e-courts** project, vans fitted as courtrooms with computer, printer, and internet connectivity visit villages.
- The policy idea is to deliver justice at the doorsteps of people in remote areas who struggle to reach district courts.
- UKPCS 2025 tested **both** the equipment description and the access purpose as true.
