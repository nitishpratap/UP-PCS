## 2.1 Public services and UKPSC

- State civil services are recruited mainly through the **Uttarakhand Public Service Commission**.
- Rules on domicile / special selection criteria appear in coaching dumps for UKPCS — keep them under Topic 4 when named in a stem.
- Centre–State administrative relations follow the general federal model; UK-only colour is in schemes and local bodies.

---

## 2.2 Advocate General and auditing

- The **Advocate General** advises the state government and performs functions analogous to the Union Attorney General at state level.
- **Auditing** of state expenditure sits with the CAG’s audit architecture; Assembly PAC / Estimates-type scrutiny is the legislative side.
- Prelims so far has stressed **first office-holder names** more than deep AG procedure.

---

## Must-Score Facts

1. UKPSC = state PSC.
2. Advocate General ≠ Attorney General of India.
3. First AG often listed as Nanda Ballabh Tiwari — still verify against the specific stem.
4. Do not confuse State Finance Commission chair with PSC chair.
5. Fund articles → Topic 5.
