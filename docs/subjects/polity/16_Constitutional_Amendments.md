## Article 368 — procedure

**Part XX.** Only article in that Part. Parliament amends; **not** a State Bill; **not** a Money Bill.

- Introduced in **either** House.
- **No** joint sitting (108 does not apply). If one House rejects, the Bill **dies**.
- After 24th: President **shall** assent. No pocket/return once it is a 368 Bill presented for assent.
- Where ratification is needed: **half** of State *legislatures* (not half of MLAs in India). **No time-limit** in 368. No State can “veto” after half have ratified.
- *Kesavananda / Minerva*: 368 is **limited** by basic structure. 42nd’s “no court” clause is **dead**.
- Who moves: Minister **or** private member. **No** prior Presidential recommendation (unlike a Money Bill / Art. 3).
- Each House must pass **separately**. RS is a full equal — it cannot be bypassed.
- *Kesavananda*: Preamble **can** be amended, but not so as to tear the basic structure (42nd did add words).
- Informal change: judicial interpretation, conventions, laws under the Constitution — **not** 368. Exam still wants the **formal** three-lane map.
- Indian type: **neither** purely flexible nor purely rigid — three lanes.
- “Total membership” = all seats of that House — **vacancies still count** in the denominator.
- “Present and voting” = only those who **cast a vote**. Abstention / walkout does **not** help the 2/3.
- State ratification: State legislature — **ordinary majority**. **No** special majority inside the State. **No** referendum. **UTs do not** ratify.
- Time-limit: **none** in 368. President may wait till half the States have ratified, then assent.
- First Amd = **1951** (Nehru). Latest syllabus bullet = **106th (2023)**.

> **Exam note:** RS is equal on a 368 Bill; vacancies still count in “total membership.”

## Three classes of amendment

| Type | Procedure | Typical use |
|------|-----------|-------------|
| **Simple majority** | Ordinary Bill; **outside** 368 | New State, 169 Council, 5th/6th Sch, salaries, quorum |
| **Special majority** | 368(2): majority of **total membership** + **2/3 present & voting** | Most amendments (FRs, DPSPs, 368-without-proviso) |
| **Special + States** | Same special majority **plus** legislatures of **not less than half** the States | Federal list (President, 73/162, SC/HC, 7th Sch, 368 itself, State RS seats, **279A**) |

### 1. Simple majority (not under 368)

These look like Constitution-editing but travel as **ordinary Bills** (Art. 4 says they are **not** 368 amendments).

- New State / change name-boundary — Arts. **3–4**.
- Legislative Council create/abolish — **169**.
- 5th / 6th Schedule — 244 machinery.
- 2nd Schedule (emoluments).
- Quorum, salaries of MPs/judges (as the article allows) — 100, 125, 221…
- Official language of a **State** — **345**.
- Citizenship rules as Parliament provides — **11**.
- UT legislature (Puducherry etc.) — **239A**.
- Number of puisne judges / acting CJ (as the article allows) — 124, 126, 223…
- English in Parliament / Union official language continuation — **120 / 343** machinery.
- Use of Hindi in courts as Parliament provides — **348**.
- All-India Services creation — **312** (RS special 2/3 — still **not** a 368 federal-ratification Bill).

### 2. Special majority — Art. 368(2)

Majority of the **total membership** of each House **and** **2/3 of members present and voting**. Most amendments (FRs, DPSPs, 368-without-proviso).

### 3. Special majority + State ratification

Same special majority **plus** legislatures of **not less than half** the States. Federal list:

1. Election of **President** (54, 55)
2. Extent of **executive** power Union/States (73, 162)
3. **Supreme Court / High Courts**
4. **7th Schedule** distribution
5. Representation of States in **Parliament**
6. **Art. 368** itself
7. GST Council **279A** (added by **101st**)

> **Exam note:** “Every amendment needs half the States” is **false**. “State reorganisation needs 368 special majority” is **false** — **simple**, Art. 4.

## Basic Structure — case ladder (2024/2021 lock)

**Kesavananda 1973** is the lock; the ladder is the 2024/2021 PYQ.

- *Shankari Prasad* **1951** — 368 includes FR. 1st Amd **valid**.
- *Sajjan Singh* **1965** — same; 17th Amd valid.
- *Golaknath* **1967** — FR **cannot** be amended; 368 is not a “law” under 13; ruling **prospective**.
- **24th Amd, 1971** — Parliament’s reply — see card below.
- *Kesavananda Bharati*, **24 Apr 1973** — Parliament **can** amend any part **including FR**, but **not** the **basic structure**. 13-judge Bench. 24th **valid**. 25th: 31C first limb (39b/c) **valid**; “no judicial review” limb **struck**.
- **42nd Amd, 1976** — inserted 368(4)(5): no court shall question; unlimited amending power.
- *Minerva Mills* **1980** — **368(4)(5) struck**. Limited amending power **is** basic structure. 42nd’s extension of **31C to all DPSP** struck.
- *Waman Rao* **1981** — 9th Schedule laws **after 24 Apr 1973** open to basic-structure review.
- *IR Coelho* **2007** — same: 9th Schedule is **not** a penthouse above basic structure.
- *NJAC / Second Judges* **2015** — **99th Amd** (NJAC) **struck** — judicial independence is basic structure. An Amd **can die**.

## 1st Amendment, 1951

**Nehru’s reply** to *Champakam Dorairajan* (caste admissions vs 15/29) · *Romesh Thappar* (press) · zamindari strikes. Assent **18 Jun 1951**. *Shankari Prasad* **upheld** it.

- **15(4)** — special provision for SEBCs + SC/ST (opens the reservation door after *Champakam*).
- **19(2)** — added **public order, friendly relations with foreign States, incitement to an offence**.
- **19(6)** — State monopoly / nationalisation is a reasonable restriction.
- **31A** — saving of laws taking **estates** (agrarian reform).
- **31B + 9th Schedule** — laws listed in the Schedule **cannot be void for FR**. Started with **13** Acts.
- Later cap: *Waman Rao / IR Coelho* — 9th Schedule laws **after 24 Apr 1973** still face **basic structure**.

**17th, 1964** (*Sajjan Singh*’s target): more Acts into the 9th Schedule; widened “estate”. Not a syllabus major — only the **1965** case’s subject.

**9th Amendment, 1960** (name-trap, not a major bullet): **Berubari** / India–Pakistan boundary. **Not** 350B (that is **7th**). **Not** the 9th *Schedule*.

## 7th Amendment, 1956

**States Reorganisation** (Fazl Ali · Kunzru · Panikkar). In force **1 Nov 1956**. Abolished Part A/B/C/D classification. Picture then: **14 States + 6 UTs**. 1st Schedule redrawn; **4th Schedule** (RS seats) recast.

- High Courts: **222** transfer of HC judges · **231** common HC for two+ States · **241** HC for UTs.
- **350A** — mother-tongue instruction at the **primary** stage.
- **350B** — **one** Special Officer for Linguistic Minorities for **all India** — **7th Amd, not 9th**. Constitutional office, not a commission.
- 239 / 239A — UT administration as a class.
- **258A** — a **State** may **entrust** functions to the Union (reverse of 258).
- Zonal Councils are **not** 7th Amd — **SRC Act 1956** (statutory). Don’t park them here.

## 24th Amendment, 1971

**Parliament’s answer to *Golaknath*.** Assent **5 Nov 1971**.

- **13(4)** — nothing in Art. 13 applies to a **368** amendment.
- **368(1)** — Parliament **may** amend **any** provision.
- President **shall** assent — pocket/return gone for a 368 Bill.
- Heading widened: “Power of Parliament to amend… and procedure.”
- *Kesavananda* — 24th **upheld**, but basic structure **caps** it.
- *Golaknath* was **prospective** — pre-1967 amendments **stayed**; 24th tried to kill the bar for the future.

## 25th Amendment, 1971

**Bank-nationalisation reply.** Triggered by *R.C. Cooper* (**1970**) — Court had read “compensation” as market value.

- Property: “compensation” in 31(2) → **amount** fixed by law.
- **31C born** — a law giving effect to **39(b) and 39(c)** cannot be void for **14 / 19**.
- Second limb: “shall not be questioned in any court” whether it gives effect to 39(b)(c).
- *Kesavananda*: first limb (39b/c shield) **valid**. Second limb (**no judicial review**) **struck**.
- Later: 42nd stretched 31C to **all** DPSP → *Minerva* **cut it back** to 39(b)(c).

## 26th Amendment, 1971

**Privy purses abolished.** Assent **28 Dec 1971**.

- Why 368: *Madhav Rao Scindia* (**1970**) struck an **executive** derecognition — so Parliament used an **amendment**.
- Omitted Arts. **291** (purses) and **362** (personal rights of rulers).
- Inserted **363A** — rulers **cease to be recognised**; purses **stop**; no court for this.
- Trap: this is **not** the 26th *January* Republic story. It is the **26th Amendment**.

## 36th Amendment, 1975

**Sikkim = 22nd State.** In force **26 Apr 1975**. **Art. 371F** special (old laws, Assembly, one LS seat, HC). 1st / 4th Schedules amended.

- **35th, 1974** — Sikkim as an **“associate State”**; a **10th Schedule** held the terms of association.
- **36th** — full statehood; that **old 10th Schedule repealed**.
- **52nd, 1985** — inserted the **present** 10th Schedule (defection). Two different 10th Schedules — **do not mix**.

## 42nd Amendment, 1976

**Mini-Constitution** (during Emergency). **Swaran Singh Committee** for duties. Date lock **1976** (2024 year-match). Act date **18 Dec 1976** (not 1975 Emergency day / not 1978).

- **Socialist, Secular, Integrity** in Preamble (2025: Socialist + Secular — **not** Sovereign/Republic, those were original).
- **DPSP:** **39A** equal justice / legal aid · **43A** workers in management (**2019**) · **48A** environment · **39(f)** children recast.
- **51A** Fundamental Duties (10 then; 11th by **86th**). Swaran Singh proposed **8**; Constitution got **10**.
- **31C** extended to **all** DPSP → *Minerva Mills* **struck** this extension.
- **368(4)(5)** — no judicial review of amendments → *Minerva* **struck**.
- **74** — advice of CoM **binding** (44th later = one reconsideration).
- **Five** State List subjects to Concurrent: education · forests · weights & measures · protection of wild animals/birds · administration of justice (except SC/HC) / courts.
- Judiciary: **32A, 131A, 144A, 226A, 228A** — curbed HC/SC; **Part XIVA** tribunals (**323A/B**).
- **43rd (1977)** repealed the judicial **bars** (32A/131A…). Tribunals **stayed**.
- Federal: 257A (Union armed units in States) — **44th repealed**.
- LS / Assembly life **6 years** (83, 172) → **44th restored 5**.
- Seat freeze: population for LS/Assembly/**President’s** value tied to **1971** census (84th later froze till the first census after **2026**).
- **31D** anti-national activities / associations — **43rd repealed**.
- **139A** transfer of certain cases to SC (this one **survived**).
- Privileges: 105/194 frozen to pre-42nd meaning; **44th** set the freeze date as **20 June 1979**.
- **312A** — Parliament may vary conditions of service of AIS officers appointed before 1966.

> **Exam note:** Mini-Constitution = largest single rewrite. 42nd packed; 44th unpacked Emergency and property — FDs, 39A/43A/48A, Concurrent shift **stayed**.

## 44th Amendment, 1978

**Janata unwind of 42nd.** Assent **30 Apr 1979** (Act year still **1978**). Unwinds the worst of 42nd: **armed rebellion** replaces internal disturbance; **written Cabinet** advice for 352; Arts. **20 and 21** unsuspendable; property → **300A**; LS can force-revoke 352.

- Property: deleted **19(1)(f)** and **31** from Part III → **300A** in Part **XII** (legal right, **not** FR).
- 352 ground: “internal disturbance” → **armed rebellion**.
- 352 process: **written** Union Cabinet · approval **1 month** · **special** majority (was **simple**) · LS **disapproval** can kill it.
- FR overlay: **358** only in **war / external aggression** · **359** never **20 & 21**.
- **74** — President may send CoM advice back **once**, then **must** act.
- **226** restored the wide HC writ (42nd had narrowed it).
- House term: LS **and** Assemblies back to **5 years** (42nd had made **6**).
- 38th “satisfaction final” gone — 352/356/360 justiciable again.
- **38(2)** — minimise inequalities of income / status / facilities — **44th, not 42nd**.
- **361A** — protection for **substantially true** reports of parliamentary proceedings.
- **22** — advisory-board safeguard for preventive detention restored.
- **71** — doubts on President / VP election back with the **SC** (39th had parked them).
- **257A** repealed (Union deploying armed units in a State).
- Privileges freeze: 105/194 freeze date set as **20 Jun 1979**.
- What 44th did **not** undo: Preamble words · 51A duties · 39A/43A/48A · 5 Concurrent subjects · tribunals 323A/B.

## 52nd Amendment, 1985

**Present 10th Schedule (anti-defection).** In force **1 Mar 1985** (Rajiv). Whip on a confidence/money/10th-Sch. vote can trigger defection.

- Grounds (para 2): voluntarily giving up membership **or** voting / abstaining vs the party whip (unless condoned in **15 days**).
- Who decides: **Speaker / Chairman** (para 6).
- Review: *Kihoto Hollohan* (**1992**) — para 7’s total bar is read down; order is **judicially reviewable**.
- Original split (para 3): **1/3** of a legislature party could split and keep seats — **91st deleted para 3**.
- Merger that remains (para 4): **≥2/3** of the legislature party.
- Independent: joining a party = defect. **No** 6-month window.
- Nominated: **6 months** to join a party; thereafter bound.
- Earlier 10th Schedule was **Sikkim associate** (35th). **36th repealed** it. **52nd wrote a new one**.

**52nd = Schedule goes in.** **91st = Schedule tightens + ministry cap + 361B.**

## 61st Amendment, 1988

**Voting age 21 → 18.** In force **28 Mar 1989**. Art. **326** for LS and State Assemblies. Does **not** change **membership** age: LS **25** / RS **30** / Assembly **25**.

- **61st** = adult suffrage age **18**.
- **62nd, 1989** = extended **SC/ST + Anglo-Indian** reservation (a **334** extension, not franchise).
- **63rd / 64th / 65th** = failed or unrelated local-body / SC-ST Bills — **not** the voting-age Amd.
- 2021 options try **62 / 63 / 66**. Answer **61st**.

## 69th Amendment, 1991

**NCT of Delhi.** Arts. **239AA / 239AB**. In force **1 Feb 1992**. Assembly + CoM with CM; **LG** as administrator. **Not** a full State.

- Delhi **cannot** legislate **public order, police, land** (and related entries) — stay Union.
- **239AB** — President may suspend 239AA machinery (Delhi analogue of **356**).
- Assembly strength by law (presently **70** elected).
- CoM collectively responsible to the **Assembly**.
- **70th, 1992** included **NCT + Puducherry** in the **Presidential electoral college** (54/55). **Not** the Amd that created NCT.
- 2022 wording: paper said “National Capital **Region**” — they mean **NCT**. NCR planning belt = **NCRPB Act 1985**, not 69th.
- GNCTD (Amendment) Act **2021** and the 2023 services tussle = **statute + SC**, not a new constitutional amendment. Don’t rename 69th.

## 86th Amendment, 2002

**RTE as a Fundamental Right.** Assent **12 Dec 2002**. Constitutionalises *Unnikrishnan* (1993) for a **defined age band**.

- **21A** — free and compulsory education, **6–14** — a **FR** (State shall provide).
- **45** recast: early childhood care and education **below 6**. Old 45 was education **till 14**.
- **51A(k)** — parent / guardian **duty** to provide education **6–14** — the **11th** Fundamental Duty.
- RTE Act **2009** is the **statute**. **21A** commenced **1 Apr 2010** — not on the 2002 assent day.
- **93rd, 2005** = **15(5)** — reservation in **private unaided** (except minority). **Not** RTE.
- **103rd, 2019** = **EWS 10%**. 2022 trap: RTE ≠ 103rd.

## 91st Amendment, 2003

**Jumbo-ministry cap + 1/3 split killed.** Stops “split then become minister.”

- Union CoM: PM + ministers ≤ **15%** of **LS** strength (**75(1A)**). RS strength is **irrelevant**.
- State CoM: CM + ministers ≤ **15%** of the Assembly, **floor 12** (**164(1A)**).
- NCT Delhi: cap is **10%** of the Assembly (**239AA**, as amended).
- 10th Schedule: **para 3 (1/3 split) deleted**. Only **≥2/3 merger** (para 4) remains.
- Defector **cannot** be a minister till re-elected — **75(1B) / 164(1B)**.
- **361B** — defector also barred from any **remunerative political post** till the term ends or (s)he is re-elected.

## 97th Amendment, 2011

**Co-operative societies.** In force **15 Feb 2012**. Three inserts:

- **19(1)(c)** — “co-operative societies” added to the freedom-to-form-associations clause.
- **43B** — DPSP: State to promote voluntary, democratic, professional co-ops.
- **Part IXB** — Arts. **243ZH–243ZT**: board size, elections, term, reservation, audit.

*Union of India v. Rajendra N. Shah* (**Jul 2021**): Part IXB as applied to **State** co-ops touched **State List Entry 32** and needed **ratification** — Parliament had **not** taken that lane. **Struck for State societies.** Survives for **multi-State** co-ops (Union List). **19(1)(c) and 43B survived** (they are not federal-list provisions).

## 101st Amendment, 2016

**GST** (levy from **1 Jul 2017**). Assent **8 Sep 2016**. Needed **State ratification** (279A is on the federal list).

- **246A** — Union **and** States may levy GST (special concurrent). **IGST** exclusive to Union — **246A(2)**.
- **269A** — IGST collected by Union, **apportioned**.
- **279A** — **GST Council**: Union FM chairs; Centre weight **1/3**, States **2/3**; decision **3/4** of weighted votes; quorum **½**.
- **366(12A)** — definition of GST.
- Omitted **268A** (service tax of the 88th).
- Out of GST: alcohol for **human consumption**. Five petroleum goods — Council may recommend bringing them in later.
- Compensation: 101st said Parliament **shall** provide. Statute: **5 years** from 2017 (later cess-extended — CA, not the Amd text).

2024 / 2021: GST Amd = **101st**, not 91 / 86 / 73 / 102 / 103 / 104.

## 102nd Amendment, 2018

**NCBC constitutionalised.** Assent **11 Aug 2018**. Constitutionalises the old **NCBC Act 1993** body. Standing NCBC = 338B from 102nd.

- **338B** — NCBC: Chair + Vice-Chair + **3** members; **President** appoints; **Parliament** may add duties.
- **342A** — **President** notifies SEBCs for a State / UT (consult Governor). **Parliament** may amend that **Central** list.
- **366(26C)** — definition of SEBC.
- Match raata: SC **338+341** · ST **338A+342** · OBC **338B+342A**.

*Jaishri Laxmanrao Patil* (Maratha, **2021**) read 342A as **only the President** may identify SEBCs even for a State — that reading is why **105th** had to follow.

## 103rd Amendment, 2019

**EWS 10%.** Assent **12 Jan 2019**. A **new** slice, not a reshuffle of 15(4)/16(4).

- **15(6)** — up to **10%** in educational institutions, **including private unaided**, **except minority** (30).
- **16(6)** — up to **10%** in public employment.
- Ceiling: **over and above** SC/ST/OBC. Does **not** eat 15(4)/16(4).
- Criteria: “economically weaker” — **State/Centre** notify (income / land / house). Not a caste list.
- *Janhit Abhiyan* (**7 Nov 2022**) — **upheld 3–2**. 50% ceiling is **not** inviolable for this slice. Dissent: economic-only + breach of 50%.
- Trap: 2022 RTE question options include **103rd** — RTE is **86th**.

## 104th Amendment, 2019

**Anglo-Indian nomination ends.** Effect **25 Jan 2020**. Art. **334** originally **10 years** from 26 Jan 1950; kept alive every decade.

- 8th · 23rd · 45th · 62nd · 79th · **95th** — successive **10-year** extensions (95th took SC/ST + Anglo-Indian to **25 Jan 2020**).
- **104th** — **SC/ST** seats in LS (**330**) and Assemblies (**332**) → **25 Jan 2030**. **Anglo-Indian nomination (331 / 333) ended** — **not** extended.
- SC/ST reserved *seats* in LS/LA continue till **25 Jan 2030**. Delimitation of those constituencies stays frozen till the census after **2026**.

## 105th Amendment, 2021

**State OBC lists restored.** Assent **18 Aug 2021**. Repair after the Maratha / 342A reading.

- Restores: States **may identify and notify SEBCs** for **their own** services and education.
- Does **not** touch: **Central** list remains **342A** with the President. **338B** (NCBC) stays.
- Two lists: **Central SEBC list** (Union jobs / central education) + **State SEBC list**.
- Pair: **102nd** = Centre’s NCBC + Central list. **105th** = States get list-power **back**.

## 106th Amendment, 2023

**Nari Shakti Vandan — women 33%.** Assent **28 Sep 2023**. Bill was the **128th** Amendment Bill — **106th** is the Act number.

- Where: **330A** LS · **332A** State Assemblies · also **Delhi** (239AA).
- How much: **one-third** of **total** seats, **including** within SC/ST reserved seats.
- **Not commenced.** Starts only after a **delimitation based on the first census after the Act** (**334A**). 84th freeze on LS seats lasts till the census **after 2026** — so 2024 LS had **no** 106th quota.
- How long: **15 years** from commencement (Parliament may extend).
- Rotation: seats reserved for women **rotate** after each delimitation.
- **Not** covered: **Rajya Sabha**. PRI/ULB women **≥ 1/3** is already **73rd/74th** (in force).
