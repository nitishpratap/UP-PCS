## Parts of the Constitution

- Constitution organised into **Parts** (now **25 Parts**, including IVA, IXA, IXB, XIVA).
- Original 1950: **22 Parts**; later amendments added Parts (e.g. IVA by 42nd; IX/IXA by 73rd/74th; IXB by 97th; XIVA by 42nd).
- **Part VII** repealed (States in Part B of First Schedule — historical).

### High-yield Part locks
- **III** = FR · **IV** = DPSP · **IVA** = Duties (42nd Amendment)
- **IX / IXA / IXB** = Panchayats / Municipalities / Cooperatives
- **X** = Scheduled & Tribal Areas · **XI** = Union–State relations
- **XIVA** = Tribunals · **XV** = Elections · **XVIII** = Emergency
- **Attorney General** sits under **Part V** (not Part XIII)

## Fifth vs Sixth Schedule

| Point | Fifth Schedule | Sixth Schedule |
|-------|----------------|----------------|
| Coverage | Scheduled Areas & STs in States **other than** 6th Schedule areas | Tribal areas of **Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura, Mizoram** only |
| Body | Tribes Advisory Council (State) | Autonomous District / Regional Councils |
| Focus | Administration & control; Governor’s role; Presidential notifications | Legislative/executive autonomy for listed tribal areas |
| Memory | Broader ST/Scheduled Areas tool | **AMTM** only |

## Seventh Schedule — Union / State / Concurrent Lists

- **Art. 246** + **7th Schedule** distribute legislative powers.
- **Union List** — Centre exclusive (defence, foreign affairs, atomic energy, banking, census, UPSC, etc.).
- **State List** — States exclusive (public order, police, public health & sanitation, agriculture, local government, etc.) — subject to emergency/other exceptions.
- **Concurrent List** — both can legislate; in conflict **Union law prevails** (Art. 254), with assent exceptions.
- Residuary powers → **Centre** (Art. 248 / Union List Entry 97).

### Exam-tested list items
| Item | List |
|------|------|
| Census | **Union** |
| Public Health and Sanitation | **State** |
| Adulteration of foodstuffs / goods | **Concurrent** |
| Succession | **Concurrent** |
| Criminal law / Criminal procedure | **Concurrent** |
| Trade unions; industrial disputes | **Concurrent** |
| Education (after 42nd) | **Concurrent** |
| Forests; protection of wild animals (after 42nd) | **Concurrent** |
| “Trade and Commerce” as a bare unqualified label | **Not** safely treated as whole Concurrent (Union/State split exists) — UPPCS 2024 trap |

## Eighth Schedule — Languages (22)

**22 languages:** Assamese, Bengali, Bodo, Dogri, Gujarati, Hindi, Kannada, Kashmiri, Konkani, Maithili, Malayalam, Manipuri, Marathi, Nepali, Odia, Punjabi, Sanskrit, Santhali, Sindhi, Tamil, Telugu, Urdu.

### How the number grew (exam locks)
- Original: **14** languages (1950).
- **Sindhi** added by **21st Amendment (1967)** → 15.
- **Konkani, Manipuri, Nepali** by **71st Amendment (1992)** → 18.
- **Bodo, Dogri, Maithili, Santhali** by **92nd Amendment (2003)** → **22**.

### NOT in Eighth Schedule (common traps)
- **Bhojpuri**, Rajasthani, English (English is official link language by law, not 8th Schedule), Tulu, etc.

## Ninth & Tenth Schedules

### Ninth Schedule
- Added by **1st Constitutional Amendment, 1951**.
- Protects certain Acts/Regulations (esp. land reforms) from being challenged for violating FR (historical purpose).
- After **Kesavananda** / **I.R. Coelho (2007)**: laws inserted in 9th Schedule **after 24 April 1973** can be tested on **Basic Structure**.
- **Trap:** Ninth ≠ Rajya Sabha seats.

### Tenth Schedule
- Added by **52nd Amendment, 1985** — **Anti-defection law**.
- Disqualification of MPs/MLAs on defection; Speaker/Chairman decides (subject to judicial review after *Kihoto Hollohan*).
- **91st Amendment (2003)** tightened (e.g. size of CoM; merger rules).

## Common Traps

1. Part XI ≠ Municipalities (**IXA**) — 2025
2. Part XI ≠ Tribunals (**XIVA**) — 2023
3. Ninth ≠ RS seats (**Fourth**) — 2023
4. Second ≠ RS seats (Second = emoluments) — 2019
5. Attorney General ≠ Part XIII (**Part V**) — 2020
6. 11th Schedule = **29**, not 22/24/32 — 2019
7. 12th Schedule = **18**, not 29
8. Sixth Schedule = **AMTM only** — not Nagaland/Sikkim — 2019
9. **Bhojpuri** not in 8th Schedule — 2025
10. Nepali / Dogri / Bodo **are** in 8th Schedule
11. Bare “Trade and Commerce” ≠ whole Concurrent — 2024
12. Public Health = **State** List; Census = **Union** List
13. Duties = **Part IVA**, not Part IV
14. Anti-defection = **10th**, not 9th
15. Citizenship = **Part II** — 2018
16. 9th Schedule added by **1st** Amendment; 10th by **52nd**
