## Core heads (state)

- Physical geography of UP — location, boundaries, physiography, rivers, climate, soils, forests, minerals, agro-climatic regions
- History, culture, folk arts, literature, heritage sites, crafts / ODOP, freedom movement in UP
- State polity and administration — Governor, CM, legislature, High Court, districts/divisions, PRIs, ULBs, UPSCs / SEC / SFC
- UP economy — GSDP, industry, MSME, industrial policy, corridors, power and logistics
- Agriculture, irrigation, rural economy, livestock, farmer welfare
- Society and demography — Census UP, literacy, SC/ST, education, social welfare
- Transport, tourism, environment (PA / Ramsar), disaster management
- Schemes, policies, GI tags, awards, symbols, firsts and rolling CA
