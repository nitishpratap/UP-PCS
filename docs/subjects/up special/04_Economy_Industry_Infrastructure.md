### 2.1 Macroeconomic Profile & Sectoral Structure of Uttar Pradesh

#### A. State Gross Domestic Product (GSDP) & Economic Ambition
- **Economy Scale**: Uttar Pradesh is the **third largest state economy in India** by nominal GSDP (after Maharashtra and Tamil Nadu / Gujarat), contributing ~8.5%–9.0% to India's national GDP.
- **The $1 Trillion Dollar Target**: The Government of Uttar Pradesh has formally set a strategic goal to scale the state's GSDP to **$1 Trillion USD**, anchoring development around:
  1. Expressways and multimodal logistics corridors.
  2. The UP Defence Industrial Corridor and dedicated manufacturing clusters.
  3. Export promotion via the ODOP scheme.
  4. World-class civil aviation infrastructure (Noida International Airport at Jewar, Ayodhya, Varanasi).
- **Sectoral Contribution**:
  - **Primary Sector (Agriculture & Allied)**: Contributes ~24%–26% of GSDP, employing nearly 45%–50% of the state's total workforce.
  - **Secondary Sector (Manufacturing, Mining, Construction, Electricity)**: Contributes ~24%–26% of GSDP.
  - **Tertiary Sector (Services, IT, Banking, Tourism, Real Estate)**: Contributes the largest share (~48%–50%) of GSDP.
- **Per Capita Income Dynamics**: Despite high aggregate GSDP, UP's per capita Net State Domestic Product (NSDP) remains below the national average due to the massive population base (~24 crore).

### 2.2 Major Traditional & Modern Industries

#### A. The Agro-Based Sugar Industry
- **Pioneering Legacy**: India's first modern vacuum-pan sugar mill was established in **1903 at Pratappur in Deoria district** of Uttar Pradesh.
- **Production Dynamics**: UP is the **largest sugarcane-producing state in India** (~45%–50% of national production) and alternates with Maharashtra for the #1 spot in refined sugar output.
  - *Distinction*: UP leads in **total acreage and sugarcane volume** (sub-tropical cane belt), whereas Maharashtra leads in **cane yield per hectare and recovery rate/cooperative structure** (tropical cane belt).
- **Ethanol Blending Programme**: UP is India's leading producer of **molasses-based fuel ethanol**, contributing massively to the National Biofuel Policy target of 20% ethanol blending in petrol.

#### B. Leather, Textiles & Cottage Clusters
1. **Leather & Saddlery**:
   - Centred at **Kanpur, Agra, and Unnao**.
   - Kanpur produces world-class equestrian saddlery and safety boots; Agra specializes in commercial footwear manufacturing for domestic and export retail.
2. **Textiles & Handlooms**:
   - Kanpur was historically dubbed the **"Manchester of the East"** due to British textile mills along the Ganga.
   - Handloom and powerloom weaving clusters flourish in **Varanasi (Banarasi Silk)**, **Mau**, **Azamgarh (Mubarakpur)**, **Tanda (Ambedkar Nagar)**, and **Meerut**.
3. **Glassware at Firozabad**:
   - Known as *Suhag Nagari*, producing millions of colourful glass bangles, blown glassware, and scientific glass instruments.
4. **Carpets at Bhadohi-Mirzapur**:
   - "Carpet City" Bhadohi houses the Indian Institute of Carpet Technology (IICT). The region accounts for the majority of India's hand-knotted export carpets.

### 2.3 Mineral Wealth and Geological Distribution

Uttar Pradesh is generally poor in metallic minerals as over 70% of its area is covered by deep quaternary Gangetic alluvium. Minerals are confined almost exclusively to the **Southern Plateau and Hill Region** (Vindhyan and Precambrian formations):

1. **Coal**: Confined to the **Singrauli coalfields** in the southern part of **Sonbhadra district**.
2. **Limestone**: Massive deposits in the Vindhyan rock formations at **Kajrahat and Rohtas (Sonbhadra)** and **Bhalua, Guruma-Kanach (Mirzapur)**; supplies the regional cement industry.
3. **Silica Sand (Glass Sand)**: Extensively quarried from the weathered quartzite sandstones of the Vindhyan range at **Shankargarh, Lohgara (Prayagraj)** and **Banda/Chitrakoot**.
4. **Bauxite (Aluminium Ore)**: Found in **Banda, Chitrakoot, and Varanasi (Chandauli)**.
5. **Dolomite**: Found in **Sonbhadra (Bari village)** and **Banda**; used as a refractory flux in the steel industry.
6. **Uranium**: Traces discovered in **Lalitpur district**.
7. **Copper**: Found in the **Sonrai region of Lalitpur district**.
8. **Diaspore and Pyrophyllite**: Extensive reserves in **Jhansi, Lalitpur, Mahoba, and Hamirpur**; UP is India's leading producer.
9. **Rock Phosphate**: Extracted in **Lalitpur district** (used in phosphatic fertilizers).
10. **Gold Traces**: Found in the river sands of **Sharda and Ramganga**, and mineralized quartz veins in **Son Pahadi / Hardi area (Sonbhadra)** and **Lalitpur**.

### 2.4 Energy Profile: Thermal, Nuclear, Hydro & Renewable

#### A. Thermal Power Infrastructure
- Sonbhadra district is known as the **"Energy Capital of India"** because of the dense concentration of pithead thermal power stations:
  - **NTPC Singrauli Super Thermal Power Station (Shaktinagar, Sonbhadra)**: NTPC's first power plant (commissioned 1982).
  - **NTPC Rihand Super Thermal Power Station (Rihand Nagar, Sonbhadra)**.
  - **Obra Thermal Power Station (Sonbhadra - UPRVUNL)**.
  - **Anpara Thermal Power Station (Sonbhadra - UPRVUNL)**: Major state-owned base load station.
- **Harduaganj Thermal Power Station (Aligarh)**: The oldest thermal power station in Uttar Pradesh, established in **1942**.

#### B. Nuclear Power
- **Narora Atomic Power Station (NAPS)** located at Narora in **Bulandshahr district**.
- Operated by the Nuclear Power Corporation of India Limited (NPCIL).
- Consists of two indigenous Pressurized Heavy Water Reactors (PHWR) of $220	ext{ MWe}$ each. Uses water from the Lower Ganga Canal for secondary cooling.

#### C. Renewable Solar Power
- Uttar Pradesh Solar Energy Policy targets significant capacity expansion.
- Large-scale solar parks set up in **Bundelkhand (Jalaun, Jhansi, Lalitpur, Banda)** and **Mirzapur** (the 75 MW Dadar Kalan solar power plant was inaugurated jointly with French assistance).

### 2.5 Strategic Industrial Corridors & Flagship Policies

#### A. Uttar Pradesh Defence Industrial Corridor (UPDIC)
- Announced in the Union Budget 2018–19 to reduce import dependence in aerospace and defence manufacturing.
- Implemented by the **UP Expressways Industrial Development Authority (UPEIDA)** across **6 strategic nodes**:
  1. **Lucknow Node**: **BrahMos Aerospace** integration unit for BrahMos-NG cruise missiles and aerospace components.
  2. **Kanpur Node**: Small arms, artillery components, defence textiles, and UAV systems.
  3. **Jhansi Node**: Bharat Dynamics Limited (BDL) propulsion plant, weapon systems, and tank overhauling.
  4. **Aligarh Node**: Precision hardware, locks, drones, and small arm components.
  5. **Agra Node**: Aviation parts, logistics, and electronics.
  6. **Chitrakoot Node**: Defence testing infrastructure, propellants, and auxiliary units.
