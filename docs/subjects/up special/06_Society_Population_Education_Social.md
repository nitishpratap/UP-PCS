### 2.3 Ethnography of Major Tribes in Uttar Pradesh

#### A. The Tharu Tribe
- Largest scheduled tribe in Uttar Pradesh, inhabiting the sub-Himalayan Terai forest tract of **Lakhimpur Kheri, Bahraich, Shravasti, Balrampur, and Maharajganj**.
- **Social Organization**:
  - Matrilineal traditions where women hold high status; divided into endogamous sub-groups (*Batta, Rawat, Birtia, Mahto*).
  - Marriage: Cross-cousin marriage and negotiation through middleman (*Mazpatia*); wedding feast is called **Lathmarva Bhoj**.
- **Distinctive Religious Rituals**:
  - **Diwali as a Period of Mourning (*Shoka Parva*)**: Unlike mainstream Hindus who celebrate lights, the Tharus gather to mourn and commemorate ancestors by offering effigies and food (*Putla Vidhan / Jhad*).
  - Worship *Bhumsen* (village deity) and celebrate the **Bajhar festival** with community feasts in summer.
  - Traditional drink: Fermented rice liquor called **Jad** (*Jaad*).

#### B. The Buxa (Bhoksa) Tribe
- Concentrated in **Bijnor district** (occupying 130 villages in the Buxar forest tract).
- Claim Rajput lineage; organized into sub-castes (Buxa Rajput, Buxa Brahmin, Buxa Ahir).
- Primary deity: **Chamunda Devi** (worshipped at Kashipur).
- Practice bride-price (*Kanya Shulk*) and village council (*Biradari Panchayat*) headed by a *Takht*.

#### C. The Sahariya Tribe
- Inhabits the drought-prone, rocky forested terrain of **Lalitpur district** in Bundelkhand.
- Highly marginalized; dependent on forest minor produce collection, stone quarrying, and seasonal agriculture.

#### D. The Agariya & Baiga Tribes of Sonbhadra
- **Agariya**: Traditional indigenous iron-smelting blacksmiths who extracted iron ore from local Vindhyan rocks using clay blast furnaces.
- **Baiga**: Forest dwellers renowned for traditional botanical knowledge, herbal medicine, and acting as tribal priests (*Guniya* / *Pahan*).

### 2.4 Universities, Scientific Research and Higher Education in UP

#### A. Historic Central Universities
1. **University of Allahabad (1887)**:
   - Established on 23 September 1887; designed by Sir William Emerson.
   - Known as the **"Oxford of the East"**; granted Central University status in 2005.
2. **Banaras Hindu University (BHU), Varanasi (1916)**:
   - Established by **Pandit Madan Mohan Malaviya** under the BHU Act, 1915, with support from Dr. Annie Besant (who founded the Central Hindu College in 1898) and Darbhanga/Kashi royal houses.
   - Largest residential university in Asia.
3. **Aligarh Muslim University (AMU), Aligarh (1920)**:
   - Originated as the **Muhammadan Anglo-Oriental (MAO) College** founded in **1875** by social reformer **Sir Syed Ahmad Khan** to advance modern Western scientific education among Indian Muslims. Upgraded to a Central University in 1920.

#### B. The Lucknow CSIR & Scientific Research Cluster
Lucknow is home to the dense concentration of national scientific laboratories:

- **Birbal Sahni Institute of Palaeosciences (BSIP)** (est. 1946): Dedicated to palaeobotany, plant fossils, and radiometric dating.
