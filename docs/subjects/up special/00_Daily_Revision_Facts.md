### Boundary Touch-Points Master Grid
- **Northern Boundary:** Uttarakhand & Himachal Pradesh (Himachal touches only **Saharanpur** - shortest border).
- **North-Eastern Boundary:** **Nepal** (Touches **7 Districts:** Pilibhit, Lakhimpur Kheri, Bahraich, Shrawasti, Balrampur, Siddharthnagar, Maharajganj — 579 km border).
- **Western Boundary:** Haryana, Delhi (UT), Rajasthan.
  - Touches Haryana: Saharanpur, Shamli, Baghpat, Gautam Buddha Nagar, Aligarh, Mathura (6 districts; separated by Yamuna river).
  - Touches Delhi: Ghaziabad & Gautam Buddha Nagar.
  - Touches Rajasthan: **Mathura & Agra** (2 districts).
- **Southern Boundary:** **Madhya Pradesh** (Touches **11 Districts:** Agra, Etawah, Jalaun, Jhansi, Lalitpur, Mahoba, Banda, Chitrakoot, Prayagraj, Mirzapur, Sonbhadra — longest inter-state border).
- **South-Eastern Boundary:** **Chhattisgarh** (Touches only **Sonbhadra**) & **Jharkhand** (Touches only **Sonbhadra**).
- **Eastern Boundary:** **Bihar** (Touches 7 Districts: Sonbhadra, Chandauli, Ghazipur, Ballia, Deoria, Kushinagar, Maharajganj).

> **Four District Extremes:**  
> - **Northernmost District:** **Saharanpur** (surrounded by Haryana, HP, Uttarakhand)

## 2. Drainage System, Rivers, Canals & Wetlands of UP

### A. Major Rivers of Uttar Pradesh
| River | Entry Point in UP | Exit Point / Confluence | Total Districts in UP & Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ganga** | Enters at **Bijnor** | Exits UP at **Ballia** into Bihar | Traverses **28 districts** (~1,450 km in UP). Receives Ramganga at Kannauj, Yamuna at Prayagraj, Gomti at Saidpur (Ghazipur), Ghaghara at Chhapra (Bihar border). |
| **Yamuna** | Enters at **Saharanpur** (Faizabad village) | Joins Ganga at **Prayagraj (Triveni Sangam)** | Traverses **19 districts**. Receives Hindon at Noida, Chambal at Pachnada (Etawah), Betwa at Hamirpur, Ken at Chilla (Banda). |
| **Ramganga** | Enters at **Bijnor** (near Kalagarh) | Joins Ganga near **Kannauj** | Passes through Corbett National Park; flows via Moradabad, Bareilly. |
| **Gomti** | Originates at **Fulhar Lake (Panghaili Jheel), Pilibhit** | Joins Ganga near Saidpur, Ghazipur | **Only major Himalayan tributary originating in the plains of UP**. Lucknow, Sultanpur, Jaunpur situated on its banks. |
| **Ghaghara (Saryu)** | Enters near Kheri/Bahraich | Joins Ganga at Chhapra (Bihar) | Originates at Mapchachungo glacier (Tibet); called Karnali in Nepal, Saryu at Ayodhya. |
| **Betwa (Vetravati)** | Enters at **Lalitpur** | Joins Yamuna at **Hamirpur** | Originates in Vindhyan range (MP); Rajghat, Parichha, and Matatila dams built on it. |
| **Ken (Karnavati)** | Enters at **Banda** | Joins Yamuna at **Chilla (Banda)** | Ken-Betwa river interlinking project is India's first river link project. |
| **Son** | Enters and exits in **Sonbhadra** | Joins Ganga near Patna | Originates at Amarkantak plateau (MP). |

### B. Major Canals & Reservoirs
- **Sharda Canal:** Longest canal network in Uttar Pradesh (originates from Sharda river at Banbasa, India-Nepal border).
- **Upper Ganga Canal:** Constructed by Sir Proby Cautley (1854); originates at Haridwar (Bhimoda).
- **Govind Ballabh Pant Sagar:** **Largest artificial lake in India**, formed by the **Rihand Dam** on Rihand river at Pipri in **Sonbhadra**.
- **Parichha Dam & Matatila Dam:** Built on **Betwa river** (Jhansi / Lalitpur).
- **Keetham Lake (Sur Sarovar):** Ramsar site in **Agra**.

### C. All 10 Ramsar Wetland Sites in Uttar Pradesh
1. **Upper Ganga River (Brijghat to Narora Stretch):** First Ramsar site in UP (designated 2005).
2. **Nawabganj Bird Sanctuary** (Unnao) - renamed Shahid Chandra Shekhar Azad Bird Sanctuary.
3. **Parvati Arga Bird Sanctuary** (Gonda) - oxbow lake.
4. **Saman Bird Sanctuary** (Mainpuri).
5. **Samaspur Bird Sanctuary** (Rae Bareli).
6. **Sandi Bird Sanctuary** (Hardoi).
7. **Sarsai Nawar Jheel** (Etawah) - sarus crane habitat.
8. **Sur Sarovar (Keetham Lake)** (Agra).
9. **Haiderpur Wetland** (Muzaffarnagar & Bijnor, designated 2021).
10. **Bakhira Wildlife Sanctuary** (Sant Kabir Nagar, designated 2022).

## 3. National Parks, Tiger Reserves & Wildlife Sanctuaries

- **Only National Park in UP:** **Dudhwa National Park** (Lakhimpur Kheri, established 1977). Famous for Swamp Deer (Barasingha) and One-horned Rhinoceros reintroduction.
- **Tiger Reserves in UP (4 Total):**
  1. **Dudhwa Tiger Reserve** (Lakhimpur Kheri - declared 1987)
  2. **Pilibhit Tiger Reserve** (Pilibhit - declared 2014; won TX2 global award)
  3. **Amangarh Tiger Reserve** (Bijnor - buffer zone of Jim Corbett NP retained in UP after UK partition)
  4. **Ranipur Tiger Reserve** (**Chitrakoot** - declared 2022 as UP's 4th and India's 53rd Tiger Reserve, in Bundelkhand region).
- **Elephant Reserve:** Terai Elephant Reserve (TER) established in Lakhimpur Kheri/Pilibhit belt (2022).
- **Hastinapur Wildlife Sanctuary:** **Largest wildlife sanctuary in UP** (spread across Meerut, Muzzafarnagar, Bijnor, Amroha, Hapur).
- **Mahavir Swami Wildlife Sanctuary:** **Smallest wildlife sanctuary in UP** (Lalitpur, ~5.4 sq km).
- **Kachhua (Turtle) Sanctuary:** Varanasi (now shifted to Bhadohi/Prayagraj stretch).
- **State Symbols:** Bird: **Sarus Crane** (*Grus antigone*); Animal: **Swamp Deer** (Barasingha); Tree: **Ashoka**; Flower: **Palash / Dhak** (*Butea monosperma* - Flame of the Forest); Aquatic Animal: **Gangetic Dolphin** (*Platanista gangetica*).

## 4. 9 Agro-Climatic Zones & Agriculture of UP

1. **Tarai Zone:** Bhabhar and Terai belt along foothills (high rainfall, sugarcane, paddy).
2. **Western Plain Zone:** Meerut division, high agricultural productivity (Green Revolution heartland).
3. **Mid-Western Plain Zone:** Bareilly & Moradabad divisions.
4. **South-Western Semi-Arid Zone:** Agra, Mathura, Aligarh (low rainfall, mustard, bajra).
5. **Central Plain Zone:** Lucknow, Kanpur, Ayodhya divisions (alluvial doab).
6. **Bundelkhand Zone:** 7 districts (Jhansi, Lalitpur, Jalaun, Hamirpur, Mahoba, Banda, Chitrakoot) — semi-arid, rocky terrain, pulses, oilseeds.
7. **North-Eastern Plain Zone:** Gorakhpur division (floods, sugarcane, rice).
8. **Eastern Plain Zone:** Varanasi, Azamgarh, Ghazipur, Jaunpur divisions.
9. **Vindhyan Zone:** Mirzapur, Sonbhadra, and southern Chandauli (plateau, red soil, coarse grains).

> **UP Agricultural Ranks in India:**  
> - **Rank 1 in:** Total Foodgrain production, Wheat, Sugarcane, Potato, Milk, Mango, Guava, Ethanol production.  
> - **Rank 2 in:** Rice (after West Bengal).

## 5. Minerals & Industrial Belts in Uttar Pradesh

- Uttar Pradesh is generally poor in metallic minerals; mineral wealth is concentrated in the southern plateau districts (Bundelkhand & Vindhyan region):
  - **Uranium:** **Lalitpur** (only district in UP with Uranium occurrences)
  - **Copper:** **Sonrai area, Lalitpur**
  - **Limestone:** **Sonbhadra** (Gurma, Kanach, Kajrahat) & **Mirzapur** (supplies raw material for cement factories at Churk, Dalla, Chunar)
  - **Glass Sand (Silica Sand):** **Shankargarh & Lohgara (Prayagraj)**, Chitrakoot, Banda (derived from disintegration of Vindhyan sandstones)
  - **Bauxite (Aluminium ore):** **Banda, Chitrakoot, Chandauli** (supplies Renukoot Hindalco smelter)
  - **Coal:** **Singrauli (Sonbhadra)** — power capital of India (NTPC plants)
  - **Dolomite:** **Sonbhadra & Banda**
  - **Pyrophyllite & Diaspore:** **Jhansi, Lalitpur, Mahoba, Hamirpur**
  - **China Clay:** **Sonbhadra & Banda**
  - **Gold:** Small placer deposits in **Sharda and Ramganga river sands**; Hardi village in Sonbhadra.

## 7. Cultural Gharanas, Folk Music & Dances

- **Kathak:** Only Classical Dance of North India. Reached its zenith under **Nawab Wajid Ali Shah** of Awadh.
  - **Lucknow Gharana exponents:** Thakur Prasad, Kalka-Bindadin Maharaj, Achchan Maharaj, Shambhu Maharaj, Lachhu Maharaj, **Pt. Birju Maharaj**.
  - **Banaras Gharana exponents:** Sitara Devi, Pt. Kishan Maharaj (Tabla), Pt. Samta Prasad (Gudai Maharaj - Tabla), Bismillah Khan (Shehnai - Bharat Ratna 2001).
  - **Kirana Gharana:** Founded by **Ustad Abdul Karim Khan & Abdul Wahid Khan** in Muzaffarnagar district (Bhimsen Joshi, Gangubai Hangal belonged to this tradition).
  - **Atrali Gharana:** Aligarh.
- **Folk Music Genres:**
  - **Rasiya:** Braj region (Mathura, Vrindavan) — themed on Radha-Krishna romance.
  - **Kajri:** Mirzapur & Varanasi region — sung during monsoon / rainy season.
  - **Birha:** Eastern UP (Bhojpuri belt) — heroic and narrative folklore.
  - **Alha:** Bundelkhand region (Mahoba) — martial epic commemorating heroic deeds of Alha & Udal.
  - **Chaiti & Hori:** Purvanchal & Braj region.
- **Folk Dances of UP:**
  - **Charkula Dance:** **Braj region** — women balance a multi-tiered wooden pyramid with 108 oil lamps on their heads.
  - **Karma Dance:** **Sonbhadra & Mirzapur** (performed by tribal communities like Kharwar).
  - **Pai Danda & Diwari:** **Bundelkhand** (stick dance reminiscent of Raas Leela).
  - **Nautanki:** Traditional folk theatre of UP (Hathras & Kanpur schools).
