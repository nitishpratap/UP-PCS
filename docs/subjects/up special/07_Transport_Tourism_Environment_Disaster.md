### 2.2 Wildlife Protection, Tiger Reserves and Conservation Infrastructure

#### A. The Four Tiger Reserves of Uttar Pradesh
1. **Dudhwa Tiger Reserve (Lakhimpur Kheri & Bahraich)**:
   - Notified under Project Tiger in **1987–88**.
   - Includes Dudhwa National Park, Kishanpur Sanctuary, and Katarniaghat Sanctuary.
   - Rich in swamp deer (over 1,500 barasingha), tigers, Asian elephants, and reintroduced Indian one-horned rhinoceros.
2. **Pilibhit Tiger Reserve (Pilibhit)**:
   - Notified in **2014**; sits at the base of Siwalik foothills.
   - Drained by Sharda, Gomti, and Khakra rivers. Won the global **TX2 Award in 2020** for doubling wild tiger numbers from 25 to 65 within a decade.
3. **Amangarh Tiger Reserve (Bijnor)**:
   - Notified in **2012**; covers 95 km² on the border of Uttarakhand.
   - Functions as an essential buffer corridor for Corbett National Park tigers and elephants.
4. **Ranipur Tiger Reserve (Chitrakoot)**:
   - Notified in **October 2022** as India's 53rd Tiger Reserve and UP's 4th Tiger Reserve.
   - Covers 529.8 km² in the Bundelkhand Vindhyan hill tract, serving as a biological bridge for tigers migrating from Madhya Pradesh's Panna Tiger Reserve.

#### B. Elephant Conservation (Project Elephant)
- **Uttar Pradesh Elephant Reserve (UPER)**: Notified in **2009**, spanning 744 km² across Saharanpur and Bijnor (Shivalik Elephant Reserve).
- **Terai Elephant Reserve (TER)**: Notified in **2022** as the 33rd Elephant Reserve in India, covering 3,049 km² across Dudhwa, Katarniaghat, and Kishanpur tracts.

### 2.4 Environmental Pollution Hotspots and Disaster Vulnerability

#### A. Environmental Pollution Hotspots
1. **River Pollution (Ganga, Yamuna, Gomti)**:
   - *Kanpur Stretch of Ganga*: Heavy chromium and chemical effluent pollution from tanneries (Jajmau industrial cluster) and untreated municipal sewage.
   - *Yamuna Stretch (Delhi to Agra/Etawah)*: High biological oxygen demand (BOD) and toxic foam due to industrial discharges and lack of environmental base flow.
   - *Gomti River at Lucknow*: Critical urban pollution load; rejuvenated through the Gomti Riverfront interceptor drain project and sewage treatment plants (STPs).
2. **Taj Trapezium Zone (TTZ)**:
   - Defined geographical trapezoidal area of **10,400 km²** around the Taj Mahal covering Agra, Firozabad, Mathura, Hathras, and Etah in UP (and Bharatpur in Rajasthan).
   - Set up following the landmark Supreme Court ruling (*M.C. Mehta vs Union of India, 1996*) to protect the white marble of the Taj Mahal from acid rain ($SO_2$ and $NO_x$ emissions from Mathura Refinery, glass foundries, and brick kilns).
3. **Air Pollution in NCR Belt**:
   - Ghaziabad, Noida, Greater Noida, and Meerut frequently register severe Air Quality Index (AQI) levels during post-monsoon winter inversion months due to vehicular emissions, construction dust, and stubble burning.

#### B. Disaster Vulnerability Matrix of UP
1. **Floods (Eastern UP & Terai)**:
   - Drained by snow-fed Himalayan rivers: **Ghaghara, Rapti, Gandak, Sharda, Rohini**.
   - Districts chronically affected: Gorakhpur, Maharajganj, Deoria, Siddharthnagar, Basti, Gonda, Balrampur, Bahraich, Shravasti, Ballia.
2. **Droughts (Bundelkhand & Southern Plateau)**:
   - Highly erratic monsoon, poor soil water-retention capacity of crystalline granitic/lateritic soils, and deep water tables.
   - Chronically drought-affected districts: **Jhansi, Jalaun, Lalitpur, Hamirpur, Mahoba, Banda, Chitrakoot**.
3. **Heat Waves (*Loo*) & Lightning**:
   - Severe summer heatwaves ($>45^\circ	ext{C} - 48^\circ	ext{C}$) in Bundelkhand, Agra, Mathura, and Prayagraj.
   - Increasing incidence of thunderstorm lightning strikes during pre-monsoon and monsoon months in Mirzapur, Sonbhadra, and Bundelkhand.
4. **Institutional Setup**:
   - **Uttar Pradesh State Disaster Management Authority (UPSDMA)**: Constituted under the Disaster Management Act, 2005; headed by the **Chief Minister**. Implements the *State Disaster Management Plan (SDMP)* and the early warning emergency alert system (*Sachet*).
