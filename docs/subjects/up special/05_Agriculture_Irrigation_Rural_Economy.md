## Consolidated — 50 Must-Score Facts

1. Uttar Pradesh is divided into **9 Agro-Climatic Zones** by the Planning Commission.
2. The **South-Western Semi-Arid Zone** (Agra, Mathura) receives the **lowest rainfall** in Uttar Pradesh.
3. The **Bhabhar & Terai Zone** receives the **highest rainfall** in Uttar Pradesh.
4. **Tube-wells (Nalkoop)** account for approximately **74.9%** of the net irrigated area in UP.
5. **Canals (Nahar)** constitute the second largest source of irrigation in UP (~**15.2%**).
6. **Private tube-wells** alone irrigate over 71% of UP's cultivated farm area.
7. Uttar Pradesh has the largest total replenishable groundwater resource in India (~**40.7 bcm**).
8. The **Upper Ganga Canal** was opened on **8 April 1854**, engineered by **Sir Proby Cautley** with its offtake at **Bhimgoda, Haridwar**.
9. The **Lower Ganga Canal** was commissioned in **1878** with its offtake barrage at **Narora, Bulandshahr**.
10. The **Sharda Canal System**, commissioned in **1928** from **Banbasa**, is the **longest canal network** in UP (~12,368 km total system).
11. The **Eastern Yamuna Canal** has its offtake at **Tajewala / Hathinikund Barrage** (Saharanpur border).
12. The **Agra Canal** was opened in **1874** with its headworks at **Okhla Barrage (Delhi)** on the Yamuna.
13. The **Saryu Nahar National Project**, dedicated in December 2021, interlinks 5 rivers (Ghaghara, Saryu, Rapti, Banganga, Rohini) across 9 districts.
14. The **Ken-Betwa River Interlinking Project** is India's first national river interlink, transferring water via **Daudhan Dam** to Bundelkhand.
15. **Matatila Dam (Rani Lakshmibai Dam)** is built on the **Betwa River** in **Lalitpur district**.
16. **Rihand Dam (Govind Ballabh Pant Sagar)**, the largest reservoir by volume in India, is built on the **Rihand River** at Pipri (**Sonbhadra**).
17. **Chandauli district** is traditionally known as the **"Rice Bowl of Uttar Pradesh"** (Dhan ka Katora).
18. Uttar Pradesh ranks **#1 in India** in the production of **Wheat, Sugarcane, Potato, and Milk**.
19. UP produces over **32%–35% of total wheat** and ~**35% of total potatoes** in India.
20. The International Potato Center - South Asia Regional Centre (**CIP-SARC**) is located at **Singna, Agra** (NOT Aligarh).
21. The International Rice Research Institute - South Asia Regional Centre (**IRRI-SARC**) is located at **Varanasi**.
22. The **Indian Institute of Pulses Research (IIPR)** is situated at **Kalyanpur, Kanpur**.
23. The **Indian Institute of Sugarcane Research (IISR)** is situated at **Lucknow**.
24. The **National Sugar Institute (NSI)** is situated at **Kalyanpur, Kanpur** (under the Ministry of Consumer Affairs, GoI).
25. The **Indian Institute of Vegetable Research (IIVR)** is situated at **Jakhini, Varanasi**.
26. The **Indian Veterinary Research Institute (IVRI)** and **Central Avian Research Institute (CARI)** are located at **Izatnagar, Bareilly**.
27. The **Central Institute for Research on Goats (CIRG)** is located at **Makhdoom, Farah in Mathura district**.
28. The **Indian Grassland and Fodder Research Institute (IGFRI)** and **CAFRI** are located at **Jhansi**.
29. The **Central Institute for Subtropical Horticulture (CISH)** is situated at **Rehmankhera, Lucknow**.
30. The **Central Institute of Medicinal and Aromatic Plants (CIMAP)** is located at **Lucknow**.
31. Uttar Pradesh accounts for over **80% of India's total Mentha (Peppermint) oil output**, centred in **Barabanki, Badaun, Rampur, and Sambhal**.
32. **Malihabad (Lucknow)** is world-renowned for its GI-tagged **Dasheri Mango**.
33. **Prayagraj (Allahabad)** and Kaushambi are famous for the **Allahabad Surkha Guava** (red flesh, GI-tagged).
34. **Pratapgarh district** is the largest producer of **Aonla (Indian Gooseberry)** in India.
35. **Mahoba** is historically celebrated for its high-grade **Paan (Betel Vine / Bareja)** cultivation.
36. **Chandra Shekhar Azad University of Agriculture & Technology (CSAUAT)** was established at **Kanpur in 1975**.

### 2.1 Agrarian Profile, Cropping Patterns and Major Crops of Uttar Pradesh

#### A. Land Utilization & Cropping Intensity
- **Total Geographical Area**: 240.93 lakh hectares; **Net Sown Area**: ~165.7 lakh hectares (~68.7% of total area).
- **Cropping Intensity**: ~160%–165%, indicating extensive double-cropping facilitated by pervasive tube-well irrigation and canal networks.
- **Three Core Cropping Seasons**:
  1. **Kharif Season (Sowing: June–July; Harvesting: Sept–October)**:
     - Dependent on the South-West Monsoon.
     - Major Crops: **Paddy (Rice), Maize, Jowar, Bajra, Arhar (Tur), Urad, Moong, Groundnut, Soybean, Sesame (Til)**.
  2. **Rabi Season (Sowing: October–November; Harvesting: March–April)**:
     - Dependent on winter soil moisture and Western Disturbance rains.
     - Major Crops: **Wheat, Barley, Gram (Chana), Pea (Matar), Lentil (Masoor), Mustard (Sarson), Potato, Linseed (Alsi)**.
  3. **Zaid Season (Sowing: March–April; Harvesting: May–June)**:
     - Short summer window requiring assured irrigation.
     - Major Crops: **Watermelon, Muskmelon, Cucumber, Gourds, Moong, Sunflower, Fodder maize**.

#### B. Detailed Crop Geography
1. **Wheat**:
   - Uttar Pradesh is the **largest producer of wheat in India**, contributing over **32%–35%** of national wheat output.
   - Requires $10^\circ	ext{C} - 15^\circ	ext{C}$ during sowing/vegetative growth and $20^\circ	ext{C} - 25^\circ	ext{C}$ during grain filling/harvesting, with 50–75 cm rainfall.
   - Major producing districts: **Meerut, Muzaffarnagar, Bulandshahr, Moradabad, Bareilly, Badaun, Aligarh, Gorakhpur**.
2. **Sugarcane**:
   - Cash crop occupying ~22–25 lakh hectares; UP is the **#1 producer of sugarcane** in India.
   - Primary concentration in two belts:
     - *Western UP Belt*: Meerut, Muzaffarnagar, Saharanpur, Bijnor, Moradabad, Baghpat, Bulandshahr.
     - *Terai / Eastern Belt*: Deoria, Kushinagar, Gorakhpur, Lakhimpur Kheri, Basti, Gonda.
   - *Exam Distinction*: UP produces the highest **gross volume** of cane, but Maharashtra typically records higher **sucrose recovery percentage and yield per hectare** due to tropical maritime climatic advantages.
3. **Paddy (Rice)**:
   - Primary kharif staple occupying ~60 lakh hectares.
   - Densely cultivated in the high-rainfall **Terai and Eastern Plains (Purvanchal)**: Siddharthnagar, Maharajganj, Kushinagar, Gorakhpur, Deoria, Varanasi, Chandauli (known as the **"Rice Bowl of UP"**).
   - **Kalanamak Rice**: Cultivated in Siddharthnagar, Balrampur, and Basti; prized for its rich aroma, black husk, and high iron-zinc content (GI-tagged).
4. **Potato**:
   - UP ranks **#1 in India** in potato production (~35% of national output).
   - Major potato belt: **Agra, Firozabad, Farrukhabad, Kannauj, Mainpuri, Etah, Aligarh, Hathras, Mathura**.
   - International research hub: **CIP-SARC located at Singna, Agra**.
5. **Pulses and Oilseeds**:
   - Pulses (Gram, Lentil, Arhar) are heavily concentrated in the rainfed **Bundelkhand region** (Hamirpur, Banda, Jalaun, Jhansi, Mahoba, Lalitpur).
   - Mustard and Rapeseed dominate in **Western UP and South-Western semi-arid districts** (Agra, Mathura, Aligarh).
6. **Mentha (Peppermint)**:
   - UP accounts for over **80% of India's total mentha oil production** (India is the world's largest exporter of natural menthol).
   - Major clusters: **Barabanki, Badaun, Rampur, Sambhal, Moradabad, Bareilly, Jalaun**. Supported by CSIR-CIMAP, Lucknow.

#### B. Groundwater Extraction & "Dark Zones"
- UP has the largest replenishable groundwater resources in India (~**40.7 billion cubic metres - bcm**).
- However, heavy concentration of private electric/diesel tube-wells in **Western UP** (Meerut, Saharanpur, Muzaffarnagar, Baghpat, Aligarh) has led to severe over-exploitation, creating notified **Over-Exploited Blocks ("Dark Zones")** and critical water table depletion.
- State interventions: **Mukhyamantri Khet Talab Yojana** (farm ponds in Bundelkhand), promotion of micro-irrigation (drip/sprinkler), and solar pump energization under PM-KUSUM.

### 2.4 Major State Agricultural Universities (SAUs)

1. **Chandra Shekhar Azad University of Agriculture & Technology (CSAUAT), Kanpur**:
   - Established in **1975** (evolved from the Government Agricultural College, Kanpur, founded 1906).
2. **Acharya Narendra Deva University of Agriculture & Technology (ANDUAT), Kumarganj (Ayodhya)**:
   - Established in **1975**; caters to the agro-development of eastern UP.
3. **Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel University of Agriculture & Technology (SVPUAT), Modipuram (Meerut)**:
   - Established in **2000**; caters to the agricultural needs of the Western Plain.
4. **Banda University of Agriculture & Technology (BUAT), Banda**:
   - Established in **2010**; dedicated to dryland farming and pulses research in the Bundelkhand region.
5. **Sam Higginbottom University of Agriculture, Technology and Sciences (SHUATS), Naini (Prayagraj)**:
   - Established in **1910** by Dr. Sam Higginbottom as Allahabad Agricultural Institute (now a premier private deemed university).
6. **Rani Lakshmi Bai Central Agricultural University (RLBCAU), Jhansi**:
   - Established in **2014** by an Act of Parliament as a **Central Agricultural University** for the Bundelkhand region.
