## Quick Revision — Spine Only

| Tribe / people | Remember |
|----------------|----------|
| **Kirata** | Early hill people in epic and Puranic geography of the Himalaya |
| **Khasa** | Long-lived Himalayan ethnonym; later linked in traditions to several hill polities |
| **Kuninda** | First well-attested **coin-issuing** hill polity of the UK–HP foothills |
| **Yaudheya** | Warrior **gana** of the plains fringe; coins also reach Garhwal–Kumaon finds |
| **Tanganas / other epic names** | Appear in Mahabharata geography of the north; treat as literary tribes, not full dynasties |

## Confused Pairs

| Pair | Lock |
|------|------|
| Tribe name vs dynasty capital | **Kuninda** is a people/polity; **Kartikeyapur** is a later capital seat |
| Kirata vs Khasa | Both are Himalayan ethnonyms; do not force one capital city on either without a source |
| Yaudheya vs Kuninda | Yaudheya = famous **plains republic/gana**; Kuninda = **foothill** coin power rooted in UK–HP |

---

## 2.1 How UKPCS asks “ancient tribes”

- Syllabus line **“Ancient tribes of Uttarakhand”** expects ethnonyms from epics/Puranas **plus** the first historic coin powers.
- Prefer one-sentence identity: who they were, where they sat, what source proves them (epic / coin / Chinese traveller).

---

## 2.2 Literary tribes

- Epic geography places several Himalayan peoples in the north — **Kirata** and related hill names appear in Mahabharata and later Puranic lists.
- **Khasa** survives as a durable Himalayan identity label in later traditions of Kumaon–Garhwal.
- These names prove **early peopling and naming**, not a single dated kingdom list.

---

## 2.3 Historic tribal / gana powers

- After the Mauryan–post-Mauryan churn, **Kuninda** coinage marks the first clear political economy of the central Himalayan foothills.
- **Yaudheya** power sits mainly in the Sapta-Sindhu / Haryana–Punjab–Rajasthan belt, but coin finds in the hills show trade and contact with Garhwal–Kumaon.
- Full teaching cards for both sit in [Topic 3](03_Kuninda_and_Yaudheya.md).

---

## Must-Score Facts

1. “Ancient tribes” = literary Himalayan ethnonyms + early coin polities.
2. Kuninda is the first strong **Uttarakhand-linked** political name for Prelims.
3. Yaudheya is a **gana**; do not call it a Chandela-style monarchy.
