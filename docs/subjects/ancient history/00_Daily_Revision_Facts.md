### B. Vedic Literature & Upanishadic Dialogues (Match Goldmine)
- **Rigveda**: 10 Mandalas, 1,028 Suktas (hymns); 3rd Mandala contains **Gayatri Mantra** (composed by Vishvamitra dedicated to solar deity Savitr); 7th Mandala describes **Dasrajan Yudha** (Battle of 10 Kings on Parushni/Ravi river won by Sudas of Bharata tribe); 9th Mandala dedicated entirely to **Soma**; 10th Mandala contains **Purusha Sukta** (first mention of 4 Varnas).
- **Samaveda**: Book of chants / melodies (*Ragas*); origin of Indian classical music; chanted by *Udgatri* priests.
- **Yajurveda**: Book of sacrificial prayers and rituals; divided into **Shukla Yajurveda** (White - poetry) and **Krishna Yajurveda** (Black - prose + verse).
- **Atharvaveda**: Charms, spells, magical formulas, everyday medicine; mentions *Sabha* and *Samiti* as twin daughters of Prajapati.
- **Key Brahmanas**:
  - *Aitareya & Kaushitaki*: Rigveda.
  - *Shatapatha Brahmana*: Largest and oldest Brahmana (attached to Shukla Yajurveda); story of Videgha Mathava carrying Agni across Sadaneera/Gandak.
  - *Gopatha Brahmana*: Attached to Atharvaveda.
- **Famous Upanishadic Dialogues (Direct UPPSC/UKPSC Match-ups)**:
  - **Mundaka Upanishad**: Sourced national motto **"Satyam Eva Jayate"** (*Truth Alone Triumphs*).
  - **Katha Upanishad**: Famous philosophical dialogue between young boy **Nachiketa and Yama (God of Death)** on the nature of soul and immortality.
  - **Brihadaranyaka Upanishad**: Largest Upanishad; contains celebrated philosophical debate between sage **Yajnavalkya and scholarly woman Gargi**; phrase *"Tamaso Ma Jyotirgamaya"*.
  - **Chhandogya Upanishad**: Contains story of **Satyakama Jabala** (boy who spoke truth about his parentage admitted by sage Gautama); dialogue between Uddalaka Aruni and son Svetaketu (*Tat Tvam Asi*).
  - **Jabala Upanishad**: First explicit mention of the **Four Ashramas** (Brahmacharya, Grihastha, Vanaprastha, Sanyasa).

### C. Six Vedangas (Limbs of the Vedas)
1. **Shiksha**: Phonetics and Pronunciation.
2. **Kalpa**: Ritual instructions (Shrauta Sutras, Grihya Sutras, Dharma Sutras, and **Sulba Sutras — ancient Indian Geometry & measurement!**).
3. **Vyakarana**: Grammar (Panini's *Ashtadhyayi*).
4. **Nirukta**: Etymology of Vedic words (Yaska's *Nirukta*).
5. **Chhanda**: Metrics and Poetic meters (Pingala's *Chhandasastra*).
6. **Jyotisha**: Astronomy and calculation of auspicious ritual calendar (Lagadha's *Vedanga Jyotisha*).

### D. Six Schools of Indian Philosophy (Shad-Darshana)
| School of Philosophy | Founder / Sage | Foundational Text | Core Philosophical Principle |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Samkhya** (*Oldest school*) | **Kapila Muni** | *Samkhya Sutra* | Dualistic realism: **Purusha** (Consciousness) and **Prakriti** (Matter); 25 basic elements |
| **2. Yoga** | **Patanjali** | *Yoga Sutra* | Eightfold path (**Ashtanga Yoga**: Yama, Niyama, Asana, Pranayama, Pratyahara, Dharana, Dhyana, Samadhi) |
| **3. Nyaya** | **Akshapada Gautama** | *Nyaya Sutra* | Logic, epistemology, and rigorous reasoning; 4 Pramanas (Pratyaksha, Anumana, Upamana, Shabda) |
| **4. Vaisheshika** | **Kanada (Uluka)** | *Vaisheshika Sutra* | **Atomic theory (Paramanu)**; universe composed of 9 physical substances; scientific classification |
| **5. Purva Mimamsa** | **Jaimini** | *Mimamsa Sutra* | Unquestioned authority of Vedas; ritualism, Dharma, and Vedic karma |
| **6. Uttara Mimamsa (Vedanta)** | **Badarayana (Vyasa)** | *Brahma Sutra* | Philosophy of Upanishads; Brahman is supreme reality; developed by Shankara (Advaita) & Ramanuja |

## 4. Ashoka's 14 Major Rock Edicts (MRE) — Complete Cheat-Sheet

| Edict | Core Subject / Mandate Inscribed |
|---|---|
| **Major Rock Edict I** | Prohibition of animal slaughter and festive gatherings (*Samaja*). |
| **Major Rock Edict II** | Medical treatment for men and animals in his empire and border lands; mentions **Cholas, Pandyas, Satiyaputras, Keralaputras, and Antiochus II Theos of Syria**. |
| **Major Rock Edict III** | Liberal attitude towards Brahmanas and Shramanas; ordering **Yuktas, Rajukas, and Pradeshikas** to go on tour of inspection every 5 years (*Anusamyana*). |
| **Major Rock Edict IV** | Sound of Dhamma-ghosha replaced the sound of Bheri-ghosha (war drum). |
| **Major Rock Edict V** | Appointment of **Dhamma Mahamattas** (in 14th regnal year) to propagate morality. |
| **Major Rock Edict VI** | King's accessibility: reporters (**Pativedakas**) ordered to report public matters to Ashoka at all times and places. |
| **Major Rock Edict VII** | Universal religious tolerance among all sects. |
| **Major Rock Edict VIII** | Replaced royal pleasure trips (*Viharayatras*) with religious tours (**Dhammayatras**); visited Bodh Gaya in 10th regnal year. |
| **Major Rock Edict IX** | Condemned meaningless rituals and ceremonies; extolled Dhamma-mangala. |
| **Major Rock Edict X** | Denounced individual fame and glory in favour of moral attainment. |
| **Major Rock Edict XI** | Declared that the gift of Dhamma (*Dhamma-dana*) is the highest of all gifts. |
| **Major Rock Edict XII** | Directive on mutual tolerance among all religious sects; appointment of *Ithijhakha Mahamattas* (female affairs officers). |
| **Major Rock Edict XIII** | **MOST IMPORTANT EDICT**: Records the **Kalinga War (261 BCE, 8th regnal year)**, deep remorse over 1,00,000 killed and 1,50,000 captured; victory by Dhamma declared supreme; **mentions 5 contemporary Greek Kings**:  <br>1. **Antiochus II Theos** (Syria)  <br>2. **Ptolemy II Philadelphus** (Egypt)  <br>3. **Antigonus Gonatas** (Macedonia)  <br>4. **Magas** (Cyrene)  <br>5. **Alexander** (Epirus). |
| **Major Rock Edict XIV** | Summarizes the purpose and recording of the various rock inscriptions. |

### B. Buddha's 5 Great Life Events & Symbols
1. **Janma (Birth)**: Lotus and Bull (Lumbini, Kapilavastu).
2. **Mahabhinishkramana (Great Renunciation)**: Horse (Kanthaka).
3. **Nirvana / Sambodhi (Enlightenment)**: Bodhi Tree (Pipal / Ficus religiosa) at Bodh Gaya on Niranjana (Phalgu) river.
4. **Dharmachakrapravartana (First Sermon)**: Wheel / 8-Spoked Chakra (Deer Park at Sarnath / Rishipattana).
5. **Mahaparinirvana (Death)**: Stupa / Footprints (Sal tree grove at Kushinagar).

### C. Jain Tirthankaras & Symbols (High-Frequency Match)
- **1st Rishabhanatha (Adinatha)**: **Bull** (Emblem) | Born in Ayodhya | Mentioned in Rigveda with Arishtanemi.
- **2nd Ajitanatha**: **Elephant** | Born in Ayodhya.
- **16th Shantinaatha**: **Deer**.
- **19th Mallinatha**: **Water Jar (Kalasha)** | Shvetambaras consider Mallinatha a female.
- **22nd Neminatha (Arishtanemi)**: **Conch (Shankha)** | Cousin of Krishna.
- **23rd Parshvanatha**: **Serpent / Hooded Cobra** | Born in Varanasi (Ashvasena's son); propounded 4 vows (*Chaturyama*).
- **24th Mahavira (Vardhamana)**: **Lion** | Born in Kundagrama (Vaishali), died at Pavapuri; added 5th vow: **Brahmacharya**.
- **Jain Councils**:
  - **1st Council (c. 300 BCE)**: Pataliputra | Presided by **Sthulabhadra** | 12 Angas compiled | Split into Shvetambara (white-clad, led by Sthulabhadra) and Digambara (sky-clad, led by Bhadrabahu).
  - **2nd Council (512 CE)**: Vallabhi (Gujarat) | Presided by **Devardhi Kshamasramana** | Final compilation of 12 Angas & 12 Upangas in Ardhamagadhi Prakrit.

## 8. Ancient Ports & Ancient Coins

### A. Major Ancient Indian Ports (Direct Match Questions)
- **Western Coast**:
  - **Bharuch / Barygaza** (Narmada estuary, Gujarat): Most active port for trade with Roman Empire.
  - **Sopara (Surparaka)** (near Mumbai, Maharashtra): Ashokan edict site; major trade center.
  - **Kalyan (Calliena)** (Thane, Maharashtra).
  - **Muziris / Kodungallur** (Periyar river mouth, Kerala): Roman trade hub; exported black pepper (**"Yavanapriya"**); temple of Augustus built here.
  - **Nelcynda & Tyndis** (Kerala coast).
- **Eastern Coast**:
  - **Tamralipti / Tamluk** (Midnapore, West Bengal - Hooghly river): Premier port for trade with Southeast Asia, China, and Sri Lanka; Fa-Hien sailed back to China from here.
  - **Arikamedu (Podouke)** (near Puducherry): Roman trading post; excavated by Mortimer Wheeler; Roman amphorae, Arretine ware, and Roman coins found.
  - **Poompuhar / Kaveripattinam** (Kaveri mouth, Tamil Nadu): Capital port of the Early Cholas (celebrated in *Silappadikaram*).
  - **Korkai** (Tamil Nadu): Famous pearl-fishery port of the Pandyas.

### B. Ancient Coins & Currency Units
- **Nishka / Satamana**: Gold ornaments/weights in Vedic period; later evolved into currency terms.
- **Karshapana / Pana**: Standard **silver punch-marked coins** (bearing symbols of sun, tree, hill, peacock) in Mauryan and Mahajanapada era.
- **First Gold Coins in India**: Issued by **Indo-Greeks (2nd c. BCE)**; also first to issue coins with portraits and legends of kings.
- **Largest Number of Pure Gold Coins**: Issued by **Kushanas** (Vima Kadphises and Kanishka - *Dinar* based on Roman denarius weight standard).
- **Largest Quantity of Gold Coins**: Issued by **Guptas** (*Dinar* for gold; *Rupaka* for silver, started by Chandragupta II after defeating Shakas).
- **Lead and Potin Coins**: Distinctive currency issued by the **Satavahanas**.
- **Cowries (Kaudi)**: Common medium of exchange for daily petty transactions (recorded by Fa-Hien in Gupta era).
