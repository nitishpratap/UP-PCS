#### Major Tax Reforms & Proposals (Union Budget 2026–27)

- **Cloud Data Centres Tax Holiday to 2047**: 100% tax holiday granted until the year **2047** for foreign companies providing global cloud services using Indian data centres (subject to routing through Indian resellers).
- **IFSC / GIFT City Tax Holiday Doubled**: Extended from **10 years to 20 years** for IFSC units and Offshore Banking Units, with a concessional 15% rate applicable thereafter.
- **Minimum Alternate Tax (MAT) Rationalisation**: Corporate MAT rate reduced from **15% to 14%**; MAT credit accumulation discontinued from April 2026.
- **TCS Relief**: Tax Collected at Source (TCS) on overseas education remittances, medical treatments, and tour packages slashed to **2%**.
- **Share Buyback Taxation Overhaul**: Buybacks taxed as capital gains; additional buyback tax levied on promoters raising effective tax to **22% for corporate promoters and 30% for non-corporate promoters**.
- **Securities Transaction Tax (STT)**: Calibrated upward revision on futures and options (F&O) contracts.
- **Foreign Assets Disclosure Scheme 2026**: Introduced for small taxpayers, providing graded relief and immunity upon voluntary declaration.
- **FDI / Portfolio Liberalisation**: Persons Resident Outside India (PROI) allowed to invest up to **10%** in listed Indian companies under the Portfolio Investment Scheme.

### Economic Survey 2025–26 | Comprehensive Master Analysis

**Overview & Philosophy**
The **Economic Survey 2025–26** was presented in Parliament by Union Finance Minister **Smt. Nirmala Sitharaman**, prepared by the Economic Division of the Department of Economic Affairs under the leadership of Chief Economic Adviser (CEA) **Dr. V. Anantha Nageswaran**.

- **Reorganised Architecture:** Structured into **17 chapters** by depth and time-relevance (broadened and longer than prior editions), featuring dedicated special essays on:
  1. **Artificial Intelligence** (opportunities and risks for labour markets);
  2. **Quality of Life in Indian Cities** (urbanisation, mobility, and liveability);
  3. **State Capacity and Private Sector Strategic Resilience** (building indispensable national capabilities).
- **Core Framing — "Marathon and Sprint":** Balancing long-term structural growth and resilience (*the marathon*) with agile short-term shock absorption and stabilization (*the sprint*).
- **Paradigm Shift — The "Entrepreneurial State":** Urges a transition from compliance-driven regulation to capability-driven **entrepreneurial policymaking** under global uncertainty. An Entrepreneurial State acts early, structures risks, learns through experimentation, and adapts dynamically without paralysis.
- **Three Global Scenarios for 2026:**
  1. *Managed disorder*;
  2. *Disorderly multipolar breakdown*;
  3. *Low-probability high-impact shock cascade* (worse than the 2008 Global Financial Crisis).
  - Across all scenarios, India is relatively better placed due to its large domestic market, less financialised growth model, strong reserves, and credible strategic autonomy.
  - Prescribes **strategic sobriety over defensive pessimism**: supply stability, strategic buffers, diversified trade routes, and alternative payment architectures.
  - Emphasises that India's scale (**145 crore population**) and vibrant democracy make copying external development blueprints unrealistic.

#### 2. Fiscal Developments & Revenue Metrics

- **Centre's Revenue Receipts:** Rose to **9.2% of GDP in FY25 (Provisional Actuals)**, up from an average of ~8.5% during FY16–FY20.
- **Non-Corporate Tax Collections:** Structurally expanded from ~2.4% of GDP pre-pandemic to **3.3% of GDP post-pandemic**.
- **Direct Tax Base Widening:** Income Tax Returns (ITR) filed increased dramatically from **6.9 crore (FY22) to 9.2 crore (FY25)**.
- **Gross GST Collections:** Stood at **₹17.4 lakh crore** during April–December 2025 (+6.7% YoY growth); e-way bill generation surged by **21% YoY**.
- **Effective Capital Expenditure:** Reached **4.0% of GDP in FY25** (vs. 2.7% pre-pandemic).
- **SASCI Impact:** States maintained capital spending at ~**2.4% of GDP** via the Special Assistance to States for Capital Expenditure scheme.
- **Combined State Fiscal Deficit:** Edged up to **3.2% of GDP in FY25** (from ~2.8% post-pandemic).
- **Public Debt Trajectory:** General government debt-to-GDP ratio reduced by **7.1 percentage points** since 2020 peak.

---

#### 3. Monetary Management & Financial Intermediation

- **Banking Asset Quality (SCBs):**
  - Gross Non-Performing Assets (GNPA): Plunged to **2.2%** as of September 2025 (multi-decadal low).
  - Net Non-Performing Assets (NNPA): Declined to **0.5%** as of September 2025 (historic record low).
- **Credit Growth:** Outstanding credit of Scheduled Commercial Banks rose **14.5% YoY** as of 31 December 2025 (up from 11.2% in Dec 2024).
- **Financial Inclusion (PMJDY):**
  - Total bank accounts opened: **55.02 crore** (as of March 2025).
  - Rural and semi-urban accounts: **36.63 crore** (>66%).
- **Capital Markets & Retail Deepening:**
  - Unique equity investors crossed **12 crore** (September 2025), with nearly **25% being women**.
  - Total demat accounts crossed **21.6 crore** (adding 235 lakh demat accounts during FY26 till Dec 2025).
  - Unique mutual fund investors expanded to **5.9 crore** (December 2025), driven strongly by non-tier I and tier II/III cities.
- **Regulatory Stress Resilience:** The joint **IMF–World Bank Financial Sector Assessment Program (FSAP 2025)** validated the resilience, regulatory depth, and diversification of the Indian financial architecture.

---

#### 4. External Sector & International Trade

- **Global Trade Footprint:**
  - India's share of **global merchandise exports** nearly doubled from **1.0% (2005) to 1.8% (2024)**.
  - India's share of **global commercial services exports** more than doubled from **2.0% (2005) to 4.3% (2024)**.
  - India is the **world's 7th largest services exporter**.
- **Export Totals (FY25):**
  - Total Exports: Record **USD 825.3 billion** (+6.1% YoY).
  - Services Exports: Record high of **USD 387.6 billion** (+13.6% YoY).
  - Over **80% of total FDI inflows** during FY23–FY25 went into services.
- **Global Remittances Leader:** India remained the **world's #1 recipient of remittances**, receiving **USD 135.4 billion in FY25**, which significantly buffered the merchandise trade deficit.
- **Current Account Deficit (CAD):** Remained moderate and sustainable at around **1.3% of GDP** in Q2 FY26.
- **Foreign Exchange Reserves:** Touched **USD 701.4 billion** (as of 16 January 2026), providing **~11 months of import cover** and covering over **94% of India's external debt**.
- **Foreign Direct Investment (FDI):**
  - Gross FDI inflows reached **USD 64.7 billion** (April–November 2025).
  - Greenfield Investments: India ranked **4th globally** in Greenfield announcements (2024) and emerged as the **#1 destination worldwide for Greenfield digital investments (2020–24)**.

#### 5. Inflation Dynamics

- **Record Headline Disinflation:** India recorded its **lowest headline CPI inflation since the inception of the CPI series**, averaging **1.7% during April–December 2025**.
- **Drivers:** Primarily driven by sharp deflation/moderation in food and fuel prices, which jointly constitute **52.7% of the CPI basket**.
- **Global Standing:** Among major Emerging Markets and Developing Economies (EMDEs), India achieved one of the sharpest disinflations, with headline CPI falling by **1.8 percentage points in 2025 over 2024**.

- **Digital Agri Platforms & Subsidies:**
  - **e-NAM:** Onboarded **1.79 crore farmers**, **2.72 crore traders**, and **4,698 FPOs** across **1,522 regulated mandis** (by 31 December 2025).
  - **PM-KISAN:** Disbursed over **₹4.09 lakh crore** directly to farmer accounts.
  - **PM-KMY (Kisan Maandhan):** Enrolled **24.92 lakh farmers**.
  - **SVAMITVA:** Completed drone mapping surveys across **3.28 lakh villages**; scaled livelihood training through **Drone Didis**.

#### 7. Industry, Infrastructure & Technology

- **Production-Linked Incentive (PLI):** Operational across **14 sectors**; attracted **₹2.0 lakh crore** in actual investment, generated **₹18.7 lakh crore** in incremental production, and created over **12.6 lakh jobs**.
- **India Semiconductor Mission (ISM):** Advanced with **10 approved projects** involving **₹1.60 lakh crore** cumulative investment across 6 states.
- **Capital Expenditure Quantum:** Central government capex multiplied **4.2 times** from ₹2.63 lakh crore (FY18) to **₹11.21 lakh crore in FY26 (BE)**; effective capex reached **₹15.48 lakh crore**.
- **National Highways:** Network expanded by **~60% since FY14**; high-speed 4/6/8-lane expressways increased **nearly 10-fold**.
- **Indian Railways:** Route network reached **69,439 route km**, achieving **99.1% electrification**, with 3,500 km new additions targeted in FY26.
- **Civil Aviation:** India emerged as the **world's 3rd largest domestic aviation market**; operational airports jumped from **74 (2014) to 164 (2025)**.
- **Power & Energy Transition:**
  - Historic turnaround: Power DISCOMs posted a aggregate **positive PAT of ₹2,701 crore** in FY25.
  - Renewable energy accounts for **nearly 50% of total installed capacity**.
  - Capacity added in FY26 (till Dec 2025): **38.61 GW of renewables**, led by **30.16 GW of solar power**.
- **Telecom & Digital:** 5G services cover **>99.9% of all districts**; overall tele-density reached **86.76%**; rural tap water coverage under Jal Jeevan Mission crossed **81%**.
- **Space Tech:** India became the **4th nation in the world** to achieve autonomous space docking capability (**SpaDeX**).

#### 8. Social Infrastructure, Human Development & Employment

- **School Education:** Operates one of the world's largest schooling networks, serving **24.69 crore students** across **14.71 lakh schools**. Gross Enrolment Ratios (GER): **90.9%** (Primary), **90.3%** (Upper Primary), and **78.7%** (Secondary).
- **Higher Education Expansion:** Expanded to **70,018 institutions** (including **23 IITs, 21 IIMs, and 20 AIIMS**), alongside international offshore campuses (**IIT Zanzibar** in Tanzania and **IIT Delhi-Abu Dhabi** in UAE). Implementation of National Credit Framework (NCrF) and Academic Bank of Credits (ABC).
- **Health Indicators:**
  - Maternal Mortality Ratio (MMR): **-86% decline** since 1990.
  - Under-Five Mortality Rate (U5MR): **-78% decline** since 1990.
  - Infant Mortality Rate (IMR): **-37% decline** over the past decade (down from 40 per 1,000 live births in 2013 to **25 in 2023**).
- **Employment & Skilling:**
  - Total employment reached **56.2 crore** in Q2 FY26.
  - Organised manufacturing employment grew **6% in FY24**, adding over **10 lakh jobs**.
  - Formal recognition of gig and platform workers under Labour Codes; **e-Shram** portal registered over **31 crore workers**, with women constituting **54%**.
  - **National Career Service (NCS):** Mobilised ~**80 million vacancies**.
  - Proposed upgradation of **1,000 ITIs** to align with Industry 4.0.
- **Poverty Reduction:** As per the revised **World Bank international poverty line ($2.15/day)**, India's extreme poverty plummeted to **5.3% in 2022–23**.
- **Social Services Spending:** Government expenditure on social services rose to **7.9% of GDP in FY26 (BE)**.

### Banking Laws (Amendment) Act, 2025 | Effective 1 August 2025

**What happened**
The **Banking Laws (Amendment) Act, 2025** came into force on **1 August 2025**, amending five major banking statutes: Reserve Bank of India Act 1934, Banking Regulation Act 1949, State Bank of India Act 1955, Banking Companies (Acquisition and Transfer of Undertakings) Acts 1970 and 1980.

**Key High-Yield Amendments**
- **Substantial Interest Limit Enhanced**: The monetary threshold defining "substantial interest" in a company/firm for bank directors was **raised from ₹5 lakh to ₹2 crore** (the first revision since 1968, correcting for 57 years of economic expansion).
- **Tenure of Directors in Cooperative Banks**: Maximum tenure of directors (other than Chairman/Whole-time Director) in Primary Cooperative Banks capped at **10 consecutive years** (up from 8 years).
- **Nomination Facility Multiplied (Max 4 Nominees)**:
  - **Bank Deposit Accounts**: Depositors can now nominate up to **4 nominees**, either *successively* (in order of priority) or *simultaneously* (with specified percentage shares of deposits).
  - **Safe Deposit Lockers / Safe Custody Articles**: Up to **4 nominees** allowed *successively*.
- **Standardized Reporting Fortnights**: Statutory reporting day for banks changed from alternate Fridays to fixed calendar fortnights: **1st to 15th** of the month and **16th to the end of the month**.
- **Investor Protection**: Unclaimed dividends, matured deposits, and interest on shares transferred to the **Investor Education and Protection Fund (IEPF)**.

### India–EFTA Trade and Economic Partnership Agreement (TEPA) | In Force 1 October 2025

**What happened**
The landmark **Trade and Economic Partnership Agreement (TEPA)** between India and the four developed nations of the **European Free Trade Association (EFTA — Switzerland, Norway, Iceland, and Liechtenstein)**, signed on 10 March 2024, officially **entered into force on 1 October 2025**.

**Core Provisions & Economic Commitments**
- **First FTA with Developed European Nations**: First trade agreement of its kind signed by India with a developed European bloc.
- **14 Comprehensive Chapters**: Covering goods, services, rules of origin, IPR, investment promotion, and sanitary measures.
- **Tariff Concessions**:
  - EFTA offers duty concessions on **92.2% of its tariff lines, covering 99.6% of India's exports** (zero duty on non-agricultural and processed food products).
  - India offers market access on **82.7% of its tariff lines, covering 95.3% of EFTA exports** (sensitivities in dairy, soya, coal, and domestic pharma strictly protected).
- **$100 Billion Investment Pledge**: EFTA countries legally committed to facilitate **USD 100 billion in foreign direct investment into India over 15 years**, generating an estimated 1 million direct jobs.
- **Gold Import Dynamics**: Over **80% of India's imports from EFTA consist of gold**, which remains insulated from tariff reductions.
