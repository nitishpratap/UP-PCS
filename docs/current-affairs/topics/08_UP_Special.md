## February 2026

### Uttar Pradesh Budget 2026–27

**What happened**  
State Finance Minister **Suresh Khanna** presented the UP Budget for **2026–27**.

**Core locks**

- Budget size: about **₹9.13 lakh crore** (detailed figure near **₹9,12,696 crore**).
- Rise over previous year: about **12.2%**.
- Capital expenditure share: about **19.5%**.
- Fiscal deficit target: **3%**, in line with **16th Finance Commission** guidance up to **2030–31**.

**Selected spending ideas**

- Convert primary schools into **Smart Schools** under Samagra Shiksha.
- About **₹580 crore** for girls’ schools in blocks that do not have Kasturba Gandhi Balika Vidyalayas.
- About **₹3,500 crore** under the Chief Minister’s **Urban Expansion and New City Promotion Scheme**.
- Health: money for new medical colleges through PPP, plus specialty care lines such as the Cancer Institute in Lucknow in Budget highlights.

### How the Union Budget touches UP

- UP’s combined central share was described as near **₹4.26 lakh crore**, up by about **₹34,000 crore**.
- Tax devolution to UP was described as rising to about **₹2.68 lakh crore**.
- Place-linked Budget ideas: ship repair at **Varanasi**; heritage work at **Sarnath** and **Hastinapur**; high-speed rail connectivity stories around Varanasi; support ideas linked to ODOP and Gram Swaraj.

### Other February UP current affairs

**CCSU Meerut AI pilot**

- **Chaudhary Charan Singh University, Meerut** was named a national pilot for an AI-enabled university.
- Partners named in reports: Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship, Google Cloud, and Placecom.

**UP Pharma Conclave 1.0, Lucknow**

- The state pitched pharma and medical-device investment.
- MoUs of about **₹10,000 crore** were reported.
- Existing pitch points include the Bulk Drug Pharma Park in **Lalitpur** and a Medical Device Park near the Yamuna Expressway.

**Sarus crane census**

- The sarus crane is the **state bird** of Uttar Pradesh.
- Latest winter count reported: about **20,628** birds.
- Earlier points in the same series: about **19,994**, and about **19,196** in 2023.
- Highest counts were reported from **Etawah** and **Mainpuri** forest divisions.

**Other short locks**

- Semiconductor unit narrative in **Greater Noida (Gautam Buddh Nagar)** as North India’s first — confirm status before using “commissioned”.
- Farmer helpline from Lucknow: **0522-2317003**, Monday to Friday, for scheme information.
- Magh Mela 2026 management may appear as a soft culture question.

## June 2026

### Surha Tal / Jai Prakash Narayan Bird Sanctuary | Ballia | 100th Ramsar

**What happened**  
On **World Environment Day (5 June 2026)**, **Surha Tal** in **Ballia** became India’s **100th** Ramsar site and Uttar Pradesh’s **13th**. The announcement was celebrated at a WED programme in **Lucknow** with CM **Yogi Adityanath**.

**Core locks**

- District: **Ballia**.
- Official name: **Jai Prakash Narayan Bird Sanctuary**.
- India **100** | UP **13**.
- Full ecology card → [Environment](05_Environment_Days.md).

### Noida International Airport (Jewar) | commercial flights begin 15 June 2026

**What happened**  
**Noida International Airport** at **Jewar** began commercial operations on **15 June 2026**.

**Core locks**

- Place: **Jewar**, **Gautam Buddha Nagar**.
- First scheduled airline: **IndiGo**.
- First arrival highlighted: from **Lucknow** (Chaudhary Charan Singh International Airport).
- Same aircraft then flew onward toward **Bengaluru** in coverage.
- Farmers who gave land travelled on a symbolic inaugural flight to Lucknow.

- International flights targeted later in **2026**.

### Ganga Expressway to Haridwar + Jhansi Link Expressway | late August 2026

**What happened**  
The UP Cabinet cleared two big road projects to grow the state’s expressway network.

**Ganga Expressway extension to Haridwar**

- Aim: extend the Ganga Expressway toward **Haridwar** (Uttarakhand).
- Cost spoken in state briefs: about **₹10,761 crore** (state-funded).
- Design idea: six-lane greenfield road, expandable later.
- Why it matters: faster link between two Kumbh cities — **Prayagraj** and **Haridwar** — and better Bijnor–Haridwar travel.

**Jhansi Link Expressway**

- Cost: about **₹7,968 crore**.
- Length idea: about **103 km**.
- Link: **Jhansi** to the **Bundelkhand Expressway** near **Jalaun**.
- Why it matters: better access to **BIDA** and the **Defence Corridor** around Jhansi.
