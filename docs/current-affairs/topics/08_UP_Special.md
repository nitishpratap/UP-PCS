**What happened**

**Core facts**

- Budget size: about **₹9.13 lakh crore** (detailed figure near **₹9,12,696 crore**).
- Rise over previous year: about **12.2%**.
- Capital expenditure share: about **19.5%**.
- Fiscal deficit target: **3%**, in line with **16th Finance Commission** guidance up to **2030–31**.

**Uttar Pradesh Economic Review 2025–26 (Presented 9 February 2026)**

- **UP GSDP Growth Trajectory:**
  - GSDP in 2016–17: **~₹13.30 lakh crore**.
  - GSDP Quick Estimates for 2024–25: **~₹30.25 lakh crore** (more than doubled).

**Selected spending ideas**

- Convert primary schools into **Smart Schools** under Samagra Shiksha.
- About **₹580 crore** for girls’ schools in blocks that do not have Kasturba Gandhi Balika Vidyalayas.
- About **₹3,500 crore** under the Chief Minister’s **Urban Expansion and New City Promotion Scheme**.
- Health: money for new medical colleges through PPP, plus specialty care lines such as the Cancer Institute in Lucknow in Budget highlights.

### How the Union Budget touches UP

- UP’s combined central share was described as near **₹4.26 lakh crore**, up by about **₹34,000 crore**.
- Tax devolution to UP was described as rising to about **₹2.68 lakh crore**.
- Place-linked Budget ideas: ship repair at **Varanasi**; heritage work at **Sarnath** and **Hastinapur**; high-speed rail connectivity stories around Varanasi; support ideas linked to ODOP and Gram Swaraj.

**CCSU Meerut AI pilot**

- **Chaudhary Charan Singh University, Meerut** was named a national pilot for an AI-enabled university.
- Partners named in reports: Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship, Google Cloud, and Placecom.

- The state pitched pharma and medical-device investment.

- Existing pitch points include the Bulk Drug Pharma Park in **Lalitpur** and a Medical Device Park near the Yamuna Expressway.

**Sarus crane census**

- The sarus crane is the **state bird** of Uttar Pradesh.
- Latest winter count reported: about **20,628** birds.
- Earlier points in the same series: about **19,994**, and about **19,196** in 2023.
- Highest counts were reported from **Etawah** and **Mainpuri** forest divisions.
