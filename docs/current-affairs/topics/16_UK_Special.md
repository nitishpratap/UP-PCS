### 1.1 Uniform Civil Code (UCC) of Uttarakhand (2024)
- **Constitutional Basis:** Enacted under **Article 44** (Directive Principles of State Policy).
- **Milestone Dates:** Passed by Vidhan Sabha on **7 February 2024**; received Presidential Assent from President Droupadi Murmu on **11 March 2024**.
- **Drafting Committee:** 5-member committee headed by **Justice (Retd.) Ranjana Prakash Desai** (former Supreme Court Judge). Other members: Shatrughan Singh (ex-Chief Secretary), Prof. Surekha Dangwal (VC Doon University), Manu Gaur, and Pramod Kohli.
- **Key Legal Provisions:**
  - **Tribal Exemption:** Explicitly **exempts all Scheduled Tribes (STs)** protected under Article 366(25).
  - **Marriage & Polygamy:** Completely prohibits polygamy and bigamy across all religions; minimum age of marriage fixed at **21 for males and 18 for females**.
  - **Abolition of Practices:** Bans *Iddat*, *Nikah Halala*, and extra-judicial divorces.
  - **Equal Inheritance:** Grants equal property inheritance rights to sons and daughters, eliminating discrimination in ancestral estates.
  - **Mandatory Live-In Registration:** Couples residing in live-in relationships must register on the state portal within **1 month** of cohabitation; failure attracts up to 3 months imprisonment; children born from live-in relationships enjoy full legal legitimacy.

### 1.2 Uttarakhand Public Services (Horizontal Reservation for Women) Act, 2022
- **Enactment:** Passed by Vidhan Sabha and signed by Governor Lt. Gen. Gurmit Singh on **10 January 2023**.
- **Core Provision:** Guarantees **30% horizontal reservation** in direct recruitment to public services and posts under the State Government exclusively for **women domicile residents of Uttarakhand**.
- **Legal Background:** Restored domicile reservation after the Uttarakhand High Court had stayed the previous 2006 government order.

### 1.3 Uttarakhand Anti-Cheating & Paper Leak Act, 2023
- Enacted as *Uttarakhand Competitive Examination (Measures for Prevention and Redressal of Unfair Means in Recruitment) Act, 2023*.
- **Punishment for Syndicates:** Imprisonment from 10 years to **Life Imprisonment** and a fine of **₹2 Crore to ₹10 Crore**, plus attachment and confiscation of properties acquired through copying syndicates.
- **Punishment for Examinees:** Banned from state recruitment examinations for **10 years** on the first offense, with 3 to 10 years imprisonment.

## 4. International Summits & Defense Exercises in Uttarakhand

### 4.1 G20 Meetings Hosted in Uttarakhand (2023)
Uttarakhand successfully hosted **three official G20 meetings**:
1. **1st Meeting — Chief Science Advisers' Roundtable (CSAR):** Held at **Ramnagar (Corbett, Nainital)** on 28–30 March 2023. Discussed global disease surveillance and open access to scientific knowledge.
2. **2nd Meeting — Anti-Corruption Working Group (ACWG):** Held at **Narendra Nagar / Rishikesh (Tehri Garhwal)** on 25–27 May 2023.
3. **3rd Meeting — Infrastructure Working Group (IWG):** Held at **Narendra Nagar / Rishikesh** on 26–28 June 2023. Discussed financing future cities and resilient infrastructure.

### 4.2 Joint Military Exercises Hosted in Uttarakhand
- **Exercise Surya Kiran (India–Nepal):** Annually alternated; conducted regularly at the military training grounds in **Pithoragarh**.
- **Exercise Yudh Abhyas (India–USA):** 18th edition conducted at the high-altitude cold-weather warfare centre in **Auli (Chamoli)** at 9,500 feet.
- **Exercise Dustlik (India–Uzbekistan):** 4th edition held at **Pithoragarh** in February 2023.

## 7. Key Environmental, Ecological & Wildlife Current Affairs

- **First Village Declaration:** In April 2023, the Border Roads Organisation (BRO) erected signboards formally designating **Mana Village (Chamoli)** near Badrinath as **"India's First Village"** under the **Vibrant Villages Programme (VVP)**, reversing the colonial concept of the "last Indian village".
- **Asan Conservation Reserve (Ramsar Site):** Situated at the confluence of the Asan and Yamuna rivers in Dehradun. In October 2020, it was declared **Uttarakhand's first Ramsar site** (444 hectares; premier wintering stop for 53 species of trans-Himalayan migratory birds, including the Brahminy duck and bar-headed goose).
- **Pirul Lao–Paise Pao Campaign:** Flagship community forestry campaign launched to tackle devastating summer forest fires (*Vanagni*). Villagers and Van Panchayats collect dry fallen Chir pine needles (*Pirul*) and deposit them at government collection centres at an incentive rate of **₹50 per kilogram**, preventing slope combustion and supplying biomass for clean bio-energy pellets.
- **Project Tiger Golden Jubilee (1973–2023):** Commemorated across Uttarakhand; Corbett National Park maintains the highest tiger density in India (over 260 tigers).
- **First Snow Leopard Conservation Centre:** Established at **Lanka, near Bhaironghati in Uttarkashi district** in collaboration with UNDP under the *SECURE Himalaya* project.
- **Lichen Park (Munsyari):** India’s first lichen park developed at **Munsyari (Pithoragarh)** sheltering over 150 species of lichens (*Jhula ghaas*).
- **Cryptogamic Garden (Deoban, Chakrata):** India’s first cryptogamic garden at Deoban (2,700 m altitude) housing 76 species of ferns, fungi, algae, and bryophytes.
- **Moss Garden (Khurpatal, Nainital):** India’s first dedicated moss garden conservation project.
- **First Pollinator Park:** Established at **Haldwani (Nainital)** across 4 acres conserving 40+ species of butterflies, honeybees, and birds.
