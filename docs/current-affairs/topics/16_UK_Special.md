## 1. Uniform Civil Code (UCC) — Historic First in India

**Context & Core Facts:**
- **Status:** Uttarakhand became the **first state in independent India** to enact a Uniform Civil Code (UCC) under Article 44 of the Directive Principles of State Policy. (Goa's civil code dates back to Portuguese rule in 1867).
- **Legislative Passage:** Passed by the Uttarakhand Legislative Assembly on **7 February 2024** and received assent from President Droupadi Murmu on **11 March 2024**.
- **Drafting Committee:** Chaired by **Justice (Retd.) Ranjana Prakash Desai** (former Supreme Court Judge and head of the Delimitation Commission).
  - Other key members: Shatrughan Singh (former Chief Secretary), Pramod Kohli, Manu Gaur, Prof. Surekha Dangwal.
- **Key Legal Provisions:**
  - **Exemption:** **Scheduled Tribes (STs)** (such as Jaunsari, Tharu, Buxa, Bhotia, Raji) are completely **exempted** from the purview of the UCC.
  - **Marriage & Divorce:** Mandatory registration of marriages; complete ban on polygamy, polyandry, *Nikah Halala*, *Iddat*, and child marriage. Minimum marriage age fixed at 18 for women and 21 for men.
  - **Equal Succession Rights:** Equal inheritance rights granted to sons and daughters in ancestral property; abolition of coparcenary distinction.
  - **Live-in Relationships:** Mandatory registration of live-in relationships within 1 month of cohabitation on a state portal; children born from live-in relationships are recognized as legitimate.

## 3. Major Infrastructure & Connectivity Milestones

### Rishikesh–Karnaprayag Broad Gauge Rail Link
- **Length:** **125.2 km** connecting Rishikesh to Karnaprayag across five districts (Dehradun, Tehri, Pauri, Rudraprayag, Chamoli).
- **Specialty:** ~**105 km (over 84%) runs inside 17 tunnels**; includes the longest railway tunnel in India (~15.1 km between Devprayag and Janasu).
- **Agency:** Rail Vikas Nigam Limited (RVNL).

### Char Dham All-Weather Road Project
- **Length:** ~**889 km** highway expansion under MoRTH connecting Yamunotri (Barkot), Gangotri (Dharasu), Kedarnath (Rudraprayag), Badrinath (Joshimath), and Tanakpur–Pithoragarh border stretch.
- High Powered Committee on environmental compliance chaired by Supreme Court oversight.

### Silkyara–Barkot Tunnel Episode (Operation Zindagi)
- **Location:** Silkyara bend, **Uttarkashi** district (part of Char Dham route).
- **Incident & Rescue:** On 12 November 2023, a portion of the tunnel collapsed, trapping **41 workers for 17 days**. Rescued on 28 November 2023 using **rat-hole mining specialists** (Munna Qureshi, Wakeel Hassan) after Auger drilling machines broke down.
- **Key figure:** Arnold Dix (President, International Tunnelling and Underground Space Association).

### Parvatmala Ropeway Projects
- **Kedarnath Ropeway:** Sonprayag to Kedarnath (~9.7 km; reduces travel time from 8 hours to ~30 minutes).
- **Hemkund Sahib Ropeway:** Govindghat to Hemkund Sahib (~12.4 km).

## 4. Environment, Wildlife, Disasters & Forests

### Wildlife Sanctuaries & National Parks
- **Corbett National Park (Nainital/Pauri):** India's **oldest national park** (established 1936 as Hailey National Park; renamed Ramganga in 1952, Corbett in 1957). Core river is **Ramganga**. First to come under **Project Tiger (1973)**.
- **Rajaji National Park (Haridwar/Dehradun/Pauri):** Formed in 1983 by merging Chilla, Motichur, and Rajaji sanctuaries. Known for Asian elephants and tiger translocation.
- **Asan Barrage Conservation Reserve (Dehradun):** Uttarakhand's **first Ramsar site** (designated 2020; Yamuna-Asan confluence).
- **Nanda Devi & Valley of Flowers:** UNESCO World Heritage Sites (Nanda Devi Biosphere Reserve).
- **Lakhwar Multipurpose Project (300 MW):** On **Yamuna river** in Dehradun district.
- **Kishau Dam Project (660 MW):** On **Tons river** (tributary of Yamuna) on the Uttarakhand–Himachal border.

### Environmental Issues & Movements
- **Joshimath Land Subsidence (Chamoli):** Rapid cracks and sinking in Jan 2023 due to fragile moraine geology, toe erosion by Alaknanda, and unmanaged drainage. Historical reference: **M.C. Mishra Committee Report (1976)** had warned against heavy construction in Joshimath.
- **Pirul Lao–Paise Pao Scheme:** Community incentive scheme to collect fallen dry Chir pine needles (*Pirul*) to prevent destructive **forest fires (Vanagni)** and generate bio-briquettes / energy.

## 6. Key State Schemes & Social Welfare

- **Mukhyamantri Mahalakshmi Yojana:** Post-natal care and maternal health kit containing clothing, nutrition, and hygiene essentials for newly born babies and mothers.
- **Mukhyamantri Anchal Amrit Yojana:** Providing free fortifying milk twice a week to children aged 3–6 in Anganwadi centers to combat stunting and anemia.
- **Mukhya Mantri Saur Swarojgar Yojana (MMSSY):** Allotment of 20–25 kW solar power plants to unemployed youth and returnee migrants.
- **Ghasyari Kalyan Yojana:** Supplying packed green fodder silage (animal feed) at subsidized rates (₹3/kg) directly to rural women in hill villages to eliminate drudgery and accidents while foraging in forests.
- **Apuni Sarkar Portal:** State e-governance portal delivering over 400 citizen-centric services online.
- **U-Rise / UKPSC reforms:** Computerized, biometric, and one-time registration (OTR) reforms for recruitment transparency.
