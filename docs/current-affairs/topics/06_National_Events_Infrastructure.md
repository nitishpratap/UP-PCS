## March 2026

### Gujarat Uniform Civil Code Bill | passed 24 March 2026

**What happened**
The Gujarat Assembly passed the **Gujarat Uniform Civil Code, 2026** after a long debate. Gujarat became the **second state** in India to pass a full state UCC law. The first was **Uttarakhand**.

**What the law tries to cover**

- Marriage, divorce, succession, adoption, and related civil matters.
- Live-in relationships are also brought under registration rules in coverage.
- Scheduled Tribes in the state were reported as exempt.

**Constitution link**

- Uniform Civil Code is mentioned in **Article 44** (Directive Principles).

## In-Depth National Affairs & Infrastructure (Ghatna Chakra Eye Drishti 2026)

### Ahmedabad Metro Rail Phase 2A
- **Project Scope**: Extension covering **6.032 km** with **5 elevated stations**.
- **Sanctioned Cost**: **₹2,169.04 crore**.
- **Implementing Agency**: Gujarat Metro Rail Corporation (GMRC) Limited.
- **Milestone**: With the operationalization of this phase, the total operational Ahmedabad Metro network expanded to **77.63 km**.
- **Connectivity**: Enhances high-speed urban transit connecting Ahmedabad and Gandhinagar (including GIFT City).

### ULLAS Scheme — Full Literacy Sequence
- **Scheme Target**: Achieving full functional literacy (95%+ literacy rate benchmark under ULLAS — Understanding Lifelong Learning for All in Society).
- **Chronological sequence of full literacy declarations**:
  1. **Ladakh**: First Union Territory to achieve full functional literacy under ULLAS.
  2. **Mizoram**: First State in India (and Northeast) to achieve full functional literacy under ULLAS.
  3. **Goa**: Declared fully literate (first in Western region).
  4. **Tripura**: Declared fully literate.
  5. **Himachal Pradesh**: Declared fully literate.
  6. **Sikkim**: Joined the fully literate states.

### Election Commission of India — Special Intensive Revision (SIR) Phase III
- **Constitutional Basis**: Carried out under plenary powers of superintendence, direction, and control of elections under **Article 324**.
- **Scale**: Covered **16 States and 3 UTs**.
- **Ground Workforce**: Mobilized **3.94 lakh Booth Level Officers (BLOs)** for 100% door-to-door physical verification of electors.
- **Regional Grouping**: Group 3 notably covered **Uttarakhand** among other states.

### NCRB Accidental Deaths & Suicides in India (ADSI) 2024 — National Findings
- **Total Accidental Deaths**: **4,67,857** accidental deaths recorded nationally.
- **Forces of Nature**:
  - **Lightning** accounted for **35.7%** of all accidental deaths caused by forces of nature (the single largest natural killer).
  - Highest number of lightning deaths reported in **Madhya Pradesh**.
- **Traffic Accidents**:
  - Road accident fatalities showed that **59.7%** occurred in **rural areas** (contradicting the misconception that urban roads are more lethal).
- **Suicides in India**:
  - Total reported suicides: **1,70,746**.
  - All-India suicide rate: **12.2** per 1,00,000 population.

### Noida International Airport (Jewar) — Phase-1 Specifications
- **Capital Outlay**: **₹11,200 crore** for Phase-1 completion.
- **Developer / Concessionaire**: **Yamuna International Airport Private Limited (YIAPL)**, a 100% owned subsidiary of **Zurich Airport International AG**.

### Assam Uniform Civil Code Bill, 2026
- **Legislative Timeline**: Passed by the Assam Legislative Assembly on **27 May 2026**.
- **National Milestone**: Assam became the **first state in Northeast India** and the **third state in India** (after Uttarakhand and Gujarat) to pass a state UCC under **Article 44**.
- **Repealed Act**: Repeals the *Assam Compulsory Registration of Muslim Marriages and Divorces Act, 2024*.
- **Key Clauses**:
  - Monogamy strictly mandated; bigamy/polygamy punishable by imprisonment up to 7 years.
  - Legal marriage age: 21 for males, 18 for females.
  - Mandatory registration of marriages/divorces within 60 days.
  - Mandatory registration of live-in relationships within 1 month (unregistered faces up to 6 months jail / ₹25,000 fine; children born from live-in relationships are deemed fully legitimate).
  - Prohibits child marriage (up to 2 years imprisonment).
  - *Constitutional Note*: Assam MLA Tuliram Ronghang simultaneously invoked **Article 244(A)** demanding an autonomous state within Assam for Karbi Anglong, West Karbi Anglong, and Dima Hasao.

### Vande Mataram Under National Honour Law
- **Cabinet Approval**: 5 May 2026 approved amendment to **The Prevention of Insults to National Honour Act, 1971**.
- **Substantive Change**: Grants equal statutory penal protection to National Song **Vande Mataram** alongside National Anthem *Jana Gana Mana*. Intentionally preventing its singing or causing disturbance invites imprisonment up to 3 years, fine, or both (under Section 3 parity).
- **Historical Lineage**: Composed by Bankim Chandra Chatterjee (*Bangadarshan*, 1875 / *Anandamath*, 1882); first sung by Rabindranath Tagore at 1896 INC session; given equal honour by Constituent Assembly on 24 January 1950.

### BCCI Status under RTI Act (CIC Landmark Ruling)
- **Ruling Date**: 18 May 2026 by the Central Information Commission (CIC).
- **Holding**: BCCI is **NOT a "public authority"** under **Section 2(h)** of the Right to Information (RTI) Act, 2005.
- **Legal Grounds**: Registered as a private autonomous society under Tamil Nadu Societies Registration Act, 1975; is not substantially financed by the government; does not constitute "State" under **Article 12** of the Constitution.

### Passive Euthanasia Procedure — Supreme Court (*Harish Rana Case*)
- **Case**: *Harish Rana v. Union of India* (decided 11 March 2026; Bench of Justices J.B. Pardiwala and K.V. Viswanathan).
- **Ruling**: Allowed withdrawal of life-sustaining treatment for a patient in a 13-year permanent vegetative state.
- **Constitutional Basis**: **Article 21** right to live with dignity includes the right to die with dignity (not an unrestricted right to die).
- **Simplified Procedure**: Replaced earlier cumbersome judicial-magistrate procedures with a two-tier medical board (Primary treating board + Secondary board with 2 specialists).
- **Evolution of Doctrine**: *Gian Kaur* (1996) $\rightarrow$ *Aruna Shanbaug* (2011) $\rightarrow$ *Common Cause* (2018 - 5-judge bench recognized Living Wills) $\rightarrow$ *Harish Rana* (2026).

### Supreme Court Tech Initiatives: 'One Case One Data' and 'Su Sahayak'
- **Announced**: 11 May 2026 by CJI **Justice Surya Kant**.
- **'One Case One Data'**: Integrated data platform streamlining case management across all levels of the judiciary from taluka courts to the Supreme Court.
- **'Su Sahayak'**: AI-driven multilingual chatbot integrated into the Supreme Court website, developed by NIC with the SC Registry to assist litigants.

### The Constitution (One Hundred & Thirty-First Amendment) Bill, 2026
- **Legislative Outcome**: Introduced on 16 April 2026; **FAILED in Lok Sabha on 17 April 2026** due to lack of the required two-thirds special majority under **Article 368**.
- **Voting Division**: 298 votes in favour, 230 against (528 present & voting $\implies$ 352 required; **fell short by 54 votes**).
- **Core Amendments Proposed**:
  - **Article 81**: Proposed raising maximum Lok Sabha strength from **550 to 850 members** (815 from States, 35 from UTs).
  - **Article 82 & 170**: Proposed restarting nationwide delimitation on a new census determined by Parliament.
  - **Article 334A**: Sought to eliminate the waiting condition for a future census/delimitation enacted under the 106th Amendment Act (2023), thereby **advancing 33% Women's Reservation** immediately.
  - **Article 55**: Redefined "population" impacting the vote value calculation of MLAs in Presidential elections.

### Removal Motion Against Lok Sabha Speaker Om Birla
- **Constitutional Provision**: Notice moved under **Article 94(c)** on 10 February 2026.
- **Presiding Rule (Article 96)**: Under Article 96, the Speaker **cannot preside** over the Lok Sabha while a resolution for his removal is under consideration (though he retains the right to speak and vote in the first instance).
- **Outcome**: Debated on 10–11 March 2026; **rejected by voice vote** in Lok Sabha on 11 March 2026.

### The Jan Vishwas (Amendment of Provisions) Act, 2026
- **Assent**: 7 April 2026.
- **Scope**: Amends **79 Central Acts** administered by 23 ministries, decriminalising **784 provisions** to replace imprisonment with civil monetary penalties.
- **Major Examples**:
  - *Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940*: Illegal cosmetics manufacture/sale attracts civil penalty of ₹1 lakh or 3x value (no jail).
  - *National Highways Act, 1956*: Obstructing/damaging highways invites civil penalties of ₹10 lakh to ₹1 crore instead of imprisonment.
  - *Legal Metrology Act, 2009*: Establishes improvement notices for first-time non-compliance.

### Indian Ports Act, 2025
- **Enactment**: Replaces the colonial **Indian Ports Act, 1908**.
- **Maritime State Development Council (MSDC)**: Conferred full statutory consultative status under the chairmanship of the Union Minister of Ports, Shipping and Waterways.
- **State Maritime Boards (SMBs)**: Granted statutory recognition.
- **Dispute Redressal**: Mandatory Dispute Resolution Committee (DRC) in each maritime state (appeals lie directly to the High Court; civil court jurisdiction barred).
- **Environmental Safeguards**: Enforces Port Waste Reception and Handling Plans under MARPOL and Ballast Water Management Conventions.
- **Mega Ports**: Empowers Central Government to notify designated high-capacity deep-water ports as "Mega Ports".

### Supreme Court Stay on UGC Equity Regulations, 2026
- **Order Date**: 29 January 2026 (Bench of Justices Surya Kant and Ujjal Bhuyan).
- **Status**: Kept in abeyance the newly notified *UGC (Promotion of Equity in Higher Education Institutions) Regulations, 2026*; older 2012 Regulations remain operative.
- **Origin**: Followed Supreme Court directions in *Abeda Salim Tadvi v. Union of India (2019)* on campus discrimination and suicide prevention.
