## January 2026

### New Ramsar sites in the news | late January 2026

**What happened**  
Just before World Wetlands Day, India publicly celebrated new wetlands of international importance under the **Ramsar Convention**.

**Two sites highlighted together in the public announcement**

1. **Patna Bird Sanctuary** — **Etah** district, **Uttar Pradesh** (Jalesar area).  
2. **Chhari-Dhand** — **Kutch**, **Gujarat**.

**About Patna Bird Sanctuary**

- It is one of the **smallest** bird sanctuaries in Uttar Pradesh (about **1 square kilometre** / around **108 hectares** in detailed notes).
- Even though it is small, it hosts tens of thousands of birds in peak winter.
- It has marshes, woodland, and grassland, with farms around it.

**About Chhari-Dhand**

- It is a seasonal wetland in the desert region of Kutch.
- It is an important stop for birds entering India on the western route.

**Count caution**

- February notes often said India had about **98** Ramsar sites after Patna + Chhari-Dhand.
- Later: Shekha Jheel = **99**; Surha Tal = **100**; Glaw Lake = **101**.
- Full living table → [Master tables](00_Master_Tables.md). Do **not** treat Surha Tal as a January public headline; its big WED announcement was in **June**.

### Solid Waste Management Rules, 2026 | notified 28 January 2026

**What happened**  
The Environment Ministry notified new **Solid Waste Management (SWM) Rules, 2026**. They replace the older **2016** Rules.

**Core locks**

- Notification date: **28 January 2026**.
- Full effect from: **1 April 2026**.
- Old rules replaced: **SWM Rules, 2016**.

!!! trap "Easy wrong options"
    Mixing the notification date with the start date. Saying these rules replace plastic rules of another year.

The Subhash Chandra Bose Aapda Prabandhan Puraskar 2026 is explained under [Awards](04_Awards.md). Winners: **SSDMA** and Lt Col **Seeta Ashok Shelke**.

## April 2026

### Shekha Jheel Bird Sanctuary | India’s 99th Ramsar site | April 2026

**What happened**  
**Shekha Jheel Bird Sanctuary** in **Aligarh, Uttar Pradesh** was designated a **Ramsar site**. India moved to **99** wetlands of international importance. Uttar Pradesh’s tally rose to **12**, keeping UP among the leading states.

**Core locks**

- Site: **Shekha Jheel** (also written Shekha / Sheikha in some older notes).
- District: **Aligarh**, Uttar Pradesh.
- India total after this tag: **99**.
- UP total after this tag: **12**.
- It lies on the **Central Asian Flyway** and hosts winter visitors such as the bar-headed goose and painted stork.
- The wetland complex grew beside the **Upper Ganges Canal** (built in the 1850s).
- It is also an Important Bird and Biodiversity Area (**IBA**).

!!! tip "UPPCS angle"
    Pair this with January’s **Patna Bird Sanctuary (Etah)** Ramsar. Surha Tal (Ballia) as India’s **100th** came later (June) — do not push it into April.

### Earth Day | 22 April 2026

- Theme: **Our Power, Our Planet**.
- Focus: everyday community action and renewable / climate choices.

### Other April day themes

- **World Autism Awareness Day (2 April):** Autism and Humanity — Every Life Has Value.
- **World Heritage Day (18 April):** keep the year’s ICOMOS theme only after locking an official note.
- **World Malaria Day (25 April):** Driven to End Malaria: Now We Can. Now We Must.
- **National Panchayati Raj Day:** **24 April** every year (Constitution Day link for Panchayats is 24 April 1993).

### India’s first water-neutral railway depot

- Name: **Kankaria Coaching Depot**, **Ahmedabad**.
- Zone: **Western Railway**.
- Method highlighted: **phytoremediation** of wastewater.
- Water saving noted: about **1.60 lakh litres** a day.

### Surha Tal | India’s 100th Ramsar site | Ballia, UP

**What happened**  
On World Environment Day, India designated **Jai Prakash Narayan Bird Sanctuary (Surha Tal)** in **Ballia, Uttar Pradesh** as its **100th Ramsar site**. Uttar Pradesh’s tally rose to **13**.

**Core locks**

- Official name: **Jai Prakash Narayan Bird Sanctuary**.
- Common name: **Surha Tal**.
- District: **Ballia**, Uttar Pradesh.
- India total: **100**.
- UP total: **13**.
- Freshwater wetland in the middle **Ganga** basin; formed from a Ganga meander.
- On the **Central Asian Flyway**.
- Bird richness: over **310** bird species; up to about **200,000** migratory birds in winter.
- Also supports the fishing cat and threatened fish such as **Wallago attu**.
- Global Ramsar sites: about **2,595**.
- India is highest in Asia and third in the world after the **UK** and **Mexico**.

| Easy compare | Shekha Jheel | Surha Tal |
|--------------|--------------|-----------|
| District | **Aligarh** | **Ballia** |
| India count | **99** | **100** |
| UP count | **12** | **13** |
| Month news broke | April | June (WED) |

!!! tip "Confused pair"
    Shekha Jheel (Aligarh) = **99** / UP **12**. Surha Tal (Ballia) = **100** / UP **13**. Patna Bird Sanctuary is **Etah**.

## August 2026

### Glaw Lake | Arunachal Pradesh | India’s 101st Ramsar site

**What happened**  
**Glaw Lake** in **Arunachal Pradesh** became the state’s **first** Ramsar site and India’s **101st**.

**Core locks**

- Site: **Glaw Lake**.
- State: **Arunachal Pradesh** (first Ramsar).
- India total: **101**.
- Location setting: inside **Kamlang Tiger Reserve** and Wildlife Sanctuary (**Lohit** district in detailed notes).
- Type: freshwater lake in the **Eastern Himalayas**.
- Soft ecology: many tree and orchid species recorded in catchment coverage.

| Step | Site | Place | India # |
|------|------|-------|---------|
| 1 | Shekha Jheel | Aligarh, UP | 99 |
| 2 | Surha Tal | Ballia, UP | 100 |
| 3 | **Glaw Lake** | Arunachal (Kamlang) | **101** |

Full board → [Master tables](00_Master_Tables.md).
