## August 2026

### Vande Mataram @ 150 years

**What happened**  
Independence Day 2026 marked **150 years** of the national song **Vande Mataram**.

**Core locks**

- Composer: **Bankim Chandra Chattopadhyay**.
- First published: **1875** in *Bangadarshan*; later in novel **Anandamath** (**1882**).
- Equal honour with the National Anthem was affirmed in **1950**.
- First official Red Fort I-Day band performance of the song in 2026.

### Soft beauty / pageant culture

Miss Universe India 2026 winner card → [Awards](04_Awards.md).
