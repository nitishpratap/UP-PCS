## February 2026

### Creative economy and heritage from the Budget

**Orange Economy / AVGC**

- AVGC means Animation, Visual Effects, Gaming, and Comics.
- The Budget supported Content Creator Labs and the **Indian Institute of Creative Technologies, Mumbai**.
- More money detail → [Economy](10_Economy_Budget_Trade.md).

**Heritage sites**

- The Budget proposed development of **15 archaeological sites**.
- Two UP names often listed in explainers are **Sarnath (Varanasi)** and **Hastinapur (Meerut)**.

**Ayurveda and traditional medicine**

- **3 new All India Institutes of Ayurveda** were proposed.
- The **WHO Global Traditional Medicine Centre** at **Jamnagar** is to be upgraded.

**Soft culture notes**

- India participation messaging for the **61st Venice Biennale** appeared in February coverage.
- Magh Mela 2026 in UP may appear as a soft culture or management question.

## August 2026

### Vande Mataram @ 150 years

**What happened**  
Independence Day 2026 marked **150 years** of the national song **Vande Mataram**.

**Core locks**

- Composer: **Bankim Chandra Chattopadhyay**.
- First published: **1875** in *Bangadarshan*; later in novel **Anandamath** (**1882**).
- Equal honour with the National Anthem was affirmed in **1950**.
- First official Red Fort I-Day band performance of the song in 2026.
