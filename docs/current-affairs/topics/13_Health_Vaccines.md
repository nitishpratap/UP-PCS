## February 2026

### Health ideas from Union Budget 2026–27

**Biopharma SHAKTI**

- Aim: make India strong in biologics and biosimilars.
- Money: **₹10,000 crore** over five years.
- **3 new NIPER** institutes and upgrade of **7** existing ones.
- More than **1,000** clinical trial sites.
- Stronger **CDSCO**.
- Full scheme card → [Schemes](02_Schemes_Policies.md).

**Allied health and care workers**

- Existing Allied Health Professional institutes will be upgraded, and new ones will be set up.
- About **10** selected disciplines are covered, including optometry, radiology, anaesthesia technology, OT technology, and applied psychology.
- Target noted: add about **1,00,000** Allied Health Professionals over five years.
- About **1.5 lakh** caregivers are to be trained in the coming year.

**Medical tourism hubs**

- A scheme will help states set up **five Regional Medical Hubs** with private partnership.
- These hubs will combine treatment, education, research, AYUSH, and post-care facilities.

**Mental health**

- A second **NIMHANS** (**NIMHANS-2**) is proposed in **North India**.
- National mental health institutes at **Ranchi** and **Tezpur** are to be upgraded.

**AYUSH**

- **3 new All India Institutes of Ayurveda**.
- Upgrade of AYUSH pharmacies and drug testing labs.
- Upgrade of the **WHO Global Traditional Medicine Centre** at **Jamnagar**.

**Hospital capacity**

- Budget and UP explainers spoke of expanding district hospital capacity, including emergency and trauma care. One common figure used in explainers is about a **50%** capacity push.

## April 2026

### World Health Day | 7 April 2026

**What happened**
**7 April** is World Health Day. It marks the founding anniversary of the **World Health Organization (WHO)** in **1948**.

**2026 theme**

- **Together for health. Stand with science.**
- The campaign stresses science-led action and the **One Health** idea (people, animals, plants, and the planet together).

### Other April health days

- **World Autism Awareness Day (2 April):** Autism and Humanity — Every Life Has Value.
- **World Malaria Day (25 April):** Driven to End Malaria: Now We Can. Now We Must.
- **World Liver Day (19 April):** Solid Habits, Strong Liver (compilation theme).

## May 2026

### WHO declares Ebola a Public Health Emergency of International Concern

**What happened**
WHO declared an **Ebola** outbreak linked with the **Democratic Republic of Congo (DRC)** and **Uganda** a global health emergency in May coverage.

**Core facts**

- Disease: **Ebola** (severe viral haemorrhagic fever).
- Declaring body: **WHO**.
- Director-General: **Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus**.
- WHO HQ: **Geneva**.

### May health days

- **World Thalassaemia Day:** **8 May**.
- **International Nurses Day:** **12 May** (Florence Nightingale’s birthday; some calendars also note 13 May in compilations — fact **12 May** from WHO).
- **World No Tobacco Day:** **31 May**.

!!! tip "Easy memory"
 Keep Nurses Day as **12 May** for standard key facting unless a stem quotes another date.
