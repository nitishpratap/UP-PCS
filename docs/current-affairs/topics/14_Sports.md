## March 2026

### ICC Men’s T20 World Cup 2026 final | 8 March 2026 | Ahmedabad

**What happened**  
India played **New Zealand** in the final of the **ICC Men’s T20 World Cup 2026** at **Narendra Modi Stadium**, Ahmedabad. India won by **96 runs**.

**Score locks**

- India made **255 for 5**. This was called the highest total in a Men’s T20 World Cup final.
- New Zealand were all out for **159**.
- **Sanju Samson** scored **89**.
- **Jasprit Bumrah** took **4 wickets for 15 runs** and was Player of the Match in coverage.
- Hosts: **India and Sri Lanka** for the tournament.

**History locks**

- This was India’s **third** Men’s T20 World Cup title.
- India became the **first** team to **defend** the title (win it back-to-back).
- India became the **first host** country to win the Men’s T20 World Cup.

### Athletics hosting note

Coverage also said India would host the **World Indoor Athletics Championships** in **2028** at **Bhubaneswar** (Kalinga Indoor Stadium). Keep the year and city ready.

## July 2026

### FIFA World Cup 2026 final | 19 July 2026 | New York / New Jersey

**What happened**  
**Spain** beat **Argentina 1–0** after extra time in the FIFA World Cup final and became world champions for the **second** time (after **2010**).

**Core locks**

- Date: **19 July 2026**.
- Venue: **New York / New Jersey Stadium**.
- Score: **Spain 1–0 Argentina** (extra time).
- Winning goal: **Ferran Torres** (about the **106th** minute).
- Hosts of the tournament: **USA, Canada, Mexico**.
- Spain also held the women’s FIFA World Cup from **2023**, so coverage called Spain the first nation to hold both men’s and women’s titles at once.

## August 2026

### Commonwealth Games 2026 conclude | Glasgow | to 2 August 2026

**What happened**  
The **23rd** Commonwealth Games ended in **Glasgow, Scotland**. **Australia** topped the medal table. India finished **4th**.

**India medal locks**

- Total: **39** medals — **13** gold, **17** silver, **9** bronze.
- Opening flag bearer: **Mirabai Chanu**.
- Closing flag bearer: **Jaismine Lamboria**.
- Boxing haul: **10** medals including **7** golds (most by any nation in boxing at one Games in coverage).
- Armed forces athletes contributed strongly; Army alone won **16** medals (8 gold) in Defence Ministry felicitations.

### Soft individual feats

- **Mirabai Chanu** — third successive Commonwealth Games weightlifting gold (second Indian with three successive golds in coverage).
- **Gulveer Singh** — first Indian track athlete with two individual medals at one Commonwealth Games in coverage.
- Best-ever Indian judo haul noted: three medals.
