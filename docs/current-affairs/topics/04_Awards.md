# Awards

> **Priority A** | About **8%** of Current Affairs
> **2025** papers asked many award questions. Learn winner, field, and award level carefully.

## What to note every time

| Ask yourself | Why |
|--------------|-----|
| Who won? | Exact name; note if posthumous |
| Which award and level? | Vibhushan / Bhushan / Shri, or prize name |
| For what work? | Art, sports, public affairs, teaching… |
| Who gives it, and when? | President, Republic Day eve, 23 January, etc. |
| Any UP or India angle? | State names and Indian winners abroad |

## February 2026

### Global Teacher Prize 2026 | Rouble Nagi (India)

**What happened**
Indian educator and artist **Rouble Nagi** won the **GEMS Education Global Teacher Prize 2026**. The prize is worth **1 million US dollars**. It is run by the Varkey Foundation with UNESCO support. The winner was announced at the **World Governments Summit** in Dubai.

**Why she won**

- She turns broken or neglected walls into open-air learning murals.
- She calls this idea **Living Walls of Learning**.
- The murals teach reading, numbers, hygiene, environment, and social values.
- Over about two decades, her work has helped bring more than **one million** children into formal schooling.
- She was chosen from more than **5,000** nominations from **139** countries.
- This was the **10th** edition of the prize.
