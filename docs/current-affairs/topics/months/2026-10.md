## Confused pairs

| Wrong idea | Correct fact |
|------------|--------------|
| Total Classical Languages in India = 6 or 8 | Total is now **11** (Tamil, Sanskrit, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Odia + Marathi, Pali, Prakrit, Assamese, Bengali) |
| Hindi is a Classical Language | **No** — Hindi is the official language of the Union (Art. 343) and an Eighth Schedule language, but **not** a notified Classical Language |
| 51st CJI = Justice B.R. Gavai | **Justice Sanjiv Khanna** is the 51st CJI; Justice B.R. Gavai is slated to succeed him later in 2025 |
| Nobel Physics was for quantum mechanics | It was awarded for **artificial neural networks & machine learning** (Hopfield & Hinton) |
| Wheat MSP = ₹2,275 per quintal | That was the older rate; revised MSP is **₹2,425 per quintal** (+₹150 hike) |
| Damagundam VLF station is India's first | It is India's **second** VLF naval communication facility; 1st is **INS Kattabomman** (Tirunelveli, Tamil Nadu, 1990) |
| ICC Women's T20 World Cup won by Australia/India | Won by **New Zealand** (defeating South Africa by 32 runs in Dubai; maiden title) |
| 23rd SCO Heads of Government Summit was in Beijing | Held in **Islamabad, Pakistan** (attended by EAM S. Jaishankar — 1st Indian EAM visit to Pakistan in 9 years) |

## Also useful

- **Global Hunger Index (GHI) 2024/2026:** India ranked **105th out of 127 countries** (Score: **27.3**, Category: **Serious**; Child Wasting: **18.7%**; published by Welthungerhilfe and Concern Worldwide).
- **Global Innovation Index (GII) 2024/2026:** India rose to **39th rank** out of 133 economies (published by WIPO; Switzerland ranked #1 for 14th year; India #1 in Central/Southern Asia and lower-middle-income group).
- **54th Dadasaheb Phalke Award:** Conferred upon veteran actor **Mithun Chakraborty** at the 70th National Film Awards ceremony (Best Actor: Rishab Shetty for *Kantara*; Best Actress: Nithya Menen & Manasi Parekh; Best Feature Film: *Aattam*).
- **Space Milestones:** SpaceX Starship Flight 5 made history on **13 Oct** by catching the Super Heavy booster mid-air with launch tower mechanical arms ("chopsticks"). NASA launched **Europa Clipper** on **14 Oct** atop SpaceX Falcon Heavy to explore Jupiter's icy moon.
- **Assembly Elections:** **Nayab Singh Saini** sworn in as Haryana CM (BJP 48 seats) on **17 Oct**; **Omar Abdullah** sworn in as the **first Chief Minister of the Union Territory of Jammu and Kashmir** on **16 Oct**.
- **Exercise Malabar 2026:** Quad maritime exercise (India, US, Japan, Australia) conducted off Visakhapatnam and the Bay of Bengal from **8–18 Oct**.
- **RBI Monetary Policy (Oct):** Repo rate unchanged at **6.50%**; stance shifted to **"Neutral"**.
