## Format shift (2025)

Historically about **two-thirds** of environment questions were direct MCQs. In **2025** about **two-thirds** were multi-statement, A/R, Match, or NOT-matched.

Practise two-statement and A/R in the Environment Practice Zones. One-liners alone no longer match the paper.

---

## Recurring traps

- **LiFE:** COP26 idea (2021), launch June 2022 — not COP25 Madrid.
- **MISHTI:** 2023–24 Budget, not 2019.
- **Montreal = ozone**; Kyoto / Paris = climate.
- **GEF** year **1991**; India net zero **2070**; **DPAP** **1973**.
- UP negative geography: Ramsar / sanctuary / NP that is **not** in Uttar Pradesh.
