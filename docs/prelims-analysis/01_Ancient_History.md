| Priority | Cluster | What the paper actually tests |
|----------|---------|-------------------------------|
| **S** | Indus Valley | Sites, finds, extent, dockyard, UP-adjacent archaeology |

| **C** | Stone Age / post-Gupta / economy | Appears, but rarely as a large cluster |

**Core insight:** UPPCS Ancient is a **site–officer–text** paper, not a narrative-of-empires paper.

---

## Format

Recent papers prefer **Match List**, **NOT correctly matched**, and **two-statement** stems over one-line “who founded X”. Practise officer titles, edict locations, and site-feature pairs.

---

## Recurring traps

- Confusing **Harappan site features** (dockyard at Lothal, ploughed field at Kalibangan, horse evidence claims at Surkotada).
- Mixing **Ashokan edict types** (major rock, pillar, separate Kalinga).
- Treating **Agronomai / Sitadhyaksha / Rajuka** as interchangeable Mauryan officers.
- Putting **Gupta inscriptions** on the wrong ruler.
- Counting **2019’s zero Ancient questions** as a trend. It was a one-year tilt toward Modern.
