## Exam Decision

Modern India is the **main History scorer**. If time is limited, finish Modern before polishing Ancient/Medieval details. The paper rewards **chronology**, **movement-person pairs**, **reform organisation-founder pairs**, and **newspaper/book links**.

| What to expect | Revision decision |
|----------------|-------------------|
| **9–13 questions** in normal years | Give Modern a full revision block. |
| Spike possible, as in 2019 | Do not assume History will be evenly split. |
| Chronology-heavy stems | Make a 1757–1947 spine before reading details. |

| Priority | Cluster | What the paper actually tests |
|----------|---------|-------------------------------|
| **S** | National movement chronology | Congress sessions, movements in order, 1919–1942 sequence |

| **A** | Revolt of 1857 | Centres, leaders, causes — UP-heavy |

| **A** | Governors-General and Viceroys | Reform ↔ person; war ↔ person |
| **B** | British expansion | Plassey, Buxar, Mysore, Maratha, Sikh wars; Doctrine of Lapse |

**Core insight:** UPPCS Modern rewards **sequence memory** and **person–event–newspaper** triples. Reading chapters as stories without a chronology spine loses marks.

## Must-Know Locks

- Build one timeline from **Plassey (1757)** to **Independence (1947)**.
- Know **Congress session place-president-resolution** pairs.
- Learn **1857 leader-centre** pairs, especially Awadh/UP.
- Revise **revolutionary organisation-newspaper-person** triples.
- Know **reform society-founder-place** pairs.
- Keep **Viceroy/Governor-General** title periods separate before and after 1858.

## How UPPCS Frames It

Chronology lists are a Modern specialty. 2024–2025 add two-statement and A/R (“this act caused that movement”). Match List for sessions, books, and organisations remains a staple.

---

## Trap List

- Swapping **session presidents** and **session places**.
- Putting **revolutionary newspapers** under the wrong group.
- Mixing **land revenue systems** (Permanent / Ryotwari / Mahalwari) with the wrong region.
- 1857 **leader–centre** mismatches (especially Awadh / UP).
- Treating **Viceroys** as Governors-General after 1858 without checking the title in the stem.

## Study Order

1. Rise of Nationalism + Gandhian Era + Final Phase (Topics 9, 13, 14).
2. Revolt of 1857 (Topic 5) and Revolutionary Movement (Topic 11).
3. Governors-General & Viceroys (Topic 3) and Reform Movements (Topic 6).
4. Expansion + Administration/Economy (Topics 2 and 4).
5. Books & Authors, Press, Miscellaneous (Topics 10, 7, 16) as a last-week pack.

## Mock Audit

After every mock, check:

- Did you lose marks in **chronology**?
- Did you confuse a **Congress session** detail?
- Did you know the movement but miss its **leader/place/date**?
- Did a book/newspaper question expose a weak last-week list?
