**Core insight:** UPPCS Modern rewards **sequence memory** and **person–event–newspaper** triples. Reading chapters as stories without a chronology spine loses marks.

## Format

Chronology lists are a Modern specialty. 2024–2025 add two-statement and A/R (“this act caused that movement”). Match List for sessions, books, and organisations remains a staple.

---

## Recurring traps

- Swapping **session presidents** and **session places**.
- Putting **revolutionary newspapers** under the wrong group.
- Mixing **land revenue systems** (Permanent / Ryotwari / Mahalwari) with the wrong region.
- 1857 **leader–centre** mismatches (especially Awadh / UP).
- Treating **Viceroys** as Governors-General after 1858 without checking the title in the stem.
