## How to read these files

1. **Volume table** tells you how many marks to budget.
2. **Heat map** tells you which topic notes to finish first.
3. **Format shift** tells you how to practise (direct MCQ is no longer enough).
4. Then open the matching chapter in **Subject Notes** and drill confused pairs.

!!! note "Tagging caveat"
    2018–2020 files sometimes dump mixed questions under **General Studies**. Art and Culture is under-tagged because many culture stems sit inside History. Environment ROI is higher than the tagged count because schemes, reports, and protected areas leak into Economy and Geography.
