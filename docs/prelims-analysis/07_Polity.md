## Format shift (2024–2025)

Whole-paper GS-I moved from mostly direct MCQs (2018–2023) to **multi-statement + A/R + Match** in 2024–2025. Polity followed that shift. Practise:

1. Two-statement “which of the following is/are correct”.
2. Article matching (324 / 315 / 280 / 338).
3. Part matching (VIII, X, XI, XVII — including the **wrong** pair).
4. Assertion–Reason on federal and parliamentary claims.

---

## Recurring traps

- **Tribunals are Part XIVA, not Part XI** (2023-style NOT-matched).
- Money Bill **article** vs definition vs Rajya Sabha power.
- **11th vs 12th Schedule** item counts.
- Sixth Schedule areas vs Fifth Schedule.
- UP: Assembly strength, Council, Allahabad High Court + Lucknow Bench, UPPSC vs UPSC.
- Calling a **statutory** body a **constitutional** body (and the reverse).
