| Priority | Cluster | What the paper actually tests |
|----------|---------|-------------------------------|
| **S** | Delhi Sultanate | Alauddin Khalji, Firoz Tughlaq, officers, market/revenue experiments |

| **A** | Medieval literature / chronicles | Who wrote what; Persian translations; court historians |

| **B** | Marathas | Peshwas, later medieval polity |
| **C** | Bhakti–Sufi as “Medieval History” | Often tagged Art & Culture or mixed; still revise saints and silsilas |

**Core insight:** UPPCS Medieval is an **officers + books + Sultanate experiments** paper. Narrative battles matter less than *who held which post* and *who wrote which chronicle*.

---

## Format

Match List (ruler–work, officer–duty) and Assertion–Reason on administrative claims are the live formats. Chronology of dynasties still appears.

---

## Recurring traps

- Mixing **Alauddin’s market control** with **Firoz’s welfare/irrigation** measures.
- Wrong **Mughal chronicler** paired with the wrong emperor.
- Treating **Sher Shah** reforms as Akbar’s (roads, rupee, sarkar–pargana).
- Rajput clan–capital matching.
- Bhakti poetess–work pairs leaking from Art and Culture.
