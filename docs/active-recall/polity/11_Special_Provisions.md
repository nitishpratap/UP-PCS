## Round 3 — Article 371A (Nagaland)

**Q23.** Which State is covered by Article 371A?

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Nagaland.** It is **not** a Sixth Schedule State.

</details>

**Q24.** What four broad areas are protected under Article 371A?

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans:** Religious or social practices of the Nagas. Naga customary law and procedure. Administration of civil and criminal justice involving Naga customary law. Ownership and transfer of **land and its resources**.

</details>

**Q25.** Can Parliament’s laws relating to religious or social practices of Nagas automatically apply to Nagaland?

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: No.** They apply only if the **Nagaland Legislative Assembly** so decides.

</details>

**Q26.** What is the role of the Nagaland Legislative Assembly in such matters?

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Its resolution is required** before a Parliamentary law on those four subjects applies in Nagaland.

</details>

**Q27.** What does Article 371A provide regarding Naga customary law and procedure?

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Parliament cannot impose a law on it** unless the Assembly agrees.

</details>

**Q28.** What does it provide regarding administration of civil and criminal justice involving Naga customary law?

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: The same Assembly-consent shield** as for custom and land.

</details>

**Q29.** What does it provide regarding ownership and transfer of land and its resources?

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Land and resources (including mineral-type resources in the Naga reading) stay under the Assembly-consent shield.** This is why 371A is called particularly strong.

</details>

**Q30.** Why is Article 371A considered particularly strong?

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Union law on Naga religion, custom, customary justice, and land and resources cannot apply without the Assembly.** The Governor also has a special responsibility for **law and order**.

</details>

## Round 12 — Fifth Schedule

**Q82.** Which Article deals with administration of Scheduled Areas and Scheduled Tribes?

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Article 244(1)** for Scheduled Areas (Fifth Schedule). Article **244(2)** is Sixth Schedule tribal areas.

</details>

**Q83.** Which Schedule deals with Scheduled Areas and Scheduled Tribes?

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: The Fifth Schedule.**

</details>

**Q84.** What is a Scheduled Area?

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: An area notified by the President** as a Scheduled Area under the Fifth Schedule, for special administration of STs. Having STs on the Art. **342** list is **not** the same as having a Scheduled Area. UP has STs but **no** Fifth Schedule area.

</details>

**Q85.** Who declares an area as a Scheduled Area?

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: The President.**

</details>

**Q86.** Can the President alter the boundaries of a Scheduled Area?

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Yes** — increase, decrease, alter, or make a new area, after consulting the Governor.

</details>

**Q87.** Who can modify the boundaries of Scheduled Areas?

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: The President**, after consulting the Governor.

</details>

**Q88.** What is the role of the Governor in administration of Scheduled Areas?

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Special responsibility; annual report to the President; regulations for peace and good government**, including land transfer, allotment, money-lending, and disapplying or modifying Union/State law in the area.

</details>

**Q89.** What is the role of the Tribes Advisory Council?

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: It advises the Governor on ST welfare and Scheduled Area administration.** It is **advisory**, not a legislature.

</details>

**Q90.** Which States are required to have a Tribes Advisory Council?

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Every State that has Scheduled Areas.** Ten States: AP, Telangana, Chhattisgarh, Gujarat, HP, Jharkhand, MP, Maharashtra, Odisha, Rajasthan. A State with STs but no SA may still get a TAC if the President so directs.

</details>

**Q91.** Who constitutes the Tribes Advisory Council?

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: The Governor / State**, under the Fifth Schedule.

</details>

**Q92.** What proportion of TAC members should be representatives of Scheduled Tribes in the Legislative Assembly?

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Three-fourths** of a council of not more than **20** members.

</details>

**Q93.** Can the Governor make regulations regarding transfer of tribal land, allotment of land, and money-lending to STs?

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Yes.**

</details>

**Q94.** Do such regulations require Presidential assent?

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Yes.**

</details>

**Q95.** What is the difference between a Scheduled Area and a Tribal Area?

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Scheduled Area = Fifth Schedule (central/peninsular belt), Governor + TAC.** Tribal Area in the constitutional sense of **244(2) = Sixth Schedule (AMTM)**, ADC. Do not call Nagaland a Sixth Schedule tribal area.

</details>

</details>

</details>

**Q101.** What are Regional Councils?

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Councils for autonomous regions inside a district**, if different tribes need a separate unit. The Governor may create them.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: The Governor.**

</details>

**Q105.** What legislative powers do Autonomous District Councils possess?

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

</details>

**Q106.** What judicial powers do they possess?

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: They may constitute village and district courts** for specified suits between tribals, subject to the High Court’s superintendence.

</details>

**Q107.** What taxation powers do they possess?

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: They may levy land revenue and specified taxes and tolls**, and receive grants.

</details>

**Q108.** Can Autonomous District Councils make laws relating to land, forests, marriage, inheritance and social customs?

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Yes**, within the Sixth Schedule list (forests **other than reserved** forests).

</details>

**Q109.** Who has the power to modify laws made by Autonomous District Councils?

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: The Governor** may assent, withhold, or return a law, and may direct that a Parliament or State Act shall not apply, or shall apply with exceptions.

</details>
