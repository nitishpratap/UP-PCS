## Samudragupta

**Q7.** Where is the Prayaga Prashasti, who wrote it, and on whose pillar shaft does it stand?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Allahabad / Prayagraj pillar** (Ashokan shaft reused). Text by **Harishena**, court poet / **sandhivigrahika**. V.A. Smith called Samudragupta the **“Napoleon of India.”**

</details>

**Q8.** What were Samudragupta’s three regional policies — north, south, and forest/frontier?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**North** — about **nine** kings **uprooted** and annexed. **South** — about **twelve** kings **defeated then restored** as tribute (distance). **Vishnugopa of Kanchi** is the 2018 fact. **Atavika** forest chiefs and frontier states pay tribute. He does **not** try to run Tamilakam as districts.

</details>

**Q9.** Which Samudragupta coin types are famous, and how do they link him to the Ashvamedha?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Ashvamedha** types; **veena / lyrist** type. Gold **dinara** shows a king who claims both war and culture.

</details>

## Administration and economy

**Q18.** How did Gupta administration differ from the Mauryan state in units and officers?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: More decentralised.** King + mantriparishad. **Bhukti** (province) under **Uparika**. **Vishaya** (district) under **Vishayapati / kumaramatya**. **Vithi** smaller. Village **grama / gramika**. **Mahadandanayaka** = high judicial-military. **Sandhivigrahika** = peace and war. **Samanta** feudatories ≠ Mauryan spy-state.

</details>

## Art, UP sites, literature, science

**Q28.** Name the main Gupta sculpture schools and the key UP temple sites.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Sarnath** — calm **Dharmachakra Buddha**, thin drape. **Mathura** continues. **Udayagiri (MP)** — **Varaha**. **Deogarh (Lalitpur, UP)** — **Dashavatara**, early **Nagara**. **Bhitargaon (Kanpur Dehat)** — early **brick** temple. **Mehrauli iron pillar** — rust-resistant, often linked to **CG II / Vishnu**.

</details>

**Q29.** Which works are linked to Kalidasa, and which famous text is **not** his (2025 trap)?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Abhijnanashakuntalam, Meghaduta, Raghuvamsha** (also Kumarasambhava, Malavikagnimitra). **Shringara Shataka is Bhartrihari**, not Kalidasa (2025). Elite language = **Sanskrit**. **Vishakhadatta** = *Mudrarakshasa*. **Amarasimha** = *Amarakosha*.

</details>

**Q30.** Distinguish the scientific contributions of Aryabhata and Varahamihira.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Aryabhata** — *Aryabhatiya*; Earth **rotates**; **π**; eclipses as shadows; place-value maths (modern zero **glyph** is later). **Varahamihira** — *Brihat Samhita* and *Panchasiddhantika*.

</details>

**Q31.** Which Gupta ruler is traditionally linked with Nalanda, and what subjects were taught there?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Kumaragupta I** in tradition. Buddhist philosophy, logic, grammar, medicine. Later Chinese pilgrims. Not Taxila, not Vikramashila.

</details>

## Decline and “Golden Age”

**Q32.** Why decline, and is “Golden Age” fully true?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Hunas**, weak heirs, **samantas**, land grants, thinner coins, 6th-century breakup. **Golden Age** fits **Sanskrit, science, Sarnath, temples**. It does **not** mean equal comfort for women, Shudras, or Chandalas.

</details>

**Q33.** Why did the Guptas follow a different policy in the south?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Influence without garrisons — tribute, not full annexation.**

</details>


**Q34.** Why was Gujarat prized?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Ports and silver.**

</details>


**Q35.** Why did Guptas rely more on feudatories than Mauryas?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Cheaper to run a huge map through samantas than Mauryan kumara machine.**

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

</details>

**Q41.** Who wrote ***Mudrarakshasa***, and what is its subject?
