## Origin

**Q1.** Founder, Ghatotkacha, first Maharajadhiraja, dates, core?

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Sri Gupta** (title **Maharaja**). Son **Ghatotkacha** (still Maharaja). Imperial clock: **Chandragupta I** as **Maharajadhiraja**, ~**320 CE**. Empire about **320–550 CE**. Core **Magadha / eastern UP–Bihar**; capital **Pataliputra**. **Not** Chandragupta Maurya.

</details>

**Q2.** Chandragupta I, Kumaradevi, Lichchhavi coins?

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans:** Marriage to **Kumaradevi** (Lichchhavi) brings prestige and a northern foothold. Joint **king-and-queen coins** advertise that alliance. He is the **title** founder of empire; **Samudragupta** is the great conqueror.

</details>

## Samudragupta

**Q3.** Prayaga Prashasti — where, who wrote it, whose shaft?

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Allahabad / Prayagraj pillar** (Ashokan shaft reused). Text by **Harishena**, court poet / **sandhivigrahika**. V.A. Smith called Samudragupta the **“Napoleon of India.”**

</details>

**Q4.** Samudragupta’s three policies: north, south, forest / frontier?

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**North** — about **nine** kings **uprooted** and annexed. **South** — about **twelve** kings **defeated then restored** as tribute (distance). **Vishnugopa of Kanchi** is the 2018 lock. **Atavika** forest chiefs and frontier states pay tribute. He does **not** try to run Tamilakam as districts.

</details>

**Q5.** Samudragupta coins and Ashvamedha?

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Ashvamedha** types; **veena / lyrist** type. Gold **dinara** shows a king who claims both war and culture.

</details>

## Chandragupta II

**Q6.** Title, western war, why Gujarat, Fa-Hien?

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Vikramaditya.** He ends **Western Kshatrapas**. Proof includes **silver coins** of ~**33 grains** (2022). Gujarat = **Arabian Sea** trade. Second court **Ujjain**. **Fa-Hien** visits in this reign for Buddhist texts. He describes mild punishments, prosperity, and **Chandalas** living outside towns. **Fa-Hien ≠ Xuanzang** (Harsha).

</details>

## Kumaragupta and Skandagupta

**Q7.** Kumaragupta I — title, Nalanda, late troubles?

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Mahendraditya.** Tradition credits him with **Nalanda**. Late reign: **Pushyamitras** and the first **Huna** pressure. **Taxila** is older; **Vikramashila** is Pala; **Vallabhi** is Maitraka.

</details>

**Q8.** Skandagupta vs Hunas, Junagadh, Bhitari?

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans:** He **checks the Hunas** for a time. **Junagadh**: **Parnadatta** repairs **Sudarshana Lake**. **Bhitari (Ghazipur, UP)** is his prasasti. Later coin quality falls. **Mihirakula** is a later Huna; **Yashodharman** of Malwa also claims a Huna defeat.

</details>

## Administration and economy

**Q9.** Gupta vs Mauryan state: units and officers?

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: More decentralised.** King + mantriparishad. **Bhukti** (province) under **Uparika**. **Vishaya** (district) under **Vishayapati / kumaramatya**. **Vithi** smaller. Village **grama / gramika**. **Mahadandanayaka** = high judicial-military. **Sandhivigrahika** = peace and war. **Samanta** feudatories ≠ Mauryan spy-state.

</details>

**Q10.** Land grants, bhaga / bhoga / kara, gold vs later decline?

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Bhaga** = produce share. **Bhoga** = dues in kind. **Kara** = tax. Grants to Brahmanas shift **revenue and local power** — feudal tendency. Gold **dinara** under Samudra and CG II; later issues thin out. Long-distance trade **softens** after the western ports peak.

</details>

## Society and religion

**Q11.** Varna, women, sati, Chandalas?

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans:** Caste **hardens**. Women’s public room shrinks. **Eran (510 CE)** is the famous **sati** inscription. Fa-Hien: **Chandalas** outside the settlement. Royal cult is **Vaishnava / Vishnu**; **Ashvamedha** for Samudra and Kumaragupta. Buddhism is **not** persecuted (Nalanda, Sarnath images).

</details>

## Art, UP sites, literature, science

**Q12.** Gupta sculpture schools and UP temples?

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Sarnath** — calm **Dharmachakra Buddha**, thin drape. **Mathura** continues. **Udayagiri (MP)** — **Varaha**. **Deogarh (Lalitpur, UP)** — **Dashavatara**, early **Nagara**. **Bhitargaon (Kanpur Dehat)** — early **brick** temple. **Mehrauli iron pillar** — rust-resistant, often linked to **CG II / Vishnu**.

</details>

**Q13.** Kalidasa and the 2025 trap?

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Abhijnanashakuntalam, Meghaduta, Raghuvamsha** (also Kumarasambhava, Malavikagnimitra). **Shringara Shataka is Bhartrihari**, not Kalidasa (2025). Elite language = **Sanskrit**. **Vishakhadatta** = *Mudrarakshasa*. **Amarasimha** = *Amarakosha*.

</details>

**Q14.** Aryabhata vs Varahamihira?

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Aryabhata** — *Aryabhatiya*; Earth **rotates**; **π**; eclipses as shadows; place-value maths (modern zero **glyph** is later). **Varahamihira** — *Brihat Samhita* and *Panchasiddhantika*.

</details>

**Q15.** Nalanda lock?

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Kumaragupta I** in tradition. Buddhist philosophy, logic, grammar, medicine. Later Chinese pilgrims. Not Taxila, not Vikramashila.

</details>

## Decline and “Golden Age”

**Q16.** Why decline, and is “Golden Age” fully true?

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Hunas**, weak heirs, **samantas**, land grants, thinner coins, 6th-century breakup. **Golden Age** fits **Sanskrit, science, Sarnath, temples**. It does **not** mean equal comfort for women, Shudras, or Chandalas.

</details>

**Q17.** Why south policy, why Gujarat, why feudatories vs Mauryas?

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**South** = influence without garrisons. **Gujarat** = ports and silver. **Feudatories** = cheaper than Mauryan *mahamatras* across a huge map.

</details>
