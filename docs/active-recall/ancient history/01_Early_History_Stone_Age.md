## Round 1 — Rapid recall

**Q1.** What is Prehistory?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: The period before writing.** Indian Stone Age evidence is tools, fossils, and sites — not texts.

</details>

**Q2.** Which source is absent in the prehistoric period?

<details markdown="1">

**Ans: Written records.**

</details>

**Q3.** Name the three Stone Age divisions.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Palaeolithic, Mesolithic, Neolithic.** C.J. **Thomsen** (1836) first set Stone → Bronze → Iron. Chalcolithic is **copper-stone**, not a fourth Stone Age.

</details>

**Q4.** Which is the earliest Stone Age?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Palaeolithic** (Old Stone Age).

</details>

**Q5.** What was the main tool material of the Palaeolithic Age?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Stone** — typically quartzite, chert, agate, jasper. No metal.

</details>

**Q6.** Which age is called the Old Stone Age?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Palaeolithic.**

</details>

**Q7.** Which age is called the Middle Stone Age?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Mesolithic.**

</details>

**Q8.** Which age is called the New Stone Age?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Neolithic.**

</details>

**Q9.** What is a microlith?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: A small stone blade**, about **1–8 cm**, hafted in wood or bone as a composite tool. It is the Mesolithic lock.

</details>

**Q10.** What is the meaning of Neolithic?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: New Stone.** Tools are **ground / polished**, not only chipped.

</details>

**Q11.** What is the meaning of Chalcolithic?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Copper-Stone Age** — copper used **together with** stone tools.

</details>

**Q12.** Which metal became common in the Iron Age?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Iron.**

</details>

**Q13.** What does “Megalith” literally mean?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Large stone** — usually a burial or memorial monument.

</details>

**Q14.** What is pottery?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Baked clay vessels and objects.** Palaeolithic people did **not** use pottery.

</details>

## Round 2 — Palaeolithic

**Q15.** What is the approximate time span of Indian Palaeolithic culture?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: About 2 million BCE to 10,000 BCE** (Pleistocene). Dates are regional, not one national switch.

</details>

**Q16.** Name the three subdivisions of the Palaeolithic Age, with the typical tool of each.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Lower** — **handaxe and cleaver** (Acheulian). **Middle** — **flake** tools and **Levallois** prepared cores. **Upper** — **blades, burins, scrapers**, plus bone tools.

</details>

**Q17.** What is the most characteristic tool of the Lower Palaeolithic?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Handaxe** (biface), with the **cleaver**. Polished stone axe is **Neolithic**, not Lower Palaeolithic.

</details>

**Q18.** Name a famous Lower Palaeolithic site in Tamil Nadu.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Attirampakkam** in the **Kortallayar** valley. Acheulian tools are about **1.5 million years** old. It is a **tool** site, not a human fossil site.

</details>

**Q19.** Which Narmada-valley site is associated with early humans, and what was found?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Hathnora (Madhya Pradesh).** Arun **Sonakia** found a ***Homo erectus*** cranium in **1982**. Do not call it *Homo sapiens*.

</details>

**Q20.** What was the main economic activity of Palaeolithic people?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Hunting and gathering.** They were nomadic bands.

</details>

**Q21.** Did Palaeolithic people know agriculture?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: No.** Food production begins in the **Neolithic**.

</details>

**Q22.** Did Palaeolithic people use pottery?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: No.**

</details>

**Q23.** Evidence of use of fire appears in which broad Palaeolithic stage?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Upper Palaeolithic**, in the broad textbook sense. Fire becomes clearer later; do not treat every Lower Palaeolithic camp as a fire site.

</details>

## Round 3 — Mesolithic

**Q24.** What is the approximate period of the Mesolithic Age in India?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: About 10,000–6000 BCE** (Holocene warming). Some regions run later, toward 4000 BCE.

</details>

**Q25.** What is the main Mesolithic tool type?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Microliths.** Earlier ones are often **non-geometric**; later ones are **geometric** (lunate, triangle, trapeze).

</details>

**Q26.** Name a famous Mesolithic site in Rajasthan, and why it is asked.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Bagor** (Bhilwara, **Kothari** river). It is the standard key for Mesolithic **animal domestication**. **V.N. Misra** excavated it. **Bagor ≠ Baghor** (Upper Palaeolithic shrine, Son valley, MP).

</details>

**Q27.** Name the famous rock-shelter site in Madhya Pradesh.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Bhimbetka** (Raisen). **V.S. Wakankar** discovered it in **1957–58**. UNESCO World Heritage in **2003**. About **750** shelters, of which about **500** are painted.

</details>

**Q28.** Name one major new Mesolithic feature besides hunting.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Early animal domestication** (Bagor) and **fishing**. Bow and arrow likely spread with microlith arrowheads.

</details>

**Q29.** What do Bhimbetka paintings mainly depict?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Hunting scenes, animals, dances, and daily life.** Occupation of the shelters begins in the **Lower Palaeolithic**; most paintings are later Mesolithic and after.

</details>

## Round 4 — Neolithic

**Q30.** Name two defining features of Neolithic culture.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Food production** (agriculture and animal domestication) and **polished / ground stone tools**. Pottery and villages usually come with this package. Gordon **Childe** called it the **Neolithic Revolution**.

</details>

**Q31.** What type of stone tools were used in the Neolithic?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Polished and ground tools** — celts (axes), querns, pounders. Palaeolithic tools were only **chipped**.

</details>

**Q32.** What was the first major economic revolution of this age?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Food production** — growing crops instead of only hunting and gathering.

</details>

**Q33.** Name the famous Neolithic site in Kashmir, and its lock.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Burzahom.** It is known for **pit dwellings** and **dog burial**. **Gufkral** nearby means “cave of the potter.”

</details>

**Q34.** Name a famous South Indian Neolithic tradition known for ash mounds.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Ash-mound pastoral sites** in Karnataka–Andhra — **Tekkalakota, Maski, Brahmagiri, Utnur, Budihal**. Cattle-pen burning is the usual explanation. Crops here are mainly **millets**, not Mehrgarh wheat.

</details>

**Q35.** Name a famous Neolithic site in Bihar, and its lock.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Chirand** (Saran, Ganga). It is known for **bone tools**.

</details>

**Q36.** Which animals were commonly domesticated by the full Neolithic?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Cattle, sheep, goat, buffalo, pig, and dog.**

</details>

## Round 5 — Chalcolithic

**Q37.** Which metals and materials define the Chalcolithic Age?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Copper and stone.** Tin-bronze and Harappan-style cities are **not** this package. Villages were **rural**.

</details>

**Q38.** Did stone tools disappear completely in the Chalcolithic?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: No.** Stone continued beside copper.

</details>

**Q39.** Name a famous Chalcolithic site in Maharashtra, and its culture.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Inamgaon** on the **Bhima** — type site of **Jorwe** culture. Jorwe is **Maharashtra**, not Kashmir or Rajasthan.

</details>

**Q40.** Which Chalcolithic culture is the standard lock for white-painted black-and-red ware in western India?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Ahar–Banas** (Udaipur belt, Rajasthan) — copper smelting and terracotta bulls. **Jorwe** uses painted **black-on-red** ware in Maharashtra. **Malwa** is Narmada / **Navdatoli** (MP), excavated by **Sankalia**.

</details>

**Q41.** What was the main economy of Chalcolithic communities?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Agriculture with pastoralism.** Not Harappan urban trade-grid cities.

</details>

## Round 6 — Iron Age

**Q42.** When does the Iron Age broadly begin in northern India?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: About 1200–1000 BCE.**

</details>

**Q43.** Which pottery is closely associated with the early Iron Age in the Ganga valley?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Painted Grey Ware (PGW)** — upper Ganga / **Kuru–Panchala** belt.

</details>

**Q44.** What major change did iron tools bring?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Forest clearance and expansion of agriculture** on the heavier Ganga soils.

</details>

**Q45.** Name one Mahajanapada region where iron use became widespread.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Kuru–Panchala** in the upper Ganga valley. Later **NBPW** towns sit on this iron-agriculture base.

</details>

## Round 7 — Megalithic culture

**Q46.** In which broad region are Indian megaliths most concentrated?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: South India** — Deccan and peninsular India. They are mainly **Iron Age** burials.

</details>

**Q47.** What do megaliths usually represent?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Burials and memorial monuments.**

</details>

**Q48.** Name any two megalithic burial types.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Cist, dolmen, cairn circle, menhir, urn burial** — any two. A **dolmen** is a stone-table grave with uprights and a capstone.

</details>

**Q49.** Which metal is commonly found in megalithic burials?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Iron** (tools and weapons), often with pottery and beads.

</details>

**Q50.** Name one megalithic site in Karnataka. Name one famous site that is **not** in Karnataka.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Karnataka — Brahmagiri, Maski, or Hallur.** **Adichanallur** is in **Tamil Nadu**, not Karnataka.

</details>

## Extra locks the drill skipped (UPPCS / NCERT)

**Q57.** Who is called the Father of Indian Prehistory, and for which find?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Robert Bruce Foote.** In **1863** at **Pallavaram** (Chennai belt) he recognised the first Palaeolithic tools in India.

</details>

**Q58.** Distinguish Soanian and Acheulian (Madrasian) Lower Palaeolithic traditions.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Soanian** = pebble **chopper-chopping** tools in the **Soan–Siwalik** belt. **Acheulian** = **handaxe-cleaver** in the peninsula, Narmada, and Kortallayar. Older books call the southern Acheulian **Madrasian**.

</details>

**Q59.** Distinguish Hathnora and Attirampakkam in one line each.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Hathnora** = ***Homo erectus* skull**, Narmada, MP, **Sonakia 1982**. **Attirampakkam** = very old **Acheulian tools**, Tamil Nadu — **no fossil**.

</details>

**Q60.** Distinguish Bagor and Baghor.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Bagor** = Mesolithic **domestication**, Rajasthan. **Baghor** = Upper Palaeolithic stone **shrine**, **Son valley, MP**.

</details>

**Q61.** Which UP Mesolithic sites are known for burials, not for the 2018 domestication key?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Sarai Nahar Rai, Mahadaha, and Damdama** in **Pratapgarh**. Bagor (Rajasthan) is the domestication key. **Bori** (Maharashtra) is a claimed Palaeolithic site, not Mesolithic domestication.

</details>

**Q62.** What is Mehrgarh known for?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Earliest farming village** on the subcontinent, in **Balochistan**, about **7000 BCE**. First crops are **wheat and barley**. Period I is **aceramic** (no pottery).

</details>

**Q63.** Which UP Belan-valley sites are the rice lock, and what extra lock sits at Mahagara?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Koldihwa and Mahagara** — early **rice**. **Mahagara** has a **cattle pen** with **hoof marks**. **Lahuradewa** (Sant Kabir Nagar) is early Ganga-plain rice, **not** on the Belan.

</details>

**Q64.** Who excavated Langhnaj, and in which state is it?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: H.D. Sankalia**, in **Gujarat**. Microliths and human skeletons. Do not give Bhimbetka to Sankalia — that is **Wakankar**.

</details>

| Culture | Region / lock |
| ------- | ------------- |
| Ahar–Banas | ? |
| Kayatha | ? |
| Malwa | ? |
| Jorwe | ? |
| OCP | ? |

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

| Culture | Region / lock |

| **Ahar–Banas** | Rajasthan; copper; white-painted **BRW** |

**Q66.** Why is Chalcolithic not the same as Harappan?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Chalcolithic sites are rural copper-stone villages.** Harappan cities have planning, drains, citadel, and bronze. Do not treat Ahar or Inamgaon as Indus capitals.

</details>

**Q67.** What is the correct order of these Indian prehistory stages: Palaeolithic hunter, microlith user, Neolithic farmer, Chalcolithic copper farmer, Iron Age farmer, and megalith builder?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans:** **Palaeolithic → Mesolithic → Neolithic → Chalcolithic → Iron Age → Megalithic.** Old Stone → Middle Stone → New Stone → Copper-Stone → Iron → large-stone burials.

</details>
