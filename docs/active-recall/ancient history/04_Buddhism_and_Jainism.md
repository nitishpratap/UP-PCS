## Part A — Jainism

**Q1.** Who is regarded as the first Tirthankara of Jainism?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Rishabhanatha / Adinatha**, the first tirthankara. Symbol is the **bull**. Birth tradition places him at **Ayodhya (UP)**. Nirvana is linked with **Ashtapada**.

</details>

**Q2.** Who was the 23rd Tirthankara?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Parshvanatha**, the 23rd. Symbol is the **serpent**. Tradition places him at **Varanasi**. He taught **four** vows. Mahavira added **Brahmacharya** as the fifth.

</details>

**Q3.** Who was the 24th and last Tirthankara?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Mahavira (Vardhamana).** Symbol is the **lion**. Nirvana at **Pavapuri**.

</details>

**Q4.** What was Mahavira’s original name?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Vardhamana.**

</details>

**Q5.** Where was Mahavira born?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Kundagrama**, near **Vaishali**. The clan is **Jnatrika / Nata**, inside the **Vajji** league.

</details>

**Q6.** Who were Mahavira’s parents?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Siddhartha and Trishala.**

</details>

**Q7.** What was Mahavira’s wife’s name?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Yashoda.** Do not mix with Buddha’s wife **Yashodhara**.

</details>

**Q8.** At what age did Mahavira renounce worldly life, and how long was the penance before Kevala Jnana?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Renunciation at 30.** Severe penance for **12 years**. Enlightenment at **Jrimbhikagrama** on the **Rijupalika**.

</details>

**Q9.** Where did Mahavira attain Nirvana?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Pavapuri (Magadha / Bihar).** This is **not** Malla **Pava**, where the Buddha’s last meal tradition sits near Kushinagar.

</details>

**Q10.** What does Tirthankara mean?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Ford-maker** — one who shows the path across the cycle of rebirth.

</details>

**Q11.** What are the Three Jewels (Triratna) of Jainism?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Right Faith, Right Knowledge, Right Conduct** (Samyak Darshana, Jnana, Charitra). Buddhist Triratna is **Buddha, Dhamma, Sangha** — a different triad.

</details>

**Q12.** What are the Five Great Vows (Mahavratas)?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Ahimsa, Satya, Asteya, Aparigraha, Brahmacharya.** Householders keep milder **anuvratas**.

</details>

**Q13.** Define Ahimsa, Aparigraha, Asteya, Satya, and Brahmacharya.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ahimsa** — non-violence. **Satya** — truth. **Asteya** — non-stealing. **Aparigraha** — non-possession. **Brahmacharya** — chastity. Ahimsa is the first lock.

</details>

**Q14.** What is Anekantavada, and what is Syadvada?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Anekantavada** means reality has **many sides**. **Syadvada** is how one *states* that, as **conditional predication** (“syat” / maybe, in a stated respect). Do not treat them as identical.

</details>

**Q15.** What are Jiva and Ajiva?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Jiva** is the living soul. **Ajiva** is non-living substance (matter, space, time, motion, rest). Jainism **accepts a real soul**. Buddhism teaches **Anatta**.

</details>

**Q16.** What is Karma and Moksha / Kaivalya in Jainism?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Karma is a material dust** that sticks to the soul. **Moksha / Kevala** is freedom from that bondage and from rebirth. There is **no creator God**.

</details>

**Q17.** Why did Jainism appeal to merchants, and what was its stance on varna and the Vedas?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans:** Ahimsa and aparigraha fit urban trade ethics. Liberation is **not by birth-varna**. Jainism **rejects Vedic authority** (nastika). Strict tapas aims to burn karmic matter.

</details>

**Q18.** Into which two major sects did Jainism divide, and what is the clothing / women’s-moksha split?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Digambara** monks are sky-clad and hold that women cannot attain moksha **as women**. **Svetambara** monks wear white and hold that women **can**. Scriptures and nudity are the other splits.

</details>

**Q19.** Where was the Jain council associated with Sthulabhadra, and what was compiled?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Pataliputra.** Canonical material / Angas in the Svetambara line. Held after a famine when Bhadrabahu’s group had gone south (Digambara memory).

</details>

**Q20.** Which council finalised the Svetambara canon at Vallabhi, and who is named?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Vallabhi**, under **Devardhigani Kshamashramana**.

</details>

**Q21.** What are the main Svetambara canonical texts, in which language, and what is the Kalpasutra?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Angas / Agamas**, largely in **Ardhamagadhi Prakrit**. **Kalpasutra** gives Tirthankara lives, especially Mahavira. **Tattvartha Sutra** is accepted by **both** sects.

</details>

## Part B — Life of the Buddha

**Q22.** What was the original name of the Buddha, and which clan?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Siddhartha Gautama**, of the **Shakya** clan. Kapilavastu sits under **Kosala**. Shakya is **not** one of the sixteen mahajanapadas.

</details>

**Q23.** Where was he born, and who were his parents?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Lumbini** in Nepal, marked by an Ashokan pillar. Father is **Suddhodana**. Mother is **Mahamaya**. Foster mother and first nun is **Mahapajapati Gotami**.

</details>

**Q24.** Wife, son, horse, charioteer?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Yashodhara, Rahula, Kanthaka, Channa.**

</details>

**Q25.** What are the Four Sights, at what age the Great Renunciation, and what is it called?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Old age, sickness, death, and an ascetic.** Age **29**. Event = **Mahabhinishkramana**.

</details>

**Q26.** Where and under which tree did he attain enlightenment, at about what age?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Bodh Gaya (Uruvela)**, under the **Bodhi / peepal** tree, at about **35**, after extreme fasting failed. Teachers were Alara Kalama and Udraka Ramaputra.

</details>

**Q27.** Where was the first sermon, what was it called, and to whom?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Sarnath / Isipatana / Mrigadava (Deer Park), Varanasi, UP.** The sermon is **Dharmachakra Pravartana**. He taught the five former companions, the **Panchavargiya**. **Kaundinya** understood first.

</details>

**Q28.** Where did the Buddha attain Mahaparinirvana, and at what age?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Kushinagar (Kusinara), UP**, among sala trees, at about **80**. Relics went into **eight** original stupas. Early art is **aniconic** (footprint, wheel, stupa, tree, deer).

</details>

## Four Noble Truths and Eightfold Path

**Q29.** What are the Four Noble Truths?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans:** There is **Dukkha**. Its origin is **Tanha** (craving). Its cessation is **Nirvana**. The path is the **Noble Eightfold Path**.

</details>

**Q30.** What is the Middle Path?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Madhyama Pratipad** — neither palace luxury nor corpse-like tapas.

</details>

**Q31.** Name the eight limbs and the three groups.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Prajna:** Right View, Right Intention. **Sila:** Right Speech, Right Action, Right Livelihood. **Samadhi:** Right Effort, Right Mindfulness, Right Concentration.

</details>

**Q32.** Define Anitya, Anatman, Dukkha, and Pratityasamutpada.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Anicca** — impermanence. **Anatta** — **no permanent soul**. **Dukkha** — unsatisfactoriness. **Pratityasamutpada** — things arise from **dependent causes**, not from a creator God. Buddha **rejects Veda and yajna**.

</details>

**Q33.** What is Nirvana in Buddhism?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Cessation of craving and dukkha**, going beyond rebirth. It is not a Jain-style soul flying to the siddha-loka.

</details>

## Sangha and Tripitaka

**Q34.** What is the Sangha, and could women join?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Community of monks and nuns.** Women could join. **Mahapajapati Gotami** is the first bhikkhuni. Entry is not closed by birth-varna, subject to Vinaya.

</details>

**Q35.** What is Vinaya, and what are the three Pitakas?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Vinaya** is monastic rules. The **Tripitaka** is **Vinaya** (Upali), **Sutta** (Ananda), and **Abhidhamma**. Language of the Theravada canon is **Pali**.

</details>

## Buddhist councils

**Q36.** First Buddhist Council — where, who presided, which king?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Rajagriha.** **Mahakassapa** presided. **Ajatashatru** supported. Sutta and Vinaya recited.

</details>

**Q37.** Second Buddhist Council — where, which king, what split?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Vaishali.** **Kalashoka.** Dispute over **Vinaya laxity**. Split: **Sthavira** vs **Mahasanghika**. About **383 BCE**.

</details>

**Q38.** Third Buddhist Council — where, king, president, result?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Pataliputra.** **Ashoka.** **Moggaliputta Tissa.** *Kathavatthu* / Abhidhamma work and **missions** (Sri Lanka: **Mahendra and Sanghamitra**).

</details>

**Q39.** Fourth Buddhist Council (Kanishka) — where, who presided, which tradition?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Kundalavana, Kashmir.** **Vasumitra** presided. **Ashvaghosha** is associated. Sarvastivada compilation in **Sanskrit**. Later books link it with **Mahayana** growth. Details are debated.

</details>

**Q40.** Recite the four-council place chain (2025-style).

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Rajagriha → Vaishali → Pataliputra → Kundalavana.** First is Rajagriha, not Vaishali.

</details>

## Buddhist sects

**Q41.** Distinguish Theravada / Hinayana and Mahayana.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Theravada** keeps the early Pali canon and the **arhat** ideal. **Mahayana** keeps the **bodhisattva** ideal, **Sanskrit** sutras, and Buddha-images. **Hinayana** (“lesser vehicle”) is a **polemical** Mahayana label; prefer **Theravada**. **Vajrayana** is later **tantra**.

</details>

**Q42.** What is a Bodhisattva?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: One who seeks enlightenment while working for all beings.** Mahayana lock.

</details>

## Buddhism vs Jainism

**Q43.** Name major similarities and the soul / path split.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Both:** 6th century BCE, reject Veda and yajna, karma and rebirth, urban / merchant support, Prakrit or Pali. **Jainism** accepts **Jiva**. **Buddhism** teaches **Anatta**. **Buddhism** = Middle Path. **Jainism** = stricter ahimsa and tapas.

</details>

## Patronage and spread

**Q44.** Magadhan and Mauryan Buddhist patronage?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Bimbisara** met the Buddha. **Ajatashatru** hosted the First Council. **Ashoka** is the great spreader — Third Council, missions, Dhamma. **Mahendra and Sanghamitra** go to Sri Lanka.

</details>

**Q45.** Which dynasty and king are linked with the Fourth Council and Mahayana in the north-west?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Kushanas.** **Kanishka.** Gandhara art. **Mathura** (UP) is the other image school.

</details>

**Q46.** Did a Gupta emperor “convert” like Ashoka, and is Harsha a Gupta?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: No.** Gupta royal ideology stayed largely **Brahmanical**, while **Nalanda** grew (often linked to **Kumaragupta I**). **Harsha** is **Pushyabhuti, 7th century**, not Gupta.

</details>

## Architecture and art

**Q47.** What is a stupa, and name its parts?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Relic / commemorative mound.** Parts: **anda, harmika, yasti, chhatra, vedika, torana** (gateway). Purpose is relic honour and circumambulation.

</details>

**Q48.** Distinguish Chaitya and Vihara. Name two cave sites.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Chaitya** = prayer hall (often with a stupa). **Vihara** = monastery. Caves: **Ajanta, Karle** (also Bhaja, Kanheri, Ellora Buddhist caves). **Barabar** is **Ajivika**, not Buddhist.

</details>

**Q49.** Sanchi and Nalanda locks?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Sanchi (MP)** — Ashokan brick core, later **toranas**. **Nalanda** — mahavihara, Gupta and later; Chinese pilgrims.

</details>

## High-yield conceptual

**Q53.** Why did Buddhism and Jainism emerge in the 6th century BCE?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans:** Iron, surplus, **second urbanisation**, **setthi** merchants, punch-marked coins, and reaction to costly Vedic yajna and birth-varna. Towns needed an ethic that was not a village sacrifice.

</details>

**Q54.** Why did merchants and towns support them, and why did Buddhism spread farther in Asia?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans:** Ahimsa and honesty fit trade. Sangha rest-houses sat on routes. Buddhism used **missions, royal backing (Ashoka, Kanishka), and a milder path**. Jainism stayed stronger in western Indian merchant belts.

</details>

**Q55.** How did Prakrit / Pali and the Sangha help?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans:** Teachings reached beyond Sanskrit elites. The Sangha stored Vinaya, copied texts, and gave rulers a ready network. Monasteries sat on **trade routes** as rest and credit nodes.

</details>
