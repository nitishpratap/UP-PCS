## Origin and chronology

**Q1.** Who founded the Satavahanas, what other names, roughly when, and which core?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Simuka.** Puranas call them **Andhras / Andhra-Satavahanas**. About **1st century BCE to 3rd century CE**. Core = **Deccan** (Maharashtra–Andhra). Title **Dakshinapathapati**. Dates wobble because Puranas, coins, and inscriptions do not line up.

</details>

**Q2.** What were the main Satavahana capitals, and which western rivals did they face?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Pratishthana (Paithan)** on the Godavari, and **Amaravati / Dhanyakataka** on the Krishna. Rivals = **Western Kshatrapas (Shakas)**. **Paithan ≠ Madurai.** **Potana** is Asmaka’s mahajanapada capital, **not** Satavahana Paithan.

</details>

## Rulers

**Q3.** What is the exam lock for Simuka, Satakarni I, and Queen Naganika?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Simuka** = traditional founder. **Satakarni I** = early expansion; **Ashvamedha**. Queen **Naganika** = **Naneghat** inscription (genealogy, Vedic sacrifices, donations).

</details>

**Q4.** Why is Gautamiputra Satakarni the greatest, whom did he beat, which inscription?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans:** He restored the Deccan after Shaka pressure. He defeated **Nahapana** (Kshaharata). Mother **Gautami Balashri**’s **Nasik prasasti** claims wins over **Shakas, Yavanas, and Pahlavas**. He **restruck** Nahapana’s silver (Jogalthembi-type hoard). Successor **Vashishthiputra Pulumavi**.

</details>

**Q5.** Who finally ended the Western Kshatrapas, and what is Yajna Sri’s lock?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Chandragupta II (Gupta)** ended the Kshatrapas, not a Satavahana. **Yajna Sri Satakarni** is a **late revival** king with **ship coins** = maritime trade. Do not give him the final Shaka kill.

</details>

**Q6.** Who is Hala?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans:** A Satavahana king linked with ***Gatha Saptasati / Saptashati*** in **Maharashtri Prakrit** — short love lyrics, not a Sanskrit epic.

</details>

**Q7.** Why fight over the western Deccan, and what does restriking prove?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Gujarat–Malwa–Konkan ports** (Bharuch / Barygaza, Sopara, Kalyan) sit on **Roman / Indian Ocean** trade. Overstriking Nahapana’s coins is **conquest made visible** on silver.

</details>

## Administration

**Q8.** How centralised was the state, and what are ahara, amatya, maharathi, mahabhoja?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Not Mauryan-central.** King on top; **ahara** = district. **Amatyas** = officers. **Maharathis** and **Mahabhojas** = feudatory chiefs who actually hold the plateau.

</details>

**Q9.** What do matronymics (Gautamiputra, Vashishthiputra) mean — matriarchy?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Son of a Gautami / Vashishthi** — maternal gotra in the royal name. Society stays **patriarchal**. Queens can donate and issue prasastis. This is **not** a matriarchal state.

</details>

## Economy, coins, Roman trade

**Q10.** What was the Satavahana economic base, and what role did guilds, ports, and Roman trade evidence play?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Agriculture + crafts + long-distance trade.** **Shrenis** produce, lend, and gift to caves. Ports: **Bharuch, Sopara, Kalyan**. Roman **gold coins, amphorae, pepper/luxury** traffic. Deccan roads join north India, Konkan, and the Krishna delta.

</details>

**Q11.** Which metals and symbols appear on typical Satavahana coins?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Lead** (Deccan metal), also copper, potin, some silver. **Elephant** is a common type. **Ship** = **Yajna Sri**. Lead coins are a Deccan fingerprint, not a poverty mark.

</details>

## Society and religion

**Q12.** How did the Satavahanas patronise both Brahmanism and Buddhism?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans:** Kings do **Ashvamedha** and **land grants to Brahmanas**, and also gift **caves and stupas**. Plural patronage. UPPCS **2021**: they **nurtured Prakrit** and **public art** — both true.

</details>

**Q13.** What was the position of women in Satavahana society?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans:** Royal women donate and speak in stone (**Naganika, Gautami Balashri**). Property and cave-gifts by lay women too. Still a **male-line** kingdom.

</details>

**Q14.** Distinguish Amaravati from Nagarjunakonda in period and patronage.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Amaravati** = Satavahana-age **limestone** narrative stupa, Krishna valley. **Nagarjunakonda** flowers mainly under **Ikshvakus**, who **follow** the Satavahanas in coastal Andhra.

</details>

---

## Art and language

**Q15.** What are the main features of the Amaravati school, and which Deccan cave sites are linked to this age?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: White limestone**, busy narrative reliefs, Buddhist jatakas. Caves: **Nasik, Karle, Bhaja, Kanheri** (early **Ajanta** too). **Chaitya** = prayer hall with stupa. **Vihara** = monastery. **Karle** = huge chaitya paid by **merchants / guilds** on the trade road.

</details>

**Q16.** What language and script do Satavahana inscriptions use, and how does that contrast with Rudradaman’s Junagadh inscription?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Prakrit in Brahmi.** Sanskrit prestige rises later. Contrast: **Satavahana → Gautamiputra → Prakrit → Nasik** vs **Shaka → Rudradaman → Sanskrit → Junagadh**.

</details>

## Inscriptions

**Q17.** What do the Naneghat and Nasik inscriptions tell us, and who speaks in each?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Naneghat** — **Naganika**: early kings, **Ashvamedha**, donations. **Nasik** — **Gautami Balashri**: Gautamiputra’s victories and **land grants**. Both are **queen-voiced** prasastis.

</details>

## Conceptual

**Q18.** Why do Satavahanas matter in Deccan history?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans:** First durable **post-Mauryan Deccan state**. They join the Ganga world to Tamilakam and the sea. Decline ~3rd century; **Ikshvakus** take the eastern Krishna.

</details>

**Q19.** Explain the trade-to-caves chain in the Satavahana age.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Routes → shrenis → gifts → chaityas/viharas.** Merchants buy merit beside the road they already use.

</details>

## Extra lock

**Q21.** Which six exam locks define the Satavahana period in one UPPCS card?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans:** **Deccan** power under **Gautamiputra Satakarni**, defeat of **Nahapana**, **Prakrit** inscriptions, **Amaravati** art, **Roman** trade, and **lead** coins. Not Kanishka. Not Rudradaman’s Sanskrit Junagadh style. Not the final Gupta blow to the Western Kshatrapas.
