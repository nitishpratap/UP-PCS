## Background and order

**Q1.** Which major Central Asian / west-Asian groups entered north-west India after the Mauryas, and in what order?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Indo-Greeks → Shakas → Indo-Parthians → Kushanas.** UPPCS **2023 Q23:** Greeks — Sakas — Kushans. Do **not** put Kushanas before Shakas. Persians (**Darius I**, ~518 BCE) sat on Gandhara **before** Alexander.

</details>

**Q2.** Why did Central Asian groups move toward India, and who were the Yuezhi?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans:** Pressure from other nomads (including the Xiongnu on the Yuezhi), pasture, and open **Khyber / Bolan** doors after Mauryan collapse. The **Yuezhi** moved west, displaced **Shakas**, and one branch became the **Kushana** house.

</details>

**Q3.** Who were the Scythians in the Indian story?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Shakas.** Iranian–Central Asian nomads. In India they become **Northern Shakas** and **Western Kshatrapas**.

</details>

## Indo-Greeks

**Q4.** Who was the most famous Indo-Greek, Indian name, capital, text?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Menander = Milinda.** Capital **Sagala (Sialkot)**. ***Milindapanho*** = dialogue with monk **Nagasena**. Tradition says he leaned to **Buddhism**. Do not mix Nagasena with **Nagarjuna**.

</details>

**Q5.** What is the coinage lock for Indo-Greeks, and roughly when did they rule?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Portrait, die-struck, bilingual** (Greek + **Kharoshthi**). About **2nd–1st centuries BCE**. **Demetrius** first pushed into India from Bactria. **Agathocles / Pantaleon** show early **Brahmi** and **Balarama / Vasudeva** types. **Heliodorus** (~113 BCE) raised the **Besnagar** Garuda pillar.

</details>

---

## Shakas and Western Kshatrapas

**Q6.** Who were the Shakas, and which Indian king is famous for beating a Western Kshatrapa?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans:** Scythians from Central Asia. **Gautamiputra Satakarni** defeated **Nahapana** of the **Kshaharata** house. The **Nasik / Nashik** inscription of his mother **Gautami Balashri** records the victory. **Chandragupta II** later ends Western Kshatrapas in Gujarat.

</details>

**Q7.** Who was Rudradaman I, which inscription, which language, which lake?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Greatest Kardamaka Western Kshatrapa.** **Junagadh (Girnar)** inscription in **Sanskrit prose** — an early long royal Sanskrit text. He repaired **Sudarshana Lake** (Mauryan-period dam).

</details>

**Q8.** What were Western Kshatrapas, Kshatrapa vs Mahakshatrapa, and why Gujarat?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Shaka satraps of Gujarat–Malwa.** Titles mark rank. Gujarat = **Arabian Sea** trade. **Chashtana** founds the Kardamaka line. Guptas take the ports for the same reason.

</details>

## Indo-Parthians

**Q9.** Most famous Indo-Parthian, capital, Christian tradition?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Gondophernes.** Centre **Taxila**. Later Christian memory links **St. Thomas** with his court. Treat the Thomas story as **tradition**, not a dated inscription.

</details>

## Kushanas

**Q10.** Origin, founder, greatest king, dates, capitals?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Yuezhi.** Early king **Kujula Kadphises**. Greatest **Kanishka I**. Era often **78 CE (Shaka era)** — exact year is debated. Capitals **Purushapura (Peshawar)** and **Mathura**. **Wima Kadphises** is the gold-coin expander before Kanishka. **Vikrama era 57 BCE** is a **different** count.

</details>

**Q11.** Extent and why the empire was a bridge?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans:** Oxus / Central Asia, Afghanistan, north-west India, into the **Ganga–Yamuna** belt. It sat on the **Silk Route** between **China, Central Asia, India, and Rome**.

</details>

**Q12.** Kanishka and Buddhism — council, president, scholars?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Mahayana** patronage. **Fourth Council** at **Kundalavana, Kashmir**. **Vasumitra** presided. **Ashvaghosha** at court. **Nagarjuna** is of the broader age; do not date him as Kanishka’s minister. Missions ride the Silk Route.

</details>

## Gandhara and Mathura

**Q13.** Compare the two Kushana art schools.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

| | **Gandhara** | **Mathura** |
|--|-------------|-------------|
| Centre | Peshawar–Taxila | **Mathura (UP)** |
| Stone | Grey **schist** | Red **spotted sandstone** |
| Look | Greco-Roman drapery, wavy hair | Indigenous, fuller body |
| Theme | Anthropomorphic **Buddha** | Buddha, Jina, Yaksha, royal portraits |

Both make the **human Buddha image** after aniconic Mauryan signs. Kanishka’s Mathura portraits are a UP lock.

</details>

## Trade and coins

**Q14.** Why was Kushana trade rich, and why so much gold?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Silk Route + Arabian Sea.** Roman gold and goods. Kushana **gold dinars** copy Roman weight/look. Roman coins in India = Indo-Roman trade, not a Roman province.

</details>

**Q15.** What is special about Kanishka’s coins?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans:** Greek / Bactrian legends and a **pantheon** — Iranian, Greek, Indian, and **Buddha**. They show a multi-faith empire, not one temple-state.

</details>

## Conceptual

**Q16.** Why was the north-west open, and why are these not only “destructive invasions”?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans:** Passes + post-Mauryan fragments. New houses brought **coins, Silk Route, Gandhara–Mathura images, Mahayana, and Sanskrit public prose** (Rudradaman). Shakas **assimilate** (satrap titles, Indian queens, inscriptions).

</details>

**Q17.** One-line contribution of Indo-Greeks, Shakas, Kushanas?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Indo-Greeks** — portrait bilingual coins; Milinda. **Shakas** — western ports; Junagadh Sanskrit; Satavahana fight. **Kushanas** — Silk Route; Mahayana; two art schools.

</details>

## UPPCS — Mathura

**Q18.** Why is Mathura a Kushana–UP lock?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans:** Second capital / mint and **Mathura school** in red sandstone. Kanishka’s royal images. Indigenous Buddha and Jina types. Do not give Mathura to the Indo-Greeks as their main capital (that is **Sagala**).

</details>

## Extra locks the drill skipped

**Q20.** Alexander vs later “Greeks” — Hydaspes, Beas, Ambhi, Porus?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Alexander 326 BCE** is Macedon, not Indo-Greek. **Hydaspes (Jhelum)** vs **Porus**. **Beas (Hyphasis)** = army turns back. **Ambhi** of Taxila is an **ally**. **Seleucus ~303** vs Chandragupta is the next Greek layer. Indo-Greeks are **Bactrian** Greeks inside India later.

</details>

**Q21.** Distinguish Shaka era and Vikrama era.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: Shaka era 78 CE** (often tied to **Kanishka**). **Vikrama 57 BCE** is a different traditional count. Do not date Menander to 78 CE.

</details>
