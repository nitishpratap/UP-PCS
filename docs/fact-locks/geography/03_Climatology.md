## 1. 🌍 Atmosphere — Basic Facts

### 🔴 Must ratta

| Fact | Ratta |
| --- | --- |
| Atmosphere | Envelope of gases surrounding Earth |
| Most abundant gas | **Nitrogen ~78%** |
| Second | **Oxygen ~21%** |
| Argon | ~0.93% |
| CO₂ | ~0.04% |
| Water vapour | Variable |
| Most atmospheric mass | Lower atmosphere |
| Most weather phenomena | **Troposphere** |
| Ozone maximum concentration | **Stratosphere** |
| Ionosphere | Mainly thermosphere/upper atmosphere |
| Atmosphere's upper boundary | Gradually merges into outer space |

!!! mnemonic "Golden sequence"
    **N₂ → O₂ → Ar → CO₂**

## 2. Atmospheric Composition

**Permanent gases:** Nitrogen + Oxygen + Argon + other trace gases

**Variable gases:** Water vapour + Carbon dioxide + Ozone + aerosols

!!! prelims "Water vapour"
    **Highly variable** spatially and temporally — clouds, precipitation, greenhouse effect.

## 3. 🌡️ Layers of Atmosphere

From Earth's surface upward:

> **Troposphere → Stratosphere → Mesosphere → Thermosphere → Exosphere**

!!! mnemonic "Layer order"
    **T S M T E**

## 4. ☁️ Troposphere

Lowest layer. Thickness **~8 km at poles → ~18 km near Equator** (average ~13 km). Contains most atmospheric mass + water vapour. Almost all ordinary weather.

> **Troposphere → Weather**

> **Temperature generally decreases with altitude** — average lapse **~6.5°C/km**

## 5. 🌤️ Tropopause

Boundary: **Troposphere + Stratosphere**. Height variable — **higher at Equator**, **lower at Poles**.

## 6. 🟦 Stratosphere

Above troposphere. **Ozone layer**. Temperature **increases upward** (ozone absorbs UV).

> **Stratosphere → Ozone → UV absorption**

## 7. 🟣 Mesosphere

Above stratosphere. Temperature **decreases upward**. **Most meteors burn up here.**

## 8. 🔥 Thermosphere

Above mesosphere. Temperature **increases strongly**. **Ionosphere** region. **Auroras.**

## 9. 🚀 Exosphere

Outermost layer — extremely thin gas; gradually merges with **outer space**.

## 10. 🌡️ Atmospheric Temperature

Sun is main source, but atmosphere heated **indirectly from Earth's surface**:

> Sun → shortwave → surface → longwave terrestrial → atmosphere

> **Atmosphere is heated largely from below.**

## 11. Insolation

**Insolation = incoming solar radiation.** Varies by latitude, season, daylight duration, angle of incidence, atmospheric conditions, cloud cover.

> **Vertical rays → concentrated energy** · **Oblique rays → spread → weaker heating**

## 12. 🌞 Angle of Sun's Rays

High Sun → concentrated → stronger heating. Low Sun → spread → weaker heating.

## 13. 🌍 Heat Budget

Incoming solar ≈ outgoing terrestrial (long-term **global heat balance**). Tropics → energy surplus; poles → deficit; circulation redistributes heat.

## 14. 🌡️ Albedo

**Albedo = fraction of solar radiation reflected.**

| Type | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| High albedo | Snow, ice, clouds |
| Low albedo | Forest, ocean, dark surfaces |

## 15. 🌡️ Normal Lapse Rate

Troposphere average: **6.5°C/km** (~1°C per 165 m). **Height ↑ → Temperature ↓**

## 16. 🌡️ Inversion of Temperature

Normally temperature falls with height; in **inversion** it **increases with altitude** in a layer → cold air trapped → fog, pollution, stable air.

!!! trap "Inversion"
    **Temperature inversion → pollution trapping**

## 17. 🌬️ Atmospheric Pressure

Force of air weight. **Barometer** · **mb / hPa** · Standard sea level **1013.25 hPa**

## 18. Altitude & Pressure

**Height ↑ → Pressure ↓** (less air above)

## 19. 🌍 Global Pressure Belts

| Latitude | Belt |
| --- | --- |
| **0°** | Equatorial Low |
| **30°** | Subtropical High (N & S) |
| **60°** | Subpolar Low (N & S) |
| **90°** | Polar High (N & S) |

!!! mnemonic "Pressure belt master"
    **0 – Low · 30 – High · 60 – Low · 90 – High**

## 20. Equatorial Low Pressure Belt

Around Equator: heating → rising air → weak winds → humidity → convectional rain. **Doldrums / ITCZ region**

## 21. Subtropical High Pressure Belt

**~25°–35°** — descending air → dry. Major deserts **~20°–30°**.

> **30° → Subtropical High → Descending air → Deserts**

## 22. Subpolar Low Pressure Belt

**~60°** — convergence of Westerlies + Polar Easterlies → rising air, frontal activity.

## 23. Polar High Pressure Belt

**~90°** — cold dense air sinks.

## 24. 🌬️ Planetary Winds

> **Trade Winds · Westerlies · Polar Easterlies**

## 25. Trade Winds

| Hemisphere | Flow |
| --- | --- |
| NH | **NE → SW** |
| SH | **SE → NW** |

From subtropical highs → equatorial low. **Trades → East to West**

## 26. Westerlies

**30°–60°**, **West → East**. NH: SW→NE · SH: NW→SE

## 27. Polar Easterlies

**East → West** — Polar High → Subpolar Low

## 28. Coriolis Effect on Winds

**N → Right · S → Left · Equator → zero**

## 29. ITCZ

**Inter Tropical Convergence Zone** — trades converge → rising air → clouds → heavy rain → migrates seasonally.

## 30. ITCZ & Indian Monsoon

Summer NH: ITCZ shifts north over Indian subcontinent → **SW monsoon** mechanism.

## 32. 🔥 Loo

**Hot + dry** · North Indian plains · **May–June** · Rajasthan → Punjab/Haryana → UP/Bihar

## 33. 🌩️ Kal Baisakhi

**Nor'westers** · West Bengal + Assam · violent pre-monsoon thunderstorms

## 34. 🌧️ Mango Showers

Pre-monsoon · **Kerala + Karnataka** · **mangoes**

## 35. ☕ Blossom Showers

**Kerala + Karnataka** · **coffee plantations**

## 36. 🏔️ Chinook

Warm, dry downslope · **Rockies** · rapid warming · **Snow eater**

## 37. 🏔️ Foehn

Warm, dry downslope · **Alps** · **Chinook → Rockies · Foehn → Alps**

## 38. 🌬️ Mistral

Cold, dry · **Southern France** · Rhône Valley → Mediterranean

## 39. 🌬️ Sirocco

**Sahara → Mediterranean** · hot + dry (may pick up moisture)

## 40. 🌬️ Harmattan

**West Africa** · dry + dusty continental wind

## 41. 🌬️ Jet Streams

Fast upper-air winds near **tropopause** · generally **West → East** · Polar front jet · Subtropical jet · Tropical easterly jet

## 42. ✈️ Subtropical Westerly Jet

**25°–35°** · W→E · **Winter India** · **Western Disturbances**

## 43. 🌧️ Tropical Easterly Jet

Strong **NH summer** · **E→W** over South Asia · **Indian summer monsoon**

## 44. 🌀 Air Mass

Uniform **temperature + humidity** over source region.

| Code | Type |
| --- | --- |
| **cT** | Continental Tropical — hot + dry |
| **mT** | Maritime Tropical — warm + moist |
| **cP** | Continental Polar — cold + dry |
| **mP** | Maritime Polar — cold + moist |

## 45. 🌡️ Fronts

Boundary between contrasting air masses.

| Front | Movement |
| --- | --- |
| Warm | Warm air over cold |
| Cold | Cold under warm |
| Stationary | Neither advances much |
| Occluded | Cold overtakes warm |

## 46. 💧 Humidity

| Type | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Absolute | Mass of vapour per volume |
| Specific | Mass of vapour per mass of moist air |
| Relative | % of saturation at that temperature |

> **Relative humidity depends strongly on temperature**

## 47. 🌡️ Relative Humidity

Temp **↑** → RH **↓** (if moisture constant). Temp **↓** → RH **↑**. Saturation **RH = 100%**

## 48. 💧 Dew Point

Temperature at which air becomes **saturated** when cooled at constant pressure/moisture.

## 49. ☁️ Clouds — Basic Classification

> **Cirrus · Cumulus · Stratus · Nimbus**

## 50. Cirrus

High, thin, wispy · **ice crystals**

## 51. Cumulus

Puffy, heap-like · **convection** → can become **Cumulonimbus**

## 52. Stratus

Layered/sheet-like · usually low

## 53. Nimbus

**Rain-bearing** — Nimbostratus, Cumulonimbus

## 54. ⛈️ Cumulonimbus

Vertical thunderstorm cloud — heavy rain, lightning, hail, strong winds; tornadoes possible

## 55. 🌧️ Types of Rainfall

> **Convectional · Orographic · Cyclonic/frontal**

## 56. ☀️ Convectional Rainfall

Heating → rise → cool → condense → rain. Equatorial · heavy, short, often afternoon.

## 57. 🏔️ Orographic Rainfall

Moist air forced over mountains — **windward wet**, **leeward rain shadow**

## 58. 🌧️ Rain Shadow

Dry **leeward** side — descending air warms/dries. **Western Ghats → wet west coast · dry Deccan interior**

## 59. 🌀 Cyclone

**Low pressure** centre · NH **counter-clockwise** · SH **clockwise**

## 60. Anticyclone

**High pressure** · NH **clockwise** · SH **counter-clockwise**

## 61. 🌀 Tropical Cyclone

Warm-core · warm tropical oceans · Coriolis · weakens over land · strongest winds in **eyewall**. Needs warm ocean + moisture + Coriolis + low vertical wind shear

## 62. 🌀 Tropical Cyclone — Coriolis

Rare **exactly at Equator** — Coriolis ≈ zero

## 63. 🌀 Eye of Cyclone

Calm, low-pressure centre · surrounded by **eyewall** (strongest winds)

## 64. 🌀 Cyclone Names

| Basin | Term |
| --- | --- |
| Atlantic / E Pacific | Hurricane |
| W Pacific | Typhoon |
| Indian Ocean / Australia | Cyclone |

## 65. 🌪️ Tornado

Narrow violent vortex from thunderstorm to surface · smaller, shorter-lived than tropical cyclone

## 66. 🌀 Tropical vs Temperate Cyclone

| Feature | Tropical | Temperate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Core | Warm | Generally cold |
| Main energy | Latent heat | Temperature gradients |
| Fronts | Generally absent | **Present** |
| Location | Tropics/subtropics | Mid/high latitudes |
| Size | Generally smaller | Generally larger |
| Eye | Often present | Usually absent |
| Structure | Warm-core | Frontal |

## 67. 🌊 El Niño

**Abnormal warming** of central/eastern tropical Pacific · **ENSO** · weakens Walker circulation · India: **often weaker monsoon**

## 68. ❄️ La Niña

Cool central/eastern Pacific · stronger Walker · India: **often stronger monsoon**

## 69. ENSO

**El Niño–Southern Oscillation** = ocean (El Niño/La Niña) + atmosphere (Southern Oscillation)

## 70. Walker Circulation

Normal Pacific: **warm west + rising** · **cool east + sinking** · surface **easterly trades**

## 71. El Niño & Walker Circulation

El Niño: trades weaken → warm water east → eastern Pacific warmer/rising → **Walker weakens**

> **El Niño → Trade winds weaken → Warm water east → Walker weakens**

## 72. Indian Ocean Dipole — IOD

SST difference **western vs eastern** tropical Indian Ocean.

| Phase | Western IO | Eastern IO | Monsoon hint |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Positive** | Warmer | Cooler | Often more favourable |
| **Negative** | Cooler | Warmer | Often less favourable |

## 73. 🧠 IOD Master Ratta

> **Positive IOD → West warm + East cool**

> **Negative IOD → West cool + East warm**

IOD can **moderate El Niño's effect** on Indian monsoon.

## 74. 🌧️ Western Disturbances

Extra-tropical systems from **Mediterranean / West Asia** · **STWJ** · **NW India winter rain** · Punjab, Haryana, Rajasthan, western UP, J&K/Himachal

## 75. ❄️ Western Disturbances & Agriculture

Beneficial **winter rain for Rabi** — especially **wheat** (excess hail can damage crops)

## 76. 🇮🇳 Indian Monsoon — Core

**Seasonal reversal of winds** — land–sea heating, pressure, ITCZ, cross-equatorial flow, jets, Tibetan heating, ocean–atmosphere coupling

## 77. 🌡️ Summer Land–Sea Contrast

Summer: land heats → **low pressure NW/N India** · ocean higher pressure → moist winds inland → **SW monsoon**

## 78. 🇮🇳 Southwest Monsoon

Two branches: **Arabian Sea** · **Bay of Bengal**

## 79. 🌊 Arabian Sea Branch

Western coast · heavy **Western Ghats** rain · **rain shadow** on interior Deccan

## 80. 🌊 Bay of Bengal Branch

NE India + Ganga plains · **Meghalaya / Mawsynram–Cherrapunji** — extreme rainfall

## 81. 🌧️ Mumbai vs Pune

**Mumbai windward wet · Pune leeward drier** — Western Ghats

## 82. 🌧️ Meghalaya Rainfall

Bay moisture uplifted on Khasi/Jaintia/Garo hills → **extreme orographic rain**

## 83. 🇮🇳 Monsoon Onset

First **Kerala** (~**1 June**) · advances north · withdrawal starts **NW India** then south

## 84. 🌧️ Monsoon Burst

Sudden widespread heavy rain after arrival

## 85. 🌧️ Monsoon Break

Temporary rainfall decrease · monsoon trough shift · Himalaya foothills may get more rain during breaks

## 86. 🇮🇳 Monsoon Trough

Low-pressure belt NW India → Bay of Bengal · controls rainfall distribution · shifts north/south

## 87. 🌧️ Orographic Effect — India

**Western Ghats** — wet coast, dry interior · **Himalayas** — block/uplift · **NE hills** — orographic enhancement

## 88. 🏔️ Tibetan Plateau & Monsoon

Summer heating helps **South Asian monsoon circulation**

## 89. 🌬️ Cross-Equatorial Flow

Summer NH: flow toward Asian low · SH winds cross Equator · Coriolis → **south-westerly monsoon**

## 90. 🌧️ Monsoon — Master Mechanism

> **Summer heating → Land low pressure → ITCZ north → Cross-equatorial flow → Coriolis → SW winds → Arabian Sea + Bay branches → Indian monsoon**

## 91. 🇮🇳 Monsoon Variability

ENSO · IOD · MJO · Himalayan snow · land–sea contrast · monsoon trough · jets · tropical disturbances

| Factor | Typical association |
| --- | --- |
| El Niño | Weaker monsoon |
| La Niña | Stronger monsoon |
| Positive IOD | Favourable |
| Western Disturbance | Winter NW rain |

## 92. 🌏 MJO

**Madden–Julian Oscillation** — eastward tropical convection/circulation · **30–60 days** · affects active/break cycles

## 93. 🌧️ Active vs Break Monsoon

**Active** — widespread/intense rain · **Break** — reduced rain over much of central India

## 94. 🌪️ Indian Cyclones

**Bay of Bengal** + **Arabian Sea** (Bay more frequent) · seasons: **Apr–May** pre-monsoon · **Oct–Nov** post-monsoon

## 95. 🌊 Bay of Bengal Cyclones

Warm water · moisture · funnel-shaped north Bay geography

## 96. 🌊 Storm Surge

Sea-level rise from cyclone **winds + low pressure** — major coastal hazard
