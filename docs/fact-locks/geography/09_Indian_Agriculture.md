## 1. INDIAN AGRICULTURE — BASIC

India:

> **Agriculture is predominantly monsoon-dependent**

Major determinants:

> Climate + soil + relief + irrigation + technology + markets.

Major agricultural systems:

> Subsistence + commercial + plantation + mixed farming.

### MASTER

> **Indian agriculture = Monsoon + Soil + Irrigation + Technology + Markets**

## 2. CROPPING SEASONS OF INDIA

Three major agricultural seasons:

1. **Kharif**
2. **Rabi**
3. **Zaid**


> **Kharif → Monsoon**

> **Rabi → Winter**

> **Zaid → Summer**

## 3. KHARIF CROPS

Sowing:

> **June–July**

Harvesting:

> **September–October/November**

Depend mainly on:

> **Southwest Monsoon**

Major crops:

> Rice

> Maize

> Jowar

> Bajra

> Cotton

> Groundnut

> Soybean

> Sugarcane

> Arhar/Tur.


> **Kharif → Rainy season**

## 4. RABI CROPS

Sowing:

> **October–December**

Harvesting:

> **March–April**

Depend on:

> Winter moisture + irrigation.

Major crops:

> **Wheat**

> Barley

> Gram

> Mustard

> Peas

> Lentils.


> **Rabi → Winter → Wheat**

## 5. ZAID CROPS

Season:

> Between Rabi and Kharif.

Period:

> **Summer**

Major crops:

> Watermelon

> Muskmelon

> Cucumber

> Vegetables

> Fodder crops.


> **Zaid → Summer → Short duration**

## 6. KHARIF–RABI–ZAID MASTER

| Season     | Sowing  | Harvest | Major crops            |
| ---------- | ------- | ------- | ---------------------- |
| **Kharif** | Jun–Jul | Sep–Nov | Rice, maize, cotton    |
| **Rabi**   | Oct–Dec | Mar–Apr | Wheat, gram, mustard   |
| **Zaid**   | Mar–Apr | May–Jun | Watermelon, vegetables |


> **Kharif = Rain**

> **Rabi = Winter**

> **Zaid = Summer**

Temperature:

> Approximately **20–27°C** during growing season.

Rainfall:

> Generally **100 cm or more** preferred.

> Irrigation supports rice cultivation.


> **Rice → Warm + Wet**

## 80. CROP INTENSITY

> **Gross Cropped Area ÷ Net Sown Area × 100**

> More crops grown on same land during year.


> **Cropping intensity = GCA / NSA × 100**
