## 1. INDUSTRY — BASIC CLASSIFICATION

Industries can be classified according to:

### Raw material

> Agro-based

> Mineral-based

> Forest-based

> Marine-based.

### Ownership

> Public sector

> Private sector

> Joint sector

> Cooperative sector.

### Size

> Cottage

> Small-scale

> Medium

> Large-scale.

### MASTER

> **Agro → Agriculture**

> **Mineral → Minerals**

> **Forest → Forest products**

> **Marine → Fisheries/ocean**

## 2. AGRO-BASED INDUSTRIES

Use agricultural products as raw material.

Major examples:

> Cotton textiles

> Jute

> Sugar

> Vegetable oil

> Food processing

> Dairy

> Tobacco.

## 3. MINERAL-BASED INDUSTRIES

Use minerals as major raw materials.

> Match industry.

## 5. MARINE-BASED INDUSTRIES

> Seafood processing.

## 6. LOCATION FACTORS OF INDUSTRIES

Major factors:

> Raw material

> Power

> Labour

> Capital

> Market

> Transport

> Water

> Land

> Government policy

> Technology.


> **Raw material + Power + Labour + Market + Transport + Capital**

## 7. RAW MATERIAL-ORIENTED INDUSTRY

Located near:

> Source of bulky/perishable raw materials.

Examples:

> Sugar

> Iron & steel in traditional coal/ore belts.


> **Bulky raw material → Near raw-material source**

## 8. MARKET-ORIENTED INDUSTRY

Located near:

> Major consumers/markets.

Advantages:

> Lower distribution costs

> Faster delivery.

Examples:

> Consumer goods

> Perishable processed foods.

## 9. POWER-ORIENTED INDUSTRY

Located near:

> Cheap/reliable electricity.

Examples:

> Aluminium

> Some chemical industries

> Electro-metallurgical industries.


> **Aluminium → Electricity intensive**

## 10. WATER-ORIENTED INDUSTRY

Industries requiring large quantities of water:

> Paper

> Textile processing

> Chemicals

> Steel.

## 11. PORT-ORIENTED INDUSTRY

Located near:

> Ports.

Advantages:

> Import raw materials

> Export finished products.

Examples:

> Petroleum refining

> Petrochemicals

> Shipbuilding.

## 12. LABOUR-ORIENTED INDUSTRY

Needs:

> Large labour force.

Examples:

> Textile

> Garments

> Footwear

> Food processing.

## 13. INDUSTRIAL AGGLOMERATION

Definition:

> Concentration of industries in a particular region.

Reasons:

> Shared infrastructure

> Labour pool

> Markets

> Suppliers

> Transport

> Knowledge spillovers.


> **Agglomeration → Industries cluster together**

> Bulk goods

> Coal

> Iron ore

> Cement.

### Roads

Best for:

> Short/medium-distance movement

> Door-to-door delivery.

### Waterways

> Heavy bulk goods.

### Air
