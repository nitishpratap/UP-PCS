## 1. TRANSPORT — BASIC

Transport means:

> Movement of **people and goods** from one place to another.

Major modes:

1. **Roadways**
2. **Railways**
3. **Waterways**
4. **Airways**
5. **Pipelines**

Modern transport also includes:

> Multimodal logistics

> Dedicated freight corridors

> Inland waterways

> Ropeways.

### MASTER

> **Road → Flexible**

> **Rail → Bulk + long distance**

> **Water → Cheapest bulk transport**

> **Air → Fastest**

> **Pipeline → Liquids/gases**

## 2. ROAD TRANSPORT

Major advantages:

> Door-to-door service

> Flexible routes

> Short/medium-distance efficiency

> Connects villages and remote areas.

Best suited for:

> Perishable goods

> Passenger transport

> Short-distance freight.


> **Road → Door-to-door**
