# Daily Study Planner

Office-day sheet for Prelims on **6 Dec 2026**. Wake **8:00**. Morning = **one** ratta layer only (MSF **or** HYT — never both). Evening = one subject deep read.

**MSF** = Must-Score Facts · **HYT** = High-Yield Tables

**Desks to open while filling:**

- [Must-Score Facts](revision/must-score-facts/index.md) — MSF mornings only
- [High-Yield Tables](revision/high-yield-tables/index.md) — HYT mornings only
- [Subject Notes](subjects/index.md) — evening reading
- [Chapter Priority Tracker](chapter-tracker.md) — tick chapters when evening block is done
- [Error & Gap Tracker](error-tracker.md) — park weak facts

---

## 1. Fixed daily clock (office days) — ~2 hour morning

You have roughly **08:00 → 10:00** before leaving for a **10:30** office. That is **one** deep ratta block, not two half blocks.

| Time | Block | What you do |
|------|--------|-------------|
| **08:00** | Wake | Out of bed. No phone scroll. |
| **08:00–08:20** | Prep | Wash, water/tea, open today’s layer desk only. |
| **08:20–10:00** | **Morning ratta (~100 min)** | **Only MSF *or* only HYT** — see today’s layer in §2. |
| **10:00–10:15** | Buffer + leave | Fill morning log. Bag. Leave for office. |
| **10:30–18:00** | Office | Optional: one 10-min weak-fact flash if you get a clean gap. |
| **18:00–19:00** | Reset | Commute / dinner / rest. No heavy reading yet. |
| **19:00–21:30** | **Subject deep read** | One subject from Subject Notes (see §3). |
| **21:30–21:50** | Close the day | Tick Chapter Tracker. Log evening. 2–3 weak lines into Error Tracker. |
| **22:30** | Sleep target | Protect tomorrow’s morning ratta. |

**Weekend / holiday clock**

| Time | Block |
|------|--------|
| 08:00–08:20 | Prep |
| 08:20–10:20 | Same rule: **one** layer only (MSF *or* HYT for that day) — you can go slower / cover more chapters |
| 10:30–13:00 | Subject deep read (session 1) |
| 14:30–17:30 | Subject deep read (session 2) or second subject if first is done |
| 19:00–20:00 | Weak facts + Error Tracker + light Active Recall |

## 2. Morning rule: one layer per day (alternate)

Do **not** open MSF and HYT in the same morning. Use the full ~100 minutes on **one** desk.

### 6-day loop (covers both layers)

| Calendar day in loop | Morning layer | Slice | Subjects |
|----------------------|---------------|-------|----------|
| **Day A** | **MSF only** | MSF-1 | Geography · Polity · Environment |
| **Day B** | **HYT only** | HYT-1 | Geography · Polity · Environment |
| **Day C** | **MSF only** | MSF-2 | Modern · Medieval · Ancient · Art & Culture |
| **Day D** | **HYT only** | HYT-2 | Modern · Medieval · Ancient · Art & Culture |
| **Day E** | **MSF only** | MSF-3 | Economy · S&T · Census · UP Special |
| **Day F** | **HYT only** | HYT-3 | Economy · S&T · Census · UP Special |

Then restart at Day A.

- Full **MSF** pass = Days A + C + E (3 mornings)  
- Full **HYT** pass = Days B + D + F (3 mornings)  
- Both layers complete once every **6 calendar mornings**

**Start this week:** Mon 6 Oct = Day A (**MSF-1**).

### How to use the ~100 minutes

1. Open **only** today’s desk (MSF *or* HYT) and **only** today’s subject pack.
2. Ratta aloud / under breath. Mark weak lines as you go.
3. Aim: finish that pack’s chapters for this loop — or mark what rolls to the **same slice next loop**.
4. If 10 minutes remain: run that chapter’s quick MCQs — otherwise leave for weekend.
5. Stop at **10:00**. Do **not** “also squeeze HYT” if it was an MSF day.

### Why this fits a 2-hour morning

| Approach | Reality for you |
|----------|-----------------|
| MSF + HYT same morning | ~40–50 min each → both stay shallow |
| **One layer / morning** | ~100 min deep ratta → real coverage, still both done every 6 days |

## 3. Evening subject deep read

Pick **one** subject per office evening. Finish open chapters before switching.

| Priority | Subject | Why first |
|----------|---------|-----------|
| 1 | [Geography](subjects/geography/index.md) | Heaviest static scoring base |
| 2 | [Polity](subjects/polity/index.md) | Articles, bodies, Parliament, PRIs |
| 3 | [Environment & Ecology](subjects/environments%20&%20ecology/index.md) | SDGs, protected areas, climate |
| 4 | [Modern India](subjects/mordern%20india/index.md) | Chronology + movements |
| 5 | [Medieval India](subjects/medieval%20india/index.md) | Rising weight |
| 6 | [Ancient History](subjects/ancient%20history/index.md) | Precise site / text traps |
| 7 | [Art & Culture](subjects/art%20and%20culture/index.md) | Often rides inside History |
| 8 | [Economy](subjects/economy/index.md) | Schemes + basics |
| 9 | [Science & Technology](subjects/science%20and%20technology/index.md) | UKPCS Unit 5 + GS misc |
| 10 | [Census & Urbanisation](subjects/census%20and%20urbanisation/index.md) | Data traps |
| 11 | [UP Special](subjects/up%20special/index.md) | State paper share |

**Evening rules**

- One subject only (weekend may add a second).
- Read from Subject Notes teaching cards — not random YouTube.
- End by ticking finished chapters in [Chapter Priority Tracker](chapter-tracker.md).
- Park wrong / shaky facts in [Error Tracker](error-tracker.md).

## 7. Non-negotiables

1. **One morning layer only** — MSF *or* HYT, never both.
2. Give that layer the full **08:20–10:00** block (~100 min).
3. **Morning ratta is sacred** — a thin MSF-only day still beats skipping.
4. **Do not mix** morning ratta with evening subject reading in the same sitting.
5. **One evening subject** on office days.
6. After Day F, restart Day A — do not invent a seventh morning type.
