4. The population density of Uttarakhand is 189.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

A. Only 2 is correct
B. Only 1, 2 and 3 are correct
C. Only 1, 3 and 4 are correct
D. Only 3 is correct

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: C (Series B provisional key).** Rudraprayag is the least populous district of Uttarakhand with about 2.42 lakh people, so statement 1 is correct.
