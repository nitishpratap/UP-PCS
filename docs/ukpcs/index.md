## How this library works for UKPCS

1. **Shared first.** Indian History, Geography, Polity, Environment, Art & Culture notes already cover most national Unit heads for both UPPCS and UKPCS.
2. **State second.** Open the subject’s **Uttarakhand** nav section for UK-only heads (history dynasties, UK geography, UK polity, UK economy, UK natural resources).
3. **PYQs next.** The [2025 GS Paper I bank](pyqs/2025_GS_I_Complete_Bank.md) is tagged and analysed in the [UKPCS Prelims Analysis](../prelims-analysis/09_UKPCS_Overview.md). Further years join the same track, as with UPPCS 2018–2025.
4. **New subjects.** UKPCS Unit 4 (Economy) and Unit 5 (Science & Technology) have scaffolding under Subject Notes; fill as dumps arrive.
