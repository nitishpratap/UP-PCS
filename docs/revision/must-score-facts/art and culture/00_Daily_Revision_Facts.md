## 🎯 Consolidated Must-Score Facts

1. **Eight Classical Dances:** Sangeet Natak Akademi officially recognizes strictly **eight** classical dances: Bharatanatyam (Tamil Nadu), Kathak (Uttar Pradesh/North India), Kathakali (Kerala), Kuchipudi (Andhra Pradesh), Mohiniyattam (Kerala), Odissi (Odisha), Manipuri (Manipur), and **Sattriya (Assam — recognized in November 2000)**.
2. **Chhau Dance** is recognized as classical by the Ministry of Culture, but is **NOT** included in the Sangeet Natak Akademi official list of eight; Chhau was inscribed on the UNESCO Representative List of Intangible Cultural Heritage in 2010.
3. **Eleven Classical Languages:** India now recognizes **11 Classical Languages**: Tamil (2004), Sanskrit (2005), Telugu (2008), Kannada (2008), Malayalam (2013), Odia (2014), and five newly approved languages in October 2024: **Marathi, Pali, Prakrit, Assamese, and Bengali**.
4. **Temple Architecture Styles:** 
   - **Nagara Style (North India):** Curvilinear beehive tower (**Shikhara**), square sanctum (**Garbhagriha**), absence of boundary walls, elevated platform (**Jagati**), crowned by **Amalaka** and **Kalasha**.
   - **Dravida Style (South India):** Stepped pyramidal tower (**Vimana**), monumental entrance gateways (**Gopurams**), boundary enclosure wall (**Prakara**), and temple water tank (**Kalyani**).
   - **Vesara Style (Deccan Hybrid):** Blends Nagara and Dravida elements on a **star-shaped (stellate) ground plan**, developed under the Chalukyas and Hoysalas.
5. **Khajuraho Temples (MP):** Built of sandstone by the **Chandela dynasty** (950–1050 CE) in the Panchayatana Nagara style; features clustered miniature towers (**Urushringas**); supreme example: **Kandariya Mahadeva Temple**.
6. **Sun Temple at Konark (Odisha):** Built in the 13th century by Eastern Ganga King **Narasimhadeva I**; conceived as the colossal stone chariot of Surya with **24 carved wheels** pulled by **7 horses**; called the **Black Pagoda**.
7. **Brihadeeswara Temple at Thanjavur (Tamil Nadu):** Built in **1010 CE** by Imperial Chola Emperor **Rajaraja I**; features a 66-meter granite **Vimana** crowned by an **80-ton single monolithic granite cupola**; part of UNESCO 'Great Living Chola Temples'.
8. **Kailasa Temple at Ellora (Cave 16, Maharashtra):** Commissioned in the 8th century by Rashtrakuta King **Krishna I**; the world's largest **monolithic rock-cut excavation**, carved top-down from a single basalt cliff.
9. **Oldest Complete Terracotta Brick Temple:** **Bhitargaon Temple** in Kanpur district, Uttar Pradesh (Gupta period, 5th–6th c. CE), featuring an early true arch with keystone in its sanctum roof.
10. **Oldest Panchayatana Stone Temple:** **Dashavatara Temple at Deogarh** (Lalitpur district, UP; Gupta period), adorned with the famous **Sheshashayi Vishnu** wall relief.
11. **Six Limbs of Painting (Shadanga):** Codified by Vatsyayana in the *Kamasutra* and Yashodhara: **Rupa-bheda** (forms), **Pramanam** (proportion/measure), **Bhava** (emotion/mood), **Lavanya-yojanam** (grace/luster), **Sadrisyam** (similitude/likeness), and **Varnikabhanga** (brushwork and colour harmony).
12. **Ajanta vs Ellora Caves:** Ajanta (29 caves, Aurangabad, MH) is **exclusively Buddhist** rock-cut caves famous for fresco-secco murals (Padmapani & Vajrapani in Cave 1, Dying Princess in Cave 16); Ellora (34 caves) represents **three religions** (Caves 1–12 Buddhist, 13–29 Hindu, 30–34 Jain).
13. **Mughal Painting Titans:** Under Akbar: **Daswanth** and **Basawan** (illustrated the *Hamzanama* and *Razmnama*); under Jahangir (Golden Age): **Ustad Mansur** (*Nadir-ul-Asr*; flora, fauna, Siberian crane, dodo) and **Bishandas** (lifelike human portraiture).
14. **Bani Thani ('Indian Mona Lisa'):** Masterpiece of the **Kishangarh School** of Rajasthan, painted by court artist **Nihal Chand** under ruler-poet **Raja Savant Singh (Nagari Das)**; characterized by arched eyebrows, lotus eyes, pointed nose, and gossamer gold-bordered veil.
15. **Pahari Painting Schools:** **Basohli** (earliest, bold fiery palette, iridescent green beetle-wing insets; Kirpal Pal patron) and **Kangra** (lyrical zenith, soft cool pastel shades, Radha-Krishna devotion; Sansar Chand patron).
16. **Folk Paintings of India:** 
   - **Madhubani (Bihar):** Wall (*Bhitti*) and floor (*Aripana*) paintings using natural dyes and double outlines; GI tagged.
   - **Warli (Maharashtra):** Tribal mud-wall art using white ground rice paste in geometric triangles and circles, famous for circular *Tarpa* dance.
   - **Pithora (Gujarat/MP):** Rathwa tribal sacred wall murals dedicated to Baba Pithora featuring painted horses.
   - **Patachitra (Odisha/WB):** Treated cloth-scroll paintings of Lord Jagannath Triad from Raghurajpur village.
   - **Phad (Rajasthan):** Long 30-foot cloth scrolls of folk deities Pabuji and Devnarayan, recited at night by Bhopa-Bhopi bards with *Ravanahatha*.
   - **Pichwai (Rajasthan):** Devotional cloth hangings behind the idol of Shrinathji at Nathdwara.
   - **Kalamkari (Andhra Pradesh):** Srikalahasti (freehand pen drawing of temple epics) and Machilipatnam (block-printed).
17. **Hindustani vs Carnatic Music:** Hindustani (North India; 10 Thaats by Bhatkhande; improvisation-heavy; accompanied by Tabla and Pakhawaj; strict time-of-day *Samay Siddhanta*); Carnatic (South India; 72 Melakartas by Venkatamakhin; composition-heavy *Kritis*; accompanied by Mridangam; vocal-centric).
18. **Father of Carnatic Music:** **Purandara Dasa** (1484–1564), venerated as the **'Sangita Pitamaha'**; standardized the foundational pedagogical grading beginning with raga *Mayamalavagowla*.
19. **Trinity of Carnatic Music (Trimurti of Thiruvarur):** **Tyagaraja** (composed predominantly in **Telugu**; *Pancharatna Kritis*), **Muthuswami Dikshitar** (composed exclusively in **Sanskrit**; *Navagraha Kritis*), and **Syama Sastri** (composed in **Telugu and Sanskrit**; complex talas and raga *Anandabhairavi*).
20. **Oldest Classical Vocal Genre:** **Dhrupad** (austere, spiritual, accompanied by Pakhawaj; four banis: Gauhar, Khandar, Nauhar, Dagar); patronized by Raja Man Singh Tomar of Gwalior and Akbar; greatest exponent: **Mian Tansen** (original name: Ramtanu Pandey; court of Rewa then Akbar).
21. **Khayal Pioneers:** Developed from Amir Khusrau; popularized during the 18th century under Emperor Muhammad Shah 'Rangila' by maestros **Sadarang (Niyamat Khan)** and **Adarang (Feroz Khan)**; accompanied by the Tabla.
22. **Oldest Khayal Gharana:** **Gwalior Gharana**, founded by Nathan Pir Bakhsh, Hassu Khan, and Haddu Khan; open-throated singing (*Akar*); luminaries: V.D. Paluskar and Krishnarao Shankar Pandit.
23. **Six Musician Bharat Ratnas:** M.S. Subbulakshmi (1998 — First musician honored), Pandit Ravi Shankar (1999), Ustad Bismillah Khan (2001), Lata Mangeshkar (2001), Pandit Bhimsen Joshi (2009), and Dr. Bhupen Hazarika (2019).
24. **Four Instrument Classes (Vadya):** **Tata** (Chordophones: Sitar, Sarod, Veena, Santoor), **Sushira** (Aerophones: Flute, Shehnai, Nadaswaram), **Avanaddha** (Membranophones: Tabla, Mridangam, Pakhawaj), and **Ghana** (Idiophones: Ghatam, Manjira, Kartal).
25. **The 4 Kumbh Mela Sites & Sacred Rivers:** Prayagraj (Triveni Sangam: Ganga, Yamuna, Saraswati), Haridwar (Ganga), Ujjain (Shipra), and Nashik (Godavari); occurs every 12 years; UNESCO ICH 2017.
26. **Pushkar Fair vs Pushkar Kumbh:** Pushkar Fair is the famous annual **camel and livestock fair** in Rajasthan on Kartik Purnima; Pushkar Kumbh (May 2025) is a Himalayan river fair at **Mana / Keshav Prayag in Chamoli district, Uttarakhand**.
27. **Major UP Fairs:** **Nauchandi Mela** (Meerut; Hindu-Muslim communal harmony after Holi), **Bateshwar Mela** (Agra; 101 Shiva temples & livestock fair), **Deva Sharif Mela** (Barabanki; Waris Ali Shah Urs), **Dadri Mela** (Ballia; cattle fair), and **Lathmar Holi** (Barsana and Nandgaon).
28. **Nanda Devi Raj Jat (Uttarakhand):** 280-km barefoot alpine pilgrimage organized **every 12 years** from Nauti village to Homkund at the base of Mount Trishul, led by a four-horned ram (*Chausingha Khadu*).
29. **Hornbill Festival ('Festival of Festivals'):** Held annually from **1 to 10 December** at **Kisama Heritage Village**, Kohima, **Nagaland**, bringing together all 17 major Naga tribes.
30. **Ambubachi Mela:** Held in mid-June at **Kamakhya Temple on Nilachal Hill in Guwahati, Assam**, celebrating the annual menstruation cycle and creative fertility of Mother Earth.
31. **Bastar Dussehra (Chhattisgarh):** **Longest festival in the world (75 days)**; has **nothing to do with Rama defeating Ravana**, but is dedicated strictly to the tribal goddess **Ma Danteshwari**.
32. **Medaram Jathara (Telangana):** Biennial tribal pilgrimage of the **Koya tribe** honoring mother-daughter deities Sammakka and Saralamma; **second largest human gathering in India after Kumbh**.
33. **Harvest Festivals:** **Pongal** (Tamil Nadu; 4 days in January: Bhogi, Surya, Mattu, Kaanum; Jallikattu bull-taming), **Onam** (Kerala; 10 days in Aug–Sept; King Mahabali, Pookkalam, Nehru Trophy snake boat race), **Bohag Bihu** (Assam New Year in April), **Makar Sankranti** (Pan-India sun transit into Capricorn).
34. **Shad Darshana (Six Orthodox Schools):** **Samkhya** (Sage Kapila; dualism of Purusha & Prakriti), **Yoga** (Sage Patanjali; Ashtanga Yoga), **Nyaya** (Sage Aksapada Gautama; 4 Pramanas), **Vaisheshika** (Sage Uluka Kanada; Atomic pluralism / *Paramanu-vada*), **Purva Mimamsa** (Sage Jaimini; Vedic rituals & *Apaurusheya*), and **Uttara Mimamsa / Vedanta** (Sage Badarayana; Brahma Sutras).
35. **Vedanta Sub-Schools:** Advaita (**Adi Shankaracharya**; Nirguna Brahman, world is Maya), Vishishtadvaita (**Ramanujacharya**; Saguna Narayana, world is real body of God), Dvaita (**Madhvacharya**; absolute dualism, Pancha Bheda), Shuddhadvaita (**Vallabhacharya**; Pushtimarg), and Dvaitadvaita (**Nimbarkacharya**).
36. **Four Shankaracharya Mathas:** Sringeri Sharada Peetham (South / Karnataka / Yajurveda), Govardhan Math (East / Puri / Rigveda), Dwaraka Kalika Peeth (West / Gujarat / Samaveda), and Jyotirmath (North / Badrinath / Atharvaveda).
37. **Four Buddhist Councils:** 1st at **Rajagriha** (Ajatashatru; Sutta & Vinaya Pitakas), 2nd at **Vaishali** (Kalasoka; first schism), 3rd at **Pataliputra** (Ashoka; Abhidhamma Pitaka completes Tripitaka), 4th at **Kundalvana, Kashmir** (Kanishka; permanent split into Hinayana and Mahayana).
38. **Buddhism Holy Four:** Birth at **Lumbini** (Nepal), Enlightenment at **Bodh Gaya** (Bihar), First Sermon at **Sarnath** (UP; *Dharmachakrapravartana*), and Mahaparinirvana at **Kushinagar** (UP).
39. **Jainism Core Pillars:** Triratna (Right Faith, Knowledge, Conduct), Five Great Vows (**Ahimsa, Satya, Asteya, Aparigraha, Brahmacharya** — Brahmacharya added by 24th Tirthankara Mahavira); Metaphysics: **Anekantavada** (many-sidedness of reality) and **Syadvada** (sevenfold conditional judgment); Schism: **Digambara** (Bhadrabahu, sky-clad) and **Svetambara** (Sthulabhadra, white-clad).
40. **Bhakti Bridge from South to North:** **Swami Ramananda** (born at Prayagraj, preached in Hindi at Varanasi); admitted disciples across caste lines: Kabir (weaver), Ravidas (cobbler), Sena (barber), Dhanna (peasant).
41. **Traditional Folk Theatres:** **Nautanki** (Uttar Pradesh; Hathras & Kanpur schools; Nagara drum), **Bhavai** (Gujarat; Asaita Thakar; *Bhungal* horn, *Rangla*), **Tamasha** (Maharashtra; Lavani dance & Dholki), **Jatra** (Bengal; open-air melodrama), **Yakshagana** (coastal Karnataka; ornate headgear, *Bhagavata* singer), **Ankiya Nat / Bhaona** (Assam; Sankaradeva, Brajavali language), and **Koodiyattam** (Kerala; world's oldest Sanskrit temple theatre; UNESCO ICH 2008).
42. **Four Puppetry Forms:** String Puppets (**Kathputli** of Rajasthan, **Kundhei** of Odisha, **Gombeyatta** of Karnataka), Shadow Puppets (**Tholu Bommalata** of Andhra, **Ravanachhaya** of Odisha [opaque deerskin, no joints, no color]), Rod Puppets (**Putul Nach** of Bengal, **Yampuri** of Bihar), and Glove Puppets (**Pavakoothu** of Kerala; Kathakali style).
43. **Classical Sanskrit Dramas:** Bhasa (*Svapnavasavadattam*, *Urubhanga*), Kalidasa (*Abhijnanasakuntalam*, *Malavikagnimitram*, *Vikramorvasiyam*), Shudraka (*Mrichchhakatika* / The Little Clay Cart), Vishakhadatta (*Mudrarakshasa* / political espionage thriller without female lead), Bhavabhuti (*Uttararamacharita* / masterpiece of *Karuna Rasa*), and Harshavardhana (*Ratnavali*, *Priyadarsika*, *Nagananda*).
44. **Sanskrit Kavyas — Brihattrayi vs Laghuttrayi:** 
   - **Brihattrayi (The Great Trio):** *Kiratarjuniya* by Bharavi, *Shishupalavadha* by Magha, and *Naishadhacharita* by Sri Harsha.
   - **Laghuttrayi (The Lesser Trio):** *Raghuvamsha*, *Kumarasambhava*, and *Meghaduta* — all by Kalidasa.
45. **Sangam Tamil Epics:** *Silappadikaram* (The Tale of an Anklet by Ilango Adigal; Kovalan, Kannagi, and Madhavi; Pattini cult) and *Manimekalai* (Buddhist sequel by Sittalai Sattanar; daughter Manimekalai with the *Amrita Surabhi* bowl).
46. **Traditional Martial Arts of India:**
   - **Kalaripayattu** (Kerala — 'Mother of all martial arts'; practiced in subterranean arena called *Kalari*; uses *Urumi* flexible sword).
   - **Silambam** (Tamil Nadu — ancient bamboo staff martial art).
   - **Thang-Ta / Huyen Langlon** (Manipur — armed martial art using *Thang* / sword and *Ta* / spear).
   - **Gatka** (Punjab — Sikh martial art using wooden sticks and leather shields, performed during Vaisakhi and Hola Mohalla).
   - **Mardani Khel** (Maharashtra — traditional Maratha martial art using sword, shield, and *Pata* / gauntlet sword).
   - **Pari-Khanda** (Bihar & Jharkhand — Rajput martial art using *Pari* / shield and *Khanda* / sword; stepwork foundation of Chhau dance).
   - **Musti Yuddha** (Varanasi, UP — ancient unarmed boxing art).
   - **Lathi Khela** (Bengal & Bihar — wooden staff combat).
   - **Kuttu Varisai** (Tamil Nadu — unarmed hand-to-hand combat).
   - **Inbuan** (Mizoram — traditional wrestling).
47. **Ancient Universities:** **Takshashila** (Gandhara/Pakistan; Chanakya, Panini, Jivaka; decentralized), **Nalanda** (Bihar; Kumaragupta I in 5th c.; Mahayana Buddhism; libraries: Ratnasagara, Ratnodadhi, Ratnaranjaka; destroyed by Bakhtiyar Khalji ~1193), **Vikramashila** (Bihar; Dharmapala in 8th c.; Vajrayana Buddhism; Atisa Dipankara), **Valabhi** (Gujarat; Maitrakas; Hinayana Buddhism and administration), and **Kanchi** (Tamil Nadu; Pallava Ghatika).
48. **Colonial Institutions:** Asiatic Society of Bengal (1784; Sir William Jones; Warren Hastings declined presidency), Calcutta Madrasa (1780; Warren Hastings), Sanskrit College Banaras (1791; Jonathan Duncan), Fort William College (1800; Lord Wellesley; John Gilchrist), Indian Museum Kolkata (1814; Dr. Nathaniel Wallich; oldest in India), and Archaeological Survey of India (1861; Alexander Cunningham).
49. **Pioneers of Indian Archaeology:** Robert Bruce Foote (**'Father of Indian Prehistory'**; discovered first stone hand-axe at Pallavaram on 30 May 1863), Sir Alexander Cunningham (**'Father of Indian Archaeology'**; 1st DG of ASI 1861), Sir John Marshall (announced Indus Civilization in 1924), Sir Mortimer Wheeler (introduced stratigraphic grid excavation in 1944), and Dr. V.S. Wakankar (discovered Bhimbetka caves in 1957).
50. **Key Archaeological Sites of Uttar Pradesh:**
   - **Koldihwa & Lahuradewa:** Earliest cultivated rice in South Asia (~6500–8000 BCE).
   - **Chopani Mando:** Earliest evidence of handmade pottery.
   - **Sarai Nahar Rai, Mahadaha, Damdama:** Mesolithic burials, bone ornaments, double burial.
   - **Atranjikhera:** Earliest stratified iron smelting in Ganga Valley (~1000 BCE).
   - **Hastinapur:** B.B. Lal’s Mahabharata PGW excavations; flood layer.
   - **Piprahwa:** Shakya relic casket inscribed to Buddha; identified with Kapilavastu.
   - **Sinauli:** Bronze Age war chariots, copper swords, and royal burials.
   - **Alamgirpur:** Easternmost Harappan boundary site on the Hindon River.
