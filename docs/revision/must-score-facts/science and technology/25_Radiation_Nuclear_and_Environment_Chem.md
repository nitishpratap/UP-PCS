## 🎯 Revision Practice MCQs (Mastery Drill)

> **Mastery Rule:** Read the consolidated facts above, attempt these high-yield questions, and score &ge;80% to conquer this chapter.

**Q1.** Radioactivity** is the spontaneous, uncontrolled nuclear disintegration of unstable heavy atomic nuclei accompanied by the emission of ionizing particles or electromagnetic radiation.
2. Radioactivity was discovered in **1896 by French physicist Henri Becquerel** while investigating phosphorescence in uranium potassium sulphate crystals wrapped in black paper.
3. In 1898, **Marie Curie and Pierre Curie** isolated two new radioactive elements from pitchblende ore ($U_3O_8$): **Polonium ($Po$, named after Poland)** and **Radium ($Ra$)**. Marie Curie is the only scientist to win Nobel Prizes in two distinct sciences (Physics 1903, Chemistry 1911).
4. **Nuclear Property**: Radioactivity is governed exclusively by nuclear stability (neutron-to-proton ratio, $N/Z$); it is completely **independent of temperature, pressure, chemical combination, or physical state**.
5. **Alpha ($\alpha$) Particles**: Helium nuclei carrying a double positive charge (**$^4_2He^{2+}$**). Possess the highest mass ($4\text{ u}$), **highest ionizing power ($\approx 10,000\times \gamma$)**, but the **lowest penetrating power**; completely stopped by a single sheet of paper or the dead outer keratin layer of human skin.
6. **Beta ($\beta^-$) Particles**: Fast-moving electrons (**$^0_{-1}e$**) originating from the nucleus when a neutron decays into a proton, electron, and antineutrino ($n \to p + e^- + \bar{\nu}$). Moderate ionizing power and moderate penetrating power; stopped by a few millimeters of aluminium foil.
7. **Gamma ($\gamma$) Rays**: High-energy, short-wavelength electromagnetic photons emitted by excited nuclei transitioning to the ground state. Carry zero charge and zero rest mass; travel at the speed of light ($3 \times 10^8\text{ m/s}$). Possess the **lowest ionizing power**, but the **highest penetrating power** among nuclear emissions; require thick lead blocks or dense reinforced concrete barriers for shielding.
8. **Soddy-Fajans Group Displacement Law**:
   - Emission of an **$\alpha$-particle** reduces the atomic number ($Z$) by 2 units and the mass number ($A$) by 4 units:
     $$^A_Z X \xrightarrow{\alpha} {}^{A-4}_{Z-2} Y + {}^4_2He$$
     (The daughter element shifts 2 groups to the left in the Periodic Table).
   - Emission of a **$\beta^-$-particle** increases the atomic number ($Z$) by 1 unit while leaving the mass number ($A$) unchanged:
     $$^A_Z X \xrightarrow{\beta^-} {}^A_{Z+1} Y + {}^0_{-1}e$$
     (The daughter element shifts 1 group to the right; forms an **isobar** of the parent).
   - Emission of a **$\gamma$-ray** causes no change in atomic number or mass number, merely dissipating excess nuclear excitation energy.
9. **Radioactive Decay Kinetics & Half-Life ($t_{1/2}$)**:
   - Radioactive decay strictly follows **first-order reaction kinetics**:
     $$-\frac{dN}{dt} = \lambda N \implies N(t) = N_0 e^{-\lambda t}$$
   - The Half-Life ($t_{1/2}$) is the time required for half the radioactive nuclei in a sample to disintegrate:
     $$t_{1/2} = \frac{\ln 2}{\lambda} = \frac{0.693}{\lambda}$$
   - After $n$ half-lives, the remaining radioactive fraction is: $N = N_0 \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^n$.
10. **Units of Radioactivity**:
    - **Becquerel (Bq)**: SI unit; $1\text{ Bq} = 1\text{ nuclear disintegration per second}$.
    - **Curie (Ci)**: Traditional historical unit; $1\text{ Ci} = 3.7 \times 10^{10}\text{ disintegrations/sec}$ (the activity of $1\text{ g}$ of pure Radium-226).
    - **Rutherford (Rd)**: $1\text{ Rd} = 10^6\text{ disintegrations/sec}$.
11. **Radiometric Dating Techniques**:
    - **Radiocarbon Dating ($^{14}C$)**: Devised by Willard Libby (1949, Nobel Prize 1960). Based on the decay of cosmogenic $^{14}C$ ($t_{1/2} = 5,730\text{ years}$) into $^{14}N$. Used for dating once-living biological archaeological artifacts (wood, bone, charcoal, fossils) up to $\approx 50,000\text{ years}$ old.
    - **Uranium-Lead Dating ($^{238}U \to {}^{206}Pb$)**: Half-life $\approx 4.5\text{ billion years}$; used to calculate the geological age of the Earth ($\approx 4.54\text{ billion years}$), ancient continental cratons, and lunar meteorites.
    - **Potassium-Argon Dating ($^{40}K \to {}^{40}Ar$)**: Half-life $\approx 1.25\text{ billion years}$; used to date ancient volcanic igneous rocks and early hominid fossil strata.
12. **Nuclear Fission**:
    - Discovered in 1938 by German chemists **Otto Hahn and Fritz Strassmann**; theoretical mechanism explained by Lise Meitner and Otto Frisch.
    - The splitting of a heavy, fissile atomic nucleus (such as **Uranium-235** or **Plutonium-239**) upon absorbing a slow-moving **thermal neutron ($0.025\text{ eV}$)** into two medium-sized daughter nuclei, releasing $2\text{ to }3$ fast neutrons and an enormous quantity of energy ($\approx 200\text{ MeV}$ per fission event):
      $$^{235}_{92}U + {}^1_0n \to {}^{236}_{92}U^* \to {}^{141}_{56}Ba + {}^{92}_{36}Kr + 3{}^1_0n + 200\text{ MeV}$$
    - The liberated energy arises from the conversion of missing mass into energy according to Einstein's mass-energy equivalence equation ($E = \Delta m \cdot c^2$).
13. **Atomic Bomb (Fission Weapon)**:
    - Operates on an **uncontrolled, supercritical nuclear chain reaction**.
    - If the mass of fissile material exceeds the **Critical Mass** ($k > 1$), neutron multiplication is exponential ($1 \to 3 \to 9 \to 27 \dots$), causing a catastrophic thermonuclear explosion within microseconds.
    - World's first nuclear detonations: "Little Boy" (Uranium-235, Hiroshima, August 6, 1945) and "Fat Man" (Plutonium-239, Nagasaki, August 9, 1945).
14. **Nuclear Power Reactor (Controlled Fission)**:
    - Maintains a steady critical chain reaction ($k = 1.0$) to generate commercial steam for electricity.
    - **Moderator**: Material with light atomic mass used to slow down energetic fast neutrons ($2\text{ MeV}$) to thermal energy ($0.025\text{ eV}$) via elastic collisions without absorbing them. Standard moderators: **Heavy Water ($D_2O$, deuterium oxide)** and high-purity **Graphite**.
    - **Control Rods**: Neutron-absorbing materials inserted into the core to regulate reaction rate or shut down the reactor: **Cadmium ($Cd$)** and **Boron ($B$)** (have exceptionally high neutron capture cross-sections).
    - **Coolant**: Fluid circulated through the core to extract fission heat: **Light water ($H_2O$)**, Heavy water ($D_2O$), or liquid metallic Sodium ($Na$).
15. **Nuclear Fusion**:
    - The process where two light atomic nuclei combine to form a heavier, more stable nucleus at extremely high kinetic energies, liberating vast energy per unit mass:
      $$^2_1H\text{ (Deuterium)} + {}^3_1H\text{ (Tritium)} \xrightarrow{\text{extreme } T, P} {}^4_2He + {}^1_0n + 17.6\text{ MeV}$$
    - Nuclear fusion powers the **Sun and all celestial stars** (via the proton-proton chain and CNO cycle).
    - Fusion requires overcoming immense electrostatic Coulomb repulsion between positively charged nuclei, requiring **temperatures exceeding $10^7\text{ K}$ (100 million $^\circ\text{C}$)** and massive pressures to form a thermonuclear plasma.
16. **Hydrogen Bomb (Thermonuclear Bomb)**:
    - Operates primarily on **uncontrolled Nuclear Fusion**.
    - Conceived by American physicists **Edward Teller and Stanislaw Ulam** (first detonated in 1952, "Ivy Mike").
    - *Must-Score Fact*: Because thermonuclear fusion requires stellar ignition temperatures, **a hydrogen bomb incorporates an internal atomic (fission) bomb as its primary trigger**. The fission detonation generates the extreme heat ($>10^7\text{ K}$) and radiation pressure needed to ignite the secondary fusion fuel (lithium deuteride).
    - Yields are measured in **Megatons of TNT** (thousands of times more destructive than atomic fission bombs) and produce less long-lived direct radioactive fission fallout per megaton.
17. **Dynamite**:
    - Invented and patented in **1867 by Swedish chemist Alfred Nobel**.
    - Composed of liquid **Nitroglycerin (Glyceryl Trinitrate, GTN)** absorbed into an inert, porous siliceous earth called **Kieselguhr (diatomaceous earth)** or wood pulp, mixed with sodium nitrate.
    - Nitroglycerin is shock-sensitive; absorbing it into Kieselguhr created the first safe, transportable, shock-stable high explosive, revolutionizing civil engineering (tunnels, canals, railways) and establishing the fortune that funds the **Nobel Prizes**.
18. **TNT (Trinitrotoluene)**:
    - Chemically **2,4,6-Trinitrotoluene ($C_7H_5N_3O_6$)**; formed by the stepwise nitration of toluene with mixed acid ($HNO_3 + H_2SO_4$).
    - A yellow crystalline military high explosive; insensitive to friction and mechanical shock; can be safely melted at $80^\circ\text{C}$ and poured directly into artillery shells.
19. **RDX (Research Department Explosive / Cyclonite / Hexogen / T4)**:
    - Chemically **Cyclotrimethylenetrinitramine ($C_3H_6N_6O_6$)**; white crystalline solid.
    - High velocity of detonation ($\approx 8750\text{ m/s}$) and brisance. When blended with motor oil and plasticizers (polyisobutylene), it forms malleable **C-4 Plastic Explosive**, favored in military demolition and counter-terrorism scenarios.
    - Known as **Hexogen** in Germany, **T4** in Italy, and **RDX** in the USA and Commonwealth nations.
20. **PETN (Pentaerythritol Tetranitrate)**:
    - One of the most powerful known secondary high explosives; highly shock-sensitive; used in military detonating cords (Primacord) and booster charges.
21. **ANFO (Ammonium Nitrate Fuel Oil)**:
    - Industrial blasting explosive consisting of **$94\%\text{ porous prilled Ammonium Nitrate } (NH_4NO_3)$** mixed with **$6\%\text{ No. 2 Fuel Oil}$**.
    - Low sensitivity; requires a high-explosive booster charge (TNT or dynamite) to detonate; widely used in commercial open-cast coal mining and limestone quarrying.
22. **Gunpowder (Black Powder)**:
    - The oldest known chemical explosive, invented in 9th-century China.
    - A mechanical mixture of: **Potassium Nitrate (Saltpetre, $KNO_3, \approx 75\%$)**, **Charcoal (Carbon fuel, $\approx 15\%$)**, and **Sulphur ($\approx 10\%$)**. Burns rapidly via deflagration to generate high volumes of hot $N_2, CO_2,$ and potassium salts.
23. **Picric Acid (2,4,6-Trinitrophenol)**: A strong, yellow crystalline organic acid and explosive; historically used under the names **Lyddite** and **Melinite** as an artillery shell filling in the late 19th and early 20th centuries.
24. **Acid Rain (Acid Precipitation)**:
    - Defined as any precipitation (rain, snow, fog) having a **$\text{pH} < 5.6$**.
    - *Natural Background Rain*: Normal unpolluted rainwater is naturally slightly acidic ($\text{pH} \approx 5.6$) due to atmospheric $CO_2$ dissolving to form weak carbonic acid:
      $$CO_2 + H_2O \rightleftharpoons H_2CO_3 \rightleftharpoons H^+ + HCO_3^-$$
    - *Anthropogenic Acid Rain*: Caused primarily by atmospheric emissions of **Sulphur Dioxide ($SO_2$)** from coal-fired thermal power plants and petroleum refineries, and **Nitrogen Oxides ($NO_x$)** from high-temperature vehicular exhaust.
25. **Acid Rain Reactions**:
    - Sulphuric acid formation accounts for **$\approx 65–70\%$ of total acidity**:
      $$2SO_2 + O_2 + 2H_2O \xrightarrow{\text{catalytic dust}} 2H_2SO_4$$
    - Nitric acid formation accounts for **$\approx 30–35\%$ of total acidity**:
      $$4NO_2 + O_2 + 2H_2O \to 4HNO_3$$
26. **Marble Cancer (Stone Leprosy)**:
    - Sulphuric acid in acid rain attacks calcium carbonate ($CaCO_3$) in historical white marble monuments (notably the **Taj Mahal in Agra**, threatened by emissions from the nearby Mathura Oil Refinery and Firozabad glass foundries):
      $$CaCO_3\text{ (white marble)} + H_2SO_4 \to CaSO_4 \cdot 2H_2O\text{ (Gypsum)} + CO_2 \uparrow$$
    - The formation of soluble, crumbly gypsum causes the marble surface to blister, turn yellowish, flake away, and suffer irreversible pitting corrosion.
27. **Ecological Damage of Acid Rain**:
    - Drops lake and river water pH below $5.0$, triggering the leaching of toxic free **Aluminium ($Al^{3+}$) ions** from surrounding soils into waterways, precipitating mucus on fish gills and causing asphyxiation.
    - Strips essential magnesium and calcium nutrients from forest soils, causing dieback in coniferous forests.
28. **Classical Smog (London-type Smog)**:
    - First documented extensively during the catastrophic London Great Smog of December 1952.
    - Occurs in **cool, humid winter climates**.
    - Chemically, it is a **Reducing Smog** consisting of a dense mixture of coal smoke, particulate soot, atmospheric fog, and high concentrations of **Sulphur Dioxide ($SO_2$)**.
29. **Photochemical Smog (Los Angeles-type Smog)**:
    - Occurs in **warm, dry, sunny climates** with heavy automotive vehicular traffic.
    - Chemically, it is an **Oxidizing Smog** formed by complex solar ultraviolet photolytic reactions between **Nitrogen Oxides ($NO_x$)** and **Volatile Organic Compounds (Hydrocarbons)**.
    - **Key Ingredients & Byproducts**:
      - **Ozone ($O_3$)**: Formed at ground level via photolysis of $NO_2$:
        $$NO_2 \xrightarrow{h\nu} NO + O^\bullet, \quad O^\bullet + O_2 \to O_3$$
      - **PAN (Peroxyacetyl Nitrate, $CH_3COOONO_2$)**: Potent eye and lung irritant, causing severe tearing (lachrymation) and plant leaf bronzing.
      - **Acrolein ($CH_2=CH-CHO$) and Formaldehyde ($HCHO$)**.
30. **Stratospheric Ozone Layer**:
    - Concentrated in the lower stratosphere between **$15\text{ and }35\text{ kilometers}$** altitude ("Ozono-sphere").
    - Formed and maintained naturally via the dynamic **Chapman Cycle**:
      $$O_2 \xrightarrow{\text{solar UV-C } (<240\text{ nm})} O + O$$
      $$O + O_2 + M \to O_3 + M$$
      $$O_3 \xrightarrow{\text{UV-B } (240–310\text{ nm})} O_2 + O$$
    - Shields terrestrial life by absorbing $>99\%$ of solar UV-C and biological harmful UV-B radiation ($280–315\text{ nm}$), which causes human skin melanoma, cataract blindness, and immune suppression.
31. **Dobson Unit (DU)**:
    - The standard physical unit used to measure total column atmospheric ozone thickness.
    - $1\text{ DU}$ is equivalent to a pure ozone layer thickness of **$0.01\text{ mm}$ ($10\text{ }\mu\text{m}$)** at standard temperature and pressure ($0^\circ\text{C}, 1\text{ atm}$).
    - Normal atmospheric ozone levels range from **$300\text{ to }350\text{ DU}$**. An **"Ozone Hole"** is technically defined when total column ozone falls below **$220\text{ Dobson Units}$**.
32. **Catalytic Ozone Depletion by Chlorofluorocarbons (CFCs)**:
    - Discovered in 1974 by **Mario Molina and F. Sherwood Rowland** (Nobel Prize in Chemistry, 1995).
    - CFCs (Freons, such as $CF_2Cl_2$ / CFC-12) are non-flammable, non-toxic refrigerants and aerosol propellants that do not break down in the troposphere.
    - Upon diffusing into the stratosphere, high-energy UV radiation photolytically cleaves $C-Cl$ bonds, releasing reactive **free chlorine radicals ($Cl^\bullet$)**:
      $$CF_2Cl_2 \xrightarrow{h\nu} CF_2Cl^\bullet + Cl^\bullet$$
      $$Cl^\bullet + O_3 \to ClO^\bullet + O_2$$
      $$ClO^\bullet + O \to Cl^\bullet + O_2$$
    - *Catalytic Cycle*: The chlorine radical is continually regenerated; **a single chlorine atom can catalytically destroy over $100,000\text{ ozone molecules}$** before being removed by reservoir species ($HCl, ClONO_2$).
33. **The Antarctic Ozone Hole**:
    - Discovered in 1985 by British Antarctic Survey scientists (Joe Farman, Brian Gardiner, and Jonathan Shanklin) over Halley Bay.
    - Develops intensely each austral spring (September–October) due to unique meteorological conditions: the **Polar Vortex** isolates Antarctic air, chilling temperatures below $-80^\circ\text{C}$ and forming **Polar Stratospheric Clouds (PSCs / nacreous clouds)** of nitric acid trihydrate. PSC surfaces catalyze the conversion of inactive chlorine reservoirs ($HCl, ClONO_2$) into active molecular chlorine ($Cl_2$), which is photolyzed by returning spring sunlight into massive bursts of destructive $Cl^\bullet$ radicals.
34. **Montreal Protocol**:
    - Landmark international environmental treaty adopted on **September 16, 1987** (celebrated annually as **World Ozone Day**); successfully phased out the global production and consumption of ozone-depleting substances (CFCs, halons, carbon tetrachloride).
    - **Kigali Amendment (2016)**: Mandates the phased reduction of Hydrofluorocarbons (HFCs), which do not deplete ozone but are potent greenhouse gases.
35. **Greenhouse Effect**:
    - The natural thermodynamic phenomenon discovered by Joseph Fourier (1824) and quantified by Svante Arrhenius (1896).
    - Earth absorbs short-wavelength solar radiation (visible and UV) and re-radiates it toward space as **long-wavelength infrared (thermal IR) radiation ($\lambda \approx 4–50\text{ }\mu\text{m}$)**.
    - Polyatomic greenhouse gas molecules absorb outgoing thermal IR photons via molecular bond stretching and bending vibrations, trapping heat within the lower troposphere and raising Earth's mean surface temperature from $-18^\circ\text{C}$ (inhospitable ice world) to a habitable **$+15^\circ\text{C}$**.
36. **Master Greenhouse Gases (GHGs)**:
    - **Water Vapor ($H_2O$)**: The largest natural contributor to the natural greenhouse effect ($\approx 60\%$).
    - **Carbon Dioxide ($CO_2$)**: Responsible for $\approx 26\%$ of overall warming; baseline reference gas ($\text{GWP} = 1.0$).
    - **Methane ($CH_4$)**: Responsible for $\approx 9\%$; $\text{GWP}_{100} \approx 28–36$.
    - **Nitrous Oxide ($N_2O$)**: $\text{GWP}_{100} \approx 265–298$; emitted from nitrogenous fertilizers.
    - **Fluorinated Gases**: Hydrofluorocarbons (HFCs), Perfluorocarbons (PFCs), and **Sulphur Hexafluoride ($SF_6$, highest GWP $\approx 23,500$)**.
37. **Greenhouse Non-Absorbers (State PCS Distractor Trap)**:
    - **Argon ($Ar$)**, **Nitrogen ($N_2$)**, and **Oxygen ($O_2$)** are **NOT greenhouse gases**!
    - Homonuclear diatomic molecules ($N_2, O_2$) and noble gases ($Ar, He$) possess no permanent dipole moment, and symmetrical vibrations produce zero dynamic dipole change; therefore, they are transparent to infrared radiation and cannot absorb thermal IR.
38. **Minamata Disease (Methylmercury Toxicity)**:
    - Severe neurological poisoning caused by chronic ingestion of fish contaminated with **Methylmercury ($CH_3Hg^+$)**, first identified in 1956 around Minamata Bay, Kumamoto Prefecture, Japan.
    - Industrial effluent from the Chisso chemical factory discharged inorganic mercury into the bay, where anaerobic sulfate-reducing bacteria methylated it into lipid-soluble methylmercury.
    - Biomagnified through marine food webs into human consumers, crossing the blood-brain barrier to cause ataxia, peripheral numbness, tunnel vision, muscle weakness, and congenital neurological deformities.
39. **Itai-Itai ("Ouch-Ouch") Disease (Cadmium Toxicity)**:
    - Mass chronic **Cadmium ($Cd$) poisoning** documented along the Jinzū River basin in Toyama Prefecture, Japan, in 1912.
    - Upstream mining runoff contaminated river water used to irrigate downstream paddy fields.
    - Cadmium replaced calcium in the human skeletal matrix, causing severe **osteomalacia**, bone softening, multiple pathological fractures, and severe renal tubular failure accompanied by excruciating skeletal pain ("itai-itai").
40. **Blackfoot Disease (Chronic Arsenic Toxicity)**:
    - Caused by prolonged consumption of drinking water contaminated with high levels of inorganic **Arsenic ($As > 0.05\text{ mg/L}$)**, endemic in the Bengal delta basin (West Bengal and Bangladesh).
    - Arsenic binds to cellular sulfhydryl groups, disrupting cellular respiration and inducing peripheral vascular gangrene (causing blackened, necrotic feet that require amputation), hyperkeratosis of palms/soles, and skin cancers.
41. **Blue Baby Syndrome (Methemoglobinemia / Nitrate Poisoning)**:
    - Occurs in human infants under 6 months of age who consume infant formula prepared with well water containing excess **Nitrate ($NO_3^- > 45\text{ mg/L}$)** from agricultural fertilizer runoff.
    - Intestinal bacteria reduce nitrate to nitrite ($NO_2^-$), which oxidizes the ferrous iron ($Fe^{2+}$) in normal hemoglobin to ferric iron ($Fe^{3+}$), generating **Methemoglobin**.
    - Methemoglobin cannot bind oxygen, causing systemic tissue hypoxia and characteristic infant cyanosis (turning skin a bluish-slate color), which can be fatal without methylene blue therapy.
42. **Skeletal & Dental Fluorosis**:
    - Caused by excessive fluoride concentration in drinking water (**$>1.5\text{ mg/L}$ or ppm**); widely prevalent across Rajasthan, Gujarat, and Uttar Pradesh.
    - At optimal concentrations ($0.7–1.0\text{ ppm}$), fluoride converts dental hydroxyapatite to acid-resistant fluorapatite ($Ca_5(PO_4)_3F$), preventing cavities.
    - Above $1.5\text{ ppm}$, it causes **Dental Fluorosis** (permanent brownish-yellow mottling of tooth enamel).
    - Above $4.0\text{ ppm}$, it deposits in bones, causing **Skeletal Fluorosis** (calcification of spinal ligaments, severe joint stiffness, and debilitating "knock-knee" deformity / genu valgum).
43. **Lead ($Pb$) Toxicity (Plumbism / Saturnism)**:

44. **Biochemical Oxygen Demand (BOD)**:

    - Pure drinking water has a $\text{BOD} < 1\text{ ppm}$; highly polluted domestic sewage has a $\text{BOD} > 100–400\text{ ppm}$. High BOD indicates severe organic pollution and depletion of dissolved oxygen ($DO$), causing fish kills.
45. **Chemical Oxygen Demand (COD)**:

For any polluted wastewater, **$\text{COD}$ is always greater than $\text{BOD}$** ($\text{COD} > \text{BOD}$).

## Detailed Theory Notes

### 8.1 Radioactivity Kinetics & Radiometric Dating Principles

#### 1. Fundamental Decay Physics
- Unstable nuclei with atomic numbers $Z > 82$ (beyond Lead) possess excess positive charges causing electrostatic repulsion that overcomes the attractive strong nuclear force.
- The decay rate is purely statistical:
  $$\frac{dN}{dt} = -\lambda N$$
  Integration yields the exponential law: $N(t) = N_0 e^{-\lambda t}$.
- **Activity ($A$)**: The rate of nuclear disintegrations per unit time ($A = \lambda N$). Measured in Becquerels ($\text{Bq}$).

#### 2. Radiocarbon Dating ($^{14}C$)
- Natural nitrogen in the upper atmosphere is bombarded by cosmic-ray neutrons:
  $$^{14}_7N + {}^1_0n \to {}^{14}_6C + {}^1_1p$$
- $^{14}C$ rapidly oxidizes into $^{14}CO_2$, which enters the terrestrial carbon cycle via plant photosynthesis and food chains.
- While an organism is alive, continuous metabolic exchange maintains a constant equilibrium ratio of $^{14}C / {}^{12}C \approx 1 : 10^{12}$.
- Upon death, metabolic assimilation ceases. The radioactive $^{14}C$ within tissues disintegrates with a half-life of **$5,730\text{ years}$**:
  $${}^{14}_6C \xrightarrow{\beta^-} {}^{14}_7N + {}^0_{-1}e$$
- Measuring the remaining $^{14}C / {}^{12}C$ ratio via accelerator mass spectrometry enables precise dating of organic relics up to $\approx 50,000\text{ years}$.

---

### 8.2 Nuclear Weapons & Reactor Core Physics

#### 1. Fission vs. Fusion Energy Dynamics
- **Nuclear Fission**: Heavy nuclei ($A \approx 235$) have a binding energy per nucleon of $\approx 7.6\text{ MeV}$. Splitting into fragments ($A \approx 120$) with higher binding energy ($\approx 8.5\text{ MeV}$) releases $\approx 0.9\text{ MeV}$ per nucleon, or $\approx 200\text{ MeV}$ per fission event.
- **Nuclear Fusion**: Light nuclei ($^2H, ^3H$) with low binding energy ($\approx 1.1\text{ to }2.8\text{ MeV}$) fuse into tightly bound $^4He$ ($\approx 7.07\text{ MeV/nucleon}$), releasing $\approx 17.6\text{ MeV}$ for only 5 nucleons, yielding $\approx 3.5\text{ MeV}$ per nucleon. Thus, **gravimetrically, nuclear fusion yields roughly four times more energy per kilogram of fuel than nuclear fission**.

```
[ Nuclear Fission ]                           [ Nuclear Fusion ]
Splits heavy nuclei (U-235, Pu-239)           Fuses light nuclei (Deuterium + Tritium)
Triggered by slow thermal neutrons             Triggered by extreme heat (>10⁷ K) & pressure
Used in Nuclear Reactors & Atomic Bombs        Powers the Sun & Hydrogen Bombs
High radioactive fission waste products        Minimal direct radioactive waste products
```

---

### 8.3 Atmospheric Chemistry: Acid Rain & Ozone Depletion

#### 1. Acid Rain Dynamics
- High-temperature combustion in automobile engines oxidizes atmospheric nitrogen to nitric oxide:
  $$N_2 + O_2 \xrightarrow{>1500^\circ\text{C}} 2NO, \quad 2NO + O_2 \to 2NO_2$$
- Coal combustion oxidizes pyrite and organic sulphur impurities:
  $$S + O_2 \to SO_2$$
- In the presence of suspended particulate matter (soot, metal dust) and sunlight, $SO_2$ and $NO_2$ undergo aqueous-phase catalytic oxidation in cloud droplets into $H_2SO_4$ and $HNO_3$.
- Marble stone corrosion (Stone Leprosy):
  $$CaCO_3\text{ (Insoluble marble)} + H_2SO_4 \to CaSO_4 \cdot 2H_2O\text{ (Soluble gypsum)} + CO_2 \uparrow$$

#### 2. Stratospheric Ozone Destruction by CFCs
- The stability of CFCs enables them to survive intact in the troposphere for 50–100 years.
- In the stratosphere, solar UV radiation photolyzes CFCs to release atomic chlorine:
  $$\text{Initiation: } CF_2Cl_2 \xrightarrow{h\nu\ (\lambda < 220\text{ nm})} CF_2Cl^\bullet + Cl^\bullet$$
  $$\text{Propagation 1: } Cl^\bullet + O_3 \to ClO^\bullet + O_2$$
  $$\text{Propagation 2: } ClO^\bullet + O^\bullet \to Cl^\bullet + O_2$$
  $$\text{Net Reaction: } O_3 + O^\bullet \to 2O_2$$
- The $Cl^\bullet$ radical acts as a true homogeneous catalyst: it accelerates the destruction of ozone without being consumed in the net reaction.
