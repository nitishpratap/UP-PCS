## 🎯 Consolidated Must-Score Facts

1. The word **'Science'** originates from the Latin word *scientia*, meaning **'knowledge'**; science is the systematic empirical knowledge of the physical world gained through observation and experimentation.
2. The term **'Biology'** was coined independently by French biologist **Jean-Baptiste Lamarck** and German naturalist **Gottfried Reinhold Treviranus** in **1802**; **Aristotle** is the Father of Biology and Zoology; **Theophrastus** is the Father of Botany (*Historia Plantarum*); **Hippocrates** is the Father of Medicine.
3. Defining properties of life are **Cellular organisation**, **Metabolism** (sum of all chemical reactions), and **Consciousness** (response to stimuli); growth and reproduction are **not** absolute defining properties (mules, worker bees, infertile human couples do not reproduce).
4. **Key Specialized Biological Branches**: **Morphology** (external forms), **Anatomy** (internal structure), **Histology** (tissues), **Cytology** (cells), **Embryology** (gametogenesis and embryo development), **Gerontology** (study of aging), **Pedology** (soil), **Paleontology** (fossils), **Mycology** (fungi), and **Phycology** (algae).
5. **Key Agricultural & Applied Culture Terminologies**: **Sericulture** (silkworm rearing), **Apiculture** (honeybee keeping), **Pisciculture** (fish farming), **Viticulture** (grape cultivation), **Vermiculture** (earthworm rearing), **Moriculture** (mulberry cultivation for silkworms), **Olericulture** (vegetable farming), **Arboriculture** (trees and shrubs), **Silviculture** (forest timber trees), and **Floriculture** (flower farming).
6. **Origin of Life (Oparin-Haldane Hypothesis, 1924–1929)**: Life originated abiogenetically through gradual chemical evolution (prebiotic synthesis) of organic compounds in a primordial reducing atmosphere; primitive ocean acted as Haldane's **'hot dilute soup'** (*prebiotic soup*).
7. **The Primitive Earth Atmosphere**: Strongly **reducing** in nature with **no free molecular oxygen ($O_2$)**; dominated by Methane ($CH_4$), Ammonia ($NH_3$), Hydrogen ($H_2$), and Water vapour ($H_2O$), driven by intense solar UV radiation and lightning.
8. **The Miller-Urey Experiment (1953)**: Harold Urey and Stanley Miller simulated primitive Earth reducing conditions by passing electric sparks (75,000 V) through $CH_4, NH_3, H_2,$ and $H_2O$ vapour (ratio $2:1:2$) for a week, synthesizing amino acids (**glycine, alanine, aspartic acid**).
9. **Geological Chronology of Cenozoic Epochs (Oldest to Most Recent)**: **Paleocene → Eocene → Oligocene → Miocene → Pliocene → Pleistocene → Holocene** (Recent Epoch); human civilization flourishes in the Holocene.
10. **Lamarckism (Theory of Inheritance of Acquired Characteristics)**: Proposed by J. B. Lamarck (*Philosophie Zoologique*, 1809); based on use and disuse of organs (e.g. elongation of giraffe's neck) and inheritance of acquired traits; disproved by August Weismann's Germplasm Theory (mutilated mouse tails for 22 generations).
11. **Darwinism (Theory of Natural Selection)**: Charles Darwin (*On the Origin of Species*, 1859, based on HMS Beagle voyage); key tenets: overproduction (geometric progression), struggle for existence, variation, and **survival of the fittest** (term coined by Herbert Spencer).
12. **Neo-Darwinism & Modern Synthetic Theory**: Synthesized by Dobzhansky, Ernst Mayr, Julian Huxley, Simpson, and Stebbins; integrates Darwin's natural selection with Mendelian genetics, gene mutations, genetic recombination, gene flow, and reproductive isolation.
13. **Hugo de Vries' Mutation Theory (1901)**: Proposed based on breeding experiments on Evening Primrose (*Oenothera lamarckiana*); stated that evolution occurs through large, sudden, discontinuous, single-step saltatory mutations (**Saltation** = single-step large mutation), unlike Darwin's gradual minor continuous variations.
14. **Landmark Evolutionary Fossils & Missing Links**:
    - ***Archaeopteryx lithographica***: Missing link between **Reptiles and Birds** (Upper Jurassic Solnhofen limestone; reptilian teeth, long bony tail, clawed fingers + avian feathers, wings, and wishbone).
    - ***Seymouria***: Connecting link between **Amphibians and Reptiles**.
    - ***Ichthyostega***: Connecting link between **Fishes and Amphibians**.
    - ***Echidna*** (spiny anteater) & ***Ornithorhynchus*** (duck-billed platypus): Connecting links between **Reptiles and Mammals** (egg-laying monotremes).
15. **Augustin Pyramus de Candolle (A. P. de Candolle)**: Swiss botanist who **first coined the term 'Taxonomy'** (1813) and was the **first to use vascular tissue characteristics** to classify plants into *Vasculares* (with vascular bundles) and *Cellulares* (without vascular bundles).
16. **Carolus Linnaeus** (*Systema Naturae*, 1758) established **Binomial Nomenclature** and is the Father of Modern Taxonomy; scientific names consist of **Genus** (capitalised) and **species epithet** (lowercase), printed in *italics*.
17. The taxonomic hierarchy in ascending order is: **Species → Genus → Family → Order → Class → Phylum / Division → Kingdom**; the basic and real unit of biological classification is **Species** (group of individuals with fundamental similarities capable of interbreeding).
18. **R. H. Whittaker (1969)** proposed the **Five-Kingdom System**: **Monera** (prokaryotes), **Protista** (unicellular eukaryotes), **Fungi** (chitinous heterotrophs), **Plantae** (autotrophs with cellulose wall), and **Animalia** (heterotrophs without cell wall).
19. **Carl Woese (1990)** introduced the **Three-Domain System** based on 16S rRNA sequences: **Bacteria**, **Archaea**, and **Eukarya**; Archaebacteria have branched ether lipids allowing them to survive in extreme environments (methanogens in ruminant guts, halophiles in salt lakes, thermoacidophiles in hot springs).
20. **Antony van Leeuwenhoek (1632–1723)** is the Father of Microbiology; discovered bacteria ("animalcules") using single high-power lenses; bacteria are 0.5–5.0 $\mu m$ in size and visible under a compound microscope.
21. The smallest known living cell capable of autonomous growth and reproduction is ***Mycoplasma gallisepticum*** (0.1–0.3 $\mu m$, lacks a cell wall, naturally insensitive to penicillin and beta-lactam antibiotics); the largest single cell is an **Ostrich egg**; the longest human cell is the **Neuron**.
22. **Prokaryotes** (bacteria, cyanobacteria) have naked circular DNA in a **nucleoid**, lack membrane-bound organelles, and possess **70S ribosomes** (50S + 30S); **Eukaryotes** have membrane-bound nuclei, linear DNA with histones, and cytoplasmic **80S ribosomes** (60S + 40S).
23. **Cell Wall** chemical facts: Plants = **Cellulose + Hemicellulose + Pectin**; Fungi = **Chitin** (polymer of N-acetylglucosamine); Bacteria = **Peptidoglycan / Murein**; Algae = **Cellulose + Galactans + Mannans + $CaCO_3$**.
24. **Singer and Nicolson (1972)** proposed the **Fluid Mosaic Model** of the plasma membrane: a quasi-fluid bilayer of phospholipids with protein "icebergs floating in a lipid sea"; selectively permeable.
25. **Mitochondria** (Kolliker 1857, Benda 1898) are the powerhouses of the cell (ATP generation via oxidative phosphorylation on inner membrane cristae); contain circular DNA and 70S ribosomes (semi-autonomous); inherited maternally.
26. **Plastids** (Schimper): **Chloroplasts** (photosynthesis, contain chlorophyll and circular DNA); **Chromoplasts** (pigment storage: Lycopene in tomato, Capsanthin in red chilli, Carotene in carrot, Betanin in beetroot); **Leucoplasts** (storage: Amyloplasts = starch, Elaioplasts = oils/fats, Aleuroplasts = proteins).
27. **Endoplasmic Reticulum (ER)**: **Rough ER (RER)** has 80S ribosomes on its surface and synthesises proteins; **Smooth ER (SER)** lacks ribosomes and synthesises lipids, steroid hormones, and detoxifies drugs in the liver.
28. **Golgi Apparatus** (Camillo Golgi, 1898) packages and secretes macromolecules, modifies proteins (glycosylation to form glycoproteins and glycolipids), and forms the **acrosome** of sperm and primary lysosomes.
29. **Lysosomes** (Christian de Duve, 1955) are "suicidal bags" containing hydrolytic enzymes (acid hydrolases active at pH 4.5–5.0) that digest cellular waste and foreign pathogens.
30. **Ribosomes** (George Palade, 1953) are non-membrane-bound ribonucleoprotein particles (rRNA + protein) responsible for protein synthesis; known as the "protein factories" of the cell.
31. **Centrosome / Centrioles** (Boveri, 1888) have a $9+0$ cartwheel microtubule arrangement; present in animal cells to form the mitotic spindle and basal bodies; **absent in higher plant cells**.
32. **Peroxisomes** contain catalase and urate oxidase to break down toxic $H_2O_2$ and perform photorespiration in plants; **Glyoxysomes** convert fats into carbohydrates via the glyoxylate cycle in germinating fatty seeds (castor, groundnut).
33. **Nucleus** was discovered by **Robert Brown (1831)**; the **Nucleolus** (Fontana 1781) is the site of active ribosomal RNA (**rRNA**) synthesis; **Chromatin** was named by Walther Flemming.
34. Human somatic cells have **46 chromosomes (23 pairs)**; gametes have **23 chromosomes (haploid, $n$)**; 22 pairs are autosomes and 1 pair is sex chromosomes (allosomes: XX in female, XY in male).
35. **Watson and Crick (1953)** proposed the **double-helix model of DNA** based on Rosalind Franklin and Maurice Wilkins' X-ray crystallography; won the Nobel Prize in 1962.
36. In DNA, **Adenine pairs with Thymine** ($A = T$, two hydrogen bonds) and **Guanine pairs with Cytosine** ($G \equiv C$, three hydrogen bonds); purines ($A, G$) always pair with pyrimidines ($C, T, U$).
37. **Chargaff's Rule**: In double-stranded DNA, the ratio of Adenine to Thymine and Guanine to Cytosine is equal, i.e., $A + G = T + C$ or $(A+G)/(T+C) = 1$.
38. **RNA** is typically single-stranded, contains **Ribose sugar**, and replaces Thymine with **Uracil**; three functional types: mRNA (messenger), tRNA (transfer / soluble adaptor with cloverleaf secondary structure), and rRNA (ribosomal, structural and catalytic).
39. Nitrogen is an essential constituent of **DNA, RNA, proteins, and chlorophyll**, but is **not** an essential constituent of ordinary **carbohydrates** (composed solely of C, H, and O).
40. The **Cell Cycle** consists of Interphase ($G_1 
ightarrow S 
ightarrow G_2$) and M-Phase; **DNA replication and centriole duplication occur strictly during the S (Synthesis) phase**; chromosome number remains $2n$ while DNA content doubles from $2C$ to $4C$.
41. **Mitosis** (equational division) occurs in somatic cells, yielding 2 genetically identical diploid ($2n$) daughter cells; metaphase is the best stage to study chromosome morphology and count; anaphase is the stage where sister chromatids separate.
42. **Colchicine** (alkaloid from *Colchicum autumnale*) inhibits mitotic spindle microtubule formation, arresting cell division at **metaphase**; widely used to induce artificial **polyploidy** in plant breeding.
43. **Meiosis** (reductional division) occurs in germ cells, producing 4 genetically diverse haploid ($n$) daughter cells; chromosome number is halved during **Anaphase-I**.
44. **Prophase-I of Meiosis has 5 stages**: Leptotene (bouquet stage) → **Zygotene** (synapsis, synaptonemal complex formation) → **Pachytene** (**crossing over** between non-sister chromatids via enzyme **recombinase**) → **Diplotene** (**chiasmata** formation) → Diakinesis (terminalisation of chiasmata).
45. **Gregor Johann Mendel (1822–1884)**, Father of Genetics, performed hybridisation on garden pea (*Pisum sativum*) for 7 years (1856–1863), tracking 7 contrasting characters; work rediscovered in **1900** by **Hugo de Vries, Carl Correns, and Erich von Tschermak**.
46. Mendel's phenotypic ratios: Monohybrid = **3:1** (genotypic **1:2:1**); Dihybrid = **9:3:3:1**; Test cross ratios = **1:1** (monohybrid) and **1:1:1:1** (dihybrid).
47. **Non-Mendelian inheritance**: **Incomplete dominance** gives an intermediate phenotype (e.g., pink 4 o'clock plant *Mirabilis jalapa* and snapdragon *Antirrhinum* with 1:2:1 phenotypic ratio); **Codominance** expresses both alleles fully (e.g., ABO blood group $I^A I^B$ producing AB blood); **Multiple alleles** (ABO system has 3 alleles $I^A, I^B, i$, yielding 6 genotypes and 4 phenotypes).
48. **Pleiotropy** is when a single gene influences multiple phenotypic traits (e.g., Phenylketonuria, Sickle-cell anaemia); **Polygenic inheritance** is when a single trait is controlled by three or more genes (e.g., human skin colour, human height).
49. **Sickle-cell anaemia** is an autosomal recessive point mutation on chromosome 11: substitution of **Glutamic acid by Valine** at position 6 of the $eta$-globin chain ($GAG 
ightarrow GUG$).
50. Sex-linked recessive disorders carried on the X chromosome include **Haemophilia** (bleeder's / royal disease, factor VIII or IX deficiency) and **Red-Green Colour Blindness** (Daltonism); males are affected far more frequently ($XY$) while females are usually carriers ($X^h X$).
51. **Cell Discovery Milestones:** **Robert Hooke (1665)** first observed cells in dead cork tissue using compound microscope, coining term 'cell' (*Micrographia*); **Anton van Leeuwenhoek (1674)** first observed living free cells (bacteria, protozoa, RBCs, sperm); **Robert Brown (1831)** discovered the cell **Nucleus** in orchid root cells; **J.E. Purkinje (1839)** coined term **Protoplasm** for the living fluid substance of cell.
52. **Cell Theory:** Formulated by **Matthias Schleiden (1838 - botanist)** and **Theodor Schwann (1839 - zoologist)**; stated: (1) All living organisms are composed of one or more cells; (2) Cell is the basic structural and functional unit of life; expanded by **Rudolf Virchow (1855)** with immortal aphorism: **'Omnis cellula e cellula'** (all living cells arise from pre-existing cells); **Viruses are an exception to Cell Theory** because they lack cellular machinery and metabolic autonomy.
53. **Smallest and Largest Cells:** Smallest known living cell is **Mycoplasma genitalium / PPLO** (Pleuropneumonia-like organisms, ~0.1 to 0.3 micrometers); largest single isolated cell is the **unfertilized egg of an Ostrich** (~15 to 18 cm); longest animal cell is the human **Nerve Cell (Neuron)** (can exceed 1 metre in length); human RBC is anucleated with biconcave disc shape (~7 micrometers).
54. **Mitochondria ('Powerhouse of the Cell'):** Discovered by Albert von Kolliker (1857), termed 'bioblasts' by Richard Altmann (1890), named 'Mitochondria' by **Carl Benda (1898)**; site of aerobic cellular respiration generating ATP (**Adenosine Triphosphate** - 'Energy Currency of the Cell') through **Krebs Cycle** (Citric Acid Cycle) on inner membrane foldings called **Cristae**; contains its own circular DNA and 70S ribosomes (semi-autonomous organelle; derived via endosymbiosis).
55. **Ribosomes & Protein Synthesis:** Discovered by Romanian-American cell biologist **George Palade (1955)** using electron microscopy ('Palade granules'); non-membrane bound granules made of ribosomal RNA (rRNA) and proteins; site of **Protein Synthesis** (Translation); Prokaryotes possess **70S ribosomes** (50S + 30S subunits); Eukaryotes possess **80S ribosomes** (60S + 40S subunits); 'S' represents Svedberg unit (sedimentation coefficient).
56. **Lysosomes ('Suicidal Bags of the Cell'):** Discovered by Belgian cytologist **Christian de Duve (1955)**; single-membrane vesicles containing over 50 acidic hydrolytic enzymes (hydrolases: proteases, lipases, nucleases) active at acidic pH (~4.5 to 5.0 maintained by proton pumps); digest foreign bacteria, cellular debris, and damaged organelles (autophagy); when cell is fatally damaged, lysosomes burst and digest the host cell itself.
57. **Mendel's Laws of Heredity:** Formulated by Austrian monk **Gregor Johann Mendel (1866)** through hybridization experiments on garden pea (**Pisum sativum**) tracking 7 contrasting pairs of traits; rediscovered independently in 1900 by **Hugo de Vries, Carl Correns, and Erich von Tschermak**; (1) **Law of Segregation** (Purity of Gametes): Allele pairs separate during gamete formation so each gamete carries only one allele; (2) **Law of Independent Assortment:** Genes for different traits assort independently during gamete formation; (3) Monohybrid phenotypic ratio = **3:1** (genotypic 1:2:1); Dihybrid phenotypic ratio = **9:3:3:1**.
58. **DNA Structure & Genetic Code:** Double-helical model elucidated in 1953 by **James Watson and Francis Crick** using X-ray diffraction images of **Rosalind Franklin and Maurice Wilkins** (awarded Nobel Prize 1962); antiparallel strands with sugar-phosphate backbone; purine bases (**Adenine [A], Guanine [G]**) pair with pyrimidine bases (**Thymine [T], Cytosine [C]**) via hydrogen bonds ($A=T$ with 2 H-bonds, $G \equiv C$ with 3 H-bonds); **RNA contains Uracil [U] instead of Thymine**; **Dr. Har Gobind Khorana** shared Nobel Prize in 1968 for deciphering the genetic triplet codon.
59. **Recombinant DNA Technology & PCR:** **Restriction Endonucleases ('Molecular Scissors'):** Discovered by Arber, Nathans, and Smith (1970); cleave double-stranded DNA at specific palindromic sequences; **DNA Ligase ('Molecular Glue')** seals DNA fragments; **Polymerase Chain Reaction (PCR):** Invented by **Kary Mullis (1983)**; uses heat-stable *Taq polymerase* (from thermophilic bacterium *Thermus aquaticus*) to amplify millions of copies of DNA target sequence in vitro in minutes.
60. **CRISPR-Cas9 & Gene Editing:** Revolutionary bacterial adaptive immune system adapted for targeted genome editing by **Emmanuelle Charpentier and Jennifer Doudna** (awarded Nobel Prize in Chemistry 2020); guide RNA (gRNA) directs the Cas9 endonuclease enzyme to introduce precise double-stranded breaks at target DNA locations, enabling gene deletion, correction, or insertion.

## 🧠 Memory Anchors & Mnemonics

### 1. Meiosis Prophase-I Sequential Stages: **L-Z-P-D-D**
> **"Lazy Zebras Paint Double Doors"**
- **L**eptotene: Condensation of chromatin; "bouquet stage".
- **Z**ygotene: **Synapsis** of homologous chromosomes; synaptonemal complex forms.
- **P**achytene: **Crossing over** between non-sister chromatids (enzyme **recombinase**).
- **D**iplotene: Dissolution of synaptonemal complex; **Chiasmata** (X-shaped points) visible.
- **D**iakinesis: **Terminalisation** of chiasmata; nucleolus disappears; nuclear envelope breaks down.

### 2. Eukaryotic Cell Cycle Sequence: **$G_1 	o S 	o G_2 	o M$**
> **"Go Slowly, Grow More"**
- **$G_1$ (Gap 1)**: Cell growth, protein and RNA synthesis; metabolically active.
- **$S$ (Synthesis)**: **Strictly the site of DNA replication** ($2C 	o 4C$, chromosome number remains $2n$) and centriole duplication.
- **$G_2$ (Gap 2)**: Tubulin protein synthesis in preparation for mitotic spindle.
- **$M$ (Mitosis)**: Karyokinesis (Prophase $	o$ Metaphase $	o$ Anaphase $	o$ Telophase) followed by Cytokinesis.

### 3. Biological Taxonomic Hierarchy: **K-P-C-O-F-G-S**
> **"Keep Pond Clean Or Fish Get Sick"**
- **K**ingdom $	o$ **P**hylum / Division $	o$ **C**lass $	o$ **O**rder $	o$ **F**amily $	o$ **G**enus $	o$ **S**pecies.
- *Rule:* Moving from Species to Kingdom, common shared characteristics **decrease**; moving down to Species, shared characteristics **increase**.

### 4. DNA Nitrogenous Bases: **Pure As Gold** & **$A=T / G \equiv C$**
- **Purines** (double-ring): **A**denine and **G**uanine (**"Pure As Gold"**).
- **Pyrimidines** (single-ring): **C**ytosine, **U**racil, **T**hymine (**"CUT the Py"**).
- **Hydrogen Bonds:** $A$ pairs with $T$ with **2 hydrogen bonds** ($A=T$); $G$ pairs with $C$ with **3 hydrogen bonds** ($G \equiv C$).

### 5. Cenozoic Geological Epochs: **P-E-O-M-P-P-H**
> **"Pea-soup Early Oligo-Mio-Plio Pleisto-Holo"**
- **P**aleocene $	o$ **E**ocene $	o$ **O**ligocene $	o$ **M**iocene $	o$ **P**liocene $	o$ **P**leistocene $	o$ **H**olocene.
