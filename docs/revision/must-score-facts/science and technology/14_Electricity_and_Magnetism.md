## 🎯 Consolidated Must-Score Facts

1. Electric current is defined as the rate of flow of electric charge: $I = Q/t$; its SI unit is the **Ampere ($1\text{ A} = 1\text{ C/s}$)**.
2. By international convention, the direction of electric current is the direction of flow of positive charge, which is **opposite to the drift of electrons**.
3. **Current density ($\vec{j} = I/A$) is a VECTOR quantity** (pointing in the direction of positive charge motion), whereas electric current ($I$) and electric potential ($V$) are **scalars**.
4. The **dielectric constant** (relative permittivity $\varepsilon_r$) is a dimensionless quantity ($M^0 L^0 T^0$).
5. The kinetic energy acquired by an electron of charge $e$ falling through potential difference $V$ is **$E = eV\text{ Joules} = 1.602 \times 10^{-19}\text{ J}$** for $1\text{ volt}$.
6. The commercial unit of electrical energy is the **kilowatt-hour (kWh)**, commonly called one **'unit'**: $1\text{ kWh} = 1000\text{ W} \times 3600\text{ s} = 3.6 \times 10^6\text{ Joules}$.
7. Household electrical consumption of $250\text{ units} = 250 \times 3.6 \times 10^6 = \mathbf{9 \times 10^8\text{ Joules}}$ (71st BPSC 2025).
8. Electric power equations in simple ohmic circuits: $P = VI = I^2 R = V^2 / R$. The expression **$IR^2$ DOES NOT represent electric power** (71st BPSC 2025).
9. Resistance of a conductor: $R = \rho L / A$, where $\rho$ is specific resistance (electrical resistivity), measured in **Ohm-metre ($\Omega\cdot\text{m}$)**.
10. If a wire is stretched so that its length doubles ($L' = 2L$) at constant volume, its area halves ($A' = A/2$), causing its resistance to quadruple ($R' = 4R$).
11. Of two domestic light bulbs connected to the same voltage supply, **the dimmer bulb has a LARGER electrical resistance** ($P = V^2 / R$).
12. Connected across the mains in parallel, two identical bulbs glow significantly brighter than when connected in series ($P_{\text{parallel}} = 2V^2/R$ vs $P_{\text{series}} = V^2/2R$).
13. Resistor colour code memory key **BBROYGBVGW**: Brown = 1, Green = 5, Black = 0 (multiplier $10^0 = 1$). A resistor with bands Brown-Green-Black has resistance $(15) \times 10^0 = 15\,\Omega$; under $15\text{ V}$, it draws **$1\text{ Ampere}$** (UPPCS RO/ARO 2023).
14. Capacitive reactance is inversely proportional to frequency: $X_C = \frac{1}{2\pi f C} \propto \frac{1}{f}$.
15. Inductive reactance is directly proportional to frequency: $X_L = 2\pi f L \propto f$.
16. A **dynamo (DC generator)** converts mechanical energy into direct electrical energy using a **commutator (split rings)**.
17. An **AC generator (alternator)** converts mechanical energy into alternating current using **continuous slip rings**.
18. An **electric motor** converts electrical energy into mechanical rotational energy based on the Lorentz force on a current-carrying loop.
19. Electric motors operating at severely depressed voltages burn out because they **draw excessively high current** ($I = P/V$) to deliver rated mechanical power, generating excessive Joule heating ($I^2 R$).
20. The **Faraday constant ($F \approx 96,485\text{ C/mol}$)** represents the magnitude of electric charge per mole of electrons and is a universal physical constant.
21. Motor car lead-acid batteries have six $2\text{ V}$ cells connected in series giving a nominal **$12\text{ V}$**; capacity is rated in **ampere-hours (Ah)**; electrolyte is approx $35\%$ sulphuric acid ($\text{H}_2\text{SO}_4$) in water; positive electrode is lead dioxide ($\text{PbO}_2$) and negative electrode is spongy lead ($\text{Pb}$).
22. **Nickel-Cadmium (Ni-Cd)** rechargeable batteries use nickel oxide hydroxide and metallic cadmium electrodes with potassium hydroxide ($\text{KOH}$) alkaline electrolyte; used in calculators, cordless tools, and transistors.
23. The filament of an incandescent light bulb is made of **Tungsten** (Wolfram, W), discovered by Juan José and Fausto Elhuyar; melting point $\approx 3422^\circ\text{C}$, operating temperature $1500 - 2500^\circ\text{C}$.
24. Ordinary incandescent bulbs have short lifespans because: (i) filament thickness is non-uniform, (ii) complete vacuum cannot be maintained, (iii) support wires degrade. They are filled with inert **Argon and Nitrogen** to inhibit tungsten evaporation.
25. Incandescent bulbs convert only **$5\% - 10\%$** of electrical energy into visible light; the remaining $90\% - 95\%$ is dissipated as infrared heat.
26. **Lightning** contains tremendous electrical energy ($10^8 - 10^9\text{ Joules}$), sufficient to vaporize moisture inside a tree and cause explosive combustion.
27. A **lightning conductor** is a copper rod mounted at the apex of a building, bonded to ground through thick conductor wire, directing atmospheric surge harmlessly into Earth.
28. In a **three-pin electrical plug, the Earth pin is LONGER and THICKER** than the live and neutral pins so that the chassis is grounded first upon insertion and disconnected last upon removal.
29. Electric current **cannot flow** between two connected conductors if both are maintained at the **SAME electrical potential** ($I = \Delta V / R = 0$).
30. An **electric fuse** is a critical safety device connected in **SERIES** with the live line; it operates on the **heating effect of electric current** ($H = I^2 R t$).
31. A fuse wire must possess **HIGH RESISTIVITY and a LOW MELTING POINT**.
32. Domestic fuse wires are made of an alloy of **$63\%$ Tin (Sn) and $37\%$ Lead (Pb)**, with a eutectic melting point of only **$183^\circ\text{C}$** (vastly lower than copper at $1085^\circ\text{C}$ or aluminium at $660^\circ\text{C}$).
33. For high-current industrial circuits ($> 15\text{ A}$), tin-coated copper fuse wires are standard.
34. During a **short circuit**, line resistance drops to near zero, causing electric current to **increase drastically / surge massively** (71st BPSC 2025).
35. The filament of a **halogen lamp** is an alloy of **Tungsten and Sodium**, producing intense yellowish-white light.
36. When electric current flows through a metal wire, temperature rises because moving drift electrons collide continuously with vibrating lattice atoms, transferring kinetic energy into thermal vibrations.
37. Domestic electrical distribution wiring is connected in **PARALLEL**; every appliance receives the full rated voltage ($220\text{ V}$), and turning off one appliance does not disrupt others.
38. Standard Indian household insulation colour coding: **Red/Brown = Live**, **Black/Blue = Neutral**, **Green/Yellow-Green = Earth**.
39. The electrical resistance of a dry human body is on the order of **$10^4\,\Omega$ ($10,000\,\Omega$)** to $10^5\,\Omega$; wet or broken skin drops resistance to below $1000\,\Omega$.
40. **CFL (Compact Fluorescent Lamp)** contains mercury vapour and argon; electric discharge excites mercury atoms to emit ultraviolet radiation, which strikes inner phosphor coating to fluoresce visible white light ($22\%$ energy efficiency; lasts $6,000 - 15,000\text{ hours}$).
41. **Light Emitting Diode (LED)** operates on the principle of **ELECTROLUMINESCENCE**; electrons recombine with electron holes in a direct-bandgap semiconductor (e.g. Gallium Arsenide/Phosphide), releasing energy directly as photons.
42. LEDs consume $\sim 85\%$ less power than incandescent bulbs, emit directional light ($180^\circ$), and have an enormous operational lifespan of **$50,000 - 100,000\text{ hours}$**.
43. **Sodium vapour street lamps** are omnidirectional ($360^\circ$) and emit monochromatic yellow light ($589\text{ nm}$), producing shadowy-black colour rendering compared to high-CRI multi-spectrum LEDs.
44. The **choke coil** in a fluorescent tubelight acts as an inductor that produces a high-voltage inductive kick to ignite gas discharge, and subsequently limits and stabilizes alternating current through the tube.
45. If potential difference ($V$) and current ($I$) across a load are each doubled, power increases **fourfold** ($P' = 2V \times 2I = 4P$).
46. If $n$ small identical spherical drops charged to potential $V$ coalesce into a single large drop, the potential of the composite drop becomes **$V' = n^{2/3} V$** (UPPCS Mains).
47. Magnetic field at the centre of a flat circular coil of $N$ turns carrying current $I$: $B = \frac{\mu_0 N I}{2r}$. For $N = 100$, $r = 0.1\text{ m}$, $I = 1\text{ A} \implies B = \mathbf{6.28 \times 10^{-4}\text{ Tesla}}$ (UKPCS 2024).
48. A **transformer** functions exclusively on alternating current (AC) via **mutual electromagnetic induction**; it cannot operate on steady direct current (DC).
49. A **mobile phone charger** incorporates a **step-down transformer** (stepping down $220\text{ V AC} \to 5-10\text{ V AC}$) followed by a **rectifier** (converting AC to DC).
50. Earth's geomagnetic field is generated by convective electric currents in the molten, iron-rich outer liquid core (**geodynamo effect**).
