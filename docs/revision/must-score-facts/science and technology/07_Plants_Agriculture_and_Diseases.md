## 🎯 Consolidated Must-Score Facts

1. **Plant Taxonomy Hierarchy**: Categorized from higher to lower: **Kingdom $\rightarrow$ Phylum (Division) $\rightarrow$ Class $\rightarrow$ Order $\rightarrow$ Family $\rightarrow$ Genus $\rightarrow$ Species**. **Species** is the basic taxonomic unit consisting of individuals with fundamental morphological similarities capable of interbreeding.
2. **Augustin Pyramus de Candolle (A.P. de Candolle)**: Swiss botanist who **first coined the term "Taxonomy" (1813)** and first used internal **vascular tissue characteristics** to divide plants into *Vasculares* (with vascular bundles) and *Cellulares* (without vascular bundles). (Linnaeus is the "Father of Modern Taxonomy").
3. **Plant Tissues**: **Apical Meristem** (vertical height / primary growth); **Intercalary Meristem** (internode bases, re-growth of grazed grasses); **Lateral Meristem / Cambium** (secondary growth, increases **girth / diameter** of stems).
4. **Simple Permanent Tissues**: **Parenchyma** (living packing tissue, modified into **Aerenchyma** with large air chambers in aquatic hydrophytes for buoyancy); **Collenchyma** (living with pectin corner thickenings, provides mechanical flexibility); **Sclerenchyma** (dead, heavily lignified; commercial jute/hemp fibres and gritty sclereids/stone cells of guava and pear).
5. **Complex Vascular Tissues (Xylem and Phloem)**: **Xylem** conducts water and dissolved mineral salts unidirectionally from roots to leaves; composed of Tracheids, Vessels (unique to angiosperms), Xylem Fibres, and Xylem Parenchyma (only living xylem cell); **Phloem** translocates photosynthetic organic solutes bidirectionally from source to sink; composed of Sieve Tubes (enucleated at maturity), Companion Cells, Phloem Fibres (bast fibres), and Phloem Parenchyma.
6. **Ascent of Sap**: Driven by **Transpiration Pull** based on the **Cohesion-Tension Theory** of **Dixon and Joly (1894)**. Positive **Root Pressure** causes **Guttation** through marginal leaf **Hydathodes** in moist early mornings.
7. **Modified Underground Stems (Food Storage)**: **Rhizome**: Prostrate horizontal underground stem with distinct nodes, internodes, and scale leaves (e.g., **Ginger, Turmeric, Banana**); **Tuber**: Swollen distal end of underground branch bearing eyes/axillary buds (e.g., **Potato**); **Bulb**: Reduced disc-like stem surrounded by concentric fleshy scale leaves (e.g., **Onion, Garlic**); **Corm**: Solid, unbranched, vertically growing swollen underground stem (e.g., **Colocasia / Arbi, Amorphophallus / Zamikand, Saffron / Crocus**).
8. **Modified Storage Roots (NOT Stems)**: Derived from the root system, lack nodes, internodes, and scale leaves: **Tuberous Adventitious Root** (e.g., **Sweet Potato / Shakarkand**); **Conical Taproot** (e.g., **Carrot**, rich in $\beta$-carotene); **Fusiform Taproot** (e.g., **Radish**); **Napiform Taproot** (e.g., **Beetroot, Turnip / Shalgam**).
9. **Aerial Stem Storage**: In **Sugarcane (*Saccharum officinarum*, Family Gramineae/Poaceae)**, sucrose is stored predominantly in the **aerial stalk / stem** (propagated by stem cuttings with nodes).
10. **The Pea Family (Fabaceae / Leguminosae)**: Dicotyledonous plants with root nodules harbouring nitrogen-fixing bacteria: **Pea (*Pisum sativum*, herb with leaf tendrils)**, **Groundnut / Peanut (*Arachis hypogaea*)**, **Horse-gram / Kulthi (*Macrotyloma uniflorum*)**, **Soybean (*Glycine max*)**.
11. **Spices & Floral Anatomy**: **Clove (*Laung*)** is the dried unopened **flower bud** of *Syzygium aromaticum* (contains eugenol); **Saffron (*Kesar*)** consists of the dried **stigma and style** of *Crocus sativus*; **Cinnamon (*Dalchini*)** is the dried **inner bark** of *Cinnamomum verum*; **Black Pepper** is the dried unripe **berry/drupe** fruit of *Piper nigrum*; **Cardamom (*Elaichi*)** is the dried **capsule fruit and seeds** of *Elettaria cardamomum*.
12. **True Fruits vs False Fruits**: **True Fruits** develop exclusively from the fertilized ovary of a flower (e.g., **Mango, Grape, Tomato, Banana**); **False Fruits (Pseudocarps)** develop from other floral parts like the fleshy receptacle / thalamus in addition to the ovary (e.g., **Apple and Pear** are pomes where the edible part is the fleshy thalamus; **Cashew Nut** where the fleshy stalk is the swollen peduncle; **Strawberry** where the edible part is the fleshy thalamus).
13. **Fruit Classifications & Edible Morphology**: **Drupe (Stone Fruit)**: Single seed enclosed in a hard stony endocarp (e.g., **Mango** where edible part is fleshy **mesocarp**; **Coconut** where edible part is the cellular and liquid **endosperm**); **Berry**: Fleshy throughout with multiple seeds (e.g., Tomato, Guava); **Hesperidium**: Berry with leathery glandular peel and endocarpic juicy hair segments (e.g., Orange, Lemon); **Syconus / Sorosis (Composite Fruits)**: Derived from whole inflorescence (e.g., **Fig, Pineapple, Mulberry, Jackfruit**); **Litchi**: Edible part is the fleshy succulent **Aril** (outgrowth of the funicle).
14. **Plant Pigments Master Fact**: **Chlorophyll**: Green photosynthetic pigment containing central **Magnesium ($Mg^{2+}$)**; **Lycopene**: Bright red carotenoid in ripe **tomatoes**; **Anthocyanin**: Water-soluble vacuolar pigment responsible for red/purple/blue colours in **apple skin, beetroot, purple cabbage, and rose petals**; **Carotene**: Orange pigment in **carrots**; **Betacyanin**: Red-violet pigment in **beetroot**; **Curcumin**: Yellow phenolic compound in **turmeric**; **Capsanthin**: Red carotenoid pigment in **ripe red chillies**; **Xanthophyll / Lutein**: Yellow pigment in egg yolk and autumn leaves.
15. **Plant Odour & Pungency Chemistry**: **Pungency of Chillies**: Caused by the alkaloid **Capsaicin**; **Pungency of Mustard Oil**: Caused by the sulfur glucoside **Allyl Isothiocyanate** (hydrolyzed from sinigrin by myrosinase); **Pungency of Garlic**: Caused by the sulfur-containing amino acid derivative **Allicin**; **Pungency & Tearing in Onions**: Caused by volatile **Syn-propanethial-S-oxide** (formed by the enzyme lachrymatory factor synthase acting on S-alkenyl-L-cysteine sulfoxides); **Bitterness in Bitter Gourd (Karela)**: Caused by tetracyclic triterpene glucosides called **Momordicines**.
16. **Commercial Fibres & Economic Products**: **Cotton**: Surface epidermal hairs of seeds (*Gossypium*), composed of nearly pure **Cellulose (~90%)**; **Jute and Sunn Hemp**: Phloem / bast sclerenchyma fibres extracted by biological water retting; **Cork**: Harvested from the outer protective bark (*phellem*) of the Mediterranean cork oak tree ***Quercus suber***; **Natural Rubber**: Polyisoprene polymer harvested as milky latex from the bark of ***Hevea brasiliensis***; **Quinine**: Powerful antimalarial alkaloid extracted from the dried stem bark of the ***Cinchona tree*** (*Cinchona calisaya / ledgeriana*); **Triticale**: First man-made artificial cereal hybrid developed by cross-breeding **Wheat (*Triticum*) $\times$ Rye (*Secale*)**.
17. **Ecological Plant Adaptations**: **Hydrophytes**: Aquatic plants with poorly developed roots, reduced vascular tissue, thin cuticle, and extensive **Aerenchyma** tissue for buoyancy (e.g., *Hydrilla, Eichhornia, Lotus*); **Xerophytes**: Desert drought-adapted plants with sunken stomata, thick waxy cuticle, reduced scale-like leaves or spines to minimize transpiration, and fleshy photosynthetic stems (**Phylloclade**, e.g., *Opuntia, Cactus*); **Halophytes**: Salt-tolerant mangrove vegetation growing in coastal saline marshes; develop specialized negatively geotropic breathing roots called **Pneumatophores** that emerge vertically out of saline mud to absorb oxygen (e.g., *Rhizophora, Avicennia*).
18. **Epiphytes & Velamen Tissue**: **Epiphytes** are space-parasites that grow physically supported upon branches of other trees for sunlight without drawing nutrition or water from the host (e.g., **Vanda, Orchids**); they absorb moisture directly from humid atmospheric air using specialized spongy, hygroscopic aerial root tissue called **Velamen**.
19. **Forensic Utility of Diatoms**: **Diatoms** are unicellular photosynthetic algae with indestructible silica shells (*frustules*); inhalation of water during drowning draws diatoms into the bloodstream and internal organs (bone marrow, liver); **presence of diatoms in closed organs is a definitive forensic test confirming death by drowning**.
20. **Insectivorous & Carnivorous Plants**: Grow in acidic, waterlogged bogs **severely deficient in nitrates/nitrogen**; photosynthesise carbohydrates normally, but trap and digest small insects using proteases to fulfill nitrogen demands: **Pitcher Plant (*Nepenthes*)** (leaf lamina modified into hollow pitcher, leaf apex into lid), **Venus Flytrap (*Dionaea*)**, **Sundew (*Drosera*)**, and **Bladderwort (*Utricularia*)**.
21. **Photosynthesis Light Reactions (Photochemical Phase)**: Occurs in the **Thylakoid membranes / Grana** of chloroplasts; light energy absorbed by chlorophyll pigments excites electrons in Photosystems II and I; driven by the **Hill Reaction / Photolysis of Water**: water molecules are split into protons, electrons, and **Oxygen gas ($O_2$)**: $$2H_2O \rightarrow 4H^+ + 4e^- + O_2\uparrow$$; **all oxygen liberated during photosynthesis originates from water ($H_2O$), NOT from carbon dioxide ($CO_2$)** (proven by Ruben and Kamen using heavy oxygen isotope $^{18}O$); generates assimilatory power in the form of **ATP and NADPH**.
22. **Photosynthesis Dark Reactions (Calvin Cycle / Biosynthetic Phase)**: Occurs in the **Stroma** of chloroplasts; does not directly require light, but uses ATP and NADPH produced during light reactions; atmospheric $CO_2$ is fixed by the primary carboxylating enzyme **RuBisCO (Ribulose-1,5-bisphosphate carboxylase-oxygenase)**, the most abundant protein on Earth; primary stable product is 3-phosphoglyceric acid (3-PGA, a 3-carbon compound, hence **$C_3$ pathway**).
23. **$C_3$ vs $C_4$ vs CAM Pathways**: **$C_3$ Plants**: Moderate climates (e.g., Wheat, Rice, Barley, Soybean); suffer from wasteful **Photorespiration** under high temperature; **$C_4$ Plants**: Tropical grasses (e.g., **Maize, Sugarcane, Sorghum / Jowar, Bajra**); exhibit specialized **Kranz Anatomy** (bundle sheath cells surrounded by mesophyll); fix $CO_2$ initially using **PEP Carboxylase** into 4-carbon Oxaloacetic Acid (OAA); show **zero photorespiration** and high water-use efficiency; **CAM Plants**: Succulents and epiphytes (e.g., **Pineapple, Agave, Cactus, Opuntia**); open stomata at night (**Scotoactive stomata**) to fix $CO_2$ into malic acid, keeping stomata closed during scorching daylight to conserve water.
24. **Action Spectrum of Photosynthesis**: Photosynthesis absorbs light in the Photosynthetically Active Radiation (PAR) spectrum ($400–700\text{ nm}$); maximal photosynthetic rate occurs under **Red Light** ($\sim 660\text{ nm}$) followed by **Blue Light**; **Green Light** is reflected by chlorophyll and is least effective for photosynthesis.
25. **Plant Respiration & Cold Storage**: Plant respiration is an oxidative catabolic process converting glucose and oxygen into $CO_2$, $H_2O$, and ATP; in agricultural post-harvest storage, cold storage extends fruit and vegetable shelf life because **low temperatures drastically slow down the rate of cellular respiration, transpiration, and ethylene synthesis** (UPPCS 2021).
26. **Auxins (Indole-3-Acetic Acid / IAA)**: Synthesised in stem apical meristems; promotes **Apical Dominance** (inhibits growth of lateral axillary buds); stimulates root initiation in stem cuttings; synthetic auxin **2,4-D (2,4-Dichlorophenoxyacetic acid)** is widely used as a selective weedicide / herbicide to eradicate broad-leaved dicot weeds in cereal crop fields without harming monocot crops.
27. **Gibberellins (Gibberellic Acid / $GA_3$)**: Discovered by **Eiichi Kurosawa (1926)** from the fungus *Gibberella fujikuroi* causing "foolish seedling" (*Bakanae*) disease in rice; promotes stem internode elongation (bolting in rosette plants like cabbage), breaks seed and bud dormancy, and induces **parthenocarpy** (seedless fruit production) in grapes and apples.
28. **Cytokinins (Zeatin / Kinetin)**: Discovered by Skoog and Miller; naturally abundant in regions of rapid cell division (coconut milk, corn kernels); promotes **cell division (cytokinesis)**, delays leaf senescence (**Richmond-Lang Effect**), and breaks apical dominance by promoting lateral bud outgrowth.
29. **Ethylene ($C_2H_4$)**: The **only gaseous plant hormone**; synthesised in ripening fruits and aging tissues; acts as the primary natural **Fruit Ripening Hormone**; commercial fruit ripening uses **Ethephon (Ethrel)**, which releases ethylene; *Lucent trap*: artificial ripening with calcium carbide ($CaC_2$) releases toxic **Acetylene gas ($C_2H_2$)**, which mimics ethylene but leaves dangerous arsenic and phosphorus residues.
30. **Abscisic Acid (ABA / "Stress Hormone")**: Naturally occurring sesquiterpene plant growth inhibitor; induces **closure of stomata** during acute water deficit / drought to prevent transpirational water loss; promotes seed dormancy and accelerates abscission of leaves and flowers; acts as an antagonist to gibberellins.
31. **Photoperiodism & Vernalization**: **Photoperiodism**: Physiological response of plants to the relative length of daylight and darkness; mediated by the photoreceptor pigment **Phytochrome** (exists in red-absorbing $P_r$ and far-red-absorbing $P_{fr}$ forms); **Vernalization**: Promotion of flowering by exposure to a prolonged period of low winter temperatures ($1–7^\circ\text{C}$); prevents precocious reproductive development in autumn, common in winter varieties of wheat and barley.
32. **Biological Nitrogen Fixation (BNF)**: Conversion of inert atmospheric dinitrogen ($N_2$) into ammonia ($NH_3$) catalyzed by the oxygen-sensitive microbial enzyme complex **Nitrogenase**; nitrogenase contains **Molybdenum and Iron (Mo-Fe protein)**; protected from oxygen poisoning inside legume root nodules by the oxygen-scavenging pink-red pigment **Leghaemoglobin**.
33. **Symbiotic Nitrogen Fixers**: ***Rhizobium leguminosarum*** forms symbiotic root nodules with leguminous crops (Pea, Gram, Lentil, Soybean); ***Frankia*** is an actinomycete that fixes nitrogen in non-leguminous trees (e.g., *Casuarina, Alnus*); ***Anabaena azollae*** is a cyanobacterium living in the leaf cavities of the tiny free-floating freshwater fern ***Azolla pinnata*** (widely applied as an exceptionally productive biofertiliser in flooded wetland **paddy / rice fields**).
34. **Free-Living & Associative Biofertilisers**: **Free-living Aerobic Nitrogen Fixers**: ***Azotobacter*** (applied in wheat, maize, cotton crops); **Free-living Anaerobic Fixers**: ***Clostridium***; **Associative Symbionts**: ***Azospirillum*** (colonizes rhizosphere of sorghum, maize, sugarcane); **Cyanobacteria / Blue-Green Algae (BGA)**: *Nostoc, Anabaena, Tolypothrix, Aulosira* fix nitrogen via specialized thick-walled cells called **Heterocysts** in alkaline flooded soils.
35. **Mycorrhiza & Phosphorus Solubilization**: **Mycorrhiza** is a mutualistic symbiotic association between fungal hyphae and roots of higher plants (e.g., *Glomus* genus); fungal hyphae vastly expand the root absorption surface, selectively absorbing immobile soil **Phosphorus ($P$)** and water for the plant, while the host plant provides carbohydrates to the fungus; **VAM (Vesicular-Arbuscular Mycorrhiza)** is an apex biofertiliser for phosphate nutrition.
36. **Macronutrients in Plant Nutrition**: Plants require 9 essential macronutrients absorbed in large quantities ($>10\text{ mmol/kg}$ dry matter): **Carbon ($C$), Hydrogen ($H$), Oxygen ($O$)** (framework elements from air and water); primary fertilizer elements **Nitrogen ($N$), Phosphorus ($P$), Potassium ($K$)**; and secondary elements **Calcium ($Ca$), Magnesium ($Mg$), Sulfur ($S$)**.
37. **Micronutrients in Plant Nutrition**: Essential trace elements required in minute quantities ($<10\text{ mmol/kg}$ dry matter): **Iron ($Fe$), Manganese ($Mn$), Copper ($Cu$), Zinc ($Zn$), Boron ($B$), Molybdenum ($Mo$), Chlorine ($Cl$), Nickel ($Ni$)**; **Zinc** is required for auxin (IAA) synthesis; **Boron** is essential for pollen germination and sugar translocation; **Molybdenum** is a component of nitrate reductase and nitrogenase; **Magnesium** is the central metal ion of chlorophyll.
38. **Classic Plant Nutritional Deficiency Disorders**: **Khaira Disease of Rice**: Caused by severe **Zinc ($Zn$) deficiency**; symptoms include reddish-brown chlorotic spots on young leaves; treated by spraying zinc sulfate ($ZnSO_4$); discovered by Y.L. Nene at Pantnagar; **Marsh Spot of Pea**: Caused by **Manganese ($Mn$) deficiency**; **Whiptail of Cauliflower**: Caused by **Molybdenum ($Mo$) deficiency**; **Black Heart of Potato**: Caused by **Oxygen deficiency / hypoxia** in poorly ventilated cold storage warehouses; **Hollow Heart of Potato**: Caused by excessive nitrogen fertilizer and rapid growth; **Browning / Hollow Stem of Cauliflower**: Caused by **Boron ($B$) deficiency**.
39. **Fungal Diseases of Cereal Crops**: **Late Blight of Potato**: Caused by the oomycete fungus ***Phytophthora infestans***; devastated Ireland's potato harvest, causing the catastrophic **Irish Famine (1845–1849)**; **Ergot of Rye and Bajra**: Caused by ***Claviceps purpurea***; replaces grain with dark poisonous sclerotia containing ergotamine and lysergic acid alkaloids; ingestion causes painful gangrenous and convulsive ergotism ("St. Anthony's Fire"); **Loose Smut of Wheat**: Internally seed-borne fungal disease caused by ***Ustilago tritici***; transforms ears into a mass of black powdery spores; **Black Stem Rust of Wheat**: Caused by heteroecious fungus ***Puccinia graminis tritici*** (requires wheat and barberry host plants to complete lifecycle; pioneered in India by Dr. K.C. Mehta).
40. **Bacterial & Viral Crop Diseases Drill**: **Bacterial Blight of Rice**: Caused by ***Xanthomonas oryzae***; produces translucent water-soaked lesions and kresek seedling wilt; **Citrus Canker**: Caused by ***Xanthomonas axonopodis pv. citri***; produces raised corky necrotic lesions on leaves and fruit of lemons and limes; **Crown Gall Disease**: Plant tumors caused by the soil bacterium ***Agrobacterium tumefaciens*** (harbours the tumor-inducing Ti plasmid used as a natural genetic engineer in biotechnology); **Tobacco Mosaic Disease (TMD)**: Caused by Tobacco Mosaic Virus (TMV, first isolated in crystalline form by Wendell Stanley, 1935); **Bunchy Top of Banana**: Caused by Banana bunchy top virus transmitted by the banana aphid *Pentalonia nigronervosa*; **Yellow Vein Mosaic of Bhindi (Okra)**: Devastating viral disease transmitted by the insect vector **Whitefly (*Bemisia tabaci*)**.
41. **Nematode Crop Infestations**: **Root-knot disease** in tobacco, tomato, and eggplant crops is caused by the microscopic endoparasitic soil nematode ***Meloidogyne incognita***; infects roots inducing characteristic giant multinucleated gall cells, stunting plant growth; controlled modernly using **RNA interference (RNAi) / gene silencing** technology.
42. **Organic Farming & Vermicomposting**: Organic farming avoids synthetic fertilizers, chemical pesticides, and GMOs; relies on crop rotations, green manures, and **Vermicompost**; vermicompost is produced by earthworm species like ***Eisenia foetida*** (red wigglers) digesting animal dung and farm biomass; earthworm castings are enriched with available nitrogen, phosphorus, exchangeable potassium, and beneficial soil microbes.
43. **Green Manuring Crops**: Sowing and plowing green succulent leguminous crops into the soil at flowering stage to enrich organic matter and nitrogen: apex green manure crops are **Dhaincha (*Sesbania aculeata*)**, **Sunn hemp (*Crotalaria juncea*)**, **Cowpea / Lobia (*Vigna unguiculata*)**, and **Guar / Cluster bean (*Cyamopsis tetragonoloba*)**; Dhaincha is exceptionally tolerant of waterlogging and alkaline/saline soils.
44. **Major Cash Crops & Agricultural Classification**: **Kharif Crops**: Sown in June–July at onset of monsoon, harvested in September–October (e.g., **Rice/Paddy, Maize, Jowar, Bajra, Cotton, Groundnut, Soybean, Jute, Arhar/Tur**); **Rabi Crops**: Sown in October–November (winter), harvested in March–April (e.g., **Wheat, Barley, Mustard / Rapeseed, Gram / Chickpea, Pea, Potato, Linseed**); **Zaid Crops**: Grown between March and June summer months (e.g., **Watermelon, Muskmelon, Cucumber, Gourd, Fodder crops**).
45. **Genetically Modified (GM) Crops in India**: **Bt Cotton** is the **only GM commercial crop permitted for commercial cultivation in India** (approved in 2002 by the Genetic Engineering Appraisal Committee / GEAC); incorporates the *Cry1Ac* and *Cry2Ab* endotoxin genes from soil bacterium *Bacillus thuringiensis* targeting American bollworm; **Bt Brinjal** was placed under commercial moratorium in 2010; **GM Mustard (DMH-11)** is a hybrid mustard developed by Delhi University using the barnase-barstar system.
46. **Pesticides & Bio-Pesticides Drill**: **DDT (Dichlorodiphenyltrichloroethane)**: Persistent organochlorine insecticide; bioaccumulates and biomagnifies up trophic levels, causing thinning of eggshells in raptor birds; largely banned globally under the Stockholm Convention; **Bio-pesticides**: Environmentally safe formulations derived from natural organisms, including **Neem-based formulations** containing the triterpenoid limonoid **Azadirachtin** (antifeedant and insect growth regulator from *Azadirachta indica*), and fungal bio-agents like ***Trichoderma viride*** (controls fungal root pathogens).
47. **Hydroponics & Aeroponics**: **Hydroponics**: Technique of cultivating terrestrial plants in a sterile **soil-less water culture** containing precisely calibrated aqueous mineral nutrient solutions (developed by Julius von Sachs, 1860); **Aeroponics**: Growing plants suspended in an air or mist environment without soil or aggregate medium, roots misted periodically with nutrient-rich sprays; maximizes root oxygenation and water efficiency.
48. **Plant Cell Totipotency & Tissue Culture**: **Totipotency** is the intrinsic cellular capacity of any nucleated plant cell / explant to regenerate into a complete, fertile multicellular plant organism (concept formulated by Gottlieb Haberlandt, 1902); utilized in commercial **Micropropagation** to mass-produce pathogen-free clonal plantlets (e.g., Banana, Orchids, Potato) in sterile synthetic media containing balanced auxin and cytokinin ratios.
49. **Polyembryony & Parthenocarpy**: **Polyembryony**: Phenomenon of occurrence of more than one embryo in a single seed (discovered by Antony van Leeuwenhoek, 1719 in citrus seeds); prominent in **Citrus (Orange, Lemon)** and **Mango**; **Parthenocarpy**: Formation and development of fruits without fertilization of ovules, yielding naturally seedless fruits (e.g., **Banana, Pineapple, Seedless Grapes**); can be induced artificially by applying auxins or gibberellins.
50. **Key Botanical Taxonomy & Economic Families Drill**: **Poaceae / Gramineae (Grass Family)**: Rice, Wheat, Maize, Sugarcane, Bamboo (fastest-growing woody grass); **Fabaceae / Leguminosae (Pulse Family)**: Gram, Pea, Arhar, Soybean, Groundnut; **Solanaceae (Nightshade Family)**: Potato, Tomato, Brinjal, Chilli, Tobacco (*Nicotiana tabacum*, leaves rich in nicotine), Belladonna (*Atropa belladonna*, source of atropine); **Brassicaceae / Cruciferae (Mustard Family)**: Mustard, Radish, Cabbage, Cauliflower, Turnip; **Cucurbitaceae (Gourd Family)**: Bottle gourd, Bitter gourd, Cucumber, Pumpkin, Watermelon; **Malvaceae (Mallow Family)**: Cotton, Okra / Bhindi, Hibiscus.

## 🧠 Memory Anchors & Mnemonics

### 1. Plant Hormone Functions: **A-G-C-E-A**
- **A**uxin: **A**pical dominance & rooting (2,4-D weedicide).
- **G**ibberellin: **G**igantic stem elongation & bolting ($GA_3$).
- **C**ytokinin: **C**ell division & anti-aging (delays senescence).
- **E**thylene: **E**xclusively gaseous; fruit ripening agent.
- **A**BA (Abscisic Acid): **A**nti-stress; closes stomata in drought.

### 2. $C_4$ Heavyweight Crops: **M-S-S-P**
> **"Maize, Sugarcane, Sorghum, Pearl-millet"**
- All 4 are tropical $C_4$ plants with Kranz anatomy, PEP carboxylase, zero photorespiration, and high water-use efficiency.

### 3. Edible Modified Plant Parts Ratta Anchor:
- **Tuber** $	o$ Potato (Stem)
- **Rhizome** $	o$ Ginger, Turmeric (Stem)
- **Bulb** $	o$ Onion, Garlic (Fleshy scale leaves)
- **Corm** $	o$ Colocasia, Saffron (Stem)
- **Clove** $	o$ Dried flower bud
- **Saffron** $	o$ Stigma & style of flower

### 4. Insectivorous Nitrogen Seekers: **N-D-U**
> **"Nepenthes, Drosera, Utricularia"**
- Trap insects strictly for **Nitrogen ($N_2$)**, NOT for carbohydrates (they have chlorophyll!).
