## 🎯 Consolidated Must-Score Facts

1. By mass, **Oxygen** is the most abundant element in the human body (~65%), followed by Carbon (~18.5%), Hydrogen (~9.5%), and Nitrogen (~3.2%); by total number of atoms, **Hydrogen** is the most abundant.
2. **Calcium** is the most abundant mineral element in the human body (~1.5% of body weight; 99% in bones and teeth as hydroxyapatite crystals); **Iron** is the most abundant essential micromineral (3–4 grams, primarily inside haemoglobin).
3. The adult human brain accounts for only **~2% of total body weight**, yet consumes **~20% of total resting oxygen and glucose**.
4. Energy values of macronutrients: **Carbohydrates yield $\mathbf{\sim 4.0\text{ kcal/g}}$**; **Proteins yield $\mathbf{\sim 4.0\text{ kcal/g}}$**; **Fats / Lipids yield $\mathbf{\sim 9.0\text{ kcal/g}}$** (highest physiological fuel value).
5. **Glucose** (dextrose / grape sugar) and **Fructose** (fruit sugar / levulose) are functional isomers with identical empirical formula ($C_6H_{12}O_6$); **Fructose is the sweetest naturally occurring sugar**.
6. **Sucrose** (table / cane sugar) is a non-reducing disaccharide composed of **Glucose + Fructose**; **Lactose** (milk sugar) is **Glucose + Galactose**; **Maltose** (malt sugar) is **Glucose + Glucose**.
7. **Starch** (plant storage carbohydrate) and **Glycogen** ("animal starch", stored in liver and skeletal muscles) are polymers of $\alpha$-D-glucose digested by human amylases; **Cellulose** is a structural polymer of $\beta$-D-glucose that humans **cannot digest** due to the total absence of the enzyme cellulase (acts as roughage / dietary fibre).
8. Proteins are polymers of amino acids linked by peptide bonds; of the 20 standard amino acids, **9 are essential amino acids** in adults (must be supplied in diet: Histidine, Isoleucine, Leucine, Lysine, Methionine, Phenylalanine, Threonine, Tryptophan, Valine).
9. **Kwashiorkor** is caused by severe **protein deficiency with adequate calorie intake**; characterized by pitting **oedema**, swollen "pot belly", and "flaky-paint" dermatosis; **Marasmus** is caused by combined **protein-energy malnutrition (PEM)** in infants $<1\text{ year}$; characterized by extreme emaciation, dry wrinkled skin ("little old man" face), and **absence of oedema**.
10. **Fat-Soluble Vitamins**: **A, D, E, K**; dissolve in organic solvents and fats, stored in the liver and adipose tissues; excessive intake causes toxic **Hypervitaminosis**.
11. **Water-Soluble Vitamins**: **B-Complex group and Vitamin C**; not stored in large amounts in the human body (readily excreted in urine, require daily dietary intake); **Vitamin B12 is the unique exception** (stored in the human liver for 3–5 years).
12. **Vitamin A (Retinol)**: Essential for rhodopsin synthesis in retinal rods; deficiency causes **Nyctalopia (Night Blindness)**, **Xerophthalmia** (dry, keratinized cornea), and **Bitot's spots**; rich sources: Cod-liver oil, liver, egg yolk, and plant precursors like **$\beta$-Carotene** in carrots, mangoes, and papayas.
13. **Vitamin D (Calciferol / Sunshine Vitamin)**: Synthesised in human skin upon exposure to solar UV-B radiation from 7-dehydrocholesterol; acts as a pro-hormone promoting intestinal absorption of calcium and phosphorus; deficiency causes **Rickets** in children (bowed legs, pigeon chest) and **Osteomalacia** in adults (soft, fragile bones).
14. **Vitamin E (Tocopherol / Anti-Sterility Vitamin)**: Powerful lipid-soluble biological **antioxidant** protecting cell membranes from free radical oxidation; richest natural dietary source is **Wheat-germ oil**, almonds, and sunflower oil.
15. **Vitamin K (Phylloquinone / Naphthoquinone)**: Required in the liver as a cofactor for the post-translational carboxylation of clotting factors **II (Prothrombin), VII, IX, and X**; deficiency causes prolonged bleeding time and haemorrhage; synthesized by symbiotic colon bacteria (*E. coli*); rich plant source: **Alfalfa** and dark green leafy vegetables.
16. **Vitamin C (Ascorbic Acid / Anti-Scurvy Vitamin)**: Required for the enzymatic hydroxylation of proline and lysine during **Collagen synthesis**; deficiency causes **Scurvy** (bleeding and spongy gums, loose teeth, poor wound healing, petechial haemorrhages); destroyed by prolonged boiling and cooking heat; richest sources: **Amla (Indian gooseberry)**, guava, citrus fruits (lemon, orange); **absent in milk, meat, and eggs**.
17. **Vitamin B1 (Thiamine)**: Active coenzyme TPP (Thiamine Pyrophosphate) required in carbohydrate metabolism; deficiency causes **Beriberi** (Dry Beriberi = peripheral neuropathy; Wet Beriberi = congestive heart failure and oedema); **eating polished / milled parboiled rice strips the thiamine-rich outer aleurone layer**, precipitating beriberi.
18. **Vitamin B2 (Riboflavin)**: Precursor of coenzymes FMN and FAD; imparts characteristic yellow colour to cow milk and whey; deficiency causes **Ariboflavinosis**: **Cheilosis** (painful cracks at the corners of the mouth), **Glossitis** (magenta-red inflamed tongue), and keratitis.
19. **Vitamin B3 (Niacin / Nicotinic Acid)**: Precursor of NAD and NADP; synthesised in small quantities from the essential amino acid **Tryptophan**; deficiency causes **Pellagra**, classically remembered by the **"4 Ds"**: **Dermatitis** (Casal's necklace pattern on sun-exposed skin), **Diarrhoea**, **Dementia**, and **Death**; prevalent in populations relying exclusively on maize / corn diets (maize is deficient in tryptophan and contains bound niacytin).
20. **Vitamin B5 (Pantothenic Acid)**: Essential constituent of **Coenzyme-A (CoA)**; deficiency causes "Burning Feet Syndrome" (*Gopalan's syndrome*).
21. **Vitamin B6 (Pyridoxine)**: Coenzyme for amino acid transamination and decarboxylation; required for synthesis of haemoglobin, serotonin, and GABA; deficiency causes peripheral neuropathy, microcytic anaemia, and convulsions in infants; *Lucent note*: **Vitamin B6 helps in remembering dreams**.
22. **Vitamin B7 (Biotin / Vitamin H)**: Sulfur-containing coenzyme for carboxylation reactions; raw egg white contains the protein **Avidin**, which binds biotin tightly and blocks absorption, causing induced biotin deficiency.
23. **Vitamin B9 (Folic Acid / Folate)**: Required for DNA synthesis and purine/thymidylate production; deficiency causes **Megaloblastic Anaemia**; maternal folate deficiency in periconceptional period causes fatal **Neural Tube Defects (Spina Bifida and Anencephaly)** in newborns.
24. **Vitamin B12 (Cyanocobalamin)**: The only vitamin containing a metallic trace element (**Cobalt**); absent in ordinary plant foods; synthesized exclusively by microorganisms; absorbs in the terminal ileum mediated by **Castle's Intrinsic Factor** secreted by gastric parietal cells; deficiency causes **Pernicious Anaemia** (megaloblastic anaemia + irreversible subacute combined degeneration of the spinal cord).
25. **Minamata Disease**: Severe neurological disease caused by methylmercury poisoning from consuming contaminated fish and shellfish in Minamata Bay, Japan; damages sensory cortex and cerebellum, producing ataxia, sensory numbness, and tremors.
26. **Itai-Itai Disease ("Ouch-Ouch" Disease)**: Chronic **Cadmium ($Cd$)** poisoning occurring in Toyama Prefecture, Japan; causes severe painful osteomalacia, multiple bone fractures, and renal tubular dysfunction.
27. **Blue Baby Syndrome (Infantile Methemoglobinemia)**: Caused by high **Nitrate ($NO_3^-$)** concentrations in rural drinking water ($>45\text{ mg/L}$); intestinal microbes convert nitrate to nitrite, which oxidizes hemoglobin $Fe^{2+}$ into $Fe^{3+}$ **Methemoglobin**, incapable of releasing oxygen to tissues, producing cyanotic slate-blue skin discoloration.
28. **Blackfoot Disease & Arsenicosis**: Chronic **Arsenic ($As$)** toxicity from contaminated tube-well groundwater (prevalent in the Ganga-Brahmaputra alluvial plains of West Bengal and eastern UP); causes severe peripheral vascular gangrene (Blackfoot disease) and hyperkeratosis.
29. **Fluorosis (Skeletal and Dental)**: Chronic ingestion of excess **Fluoride ($F^-$)** in groundwater ($>1.5\text{ mg/L}$); causes mottling of dental enamel (Dental Fluorosis), calcification of spinal ligaments, and crippling skeletal deformities ("Knock-knee" syndrome / Genu valgum).
30. **Wilson's Disease**: Autosomal recessive inborn error of copper metabolism leading to toxic accumulation of **Copper ($Cu$)** in the liver, brain basal ganglia, and cornea (pathognomonic golden-brown **Kayser-Fleischer rings** in the cornea).
31. **Cholera**: Caused by the comma-shaped bacterium ***Vibrio cholerae***; transmitted via fecal-oral contamination of water/food; secretes cholera enterotoxin stimulating massive intestinal chloride secretion, resulting in profuse painless **"rice-water" diarrhea** and severe lethal dehydration; treated with Oral Rehydration Solution (ORS).
32. **Tuberculosis (TB)**: Chronic infectious airborne disease caused by acid-fast bacillus ***Mycobacterium tuberculosis*** (discovered by **Robert Koch, 1882**); primarily attacks lungs (pulmonary TB); confirmed by sputum smear, chest X-ray, and **Mantoux Tuberculin Skin Test**; protected by the **BCG (Bacille Calmette-Guérin)** live attenuated vaccine; treated via WHO **DOTS (Directly Observed Treatment, Short-course)**.
33. **Typhoid Fever (Enteric Fever)**: Caused by the rod-shaped Gram-negative bacterium ***Salmonella typhi***; transmitted via contaminated food and water; classic signs include step-ladder remittent fever, coated tongue, relative bradycardia, and intestinal ulceration; confirmed by the **Widal Test** (detects agglutinating antibodies against O and H antigens).
34. **Tetanus (Lockjaw / धनुस्तंभ)**: Caused by anaerobic, spore-forming, drumstick-shaped soil bacterium ***Clostridium tetani***; enters via contaminated lacerations and rustic puncture wounds; releases the neurotoxin **Tetanospasmin**, which blocks inhibitory glycine/GABA release, causing painful involuntary spasms of masseter muscles (trismus / lockjaw) and arched body posture (opisthotonos); prevented by tetanus toxoid (TT) vaccine.
35. **Diphtheria**: Acute bacterial infection caused by ***Corynebacterium diphtheriae***; forms a thick, grey, leathery pseudomembrane over the tonsils and pharynx, causing lethal airway asphyxiation; confirmed by the **Schick Test**; prevented as part of the **DPT (Diphtheria, Pertussis, Tetanus)** trivalent pediatric vaccine.
36. **Leprosy (Hansen's Disease / कुष्ठ रोग)**: Chronic mycobacterial infection caused by ***Mycobacterium leprae*** (discovered by **Gerhard Hansen, 1873**); targets cooler peripheral nerves and skin; produces hypopigmented anaesthetic skin macules and claw hands; cured by standard **Multi-Drug Therapy (MDT)** (Rifampicin, Dapsone, and Clofazimine).
37. **Poliomyelitis (Polio)**: Enteroviral infection transmitted via fecal-oral route; the **Poliovirus** destroys anterior horn motor neurons of the spinal cord, causing asymmetric flaccid paralysis; prevented by **Salk Vaccine** (inactivated injectable polio vaccine, Jonas Salk, 1952) and **Sabin Vaccine** (live attenuated oral polio vaccine drops, Albert Sabin, 1961).
38. **Rabies (Hydrophobia / अलर्क रोग)**: Fatal neurotropic bullet-shaped rhabdovirus infection transmitted through saliva via bites of rabid mammals (dogs, wolves, bats); virus ascends peripheral nerves into the CNS, causing intense pharyngeal spasm upon attempting to swallow water (**Hydrophobia**); pathognomonic diagnostic **Negri bodies** form inside cerebral neurons; post-exposure rabies vaccine was developed by **Louis Pasteur (1885)**.
39. **Dengue Fever ("Breakbone Fever")**: Caused by the **Dengue flavivirus** (4 serotypes: DENV 1–4); transmitted by the diurnal day-biting mosquito ***Aedes aegypti*** (the tiger mosquito); hallmarks are acute retro-orbital headache, high fever, severe arthralgia/myalgia ("breakbone"), and precipitous drop in **blood platelet count (thrombocytopenia)**; also transmits Chikungunya and Zika viruses.
40. **Malaria**: Mosquito-borne parasitic disease caused by protozoan parasites of the genus ***Plasmodium*** (*P. vivax, P. falciparum, P. malariae, P. ovale*); transmitted exclusively by the bite of infected female ***Anopheles*** mosquitoes; **Sir Ronald Ross (1897, Secunderabad, India)** discovered the mosquito transmission vector; *P. falciparum* causes lethal **Malignant Tertian / Cerebral Malaria**; natural treatment is **Quinine** (extracted from the bark of the **Cinchona tree**, *Cinchona calisaya*).
41. **Kala-Azar (Visceral Leishmaniasis / Dumdum Fever)**: Caused by flagellated protozoan ***Leishmania donovani***; transmitted by the vector **Sandfly (*Phlebotomus argentipes*)**; endemic in Bihar, Jharkhand, and eastern UP; infects the reticuloendothelial system, causing massive hepatosplenomegaly, severe anaemia, and dark skin hyperpigmentation.
42. **Sleeping Sickness (African Trypanosomiasis)**: Caused by protozoan flagellate ***Trypanosoma brucei***; transmitted by the bite of the **Tsetse fly (*Glossina palpalis*)**; attacks the central nervous system, producing daytime sleepiness and fatal coma.
43. **Amoebiasis (Amoebic Dysentery)**: Protozoan intestinal infection caused by ***Entamoeba histolytica***; invades large intestinal mucosa producing characteristic "flask-shaped" ulcers; causes frequent bloody stools with mucus, abdominal cramps, and potential metastatic amoebic liver abscesses.
44. **Cancer Biology & Classification**: Malignant neoplasia characterized by uncontrolled cellular proliferation and loss of contact inhibition: **Carcinoma** (cancers of epithelial origin; accounts for ~85% of all human cancers, e.g., breast, lung, cervical, colon cancer); **Sarcoma** (cancers of mesenchymal/connective tissue origin, e.g., bone/osteosarcoma, muscle, cartilage); **Leukemia** (cancer of hematopoietic white blood cells in bone marrow); **Lymphoma** (cancer of lymphatic nodal tissue, e.g., Hodgkin's disease).
45. **Oncogenes & Metastasis**: Normal cells contain regulatory genes called **Proto-oncogenes**, which mutate into active cancer-causing **Oncogenes** under exposure to physical, chemical, or biological carcinogens; **Metastasis** (detachment, bloodstream dissemination, and colonisation of distant organ sites by malignant tumor cells) is the single most definitive clinical hallmark of malignancy.
46. **Diabetes Mellitus**: Metabolic disorder caused by absolute deficiency of insulin (**Type 1 Diabetes**, autoimmune destruction of pancreatic beta cells) or peripheral tissue insulin resistance (**Type 2 Diabetes**); characterized by fasting hyperglycemia, **Glycosuria** (glucose in urine), polyuria (excessive urination), polydipsia (excessive thirst), and polyphagia; diagnosed by Fasting Blood Sugar and **$HbA_{1c}$ test** ($\ge 6.5\%$ indicates diabetes).
47. **Diabetes Insipidus**: Rare endocrinological disorder completely unrelated to insulin or blood sugar levels; caused by deficiency of **Antidiuretic Hormone (ADH / Vasopressin)** secreted by the posterior pituitary or renal insensitivity to ADH; characterized by massive excretion of extremely dilute, hypotonic urine (up to 15–20 litres/day) and extreme thirst; urine is non-sweet (lacks glucose entirely).
48. **Vaccine Platforms**: **Inactivated / Killed Vaccines**: Pathogen destroyed by heat or chemical formalin (e.g., Covaxin, Salk Polio vaccine, Rabies vaccine); **Live Attenuated Vaccines**: Weakened living pathogen inducing strong lifelong immunity (e.g., BCG, MMR, Sabin Oral Polio vaccine, Yellow Fever vaccine); **Subunit / Recombinant Vaccines**: Specific antigenic fragments (e.g., Hepatitis B recombinant surface antigen, Corbevax); **mRNA Vaccines**: Synthetic mRNA in lipid nanoparticles (e.g., Pfizer-BioNTech, Moderna); **DNA Vaccines**: Plasmid DNA encoding viral antigen (e.g., ZyCoV-D, world's first needle-free plasmid DNA vaccine).
49. **Landmark Clinical Diagnostic Tests**: **ELISA (Enzyme-Linked Immunosorbent Assay)** and **Western Blot**: Standard screening and confirmatory tests for HIV / AIDS antibodies; **Widal Test**: Diagnostic agglutination test for Typhoid fever; **Mantoux Test / Tuberculin Skin Test**: Diagnostic delayed hypersensitivity test for Tuberculosis exposure; **Schick Test**: Susceptibility test for Diphtheria; **Biopsy / Histopathology**: Gold-standard definitive diagnostic test for cancer malignancy; **Amniocentesis**: Prenatal testing of amniotic fluid for fetal chromosomal abnormalities (e.g., Down syndrome), strictly regulated in India under the PCPNDT Act to prevent female foeticide.
50. **Clinical Diagnostic Instruments**: **Electrocardiogram (ECG)**: Graphical recording of cardiac electrical conduction; **Electroencephalogram (EEG)**: Recording of cerebral cortex neuroelectrical activity (diagnoses epilepsy, coma, brain death); **Electromyogram (EMG)**: Recording of skeletal muscle and peripheral nerve electrical potential; **Endoscopy**: Uses flexible fiber-optic bundles applying **Total Internal Reflection** to inspect hollow internal viscera (stomach, duodenum).
51. **Vitamins Classification & Solubilities:** Term 'Vitamine' coined by Polish biochemist **Casimir Funk (1912)**; organic micronutrients essential in trace amounts; classified into: (1) **Fat-Soluble Vitamins:** **A, D, E, K** (stored in liver and adipose tissue; excess can cause hypervitaminosis); (2) **Water-Soluble Vitamins:** **B-Complex and Vitamin C** (excreted in urine; require daily dietary replenishment; destroyed by heat/cooking).
52. **Vitamin A (Retinol):** Precursor is beta-carotene (yellow/orange fruits, carrots, golden rice); component of visual pigment **Rhodopsin** in rod cells of retina; deficiency causes **Nyctalopia (Night Blindness)** and **Xerophthalmia** (dry keratinized cornea leading to irreversible blindness); Bitot's spots.
53. **Vitamin B-Complex Spectrum:** (1) **B1 (Thiamine):** Deficiency causes **Beriberi** (wet beriberi affects heart, dry beriberi affects nervous system); common in populations consuming polished/milled white rice; (2) **B2 (Riboflavin):** Yellow enzyme cofactor (FMN/FAD); deficiency causes **Cheilosis** (cracking at corners of mouth), glossitis (red tongue), and photophobia; (3) **B3 (Niacin / Nicotinic acid):** Deficiency causes **Pellagra** (characterized by the **4Ds: Dermatitis, Diarrhea, Dementia, and Death**); common in maize-staple diets.
54. **Vitamin B-Complex Spectrum (Continued):** (4) **B5 (Pantothenic Acid):** Part of Coenzyme A; deficiency causes 'Burning Feet Syndrome'; (5) **B6 (Pyridoxine):** Essential for amino acid metabolism and neurotransmitter synthesis; deficiency causes peripheral neuropathy and convulsions in infants; (6) **B7 / H (Biotin):** Sulphur-containing; egg white contains *Avidin* which binds biotin preventing absorption; (7) **B9 (Folic Acid):** Deficiency causes **Megaloblastic Anemia** and fetal **Neural Tube Defects (Spina Bifida)** during pregnancy; (8) **B12 (Cyanocobalamin):** Only vitamin containing a metal ion (**Cobalt**); synthesized only by intestinal microbes (absent in plant foods); absorbed with gastric **Intrinsic Factor of Castle**; deficiency causes **Pernicious Anemia** (destruction of gastric parietal cells) and spinal cord degeneration.
55. **Vitamin C (Ascorbic Acid):** Strong water-soluble antioxidant; essential for **Collagen synthesis**, wound healing, and non-heme iron absorption; heat-labile (destroyed by boiling); richest natural sources: **Aonla (Indian Gooseberry)**, Guava, Citrus fruits (lemons, oranges); deficiency causes **Scurvy** (bleeding gums, skin petechiae, delayed wound healing, loose teeth).
56. **Vitamin D (Calciferol) & Vitamin E & K:** (1) **Vitamin D (Cholecalciferol):** 'Sunshine Vitamin'; synthesized in skin from 7-dehydrocholesterol by solar UV-B radiation; functions as a hormone regulating calcium and phosphate absorption; deficiency causes **Rickets** in children (bowed legs, pigeon chest) and **Osteomalacia** in adults (soft brittle bones); (2) **Vitamin E (Tocopherol):** 'Beauty Vitamin' / Anti-sterility vitamin; potent lipid antioxidant protecting cell membranes; deficiency causes hemolytic anemia and infertility; (3) **Vitamin K (Phylloquinone / Menaquinone):** Essential for hepatic synthesis of blood clotting factors **II (Prothrombin), VII, IX, and X**; deficiency causes delayed blood coagulation and hemorrhagic disease.
57. **Major Bacterial Diseases in Humans:** (1) **Tuberculosis (TB):** *Mycobacterium tuberculosis*; acid-fast bacillus discovered by **Robert Koch (1882)**; airborne; Mantoux test diagnostic; BCG vaccine preventive; (2) **Cholera:** *Vibrio cholerae*; waterborne comma-shaped bacterium; profuse 'rice-water stools'; dehydration treated with ORS; (3) **Typhoid:** *Salmonella typhi*; transmitted via contaminated food/water; affects Peyer's patches of intestine; diagnosed by **Widal Test**; (4) **Tetanus ('Lockjaw'):** *Clostridium tetani*; anaerobic spore-former in soil; produces neurotoxin *tetanospasmin*; prevented by ATS / Tetanus toxoid vaccine; (5) **Diphtheria:** *Corynebacterium diphtheriae*; greyish pseudomembrane over throat; **Schick Test** diagnostic; part of DPT triple vaccine.
58. **Major Viral Diseases in Humans:** (1) **Poliomyelitis:** Enterovirus; destroys anterior horn motor neurons of spinal cord causing infantile flaccid paralysis; injectable killed Salk vaccine (1955) and oral live Sabin vaccine (1961); India declared polio-free by WHO in 2014; (2) **Rabies (Hydrophobia):** Rhabdovirus; bullet-shaped negative-strand RNA; transmitted via bite of rabid animals (dogs, bats); fatal acute encephalomyelitis; Louis Pasteur developed rabies vaccine; (3) **Hepatitis:** Viral inflammation of liver; Hepatitis A & E waterborne (fecal-oral route); Hepatitis B & C blood-borne and sexually transmitted (Hepatitis B has recombinant DNA vaccine); (4) **Dengue Fever ('Breakbone Fever'):** Flavivirus transmitted by daytime-biting female **Aedes aegypti** mosquito; causes acute drop in blood platelet count (thrombocytopenia); (5) **Chikungunya & Zika:** Also transmitted by *Aedes aegypti* mosquito.
59. **Protozoan & Helminthic Infections:** (1) **Malaria:** Caused by protozoan parasite **Plasmodium** (*P. vivax, P. falciparum* [deadliest, causes cerebral malaria], *P. malariae, P. ovale*); life cycle elucidated by **Sir Ronald Ross (1897)** at Begumpet, Hyderabad (awarded Nobel Prize 1902); vector is female **Anopheles** mosquito; parasite infects human liver cells (*sporozoites*) and RBCs (*merozoites*); rupture of RBCs releases toxic pigment **Haemozoin** causing cyclic chills and high fever; treated with **Quinine** (extracted from bark of *Cinchona* tree) and Artemisinin; (2) **Kala-Azar (Visceral Leishmaniasis / 'Black Fever'):** Caused by *Leishmania donovani*; vector is the **Sandfly (Phlebotomus)**; causes enlargement of spleen (splenomegaly), liver, and severe anemia; (3) **Amoebiasis / Amoebic Dysentery:** *Entamoeba histolytica*; infects large intestine; cysts passed in stool; (4) **Filariasis / Elephantiasis:** Helminthic roundworm *Wuchereria bancrofti*; transmitted by **Culex mosquito**; blocks lymphatic vessels causing grotesque swelling of lower limbs and scrotum.
60. **Antibiotics & Diagnostic Tests Master Key:** **Penicillin:** First commercial antibiotic discovered accidentally by **Alexander Fleming (1928)** from fungus *Penicillium notatum*; **Diagnostic Tests:** (1) Widal Test -> Typhoid; (2) Mantoux Test -> Tuberculosis; (3) ELISA & Western Blot -> HIV/AIDS; (4) Schick Test -> Diphtheria; (5) Dick Test -> Scarlet Fever; (6) VDRL / Wassermann Test -> Syphilis; (7) Biopsy -> Cancer diagnosis.

## 🧠 Memory Anchors & Mnemonics

- **D** = Vitamin D (Calciferol — Bone mineralization / Sunlight hormone)
