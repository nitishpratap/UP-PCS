## 🎯 Consolidated Must-Score Facts

1. **Tube-wells (Nalkoop)** account for approximately **74.9%** of the net irrigated area in UP.
2. **Canals (Nahar)** constitute the second largest source of irrigation in UP (~**15.2%**).
3. **Private tube-wells** alone irrigate over 71% of UP's cultivated farm area.
4. **Matatila Dam (Rani Lakshmibai Dam)** is built on the **Betwa River** in **Lalitpur district**.
5. **Rihand Dam (Govind Ballabh Pant Sagar)**, the largest reservoir by volume in India, is built on the **Rihand River** at Pipri (**Sonbhadra**).
6. **Chandauli district** is traditionally known as the **"Rice Bowl of Uttar Pradesh"** (Dhan ka Katora).
7. **Malihabad (Lucknow)** is world-renowned for its GI-tagged **Dasheri Mango**.
8. **Prayagraj (Allahabad)** and Kaushambi are famous for the **Allahabad Surkha Guava** (red flesh, GI-tagged).
9. **Pratapgarh district** is the largest producer of **Aonla (Indian Gooseberry)** in India.
10. **Mahoba** is historically celebrated for its high-grade **Paan (Betel Vine / Bareja)** cultivation.
11. **Chandra Shekhar Azad University of Agriculture & Technology (CSAUAT)** was established at **Kanpur in 1975**.
12. **Acharya Narendra Deva University of Agriculture & Technology (ANDUAT)** was established at **Kumarganj (Ayodhya) in 1975**.
13. **Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel University of Agriculture & Technology (SVPUAT)** was established at **Meerut in 2000**.
14. **Banda University of Agriculture & Technology (BUAT)** was established at **Banda in 2010**.
15. **Rani Lakshmi Bai Central Agricultural University** was established in **Jhansi in 2014** by an Act of Parliament.
16. **Western Uttar Pradesh** was the principal beneficiary of the **Green Revolution (1960s)** in UP due to canal and tubewell networks.
17. **Bundelkhand** is the primary **pulses (gram, lentil, arhar)** and **oilseeds (sesame, mustard)** producing region of UP.
18. **Saryupar Plain** (Gorakhpur, Deoria, Basti) has witnessed the fastest growth in shallow tubewell irrigation.
19. **Badua Dam** is located in **Bihar** (often placed as a distractor in UP PSC dam matching questions).
20. **Ahraura Dam, Adwa Dam, and Sirsi Dam** are located in **Mirzapur district**.
21. **Parichha Dam** is built on the **Betwa River in Jhansi district**.
22. **Total Geographical Area**: 240.93 lakh hectares; **Net Sown Area**: ~165.7 lakh hectares (~68.7% of total area).
23. **Cropping Intensity**: ~160%–165%, indicating extensive double-cropping facilitated by pervasive tube-well irrigation and canal networks.
