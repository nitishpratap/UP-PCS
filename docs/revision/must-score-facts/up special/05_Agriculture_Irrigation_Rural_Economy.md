## 🎯 Consolidated Must-Score Facts

1. Uttar Pradesh is divided into **9 Agro-Climatic Zones** by the Planning Commission.
2. The **South-Western Semi-Arid Zone** (Agra, Mathura) receives the **lowest rainfall** in Uttar Pradesh.
3. The **Bhabhar & Terai Zone** receives the **highest rainfall** in Uttar Pradesh.
4. **Tube-wells (Nalkoop)** account for approximately **74.9%** of the net irrigated area in UP.
5. **Canals (Nahar)** constitute the second largest source of irrigation in UP (~**15.2%**).
6. **Private tube-wells** alone irrigate over 71% of UP's cultivated farm area.
7. Uttar Pradesh has the largest total replenishable groundwater resource in India (~**40.7 bcm**).
8. The **Upper Ganga Canal** was opened on **8 April 1854**, engineered by **Sir Proby Cautley** with its offtake at **Bhimgoda, Haridwar**.
9. The **Lower Ganga Canal** was commissioned in **1878** with its offtake barrage at **Narora, Bulandshahr**.
10. The **Sharda Canal System**, commissioned in **1928** from **Banbasa**, is the **longest canal network** in UP (~12,368 km total system).
11. The **Eastern Yamuna Canal** has its offtake at **Tajewala / Hathinikund Barrage** (Saharanpur border).
12. The **Agra Canal** was opened in **1874** with its headworks at **Okhla Barrage (Delhi)** on the Yamuna.
13. The **Saryu Nahar National Project**, dedicated in December 2021, interlinks 5 rivers (Ghaghara, Saryu, Rapti, Banganga, Rohini) across 9 districts.
14. The **Ken-Betwa River Interlinking Project** is India's first national river interlink, transferring water via **Daudhan Dam** to Bundelkhand.
15. **Matatila Dam (Rani Lakshmibai Dam)** is built on the **Betwa River** in **Lalitpur district**.
16. **Rihand Dam (Govind Ballabh Pant Sagar)**, the largest reservoir by volume in India, is built on the **Rihand River** at Pipri (**Sonbhadra**).
17. **Chandauli district** is traditionally known as the **"Rice Bowl of Uttar Pradesh"** (Dhan ka Katora).
18. Uttar Pradesh ranks **#1 in India** in the production of **Wheat, Sugarcane, Potato, and Milk**.
19. UP produces over **32%–35% of total wheat** and ~**35% of total potatoes** in India.
20. The International Potato Center - South Asia Regional Centre (**CIP-SARC**) is located at **Singna, Agra** (NOT Aligarh).
21. The International Rice Research Institute - South Asia Regional Centre (**IRRI-SARC**) is located at **Varanasi**.
22. The **Indian Institute of Pulses Research (IIPR)** is situated at **Kalyanpur, Kanpur**.
23. The **Indian Institute of Sugarcane Research (IISR)** is situated at **Lucknow**.
24. The **National Sugar Institute (NSI)** is situated at **Kalyanpur, Kanpur** (under the Ministry of Consumer Affairs, GoI).
25. The **Indian Institute of Vegetable Research (IIVR)** is situated at **Jakhini, Varanasi**.
26. The **Indian Veterinary Research Institute (IVRI)** and **Central Avian Research Institute (CARI)** are located at **Izatnagar, Bareilly**.
27. The **Central Institute for Research on Goats (CIRG)** is located at **Makhdoom, Farah in Mathura district**.
28. The **Indian Grassland and Fodder Research Institute (IGFRI)** and **CAFRI** are located at **Jhansi**.
29. The **Central Institute for Subtropical Horticulture (CISH)** is situated at **Rehmankhera, Lucknow**.
30. The **Central Institute of Medicinal and Aromatic Plants (CIMAP)** is located at **Lucknow**.
31. Uttar Pradesh accounts for over **80% of India's total Mentha (Peppermint) oil output**, centred in **Barabanki, Badaun, Rampur, and Sambhal**.
32. **Malihabad (Lucknow)** is world-renowned for its GI-tagged **Dasheri Mango**.
33. **Prayagraj (Allahabad)** and Kaushambi are famous for the **Allahabad Surkha Guava** (red flesh, GI-tagged).
34. **Pratapgarh district** is the largest producer of **Aonla (Indian Gooseberry)** in India.
35. **Mahoba** is historically celebrated for its high-grade **Paan (Betel Vine / Bareja)** cultivation.
36. **Chandra Shekhar Azad University of Agriculture & Technology (CSAUAT)** was established at **Kanpur in 1975**.
37. **Acharya Narendra Deva University of Agriculture & Technology (ANDUAT)** was established at **Kumarganj (Ayodhya) in 1975**.
38. **Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel University of Agriculture & Technology (SVPUAT)** was established at **Meerut in 2000**.
39. **Banda University of Agriculture & Technology (BUAT)** was established at **Banda in 2010**.
40. **Rani Lakshmi Bai Central Agricultural University** was established in **Jhansi in 2014** by an Act of Parliament.
41. The first agricultural university of undivided Uttar Pradesh was **Govind Ballabh Pant University of Agriculture & Technology (GBPUAT)**, set up at **Pantnagar in 1960** (now in Uttarakhand).
42. The **Arjun Sahayak Project** provides irrigation to Mahoba, Banda, and Hamirpur using waters of the Dhasan River.
43. **Western Uttar Pradesh** was the principal beneficiary of the **Green Revolution (1960s)** in UP due to canal and tubewell networks.
44. **Bundelkhand** is the primary **pulses (gram, lentil, arhar)** and **oilseeds (sesame, mustard)** producing region of UP.
45. **Saryupar Plain** (Gorakhpur, Deoria, Basti) has witnessed the fastest growth in shallow tubewell irrigation.
46. The **Pradeshik Cooperative Dairy Federation (PCDF)** markets milk under the popular brand name **"Parag"**.
47. Under the UP Bovine Breeding Policy, the **Frieswal crossbred cattle** was developed at **CIRC, Meerut**.
48. **Badua Dam** is located in **Bihar** (often placed as a distractor in UP PSC dam matching questions).
49. **Ahraura Dam, Adwa Dam, and Sirsi Dam** are located in **Mirzapur district**.
50. **Parichha Dam** is built on the **Betwa River in Jhansi district**.
