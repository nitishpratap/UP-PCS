## 🎯 Consolidated Must-Score Facts

1. **Uttar Pradesh Diwas (UP Day)** is officially celebrated every year on **24 January**.
2. On **24 January 1950**, the United Provinces was officially renamed as **Uttar Pradesh**.
3. The official **State Seal / Emblem** of Uttar Pradesh was formally adopted in **1938**.
4. The State Emblem of UP incorporates a **Bow and Arrow** (Lord Rama), a **Pair of Fish** (Nawabs of Awadh), and **Three Flowing Streams** (Triveni Sangam).
5. The **State Animal** of Uttar Pradesh is the **Swamp Deer (Barasingha)** (*Rucervus duvaucelii*).
6. The **State Bird** of Uttar Pradesh is the **Sarus Crane** (*Antigone antigone*).
7. The **State Tree** of Uttar Pradesh is the **Ashoka (Sita Ashoka)** (*Saraca asoca*).
8. The **State Flower** of Uttar Pradesh is the **Palash / Dhak / Tesu** (*Butea monosperma*).
9. **Palash** was officially declared as the State Flower on **4 January 2011**, replacing Brahmakamal.
10. The **State Aquatic Animal** of Uttar Pradesh is the **Gangetic River Dolphin** (*Platanista gangetica*).
11. The financial assistance under **Mukhyamantri Kanya Sumangala Yojana** is provided in **6 distinct stages**, totaling **₹25,000**.
12. Under Kanya Sumangala Yojana, the annual family income ceiling is capped at **₹3 Lakh**.
13. The **Mukhyamantri Abhyudaya Yojana** was launched on **Basant Panchami in February 2021** to provide free competitive exam coaching.
14. The **Vishwakarma Shram Samman Yojana** provides free 6-day skill training, modern toolkits, and subsidized bank loans to traditional village craftsmen.
15. The **Swami Vivekananda Youth Empowerment Scheme** distributes free smartphones and tablets to final-year college/technical students.
16. The **Nand Baba Milk Mission** was launched with an outlay of **₹1,000 Crore** in **June 2023** to boost indigenous bovine milk output.
17. The **Nandini Krishak Samriddhi Yojana** provides a 50% capital subsidy for establishing 25-cow dairy farms of indigenous breeds.
18. **Kamdhenu Dairy Yojana** is an older standalone scheme and is **NOT** a component of the current Nand Baba Milk Mission.
19. Under the **UP Krishak Durghatna Kalyan Yojana**, compensation of **₹5 Lakh** is provided for accidental death or permanent disability of farmers.
20. **Mission Shakti** was launched during Shardiya Navratri in **October 2020** to ensure women's security, dignity, and self-reliance.
21. Dedicated **Women Help Desks (Mahila Helpdesk)** have been set up in all **1,535+ police stations** across Uttar Pradesh.
22. **Nivesh Mitra** is Uttar Pradesh's flagship digital single-window portal for industrial clearances and investor facilitation.
23. The **UP Gaurav Samman** is the highest state civilian honor, carrying a cash prize of **₹11 Lakh**.
24. The highest Hindi literary award of the Uttar Pradesh Hindi Sansthan is the **Bharat Bharti Puraskar**, carrying a cash award of **₹8 Lakh**.
25. The first recipient of the prestigious Bharat Bharti Puraskar was **Mahadevi Varma (1982)**.
26. The highest sports awards in Uttar Pradesh are the **Laxman Award (for Men)** and **Rani Lakshmibai Award (for Women)**, carrying a cash prize of **₹3.11 Lakh**.
27. **Smt. Sarojini Naidu** was the **First Woman Governor** of Uttar Pradesh and in India (1947–1949).
28. **Pandit Govind Ballabh Pant** was the **First Chief Minister** of Uttar Pradesh (1950–1954).
29. **Smt. Sucheta Kripalani** was the **First Woman Chief Minister** of Uttar Pradesh and in India (1963–1967).
30. The first Chief Justice of the High Court of Judicature at Agra (1866) was **Sir Walter Morgan**.
31. The first Indian Chief Justice of the Allahabad High Court after independence was **Justice Bidhu Bhushan Malik**.
32. The first Lokayukta of Uttar Pradesh was **Justice Vishwambhar Dayal** (appointed September 1977).
33. India's first modern vacuum-pan sugar mill was established in **1903 at Pratappur in Deoria district**.
34. The **Yamuna Expressway** (165 km) was the first access-controlled super expressway built in Uttar Pradesh.
35. The world's longest river cruise, **MV Ganga Vilas**, was flagged off from **Varanasi** to Dibrugarh (via Bangladesh) in **January 2023**.
36. **Varanasi** was declared the first **Cultural and Tourism Capital of the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO)** for 2022–2023.
37. **Kannauj** is known as the **"Attar Nagari"** and the **"Grasse of the East"**.
38. **Firozabad** is famous as **"Suhag Nagari"** and the **"Glass City of India"**.
39. **Moradabad** is internationally renowned as **"Peetal Nagari" (Brass City)**.
40. **Aligarh** is known as **"Taala Nagari" (City of Locks)**.
41. **Bhadohi** is celebrated as the **"Carpet City" (Kaleen Nagari)**.
42. **Kanpur** was historically called the **"Manchester of the East"** and the **"Leather City of the World"**.
43. **Jaunpur** was nicknamed **"Shiraz-e-Hind"** during the Sharqi Sultanate.
44. **Meerut** is known as **"Kranti Nagar"** (1857 spark) and **"Kainchi Nagari" (Scissor City)**.
45. **Pratapgarh** is renowned as **"Aonla Nagari"** (Gooseberry Capital).
46. **Malihabad (Lucknow)** is celebrated as the **"Mango City"** for its world-famous Dasheri mango orchards.
47. **Sonbhadra** is known as the **"Energy Capital of India"** due to its pithead mega thermal power plants.
48. Under the **One District One Cuisine (ODOC)** scheme launched in 2026, signature traditional culinary dishes of all 75 districts are branded and promoted.
49. The **Ramnagar Ramlila of Varanasi** (2008) and **Kumbh Mela of Prayagraj** (2017) are inscribed on **UNESCO's Intangible Cultural Heritage List**.
50. North India's first hyperscale data centre, **Yotta D1**, was inaugurated at **Greater Noida** in 2022.
