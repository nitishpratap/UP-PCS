## 🎯 Consolidated Must-Score Facts

1. **712 CE:** Muhammad bin Qasim captures Debal and Multan, defeating Raja Dahir of Sind; marks the first Arab conquest and first levy of **Jizya** in the subcontinent.
2. **c. 750 CE:** Dantidurga founds the **Rashtrakuta dynasty** at Manyakheta, performing the *Hiranyagarbha* mahadana at Ujjain; Gopala is elected founder of the **Pala dynasty** in Bengal.
3. **c. 750–1000 CE:** **Tripartite Struggle (त्रिपक्षीय संघर्ष)** among the Palas (Bengal), Gurjara-Pratiharas (western India), and Rashtrakutas (Deccan) for imperial mastery over **Kannauj (Kanyakubja)**.
4. **836–885 CE:** Mihir Bhoja of the Gurjara-Pratiharas reigns from Kannauj, assuming the title **Adivaraha (आदिवराह)** and praised by Arab chronicler Sulaiman.
5. **949 CE:** **Battle of Takkolam** — Rashtrakuta King Krishna III decisively defeats the imperial Cholas, killing crown prince Rajaditya on elephant-back.
6. **985–1014 CE:** Reign of **Rajaraja I (Chola)**; defeats Chera navy at Kandalur Salai, conquers northern Sri Lanka, and completes the **Brihadishwara Temple (1010 CE)** at Thanjavur.
7. **1014–1044 CE:** Reign of **Rajendra I (Chola)**; conquers all of Sri Lanka (capturing King Mahendra V), defeats Mahipala of Bengal, founds **Gangaikondacholapuram (c. 1025 CE)**, and launches naval raids against Srivijaya/Kadaram.
8. **1000–1027 CE:** **Mahmud of Ghazni** leads 17 plundering expeditions into northern India; Abu Raihan **Al-Biruni** accompanies his campaigns (*Kitab-al-Hind*).
9. **1025–1026 CE:** Mahmud of Ghazni's most famous 16th expedition sacks the **Somnath Temple** in Gujarat during the reign of Solanki King Bhima I.
10. **1191 CE:** **First Battle of Tarain** — Rajput confederacy led by **Prithviraj Chauhan III** of Ajmer/Delhi decisively routs Mu'izz al-Din Muhammad Ghori.
11. **1192 CE:** **Second Battle of Tarain** — Muhammad Ghori returns with superior Turkish mounted archers and decisively crushes Prithviraj Chauhan, establishing Turkish rule in North India.
12. **1194 CE:** **Battle of Chandawar** — Muhammad Ghori defeats and slays **Jay Chandra**, the Gahadavala monarch of Kannauj and Varanasi.
13. **1206 CE:** Qutbuddin Aibak establishes the **Delhi Sultanate** at Lahore upon the assassination of Muhammad Ghori; initiates the **Slave (Mamluk) Dynasty (1206–1290 CE)**.
14. **1210–1236 CE:** **Shamsuddin Iltutmish** consolidates the Delhi Sultanate; shifts capital from Lahore to **Delhi**; introduces pure silver **Tanka (175 gr)** and copper **Jital**; institutes the corporate oligarchy of forty Turkish nobles (**Turkan-i-Chahalgani**).
15. **1221 CE:** **Genghis Khan** pursues Jalaluddin Mangbarani to the banks of the Indus; Iltutmish astutely refuses asylum, saving the nascent Sultanate from Mongol devastation.
16. **1236–1240 CE:** Reign of **Razia Sultan** — first and only sovereign female monarch of the Delhi Sultanate.
17. **1266–1287 CE:** Reign of **Ghiyasuddin Balban**; implements the ruthless **Blood and Iron policy (लौह एवं रक्त)**, liquidates the Chahalgani, and introduces Persian court etiquette (**Sijda, Paibos, Nauroz**).
18. **1290 CE:** **Khalji Revolution** — Jalaluddin Khalji overthrows the Slave dynasty, ending the monopoly of the Ilbari Turkish aristocracy and founding the **Khalji Dynasty (1290–1320 CE)**.
19. **1296–1316 CE:** Reign of **Alauddin Khalji**; fixes prices via **Shahna-i-Mandi** and Diwan-i-Riyasat, introduces horse-branding (**Dagh**) and troop rolls (**Chehra**), institutes 50% land tax based on measurement (**Masahat**).
20. **1301 CE & 1303 CE:** Alauddin Khalji captures **Ranthambore (1301)** from Hammiradeva and **Chittor (1303)** from Rana Ratan Singh (witnessing Rani Padmini's Jauhar).
21. **1309–1311 CE:** **Malik Kafur's** southern military blitzkrieg captures Devagiri (Yadavas), Warangal (Kakatiyas), Dvarasamudra (Hoysalas), and Madurai (Pandyas).
22. **1320 CE:** Ghiyasuddin Tughlaq (Ghazi Malik) slays Khusrau Khan and establishes the **Tughlaq Dynasty (1320–1414 CE)**, the longest-ruling dynasty of the Delhi Sultanate (94 years).
23. **1325–1351 CE:** Reign of **Muhammad bin Tughlaq (Jauna Khan)**; launches five radical experiments: Doab tax hike (1326), capital transfer to Daulatabad (1327), token copper currency (1329–30), Khurasan expedition (1330), Qarachil expedition (1330–31).
24. **1336 CE:** Foundation of the **Vijayanagara Empire** on the Tungabhadra by brothers **Harihara I and Bukka I** (Sangama dynasty) under the spiritual guidance of sage Vidyaranya.
25. **1347 CE:** Foundation of the **Bahmani Kingdom** at Gulbarga by **Alauddin Hasan Bahman Shah (Hasan Gangu)** in rebellion against Muhammad bin Tughlaq.
26. **1351–1388 CE:** Reign of **Firoz Shah Tughlaq**; builds extensive canal networks, founds cities (Jaunpur, Firozabad, Hissar), levies irrigation tax (**Haqq-i-Sharb**), and imposes **Jizya on Brahmins**.
27. **1398 CE:** Central Asian conqueror **Amir Timur (Tamerlane)** invades India, sacking and massacring Delhi during the reign of Nasiruddin Mahmud Tughlaq.
28. **1414 CE:** Khizr Khan founds the **Sayyid Dynasty (1414–1451 CE)** as a Timurid viceroy, assuming the modest title *Raiyat-i-Ala*.
29. **1451 CE:** Bahlul Lodi founds the **Lodi Dynasty (1451–1526 CE)**, the first Afghan dynasty in India; introduces the heavy copper *Bahloli* coin.
30. **1489–1517 CE:** Reign of **Sikandar Lodi**; founds the city of **Agra in 1504 CE** (made capital in 1506), introduces the 39-digit measuring yard **Gaz-i-Sikandari**, and composes Persian poetry under pen-name *Gulrukhi*.
31. **1509–1529 CE:** Zenith of the Vijayanagara Empire under **Krishnadeva Raya (Tuluva dynasty)**; author of Telugu statecraft epic *Amuktamalyada*; patron of the *Ashtadiggajas*.
32. **21 April 1526:** **First Battle of Panipat** — Zahiruddin Muhammad **Babur** defeats and slays **Ibrahim Lodi** using Ottoman *Tulughma* and gun-carts (*Araba*), founding the **Mughal Empire**.
33. **16 March 1527:** **Battle of Khanwa** — Babur crushes the Rajput confederacy led by **Rana Sanga** of Mewar, declaring Jihad and assuming the title **Ghazi**.
34. **26 June 1539:** **Battle of Chausa** — Afghan leader Sher Khan routs **Humayun** on the banks of the Ganges, assuming the imperial title **Sher Shah**.
35. **17 May 1540:** **Battle of Kannauj (Bilgram)** — Sher Shah Suri decisively defeats Humayun, expelling the Mughals and establishing the **Second Afghan Empire (1540–1555 CE)**.
36. **1540–1545 CE:** Administrative zenith of **Sher Shah Suri**; builds the **Grand Trunk Road (Sadak-i-Azam)** from Sonargaon to the Indus, introduces the pure silver **Rupiya (178 gr)** and copper **Dam (380 gr)**, and institutes land measurement (**Patta & Qabuliyat**).
37. **22 May 1545:** Sher Shah fatally burned by an accidental gunpowder explosion (*Uqqa*) during the siege of **Kalinjar Fort (Banda, UP)**.
38. **22 June 1555:** **Battle of Sirhind** — Humayun and Bairam Khan defeat Sikandar Suri, restoring the Mughal dynasty to Delhi.
39. **5 November 1556:** **Second Battle of Panipat** — 13-year-old **Akbar** (guided by regent Bairam Khan) defeats Hemu (Hemchandra Vikramaditya), securing the Mughal restoration.
40. **1564 CE:** Akbar formally abolishes the religious poll tax **Jizya** on non-Muslim subjects (reimposed 115 years later by Aurangzeb in 1679).
41. **23 January 1565:** **Battle of Talikota (Rakshasi-Tangadi)** — The combined confederacy of four Deccan Sultanates (Bijapur, Ahmadnagar, Golconda, Bidar; **Berar was ABSENT**) destroys the army of Vijayanagara, slaying regent Rama Raya and sacking Hampi.
42. **18 June 1576:** **Battle of Haldighati** — Imperial Mughal army led by Raja Man Singh of Amber clashes with **Maharana Pratap** of Mewar; Pratap sustains heroic resistance from the Aravalli hills.
43. **1579 CE & 1582 CE:** Akbar issues the **Mahzar** (Infallibility Decree) in September 1579, assuming supreme arbiter status (*Imam-i-Adil*), and promulgates the syncretic ethical code **Din-i-Ilahi** in 1582.
44. **1580 CE:** Introduction of the **Dahsala / Zabt System** of land revenue by Akbar's finance minister **Raja Todar Mal**, basing revenue on 10-year average crop yields and price indices.
45. **1600 CE:** Charter granted by Queen Elizabeth I establishing the English East India Company on 31 December 1600 (during the reign of Akbar).
46. **1606 CE:** Execution of the 5th Sikh Guru, **Guru Arjan Dev**, at Lahore by order of Emperor Jahangir; Guru Hargobind constructs the **Akal Takht** and adopts the twin swords of **Miri and Piri**.
47. **6 June 1674:** Formal coronation of **Shivaji Maharaj** as *Chhatrapati* at **Raigad Fort** by Varanasi scholar **Gaga Bhatt**; took title *Haidava Dharmoddharak* and initiated the *Rajyabhisheka Shaka* era.
48. **24 November 1675:** Execution of the 9th Sikh Guru, **Guru Tegh Bahadur** (*Hind ki Chadar*), at Chandni Chowk, Delhi, by order of Aurangzeb; site marked by Gurdwara Sis Ganj Sahib.
49. **13 April 1699:** **Guru Gobind Singh** establishes the **Khalsa** at Anandpur Sahib on Baisakhi day, ordaining the Panj Pyare and the Five Ks.
50. **3 March 1707:** Demise of Emperor **Aurangzeb (Alamgir)** at Ahmadnagar after a 49-year reign; initiates the rapid decline of the Mughal Empire and rise of autonomous regional kingdoms.
