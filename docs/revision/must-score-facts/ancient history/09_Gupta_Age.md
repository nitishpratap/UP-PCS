## 🎯 Consolidated Must-Score Facts

1. **Sri Gupta** and **Ghatotkacha** carry only the title **Maharaja** (महाराज). The first Gupta **Maharajadhiraja** (महाराजाधिराज) is **Chandragupta I**.
2. **Samudragupta** is the digvijaya (**Digvijaya** / दिग्विजय) king. **V. A. Smith** called him the **Napoleon of India**. In the north he **uprooted** nine kings and annexed land; in the south he **defeated then restored** twelve kings as tribute.
3. **Chandragupta II (Vikramaditya)** (विक्रमादित्य) ended the Western Kshatrapas (क्षत्रप) by defeating **Rudrasimha III**. He took the title **Sakari** (शकारि - destroyer of Shakas). **Ujjain** (उज्जैन) became a second pole while Pataliputra stayed the old capital.
4. **Fa-Hien** (फाह्यान) visited India about **399–414 CE** under Chandragupta II. Traveller order is **Fa-Hien → Hiuen Tsang → I-Tsing → Al-Biruni**.
5. **Kumaragupta I (Mahendraditya)** (महेन्द्रादित्य) is the traditional founder-patron of **Nalanda** (नालंदा). Chinese visitors later studied there; they did not found it.
6. **Skandagupta** is the last great imperial Gupta. The **Bhitari** pillar (Ghazipur, UP) records his **Huna** war, and he also repaired the **Sudarsana** lake at Girnar.
7. **Nalanda** is Gupta (Kumaragupta I). **Takshashila** is the older Gandhara town. **Vikramashila** (विक्रमशिला) is Pala **Dharmapala** (धर्मपाल). **Vallabhi** is Maitraka Gujarat.
8. **Kalidasa** (कालिदास) belongs to the Chandragupta II (चंद्रगुप्त द्वितीय) court tradition. Plays (नाटक): *Abhijnanashakuntalam* (अभिज्ञानशाकुन्तलम्), *Malavikagnimitra* (मालविकाग्निमित्रम्), *Vikramorvashiyam* (विक्रमोर्वशीयम्). Mahakavyas (महाकाव्य): *Raghuvamsa* (रघुवंश), *Kumarasambhava* (कुमारसंभव). Lyrics (गीतिकाव्य): *Meghaduta* (मेघदूत), *Ritusamhara* (ऋतुसंहार). *Shringara Shataka* (शृंगार शतक) is **Bhartrihari** (भर्तृहरि), not Kalidasa.
9. **Bhitargaon** (भीतरगाँव) (Kanpur belt) is a Gupta-age brick temple in Uttar Pradesh. **Deogarh** (देवगढ़) Dashavatara is an early stone shikhara temple.
10. **Dashpur / Mandasor** silk-weavers’ guild under **Bandhuvarman** (Kumaragupta I age) built and later repaired a **Sun** temple — often called India’s earliest “advertisement” style record.
11. **Dhanvantari** (धन्वंतरि) is the Navaratna medicine name. **Bhaskara II** (भास्कर द्वितीय) (*Lilavati* (लीलावती), *Siddhanta Shiromani* (सिद्धांत शिरोमणि)) is **12th century**, not a Gupta contemporary.
12. **Kalidasa** (कालिदास) is not the author of *Shringara Shataka* (शृंगार शतक) (**Bhartrihari** (भर्तृहरि)).
13. **V. A. Smith** in *The Early History of India* called him the **Napoleon of India** for his campaigns.
14. **Ujjain** becomes a second pole. **Pataliputra** stays the old capital.
15. **Udayagiri** in Madhya Pradesh has Varaha / Vishnu caves.
16. **Fa-Hien (Faxian)** was in India **399–414 CE** during this reign to collect authentic Buddhist Vinaya texts.
17. **Crucial UPPCS Trap:** In his entire travelogue (*Fo-Kwo-Ki* / *Record of Buddhist Kingdoms*), **Fa-Hien NEVER mentions the name of Emperor Chandragupta II or his royal court**, despite praising the peace, absence of capital punishment, and prosperity of the Middle Kingdom (Madhyadesha).
18. **Brahmadeya / agrahara** grants punch holes in the crown’s direct take.
19. **Master Land Classifications:** **Kshetra** (cultivated land), **Khila** (waste/fallow land), **Aprahata** (jungle/forest land), **Vasti** (habitable land), **Gapata-Sarah** (pasture land).
20. **Revenue & Taxes:** **Bhaga** (king's 1/6th customary share), **Bhoga** (daily gift of fruits/flowers), **Kara** (periodic tax), **Bali** (customary offering), **Udranga** (water tax / fixed land tax), **Uparikara** (extra tax on temporary tenants), **Vishti** (forced unpaid labour).
21. **Kumaramatya** = all-purpose district executive. Damodarpur plates name him.
22. **Ayuktaka** = appointed district officer. Same band as kumaramatya.
23. **Pustapala** = record-keeper of grants.
24. **Dutaka** = carries the grant / royal order.
25. **Ishanavarman**–Haraha / Sarvavarman–Gaya pairs are **Maukhari / later Gupta**, not this imperial set. Gaya plate is still Samudragupta.
26. **Silver rupaka** = Chandragupta II after the Shaka war. **~33 grains**.
27. **Hiuen Tsang** (ह्वेनसांग) studies here during **Harsha’s** reign. **I-tsing** visits later. Both are **visitors**, not founders.
28. **Takshashila (Taxila)** is the older northwest teaching town in Gandhara. **Chanakya** (चाणक्य), **Panini** (पाणिनि), and **Jivaka** belong to its tradition. It is **not** a Gupta foundation.
29. **Vikramashila** in the Bhagalpur belt is a **Pala** foundation under **Dharmapala**. It peaks after Nalanda’s Gupta start.
30. **Vallabhi** in Gujarat is a **Maitraka** centre of learning. The **Svetambara Jain canon** council is linked to Vallabhi. Vallabhi is **not** Vaishali.
31. **Bhitargaon** in the Kanpur belt of Uttar Pradesh is a brick temple.
32. **Eran** has a Varaha and pillars.
33. **Tigawa** in Madhya Pradesh is a flat-roof shrine.
34. **Kalidasa** (कालिदास) belongs to the Chandragupta II (चंद्रगुप्त द्वितीय) tradition.
35. **Sudraka** (शूद्रक) wrote *Mrichchhakatika* (मृच्छकटिकम्) (*The Little Clay Cart*). It is the urban love story of merchant **Charudatta** (चारुदत्त) and courtesan **Vasantasena** (वसन्तसेना), and it also mirrors city administration and society in a Gupta-age Ujjain setting.
