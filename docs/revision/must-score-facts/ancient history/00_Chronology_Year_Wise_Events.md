## 🎯 Consolidated Must-Score Facts

1. Culture ladder: **Palaeolithic → Mesolithic → Neolithic → Chalcolithic → Iron Age / NBPW**.
2. Harappan: **Early c. 3300–2600 → Mature c. 2600–1900 → Late c. 1900–1300 BCE**.
3. Vedic: **Early / Rigvedic c. 1500–1000 → Later Vedic c. 1000–600 BCE**.
4. Religious founders (traditional coaching dates): **Mahavira c. 599–527 BCE**; **Buddha c. 563–483 BCE**.
5. Magadha dynasties: **Haryanka → Shishunaga → Nanda → Maurya**.
6. Magadha kings: **Bimbisara → Ajatashatru** (Haryanka peak) → **Udayin** (Pataliputra capital) → **Shishunaga** (takes Avanti) → **Mahapadma / Dhana Nanda**.
7. **Alexander 326–325 BCE** (Hydaspes vs Porus; mutiny at Beas) → **Chandragupta Maurya c. 321 BCE**.
8. Maurya spine: **Chandragupta → Bindusara → Ashoka (c. 268–232) → decline → Pushyamitra Shunga 185 BCE**.
9. **Kalinga War c. 261 BCE** — Ashoka’s turning point to Dhamma; **Third Council c. 250 BCE** at Pataliputra.
10. Post-Mauryan: **Shunga → Kanva**; Deccan **Satavahana**; NW **Indo-Greek → Shaka → Parthian → Kushana**.
11. Eras: **Vikrama 57 BCE**; **Saka 78 CE** (Kanishka-linked). Difference = **135 years**.
12. Kushana peak: **Kujula → Vima (gold) → Kanishka** (Fourth Council, Kashmir / Kundalvana tradition).
13. Gupta Era begins **c. 319–320 CE** with **Chandragupta I** (first *Maharajadhiraja*).
14. Gupta spine: **Chandragupta I → Samudragupta → Chandragupta II → Kumaragupta I → Skandagupta**.
15. Pilgrims: **Fa-Hien** under Chandragupta II (~399–414); **Xuanzang** under Harsha (~630–644); **I-tsing** after Harsha (~671–695).
16. Science tags: **Aryabhata** (born **476 CE**); **Varahamihira**; **Brahmagupta 628 CE**.
17. **Harsha 606–647 CE**; defeated on the Narmada by **Pulakeshin II** (Aihole / Ravikirti).
18. South spine: **Sangam → Satavahana → Pallava → Badami Chalukya → Rashtrakuta → Imperial Chola** (later = Medieval).
19. Do not swap **Fa-Hien / Xuanzang** or **Chandragupta Maurya / Chandragupta I / Chandragupta II**.
20. Do not swap **First Buddhist Council (Rajagriha)** with **Third (Pataliputra / Ashoka)** or **Fourth (Kanishka)**.
21. Pottery clock: **OCP → PGW → NBPW** sits beside the culture ladder for doab stems.
22. Invader order into NW India: **Greeks → Sakas → Kushans** (Parthians bridge Shaka and Kushan).
23. Seleucus–Chandragupta treaty **~305–303 BCE** (500 elephants; Megasthenes). Diadochi **Ipsus 301 BCE**.
24. Huna check: **Toramana → Mihirakula**; **Yashodharman** of Malwa at Mandasor **~532 CE**.
25. Harsha capitals: early **Thanesar** → later **Kannauj**; Chinese title **Siladitya**.
26. Sangam Muvendar: **Chera–Vanji**, **Chola–Uraiyur**, **Pandya–Madurai**; Karikala’s **Kallanai**.
27. Key inscription pegs: **Hathigumpha** (Kharavela), **Junagadh** (Rudradaman), **Prayag Prashasti** (Samudragupta / Harishena), **Aihole** (Pulakeshin II).
28. UP chronological anchors: Belan Neolithic → Alamgirpur / Sanauli fringe → Later Vedic doab → UP mahajanapadas → Sarnath / Kushinagar → Mathura Kushan → Bhitari Skandagupta → Kannauj Harsha.
29. **First Buddhist Council (483 BCE)** took place at Rajagriha immediately after Buddha's Mahaparinirvana under king Ajatashatru.
30. **Second Buddhist Council (383 BCE)** took place exactly 100 years later at Vaishali under king Kalashoka (Kakavarna).
31. **Third Buddhist Council (c. 250 BCE)** occurred at Pataliputra under emperor Ashoka, presided by Moggaliputta Tissa.
32. **Fourth Buddhist Council (72 CE)** occurred at Kundalvana (Kashmir) under Kushana king Kanishka, presided by Vasumitra with Ashvaghosha as vice-president.
33. **First Jain Council (c. 300 BCE)** took place at Pataliputra under Sthulabhadra during Chandragupta Maurya's reign.
34. **Second Jain Council (512 CE)** met at Vallabhi (Gujarat) under Devardhi Kshamasramana to write down the Jain Agamas.
35. Alexander's campaign in India lasted **19 months** (326–325 BCE); he died at Babylon in **323 BCE**.
36. Ashoka's coronation took place in **268 BCE**, four years after his accession (272 BCE), following a war of succession.
37. **Kalinga War (261 BCE)** was fought in Ashoka's 8th regnal year (recorded in Major Rock Edict XIII).
38. Ashoka visited Bodh Gaya in his **10th regnal year** (Major Rock Edict VIII) and Lumbini in his **20th regnal year** (Rummindei pillar).
39. The Mauryan dynasty was brought to an end in **185 BCE** when commander-in-chief **Pushyamitra Shunga** assassinated Brihadratha.
40. **Heliodorus** (Greek ambassador of Antialcidas of Taxila) erected the Garuda pillar at Besnagar (Vidisha) during the reign of Shunga king Bhagabhadra (~113 BCE).
41. **Kharavela of Kalinga** belongs to the 1st century BCE (Hathigumpha inscription mentions Nandaraja canal dug 300 years earlier).
42. **Rudradaman I's Junagarh Inscription** is dated **150 CE** (Saka year 72), representing the first long Sanskrit prashasti in chaste kavya style.
43. **Chandragupta II Vikramaditya (375–415 CE)** defeated the Western Kshatrapa ruler Rudrasimha III around 395 CE, annexing Malwa and Kathiawar.
44. **Nalanda University** was founded by Gupta emperor **Kumaragupta I (Mahendraditya)** in the first half of the 5th century CE (~427 CE).
45. **Bhitari Pillar Inscription (Ghazipur, UP)** records **Skandagupta's** fierce defense of the empire against the invading Hunas (~455–467 CE).
46. **Eran Stone Inscription (510 CE)** of Bhanugupta provides the earliest epigraphic date of the practice of Sati (funeral of general Goparaja).
47. **Battle of Narmada (~618–619 CE)**: Chalukya king **Pulakeshin II** halted Harshavardhana's southern imperial expansion on the Narmada river.
48. **Pallava-Chalukya conflict**: Narasimhavarman I defeated Pulakeshin II and sacked Vatapi in **642 CE**, earning the title *Vatapikonda*.
49. **Kannauj Assembly and Prayag Mahamoksha Parishad** were convened by Harshavardhana in **643 CE**, attended by Chinese pilgrim Xuanzang.
50. King Harsha died in **647 CE** without an heir, marking the formal threshold between Classical Ancient India and the Early Medieval period.

> **Crucial Formula Trap:** Difference between Vikrama Era (57 BCE) and Saka Era (78 CE) = **57 + 78 = 135 years**. To convert Vikrama year to Saka year: Subtract 135.
