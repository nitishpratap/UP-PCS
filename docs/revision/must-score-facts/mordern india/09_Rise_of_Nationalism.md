## 🎯 Consolidated Must-Score Facts

1. **Safety Valve Theory** (सेफ्टी वाल्व) treats Hume as a British vent for discontent. The rival view is that Congress grew from decades of Indian political work.
2. **Badruddin Tyabji** (Madras **1887**) was the first **Muslim** Congress President. **George Yule** (Allahabad (इलाहाबाद) **1888**) was the first **English** President.
3. **Vande Mataram** was sung at the Calcutta Congress of **1896**. **Jana Gana Mana (माना)** was sung at Calcutta in **1911**.
4. **Annie Besant (बेसेंट)** (Calcutta **1917**) was the first **woman** Congress President. **Sarojini Naidu** (Kanpur (कानपुर) **1925**) was the first **Indian** woman President.
5. **Belgaum 1924** (बेलगाव) is Gandhi (गांधी)’s **only** Congress presidentship. **Lahore 1929** (लाहौर) (Nehru (नेहरू)) passed **Purna Swaraj** (पूर्ण स्वराज).
6. **Karachi 1931** (Patel) adopted (अंगीकृत) the **Fundamental Rights** (मौलिक अधिकार) resolution. **Faizpur 1937** was the first **village** Congress session.
7. **Subhas Chandra Bose** (सुभाष चंद्र बोस) presided at **Haripura 1938** and **Tripuri 1939**. **Abul Kalam Azad** presided at **Ramgarh 1940**.
8. **Bal Gangadhar Tilak (बाल गंगाधर तिलक) never** presided over any INC session. **Gokhale** (गोखले) did preside (Banaras **1905**).
9. **Poverty and Un-British Rule (अन-ब्रिटिश शासन) in India** by Naoroji was published in **1901**. Drain Theory (ड्रेन) is wealth outflow; “Un-British rule” means the Raj betrayed British liberal ideals.
10. **Moderates** used petition, prayer, and faith in British justice; leaders include **Gokhale, Mehta, Banerjee, Naoroji**. **Extremists** pushed boycott, Swadeshi, and assertive Swaraj; leaders include **Tilak, Bipin Chandra Pal, Lajpat Rai, Aurobindo**.
11. **Lajpat Rai’s Mandalay** deportation (**1907**) lasted about **six months**. **Tilak’s Mandalay** sentence (**1908–14**) lasted about **six years**.
12. **Dadabhai Naoroji** was Congress President at Calcutta **1886** and again at Calcutta **1906** (his third term).
13. **Poona Sarvajanik Sabha** petitioned the **House of Commons in 1875**. **Indian Association** is the strongest pre-Congress all-India-leaning body; Banerjee was dismissed from ICS (**~1874**).
14. **Lord Dufferin** called Congress a **microscopic minority**. British Committee of INC in London (**1889**, **Wedderburn**) published weekly *India*.
15. **Valentine Chirol** called Tilak the **Father of Indian Unrest**. Tilak’s **Ganapati / Shivaji** festivals politicised public religion; he wrote *Gita Rahasya* in Mandalay jail.
16. **Economic critique spine:** Naoroji Drain + “Un-British rule”; **R.C. Dutt** *Economic History*; Moderate method = constitutional agitation, not Swadeshi boycott depth.
17. **Extremist programme triad:** Swaraj + Swadeshi + Boycott (+ national education) — distinct from Moderate 3Ps (petition, prayer, protest).
18. **Surat 1907** split is about method and Swaraj language after Partition heat; **Lucknow 1916** reunites Moderates–Extremists **and** seals Congress–League Pact — two different reunions of different pairs.
19. **Nabagopal Mitra (मित्र)**, inspired by Rajnarayan Bose, founded **Hindu Mela** to promote autonomy and **indigenous goods**.
20. **Lala Lajpat Rai** (लाला लाजपत राय) and later **R.P. Dutt** argued that Hume founded Congress as a **safety valve**.
21. **Bal Gangadhar Tilak** (बाल गंगाधर तिलक) **never** presided over an INC session.
22. **Lucknow Pact is 1916**, not 1911.
23. **Ramgarh 1940** came after Congress ministries resigned in **1939**, before the August Offer (अगस्त प्रस्ताव) and Cripps (क्रिप्स) Mission.
24. **Surendranath Banerjee** also belongs to this phase.
25. **Gokhale** is the stock Moderate.
26. **Two Congresses existed in practice** until the **1916 Lucknow reunion**.
27. **Home Rule in 1916** under Tilak and Besant carried Extremist energy into a new constitutional form (full detail in Topic 12).
28. **Valentine Chirol** called him the **Father of Indian Unrest**.
29. **Behramji Malabari (मालाबारी)** advocated the Act.
30. ***Poverty and Un-British Rule in India*** was published in **1901**. “Un-British” here means rule that violates Britain’s own standards — the same Moderate vocabulary as the “Un-British rule” critique.
31. **Hume was first President** → **W.C. Bonnerjee**; Hume organised.
32. **Indian Association = 1866** → **1876**; 1866 = **East India Association**.
33. **East India Association = Banerjee / Calcutta** → **Naoroji / London**.
34. **Tilak presided at Surat** → he **never** presided over any INC session.
35. **Gokhale never presided** → he did, **Banaras 1905**.

**Q-GC-ORG-1. Uttarakhand P.C.S. (Pre) 2010 / I.A.S. (Pre) 1993**

The first Political Organization established in India in

1838 was known as

- (a) British India Society

- (b) Bengal British India Society

- (c) Settlers Association

- (d) Zamindari Association

Show answer

**Answer: (d)**

**Explanation:**
The first political organization established in India was known

as the Zamindari Association or Landholders Society (1838

A.D.) in Calcutta. It was the first political organisation which

introduced organized efforts and constitutional remedies for

complaints. Its main purpose was to secure the interests of

landlords. The founder members were Dwaraka Nath Tagore

and his associate landlords.
