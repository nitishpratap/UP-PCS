## 🎯 Consolidated Must-Score Facts

1. **Warren Hastings (1772–1785)**:
2. **Regulating Act of 1773**; became first **Governor-General of Bengal**.
3. **Lord Cornwallis (1786–1793)**:
4. **Lord Wellesley (1798–1805)**:
5. **Lord Hastings (1813–1823)**:
6. **Anglo-Nepal War (1814–1816)**: Concluded by **Treaty of Sagauli (1816)** — Kumaon and Garhwal ceded to the British (**crucial UKPCS fact!**).
7. **Lord William Bentinck (1828–1835)**:
8. **Charter Act of 1833**: Became the **First Governor-General of India**.
9. **Abolition of Sati (Regulation XVII of 1829)** with active support of Raja Ram Mohan Roy.
10. **Macaulay’s Minute (1835)**: Made English the official medium of higher education in India (*Filtration Theory*).
11. **Lord Dalhousie (1848–1856)**:
12. **Doctrine of Lapse** (*Satara 1848 → Jaitpur & Sambalpur 1849 → Baghat 1850 → Udaipur 1852 → Jhansi 1853 → Nagpur 1854*).
13. **First Railway line (1853)**: Bombay to Thane (34 km).
14. **First Telegraph line (1853)**: Calcutta to Agra.
15. **Wood’s Despatch (1854)**: Magna Carta of English education in India.
16. **Post Office Act (1854)**: Uniform 2-paisa postal rate.
17. **Lord Canning (1856–1862)**:
18. **Lord Lytton (1876–1880)**:
19. **Vernacular Press Act IX of 1878** (*Gagging Act* — aimed directly at Shishir Kumar Ghosh's *Amrita Bazar Patrika*).
20. **Arms Act of 1878** (prevented Indians from possessing weapons without license).
21. **Lord Ripon (1880–1884)** (*The Good Viceroy*):
22. **Local Self-Government Resolution (1882)** → **"Father of Local Self-Government in India"**.
23. **First Regular Census of India (1881)** (conducted under Census Commissioner W.W. Plowden).
24. **First Factory Act (1881)** (restricted child labour below 7 years).
25. **Ilbert Bill Controversy (1883)**: Proposed allowing Indian judges to try European offenders; triggered racist Anglo-Indian agitation.
26. **Lord Curzon (1899–1905)**:
27. **Partition of Bengal**: Announced on **20 July 1905**, came into effect on **16 October 1905** (day observed as *National Mourning & Raksha Bandhan*).
28. **Ancient Monuments Preservation Act (1904)**; established Archaeological Survey of India (ASI) under Sir John Marshall.
29. **Indian Universities Act (1904)** (based on Thomas Raleigh Commission 1902).
30. **Charles Wood's Despatch (1854)**: "Magna Carta of English Education in India"; recommended three-tier education, female education, vernacular at school, English at university; set up Universities of Calcutta, Bombay, and Madras (1857).
31. **Hunter Commission (1882)**: Appointed by Lord Ripon under Sir William Hunter; emphasized primary education in vernacular and expansion of private enterprise.
32. **Raleigh Commission / Indian Universities Act (1902/1904)**: Appointed by Lord Curzon under Sir Thomas Raleigh; focused on university education and stricter government control.
33. **Sadler Commission (1917)**: Appointed by Lord Chelmsford under Sir Michael Sadler to investigate Calcutta University; recommended 12-year school course (10+2 pattern) and 3-year degree.
34. **Hartog Committee (1929)**: Appointed by Simon Commission under Sir Philip Hartog; critiqued rapid expansion, emphasized quality and consolidation of primary education.
35. **Wardha Scheme of Basic Education (1937)**: Formulated by Mahatma Gandhi (*Nai Talim*); detailed scheme drafted by **Zakir Hussain Committee**; free, compulsory learning through mother tongue and crafts.
