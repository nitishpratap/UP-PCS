## 🎯 Consolidated Must-Score Facts

1. Political parties are voluntary associations or organized groups of individuals who share the same political views and strive to gain political power through constitutional means to promote the national interest.
2. The Indian political system is characterized by a **Multi-Party System**; unlike the UK and USA (Two-Party system) or China (One-Party system), India has numerous national and regional parties reflecting its immense social, cultural, and religious diversity.
3. **Registration of Political Parties (Section 29A of RPA 1951):** Political parties must register with the **Election Commission of India (ECI)** within 30 days of formation; party constitution must contain an explicit provision declaring allegiance to the Constitution of India, socialism, secularism, and democracy, and upholding the sovereignty, unity, and integrity of India.
4. **Election Symbols Order, 1968:** Issued by the ECI; classifies symbols into **Reserved Symbols** (exclusive to recognized National and State parties) and **Free Symbols** (available for unrecognized registered parties and independent candidates).
5. **Party Splits and Symbol Disputes (Paragraph 15):** The **Election Commission of India has exclusive jurisdiction** to decide disputes among rival factions of a recognized political party claiming the party name and symbol; in *Sadiq Ali v. ECI (1971)*, SC upheld ECI's power and established the **Test of Numerical Majority** in both organizational and legislative wings.
6. **Criteria for Recognition as a National Party (Election Symbols Order, 1968):** A party is recognized as a National Party if it satisfies ANY ONE of three conditions:
   - (1) Secures at least **6% of valid votes polled in 4 or more States** at a general election to Lok Sabha or Assembly, AND in addition, **wins at least 4 seats in the Lok Sabha** from any State(s); OR
   - (2) Wins at least **2% of total seats in the Lok Sabha (11 seats)**, and these members are elected from **at least 3 different States**; OR
   - (3) Is recognized as a **State Party in at least 4 States**.
7. **Criteria for Recognition as a State Party:** A party is recognized as a State Party if it satisfies ANY ONE of five conditions:
   - (1) Secures at least **6% valid votes in State** at Assembly election AND wins at least **2 seats in Assembly**; OR
   - (2) Secures at least **6% valid votes in State** at Lok Sabha election AND wins at least **1 seat in Lok Sabha**; OR
   - (3) Wins at least **3% of total seats in Assembly** or at least **3 seats**, whichever is more; OR
   - (4) Wins at least **1 seat in Lok Sabha for every 25 seats** allotted to the State; OR
   - (5) Secures at least **8% of total valid votes** in the State at a general election to Lok Sabha or Assembly (no seats required).
8. **Present National Parties (2024):** Exactly **6 National Parties**:
   - (1) **Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP)** — Founded 1980
   - (2) **Indian National Congress (INC)** — Founded 1885
   - (3) **Communist Party of India (Marxist) [CPI(M)]** — Founded 1964
   - (4) **Bahujan Samaj Party (BSP)** — Founded 1984
   - (5) **National People's Party (NPP)** — Founded 2013 (first party from North-East recognized as national party in 2019)
   - (6) **Aam Aadmi Party (AAP)** — Founded 2012 (recognized as national party in April 2023).
9. **Parties that Lost National Status in April 2023:** All India Trinamool Congress (TMC), Nationalist Congress Party (NCP), and Communist Party of India (CPI).
10. **Privileges of National Parties:** (1) Dedicated reserved symbol nationwide; (2) Up to **40 Star Campaigners** whose travel expenses are not added to candidates' election expense accounts (unrecognized parties get 20); (3) Free airtime on Doordarshan and All India Radio; (4) Two free copies of electoral rolls; (5) Subsidized government land for party offices in New Delhi.
11. **First Political Party in India:** The **Indian National Congress (INC)**, founded on **28 December 1885** at Gokuldas Tejpal Sanskrit College in Bombay by **Allan Octavian Hume (A.O. Hume)**; Womesh Chandra Bonnerjee was its first President; attended by 72 delegates.
12. **Communist Movement in India:** The **Communist Party of India (CPI)** was formed in **1925** at Kanpur (earlier founded in Tashkent in 1920 by M.N. Roy); in **1964**, the party split, leading to the formation of the **CPI(Marxist)** under E.M.S. Namboodiripad, Jyoti Basu, and A.K. Gopalan.
13. **Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP):** Traces origin to the **Bharatiya Jana Sangh**, founded in **1951** by **Dr. Syama Prasad Mookerjee**; merged into the Janata Party in 1977; re-formed as the **Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP)** on **6 April 1980** with **Atal Bihari Vajpayee** as its first President.
14. **Regional Parties Timeline (UPPCS PYQ Sequence):**
    - **DMK (Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam):** Founded in **1949** by C.N. Annadurai (split from Periyar's Dravidar Kazhagam).
    - **AIADMK:** Founded in **1972** by M.G. Ramachandran (split from DMK).
    - **Telugu Desam Party (TDP):** Founded in **March 1982** by N.T. Rama Rao.
    - **Bahujan Samaj Party (BSP):** Founded on **14 April 1984** by Kanshi Ram.
    - **Asom Gana Parishad (AGP):** Formed in **1985** by Prafulla Kumar Mahanta following the Assam Accord.
    - **Samajwadi Party (SP):** Founded in **October 1992** by Mulayam Singh Yadav.
    - **Rashtriya Janata Dal (RJD):** Founded in **1997** by Lalu Prasad Yadav.
    - **Biju Janata Dal (BJD):** Founded in **December 1997** by Naveen Patnaik.
    - **All India Trinamool Congress (TMC):** Founded in **January 1998** by Mamata Banerjee.
    - **Nationalist Congress Party (NCP):** Founded in **June 1999** by Sharad Pawar, P.A. Sangma, and Tariq Anwar.
    - **Aam Aadmi Party (AAP):** Founded in **November 2012** by Arvind Kejriwal.
15. **Coalition Governments in India:** First non-Congress coalition at the Centre formed in **1977** under **Morarji Desai** (Janata Party); minority coalition led by Charan Singh (1979); National Front led by V.P. Singh (1989); United Front led by Deve Gowda and I.K. Gujral (1996–98); National Democratic Alliance (NDA) led by A.B. Vajpayee (1998, 1999) and Narendra Modi (2014, 2019, 2024); United Progressive Alliance (UPA) led by Manmohan Singh (2004, 2009).
16. **Leader of Opposition (LoP):** Leader of largest opposition party having **not less than 1/10th seats (10%)** of total strength of the House (55 in Lok Sabha); accorded statutory status by the **Salaries and Allowances of Leaders of Opposition in Parliament Act, 1977**; enjoys rank and status of a **Cabinet Minister**; Ram Subhag Singh became first formal LoP in Lok Sabha in 1969.
17. **Pressure Groups Definition:** Voluntary organized associations that seek to influence government policies, decisions, and administrative actions **WITHOUT themselves aiming to capture political power or run the government**; also known as interest groups, lobby groups, or non-party political formations.
18. **Differences Between Political Parties and Pressure Groups:**
    - Political parties aim to **capture political power and contest elections**; pressure groups **do not contest elections** or seek formal government office.
    - Political parties have a broad, comprehensive ideology; pressure groups have **specific, narrow interests** (e.g., labor, trade, caste, environment).
    - Political parties are accountable to the electorate; pressure groups are accountable only to their members.
19. **Techniques Used by Pressure Groups:** Electioneering (supporting candidates), **Lobbying** (persuading legislators and bureaucrats), Propagandizing (shaping public opinion), Strikes, Demonstrations, Bandhs, Dharnas, Public Interest Litigation (PIL), Boycotts.
20. **Major Business Groups in India:**
    - **FICCI (Federation of Indian Chambers of Commerce and Industry):** Established in **1927** on recommendation of Mahatma Gandhi by **G.D. Birla** and Purshottamdas Thakurdas; oldest and largest apex business organization.
    - **ASSOCHAM (Associated Chambers of Commerce and Industry of India):** Established in **1920**.
    - **CII (Confederation of Indian Industry):** Originated in 1895 as Engineering and Iron Trades Association; transformed into CII in **1992**.
21. **Major Trade Unions & Political Affiliations:**
    - **AITUC (All India Trade Union Congress):** Oldest trade union federation, established on **31 October 1920** in Bombay with **Lala Lajpat Rai as first President** and Dewan Chaman Lall as General Secretary; affiliated with CPI.
    - **INTUC (Indian National Trade Union Congress):** Formed in **May 1947** under leadership of **Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel**; affiliated with Indian National Congress.
    - **BMS (Bharatiya Mazdoor Sangh):** Founded on **23 July 1955** by **Dattopant Thengadi**; affiliated with RSS / BJP; largest trade union in India by verified membership.
    - **CITU (Centre of Indian Trade Unions):** Formed in **1970**; affiliated with CPI(M).
    - **HMS (Hind Mazdoor Sabha):** Formed in **1948** by socialist leaders (Ashoka Mehta, Jayaprakash Narayan).
22. **Agrarian Pressure Groups:**
    - **All India Kisan Sabha (AIKS):** Founded in **April 1936** at Lucknow with **Swami Sahajanand Saraswati as first President** and N.G. Ranga as General Secretary.
    - **Bharatiya Kisan Union (BKU):** Formed in **1987** under **Chaudhary Mahendra Singh Tikait** in Western Uttar Pradesh; non-partisan peasant mobilization group.
    - **Shetkari Sanghatana:** Founded by **Sharad Joshi** in Maharashtra (1979); advocated pro-market agrarian policies.
23. **Student Pressure Groups:**
    - **AISF (All India Students' Federation):** Founded in **1936**; oldest national student union; affiliated with CPI.
    - **ABVP (Akhil Bharatiya Vidyarthi Parishad):** Founded in **1949**; affiliated with RSS / BJP.
    - **NSUI (National Students' Union of India):** Founded in **1971**; affiliated with Congress.
    - **SFI (Students' Federation of India):** Founded in **1970**; affiliated with CPI(M).
24. **Caste-Based Pressure Groups:** Harijan Sevak Sangh (1932, Gandhi); Dalit Panthers (1972, Maharashtra); Jat Mahasabha; All India Backward Classes Federation.
25. **Religious and Linguistic Groups:** Rashtriya Swayamsevak Sangh (RSS, 1925, Dr. K.B. Hedgewar); Vishva Hindu Parishad (VHP, 1964); Jamaat-e-Islami Hind (1948); Shiromani Gurdwara Parbandhak Committee (SGPC, 1925 statutory body); Hindi Sahitya Sammelan; Tamil Sangam.
26. **Professional Associations:** Bar Council of India (BCI); Indian Medical Association (IMA); All India Federation of University and College Teachers' Organisations (AIFUCTO).
27. **Anomic Pressure Groups:** Spontaneous, non-structural groups that break through into the political system through direct, unconventional, or violent actions (e.g., student agitations, riots, anti-reservation protests).
28. **Mazdoor Kisan Shakti Sangathan (MKSS):** Grassroots movement founded in Rajasthan in **1990** by **Aruna Roy and Nikhil Dey**; spearheaded the campaign for transparency that led directly to the enactment of the **Right to Information (RTI) Act, 2005**.
29. **Anti-Corruption Movement (India Against Corruption, 2011):** Mass civil society movement led by **Anna Hazare** demanding the enactment of the Jan Lokpal Bill; led directly to the passage of the **Lokpal and Lokayuktas Act, 2013** and the birth of the Aam Aadmi Party.
30. **Electoral Bonds Case (*ADR, 2024*):** Struck down the 2018 scheme allowing anonymous unlimited corporate donations to political parties; held that political party financing must be transparent to protect free and fair elections and democratic accountability.
31. **Party System Typology:** Indian party system was famously described by **Rajni Kothari** as the **"One-Party Dominance System"** or the **"Congress System"** from 1952 to 1967.
32. **Section 29A RPA Allegiance Oath:** Mandates that every political party must explicitly affirm allegiance to "the principles of socialism, secularism and democracy and would uphold the sovereignty, unity and integrity of India".
33. **Sadiq Ali Majority Rule (1971):** Supreme Court established that when two rival groups claim to be the official party, the ECI must resolve the dispute primarily on the basis of support among the elected MPs and MLAs of that party.
34. **First Opposition Party Strength:** In 1969, the Congress (O) led by Ram Subhag Singh had the requisite 10% seats, becoming the first officially recognized opposition party in Lok Sabha.
35. **Morarji Desai Janata Party Era (1977):** Formed by the amalgamation of Congress (O), Bharatiya Lok Dal, Bharatiya Jana Sangh, and Socialist Party to restore constitutional democracy post-Emergency.
36. **FICCI Historic Genesis:** Mahatma Gandhi advised Indian business leaders to establish an indigenous apex chamber so that Indian commercial interests could effectively counter British colonial trade bodies.
37. **Lala Lajpat Rai AITUC Leadership:** At the inaugural session of AITUC in Bombay in 1920, Lala Lajpat Rai declared that "Indian labor cannot afford to isolate itself from the world labor movement."
38. **BMS Formation Significance:** Dattopant Thengadi founded BMS on Lokmanya Tilak's birth anniversary, rejecting the Western concept of class conflict in favor of cooperative national interest.
39. **Swami Sahajanand Saraswati AIKS Leadership:** Presided over the historic 1936 Lucknow session of the All India Kisan Sabha, bringing peasant struggles to the forefront of the national movement.
40. **BKU Boat Club Rally (1988):** Mahendra Singh Tikait led a historic 500,000-farmer siege of the Boat Club in New Delhi, demonstrating the immense political mobilization power of agrarian pressure groups.
41. **Dalit Panthers Movement (1972):** Inspired by the Black Panther movement in the US; founded by Namdeo Dhasal and Arjun Dangle to combat caste discrimination and social atrocities.
42. **SGPC Statutory Status:** Established under the Sikh Gurdwaras Act, 1925; acts as the apex administrative body for historic Sikh shrines and exerts significant influence on Akali Dal politics.
43. **PIL as a Pressure Tool:** Pioneered in India by Justices **P.N. Bhagwati** and **V.R. Krishna Iyer** in the late 1970s; relaxed locus standi, allowing NGOs and pressure groups to approach courts directly on behalf of marginalized citizens.
44. **Star Campaigner Rules:** Expenses incurred on travel by recognized political party leaders (Star Campaigners) are not deemed to be expenditure in connection with the election incurred by any candidate, provided they do not share the candidate's personal campaign vehicle.
45. **Free Broadcast Time Scheme:** Notified under Section 39A of RPA 1951; provides free airtime vouchers to recognized political parties during general elections based on past electoral performance.
46. **State Funding Debate (Indrajit Gupta Committee 1998):** Emphasized that state funding should be provided strictly in kind (fuel, paper, time slots) and restricted to recognized political parties.
47. **Right to Form Associations (Article 19(1)(c)):** Guarantees freedom to form associations, trade unions, or co-operative societies; reasonable restrictions under Art. 19(4) apply only on grounds of sovereignty and integrity of India, public order, or morality.
48. **No Right to Strike:** In ***T.K. Rangarajan v. Government of Tamil Nadu (2003)***, Supreme Court ruled that government employees have **NO fundamental, statutory, or moral right to strike**.
49. **Lobbying in India vs USA:** In the USA, lobbying is legally institutionalized and regulated under the Lobbying Disclosure Act; in India, lobbying is informal, non-institutionalized, and often operates through business chambers and delegations.
50. **Summary of 6 National Parties:** BJP (Lotus), INC (Hand), CPI(M) (Hammer, Sickle, Star), BSP (Elephant), NPP (Book), AAP (Broom).
