<details class="rev-toggle rev-toggle-facts" markdown="1">
<summary><strong>🎯 Consolidated Must-Score Facts</strong> <span class="rev-toggle-hint">(Click to Expand)</span></summary>

1. **Gram Sabha (243A):** Comprises **registered voters** of the village, not all residents. Powers are fixed by **State law**, not Central notification.
2. **Three Tiers (243B):** Village, intermediate, and district. Intermediate tier is **optional** if State population (जनसंख्या) is **≤ 20 lakh**.
3. **Election (243C / 243R):** All *seats* at all levels are filled by **direct** election. The chairperson's election mode is decided by State law (usually indirect, but UP Pradhan is direct).
4. **Reservation (243D / 243T):** SC/ST reserved in proportion to population. **Women** get **at least 1/3 (floor)** of total seats and chairpersons. OBC (अन्य पिछड़ा वर्ग) reservation is optional. UP gives 50% to women by State law.
5. **Term & Age (243E, 243F / 243U, 243V):** **5-year** term. Early dissolution (विघटन) requires elections within **6 months**, remaining term only. Minimum age is **21 years** (not 25).
6. **State Finance Commission (243-I / 243Y):** Constituted by **Governor** every **5 years** to recommend (सिफारिश) tax sharing and grants.
7. **State Election Commission (243K / 243ZA):** Appointed by Governor. Runs PRI and ULB polls (not ECI). Removed like a **High Court judge**.
8. **Article 243O / 243ZG:** Bars courts from interfering in local electoral matters (similar to Art 329 for Parliament/Assemblies).
9. **DPC (243ZD):** District Planning Committee consolidates rural & urban plans. At least **4/5** members elected.
10. **MPC (243ZE):** Metropolitan Planning Committee for pop **≥ 10 lakh**. At least **2/3** members elected.
11. **Exemptions (243M):** Part IX does **not** apply to **Nagaland, Meghalaya (मेघालय), Mizoram**, hill areas of Manipur, and Darjeeling Gorkha Hill Council.
12. **PESA Act, 1996:** Extends Part IX to **Fifth Schedule** (5वीं अनुसूची) areas (10 States) but NOT to Sixth Schedule (6वीं अनुसूची).
13. **Cooperatives (97th Amd, 2011):** Part IXB (243ZH-243ZT). Art 43B DPSP (राज्य के नीति निदेशक तत्व) and Art 19(1)(c) FR. In 2021, SC struck down its application to State cooperatives.
14. **Cantonment Board:** Set up under Cantonments Act (2006). Falls under **Ministry of Defence**. Station Commander is ex-officio President (not 74th Amd ULB).
15. **Three types (Article 243Q):** **Nagar Panchayat** (transitional area), **Municipal Council** (smaller urban area), and **Municipal Corporation** (larger urban area). The **Governor** classifies; the Constitution does **not** fix one all-India population cut-off.
16. **Mayor vs Municipal Commissioner:** The **Mayor** is the political / ceremonial head; the **Municipal Commissioner** (usually IAS, State-appointed) is the executive. India follows the Commissioner system, not a US strong-mayor model.
17. **Ward Committee (Article 243S):** Mandatory in a municipality with population **≥ 3 lakh**. It is a committee of councillors — **not** an urban Gram Sabha (Part IXA has no urban Gram Sabha).
18. **First municipal corporation:** **Madras, 1688**; Bombay and Calcutta followed in **1726**.

</details>
