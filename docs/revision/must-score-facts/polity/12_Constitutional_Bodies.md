## 🎯 Consolidated Must-Score Facts

1. A **Constitutional Body** is one whose establishment, composition, powers, and functions are explicitly provided for in the text of the Constitution; they can be modified or abolished ONLY through a Constitutional Amendment under **Article 368**.
2. **Election Commission of India (ECI, Article 324):** All-India permanent independent body responsible for superintendence, direction, and control of elections to: (1) **Parliament**; (2) **State Legislatures**; (3) **Office of President**; (4) **Office of Vice-President**.
3. **Panchayat and Municipality Elections Exclusion:** Elections to local self-governments (Panchayats and Municipalities) are NOT conducted by ECI; they are conducted exclusively by the **State Election Commission** under **Articles 243K and 243ZA**.
4. **ECI Composition (Article 324(2)):** Consists of the **Chief Election Commissioner (CEC)** and such number of other Election Commissioners as the President may from time to time fix; single-member from 1950 to 1989; made a durable **3-member body** (1 CEC + 2 ECs) since **October 1993**.
5. **Parity Among Commissioners:** The CEC and the two other Election Commissioners have **equal powers, equal salary, and equal allowances** (equivalent to a **Supreme Court Judge**); in case of difference of opinion, matters are decided by the Commission by **majority**.
6. **Appointment of CEC and ECs:** Appointed by the **President** on the recommendation of a Selection Committee consisting of: (1) **Prime Minister (Chairperson)**; (2) **Leader of Opposition in Lok Sabha**; (3) A **Union Cabinet Minister** nominated by the PM (CEC and Other ECs Act, 2023).
7. **Tenure & Service Conditions of ECI:** Tenure is **6 years or up to 65 years of age**, whichever is earlier; resigns by writing to the President; eligible for further government employment after retirement (Constitution does not bar them).
8. **Removal Asymmetry Trap in ECI:** The **CEC CANNOT be removed except in like manner and on the like grounds as a Judge of the Supreme Court** (proved misbehaviour or incapacity, requires special majority in both Houses of Parliament); other Election Commissioners **can be removed by the President on the RECOMMENDATION of the CEC** (no parliamentary impeachment needed).
9. **Union Public Service Commission (UPSC, Articles 315–323, Part XIV):** Independent constitutional agency for recruitment to All-India Services, Central Services Group A & B, and advising Union Government on disciplinary matters.
10. **Composition of UPSC (Article 316):** Consists of a Chairman and other members appointed by the **President**; strength usually 9 to 11 members (determined by President); nearly **half the members** must be persons who have held office for at least **10 years** under Government of India or State government.
11. **Tenure of UPSC Members:** Hold office for a term of **6 years or until the age of 65 years**, whichever is earlier; address resignation to the President.
12. **Removal of UPSC Chairman/Members (Article 317):** Removed by President on grounds of insolvency, paid employment outside office, infirmity of mind/body, or **misbehaviour**; on grounds of misbehaviour, President **MUST refer the matter to the Supreme Court for an inquiry under Article 145**; the advice tendered by the Supreme Court is **BINDING on the President** (during inquiry, President can suspend member).
13. **Bar on Further Employment for UPSC Members (Article 319):** Chairman of UPSC is **ineligible for any further employment** under Government of India or a State; a UPSC member is eligible only for appointment as Chairman of UPSC or Chairman of an SPSC, but NOT for any other government post.
14. **State Public Service Commission (SPSC, Articles 315–323):** Chairman and members of SPSC are **appointed by the GOVERNOR**, but **CAN ONLY BE REMOVED BY THE PRESIDENT** (Governor CANNOT remove SPSC members; Governor can only suspend them pending inquiry).
15. **Tenure of SPSC Members:** Term of **6 years or until the age of 62 years**, whichever is earlier (62 years for SPSC vs 65 years for UPSC; raised from 60 to 62 by 41st Amd 1976).
16. **Joint State Public Service Commission (JSPSC):** Created by an **Act of Parliament** on the request of the concerned State legislatures (Art. 315(2)); therefore, JSPSC is a **Statutory Body**, NOT a constitutional body; Chairman and members appointed by the **President**; tenure 6 years or 62 years.
17. **Finance Commission (Article 280, Part XII):** **Quasi-judicial body** constituted by the President at the expiration of every **5th year** (or earlier as needed); consists of a **Chairman and 4 other members** appointed by the President.
18. **Qualifications of FC Members (FC Act, 1951):** Chairman must have experience in public affairs; the 4 members are selected from: (1) Judge of High Court or qualified to be such; (2) Special knowledge of finance and accounts of government; (3) Wide experience in financial matters and administration; (4) Special knowledge of economics.
19. **Mandate & Recommendations of Finance Commission:** Recommends: (1) Distribution of net tax proceeds between Union and States (vertical devolution) and among States (horizontal devolution); (2) Principles governing **Grants-in-aid** to States under Article 275; (3) Measures to augment State Consolidated Fund to supplement resources of Panchayats and Municipalities (added by 73rd & 74th Amd 1992).
20. **Advisory Nature of Finance Commission:** Recommendations made by the Finance Commission are **only advisory in nature** and are NOT binding on the Government; President lays the report along with an explanatory memorandum of action taken before both Houses of Parliament (**Article 281**).
21. **Comptroller and Auditor General of India (CAG, Articles 148–151):** Described by Dr. B.R. Ambedkar as the **"most important officer in the Constitution of India"**; guardian of the public purse; audits all expenditure from Consolidated Fund of India, States, and UTs with assemblies.
22. **Appointment & Tenure of CAG (Article 148):** Appointed by the President by warrant under his hand and seal; tenure is **6 years or up to 65 years of age**, whichever is earlier; resigns to President.
23. **Independence of CAG:** Removed by the President ONLY in the same manner and on the **same grounds as a Judge of the Supreme Court** (Art. 148(1)); salary and conditions of service determined by Parliament and cannot be varied to his disadvantage; **ineligible for further office under Government of India or any State** after retirement; administrative expenses charged on Consolidated Fund of India.
24. **Reports of CAG (Article 151):** Submits 3 audit reports to the President (Audit Report on Appropriation Accounts, Audit Report on Finance Accounts, Audit Report on Commercial Undertakings); President causes them to be laid before Parliament; Public Accounts Committee (**PAC**) examines these reports (CAG is the 'guide, friend and philosopher' of PAC).
25. **Attorney-General for India (Article 76):** Highest legal officer in the country; appointed by the President; must be qualified to be appointed a **Judge of the Supreme Court**; holds office during the **pleasure of the President**; no fixed tenure; remuneration determined by President; right of audience in all Indian courts; right to speak in Parliament without voting rights (**Article 88**).
26. **Advocate-General for the State (Article 165):** Highest legal officer in the State; appointed by the **Governor**; must be qualified to be appointed a **Judge of a High Court**; holds office during the **pleasure of the Governor**; right to speak in State Legislature without voting rights (**Article 177**).
27. **Special Officer for Linguistic Minorities (Article 350B):** Created by the **7th Constitutional Amendment Act, 1956** on recommendation of States Reorganisation Commission; appointed by the President; investigates matters relating to constitutional safeguards for linguistic minorities; submits reports to President laid before Parliament.
28. **National Commission for Scheduled Castes (NCSC, Article 338):** Consists of a Chairperson, Vice-Chairperson, and 3 other members appointed by President; tenure **3 years**; investigates and monitors safeguards for SCs; enjoys **powers of a Civil Court** while investigating complaints.
29. **National Commission for Scheduled Tribes (NCST, Article 338A):** Bifurcated from common commission by the **89th Amendment Act, 2003** (effective 2004); 5 members (Chairperson, Vice-Chairperson, 3 members, with at least 1 woman); evaluates tribal development and safeguards; civil court powers.
30. **National Commission for Backward Classes (NCBC, Article 338B):** Elevated to constitutional status by the **102nd Constitutional Amendment Act, 2018**; 5 members appointed by President; investigates complaints regarding SEBCs; civil court powers; State lists restored by 105th Amd 2021.
31. **Goods and Services Tax (GST) Council (Article 279A):** Constitutional body inserted by the **101st Amendment Act, 2016**; chaired by Union Finance Minister; decisions taken by **3/4th majority of weighted votes** (Centre holds 1/3rd, all States combined hold 2/3rds).
32. **Inter-State Council (Article 263):** Established by presidential order in **1990** on recommendation of the Sarkaria Commission; chaired by the **Prime Minister**; investigates and discusses subjects of common interest between Union and States.
33. **Classification of Executive Bodies:**
   - **Constitutional Bodies:** ECI (324), UPSC & SPSC (315), FC (280), CAG (148), AG (76), Advocate-General (165), NCSC (338), NCST (338A), NCBC (338B), GST Council (279A), Special Officer for Linguistic Minorities (350B), SEC (243K), SFC (243I).
   - **Non-Constitutional / Statutory Bodies:** NHRC, SHRC, CIC, SIC, CVC, Lokpal, National Commission for Women, National Commission for Minorities, NBI, CBI.
   - **Non-Constitutional / Non-Statutory (Executive Resolution):** NITI Aayog (replaced Planning Commission on 1 Jan 2015), National Development Council.
34. **Summary Table of Constitutional Tenures:** SC Judge (until 65); HC Judge (until 62); UPSC Member (6 yrs / 65 yrs); SPSC Member (6 yrs / 62 yrs); CAG (6 yrs / 65 yrs); CEC & ECs (6 yrs / 65 yrs); Finance Commission (5 yrs / reconstituted every 5th year); NCSC / NCST / NCBC (3 yrs per statutory rules).
35. **First Finance Commission (1951):** Chaired by **K.C. Neogy**; 15th FC was chaired by **N.K. Singh** (recommended 41% tax devolution to States, adjusting 1% for J&K/Ladakh UTs); 16th FC is chaired by **Dr. Arvind Panagariya** (constituted Dec 2023 for 2026–31).
36. **Statutory Qualifications of Finance Commission Members:** Under the FC (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1951, the 4 members must represent: High Court judge/qualified; expert in government finance/accounts; experienced in administration and finance; economist.
37. **UPSC Historical Pedigree:** Set up as Central Public Service Commission in **1926** under the GOI Act 1919 upon recommendation of the **Lee Commission (1923–24)**; first Chairman was **Sir Ross Barker**; renamed Federal PSC under GOI Act 1935, and UPSC in 1950.
38. **Matters Excluded from UPSC Consultation (Article 320(4)):** Reservation of appointments/posts for backward classes (Art. 16(4)); claims of SCs/STs to services (Art. 335); selection for chairmanship/membership of temporary commissions; regulations made by President excluding posts must be laid before Parliament for 14 days.
39. **Joint State PSC Appointment vs Removal:** Created by parliamentary statute on joint resolution of concerned States; Chairman and members appointed by the **President** (not Governor); removed by the **President** (on SC inquiry under Art. 145); tenure is 6 years or 62 years.
40. **State Election Commission Removal Barrier (Article 243K(2)):** The State Election Commissioner can ONLY be removed in the like manner and on the like grounds as a **Judge of a High Court**; his conditions of service cannot be varied to his disadvantage after appointment.
41. **State Finance Commission Mandate (Article 243I):** Recommends principles governing taxes, duties, and tolls assignable to Panchayats/Municipalities, grants-in-aid from State Consolidated Fund, and measures to improve local body finances; Governor causes recommendations to be laid before State Legislature.
42. **Advocate-General vs Attorney-General:** Attorney-General advises Government of India (Art. 76, SC judge qualification); Advocate-General advises State Government (Art. 165, HC judge qualification); both hold office during executive pleasure and can participate in legislative proceedings without the right to vote.
43. **Special Officer for Linguistic Minorities Functioning:** Headquarters at **Prayagraj (Allahabad)**; regional offices at Belgaum, Chennai, and Kolkata; falls under the administrative control of the **Ministry of Minority Affairs**.
44. **GST Council Voting Mechanics:** Quorum is 50% of total members; decisions require a **3/4th weighted majority** of members present and voting; Central Government vote carries **1/3rd weight (33.33%)**; all 28 States and 3 UT assemblies combined carry **2/3rd weight (66.67%)**. Thus, neither Centre nor States alone can pass a resolution without cross-support.
45. **Inter-State Council Composition:** Chaired by Prime Minister; members include Chief Ministers of all States and UTs having legislative assemblies, Administrators of other UTs, and 6 Union Cabinet Ministers nominated by PM (including Home Minister); permanent invitees include other Union Cabinet ministers.
46. **NCSC Powers of a Civil Court:** When investigating any complaint under Art. 338(8), NCSC has powers to: summon and enforce attendance; require discovery and production of documents; receive evidence on affidavits; requisition public records from any court or office; issue commissions for examination of witnesses.
47. **NCST Tribal Development Mandate:** In addition to constitutional safeguards, Art. 338A(5) requires NCST to participate and advise on the planning process of socio-economic development of Scheduled Tribes and evaluate their progress under Union and States.
48. **NCBC Evolution Timeline:** Set up as statutory body in 1993 pursuant to *Indra Sawhney (1992)* judgment; granted constitutional status via **102nd Amendment Act, 2018** (Art. 338B); State OBC identification power restored via **105th Amendment Act, 2021**.
49. **CAG Audit vs Accounting Separation (1976):** Until 1976, CAG maintained accounts and conducted audits for both Union and States; in 1976, accounting functions for the Union were transferred to the **Controller General of Accounts (CGA)** under the Ministry of Finance; CAG continues to compile and maintain accounts for **State Governments**.
50. **Constitutional Removal Procedures Classification:**
   - **Group A (Impeachment / Address by Parliament):** SC Judge, HC Judge, **CAG (Art. 148)**, **CEC (Art. 324)**, and **SEC (Art. 243K)**.
   - **Group B (Supreme Court Inquiry under Art. 145):** **UPSC Members (Art. 317)** and **SPSC Members (Art. 317)**.
   - **Group C (Pleasure of Executive):** **Attorney-General (Art. 76)**, **Advocate-General (Art. 165)**, and Governors.

## 🧠 Memory Anchors & Mnemonics

1. **Retirement Age 62 vs 65 Rule:**
   - **62 Years:** High Court Judges, State PSC (SPSC), Joint State PSC (JSPSC).
   - **65 Years:** Supreme Court Judges, Union PSC (UPSC), CAG, Chief Election Commissioner & ECs.
   - *Memory Key: State-level bodies top out at 62; Union-level bodies top out at 65.*
2. **UPSC & SPSC Removal Inquiry:**
   - Removal on ground of misbehaviour requires **Supreme Court Reference under Article 145**.
   - The Supreme Court's finding is **BINDING on the President**.
   - *Trap: Neither UPSC nor SPSC members are impeached by Parliament!*
3. **The 3 Constitutional Commissions Ladder:**
   - Article 338: **NCSC** (Scheduled Castes)
   - Article 338A: **NCST** (Scheduled Tribes — 89th Amd 2003)
   - Article 338B: **NCBC** (Backward Classes — 102nd Amd 2018)
   - *Order: Castes &rarr; Tribes &rarr; Backward Classes.*
4. **GST Council 1/3rd vs 2/3rd Balance:**
   - Centre: **1/3rd** vote weight
   - All States: **2/3rd** vote weight
   - Approval requires **3/4th (75%)** majority. Neither can bulldoze the other!
