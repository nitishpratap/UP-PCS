## 🎯 Consolidated Must-Score Facts

1. Constitutional Distribution of Security Powers: **'Police' and 'Public Order' are listed under Entry 1 and Entry 2 of the State List (List II)** in the Seventh Schedule, making State governments primarily responsible for law enforcement; however, under **Article 355**, the Union has a constitutional duty to **protect every State against external aggression and internal disturbance**.
2. Armed Forces of the Union: The **Army, Navy, and Air Force** sit under the **Ministry of Defence**; the President of India is the **Supreme Commander of the Defence Forces** under **Article 53(2)**; the post of **Chief of Defence Staff (CDS)** was created in December 2019 (General Bipin Rawat was first CDS) as principal military advisor to Defence Minister.
3. **Central Armed Police Forces (CAPFs):** All CAPFs function under the administrative control of the **Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA)** (NOT Ministry of Defence!); comprises 7 forces: BSF, CRPF, CISF, ITBP, SSB, NSG, and Assam Rifles.
4. **Central Reserve Police Force (CRPF):** Formed in **1939** as Crown Representative’s Police; renamed CRPF in 1949; largest CAPF in India; primary force for internal security, counter-insurgency, anti-Naxalite operations (via specialized **CoBRA - Commando Battalion for Resolute Action**), and election security; Rapid Action Force (RAF - 1992) is a specialized anti-riot wing of CRPF.
5. **Border Security Force (BSF):** Established on **1 December 1965** following the 1965 Indo-Pak war (motto: *Duty Unto Death*); guards India’s land borders with **Pakistan and Bangladesh**; in 2021, MHA extended BSF jurisdiction up to **50 km inside international borders** in Punjab, West Bengal, and Assam.
6. **Indo-Tibetan Border Police (ITBP):** Raised on **24 October 1962** during the Indo-China war; specialized high-altitude mountain warfare force; guards the **3,488 km India-China border** (Line of Actual Control - LAC) from Karakoram Pass in Ladakh to Jachep La in Arunachal Pradesh.
7. **Sashastra Seema Bal (SSB):** Originally set up in 1963 as Special Service Bureau under Cabinet Secretariat; transferred to MHA in 2001; guards India’s open borders with **Nepal and Bhutan**.
8. **Central Industrial Security Force (CISF):** Established in **1969** under an Act of Parliament; provides integrated security cover to sensitive public/private industrial establishments, nuclear installations, space facilities, sea ports, Delhi Metro, and **all major civil airports across India**.
9. **National Security Guard (NSG):** Federal contingency counter-terrorism force raised in **1984** under the National Security Guard Act, 1986 following Operation Blue Star; nicknamed **'Black Cats'**; tasks include counter-hijacking, counter-terrorist hostage rescue, and specialized bomb disposal; comprises Special Action Group (SAG - drawn from Army) and Special Ranger Group (SRG - drawn from CAPFs/police).
10. **Assam Rifles:** **Oldest paramilitary force in India**, raised in **1835 as Cachar Levy**; unique dual control: **administrative control rests with the Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA)**, while **operational control rests with the Ministry of Defence (Indian Army)**; guards the **1,643 km Indo-Myanmar border** and conducts counter-insurgency operations in the Northeast; nicknamed **'Sentinels of the Northeast'**.
11. **Indian Coast Guard (ICG):** Established on **18 August 1978** under the Coast Guard Act, 1978; operates under the **Ministry of Defence**; enforces maritime laws in India's **Territorial Waters (12 nautical miles)** and **Exclusive Economic Zone (EEZ - 200 nautical miles)**.
12. Intelligence Architecture of India: (1) **Intelligence Bureau (IB)**: Established in **1887** as Central Special Branch; India’s premier **internal intelligence and counter-intelligence agency** under the **Ministry of Home Affairs**; (2) **Research and Analysis Wing (RAW)**: Created on **21 September 1968** under **R.N. Kao** (following 1962 and 1965 wars); handles **external intelligence, counter-terrorism, and foreign espionage**; reports directly to the **Cabinet Secretariat under Prime Minister's Office**; (3) **National Technical Research Organisation (NTRO)**: Established 2004 under NSA for geospatial and technical intelligence; (4) **Defence Intelligence Agency (DIA)**: Formed 2002 under Ministry of Defence for military intelligence.
13. **National Security Council (NSC):** Established in **1998** by the Atal Bihari Vajpayee government; 3-tier structure: (1) Strategic Policy Group; (2) National Security Advisory Board (NSAB); (3) Joint Intelligence Committee (JIC); headed by the **National Security Advisor (NSA)**, who serves as primary advisor to Prime Minister on national security.
14. **Armed Forces (Special Powers) Act - AFSPA, 1958:** Enacted to confer special powers upon armed forces in **'Disturbed Areas'**; a 'Disturbed Area' is declared under **Section 3 of AFSPA** by the **Central Government (MHA), Governor of the State, or Administrator of UT**.
15. Powers under Section 4 of AFSPA: An officer/JCO may, after giving warning: (a) **Fire upon or use force even to the causing of death** against any person acting in contravention of law/order; (b) Destroy any arms dump or fortified position; (c) **Arrest without warrant** any person who has committed or is suspected of committing a cognizable offence; (d) **Search without warrant** any premises; **Section 6** provides legal immunity: no prosecution or legal proceeding can be instituted against military personnel without **prior sanction of the Central Government**.
16. Judicial Scrutiny of AFSPA: In ***Naga People’s Movement for Human Rights v. Union of India (1997)***, the Supreme Court upheld the constitutional validity of AFSPA, but laid down strict guidelines against arbitrary use; the **Justice Jeevan Reddy Committee (2005)** recommended that AFSPA should be repealed and its appropriate provisions incorporated into the UAPA.
17. **Unlawful Activities (Prevention) Act - UAPA, 1967:** India’s primary anti-terrorism law; **Section 15** defines a terrorist act; the **2019 Amendment to UAPA** empowered the Central Government to **designate individuals as terrorists** (earlier only organizations could be declared terrorist organizations); allows NIA officers of Inspector rank and above to investigate terrorism cases.
18. **National Investigation Agency (NIA):** Established under the **NIA Act, 2008** as the central counter-terrorism law enforcement agency under MHA; exercises extraterritorial jurisdiction (2019 amendment) to investigate scheduled offences committed against Indian citizens or interests abroad; special NIA Courts try scheduled offences.
19. **Police Reforms & Prakash Singh Judgment (2006):** In ***Prakash Singh v. Union of India (2006)***, the Supreme Court issued 7 binding directives for police reforms: (1) State Security Commission (to isolate police from political pressure); (2) Merit-based transparent selection and **minimum 2-year fixed tenure for DGP**; (3) Minimum 2-year fixed tenure for SPs and SHOs; (4) **Separation of Investigation and Law & Order functions**; (5) Police Establishment Board; (6) Police Complaints Authorities at State and District levels; (7) National Security Commission at Central level.
20. Cyber Security Institutional Architecture: (1) **CERT-In (Indian Computer Emergency Response Team)**: Designated nodal agency under Section 70B of Information Technology Act, 2000 under MeitY; (2) **National Critical Information Infrastructure Protection Centre (NCIIPC)**: Created under Section 70A of IT Act under NTRO to protect critical sectors (power, banking, telecom, transport); (3) **Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C)**: Set up under MHA with national portal (cybercrime.gov.in) and emergency helpline 1930.
21. **Three-Tier Coastal Security Mechanism:** Implemented post-26/11: (1) **Tier 1 (Outer Deep Sea - 12 to 200 nautical miles)**: Patrolled by the **Indian Navy** (overall maritime security coordinator); (2) **Tier 2 (Intermediate Waters - 5 to 12 nautical miles)**: Patrolled by the **Indian Coast Guard**; (3) **Tier 3 (Shallow Coastal Waters - Baseline to 5 nautical miles)**: Patrolled by **State Coastal Marine Police**.
22. **Maritime Zones of India Act, 1976:** Defines India's maritime zones: **Territorial Waters** = up to **12 nautical miles** (sovereign Indian territory); **Contiguous Zone** = up to **24 nautical miles** (customs, fiscal, immigration enforcement); **Exclusive Economic Zone (EEZ)** = up to **200 nautical miles** (sovereign rights for exploration and exploitation of natural resources).
23. **Multi-Agency Centre (MAC):** Operationalized under the **Intelligence Bureau (IB)** post-Kargil War; 24x7 nodal clearing-house for real-time intelligence sharing among armed forces, CAPFs, state police, and central agencies; State MACs (SMACs) operate in all State capitals.
24. **NATGRID (National Intelligence Grid):** Master database conceptualized after 26/11 to connect 21 individual databases (railways, airlines, bank accounts, passport, vehicle registration) for 11 central security and intelligence agencies.
25. **Crime and Criminal Tracking Network & Systems (CCTNS):** Mission Mode Project under Digital India initiated in 2009; interconnects all 16,000+ police stations across India under the **National Crime Records Bureau (NCRB)** for computerized crime and criminal tracking.
26. **Left-Wing Extremism (LWE) & SAMADHAN Strategy:** MHA formulated the SAMADHAN framework in 2017: **S**-Smart Leadership, **A**-Aggressive Strategy, **M**-Motivation and Training, **A**-Actionable Intelligence, **D**-Dashboard Based KPIs, **H**-Harnessing Technology, **A**-Action plan for each theatre, **N**-No access to financing; deployment of specialized **Greyhounds (Telangana/AP)** and **CoBRA (CRPF)**.
27. India's International Land Borders Breakdown: Total land border is **15,106.7 km** across 7 countries: **Bangladesh (4,096.7 km - longest)**, China (3,488 km), Pakistan (3,323 km), Nepal (1,751 km), Myanmar (1,643 km), Bhutan (699 km), and Afghanistan (106 km in PoK - shortest).
28. **Prevention of Money Laundering Act (PMLA), 2002:** Enacted to prevent money laundering and confiscate property derived from proceeds of crime; enforced by the **Enforcement Directorate (ED)** under the Department of Revenue, Ministry of Finance; in ***Vijay Madanlal Choudhary (2022)***, SC upheld stringent PMLA twin bail conditions and ECIR procedures.
29. **Narcotics Control Bureau (NCB):** Constituted on **17 March 1986** under the **Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act, 1985**; India’s apex drug law enforcement and intelligence agency under the **Ministry of Home Affairs**.
30. **Border Area Development Programme (BADP):** Comprehensive Centrally Sponsored Scheme implemented by MHA to meet the special developmental needs of people living in remote and inaccessible border areas along international land borders.
31. **Vibrant Villages Programme (VVP):** Centrally funded scheme launched in 2023 for comprehensive development of selected border villages in **Himachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand, Arunachal Pradesh, Sikkim, and Ladakh** along the northern border, reversing border out-migration.
32. **Theaterisation of Armed Forces:** Ongoing military modernization reforming India's 17 single-service commands into unified **Joint Theater Commands** under the **Chief of Defence Staff (CDS)** to ensure operational synergy between Army, Navy, and Air Force.
