## 🎯 Consolidated Must-Score Facts

1. **Constitutional Distribution of Security Powers:** **'Police' and 'Public Order' are State subjects** under **List II (State List), Entries 1 and 2 of the Seventh Schedule**; the Union Government derives powers to deploy armed forces and intervene from **Article 355** (duty to protect States against external aggression and internal disturbance) and **Entry 2A of List I (Union List)** (deployment of any armed force of the Union).
2. **National Security Council (NSC) Apex Architecture:** Established in **1998** under the Prime Minister's Office; headed by the **Prime Minister of India**; the **National Security Advisor (NSA)** serves as the Member-Secretary; comprises a 3-tier structure: (1) Strategic Policy Group (SPG), (2) National Security Advisory Board (NSAB), (3) National Security Council Secretariat (NSCS).
3. **Strategic Policy Group (SPG) Chairmanship:** The SPG is the primary inter-ministerial coordination mechanism for security; **chaired by the Cabinet Secretary** (not the NSA, though NSA convenes NSC meetings); includes Cabinet Secretary, Chiefs of 3 Armed Forces, IB Director, RAW Chief, and Union Secretaries.
4. **Cabinet Committee on Security (CCS):** The supreme executive decision-making body on national security and defence; consists of exactly **5 members**: (1) Prime Minister (Chair), (2) Minister of Defence, (3) Minister of Home Affairs, (4) Minister of External Affairs, (5) Minister of Finance.
5. **Central Armed Police Forces (CAPF) Nodal Ministry:** All 7 Central Armed Police Forces (CRPF, BSF, CISF, ITBP, SSB, Assam Rifles, and NSG) function administratively under the **Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA)**; none belong to the Ministry of Defence.
6. **Central Reserve Police Force (CRPF):** India's largest CAPF; originally raised in **1939 as the Crown Representative's Police**; enacted as CRPF under the CRPF Act, **1949**; primary strike force for internal security, counter-insurgency, and election security; motto: *"Service and Loyalty"* (सेवा और निष्ठा).
7. **Specialized Wings of CRPF:** Includes: (1) **Rapid Action Force (RAF)**: Raised in 1992 (15 battalions) in distinctive blue camouflage for riot control and communal harmony; (2) **CoBRA (Commando Battalion for Resolute Action)**: Raised in 2008 (10 battalions) specifically for jungle warfare against Left-Wing Extremism (LWE).
8. **Border Security Force (BSF):** Raised on **1 December 1965** under K.F. Rustamji following the 1965 Indo-Pak War; enacted under BSF Act, 1968; guards the **Indo-Pakistan (3,323 km) and Indo-Bangladesh (4,096 km) borders**; motto: *"Duty Unto Death"* (जीवन पर्यन्त कर्तव्य).
9. **Indo-Tibetan Border Police (ITBP):** Raised on **24 October 1962** during the Sino-Indian conflict under the ITBP Force Act, 1992; guards the **3,488 km India-China (LAC) border** from Karakoram Pass (Ladakh) to Jachep La (Arunachal Pradesh); high-altitude mountain warfare specialist force; motto: *"Valour, Steadfastness, and Commitment"* (शौर्य, दृढ़ता, कर्म निष्ठा).
10. **Sashastra Seema Bal (SSB):** Originally set up in **1963 as Special Service Bureau** post-Chinese aggression; placed under MHA in 2001 and renamed Sashastra Seema Bal; guards the **Indo-Nepal (1,751 km) and Indo-Bhutan (699 km)** open borders; motto: *"Service, Security and Brotherhood"* (सेवा, सुरक्षा और बन्धुत्व).
11. **Central Industrial Security Force (CISF):** Established in **1969** under an Act of Parliament; provides integrated security cover to sensitive public/private industrial establishments, nuclear installations, space research centres, **all commercial airports**, and the **Delhi Metro**; only CAPF having a dedicated Fire Wing.
12. **Assam Rifles (AR) — The Dual Control Exception:** Oldest paramilitary force in India, raised in **1835 as 'Cachar Levy'**; known as *"Sentinels of the Northeast"*; uniquely operates under **DUAL CONTROL**: **Administrative control under Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA)**, but **Operational control under the Ministry of Defence / Indian Army**; guards the **Indo-Myanmar border (1,643 km)**; headquarters located in **Shillong, Meghalaya**.
13. **National Security Guard (NSG) — 'Black Cats':** Created in **1984** under the NSG Act following Operation Blue Star; India's premier federal contingency counter-terrorism force; operates under MHA; personnel drawn entirely on deputation from the Army (Special Action Group - SAG) and CAPFs (Special Ranger Group - SRG); motto: *"Omnipresent Omnipotent Security"* (सर्वत्र सर्वोत्तम सुरक्षा).
14. **Special Protection Group (SPG):** Raised in **1985** after Indira Gandhi's assassination; enacted by SPG Act, 1988; amended in 2019 to restrict proximate security cover **EXCLUSIVELY to the Prime Minister of India** and immediate family members residing with PM at the official residence.
15. **Intelligence Bureau (IB):** India's premier domestic intelligence and counter-espionage agency; traces lineage to 1887; functions under the **Ministry of Home Affairs**; primarily responsible for internal intelligence, border intelligence, and counter-subversion within India.
16. **Research and Analysis Wing (RAW):** India's external intelligence agency; founded on **21 September 1968** under **Rameshwar Nath Kao (R.N. Kao)** following intelligence gaps in the 1962 and 1965 wars; functions directly under the **Cabinet Secretariat (Prime Minister's Office)**, NOT under the Ministry of Home Affairs.
17. **Multi-Agency Centre (MAC):** Operationalized in **2001** following the Kargil Review Committee recommendations; housed in New Delhi under the **Intelligence Bureau (IB)**; acts as a 24x7 nodal fusion centre for intelligence sharing among 28 security and financial agencies; complemented by State MACs (SMACs) in state capitals.
18. **National Intelligence Grid (NATGRID):** Approved post-26/11 Mumbai attacks in 2009; an integrated intelligence master database framework connecting **21 core databases** (banking, telecom, immigration, vehicle registration, airlines) to provide real-time tracking data to 11 security agencies; attached office of **Ministry of Home Affairs**.
19. **National Investigation Agency (NIA):** Enacted under the **National Investigation Agency Act, 2008** following the 26/11 Mumbai terror attacks; India's premier central counter-terrorism law enforcement agency; headed by a Director-General (DG); has **all-India concurrent jurisdiction without requiring State Government consent** (unlike CBI under DSPE Act).
20. **NIA Amendment Act, 2019 Expansions:** Empowers NIA to investigate scheduled offences committed **outside India affecting Indian citizens or interests** (Special Court in New Delhi); added scheduled offences: cyber-terrorism (s.66F IT Act), human trafficking, counterfeit currency, and offences under the Explosive Substances Act; empowers NIA Inspector-rank officers to investigate.
21. **Armed Forces Special Powers Act (AFSPA), 1958:** Enacted under Article 248; empowers the Central Government, Governor of a State, or Administrator of a UT to declare any area as a **"Disturbed Area" (Section 3)**; Section 4 grants armed forces officers power to use lethal force, arrest without warrant, and enter/search premises without warrant; **Section 6 provides mandatory immunity**: no prosecution or legal proceeding can be instituted against military personnel without prior central government sanction.
22. **Judicial Validity and Review of AFSPA:** In ***Naga People's Movement for Human Rights v. Union of India (1997)***, the Supreme Court upheld the constitutional validity of AFSPA but mandated adherence to the Army's "Do's and Don'ts"; in ***EEVFAM v. Union of India (2016)***, SC ruled that armed forces do not enjoy absolute immunity in cases of fake encounters; Justice **B.P. Jeevan Reddy Committee (2005)** recommended the total repeal of AFSPA.
23. **Unlawful Activities (Prevention) Act (UAPA), 1967:** India's primary anti-terror legislation; enacted under Article 19(2) reasonable restrictions; extensively amended in 2004 (incorporating POTA provisions), 2008, 2012, and 2019; **Section 35 (2019 Amendment)** empowers Central Government to designate an **INDIVIDUAL as a terrorist** (added to Fourth Schedule); allows DG NIA to sanction seizure of property without State DGP consent.
24. **Cyber Security Institutional Framework (Section 70A vs 70B):** **NCIIPC** is established under **Section 70A** of IT Act, 2000 as the nodal agency for Critical Information Infrastructure (CII) protection under **NTRO (PMO)**; **CERT-In** is established under **Section 70B** under **MeitY** as the national agency for computer security incident response.
25. **National Cyber Coordination Centre (NCCC):** Operationalized under CERT-In to generate near real-time cyber situational awareness and scan internet metadata traffic entering India's gateways.
26. **Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C):** Operationalized in **2020 under the Ministry of Home Affairs**; comprises 7 verticals including National Cybercrime Threat Analytics Unit, Citizen Financial Cyber Fraud Reporting System, and Platform for Joint Cybercrime Coordination; manages national cyber hotline **1930**.
27. **Coastal Security Three-Tier Architecture:** (1) **Tier 1 (0 to 12 nautical miles):** Marine Police of coastal States/UTs; (2) **Tier 2 (12 to 200 nautical miles / EEZ):** **Indian Coast Guard** (established under Coast Guard Act, 1978 under **Ministry of Defence**); (3) **Tier 3 (Beyond 200 nautical miles / High Seas):** **Indian Navy** (overall commander-in-chief of maritime and coastal defence).
28. **Left-Wing Extremism (LWE) State Counter-Insurgency Units:** Elite state police commando wings raised to fight Maoist insurgency: **Greyhounds** (Andhra Pradesh & Telangana - pioneered tactical jungle warfare); **C-60** (Maharashtra - Gadchiroli); **Special Operation Group - SOG** (Odisha); **District Reserve Guard - DRG** and **STF** (Chhattisgarh); **Jharkhand Jaguars** (Jharkhand).
29. **Prakash Singh v. Union of India (2006) — Police Reforms:** Landmark SC judgment laying down 7 binding directives: (1) State Security Commission; (2) Minimum 2-year tenure for DGP; (3) Minimum 2-year tenure for SP and SHO; (4) Separation of investigation from law & order; (5) Police Establishment Board (PEB) for transfers; (6) Police Complaints Authority (PCA) at State and District levels; (7) National Security Commission at Union level.
30. **National Technical Research Organisation (NTRO):** Established in **2004** under the Prime Minister's Office (Cabinet Secretariat) on recommendations of the Group of Ministers post-Kargil; premier technical intelligence and surveillance agency for satellite imagery, cyber systems, and electronic intelligence.
31. **Financial Intelligence Unit - India (FIU-IND):** Set up in **2004** under the Department of Revenue, Ministry of Finance; acts as national nodal agency for receiving, analyzing, and disseminating Cash Transaction Reports (CTRs) and Suspicious Transaction Reports (STRs) under Prevention of Money Laundering Act (PMLA), 2002.
32. **Enforcement Directorate (ED):** Formed in **1956** as an Enforcement Unit under Department of Economic Affairs; transferred to Department of Revenue, Ministry of Finance in 1960; enforces the **Prevention of Money Laundering Act (PMLA), 2002** and **Foreign Exchange Management Act (FEMA), 1999**; has powers of summons, search, seizure, and attachment of proceeds of crime.
33. **National Security Council Secretariat (NSCS):** Headed by the National Security Advisor; provides administrative and analytical support to the 3-tier NSC; coordinates integrated defence assessments and security policy inputs across intelligence, diplomacy, and defence.
34. **National Central Bureau (NCB-New Delhi) for Interpol:** The **Central Bureau of Investigation (CBI)** is designated as the sole liaison agency for all international police cooperation via Interpol; communicates Red Notices and extradition warrants on behalf of Indian law enforcement.
35. **Border Area Development Programme (BADP):** Centrally funded scheme administered by the **Department of Border Management, Ministry of Home Affairs**; covers border blocks located along international land borders to meet special developmental needs of border populations.
36. **Comprehensive Integrated Border Management System (CIBMS):** A smart border fencing initiative deploying thermal imagers, aerostats, underground vibration sensors, and laser barriers to secure riverine, desert, and forested border terrains where physical fencing is unfeasible (operationalized by BSF).
37. **Special Frontier Force (SFF) — 'Establishment 22':** Elite paramilitary special operations force raised in **1962** under Major General Sujan Singh Uban post-Sino-Indian war; primarily recruited from Tibetan refugees; operates under the direct command of the **Cabinet Secretariat / RAW** and supervised by the Prime Minister.
38. **Assam Rifles Operational Role:** Deployed in internal security in the North-East and counter-insurgency operations under Army operational commands; operates under **Director General Assam Rifles (DG AR)**, an officer of Lieutenant General rank seconded from the Indian Army; HQ at **Laitkor, Shillong**.
39. **BSF Air and Water Wings:** BSF is the only CAPF possessing an organic **Water Wing** (patrolling Sir Creek in Gujarat and the Sunderbans in West Bengal with fast patrol crafts) and an **Air Wing** (transport aircraft and helicopters for logistics and troop movement).
40. **ITBP High-Altitude Deployment:** Deployed at forward border outposts (BOPs) at altitudes ranging up to **18,750 feet** in extreme sub-zero weather conditions; trained in specialized mountaineering, ski-patrolling, and disaster response in the Himalayas.
41. **Narcotics Control Bureau (NCB):** Constituted in **1986** under Section 4(3) of the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act, 1985; operates under the **Ministry of Home Affairs**; apex coordinating and law enforcement agency for combating illicit drug trafficking and drug abuse.
42. **Central Bureau of Investigation (CBI) Statutory Roots:** Derived not from a standalone CBI Act, but from the **Delhi Special Police Establishment (DSPE) Act, 1946**; functions under the **Ministry of Personnel, Public Grievances and Pensions (DoPT)**; requires **General Consent of the State Government under Section 6** to investigate within State boundaries (unless ordered by SC or HC).
43. **Special Task Force (STF) UP Police:** Elite specialized unit of Uttar Pradesh Police formed in **1998** to neutralize organized crime syndicates, contract killers, illicit arms cartels, and interstate criminal gangs in UP.
44. **UP Anti-Terrorism Squad (ATS):** Formed in **2007** under the Uttar Pradesh Police; specialized operational wing dedicated to investigating terror threats, sleeper cells, FICN (fake Indian currency notes), and extremist conspiracies targeting UP.
45. **Armed Forces Act Exemptions vs CAPFs:** Personnel of the regular Army, Navy, and Air Force are governed by the Army Act 1950, Navy Act 1957, and Air Force Act 1950; personnel of CAPFs are governed by their respective statutory Acts (e.g., CRPF Act 1949, BSF Act 1968, ITBP Act 1992) and are legally classified as **civil armed forces of the Union**, not regular Armed Forces.
46. **Border Management Department:** Created in **2004** within the Ministry of Home Affairs on the recommendations of the Kargil Group of Ministers; oversees border fencing, roads, floodlighting, border outposts (BOPs), and integrated check posts (ICPs) managed by the Land Ports Authority of India (LPAI).
47. **Unlawful Association Declaration under UAPA:** Central Government can declare an organization an "unlawful association" under Section 3 of UAPA; notification is valid for **5 years** (amended from 2 years in 2012); must be referred within 30 days to an **Unlawful Activities (Prevention) Tribunal** headed by a High Court Judge for confirmation.
48. **Section 43D(5) UAPA Bail Restriction:** Imposes a stringent bar on bail; court shall not grant bail if, on perusal of police case diary/charge sheet, there are reasonable grounds for believing that the accusation against the person is *prima facie true*.
49. **National Counter Terrorism Centre (NCTC) Status:** Proposed post-26/11 as a centralized counter-terror intelligence and operational agency under IB; faced stiff opposition from State Chief Ministers over infringement of federalism and police powers; **NEVER established / never became operational**.
50. **Theaterisation of Armed Forces:** Ongoing military modernization reforming India's 17 single-service commands into integrated tri-service Theatre Commands (Northern, Western, and Maritime) under the supervision of the **Chief of Defence Staff (CDS)**, created in December 2019 following Kargil Committee and Shekatkar Committee recommendations.

## 🧠 Memory Anchors & Mnemonics

### 1. Border-Guarding Forces: "B-I-S-A" Border Clock
- **B (BSF):** West & East — Pakistan (3,323 km) + Bangladesh (4,096 km).
- **I (ITBP):** North & East — China / LAC (3,488 km) (Karakoram to Arunachal).
- **S (SSB):** North — Nepal (1,751 km) + Bhutan (699 km).
- **A (Assam Rifles):** Far East — Myanmar (1,643 km) (HQ Shillong, Meghalaya).

### 2. Cabinet Committee on Security (CCS): "P-H-D-E-F"
- **P:** **P**rime Minister (Chairman)
- **H:** **H**ome Minister (गृह मंत्री)
- **D:** **D**efence Minister (रक्षा मंत्री)
- **E:** **E**xternal Affairs Minister (विदेश मंत्री)
- **F:** **F**inance Minister (वित्त मंत्री)

### 3. IT Act Cyber Twin Sections: "70A = NTRO, 70B = MeitY"
- **Section 70A:** **A**pex **A**ssets protection → **NCIIPC** under **NTRO** (Critical Infrastructure).
- **Section 70B:** **B**reach reporting in 6 hours → **CERT-In** under **MeitY** (Incident response).

### 4. CRPF Specialist Twins: "Blue Riot, Green Jungle"
- **Blue Camo:** **RAF** (Rapid Action Force, 1992) — Riot and communal control.
- **Green Jungle:** **CoBRA** (2008) — Anti-Maoist jungle commando operations.
