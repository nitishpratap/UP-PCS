## 🎯 Consolidated Must-Score Facts

1. **Lengthiest Written Constitution:** India's Constitution is the most detailed written constitution in the world; drafted by the Constituent Assembly in **2 years, 11 months, and 18 days**; adopted on **26 November 1949** and came into full effect on **26 January 1950**.
2. **Original vs Present Structural Spine:** Originally contained **395 Articles, 22 Parts, and 8 Schedules**; currently contains over **470 Articles divided into 25 Parts and 12 Schedules** (no original Article number like 396 was ever added; additions exist as 21A, 51A, etc.).
3. **Preamble Opening and Sovereign Source:** Begins with *"We, the People of India"*, declaring popular sovereignty; proclaims India a **"Sovereign Socialist Secular Democratic Republic"**; promises **Justice (Social, Economic, Political)**, **Liberty (Thought, Expression, Belief, Faith, Worship)**, **Equality (Status, Opportunity)**, and **Fraternity (Dignity of Individual, Unity and Integrity of Nation)**.
4. **Preamble Sole Amendment (42nd CAA 1976):** Amended only once by the **42nd Constitutional Amendment Act, 1976**, which added three words: **"Socialist"**, **"Secular"**, and **"Integrity"**.
5. **Part I — The Union and Its Territory (Articles 1–4):** Article 1 describes India, that is Bharat, as a **"Union of States"** (not a federation); Article 2 empowers Parliament to **admit or establish new States**; Article 3 empowers Parliament to form new States, alter areas, boundaries, or names of existing States by **simple majority** without state consent.
6. **Part II — Citizenship (Articles 5–11):** Single citizenship for the whole of India; Article 11 empowers Parliament to regulate citizenship by law; Parliament enacted the **Citizenship Act, 1955** (amended 1986, 1992, 2003, 2005, 2015, and 2019).
7. **Part III — Fundamental Rights (Articles 12–35):** Borrowed from the **US Bill of Rights**; just and justiciable; described as the *Magna Carta of India*; originally 7 Fundamental Rights; **Right to Property was deleted from Part III by the 44th CAA, 1978** and made a legal/constitutional right under **Article 300A in Part XII**.
8. **Article 12 Definition of 'State':** Includes the Government and Parliament of India, Government and Legislature of States, all local authorities (Panchayats, Municipalities), and other authorities substantially financed or controlled by the government.
9. **Article 13 Judicial Review Anchor:** Article 13(2) prohibits the State from making any law which takes away or abridges Fundamental Rights, declaring any such law void *to the extent of the contravention*; provides the constitutional bedrock for **Judicial Review**.
10. **The Six Fundamental Rights Categories:** (1) Right to Equality (Arts. 14–18); (2) Right to Freedom (Arts. 19–22); (3) Right against Exploitation (Arts. 23–24); (4) Right to Freedom of Religion (Arts. 25–28); (5) Cultural and Educational Rights (Arts. 29–30); (6) Right to Constitutional Remedies (Art. 32).
11. **Fundamental Rights Available ONLY to Indian Citizens:** **Articles 15, 16, 19, 29, and 30** are available exclusively to Indian citizens; all other Fundamental Rights (Articles 14, 20, 21, 21A, 22, 23, 24, 25, 26, 27, 28) are available to all persons (citizens and foreigners alike, except enemy aliens).
12. **Article 17 Abolition of Untouchability:** Absolute fundamental right; untouchability is abolished and its practice in any form is forbidden; penal offense under the **Protection of Civil Rights Act, 1955**.
13. **Article 19 Six Fundamental Freedoms:** Guaranteed to citizens: 19(1)(a) Speech & Expression; 19(1)(b) Peaceful assembly without arms; 19(1)(c) Form associations/unions/cooperative societies; 19(1)(d) Move freely throughout India; 19(1)(e) Reside and settle in any part of India; 19(1)(g) Practice any profession, trade, or business. (19(1)(f) Right to acquire property was omitted by 44th CAA 1978).
14. **Article 21 Protection of Life and Personal Liberty:** *"No person shall be deprived of his life or personal liberty except according to procedure established by law"*; expanded by SC in *Maneka Gandhi (1978)* to include right to live with human dignity, livelihood, clean environment, speedy trial, free legal aid, and privacy (*Puttaswamy 2017*).
15. **Article 21A Right to Education:** Enacted by the **86th CAA, 2002**; makes free and compulsory elementary education a Fundamental Right for all children aged **6 to 14 years**; operationalized via the **RTE Act, 2009 (in force 1 April 2010)**.
16. **Article 32 "Heart and Soul of the Constitution":** Dr. B.R. Ambedkar called Article 32 the very soul and heart of the Constitution; empowers citizens to move the Supreme Court directly for enforcement of Fundamental Rights via 5 prerogative writs: **Habeas Corpus, Mandamus, Prohibition, Certiorari, and Quo-Warranto**.
17. **Part IV — Directive Principles of State Policy (Articles 36–51):** Borrowed from the **Irish Constitution (1937)**; non-justiciable (Article 37) but fundamental in the governance of the country; aims to establish a **Welfare State** and achieve socio-economic democracy.
18. **Key DPSPs to Memorize:** Art. 39(b) & (c) (Equitable distribution of material resources and prevention of wealth concentration); Art. 39A (Equal justice and free legal aid); Art. 40 (Organisation of Village Panchayats); Art. 44 (Uniform Civil Code); Art. 45 (Early childhood care & education below 6 yrs); Art. 48A (Protection of environment & wildlife); Art. 50 (Separation of judiciary from executive); Art. 51 (Promotion of international peace).
19. **Part IVA — Fundamental Duties (Article 51A):** Inserted by the **42nd CAA, 1976** on the recommendations of the **Sardar Swaran Singh Committee**; borrowed from the **USSR Constitution**; originally 10 duties; the **11th duty** (parent/guardian to provide educational opportunities to child aged 6–14) was added by the **86th CAA, 2002**.
20. **President of India (Articles 52–62, Part V):** Head of the State and first citizen of India; elected by an Electoral College consisting of: **elected members of both Houses of Parliament AND elected members of State Legislative Assemblies (including Delhi and Puducherry)**; nominated members and MLCs DO NOT vote.
21. **Impeachment of the President (Article 61):** Sole ground is **"Violation of the Constitution"**; resolution requires 14 days advance written notice signed by at least 1/4th members of initiating House; must be passed by a **special majority of not less than two-thirds of the TOTAL membership** of each House; nominated members of Parliament participate; MLAs DO NOT participate.
22. **Vice-President of India (Articles 63–71):** Ex-officio Chairman of the Council of States (Rajya Sabha, Art. 64); elected by an Electoral College consisting of **all members (elected + nominated) of BOTH Houses of Parliament**; State MLAs DO NOT vote; removed by a resolution passed by Rajya Sabha by an effective majority and agreed to by Lok Sabha by a simple majority (Art. 67(b)).
23. **Union Council of Ministers & Prime Minister (Articles 74 & 75):** Article 74 mandates a Council of Ministers headed by PM to aid and advise the President, whose advice is binding (42nd and 44th CAAs); Article 75(3) establishes **Collective Responsibility**: Council of Ministers is collectively responsible to the **Lok Sabha (House of the People)**, NOT Parliament as a whole.
24. **Size Cap on Council of Ministers (91st CAA, 2003):** Total number of ministers, including the Prime Minister / Chief Minister, shall not exceed **15% of the total strength of the Lok Sabha / State Legislative Assembly** (Article 75(1A) & 164(1A)); in States, minimum number of ministers cannot be less than 12.
25. **Parliament of India (Articles 79–122):** Consists of the **President, the Council of States (Rajya Sabha), and the House of the People (Lok Sabha)** (Article 79).
26. **Rajya Sabha (Council of States, Article 80):** Maximum strength 250 (currently 245: 233 elected from States/UTs + 12 nominated by President for literature, science, art, social service); permanent body not subject to dissolution; 1/3rd members retire every 2 years; tenure 6 years; minimum age **30 years**.
27. **Lok Sabha (House of the People, Article 81):** Maximum strength 550 (originally 552; 2 Anglo-Indian seats abolished by 104th CAA 2019); currently 543 elected members; term 5 years (subject to dissolution); minimum age **25 years**.
28. **Special Powers of Rajya Sabha:** (1) **Article 249**: Can pass a resolution by 2/3rd majority authorizing Parliament to legislate on State List subjects in national interest; (2) **Article 312**: Can pass a resolution by 2/3rd majority creating one or more **All India Services (AIS)**; (3) Exclusive power to initiate removal of the Vice-President (Art. 67).
29. **Money Bill vs Financial Bill (Articles 110 & 117):** Money Bill deals exclusively with matters specified in Article 110(1) (taxes, borrowing, Consolidated Fund); certified by Speaker of Lok Sabha; introduced ONLY in Lok Sabha on President's recommendation; Rajya Sabha must return within **14 days**; no provision for joint sitting.
30. **Ordinance-Making Power (Article 123 & 213):** Article 123 empowers President to promulgate ordinances when either House of Parliament is not in session; Article 213 empowers Governor for State; has the same force and effect as an Act; must be approved by Parliament/Assembly within **6 weeks from reassembly**, or it lapses; maximum life is **6 months and 6 weeks**.
31. **Supreme Court of India (Articles 124–147):** Established on **28 January 1950** succeeding Federal Court of India; currently consists of **34 judges (1 CJI + 33 judges)**; retirement age is **65 years**; appointed by President via Collegium; removed only by President on address by Parliament with special majority under Article 124(4) on grounds of proven misbehaviour or incapacity.
32. **Jurisdiction of the Supreme Court:** (1) **Original Jurisdiction (Article 131)**: Federal disputes between Centre and States or between States; (2) **Writ Jurisdiction (Article 32)**: Enforcement of FRs; (3) **Appellate Jurisdiction (Articles 132–134A)**; (4) **Special Leave Petition (Article 136)**: Discretionary power to grant leave to appeal against any judgment; (5) **Advisory Jurisdiction (Article 143)**: President can seek advisory opinion on questions of law/fact; (6) **Court of Record (Article 129)**: Contempt power.
33. **High Courts in India (Articles 214–231):** Currently **25 High Courts** across India; newest is Andhra Pradesh High Court at Amaravati (2019); judges retire at **62 years**; appointed by President; removed in same manner as an SC judge; under **Article 226**, exercises writ jurisdiction for Fundamental Rights as well as ordinary legal rights.
34. **Order of Precedence Top Ranks:** 1. President &rarr; 2. Vice-President &rarr; 3. Prime Minister &rarr; 4. Governors of States within their states &rarr; 5. Former Presidents &rarr; 5A. Deputy Prime Minister &rarr; 6. Chief Justice of India & Speaker of Lok Sabha.
35. **Article 32 vs Article 226 Scope:** Article 32 is a **Fundamental Right** and can be invoked ONLY for Fundamental Rights; Article 226 is a constitutional right with **wider jurisdiction**, exercisable for Fundamental Rights and "for any other purpose" (ordinary legal rights).
36. **Five Prerogative Writs Snapshot:** (1) **Habeas Corpus** (against illegal detention; public & private); (2) **Mandamus** (to perform public duty; not against President/Governor); (3) **Prohibition** (preventive to lower courts exceeding jurisdiction); (4) **Certiorari** (curative to quash illegal judicial/quasi-judicial orders); (5) **Quo-Warranto** (inquires into legality of claim to public office).
37. **The 12 Schedules Master Mnemonic ("TEARS OF OLD PM"):**
   - **T:** Territories & States (1st)
   - **E:** Emoluments & Salaries (2nd)
   - **A:** Affirmations & Oaths (3rd)
   - **R:** Rajya Sabha seat allocation (4th)
   - **S:** Scheduled Areas administration (5th)
   - **O:** Other Tribal Areas — AMTM (6th)
   - **F:** Federal Lists — Union, State, Concurrent (7th)
   - **O:** Official Languages — 22 languages (8th)
   - **L:** Land Reforms & protected Acts (9th)
   - **D:** Defection disqualification rules (10th)
   - **P:** Panchayats — 29 functional items (11th)
   - **M:** Municipalities — 18 functional items (12th)
38. **Eighth Schedule 22 Languages Evolution:** Originally 14 languages; 15th was **Sindhi** (21st CAA, 1967); 16th, 17th, 18th were **Konkani, Manipuri, Nepali** (71st CAA, 1992); 19th, 20th, 21st, 22nd were **Bodo, Dogri, Maithili, Santhali** (92nd CAA, 2003); **English and Bhojpuri are NOT in the Eighth Schedule**.
39. **Seventh Schedule Three Lists Distribution:** (1) **Union List** (100 subjects: defence, foreign affairs, banking, atomic energy, railways, citizenship); (2) **State List** (61 subjects: police, public order, public health, agriculture, local government); (3) **Concurrent List** (52 subjects: criminal law, marriage, civil procedure, education, forests, population control).
40. **42nd CAA 1976 Five Subjects Transfer to Concurrent List:** (1) **Education**; (2) **Forests**; (3) **Weights and Measures**; (4) **Protection of Wild Animals and Birds**; (5) **Administration of Justice** (constitution of lower courts).
41. **Emergency Provisions Trifecta (Part XVIII):** (1) **Article 352** (National Emergency): War, External Aggression, Armed Rebellion; approved within 1 month by special majority; (2) **Article 356** (President's Rule): Failure of constitutional machinery; approved within 2 months by simple majority; maximum 3 years; (3) **Article 360** (Financial Emergency): Financial instability; approved within 2 months by simple majority; **NEVER invoked in India**.
42. **Fundamental Rights during Emergency (Articles 358 vs 359):** Under **Article 358**, Article 19 is automatically suspended only during National Emergency declared on grounds of war or external aggression; under **Article 359**, enforcement of other FRs can be suspended by Presidential order, but **ARTICLES 20 AND 21 CAN NEVER BE SUSPENDED** (44th CAA, 1978).
43. **Joint Sitting of Parliament (Article 108):** Summoned by the **President**; presided over by the **Speaker of Lok Sabha** (or Deputy Speaker, or Deputy Chairman of Rajya Sabha; Chairman of RS never presides); decided by simple majority; applicable only to Ordinary Bills and Financial Bills; **CANNOT be held for Money Bills or Constitutional Amendment Bills**.
44. **Parliamentary Financial Committees Trifecta:** (1) **Public Accounts Committee (PAC)**: 22 members (15 LS + 7 RS), chaired by Opposition member, examines CAG reports; (2) **Estimates Committee**: 30 members (**ALL from Lok Sabha**, 0 from RS), largest committee; (3) **Committee on Public Undertakings (COPU)**: 22 members (15 LS + 7 RS). Ministers cannot be members.
45. **Constitutional Amendment Types under Article 368:** (1) Simple majority outside Art. 368 (creation of states Arts. 2 & 3, abolition of councils Art. 169); (2) Special majority under Art. 368 (majority of total membership + 2/3rd present and voting for FRs, DPSPs); (3) Special majority + Ratification by at least half of State Legislatures (election of President, 7th schedule, SC/HC powers, Art. 368 itself).
46. **Landmark Amendments Fast Checklist:** 1st (1951: 9th Sch, Art. 15(4)); 7th (1956: Reorganisation, common HCs); 24th (1971: Parliament can amend FRs); 42nd (1976: Mini-Constitution, Preamble, Duties); 44th (1978: Restored liberties, removed Property from FR); 52nd (1985: Anti-defection); 61st (1988: Voting age 21 to 18); 73rd/74th (1992: PRIs/ULBs); 86th (2002: Art. 21A); 91st (2003: 15% ministry limit); 101st (2016: GST); 102nd (2018: NCBC); 103rd (2019: 10% EWS); 104th (2019: Anglo-Indian abolished); 106th (2023: 33% Women quota).
47. **Borrowed Features of Constitution (Key Origins):** Government of India Act 1935 (Federal scheme, Governor, Judiciary, PSCs, Emergency); UK (Parliamentary system, Rule of Law, Single citizenship, Cabinet); USA (Fundamental Rights, Judicial Review, Preamble, Impeachment); Ireland (DPSP, Rajya Sabha nominations); Canada (Strong Centre, Residuary powers to Centre, Advisory SC jurisdiction); Australia (Concurrent List, Joint sitting Art. 108); South Africa (Amendment procedure Art. 368); USSR (Fundamental Duties, Justice triad).
48. **Constitutional vs Statutory vs Executive Bodies:** Constitutional (ECI Art. 324, UPSC Art. 315, FC Art. 280, NCSC Art. 338, NCST Art. 338A, NCBC Art. 338B, CAG Art. 148, AG Art. 76); Statutory (NHRC, CVC, CIC, Lokpal, NCW, NGT, UIDAI, NDMA); Executive (NITI Aayog, Law Commission, NDC).
49. **Age Limits in Indian Constitution:** Minimum age to vote = **18 years** (Art. 326); Panchayat/Municipality election = **21 years** (Arts. 243V, 243ZG); Lok Sabha / Vidhan Sabha MLA = **25 years** (Arts. 84, 173); Rajya Sabha / Vidhan Parishad MLC = **30 years** (Arts. 84, 173); President / Vice-President / Governor = **35 years** (Arts. 58, 66, 157); High Court judge retirement = **62 years** (Art. 217); Supreme Court judge / CAG / CEC / UPSC retirement = **65 years** (Arts. 124, 148, 316, 324).
50. **Oaths Constitutional Placement:** Oaths of President (**Article 60**), Vice-President (**Article 69**), and Governor (**Article 159**) are placed in dedicated constitutional articles; all other constitutional functionaries (Ministers, Judges, CAG, MPs, MLAs) take oath under the **Third Schedule**.

| **Sixth Schedule States** | Claiming UP or Bihar is in the Sixth Schedule. | Sixth Schedule applies strictly to **AMTM (Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura, Mizoram)**. | छठी अनुसूची (असम, मेघालय, त्रिपुरा, मिजोरम) |
| **Article 32 vs Article 226 Scope** | Claiming Article 32 is wider than Article 226. | **Article 226 is WIDER** (covers FRs + ordinary legal rights); Art. 32 covers ONLY FRs. | अनुच्छेद 226 व्यापक है (अनुच्छेद 32 से) |
| **Vice-President vs President Electors** | Claiming State MLAs vote in Vice-President election. | Vice-President is elected by **Parliament members ONLY (both elected & nominated)**; NO MLAs. | उपराष्ट्रपति चुनाव (केवल सांसद, विधायक नहीं) |
| **Article 352 Grounds** | Stating "internal disturbance" is a valid ground today. | "Internal disturbance" was replaced with **"Armed Rebellion"** by the 44th CAA 1978. | सशस्त्र विद्रोह (44वां संशोधन) |
| **Financial Emergency Invocations** | Stating Article 360 was imposed during 1991 BOP crisis. | Article 360 has **NEVER been declared in India**. | वित्तीय आपातकाल (कभी लागू नहीं हुआ) |
| **Article 358 vs 359 Operation** | Claiming Article 358 suspends all Fundamental Rights. | Art. 358 automatically suspends **Article 19 ONLY** (and only during war/aggression). | अनुच्छेद 358 (केवल अनुच्छेद 19) |
| **Non-Suspendable Rights** | Claiming Article 21 can be suspended by Presidential order. | Under Article 359, **Articles 20 and 21 CAN NEVER BE SUSPENDED**. | अनुच्छेद 20 व 21 कभी निलंबित नहीं |
| **Panchayat Election Conduct** | Claiming ECI conducts Panchayat and Municipal elections. | Conducted exclusively by the **State Election Commission (Articles 243K & 243ZA)**. | पंचायत चुनाव (राज्य निर्वाचन आयोग) |
| **President's Oath vs Third Schedule** | Claiming President takes oath under the Third Schedule. | President takes oath under **Article 60**; Governor under **Article 159**. | राष्ट्रपति शपथ (अनुच्छेद 60) |
| **42nd vs 44th CAA Years** | Interchanging 1976 and 1978. | **42nd CAA = 1976** (Emergency); **44th CAA = 1978** (Janata Govt restoration). | 42वां (1976) / 44वां (1978) |
| **Residuary Powers Origin** | Claiming Residuary Powers lie with States like in the US. | Borrowed from **Canada**; Residuary powers vest strictly with the **Centre (Article 248)**. | अवशिष्ट शक्तियां (कनाडा / केंद्र के पास) |

## 🧠 Memory Anchors & Mnemonics

### 1. Twelve Schedules Master Mnemonic: "TEARS OF OLD PM"
- **T:** **T**erritories & States (1st)
- **E:** **E**moluments, salaries & allowances (2nd)
- **A:** **A**ffirmations & Oaths (3rd)
- **R:** **R**ajya Sabha seat allocation (4th)
- **S:** **S**cheduled Areas & Tribes administration (5th)
- **O:** **O**ther Tribal Areas — AMTM (6th)
- **F:** **F**ederal Lists — Union, State, Concurrent (7th)
- **O:** **O**fficial Languages — 22 languages (8th)
- **L:** **L**and Reforms & validation of Acts (9th)
- **D:** **D**efection disqualification rules (10th)
- **P:** **P**anchayats — 29 functional items (11th)
- **M:** **M**unicipalities — 18 functional items (12th)

### 2. Five Prerogative Writs: "H-M-P-C-Q"
- **H:** **H**abeas Corpus (बंदी प्रत्यक्षीकरण — "To have the body")
- **M:** **M**andamus (परमादेश — "We command public duty")
- **P:** **P**rohibition (प्रतिषेध — "To forbid lower courts")
- **C:** **C**ertiorari (उत्प्रेषण — "To be certified / quash illegal orders")
- **Q:** **Q**uo-Warranto (अधिकार-पृच्छा — "By what authority")

### 3. FRs Exclusive to Citizens: "15, 16, 19, 29, 30"
- Only Indian citizens enjoy these five articles. All other FRs (14, 20, 21, 21A, 22, 23, 24, 25, 26, 27, 28) apply to all persons.

### 4. Sixth Schedule Tribal States: "AMTM"
- **A:** **A**ssam
- **M:** **M**eghalaya
- **T:** **T**ripura
- **M:** **M**izoram
*(Manipurs, Nagaland, and Arunachal are NOT in the Sixth Schedule!)*
