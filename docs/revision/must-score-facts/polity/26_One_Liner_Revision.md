## 🎯 Consolidated Must-Score Facts

1. **Parts Count & Additions:** Originally 22 Parts in 1950; currently **25 Parts** (Parts IVA, IXA, IXB, and XIVA added; Part VII repealed by the 7th Amendment Act, 1956).
2. **Key Parts Cheat-Sheet:** Part III (Fundamental Rights, Arts. 12–35), Part IV (DPSP, Arts. 36–51), Part IVA (Fundamental Duties, Art. 51A), Part IX (Panchayats), Part IXA (Municipalities), Part IXB (Co-operatives), Part XI (Union–State Relations — *Trap: NOT Municipalities or Tribunals!*), Part XIVA (Tribunals, Arts. 323A–323B), Part XV (Elections, Arts. 324–329A), Part XVIII (Emergency Provisions, Arts. 352–360), Part XX (Constitutional Amendment, Art. 368).
3. **Schedules Count & Growth:** Originally 8 Schedules; currently **12 Schedules**.
4. **Schedules Master Summary:** 1st (28 States & 8 UTs); 2nd (Salaries & Emoluments of President, Governors, Judges, CAG — *Ministers are NOT in 2nd Sch*); 3rd (Oaths & Affirmations — *President, VP, and Governors take oaths under Arts. 60, 69, 159, NOT 3rd Sch*); 4th (Rajya Sabha seat allocation — UP has highest with 31 seats, UK has 3); 5th (Scheduled Areas & STs in 10 states); 6th (Tribal Areas in **AMTM**: Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura, Mizoram — *Manipur is NOT included*).
5. **Schedules 7 to 12:** 7th (Union: 100, State: 61, Concurrent: 52 entries); 8th (22 Languages — *English and Bhojpuri are NOT in the 8th Schedule*); 9th (Validation of Acts, added by 1st Amendment 1951; open to judicial review post 24 April 1973 per *Coelho case 2007*); 10th (Anti-Defection Law, added by 52nd Amendment 1985; 1/3rd split deleted by 91st Amd 2003); 11th (Panchayats: **29 functional subjects**, 73rd Amd); 12th (Municipalities: **18 functional subjects**, 74th Amd).
6. **Eighth Schedule Additions:** 21st Amendment 1967 added **Sindhi** (15th); 71st Amendment 1992 added **Konkani, Manipuri, Nepali** (KMN); 92nd Amendment 2003 added **Bodo, Dogri, Maithili, Santhali** (BDMS).
7. **Article 1 to 4 (Part I):** Art. 1 ("India, that is Bharat, shall be a Union of States"); Art. 2 (Admission or establishment of *new* states from foreign territories); Art. 3 (Formation or alteration of boundaries/names of *existing* states by Parliament by **simple majority**; prior presidential recommendation required; state legislature's views are non-binding); Art. 4 (Laws under Arts. 2 & 3 are not constitutional amendments under Art. 368).
8. **Acquisition vs Cession:** Acquisition of foreign territory requires ordinary law; **Cession of Indian territory requires a Constitutional Amendment** (*Berubari Union Case 1960*; 9th Amd 1960 for Berubari, 100th Amd 2015 for India-Bangladesh enclaves).
9. **Citizenship Articles (Part II, Arts. 5–11):** Single citizenship borrowed from UK; Citizenship Act enacted in **1955** (5 modes of acquisition: Birth, Descent, Registration, Naturalisation, Incorporation; 3 modes of loss: Renunciation, Termination, Deprivation); dual citizenship is strictly prohibited (Article 9).
10. **Fundamental Rights for Citizens ONLY:** Articles **15, 16, 19, 29, and 30** are available ONLY to Indian citizens. Other rights (Arts. 14, 20, 21, 21A, 22, 25, 26, 27, 28) apply to both citizens and non-citizens (foreigners).
11. **Article 12 & 13:** Art. 12 defines "State" (includes statutory and non-statutory bodies like LIC, ONGC, SAIL); Art. 13 lays down that laws violative of Fundamental Rights are void (*Doctrines of Judicial Review, Severability, and Eclipse*).
12. **Right to Equality (Arts. 14–18):** Art. 14 (Equality before law — UK origin, negative; Equal protection of laws — USA origin, positive); Art. 15 (No discrimination *only* on grounds of religion, race, caste, sex, place of birth; 15(4) added by 1st Amd 1951; 15(6) 10% EWS added by 103rd Amd 2019); Art. 16 (Equal opportunity in public employment; 16(4) reservation; 16(6) EWS); Art. 17 (Abolition of Untouchability — absolute right, enforced via Protection of Civil Rights Act 1955); Art. 18 (Abolition of titles; Bharat Ratna/Padma awards are decorations, not titles — *Balaji Raghavan 1996*).
13. **Article 19 (Six Democratic Freedoms):** 19(1)(a) Speech & expression (includes freedom of press and right to silence); 19(1)(b) Peaceful assembly without arms; 19(1)(c) Form associations/unions/cooperatives (97th Amd); 19(1)(d) Free movement throughout India; 19(1)(e) Reside & settle in any part; 19(1)(g) Practice any trade or profession. *(Right to property under 19(1)(f) deleted by 44th Amd 1978).*
14. **Article 20 (Three Protections):** 20(1) Protection against ex-post-facto criminal legislation; 20(2) Protection against Double Jeopardy; 20(3) Protection against Self-Incrimination (cannot be compelled to witness against oneself).
15. **Article 21 & 21A:** Art. 21 (Protection of Life & Personal Liberty; *Maneka Gandhi 1978* expanded to "Due Process of Law", Right to Privacy — *Puttaswamy 2017*, Right to clean environment, livelihood); Art. 21A (Right to Free & Compulsory Education for ages 6–14 inserted by **86th Amendment 2002**; RTE Act operative from 1 April 2010).
16. **Non-Suspendable Rights in Emergency:** Under Article 359 (amended by 44th Amd 1978), the enforcement of **Articles 20 and 21 CAN NEVER BE SUSPENDED** even during National Emergency!
17. **Writs (Art. 32 vs Art. 226):** Dr. Ambedkar called Article 32 the **"Heart and Soul of the Constitution"**. High Court's writ jurisdiction under Article 226 is **wider** than Supreme Court's under Article 32 (HC can issue writs for Fundamental Rights AND ordinary legal rights; SC only for FRs).
18. **The 5 Writs:** Habeas Corpus ('To have the body of', against both public and private entities); Mandamus ('We Command', against public officials for non-performance of statutory duty; *not against President or Governors*); Prohibition ('To Forbid', preventive against inferior courts); Certiorari ('To be Certified', preventive and curative against judicial/quasi-judicial/administrative orders); Quo-Warranto ('By what authority', challenges illegal occupation of substantive public office; can be filed by any non-aggrieved citizen).
19. **Directive Principles of State Policy (Part IV, Arts. 36–51):** Non-justiciable; fundamental in governance of the country; borrowed from Ireland.
20. **Key DPSPs to Memorize:** Art. 39A (Equal Justice & Free Legal Aid — 42nd Amd); Art. 40 (Organization of Village Panchayats); Art. 41 (Right to work, education, public assistance); Art. 42 (Humane work conditions & maternity relief); Art. 43 (Living wage); Art. 43A (Workers' participation in management — 42nd Amd); Art. 43B (Promotion of Cooperatives — 97th Amd); Art. 44 (Uniform Civil Code — *Uttarakhand first post-independence state to pass UCC in 2024*); Art. 45 (Early childhood care up to age 6 — 86th Amd); Art. 48 (Prohibition of cow/cattle slaughter); Art. 48A (Environment & Wildlife protection — 42nd Amd); Art. 50 (Separation of Judiciary from Executive); Art. 51 (Promotion of International Peace).
21. **Fundamental Duties (Part IVA, Art. 51A):** Added by **42nd Amendment Act 1976** on recommendation of **Swaran Singh Committee**; non-justiciable; originally 10 duties; **11th duty** (parent/guardian to provide education to child aged 6–14) added by **86th Amendment 2002**.
22. **Presidential Election (Art. 54 & 55):** Electoral College consists of: (1) Elected MPs of both Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha; (2) Elected MLAs of all States; (3) Elected MLAs of Delhi and Puducherry (70th Amd 1992). *(Nominated members of Parliament and State Assemblies and ALL MLCs DO NOT VOTE!)* Method: Proportional Representation by Single Transferable Vote (PR-STV).
23. **Vice-Presidential Election (Art. 66):** Electoral College consists of: **ALL MPs of both Houses of Parliament (both Elected AND Nominated)**. State MLAs and MLCs DO NOT VOTE!
24. **Impeachment of President (Art. 61):** Quasi-judicial procedure; charges can be initiated in **either House**; requires 1/4th members to sign 14-day notice; passed by a **majority of NOT LESS THAN 2/3rds of the TOTAL MEMBERSHIP** of each House. *(Nominated MPs participate; State MLAs DO NOT participate!)*
25. **Pardoning Powers (Art. 72 vs Art. 161):** President (Art. 72) can pardon, reprive, respite, or remit punishments in all cases including **Death Sentences** and **Court Martial (military) sentences**. Governor (Art. 161) cannot pardon death sentences or court-martial sentences.
26. **Ordinance Promulgation (Art. 123 vs Art. 213):** President (Art. 123) and Governor (Art. 213) can issue ordinances only during recess of legislature; ceases to operate **6 weeks from reassembly**; maximum possible lifespan = **6 months + 6 weeks**.
27. **Council of Ministers Limits (91st Amendment 2003):** Total number of Ministers including PM/CM shall not exceed **15% of the total strength** of Lok Sabha / State Legislative Assembly; in States, minimum number of ministers is **12** (Art. 164(1A)).
28. **Attorney General vs Advocate General:** Attorney General for India (Art. 76, appointed by President, pleasure of President, qualified as SC Judge, right to speak in both Houses of Parliament but **NO vote** per Art. 88); Advocate General for the State (Art. 165, appointed by Governor, pleasure of Governor, qualified as HC Judge).
29. **Joint Sitting of Parliament (Art. 108):** Summoned by President to resolve deadlock on ordinary or financial bills; presided over by **Speaker of Lok Sabha** (or Deputy Speaker, or Deputy Chairman of Rajya Sabha; *Chairman of Rajya Sabha NEVER presides!*); decided by simple majority. *(NO joint sitting for Money Bills or Constitutional Amendment Bills! NO joint sitting in State Legislatures!)*
30. **Money Bill (Art. 110):** Introduced only in Lok Sabha on President's prior recommendation; Speaker's certificate is final; Rajya Sabha cannot reject or amend, can only delay for max **14 days**.
31. **Comptroller and Auditor General of India (CAG, Art. 148):** Appointed by President; tenure: **6 years or 65 years**; removed in the same manner and on like grounds as a Supreme Court Judge; "Guardian of the Public Purse"; reports submitted to PAC.
32. **Finance Commission (Art. 280):** Quasi-judicial body constituted by President every 5th year; consists of Chairman + 4 other members; recommends sharing of net tax proceeds between Union and States.
33. **Election Commission of India (ECI, Art. 324):** Consists of Chief Election Commissioner (CEC) and 2 Election Commissioners; CEC removed in the same manner and grounds as an SC Judge (6 years or 65 years). State Election Commission (Arts. 243K & 243ZA) conducts Panchayat and Municipality elections, NOT ECI!
34. **All-India Services (Art. 312):** Rajya Sabha has the exclusive power to pass a resolution by 2/3rd majority of members present and voting to create new All-India Services (IAS, IPS, IFS created 1966).
35. **Emergency Provisions (Part XVIII):** Art. 352 (National Emergency on grounds of War, External Aggression, or Armed Rebellion; approved within 1 month by special majority); Art. 356 (President's Rule on failure of constitutional machinery; approved within 2 months by simple majority; max 3 years); Art. 360 (Financial Emergency; approved within 2 months by simple majority; *NEVER declared in India!*).
36. **Constitutional Amendment (Art. 368, Part XX):** Borrowed from South Africa; three types: Simple majority (not deemed 368), Special majority (majority of total + 2/3rd present and voting), Special majority with ratification by at least half of State Legislatures (for federal matters). President **MUST give assent** to a constitutional amendment bill (24th Amd 1971).
37. **Warrant of Precedence (1979):** 1. President → 2. Vice-President → 3. Prime Minister → 4. Governors (in respective states) → 5. Former Presidents → 6. CJI and Speaker of Lok Sabha (*Equal Rank!*) → 7. Cabinet Ministers, CMs (in state), LoP (LS & RS) → 7A. Bharat Ratna holders → 9. SC Judges → 9A. CEC, CAG, UPSC Chairman → 11. Attorney General for India.
38. **Landmark Constitutional Amendments Cheat-Sheet:** 1st (1951, 9th Schedule); 7th (1956, State Reorganisation, 14 states + 6 UTs); 24th (1971, Parliament can amend FRs, mandatory presidential assent); 42nd (1976, 'Mini-Constitution', Preamble amended, Duties Part IVA, Tribunals Part XIVA); 44th (1978, Property right deleted to Art. 300A, Arts. 20 & 21 non-suspendable, 'Armed Rebellion'); 52nd (1985, Anti-Defection 10th Sch); 61st (1988, voting age lowered 21 → 18); 73rd & 74th (1992, Panchayats 11th Sch & Municipalities 12th Sch); 86th (2002, Art. 21A RTE & 11th Duty); 91st (2003, CoM capped at 15%); 101st (2016, GST); 102nd (2018, NCBC constitutional status Art. 338B); 103rd (2019, 10% EWS quota); 104th (2020, SC/ST extended to 2030, Anglo-Indian nomination ended); 106th (2023, 33% women reservation in LS and Assemblies).
39. **Panchayati Raj Committees Chronology:** Balwant Rai Mehta (1957, 3-tier) → Ashok Mehta (1977, 2-tier) → G.V.K. Rao (1985, "grass without roots") → L.M. Singhvi (1986, first constitutional status recommendation) → P.K. Thungon (1988, 5-yr term).
40. **Centre-State Commissions:** Rajamannar Committee (1969, Tamil Nadu DMK, urged repeal of Art. 356 & abolition of IAS/IPS); Sarkaria Commission (1983–88, Art. 356 last resort, Inter-State Council set up 1990 under Art. 263); Punchhi Commission (2007–10, localized emergency Art. 355, 5-yr fixed tenure for Governors).
41. **Key Anti-Corruption & Electoral Bodies:** Santhanam Committee (1962–64, recommended CVC set up 1964); Dinesh Goswami Committee (1990, EVMs); Indrajit Gupta Committee (1998, State funding of elections in kind); Tarkunde Committee (1974, voting age 18); Vohra Committee (1993, politician-criminal nexus).
42. **Backward Classes Commissions:** 1st Backward Classes Commission: Kaka Kalelkar (1953 under Art. 340); 2nd Backward Classes Commission: B.P. Mandal (1979 under Art. 340, 27% OBC reservation); Sub-categorization of OBCs: Justice G. Rohini Commission (2017 under Art. 340).
43. **Landmark SC Cases:** *Kesavananda Bharati (1973)* = 13 judges, Basic Structure; *Maneka Gandhi (1978)* = Art. 21 Due Process; *Minerva Mills (1980)* = Balance between FR & DPSP; *Indira Sawhney (1992)* = 50% reservation cap & Creamy Layer; *S.R. Bommai (1994)* = Art. 356 curbs & Secularism; *I.R. Coelho (2007)* = 9th Schedule judicial review post-1973; *Puttaswamy (2017)* = Right to Privacy; *Navtej Johar (2018)* = Decriminalization of Section 377 IPC.
44. **UP Political Structure Facts:** Vidhan Sabha (403 seats: 84 SC, 2 ST); Vidhan Parishad (100 seats, bicameral); Lok Sabha (80 seats: 17 SC, 0 ST — highest in India); Rajya Sabha (31 seats — highest in India); First Governor: Sarojini Naidu (1947); First Chief Minister: Govind Ballabh Pant; First Woman Chief Minister of UP and India: Sucheta Kripalani (1963); Allahabad High Court founded at Agra in 1866, moved to Allahabad in 1869, Lucknow Bench created 1948.
