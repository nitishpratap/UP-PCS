## 🎯 Consolidated Must-Score Facts

1. **Lengthiest Written Constitution:** India's Constitution is the most detailed and voluminous written constitution in the world, originally containing 395 Articles, 22 Parts, and 8 Schedules, currently containing **25 Parts and 12 Schedules**.
2. **Constituent Assembly Dates:** First meeting on **9 December 1946** (interim President: Dr. Sachchidananda Sinha); permanent President elected on **11 December 1946** (**Dr. Rajendra Prasad**); Objectives Resolution moved by Nehru on **13 December 1946** and adopted on **22 January 1947**; Drafting Committee appointed on **29 August 1947** (Chairman: **Dr. B.R. Ambedkar**); Constitution adopted on **26 November 1949** (signed by 284 members); enforced on **26 January 1950**; total time taken: **2 years, 11 months, 18 days** across **11 sessions**.
3. **Key Constituent Assembly Office Bearers:** Constitutional Adviser = **Sir B.N. Rau**; Chief Draftsman = **S.N. Mukherjee**; English Calligrapher = **Prem Behari Narain Raizada**; Hindi Calligrapher = **Vasant Krishan Vaidya**; Illustrator/Artist = **Nandalal Bose**; Seal = **Elephant**.
4. **Preamble Exact Phrasing:** Declares India a **'SOVEREIGN SOCIALIST SECULAR DEMOCRATIC REPUBLIC'**; secures **3 types of Justice** (Social, Economic, Political), **5 types of Liberty** (Thought, Expression, Belief, Faith, Worship), and **2 types of Equality** (Status, Opportunity); terms **'Socialist'**, **'Secular'**, and **'Integrity'** were added by the **42nd Amendment, 1976**.
5. **Preamble Legal Status:** In ***Berubari (1960)***, SC held Preamble is *not* part of Constitution; overruled in ***Kesavananda Bharati (1973)***, holding Preamble **is an integral part** of the Constitution and amendable under Art. 368 without altering the Basic Structure; non-justiciable and non-enforceable.
6. **All 12 Schedules Fast Recall (TEARS OF OLD PM):** 1st (28 States & 8 UTs); 2nd (Salaries: President, Governors, Judges, CAG — *Ministers NOT included*); 3rd (Oaths — *President, VP, Governor NOT included*); 4th (Rajya Sabha seats: UP 31); 5th (Scheduled Areas in 10 States); 6th (AMTM: Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura, Mizoram); 7th (Union: 100, State: 61, Concurrent: 52); 8th (22 Languages); 9th (Land reforms, added by 1st Amd 1951, judicial review post-1973 per *Coelho*); 10th (Anti-Defection, 52nd Amd 1985); 11th (Panchayats: **29 subjects**, 73rd Amd 1992); 12th (Municipalities: **18 subjects**, 74th Amd 1992).
7. **8th Schedule Languages:** 22 official languages; additions: **Sindhi** (21st Amd 1967), **Konkani, Manipuri, Nepali** (71st Amd 1992), and **Bodo, Dogri, Maithili, Santhali** (92nd Amd 2003); *English and Bhojpuri are NOT in 8th Schedule*.
8. **Union & Territory (Articles 1–4):** Art. 1 ('Union of States'); Art. 2 (admission/establishment of new foreign territory); Art. 3 (internal reorganisation of existing states by Parliament by **Simple Majority**; prior presidential recommendation required, state assembly view non-binding); Art. 4 (laws under Arts. 2 & 3 are not Art. 368 amendments); Cession of territory requires Art. 368 amendment (*Berubari 1960*).
9. **Citizenship (Articles 5–11):** Single citizenship; Citizenship Act 1955 prescribes **5 modes of acquisition** (Birth, Descent, Registration, Naturalisation, Incorporation) and **3 modes of loss** (Renunciation, Termination, Deprivation); voluntary acquisition of foreign citizenship terminates Indian citizenship (**Article 9**).
10. **Fundamental Rights Exclusively for Citizens:** Articles **15, 16, 19, 29, and 30** are available ONLY to Indian citizens; all other Fundamental Rights apply to all persons.
11. **Right to Equality (Articles 14–18):** Art. 14 (Equality before law / Equal protection); Art. 15 (No discrimination only on religion, race, caste, sex, place of birth; 15(4) SC/ST/OBC quota; 15(6) 10% EWS quota); Art. 16 (Equal opportunity in public employment; 16(4) reservation; 16(4A) promotion quota 77th Amd); Art. 17 (Abolition of Untouchability - absolute right); Art. 18 (Abolition of titles; Bharat Ratna/Padma awards are decorations, not titles per *Balaji Raghavan*).
12. **Article 19 (Six Freedoms):** Speech & Expression, Assembly without arms, Associations/Unions/Cooperatives, Movement, Residence, Trade/Profession; property right under 19(1)(f) deleted by **44th Amendment, 1978**.
13. **Article 20 (Three Safeguards):** 20(1) No ex-post-facto criminal law; 20(2) No Double Jeopardy; 20(3) No Self-Incrimination.
14. **Article 21 & 21A:** Art. 21 (Protection of life & personal liberty; 'Due Process' read in *Maneka Gandhi 1978*; Right to Privacy held fundamental in *Puttaswamy 2017*); Art. 21A (Right to Free and Compulsory Education ages **6–14** inserted by **86th Amendment, 2002**; RTE Act in force 1 April 2010).
15. **Non-Suspendable Rights in Emergency:** Under **Article 359** (amended by 44th Amd 1978), the enforcement of **Articles 20 and 21 CAN NEVER BE SUSPENDED** even during a National Emergency.
16. **Prerogative Writs (Art. 32 vs Art. 226):** Dr. Ambedkar called Article 32 the **'Heart and Soul of the Constitution'**; SC writ power under Art. 32 is restricted to Fundamental Rights; HC writ power under **Article 226 is wider**, protecting Fundamental Rights AND ordinary legal rights; 5 writs: Habeas Corpus, Mandamus, Prohibition, Certiorari, Quo-Warranto.
17. **Key Directive Principles (Part IV):** Art. 39A (Free Legal Aid - 42nd Amd); Art. 40 (Village Panchayats); Art. 43B (Cooperatives - 97th Amd); Art. 44 (Uniform Civil Code - *Uttarakhand first state in 2024*); Art. 45 (Early childhood education - 86th Amd); Art. 48A (Environment & Wildlife - 42nd Amd); Art. 50 (Separation of Judiciary from Executive).
18. **Fundamental Duties (Part IVA, Article 51A):** Added by **42nd Amendment, 1976** on recommendation of **Swaran Singh Committee**; borrowed from **USSR**; non-justiciable; originally 10 duties; **11th duty** (education of child 6–14 by parent) added by **86th Amendment, 2002**.
19. **Presidential Election (Articles 54 & 55):** Electoral College = Elected MPs (both Houses) + Elected MLAs (all States + Delhi & Puducherry); *Nominated MPs/MLAs and ALL MLCs DO NOT VOTE*; PR-STV secret ballot; UP has highest MLA vote value (**208**), Sikkim lowest (**7**).
20. **Vice-Presidential Election (Article 66):** Electoral College = **ALL MPs of both Houses of Parliament (both Elected and Nominated)**; State MLAs/MLCs have zero role; removed by Rajya Sabha resolution passed by **Effective Majority** and agreed to by Lok Sabha by **Simple Majority** (Art. 67(b)).
21. **Impeachment of President (Article 61):** Sole ground: 'Violation of the Constitution'; initiated in either House on 14 days' notice signed by 1/4th members; passed by **not less than 2/3rds of the TOTAL MEMBERSHIP of each House**; nominated MPs vote, MLAs do not vote.
22. **Pardoning Power (Art. 72 vs Art. 161):** President (Art. 72) can pardon **Death Sentences** and **Court-Martial sentences**; Governor (Art. 161) cannot pardon death sentences or court-martial sentences.
23. **Ordinance Power (Art. 123 vs Art. 213):** Promulgated when legislature is in recess; ceases to operate **6 weeks from reassembly of legislature** (maximum duration = 6 months + 6 weeks).
24. **Joint Sitting of Parliament (Article 108):** Summoned by President for deadlocks on Ordinary or Financial Bills; presided over by **Speaker of Lok Sabha** (Chairman of Rajya Sabha never presides); passed by Simple Majority of members present and voting; *NO joint sitting for Money Bills or Amendment Bills; NO joint sitting in State Legislatures*.
25. **Money Bill (Article 110):** Introduced only in Lok Sabha on prior presidential recommendation; Speaker certifies; Rajya Sabha has only **14 days** to recommend; cannot be rejected by RS.
26. **Parliamentary Financial Committees:** (1) **Public Accounts Committee (PAC)**: 22 members (15 LS + 7 RS), term 1 yr, Chairman from Opposition by convention since 1967, examines CAG reports; (2) **Estimates Committee**: **30 members, ALL FROM LOK SABHA ONLY** (largest committee, John Mathai 1950); (3) **COPU**: 22 members (15 LS + 7 RS), Krishna Menon 1964.
27. **Council of Ministers 15% Cap (91st Amendment, 2003):** Total Ministers including PM/CM cannot exceed **15% of Lower House strength** (Art. 75(1A) & 164(1A)); in States, minimum cannot be less than **12 ministers**.
28. **Rajya Sabha Special Powers:** Art. 249 (legislate on State List for 1 yr by 2/3rd present & voting); Art. 312 (create new All-India Services by 2/3rd present & voting); Art. 67(b) (removal of Vice-President originates only in RS).
29. **Supreme Court Key Facts:** Inaugurated **28 January 1950** (first CJI: H.J. Kania); current strength **34 judges (1 CJI + 33 judges)**; retirement age **65 years**; appointed by President under Collegium system (CJI + 4 seniormost SC judges); removed on proved misbehaviour/incapacity by special majority under Art. 124(4); Art. 131 (Original jurisdiction in federal disputes); Art. 141 (SC law binding on all courts); Art. 142 (complete justice); Art. 143 (Advisory jurisdiction).
30. **High Court Key Facts:** 25 High Courts; retirement age **62 years** (15th Amd 1963); appointed by President; removed by President like SC judge; salaries charged on State Consolidated Fund, pensions charged on Central Consolidated Fund; Art. 226 (writs); Art. 227 (superintendence).
31. **Panchayati Raj Key Facts:** Entry 5 State List; 73rd Amendment 1992 in force **24 April 1993** (Part IX, Arts. 243–243O, 11th Schedule: **29 subjects**); 3 tiers (intermediate optional for < 20 lakh); **minimum age 21 years**; 5-year term (elections within 6 months of dissolution); **minimum 1/3rd seats and chairpersons for women**; SFC (Art. 243-I); SEC (Art. 243K); PESA Act 1996 for 5th Schedule areas.
32. **Emergency Provisions (Part XVIII):** Art. 352 (National Emergency: War, External Aggression, Armed Rebellion; written Cabinet advice; approved within 1 month by special majority; valid 6 months); Art. 356 (President's Rule: failure of constitutional machinery; approved within 2 months by simple majority; max 3 years; S.R. Bommai guidelines); Art. 360 (Financial Emergency: never invoked).
33. **Constitutional Amendments (Article 368):** Borrowed from South Africa; introduced in either House; no presidential prior sanction; passed separately in each House by Special Majority; federal matters require ratification by ≥ 50% State legislatures; President **must give assent** (24th Amd 1971); cannot violate **Basic Structure** (*Kesavananda 1973*).
34. **Order of Precedence Top Ranks:** 1. President → 2. Vice-President → 3. Prime Minister → 4. Governors in States → 5. Former Presidents → 6. CJI & Lok Sabha Speaker (Equal) → 7. Union Cabinet Ministers & CMs in States → 7A. Bharat Ratna awardees → 9. SC Judges → 9A. CEC, CAG, UPSC Chairman → 11. Attorney General & Cabinet Secretary.
