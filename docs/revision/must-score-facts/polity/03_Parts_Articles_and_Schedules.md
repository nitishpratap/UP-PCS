## 🎯 Consolidated Must-Score Facts

1. The original Constitution of 1950 contained **22 Parts, 395 Articles, and 8 Schedules**; through subsequent amendments, it now effectively contains **25 Parts, 12 Schedules, and around 470 Articles** (with the highest numbering remaining Article 395).
2. Four Parts were added to the Constitution: **Part IVA** (Fundamental Duties, 42nd Amd 1976), **Part IXA** (Municipalities, 74th Amd 1992), **Part IXB** (Co-operative Societies, 97th Amd 2011), and **Part XIVA** (Tribunals, 42nd Amd 1976).
3. **Part VII** (dealing with Part B States) was deleted by the **7th Constitutional Amendment Act, 1956**; Part IX was originally omitted by the 7th Amd 1956 and later reintroduced with a completely new character (Panchayats) by the **73rd Constitutional Amendment Act, 1992**.
4. In *Union of India v. Rajendra N. Shah (2021)*, the Supreme Court struck down provisions of **Part IXB (Co-operative Societies)** inserted by the 97th Amendment to the extent they applied to single-State cooperative societies, holding that ratification by half the State legislatures under Art. 368(2) was mandatory; Part IXB now operates only for Multi-State Co-operative Societies.
5. **Part I (Articles 1–4):** Art. 1 defines India as a **'Union of States'**; Art. 2 deals with the **admission or establishment of new States** not part of India; Art. 3 deals with the **formation of new States, alteration of areas, boundaries, or names** of existing States; Art. 4 specifies that laws made under Arts. 2 and 3 require only a **simple majority** and are NOT deemed amendments under Art. 368.
6. **Part II (Articles 5–11):** Deals with **Citizenship** at the commencement of the Constitution; Art. 9 establishes that any person voluntarily acquiring citizenship of a foreign State automatically loses Indian citizenship (**single citizenship** principle); Art. 11 empowers **Parliament** to regulate citizenship by law (Citizenship Act, 1955).
7. **Part III (Articles 12–35):** Fundamental Rights; Art. 12 defines the **'State'**; Art. 13 establishes **Judicial Review** declaring laws inconsistent with or in derogation of Fundamental Rights void (*Doctrine of Eclipse*, *Doctrine of Severability*).
8. Articles available **exclusively to Indian citizens**: **15, 16, 19, 29, and 30**; all other Fundamental Rights (Arts. 14, 20, 21, 21A, 22, 25, 26, 27, 28) apply to all persons (citizens and aliens, except enemy aliens).
9. **Absolute Fundamental Rights:** **Article 17** (Abolition of Untouchability) and **Article 24** (Prohibition of employment of children below 14 in hazardous work) contain no constitutional exceptions or reasonable restrictions.
10. **Articles 20 and 21** enjoy supreme constitutional protection: under **Article 359** (as amended by 44th Amd 1978), the right to move any court for the enforcement of Arts. 20 and 21 **CANNOT be suspended** even during a National Emergency.
11. **Part IV (Articles 36–51):** Directive Principles of State Policy (**DPSP**); non-justiciable (Art. 37) but fundamental in the governance of the country; borrowed from Ireland (which borrowed from Spain).
12. Key DPSP Articles: **Art. 39A** (Equal Justice and Free Legal Aid — 42nd Amd 1976); **Art. 40** (Organisation of Village Panchayats); **Art. 43B** (Promotion of Co-operative Societies — 97th Amd 2011); **Art. 44** (Uniform Civil Code); **Art. 45** (Early childhood care up to 6 years — 86th Amd 2002); **Art. 48A** (Protection of Environment and Wildlife — 42nd Amd 1976); **Art. 50** (Separation of Judiciary from Executive).
13. **Part IVA (Article 51A):** Fundamental Duties; added on the recommendation of the **Swaran Singh Committee** by the **42nd Amendment, 1976** (10 duties originally); the **11th duty** (education of child aged 6–14 years) was added by the **86th Amendment, 2002** (Art. 51A(k)).
14. **Part V (Articles 52–151 — The Union):** Divided into 5 Chapters: Chapter I (The Executive: Arts. 52–78), Chapter II (Parliament: Arts. 79–122), Chapter III (Legislative Powers of President: Art. 123), Chapter IV (Union Judiciary: Arts. 124–147), Chapter V (CAG: Arts. 148–151).
15. Key Presidential Articles: **Art. 52** (President of India); **Art. 54** (Electoral College); **Art. 55** (Manner of Election — PR-STV); **Art. 61** (Procedure for Impeachment — 2/3rd total membership); **Art. 72** (Pardoning Power); **Art. 111** (Assent to Bills/Veto); **Art. 123** (Ordinance Making Power); **Art. 143** (Power to Consult Supreme Court).
16. Key Vice-Presidential Articles: **Art. 63** (Vice-President of India); **Art. 64** (Ex-officio Chairman of the Council of States); **Art. 66** (Election of VP by all MPs of both Houses); **Art. 67(b)** (Removal by resolution of Rajya Sabha agreed to by Lok Sabha).
17. Key Union Executive Articles: **Art. 74** (Council of Ministers to aid and advise President; advice tendered cannot be inquired into in any court); **Art. 75** (PM appointed by President; Ministers hold office during pleasure of President; Council of Ministers collectively responsible to Lok Sabha; size capped at 15% of LS by 91st Amd 2003); **Art. 76** (Attorney-General for India).
18. Key Parliamentary Articles: **Art. 79** (Constitution of Parliament: President + Rajya Sabha + Lok Sabha); **Art. 80** (Composition of Rajya Sabha: max 250; 12 nominated by President); **Art. 81** (Composition of Lok Sabha: max 550 post-104th Amd); **Art. 85** (Sessions, prorogation, dissolution; gap between sessions max 6 months); **Art. 108** (Joint Sitting presided over by Speaker); **Art. 110** (Definition of Money Bill); **Art. 112** (Annual Financial Statement/Budget).
19. Key Supreme Court Articles: **Art. 124** (Establishment and constitution of SC); **Art. 129** (Supreme Court to be a Court of Record and power to punish for contempt); **Art. 131** (Original Jurisdiction in inter-governmental disputes); **Art. 136** (Special Leave Petition — SLP); **Art. 137** (Review of judgments); **Art. 141** (Law declared by Supreme Court binding on all courts); **Art. 142** (Enforcement of decrees and orders for 'complete justice').
20. **Comptroller and Auditor General (CAG):** **Article 148** provides for the CAG of India; appointed by President; removed like an SC judge; tenure 6 years or 65 years; **Art. 149** prescribes duties/powers; **Art. 151** requires audit reports to be submitted to President/Governors who lay them before Parliament/State Legislatures.
21. **Part VI (Articles 152–237 — The States):** **Art. 153** (Governors of States; 7th Amd 1956 permits same person as Governor for 2 or more States); **Art. 154** (Executive power of State vested in Governor); **Art. 161** (Pardoning power of Governor — cannot pardon death sentence or court-martial); **Art. 163** (Council of Ministers to aid and advise Governor; Governor's discretionary decisions are final); **Art. 164** (CM appointed by Governor; collective responsibility to Legislative Assembly; min 12 ministers, max 15% of SLA); **Art. 165** (Advocate-General for the State).
22. Key State Legislature Articles: **Art. 169** (Abolition or creation of Legislative Councils in States by Parliament upon resolution passed by Legislative Assembly by special majority); **Art. 200** (Assent to Bills by Governor, or reservation of Bill for consideration of the President); **Art. 201** (Bills reserved by Governor for President's consideration); **Art. 213** (Governor's Ordinance-making power).
23. Key High Court Articles: **Art. 214** (High Courts for States); **Art. 215** (High Courts to be Courts of Record); **Art. 226** (Power of High Courts to issue writs for Fundamental Rights and ordinary legal rights); **Art. 227** (Power of superintendence over all courts and tribunals throughout territories in relation to which it exercises jurisdiction); **Art. 231** (Establishment of a common High Court for two or more States).
24. **Part VIII (Articles 239–242 — The Union Territories):** **Art. 239** (Administration of UTs by President through Administrator/LG); **Art. 239AA** (Special provisions with respect to Delhi — inserted by 69th Amd 1991, creating 70-member Assembly and 10% CoM limit); **Art. 239AB** (Failure of constitutional machinery in Delhi).
25. **Part IX (Articles 243 to 243-O — The Panchayats):** 73rd Amd 1992; **Art. 243A** (Gram Sabha); **Art. 243B** (Constitution of Panchayats at 3 levels; intermediate level optional for States with pop < 20 lakh); **Art. 243D** (Reservation of seats: SC/ST proportional, min 1/3rd for women); **Art. 243E** (Duration of 5 years; fresh election within 6 months of dissolution); **Art. 243I** (State Finance Commission constituted every 5 years); **Art. 243K** (State Election Commission).
26. **Part IXA (Articles 243P to 243ZG — The Municipalities):** 74th Amd 1992; **Art. 243Q** (Constitution of Nagar Panchayat, Municipal Council, Municipal Corporation); **Art. 243S** (Constitution and composition of Wards Committees); **Art. 243T** (Reservation of seats: min 1/3rd for women); **Art. 243ZD** (Committee for District Planning — DPC); **Art. 243ZE** (Committee for Metropolitan Planning).
27. **Part XI (Articles 245–263 — Relations Between Union and States):** Chapter I (Legislative Relations: Arts. 245–255); Chapter II (Administrative Relations: Arts. 256–263).
28. Key Legislative Relations Articles: **Art. 248** (Residuary powers of legislation vest exclusively in Parliament); **Art. 249** (Power of Parliament to legislate with respect to a State List matter in national interest upon Rajya Sabha resolution supported by 2/3rd present and voting, valid for 1 year); **Art. 250** (Parliament's power to legislate on State List during National Emergency); **Art. 252** (Power of Parliament to legislate for two or more States by consent); **Art. 254** (Inconsistency between laws made by Parliament and laws made by State Legislatures — Union law prevails).
29. Key Administrative Relations Articles: **Art. 256** (Obligation of States and Union); **Art. 257** (Control of Union over States in specified matters); **Art. 262** (Adjudication of disputes relating to waters of inter-state rivers or river valleys; courts' jurisdiction barred); **Art. 263** (Provisions with respect to an Inter-State Council established by the President).
30. **Part XII (Articles 264–300A — Finance, Property, Contracts and Suits):** **Art. 265** (No tax levied or collected except by authority of law); **Art. 266** (Consolidated Fund of India and Public Account of India); **Art. 267** (Contingency Fund of India held by Finance Secretary on behalf of President); **Art. 275** (Statutory Grants-in-aid to States); **Art. 279A** (GST Council); **Art. 280** (Finance Commission constituted every 5th year: Chairman + 4 members); **Art. 300A** (Right to Property — constitutional right, not Fundamental Right).
31. **Part XIII (Articles 301–307 — Trade, Commerce and Intercourse):** **Art. 301** declares freedom of trade, commerce, and intercourse throughout the territory of India; subject to reasonable restrictions imposed by Parliament (Art. 302) or States with prior presidential sanction (Art. 304(b)).
32. **Part XIV (Articles 308–323 — Services Under Union and States):** **Art. 310** (Doctrine of Pleasure); **Art. 311** (Safeguards against dismissal, removal, or reduction in rank of civil servants); **Art. 312** (All-India Services created by Parliament after Rajya Sabha resolution by 2/3rd present and voting); **Art. 315** (Public Service Commissions for Union and States); **Art. 320** (Functions of PSCs); **Art. 323** (Reports of PSCs).
33. **Part XIVA (Articles 323A–323B — Tribunals):** Added by 42nd Amd 1976; **Art. 323A** (Administrative Tribunals established by Parliament only for public service disputes — CAT/SAT); **Art. 323B** (Tribunals for other matters established by both Parliament and State legislatures).
34. **Part XV (Articles 324–329 — Elections):** **Art. 324** (Superintendence, direction, and control of elections vested in Election Commission of India); **Art. 325** (No person ineligible for inclusion in electoral roll on grounds only of religion, race, caste, or sex; single general electoral roll); **Art. 326** (Elections to Lok Sabha and State Assemblies on the basis of **Adult Suffrage**; voting age reduced from 21 to 18 by **61st Amendment Act, 1988**); **Art. 329** (Bar to interference by courts in electoral matters).
35. **Part XVI (Articles 330–342A — Special Provisions Relating to Certain Classes):** **Art. 330** (Reservation of seats for SCs and STs in Lok Sabha); **Art. 332** (Reservation for SCs and STs in State Legislative Assemblies); **Art. 334** (Reservation period extended every 10 years; 104th Amd 2019 extended SC/ST reservation to 2030 but ended Anglo-Indian nominations under Arts. 331 & 333); **Art. 335** (Claims of SCs/STs to services consistent with maintenance of efficiency); **Art. 338** (NCSC); **Art. 338A** (NCST); **Art. 338B** (NCBC); **Art. 340** (Appointment of Commission to investigate backward classes).
36. **Part XVII (Articles 343–351 — Official Language):** Chapter I (Language of the Union: Arts. 343–344); Chapter II (Regional Languages: Arts. 345–347); Chapter III (Language of SC/HCs: Arts. 348–349); Chapter IV (Special Directives: Arts. 350–351).
37. Key Official Language Articles: **Art. 343** (Official language of Union is Hindi in Devanagari script; international form of Indian numerals); **Art. 344** (Committee of Parliament on Official Language: 30 members — 20 LS, 10 RS); **Art. 348** (Authoritative texts of all Bills, Acts, Ordinances, and SC/HC proceedings shall be in English until Parliament provides otherwise); **Art. 350A** (Mother tongue instruction at primary stage for linguistic minorities); **Art. 350B** (Special Officer for Linguistic Minorities); **Art. 351** (Directive for development of Hindi language).
38. **Part XVIII (Articles 352–360 — Emergency Provisions):** **Art. 352** (National Emergency: War, External Aggression, Armed Rebellion; approved within 1 month by special majority); **Art. 355** (Duty of Union to protect States against external aggression and internal disturbance); **Art. 356** (President's Rule upon failure of constitutional machinery in State; approved within 2 months by simple majority; max 3 years); **Art. 358** (Automatic suspension of Art. 19 during external emergency only); **Art. 359** (Suspension of right to move courts for enforcement of specified FRs, except Arts. 20 & 21); **Art. 360** (Financial Emergency; never imposed).
39. **Part XIX (Articles 361–367 — Miscellaneous):** **Art. 361** (Immunity of President and Governors from criminal proceedings and arrest during term of office); **Art. 365** (Effect of failure to comply with, or to give effect to, directions given by the Union — ground for President's Rule under Art. 356).
40. **Part XX (Article 368 — Amendment of the Constitution):** Sole Article in Part XX; prescribes two types of majorities for amendment: (1) Special majority (majority of total membership + 2/3rd present and voting), and (2) Special majority + Ratification by half the State legislatures by simple majority; President must give assent (24th Amd 1971); basic structure cannot be amended (*Kesavananda Bharati*, 1973).
41. **Special Provisions under Articles 371 to 371-J (Part XXI):** Art. 371 (Maharashtra and Gujarat); Art. 371A (Nagaland); Art. 371B (Assam); Art. 371C (Manipur); Art. 371D & 371E (Andhra Pradesh & Telangana); Art. 371F (Sikkim); Art. 371G (Mizoram); Art. 371H (Arunachal Pradesh); Art. 371-I (Goa); Art. 371-J (Karnataka — Hyderabad-Karnataka region, 98th Amd 2012).
42. **Article 243ZD & 243ZE (Local Planning):** Art. 243ZD mandates the creation of a **District Planning Committee (DPC)** in every district to consolidate plans prepared by Panchayats and Municipalities; at least 4/5ths of DPC members must be elected by and from amongst elected members of Zila Panchayat and Municipalities. Art. 243ZE provides for Metropolitan Planning Committees.
43. **Article 262 vs 263 (Inter-State Coordination):** **Article 262** gives Parliament power to adjudicate inter-state river water disputes and bar courts' jurisdiction (Inter-State River Water Disputes Act, 1956); **Article 263** empowers the President to establish an **Inter-State Council** to investigate and coordinate policy across States (constituted 1990 on Sarkaria Commission advice).
44. **Article 266 vs 267 (Government Funds):** **Article 266** establishes the **Consolidated Fund of India** (all revenues, loans, and loan recoveries; no withdrawal without parliamentary Appropriation Act under Art. 114) and **Public Account of India** (provident funds, judicial deposits; executive operated); **Article 267** establishes the **Contingency Fund of India** (corpus ₹30,000 crore, held by Finance Secretary on behalf of President for unforeseen emergencies).
45. **Article 279A (GST Council):** Inserted by the **101st Amendment Act, 2016**; constitutional forum chaired by the Union Finance Minister; includes Union MoS Finance and Finance/Taxation Ministers of all States; decisions taken by a 3/4th weighted majority (Centre holds 1/3rd vote, all States combined hold 2/3rd vote).
46. **Article 300A (Right to Property Shift):** The Right to Property was abolished as a Fundamental Right under Arts. 19(1)(f) and 31 by the **44th Amendment Act, 1978** and re-enacted as a constitutional and legal right in Part XII under **Article 300A**; it is enforceable against executive action but not against valid legislative confiscation.
47. **Article 312 vs Article 315 (Services Matrix):** **Article 312** empowers Parliament to create new All-India Services provided the Rajya Sabha passes a resolution supported by a **2/3rd majority of members present and voting**; **Article 315** provides for the establishment of the Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) and State Public Service Commissions (SPSCs).
48. **Article 323A vs 323B (Tribunals Comparison):** **Article 323A** permits tribunals exclusively for administrative and service matters of public employees and can ONLY be established by **Parliament** (Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985); **Article 323B** covers tribunals for other subjects (taxation, foreign exchange, land reforms, election to legislatures) and can be established by **both Parliament and State Legislatures**.
49. **Articles 338, 338A, and 338B (Three Commissions Ladder):** **Article 338** governs the **National Commission for Scheduled Castes (NCSC)**; **Article 338A** governs the **National Commission for Scheduled Tribes (NCST)** (bifurcated by 89th Amd 2003); **Article 338B** governs the **National Commission for Backward Classes (NCBC)** (elevated from statutory to constitutional status by 102nd Amd 2018).
50. **Articles 350A and 350B (Linguistic Safeguards):** **Article 350A** places a constitutional duty on every State and local authority to provide adequate facilities for instruction in the **mother tongue at the primary stage** of education to children belonging to linguistic minority groups; **Article 350B** provides for a **Special Officer for Linguistic Minorities** appointed by the President to investigate and report on statutory safeguards.
51. **Schedule 1 (States and UTs):** Enumerates the names of 28 States and 8 Union Territories and specifies their territorial extents and jurisdictions.
52. **Schedule 2 (Emoluments & Allowances):** Specifies provisions relating to salaries and allowances of President, Governors, Speaker/Deputy Speaker of LS/LA, Chairman/Deputy Chairman of RS/LC, Judges of SC/HC, and the CAG (Note: PM and Ministers are NOT mentioned in Schedule 2).
53. **Schedule 3 (Forms of Oaths and Affirmations):** Contains 8 oath templates for Union Ministers, MPs, SC Judges, CAG, State Ministers, MLAs/MLCs, and HC Judges (Note: Oaths of President [Art. 60], Vice-President [Art. 69], and Governor [Art. 159] are NOT in Schedule 3).
54. **Schedule 4 (Rajya Sabha Seat Allocation):** Details the allocation of seats in the Council of States to States and UTs based on population; Uttar Pradesh has the highest allocation (**31 seats**), followed by Maharashtra (**19 seats**); 3 UTs have representation: Delhi (3), Puducherry (1), Jammu & Kashmir (4).
55. **Schedule 5 (Scheduled Areas & Tribes Administration):** Governs administration and control of Scheduled Areas and STs in 10 states (excluding the 4 Assam/Meghalaya/Tripura/Mizoram states); mandates Tribes Advisory Councils (TAC) consisting of up to 20 members (3/4th ST legislators).
56. **Schedule 6 (Tribal Areas in 4 NE States - ATM-M):** Special autonomous administration for **Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura, and Mizoram** through Autonomous District Councils (ADCs) of up to 30 members (26 elected, 4 nominated by Governor) with legislative, executive, and judicial powers.
57. **Schedule 7 (Three Legislative Lists):** Division of legislative powers under Article 246; **List I (Union List):** 97 original (100 present) items; **List II (State List):** 66 original (61 present) items; **List III (Concurrent List):** 47 original (52 present) items.
58. **Five Subjects Transferred by 42nd Amendment (1976):** Transferred from State List to Concurrent List: (1) Education, (2) Forests, (3) Weights and Measures, (4) Protection of wild animals and birds, and (5) Administration of justice, constitution and organization of all courts except SC and HCs.
59. **Schedule 8 (Official Languages Evolution):** Originally listed **14 languages**; currently **22 languages**; 21st Amendment 1967 added **Sindhi**; 71st Amendment 1992 added **Konkani, Manipuri, Nepali** (KMN); 92nd Amendment 2003 added **Bodo, Dogri, Maithili, Santhali** (BDMS); English is NOT in the 8th Schedule.
60. **Schedule 9 (Validation of Certain Acts):** Added by the **First Amendment Act, 1951** to protect land agrarian reform laws from judicial review on Fundamental Rights grounds; *I.R. Coelho (2007)* held laws placed in 9th Schedule after **24 April 1973** are open to judicial review.
61. **Schedule 10 (Anti-Defection Law):** Inserted by the **52nd Amendment Act, 1985** under Rajiv Gandhi; specifies disqualification of legislators on grounds of defection; split exception (1/3rd) was deleted by the **91st Amendment Act, 2003** (now only 2/3rd merger is protected).
62. **Schedule 11 (Panchayati Raj Functional Items):** Added by the **73rd Amendment Act, 1992**; lists **29 functional matters** within the jurisdiction of Panchayats (Article 243G), ranging from agriculture, minor irrigation, and animal husbandry to poverty alleviation and rural roads.
63. **Schedule 12 (Municipalities Functional Items):** Added by the **74th Amendment Act, 1992**; specifies **18 functional items** entrusted to Urban Local Bodies (Article 243W), including urban planning, regulation of land use, roads, bridges, and public health.
64. **Part IV-A (Fundamental Duties - Article 51A):** Inserted by the **42nd Amendment Act, 1976** on the recommendation of the **Swaran Singh Committee**; originally 10 duties; 11th duty (education for children 6–14 years) added by **86th Amendment Act, 2002**.
65. **Part IX-B (The Co-operative Societies - Articles 243ZH to 243ZT):** Added by the **97th Amendment Act, 2011**; gave constitutional status to cooperatives; Supreme Court in *Union of India v. Rajendra N. Shah (2021)* struck down Part IX-B in so far as it applies to state-level cooperatives for lack of state ratification under Article 368(2).
66. **Part XIV-A (Tribunals - Articles 323A and 323B):** Added by the **42nd Amendment Act, 1976**; Article 323A provides for Administrative Tribunals (CAT and SATs) for public service matters; Article 323B covers tribunals for other matters (taxation, foreign exchange, industrial disputes).
67. **Part XVIII (Emergency Provisions - Articles 352 to 360):** Article 352 (National Emergency on grounds of War, External Aggression, or Armed Rebellion); Article 356 (President’s Rule on failure of constitutional machinery in a State); Article 360 (Financial Emergency - never invoked so far).
68. **Part XX (Amendment of the Constitution - Article 368):** Outlines two specific amendment procedures: (a) by special majority of Parliament, and (b) by special majority plus ratification by at least half of State Legislatures by simple majority.
69. **Article 370 & Article 371 Series (Special Provisions):** Article 371 (Maharashtra & Gujarat); Article 371A (Nagaland); Article 371B (Assam); Article 371C (Manipur); Article 371D & 371E (Andhra Pradesh & Telangana); Article 371F (Sikkim); Article 371G (Mizoram); Article 371H (Arunachal Pradesh); Article 371-I (Goa); Article 371J (Karnataka - Hyderabad-Karnataka region).
70. **Article 1 to Article 4 (Part I Summary):** Article 1 (India, that is Bharat, shall be a Union of States); Article 2 (Admission or establishment of new States by Parliament); Article 3 (Formation of new States and alteration of areas, boundaries or names of existing States by ordinary law); Article 4 (Laws under Art. 2 and 3 not deemed amendments under Art. 368).
71. **Article 12 (Definition of 'State'):** Encompasses: (1) Government and Parliament of India, (2) Government and Legislature of each State, (3) All local authorities (Panchayats, Municipalities), and (4) Other authorities under control of Govt of India (ONGC, LIC, SAIL, statutory corporations).
72. **Article 13 (Laws Inconsistent with Fundamental Rights):** Article 13(1) pre-constitutional laws; 13(2) post-constitutional laws void to the extent of contravention; 13(3) defines 'law' to include ordinances, orders, bye-laws, rules, regulations, and customs; 13(4) inserted by 24th CAA 1971 declaring Art. 368 amendments outside Art. 13.
73. **Article 248 (Residuary Powers):** Exclusively vests residuary powers of legislation (matters not enumerated in any of the three Lists of the 7th Schedule) in the **Parliament of India**; borrowed from Canada.
74. **Article 280 (Finance Commission Composition):** Mandates President to constitute a Finance Commission every 5th year; consists of a Chairman and **4 other members** appointed by the President based on qualifications determined by Parliament (Finance Commission Act, 1951).
75. **Article 312 (All-India Services):** Parliament can create new All-India Services common to Union and States only if the **Rajya Sabha passes a resolution supported by not less than two-thirds of members present and voting** declaring it necessary in the national interest.
