## 🎯 Consolidated Must-Score Facts

1. **K. Santhanam Committee on Prevention of Corruption (1962–64):** Appointed by Lal Bahadur Shastri; recommended the establishment of the **Central Vigilance Commission (CVC)**, which was set up in 1964; recommended statutory backing for CBI under DSPE Act 1946; formulated a code of conduct for ministers.
2. **First Administrative Reforms Commission - ARC-I (1966–70):** Initially chaired by **Morarji Desai**, and upon his appointment as Deputy PM, by **K. Hanumanthaiah**; recommended the establishment of **Lokpal and Lokayuktas** ombudsman institutions, setting up the Inter-State Council under Art. 263, and introduction of performance budgeting.
3. **Second Administrative Reforms Commission - ARC-II (2005–09):** Chaired initially by **Veerappa Moily**, and later by **V. Ramachandran**; submitted **15 landmark reports** including *Right to Information (Report 1)*, *Unlocking Human Capital (Report 2)*, *Ethics in Governance (Report 4)*, *Local Governance (Report 6)*, and *Citizen-Centric Administration (Report 12)*.
4. **Balwant Rai Mehta Committee (1957):** Appointed to examine the working of the Community Development Programme (1952); recommended the establishment of a **3-tier Panchayati Raj system** (Gram Panchayat at village, Panchayat Samiti at block, and Zila Parishad at district); coined the term **'Democratic Decentralisation'**.
5. **Ashok Mehta Committee on Panchayati Raj (1977–78):** Appointed by Janata Party government; recommended replacing the 3-tier structure with a **2-tier system** consisting of a **Zila Parishad** at the district level and a **Mandal Panchayat** (covering a population of 15,000–20,000); recommended official participation of political parties and constitutional recognition.
6. **G.V.K. Rao Committee (1985):** Appointed by Planning Commission; warned that PRIs were being marginalized by bureaucracy, famously describing them as **'grass without roots'**; recommended making Zila Parishad the apex planning authority and creating the post of District Development Commissioner (DDC).
7. **Dr. L.M. Singhvi Committee (1986):** Appointed by Rajiv Gandhi government; **first committee to firmly recommend that Panchayati Raj institutions should be constitutionally recognized, protected, and preserved**; recommended constitutional provisions for free, regular, and fair local elections, and judicial Nyaya Panchayats.
8. **P.K. Thungon Committee (1988):** Sub-committee of Parliamentary Consultative Committee; recommended constitutional status for Panchayats, a mandatory fixed **5-year term**, and establishment of a **State Finance Commission** in every State.
9. **V.N. Gadgil Committee (1988):** Committee on Policy and Programmes; recommended a 3-tier system, direct elections for all members, reservations for SC/ST/women, and State Election and Finance Commissions; served as the direct basis for drafting the 73rd Amendment Bill.
10. **Dileep Singh Bhuria Committee (1994–95):** Appointed to formulate recommendations for extending Panchayati Raj to tribal scheduled areas; recommended empowering the Gram Sabha with customary resource control, leading directly to the enactment of the **PESA Act, 1996**.
11. **P.V. Rajamannar Committee (1969):** 3-member committee appointed by the **DMK government of Tamil Nadu** to examine Centre-State relations; recommended radical state autonomy: **repeal of Articles 356 and 365**, complete abolition of **All-India Services (IAS, IPS, IFS)**, and vesting residuary powers in States.
12. **Justice R.S. Sarkaria Commission (1983–88):** 3-member commission (Justice Sarkaria, B. Sivaraman, Dr. S.R. Sen) on Centre-State relations; submitted 247 recommendations; held that a strong Centre is vital for national integrity; recommended **Article 356 must be used only as an extreme last resort**, setting up a permanent **Inter-State Council under Art. 263** (established 1990), and retaining All-India Services.
13. **Justice M.M. Punchhi Commission (2007–10):** 5-member commission on Centre-State relations; recommended a **fixed 5-year tenure for Governors** with removal only through **impeachment by State Legislature**; recommended amending Arts. 355/356 to permit **'Localised Emergency'** in troubled districts without dissolving State Assemblies.
14. **V.M. Tarkunde Committee on Electoral Reforms (1974–75):** Appointed by Jayaprakash Narayan (Citizens for Democracy); recommended lowering the **voting age from 21 to 18 years**, making ECI a multi-member body, and granting television/radio broadcast time to recognized parties.
15. **Dinesh Goswami Committee on Electoral Reforms (1990):** Recommended the introduction of **Electronic Voting Machines (EVMs)**, rotation of reserved SC/ST constituencies, speedy disposal of election petitions, and stringent penalties for booth capturing.
16. **Indrajit Gupta Committee on State Funding of Elections (1998):** Recommended **partial State funding of elections**, restricted to recognized political parties and provided strictly **in kind** (free fuel, vehicles, paper, microphones, and airtime) rather than cash.
17. **N.N. Vohra Committee on Criminalisation of Politics (1993):** Investigated the nexus between organized criminal gangs, politicians, and bureaucrats; concluded that mafia networks had penetrated deep into political and administrative institutions.
18. **Justice J.S. Verma Committee on Amendments to Criminal Law (2013):** Formed after the 2012 Delhi Nirbhaya case; recommended widening definition of rape, criminalizing voyeurism and stalking, higher punishments for sexual offences, and police accountability; led to the Criminal Law (Amendment) Act, 2013.
19. **National Police Commission - NPC (1977–81):** Chaired by **Dharma Vira**; produced 8 comprehensive reports; recommended replacing the archaic Police Act of 1861, institutional mechanisms to insulate police from political interference, and mandatory judicial inquiry into custodial deaths and rapes.
20. **Julio Ribeiro Committee on Police Reforms (1998):** Appointed by Supreme Court order; endorsed NPC recommendations and proposed a model Police Act, State Security Commissions, and fixed 2-year tenure for police chiefs.
21. **K. Padmanabhaiah Committee on Police Reforms (2000):** Emphasized recruitment reforms, modernized training, community policing, and the **functional separation of investigation from law and order**.
22. **Justice V.S. Malimath Committee on Reforms of Criminal Justice System (2003):** Recommended shifting from adversarial to inquisitorial elements, modifying standard of proof to **'court convinced'** (instead of 'beyond reasonable doubt'), victim compensation schemes, and making confessional statements to SP-rank police admissible.
23. **First Backward Classes Commission - Kaka Kalelkar Commission (1953–55):** Appointed by President under **Article 340**; identified 2,399 backward castes; recommended caste as criterion for backwardness, but Chairman Kalelkar appended a dissenting note urging economic criteria; recommendations were not implemented.
24. **Second Backward Classes Commission - Mandal Commission (1979–80):** Appointed under **Article 340** by Morarji Desai government chaired by **B.P. Mandal**; submitted report in December 1980 identifying 3,743 castes (52% of population) and recommending **27% reservation for Other Backward Classes (OBCs)** in Central government jobs and public sector undertakings; implemented in 1990 by V.P. Singh government.
25. **Justice G. Rohini Commission (2017–2023):** 4-member commission constituted under **Article 340** to examine the **sub-categorization of Other Backward Classes (OBCs)** in the Central List, ensuring equitable distribution of the 27% quota among dominant and marginalized OBC groups.
26. **National Commission to Review the Working of the Constitution - NCRWC (2000–2002):** 11-member commission appointed under former CJI **Justice M.N. Venkatachaliah**; recommended retaining the parliamentary system, introducing a constructive vote of no-confidence, enlarging Part III rights (rural development, safe environment), and establishing a National Judicial Commission.
27. **S.K. Dhar Commission (June 1948):** Appointed by President of CA Dr. Rajendra Prasad to examine linguistic state reorganization; **firmly rejected language** as state reorganization criterion, giving primacy to administrative convenience, geographical contiguity, and financial self-sufficiency.
28. **JVP Committee (December 1948):** Three-member committee consisting of **Jawaharlal Nehru, Vallabhbhai Patel, and Pattabhi Sitaramayya**; formally endorsed Dhar Commission findings and **rejected language as the basis of state reorganization**.
29. **States Reorganisation Commission - SRC / Fazl Ali Commission (1953–55):** 3-member commission (**Justice Fazl Ali, H.N. Kunzru, K.M. Panikkar**); accepted language as a major basis of state boundary demarcation but rejected 'one language, one state'; recommended creating 16 states and 3 union territories; led to the **7th Amendment Act, 1956**.
30. **Aitchison Commission on Public Services (1886):** Appointed by Lord Dufferin; recommended dropping the terms 'Covenanted' and 'Uncovenanted', creating a **3-tier civil service: Imperial Civil Service, Provincial Civil Service, and Subordinate Civil Service**.
31. **Lee Commission on Superior Civil Services (1923–24):** Recommended 50:50 parity between British and Indian officers in the ICS within 15 years, and urged the immediate establishment of a statutory **Public Service Commission** (established in **1926** under GOI Act 1919).
32. **D.S. Kothari Committee on Recruitment Policy and Selection Methods (1974–76):** Recommended the modern **3-stage Civil Services Examination structure**: Civil Services Preliminary Examination (objective), Main Examination (written), and Personality Test (interview).
33. **Raja Chelliah Committee on Tax Reforms (1991–93):** Laid the foundation for India's modern tax reforms post-liberalization; recommended lowering personal and corporate income tax rates, broadening tax base, reducing peak customs tariffs, and introducing Service Tax (introduced in 1994).
34. **Vijay Kelkar Committee on Fiscal Responsibility (2002):** Formulated the roadmap for the **Fiscal Responsibility and Budget Management (FRBM) Act, 2003**; recommended phased reduction of fiscal deficit to 3% of GDP and first proposed a nationwide integrated **Goods and Services Tax (GST)**.
35. **Justice J.C. Shah Commission of Inquiry (1977–78):** Appointed by Morarji Desai government to investigate excesses, illegal detentions, and misuse of power committed during the **Emergency (1975–77)**; its findings were the catalyst for the remedial **44th Constitutional Amendment Act, 1978**.
