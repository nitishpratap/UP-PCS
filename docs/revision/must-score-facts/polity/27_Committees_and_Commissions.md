## 🎯 Consolidated Must-Score Facts

1. **Santhanam Committee (1962–64):** Committee on Prevention of Corruption; recommended establishment of the **Central Vigilance Commission (CVC, set up in 1964)**; granted statutory status in 2003. *(Also note: K. Santhanam headed a 1963 committee on Panchayati Raj finances).*
2. **Swaran Singh Committee (1976):** Recommended inclusion of **Fundamental Duties** (Part IVA, Article 51A) incorporated by the **42nd Constitutional Amendment Act 1976** (recommended 8 duties; 10 enacted in 1976; 11th added in 2002 via 86th Amd).
3. **Verma Committee (1999):** Chaired by Justice J.S. Verma; identified legal provisions for the implementation and enforcement of Fundamental Duties under existing statutes.
4. **Balwant Rai Mehta Committee (1957):** Recommended a **3-tier Panchayati Raj system** (Gram Panchayat at village, Panchayat Samiti at block, Zila Parishad at district) under the principle of **"Democratic Decentralization"**; first adopted by Rajasthan (Nagaur, 2 Oct 1959).
5. **Ashok Mehta Committee (1977):** Appointed by Janata Party Govt; recommended replacing the 3-tier structure with a **2-tier system** (Mandal Panchayat for a cluster of 15,000–20,000 pop, and Zila Parishad at district); advocated official participation of political parties and Nyaya Panchayats.
6. **G. V. K. Rao Committee (1985):** Appointed by Planning Commission; criticized bureaucratic dominance of rural administration, famously warning that PRIs had become **"grass without roots"**; recommended creating the post of District Development Commissioner (DDC).
7. **L. M. Singhvi Committee (1986):** Appointed by Rajiv Gandhi Govt; **FIRST to recommend according Constitutional Status to Panchayati Raj Institutions** (culminating in 73rd Amendment 1992); advocated revival of Nyaya Panchayats.
8. **P. K. Thungon Committee (1988):** Sub-committee of Parliamentary Consultative Committee; strongly urged **constitutional recognition** for PRIs, 5-year fixed tenure, and establishing State Finance Commissions.
9. **V. N. Gadgil Committee (1988):** Policy and Programmes Committee of Congress Party; formulated the actual legislative blueprint for the 73rd Amendment Bill (3 tiers, reservations, SEC, SFC).
10. **Chronology of Panchayati Raj Committees:** Balwant Rai Mehta (1957) → K. Santhanam (1963) → Ashok Mehta (1977) → Dantwala (1978) → G.V.K. Rao (1985) → L.M. Singhvi (1986) → P.K. Thungon (1988) → V.N. Gadgil (1988).
11. **Rajamannar Committee (1969):** Appointed by Tamil Nadu (DMK) Govt; strongly advocated State Autonomy; recommended repeal of Article 356, deletion of Article 365, vesting residuary powers in States, and abolishing All-India Services (IAS/IPS).
12. **Sarkaria Commission (1983–88):** Appointed by Central Govt on Centre-State Relations (Justice R.S. Sarkaria, B. Sivaraman, Dr. S.R. Sen); landmark recommendations: **Article 356 must be used as an extreme last resort**; set up permanent **Inter-State Council under Art. 263** (established 1990); retain and strengthen All-India Services.
13. **M. M. Punchhi Commission (2007–10):** Appointed by Central Govt on Centre-State Relations; recommended localized emergency under Art. 355/356 without dissolving state assemblies, fixed 5-year tenure for Governors, removal of Governors through state legislative impeachment, and stripping Governors of Chancellor posts.
14. **Dinesh Goswami Committee (1990):** Landmark report on **Electoral Reforms**; recommended extensive deployment of Electronic Voting Machines (EVMs), strict checks on booth capturing, and checking misuse of government machinery.
15. **Indrajit Gupta Committee (1998):** Examined **State Funding of Elections**; recommended financial assistance in kind (fuel, paper, airtime) exclusively to recognized political parties and their candidates.
16. **Tarkunde Committee (1974):** Appointed by Jayaprakash Narayan (Citizens for Democracy); recommended lowering the **voting age from 21 to 18 years** (enacted via 61st Amendment 1988).
17. **Vohra Committee (1993):** Chaired by Home Secretary N.N. Vohra; investigated the dangerous **nexus among politicians, bureaucrats, and underworld criminal syndicates** (criminalization of politics).
18. **First Backward Classes Commission (1953):** Appointed under Article 340 under the chairmanship of **Kaka Kalelkar**; submitted report in 1955 listing 2,399 backward castes.
19. **Second Backward Classes Commission (1979):** Appointed under Article 340 by Morarji Desai Govt under **B. P. Mandal (Mandal Commission)**; recommended **27% reservation for OBCs** in Union government civil posts and public enterprises (implemented August 1990).
20. **Justice G. Rohini Commission (2017):** Appointed under Article 340 for the **Sub-categorization of OBCs** to ensure equitable sharing of reservation benefits among marginalized backward groups.
21. **Kothari Commission (1964–66):** National Education Commission chaired by Dr. D.S. Kothari; recommended the **10+2+3 educational structure**, a common school system, and allocating at least 6% of GNP to education.
22. **Dr. K. Kasturirangan Committee (2019):** Drafted the **National Education Policy (NEP 2020)**, replacing the 10+2 format with a **5+3+3+4 curricular structure** spanning foundational, preparatory, middle, and secondary stages.
23. **Sachar Committee (2005–06):** Chaired by Justice Rajindar Sachar; comprehensively documented socio-economic, educational, and employment deficits of the Indian Muslim community.
24. **Xaxa Committee (2013):** High-level committee chaired by Prof. Virginius Xaxa; studied socio-economic, educational, health, and land-alienation issues of Scheduled Tribes across India.
25. **M. S. Swaminathan Commission (2004–06):** National Commission on Farmers; recommended that the Minimum Support Price (MSP) should be pegged at least **C2 + 50%** (comprehensive cost plus 50% margin).
26. **1st Administrative Reforms Commission (1966):** Chaired initially by **Morarji Desai** (later **K. Hanumanthaiah**); submitted 20 reports; famously recommended the creation of **Lokpal and Lokayukta** institutions.
27. **2nd Administrative Reforms Commission (2005):** Chaired by **Veerappa Moily** (later V. Ramachandran); submitted 15 landmark reports covering Right to Information, Ethics in Governance, Crisis Management, and Public Order.

A. Verma Committee
B. Swaran Singh Committee

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: B** — The Swaran Singh Committee (1976) recommended the inclusion of a separate chapter on Fundamental Duties. In response, the 42nd Amendment Act 1976 introduced Part IVA and Article 51A containing 10 Fundamental Duties (an 11th duty was added by the 86th Amendment 2002).
