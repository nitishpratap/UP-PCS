## 🎯 Consolidated Must-Score Facts

1. **K. Santhanam Committee on Prevention of Corruption (1962–64):** Appointed by Lal Bahadur Shastri; recommended the establishment of the **Central Vigilance Commission (CVC)**, which was set up in 1964; recommended statutory backing for CBI under DSPE Act 1946; formulated a code of conduct for ministers.
2. **First Administrative Reforms Commission - ARC-I (1966–70):** Initially chaired by **Morarji Desai**, and upon his appointment as Deputy PM, by **K. Hanumanthaiah**; recommended the establishment of **Lokpal and Lokayuktas** ombudsman institutions, setting up the Inter-State Council under Art. 263, and introduction of performance budgeting.
3. **Second Administrative Reforms Commission - ARC-II (2005–09):** Chaired initially by **Veerappa Moily**, and later by **V. Ramachandran**; submitted **15 landmark reports** including *Right to Information (Report 1)*, *Unlocking Human Capital (Report 2)*, *Ethics in Governance (Report 4)*, *Local Governance (Report 6)*, and *Citizen-Centric Administration (Report 12)*.
4. **Balwant Rai Mehta Committee (1957):** Appointed to examine the working of the Community Development Programme (1952); recommended the establishment of a **3-tier Panchayati Raj system** (Gram Panchayat at village, Panchayat Samiti at block, and Zila Parishad at district); coined the term **'Democratic Decentralisation'**.
5. **Ashok Mehta Committee on Panchayati Raj (1977–78):** Appointed by Janata Party government; recommended replacing the 3-tier structure with a **2-tier system** consisting of a **Zila Parishad** at the district level and a **Mandal Panchayat** (covering a population of 15,000–20,000); recommended official participation of political parties and constitutional recognition.
6. **G.V.K. Rao Committee (1985):** Appointed by Planning Commission; warned that PRIs were being marginalized by bureaucracy, famously describing them as **'grass without roots'**; recommended making Zila Parishad the apex planning authority and creating the post of District Development Commissioner (DDC).
7. **Dr. L.M. Singhvi Committee (1986):** Appointed by Rajiv Gandhi government; **first committee to firmly recommend that Panchayati Raj institutions should be constitutionally recognized, protected, and preserved**; recommended constitutional provisions for free, regular, and fair local elections, and judicial Nyaya Panchayats.
8. **P.K. Thungon Committee (1988):** Sub-committee of Parliamentary Consultative Committee; recommended constitutional status for Panchayats, a mandatory fixed **5-year term**, and establishment of a **State Finance Commission** in every State.
9. **V.N. Gadgil Committee (1988):** Committee on Policy and Programmes; recommended a 3-tier system, direct elections for all members, reservations for SC/ST/women, and State Election and Finance Commissions; served as the direct basis for drafting the 73rd Amendment Bill.
10. **Dileep Singh Bhuria Committee (1994–95):** Appointed to formulate recommendations for extending Panchayati Raj to tribal scheduled areas; recommended empowering the Gram Sabha with customary resource control, leading directly to the enactment of the **PESA Act, 1996**.
11. **P.V. Rajamannar Committee (1969):** 3-member committee appointed by the **DMK government of Tamil Nadu** to examine Centre-State relations; recommended radical state autonomy: **repeal of Articles 356 and 365**, complete abolition of **All-India Services (IAS, IPS, IFS)**, and vesting residuary powers in States.
12. **Justice R.S. Sarkaria Commission (1983–88):** 3-member commission (Justice Sarkaria, B. Sivaraman, Dr. S.R. Sen) on Centre-State relations; submitted 247 recommendations; held that a strong Centre is vital for national integrity; recommended **Article 356 must be used only as an extreme last resort**, setting up a permanent **Inter-State Council under Art. 263** (established 1990), and retaining All-India Services.
13. **Justice M.M. Punchhi Commission (2007–10):** 5-member commission on Centre-State relations; recommended a **fixed 5-year tenure for Governors** with removal only through **impeachment by State Legislature**; recommended amending Arts. 355/356 to permit **'Localised Emergency'** in troubled districts without dismissing the entire state government.
14. **Swaran Singh Committee (1976):** Appointed by Congress government under Indira Gandhi; recommended incorporating **Fundamental Duties** into the Constitution; recommended 8 duties; Parliament enacted the **42nd Amendment Act, 1976 inserting Part IVA and Article 51A with 10 duties** (Swaran Singh's duty to pay taxes was omitted).
15. **Justice J.S. Verma Committee on Fundamental Duties (1999):** Operationalized identification of legal provisions for the enforcement of Fundamental Duties (e.g., Prevention of Insults to National Honour Act 1971, Wildlife Protection Act 1972).
16. **Tarkunde Committee on Electoral Reforms (1974–75):** Appointed by Jayaprakash Narayan (Citizens for Democracy); **first committee to recommend lowering the voting age from 21 to 18 years**; recommended 3-member Election Commission.
17. **Dinesh Goswami Committee on Electoral Reforms (1990):** Recommended the introduction of **Electronic Voting Machines (EVMs)**, state assistance in kind, and amending the Anti-Defection Law to restrict disqualification only to voluntary resignation or defying party whip.
18. **N.N. Vohra Committee on Criminalisation of Politics (1993):** Investigated the nexus between criminal syndicates, politicians, and bureaucrats post-1993 Bombay blasts; recommended creating a central nodal intelligence cell.
19. **Indrajit Gupta Committee on State Funding of Elections (1998):** Recommended **partial state funding of elections strictly in kind** (paper, fuel, airtime) to recognized political parties, not cash; recommended creating a ₹600 crore election fund.
20. **Justice M.N. Venkatachaliah Commission (NCRWC, 2000–2002):** National Commission to Review the Working of the Constitution; 11-member body; recommended retaining parliamentary democracy, introducing constructive vote of no-confidence, creating National Judicial Commission, and enlarging fundamental rights.
21. **Kaka Kalelkar Commission (1953–55):** **First Backward Classes Commission** appointed under Article 340 by President Dr. Rajendra Prasad; identified 2,399 backward castes; recommended caste-based census and reservations; chairman himself dissented in the forwarding letter.
22. **B.P. Mandal Commission (1979–80):** **Second Backward Classes Commission** appointed under Article 340 by Janata Party government; identified 3,743 castes comprising 52% of the population; recommended **27% reservation for OBCs in government jobs and educational institutions**; implemented in 1990 by V.P. Singh government.
23. **Justice G. Rohini Commission (2017–2023):** 4-member commission constituted under **Article 340** to examine **sub-categorization of Other Backward Classes (OBCs)** in the Central List to ensure equitable distribution of the 27% quota.
24. **S.K. Dhar Commission (June 1948):** Linguistic Provinces Commission appointed by CA President Dr. Rajendra Prasad; **firmly rejected language as the basis of state reorganization**, prioritizing administrative convenience and economic viability.
25. **JVP Committee (December 1948):** Comprising **Jawaharlal Nehru, Vallabhbhai Patel, and Pattabhi Sitaramayya**; formally endorsed Dhar Commission findings and **rejected language as the basis of reorganization of states**.
26. **Fazl Ali Commission / States Reorganisation Commission (SRC, 1953–55):** 3 members: **Justice Fazl Ali (Chair), H.N. Kunzru, and K.M. Panikkar**; accepted language as a major basis but rejected 'one language, one state'; recommended creating 16 states and 3 UTs; led to the **7th Constitutional Amendment Act, 1956**.
27. **Aitchison Commission on Public Services (1886):** Appointed by Lord Dufferin; created a **3-tier civil service: Imperial, Provincial, and Subordinate Civil Services**.
28. **Lee Commission on Superior Civil Services (1923–24):** Recommended 50:50 parity between British and Indian officers in ICS within 15 years; recommended immediate creation of a statutory **Public Service Commission** (established in 1926).
29. **D.S. Kothari Committee on Recruitment Policy (1974–76):** Designed the modern **3-stage Civil Services Examination**: Preliminary (objective), Main (written), and Personality Test (interview).
30. **Raja Chelliah Committee on Tax Reforms (1991–93):** Laid the foundation for modern tax reforms post-liberalization; recommended lowering personal/corporate taxes, broadening tax base, and introducing Service Tax (1994).
31. **Vijay Kelkar Committee on Fiscal Responsibility (2002):** Formulated roadmap for the **FRBM Act, 2003**; recommended phased reduction of fiscal deficit to 3% of GDP and first proposed a nationwide integrated **GST**.
32. **Justice J.C. Shah Commission of Inquiry (1977–78):** Appointed to investigate excesses, illegal detentions, and misuse of power committed during the **Emergency (1975–77)**; catalyst for the **44th CAA, 1978**.
33. **Radhakrishnan Commission (1948–49):** University Education Commission chaired by Dr. S. Radhakrishnan; recommended establishing the **University Grants Commission (UGC)** (established 1953, statutory 1956).
34. **Mudaliar Commission (1952–53):** Secondary Education Commission chaired by Dr. A.L. Mudaliar; recommended diversifying secondary curriculum and introducing 3-year secondary and 4-year higher education structure.
35. **D.S. Kothari Commission on Education (1964–66):** Core National Education Commission; motto: *"The destiny of India is now being shaped in her classrooms"*; recommended the **10+2+3 educational structure** and spending 6% of national income on education; led to National Policy on Education 1968.
36. **High-Level Committee on Simultaneous Elections (2023–24):** Chaired by former President **Ram Nath Kovind**; submitted report in March 2024 recommending synchronous elections for Lok Sabha and State Assemblies (Step 1), followed by local bodies within 100 days (Step 2).
37. **22nd Law Commission of India (2020–24):** Chaired by **Justice Ritu Raj Awasthi**; submitted landmark reports on Uniform Civil Code (UCC) and sedition law safeguards.
38. **Justice Ranjana Prakash Desai Delimitation Commission (2020–22):** Redrew J&K assembly seats (90 seats: 43 Jammu, 47 Kashmir) and 5 parliamentary constituencies; reserved 9 seats for STs and 7 for SCs.
39. **Dr. K. Kasturirangan Committee (2017–20):** Drafted **NEP 2020**; replaced 10+2 with 5+3+3+4 schooling model; promoted foundational literacy and multidisciplinary education.
40. **Justice J.S. Verma Committee on Criminal Law Amendments (2012–13):** Formed post-2012 Delhi gang rape; recommended expanding sexual assault laws, strict punishment for stalking and acid attacks; resulted in the **Criminal Law (Amendment) Act, 2013**.
41. **Justice V.S. Malimath Committee on Criminal Justice Reforms (2000–03):** Recommended introducing inquisitorial features into adversarial trial system; proposed standard of proof as *"court is convinced that it is true"* rather than "beyond reasonable doubt".
42. **Soli Sorabjee Committee on Model Police Act (2005–06):** Drafted the **Model Police Act, 2006** to replace the archaic Police Act, 1861; recommended separating law and order from investigation.
43. **National Police Commission - NPC (1977–81):** Chaired by **Dharam Vira**; submitted 8 reports; first post-independence police commission recommending statutory State Security Commissions and fixed tenures for police leadership.
44. **T.S.R. Subramanian Committee on Civil Services Reforms (2004):** Recommended fixed tenure for civil servants, creation of independent Civil Services Boards (CSBs), and mandatory recording of verbal instructions from political executives.
45. **B.K. Chaturvedi Committee on CSS (2011):** Recommended rationalizing Centrally Sponsored Schemes (CSS) into flexible umbrella classifications to give states greater fiscal autonomy.
46. **Urjit Patel Committee on Monetary Policy (2014):** Recommended adopting CPI inflation as the primary nominal anchor and creating the statutory **Monetary Policy Committee (MPC)** with 4% (+/- 2%) inflation target.
47. **Justice B.P. Jeevan Reddy Committee on AFSPA (2004–05):** Reviewed the Armed Forces Special Powers Act; recommended **repealing AFSPA, 1958** and incorporating necessary provisions into the Unlawful Activities (Prevention) Act (UAPA).
48. **Anandpur Sahib Resolution (1973):** Passed by Shiromani Akali Dal; demanded restricting Union government powers strictly to 4 subjects: **Defence, Foreign Affairs, Currency, and Communications**, transferring all other subjects and residuary powers to the States.
49. **West Bengal Memorandum on Centre-State Relations (1977):** Published by the Left Front government of West Bengal; demanded replacing the word 'Union' with 'Federation' in the Constitution, repealing Articles 356, 357, and 360, and transferring residuary powers to States.
50. **Drafting Committee of Constituent Assembly (1947–50):** Appointed on **29 August 1947**; chaired by **Dr. B.R. Ambedkar**; comprised 7 members: (1) Dr. B.R. Ambedkar (Chairman); (2) N. Gopalaswami Ayyangar; (3) Alladi Krishnaswami Ayyar; (4) Dr. K.M. Munshi; (5) Syed Mohammad Saadulla; (6) N. Madhava Rau (replaced B.L. Mitter); (7) T.T. Krishnamachari (replaced D.P. Khaitan).

**Q1.**
The establishment of the Central Vigilance Commission (CVC) in 1964 was recommended by which of the following committees?

A. Santhanam Committee
B. Gorwala Committee
C. Administrative Reforms Commission
D. Kripalani Committee

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: A** — The Central Vigilance Commission (CVC) was set up in 1964 pursuant to the recommendations of the Committee on Prevention of Corruption chaired by K. Santhanam (1962–64). It was later granted statutory status by the CVC Act, 2003.

</details>

**Q2.**
Which committee recommended the incorporation of Fundamental Duties into the Constitution of India, which was subsequently enacted via the 42nd Constitutional Amendment Act, 1976?

A. Verma Committee
B. Swaran Singh Committee
C. Sarkaria Commission
D. Dhar Commission

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: B** — The Swaran Singh Committee (1976) recommended the inclusion of a separate chapter on Fundamental Duties. In response, the 42nd Amendment Act 1976 introduced Part IVA and Article 51A containing 10 Fundamental Duties (an 11th duty was added by the 86th Amendment 2002).

</details>

**Q3.**
The Justice J.S. Verma Committee (1999) on Fundamental Duties was constituted primarily to:

A. Recommend adding more duties to Part IVA
B. Identify the existence of legal provisions for the implementation and enforcement of Fundamental Duties
C. Recommend punitive measures for non-performance of duties
D. Examine the relationship between Fundamental Rights and Directive Principles

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: B** — The Verma Committee (1999) identified existing legal enactments and statutory provisions that enforce various Fundamental Duties (e.g., Prevention of Insults to National Honour Act, Wildlife Protection Act, Representation of the People Act).

</details>

**Q4.**
Which committee recommended the establishment of a 'Three-Tier Panchayati Raj System' based on the principle of 'Democratic Decentralization'?

B. Ashok Mehta Committee
C. G.V.K. Rao Committee
D. L.M. Singhvi Committee

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: A** — The Balwant Rai Mehta Committee (appointed January 1957, submitted November 1957) recommended a 3-tier Panchayati Raj system: Gram Panchayat at village level, Panchayat Samiti at block level, and Zilla Parishad at district level.
