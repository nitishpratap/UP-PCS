## 🎯 Consolidated Must-Score Facts

1. **Walter G. Rosen (1985)** coined the word *biodiversity*. **E.O. Wilson** popularised it. **Whittaker (1960)** advanced **alpha, beta, and gamma** diversity.
2. **Genetic diversity** is variation **within** a species. **Species diversity** is variety **among** species. **Ecosystem diversity** is variety of habitats and ecosystems.
3. **Alpha** = mean species diversity at a local habitat (वास स्थान). **Beta** = change between ecosystems. **Gamma** = overall regional diversity.
4. **Silent Valley (साइलेंट वैली) (Kerala)** is among India’s richest rainforest biodiversity sites (NP **1984**; Silent Valley Project = Kerala). **Valley of Flowers** (फूलों की घाटी) is in **Chamoli (चमोली), Uttarakhand (उत्तराखंड)**. Himalayan richness reflects confluence of biogeographic zones.
5. **In-situ** (स्थान पर) = natural habitat (NP, sanctuary, BR, sacred grove). **Ex-situ** (स्थानांतरित) = outside habitat (zoo, botanical garden (वनस्पति उद्यान), seed bank (बीज बैंक)). Cryo-banks use **liquid nitrogen**. Biosphere reserves (जैवमंडल आरक्षित क्षेत्र) best join biodiversity with traditional human life / cultural diversity.
6. **TRAFFIC** (WWF (डब्ल्यूडब्ल्यूएफ)–IUCN alliance, 1976) monitors wildlife trade (पण्याध्यक्ष) — not a UNEP (संयुक्त राष्ट्र पर्यावरण कार्यक्रम) bureau.
7. **Diclofenac** (डाइक्लोफेनाक) veterinary painkiller caused India’s vulture crash. **Dugong** = herbivorous marine mammal (Schedule I); Gulf of Mannar / Palk. **Indian wild ass** = Rann of Kachchh. **Lion-tailed macaque** = Western Ghats (Kerala, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu (तमिलनाडु)).
8. **Sea buckthorn** (Ladakh (लद्दाख) / cold Himalaya) checks erosion and is nutritious — not primarily a biodiesel (बायो-डीजल) timber crop.
9. **TEEB** makes nature’s economic values visible for decision-makers. **Wetlands International** is an independent NGO (HQ Netherlands), not a Ramsar intergovernmental organ.
10. **CEBPOL** (Centre for Biodiversity Policy and Law) sits with NBA, **Chennai** (India–Norway).
11. **Gulf of Mannar Biosphere Reserve (जैवमंडल आरक्षित क्षेत्र)** is often cited as India’s richest marine biodiversity region (21 islands mosaic).
12. **Biodiversity** is the variety and variability of life at **genetic**, **species**, and **ecosystem** (पारिस्थितिकी तंत्र) levels in a given area — the diverse range of plants and animals in an ecosystem.
13. **Walter G. Rosen (1985)** coined the word *biodiversity* (from “biological diversity”) for a National Research Council seminar title. **E.O. Wilson** popularised the term thereafter.
14. **International Day for Biological Diversity** is **22 May** (UN moved it from the older **29 December** CBD entry-into-force date). The UN declared **2011–2020** the **Decade on Biodiversity**.
15. **Genetic diversity** is variation in genes and alleles **within** a species.
16. **Species diversity** depends on **species richness** (how many species) and **evenness** (how evenly individuals are spread).
17. **R.H. Whittaker (1960)** advanced the **alpha–beta–gamma** scale for species diversity.
18. **Alpha diversity** (अल्फा विविधता) is the **mean species diversity in habitats at a local scale**.
19. **Beta diversity** (बीटा विविधता) is the comparison / change of species **between** ecosystems.
20. **Gamma diversity** is overall diversity across different ecosystems in a **region**.
21. **Ecosystem diversity** is the variety of habitats and ecosystems in a region such as forests, wetlands, grasslands, and deserts.
22. **17 megadiverse countries** together hold most of the world's species. **India** is one of them.
23. **Russia is not** on the standard megadiverse list of 17.
24. **Silent Valley (Kerala, Palakkad)** is remnant moist evergreen rainforest of the Western Ghats.
25. **Direct values** include food, fuel, fibre, medicine, and timber taken from nature.
26. **Indirect values** include soil formation, prevention of soil erosion, recycling of waste, pollination of crops, climate regulation, and flood control.
27. **Ethical and aesthetic values** cover cultural, spiritual, and recreational benefits.
28. **Option value** is the potential future use of species and genes not yet exploited.
29. **Threatened species** = **VU + EN + CR**.
30. **Endangered** means **EN only**, not all threatened categories.
31. **DD** (Data Deficient) and **NE** (Not Evaluated) mean insufficient data for a full assessment.
32. **IUCN** (आईयूसीएन) is headquartered in **Gland, Switzerland**. It assesses species status but does **not** regulate international trade (**CITES** (साइट्स) does that).
33. **IPBES** (Intergovernmental Science-Policy Platform on Biodiversity and Ecosystem Services) is headquartered in **Bonn, Germany**. It gives biodiversity assessment reports similar to IPCC (आईपीसीसी) for climate.
34. **State Biodiversity Boards (SBB)** and **Biodiversity Management Committees (BMC)** operate at state and local levels.
35. **Biodiversity Management Committees (BMCs)** are central to Nagoya-style ABS; they may levy **collection fees** on access to biological resources in their jurisdiction.

## ⚡ Confused Pairs & Common Examiner Traps

— Don't Fall For These

| Trap | Correct fact |
|------|----------------|
| Biodiversity = species count only | **Genetic + species + ecosystem** |
| Wilson coined “biodiversity” | **Walter G. Rosen (1985)** coined; Wilson popularised |
| Alpha = regional diversity | Alpha = **local** mean; gamma = regional |
| Russia is megadiverse | **Not** in the standard 17 list |
| India has 6 hotspots | **4** — Himalaya, Indo-Burma, WG–Sri Lanka, Sundaland |
| Sundaland = Andaman + Nicobar | **Nicobar** under Sundaland; **Andaman** under Indo-Burma |
| Eastern Ghats / Vindhyas = hotspot | **Not** hotspots |
| Hotspots only in tropics | Temperate hotspots also exist |
| Threatened = Endangered | Threatened = **VU + EN + CR** |
| Botanical garden = in-situ | **Ex-situ** |
| Cryo-bank gas = oxygen / CO₂ | **Liquid nitrogen** |
| Lichens thrive in pollution | **Sensitive** indicators — fail in pollution |
| Ex-situ preferred over in-situ | **In-situ preferred** |
| NBA headquarters Delhi | **Chennai** |
| BirdLife invented hotspots | **Norman Myers**; BirdLife = IBAs |
| TRAFFIC = UNEP bureau | **WWF–IUCN** NGO alliance |
| Coral reefs in Sundarbans | Sundarbans = **mangroves**; reefs = GoM, GoK, A&N, Lakshadweep |
| Vegetarianism threatens biodiversity | **Not** a threat |
| NCMB = Mumbai / Bhavnagar | **Jamnagar** |
| Silent Valley = Uttarakhand | **Kerala**; Valley of Flowers = Chamoli UK |
| India Ramsar ≈ 26 forever | About **80**; TN **16**, UP **10**, Punjab **6** |
| Gangetic Plain is a hotspot | **Not** a global hotspot |
