## 🎯 Consolidated Must-Score Facts

1. Headquarters map: **Kolkata** — BSI and ZSI; **Dehradun** (देहरादून) — FSI, FRI, and WII; **New Delhi** — NBPGR; **Chennai** — NBA.
2. **BSI (1890, Kolkata)** surveys **plants** (*Flora* (वनस्पति), plant Red Data Book (रेड डेटा बुक)). **ZSI (1916, Kolkata)** surveys **animals** (*Fauna* (प्राणीजात), animal Red Data Book).
3. **FSI (1981, Dehradun)** maps forest cover (वन आवरण) and publishes **ISFR every two years**. **FRI (1906, Dehradun)** does forestry **research** under the ICFRE world — it does **not** publish ISFR.
4. **WII (1982, Dehradun)** does wildlife research, IFS/officer training, and tiger-census protocols.
5. **NBA** under the Biological Diversity Act sits in **Chennai**, not Delhi. It handles foreign access and ABS at the national level.
6. Biodiversity pyramid (पिरामिड): **NBA (national) → SBB (state) → BMC (local)**. BMCs prepare the **People’s Biodiversity Register (PBR)**.
7. **NBPGR (1978, New Delhi, ICAR)** is the plant gene bank. It is **not** Kolkata BSI and **not** Karnal **NBAGR** (animals).
8. ISFR **2023** class figures often cited: India about **21.76%** forest cover; Uttar Pradesh (उत्तर प्रदेश) about **6.24%**. UP districts with **>20%** forest cover in that reading (रीडिंग) were **Chandauli and Shravasti only** — **Bahraich** was not in that pair.
9. BSI ≠ animals; ZSI ≠ plants; FRI ≠ ISFR; NBA ≠ Delhi; PBR ≠ NBA headquarters function.
10. SBB is the state biodiversity board — not the State Pollution Control Board.
11. NBPGR ≠ Kolkata. Gene bank is Delhi; plant survey is Kolkata.
12. ISFR is biennial, not annual.
13. UPSBB sits in the Lucknow (लखनऊ) state-biodiversity lane; WII supports Terai (तराई) wildlife science links.
14. BMC is local and owns PBR documentation of local biodiversity knowledge.
15. SBB supervises state-level commercial access and BMC work under BDA — domestic track beside NBA’s foreign-access focus.
16. FRI research ≠ FSI satellite forest-cover accounting, even though both are Dehradun forestry institutions.
17. WII is wildlife science/training — not the ISFR publisher and not the plant gene bank.
18. Red Data Books from BSI/ZSI are national catalogues; IUCN (आईयूसीएन) Red List remains the global risk ladder (different chapter neighbour).
19. NBA year fact rides on BDA **2002** institutionalisation — headquarters city remains Chennai.
20. Gene-bank NBPGR serves crop genetic resources for all states, including UP, from New Delhi.
21. Forest-cover percentage traps for UP hinge on Chandauli + Shravasti, not a long distractor list of Terai districts.
22. PBR is prepared locally by BMCs; it is not an NBA HQ publication series.
23. Match institution → city before matching function. Wrong city is enough to kill a match list.
24. Dehradun trio: FSI (cover/ISFR), FRI (research), WII (wildlife). Keep the three jobs separate.
25. **BSI** is the national organisation for survey, taxonomy, and documentation of **Indian plant diversity**.
26. **Does not** survey animals (ZSI), map forest cover (FSI), or store crop gene banks (NBPGR).
27. **ZSI** documents **Indian animal (faunal) diversity** — taxonomy and inventories.
28. **ZSI ≠ WII:** ZSI = species inventory/taxonomy; WII = applied wildlife ecology (पारिस्थितिकी) and training.
29. **FSI** monitors India's **forest and tree cover (वृक्ष आवरण)** using satellite imagery and ground verification.
30. **ISFR 2023:** India forest cover **21.76%**; UP **6.24%**.
31. **FRI** is India's premier **forestry research** institute — silviculture, pathology, entomology, wood science, NTFPs.
32. **FRI ≠ FSI:** both in Dehradun — FRI = **how to grow/manage forests**; FSI = **how much forest exists on the map**.
33. **WII** is MoEFCC's autonomous institute for **wildlife research, training, and conservation science**.
34. **Does not** do national plant/animal taxonomy (BSI/ZSI) or publish ISFR (FSI).
35. **NBA** is the national statutory body under the **Biological Diversity Act, 2002**.
36. **Does not** prepare People's Biodiversity Registers — that is **BMC**.
37. **Uttar Pradesh State Biodiversity Board (UPSBB)** sits at **Lucknow** (लखनऊ).
38. **SBB ≠ SPCB:** SBB = biodiversity (BDA); SPCB = pollution (Water/Air Acts).
39. **BMC** is the **local** committee under BDA 2002 — at panchayat, municipality, or cantonment level.
40. **PBR is not prepared by NBA or BSI**.
41. **NBPGR** conserves **plant genetic resources** — seeds, tissue, DNA — for food and agriculture.
42. **Ex-situ** (स्थानांतरित) conservation — complementary to in-situ (स्थान पर) protected areas.
43. **NBPGR ≠ BSI:** BSI surveys/taxonomy (Kolkata); NBPGR stores germplasm (Delhi).
44. **NBPGR ≠ NBAGR:** animals/livestock gene bank is **NBAGR, Karnal**.

## ⏱️ Timeline / Chronology

### Chronological Sequence of Environmental Legislation & Governance in India

```
1972 ──► Wildlife (Protection) Act (WPA) ➔ Apex PA statute, hunting prohibition, statutory schedules
1974 ──► Water (Prevention & Control of Pollution) Act ➔ Established Central & State Pollution Control Boards (CPCB/SPCB)
1980 ──► Forest (Conservation) Act (FCA) ➔ Mandatory prior central approval for diversion of forest land
1981 ──► Air (Prevention & Control of Pollution) Act ➔ Air pollution control; noise included via 1987 amendment
1986 ──► Environment (Protection) Act (EPA) ➔ Post-Bhopal umbrella statute under Art 253; EIA, CRZ, ESZ notifications
1991 ──► Public Liability Insurance Act ➔ Mandatory insurance for handling hazardous substances
2002 ──► Biological Diversity Act (BDA) ➔ 3-tier decentralized framework: NBA (Chennai) -> SBB -> BMC
2006 ──► Scheduled Tribes & Other Traditional Forest Dwellers (FRA) Act ➔ Individual & community forest rights
2010 ──► National Green Tribunal (NGT) Act ➔ Specialized judicial body for environmental disputes under Article 21
```

## 🧠 Memory Anchors & Mnemonics

- **4 Biodiversity Hotspots of India**: **H-I-W-S** (Himalaya, Indo-Burma, Western Ghats–Sri Lanka, Sundaland)
- **6 Kyoto Protocol Greenhouse Gases**: **C-M-N-H-P-S** (Carbon dioxide, Methane, Nitrous oxide, Hydrofluorocarbons, Perfluorocarbons, Sulphur hexafluoride; NF3 added later)
- **Trophic Energy Transfer Rule**: **Lindeman's 10% Law (1942)** — Only ~10% of energy is transferred to the next higher trophic level; 90% lost as metabolic heat.
- **WPA Schedules (Post-2022 Amendment)**:
  - **Schedule I**: Animal species enjoying highest level of protection (Blackbuck, Lion-tailed Macaque, Great Indian Bustard)
  - **Schedule II**: Animal species with lesser protection
  - **Schedule III**: Protected Plant species
  - **Schedule IV**: Specimens listed under CITES Appendices
- **Ozone Units**: Measured in **Dobson Units (DU)** using spectrophotometers (1 DU = 0.01 mm thickness at STP; normal ozone layer is ~300 DU; <220 DU is classified as an ozone hole).
- **Montreux Record**: Ramsar sites where ecological character has changed or is changing (In India: **Keoladeo National Park** and **Loktak Lake**; Chilika Lake was removed post-restoration).
