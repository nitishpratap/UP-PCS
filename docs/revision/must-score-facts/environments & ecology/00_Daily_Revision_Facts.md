22. **Pyramid of Energy**:
23. **ALWAYS UPRIGHT, WITHOUT EXCEPTION!**
24. **Pyramid of Biomass**:
25. **Terrestrial Ecosystems (Grassland, Forest)**: **UPRIGHT** (Producer biomass > Herbivore > Carnivore).
26. **Aquatic Ecosystems (Pond, Lake, Ocean)**: **INVERTED** (Phytoplankton standing crop biomass is much smaller than zooplankton and predatory fish).
27. **Pyramid of Numbers**:
28. **Grassland & Cropland**: **UPRIGHT** (Millions of grasses support fewer herbivores, and even fewer carnivores).
29. **Tree / Parasitic Ecosystem**: **INVERTED** (A single large tree supports hundreds of birds, which host thousands of parasites and hyper-parasites).
30. **Forest Ecosystem**: **Spindle-shaped** (Few large trees support many herbivores, which are eaten by fewer carnivores).
31. **Bioaccumulation**: Increase in the concentration of a chemical pollutant in an **individual organism over time** (rate of intake > rate of excretion).
32. **Biomagnification**: Progressive increase in the concentration of persistent, non-biodegradable, fat-soluble toxins (e.g., **DDT, Mercury, PCBs**) at **successively higher trophic levels** of a food chain.
33. **Water Vapour**: Most abundant natural greenhouse gas contributing greatest share to natural greenhouse effect (~60%), but has short atmospheric residence time.
34. **Carbon Dioxide (CO2)**: Primary anthropogenic driver of global climate change due to massive volume and century-long residence time.
35. **Methane (CH4)**: Major sources: Paddy/rice fields, ruminant livestock enteric fermentation, landfills, wetlands, coal mining.
