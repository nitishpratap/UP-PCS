## 🎯 Consolidated Must-Score Facts

1. **IPCC** sits in **Geneva**, issues Assessment Reports, and synthesises climate science. It does **not** set NDCs and is **not** the UNFCCC treaty body.
2. IPCC was established in **1988** by **WMO and UNEP** and shared the Nobel Peace Prize in **2007**.
3. **UNEP** (संयुक्त राष्ट्र पर्यावरण कार्यक्रम) headquarters is **Nairobi**. Flagship products include the **Emissions Gap Report** (उत्सर्जन अंतराल रिपोर्ट) and GEO. UNEP was created after Stockholm **1972**.
4. UNEP hosts several MEA secretariats (including Montreal, Basel, and Minamata lines) and works with GEF-linked convention finance.
5. **UNDP** headquarters is **New York**. It publishes the **Human Development Report** (from **1990**) and the **HDI**. HDR is UNDP, not UNEP.
6. HDI is linked to **Mahbub ul Haq** and **Amartya Sen**. Do not invent a wrong founding-name trap for HDR.
7. **IUCN** (आईयूसीएन) headquarters is **Gland**. It publishes the **Red List** of extinction risk. IUCN assesses risk; it does **not** regulate wildlife trade (पण्याध्यक्ष) — that is **CITES** (साइट्स).
8. On the Red List, **Threatened** means **Vulnerable + Endangered + Critically Endangered** (VU + EN + CR). “Endangered alone” is not the full threatened set.
9. **WWF** is a conservation NGO also linked to Gland, known for the panda logo and the **Living Planet Report / Living Planet Index**.
10. **FAO** headquarters is **Rome**. Environment-facing outputs include the **State of the World’s Forests (SOFO)** and agriculture–forest links. FAO partners UNEP on some chemical-convention work such as Rotterdam.
11. HQ memory: Nairobi = UNEP; New York = UNDP; Geneva = IPCC; Gland = IUCN and WWF; Rome = FAO.
12. IPCC ≠ UNFCCC. Science assessment is not the same as the climate treaty and COP process.
13. UNEP ≠ UNDP. Environment (पर्यावरण) programme versus development agency — swap reports and you lose marks.
14. Emissions Gap Report → **UNEP**. Human Development Report → **UNDP**. Red List → **IUCN**. Living Planet → **WWF**. Assessment Reports → **IPCC**.
15. IUCN ≠ CITES. Risk categories versus trade appendices.
16. WWF ≠ IUCN. NGO Living Planet work versus Red List authority.
17. FAO is food and agriculture first; it is not the Red List body and not the Emissions Gap publisher.
18. GEF (**1991**) finances global environmental conventions; do not confuse GEF with IPCC authorship of Assessment Reports.
19. Red List codes to know: CR, EN, VU as the threatened band; also EX, EW, NT, LC, DD, NE in the wider ladder.
20. UNEP’s Green Economy (हरित अर्थव्यवस्था) work and GEO sit in the environment-programme lane — not in UNDP’s HDI lane.
21. IPCC reports inform negotiators; parties still adopt NDCs under the UNFCCC/Paris track.
22. Living Planet Index tracks vertebrate abundance trends; it is not a legal trade permit system.
23. SOFO is FAO’s forest flagship — not FSI’s India State of Forest Report.
24. Match org → city → product before matching slogans. Geography of headquarters is a frequent prelims filter.
25. **Headquarters** is in **Geneva, Switzerland**.
26. **Working Group I** covers physical science. **WG II** covers impacts and adaptation. **WG III** covers mitigation.
27. **UNFCCC** (यूएनएफसीसीसी) is the **treaty body** where countries negotiate **NDCs** at **COP** meetings. IPCC informs but does not decide policy.
28. **UNEP** was created by the **Stockholm Conference (1972)** (स्टॉकहोम सम्मेलन) as the UN's principal environmental authority.
29. **Headquarters** is in **Nairobi, Kenya** — the only major UN environment HQ in the Global South.
30. **GEO (Global Environment Outlook)** is UNEP's comprehensive state-of-environment assessment.
31. **UNDP** is the UN's global **development** network working on poverty, governance, and SDG implementation.
32. **Headquarters** is in **New York**.
33. **HDI** combines **life expectancy**, **education**, and **GNI per capita (PPP)**.
34. **IUCN** is a global conservation union of states and NGOs, founded in **1948**.
35. **Headquarters** is in **Gland, Switzerland**.
36. **Threatened species** (संकटग्रस्त प्रजातियां) means **Vulnerable + Endangered + Critically Endangered (VU + EN + CR)**.
37. **WWF (World Wide Fund for Nature)** is a global conservation **NGO**, not a UN agency.
38. **Headquarters** is also in **Gland, Switzerland** — same city as IUCN but **different organisation and role**.
39. **FAO (Food and Agriculture Organization)** is a UN specialised agency for **food and agriculture**.
40. **Headquarters** is in **Rome, Italy**.

## ⏱️ Timeline / Chronology

### Chronological Sequence of Global Climate Summits & Treaties

```
1972 ──► UN Conference on Human Environment (Stockholm) ➔ Creation of UNEP (Nairobi) & World Environment Day (5 June)
1979 ──► First World Climate Conference (Geneva) ➔ Established World Climate Programme
1987 ──► Montreal Protocol on Substances that Deplete the Ozone Layer (Signed 16 Sep; ODS phase-out)
1988 ──► IPCC established by UNEP and WMO (Co-founders: Bert Bolin; Headquarters: Geneva)
1992 ──► UN Conference on Environment & Development (Rio Earth Summit) ➔ Rio Declaration, Agenda 21, UNFCCC, CBD, UNCCD
1997 ──► Kyoto Protocol (COP 3) ➔ Legally binding Annex-I emission reduction targets (Common But Differentiated Responsibilities)
2002 ──► World Summit on Sustainable Development (Johannesburg Earth Summit) ➔ Focus on water, energy, health, agriculture
2012 ──► Rio+20 Conference (UNCSD, Rio de Janeiro) ➔ "The Future We Want", process to launch SDGs
2015 ──► Paris Agreement (COP 21) ➔ Universal pledge-and-review; limit warming to "well below 2°C, pursue 1.5°C"; NDCs
2016 ──► Kigali Amendment to Montreal Protocol ➔ Phase-down of climate-warming Hydrofluorocarbons (HFCs)
2021 ──► Glasgow Climate Pact (COP 26) ➔ "Phase-down" of unabated coal; launch of LiFE (Lifestyle for Environment) by India
2022 ──► Sharm el-Sheikh Implementation Plan (COP 27) ➔ Landmark decision to establish a Loss and Damage Fund
2022 ──► Kunming-Montreal Global Biodiversity Framework (CBD COP 15) ➔ 30×30 conservation target by 2030
2023 ──► COP 28 (Dubai, UAE) ➔ "UAE Consensus", First Global Stocktake, agreement to "transition away from fossil fuels"
```

## 🧠 Memory Anchors & Mnemonics

- **4 Biodiversity Hotspots of India**: **H-I-W-S** (Himalaya, Indo-Burma, Western Ghats–Sri Lanka, Sundaland)
- **6 Kyoto Protocol Greenhouse Gases**: **C-M-N-H-P-S** (Carbon dioxide, Methane, Nitrous oxide, Hydrofluorocarbons, Perfluorocarbons, Sulphur hexafluoride; NF3 added later)
- **Trophic Energy Transfer Rule**: **Lindeman's 10% Law (1942)** — Only ~10% of energy is transferred to the next higher trophic level; 90% lost as metabolic heat.
- **WPA Schedules (Post-2022 Amendment)**:
  - **Schedule I**: Animal species enjoying highest level of protection (Blackbuck, Lion-tailed Macaque, Great Indian Bustard)
  - **Schedule II**: Animal species with lesser protection
  - **Schedule III**: Protected Plant species
  - **Schedule IV**: Specimens listed under CITES Appendices
- **Ozone Units**: Measured in **Dobson Units (DU)** using spectrophotometers (1 DU = 0.01 mm thickness at STP; normal ozone layer is ~300 DU; <220 DU is classified as an ozone hole).
- **Montreux Record**: Ramsar sites where ecological character has changed or is changing (In India: **Keoladeo National Park** and **Loktak Lake**; Chilika Lake was removed post-restoration).
