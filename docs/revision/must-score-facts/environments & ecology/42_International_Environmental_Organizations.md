## 🎯 Consolidated Must-Score Facts

1. **UNEP** was created after **Stockholm 1972** (स्टॉकहोम सम्मेलन). Its HQ is **Nairobi**. Flagship products include the **Emissions Gap Report** (उत्सर्जन अंतराल रिपोर्ट) and **GEO**.
2. **UNDP** sits in **New York** and publishes the **Human Development Report / HDI** (from **1990**).
3. **FAO** sits in **Rome** and publishes **SOFO**. It co-hosts Rotterdam work with UNEP.
4. **UNESCO** sits in **Paris** and runs World Heritage (विश्व धरोहर) and the **MAB** Biosphere Reserve (जैवमंडल आरक्षित क्षेत्र) programme.
5. **IUCN** (आईयूसीएन) sits in **Gland** and publishes the **Red List** of extinction risk.
6. **WWF** also sits in **Gland** and publishes the **Living Planet Report / LPI**.
7. **IPCC** sits in **Geneva**. It was created by **WMO + UNEP** in **1988** and shared the **2007** Nobel Peace Prize.
8. Emissions Gap Report = **UNEP**, not IPCC or UNDP. HDR = **UNDP**, not UNEP.
9. IPCC does science assessment. **UNFCCC** (यूएनएफसीसीसी) is the climate treaty. Do not merge the two.
10. IUCN assesses extinction risk. **CITES** (साइट्स) regulates wildlife **trade** (पण्याध्यक्ष). Different jobs.
11. IUCN and WWF share a city but differ: assessment union versus conservation NGO campaigning and Living Planet metrics.
12. UNEP coordinates environment (पर्यावरण). UNDP coordinates development. HQ and report facts separate them.
13. UNESCO heritage–education–MAB is not the same as UNEP environment coordination.
14. The **GEF (1991)** is a multilateral funder. Donors are not “developed countries only” as a rigid fact.
15. IPCC Assessment Reports synthesize climate science. They do not set national NDCs.
16. FAO’s food–forest–agriculture mandate explains why SOFO and pesticide PIC work sit there.
17. Red List categories are IUCN risk ranks, not CITES Appendix numbers.
18. Living Planet Index tracks vertebrate abundance trends for WWF messaging.
19. Nairobi versus Geneva versus New York versus Rome versus Paris versus Gland is a high-yield HQ ladder.
20. Do not credit IPCC with the Emissions Gap Report.
21. Do not credit UNEP with HDI.
22. Treaty secretariats hosted or supported by UNEP (Montreal, Basel, Stockholm POPs, Minamata) are still treaty bodies, not UNEP synonyms.
23. WWF is not the Red List author. IUCN is.
24. FAO HQ is Rome, not Paris or Geneva.
25. **WMO** headquarters is **Geneva**. It grew from the International Meteorological Organization (**1873**) and became WMO on **17 March 1951**. It co-created the **IPCC** with UNEP in **1988**.
26. **Greenpeace International** is an environmental NGO headquartered in **Amsterdam**, Netherlands.
27. The **US EPA** was established on **2 December 1970**. Do not confuse it with India’s **Environment (Protection) Act, 1986**.
28. **OPCW** implements the **Chemical Weapons Convention** (force **29 April 1997**); HQ is **The Hague**. It won the Nobel Peace Prize in **2013**. It is **not** an EU, NATO, or WHO body.
29. **UN-Habitat** (UN Human Settlements Programme; HQ **Nairobi**, from **1978**) promotes sustainable towns and shelter. Partners are **not** governments-only.
30. **UNEP (United Nations Environment Programme)** is the UN’s principal environment coordination body, created after the **Stockholm Conference (1972)** (स्टॉकहोम सम्मेलन).
31. **HQ: Nairobi, Kenya** — not Geneva or New York.
32. **GEF (Global Environment Facility, 1991)** finances projects under major environment conventions; UNEP is an implementing agency. Donors are **not** “developed countries only.”
33. **UNDP (United Nations Development Programme)** is the UN’s development network agency.
34. **FAO (Food and Agriculture Organization)** is the UN specialised agency for food and agriculture.
35. **UNESCO** leads education, science, and culture — environment enters via heritage and biosphere programmes.
36. **Limits to Growth** is **Club of Rome**, not UNESCO.
37. **IUCN (International Union for Conservation of Nature)** is a global conservation union of governments and NGOs, founded **1948**.
38. **HQ: Gland, Switzerland**.
39. **Threatened** categories commonly = **VU + EN + CR**.
40. **WWF (World Wide Fund for Nature)** is a global conservation **NGO**, not a UN agency.
41. **HQ: Gland, Switzerland** — same city as IUCN, different organisation.
42. **IPCC (Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change (जलवायु परिवर्तन))** was established in **1988** by **WMO** and **UNEP**.
43. **UNFCCC/COP** is the policy–treaty track; IPCC informs it.
44. **WMO headquarters is Geneva, Switzerland.** It co-created the **IPCC** with UNEP in **1988**.
45. **OPCW (Organisation for the Prohibition (प्रतिषेध) of Chemical Weapons)** implements the **Chemical Weapons Convention** (force **29 April 1997**); HQ **The Hague**, Netherlands. It verifies destruction/non-re-emergence of chemical weapons and assists states against chemical threats — **not** an EU/NATO/WHO body. Nobel Peace Prize **2013**.

## ⏱️ Timeline / Chronology

### Chronological Sequence of Global Climate Summits & Treaties

```
1972 ──► UN Conference on Human Environment (Stockholm) ➔ Creation of UNEP (Nairobi) & World Environment Day (5 June)
1979 ──► First World Climate Conference (Geneva) ➔ Established World Climate Programme
1987 ──► Montreal Protocol on Substances that Deplete the Ozone Layer (Signed 16 Sep; ODS phase-out)
1988 ──► IPCC established by UNEP and WMO (Co-founders: Bert Bolin; Headquarters: Geneva)
1992 ──► UN Conference on Environment & Development (Rio Earth Summit) ➔ Rio Declaration, Agenda 21, UNFCCC, CBD, UNCCD
1997 ──► Kyoto Protocol (COP 3) ➔ Legally binding Annex-I emission reduction targets (Common But Differentiated Responsibilities)
2002 ──► World Summit on Sustainable Development (Johannesburg Earth Summit) ➔ Focus on water, energy, health, agriculture
2012 ──► Rio+20 Conference (UNCSD, Rio de Janeiro) ➔ "The Future We Want", process to launch SDGs
2015 ──► Paris Agreement (COP 21) ➔ Universal pledge-and-review; limit warming to "well below 2°C, pursue 1.5°C"; NDCs
2016 ──► Kigali Amendment to Montreal Protocol ➔ Phase-down of climate-warming Hydrofluorocarbons (HFCs)
2021 ──► Glasgow Climate Pact (COP 26) ➔ "Phase-down" of unabated coal; launch of LiFE (Lifestyle for Environment) by India
2022 ──► Sharm el-Sheikh Implementation Plan (COP 27) ➔ Landmark decision to establish a Loss and Damage Fund
2022 ──► Kunming-Montreal Global Biodiversity Framework (CBD COP 15) ➔ 30×30 conservation target by 2030
2023 ──► COP 28 (Dubai, UAE) ➔ "UAE Consensus", First Global Stocktake, agreement to "transition away from fossil fuels"
```

## 🧠 Memory Anchors & Mnemonics

- **4 Biodiversity Hotspots of India**: **H-I-W-S** (Himalaya, Indo-Burma, Western Ghats–Sri Lanka, Sundaland)
- **6 Kyoto Protocol Greenhouse Gases**: **C-M-N-H-P-S** (Carbon dioxide, Methane, Nitrous oxide, Hydrofluorocarbons, Perfluorocarbons, Sulphur hexafluoride; NF3 added later)
- **Trophic Energy Transfer Rule**: **Lindeman's 10% Law (1942)** — Only ~10% of energy is transferred to the next higher trophic level; 90% lost as metabolic heat.
- **WPA Schedules (Post-2022 Amendment)**:
  - **Schedule I**: Animal species enjoying highest level of protection (Blackbuck, Lion-tailed Macaque, Great Indian Bustard)
  - **Schedule II**: Animal species with lesser protection
  - **Schedule III**: Protected Plant species
  - **Schedule IV**: Specimens listed under CITES Appendices
- **Ozone Units**: Measured in **Dobson Units (DU)** using spectrophotometers (1 DU = 0.01 mm thickness at STP; normal ozone layer is ~300 DU; <220 DU is classified as an ozone hole).
- **Montreux Record**: Ramsar sites where ecological character has changed or is changing (In India: **Keoladeo National Park** and **Loktak Lake**; Chilika Lake was removed post-restoration).
