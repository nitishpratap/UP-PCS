## 🎯 Consolidated Must-Score Facts

1. India’s Census 2011 population was **1,210,854,977 (121.08 crore)**. Males **51.54%**, females **48.46%**. India holds about **17.5%** of world population on **2.4%** of world land area.
2. Decadal growth **2001–2011** was about **17.7%** (Rural **12.3%**, Urban **31.8%**). Highest State growth: **Meghalaya (27.9%)**. Negative State growth: **Nagaland (−0.6%)**.
3. Population density India **382**/km². Top States: **Bihar (1,106)** > West Bengal (1,028) > Kerala (860) > Uttar Pradesh (**829**). Lowest State: **Arunachal Pradesh (17)**.
4. Highest density UT: **Delhi (11,320)**; lowest UT: **Andaman & Nicobar (46)**.
5. Overall sex ratio **943** (+10 from 933 in 2001). Top State: **Kerala (1,084)**. Lowest State: **Haryana (879)**. Highest UT: Puducherry (1,037); lowest UT: Daman & Diu (618).
6. Child sex ratio (0–6) **919** (fell from 927 in 2001). Highest State: **Arunachal Pradesh (972)**. Lowest State: **Haryana (834)**.
7. Literacy **74.04%** (male **82.14%**, female **65.46%**; gap **16.68%**), counted for age **7+**. Top State: **Kerala (94.0%)**. Lowest State: **Bihar (61.8%)**. Lowest female literacy State: **Rajasthan (52.1%)**.
8. SC population **20.14 crore (16.6%)**. Highest absolute SC: **Uttar Pradesh**. Highest SC proportion: **Punjab (31.9%)**. Zero SC: **Nagaland, Lakshadweep, Andaman & Nicobar**.
9. ST population **10.43 crore (8.6%)**. Highest absolute ST: **Madhya Pradesh**. Highest ST proportion: **Lakshadweep (94.8%)** / **Mizoram (94.4%)**. Zero ST: **Punjab, Haryana, Chandigarh, Delhi, Puducherry**.
10. Urban share **31.16%**; rural **68.84%**. Highest urban % State: **Goa (62.2%)**. Lowest urban % State: **Himachal Pradesh (10.0%)**. Highest absolute urban population: **Maharashtra**.
11. Million-plus UAs: **53** in 2011 (35 in 2001). Top three: Greater Mumbai > Delhi UA > Kolkata UA. States with most million-plus cities: **Uttar Pradesh (7)** and **Kerala (7)**.
12. Religious shares: Hindus **79.80%**; Muslims **14.23%**; Christians **2.30%**; Sikhs **1.72%**; Buddhists **0.70%**; Jains **0.37%**.
13. Highest Muslim % pockets include Lakshadweep, J&K, Assam, West Bengal, Kerala. Highest Christian %: Nagaland, Mizoram, Meghalaya. Highest Jain % State tag: Maharashtra.
14. Most populous State: **Uttar Pradesh (19.98 crore)**. Least populous State: **Sikkim (6.10 lakh)**. Most populous UT: **Delhi**; least: **Lakshadweep**.
15. Uttar Pradesh = **16.51%** of India (~**19.98 crore**). If treated as a country in 2011 framing, it ranks among the world’s largest populations.
16. UP decadal growth **20.23%**. Highest growth district: **Gautam Buddha Nagar (49.1%)**. Lowest growth district: **Kanpur Nagar (9.9%)**.
17. UP density **829**/km². Highest density district: **Ghaziabad (3,971)**. Lowest density district: **Lalitpur (242)**.
18. UP sex ratio **912**. Highest: **Jaunpur (1,024)**. Lowest: **Gautam Buddha Nagar (851)**.
19. UP child sex ratio **902**. Highest: **Balrampur (950)**. Lowest: **Baghpat (841)**.
20. UP literacy **67.72%** (male **77.28%**, female **57.18%**). Highest overall literacy district: **Gautam Buddha Nagar**. Lowest overall / female literacy district: **Shrawasti**.
21. UP SC share about **20.69%**. Highest SC % district tag: **Kaushambi**. Highest absolute SC district tag: **Sitapur**.
22. UP ST share only about **0.57%**. Highest ST district: **Sonbhadra**. Zero ST reported: **Ayodhya (Faizabad)** and **Jalaun** in 2011.
23. UP urbanisation **22.27%**. Highest urban %: **Ghaziabad**. Lowest urban %: **Shrawasti**.
24. Uttarakhand population about **1.01 crore (0.83% of India)**. Decadal growth **18.81%**. Negative growth districts: **Pauri Garhwal** and **Almora**.
25. Uttarakhand density **189**/km² (Haridwar highest; Uttarkashi lowest). Sex ratio **963**. Highest sex ratio district: **Almora**; lowest: **Haridwar**.
26. Uttarakhand child sex ratio **890** (Pithoragarh lowest). Literacy **78.82%** (Dehradun highest; Udham Singh Nagar / Haridwar among lower).
27. First non-synchronous Census: **1872** (Lord Mayo). First synchronous decennial Census: **1881** (Lord Ripon; W.C. Plowden).
28. **1921** is the **Year of the Great Divide** (negative growth about **−0.31%**).
29. Census is governed by the **Census Act, 1948**. It is Entry **69** of the **Union List**. ORGI works under the **Ministry of Home Affairs**.
30. Census 2011 was the **15th** national census and the **7th** since Independence. Motto: *Our Census, Our Future*. Commissioner: **C. Chandramouli**. Mascot: female enumerator.
31. Keep **Census 2011** figures until **Census 2027** results replace them. UN “most populous” estimates around **2023** do not rewrite the 2011 table.
32. Absolute SC leader (**UP**) ≠ highest SC percentage (**Punjab**). Absolute ST leader (**MP**) ≠ highest ST percentage (**Lakshadweep/Mizoram**).
33. Overall sex ratio improved 2001→2011 while child sex ratio worsened — opposite directions are a standard trap.
34. Highest population State (**UP**) ≠ highest density State (**Bihar**). Lowest population State (**Sikkim**) ≠ lowest density State (**Arunachal**).
35. UP most populous district teaching tag is **Prayagraj**; densest is **Ghaziabad** — do not swap those two district crowns.
36. Literacy age cut is **7+**; do not key Census literacy as 0–6 or 18+.
37. Million-city top order Mumbai > Delhi > Kolkata is the **2011** order; Mumbai > Kolkata > Delhi is the **2001** trap.
38. Dadra & Nagar Haveli had the highest UT decadal growth in the 2011 set (**55.9%**).
39. Among large States, West Bengal and Kerala follow Bihar in density; Delhi’s extreme density is a UT fact, not a “State density” answer.
40. Jains are the most urbanised religion in the usual national composition teaching set paired with Census chapters.
41. Natural growth uses CBR−CDR; Census counts also embed migration when comparing places — district growth can be migration-led (e.g. GB Nagar).
42. Uttarakhand “ghost village” negative growth in Pauri and Almora is a hill out-migration teaching tag.
43. Zero-SC and zero-ST State/UT lists are mutually different — Nagaland has ST but zero SC; Punjab has SC but zero ST.
44. Female literacy gap nationally (~16.7 points) is wider in UP (~20.1 points) — useful for UP vs India comparison stems.
45. Census Act piloting association with Sardar Patel in Constituent Assembly notes sits beside the Union List Entry 69 fact.


---
46. **Uttarakhand Population Density & Sex Ratio 2011**: Uttarakhand recorded a population density of **189 persons/km²** (Haridwar highest at 801; Uttarkashi lowest at 41); overall sex ratio was **963** (higher than national average 943; Almora highest at 1,139; Haridwar lowest at 880).
47. **Uttarakhand Literacy 2011**: Overall literacy in Uttarakhand stood at **78.82%** (Male 87.40%, Female 70.01%); **Dehradun** recorded the highest literacy (84.25%); **Udham Singh Nagar** recorded the lowest (73.10%).
48. **Uttar Pradesh Scheduled Caste Count**: Scheduled Castes constitute **20.70% (41,357,608)** of Uttar Pradesh's total population; UP holds the largest absolute SC population in India (~20.5% of all Indian SCs); **Sitapur** has the highest SC population; **Kaushambi** has the highest SC percentage (34.7%); **Baghpat** has the lowest SC population and percentage (11.4%).
49. **Uttar Pradesh Scheduled Tribe Count**: Scheduled Tribes constitute only **0.57% (1,134,273)** of Uttar Pradesh's population; **Sonbhadra** has both the highest ST population (385,013) and highest percentage (20.67%); **Baghpat and Kannauj** have the lowest ST numbers in UP.
50. **Zero Scheduled Castes / Tribes States**: There are **NO Scheduled Castes** in Nagaland, Mizoram, Lakshadweep, and Andaman & Nicobar; there are **NO Scheduled Tribes** in Punjab, Haryana, Delhi, Chandigarh, and Puducherry.

  │           Conducted non-synchronously in different provinces of British India under Viceroy Lord Mayo.
  │
  ▼
  [1881] ───> FIRST COMPLETE SYNCHRONOUS CENSUS:
  │           Conducted simultaneously across the entire Indian subcontinent under Lord Ripon (W. C. Plowden).
  │
  ▼
  [1921] ───> "YEAR OF THE GREAT DIVIDE" (महाविभाजक वर्ष):
  │           Only census in Indian history showing negative decadal growth (-0.31%);
  │           massive mortality from 1918 influenza pandemic and widespread famine.
  │
  ▼
  [1931] ───> LAST CASTE CENSUS OF BRITISH INDIA:
  │           Last census enumerating all castes; later used by the Mandal Commission (1980).
  │
  ▼
  [1948] ───> CENSUS ACT, 1948 ENACTED:
  │           Permanent statutory framework; Office of Registrar General & Census Commissioner of India (MHA).
  │
  ▼
  [1951] ───> FIRST CENSUS OF INDEPENDENT INDIA (8th National Census):
  │           Conducted under R. A. Gopalaswami; population recorded at 36.1 crore.
  │
  ▼
  [2011] ───> 15TH NATIONAL CENSUS (7th post-independence):
  │           Dr. C. Chandramouli, Census Commissioner; motto: "Our Census, Our Future"; 121.08 crore.
