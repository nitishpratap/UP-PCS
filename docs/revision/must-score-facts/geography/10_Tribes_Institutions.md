## 🎯 Consolidated Must-Score Facts

1. **Kuki** is a North-East tribe and is **not** a Uttar Pradesh Scheduled Tribe. **Keria / Kharia** fact to Odisha (also Jharkhand for Kharia). **Bodo = Assam**; **Lepcha = Sikkim**; **Meena = Rajasthan**; **Warli (वारली) = Maharashtra**; **Apatani = Arunachal**.
2. **Khasi and Garo (गारो)** of Meghalaya are classic **matrilineal** societies.
3. **Janjatiya Gaurav Diwas** is observed on **15 November** (Birsa Munda’s birth anniversary).
4. **Khirghiz** are Central Asian, not Sudanese. **Koryak** live in north-east Siberia, not Alaska.
5. **IMD** headquarters is New Delhi (**1875**). **ICAR** headquarters is New Delhi (**1929**). **IARI** is at **Pusa, New Delhi** — it is not the apex ICAR council itself.
6. **FRI** and **ICFRE** are at **Dehradun** (देहरादून). **WII** is also Dehradun but is not the FRI answer. **NDRI** is at **Karnal** (dairy lab). **IVRI** is at **Izatnagar, Bareilly (UP)**. **NDDB** is at **Anand** and ran **Operation Flood** (ऑपरेशन फ्लड) — it is not NDRI.
7. **IIHR** is at Bengaluru. **CISH** is at Lucknow (लखनऊ). **CSAUAT** is at Kanpur (कानपुर). **Pantnagar** is remembered as the first State Agricultural University tradition.
8. **Archaeopteryx** is dated at about **150 million years ago**.
9. **Khasi = Arunachal** — FALSE. **Meghalaya**.
10. **Rengma = Andaman native** — FALSE. **Nagaland**.
11. **Kuki = Uttar Pradesh** — FALSE. **North-East**.
12. **Yanadi = Rajasthan** — FALSE. **Andhra Pradesh**. Paliyan = **Tamil Nadu**. Keria = **Odisha**.
13. **Masai = West Africa** — FALSE. **East Africa**.
14. **Khirghiz = Sudan** — FALSE. **Central Asia**.
15. **Alaska = Koryak** — FALSE. Koryak = **north-east Siberia**.
16. **Tharu = Sonbhadra only** — FALSE. **Terai**.
17. **UP PVTG = Tharu + Gond** — FALSE. **Buksa + Raji**.
18. **Agariya = only farmers** — FALSE. Traditional **iron smelting**.
19. **Birjia / Santhal = island tribes** — FALSE. **Jharkhand / mainland**.
20. **NDRI = Izatnagar / IVRI = Karnal** — SWAP. NDRI **Karnal**; IVRI **Bareilly**.
21. **FRI = Delhi or Lucknow** — FALSE. **Dehradun**.
22. **Toda = Nagaland** — FALSE. **Tamil Nadu** (Nilgiris).
23. **IMD / ICAR HQ = a regional campus** — FALSE. Both **New Delhi**. NDRI ≠ NDDB; FRI ≠ WII.
24. **PVTG count = 95** — FALSE. There are **75** groups. Odisha has the most communities.
25. **Fifth Schedule = only four NE states** — FALSE. Those four are the **Sixth Schedule** set.
26. **PTG rename year = 1973** — FALSE. Dhebar identified PTGs in 1973. The rename to PVTG is **2006**.
27. **One all-India ST roster** — FALSE. Lists are **state/UT-wise** under Article 342.
28. **Any forest dweller = PVTG** — FALSE. PVTG is a tighter vulnerability subset with set criteria.
