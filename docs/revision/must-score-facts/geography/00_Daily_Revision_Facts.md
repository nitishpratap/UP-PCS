## 🎯 Consolidated Must-Score Facts

1. **Thalghat (Kasara Ghat)**: Connects **Mumbai to Nashik / Nagpur / North India** (National Highway 160 & railway line).
2. **Bhorghat**: Connects **Mumbai to Pune / Bengaluru / South India** (Mumbai-Pune Expressway).
3. **Palghat (Palakkad Gap)**: Located between **Nilgiri Hills (North) and Anaimalai Hills (South)**; connects **Coimbatore (Tamil Nadu) to Palakkad / Kochi (Kerala)**.
4. **Shenkottai Pass**: Located between **Cardamom Hills and Agasthyamalai Hills**; connects **Kollam (Kerala) to Madurai / Tenkasi (Tamil Nadu)**.
5. **Vishnuprayag**: **Alaknanda** + **Dhauliganga** (Chamoli)
6. **Nandaprayag**: **Alaknanda** + **Nandakini** (Chamoli)
7. **Karnaprayag**: **Alaknanda** + **Pindar** (Chamoli)
8. **Rudraprayag**: **Alaknanda** + **Mandakini** (Rudraprayag)
9. **Devprayag**: **Alaknanda** + **Bhagirathi** → Official **GANGA** begins here! (Tehri Garhwal)
10. **Left Bank Tributaries (West to East)**:
11. **Ramganga** (originates in Dudhatoli range, Pauri Garhwal; flows through Jim Corbett NP; meets Ganga at Kannauj)
12. **Gomati** (originates in plains at **Gomat Taal / Fulhar Lake, Pilibhit, UP** — *only Himalayan/Ganga tributary originating in the plains!*; Lucknow & Jaunpur on its banks; joins Ganga near Saidpur, Ghazipur)
13. **Ghaghra / Karnali** (originates from Mapchachungo glacier near Mansarovar; joined by Sarda/Kali; **largest tributary by water volume**; joins Ganga at Chhapra, Bihar)
14. **Gandak** (originates in Nepal Himalayas; joins Ganga at Sonpur / Hajipur, Bihar)
15. **Kosi** (*Sorrow of Bihar*; 7 streams / Saptakoshi; notorious for course-shifting; joins at Kursela, Katihar)
16. **Mahananda** (originates in Darjeeling hills; **last left-bank tributary of Ganga in India**).
17. **Right Bank Tributaries**:
18. **Yamuna** (longest tributary, 1376 km; Yamunotri glacier, Bandarpunch peak; joins at Prayagraj Triveni Sangam; its tributaries: Chambal, Sind, Betwa, Ken, Tons, Hindon)
19. **Son** (originates from **Amarkantak Plateau, MP**; flows north-eastwards through Sonbhadra UP; joins Ganga near Danapur/Patna, Bihar)
20. **Punpun** (originates in Palamu, Jharkhand; joins Ganga at Fatuha near Patna).
21. **West-Flowing (into Arabian Sea — form Estuaries, NO deltas)**:
22. **Narmada** (1312 km): Amarkantak plateau (MP); flows through **Rift Valley** between Vindhyas (North) and Satpuras (South); forms **Dhuandhar Falls** at Marble Rocks (Bhedaghat, Jabalpur); Sardar Sarovar Dam.
23. **Tapi / Tapti** (724 km): Multai (Betul district, MP); flows in rift valley south of Satpuras; Ukai Dam & Kakrapar Project (Gujarat).
24. **Sabarmati**: Dhebar Lake (Aravalli Range, Udaipur); flows through Ahmedabad and Gandhinagar into Gulf of Khambhat.
25. **Mahi**: Vindhyan hills (Dhar, MP); **CUTS THE TROPIC OF CANCER TWICE!**
26. **Luni**: Originates near Ajmer (Aravallis) as Sagarmati; **ends in the marshy Rann of Kutch (Inland drainage, never reaches the sea)**; sweet water up to Balotra, then saline.
27. **Sharavati**: Shimoga (Karnataka); forms famous **Jog Falls (Gersoppa / Mahatma Gandhi Falls)**.
28. **Periyar & Bharathappuzha (Ponnani)**: Kerala.
29. **East-Flowing (into Bay of Bengal — form large Deltas)**:
30. **Mahanadi** (851 km): Sihawa hills (Dhamtari, Chhattisgarh); flows through Odisha; Hirakud Dam.
31. **Godavari** (1465 km — **Dakshin Ganga / Vriddha Ganga**): Trimbakeshwar (Nashik, Maharashtra); **longest river in Peninsular India**; Tributaries: Pravara, Manjra, Penganga, Wainganga, Wardha, **Pranhita** (largest), Indravati, Sabari.
32. **Krishna** (1401 km): Mahabaleshwar (Maharashtra); Tributaries: Koyna, Ghataprabha, Malaprabha, Bhima, **Tungabhadra** (largest), Musi (Hyderabad on its banks), Munneru.
33. **Kaveri** (800 km — **Ganga of the South**): Talakaveri (Brahmagiri Range, Coorg, Karnataka); flows year-round (perennial due to SW & NE monsoons); creates river islands: **Srirangapatna, Shivanasamudra, and Srirangam**; Tributaries: Hemavati, Harangi, Shimsha, Arkavathi (left); Lakshmantirtha, Kabini, Bhavani, Noyyal, Amaravati (right).
34. **Deendayal Port (Kandla)** (Gulf of Kutch, Gujarat): Tidal port; free trade zone (SEZ); handles crude oil & grains.
35. **Mumbai Port** (Maharashtra): Largest natural port and harbour in India.
