## 🎯 Consolidated Must-Score Facts

1. About **77%** of India’s drainage **area** faces the **Bay of Bengal** and about **23%** faces the **Arabian Sea**; inland basins cover ~**8%** of area. Over **90% of river water** still goes to the Bay of Bengal.
2. Water-share ladder: **Brahmaputra ~40%**, **Ganga ~25%**, **Godavari ~6%**. Brahmaputra leads volume; **Ganga** leads basin area **inside India**.
3. Western Ghats are the main peninsular divide. From **Amarkantak**, drainage is radial — **Narmada** west and **Son** toward the Ganga.
4. **Ganga** = largest basin in India; **Godavari** = largest **peninsular** basin. Among Tapi / Narmada / Mahanadi / Cauvery alone, **Mahanadi** is the largest basin.
5. Himalayan rivers are mostly **perennial** (melt + rain) and often **antecedent**. Peninsular rivers are mostly **rain-fed**, older and graded.
6. Drainage types: **consequent** follows original slope; **subsequent** follows weak belts later; **antecedent** (Indus, Sutlej, Brahmaputra) cuts rising Himalaya; **superimposed** (Chambal) inherits an older course.
7. **Trans-Himalayan** rivers rise north of the Great Himalaya in Tibet and cut through — Indus, **Sutlej** and Brahmaputra (Tsangpo). Among Jhelum / Sutlej / Ganga / Ravi, the trans-Himalayan pick is **Sutlej**.
8. Indus reaches the Arabian Sea in Pakistan. Punjab rivers E→W: **Sutlej–Beas–Ravi–Chenab–Jhelum**. Indus Treaty: India gets Ravi, Beas, Sutlej; Pakistan gets Indus, Jhelum, Chenab.
9. Dam–river pairs: **Baglihar = Chenab**, **Pandoh = Beas** (≠ Ravi), **Srisailam = Krishna** (≠ Tungabhadra), **Tulbul = Jhelum / Wular**.
10. The river is named **Ganga** only at **Devprayag** (Alaknanda + Bhagirathi). Length ~**2525 km**. In Bangladesh it is the **Padma**; Brahmaputra is the **Jamuna**; both join the Meghna system to the Bay.
11. Ganga joins: Yamuna at **Prayagraj**, Ramganga at **Kannauj**, Ghaghara at **Chhapra**, Gandak at **Sonpur**, Son at **Patna**, Kosi at **Kursela**.
12. Left-bank Himalayan Ganga tributes include Ramganga, Ghaghara, Gandak, Kosi and Mahananda; **Gomti** is a **plains-origin** left-bank river. Right-bank facts: Yamuna, Son, Damodar.
13. Yamuna right-bank tributes W→E: **Chambal–Sind–Betwa–Ken**. Chambal joins near Etawah; Betwa at **Hamirpur**; Ken at **Banda**. Betwa does **not** meet at Prayagraj.
14. **Jonk** joins the **Mahanadi**, not the Ganga. **Gandak** does **not** flow through Uttar Pradesh in the usual state list. **Punpun** meets near Fatuha. Chambal ravines start near **Mhow**.
15. Panch Prayag upstream → downstream: **Vishnu** (Dhauli) → **Nanda** (Nandakini) → **Karn** (Pindar) → **Rudra** (Mandakini) → **Dev** (Bhagirathi) = Ganga. Badrinath is on the **Alaknanda**; Kedarnath is on the **Mandakini**.
16. The eastern **Dhauliganga** joins the **Kali**, not the Alaknanda — do not confuse it with Vishnuprayag’s Dhauli.
17. Brahmaputra rises as Angsi / Tsangpo, makes a **U-turn near Namcha Barwa**, and enters India as Siang / Dihang. **Majuli** is the classic river island. North/right tributes include Subansiri, Manas, Sankosh, Teesta; south/left include Burhi Dihing and Dhansiri.
18. **Teesta** was captured from the Ganga system into the Brahmaputra. **Barak** becomes Meghna and reaches the Bay — it is **not** inland drainage.
19. Peninsular length ladder: **Godavari (~1465 km) > Krishna (~1400) > Narmada (~1312) > Mahanadi (~851) > Tapi (~724)**. Godavari (Dakshin / Vriddha Ganga) rises near **Trimbak**.
20. Origins: Yamuna–Yamunotri; Krishna–Mahabaleshwar; Godavari–Nashik / Trimbak; Mahanadi–Sihawa; Narmada–Amarkantak; Tapi–**Multai (Satpura)** not Western Ghats; Kaveri–Brahmagiri.
21. **Pranhita** → Godavari; **Hemavati** → Kaveri; **Malaprabha** → Krishna. Hyderabad stands on the **Musi**, not the Krishna stem. Ludhiana is on the **Sutlej**, not the Ravi.
22. West-flowing set: Narmada, Tapi, Mahi, Sabarmati, **Periyar**. Narmada and Tapi form **estuaries** in rift valleys; most east-flowing peninsular rivers build **deltas**.
23. **Luni** is inland drainage: upper course fresh, lower course **saline**. Classic fault-valley tag = **Damodar**; Narmada and Tapi are also rift valleys.
24. Delta types: **arcuate** = Sundarbans; **bird’s-foot** = Mississippi (not Ganga). **Trellis** = folded structure (e.g. Singhbhum); **radial** = Amarkantak; **dendritic** = homogeneous slope (Ganga / Godavari / Krishna / Kaveri class).
25. National Waterways: **NW-1** Ganga–Hooghly; **NW-2** Brahmaputra; **NW-3** Kerala; **NW-4** Krishna–Godavari; **NW-5** Brahmani–Mahanadi. National Waterways Act (2016) lists **106** NWs.
26. **Ken–Betwa** is the **only** National Perspective Plan link under implementation (Bundelkhand MP–UP; Panna TR forest link).
27. UP anchors: Sangam at **Prayagraj**; **Gomti** = Lucknow’s river; **Ghaghara** = Ayodhya’s river; core plain = Ganga–Yamuna **Doab**.
28. National Chambal Sanctuary spans **UP + MP + Rajasthan** — not Haryana.
29. Kaveri island sequence: **Srirangapatna → Shivasamudram → Srirangam**. Bhubaneswar is **not** a Mahanadi-bank city in the usual trap set.
30. East-flowing peninsular rivers N→S often run **Subarnarekha → Mahanadi → Godavari → Krishna → Pennar → Cauvery → Vaigai**.
31. Indus is longest among the four Himalayan systems overall, but most of its course lies in Pakistan. **Ganga** is the longest river **in India**.
32. Concordant drainage follows structure; **discordant** drainage cuts across it — Himalayan antecedents are the classic discordant case.
33. **Kosi** = Sorrow of Bihar; **Damodar** = Sorrow of Bengal. Do not swap the titles.
34. Indian **trellis** ground = folded **Singhbhum** (Chotanagpur). **Rectangular** streams follow joints on **Vindhyan** rocks. **Sharavati** shows a **parallel** Western Ghats pattern.
35. **Centripetal** streams drain into **Loktak** and into **Sambhar** (Mendha, Rupangarh).
36. Gangetic alluvium depth teaching: Oldham ~**4000–6000 m**; Glennie ~**2000 m**.
37. **Son** rises from the Amarkantak belt and has the **largest catchment among major Ganga tributes** in the usual comparison set; it joins the Ganga near Patna.
38. The **Kaimur Range** divides the Son system (south) from the Tons / Yamuna system (north).
39. In Nepal, Gandak = **Gandaki / Narayani**. **Triveni Canal** draws Gandak water in West Champaran (Bihar). Gandak joins Ganga at Sonpur and does **not** traverse UP.
40. In Bihar the Ganga passes through **12 districts**; longest district stretches are often tagged **Patna** then **Bhagalpur**. Punpun joins at **Fatuha**.
41. Kosi is formed by seven Himalayan streams (**Arun** principal) and is **not** a simple glacier-origin stem. **Kanwar Lake** (Begusarai) is an oxbow of the **Burhi Gandak**, not the Gandak.
42. **Nagavali** and **Vamsadhara** rise from the Eastern Ghats; **Brahmani** and **Subarnarekha** rise from the Chotanagpur / Ranchi plateau.
43. **Chandra** and **Bhaga** unite at **Tandi** (Lahaul) to form the **Chenab (Chandrabhaga)**. Punjab doabs: **Bist** (Beas–Sutlej), **Bari** (Beas–Ravi), **Rechna** (Ravi–Chenab), **Chaj** (Chenab–Jhelum).
44. **Gandikota canyon** (Kadapa, AP) was carved by the **Pennar**. In S.P. Chatterjee’s division, the Mahanadi Basin sits under the **Northern Deccan Plateau**.
45. City–river anchors: **Kanpur** largest city on the Ganga; **Uttarkashi** on Bhagirathi; **Leh** on the **right bank** of the Indus; **Pahalgam** on the **Lidder**; **Hampi** on the **Tungabhadra**; **Pandharpur** on the Bhima / Chandrabhaga; **Govindghat** on Alaknanda / Lakshman Ganga (≠ Mandakini).
46. Rectangular / joint-controlled streams on Vindhyan rocks differ from dendritic plains patterns — pattern stems often park both traps together.
47. **Periyar** is a major west-flowing Kerala river; do not list it with east-coast delta builders like Godavari / Krishna.
48. River rejuvenation / knickpoints matter for waterfall chapters, but drainage stems still ask rejuvenated Himalayan / peninsular course evidence via gorges and terraces.
49. Inland drainage classic = **Luni** (Thar) plus centripetal playa catchments such as **Sambhar** — not Barak / Meghna.
50. Namami Gange / GAP are **cleaning programmes**, not river-origin facts; do not use them to answer confluence or source stems.
51. **Himalayan vs Peninsular River Systems (Key Distinctions):** Himalayan rivers are antecedent, perennial (snow and rain-fed), youth-stage with deep V-shaped valleys and gorges, long courses with extensive meanders and braided channels, and form massive deltas; Peninsular rivers are superimposed/consequent, seasonal/rain-fed, mature-stage with graded shallow U-shaped valleys, fixed courses with little meander, and flow along rift valleys (forming estuaries) or into shallow deltas.
52. **Panch Prayag Confluences of Alaknanda (North to South Order):** (1) **Vishnuprayag:** Alaknanda meets **Dhauliganga**; (2) **Nandaprayag:** Alaknanda meets **Nandakini**; (3) **Karnaprayag:** Alaknanda meets **Pindar**; (4) **Rudraprayag:** Alaknanda meets **Mandakini (Kali Ganga)**; (5) **Devprayag:** Alaknanda meets **Bhagirathi** to formally form the **Ganga**.
53. **Sources of Major Himalayan Rivers:** Ganga: Gangotri Glacier (Gaumukh) as Bhagirathi; Yamuna: Yamunotri Glacier (Bandarpunch range); Indus: Bokhar Chu glacier near Lake Mansarovar (Tibet); Brahmaputra: Chemayungdung / Angsi Glacier (Tibet); Sutlej: Rakas Lake (Raksas Tal) near Mansarovar; Kosi: Mount Everest region (confluence of Sun Kosi, Arun, and Tamur); Gandak: Dhaulagiri and Annapurna; Ghaghara: Mapchachungo Glacier near Mansarovar.
54. **Indus River & Key Tributaries:** Enters India in Ladakh, flows between Ladakh and Zanskar ranges; joined by Shyok, Gilgit, Zanskar, Hunza, and Kabul; left-bank tributaries (Panjnad): (1) **Jhelum:** Originates at Verinag spring (Kashmir), flows through Wular Lake, forms boundary between India and Pakistan; (2) **Chenab (Asikni):** Formed by confluence of Chandra and Bhaga at Tandi (Himachal Pradesh); largest tributary of Indus; (3) **Ravi (Parushni):** Originates near Rohtang Pass, flows between Pir Panjal and Dhauladhar; (4) **Beas (Vipasha):** Originates near Beas Kund (Rohtang Pass), meets Sutlej at **Harike (Punjab)**; entirely within India; (5) **Sutlej (Shutudri):** Enters India through Shipki La, feeds Bhakra Dam.
55. **Indus Waters Treaty (1960):** Brokered by the World Bank; signed by Jawaharlal Nehru and Ayub Khan; allocated three **Eastern Rivers (Ravi, Beas, Sutlej)** exclusively to India; allocated three **Western Rivers (Indus, Jhelum, Chenab)** to Pakistan, with India permitted specified run-of-the-river hydroelectric generation and non-consumptive agricultural use.
56. **Ganga Left-Bank Tributaries (West to East Sequence):** **Ramganga** (originates in Garhwal, flows through Jim Corbett NP, meets Ganga near Kannauj) -> **Gomti** (originates from Fulhar / Gomat Taal in Pilibhit, UP; Lucknow and Jaunpur on banks; meets Ganga near Saidpur, Ghazipur) -> **Ghaghara / Saryu** (largest tributary by water volume, meets Ganga at Chhapra, Bihar) -> **Gandak** (meets Ganga at Sonpur / Hajipur, Bihar) -> **Burhi Gandak** -> **Kosi** ('Sorrow of Bihar' due to frequent course shifts, meets Ganga at Kursela, Katihar) -> **Mahananda** (easternmost left-bank tributary, originates in Darjeeling hills).
57. **Ganga Right-Bank Tributaries:** (1) **Yamuna:** Longest right-bank tributary (1,376 km); joins Ganga at Prayagraj (Triveni Sangam); right-bank tributaries of Yamuna: **Chambal** (originates in Janapao Hills, Singar Chouri peak near Mhow, MP; famous for badland topography/ravines), **Sindh**, **Betwa** (originates in Vindhyan range near Raisen, MP; meets Yamuna near Hamirpur), **Ken** (originates in Kaimur range, meets Yamuna at Chilla, Banda); (2) **Tons (Tamas):** Originates in Kaimur Hills, joins Ganga at Sirsa, Prayagraj; (3) **Son:** Originates at Amarkantak plateau, flows north through Kaimur escarpment, joins Ganga near Patna; right-bank tributary is Rihand (Govind Ballabh Pant Sagar).
58. **Brahmaputra Course & Key Features:** Known as **Tsangpo** ('Purifier') in Tibet; cuts deep gorge at **Namcha Barwa** and enters Arunachal Pradesh as **Siang / Dihang**; joined by Dibang and Lohit at Sadiya (Assam) to become **Brahmaputra**; forms world's largest inhabited riverine island, **Majuli**; right-bank tributaries: Subansiri, Kameng (Jia Bhoreli), Manas, Sankosh, Teesta; left-bank tributaries: Burhi Dihing, Dhansiri, Kalang; enters Bangladesh as **Jamuna**, joins Padma (Ganga), and finally empties as **Meghna** into Bay of Bengal.
59. **Teesta River Dynamics:** Originates from Tso Lhamo Lake / Pahunri Glacier in Sikkim; previously a tributary of Ganga, shifted eastward in 1787 megaflood to join Brahmaputra (Jamuna) in Bangladesh; river dispute subject between India and Bangladesh.
60. **Godavari Basin ('Dakshin Ganga' / 'Vridha Ganga'):** Longest peninsular river (1,465 km); originates at **Trimbakeshwar (Nashik, Maharashtra)**; left-bank tributaries: **Penganga, Wardha, Wainganga** (confluence forms **Pranhita**), **Indravati** (Chitrakote Falls, Bastar), and **Sabari**; right-bank tributaries: **Manjra / Manjira** (Nizam Sagar); empties into Bay of Bengal forming lobate delta with Rajahmundry as apex.
61. **Krishna River Basin:** Second largest east-flowing peninsular river (1,401 km); originates at **Mahabaleshwar (Maharashtra)**; right-bank tributaries: **Koyna** (Shivajisagar), **Ghataprabha, Malaprabha, Tungabhadra** (formed by Tunga and Bhadra, passes Hampi); left-bank tributaries: **Bhima, Musi** (Hyderabad on banks), **Munneru**; major dams: Nagarjuna Sagar, Srisailam, Almatti.
62. **Kaveri River ('Ganga of the South'):** Originates at **Talakaveri in Brahmagiri Hills (Kodagu / Coorg, Karnataka)**; unique peninsular river with perennial flow because upper catchment receives South-West Monsoon and lower catchment receives North-East Monsoon; forms riverine islands of **Srirangapatna and Shivanasamudra** (second oldest hydroelectric plant in India, 1902); left-bank tributaries: Harangi, Hemavati, Shimsha, Arkavathi; right-bank tributaries: Lakshmantirtha, Kabini, Bhavani, Noyyal, Amaravati; major dams: Krishnarajasagara (KRS, Mysuru), Mettur Dam (Stanley Reservoir, TN).
63. **Mahanadi River Basin:** Originates near **Sihawa in Dhamtari district, Chhattisgarh (Dandakaranya)**; flows through Odisha; major tributaries: Seonath, Hasdeo, Mand, Ib (left bank); Ong, Tel, Jonk (right bank); hosts **Hirakud Dam** (longest earthen dam in the world).
64. **West-Flowing Peninsular Rivers (Rift Valley Rivers):** (1) **Narmada (1,312 km):** Originates at **Amarkantak Plateau (MP)**; flows westward in a rift valley between Vindhyas (north) and Satpuras (south); famous marble rocks and **Dhuandhar Falls** at Bhedaghat (Jabalpur); major project: Sardar Sarovar Dam; (2) **Tapi / Tapti (724 km):** Originates at **Multai in Betul district (MP)**; flows westward between Satpura range and Ajanta hills; major projects: Ukai Dam and Kakrapar; (3) **Mahi:** Originates in Vindhyan hills (MP), cuts across the **Tropic of Cancer twice**, and empties into Gulf of Khambhat; (4) **Sabarmati:** Originates in Aravalli hills (Udaipur, Rajasthan), Ahmedabad and Gandhinagar on banks; (5) **Luni:** Only major river in western arid Rajasthan; originates near Ajmer (Govindgarh) as Sabarmati and Saraswati; inland drainage river, loses itself into the marshy **Rann of Kutch**.
65. **West-Flowing Coastal Rivers of Goa, Karnataka & Kerala:** Goa: **Mandovi** (Dudh Sagar Falls) and **Zuari**; Karnataka: **Sharavathi** (creates **Jog Falls / Gersoppa Falls**, 253 m high), **Kalinadi**, **Netravati**; Kerala: **Periyar** (longest river of Kerala, Idukki Arch Dam), **Bharatpuzha / Ponnani** (second longest), **Pamba** (sacred river of Sabarimala).

## 🧠 Memory Anchors & Mnemonics

### 1. Sacred Panch Prayags North to South: **V-N-K-R-D**
- **V**ishnuprayag: Alaknanda + **Dhauliganga**
- **N**andaprayag: Alaknanda + **Nandakini**
- **K**arnaprayag: Alaknanda + **Pindar**
- **R**udraprayag: Alaknanda + **Mandakini**
- **D**evprayag: Alaknanda + **Bhagirathi** → Forms **Ganga**
- *(Mnemonic: **V**isiting **N**ature's **K**ingdom **R**equires **D**evotion)*

### 2. Left-Bank Tributaries of Ganga (West to East): **R-G-G-G-K-M**
- **R**amganga → **G**omti → **G**haghara → **G**andak → **K**osi → **M**ahananda
- *(Mnemonic: **R**am **G**oes to **G**haghara, **G**andak, **K**osi, and **M**ahananda)*

### 3. Indus Tributaries North to South: **I-J-C-R-B-S**
- **I**ndus → **J**helum → **C**henab → **R**avi → **B**eas → **S**utlej
- *(Mnemonic: **I**n **J**une **C**old **R**ain **B**eats **S**ummer)*

### 4. Major West-Flowing Rivers into Arabian Sea: ** समानता (SA-MA-NA-TA)**
- **Sa** = Sabarmati
- **Ma** = Mahi (cuts Tropic of Cancer twice!)
- **Na** = Narmada (longest west-flowing; rift valley)
- **Ta** = Tapi / Tapti (Multai origin; rift valley)

### 5. Right-Bank Tributaries of Yamuna (West to East): **C-S-B-K**
- **C**hambal → **S**ind → **B**etwa → **K**en
- *(Mnemonic: **C**areful **S**tudents **B**ank **K**nowledge)*

### 6. Radial Drainage Peak: **Amarkantak Trinity**
- Westward: **Narmada** (to Arabian Sea)
- Northward: **Son** (to Ganga / Bay of Bengal)
- Southward/Eastward: **Johilla / Mahanadi headwaters**
