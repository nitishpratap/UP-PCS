## 🎯 Consolidated Must-Score Facts

1. **Total Fertility Rate (TFR)** is the average number of children a woman would bear in her lifetime given current age-specific fertility rates.
2. **Crude Birth Rate (CBR)** is calculated per **1,000 mid-year total population**.
3. **Crude Death Rate (CDR)** of India in recent SRS estimates is approximately **6.0 deaths per 1,000 population**.
4. **Infant Mortality Rate (IMR)** is defined as the number of deaths of children under 1 year of age per **1,000 live births**.
5. **Kerala** has the lowest IMR among Indian States at **6 per 1,000 live births**, while **Madhya Pradesh** has the highest at **43**.
6. **Neonatal Mortality Rate (NMR)** measures deaths of infants occurring within the first **28 days of life** per 1,000 live births.
7. **Early Neonatal Mortality** refers to infant deaths within the first **7 days of life**.
8. **Maternal Mortality Ratio (MMR)** is defined as maternal deaths per **100,000 (1 Lakh) live births**.
9. **Kerala** has the lowest MMR in India at **19 per Lakh live births**, while **Assam** has the highest at **195**.
10. **Life Expectancy at Birth ($e_0$)** in India is currently estimated at **70.8 years** (Females: 72.2 years, Males: 69.5 years).
11. **Kerala** has the highest life expectancy at birth in India (**75.3 years**), while **Uttar Pradesh and Chhattisgarh** have among the lowest (~65.6 years).
12. **Gross Reproduction Rate (GRR)** measures the average number of **female daughters** born to a woman during her lifetime, ignoring mortality.
13. **Net Reproduction Rate (NRR)** measures the number of surviving daughters who will replace their mothers, accounting for female mortality.
14. **NRR = 1.0** indicates that the population has achieved the exact demographic replacement level.
15. **Janani Suraksha Yojana (JSY)** was launched in April 2005 under NRHM to promote **institutional deliveries** among BPL and SC/ST pregnant women.
16. **Janani Shishu Suraksha Karyakram (JSSK)** was launched on **1 June 2011** to provide completely free, cashless delivery and newborn care.
17. **Mission Indradhanush** was launched on **25 December 2014 (Good Governance Day)** to achieve full immunization coverage against 12 vaccine-preventable diseases.
18. **Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana (PMMVY)** was launched on **1 January 2017** under Section 4 of the National Food Security Act, 2013.
19. **Pradhan Mantri Surakshit Matritva Abhiyan (PMSMA)** provides fixed-day free comprehensive antenatal check-ups on the **9th of every month**.
20. **Ayushman Bharat - PM-JAY** provides health insurance coverage of **Rs 5 Lakh per family per year** for secondary and tertiary care.
21. **Sample Registration System (SRS)** under the Registrar General of India publishes the official annual data for CBR, CDR, IMR, TFR, and MMR in India.
22. **Definition**: The average number of children that would be born alive to a woman during her lifetime if she were to pass through her childbearing years (15–49) conforming to the age-specific fertility rates of a given year.

A. 1.0
B. 1.6
C. 2.1
D. 2.3

<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Logic:** Replacement-level TFR ≈ 2.1 children per woman in low-mortality settings.

**Ans: C.** About 2.1.
