## 🎯 Consolidated Must-Score Facts

1. As per Census 2011, India's total **Urban Population** was **37.71 Crore (31.16% of total population)**.
2. The decadal growth rate of the urban population during 2001–2011 was **31.80%** (compared to 12.30% for rural population).
3. **Goa** is the most urbanised State in India, with **62.2%** of its population living in urban areas.
4. **Mizoram** is the second most urbanised State in India (**52.1%**), followed by **Tamil Nadu (48.4%)**.
5. **Himachal Pradesh** is the least urbanised State in India, with only **10.0%** urban population.
6. **Bihar** is the second least urbanised State in India (**11.3%**), followed by Assam (14.1%).
7. Among Union Territories, the **NCT of Delhi** is the most urbanised (**97.5%**), followed by **Chandigarh (97.3%)**.
8. **Maharashtra** has the largest absolute urban population in India (**5.08 Crore**), followed by **Uttar Pradesh (4.45 Crore)**.
9. In Census 2011, there were **4,041 Statutory Towns** and **3,894 Census Towns** in India (total 7,935 towns).
10. The number of **Census Towns** surged by 186% from 1,362 in 2001 to **3,894 in 2011**, driving in-situ rural-to-urban transition.
11. A **Census Town** must have a minimum population of **5,000**, at least **75% male non-agricultural workers**, and a density $\ge \mathbf{400	ext{ persons / km²}}$.
12. **Class I Towns (Cities)** are defined as urban centres having a population of **100,000 (1 Lakh) and above**.
13. In Census 2011, there were **468 Class I Cities** in India.
14. Class I Cities account for **70.2% of India's total urban population**, demonstrating a top-heavy urban hierarchy.
15. A **Million-Plus Urban Agglomeration / City (Metropolis)** is defined as an urban area with a population of **10 Lakh (1 Million) or more**.
16. In Census 2011, there were **53 Million-Plus Cities / UAs** in India (up from 35 in 2001).
17. **Greater Mumbai Urban Agglomeration** is the most populous urban agglomeration in India with **18.4 Million inhabitants**.
18. **Delhi Urban Agglomeration** is the second most populous UA (**16.3 Million**), followed by **Kolkata UA (14.1 Million)**.
19. A **Megacity** is defined as an urban agglomeration with a population of **10 Million (1 Crore) and above**.
20. In Census 2011, India had **3 Megacities**: Greater Mumbai (18.4M), Delhi (16.3M), and Kolkata (14.1M).
21. The total urban population of **Uttar Pradesh** in Census 2011 was **4.45 Crore (22.27% of UP's total population)**.
22. Uttar Pradesh has **7 Million-Plus Cities** as per Census 2011: (1) Kanpur, (2) Lucknow, (3) Ghaziabad, (4) Agra, (5) Varanasi, (6) Meerut, and (7) Prayagraj.
23. **Kanpur** is the most populous city within municipal limits in Uttar Pradesh.
24. In Uttar Pradesh, **Ghaziabad** is the most urbanised district (**67.6% urban**), followed by **Lucknow (66.2%)**.
25. In Uttar Pradesh, **Shravasti** is the least urbanised district with only **3.5% urban population**, followed by Kushinagar (4.7%).
26. In Uttarakhand, **Dehradun** is the most urbanised district (**55.5% urban**), while **Bageshwar** is the least urbanised (**3.5%**).
27. The total **Slum Population** of India in Census 2011 was **6.55 Crore (17.4% of total urban population)**.
28. **Maharashtra** has the highest slum population in India (**1.18 Crore**), followed by Andhra Pradesh (1.02 Crore).
29. **Dharavi** in Mumbai is one of the largest informal slum settlements in Asia.
30. The **74th Constitutional Amendment Act, 1992** inserted **Part IX-A (Articles 243P to 243ZG)** into the Constitution for Municipalities.
31. The 74th Amendment added the **Twelfth Schedule**, listing **18 functional items** devolved to Urban Local Bodies.
32. The 74th Amendment came into force on **1 June 1993**.
33. Under **Article 243S**, the constitution of **Wards Committees** is mandatory for all municipalities having a population of **3 Lakh or more**.
34. Under **Article 243ZD**, a **District Planning Committee (DPC)** must be constituted in every district (at least **four-fifths / 80%** members elected from Zila Panchayat & Municipalities).
35. Under **Article 243ZE**, a **Metropolitan Planning Committee (MPC)** must be constituted in every metropolitan area with population $\ge 10	ext{ Lakh}$ (at least **two-thirds / 66.7%** members elected).
36. The **Smart Cities Mission** was launched on **25 June 2015** to develop **100 Smart Cities** through Special Purpose Vehicles (SPVs).
37. The **AMRUT Mission (Atal Mission for Rejuvenation and Urban Transformation)** was launched on **25 June 2015** covering **500 cities** with population $> 1	ext{ Lakh}$.
38. **AMRUT 2.0** was launched in **October 2021** with an outlay of **Rs 2.77 Lakh Crore** to provide 100% water security across all statutory towns.
39. **Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana - Urban (PMAY-U)** was launched on **25 June 2015** to provide pucca houses under four verticals.
40. The **Credit Linked Subsidy Scheme (CLSS)** under PMAY-U provides upfront interest subsidies of 3% to 6.5% on home loans for EWS, LIG, and MIG categories.
41. **Swachh Bharat Mission - Urban (SBM-U)** was launched on **2 October 2014** to eliminate open defecation and achieve scientific waste management.
42. Under Swachh Survekshan, **Indore (Madhya Pradesh)** has been consistently ranked as India's cleanest city for multiple consecutive years.
43. **PM SVANidhi** scheme was launched on **1 June 2020** by MoHUA to provide collateral-free working capital loans of Rs 10,000 to **Urban Street Vendors**.
44. **Urban Sprawl** refers to the rapid, unplanned spatial expansion of a city into its surrounding rural-urban fringe.
45. **Primacy / Primate City Law** was formulated by **Mark Jefferson in 1939**, stating that a country's leading city is disproportionately larger than any other city.
46. The **Rank-Size Rule** of urban settlements was formulated by **George Zipf in 1949** ($P_n = P_1 / n$).
47. **Conurbation** is an extensive continuous urban area formed by the coalescing of several expanding cities and towns, a term coined by **Patrick Geddes in 1915**.
48. **Megalopolis** refers to a massive clustered network of adjacent metropolitan areas, a term coined by **Jean Gottmann in 1961** (e.g. Boston to Washington D.C.).
49. **Gentrification** is the socio-economic process of renovating and improving urban deteriorated neighbourhoods, often displacing lower-income residents.
50. In India, the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs (**MoHUA**) is the apex Union ministry responsible for urban development policies, planning, and schemes.

## 🧠 Memory Anchors & Mnemonics

- **Census Town Criteria (3-in-1 Rule)**:
  1. Minimum population of **5,000**
  2. At least **75% of male main working population** engaged in non-agricultural pursuits
  3. Population density of at least **400 persons / sq km** (1,000 / sq mile)
- **Top 5 Million-Plus Urban Agglomerations (2011)**: **M-D-K-C-B** (Greater Mumbai 18.4M > Delhi 16.3M > Kolkata 14.1M > Chennai 8.6M > Bangalore 8.5M)
- **Most / Least Urbanised States**:
  - By Percentage: **Goa (62.2%)** > Mizoram (52.1%) > Tamil Nadu (48.4%) | Lowest: **Himachal Pradesh (10.0%)** < Bihar (11.3%)
  - By Absolute Number: **Maharashtra (5.08 Cr)** > Uttar Pradesh (4.45 Cr) > Tamil Nadu (3.49 Cr).
