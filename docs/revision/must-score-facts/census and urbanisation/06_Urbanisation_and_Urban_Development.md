## 🎯 Consolidated Must-Score Facts

1. **Goa** is the most urbanised State in India, with **62.2%** of its population living in urban areas.
2. **Mizoram** is the second most urbanised State in India (**52.1%**), followed by **Tamil Nadu (48.4%)**.
3. **Himachal Pradesh** is the least urbanised State in India, with only **10.0%** urban population.
4. **Bihar** is the second least urbanised State in India (**11.3%**), followed by Assam (14.1%).
5. **Maharashtra** has the largest absolute urban population in India (**5.08 Crore**), followed by **Uttar Pradesh (4.45 Crore)**.
6. **Class I Towns (Cities)** are defined as urban centres having a population of **100,000 (1 Lakh) and above**.
7. **Greater Mumbai Urban Agglomeration** is the most populous urban agglomeration in India with **18.4 Million inhabitants**.
8. **Delhi Urban Agglomeration** is the second most populous UA (**16.3 Million**), followed by **Kolkata UA (14.1 Million)**.
9. **Kanpur** is the most populous city within municipal limits in Uttar Pradesh.
10. **Maharashtra** has the highest slum population in India (**1.18 Crore**), followed by Andhra Pradesh (1.02 Crore).
11. **Dharavi** in Mumbai is one of the largest informal slum settlements in Asia.
12. **AMRUT 2.0** was launched in **October 2021** with an outlay of **Rs 2.77 Lakh Crore** to provide 100% water security across all statutory towns.
13. **Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana - Urban (PMAY-U)** was launched on **25 June 2015** to provide pucca houses under four verticals.
14. **Swachh Bharat Mission - Urban (SBM-U)** was launched on **2 October 2014** to eliminate open defecation and achieve scientific waste management.
15. **PM SVANidhi** scheme was launched on **1 June 2020** by MoHUA to provide collateral-free working capital loans of Rs 10,000 to **Urban Street Vendors**.
16. **Urban Sprawl** refers to the rapid, unplanned spatial expansion of a city into its surrounding rural-urban fringe.
17. **Primacy / Primate City Law** was formulated by **Mark Jefferson in 1939**, stating that a country's leading city is disproportionately larger than any other city.
18. **Conurbation** is an extensive continuous urban area formed by the coalescing of several expanding cities and towns, a term coined by **Patrick Geddes in 1915**.
19. **Megalopolis** refers to a massive clustered network of adjacent metropolitan areas, a term coined by **Jean Gottmann in 1961** (e.g. Boston to Washington D.C.).
20. **Gentrification** is the socio-economic process of renovating and improving urban deteriorated neighbourhoods, often displacing lower-income residents.

23. **Three-Tier Structure of Urban Local Bodies (ULBs)**:
24. **Nagar Panchayat**: For a transitional area (rural-to-urban transition).
25. **Municipal Council (Nagar Palika Parishad)**: For a smaller urban area.
26. **Municipal Corporation (Nagar Nigam)**: For a larger urban area.
27. **Key Constitutional Committees**:
28. **Wards Committees (Article 243S)**: Mandatory for municipalities having a population of **3 Lakh or more**.
29. **District Planning Committee - DPC (Article 243ZD)**: Consolidates plans prepared by Panchayats and Municipalities across the district. At least **four-fifths (80%)** of DPC members must be elected by and from among the elected members of the Zila Panchayat and Municipalities.
30. **Metropolitan Planning Committee - MPC (Article 243ZE)**: Mandatory for every metropolitan area with population $\ge 10	ext{ Lakh}$. At least **two-thirds (66.7%)** of MPC members must be elected by and from the elected members of Municipalities and Chairpersons of Panchayats in the metropolitan area.
31. **Census Definition of Slum**: A compact area of at least **300 population or about 60–70 households** of poorly built congested tenements in unhygienic environments, lacking basic civic amenities (clean drinking water, drainage, sanitation).
32. **Three Categories of Slums (Census 2011)**:
33. **Notified Slums**: All specified areas in a town formally notified as slums by State Governments or local authorities under Slum Acts.
34. **Recognized Slums**: Areas recognized as slums by State Governments/ULBs, although not formally notified under legislation.
35. **Identified Slums**: Compact areas identified by census enumerators satisfying slum criteria (300+ people or 60+ households).
