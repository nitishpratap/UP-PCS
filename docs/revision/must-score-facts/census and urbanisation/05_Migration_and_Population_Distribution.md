## 🎯 Consolidated Must-Score Facts

1. **Rural to Rural (R $	o$ R)** is the **largest internal migration stream** in India, accounting for over 53% of all internal migrants.
2. **Marriage** is the single largest reason for female migration in India, accounting for **66.7% (two-thirds)** of all female migrants.
3. **Work / Employment** is the single largest reason for male internal migration in India, accounting for approximately **38%** of male migrants.
4. **Urban to Rural (U $	o$ R)** is the smallest migration stream in India, representing return migration post-retirement or economic crisis.
5. **Intra-district migration** accounts for the largest share (**62%**) of internal migration in India.
6. **Inter-state migration** constitutes approximately **12%** (5.4 Crore migrants) of total internal migrants in India.
7. **Uttar Pradesh** is the **largest net source State** of out-migrants in India, followed by **Bihar**.
8. **Maharashtra** is the **largest destination State** for inter-state in-migrants in India, followed by the **NCT of Delhi**.
9. **E. G. Ravenstein** published the foundational **"Laws of Migration"** in **1885 / 1889**.
10. **Push Factors** are adverse conditions that compel people to leave their place of origin (unemployment, poverty, drought, crop failure).
11. **Pull Factors** are attractive conditions that draw migrants to a destination (better employment, higher wages, modern healthcare, urban amenities).
12. **Arithmetic Population Density** is calculated as: **Total Population divided by Total Geographical Land Area**.
13. **Physiological / Nutritional Density** is calculated as: **Total Population divided by Net Cultivated / Arable Land Area**.
14. **Agricultural Density** is calculated as: **Total Agricultural Population divided by Net Cultivated Land Area**.
15. **Bihar** has the highest arithmetic population density among Indian States (**1,106 persons / sq km**).
16. **West Bengal** ranks second in population density among States (**1,028 persons / sq km**).
17. **Arunachal Pradesh** has the lowest population density in India (**17 persons / sq km**).
18. **Step Migration** refers to a spatial process where a migrant moves in stages (e.g. from a small village to a local town, then to a regional city, and finally to a metropolis).
19. **Circular / Seasonal Migration** refers to temporary, recurring movements of rural labourers who travel for seasonal farm or construction work and return home.
20. **Feminization of Agriculture** refers to the increasing proportion of agricultural work performed by rural women due to male out-migration to urban industrial centers.
21. **Brain Drain** refers to the cross-border international migration of highly skilled and educated professionals (doctors, engineers, scientists) from developing to developed countries.
22. **Ecological / Climate Migrants** are individuals forced to migrate due to environmental degradation, rising sea levels, desertification, or natural disasters.
23. **Categorisation by Movement Scale**:
24. **Intra-District Migrants**: Movement within the same district (**62%** of all migrants — predominantly female marriage migration).
25. **Inter-District Migrants**: Movement across districts within the same State (**26%**).
26. **Inter-State Migrants**: Movement across State boundaries (**12%**, approx. 5.4 Crore migrants).
27. **Female Migration Drivers**:
28. **Marriage accounts for 66.7%** (two-thirds) of all female migration in India due to traditional patrilocal village exogamy.
29. **Male Migration Drivers**:
30. **Work / Employment / Business is the primary driver (approx. 38%–40%)** for male internal migration.
31. **Out-Migration Origins (Net Source States)**:
32. **Uttar Pradesh** is the single largest source of out-migrants in India, followed closely by **Bihar**, Rajasthan, and Madhya Pradesh.
33. **In-Migration Destinations (Net Inflow States)**:
34. **Maharashtra** is the top destination state in India (attracting ~20% of all inter-state migrants, primarily in the Mumbai Metropolitan Region and Pune).
35. **National Capital Territory of Delhi (NCT)** is the second largest destination.
