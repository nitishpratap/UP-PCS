## 🎯 Consolidated Must-Score Facts

1. **Kerala** has the highest sex ratio among Indian States at **1,084**, followed by Tamil Nadu (996) and Andhra Pradesh (993).
2. **Haryana** has the lowest sex ratio among Indian States at **879**, followed by Jammu & Kashmir (889) and Sikkim (890).
3. **Puducherry** has the highest sex ratio among Union Territories at **1,037**, while **Daman & Diu** has the lowest at **618**.
4. **Mizoram** recorded the highest Child Sex Ratio among States at **970**, followed by Meghalaya (970) and Chhattisgarh (969).
5. **Haryana** recorded the lowest Child Sex Ratio among States at **834**, followed by Punjab (846).
6. **Kerala** has the highest overall literacy rate (**94.0%**) as well as highest female literacy (**92.1%**).
7. **Bihar** has the lowest overall literacy rate (**61.8%**) and the lowest male literacy rate (**71.2%**).
8. **Rajasthan** recorded the lowest female literacy rate among all States at **52.1%** (and the widest gender literacy gap of **27.1%**).
9. **Meghalaya** recorded the narrowest gender gap in literacy in India at only **3.1 percentage points**.
10. **Uttar Pradesh** has the largest absolute Scheduled Caste population in India (**4.14 Crore**).
11. **Punjab** has the highest proportion of Scheduled Castes relative to its total population (**31.9%**).
12. **Madhya Pradesh** has the largest absolute Scheduled Tribe population in India (**1.53 Crore**).
13. **Lakshadweep (94.8%)** and **Mizoram (94.4%)** have the highest percentage of Scheduled Tribes in their total population.
14. **Muslims** constitute **14.23% (17.22 Crore)** of India's population, with the highest decadal growth (**24.6%**) among major religions.
15. **Christians** constitute **2.30% (2.78 Crore)** of India's population and have the highest sex ratio (**1,023**) among all major religious communities.
16. **Sikhs** constitute **1.72% (2.08 Crore)** of India's population and have the lowest sex ratio (**903**) among all major religious communities.
17. **Buddhists** constitute **0.70% (0.84 Crore)** of India's population, with the largest absolute community residing in **Maharashtra (65.3 Lakh)**.
18. **Jains** constitute **0.37% (0.45 Crore)** of India's population and possess the **highest literacy rate (94.9%)** of any religious community in India.
19. **Hindi** is the most spoken language in India, spoken by **43.63% (52.83 Crore)** of the total population.
20. **Bengali** is the second most spoken language in India (**8.03%**), followed by **Marathi (6.86%)** and **Telugu (6.70%)**.
21. **Sanskrit** is the least spoken Eighth Schedule language in India, with only **24,821 speakers** recorded in Census 2011.

28. **Main Workers**: Persons who engaged in any economically productive activity for **183 days (6 months) or more** during the preceding 365 days.
29. **Marginal Workers**: Persons who worked for **less than 183 days (less than 6 months)** during the preceding year (further categorized into those working for 0–3 months and 3–6 months).
30. **Non-Workers**: Persons who did not engage in any economic work during the entire year (students, homemakers, dependents, retired persons, beggars).
31. **Four Standard Industrial Categories of Workers**:
32. **Cultivators (CL)**: Persons engaged in farming/cultivating land owned or held from government/private persons (24.6% of total workers).
33. **Agricultural Labourers (AL)**: Persons who work on another person's land for wages in money or kind without holding ownership/lease rights (30.0% of total workers).
34. **Household Industry Workers (HHI)**: Workers in manufacturing, processing, servicing, or repairing conducted within the home by household members (3.8%).
35. **Other Workers (OW)**: All workers engaged in factories, government, commerce, IT, transport, mining, and services (41.6%).
