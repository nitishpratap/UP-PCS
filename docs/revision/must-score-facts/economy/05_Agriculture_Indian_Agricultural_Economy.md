## 🎯 Consolidated Must-Score Facts

1. **Small and Marginal Farmers (SMFs, holding < 2.0 ha)** account for **~86.2% of all operational land holdings** in India, but operate only **~47.3% of the total operated area**.
2. **Marginal holdings (< 1.0 ha)** alone constitute **68.5%** of all agricultural land holdings in India, with an average operational size of just **0.38 hectare**.
3. **Kharif crops (खरीफ)** are sown with the onset of the South-West Monsoon in **June–July** and harvested in **September–October**; major crops include **Rice, Maize, Jowar, Bajra, Cotton, Jute, Groundnut, Soybean, Tur/Arhar, Urad, and Moong**.
4. **Rabi crops (रबी)** are sown in **October–November** (winter onset) and harvested in **March–April** (spring); major crops include **Wheat, Barley, Gram (चना), Mustard (सरसों), Linseed (अलसी), Peas (मटर), and Potato**.
5. **Zaid crops (जायद)** are short-duration summer crops grown from **March to June** between Rabi harvest and Kharif sowing; predominantly include cucurbitaceous crops: **Watermelon (तरबूज), Muskmelon (खरबूजा), Cucumber (खीरा), Bitter gourd (करेला), Moong, and leafy vegetables/fodder**.
6. **Rice (चावल)** is the **most cultivated crop in India**, occupying the largest gross cropped area (~47 million hectares); **West Bengal** is the largest producer of Rice, while **Punjab** has the highest per-hectare productivity.
7. **Wheat (गेहूं)** is the second most important cereal crop in India, occupying ~31 million hectares; **Uttar Pradesh** is the largest producer of Wheat in terms of total absolute output, while **Punjab** records the highest yield/productivity per hectare.
8. **Shree Anna (श्री अन्न / Millets)** was championed by India, leading the UN to declare **2023 as the International Year of Millets**; **Rajasthan** is the largest producer of **Bajra** (Pearl Millet), while **Karnataka/Maharashtra** lead in **Jowar** (Sorghum).
9. **Sugarcane (गन्ना)** is a perennial tropical cash crop; **Uttar Pradesh** is the largest producer of Sugarcane and Sugar in India, but Maharashtra and Tamil Nadu record higher sucrose content and recovery rates due to maritime tropical climate.
10. **Cotton (कपास - White Gold)** is predominantly grown in the black regur soil of the Deccan plateau; **Gujarat** and **Maharashtra** are the top producing states.
11. **Uttar Pradesh** possesses the largest canal network and largest tube-well irrigated area in absolute terms in India; **Tamil Nadu and Andhra Pradesh** lead in **Tank irrigation**.
12. **Urea** is the ONLY fertilizer whose **Maximum Retail Price (MRP) is statutorily fixed** by the Central Government; the difference between the normative delivered cost and the controlled MRP is paid as subsidy directly to manufacturers.
13. **Neem Coated Urea (NCU)** was made **100% mandatory** for all indigenous and imported agricultural urea in **2015**; it prevents illegal diversion to chemical industries and improves Nitrogen Use Efficiency (NUE) by slowing degradation.
14. **PM-PRANAM** (PM Programme for Restoration, Awareness, Nourishment and Amelioration of Mother Earth) was announced in Union Budget 2023–24 to incentivise states to promote alternative fertilizers and reduce chemical fertilizer usage.
15. **Sikkim** was formally declared India's and the world's **First 100% Organic State** in **January 2016**; **Lakshadweep** is the first 100% Organic Union Territory (2020).
16. **Zero Budget Natural Farming (ZBNF)** was pioneered in India by Padma Shri **Subhash Palekar**; it relies on 4 core pillars: **Jeevamrutha** (microbial culture), **Bijamrutha** (seed treatment), **Acchadana** (mulching), and **Whapasa** (soil moisture aeration).
17. **Electronic National Agriculture Market (e-NAM)** was launched on **14 April 2016** as a pan-India electronic trading portal networking existing APMC mandis to create a unified national market for agricultural commodities; it is managed by the **Small Farmers' Agri-Business Consortium (SFAC)**.
18. **MPEDA** (Marine Products Export Development Authority) was constituted in **1972** under the Ministry of Commerce and Industry to promote marine and seafood exports.
19. **Operation Flood (ऑपरेशन फ्लड)** was launched in **1970** by the National Dairy Development Board (NDDB) under **Dr. Verghese Kurien** (Father of White Revolution in India), transforming India from a milk-deficient nation into the world's largest milk producer through the Anand cooperative pattern (AMUL).
20. **Primary Sector Contribution:** Agriculture, forestry, and fishing contribute approximately **17%–18% of India's Gross Value Added (GVA)** at current basic prices. Despite its declining economic share in GDP (from ~54% in 1950–51), it remains the backbone of the rural economy, directly employing **~45%–47% of the Indian workforce** according to the Periodic Labour Force Survey (PLFS).
21. **The Land Holding Paradigm:** The structure of land ownership in India is characterised by acute **fragmentation** and dominance of small, economically unviable plots.
22. **Agricultural Census Findings (2015–16):**
23. **Marginal Farmers (< 1 ha):** Constitute **68.5%** of all operational holdings (operating ~24.0% of area).
24. **Small Farmers (1–2 ha):** Constitute **17.7%** of holdings (operating ~23.0% of area).
25. **Combined Small & Marginal Farmers (SMFs, < 2 ha):** Constitute **86.2%** of all holdings in India, controlling **47.3%** of total operated area.
26. **Medium & Large Farmers (> 4 ha):** Make up less than **4.4%** of holdings, but control over **30.9%** of operated land.
27. **Average Operational Size:** Declined from **2.28 ha in 1970–71** to **1.15 ha in 2010–11**, and further to **1.08 ha in 2015–16**.
