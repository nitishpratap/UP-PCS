## 🎯 Consolidated Must-Score Facts

1. **Primary** = agriculture, forestry, fishing, dairy, mining / quarrying; **Secondary** = manufacturing and related industrial processing; **Tertiary** = services.
2. **Forestry and fishing**, **mining and quarrying** are **not** tertiary — they are primary (mining support exploration is still primary-side in keyed NIC teaching).
3. **Marketing**, transport, communication and commerce are tertiary; **weaving cloth** is secondary; **dairy farm** is primary.
4. **Storage of agricultural produce** is a **tertiary** activity (with trade/transport/communication), not secondary.
5. **Construction** is part of **industry** in national accounts — **not** a services-sector component in GVA share stems.
6. **IT / ITeS** (software, BPO, digital services) is a flagship tradable services cluster.
7. **Tourism** and hospitality convert natural / cultural assets into services income and jobs.
8. **Transport and logistics** (road, rail, air, ports, warehousing) enable goods and people movement — Gati Shakti links Topic 7.
9. **Education and healthcare** are social services with large private and public delivery mix.
10. **E-commerce and digital services** reshape retail, payments and delivery — regulated across IT, consumer and competition lanes.
11. **Ministry of Cooperation** was created in **2021** to give cooperatives a dedicated Central desk.
12. **Cooperative banks** sit under banking regulation with RBI / State roles (depth in Topic 3); policy identity also appears here.
13. **Companies** are artificial legal persons; **public** and **private** companies differ on share offer / member limits teaching.
14. **Companies Act, 2013** is the principal company-law statute (not a “Companies Act, 2014”).
15. **CSR (Corporate Social Responsibility)** under Section 135 requires eligible companies to spend **at least 2%** of the **average net profits of the three preceding financial years** on Schedule VII activities.
16. **Uday Kotak Committee (SEBI, 2017)** — corporate governance; Chair and MD posts should be **separated** (not kept the same).
17. **Corporate governance** covers board duties, disclosure, audit and minority protection under company and securities law.
18. **RBI** — monetary authority and banking regulator (list tag here; full toolkit Topic 3).
19. **SEBI** — securities market regulator (list tag here; markets depth Topic 4); statutory SEBI Act **1992**.
20. **IRDAI** — insurance sector regulator (IRDA Act **1999**); HQ **Hyderabad**.
21. **PFRDA** — pension sector / **NPS** regulator (PFRDA Act **2013** statutory frame).
22. **CCI (Competition Commission of India)** — Competition Act **2002**; prevents anti-competitive agreements, abuse of dominance and regulates combinations.
23. **TRAI** — telecom / broadcasting tariff and interconnection regulator; disputes go to **TDSAT** in teaching.
24. **IFSCA (International Financial Services Centres Authority)** — unified regulator for financial products / services in IFSCs, notably **GIFT City**.
25. **BIS (Bureau of Indian Standards)** — national standards body; ISI / standards marks for product quality.
26. **FSSAI** — food safety regulator under Food Safety and Standards Act **2006**; Ministry of Health & Family Welfare lane.
27. **ECOMARC** is India’s eco-label for environment-friendly products.
28. **AGMARK** grades agricultural produce quality (agri neighbour); do not swap with ECOMARC.
29. **Consumer Protection Act, 2019** modernises consumer rights, product liability and e-commerce duties (polity depth may sit in Acts notes).
30. **MRP (Maximum Retail Price)** is a **legal ceiling** on packaged goods — seller **cannot charge above** MRP; selling / bargaining **below** MRP is allowed.
31. **Primary** exploits natural resources: agriculture, forestry, fishing, dairy, mining and quarrying.
32. **Secondary** transforms natural products through manufacturing (e.g. weaving cloth from cotton fibre).
33. **Tertiary** supplies services: marketing, transport, communication, commerce, finance, real estate, administration, education, health and tourism.
34. **Forestry and fishing** and **mining / quarrying** are **not** tertiary — a recurring multi-statement trap.
35. **Mineral exploration** support activities sit with mining (primary-side) in keyed NIC teaching — not tertiary.
