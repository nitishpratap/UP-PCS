## 🎯 Consolidated Must-Score Facts

1. **Industrialisation** raises manufacturing’s share of output and employment and deepens capital goods capacity.
2. **Large-scale industry** uses heavy capital and organised labour; **SSI / MSME** and **cottage** units are smaller, more labour-intensive and dispersed.
3. **Cottage industries** are household / artisan-based with little separation of dwelling and workplace.
4. **MSME** classification (from **1 July 2020**) uses a **composite** investment + turnover criterion for manufacturing and services together — no separate mfg/services formula.
5. **2020 bands** (still keyed in many stems): Micro ≤ **₹1 crore** / ≤ **₹5 crore**; Small ≤ **₹10 crore** / ≤ **₹50 crore**; Medium ≤ **₹50 crore** / ≤ **₹250 crore**.
6. **From 1 April 2025**: Micro ≤ **₹2.5 crore** / ≤ **₹10 crore**; Small ≤ **₹25 crore** / ≤ **₹100 crore**; Medium ≤ **₹125 crore** / ≤ **₹500 crore** — read the stem year before picking a ceiling.
7. **Karve Committee (1955)** is the early Village and Small-Scale Industries committee of the plan era.
8. **Make in India (25 September 2014)** encourages companies to manufacture in India and facilitates investment; logo is a **lion** of cogs.
9. **Startup India** eases compliance and funding; start-ups raise capital via **angel investors, venture capital and crowdfunding**.
10. **Stand-Up India** (5 April 2016) facilitates bank loans (₹10 lakh–₹1 crore teaching) to **SC/ST and women** greenfield entrepreneurs; SIDBI refinance / connect centres.
11. **PMMY / MUDRA** (8 April 2015, Ministry of Finance / DFS): Shishu ≤₹50,000; Kishore ₹50,001–₹5 lakh; Tarun ₹5–10 lakh; **Tarun Plus** up to **₹20 lakh** (from Oct 2024 teaching).
12. **Industrial corridors** (e.g. **DMIC with Japan**) and **SEZs** aim to cluster infrastructure, logistics and export-oriented production; Asia’s first EPZ was **Kandla (1965)**.
13. **Public Sector Undertakings (PSUs)** are government-owned enterprises in strategic and commercial fields.
14. **Navratna / Maharatna / Miniratna** tags grant operational and investment autonomy tiers to eligible CPSE (Maharatna count is CA-sensitive — ~14 in recent teaching).
15. **Disinvestment** sells government equity in PSUs; **privatisation / strategic sale** transfers control; proceeds route via **National Investment Fund** teaching.
16. **FDI** is lasting foreign investment with control / lasting interest; **FPI** is portfolio investment (Topic 9 depth).
17. **Services** have long been a top sector for FDI equity flows in India teaching; Singapore has recently led as a source country.
18. **IIP** tracks industrial volume of **Mining + Manufacturing + Electricity** — **construction / gas–water supply are not** in IIP; publisher NSO (earlier CSO).
19. **Eight Core Industries** (~**40.27%** of IIP weight): coal, crude oil, natural gas, refinery products, fertilisers, steel, cement, electricity — **chemicals / textiles not** in the eight.
20. **Textile** is the largest industrial employer after agriculture; **powerloom** leads cotton-cloth quantity; Gujarat leads mill-made cotton cloth teaching.
21. **IT / electronics** and **pharma** are high-value strengths; India is about the **second-largest mobile-phone producer** after China in recent ICA teaching.
22. **Infrastructure** is the enabling capital stock — transport, energy, telecom, logistics and digital rails.
23. **PM Gati Shakti** launched **13 October 2021** — seven engines: roads, railways, airports, ports, mass transport, waterways, logistics infrastructure.
24. **National Infrastructure Pipeline (NIP)** covers roughly **FY 2019–25** (not “2020–30”); objective includes equitable infrastructure access; NIP projects align with Gati Shakti.
25. **Bharatmala** = roads/highways; **Sagarmala** = ports/coastal; Deepak Parekh and Vijay Kelkar committees = infrastructure financing / PPP revitalisation.
26. **NIIF** (2015) is India’s infrastructure investment fund under Finance Ministry — **not** an organ of NITI Aayog; proposed corpus teaching ≈ **₹40,000 crore** (not ₹4 lakh crore).
27. **National Manufacturing Policy (4 November 2011)**: raise manufacturing share toward **25%** of GDP, create **100 million** jobs, growth **12–14%**; tools include **NIMZs**, single-window clearance, Technology Acquisition Fund.
28. **PLI** rewards incremental domestic manufacturing; terms (incentive %, years, applicant categories) are scheme- and tranche-sensitive.
29. **PNGRB (2006)** promotes competitive markets for petroleum/gas; appeals go to the **Appellate Tribunal for Electricity**; it is **not** India’s first regulatory body.
30. **PCRA** = Petroleum Conservation Research Association.
31. **CGTMSE** = Credit Guarantee Fund Trust for Micro and Small Enterprises (GoI + SIDBI).
32. **Digital India** is a digital-empowerment mission — **not** primarily an industrial-development initiative (unlike Make in India / Startup India / EoDB).
33. **Abid Hussain Committee** recommended abolition of SSI product reservation; **Nayak Committee** examined SSI finance.
34. **Reliance Industries** — first Indian private firm on Fortune Global 500 teaching (2006); long-time Fortune India #1; first Indian firm above **USD 200 bn** market cap.
35. **MSMEs** dominate employment share in manufacturing and services micro-enterprise teaching.
