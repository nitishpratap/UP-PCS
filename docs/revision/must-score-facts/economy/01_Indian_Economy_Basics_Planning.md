## 🎯 Consolidated Must-Score Facts

1. **Adam Smith** is commonly called the **Father of Economics**. **J.M. Keynes** is the father of modern **macroeconomics**, not the blanket “Father of Economics” tag.
2. **Voluntary social service** is **not** an economic activity; farming, transport and market services are.
3. **Economic growth** is a rise in real output / real income. The best single sign among options is a **sustained rise in real per capita income**.
4. **Economic development** is broader — living standards, welfare, health, education, inequality and poverty outcomes.
5. **Hindu rate of growth** (Prof. **Raj Krishna**) = stagnant **National Income / GDP** growth around **~3.5%** from the 1950s to early 1980s.
6. **GDP** is the market value of all **final** goods and services produced **within the domestic territory** in a period.
7. **GNP / GNI** adjusts domestic product for **net factor income from abroad** (NFIA): roughly GNP = GDP + NFIA.
8. **NDP** = GDP − depreciation (never GDP + depreciation). **NNP** = GNP − depreciation. Also NNP = GDP + NFIA − depreciation.
9. **National Income** in older Indian teaching is **NNP at factor cost** (NNPFC) = GNPMp − depreciation − indirect taxes + subsidies.
10. **Real GDP** uses constant (base-year) prices. **Nominal GDP** uses **current** market prices.
11. **Base year** = the year whose **prices** are used for **real** GDP. Current National Accounts base (from **Feb 2026**): **2022–23**. Previous major base: **2011–12** (from 2015).
12. **NSO** (MoSPI) estimates / releases National Income. **CSO + NSSO** merged into NSO in **2019**. Older keys naming CSO are historically true.
13. **High capital–output ratio (COR)** explains low growth despite high savings. Slow PCI also tracks **high population growth**.
14. **Household sector** dominates India’s gross domestic savings. Within household savings, **physical assets** usually outrank deposits / currency / shares.
15. **Economic and Social Planning** sits in the **Concurrent List (List-III)** of the Seventh Schedule — joint Centre–State domain.
16. **Planning Commission** was set up by **Cabinet resolution** on **15 March 1950**. It was **non-constitutional** and **non-statutory** — an advisory / quasi-political body.
17. **NITI Aayog** replaced the Planning Commission from **1 January 2015** by **Union Cabinet resolution**. It is a policy think tank — neither constitutional nor statutory.
18. **Prime Minister** is Chairperson of NITI Aayog. NITI has a full-time **Vice-Chairperson**, not a full-time Chairman.
19. **NITI Aayog** is the **nodal institution** for implementing the **Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)** in India.
20. **DMEO** (Development Monitoring and Evaluation Office) was set up on **18 September 2015** as an **attached office of NITI Aayog** by merging **PEO** and **IEO**.
21. **NDC (National Development Council)** was constituted on **6 August 1952**. The **Prime Minister** is its ex-officio Chairman.
22. **Finance Commission** is **constitutional (Art. 280)**; Planning Commission was **non-constitutional**. FC focuses on tax devolution / grants; Planning reviewed both capital and revenue plan needs of States.
23. **First Plan** stressed agriculture, irrigation and power (**Harrod–Domar** influence teaching). Planned development model dates from **1 April 1951**.
24. **Second Plan** (**Mahalanobis**) stressed heavy / capital-goods industry, public-sector industry and a **socialistic pattern of society** (IPR 1956).
25. **Plan Holiday (1966–69)** meant three Annual Plans after war, drought and forex stress — not a Five-Year Plan.
26. **Fifth Plan** twin goals: **Garibi Hatao** (poverty removal) and **self-reliance** (zero net foreign aid teaching). It was terminated early (1978) by the Janata government.
27. **Eleventh Plan (2007–12)** — **Towards Faster and More Inclusive Growth**; GDP target **9%**; agri **4%**; industry ~**10–10.5%**; social sector largest combined expenditure share.
28. **Twelfth Plan (2012–17)** — **Faster, More Inclusive and Sustainable Growth**; NDC finally approved **8%** GDP (Approach had floated 9%); agri/forestry/fisheries **4%**; social services largest allocation share; infra investment ~**USD 1 trillion** teaching.
29. **Imperative planning** replaces the market with command hierarchy; **indicative planning** guides and improves the market — India’s later mixed-economy style.
30. **LPG reforms (1991)** under **P.V. Narasimha Rao** (Finance Minister Manmohan Singh) — liberalisation, privatisation, globalisation response to BOP crisis.
31. **Rolling Plan (1978–80)** under the **Janata** government revised targets yearly. **Gunnar Myrdal** suggested Rolling Plans for backward countries; India used them as Annual Plans after rejecting the unfinished Fifth Plan.
32. **National Planning Committee (1938)** was set up by Congress President **Subhash Chandra Bose** with **Jawaharlal Nehru** as chairman.
33. **Bombay Plan (1944)** — industrialists including **J.R.D. Tata**, **G.D. Birla**, **Lala Shri Ram**, A. Dalal, Kasturbhai Lalbhai, A.D. Shroff, Purshottamdas Thakurdas, John Mathai. **Dhirubhai Ambani** was **not** among them.
34. **Gandhian Plan (1944)** by **Sriman Narayan Agarwal** stressed decentralisation, villages and cottage industries. **Mahatma Gandhi** opposed centralised planned development among the keyed options.
35. **Lack of natural resources** is **not** an Indian-economy characteristic; productivity, capital formation and labour quality are the lagging traits.

## ⚡ Confused Pairs & Common Examiner Traps

* Mixed economy = public + private — not small/large industry or primary/secondary alone; Keynes ≠ Father of Economics (Adam Smith).
* Labour-surplus ≠ capital-/trade-surplus; lack of natural resources is **not** an Indian-economy trait.
* Underdeveloped: low PCI + low capital formation — **not** low dependency or tertiary-heavy workforce.
* Voluntary social service ≠ economic activity.
* Growth ≠ development; real GNP rise fails development if poverty/unemployment rise; best growth sign = sustained **real PCI**.
* Hindu rate (Raj Krishna) = NI/GDP ~3.5% — not population/literacy.
* NI = NNP at **factor cost**; NDP = GDP − Dep (never +); Y = C+I+G+(X−M).
* Base year = prices for **real** GDP — current **2022–23** (from Feb 2026); 2015 change was base **and** method (GVA basic prices).
* Unorganised sector / non-monetised output muddies NI; diminishing-cost is **not** a GDP method.
* Real sector (crops/textiles) ≠ financial sector (bank lending / Masala bonds).
* High COR → low growth despite high savings; household savings dominate; physical assets top household saving form.
* Stabilisation (quick, demand) ≠ structural adjustment (gradual, supply); liberalisation pioneered by Manmohan Singh as FM; started with industrial licensing.
* Meltdown ≠ recession ≠ slowdown definitions — only meltdown correctly = stock-price fall in the UPSC pair.
* GDP (domestic) vs GNP (+ NFIA); real vs nominal; deflator ≠ CPI key.
* NITI: 1 Jan **2015**, Cabinet resolution, **PM** Chair, first VC **Panagariya**; no old Plan-fund role; Governors ≠ GC; full-time VC not full-time Chair.
* Economic & Social Planning = **Concurrent List** — not Union/State exclusive.
* DMEO = attached office of **NITI** (18 Sep 2015) — not PMO / Cabinet Secretariat / Home.
* NDC (6 Aug 1952, PM Chair) = final FYP approval — not formally abolished; little work after NITI GC.
* Finance Commission (Art. 280) ≠ Planning Commission (non-constitutional Plan body).
* Imperative = command replaces market; indicative = guides market.
* Gadgil–Mukherjee: **population 60%** highest weight — not performance first.
* 11th = inclusive; 12th = inclusive + sustainable; 12th is **last** FYP.
* Plan Holiday **1966–69** ≠ Rolling Plan **1978–80** (Janata / Myrdal).
* Garibi Hatao slogan (1971) vs Fifth Plan twin objective — read the stem carefully.
* Bombay Plan ≠ Dhirubhai Ambani; Gandhian Plan = S.N. Agarwal; Sarvodaya = JP Narayan; NPC = Bose + Nehru.
* Rajiv = early liberalisation phase; 1991 Rao = LPG package.
* NITI ≠ Finance Commission (Art. 280).
* Bihar lowest PCI; Maharashtra largest State economy; Naoroji first estimate / V.K.R.V. Rao scientific / Mahalanobis 1949 committee.
* SDG nodal = **NITI**; Fiscal Health Index 2025 top = **Odisha**; UP FHI = **45.9**.
