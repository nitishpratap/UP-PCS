## 📖 Core Thematic Revision (PCS Matrix Complete Notes)

- Bishnoi Movement (1730s - Rajasthan): Led by Amrita Devi Bishnoi; 363 Bishnois sacrificed their
- lives to protect Khejri trees (खेजड़ी वृक्षिों की रक्षिा).
- Chipko Movement (1973 - Uttarakhand): Forest conservation movement where villagers hugged
- trees; led by Sunderlal Bahuguna, Chandi Prasad Bhatt, and Gaura Devi (चिपको आंदोलन).
- Appiko Movement (1983 - Karnataka): Led by Pandurang Hegde; southern counterpart of the
- Chipko movement ("Appiko" means to hug in Kannada).
- Narmada Bachao Andolan (1985): Social movement against large dams along the Narmada
- River; led by Medha Patkar and Baba Amte (नमर्पदा बचाओ आंदोलन).
- Silent Valley Movement (1970s - Kerala): Movement to save the Silent Valley tropical evergreen
- forest from being submerged by a hydroelectric project on the Kunthipuzha River.
