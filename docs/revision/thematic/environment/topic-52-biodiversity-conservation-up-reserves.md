## 📖 Core Thematic Revision (PCS Matrix Complete Notes)

### Protected Area Framework (सिंरक्षिण के तिरीके):
- In-situ Conservation (स्व-स्थिाने संरक्षिण - inside natural habitat): National Parks, Wildlife Sanctuaries,
- Biosphere Reserves, Sacred Groves (पवित्रि उपवन).
- Ex-situ Conservation (बाह्य-स्थिाने संरक्षिण - outside natural habitat): Zoological Parks, Botanical
- Gardens, Seed Banks, Cryopreservation (निम्नताप संरक्षिण), Gene Banks.

### Uttar Pradesh Protected Areas (उत्तर प्रदे: के प्रमुख सिंरɣक्षिति क्षिेत्र):
- National Park: Dudhwa National Park (Lakhimpur Kheri - दुधवा राष्ट्रीय उद्यान).
- Tiger Reserves (4):
- Dudhwa Tiger Reserve
- Pilibhit Tiger Reserve
- Amangarh Tiger Reserve (Buffer zone of Jim Corbett)
- Ranipur Tiger Reserve (Chitrakoot)
- Bird Sanctuaries (पक्षिी वििहार):
- Nawabganj: Unnao (Renamed Chandra Shekhar Azad Bird Sanctuary)
- Samaspur: Rae Bareli
- Sandi: Hardoi
- Bakhira: Sant Kabir Nagar
- khla: Gautam Buddha Nagar
- Patna Bird Sanctuary: Etah (Smallest bird sanctuary in UP - उत्तर प्रदेश का सबसे छोटा पक्षिी विहार)
- Wildlife Sanctuary (वन्यजीव अभयारण्य): Chandraprabha Wildlife Sanctuary (Chandauli - Oldest in UP,
- established in 1957).
