## 📖 Core Thematic Revision (PCS Matrix Complete Notes)

- Ramsar Convention (1971): Intergovernmental treaty for the conservation and wise use of
- Wetlands (आद्रर्पभूमि सम्मेलन).
- CITES (1973): Convention on International Trade in Endangered Species of Wild Fauna and Flora
- (लुप्तप्राय प्रजातियों के अंतरार्पष्ट्रीय व्यापार पर कन्वेंशन).
- Bonn Convention (1979/CMS): Convention on the Conservation of Migratory Species of Wild
- Animals (प्रवासी प्रजातियों के संरक्षिण हेतु बॉन कन्वेंशन).
- Vienna Convention (1985) & Montreal Protocol (1987): Protection of the Ozone Layer; Montreal
- Protocol regulates Ozone Depleting Substances (ODS) (ओजोन परत के संरक्षिण हेतु मोंटि्रयल प्रोटोकॉल).
- Rio Earth Summit (1992 Outcomes):
- UNCBD: Convention on Biological Diversity
- UNFCCC: United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change
- UNCCD: United Nations Convention to Combat Desertification
- Agenda 21: Blueprint for sustainable development
- Kyoto Protocol (1997): International treaty to reduce Greenhouse Gas (GHG) emissions;
- operationalizes UNFCCC (कियोटो प्रोटोकॉल).
- Cartagena Protocol (2000): Biosafety protocol regulating the movement of Living Modified
- Organisms (LMOs).
- Nagoya Protocol (2010): Focuses on Access and Benefit Sharing (ABS) of genetic resources
- (आनुवंशिक संसाधनों का न्यायसंगत बंटवारा).
- Minamata Convention (2013): Global treaty to protect human health and the environment from
- adverse effects of Mercury (पारा / मरकरी).
- Rotterdam & Stockholm Conventions:
- Rotterdam: Prior Informed Consent procedure for Hazardous Chemicals (खतरनाक रसायन).
- Stockholm: Eliminating or restricting Persistent Organic Pollutants (POPs) (स्थिायी काबर्पनिक प्रदूषक).
