## 📖 Core Thematic Revision (PCS Matrix Complete Notes)

- Acid Rain (अम्ल वर्षा): Caused by atmospheric emissions of Sulfur Dioxide (SO₂) and Nitrogen
- Oxides (NOₓ), which react with water molecules to produce Sulfuric Acid (H₂SO₄) and Nitric Acid
- (HNO₃).
- Photochemical Smog / Los Angeles Smog (प्रकाश-रासायनिक स्मॉग): Formed in warm, sunny climates;
- primary constituents include Ground-level Ozone (O₃), Peroxyacetyl Nitrate (PAN), and Nitrogen
- Oxides (NOₓ).
- zone Depleting Substances - ODS (ओजोन क्षियकारी पदाथर्प): Chlorofluorocarbons (CFCs), Halons, Carbon
- Tetrachloride (CCl₄), Methyl Chloroform.
- Kyoto Protocol Greenhouse Gases (6 GHGs):
1. Carbon Dioxide (CO₂)
2. Methane (CH₄)
- 3. Nitrous Oxide (N₂O)
- 4. Hydrofluorocarbons (HFCs)
- 5. Perfluorocarbons (PFCs)
- 6. Sulfur Hexafluoride (SF₆)
