## 📖 Core Thematic Revision (PCS Matrix Complete Notes)

- Acts & Legislation in Chronological Order (पयार्शविरण सिंरक्षिण कानून एविं नीतिियाँ):
- Forest Act (1927): Consolidated laws relating to forests, transit of forest produce, and duty
- leviable on timber (भारतीय वन अधिनियम).
- Wildlife Protection Act (1972): Protection of wild animals, birds, and plants (वन्यजीव संरक्षिण अधिनियम
- schedules modified in the 2022 amendment).
- Water (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act (1974): Established pollution control boards to
- maintain or restore water cleanliness (जल प्रदूषण निवारण एवं नियंत्रिण अधिनियम).
- Forest (Conservation) Act (1980): Restricts the de-reservation of forests or use of forest land
- for non-forest purposes (वन संरक्षिण अधिनियम).
- Air (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act (1981): Provides for the prevention, control, and
- abatement of air pollution (वायु प्रदूषण निवारण एवं नियंत्रिण अधिनियम).
- Environment (Protection) Act (1986): Enacted under Article 253 following the Bhopal Gas
- Tragedy; widely known as the "Umbrella Act" (छाता विधान / पयार्पवरण संरक्षिण अधिनियम).
- National Biodiversity Act (2002): Provides for conservation of biological diversity and fair/
- equitable sharing of benefits (जैव विविधता अधिनियम).
- National Green Tribunal (NGT) Act (2010): Established NGT for speedy disposal of environmental
- protection and forest conservation cases (राष्ट्रीय हरित अधिकरण).
