## 📖 Thematic Revision Summary (Reformatted from PDF)

### Major Environmental Movements in India

| Movement | Year | Location | Leader | Focus |
|---------|------|---------|--------|-------|
| Bishnoi Movement | 1730s | Rajasthan | Amrita Devi Bishnoi | 363 Bishnois sacrificed lives to protect Khejri trees |
| Chipko Movement | 1973 | Uttarakhand (Chamoli) | Sunderlal Bahuguna, Chandi Prasad Bhatt, Gaura Devi | Forest conservation — villagers hugged trees |
| Appiko Movement | 1983 | Karnataka | Pandurang Hegde | Southern counterpart of Chipko ("Appiko" = hug in Kannada) |
| Narmada Bachao Andolan | 1985 | Madhya Pradesh/Gujarat | Medha Patkar, Baba Amte | Against large dams (Sardar Sarovar) on Narmada |
| Silent Valley Movement | 1970s | Kerala | — | Protect Silent Valley tropical rainforest from hydroelectric project on Kunthipuzha river |
| Save the Western Ghats March | 1987 | Maharashtra/Kerala | — | — |
| Jungle Bachao Andolan | 1982 | Jharkhand | — | Against commercial forestry in tribal areas |

**Key Environmental Awards:**
- **Ramon Magsaysay Award:** Chandi Prasad Bhatt (Chipko) — 1982
- **Indira Gandhi Prize for Peace:** Many environmentalists

**Global Environmental Leaders:**
- Wangari Maathai: Green Belt Movement (Kenya) — Nobel Peace Prize 2004
- Greta Thunberg: Climate activist (Sweden)
- Al Gore: Climate change advocacy — Nobel Peace Prize 2007

**Examiner Traps:**
- Chipko Movement = 1973 Uttarakhand (NOT Rajasthan)
- Bishnoi Movement = Rajasthan (Khejri trees — State tree of Rajasthan)
- Appiko = Karnataka (Kannada word for "hug")
- Medha Patkar = Narmada Bachao (NOT Chipko)

## ❓ Previous Years Questions (UPPCS Prelims)


**Q1. UPPCS Prelims 2025, Q122**

Appiko Movement is associated with:

1. Uttarakhand
2. Uttar Pradesh
3. Kerala
4. Karnataka

A. 1 and 2

B. Only 4

C. 3 and 4

D. Only 1

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Logic:** Appiko state trap — Karnataka only; UK/UP = Chipko; Kerala = Silent Valley.

**Ans: B.** Appiko (1983, Sirsi/Uttara Kannada) is **Karnataka only**. **Trap:** Pairing Appiko with Garhwal because both used tree-embrace methods.

</details>

**Q2. UPSC Prelims 2012 — pattern**

Chipko Movement and Dandi March are both examples of:

A. Passive resistance / non-violent protest

B. Armed rebellion against the State

C. International treaty negotiations

D. Judicial public-interest litigation only

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Logic:** Chipko and Dandi March grouped as non-violent passive resistance in UPSC stems.

**Ans: A.** Chipko villagers hugged trees; Dandi March broke the salt law — both are classic **passive resistance**. **Trap:** Calling Chipko armed or purely legal petitioning.

</details>

**Q3. UPPCS / UPSC pattern**

Chipko Movement began in:

A. 1973 at Reni, Garhwal Himalaya (then Uttar Pradesh, now Uttarakhand)

B. 1983 in Sirsi, Karnataka

C. 1730 at Khejarli, Rajasthan

D. 1970s in Silent Valley, Kerala

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Logic:** Chipko origin — 1973 Reni, Garhwal; Gaura Devi and Bahuguna are anchor names.

**Ans: A.** Chipko = **1973**, Reni, leaders include **Gaura Devi**, **Bahuguna**, **Bhatt**. **Trap:** 1983 Appiko year or 1730 Bishnoi date applied to Chipko.

</details>
