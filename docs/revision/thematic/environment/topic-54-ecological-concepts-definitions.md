## 📖 Core Thematic Revision (PCS Matrix Complete Notes)

- Ecotone (संक्रमिका): Transition zone between two distinct biological communities (e.g.,
- Mangroves, Estuaries, Grasslands); displays high species diversity known as the Edge Effect (कोर
- प्रभाव).
- Ecological Niche (पारि्स्थितिक निकेत): The specific functional role and position occupied by a species
- within its ecosystem.
- Ecological Footprint (पारि्स्थितिक पदचिह्न): Measures human demand on Earth's ecosystems (Concept
- developed by William Rees and Mathis Wackernagel).
- Eutrophication (सुपोषण): Nutrient enrichment of water bodies (mainly Nitrogen & Phosphorus)
- causing Algal Blooms, leading to hypoxia (depleted Dissolved Oxygen / DO) and death of
- aquatic life.

### Bioaccumulation vs. Biomagnification:
- Bioaccumulation (जैव-संचयन): Increase in concentration of a pollutant within a single organism
- over time.
- Biomagnification (जैव-आवधर्पन): Increase in concentration of toxic chemicals (e.g., DDT, Mercury) as
- they move up through successive trophic levels in a food chain.

### Ecological Pyramids (पारि्स्थतििक पिरामिड):
- Pyramid of Numbers & Biomass: Can be upright or inverted (e.g., inverted biomass pyramid in
- aquatic ecosystems - जलीय पारि्स्थितिकी तंत्रि में जैवभार का उल्टा पिरामिड).
- Pyramid of Energy: Always upright (ऊजार्प का पिरामिड सदैव सीधा होता है - governed by Lindeman's 10%
- Law).
