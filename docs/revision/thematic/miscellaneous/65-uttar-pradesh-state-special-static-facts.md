## 📖 Thematic Revision Summary (Reformatted from PDF)

### UP — Geography & Boundaries

- Area: 2,40,928 sq km (4th largest state)
- Bordering States & UTs: 8 States (Himachal Pradesh, Haryana, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, Bihar, Uttarakhand) + 1 UT (Delhi) + 1 Country (Nepal)
- Southernmost district: Sonbhadra (borders 4 states: MP, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, Bihar)

**Extreme Points:**
- Westernmost: Shamli
- Easternmost: Ballia
- Northernmost: Saharanpur
- Southernmost: Sonbhadra

### State Symbols

| Symbol | Name |
|--------|------|
| State Animal | Swamp Deer / Barasingha (बारहसिंगा) |
| State Bird | Sarus Crane (सारस / क्रौंच) |
| State Tree | Ashok Tree (अशोक) |
| State Flower | Palash / Flame of the Forest (पलाश / ढाक) |
| State Fish | Chitala (चितला) |
| State Aquatic Animal | Gangetic Dolphin |
| State Butterfly | Not officially declared |

### Key UP Facts

- Capital: Lucknow
- No. of Districts: 75
- No. of Divisions: 18
- No. of Lok Sabha seats: 80 (highest in India)
- No. of Rajya Sabha seats: 31
- No. of MLAs (Vidhan Sabha): 403 seats
- No. of Vidhan Parishad: 100 seats (one of 6 states with Legislative Council)

**Examiner Traps:**
- UP has 75 districts (NOT 72 or 76)
- UP has 80 Lok Sabha seats (most in India)
- Sarus Crane = State bird; NOT the peacock (National bird)
- Barasingha = 12-antlered deer; found in Dudhwa NP

| **Plains-Origin River**: **Gomti** (Gomat Taal, Pilibhit) | **Himalayan-Origin River**: **Ramganga / Ganga / Yamuna / Sarda** | **Trap**: Gomti does NOT originate in the Himalayas; it originates from a low-lying lake in Pilibhit. |
| **Sonbhadra Borders (4 States)**: MP, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, Bihar | **Saharanpur Borders (3 States)**: Uttarakhand, Himachal Pradesh, Haryana | **Trap**: Sonbhadra touches 4 states; Saharanpur touches 3 states. |
| **Bhabhar Soil**: Porous boulders, uncultivable, streams sink underground | **Terai Soil**: Waterlogged silt, highly fertile for sugarcane, streams re-emerge | **Trap**: Bhabhar is dry on surface with underground flow; Terai is marshy and swampy. |
| **Mar Soil (Bundelkhand)**: Black fertile clayey soil (Black cotton type) | **Rakar Soil (Bundelkhand)**: Reddish, coarse gravelly soil on rocky slopes | **Trap**: Mar is fertile and retains moisture; Rakar is highly eroded and nutrient-poor. |
| **Bakhira Wetland (Ramsar)**: Located in **Sant Kabir Nagar** | **Suraha Taal (Oxbow Lake)**: Located in **Ballia** | **Trap**: Bakhira = Sant Kabir Nagar; Suraha Taal = Ballia; Keetham/Sur Sarovar = Agra. |
