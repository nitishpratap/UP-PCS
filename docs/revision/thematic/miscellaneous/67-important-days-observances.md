## 📖 Thematic Revision Summary (Reformatted from PDF)

### Key International Days

| Date | Day |
|------|-----|
| 2nd February | World Wetlands Day (Ramsar Convention — 1971) |
| 21st March | World Forestry Day / International Day of Forests |
| 22nd March | World Water Day |
| 7th April | World Health Day (WHO founded — 1948) |
| 22nd April | Earth Day |
| 22nd May | International Day for Biological Diversity |
| 5th June | World Environment Day (Stockholm Conference — 1972) |
| 11th July | World Population Day |
| 21st June | International Yoga Day (since 2015, UN declared on India's proposal) |
| 29th July | International Tiger Day |
| 16th September | International Day for Preservation of the Ozone Layer (Montreal Protocol — 1987) |
| 16th October | World Food Day (FAO founded — 1945) |
| 31st October | World Cities Day |
| 1st December | World AIDS Day |

### Key National Days (India)

| Date | Day |
|------|-----|
| 26th January | Republic Day |
| 15th August | Independence Day |
| 5th September | Teachers' Day (Dr. Radhakrishnan's birthday) |
| 2nd October | Gandhi Jayanti; International Day of Non-Violence |
| 14th November | Children's Day (Nehru's birthday) |
| 23rd March | Martyrs' Day / Bhagat Singh, Rajguru, Sukhdev hanged (1931) |

**Examiner Traps:**
- World Environment Day = 5 June (NOT 22 April which is Earth Day)
- Yoga Day = 21 June (not 5 June)
- World Food Day = 16 October (FAO founding day)
- Teachers Day = 5 September in India; World Teachers Day = 5 October

### ★ UPPCS Revision Sheet — Lucent-style (no repetition · Practice ≥25)
- Annual themes for WED / Earth Day / IDF refresh; calendar dates stay fixed (WED = **5 June**).
- Do not invent UP-only international day dates.
| A | B | Distinguishing Fact / Correct Match | Hindi Terminology |
|---|---|-------------------------------------|-------------------|
| **World Wetlands Day** | **World Wildlife Day** | **2 February** (signing of Ramsar Convention 1971) vs **3 March** (adoption of CITES 1973) | विश्व आर्द्रभूमि दिवस (2 फरवरी) / विश्व वन्यजीव दिवस (3 मार्च) |
| **World Forestry Day** | **World Water Day** | **21 March** (UN International Day of Forests) vs **22 March** (UN World Water Day) | विश्व वानिकी दिवस (21 मार्च) / विश्व जल दिवस (22 मार्च) |
| **World Earth Day** | **World Environment Day** | **22 April** (Gaylord Nelson, 1970) vs **5 June** (Stockholm Conference opening, 1972) | पृथ्वी दिवस (22 अप्रैल) / विश्व पर्यावरण दिवस (5 जून) |
| **International Biodiversity Day** | **World Earth Day** | **22 May** (Nairobi agreed text of CBD 1992) vs **22 April** (Earth Day) | जैव विविधता दिवस (22 मई) / पृथ्वी दिवस (22 अप्रैल) |
| **World Ozone Day** | **World Environment Day** | **16 September** (signing of Montreal Protocol 1987) vs **5 June** (Stockholm UNEP foundation) | विश्व ओजोन दिवस (16 सितंबर) / पर्यावरण दिवस (5 जून) |
| **Earth Day** | **Earth Hour** | Fixed annual civic observance on **22 April** vs WWF voluntary **last Saturday of March** 1-hour lights-off event | पृथ्वी दिवस (22 अप्रैल) / अर्थ आवर (मार्च अंतिम शनिवार) |
| **World Soil Day** | **World Day to Combat Desertification** | **5 December** (FAO celebration of King Bhumibol's birthday) vs **17 June** (UNCCD adoption anniversary) | विश्व मृदा दिवस (5 दिसंबर) / मरुस्थलीकरण रोकथाम दिवस (17 जून) |

## ❓ Previous Years Questions (UPPCS Prelims)


**Q1. UPPCS Prelims 2025, Q62**

Which of the following pair(s) is/are **NOT** correctly matched?

1. International Ozone Day — 16 September
2. World Environment Day — 5 June
3. World Wildlife Day — 22 March

A. 1 and 2

B. Only 3

C. 2 and 3

D. Only 1

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Logic:** Wildlife Day on 22 March is the classic wrong pair — correct date is 3 March (CITES); Water Day is 22 March.

**Ans: B.** Pairs 1 and 2 are correct. **World Wildlife Day = 3 March** (CITES 1973); **22 March = World Water Day**. **Trap:** Same “22” number chain — Wildlife vs Water vs Biodiversity (22 May).

</details>

---

**Q2. UPPCS Prelims 2023, Q147**

World Wetlands Day is observed on—

A. 10 January

B. 15 January

C. 1 February

D. 2 February

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Logic:** Ramsar adoption date — World Wetlands Day is 2 February only.

**Ans: D.** Marks Ramsar Convention adoption at Ramsar, Iran (**2 February 1971**). **Trap:** 2 March, 3 March (Wildlife), or 22 March (Water).

</details>

---

**Q3. UPPCS Prelims 2022, Q82**

On which date is Ozone Day observed?

A. October 2

B. September 16

C. April 29

D. January 30

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Logic:** Ozone Day = Montreal signing date, 16 September — not WED 5 June.

**Ans: B.** **16 September** — Montreal Protocol signed **1987**; entered into force **1 January 1989**. **Trap:** Gandhi Jayanti (2 Oct), Earth Day (22 Apr), or WED (5 Jun).

</details>
