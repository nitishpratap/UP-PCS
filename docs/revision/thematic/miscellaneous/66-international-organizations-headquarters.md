## 📖 Thematic Revision Summary (Reformatted from PDF)

### Major International Organizations

| City | Organizations |
|------|--------------|
| Geneva, Switzerland | WHO, ILO, WTO, WMO, WIPO, UNHCR, International Red Cross (ICRC) |
| Paris, France | UNESCO, OECD, FATF (Financial Action Task Force), IEA |
| Vienna, Austria | IAEA (Atomic Energy), OPEC, UNIDO, OSCE |
| Washington D.C., USA | IMF, World Bank Group, OAS |
| New York, USA | UN Headquarters, UNICEF, UNDP, UNFPA |
| The Hague, Netherlands | ICJ (International Court of Justice), ICC (International Criminal Court) |
| Nairobi, Kenya | UNEP, UN-Habitat |
| Rome, Italy | FAO, WFP (World Food Programme), IFAD |
| Brussels, Belgium | NATO, EU Headquarters |
| London, UK | Commonwealth Secretariat, IMO (International Maritime Organization) |
| Montreal, Canada | ICAO (International Civil Aviation Organization) |
| Basel, Switzerland | BIS (Bank for International Settlements) |

**Key Distinctions:**
- **ICJ** (International Court of Justice) = disputes between STATES; judges elected
- **ICC** (International Criminal Court) = prosecutes INDIVIDUALS for war crimes/crimes against humanity

**Examiner Traps:**
- OPEC HQ = Vienna (Austria), NOT Riyadh or Geneva
- WTO = Geneva (NOT New York or Washington)
- FAO = Rome (food security)
- IAEA = Vienna (NOT Geneva)
- WHO = Geneva
- ICJ and ICC are both in The Hague but are DIFFERENT bodies

**Q1. UPPCS Prelims 2024, Q6**

Match List-I (Organisation) with List-II (Report):

| List-I | List-II |
| --- | --- |
| 1. UNDP | A. Emissions Gap Report |
| 2. IMF | B. Human Development Report |
| 3. UNEP | C. World Economic Outlook |
| 4. UNCTAD | D. World Investment Report |

*Row order is not the answer code.*

A. 3 1 4 2
B. 1 3 2 4
C. 2 4 1 3
D. 4 2 3 1

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Logic:** Org–report match — Emissions Gap Report is UNEP’s; HDR is UNDP’s; WEO is IMF’s; WIR is UNCTAD’s.

**Ans: A (3 1 4 2).** HDR→**UNDP**; WEO→**IMF**; WIR→**UNCTAD**; Emissions Gap→**UNEP**. **Trap:** Crediting IPCC or UNDP with Emissions Gap Report.

</details>

**Q2. UPPCS Prelims 2025, Q132**

Consider the following statements:

1. UNDP published the first Human Development Report in 1990 with the HDI.
2. The first HDI team was led by Mahbub-ul-Haq and S. Priesner.

A. Only 2
B. Neither
C. Both
D. Only 1

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Logic:** HDR/HDI started 1990 under UNDP with Mahbub ul Haq and Amartya Sen — not “Priesner-led” team.

**Ans: D (Only 1).** Stmt 1 true; stmt 2 false — **Haq–Sen** lineage, not Priesner. **Trap:** Accepting any plausible Western name on stmt 2.

</details>

**Q3. UPPCS Prelims 2025, Q145**

With reference to the Global Environment Facility (GEF):

1. It was established in 1991.
2. Only developed countries are donors to the GEF.

A. Only 2
B. Neither
C. Both
D. Only 1

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Logic:** GEF established 1991; donors are not “developed countries only.”

**Ans: D (Only 1).** **1991** true; donor statement **false** — developing countries also contribute. **Trap:** Treating GEF as Annex-I-only club.

</details>
