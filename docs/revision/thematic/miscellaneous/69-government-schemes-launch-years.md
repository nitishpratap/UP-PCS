## 📖 Thematic Revision Summary (Reformatted from PDF)

### Major Government Schemes & Launch Years

| Scheme | Year | Full Form / Purpose |
|--------|------|---------------------|
| MGNREGA | 2005 (renamed MGNREGA 2009) | Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act — 100 days guaranteed work |
| NRLM | 2011 | National Rural Livelihood Mission — rural poverty alleviation |
| PMJDY | 2014 | Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana — financial inclusion; zero balance account |
| Make in India | 2014 | Manufacturing promotion |
| Digital India | 2015 | Digital infrastructure and services |
| PMUY | 2016 | Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana — LPG to BPL women; launched from Ballia, UP |
| Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY) | 2018 | ₹5 lakh health insurance; launched from Ranchi, Jharkhand |
| PM-KISAN | 2019 | ₹6000/year to farmers; launched from Gorakhpur, UP |
| PM-PRANAM | 2023 | Reducing chemical fertiliser use; promoting alternatives |
| PM-Vishwakarma | 2023 | Financial and skill support to traditional artisans/craftsmen |

**Key Scheme Facts:**
- MGNREGA = covers rural areas; 100 days guaranteed; registration within 15 days; work within 5 km
- Ayushman Bharat = also includes Health and Wellness Centres (HWCs)
- PM-KISAN = 3 installments of ₹2000 each (total ₹6000/year)

**UP-Specific Schemes:**
- ODOP (One District One Product) — unique product per district
- Mission Shakti — women's safety (UP)
- Mukhyamantri Samagra Samridhi Yojana

**Examiner Traps:**
- PMJDY launched = 28 August 2014 (Independence Day week)
- Ayushman Bharat launched from RANCHI (Jharkhand), NOT Delhi
- PMUY (Ujjwala) launched from BALLIA, UP
- PM-KISAN launched from GORAKHPUR, UP

## ❓ Previous Years Questions (UPPCS Prelims)


**Q1. UPPCS (Pre) 2024**
Which of the following bodies recommends the Minimum Support Prices (MSP) for agricultural crops to the Government of India?

A. Commission for Agricultural Costs and Prices (CACP)
B. NITI Aayog
C. Food Corporation of India (FCI)
D. Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR)

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Logic:** FCI procures; ICAR does research; NITI Aayog is not the MSP recommender.

**Ans: A.** CACP is the advisory recommender; CCEA gives final approval.

</details>

---

**Q2. UPPCS (Pre) 2021**
The National Food Security Act (NFSA) was enacted in which year in India?

A. 2013
B. 2011
C. 2015
D. 2009

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Logic:** Do not confuse with TPDS 1997 or AAY 2000.

**Ans: A.** NFSA enactment date is 10 September 2013 (deemed effective 5 July 2013).

</details>
