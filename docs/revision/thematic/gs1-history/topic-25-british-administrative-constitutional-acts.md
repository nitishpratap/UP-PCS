## 📖 Core Thematic Revision (PCS Matrix Complete Notes)

### Regulating Act 1773 & Pitt's India Act 1784:
- **1773:** Governor of Bengal made Governor-General of Bengal; Supreme Court at Calcutta (1774).
- **1784:** Dual system of administration (द्वैध शासन) via Board of Control & Court of Directors.

### Charter Acts (1813, 1833, 1853):
- **1813:** Abolished EIC monopoly except tea & China trade; ₹1 Lakh for education.
- **1833:** Governor-General of Bengal made Governor-General of India (Bentinck); end to EIC commercial activities.
- **1853:** Introduced open competitive examinations (खुली प्रतियोगी परीक्षा) for Civil Services.

### GOI Act 1919 & GOI Act 1935:
- **1919 (Montagu-Chelmsford):** Dyarchy in Provinces (प्रांतों में द्वैध शासन); Bicameral legislature at Center.
- **1935:** Provincial Autonomy (प्रांतीय स्वायत्तता); Proposed All-India Federation and Dyarchy at Center.
