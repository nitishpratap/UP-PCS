## 📖 Thematic Revision Summary (Reformatted from PDF)

### Classical Dance Forms of India (8 Recognised by Sangeet Natak Akademi)

| Dance Form | State of Origin | Key Feature |
|-----------|----------------|-------------|
| Bharatanatyam | Tamil Nadu | Oldest; Natya Shastra based; solo; temple dance |
| Kathak | UP (Lucknow & Jaipur gharanas) | Storytelling; Mughal influence; footwork (tatkar) |
| Kuchipudi | Andhra Pradesh | Narrative dance-drama; uses brass plate (tarangam) |
| Odissi | Odisha | Tribhanga (three-bend) posture; temple tradition |
| Kathakali | Kerala | Dramatic; elaborate makeup; male performers |
| Mohiniyattam | Kerala | Graceful feminine style; white & gold costume |
| Manipuri | Manipur | Devotional; Ras Lila tradition |
| Sattriya | Assam | Introduced by Shankaradeva (15th CE); monastery dance |

**Other Important Dance Forms:**
- Yakshagana → Karnataka
- Theyyam → Kerala (ritual, not classical)
- Chhau → West Bengal/Jharkhand/Odisha (3 variants — folk/semi-classical)

**Natya Shastra:** Written by Bharata Muni — foundational text of Indian performing arts

**Examiner Traps:**
- Sattriya = Assam (8th classical form, recognized 2000)
- Kathakali is known for makeup (NOT footwork; Kathak is footwork)
- Mohiniyattam ≠ Manipuri — both are feminine but from different states
- Odissi's Tribhanga = three bends at neck, torso, knee

- **Kalbelia** (कालबेलिया) of Rajasthan entered UNESCO ICH in **2010**.

- **Sankirtana** (संकीर्तन) of Manipur entered UNESCO ICH in **2013**. It is ritual music and dance. It is not a ninth classical dance.

## ❓ Previous Years Questions (UPPCS Prelims)


**Q. UPSC (Pre) 2014**

With reference to the famous Sattriya dance, consider the following statements:

1. Sattriya is a combination of music, dance and drama.
2. It is a centuries-old living tradition of Vaishnavites of Assam.
3. It is performed only during festive occasions.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

A. 1 only
B. 1 and 2 only
C. 1, 2 and 3
D. 2 and 3 only

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Logic:** Statements 1 and 2 are true: Sattriya mixes music, dance, and drama, and it is a living Vaishnava tradition of Assam. Statement 3 is false: it is also a daily satra ritual, not only a festival item.

**Ans: B.** Only 1 and 2.

</details>

**Q. UPSC (Pre) 2012 (pattern)**

How many of the following pairs are correctly matched?

1. Bharatanatyam — Tamil Nadu
2. Mohiniyattam — Kerala
3. Sattriya — Assam

A. Only one
B. Only two
C. All three
D. None

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Logic:** Each pair is the standard Akademi state. Bharatanatyam is Tamil Nadu. Mohiniyattam is Kerala. Sattriya is Assam. The trap is dropping Sattriya or moving Mohiniyattam out of Kerala.

**Ans: C.** All three pairs are correct.

</details>

**Q1.** With reference to Sangeet Natak Akademi’s classical dances, which of the following statements is/are correct?

1. SNA recognises eight classical dances.
2. Chhau is one of the official SNA eight.
3. Sattriya of Assam was added in 1952 with the founding of SNA.

A. Only 3
B. Only 1 and 3
C. Only 1
D. 1 and 2

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: C.** Only 1 is correct.

**Logic:** Chhau is UNESCO martial/folk and not in the SNA eight. Sattriya was added in 2000, not in 1952.

</details>
