## 📖 Core Thematic Revision (PCS Matrix Complete Notes)

### 1900–1920 Events:
- **Partition of Bengal [1905]:** Implemented by Lord Curzon.
- **Surat Split [1907]:** Moderates (नरम दल) and Extremists (गरम दल).
- **Ghadar Party [1913]:** Lala Hardayal in San Francisco.
- **Komagata Maru [1914]:** Japanese ship denied entry into Canada.
- **Berlin Committee [1915]:** Virendranath Chattopadhyaya.
- **Lucknow Pact [1916]:** Congress factions reunion & Congress-League agreement.
- **Champaran Satyagraha [1917]:** Against Tin-kathia system (तीन-कठिया प्रणाली).

### 1920–1935 Events:
- **Chauri Chaura Incident [1922]:** Withdrawal of Non-Cooperation Movement (असहयोग आंदोलन).
- **Kakori Train Action [1925]:** Train robbery by HRA members (हिंदुस्तान रिपब्लिकन एसोसिएशन).
- **Simon Commission [1927]:** All-White commission (श्वेत आयोग) for constitutional reforms.
- **Nehru Report [1928]:** Drafted by Motilal Nehru demanding Dominion Status (औपनिवेशिक स्वराज्य).
- **Sharda Act [1929]:** Minimum age of marriage: girls 14, boys 18.
- **Central Assembly Bombing [1929]:** Bhagat Singh & Batukeshwar Dutt against anti-labor bills.
- **Chittagong Armoury Raid [1930]:** Led by Surya Sen ('Master Da').
- **Dandi March [1930]:** Launch of Civil Disobedience Movement (सविनय अवज्ञा आंदोलन).
- **Gandhi-Irwin Pact [1931]:** Enabled Congress participation in 2nd RTC.

### 1935–1947 Events:
- **Resignation of Congress Ministries [1939]:** WWII forced entry; 'Deliverance Day' (मुक्ति दिवस).
- **August Offer [1940]:** Proposal by Viceroy Linlithgow offering expanded Executive Council.
- **Individual Satyagraha [1940]:** 1st: Vinoba Bhave, 2nd: Jawaharlal Nehru.
- **Cripps Mission [1942]:** Post-dated cheque (उत्तर-दिनांकित चेक) rejected by leaders.
- **Quit India Resolution [1942]:** Gowalia Tank, Bombay ('Do or Die' call).
- **C.R. Formula [1944]:** Proposed by C. Rajagopalachari for Congress-League deadlock.
- **Desai-Liaquat Pact [1945]:** Draft agreement for interim government (अंतरिम सरकार).
- **Cabinet Mission [1946]:** Proposed Constituent Assembly (संविधान सभा); rejected full Pakistan.
- **Direct Action Day [16 Aug 1946]:** Called by Muslim League.
