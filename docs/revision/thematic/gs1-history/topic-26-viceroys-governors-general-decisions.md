## 📖 Core Thematic Revision (PCS Matrix Complete Notes)

### Viceroys / Governors-General vs. Key Decisions:
- **Warren Hastings (1772–1785):** Abolished Dual System in Bengal; Ring Fence Policy (घेरे की नीति).
- **Lord Wellesley (1798–1805):** Subsidiary Alliance System (सहायक संधि — First: Hyderabad 1798, Awadh 1801).
- **Lord William Bentinck (1828–1835):** Abolition of Sati [1829]; Suppression of Thuggee; English Education Act (1835).
- **Lord Dalhousie (1848–1856):** Doctrine of Lapse (व्यपगत का सिद्धांत); Annexed Awadh on misgovernance (1856); 1st Railway line (1853), Telegraph, Postal Reforms.
- **Lord Ripon (1880–1884):** Repealed Vernacular Press Act (1882); Father of Local Self-Government; Ilbert Bill.
- **Lord Curzon (1899–1905):** Partition of Bengal [1905]; Ancient Monuments Preservation Act (1904).
