## 📖 Thematic Revision Summary (Reformatted from PDF)

### Indian Art & Architecture — Key Styles

**Rock-Cut Architecture:**
| Site | Dynasty | Feature |
|------|---------|---------|
| Ajanta Caves (1–29) | Vakataka/Gupta | Paintings; Buddhist; Maharashtra |
| Ellora Caves (1–34) | Rashtrakuta | Buddhism + Hinduism + Jainism; Kailasa Temple (largest monolithic) |
| Elephanta Caves | Rashtrakuta | Shiva sculptures; Trimurti famous |
| Mahabalipuram | Pallava | Shore Temple; Rathas (7 pagodas) |
| Badami | Chalukya | Cave temples; Agastya lake |

**Temple Architecture Styles:**
| Style | Region | Feature |
|-------|--------|---------|
| Nagara (North Indian) | UP, MP, Odisha | Curvilinear Shikhara (tower); Garbhagriha + Mandapa |
| Dravida (South Indian) | Tamil Nadu | Pyramidal Vimana; large Gopuram (gateway) |
| Vesara (Mixed) | Karnataka | Combination of both |

**Key Temples:**
- Khajuraho (MP) → Chandela dynasty → Nagara style → erotic sculptures
- Brihadisvara Temple (Thanjavur) → Chola dynasty → Dravida style
- Konark Sun Temple (Odisha) → Eastern Ganga dynasty → Kalinga style (Nagara variant)
- Dilwara Temple (Mt. Abu) → Solanki/Chaulukya → Marble Jain temple

**Mughal Architecture:**
- Humayun's Tomb (Delhi) → First Mughal garden tomb; Persian influence
- Taj Mahal → Shah Jahan → white marble; built by Ustad Ahmad Lahori
- Red Fort (Lal Qila) → Shah Jahan → on Yamuna bank, Delhi

**Examiner Traps:**
- Kailasa Temple at Ellora is dedicated to Shiva (not Buddha)
- Ajanta = paintings; Ellora = sculptures + paintings
- Qutub Minar started by Qutbuddin Aibak, completed by Iltutmish

- **Moidams** (मोइदाम) of Charaideo, Assam, entered the World Heritage list in **2024** as India’s **43rd** property.
- **Maratha (मराठा) Military Landscapes** entered in **2025** as the **44th**. The serial covers **12** forts: eleven in Maharashtra and **Gingee** in Tamil Nadu (तमिलनाडु).
- **Sarnath** (सारनाथ) was inscribed in **July 2026** as India’s **45th** property and Uttar Pradesh (उत्तर प्रदेश)’s **fourth**.
- **Deepavali** (दीपावली) entered UNESCO ICH on **10 December 2025** as India’s **16th** element, at 20.COM at the Red Fort (लाल किला), Delhi.
| Theory | Core claim |
| **Idealistic Theory** | Culture grows from **divine inspiration**, intuition, or higher ideas perceived by individuals or groups. |
| **Materialistic Theory** | **Physical environment**, climate, and **means of production** shape culture first. |
| A | B | Correct | Hindi |
| Tangible heritage | Intangible heritage | Physical monument/site vs living practice/skill | भौतिक / अभौतिक विरासत |

## ❓ Previous Years Questions (UPPCS Prelims)


**Q. UKPCS Prelims 2025, Q52**

Which of the following UNESCO World Heritage Sites is/are located in Uttarakhand (उत्तराखंड)?

A. Valley of Flowers (फूलों की घाटी)
B. Kaziranga (काजीरंगा) National Park
C. Mountain Railways of India
D. Nanda Devi (नंदा देवी) National Park

A. A only
B. A and B only
C. A, B and C only
D. A and D only

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Logic:** Kaziranga and Mountain Railways are WHS sites outside Uttarakhand.

**Ans: D (Series B provisional key).** Valley of Flowers and Nanda Devi form one WHS property in Uttarakhand. Kaziranga is in Assam; Mountain Railways are not in UK.

</details>
