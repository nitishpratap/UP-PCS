## 📖 Thematic Revision Summary (Reformatted from PDF)

### Key World Revolutions

**American Revolution (1776):**
- Cause: "No taxation without representation"; Tea Party (Boston 1773)
- Declaration of Independence: 4 July 1776 (Thomas Jefferson authored)
- Key principle: Liberty, equality, social contract (Rousseau/Locke influence)

**French Revolution (1789):**
- Cause: Absolute monarchy, inequality (Estates), financial crisis
- **Storming of Bastille:** 14 July 1789 (French National Day)
- Slogan: **Liberté, Égalité, Fraternité** (Liberty, Equality, Fraternity)
- Napoleon Bonaparte rose from revolutionary chaos → Emperor 1804
- Abolished feudalism; Declaration of Rights of Man

**Russian Revolution (1917):**
- **February Revolution:** Tsar Nicholas II abdicated; Provisional Government
- **October Revolution (Bolshevik):** Lenin led; "Peace, Land, Bread"
- **1918:** Treaty of Brest-Litovsk (Russia exited WWI)
- USSR formed: 1922

**Industrial Revolution (Britain, 1760s):**
- Steam engine: James Watt (1769)
- Started in textile industry (spinning jenny — Hargreaves; water frame — Arkwright)
- Led to urbanisation, capitalism, colonialism

**Examiner Traps:**
- French Revolution = 1789 NOT 1776 (1776 = American)
- Bastille = prison; its storming symbolises French Revolution
- Lenin's slogan = "Peace, Land, Bread" (for peasants/workers)
- Napoleon = NOT a direct product of French Revolution per se; rose after the Directory
