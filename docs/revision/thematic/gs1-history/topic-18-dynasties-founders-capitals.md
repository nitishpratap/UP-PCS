## 📖 Core Thematic Revision (PCS Matrix Complete Notes)

### Ancient Dynasties:
- **Haryanka:** Founder: Bimbisara $\rightarrow$ Capital: Rajagriha / Girivraja
- **Shishunaga:** Founder: Shishunaga $\rightarrow$ Capital: Vaishali / Rajagriha
- **Nanda:** Founder: Mahapadma Nanda $\rightarrow$ Capital: Pataliputra
- **Gupta:** Founder: Sri Gupta (Real founder: Chandragupta I) $\rightarrow$ Capital: Pataliputra
- **Vardhana / Pushyabhuti:** Founder: Pushyabhuti $\rightarrow$ Capital: Thanesar (later Kannauj)

### Medieval Dynasties:
- **Slave / Mamluk:** Founder: Qutb-ud-din Aibak $\rightarrow$ Capital: Lahore / Delhi
- **Khalji:** Founder: Jalaluddin Khalji $\rightarrow$ Capital: Delhi
- **Tughlaq:** Founder: Ghiyasuddin Tughlaq $\rightarrow$ Capital: Delhi / Tughlaqabad
- **Lodi:** Founder: Bahlul Lodi $\rightarrow$ Capital: Delhi (shifted to Agra by Sikandar Lodi 1504)
- **Sharqi:** Founder: Malik Sarwar $\rightarrow$ Capital: Jaunpur (Shiraz-i-Hind — भारत का शिराज़)
- **Bahmani Kingdom:** Founder: Alauddin Hasan Gangu $\rightarrow$ Capital: Gulbarga (later Bidar)
