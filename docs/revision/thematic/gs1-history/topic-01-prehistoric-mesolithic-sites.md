## 📖 Core Thematic Revision (PCS Matrix Complete Notes)

### Mesolithic Sites in UP:
- **Damdama:** Multiple skeletons (कंकाल) in a single grave (क़ब्र).
- **Mahadaha:** Bone artifacts (हड्डी के उपकरण) and ornaments (आभूषण).
- **Sarai Nahar Rai:** Human skeletons and microliths.

### Palaeolithic & Neolithic Sites:
- **Belan Valley (Prayagraj):** Sites spanning Palaeolithic (पुरापाषाण) to Neolithic (नवपाषाण) periods.
- **Chopani Mando:** Early evidence of pottery (मृद्भांड).
- **Koldihwa & Lahuradewa:** Earliest evidence of rice agriculture (चावल की खेती के प्राचीनतम साक्ष्य).
