## 📖 Thematic Revision Summary (Reformatted from PDF)

### Buddhism

**Gautama Buddha:**
- Born: Lumbini (Nepal), 563 BCE; Real name: Siddhartha
- Enlightenment: Bodh Gaya (under Peepal/Bo tree)
- First Sermon (Dhammachakka Pavattana): Sarnath (Deer Park) — called Dhammachakkappavattana Sutta
- Death (Mahaparinirvana): Kushinagar, 483 BCE

**Four Noble Truths (Char Arya Satya):**
1. There is suffering (Dukkha)
2. Suffering has a cause (Samudaya) — desire/attachment
3. Suffering can end (Nirodha)
4. Path to end suffering (Magga) — Eightfold Path

**Eightfold Path:** Right View, Intention, Speech, Action, Livelihood, Effort, Mindfulness, Concentration

**Buddhist Councils:**
| Council | Location | King | Outcome |
|---------|----------|------|---------|
| 1st (483 BCE) | Rajgriha | Ajatashatru | Compiled Vinaya & Sutta Pitaka |
| 2nd (383 BCE) | Vaishali | Kalasoka | Split: Sthaviravada vs Mahasanghika |
| 3rd (250 BCE) | Pataliputra | Ashoka | Compiled Abhidhamma Pitaka; sent missionaries |
| 4th (1st CE) | Kashmir | Kanishka | Split: Hinayana vs Mahayana |

---

### Jainism

**Mahavira (24th Tirthankara):**
- Born: Vaishali, 540 BCE; Real name: Vardhamana
- Enlightenment: Jrimbhikagrama
- Death: Pavapuri (Bihar), 468 BCE

**Five Vows (Pancha Mahavrata):**
Ahimsa, Satya, Asteya, Brahmacharya, Aparigraha

**Two Sects:**
- **Digambara:** Sky-clad (no clothes); women cannot attain moksha
- **Shvetambara:** White-robed; women can attain moksha

**Examiner Traps:**
- 4th Buddhist Council = Kanishka (Kashmir) — often confused with Kalasoka
- Mahavira's father = Siddhartha (same name as Buddha!), mother = Trishala
- Jain texts called Agamas (compiled at Vallabhi, 5th CE)
