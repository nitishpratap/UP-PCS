## 📖 Thematic Revision Summary (Reformatted from PDF)

### Maratha Empire

**Shivaji Maharaj (1630–1680):**
- Coronation: 1674 at Raigad; adopted title Chhatrapati
- Revenue system: Chauth (1/4 of revenue) & Sardeshmukhi (1/10 extra)
- Navy: First Indian ruler to build a strong navy
- Guerrilla warfare (Ganimi Kava)
- Capital: Raigad

**Peshwas (Prime Ministers):**
| Peshwa | Period | Key Event |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| Balaji Vishwanath | 1713–1720 | First Peshwa; real power established |
| Baji Rao I | 1720–1740 | Greatest military commander; never lost a battle |
| Balaji Baji Rao (Nanasaheb) | 1740–1761 | 3rd Battle of Panipat (1761) |
| Madhav Rao I | 1761–1772 | Revived Maratha power post-Panipat |

**3rd Battle of Panipat (1761):**
- Marathas vs Ahmad Shah Abdali (Durrani/Afghan)
- Marathas defeated — end of Maratha dominance in North India
- Viswas Rao (Peshwa's son) killed

**Anglo-Maratha Wars:**
- 1st (1775–82): Ended with Treaty of Salbai; British gained Salsette
- 2nd (1803–05): British gained Delhi, Agra; Peshwa became subsidiary ally
- 3rd (1817–19): Marathas finally defeated; Peshwaship abolished

**Examiner Traps:**
- Baji Rao I is different from Baji Rao II (last Peshwa; surrendered to British)
- Shivaji's Ashtapradhan (8 ministers): Peshwa was PM, Amatya was Finance

## ❓ Previous Years Questions (UPPCS Prelims)


**Q-GC1. (d) A high official in Punjab province who was very much, discontented with Ibrahim Lodi’s treatment of his tribe, I.A.S. (Pre) 2003**

Alam Khan, one of those who invited Babur to invade
India was :

- (a) An uncle of Ibrahim Lodi and a pretender to the throne of Delhi
- (b) A cousin of Ibrahim Lodi who was ill-treated and expelled from the country
- (c) The father of Dilawar Khan to whom cruel treatment was meted out by Ibrahim Lodi

<details><summary>Show answer</summary>

**Answer: (a)**

**Explanation:**
Alam Khan was the uncle of Ibrahim Lodi, who invited Babur

to invade India. During the fourth Indian campaign of Babur,

the relationship between Ibrahim Lodi and Daulat Khan

became bad. Ibrahim Lodi ordered Daulat Khan to visit his

capital, but Daulat Khan violated his orders and sent his son

Dilawar Khan to Babur with a message to help him dethrone

Ibrahim Lodi and enthrone Alam Khan in place of him. It was

a golden opportunity for Babur as he already received the

invitation of Rana Sanga, the king of Mewar. He was quite

assured that the opportunity to win India had come. 

Indian History General Studies B–275
</details>
