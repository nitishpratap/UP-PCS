## 📖 Thematic Revision Summary (Reformatted from PDF)

### Vedic Period — Key Facts

**Early Vedic Period (1500–1000 BCE):**
- **Political:** Tribal (Jana/Vis); King called Rajan; no standing army
- **Economy:** Pastoral; cattle = wealth (Gavishti = fight for cattle)
- **Society:** Varna based on occupation (not birth yet); women had freedom (Rig Vedic women — Gargi, Maitreyi)
- **Key River:** Saraswati (most sacred); Sindhu

**Four Vedas:**
| Veda | Content | Priestly Function |
|------|---------|------------------|
| Rigveda | Hymns (10,472 mantras, 10 mandalas) | Hotri |
| Samaveda | Musical recitations | Udgatri |
| Yajurveda | Sacrificial rituals | Adhvaryu |
| Atharvaveda | Magic, medicine, domestic rituals | Brahma |

**Later Vedic Period (1000–600 BCE):**
- Shift from pastoral → agricultural economy
- Varna system becomes rigid (birth-based)
- New texts: Brahmanas, Aranyakas, Upanishads (Vedanta)
- **Iron Age:** First use of iron tools in agriculture
- **Mahajanapadas:** 16 kingdoms emerge (Magadha, Kosala, Kuru, Panchala...)
- **Key Assembly:** Sabha (elite), Samiti (general), Vidatha (military)

**Examiner Traps:**
- Gayatri Mantra is from Rigveda (Mandala 3)
- Atharvaveda deals with magic — often asked as "which Veda deals with medicine"
- Upanishads = Vedanta (end of Vedas), not a separate scripture

Which of the following is a Samhita of Shukla Yajurveda?

A. Vajasanami
B. Maitrayani
C. Taittiriya

**Ans: A** — Vajasaneyi (paper spelling Vajasanami).
**B/C/D** = Krishna (Black) Yajurveda Samhitas. **Krishna** = Black school, not the deity.
