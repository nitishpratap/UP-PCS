## 📖 Thematic Revision Summary (Reformatted from PDF)

### Indian Languages & Literatures

**Constitutional Status:**
- **8th Schedule:** 22 Officially Recognized Languages
- Originally 14; Sindhi added (1967-21st amendment); Konkani, Manipuri, Nepali added (1992-71st amendment); Bodo, Dogri, Maithili, Santhali added (2003-92nd amendment)

**Classical Language Status (6):**
Tamil, Sanskrit, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Odia

**Key Literary Works:**

| Work | Author | Language | Period |
|------|--------|----------|--------|
| Ramcharitmanas | Tulsidas | Awadhi (Hindi) | 16th CE |
| Kumarsambhavam / Meghadutam | Kalidasa | Sanskrit | Gupta |
| Arthashastra | Kautilya | Sanskrit | Mauryan |
| Ashtadhyayi | Panini | Sanskrit | 4th BCE |
| Ain-i-Akbari | Abul Fazl | Persian | Mughal |
| Gitanjali | Rabindranath Tagore | Bengali | 1910 |
| Godan | Premchand | Hindi | 1936 |
| Mricchakatika | Shudraka | Sanskrit | 3rd CE |

**Key Awards:**
- Jnanpith Award — highest literary award in India; given for lifetime contribution in Indian languages
- Sahitya Akademi Award — given for works in 24 languages (22 official + English + Rajasthani)

**Examiner Traps:**
- Tamil is the oldest classical language (2000+ years of literature)
- Odia got Classical Language status in 2014 (6th)
- Tagore won Nobel Prize (1913) for Gitanjali — English translation by himself
- Premchand's Godan = last completed novel; Mangalsutra = unfinished

Assertion (A): Babur wrote Tuzk-e-Babri in Chagatai Turki.
Reason (R): Turki was the official language of the Mughal Court.

A. Both (A) and (R) are true, but (R) is not the correct explanation of (A)
B. (A) is false, but (R) is true
C. (A) is true, but (R) is false
