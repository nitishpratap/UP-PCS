## 📖 Core Thematic Revision (PCS Matrix Complete Notes)

### Land & Revenue Terms:
- **Iqta:** Land grant system under Delhi Sultanate (राजस्व आवंटन प्रणाली).
- **Zabt / Dahsala:** Revenue assessment by Akbar & Todar Mal (दस वर्षीय राजस्व प्रणाली).
- **Mughal Land Classification:** Polaj (Continuously cultivated — सतत खेती योग्य), Parauti (Fallow 1-2 yrs — परती), Chachar (Fallow 3-4 yrs), Banjar (Uncultivated barren — बंजर).

### Taxation Terms:
- **Jizya:** Non-Muslim protection tax (गैर-मुस्लिमों से कर).
- **Kharaj:** Agri land tax (कृषि भूमि कर).
- **Khams:** King's share of war spoils.
- **Zakat:** Muslim religious tax (धार्मिक दान कर).
- **Chauth & Sardeshmukhi:** Maratha taxes (Chauth = 1/4th, Sardeshmukhi = +10% levy).

### Official Postings:
- **Amil:** Revenue collector (राजस्व अधिकारी).
- **Kotwal:** Town administration / police chief.
- **Mir Bakshi:** Head of military department.
- **Muqaddam:** Village headman (ग्राम मुखिया).
- **Muqti / Iqtadar:** Holder of Iqta land grant.
