## 📖 Core Thematic Revision (PCS Matrix Complete Notes)

### Delhi Sultanate Battles:
- **Battle of Tarain I & II (1191, 1192):** Prithviraj Chauhan vs. Muhammad Ghori.
- **Battle of Chandawar (1194):** Muhammad Ghori defeated Jaichand of Kannauj.
- **Battle of Ranthambore (1301):** Alauddin Khalji vs. Hammiradeva.
- **Battle of Kili (1299):** Alauddin Khalji defeated the Mongol forces (मंगोल आक्रमणकारियों को पराजित किया).

### Mughal Wars:
- **Battle of Panipat I & II (1526, 1556):** First (Babur vs. Ibrahim Lodi), Second (Akbar/Bairam Khan vs. Hemu).
- **Battle of Khanwa (1527):** Babur defeated Rana Sanga.
- **Battle of Chausa (1539) & Kannauj/Bilgram (1540):** Sher Shah Suri defeated Humayun.
- **Battle of Dharmat & Samugarh (1658):** War of Succession (उत्तराधिकार का युद्ध) among Shah Jahan's sons; Aurangzeb won.

### Maratha & Regional Conflicts:
- **Battle of Palkhed (1728) & Rakshasbhuvan (1763):** Marathas defeated the Nizam of Hyderabad.
- **Carnatic Wars (1746–1763):** Anglo-French rivalry for supremacy (आंग्ल-फ्रांसीसी संघर्ष).
- **Anglo-Mysore Wars (1767–1799):** British conflict with Hyder Ali and Tipu Sultan.
