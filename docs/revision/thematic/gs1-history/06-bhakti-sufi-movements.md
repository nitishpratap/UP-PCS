## 📖 Thematic Revision Summary (Reformatted from PDF)

### Bhakti Movement

**Key Features:** Personal devotion to God; rejection of caste/rituals; vernacular languages; social equality

**Major Saints (North India):**
| Saint | Period | Region | Deity/Sect |
|-------|--------|--------|-----------|
| Kabir | 15th CE | UP | Nirgun; weaver; disciple of Ramananda |
| Mirabai | 16th CE | Rajasthan | Krishna bhakta; Rajput princess |
| Tulsidas | 16th CE | UP | Ram; wrote Ramcharitmanas |
| Surdas | 16th CE | UP | Krishna; wrote Sur Sagar |
| Guru Nanak | 15th CE | Punjab | Founded Sikhism |
| Chaitanya | 15th–16th CE | Bengal | Vaishnavism; Gaudiya sect |

**South India (Alvars & Nayanars):**
- Alvars = Vaishnava saints (Prabandhams); Nayanars = Shaiva saints (Thevaram)

---

### Sufi Movement

**Key Orders (Silsilas):**
| Silsila | Founder in India | Practice |
|---------|-----------------|---------|
| Chishti | Moinuddin Chishti (Ajmer) | Music (Sama); close contact with people |
| Suhrawardi | Bahauddin Zakariya | Accepted state patronage |
| Qadiri | Shah Niamatullah | Liberal |
| Naqshbandi | Khwaja Baqi Billah | Orthodox; anti-music |

**Key Dargahs:**
- Ajmer Sharif → Moinuddin Chishti
- Nizamuddin Auliya → Delhi (disciple: Amir Khusrau)
- Fatehpur Sikri → Salim Chishti (associated with Akbar's son birth)

**Examiner Trap:** Amir Khusrau — disciple of Nizamuddin Auliya; invented Sitar & Tabla (debated); wrote in Persian & Hindi

## ❓ Previous Years Questions (UPPCS Prelims)


**Q-GC1. U.P.P.C.S. (UDA/LDA) (Pre) 2001**

The Bhakti movement was started by –

- (a) Alvar Saints
- (b) Sufi Saints
- (c) Surdas
- (d) Tulsidas

<details><summary>Show answer</summary>

**Answer: (a)**

**Explanation:**
The Bhakti Movement is a Hindu religious movement in

which the main spiritual practise is loving devotion among

the Shaiva Nayanars and the Vaishnava Alvars. The Bhakti

movement originated in ancient Dravida region and began

to spread to the north during the late medieval ages when

North India was under Islamic rule. The Bhakti movement

was started in the 8th century by the great philosopher Adi

Shankaracharya.
</details>

---

**Q-GC2. U.P.P.C.S. (Pre) 1993**

The Bhakti culture was reborn in India during-

- (a) Vedic age
- (b) 10th century AD
- (c) 12th century AD
- (d) 15th -16th century AD

<details><summary>Show answer</summary>

**Answer: (d)**

**Explanation:**
The Bhakti Movement started with the great philosopher Adi

Shankaracharya propounding the philosophy of Advaitavada

to eliminate the influence of Buddhism during the 8th century.

The Bhakti movement was reborn in the 15th-16th century

when Kabir, Tulsi, Nanak, Sur, Mirabai etc. encouraged the

movement.
</details>

---

**Q-GC3. R.A.S./R.T.S. (Pre) 1992**

The common belief of life of both Buddha and Mirabai
was–

- (a) Following ahimsa
- (b) Austerity of Nirvana
- (c) The world is full of sorrow
- (d) Speak of truth

<details><summary>Show answer</summary>

**Answer: (c)**

**Explanation:**
Buddha and Mirabai had a common philosophy which stated

that the world is full of sorrow. 'Sorrow is first among the

Four Arya Truths of Buddhism'.
</details>
