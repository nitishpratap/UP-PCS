## 📖 Thematic Revision Summary (Reformatted from PDF)

### Uttar Pradesh — History & Culture

**Major Historical Sites in UP:**
| Site | District | Significance |
|------|---------|--------------|
| Ayodhya | Ayodhya | Birthplace of Ram; Ram Lalla Temple (consecrated Jan 2024) |
| Mathura-Vrindavan | Mathura | Krishna birthplace; major pilgrimage |
| Varanasi (Kashi) | Varanasi | Oldest living city; Kashi Vishwanath Temple |
| Prayagraj (Allahabad) | Prayagraj | Triveni Sangam; Kumbh Mela (every 12 years) |
| Sarnath | Varanasi | Buddha's first sermon; Ashoka's Lion Capital |
| Kushinagar | Kushinagar | Buddha's Mahaparinirvana |
| Shravasti | Shravasti | Buddha spent 25 rainy seasons |
| Kapilvastu | Siddharth Nagar | Buddha's early life |
| Agra | Agra | Taj Mahal, Agra Fort, Fatehpur Sikri |
| Lucknow | Lucknow | Nawabi culture; Imambara (Bara & Chota) |

**UP Cultural Heritage:**
- **Kathak Dance:** Classical dance form originated in UP (temple storytelling)
- **Thumri:** Musical form from UP (Banaras & Lucknow gharanas)
- **Chikankari:** Embroidery art from Lucknow (GI Tag)
- **Banaras Silk (Banarasi Saree):** GI Tag; heavy brocade work

**Key Personalities from UP:**
- Premchand (Lamhi, Varanasi) — Hindi & Urdu literature
- Mahadevi Verma, Sumitranandan Pant, Harivansh Rai Bachchan — Hindi poets (Chhayavaad)
- Munshi Premchand — "Upanyas Samrat"

**Examiner Traps:**
- Kumbh Mela at Prayagraj = every 12 years; Ardh Kumbh = every 6 years
- Kathak is UP's classical dance (also performed in Rajasthan/Lucknow gharana)
- Chikankari ≠ Zardosi (Zardosi = gold thread embroidery, also from Lucknow)

| **Alha (Bundelkhand)** | **Birha (Purvanchal)** | Alha is a **heroic ballad** (Alha-Udal) of Bundelkhand; Birha is an **eastern narrative genre** sung by Purvanchal Ahirs. |
| **Charkula (Braj)** | **Kajri (Mirzapur)** | Charkula is a **108-lamp head-balancing folk dance** of Braj; Kajri is a **monsoon folk song** of Mirzapur/Varanasi. |
| **UP Kisan Sabha (Feb 1918)** | **Oudh Kisan Sabha (Oct 1920)** | UP Kisan Sabha was set up in **Prayagraj** (Mishra, Dwivedi, Malaviya); Oudh Kisan Sabha was formed in **Pratapgarh** (Baba Ramchandra). |

| **Kirana Gharana** | **Atrauli Gharana** | Kirana (Shamli) was founded by **Abdul Karim Khan**; Atrauli (Aligarh) was founded by **Alladiya Khan**. |
| **Annexation of Awadh (1856)** | **Annexation of Jhansi (1853)** | Awadh was annexed for **alleged misrule** (Outram Report); Jhansi was annexed under the **Doctrine of Lapse**. |

**Q1. UPPCS (Pre) 2018**
Which of the following centres related to Indus Valley are situated in Uttar Pradesh?
A. Only Alamgirpur
B. Alamgirpur and Hulas (as per options in paper)
C. Lothal
D. Dholavira

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Logic:** Lothal/Dholavira are Gujarat — classic distractors.

**Ans: A.** Alamgirpur (Meerut) is the easternmost Harappan site in UP.

</details>

**Q2. Standard UPPCS**
The 1857 revolt’s important centres in UP included:
A. Only Delhi
B. Meerut, Lucknow, Kanpur, Jhansi, among others
C. Only Bombay
D. Only Madras

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Logic:** UP Special freedom map is Meerut–Delhi–Awadh–Kanpur–Jhansi.

**Ans: B.** Meerut spark; Awadh and Bundelkhand theatres.

</details>
