## 📖 Thematic Revision Summary (Reformatted from PDF)

### Mughal Empire (1526–1857)

**Key Rulers:**
| Ruler | Period | Key Battle/Event |
|-------|--------|-----------------|
| Babur | 1526–1530 | 1st Battle of Panipat (1526) vs Ibrahim Lodi; Battle of Khanwa (1527) vs Rana Sanga |
| Humayun | 1530–1556 | Lost to Sher Shah Suri (Battle of Kanauj 1540); regained throne 1555 |
| Akbar | 1556–1605 | 2nd Battle of Panipat (1556); Din-i-Ilahi; Ibadat Khana; Navratnas |
| Jahangir | 1605–1627 | Nur Jahan's influence; Thomas Roe (British) visited |
| Shah Jahan | 1628–1658 | Taj Mahal, Red Fort, Jama Masjid |
| Aurangzeb | 1658–1707 | Re-imposed Jizya; expanded empire maximally; Shivaji's father Shahaji |
| Bahadur Shah II | 1837–1857 | Last Mughal; exiled to Rangoon after 1857 revolt |

**Akbar's Navratnas:** Birbal, Tansen, Abul Fazl, Faizi, Todar Mal, Man Singh, Abdul Rahim Khan-i-Khana, Fakir Aziao-Din, Mullah Do Piaza

**Mughal Art:**
- Miniature painting: Persian + Indian style fusion
- Architects: Ustad Ahmad Lahori (Taj Mahal)

**Revenue System:** Todar Mal introduced Zabti/Bandobast system (land measurement)

**Examiner Traps:**
- Akbar's real name = Jalaluddin Muhammad Akbar
- Din-i-Ilahi ≠ a religion for all; only ~18 people accepted it
- Sher Shah Suri (not Mughal): Grand Trunk Road, Rupee coin, Postal system

## ❓ Previous Years Questions (UPPCS Prelims)


**Q-GC1. (d) A high official in Punjab province who was very much, discontented with Ibrahim Lodi’s treatment of his tribe, I.A.S. (Pre) 2003**

Alam Khan, one of those who invited Babur to invade
India was :

- (a) An uncle of Ibrahim Lodi and a pretender to the throne of Delhi
- (b) A cousin of Ibrahim Lodi who was ill-treated and expelled from the country
- (c) The father of Dilawar Khan to whom cruel treatment was meted out by Ibrahim Lodi

<details><summary>Show answer</summary>

**Answer: (a)**

**Explanation:**
Alam Khan was the uncle of Ibrahim Lodi, who invited Babur

to invade India. During the fourth Indian campaign of Babur,

the relationship between Ibrahim Lodi and Daulat Khan

became bad. Ibrahim Lodi ordered Daulat Khan to visit his

capital, but Daulat Khan violated his orders and sent his son

Dilawar Khan to Babur with a message to help him dethrone

Ibrahim Lodi and enthrone Alam Khan in place of him. It was

a golden opportunity for Babur as he already received the

invitation of Rana Sanga, the king of Mewar. He was quite

assured that the opportunity to win India had come. 

Indian History General Studies B–275
</details>

**Q-GC3. U.P.P.C.S. (UDA/LDA) (Pre) 2010**

The Mughal rulers of medieval India were –

- (a) Persian (Iranian)
- (b) Afghans
- (c) Chagatai Turk
- (d) None of the above

<details><summary>Show answer</summary>

**Answer: (c)**

**Explanation:**
The Mughal rulers belonged to the Chagatai branch of Turks.

This name was derived after the second son of Genghis Khan.

He was the ruler of central Asia and Turkistan.
