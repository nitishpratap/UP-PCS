## 📖 Core Thematic Revision (PCS Matrix Complete Notes)

### Major Rock Edicts & Pillar Inscriptions:
- **Major Rock Edict XIII:** Details of Kalinga War (कलिंग युद्ध का वर्णन).
- **Rummindei Pillar Inscription:** Records Ashoka's visit to Lumbini; marks the reduction of taxes (कर में छूट — Bali and Bhaga).
- **Prayagraj / Allahabad Pillar:** Originally shifted from Kaushambi. Contains Ashoka's edicts, Samudragupta's Prayag Prashasti composed by Harisena (हरिषेण द्वारा रचित प्रशस्ति), and Jahangir's inscriptions.
- **Sarnath Lion Capital:** Site of Ashoka's pillar and Buddha's first sermon (प्रथम उपदेश — धर्मचक्रप्रवर्तन).
