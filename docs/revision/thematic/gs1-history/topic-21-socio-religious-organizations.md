## 📖 Core Thematic Revision (PCS Matrix Complete Notes)

### Brahmo Samaj & Branches:
- **Brahmo Samaj (1828):** Raja Ram Mohan Roy in Calcutta.
- **Tattvabodhini Sabha (1839):** Debendranath Tagore.
- **Schism of 1866:** Adi Brahmo Samaj (Debendranath Tagore) & Brahmo Samaj of India (Keshab Chandra Sen).

### Major National Organizations:
- **Prarthana Samaj (1867):** Atmaram Pandurang (expanded by M.G. Ranade) in Bombay.
- **Satyashodhak Samaj (1873):** Jyotirao Phule in Maharashtra (निम्न जाति उद्धार).
- **Arya Samaj (1875):** Swami Dayanand Saraswati ('Go back to the Vedas' — वेदों की ओर लौटो).
- **Theosophical Society (1875):** H.P. Blavatsky & H.S. Olcott (HQ shifted to Adyar, Madras 1882).
- **Ramakrishna Mission (1897):** Swami Vivekananda at Belur, Bengal.

### UP & Regional Sabhas:
- **Sanatan Dharma Rakshini Sabha (1873):** Calcutta.
- **Dev Samaj (1887):** Shiv Narayan Agnihotri, Lahore.
- **Bharat Dharma Mahamandal (1887):** Pt. Din Dayal Sharma.
- **Radha Soami Satsang (1861):** Shiv Dayal Saheb, Agra.
