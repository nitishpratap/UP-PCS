## 📖 Thematic Revision Summary (Reformatted from PDF)

### 19th Century Socio-Religious Reform Movements

| Organization | Founder | Year | State | Key Reforms |
|-------------|---------|------|-------|-------------|
| Brahmo Samaj | Raja Ram Mohan Roy | 1828 | Bengal | Monotheism; opposed sati, child marriage; women's education |
| Arya Samaj | Swami Dayananda Saraswati | 1875 | Punjab (Mumbai) | "Back to Vedas"; opposed idol worship, caste; Shuddhi movement |
| Ramakrishna Mission | Swami Vivekananda | 1897 | Bengal | Vedanta; "service to man is service to God" |
| Prarthana Samaj | Atmaram Pandurang | 1867 | Maharashtra | Social reform; widow remarriage |
| Satyashodhak Samaj | Jyotiba Phule | 1873 | Maharashtra | Anti-caste; women's upliftment; educated Dalits |
| Aligarh Movement | Sir Syed Ahmad Khan | 1875 | UP | Modern education for Muslims; Aligarh Muslim University |
| Theosophical Society | H.P. Blavatsky & Col. Olcott (Annie Besant later) | 1875 | USA (India) | Universal brotherhood; Hindu revival |
| SNDP Yogam | Sree Narayana Guru | 1903 | Kerala | Ezhava caste upliftment; "One religion, one caste, one God" |

**Key Acts:**
- Sati Prohibition Act (1829) — Bentinck; after Roy's campaigning
- Widow Remarriage Act (1856) — after Vidyasagar's efforts
- Age of Consent Act (1891) — raised age of consent to 12 years

**Examiner Traps:**
- Arya Samaj = Dayananda (not Vivekananda)
- Vivekananda's speech at Chicago World Parliament of Religions = 1893
- Annie Besant = Theosophical Society + Home Rule League (1916)
