## 📖 Thematic Revision Summary (Reformatted from PDF)

### Post-Mauryan Period (185 BCE–320 CE)

**Key Dynasties:**
| Dynasty | Notable Ruler | Contribution |
|---------|--------------|--------------|
| Shunga | Pushyamitra Shunga | Ended Mauryas; revived Brahminism |
| Satavahana | Gautamiputra Satakarni | Deccan power; issued lead coins |
| Kushana | Kanishka I | Spread Buddhism (Mahayana); Silk Route trade |
| Gupta | Chandragupta I, Samudragupta, Chandragupta II | Golden Age |

---

### Gupta Empire (320–550 CE) — Golden Age of India

**Key Rulers:**
- **Chandragupta I:** Founded empire; married Lichchhavi princess Kumaradevi
- **Samudragupta (335–375 CE):** "Napoleon of India" (V.A. Smith); Allahabad Pillar inscription (by Harishena)
- **Chandragupta II / Vikramaditya (375–415 CE):** Defeated Shakas; Fa Hien visited India; Navratnas at court
- **Skandagupta:** Last great Gupta ruler; defeated Huns

**Cultural Achievements:**
- **Literature:** Kalidasa (Abhijnanasakuntalam, Meghaduta, Raghuvamsa)
- **Science:** Aryabhata (value of π, heliocentric theory, 0), Varahamihira, Brahmagupta
- **Art:** Ajanta paintings (5th CE), Mathura & Sarnath sculpture schools
- **Sanskrit:** Official court language; Panchatantra written

**Examiner Traps:**
- Fa Hien ≠ Hiuen Tsang (Hiuen Tsang visited under Harsha)
- "Iron Pillar" of Delhi = Chandragupta II period
- Aryabhata wrote Aryabhatiya (NOT Aryabhata Siddhanta)

> **Exam Anchor [UPPCS Prelims 2018, Q87]:** Allahabad Prasasti (Prayag) of Samudragupta identifies contemporary kings: Dhananjaya = Kusthalapur; Nilraja = Avamukta; Damana = Erandapalla; Vishnugopa = Kanchi. *(See Q4 in Complete PYQ Bank below)*

> **Exam Anchor [UPPCS Prelims 2022, Q23]:** Chandragupta II Vikramaditya was the first Gupta ruler to issue **silver coins (Rupaka)** after conquering the Western Sakas (Rudrasimha III). *(See Q5 in Complete PYQ Bank below)*

> **Exam Anchor [UPPCS Prelims 2024, Q149]:** Chronology of foreign travellers: Megasthenes (c. 302 BCE) → Faxian/Fa-Hien (c. 399–414 CE) → Xuanzang/Hiuen Tsang (c. 629–645 CE) → Yijing/I-Tsing (c. 671–695 CE). *(See Q3 in Complete PYQ Bank below)*

> **Exam Anchor [UPPCS Prelims 2025, Q48]:** *Janakiharana* was authored by **Kumaradasa** of Sri Lanka, NOT by Kalidasa (author of Meghaduta, Raghuvamsha, Kumarasambhava, etc.). *(See Q1 in Complete PYQ Bank below)*

> **Exam Anchor [UPPCS Prelims 2025, Q86]:** Works and authors: Mrichhakatikam = Shudraka; Mudrarakshasa = Vishakhadatta; Harshacharita = Banabhatta; Dashakumaracharita = Dandin. *(See Q2 in Complete PYQ Bank below)*
