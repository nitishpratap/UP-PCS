## 📖 Core Thematic Revision (PCS Matrix Complete Notes)

### Ancient Works:
- **Arthashastra:** Kautilya / Chanakya (राजनीति शास्त्र)
- **Mrichhakatika:** Sudraka (संस्कृत नाटक)
- **Mudrarakshasa:** Vishakhadatta
- **Charaka Samhita:** Charaka (आयुर्वेद ग्रंथ)
- **Indika:** Megasthenes
- **Aryabhatiya:** Aryabhata (खगोलशास्त्र एवं गणित)
- **Mahabhasya:** Patanjali (व्याकरण भाष्य)
- **Harshacharita & Kadambari:** Banabhatta
- **Nagananda, Ratnavali, Priyadarsika:** King Harshavardhana

### Medieval Works:
- **Kitab-ul-Hind:** Al-Biruni (Account of 11th-century India).
- **Tabaqat-i-Nasiri:** Minhaj-i-Siraj (History of Islamic world and Sultanate).
- **Tarikh-i-Firoz Shahi:** Ziyauddin Barani.
- **Baburnama:** Babur (Autobiography in Chagatai Turkic — आत्मकथा).
- **Humayun-nama:** Gulbadan Begum (Biography of Humayun — जीवनी).
- **Akbarnama & Ain-i-Akbari:** Abul Fazl (Official chronicle of Akbar's reign — अकबर का इतिहास).
- **Padmavat:** Malik Muhammad Jayasi (Epic poem written in Awadhi — अवधी में रचित महाकाव्य).
- **Shahnama:** Firdausi.
