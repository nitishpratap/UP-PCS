## 📖 Thematic Revision Summary (Reformatted from PDF)

### Indus Valley Civilization (IVC) — Key Facts

**Time Period:** 3300–1300 BCE (Mature Phase: 2600–1900 BCE)

**Major Sites & Locations:**

| Site | Modern Location | Unique Feature |
|------|----------------|----------------|
| Harappa | Punjab, Pakistan | First discovered site (1921) |
| Mohenjo-daro | Sindh, Pakistan | Great Bath; largest IVC city |
| Dholavira | Gujarat, India | Only site with stone structures & signboard |
| Lothal | Gujarat, India | Dockyard; bead factory |
| Kalibangan | Rajasthan, India | Fire altars; ploughed field evidence |
| Rakhigarhi | Haryana, India | Largest IVC site in India |
| Surkotada | Gujarat, India | Horse bones found |
| Banawali | Haryana, India | Toy plough found |

**Key Features:**
- **Town Planning:** Grid pattern, underground drainage (विश्व की प्राचीनतम ड्रेनेज प्रणाली)
- **Great Bath:** Mohenjo-daro — public bathing structure
- **Script:** Undeciphered Pictographic script (written right to left — Boustrophedon)
- **No evidence** of temples, palaces, or a standing army
- **Weights & Measures:** Standardised (binary system)
- **Trade:** With Mesopotamia (Dilmun route); used carnelian beads

**Decline Theories:** Climate change, drying of Saraswati river, Aryan invasion (outdated)

**Examiner Traps:**
- Lothal ≠ largest site; Rakhigarhi is largest in India
- Great Bath is at Mohenjo-daro, NOT Harappa
- IVC was contemporary with Egyptian & Mesopotamian civilizations

## ❓ Previous Years Questions (UPPCS Prelims)


### UPPCS Prelims 2025

**Q1. UPPCS Prelims 2025, Q87**

Which of the following archaeological sites related to the Indus Valley Civilization are situated in present-day Uttar Pradesh?

1. Mandi
2. Rakhigarhi
3. Hulas

A. 1 and 2

B. Only 3

C. 1 and 3

D. Only 1

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Correct Answer:** **C** (1 and 3)

**Detailed Explanation:**
- **Analysis of Statements:**
  - **1. Mandi:** Located in Muzaffarnagar district, **Uttar Pradesh**. Famous for the sensational discovery of a 10 kg hoard of Harappan gold jewelry and beads in 2000.
  - **2. Rakhigarhi:** Located in Hisar district, **Haryana**. Excavations have proved it to be the largest Harappan site in India, spanning over 350 hectares. It is not in Uttar Pradesh.
  - **3. Hulas:** Located in Saharanpur district, **Uttar Pradesh**. Excavated by K.N. Dikshit; key Late Harappan site yielding pottery, inscribed sherds, and evidence of multiple crops (wheat, barley, pulses, cotton).
- **Conclusion:** 1 and 3 only are in Uttar Pradesh, matching Option **C**.

**Key Exam Takeaway / Trap:**
- Repeat PYQ: Mandi and Hulas are in **Uttar Pradesh**, Rakhigarhi is in **Haryana**.
</details>

### UPPCS Prelims 2023

**Q2. UPPCS Prelims 2023, Q28**

The eastern boundary of the Harappan culture is indicated by which of the following?

A. Harappa B. Alamgirpur C. Rakhigarhi D. Manda

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Correct Answer:** **B** (Alamgirpur)

**Detailed Explanation:**
- **Easternmost Frontier:** **Alamgirpur** (located in Meerut district, Uttar Pradesh, on the Hindan River, an affluent of the Yamuna) was excavated by Y.D. Sharma in 1958. It marks the **easternmost extent** of the Harappan cultural realm.
- **Material Remains:** The site yielded painted Harappan pottery, terracotta beads, faience bangles, animal figurines, and cloth impressions on clay troughs.
- **Other Boundary Benchmarks:**
  - Manda (J&K) = Northernmost.
  - Daimabad (Maharashtra) = Southernmost.
  - Sutkagen-dor (Balochistan) = Westernmost.
  - Rakhigarhi (Haryana) = Largest site in India.

**Key Exam Takeaway / Trap:**
- Eastern boundary anchor = **Alamgirpur (Meerut, UP on Hindan river)**.
</details>

### UPPCS Prelims 2022

**Q3. UPPCS Prelims 2022, Q68**

From which archaeological site of the Indus Valley Civilization are figures or models of boats found?

A. Dholavira and Bhagatrav
B. Harappa and Kot Diji
C. Mohenjo-daro and Lothal
D. Kalibangan and Ropar

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Correct Answer:** **C** (Mohenjo-daro and Lothal)

**Detailed Explanation:**
- **Archaeological Discoveries:**
  - **Mohenjo-daro:** Yielded the **Great Bath**, the bronze Dancing Girl (cire perdue casting), the steatite Priest-King statue, the Great Granary, and the Pashupati Mahadeva seal.
  - **Lothal:** Yielded the engineered **tidal dockyard**, ivory scale, terracotta horse figurine, Persian Gulf seal, and **double/joint burials**.
- **Conclusion:** Mohenjo-daro and Lothal correctly answer the question stem.

**Key Exam Takeaway / Trap:**
- Core signature pairing: Great Bath = **Mohenjo-daro**; Dockyard = **Lothal**.
</details>
