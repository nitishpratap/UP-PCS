## 📖 Thematic Revision Summary (Reformatted from PDF)

### Indian National Movement — Key Milestones

**Founding of INC (1885):**
- A.O. Hume (retired British civil servant) founded; 1st session in Bombay (W.C. Bonnerjee president)

**Partition of Bengal (1905):**
- Lord Curzon partitioned Bengal (Hindu east Bengal separated)
- Triggered: Swadeshi Movement, Boycott Movement, Mass agitation

**Surat Split (1907):** Congress split into Moderates (Gokhale) and Extremists (Bal-Pal-Lal: Tilak, Bipin Pal, Lajpat Rai)

**Key Movements:**
| Movement | Year | Key Feature |
|----------|------|------------|
| Non-Cooperation | 1920 | Non-violent; called off after Chauri Chaura (1922) |
| Civil Disobedience | 1930 | Dandi March (12 March – 6 April); Salt Satyagraha |
| Quit India | 1942 | "Do or Die"; August Kranti; mass arrests |

**Gandhi's Satyagrahas before NCM:**
- Champaran (1917) — indigo farmers
- Kheda (1918) — revenue remission
- Ahmedabad (1918) — mill workers

**Revolutionary Movements:**
| Name | Organization | Event |
|------|-------------|-------|
| Bhagat Singh | HSRA | Lahore Conspiracy Case; hanged 23 March 1931 |
| Subhas Chandra Bose | INA | Azad Hind Fauj; "Give me blood, I'll give you freedom" |
| Chandra Shekhar Azad | HRSA | Never caught alive |

**Examiner Traps:**
- Chauri Chaura is in Gorakhpur district, UP
- Non-Cooperation Movement ended 1922 (NOT 1921)
- INA trials held at Red Fort, 1945
