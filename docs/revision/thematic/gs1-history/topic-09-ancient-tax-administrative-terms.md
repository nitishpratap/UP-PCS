## 📖 Core Thematic Revision (PCS Matrix Complete Notes)

### Ancient Tax & Administrative Terms:
- **Bali:** Originally voluntary tribute (स्वेच्छा से दिया उपहार); later compulsory tax (अनिवार्य कर).
- **Bhaga:** King's share of agricultural produce (कृषि उपज में हिस्सा), usually 1/6th.
- **Hiranya:** Tax paid in cash (नकद कर) rather than kind.
- **Vishti:** Forced labor (बेगार / बिना वेतन का श्रम).
- **Agrahara:** Tax-free land (कर-मुक्त भूमि) granted to Brahmins.
- **Aghanya:** "Not to be killed" (न मारने योग्य) — for cows in Rigveda.
