## 📖 Thematic Revision Summary (Reformatted from PDF)

### Global Mineral Belts & Mining Centers

**Iron Ore:**
- Pilbara (Australia) — Hamersley Range
- Kiruna & Gällivare (Sweden)
- Magnitogorsk (Russia)
- Minas Gerais (Brazil)

**Coal Fields:**
- Donbas (Ukraine)
- Kuznetsk/Kuzbass (Russia)
- Ruhr Valley & Saar (Germany) — most famous coal-steel region
- Appalachian Region (USA)
- Shanxi & Shaanxi (China)

**Copper:**
- Chuquicamata & El Teniente (Chile) — world's top copper producer
- Katanga/Copperbelt (DR Congo + Zambia)

**Gold & Diamond:**
- Witwatersrand & Kimberley (South Africa) — gold & diamond
- Kalgoorlie & Coolgardie (Australia) — gold
- Yakutia/Mirny (Russia) — diamond open-pit mines

**Petroleum:**
- Ghawar Field (Saudi Arabia) — world's largest oil field
- Burgan Field (Kuwait)
- Baku (Azerbaijan)
- Permian Basin (USA)
- North Sea (UK/Norway)

**India's Mineral Belts:**
| Mineral | Leading State |
|---------|--------------|
| Iron Ore | Odisha, Jharkhand |
| Coal | Chhattisgarh, Odisha, Jharkhand |
| Bauxite | Odisha, Jharkhand |
| Copper | Madhya Pradesh (Malanjkhand) |
| Mica | Jharkhand, Rajasthan |
| Manganese | Odisha |

**Examiner Traps:**
- Chile = No. 1 copper producer in the world (Chuquicamata mine)
- South Africa = diamonds (Kimberley) + gold (Witwatersrand)
- Ruhr Valley (Germany) = coal + steel + industrial heartland of Europe

## ❓ Previous Years Questions (UPPCS Prelims)


**Q1.** Match List-I (Mineral) with List-II (Top Producing State in India):
| List-I | List-II |
| :--- | :--- |
| A. Bauxite | 1. Madhya Pradesh |
| B. Copper | 2. Odisha |
| C. Gold | 3. Chhattisgarh |
| D. Coal | 4. Karnataka |

A. A-2, B-1, C-4, D-3  
B. A-1, B-2, C-3, D-4  
C. A-2, B-3, C-4, D-1  
D. A-4, B-1, C-2, D-3  

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>
**Ans: A.** Bauxite (Odisha), Copper (MP), Gold (Karnataka), Coal (Chhattisgarh). Note: Coal reserves are highest in Jharkhand, but production is highest in Chhattisgarh/Odisha depending on the reporting year. UP/UKPCS traditionally accepts Chhattisgarh / Odisha as top producer and Jharkhand as top reserve.
</details>
