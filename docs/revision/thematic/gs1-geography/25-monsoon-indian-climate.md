## 📖 Thematic Revision Summary (Reformatted from PDF)

### Indian Monsoon

**Mechanism:**
- Differential heating of land and sea → low pressure over Indian subcontinent in summer
- SW Monsoon (June–September): Moisture-laden winds from Indian Ocean
- NE Monsoon (October–December): Retreating; affects Tamil Nadu coast

**Branches of SW Monsoon:**
1. **Arabian Sea Branch:** Hits Western Ghats first → orographic rainfall → moves to MP, UP
2. **Bay of Bengal Branch:** Enters NE India first → moves westward

**Onset:** Kerala (~1 June); reaches UP by late June/July

**Rainfall Distribution:**
- Highest: Mawsynram (Meghalaya) — ~11,871 mm/year; "wettest place on Earth"
- Cherrapunji nearby (~11,430 mm)
- Lowest: Leh (Ladakh) < 100 mm

**El Niño Effect:**
- Warming of Pacific Ocean → weakens Indian Monsoon → drought years in India
- La Niña → cooler Pacific → stronger monsoon

**Climate Zones of India:**
| Type | Region | Characteristic |
|------|--------|---------------|
| Tropical Monsoon | Most of India | 4 seasons: hot dry, hot wet, retreating monsoon, cool dry |
| Tropical Hot Desert | W Rajasthan | < 25 cm rainfall; extreme temperature |
| Alpine/Tundra | High Himalayas | Snow-covered; cold |

**Examiner Traps:**
- Mawsynram = wettest; Cherrapunji used to hold the record
- Tamil Nadu gets rain from NE Monsoon (Oct–Dec) = winter rainfall
- UP gets most rain from Bay of Bengal branch

## ❓ Previous Years Questions (UPPCS Prelims)


**Q. UKPCS Prelims 2025, Q96**

According to Koppen's climatic classification, the (Amw) climate is found predominantly in which part of India?

A. North-Western India
B. Western coastal region
C. Thar (थार) desert
D. Northern plain

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Logic:** A = tropical, w = dry winter — western coastal strip.

**Ans: B.** Amw marks the western coastal strip (and parts of the north-east). Thar is BWhw; northern plain largely Cwg.

</details>

**Q. UKPCS Prelims 2025, Q98**

Which local wind affects some parts of Kerala and Tamil Nadu (नाडु) during the pre-monsoon season?

A. Loo
B. Mango Shower
C. Kal Baisakhi (बैसाखी)
D. Mistral

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Logic:** Loo and Kal Baisakhi are northern — southern pre-monsoon is mango shower.

**Ans: B.** Mango showers help ripen mangoes in Kerala and coastal Tamil Nadu.

</details>

**Q1. UPPCS Prelims 2025, Q45**
With reference to the ozone layer, which of the following statements is/are correct?

1. The ozone layer protects the Earth's surface from ultraviolet radiation coming from the Sun.
2. Ozone depletion has been linked to chlorofluorocarbons (CFCs).

A. Only 2

B. Neither 1 nor 2

C. Both 1 and 2

D. Only 1

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: C** — Both true. Protective ozone is in the **stratosphere**. CFCs linked to depletion. **D** drops CFCs; **A** drops UV shield.
</details>
