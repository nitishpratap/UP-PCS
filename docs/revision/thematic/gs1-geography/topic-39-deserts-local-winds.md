## 📖 Core Thematic Revision (PCS Matrix Complete Notes)

### Deserts vs. Location (मरुस्थल एविं ्स्थतिि):
- Atacama Desert (Chile): Driest non-polar desert, influenced by cold Humboldt/Peru current (विश्व
- का सबसे शुष्क मरुस्थिल).
- Patagonian Desert (Argentina): Rain-shadow desert (वृषि्ट-छाया मरुस्थिल).
- Taklamakan (China) & Gobi (Mongolia/China): Cold mid-latitude deserts (शीतोष्ण कटिबंधीय ठंडे मरुस्थिल).
- Kalahari & Namib Deserts: Southern Africa (कालाहारी एवं नामिब मरुस्थिल).
- Rub' al Khali / Dasht-e Kavir / Dasht-e Lut: Middle East / Saudi Arabia / Iran.
- Great Victoria / Gibson / Simpson Deserts: Australia.

### Local Winds vs. Region (स्थानीय पविनें एविं क्षिेत्र):
- Chinook: USA / Canada (Rocky Mountains - Warm wind known as "Snow eater" / हिम भशिक्षिणी).
- Foehn: Alps Mountains, Europe (Warm, dry wind - गर्म एवं शुष्क हवा).
- Sirocco: Sahara to Mediterranean/Italy (Hot, dust-laden wind that causes "Blood Rain" - रक्त वर्षा).
- Mistral: France / Rhone Valley (Cold, dry wind - ठंडी पवन).
- Bora: Adriatic Coast / Eastern Europe (Cold, gusty wind).
- Harmattan: West Africa / Sahara (Dry wind known as "The Doctor" - डॉक्टर हवा).
- Blizzard: Polar regions / Canada / Siberia (Snowstorm wind - हिमझंझावात).
- Simoom: Arabian Desert (Hot, suffocating dust-laden wind).
