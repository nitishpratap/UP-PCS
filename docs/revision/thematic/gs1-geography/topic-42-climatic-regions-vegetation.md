## 📖 Core Thematic Revision (PCS Matrix Complete Notes)

### Equatorial Climate (वििषुविति रेखीय जलविायु):
- Regions: Amazon Basin, Congo Basin, Southeast Asia.
- Features: Dense tropical rainforests (Selvas - अमेजन के सेल्वास वन), heavy convectional rainfall
- (संवहनीय वर्षा / 4 o'clock showers).

### Mediterranean Climate (भूमध्यसिागरीय जलविायु):
- Regions: Central Chile, California, SW Australia, Mediterranean Basin.
- Features: Characterized by winter rainfall (शीतकालीन वर्षा) and dry summers; famous for citrus
- fruits (खट्टे फल - संतरा, नींबू, जैतून) and sclerophyllous plants (कठोर पत्तेदार वनस्पति).

### Savanna / Sudan Type Climate (सिविाना जलविायु):
- Features: Tropical grasslands (उष्णकटिबंधीय घास के मैदान) with scattered trees; known as "Big Game
- Country" (शिकारी भूमि).

### Steppe Climate / Temperate Grasslands (:ीतिोष्ण कटििबंधीय घासि के मैदान):
- Pusztas: Hungary
- Prairies: North America
- Pampas: Argentina
- Veld: South Africa
- Downs: Australia
