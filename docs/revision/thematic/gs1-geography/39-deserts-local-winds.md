## 📖 Thematic Revision Summary (Reformatted from PDF)

### Major Deserts of the World

**Hot Deserts:**
| Desert | Location | Notable Feature |
|--------|---------|----------------|
| Sahara | North Africa | Largest hot desert in world |
| Arabian Desert | Middle East | Rub' al Khali (Empty Quarter) = largest continuous sand desert |
| Thar | India/Pakistan | Only major desert in South Asia |
| Atacama | Chile (South America) | Driest non-polar desert; influenced by cold Humboldt Current |
| Namib | Namibia/South Africa | Coastal desert; Benguela cold current |
| Kalahari | Southern Africa | Semi-arid; not true desert |

**Cold Deserts:**
| Desert | Location |
|--------|---------|
| Gobi | Mongolia/China |
| Taklamakan | China (mid-latitude cold desert) |
| Patagonian | Argentina (rain-shadow desert) |
| Ladakh | India (cold desert — Leh) |

---

### Local Winds

| Wind | Region | Type |
|------|--------|------|
| Chinook | Rocky Mountains (USA/Canada) | Warm, dry — "Snow Eater" |
| Foehn | Alps (Europe) | Warm, dry |
| Sirocco | Sahara to Mediterranean/Italy | Hot, dust-laden; causes "Blood Rain" |
| Mistral | France/Rhone Valley | Cold, dry |
| Bora | Adriatic Coast/Eastern Europe | Cold, gusty |
| Harmattan | West Africa/Sahara | Dry — "The Doctor" (brings relief in humid conditions) |
| Blizzard | Polar regions/Canada/Siberia | Snowstorm |
| Simoom | Arabian Desert | Hot, suffocating, dust-laden |
| Loo | UP/Rajasthan | Hot, dry summer wind |

**Examiner Traps:**
- Sahara = largest HOT desert; Antarctica = largest desert overall (cold)
- Chinook = "Snow Eater" (warm foehn-type wind in North America)
- Mistral = cold (often confused with Sirocco which is HOT)
