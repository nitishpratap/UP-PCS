## 📖 Core Thematic Revision (PCS Matrix Complete Notes)

### Soils vs. Characteristics (मृदा की विशेषताएँ):
- **Alluvial Soil:** ~40% area; Potash rich, Phosphorus deficient. Khadar (New/fertile), Bhangar (Old).
- **Black / Regur Soil:** Cotton cultivation; self-ploughing; high moisture retention; Iron/Magnesium rich.
- **Red & Yellow Soil:** Iron oxide diffusion; Nitrogen & Humus deficient.
- **Laterite Soil:** Formed via intense leaching; suitable for Cashew, Tea, Coffee.

### Crops vs. Highest Producing States:
- **Wheat / Sugarcane / Milk:** Uttar Pradesh
- **Rice:** West Bengal
- **Cotton:** Gujarat / Maharashtra
- **Soyabean / Pulses:** Madhya Pradesh
- **Coffee:** Karnataka
- **Tea:** Assam
