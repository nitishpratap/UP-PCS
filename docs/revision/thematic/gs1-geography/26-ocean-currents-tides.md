## 📖 Thematic Revision Summary (Reformatted from PDF)

### Ocean Currents

**Types:**
- **Warm Currents:** Flow from equator to poles; increase temperature of adjacent coasts
- **Cold Currents:** Flow from poles to equator; bring cool, foggy conditions; rich in fish

**Major Ocean Currents:**
| Current | Ocean | Type | Significance |
|---------|-------|------|-------------|
| Gulf Stream | North Atlantic | Warm | Keeps Western Europe warm (London warm despite latitude) |
| North Atlantic Drift | North Atlantic | Warm | Extension of Gulf Stream; European climate |
| Labrador Current | North Atlantic | Cold | Meets Gulf Stream → dense fogs (Grand Banks fishery) |
| Humboldt/Peru Current | South Pacific | Cold | Makes Atacama Desert the driest; El Niño disrupts it |
| Benguela Current | South Atlantic | Cold | Namib Desert formation |
| Brazil Current | South Atlantic | Warm | — |
| Kuroshio | North Pacific | Warm | Like Gulf Stream of Pacific |
| California Current | North Pacific | Cold | Makes California coast cool |
| Agulhas Current | Indian Ocean | Warm | SE coast of Africa |
| Somali Current | Indian Ocean | Variable | Reverses with monsoon |

**Tides:**
- **Spring Tides:** Sun + Moon in alignment (Full Moon or New Moon) → highest tides
- **Neap Tides:** Sun + Moon at right angles (Half Moon) → lowest tidal range
- **Highest Tidal Range:** Bay of Fundy (Canada) ~16 m

**Examiner Traps:**
- Humboldt/Peru Current = cold current along South America's west coast
- Grand Banks (off Newfoundland) = world's richest fishing ground (warm + cold current mixing)
- India's highest tidal range = Gulf of Khambhat (Cambay), Gujarat

## ❓ Previous Years Questions (UPPCS Prelims)


**Q1. UPPCS Prelims 2025, Q75**
Which of the following are NOT cold ocean currents?

1. Agulhas Current
2. Brazil Current
3. Humboldt Current
4. California Current

A. 1 and 3

B. 3 and 4

C. 2 and 3

D. 1 and 2

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: D (1 and 2)** — The paper asks which are **NOT cold** (= which are **warm**).

| Current | Type | Ocean |
|---------|------|-------|
| 1 Agulhas | **Warm** | Indian, SE Africa |
| 2 Brazil | **Warm** | Atlantic, E Brazil |
| 3 Humboldt | **Cold** | Pacific, Peru–Chile |
| 4 California | **Cold** | Pacific, W USA |

Pick **1 and 2**. Option B (3 and 4) is the cold pair — that is the trap if you miss the word **NOT**.
</details>
