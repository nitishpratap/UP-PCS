## 📖 Thematic Revision Summary (Reformatted from PDF)

### Forest Types in India (Champion & Seth Classification)

| Forest Type | Region | Dominant Trees |
|------------|--------|---------------|
| Tropical Evergreen | Western Ghats, NE India, Andaman | Rosewood, Ebony, Mahogany; trees shed leaves at different times |
| Tropical Deciduous (Moist) | MP, Odisha, parts of AP | Sal, Teak — "Monsoon Forests" |
| Tropical Deciduous (Dry) | UP, Bihar, Rajasthan | Tendu, Bel, Axlewood |
| Tropical Thorn | Rajasthan, Gujarat, Haryana | Babul (Acacia), Cactus, Khejri |
| Montane | Himalayan belt | Coniferous (Pine, Deodar, Cedar) at higher altitude; Rhododendron |
| Mangrove | Sundarbans (WB), Odisha, Gujarat coast | Sundari tree (gives name to Sundarbans); salt tolerant |

**Forest Cover in India:**
- Total Forest + Tree Cover: ~24% of geographic area (India State of Forest Report 2021)
- Target: 33% (National Forest Policy 1988)
- **Most forest:** Madhya Pradesh (total area)
- **Highest % forest cover:** Mizoram (>84%), Lakshadweep (90%)

**UP's Forest:**
- Terai Sal forests (northern UP — Gorakhpur, Lakhimpur, Pilibhit)
- Sonbhadra = most forested district in UP
- Total forest cover ~9.2% of UP area

**Examiner Traps:**
- Sundarbans (WB+Bangladesh) = largest mangrove forest in the world
- Khejri tree = State tree of Rajasthan; Bishnoi community protects it
- Teak = Deciduous (NOT evergreen); best teak = MP and Maharashtra
