## 📖 Thematic Revision Summary (Reformatted from PDF)

### Tropical Cyclones

**Formation:**
- Warm ocean water (>26°C), high humidity, Coriolis effect
- Anti-clockwise in Northern Hemisphere; Clockwise in Southern

**Regional Names:**
| Region | Name |
|--------|------|
| India/Bay of Bengal | Cyclone |
| Atlantic/Caribbean | Hurricane |
| Western Pacific/China Sea | Typhoon |
| Australia | Willy-Willy |

**Cyclone-prone regions in India:** Eastern coast (Bay of Bengal) — Odisha, AP, Tamil Nadu, WB

**NDMA Zones:**
- **High Risk Cyclone:** Andhra Pradesh, Odisha, Tamil Nadu, Gujarat (western coast also)

**Earthquakes:**
- **Seismic Zones in India (I to V):**
  - Zone V (highest): NE India, J&K, Uttarakhand, parts of Gujarat (Kutch)
  - Zone IV: UP (partially), Delhi, Himachal
  - Zone III: Kerala, Deccan

**Floods in India:**
- Flood-prone: Assam (Brahmaputra), Bihar (Kosi — "Sorrow of Bihar"), UP (Ghaghra/Rapti), Odisha

**Droughts:**
- Drought-prone: Rajasthan, Maharashtra (Marathwada), Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka

**India Disaster Institutes:**
- NDMA: National Disaster Management Authority (under PM)
- NDRF: National Disaster Response Force (10 battalions)

**Examiner Traps:**
- Kosi = "Sorrow of Bihar" (NOT Sorrow of Bengal — that's Damodar)
- Damodar = "Sorrow of Bengal"
- Cyclones are anti-clockwise in Northern Hemisphere (Coriolis effect)
