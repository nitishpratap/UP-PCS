## 📖 Core Thematic Revision (PCS Matrix Complete Notes)

- Mountain Ranges (Location & Highest Peak - पर्वत श्रृंखलाएँ एविं उच्चतिम चोटिियाँ):
- Andes: South America → Highest Peak: Aconcagua (दक्षिण अमेरिका की सबसे लंबी पवर्पतमाला).
- Rockies: North America → Highest Peak: Mount Elbert (Note: Denali / Mount McKinley in Alaska
- is the highest peak in North America).
- Alps: Europe → Highest Peak: Mont Blanc.
- Atlas Mountains: North Africa → Highest Peak: Toubkal (Morocco).
- Ural Mountains: Natural boundary separating Europe and Asia (यूरोप और एशिया की प्राकृतिक सीमा).
- Great Dividing Range: Australia → Highest Peak: Mount Kosciuszko.
- Drakensberg: South Africa (दक्षिण अफ्रीका).
- Caucasus Mountains: Lies between the Black Sea and Caspian Sea → Highest Peak: Mount
- Elbrus (यूरोप की उच्चतम चोटी).
