## 📖 Core Thematic Revision (PCS Matrix Complete Notes)

### Indus River System (सिंधु नदी तंत्र):
- **Jhelum:** Verinag Spring (Kashmir)
- **Chenab:** Near Bara Lacha La
- **Ravi:** Near Rohtang Pass
- **Beas:** Beas Kund
- **Sutlej:** Rakshas Tal near Mansarovar

### Ganga River System (गंगा नदी तंत्र):
- **Left Bank Tributaries:** Ramganga, Gomti (Origin: Fulhar Jheel, Pilibhit — मैदानी भाग से उद्गम), Ghaghara, Gandak, Kosi (बिहार का शोक).
- **Right Bank Tributaries:** Yamuna, Son, Punpun.
- **Yamuna Tributaries (W to E Order):** Chambal $\rightarrow$ Sind $\rightarrow$ Betwa $\rightarrow$ Ken.

### Panch Prayag (पंच प्रयाग — Origin Order):
- **Vishnuprayag:** Alaknanda + Dhauliganga
- **Nandaprayag:** Alaknanda + Nandakini
- **Karnaprayag:** Alaknanda + Pindar
- **Rudraprayag:** Alaknanda + Mandakini
- **Devprayag:** Alaknanda + Bhagirathi (यहाँ से गंगा कहलाती है)

### Peninsular Rivers (प्रायद्वीपीय नदियाँ):
- **East-Flowing:** Mahanadi, Godavari (Longest peninsular — दक्षिण गंगा; Tribs: Penganga, Indravati, Pranhita), Krishna (Tribs: Bhima, Tungabhadra, Koyna), Cauvery (Tribs: Kabini, Bhavani, Amaravati).
- **West-Flowing:** Narmada (Rift Valley; Amarkantak), Tapti (Rift Valley; Multai), Sabarmati, Mandovi/Zuari (Goa), Mahi (Crosses Tropic of Cancer twice — कर्क रेखा को दो बार काटने वाली).
