## 📖 Thematic Revision Summary (Reformatted from PDF)

### Atmosphere — Composition

**Gases:** Nitrogen (78%), Oxygen (21%), Argon (0.93%), CO₂ (0.04%)

**Layers:**
| Layer | Height | Key Feature |
|-------|--------|------------|
| Troposphere | 0–12 km | Weather phenomena; temperature decreases with altitude |
| Stratosphere | 12–50 km | Ozone layer (15–35 km); jet streams; temperature increases |
| Mesosphere | 50–80 km | Coldest layer; meteors burn here |
| Thermosphere | 80–600 km | Ionosphere (radio wave reflection); auroras |
| Exosphere | 600+ km | Satellite zone; merges with space |

**Global Wind Patterns:**
| Wind Belt | Latitude | Name |
|-----------|---------|------|
| Trade Winds | 0–30° (both hemispheres) | NE Trades (N. hemisphere), SE Trades (S. hemisphere) |
| Westerlies | 30–60° | Blow west to east |
| Polar Easterlies | 60–90° | Cold polar winds |

**Jet Streams:** High-altitude (10–16 km), fast-moving westerly air currents; affect aircraft routes; 2 types in India (subtropical + polar front)

**Local Winds:**
| Wind | Region |
|------|--------|
| Loo | Hot dry wind in UP/Rajasthan in summer |
| Kaal Baisakhi | NE storms in West Bengal (April–May) |
| Mango Showers | Pre-monsoon showers in Kerala/Karnataka |
| Cherry Blossom Rains | Karnataka (coffee cultivation) |

**Examiner Traps:**
- Ozone layer is in Stratosphere (NOT Troposphere)
- Weather occurs in Troposphere
- Jet streams = Stratosphere (upper part) / Tropopause region

## ❓ Previous Years Questions (UPPCS Prelims)


**Q. UKPCS Prelims 2025, Q96**

According to Koppen's climatic classification, the (Amw) climate is found predominantly in which part of India?

A. North-Western India
B. Western coastal region
C. Thar (थार) desert
D. Northern plain

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Logic:** A = tropical, w = dry winter — western coastal strip.

**Ans: B.** Amw marks the western coastal strip (and parts of the north-east). Thar is BWhw; northern plain largely Cwg.

</details>

**Q. UKPCS Prelims 2025, Q98**

Which local wind affects some parts of Kerala and Tamil Nadu (नाडु) during the pre-monsoon season?

A. Loo
B. Mango Shower
C. Kal Baisakhi (बैसाखी)
D. Mistral

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Logic:** Loo and Kal Baisakhi are northern — southern pre-monsoon is mango shower.

**Ans: B.** Mango showers help ripen mangoes in Kerala and coastal Tamil Nadu.

</details>

**Q1. UPPCS Prelims 2025, Q45**
With reference to the ozone layer, which of the following statements is/are correct?

1. The ozone layer protects the Earth's surface from ultraviolet radiation coming from the Sun.
2. Ozone depletion has been linked to chlorofluorocarbons (CFCs).

A. Only 2

B. Neither 1 nor 2

C. Both 1 and 2

D. Only 1

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: C** — Both true. Protective ozone is in the **stratosphere**. CFCs linked to depletion. **D** drops CFCs; **A** drops UV shield.
</details>
