## 📖 Thematic Revision Summary (Reformatted from PDF)

### Major World Rivers

| River | Length | Source | Drains Into | Notable |
|-------|--------|--------|------------|---------|
| Nile | 6,650 km (longest) | Great Lakes region, Africa | Mediterranean | Egypt's lifeline |
| Amazon | 7,062 km (by some measures) | Peru Andes | Atlantic | Largest by volume; rainforest |
| Yangtze | 6,300 km | Tibet | Pacific | Longest in Asia; China |
| Mississippi-Missouri | 6,275 km | Minnesota, USA | Gulf of Mexico | — |
| Yenisei | 5,539 km | Mongolia | Arctic Ocean | — |
| Yellow River (Huang He) | 5,464 km | Tibet | Yellow Sea | "China's Sorrow" — floods |
| Ob-Irtysh | 5,410 km | Altai, Russia | Arctic | — |
| Congo (Zaire) | 4,700 km | Central Africa | Atlantic | 2nd largest by volume |
| Volga | 3,700 km | Russia | Caspian Sea | Longest in Europe |
| Rhine | — | Switzerland | North Sea | Industrial heartland of Europe |
| Danube | — | Germany | Black Sea | Passes through most countries (10) |
| Murray-Darling | — | Australia | Southern Ocean | Most important river system in Australia |

**Rivers & Their Civilizations:**
- Nile → Egyptian civilization
- Tigris + Euphrates → Mesopotamian civilization (Iraq)
- Indus → Indus Valley Civilization
- Huang He → Chinese civilization

**Examiner Traps:**
- Nile = longest (6,650 km); Amazon may be longer by some measurements but NOT standard
- Volga = Europe's longest river (into Caspian Sea — an inland sea, NOT an ocean)
- Rhine ≠ Danube; Rhine flows north; Danube flows east

## ❓ Previous Years Questions (UPPCS Prelims)


**Q. UKPCS Prelims 2025, Q94**

The Grand Canyon of the U.S.A. is formed by which river?

A. St. Lawrence (लॉरेंस)
B. Missouri
C. Ohio
D. Colorado

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Logic:** Only one of the four (चातुर्याम) North American rivers cut the Arizona canyon.

**Ans: D (Series B provisional key).** The Colorado River cut the Grand Canyon through the Colorado Plateau.

</details>

**Q1. UPPCS Prelims 2025, Q68**
Which of the following pairs are NOT correctly matched?
(City) — (River)

1. Budapest — Danube
2. Cologne — Rhine
3. New Orleans — Missouri
4. Hyderabad (India) — Musi

A. Only 1, 2 and 3

B. Only 3 and 4

C. Only 2, 3 and 4

D. Only 1 and 2

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans (facts):** 1 and 2 matched. 3 unmatched — New Orleans = **Mississippi**. 4 matched — Hyderabad = **Musi**.
Paper options have **no “Only 3”**. Do not treat Musi as wrong. Some series print **Paleru** as 4 → then unmatched = 3 and 4 → **B**.
</details>

**Q2. UPPCS Prelims 2024, Q77**
Which one of the following pairs (River - Direction) is not correctly matched?

Options:

A. Amur River - North-East

B. Syr Darya - North-West

C. Angara River - North

D. Mekong River - South-West

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: D** — Mekong flows **south / south-east**, not south-west.
