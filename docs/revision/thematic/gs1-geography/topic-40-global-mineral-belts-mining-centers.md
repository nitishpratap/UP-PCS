## 📖 Core Thematic Revision (PCS Matrix Complete Notes)

### Iron Ore (लौह अयस्क):
- Pilbara region: Australia.
- Kiruna & Gällivare: Sweden.
- Magnitogorsk: Russia.
- Minas Gerais: Brazil.

### Coal Fields (कोयला क्षिेत्र):
- Donbas region: Ukraine.
- Kuznetsk/Kuzbass: Russia.
- Ruhr Valley & Saar: Germany (रूर घाटी - प्रसिद्ध कोयला पट्टी).
- Appalachian Region: USA.
- Shanxi & Shaanxi: China.

### Copper (तिाँबा):
- Chuquicamata & El Teniente: Chile (World's top producer - विश्व की सबसे बड़ी ताँबा खदान).
- Katanga/Copperbelt: DR Congo / Zambia.

### Gold & Diamond (स्विणर्श एविं हीरा):
- Witwatersrand & Kimberley: South Africa (विटवाटरसैंड - सोना; किम्बरले - हीरा).
- Kalgoorlie & Coolgardie: Australia (Gold mining towns - सोने की खदानें).
- Yakutia / Mirny: Russia (Diamond open-pit mines).

### Petroleum & Natural Gas (पेट्रोलियम एविं प्राकृतििक गैसि):
- Ghawar Field: Saudi Arabia (World's largest oil field).
- Burgan Field: Kuwait.
- Baku: Azerbaijan.
- Permian Basin: USA.
- North Sea: UK / Norway.
