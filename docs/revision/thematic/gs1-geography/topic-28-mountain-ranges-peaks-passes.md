## 📖 Core Thematic Revision (PCS Matrix Complete Notes)

### Mountain Ranges Order (North to South / उत्तर से दक्षिण क्रम):
1. **Karakoram Range** (काराकोरम श्रेणी)
2. **Ladakh Range** (लद्दाख श्रेणी)
3. **Zaskar Range** (जास्कर श्रेणी)
4. **Pir Panjal Range** (पीर पंजाल श्रेणी)
5. **Dhauladhar Range** (धौलाधार श्रेणी)
6. **Shivalik Range** (शिवालिक श्रेणी)

### Passes (दर्रे) vs. States / UTs:
- **Ladakh / J&K:** Zoji La, Khardung La, Banihal Pass, Aghil Pass.
- **Himachal Pradesh:** Bara Lacha La, Shipki La (सतलुज नदी का प्रवेश द्वार), Rohtang Pass.
- **Uttarakhand:** Mana Pass, Niti Pass, Lipulekh (कैलाश मानसरोवर यात्रा मार्ग), Dharma Pass.
- **Sikkim:** Nathu La, Jelep La.
- **Arunachal Pradesh:** Bom Di La, Diphu Pass, Dihang Pass, Yonggyap Pass.

### Highest Mountain Peaks (प्रमुख पर्वत चोटियाँ):
- **K2 / Godwin-Austen (8,611 m):** Highest peak in India (Karakoram Range, PoK).
- **Kanchenjunga (8,586 m):** Highest peak in India's undivided territory (Sikkim).
- **Nanda Devi:** Garhwal Himalayas, Uttarakhand.
- **Anamudi (2,695 m):** Anaimalai Hills, Kerala (Highest peak of South India & Western Ghats).
- **Doda Betta (2,637 m):** Nilgiri Hills, Tamil Nadu.
