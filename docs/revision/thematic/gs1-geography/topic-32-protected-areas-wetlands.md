## 📖 Core Thematic Revision (PCS Matrix Complete Notes)

### UP Specific Protected Areas (उत्तर प्रदेश के संरक्षित क्षेत्र):
- **Dudhwa National Park:** Lakhimpur Kheri (UP's only National Park).
- **Pilibhit Tiger Reserve:** TX2 international award for doubling tiger population.
- **Ranipur Tiger Reserve:** Chitrakoot (4th Tiger Reserve of UP).
- **Chandraprabha Wildlife Sanctuary:** Chandauli (UP's 1st WLS).

### Biosphere Reserves & Tiger Reserves:
- **Biosphere Reserves:** Nilgiri (1986 — 1st in India), Nokrek (Meghalaya), Cold Desert (HP), Similipal (Odisha), Agasthyamala (TN/KL), Great Nicobar.
- **Tiger Reserves in News:** Ramgarh Vishdhari (RJ), Guru Ghasidas (CG), Dampa (MZ), Pakke (AR), Bandipur & Nagarhole (KA).

### Ramsar Wetland Sites:
- **UP Sites:** Haiderpur Wetland, Bakhira Sanctuary, Nawabganj Bird Sanctuary, Samaspur Bird Sanctuary.
- **Key National Sites:** Keoladeo Ghana (Montreux Record), Chilika Lake (1st Ramsar site in India).
