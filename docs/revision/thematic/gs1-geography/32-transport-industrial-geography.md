## 📖 Thematic Revision Summary (Reformatted from PDF)

### India's Transport Networks

**Railways:**
- Largest railway network in Asia; 4th largest in world
- Indian Railways zones: 18 zones; HQ in various cities
- Broad Gauge (1.676 m) = most common

**Key Railway Zones (UP relevant):**
- Northern Railway: New Delhi HQ (covers major UP cities)
- North-Eastern Railway: Gorakhpur HQ (Gorakhpur station = world's longest railway platform)
- East Central Railway: Hajipur HQ

**Roadways:**
- National Highways: NH 44 (Srinagar–Kanyakumari) = longest NH in India
- **UP Expressways:**
  - Yamuna Expressway (165 km): Noida–Agra
  - Agra-Lucknow Expressway (302 km)
  - Purvanchal Expressway (341 km): Lucknow–Ghazipur
  - Ganga Expressway (594 km — under construction): Meerut–Prayagraj (longest in India)
  - Bundelkhand Expressway (296 km): Chitrakoot–Etawah

**Industrial Regions of India:**
| Region | Industries |
|--------|-----------|
| Mumbai-Pune | Cotton textiles, Petrochemicals, IT |
| Hooghly (WB) | Jute, Paper |
| Ahmedabad-Baroda | Textiles, Chemicals |
| Delhi-Agra-Meerut | Light engineering, Electronics |
| Chota Nagpur (Jharkhand) | Iron-Steel, Mining |

**UP Industries:**
- Sugar Industry: UP = No. 1 (Gorakhpur, Muzaffarnagar belt)
- Leather: Kanpur, Agra
- Carpet: Bhadohi (GI Tag)
- Locks: Aligarh
- Brass: Moradabad
- Glassware: Firozabad

**Q. UKPCS Prelims 2025, Q88**

Which of the following ports is on the western coastline?

A. Nhava Sheva
B. Visakhapatnam
C. Chennai
D. Tuticorin

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Logic:** Three eastern ports — one western settles it.

**Ans: A.** Nhava Sheva (JNPT) near Mumbai is western; the other three are eastern.

Which country has the highest density of railway network?

A. Canada
B. U.S.A.
C. Russia
D. India

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: B.** Among the given options, the answer is the **U.S.A.** India is a common distractor on railway-density stems.
