## 📖 Thematic Revision Summary (Reformatted from PDF)

### Earth's Interior

**Layers:**
| Layer | Depth | Composition |
|-------|-------|------------|
| Crust | 0–35 km | SIAL (Silicon + Aluminium); oceanic = SIMA |
| Mantle | 35–2900 km | Semi-solid; source of magma |
| Outer Core | 2900–5100 km | Liquid Iron + Nickel; generates magnetic field |
| Inner Core | 5100–6370 km | Solid Iron + Nickel; highest temperature |

**Discontinuities:**
- **Moho (Mohorovicic):** Crust–Mantle boundary
- **Gutenberg:** Mantle–Core boundary
- **Lehmann:** Outer–Inner core boundary

**Plate Tectonics:**
- **Convergent plates:** Oceanic + Continental → subduction → mountains (Andes)
- **Divergent plates:** Moving apart → Mid-oceanic ridges (Atlantic Ridge)
- **Transform plates:** Sliding past → earthquakes (San Andreas Fault)

**Indian Plate:** Moving NE at ~5 cm/year; collision with Eurasian plate formed Himalayas (Alpine-Himalayan belt)

**Seismic Zones in India:** Zone V (highest risk) = NE India, J&K, Uttarakhand, parts of Gujarat

**Examiner Traps:**
- Himalayas = fold mountains (not block or volcanic)
- Deepest point = Mariana Trench (Pacific) — 11,034 m
- Indian Plate includes Indian subcontinent + part of Indian Ocean floor

**Q. UKPCS Prelims 2025, Q95**

Which planet takes the longest time completing one revolution around the sun?

A. Saturn
B. Uranus
C. Neptune
D. Jupiter

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Logic:** Farthest planet among the four (चातुर्याम) has the longest year.

**Ans: C (Series B provisional key).** Neptune (~165 Earth years) outranks Uranus, Saturn and Jupiter.

</details>
