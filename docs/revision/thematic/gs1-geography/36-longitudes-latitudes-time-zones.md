## 📖 Thematic Revision Summary (Reformatted from PDF)

### Latitudes & Longitudes

**Key Latitudes:**
| Latitude | Name | Key Countries Passing Through |
|---------|------|------------------------------|
| 0° | Equator | Ecuador, Colombia, Brazil, Gabon, Congo, Kenya, Somalia, Indonesia |
| 23.5° N | Tropic of Cancer | Mexico, Libya, Egypt, Saudi Arabia, India, China |
| 23.5° S | Tropic of Capricorn | Chile, Brazil, South Africa, Australia |
| 66.5° N | Arctic Circle | Norway, Sweden, Finland, Russia, Canada, Alaska |
| 66.5° S | Antarctic Circle | Antarctica |
| 90° N/S | Poles | |

**Indian Latitudes:**
- India extends from 8°4'N to 37°6'N
- Tropic of Cancer (23.5°N) passes through 8 states: Gujarat, Rajasthan, MP, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, WB, Tripura, Mizoram

**Key Longitudes (Prime Meridian = 0°):**
- Countries on Prime Meridian: UK, France, Spain, Algeria, Mali, Burkina Faso, Togo, Ghana, Antarctica
- India's Standard Meridian: 82.5°E (IST = UTC +5:30)
- IST passes through: UP (Mirzapur, considered reference point), MP, Chhattisgarh, Odisha, AP

**Time Zones:**
- Earth = 360° ÷ 24 hours = 15° per hour = 1° per 4 minutes
- India = single time zone (82.5°E); difference from GMT = +5:30

**Examiner Traps:**
- IST reference point = Mirzapur (or Naini, near Prayagraj), UP (82.5°E)
- Tropic of Cancer passes through 8 Indian STATES (not 7 or 9)
- India does NOT observe daylight saving time

**Q. UKPCS Prelims 2025, Q95**

Which planet takes the longest time completing one revolution around the sun?

A. Saturn
B. Uranus
C. Neptune
D. Jupiter

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Logic:** Farthest planet among the four (चातुर्याम) has the longest year.

**Ans: C (Series B provisional key).** Neptune (~165 Earth years) outranks Uranus, Saturn and Jupiter.

</details>
