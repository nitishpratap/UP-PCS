## 📖 Core Thematic Revision (PCS Matrix Complete Notes)

### Optics (प्रकाश:की):
- Reflection & Mirrors (दपर्शण):
- Concave Mirror (अवतल दपर्पण): Used by dentists, in solar cookers, and headlights of vehicles (दाढ़ी
- बनाने तथा हेडलाइट में).
- Convex Mirror (उत्तल दपर्पण): Used as rear-view mirrors in vehicles (वाहनों के साइड मिरर में) due to a wider
- field of view.
- Refraction Phenomena (अपवतर्पन की घटनाएँ): Twinkling of stars (तारों का टिमटिमाना), apparent bending of a
- stick in water, early sunrise and delayed sunset.
- Total Internal Reflection - TIR (पूणर्श आंतिरिक परावितिर्शन):
- Examples: Sparkling of diamonds (हीरे का चमकना), Mirage in deserts (मरीचिका), working of Optical
- Fibers (ऑɣप्टकल फाइबर).
- Scattering of Light (प्रकाश का प्रकीणर्पन): Blue color of the sky and red color of the sun at sunrise/
- sunset (governed by Rayleigh Scattering: I ∝ 1/λ⁴).
- Lenses & Eye Defects (दृषि्टि दोष एविं निविारण):
- Myopia (Nearsightedness - निकट दृषि्ट दोष): Corrected using a Concave Lens (अवतल लेंस).
- Hypermetropia (Farsightedness - दूर दृषि्ट दोष): Corrected using a Convex Lens (उत्तल लेंस).
- Presbyopia (जरा दूरदृषि्टता): Corrected using Bifocal Lenses.

### Heat & Thermodynamics (ऊष्मा एविं उष्मागतििकी):
- Latent Heat (गुप्त ऊष्मा): Heat required to change state without temperature change (Latent heat
- of fusion of ice = 80 cal/g; Latent heat of vaporization of water = 540 cal/g).
- Anomalous Expansion of Water (जल का असामान्य प्रसार): Water has maximum density and minimum
- volume at 4°C.

### Sound Waves (ध्विनि तिरंगें):
- Nature: Longitudinal mechanical waves (अनुदैध्यर्प यांति्रिक तरंगें); cannot travel through a vacuum (निवार्पत में
- गमन नहीं कर सकतीं).
- Frequency Classification (आविृति्त विगर्गीकरण):
- Infrasonic (अपश्रव्य): < 20 Hz.
- Audible Range (श्रव्य तरंगें): 20 Hz - 20,000 Hz.
- Ultrasonic (पराश्रव्य): > 20,000 Hz (Used in SONAR, Ultrasonography, and by bats).
- Speed of Sound: Fastest in Solids > Liquids > Gases.
