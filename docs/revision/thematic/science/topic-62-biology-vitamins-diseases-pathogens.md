### Diseases & Causative Pathogens (प्रमुख रोग एविं कारक):
- Bacterial Diseases (जीवाणु जनित रोग): Tuberculosis (TB / तपेदिक - caused by Mycobacterium
- tuberculosis), Cholera (हैजा), Typhoid (टाइफाइड), Tetanus (टिटनेस), Diphtheria, Leprosy (कुष्ठ रोग).
- Viral Diseases (विषाणु जनित रोग): AIDS (caused by HIV - tested via ELISA Test), Dengue (transmitted
- by Aedes aegypti mosquito), Rabies (Hydrophobia), Polio, Smallpox, Hepatitis.
- Protozoan Diseases (प्रोटिोजोआ जनिति रोग):
- Malaria (मलेरिया): Caused by Plasmodium; transmitted by female Anopheles mosquito; treated
- with Quinine (कुनैन - extracted from Cinchona bark).
- Kala-azar (कालाजार): Transmitted by the Sandfly (बालू मक्खी).
- Sleeping Sickness (निद्रा रोग): Transmitted by the Tsetse fly (सीसी मक्खी).
