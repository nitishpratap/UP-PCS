## 📖 Core Thematic Revision (PCS Matrix Complete Notes)

### Digestive System (पाचन तिंत्र):
- Mouth (मुख गुहा): Digestion of carbohydrates begins here with the enzyme Salivary Amylase /
- Ptyalin (टाइलिन).
- Stomach (आमाशय): Secretes Pepsin (digests proteins) and Renin (digests milk proteins); highly
- acidic due to HCl.
- Small Intestine (छोटी आंत): Site of complete digestion and absorption of food via microvilli (पचे हुए
- भोजन का पूणर्प अवशोषण).
- Liver (यकृत): Largest gland in the human body; secretes Bile juice (पित्त रस - stored in the
- Gallbladder) which emulsifies fats.

### Circulatory System (परिसिंचरण तिंत्र):

### Blood Composition (रक्ति का संघटिन):
- Plasma (प्लाज्मा): Liquid portion (~55%).
- RBCs (Erythrocytes - लाल रक्त कशिणकाएं): Contain Hemoglobin; lifespan 120 days; produced in bone
- marrow, destroyed in the Spleen (प्लीहा / R.B.C. का कबि्रस्तान).
- WBCs (Leukocytes - श्वेत रक्त कशिणकाएं): Body's defense mechanism / immunity (रोग प्रतिरोधक क्षिमता);
- lifespan 2-4 days.
- Platelets (Thrombocytes - रक्त बिम्बाणु): Help in blood clotting (रक्त का थक्का जमाना - aided by Vitamin K
- and Calcium).
- Blood Groups (रक्त समूह): Discovered by Karl Landsteiner.
- Universal Donor (सवर्पदाता): O negative (O⁻).
- Universal Acceptor (सवर्पग्राही): AB positive (AB⁺).

### Excretory & Nervous Systems (उत्सिजर्शन एविं तिंति्रका तिंत्र):
- Kidney (वृक्क): Functional unit is the Nephron (नेफ्रॉन). Filters urea from blood.
- Brain (मसि्तष्क): Functional unit of the nervous system is the Neuron (न्यूरॉन).
- Cerebrum (प्रमसि्तष्क): Largest part; seat of intelligence, memory, and consciousness.
- Hypothalamus: Regulates body temperature, hunger, thirst, and emotions (शरीर का तापमान नियंत्रिक).
- Medulla Oblongata: Controls involuntary actions (अनै्च्छक कि्रयाएं) like heartbeat, respiration, and
- blood pressure.
