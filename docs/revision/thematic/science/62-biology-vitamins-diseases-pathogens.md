## 📖 Thematic Revision Summary (Reformatted from PDF)

### Vitamins

| Vitamin | Chemical Name | Deficiency Disease | Main Source |
|---------|--------------|-------------------|------------|
| Vitamin A | Retinol | Night Blindness, Xerophthalmia | Milk, Fish, Carrots, Mango |
| Vitamin B1 | Thiamine | Beriberi | Whole grains, Legumes |
| Vitamin B3 | Niacin | Pellagra (3D: Dermatitis, Diarrhoea, Dementia) | Meat, Fish, Whole wheat |
| Vitamin B12 | Cyanocobalamin | Pernicious Anaemia | Meat, Fish, Dairy |
| Vitamin C | Ascorbic Acid | Scurvy | Amla, Citrus fruits |
| Vitamin D | Calciferol | Rickets (children), Osteomalacia (adults) | Sunlight, Milk, Fish liver oil |
| Vitamin E | Tocopherol | Infertility, Reduced Fertility | Green vegetables, Wheat germ oil |
| Vitamin K | Phylloquinone | Delayed Blood Clotting | Green vegetables, Soybeans |

**Fat-Soluble:** A, D, E, K | **Water-Soluble:** B, C

### Diseases & Pathogens

**Bacterial Diseases:**
- Tuberculosis (TB): Mycobacterium tuberculosis
- Cholera: Vibrio cholerae
- Typhoid: Salmonella typhi
- Tetanus: Clostridium tetani
- Leprosy: Mycobacterium leprae

**Viral Diseases:**
- AIDS: HIV (tested via ELISA test)
- Dengue: Aedes aegypti mosquito (day-biting)
- Rabies (Hydrophobia): Rhabdovirus
- Polio: Poliovirus

**Protozoan Diseases:**
- Malaria: Plasmodium; female Anopheles mosquito; treated with Quinine (Cinchona bark)
- Kala-azar: Leishmania donovani; Sandfly (Phlebotomus)
- Sleeping Sickness: Trypanosoma; Tsetse fly

**Examiner Traps:**
- Dengue = Aedes mosquito (day-biting), NOT Anopheles
- Malaria = Anopheles (female, night-biting)
- Quinine for malaria comes from Cinchona bark (not Neem)
- Vitamin B12 = Pernicious Anaemia (NOT regular anaemia)
