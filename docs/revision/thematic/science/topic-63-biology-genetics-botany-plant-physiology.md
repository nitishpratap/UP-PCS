## 📖 Core Thematic Revision (PCS Matrix Complete Notes)

### Genetics & Cell Biology (आनुवंशिक:की एविं कोशिका:का वििज्ञान):
- Father of Genetics (आनुवंशिकी के जनक): Gregor Johann Mendel (Experimented on Garden Pea plants
- मटर के पौधे पर प्रयोग).
- Cell Powerhouse (कोशिकाशका का पावरहाउस): Mitochondria (Site of ATP synthesis via Cellular
- Respiration).
- Protein Factory (प्रोटीन की फैक्ट्री): Ribosome.
- Suicide Bags (आत्मघाती थैली): Lysosome.
- Chromosomes in Humans (मनुष्य में गुणसूत्रि): 46 chromosomes (23 pairs - 22 pairs of Autosomes + 1
- pair of Sex Chromosomes: XX in females, XY in males).

### Plant Physiology & Hormones (पादप कायिंकी एविं हामर्मोन):

### Vascular Tissues (पादप ऊतिक):
- Xylem (जाइलम): Transports Water and Minerals (जल एवं खनिज लवण) upward from roots.
- Phloem (फ्लोएम): Transports Prepared Food / Sugars (भोजन) from leaves to other parts.

### Photosynthesis (प्रका: सिंश्लेषण):
- Process: 6CO₂ + 12H₂O ➔ (Sunlight, Chlorophyll) ➔ C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ + 6H₂O.
- xygen released during photosynthesis comes from the splitting of Water (H₂O), not CO₂.

### Plant Hormones (पादप हामर्मोन):
- Auxin (ऑ्क्सन): Controls cell elongation and growth toward light (Phototropism).
- Gibberellins (ɣजबरेलिन): Promotes stem elongation and seed germination (अंकुरण).
- Cytokinin (साइटोकाइनिन): Promotes cell division (कोशिकाशका विभाजन).
- Ethylene (इथिलीन): Only gaseous hormone; promotes natural ripening of fruits (फलों को पकाना).
- Abscisic Acid - ABA (ए्Íससिक अम्ल): Growth inhibitor (वृशिद्ध रोधक हामर्वोन); causes wilting of leaves and
- seed dormancy.
