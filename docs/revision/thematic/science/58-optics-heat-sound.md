## 📖 Thematic Revision Summary (Reformatted from PDF)

### Optics (प्रकाशिकी)

**Mirrors:**
| Mirror | Use |
|--------|-----|
| Concave (अवतल) | Dentists, solar cookers, headlights (converging) |
| Convex (उत्तल) | Rear-view mirrors in vehicles (wider field) |

**Refraction Phenomena:**
- Twinkling of stars (atmospheric refraction)
- Apparent bending of stick in water
- Early sunrise and delayed sunset

**Total Internal Reflection (TIR):**
- Sparkling of diamonds, Mirages in deserts, Optical Fibers

**Scattering of Light:**
- Blue colour of sky: Rayleigh Scattering (I ∝ 1/λ⁴) — short wavelength (blue) scatters more
- Red colour at sunrise/sunset: blue light scattered away; only red (long wavelength) reaches eyes

**Eye Defects:**
| Defect | Problem | Correction |
|--------|---------|-----------|
| Myopia (Nearsighted) | Distant objects blurry | Concave lens |
| Hypermetropia (Farsighted) | Near objects blurry | Convex lens |
| Presbyopia | Both near & far blurry (old age) | Bifocal lenses |

### Heat & Thermodynamics

- **Latent Heat of Fusion of ice:** 80 cal/g
- **Latent Heat of Vaporization of water:** 540 cal/g
- **Anomalous Expansion of Water:** Water has maximum density at 4°C

### Sound Waves

- **Longitudinal mechanical waves** — cannot travel in vacuum
- **Frequency Classification:**
  - Infrasonic: < 20 Hz
  - Audible Range: 20 Hz – 20,000 Hz
  - Ultrasonic: > 20,000 Hz (used in SONAR, Ultrasonography)
- **Speed of Sound:** Fastest in Solids > Liquids > Gases

**Examiner Traps:**
- Sky appears blue because of SCATTERING (not reflection/refraction)
- Sound CANNOT travel in vacuum; light CAN
- Concave = converging = used in headlights; Convex = diverging = rear-view mirror

| Quantities / Optical Concepts | Critical Distinction & Common Trap Alert |
|---|---|
| **Concave vs Convex Mirror Uses** | **Concave mirror**: Shaving, dentist, car headlight (produces magnified erect image up close, parallel beam at focus). **Convex mirror**: Automobile rear-view mirror (erect, diminished, wide field of view). |
| **Myopia vs Hypermetropia** | **Myopia (Near-sightedness)**: Can see near, cannot see far; image formed *in front of retina*; corrected by **Concave lens**. **Hypermetropia (Far-sightedness)**: Can see far, cannot see near; image formed *behind retina*; corrected by **Convex lens**. |
| **Air Bubble in Water** | Physically shaped as a convex sphere, but optically behaves as a **diverging / concave lens** because light travels through water ($\mu=1.33$) into air ($\mu=1.0$). |
| **Additive vs Subtractive Colors** | **Additive (Light)**: Red + Green + Blue = **White**. **Subtractive (Pigments/Paint)**: Cyan + Magenta + Yellow = **Black**. |
| **Rayleigh Scattering vs Dispersion** | **Scattering**: Redirection of light in all directions by particles smaller than $\lambda$ ($I \propto 1/\lambda^4$; makes sky blue). **Dispersion**: Splitting of white light into constituent wavelengths by refraction in a prism or raindrop. |
| **Rainbow at Noon** | Rainbow is formed directly opposite the Sun. At 12:00 noon, the Sun is directly overhead; therefore, a natural rainbow **cannot be seen**. |
