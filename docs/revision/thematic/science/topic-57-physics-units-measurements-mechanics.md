## 📖 Core Thematic Revision (PCS Matrix Complete Notes)

### Units & Physical Quantities (भौतििक राशि:याँ एविं मात्रक):
- SI Base Units (मूल मात्रिक): Length (Meter), Mass (Kilogram), Time (Second), Electric Current
- (Ampere), Temperature (Kelvin), Luminous Intensity (Candela), Amount of Substance (Mole).
- Derived Units (व्युत्पन्न मात्रक):
- Work / Energy (कायर्प / ऊजार्प): Joule
- Power (श्क्त): Watt
- Force (बल): Newton
- Pressure (दाब): Pascal
- Astronomical Distance (खगोलीय दूरी): Light Year

### Newton's Laws of Motion (न्यूटिन के गतिि के नियम):
- First Law (Law of Inertia - जड़त्व का नियम): An object remains at rest or in uniform motion unless
- acted upon by an external force (e.g., passenger jerking backward when a bus starts).
- Second Law (F = ma): Force equals mass times acceleration (e.g., a cricketer pulling hands back
- while catching a ball).
- Third Law (Action-Reaction - कि्रया-प्रतिकि्रया का नियम): For every action, there is an equal and opposite
- reaction (e.g., Rocket propulsion, recoil of a gun).

### Gravitation & Motion (गुरुत्विाकषर्शण एविं गतिि):
- Acceleration due to Gravity (g - गुरुत्वीय त्वरण): Standard value is 9.8 m/s². Maximum at the Poles
- (ध्रुवों पर अधिकतम), minimum at the Equator (विषुवत् रेखा पर न्यूनतम), and zero at the Earth's center (पृथ्वी के
- केंद्र पर शून्य).
- Escape Velocity (vₑ - पलायन वेग): Minimum velocity needed to escape Earth's gravitational field =
- 11.2 km/s.
- rbital Velocity of Satellite (उपग्रह का कक्षिीय वेग): ~7.9 km/s.
