## 📖 Thematic Revision Summary (Reformatted from PDF)

### Genetics & Cell Biology

**Key Figures:**
- Father of Genetics: Gregor Johann Mendel (experimented on garden pea)
- Cell Powerhouse: Mitochondria (ATP synthesis via Cellular Respiration)
- Protein Factory: Ribosome
- Suicide Bags: Lysosome
- Control Room: Nucleus

**Human Chromosomes:**
- Total: 46 (23 pairs) = 22 pairs Autosomes + 1 pair Sex chromosomes
- Females: XX | Males: XY

### Plant Vascular Tissues

| Tissue | Function |
|--------|---------|
| Xylem (जाइलम) | Transports water & minerals upward from roots |
| Phloem (फ्लोएम) | Transports prepared food (sugars) from leaves to other parts |

### Photosynthesis (प्रकाश संश्लेषण)

**Equation:** 6CO₂ + 12H₂O → (Sunlight, Chlorophyll) → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ + 6H₂O

**Key point:** Oxygen released comes from **water (H₂O)**, NOT CO₂

### Plant Hormones (पादप हार्मोन)

| Hormone | Function |
|---------|---------|
| Auxin (ऑक्सिन) | Cell elongation; growth toward light (Phototropism) |
| Gibberellins (जिबरेलिन) | Stem elongation; seed germination |
| Cytokinin (साइटोकाइनिन) | Cell division |
| Ethylene (इथिलीन) | Only gaseous hormone; promotes natural ripening of fruits |
| Abscisic Acid (ABA) | Growth inhibitor; causes wilting of leaves; seed dormancy |

**Examiner Traps:**
- Photosynthesis O₂ comes from H₂O (not CO₂) — proven by heavy oxygen labeling
- Ethylene = only gaseous plant hormone
- Mitochondria = powerhouse; Ribosome = protein factory; Lysosome = suicide bag
- Mendel worked on Pisum sativum (garden pea) at Brno monastery

Double-helical structure of DNA was given by whom?

**Q-ST2. UPPCS Pre 2020**

Change in the base sequence within a gene is called:
A. Mutation  
B. Cloning  
C. Fusion  
D. Breeding  

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Logic:** Any stable, permanent, inheritable alteration in the base pair sequence of a gene's DNA is termed a Mutation (concept introduced by Hugo de Vries, 1901).

**Ans: A.** Mutation
</details>

Nitrogen is **not** an essential constituent of:
A. Chlorophyll  
B. RNA  
C. DNA  
D. Carbohydrate  

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Logic:** Simple carbohydrates (sugars, starches, glycogen, cellulose) are hydrates of carbon with general empirical formula $C_x(H_2O)_y$ and consist strictly of Carbon, Hydrogen, and Oxygen.

**Ans: D.** Carbohydrate
</details>
