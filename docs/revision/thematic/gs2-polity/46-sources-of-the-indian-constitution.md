## 📖 Thematic Revision Summary (Reformatted from PDF)

### Sources of the Indian Constitution

| Source Country | Provisions Borrowed |
|---------------|--------------------| 
| Government of India Act, 1935 | Federal scheme, Office of Governor, Judiciary, PSCs, Emergency provisions — **largest source** |
| UK (British Constitution) | Parliamentary government, Rule of Law, Single Citizenship, Cabinet system, Legislative procedure, Writs |
| USA | Fundamental Rights, Judicial Review, Impeachment of President, Removal of SC/HC judges, Preamble concept |
| Ireland | DPSP (Directive Principles), Nomination of RS members, Method of election of President |
| USSR (Russia) | Fundamental Duties, Ideals of Justice (Social, Economic, Political) in Preamble |
| Canada | Federation with strong Centre, Vesting of residual powers in Centre, Advisory jurisdiction of SC |
| Australia | Concurrent List, Joint sitting of Parliament (Art 108), Freedom of trade and commerce |
| Germany (Weimar) | Suspension of Fundamental Rights during Emergency |
| South Africa | Procedure for amendment of Constitution, Election of Rajya Sabha members |
| France | Republic, Liberty-Equality-Fraternity concepts in Preamble |
| Japan | Procedure established by law (Art 21) |

**Examiner Traps:**
- DPSP = Ireland (NOT USSR; USSR contributed Fundamental Duties)
- Fundamental Rights = USA
- Residual powers with Centre = Canada (unlike USA where residual = States)
- Parliamentary system = UK
- Concurrent List = Australia
