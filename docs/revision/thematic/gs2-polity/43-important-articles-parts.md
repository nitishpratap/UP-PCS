## 📖 Thematic Revision Summary (Reformatted from PDF)

### Important Constitutional Articles

**Part I (Articles 1–4): Union & Territory**
- Art 1: Name of the Union = "India, that is Bharat"
- Art 2: Admission of new states
- Art 3: Formation/alteration of states (Parliament's power)

**Part II (Articles 5–11): Citizenship**
- Art 11: Parliament's power to regulate citizenship

**Part III (Articles 12–35): Fundamental Rights**
| Article | Right |
|---------|-------|
| Art 14 | Equality before law & equal protection |
| Art 15 | No discrimination on grounds of religion, race, caste, sex, place of birth |
| Art 17 | Abolition of untouchability |
| Art 18 | Abolition of titles (except military/academic) |
| Art 19 | 6 Freedoms (speech, assembly, association, movement, residence, profession) |
| Art 21 | Protection of life and personal liberty |
| Art 21A | Right to Education (6–14 years) — added by 86th Amendment |
| Art 32 | Right to Constitutional Remedies — "Heart and Soul of Constitution" (Ambedkar) |
| Art 32 | 5 Writs: Habeas Corpus, Mandamus, Prohibition, Certiorari, Quo-Warranto |

**Part IV (Arts 36–51): DPSP**
| Article | Directive |
|---------|----------|
| Art 39A | Free legal aid |
| Art 40 | Village Panchayats |
| Art 44 | Uniform Civil Code |
| Art 48A | Protection of environment (added by 42nd Amendment) |
| Art 50 | Separation of Judiciary from Executive |

**Part IVA (Art 51A): Fundamental Duties** (11 duties — added by 42nd Amendment 1976; 11th by 86th Amendment 2002)

**Part XVIII: Emergency Provisions**
- Art 352: National Emergency (war, external aggression, armed rebellion)
- Art 356: President's Rule (Constitutional Emergency)
- Art 360: Financial Emergency

**Examiner Traps:**
- Art 32 = Ambedkar called it "Heart and Soul"; Art 226 = similar but in HCs (wider scope)
- 5 Writs: only Habeas Corpus and Mandamus are issued by both SC and HC
- Art 21A (Right to Education) added by 86th Amendment, 2002 — NOT original Constitution

## ❓ Previous Years Questions (UPPCS Prelims)


**Q1. UPPCS Prelims 2025, Q24**
Which of the following pairs of Parts and subjects are **NOT** correctly matched?

1. Part IVA — Fundamental Duties
2. Part VI — The States
3. Part XI — The Municipalities

Select the correct answer using the code given below:

A. 1 and 2 only
B. 3 only
C. 2 and 3 only
D. 1 only

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: B — 3 only.** Municipalities = **Part IXA**; Part XI = relations between the Union and the States.

</details>

**Q2. UPPCS Prelims 2025, Q102**
Which of the following language(s) is/are **NOT** included in the Eighth Schedule of the Constitution of India?

1. Nepali
2. Dogri
3. Bodo
4. Bhojpuri

Select the correct answer using the code given below:

A. 1 and 2 only
B. 4 only
C. 3 and 4 only
D. 1 only

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: B — 4 only (Bhojpuri).** Nepali, Dogri and Bodo are in the Eighth Schedule.

</details>
