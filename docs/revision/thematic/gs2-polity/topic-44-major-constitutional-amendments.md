## 📖 Core Thematic Revision (PCS Matrix Complete Notes)

- 1st Amendment (1951): Added the 9th Schedule (9वीं अनुसूची) to protect land reforms (भूमि सुधार)
- from judicial review.
- 7th Amendment (1956): Reorganization of states on a linguistic basis (भाषाई आधार पर राज्यों का
- पुनर्गठन); provided for a common High Court for two or more states.
- 42nd Amendment (1976 - "Mini Constitution" / लघु संविधान): Added the words Socialist (समाजवादी),
- Secular (पंथनिरपेक्षि), and Integrity (अखंडता) to the Preamble (प्रस्तावना); introduced Part IV-A
- (Fundamental Duties).
- 44th Amendment (1978): Removed the Right to Property from Fundamental Rights (made it a
- legal right under Article 300A); replaced the phrase "internal disturbance" (आंतरिक अशांति) with
- "armed rebellion" (सशस्त्र विद्रोह) for declaring National Emergency.
- 52nd Amendment (1985): Added the 10th Schedule (10वीं अनुसूची - दल-बदल विरोधी कानून / Anti-
- Defection Law).
- 61st Amendment (1989): Reduced the voting age from 21 years to 18 years under Article 326
- (मतदान की आयु सीमा में कमी).
- 73rd & 74th Amendments (1992):
- 73rd: Added 11th Schedule (Panchayati Raj - 29 functional subjects).
- 74th: Added 12th Schedule (Municipalities - 18 functional subjects).
- 86th Amendment (2002): Made free and compulsory education a Fundamental Right under
- Article 21A.
- 101st Amendment (2016): Introduced Goods and Services Tax (GST - वस्तु एवं सेवा कर).
- 103rd Amendment (2019): Introduced 10% reservation for Economically Weaker Sections (EWS -
- आथिंक रूप से कमजोर वगर्प).
