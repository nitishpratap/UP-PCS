## 📖 Core Thematic Revision (PCS Matrix Complete Notes)

- Part I (Articles 1-4): Union & Its Territory (संघ और उसका राज्य क्षिेत्र)
- Articles 1-4: Name and territory of the Union, admission or establishment of new states, and
- alteration of areas, boundaries, or names of existing states.
- Part II (Articles 5-11): Citizenship (नागरिकता)
- Articles 5-11: Provisions regarding citizenship at the commencement of the Constitution, rights
- of citizenship of certain persons, and power of Parliament to regulate citizenship by law.
- Part III: Fundamental Rights (मूल अधिकार)
- Article 14: Equality before law and equal protection of laws (विधि के समक्षि समता).
- Article 17: Abolition of Untouchability (अस्पृश्यता का अंत).
- Article 18: Abolition of Titles (उपाधियों का अंत).
- Article 21: Protection of life and personal liberty (प्राण एवं दैहिक स्वतंत्रता का अधिकार).
- Article 21A: Right to Education (शिक्षिा का अधिकार - added by 86th Amendment Act).
- Article 32: Right to Constitutional Remedies (संवैधानिक उपचारों का अधिकार - Dr. B.R. Ambedkar called it
- the "Heart and Soul of the Constitution").
- 5 Writs (5 प्रकार की रिट): Habeas Corpus (बंदी प्रत्यक्षिीकरण), Mandamus (परमादेश), Prohibition (प्रतिषेध),
- Certiorari (उत्प्रेषण), Quo-Warranto (अधिकार-पृच्छा).
- Part IV: Directive Principles of State Policy - DPSPs (राज्य के नीतिि निदर्रे:क तित्वि)
- Article 39A: Equal justice and free legal aid (समान न्याय और निःशुल्क विधिक सहायता).
- Article 40: Organization of Village Panchayats (ग्राम पंचायतों का गठन).
- Article 44: Uniform Civil Code (समान नागरिक संहिता).
- Article 45: Provision for early childhood care and education (शैशवावस्थिा की देखरेख और शिक्षिा).
- Article 48A: Protection and improvement of environment and safeguarding of forests and
- wildlife (पयार्पवरण, वन तथा वन्यजीवों की सुरक्षिा).
- Article 50: Separation of Judiciary from Executive (कायर्पालिका से न्यायपालिका का पृथक्करण).
- Part IV-A: Fundamental Duties (मूल कतिर्शव्य)
- Article 51A: Added by the 42nd Constitutional Amendment Act (1976) on the recommendation
- of the Swaran Singh Committee (स्वणर्प सिंह समिति).
- The 11th Fundamental Duty was added by the 86th Constitutional Amendment Act (2002).
- Part XVIII: Emergency Provisions (आपातिकालीन प्राविधान)
- Article 352: National Emergency (राष्ट्रीय आपातकाल - declared on grounds of war, external
- aggression, or armed rebellion).
- Article 356: President's Rule / Constitutional Emergency (राष्ट्रपति शासन / राज्य में संवैधानिक तंत्रि की विफलता).
- Article 360: Financial Emergency (वित्तीय आपातकाल).
