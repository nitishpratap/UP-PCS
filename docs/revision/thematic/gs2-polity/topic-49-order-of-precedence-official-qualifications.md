## 📖 Core Thematic Revision (PCS Matrix Complete Notes)

### Order of Precedence (विरीयतिा अनुक्रम - Top Ranks):
1. President (राष्ट्रपतिि)
2. Vice-President (उपराष्ट्रपतिि)
- 3. Prime Minister (प्रधानमंत्री)
- 4. Governors of States (राज्यों के राज्यपाल - अपने संबंधिति राज्यों के भीतिर)
- 5. Former Presidents (भूतिपूविर्श राष्ट्रपतिि)
- 6. Deputy Prime Minister (उप-प्रधानमंत्री)
- 7. Chief Justice of India & Speaker of Lok Sabha (भारति के मुख्य न्यायाधी: एविं लोकसिभा
- अध्यक्षि)

### Qualifications & Minimum Age Limits (न्यूनतिम आयु सिीमा):
- President / Vice-President / Governor: Minimum 35 years.
- Member of Rajya Sabha / Legislative Council (MLC): Minimum 30 years.
- Member of Lok Sabha / Legislative Assembly (MLA): Minimum 25 years.
- Panchayat / Municipality Member: Minimum 21 years.

### Key Appointment & Resignation Relations (नियु्क्ति एविं त्यागपत्र):
- Governor: Appointed by the President; holds office during the pleasure of the President (राष्ट्रपति के
- प्रसादपयर्पन्त).
- President: Resigns to the Vice-President (उपराष्ट्रपति को त्यागपत्रि देते हैं).
- Vice-President: Resigns to the President (राष्ट्रपति को त्यागपत्रि देते हैं).
- Judges of SC / HC: Appointed by the President; removed through Parliamentary impeachment
- procedure (संसद द्वारा महाशिभयोग जैसी प्रकि्रया से हटाया जाना).
