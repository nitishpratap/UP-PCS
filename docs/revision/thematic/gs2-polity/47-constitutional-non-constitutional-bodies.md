### Non-Constitutional / Statutory Bodies

| Body | Established Under | Nature |
|------|------------------|--------|
| NITI Aayog | Cabinet Resolution (2015) | Non-statutory; replaced Planning Commission |
| NHRC | Protection of Human Rights Act, 1993 | Statutory |
| CIC | RTI Act, 2005 | Statutory |
| CVC | CVC Act, 2003 | Statutory |
| CBI | Delhi Special Police Establishment Act, 1946 | Statutory |
| NHB | National Housing Bank Act | Statutory |
| SEBI | SEBI Act, 1992 | Statutory |

**Examiner Traps:**
- NITI Aayog = NOT constitutional, NOT statutory — executive resolution
- Planning Commission = also non-statutory (both replaced and replacer are non-statutory)
- CBI has NO suo motu jurisdiction; needs state permission or court order
- Finance Commission ≠ Planning Commission (FC is constitutional; PC/NITI = non-statutory)
