## 📖 Core Thematic Revision (PCS Matrix Complete Notes)

- 1st Schedule: Names of States & Union Territories and their territorial jurisdiction (राज्य एवं संघ राज्य
- क्षेत्र).
- 2nd Schedule: Provisions relating to emoluments, allowances, and privileges of President,
- Governors, Judges, etc. (वेतन और भत्ते).
- 3rd Schedule: Forms of Oaths or Affirmations (शपथ एवं प्रतिज्ञान के प्रारूप).
- 4th Schedule: Allocation of seats in the Rajya Sabha to States and UTs (राज्यसभा में सीटों का आवंटन).
- 5th Schedule: Administration and control of Scheduled Areas and Scheduled Tribes (अनुसूचित क्षेत्रों
- और अनुसूचित जनजातियों का प्रशासन).
- 6th Schedule: Administration of tribal areas in the states of Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura, and
- Mizoram (ATM-M - चार राज्यों के जनजातीय क्षेत्रों का प्रशासन).
- 7th Schedule: Division of powers between Union and States via Three Lists - Union List (संघ सूची),
- State List (राज्य सूची), and Concurrent List (समवतर्वी सूची).
- 8th Schedule: 22 Officially Recognized Languages (22 आधिकारिक भाषाएँ - originally 14).
- Sindhi added by 21st Amendment (1967).
- Konkani, Manipuri, Nepali added by 71st Amendment (1992).
- Bodo, Dogri, Maithili, Santhali added by 92nd Amendment (2003).
- 9th Schedule: Acts and regulations related to land reforms (भूमि सुधार से संबंधित अधिनियम).
- 10th Schedule: Provisions regarding disqualification on grounds of defection (दल-बदल के आधार पर
- अयो¹यता).
- 11th Schedule: Specifies 29 matters under the jurisdiction of Panchayats (पंचायतों के अधिकार क्षेत्र).
- 12th Schedule: Specifies 18 matters under the jurisdiction of Municipalities (नगरपालिकाओं के अधिकार
- क्षेत्र).
