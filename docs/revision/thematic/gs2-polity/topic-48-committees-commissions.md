## 📖 Core Thematic Revision (PCS Matrix Complete Notes)

### Panchayati Raj Committees (Chronological Order / कालानुक्रमिक क्रम):
1. Balwant Rai Mehta Committee (1957): Recommended 3-tier Panchayati Raj
- system (ति्र-स्तिरीय व्यविस्था).
2. Ashok Mehta Committee (1977): Recommended 2-tier system (दि्व-स्तिरीय व्यविस्था).
- 3. G.V.K. Rao Committee (1985): Focused on administrative arrangements for
- rural development.
- 4. L.M. Singhvi Committee (1986): Recommended constitutional recognition
- (सिंविैधानिक दजार्श) for Panchayats.
- 5. P.K. Thungon Committee (1988): Recommended constitutional backing for
- local bodies.

### Center-State Relations Committees (केंद्र-राज्य संबंध सिमितिियाँ):
- Rajamannar Committee (1969)
- Anandpur Sahib Resolution (1973)
- Sarkaria Commission (1983): Major commission on Center-State power distribution.
- Punchhi Commission (2007)

### Preamble & Rights Committees:
- Swaran Singh Committee: Recommended Fundamental Duties.
- Verma Committee (1999): Identified legal provisions for enforcement of Fundamental Duties.

### Parliamentary Committees (सिंसिदीय सिमितिियाँ):
- Estimates Committee (प्राकलन समिति): 30 members (all from Lok Sabha); largest parliamentary
- committee.
- Public Accounts Committee (लोक लेखा समिति - PAC): 22 members (15 Lok Sabha + 7 Rajya Sabha);
- examines CAG reports, traditionally headed by an opposition leader (विपक्षि का नेता).
- Committee on Public Undertakings (सार्वजनिक उपक्रमों संबंधी समिति): 22 members (15 Lok Sabha + 7
- Rajya Sabha).
