## 📖 Thematic Revision Summary (Reformatted from PDF)

### All 12 Schedules of the Indian Constitution

| Schedule | Content |
|----------|---------|
| 1st | Names of States & Union Territories |
| 2nd | Emoluments of President, Governors, Judges, CAG, etc. |
| 3rd | Forms of Oaths or Affirmations |
| 4th | Allocation of Rajya Sabha seats to States & UTs |
| 5th | Administration of Scheduled Areas & Scheduled Tribes |
| 6th | Administration of Tribal Areas in Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura, Mizoram (ATM-M) |
| 7th | Division of powers: **Union List** (97+33=100 subjects), **State List** (66-1=61), **Concurrent List** (52→47) |
| 8th | 22 Officially Recognised Languages |
| 9th | Acts & Regulations related to land reforms (protected from judicial review — Art 31B) |
| 10th | Anti-Defection provisions (added by 52nd Amendment 1985) |
| 11th | 29 subjects under Panchayats (73rd Amendment) |
| 12th | 18 subjects under Municipalities (74th Amendment) |

**8th Schedule Languages (22):**
- Original 14 (1950): Assamese, Bengali, Gujarati, Hindi, Kannada, Kashmiri, Malayalam, Marathi, Odia, Punjabi, Sanskrit, Tamil, Telugu, Urdu
- Sindhi added (1967 — 21st Amendment)
- Konkani, Manipuri, Nepali added (1992 — 71st Amendment)
- Bodo, Dogri, Maithili, Santhali added (2003 — 92nd Amendment)

**7th Schedule — 3 Lists:**
- **Union List:** Defence, Foreign affairs, Currency, Railways, Atomic energy
- **State List:** Police, Agriculture, Public health, Land, Irrigation, Local bodies
- **Concurrent List:** Education, Marriage, Forests, Electricity, Criminal law

**Examiner Traps:**
- 9th Schedule = land reforms protection; originally 13 items, now 284+
- 6th Schedule = only ATM-M states (Assam, Tripura, Meghalaya, Mizoram)
- Education was in State List → moved to Concurrent List by 42nd Amendment

## ❓ Previous Years Questions (UPPCS Prelims)


**Q1. UPPCS Prelims 2025, Q24**
Which of the following pairs of Parts and subjects are **NOT** correctly matched?

1. Part IVA — Fundamental Duties
2. Part VI — The States
3. Part XI — The Municipalities

Select the correct answer using the code given below:

A. 1 and 2 only
B. 3 only
C. 2 and 3 only
D. 1 only

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: B — 3 only.** Municipalities = **Part IXA**; Part XI = relations between the Union and the States.

</details>

**Q2. UPPCS Prelims 2025, Q102**
Which of the following language(s) is/are **NOT** included in the Eighth Schedule of the Constitution of India?

1. Nepali
2. Dogri
3. Bodo
4. Bhojpuri

Select the correct answer using the code given below:

A. 1 and 2 only
B. 4 only
C. 3 and 4 only
D. 1 only

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: B — 4 only (Bhojpuri).** Nepali, Dogri and Bodo are in the Eighth Schedule.

</details>
