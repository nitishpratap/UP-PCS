## 📖 Thematic Revision Summary (Reformatted from PDF)

### Order of Precedence (Top Ranks)

1. President
2. Vice-President
3. Prime Minister
4. Governors of States (within their states)
5. Former Presidents
6. Deputy Prime Minister
7. Chief Justice of India & Speaker of Lok Sabha

### Qualifications & Minimum Age

| Office | Min Age | Key Qualification |
|--------|---------|------------------|
| President | 35 years | Must be eligible for Lok Sabha membership |
| Vice-President | 35 years | Must be eligible for Rajya Sabha membership |
| Governor | 35 years | Indian citizen; must not be member of Legislature |
| Lok Sabha Member / MLA | 25 years | Indian citizen; on electoral rolls |
| Rajya Sabha Member / MLC | 30 years | — |
| Panchayat / Municipality Member | 21 years | — |

### Key Appointment & Resignation Relations

| Office | Appointed By | Resigns To |
|--------|-------------|-----------|
| President | Elected by Electoral College | Vice-President |
| Vice-President | Elected by Electoral College | President |
| Prime Minister | President | President |
| Governor | President | President |
| CAG | President | President |
| CEC | President | President |
| SC/HC Judges | President | President (removed by impeachment) |

**Examiner Traps:**
- President resigns to VP; VP resigns to President
- Governor serves at pleasure of President (can be removed anytime)
- CJI and Speaker are at same level in Order of Precedence (7th rank)
- SC judge removal requires special majority in BOTH houses + Presidential order

**Q1. UPPCS Prelims 2025, Q24**
Which of the following pairs of Parts and subjects are **NOT** correctly matched?

1. Part IVA — Fundamental Duties
2. Part VI — The States
3. Part XI — The Municipalities

Select the correct answer using the code given below:

A. 1 and 2 only
B. 3 only
C. 2 and 3 only
D. 1 only

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: B — 3 only.** Municipalities = **Part IXA**; Part XI = relations between the Union and the States.

</details>
