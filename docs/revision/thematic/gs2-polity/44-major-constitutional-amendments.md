## 📖 Thematic Revision Summary (Reformatted from PDF)

### Major Constitutional Amendments

| Amendment | Year | Key Provision |
|-----------|------|--------------|
| 1st | 1951 | Added 9th Schedule (to protect land reforms from judicial review) |
| 7th | 1956 | Reorganisation of states on linguistic basis; common HC for 2+ states |
| 24th | 1971 | Parliament's power to amend Fundamental Rights |
| 42nd | 1976 | "Mini Constitution": Added Socialist, Secular, Integrity to Preamble; added Part IVA (Fundamental Duties); gave precedence to DPSP over FRs |
| 44th | 1978 | Removed Right to Property from FRs (Art 31 deleted → made legal right under Art 300A); restored "internal disturbance" changed to "armed rebellion" in Art 352 |
| 52nd | 1985 | Added 10th Schedule (Anti-Defection Law) |
| 61st | 1989 | Reduced voting age from 21 to 18 |
| 73rd | 1992 | Panchayati Raj — 11th Schedule (29 subjects); mandatory elections, reservations |
| 74th | 1992 | Municipalities — 12th Schedule (18 subjects) |
| 86th | 2002 | Right to Education (Art 21A); 11th Fundamental Duty |
| 91st | 2003 | Anti-defection; limited Council of Ministers to 15% of Lok Sabha |
| 101st | 2016 | GST (Goods & Services Tax) — Art 279A; GST Council |
| 103rd | 2019 | 10% EWS reservation in education and govt jobs |
| 104th | 2020 | Extended SC/ST reservation in Parliament and state legislatures; ended Anglo-Indian reservation |

**Examiner Traps:**
- 42nd Amendment = "Mini Constitution" OR "Constitution of Indira Gandhi"
- Right to Property removed by 44th Amendment (1978), NOT 42nd (1976)
- GST = 101st Amendment (2016)
- Anti-Defection = 52nd Amendment + 10th Schedule

## ❓ Previous Years Questions (UPPCS Prelims)


**Q. UKPCS Prelims 2025, Q118**

The 101st Constitutional Amendment Act is related with

A. Reservation for EWS
B. Delimitation of Constituencies
C. Anti-defection Law
D. Nationwide Goods and Services Tax (GST)

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Logic:** The 101st Amendment created the GST framework; the 103rd Amendment concerns EWS reservation.

**Ans: D (Series B provisional key).** 101st Amendment (2016) introduced GST / GST Council (जीएसटी परिषद). EWS is 103rd; anti-defection is 52nd.

</details>

**Q. UKPCS Prelims 2025, Q128**

The size of the Council of Ministers was fixed up to 15 per cent of the total members of the Lok Sabha (लोकसभा) by which Amendment?

A. 91st Constitutional Amendment Act, 2003
B. 92nd Constitutional Amendment Act, 2003
C. 74th Constitutional Amendment Act, 1992
D. 86th Constitutional Amendment Act, 2002

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Logic:** The 91st Amendment imposed the ministry-size ceiling; the nearby 92nd Amendment added Eighth Schedule (आठवीं अनुसूची) languages.

**Ans: A (Series B provisional key).** 91st Amendment (2003) capped CoM at 15% of Lok Sabha / Assembly strength. 92nd added Eighth Schedule languages.

</details>
