## 📖 Thematic Revision Summary (Reformatted from PDF)

### Countries Bordering Major Seas

**Black Sea (Mnemonic: BURGER T):**
Bulgaria, Russia (Ukraine area), Georgia, Romania, Turkey — all 6 border it

**Red Sea (Mnemonic: DESSEY):**
Djibouti, Egypt, Sudan, Saudi Arabia, Eritrea, Yemen

**Caspian Sea (Mnemonic: TARIK / KAZAKH):**
Turkmenistan, Azerbaijan, Russia, Iran, Kazakhstan

**Mediterranean Sea:**
- Borders: Egypt, Libya, Algeria, Morocco, Spain, France, Italy, Greece, Turkey, Syria, Lebanon, Israel
- Does NOT Border: Jordan, Iraq, Saudi Arabia, Portugal

**Persian Gulf:**
Iran, Iraq, Kuwait, Saudi Arabia, Bahrain, Qatar, UAE, Oman

**Baltic Sea:**
Sweden, Finland, Russia, Estonia, Latvia, Lithuania, Poland, Germany, Denmark

**North Sea:**
Norway, Denmark, Germany, Netherlands, Belgium, France, UK

**Arabian Sea:**
India, Pakistan, Iran, Oman, Yemen, Somalia

### Important Straits (repeated from Topic 35 — quick revision)
- Strait of Malacca = Malaysia + Indonesia (busiest)
- Strait of Hormuz = Iran + Oman (oil)
- Strait of Gibraltar = Spain + Morocco (Mediterranean gate)
- Palk Strait = India + Sri Lanka

**Examiner Traps:**
- Jordan does NOT touch Mediterranean (it's landlocked on that side)
- Portugal does NOT touch Mediterranean (only Atlantic)
- Caspian = 5 countries (TARIK mnemonic)
- Red Sea = eastern Africa + Middle East (NOT Mediterranean countries)

## ❓ Previous Years Questions (UPPCS Prelims)


**Q1. UPPCS Prelims 2025, Q75**
Which of the following are NOT cold ocean currents?

1. Agulhas Current
2. Brazil Current
3. Humboldt Current
4. California Current

A. 1 and 3

B. 3 and 4

C. 2 and 3

D. 1 and 2

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: D (1 and 2)** — The paper asks which are **NOT cold** (= which are **warm**).

| Current | Type | Ocean |
|---------|------|-------|
| 1 Agulhas | **Warm** | Indian, SE Africa |
| 2 Brazil | **Warm** | Atlantic, E Brazil |
| 3 Humboldt | **Cold** | Pacific, Peru–Chile |
| 4 California | **Cold** | Pacific, W USA |

Pick **1 and 2**. Option B (3 and 4) is the cold pair — that is the trap if you miss the word **NOT**.
</details>

The Suez Canal connects which of the following?

1. Atlantic Ocean
2. Red Sea
3. Mediterranean Sea
4. Indian Ocean

A. 1 and 3

B. 3 and 4

C. 2 and 3

D. 1 and 2
