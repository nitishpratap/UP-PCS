## 📖 Thematic Revision Summary (Reformatted from PDF)

### Tropic of Cancer (23.5°N) — States It Passes Through

**West to East sequence:**
Gujarat → Rajasthan → Madhya Pradesh → Chhattisgarh → Jharkhand → West Bengal → Tripura → Mizoram
*(Total: 8 States)*

### Indian Standard Time (IST — 82.5°E)

**States through which IST line passes (N to S):**
Uttar Pradesh → Madhya Pradesh → Chhattisgarh → Odisha → Andhra Pradesh
*(Total: 5 States)*

**Note:** Tropic of Cancer and IST line both pass through Chhattisgarh (Koriya district) — they intersect here.

### International Border Lengths (Decreasing Order)

Bangladesh (4,156 km) → China (3,488 km) → Pakistan (3,323 km) → Nepal (1,751 km) → Myanmar (1,643 km) → Bhutan (699 km) → Afghanistan (106 km)

### States Bordering Neighboring Countries

| Country | No. of States | States |
|---------|--------------|--------|
| Bangladesh | 5 | West Bengal, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Tripura, Assam |
| Nepal | 5 | Uttarakhand, Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, West Bengal, Sikkim |
| China | 4+1 UT | Ladakh (UT), Himachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand, Sikkim, Arunachal Pradesh |
| Pakistan | 3+2 UTs | Gujarat, Rajasthan, Punjab, J&K (UT), Ladakh (UT) |
| Myanmar | 4 | Arunachal Pradesh, Nagaland, Manipur, Mizoram |
| Bhutan | 4 | Sikkim, Arunachal Pradesh, Assam, West Bengal |

### Andaman & Nicobar Islands (North to South)

North Andaman → Middle Andaman (largest) → South Andaman → Duncan Passage → Little Andaman → Ten Degree Channel → Car Nicobar → Great Nicobar

**India's southernmost point:** Indira Point, Great Nicobar Island (8°4'N) — submerged partially in 2004 tsunami

**Examiner Traps:**
- Tropic of Cancer = 8 states (not 7)
- IST reference = Mirzapur/Naini, UP (82.5°E)
- Longest border = Bangladesh (4,156 km) — NOT China or Pakistan
- Nepal borders UP (important for UP-PCS)

## ❓ Previous Years Questions (UPPCS Prelims)


**Q. UKPCS Prelims 2025, Q84**

The Shiwalik range is primarily composed of which type of material?

A. Igneous rocks
B. Consolidated rocks
C. Unconsolidated sediments
D. Metamorphic rocks

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Logic:** Youth of the Shiwaliks explains unconsolidated sediment fill.

**Ans: C.** Shiwaliks are thick unconsolidated sediments from rising Himalaya (हिमालय) — landslide-prone and dun-forming.

</details>

**Q. UKPCS Prelims 2025, Q90**

Which is the largest glacier of the Trans-Himalayas?

A. Biafo
B. Siachen (सियाचिन)
C. Baltoro
D. Hispar

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Logic:** Among Karakoram glaciers, relative (सापेक्ष) length decides it.

**Ans: B.** Siachen (~76 km) is the largest Trans-Himalayan / Karakoram glacier among the options.

</details>

**Q1. UPPCS Prelims 2025, Q37**
Match List-I with List-II and select the correct answer using the code given below the lists.
**List-I (State)**

A. Tamil Nadu

B. Rajasthan

C. Nagaland

D. Madhya Pradesh
**List-II (Highest Peak)**

1. Dhupgarh
2. Doddabetta
3. Guru Shikhar
4. Saramati

A. 2 3 1 4

B. 3 2 4 1

C. 3 2 1 4

D. 2 3 4 1

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: D** — A→2 (Doddabetta, TN), B→3 (Guru Shikhar, Rajasthan/Aravalli), C→4 (Saramati, Nagaland), D→1 (Dhupgarh, Satpura/MP). **A** swaps MP-Nagaland peaks. **B/C** put Guru Shikhar on TN or Dhupgarh on Rajasthan.
</details>
