## 📖 Thematic Revision Summary (Reformatted from PDF)

### Countries on the Equator (0° Latitude)

**South America:** Ecuador, Colombia, Brazil
**Africa:** Gabon, Republic of Congo, DRC, Uganda, Kenya, Somalia, São Tomé & Príncipe
**Asia/Oceania:** Maldives, Indonesia, Kiribati

### Countries on the Tropic of Capricorn (23.5°S)

Chile, Argentina, Paraguay, Brazil, Namibia, Botswana, South Africa, Mozambique, Madagascar, Australia

### Countries on the Prime Meridian (0° Longitude)

UK, France, Spain, Algeria, Mali, Burkina Faso, Togo, Ghana, Antarctica

### Special Intersections

| Intersection | Location |
|-------------|---------|
| Prime Meridian meets Equator | Gulf of Guinea (Atlantic Ocean) — off the coast of Ghana/Togo |
| Tropic of Cancer meets IST line | Chhattisgarh (Koriya district), India |
| Tropic of Cancer meets Prime Meridian | Sahara Desert (Algeria) |

### India's Geographic Extremes

| Extreme | Point | State |
|---------|-------|-------|
| Northernmost | Siachen Glacier area / Indira Col | J&K (Ladakh) |
| Southernmost | Indira Point | Great Nicobar (A&N Islands) |
| Easternmost | Kibithu | Arunachal Pradesh |
| Westernmost | Sir Creek area / Guhar Moti | Gujarat |

**Examiner Traps:**
- Ecuador is ON the equator (equator named after it)
- Prime Meridian meets Equator = Gulf of Guinea (NOT on land)
- India's southernmost mainland point = Kanyakumari, Tamil Nadu
- India's southernmost point overall = Indira Point, Great Nicobar

## ❓ Previous Years Questions (UPPCS Prelims)


**Q. UKPCS Prelims 2025, Q95**

Which planet takes the longest time completing one revolution around the sun?

A. Saturn
B. Uranus
C. Neptune
D. Jupiter

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Logic:** Farthest planet among the four (चातुर्याम) has the longest year.

**Ans: C (Series B provisional key).** Neptune (~165 Earth years) outranks Uranus, Saturn and Jupiter.

</details>
