## 📖 Thematic Revision Summary (Reformatted from PDF)

### European Capitals (North to South / West to East sequence)

**Selected Major Capitals:**
- Moscow (Russia) → Berlin (Germany) → London (UK) → Paris (France) → Rome (Italy) → Athens (Greece) → Ankara (Turkey) → Damascus (Syria) → Jerusalem (Israel/Palestine) → Riyadh (Saudi Arabia)

**South-East Asian Capitals (North to South):**
Hanoi (Vietnam) → Vientiane (Laos) → Bangkok (Thailand) → Phnom Penh (Cambodia) → Kuala Lumpur (Malaysia) → Singapore → Jakarta (Indonesia)

### Landlocked Countries

**South America (Only 2):**
- Paraguay and Bolivia

**Central Asia (All 5 are landlocked):**
- Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan (doubly landlocked), Turkmenistan, Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan
- **Doubly landlocked:** Surrounded by landlocked countries = **Uzbekistan** (in Central Asia) and **Liechtenstein** (in Europe)

**South Asia (3):**
- Nepal, Bhutan, Afghanistan

**Africa (most landlocked countries):**
Key ones: Mali, Niger, Chad, Central African Republic, Ethiopia, Rwanda, Burundi, Uganda, Zambia, Zimbabwe, Botswana, South Sudan, Malawi, Lesotho, Eswatini

**Important Capital Changes:**
- Myanmar: Yangon (old) → **Naypyidaw** (new capital since 2006)
- Kazakhstan: Almaty (old) → Astana → **Nur-Sultan** → **Astana** again (2022)
- Brazil: Rio de Janeiro → **Brasilia** (planned capital)
- Indonesia announced moving capital from Jakarta → **Nusantara** (East Kalimantan)

**Examiner Traps:**
- Uzbekistan = doubly landlocked (surrounded by other landlocked countries)
- Lesotho and Eswatini = inside South Africa (surrounded by single country)
- Nepal borders UP — important for UP PCS
- Bhutan = landlocked but shares border with UP? NO — Bhutan does NOT share border with UP (shares with Assam, WB, Arunachal, Sikkim)

## ❓ Previous Years Questions (UPPCS Prelims)


**Q. UKPCS Prelims 2025, Q94**

The Grand Canyon of the U.S.A. is formed by which river?

A. St. Lawrence (लॉरेंस)
B. Missouri
C. Ohio
D. Colorado

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Logic:** Only one of the four (चातुर्याम) North American rivers cut the Arizona canyon.

**Ans: D (Series B provisional key).** The Colorado River cut the Grand Canyon through the Colorado Plateau.

</details>

**Q1. UPPCS Prelims 2025, Q68**
Which of the following pairs are NOT correctly matched?
(City) — (River)

1. Budapest — Danube
2. Cologne — Rhine
3. New Orleans — Missouri
4. Hyderabad (India) — Musi

A. Only 1, 2 and 3

B. Only 3 and 4

C. Only 2, 3 and 4

D. Only 1 and 2

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans (facts):** 1 and 2 matched. 3 unmatched — New Orleans = **Mississippi**. 4 matched — Hyderabad = **Musi**.
Paper options have **no “Only 3”**. Do not treat Musi as wrong. Some series print **Paleru** as 4 → then unmatched = 3 and 4 → **B**.
</details>

**Q2. UPPCS Prelims 2024, Q77**
Which one of the following pairs (River - Direction) is not correctly matched?

Options:

A. Amur River - North-East

B. Syr Darya - North-West

C. Angara River - North

D. Mekong River - South-West

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Ans: D** — Mekong flows **south / south-east**, not south-west.
</details>
