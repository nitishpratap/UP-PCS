|------------------|----------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------|-----------------------|
| **Xerophytes** | Arid deserts, extreme water deficit | Sunken stomata, thick waxy cuticle, reduced leaves (spines), succulent water storage | Cactus, *Opuntia*, *Aloe*, *Calotropis*, Pineapple | Stems perform photosynthesis (**Cladode**) |

### Light Reactions (Hill Reaction) vs Dark Reactions (Calvin Cycle)

| Diagnostic Parameter | Light-Dependent Phase (Hill Reaction) | Light-Independent Phase (Calvin / Blackman Reaction) |
|----------------------|---------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------|
| **Primary Site** | **Grana (Thylakoid membranes)** of chloroplast | **Stroma (fluid matrix)** of chloroplast |
| **Light Requirement** | Strictly light-dependent | Light-independent (utilizes chemical energy products of light phase) |
| **Key Biochemical Events**| Photolysis of water: $2\text{H}_2\text{O} \rightarrow 4\text{H}^+ + 4e^- + \text{O}_2$; Electron transport across photosystems I & II; Photophosphorylation | Carbon fixation / carbon assimilation: atmospheric $\text{CO}_2$ reduced into 3-carbon sugars and polymerized into glucose |
| **Key Enzymes** | Oxygen-Evolving Complex (OEC containing $\text{Mn}$ and $\text{Cl}$), ATP Synthase | **RubisCO** (Ribulose-1,5-bisphosphate carboxylase/oxygenase) |
| **Energetic Currency** | Generates **ATP** and **NADPH** across transmembrane proton gradient | Consumes **18 ATP** and **12 NADPH** per single molecule of glucose synthesized |

### Master Physiological Roles & Deficiency Pathology

| Element | Uptake Chemical Form | Physiological Functions | Deficiency Symptoms & Must-Score Pathology |
|---------|----------------------|-------------------------|---------------------------------------|
| **Nitrogen ($\text{N}$)** | Nitrate ($\text{NO}_3^-$) predominantly; Ammonium ($\text{NH}_4^+$) in acidic boreal soils | Constituent of amino acids, proteins, chlorophyll, nucleic acids | Severe stunted growth, chlorosis of older leaves |
| **Phosphorus ($\text{P}$)** | Dihydrogen phosphate ($\text{H}_2\text{PO}_4^-$) | Nucleic acids, phospholipids, ATP; **stimulates early root system establishment**, fibrous branching, tillering, and early crop maturity | Dark green or purplish/reddish bronze pigmentation on foliage |
| **Potassium ($\text{K}$)** | Potassium ion ($\text{K}^+$) | **Regulates stomatal opening and closing via guard cell turgor pressure**; activates osmotic pumps and enzyme complexes | Marginal leaf burn (tip scorch), chlorosis, weak stems (lodging) |
| **Sulphur ($\text{S}$)** | Sulphate ($\text{SO}_4^{2-}$) | Cysteine, methionine, ferredoxin; **crucial for oil synthesis in mustard, rapeseed and groundnut**; legume nodulation | Yellowing of younger leaves, stunted development |
| **Calcium ($\text{Ca}$)** | Calcium ion ($\text{Ca}^{2+}$) | Component of middle lamella (calcium pectate); membrane stabilization and cell division | **Blossom-end rot** in tomatoes and peppers; death of growing root and shoot tips |
| **Magnesium ($\text{Mg}$)** | Magnesium ion ($\text{Mg}^{2+}$) | Central coordinating atom of chlorophyll porphyrin ring; activator of RuBisCO and PEPCase | Interveinal chlorosis in older leaves while veins remain green |
| **Iron ($\text{Fe}$)** | Ferrous ($\text{Fe}^{2+}$) / Ferric ($\text{Fe}^{3+}$) | Electron transport (cytochromes, ferredoxin); essential catalyst for chlorophyll synthesis | Interveinal chlorosis on youngest apical leaves |
| **Boron ($\text{B}$)** | Borate ($\text{BO}_3^{3-}$ / $\text{B}_4\text{O}_7^{2-}$) | Pollen tube elongation, cell wall pectin synthesis, calcium translocation, **nodule formation in legumes** | **"Hollow heart"** in beets and peanuts, cracked fruits, failure of flowering |
| **Zinc ($\text{Zn}$)** | Zinc ion ($\text{Zn}^{2+}$) | Biosynthesis of **Auxin (IAA)**; activator of carbonic anhydrase and alcohol dehydrogenase | **"Khaira disease" in rice (paddy)**; **"Little Leaf"** disease in citrus and mango |
| **Molybdenum ($\text{Mo}$)** | Molybdate ($\text{MoO}_4^{2-}$) | Core component of **Nitrate Reductase** and **Nitrogenase** enzymes | Whiptail disease in cauliflower; impaired symbiotic nitrogen fixation |
| **Nickel ($\text{Ni}$)** | Nickel ion ($\text{Ni}^{2+}$) | Structural cofactor of **Urease** enzyme converting urea to ammonia | Necrotic leaf tip lesions due to toxic urea accumulation |

### Comparative Spectrum: Endogenous Plant Growth Regulators

| Hormone Class | Chemical Representative | Primary Sites of Synthesis | Physiological Functions & Agronomic Applications |
|---------------|-------------------------|----------------------------|---------------------------------------------------|
| **Auxins** | **Indole-3-Acetic Acid (IAA)** (Natural); **NAA, IBA, 2,4-D, 2,4,5-T** (Synthetic) | Shoot apical buds, young expanding leaves | - **Apical Dominance**: Promotes terminal bud growth while suppressing axillary buds.<br>- **Phototropism & Geotropism**: Cell elongation on shaded side.<br>- **Root Initiation**: Indole-3-butyric acid (IBA) stimulates rapid adventitious rooting in stem cuttings.<br>- **Parthenocarpy**: Induces seedless fruit formation in tomatoes.<br>- **Weedicide**: Synthetic **2,4-D** selectively eradicates broadleaf (dicot) weeds in cereal fields. |
| **Gibberellins** | **Gibberellic Acid ($\text{GA}_3$)** | Plastids of young leaves, developing seeds, roots | - **Internodal Stem Elongation**: Overcomes dwarfism; sprayed on sugarcane to dramatically lengthen internodes and boost cane yield.<br>- **Bolting**: Induces internode lengthening prior to flowering in rosette plants (cabbage, beet).<br>- **Breaks Seed & Bud Dormancy**: Stimulates $\alpha$-amylase synthesis in barley aleurone layer.<br>- Discovered from the fungus *Gibberella fujikuroi* causing "Bakanae" (foolish seedling) disease in rice. |
| **Cytokinins** | **Kinetin, Zeatin** | Root apical meristems, endosperm of seeds | - **Cell Division (Cytokinesis)**: Promotes mitosis and shoot organogenesis.<br>- **Delays Senescence (Richmond-Lang Effect)**: Retards chlorophyll breakdown and protein degradation; preserves freshness in cut flowers and vegetables.<br>- Abundantly present in **coconut milk / liquid endosperm**. |
| **Abscisic Acid (ABA)** | Sesquiterpene carboxylic acid | Chloroplasts of leaves, vascular tissues | - **"Stress Hormone"**: Triggers rapid stomatal closure under drought stress (**antitranspirant**).<br>- **Induces Dormancy**: Maintains seed and winter bud dormancy; acts as a physiological **anti-gibberellin**.<br>- Promotes leaf senescence and fruit abscission. |
| **Ethylene** | **Gaseous hydrocarbon ($\text{C}_2\text{H}_4$)** | Ripening fruit tissues, senescent foliage, nodes | - **Fruit Ripening**: Triggers the **"respiratory climacteric"** (surge in cellular respiration) in bananas, apples, mangoes.<br>- Promotes leaf, flower, and fruit abscission.<br>- Stimulates latex exudation in rubber trees (*Hevea brasiliensis*).<br>- Synthesized biochemically from the amino acid **methionine**. |
