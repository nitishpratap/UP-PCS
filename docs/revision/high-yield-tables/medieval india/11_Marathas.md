- **Exam Anchor:** Chauth (चौथ) is a 25% (1/4) protection levy; sardeshmukhi (सरदेशमुखी) is an extra 10% (1/10) hereditary chief claim (together up to 35%). Saranzami (सरंजामी) land supported Maratha jagirdars’ livelihood.
- **Exam Anchor:** Bajirao I (1720–40) drove north expansion; Balaji Bajirao (1740–61) belongs to the Panipat III generation. By the Sangola (सांगोला) agreement (1750), the Chhatrapati became a do-nothing king and the Peshwa the real head.

- **Exam Anchor:** Nadir Shah (नादिर शाह)'s Delhi sack (1739) is a separate Afghan shock from Abdali's Panipat victory.

1. Sambhaji was captured and executed by Aurangzeb in 1689.
2. Rajaram carried on the resistance from Jinji in Tamil Nadu.
3. Tarabai led the Marathas as regent after Rajaram's death in 1700.

A. 1 and 2
B. 2 and 3
C. Only 1
D. 1, 2 and 3

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Correct Answer:** **D** (1, 2 and 3)

**Detailed Explanation:**
- **Statement 1 is correct:** **Sambhaji** was captured at Sangameshwar by Mughal commander Muqarrab Khan in 1689 and, after refusing to convert or surrender his forts, was brutally executed at Tulapur on Aurangzeb's orders.
- **Statement 2 is correct:** Following Sambhaji's execution, **Rajaram** was crowned at Raigad but was forced to flee south to the fortress of **Jinji** (Gingee, Tamil Nadu), where he sustained an eight-year Mughal siege (1690–1698) while Maratha generals Santaji Ghorpade and Dhanaji Jadhav waged devastating guerrilla war in the Deccan.
- **Statement 3 is correct:** After Rajaram's premature death at Sinhagad in 1700, his widow **Maharani Tarabai** assumed supreme leadership as regent for her young son Shivaji II, successfully defending the Maratha homeland until Aurangzeb's death in 1707.

**Key Exam Takeaway / Trap:**

**Q2.** Consider the following statements about Ashtapradhan:

1. It was an advisory body established by Shivaji in 1674.
2. The offices were strictly hereditary from the beginning.
3. Ministers were paid cash salaries rather than land grants during Shivaji's reign.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

A. 1 and 2
B. 2 and 3
C. 1 and 3 only
D. 1, 2 and 3

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Correct Answer:** **C** (1 and 3 only)

**Detailed Explanation:**
- **Statement 1 is correct:** Shivaji formally established the **Ashtapradhan** at his coronation in 1674 as an eight-member council to advise the sovereign on administrative affairs.
- **Statement 2 is incorrect:** Under Shivaji, the offices were **merit-based and strictly non-hereditary**. Shivaji retained absolute power to appoint, transfer, or dismiss any minister. Offices became hereditary only in the 18th century under the later Peshwas and Chhatrapati Shahu.
- **Statement 3 is correct:** To prevent the rise of autonomous feudal warlords, Shivaji abolished the practice of granting revenue-yielding estates (*jagirs* or *mokasa*) to ministers, paying them direct **cash salaries (*nagad*)** from the state treasury.

**Key Exam Takeaway / Trap:**
- Shivaji's cardinal administrative rule: **No hereditary ministerial office** and **no autonomous jagir grants** to high ministers. These principles were reversed during the 18th-century Peshwa era.

</details>
