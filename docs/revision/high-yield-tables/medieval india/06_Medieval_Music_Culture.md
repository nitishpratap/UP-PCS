**Q3.** With reference to Hindustani vs Carnatic divergence, which statements is/are correct?

1. After the 12th–13th century, north Hindustani and south Carnatic branches diverged.
2. Khayal gharanas are identical with Carnatic melakarta theory alone.
3. This topic’s court focus is mainly the north / Mughal–Sultanate stream.

A. Only 1
B. Only 1 and 2
C. Only 3
D. Only 1 and 3

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Correct Answer:** **D** (1 and 3 only are correct)

**Detailed Explanation:**
- **Statement 1 is correct:** Following the Turkish invasions and the establishment of the Delhi Sultanate (12th–13th century), classical Indian music diverged into two distinct traditions: the **Hindustani** tradition in northern and central India (heavily influenced by Persian-Arabic aesthetics) and the **Carnatic** tradition in southern India (which retained older Sanskrit temple traditions).
- **Statement 2 is incorrect:** Hindustani music developed through family-based stylistic lineages called **Gharanas** (e.g., Gwalior, Agra, Kirana, Delhi) emphasizing improvised exposition (*alaap*, *taan*). Carnatic music is organized theoretically around the 72 **Melakarta** raga system formulated by Venkatamakhin and centered on pre-composed devotional compositions (*Kritis*). They are fundamentally distinct systems, not identical.
- **Statement 3 is correct:** The medieval music history syllabus for UPPCS focuses predominantly on the northern Hindustani stream, court patronage under the Sultans, Mughals, and regional Nawabs.

**Key Exam Takeaway / Trap:**
- *Two Streams:* Hindustani = Gharana lineages, improvisation, Persian-Sufi synthesis; Carnatic = Melakarta scale framework, Kriti compositions (Tyagaraja, Muthuswami Dikshitar, Syama Sastri).

**Q14.** Which one of the following is NOT correctly matched?

A. Tansen — Miyan Ki Todi / Miyan Ki Malhar / Darbari Kanada
B. Man Singh Tomar → Tansen — dhrupad memory path
C. Muhammad Shah — khayal / tappa bloom
D. Tansen — primary khayal theorist of Muhammad Shah’s court

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Correct Answer:** **D** (Tansen — primary khayal theorist of Muhammad Shah’s court is NOT correctly matched)

**Detailed Explanation:**
- **Option D is incorrectly matched (Correct Answer):** Miyan Tansen lived in the **16th century** under Emperor Akbar and was the master of **Dhrupad**, not Khayal. The primary Khayal theorists and composers of Emperor Muhammad Shah's 18th-century court were Niyamat Khan (**Sadarang**) and Firoz Khan (**Adarang**).
- **Option A is correctly matched:** Tansen created *Miyan Ki Todi*, *Miyan Ki Malhar*, and *Darbari Kanada*.
- **Option B is correctly matched:** Raja Man Singh Tomar of Gwalior nurtured the Dhrupad tradition that Tansen inherited.
- **Option C is correctly matched:** Emperor Muhammad Shah Rangeela presided over the flowering of Khayal and Tappa.

**Key Exam Takeaway / Trap:**
- *Rangeela Court Musicians:* The master composers of Muhammad Shah's court were **Sadarang** and **Adarang** (Khayal) and **Shori Miyan** (Tappa), not Tansen.

**Q15.** With reference to overall medieval music synthesis, which statements is/are correct?

1. Sultanate music fused Khusrau, Chishti sama, and Hindavi experiments.
2. Akbar-phase imperial dhrupad peaked with Tansen as Navratna.
3. Later Mughal phase under Muhammad Shah flowered in khayal and tappa.

A. Only 1 and 2
B. All 1, 2 and 3
C. 2 and 3
D. Only 1

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Correct Answer:** **B** (All 1, 2 and 3 are correct)

**Detailed Explanation:**
- **Statement 1 is correct:** The Sultanate era laid the groundwork for Indo-Islamic musical fusion through Amir Khusrau's musical innovations, Chishti *Sama* gatherings, and the incorporation of the *Hindavi* vernacular.
- **Statement 2 is correct:** The early Mughal period reached its classical pinnacle under Emperor Akbar, who made Miyan Tansen a *Navratna* and established Dhrupad as the premier court music.
- **Statement 3 is correct:** The later Mughal era under Muhammad Shah "Rangeela" witnessed the democratization and evolution of music into lighter, more expressive forms, specifically Khayal (Sadarang-Adarang) and Tappa (Shori Miyan).

**Key Exam Takeaway / Trap:**
- *Complete Historical Synthesis:* Sultanate (Khusrau / Qawwali / Tarana) → Early Mughal (Akbar / Tansen / Dhrupad) → Later Mughal (Muhammad Shah / Sadarang / Khayal & Tappa).
