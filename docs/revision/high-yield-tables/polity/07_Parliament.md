## Lok Sabha vs Rajya Sabha (one table)

| Point | Lok Sabha | Rajya Sabha |
|-------|-----------|-------------|
| Nature | **Popular** chamber; can be **dissolved** | **Federal / continuing** chamber; **never** dissolved |
| Election | Direct (FPTP) | Indirect (PR-STV by elected MLAs) |
| Max strength | **550** (+ 2 Anglo-Indian, now ended) | **250** (238 + 12 nominated) |
| Present | **543** | **245** |
| Term | **5 years** (House) | **6 years** (member); 1/3 retire every 2 years |
| Min. age | **25** | **30** |
| Presiding officer | **Speaker** (a member) | **Vice-President** (not a member) |
| Money Bill | Introduced **only here**; Speaker’s certificate final | Cannot reject / amend; 14-day recommendatory role |
| Confidence | CoM responsible **only to LS** | No-confidence **cannot** be moved |
| Joint sitting | Speaker presides; LS numbers usually prevail | Sits as one body with LS |
| Representation | Population-based | **Fourth Schedule** — **not** equal per State |
