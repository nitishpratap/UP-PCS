### Table 1: Ancient & Medieval Historical Sites, Dynasties and Monuments in Uttar Pradesh

| Historical Site / Monument | Location / District | Associated Dynasty / Ruler | Era / Century | Key Historical & Archaeological Significance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sohgaura Copper Plate** | **Gorakhpur** | Mauryan Empire (Chandragupta Maurya / Ashoka) | 3rd Century BCE | Oldest known copper-plate inscription in India; mentions state granaries and drought relief measures in Brahmi script. |
| **Sarnath (Isipatana / Mrigadava)** | **Varanasi** | Mauryan to Gupta | 6th Century BCE – 5th Century CE | Site of Lord Buddha's **First Sermon (Dharmachakrapravartana)**. Features Ashoka's Lion Capital (National Emblem) and Dhamek Stupa. |
| **Shravasti (Sahet-Mahet)** | **Shravasti / Balrampur** | Kosala Kingdom (Prasenjit) | 6th Century BCE | Capital of Kosala; site where Lord Buddha spent maximum rainy season retreats (**25 Varshavas**) and performed Jetavana miracles. |
| **Kushinagar (Kasia)** | **Kushinagar** | Malla Mahajanapada | 483 BCE | Site of Lord Buddha's **Mahaparinirvana** (death/salvation). Features Parinirvana Temple (Reclining Buddha) and Ramabhar Stupa. |
| **Piprahwa / Ganwaria** | **Siddharthnagar** | Shakya Republic | 5th–3rd Century BCE | Identified with ancient **Kapilavastu**; stupa excavated with caskets containing Buddha's bodily relics. |
| **Kaushambi** | **Kaushambi** (near Prayagraj) | Vatsa Mahajanapada (King Udayana) | 6th Century BCE | Capital of Vatsa; important commercial and Buddhist centre (Ghoshitarama Monastery). Visited by Hieun Tsang. |
| **Bhitari Pillar Inscription** | **Saidpur, Ghazipur** | Gupta Empire (Skandagupta) | 5th Century CE (455–467 CE) | Details Skandagupta's decisive victory over the invading White Hunas and Pushyamitras. |
| **Dashavatara Temple** | **Deogarh, Lalitpur** | Imperial Gupta Dynasty | 6th Century CE | One of the earliest surviving stone panchayatana Hindu temples; famous for Vishnu Sheshashayi panel. |
| **Bhitargaon Brick Temple** | **Kanpur Nagar** | Imperial Gupta Dynasty | 5th–6th Century CE | Oldest surviving terraced brick temple with a shikhara and arched doorways in northern India. |
| **Kannauj (Kanyakubja)** | **Kannauj** | Harshavardhana / Gurjara-Pratiharas | 7th–10th Century CE | Capital of Emperor Harsha; epicentre of the **Tripartite Struggle** (Pratiharas, Palas, Rashtrakutas). "Mahodaya Shri". |
| **Atala Masjid** | **Jaunpur** | Sharqi Dynasty (Ibrahim Shah Sharqi) | 1408 CE | Masterpiece of Jaunpur Sharqi architecture; built on the site of Atala Devi temple, featuring sloping walls and high propylons. |
| **Agra Fort** | **Agra** | Mughal Empire (Akbar) | 1565–1573 CE | Red sandstone fortress and primary residence of Mughal emperors until 1648. UNESCO World Heritage Site (1983). |
| **Fatehpur Sikri** | **Agra District** | Mughal Empire (Akbar) | 1571–1585 CE | Akbar's ceremonial capital built in honour of Sufi Saint Sheikh Salim Chishti; features **Buland Darwaza**, Panch Mahal, and Jodha Bai's Palace. |
| **Bara Imambara (Asafi Imambara)** | **Lucknow** | Nawab Asaf-ud-Daula | 1784 CE | Built as a famine-relief project; famous for its unsupported central vaulted hall and the intricate labyrinth (**Bhul-bhulaiya**). |
| **Rumi Darwaza** | **Lucknow** | Nawab Asaf-ud-Daula | 1784 CE | 60-foot tall monumental entrance gateway to Lucknow modeled on the Sublime Porte in Constantinople (Istanbul). |

| **Faizabad / Awadh (1857)** | **Faizabad / Ayodhya** | **Maulvi Ahmadullah Shah** ("Lighthouse of Rebellion") | Colin Campbell | Fiery leader of anti-British resistance who gave immense tactical trouble to British forces; killed at Powayan. |

| **Oudh Kisan Sabha** | **Pratapgarh** | **Baba Ramchandra**, Jawaharlal Nehru, Mata Badal Pande | Formed Oct 1920 | Peasant mobilization in Pratapgarh, Rae Bareli, Sultanpur; demanded abolishing begar and illegal eviction. |

| **Hindustan Socialist Republican Association (HSRA)** | **Feroz Shah Kotla (founded), active across UP (Kanpur/Agra/Prayagraj)** | Chandrashekhar Azad, Bhagat Singh, Sukhdev | British CID | Reorganized HRA into HSRA in Sept 1928 with a commitment to socialism. Azad martyred at **Alfred Park (Prayagraj)** on **27 Feb 1931**. |
| **Ballia Parallel Government** | **Ballia** | **Chittu Pandey** ("Sher-e-Ballia") | Quit India Movement (Aug 1942) | Set up the first independent parallel government (*Swaraj Sarkar*) during Quit India Movement; released all political prisoners. |

| **Folk Dance** | **Rai Dance** | **Bundelkhand** | Beriya community women | Vibrant peacock dance performed on auspicious occasions (birth of son, festivals) to the rapid beat of *Mridang*. |
| **Folk Dance** | **Karma Dance** | **Sonbhadra & Mirzapur** | Kharwar, Kol, Baiga, and Gond tribes | Performed during Karma festival (autumn) around the sacred Karma tree invoking agricultural fertility and prosperity. |
| **Folk Dance** | **Pai Danda (Dandia)** | **Bundelkhand** | Ahir / Yadav community | Heroic stick dance resembling Gujarati Dandiya; performed during Diwali enacting Krishna's pastoral life. |
| **Folk Song** | **Alha** | **Bundelkhand (Mahoba / Jhansi)** | Traditional Alhets | Heroic narrative ballads in *Banaffari* dialect recounting 52 fierce battles of warrior brothers Alha and Udal. |
| **Folk Song** | **Kajri** | **Mirzapur & Varanasi** | Purvanchal singers | Semi-classical rainy-season (Shravan/Bhadon) song expressing romantic longing, separation, and Radha-Krishna devotion. |
| **Folk Song** | **Birha** | **Purvanchal (Eastern UP)** | Ahir / Yadav community | Narrative folk genre chronicling social events, historical ballads, and mythological episodes with high-pitched vocal delivery. |
| **Folk Theatre** | **Nautanki** | **Hathras & Kanpur** | Hathras School (Pt. Natharam Gaur), Kanpur School (Shrikrishna Pahalwan) | **Hathras style**: Classical, verse-dominated, high melody (*Doha, Chaubola*).<br>**Kanpur style**: Theatrical, prose dialogues, social dramas. |
| **Major Fair** | **Bateshwar Fair** | **Bateshwar (Agra)** | On banks of Yamuna | Ancient holy pilgrimage (101 Shiva temples) and the **largest animal/camel/cattle fair** in Western UP (Kartik Purnima). |
| **Major Fair** | **Dadri Fair** | **Ballia** | Dedicated to Sage Bhrigu | **Second largest cattle fair in India** (after Sonepur); held annually on Kartik Purnima. |
| **Major Fair** | **Nauchandi Fair** | **Meerut** | Symbol of communal harmony | Month-long post-Holi fair celebrating both Navchandi Devi Temple and Saint Bale Miyan Dargah. |
| **Major Fair** | **Dewa Sharif Fair** | **Dewa (Barabanki)** | Dargah of Haji Waris Ali Shah | Celebrated during Safar/Kartik; promotes universal brotherhood (*Jo Rab Hai Wahi Ram Hai*). |
