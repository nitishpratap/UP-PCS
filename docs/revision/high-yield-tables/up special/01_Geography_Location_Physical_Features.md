### Table 2: Physical Divisions of Uttar Pradesh (Bhabhar-Terai vs Gangetic Plain vs Southern Plateau)

| Physical Division | Geographical Extent & Districts | Geomorphological Composition | Drainage & Hydrology | Soil Types & Agricultural Characteristics |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Bhabhar & Terai Zone** | **Northern Foothills Belt**:<br>• *Bhabhar*: Saharanpur, Bijnor, Lakhimpur.<br>• *Terai*: Saharanpur to Deoria, Kushinagar. | • **Bhabhar**: Porous, pebble-strewn, coarse boulder piedmont tract at Siwalik base.<br>• **Terai**: Low-lying, marshy, waterlogged, swampy plain immediately south of Bhabhar. | • Rivers disappear underground in porous Bhabhar.<br>• Streams re-emerge in Terai creating wetlands, high water table, and swamps. | • *Bhabhar*: Unfit for cultivation (dense forests).<br>• *Terai*: Highly fertile silty alluvial soil; ideal for **Sugarcane, Paddy, and Jute**. Historically malarial. |
| **2. Gangetic Plain (Central Alluvial Plain)** | **Vast Central Basin** (accounts for ~70% of UP's area between Yamuna in west and Gandak in east). | Deep structural trough filled with quaternary alluvium deposited by Himalayan and Peninsular rivers.<br>Divided into: (1) Upper Ganga Plain, (2) Middle Ganga Plain. | Drained by Ganga, Yamuna, Ramganga, Gomti, Ghaghara, Sarda, Rapti.<br>Gentle slope from North-West to South-East. | • **Bangar (Older Alluvium)**: Elevated terrace plain, clayey loam, contains calcareous nodules (*kankar*) and alkaline patches (*reh/usar*).<br>• **Khadar (Newer Alluvium)**: Floodplain silt, renewed annually, highly fertile, non-calcareous. |
| **3. Southern Plateau & Hill Region (Bundelkhand & Baghelkhand)** | **Southern Fringe (11 Districts)**:<br>• *Bundelkhand*: Jhansi, Jalaun, Lalitpur, Hamirpur, Mahoba, Banda, Chitrakoot.<br>• *Baghelkhand*: Mirzapur, Sonbhadra, South Prayagraj. | Part of the ancient Precambrian Deccan Peninsular Shield (Vindhyan system, Bundelkhand Gneiss, granites).<br>Undulating topography, residual hills (*Tilas*), ravines (*Beehad* along Chambal and Yamuna). | Drained by Chambal, Betwa, Ken, Son, Tons, Rihand, Dhasan flowing northward into Yamuna and Ganga.<br>Water-scarce, seasonal flash floods and summer droughts. | • **Bundelkhand Soils**:<br>  - *Rakar*: Coarse red gravelly soil on rocky slopes.<br>  - *Parwa*: Light sandy/yellow loam.<br>  - *Kabar*: Coarse black clayey soil.<br>  - *Mar*: Highly fertile black soil (resembling Black Cotton Soil / Regur).<br>• Main Crops: Gram, Pulses, Mustard, Millets, Oilseeds. |

## 🔍 Key Table Observations & Memory Anchors

- **Exam Anchor:** Sonbhadra is the only district in India that shares boundaries with 4 different States: Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, and Bihar.
- **Exam Anchor:** Lalitpur district is surrounded on three sides by Madhya Pradesh.
- **Exam Anchor:** Lakhimpur Kheri is the largest district in UP by area (7,680 km²).
- **Exam Anchor:** Hapur is the smallest district in UP by area (660 km²).
- **Exam Anchor:** Sugarcane and Paddy (Rice) are the primary commercial crops cultivated extensively in the fertile Terai belt of UP.
- **Exam Anchor:** Bangar soil is the older alluvium situated above flood levels, containing calcareous deposits known as Kankar.
- **Exam Anchor:** Khadar soil is the newer, highly fertile silt deposited annually in the river floodplains.
- **Exam Anchor:** Govind Ballabh Pant Sagar on the Rihand River in Sonbhadra district is the largest artificial reservoir in India.
