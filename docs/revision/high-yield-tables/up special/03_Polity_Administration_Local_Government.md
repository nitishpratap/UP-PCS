| **Uttar Pradesh Legislative Assembly (Vidhan Sabha)** | **403 Seats** (Largest in India) | **Article 170**<br>• Term: 5 Years<br>• Minimum Age: **25 Years**<br>• Quorum: 10 or 1/10th (41) | Direct universal adult franchise from single-member constituencies across 75 districts. | • **Reserved Seats**: **84 SC**, **2 ST** (total 86 reserved seats).<br>• Exclusive power over Money Bills (Art. 198).<br>• Council of Ministers is collectively responsible solely to the Vidhan Sabha.<br>• Pre-Uttarakhand strength was **425 seats**. |
| **Uttar Pradesh Legislative Council (Vidhan Parishad)** | **100 Seats** (Largest in India) | **Articles 169 & 171**<br>• Permanent House (Cannot be dissolved)<br>• Term: 6 Years (1/3rd retire every 2 years)<br>• Minimum Age: **30 Years** | • **36 Members**: Elected by Local Authorities constituencies.<br>• **36 Members**: Elected by MLAs (Vidhan Sabha).<br>• **10 Members**: Nominated by Governor (Literature, Science, Art, Co-operative movement, Social service).<br>• **9 Members**: Elected by Graduates constituencies.<br>• **9 Members**: Elected by Teachers constituencies. *(Statutory composition totaling 100)* | • Maximum constitutional ceiling = 1/3rd of Assembly (134 seats); UP chose 100.<br>• Can delay an ordinary bill passed by Assembly for a maximum of 4 months (3 months + 1 month), but cannot veto it.<br>• No power over Money Bills (can delay max 14 days). |

| **Rajya Sabha Representation from UP** | **31 Seats** (Largest in India) | **Article 80**<br>• Term: 6 Years (1/3rd retire every 2 years)<br>• Minimum Age: 30 Years | Proportional representation by single transferable vote (STV) by 403 elected MLAs of UP. | • Largest contingent in the Council of States.<br>• Governor does NOT nominate members to Rajya Sabha.<br>• Pre-Uttarakhand strength was **34 seats**. |
| **State Executive (Governor & Council of Ministers)** | Council of Ministers capped at **15% of 403 = Max 60 Ministers** | **Articles 153 to 167**<br>• Governor appointed by President (Art. 155)<br>• 91st Constitutional Amendment Act (2003) | Council of Ministers headed by Chief Minister aids and advises the Governor. | • First Governor: **Sarojini Naidu** (15 Aug 1947 – 2 Mar 1949).<br>• First Chief Minister: **Govind Ballabh Pant** (1950–1954).<br>• First Woman CM: **Sucheta Kripalani** (1963–1967).<br>• Value of UP MLA vote in Presidential Election is highest in India (**208**). |

### Table 3: Administrative Hierarchy & Local Self-Government Framework

| Administrative Tier | Executive Head / Political Head | Statutory / Administrative Basis | Total Units in UP | Key Functions & Responsibilities |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Administrative Division (Mandal)** | **Divisional Commissioner (IAS)** | Land Revenue / Administrative Setup | **18 Divisions** | • Coordinates inter-district administration, revenue appeals, law and order, and state developmental schemes across 3 to 6 districts.<br>• Largest divisions: Lucknow, Kanpur, Meerut (6 districts each). |
| **District (Zila)** | **District Magistrate / Collector (DM / DC)** | Code of Criminal Procedure / Land Revenue Acts | **75 Districts** | • Principal representative of State Government; heads revenue collection, law & order, disaster management, and district coordination. |
| **Sub-Division / Tehsil** | **Sub-Divisional Magistrate (SDM) / Tehsildar** | UP Revenue Code 2006 | **351 Tehsils** | • Sub-divisional administration, revenue courts, land mutation, issuance of caste/income/domicile certificates. |
| **Development Block (Vikas Khand)** | **Block Development Officer (BDO)** | Community Development Administration | **826 Blocks** | • Unit of rural developmental administration; executes MNREGA, rural housing, sanitation, and agricultural extension. |
| **Zila Panchayat (District Tier)** | **Zila Panchayat Adhyaksha** (Political);<br>**Apar Mukhya Adhikari / CDO** (Executive) | UP Kshettra Panchayats and Zila Panchayats Act, 1961 | **75 Zila Panchayats** | • Apex rural local self-government tier; prepares district annual development plan, maintains rural roads, inter-block infrastructure. |
| **Kshetra Panchayat (Block Tier)** | **Block Pramukh** (Political);<br>**Block Development Officer (BDO)** (Executive) | UP Kshettra Panchayats and Zila Panchayats Act, 1961 | **826 Kshetra Panchayats** | • Intermediate rural local tier; coordinates village plans, manages community assets, minor irrigation, public health. |
| **Gram Panchayat (Village Tier)** | **Gram Pradhan** (Political);<br>**Gram Panchayat Adhikari / Secretary** (Executive) | UP Panchayat Raj Act, 1947 | **~58,189 Gram Panchayats** | • Base rural self-governance unit; accountable to **Gram Sabha** (all registered voters); 50% reservation for women in UP. |
| **Nagar Nigam (Municipal Corporation)** | **Mayor (Mahapaur)** (Political);<br>**Municipal Commissioner (Nagar Ayukt - IAS)** (Executive) | UP Municipal Corporation Act, 1959 | **17 Municipal Corporations** | • Urban local bodies for large metropolitan cities (Lucknow, Kanpur, Prayagraj, Varanasi, Agra, Ghaziabad, Meerut, Bareilly, Aligarh, Moradabad, Gorakhpur, Jhansi, Saharanpur, Firozabad, Ayodhya, Mathura-Vrindavan, Shahjahanpur). |
| **Industrial Townships (Exception under Art. 243Q)** | **Chief Executive Officer (CEO - IAS)** | UP Industrial Area Development Act, 1976 | **NOIDA, Greater Noida, YEIDA** | • Governed by statutory industrial authorities providing civic amenities; exempt from forming elected Municipal Corporations under the proviso to Art. 243Q. |
