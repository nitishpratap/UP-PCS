### Table 2: Major Canal Networks, Dams and Irrigation Systems in Uttar Pradesh

| Canal Network / Dam Project | River / Water Source | Offtake / Barrage Location | Total System Length / Command Area | Beneficiary Districts & Historical Distinction |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Upper Ganga Canal (1854)** | **Ganga River** | **Bhimgoda Barrage, Haridwar** (Uttarakhand) | Main canal: ~295 km;<br>Total system: ~6,500 km | • Designed by **Sir Proby T. Cautley**; opened on **8 April 1854**.<br>• Irrigates Western UP: Saharanpur, Muzaffarnagar, Meerut, Bulandshahr, Aligarh, Mathura, Agra, Etawah, Kanpur. |
| **Lower Ganga Canal (1878)** | **Ganga River** | **Narora Barrage, Bulandshahr** | Main canal: ~100 km;<br>Total system: ~8,200 km | • Opened in **1878**.<br>• Supplies cooling water to Narora Atomic Power Station; irrigates Bulandshahr, Aligarh, Etah, Mainpuri, Farrukhabad, Kanpur, Fatehpur, Prayagraj. |
| **Sharda Canal System (1928)** | **Sharda (Kali) River** | **Banbasa Barrage** (Pilibhit / Champawat border) | Total network length: **~12,368 km** (Longest canal network in UP) | • Commissioned in **1928**.<br>• Irrigates 16 districts of Central and Terai UP: Pilibhit, Bareilly, Shahjahanpur, Lakhimpur, Hardoi, Sitapur, Lucknow, Unnao, Rae Bareli, Barabanki, Prayagraj. |
| **Eastern Yamuna Canal** | **Yamuna River** | **Tajewala / Hathinikund Barrage** (Saharanpur border) | Total system: ~1,440 km | • Originally dug during Mughal era (Shah Jahan); remodeled by British engineers (Col. John Colvin) in **1830**.<br>• Irrigates Saharanpur, Shamli, Muzaffarnagar, Meerut, Baghpat, Ghaziabad. |
| **Agra Canal (1874)** | **Yamuna River** | **Okhla Barrage, Delhi** | Total system: ~1,600 km | • Opened in **1874**.<br>• Traverses Delhi, Haryana, and irrigates Mathura, Agra, and parts of Rajasthan (Bharatpur). |
| **Sarda Sahayak Project** | **Ghaghara & Sharda Rivers** | **Katarniaghat Barrage** (Bahraich) on Ghaghara & Sharda feeder canal | Huge feeder channel (~260 km) | • Augments water supply to the tail-ends of the Sharda Canal system in Awadh and Purvanchal (Lucknow, Rae Bareli, Sultanpur, Jaunpur, Azamgarh, Ghazipur). |
| **Saryu Nahar National Project** | **5 Rivers: Ghaghara, Saryu, Rapti, Banganga, Rohini** | Barrages on Ghaghara, Saryu, and Rapti | Over **6,600 km** of canals; dedicated Dec 2021 | • Massive project benefiting ~29 lakh farmers across **9 districts of Eastern UP**: Bahraich, Shravasti, Balrampur, Gonda, Siddharthnagar, Basti, Sant Kabir Nagar, Gorakhpur, Maharajganj. |
| **Ken-Betwa River Interlinking** | **Ken & Betwa Rivers** | **Daudhan Dam** (Chhatarpur, MP) & 221 km link canal | Transfers surplus water from Ken basin to Betwa basin | • **India's first National River Interlinking Project** under implementation.<br>• Provides critical irrigation and drinking water to drought-prone **Bundelkhand** (Banda, Mahoba, Jhansi, Lalitpur in UP). |
| **Matatila Dam (Rani Lakshmibai Dam)** | **Betwa River** | **Lalitpur District** (UP-MP border) | Storage capacity: 1,132 MCM; 30.6 MW Hydel | • Constructed in **1958**; irrigates Jhansi, Jalaun, Hamirpur, and Lalitpur via Betwa canal network. |
| **Rihand Dam (Govind Ballabh Pant Sagar)** | **Rihand River** (Tributary of Son) | **Pipri, Sonbhadra District** | Reservoir surface area: ~466 km² (Largest man-made reservoir in India) | • Constructed in **1962**; concrete gravity dam powering HINDALCO aluminium plant and thermal stations, feeding canals in Sonbhadra/Mirzapur. |
