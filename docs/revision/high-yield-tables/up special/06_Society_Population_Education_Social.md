### Table 2: Scheduled Tribes of Uttar Pradesh (Ethnography, Customs & Distribution)

| Scheduled Tribe | Primary Districts of Habitat | Cultural Customs, Social Organization & Beliefs | Traditional Festivals & Rituals | Key Examination Fact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tharu Tribe** | **Terai Belt**: Lakhimpur Kheri, Bahraich, Shravasti, Balrampur, Maharajganj | • Matrilineal joint family tradition; women hold dominant household status.<br>• Traditional houses built of bamboo and reed (*Narhul*).<br>• Claim descent from the Rajput royal line of Thar desert (Rajasthan). | • **Celebrates Diwali as a day of mourning / sorrow (*Shoka Parva*)**, offering *Putla Vidhan* to ancestors.<br>• Celebrates **Bajhar festival** in Jyeshtha/Ashadha month.<br>• Perform *Lathmarva Bhoj* in marriage ceremonies. | • **Most populous tribe in Uttar Pradesh**.<br>• Rajiv Gandhi National Tribal College / Tharu Vikas Pariyojana established in Lakhimpur Kheri (1980). |
| **Buxa / Bhoksa Tribe** | **Bijnor District** (and foothills of Pilibhit/Kumaon border) | • Patrilineal social structure with gotra divisions (Buxa Rajput, Buxa Brahmin, Buxa Ahir).<br>• Practice bride-price (*Kanya Shulk*) and levirate/sororate marriage. | • Worship **Chamunda Devi** (Kashipur) as their supreme deity.<br>• Celebrate Chaiti Mela at Kashipur and traditional Hindu festivals. | • Second major tribe in Terai; concentrated heavily in **Bijnor district**. |
| **Jaunsari Tribe** | **Foothills of Saharanpur** (bordering Dehradun/Jaunsar-Bawar) | • Historically practiced fraternal polyandry (*Draupadi Vivah*).<br>• Folk dances: *Harul*, *Raso*, *Ghumsu*. | • Worship Mahasu Devta (manifestation of Lord Shiva).<br>• Celebrate Magh Mela and Bisu festival. | • After bifurcation in 2000, main population stayed in Uttarakhand (Dehradun), with fringes in Saharanpur. |
| **Kharwar (Khairwar) Tribe** | **Sonbhadra, Mirzapur, Chandauli, Ghazipur, Deoria, Ballia** | • Historically associated with traditional extraction of Catechu (*Katha*) from Khair trees (*Acacia catechu*).<br>• Known for physical valour and martial traditions. | • Perform the famous **Karma Dance** during autumn/spring harvest.<br>• Worship Badadeoji, Dulhadeo, and Bansapti Devi. | • Major tribal group of the Vindhyan and Sonbhadra plateau region. |
| **Sahariya Tribe** | **Lalitpur District** (exclusively) | • Forest-dwelling indigenous group; highly vulnerable tribal group (PVTG). | • Worship forest deities; celebrate traditional tribal dances (*Sahar*, *Lahanga*). | • **Confined exclusively to Lalitpur district** in UP. |
| **Parhaiya / Baiga / Agariya / Chero / Bhuiya** | **Sonbhadra District** (exclusively) | • **Agariya**: Traditional iron smelters using charcoal furnaces.<br>• **Baiga**: Traditional herbal medicine practitioners and priest/shaman (*Guniya*).<br>• **Chero & Bhuiya**: Forest gatherers and marginal farmers. | • Nature-worshipping festivals, Sarhul, Karma, and Phagua. | • Sonbhadra is the most tribally diverse district in UP, hosting nearly **10 recognized ST sub-groups**. |
