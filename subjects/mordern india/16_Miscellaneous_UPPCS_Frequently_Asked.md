## 16.1 Slogans — Who Said What

Nationalism also spread through short lines people could shout. Exam asks: **which leader said this?**

| Slogan | Who | When / where it belongs |
|--------|-----|-------------------------|
| Swaraj is my birthright and I shall have it | **Bal Gangadhar Tilak** | Extremist / Home Rule mood |
| Inquilab Zindabad | **Hasrat Mohani** coined it; **Bhagat Singh** made it famous | Revolutionary stream |
| Do or Die | **Mahatma Gandhi** | Quit India, Aug 1942 |
| Give me blood and I will give you freedom | **Subhas Chandra Bose** | INA / Azad Hind |
| Jai Hind / Delhi Chalo | **Bose / INA** | Armed struggle abroad |
| Simon Go Back | Anti-Simon protests (Lala Lajpat Rai linked) | 1928 |
| Vande Mataram | From Bankim’s *Anandamath* | National song stream |
| Back to the Vedas | **Dayanand Saraswati** | Arya Samaj |
| One caste, one religion, one God for mankind | **Sri Narayana Guru** | Social reform |

**Don’t confuse**

- Tilak ≠ Do or Die (that is Gandhi).  
- Gandhi ≠ Inquilab (that is Hasrat / Bhagat).  
- Bose ≠ Do or Die.  
- Vande Mataram is Bankim’s song, not Tagore’s.

## 16.5 British Acts — Simple Ladder

Read this like climbing stairs. Each Act adds one big idea.

| Year | Act | One big idea |
|------|-----|--------------|
| 1773 | Regulating Act | First control over Company; GG of Bengal |
| 1784 | Pitt’s India Act | **Board of Control**; British Govt stronger |
| 1813 | Charter Act | Company trade monopoly ends (except tea/China); education grant |
| 1833 | Charter Act | GG of India; Company ends as trading body |
| 1853 | Charter Act | Open competition for ICS begins |
| 1858 | GOI Act | Crown rule after 1857 |
| 1861 | Councils Act | Portfolio system; Indians in councils |
| 1892 | Councils Act | Indirect election idea starts |
| **1909** | Morley-Minto | **Separate electorates for Muslims** |
| **1919** | Montagu-Chelmsford | **Dyarchy** in provinces; **Chamber of Princes** |
| **1935** | GOI Act | Provincial autonomy; federal scheme (not fully used) |
| 1878 | Vernacular Press Act | Press control (Lytton) |
| 1883 | Ilbert Bill | Indian judges & Europeans — big controversy |
| 1919 | Rowlatt Act | Jail without trial |

**Compare three reforms**

| | 1909 | 1919 | 1935 |
|---|------|------|------|
| Famous for | Separate electorates | Dyarchy | Provincial autonomy |
| Chamber of Princes | No | **Yes** | Continues in that era |

**Don’t confuse**

- Separate electorates start in **1909**, not 1919.  
- Chamber of Princes = **1919**, not 1935.  
- Board of Control = **Pitt’s 1784**, not Regulating Act.

## 16.6 Treaties — Learn by War Family

### Carnatic / early wars

- **Aix-la-Chapelle 1748** — First Carnatic War ends; Madras returned.  
- **Paris 1763** — Third Carnatic / Seven Years’ War context; French lose political edge.

### Bengal

- **Allahabad 1765** — Diwani of Bengal, Bihar, Orissa (after Buxar).

### Mysore

- Madras **1769** (1st war)  
- Mangalore **1784** (2nd war)  
- Srirangapatna **1792** (3rd) and **1799** (4th; Tipu dies)

### Maratha

- **Salbai 1782** — First Anglo-Maratha War ends.  
- **Bassein 1802** — Baji Rao II accepts Subsidiary Alliance.

### Nepal / Sikh

- **Sugauli 1816** — Nepal war; British get Kumaon–Garhwal hill areas. **Not Kathmandu.**  
- Lahore **1846** / Amritsar **1846** — Sikh war & Kashmir sale to Gulab Singh.

### Nationalist-era “pacts”

- Lucknow Pact **1916**  
- Gandhi–Irwin Pact **1931**  
- Poona Pact **1932** (reserved seats raised)

**UPPCS 2019 match**

Allahabad 1765, Mangalore 1784, Salbai 1782, Madras 1769 → code **4, 2, 1, 3**.

**Don’t confuse**

- Salbai is Maratha, not Mysore.  
- Allahabad is **1765**, not 1782.  
- Sugauli did **not** give Kathmandu to the British.

## 16.8 Revolutionary Organisations — Story in Order

Read as a story, then keep the names.

1. **Bengal groups (early 1900s):** Anushilan Samiti, Yugantar.  
2. **Maharashtra:** Abhinav Bharat (**1904**) by **V.D. Savarkar** ← UPPCS 2018.  
3. **Abroad in WWI years:** Ghadar Party (**1913**, San Francisco); Berlin Committee.  
4. **North India 1920s:**  
   - HRA formed at **Kanpur, 1924**  
   - **Kakori train action, 9 Aug 1925** (Bismil, Ashfaqullah, Roshan Singh, Rajendra Lahiri)  
   - HRA becomes **HSRA in 1928** (Bhagat Singh, Chandrashekhar Azad)  
   - Assembly Bomb **1929**  
   - Bhagat–Rajguru–Sukhdev hanged **23 Mar 1931**  
   - Azad dies at Alfred Park, Allahabad **27 Feb 1931**  
5. **Bengal 1930:** Chittagong Armoury Raid — Surya Sen.  
6. **1940s:** INA / Azad Hind under Bose.

**Hardinge bomb (1912):** UPPCS 2023 links it to **Anushilan Samiti**.

**Don’t confuse**

- Abhinav Bharat = Savarkar, not Bhagat Singh.  
- HRA = 1924; HSRA = 1928.  
- Ghadar base = USA/San Francisco, not Berlin (Berlin Committee is separate).

## Common Traps (read once before exam)

1. First woman Congress President = Besant, not Sarojini.  
2. Gandhi President only at Belgaum, not Lahore.  
3. Separate electorates = 1909.  
4. Chamber of Princes = 1919.
