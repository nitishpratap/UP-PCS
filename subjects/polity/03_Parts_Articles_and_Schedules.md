## 3.0 Framework — How Parts, Articles & Schedules Work

### Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Part** | Major division of the Constitution grouping related Articles (currently **25 Parts**, I–XXII with additions like IVA, IXA, IXB, XIVA) |
| **Article** | Numbered constitutional provision; original ~395; now ~470 after amendments/insertions |
| **Schedule** | Annexed list/table giving details referred to by Articles (**12 Schedules**) |

### How It Works

- UPPCS tests this topic mainly as **match / NOT correctly matched** — one wrong pair fails the set.
- Learn three layers: (1) Part → subject, (2) Schedule → subject, (3) high-frequency Article → subject.
- Parts organise rights, Union/State machinery, federal relations, emergencies, and special provisions.
- Schedules hold operational detail (territories, oaths, lists, languages, defection, local subjects) so Articles stay shorter.
- Original 1950 design: **22 Parts, 8 Schedules**; later amendments added Parts (IVA, IXA, IXB, XIVA) and Schedules (9–12) and expanded language/list content.
- Do not confuse neighbouring labels: **Part XI vs XIVA**, **4th vs 9th Schedule**, **5th vs 6th Schedule**, **11th vs 12th Schedule**.
- Seventh Schedule is the federal heart — Union/State/Concurrent competence.
- Eighth Schedule language questions often ask **which is NOT included** (Bhojpuri trap).
- Article mapping questions may sit on citizenship (5–11), duties (51A), elections (324+), emergency (352/356/360), or local government — keep the map tight for prelims.

> **Exam note:** First ask: Is the stem about a **Part**, a **Schedule**, or an **Article number**? Wrong bucket wastes time.

### Exam Facts (raata)

- Constitution now has **25 Parts** and **12 Schedules** (standard teaching).
- Part III = FR; Part IV = DPSP; Part IVA = Duties.
- Part IX / IXA / IXB = Panchayats / Municipalities / Cooperatives.
- Part X = Scheduled & Tribal Areas; Part XI = Centre–State relations.
- Part XIVA = Tribunals; Part XVIII = Emergency.
- 12 Schedules from territories to local subjects.
- 11th Schedule = **29** items; 12th = **18** items.
- 6th Schedule States = Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura, Mizoram.
- Eighth Schedule = **22** languages.
- Anti-defection = **10th Schedule**.

### PYQs — Framework

1. **(UPPCS Prelims 2025, Q24)** Parts NOT correctly matched — Part XI — Municipalities  
   → **B. Only 3** (Municipalities = Part IXA).

2. **(UPPCS Prelims 2023, Q1)** Provision—Part NOT matched — Tribunals — Part XI  
   → **C** (Tribunals = Part XIVA).

### Examples (3.0)

| Example | Point illustrated |
|---------|-------------------|
| Asking “Duties which Part?” | Part IVA |
| Asking “RS seats which Schedule?” | Fourth |
| Asking “Bhojpuri in 8th?” | No |

## 3.1 Parts of the Constitution (Complete Table)

### Definitions

| **Important Parts (syllabus set)** | III, IV, IVA, IX, IXA, IXB, XV, XVIII, XXI — plus PYQ neighbours VIII, X, XI, XIV, XIVA, XVII |

### How It Works

- Memorise the **full Parts map** once; exams pick both syllabus “important Parts” and trap neighbours (XI, XIVA, XIII).
- Part I–II set the Union and citizenship frame; III–IVA set rights–duties–policy; V–VI set Union and State organs; VIII UTs; IX–IXB local/cooperative democracy.

## 3.2 Important Parts — Syllabus Deep Set

### Definitions

| Part | Core content |
|------|--------------|
| **Part III** | Fundamental Rights (Arts. 12–35) |
| **Part IV** | DPSP (Arts. 36–51) |
| **Part IVA** | Fundamental Duties (Art. 51A) |
| **Part IX** | Panchayats (73rd Amendment) |
| **Part IXA** | Municipalities (74th Amendment) |
| **Part IXB** | Cooperative Societies (97th Amendment) |
| **Part XV** | Elections (includes Art. 324 Election Commission frame) |
| **Part XVIII** | Emergency Provisions (Arts. 352, 356, 360…) |
| **Part XXI** | Temporary, Transitional and Special Provisions (Arts. 371–371J; historical 370 teaching) |

### How It Works

- **Part III** is justiciable armour of liberty/equality; suspension rules link to Emergency (Part XVIII) but deep FR doctrine lives mainly in Topic 5.
- **Part IV** guides the State toward welfare; non-justiciable but Fundamental to governance and Basic Structure harmony teaching.
- **Part IVA** duties were added by **42nd Amendment (1976)**; later duty related to education linked with **86th Amendment / Art. 21A** cluster.
- **Part IX / IXA** constitutionalise local self-government; subject lists sit in **11th / 12th Schedules**.
- **Part IXB** elevates cooperative societies (97th Amendment) — polity note; court scrutiny of 97th is advanced awareness.
- **Part XV** houses electoral constitutional framework; EC’s Art. 324 is the star Article.
- **Part XVIII** National / State / Financial Emergencies — comparison detail in Topic 15; here lock Part identity.
- **Part XXI** special State provisions (371–371J) and transitional devices; J&K Art. 370 historical special status teaching often tagged here.
- Syllabus “Important Parts” are the ones UPPCS loves for Part↔subject matching — master them colder than obscure Parts.

> **Exam note:** IVA = Duties; IXA = Municipalities; XVIII = Emergency; XXI = special/temporary — do not swap with XI/XIVA.

### Exam Facts (raata)

- FR = Part III; DPSP = Part IV; Duties = Part IVA.
- Panchayats = Part IX + 11th Schedule.
- Municipalities = Part IXA + 12th Schedule.
- Cooperatives = Part IXB.
- Elections = Part XV.
- Emergency = Part XVIII.
- Special/temporary = Part XXI.
- 42nd added Part IVA.
- 73rd/74th added IX/IXA (1992).
- 97th related to Part IXB.

2. **(UPPCS Prelims 2020, Q110)** — Duties correctly Part IVA.

## 3.4 Schedules — Overview Master Table

### Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Schedules of the Constitution** | Twelve annexed schedules detailing territories, oaths/emoluments, Assembly allocation, forms of oath, Scheduled areas/tribes, Tribal areas (NE), Union–State–Concurrent lists, languages, land-reform validation Acts, anti-defection, Panchayat subjects, and Municipality subjects |

### How It Works

- Original Constitution had **8 Schedules**; **9th** added by 1st Amendment (1951); **10th** by 52nd (1985); **11th/12th** by 73rd/74th (1992).
- Almost every year asks at least one Schedule↔Subject match.
- Golden four for matching drills: **3 (Oaths), 4 (RS seats), 7 (Lists), 8 (Languages)**.
- Golden traps: **2 vs 4**, **4 vs 9**, **9 vs 10**, **11 vs 12**.
- Fifth/Sixth are polity–governance tribal administration locks, not mere GK names.

### All 12 Schedules (complete)

| Schedule | Subject |
|----------|---------|
| **First** | Names of States and UTs; territorial extent |
| **Second** | Emoluments, allowances, privileges of constitutional dignitaries (President, Governors, Speakers, Deputy Speakers, Chairman/Deputy Chairman of Councils, Judges of Supreme Court and High Courts, CAG) |
| **Third** | Forms of Oaths or Affirmations |
| **Fourth** | Allocation of seats in the Rajya Sabha (Council of States) |
| **Fifth** | Administration and control of Scheduled Areas and Scheduled Tribes |
| **Sixth** | Administration of tribal areas in Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura and Mizoram |
| **Seventh** | Union List, State List, Concurrent List |
| **Eighth** | Languages recognised by the Constitution |
| **Ninth** | Validation of certain Acts and Regulations (originally land-reform and related agrarian measures; later other validating entries also appeared) |
| **Tenth** | Disqualification on ground of defection (Anti-defection) |
| **Eleventh** | Powers/authority of Panchayats — **29** subjects |
| **Twelfth** | Powers/authority of Municipalities — **18** subjects |

> **Exam note:** If you remember only one line: **3-Oath, 4-RS, 7-Lists, 8-Lang, 9-Acts, 10-Defection, 11-29, 12-18**.

## 3.6 Fifth vs Sixth Schedule

### Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Fifth Schedule** | Provisions for Scheduled Areas and Scheduled Tribes in States **other than** the Sixth Schedule tribal areas |
| **Sixth Schedule** | Special autonomous district/regional council regime for tribal areas of **Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura, Mizoram** |
| **Scheduled Area** | Area declared under Fifth Schedule framework |
| **Autonomous District Council** | Self-governing tribal council under Sixth Schedule |

### How It Works

- **Fifth Schedule**: Governor + Tribes Advisory Council model; President declares Scheduled Areas; applies widely (e.g. parts of central/eastern Indian States) — not the AMTM council model.
- **Sixth Schedule**: stronger autonomy via **Autonomous District Councils / Regional Councils** with law-making powers on specified subjects (land, forest, water, shifting cultivation, village administration, inheritance of property, marriage and divorce, social customs — as commonly taught from Sixth Schedule subject clusters).
- Sixth Schedule States mnemonic **AMTM**: Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura, Mizoram.
- **Nagaland** and **Sikkim** are **not** Sixth Schedule States (2019 trap options).
- Part X of the Constitution houses Scheduled and Tribal Areas provisions linking to these Schedules.
- PESA (1996) extends Panchayat spirit to Fifth Schedule areas — neighbouring Topic 10 awareness; here know Fifth Schedule is the constitutional hook.
- Never say Sixth Schedule = all North-East States.

> **Exam note:** Sixth Schedule = **AMTM only**. Fifth = other Scheduled Areas/STs administration.

### Comparison Table

| Point | Fifth Schedule | Sixth Schedule |
|-------|----------------|----------------|
| Where | Scheduled Areas in many States (not AMTM tribal areas regime) | Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura, Mizoram |
| Key body | Tribes Advisory Council / Governor’s role | Autonomous District/Regional Councils |
| Autonomy flavour | Protective administration | Legislative/executive autonomy for councils |
| PYQ lock | Broad ST/Scheduled Areas | AMTM list |

### Exam Facts (raata)

- 5th Schedule = Scheduled Areas & STs.
- 6th Schedule = AMTM tribal areas.
- Tripura = Sixth Schedule State (2019).
- Nagaland ≠ Sixth Schedule (special Art. 371A path awareness).
- Sikkim ≠ Sixth Schedule.
- Autonomous District Councils = Sixth Schedule hallmark.
- Linked to Part X.

### PYQs — Fifth vs Sixth

1. **(UPPCS Prelims 2019, Q31)** Sixth Schedule applicable in which State?

## 3.7 Seventh Schedule — Union, State, Concurrent Lists

### Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Seventh Schedule** | Three Lists distributing legislative subjects between Union and States |
| **Union List** | Subjects on which Parliament has exclusive power |
| **State List** | Subjects on which State Legislatures have exclusive power (normal times) |
| **Concurrent List** | Subjects on which both can legislate; Union law prevails in conflict (Art. 254) |
| **Article 246** | Constitutional basis for the three-list distribution |

### How It Works

- Seventh Schedule is the operational chart of Indian federalism.
- Standard counts (GOI 1935 ancestry → Constitution): originally Union 97 / State 66 / Concurrent 47 — numbers shift slightly with amendments; lock **three lists exist** + famous entries.
- **Residuary powers** (Art. 248) with Union — not a fourth list, but Centre’s residual competence.
- In Concurrent field, **repugnancy** rules favour Union law unless State law has Presidential assent under Art. 254(2) exceptions teaching.
- UPPCS 2024 style: which items are Concurrent — **Adulteration of goods** and **Succession** are Concurrent; bare **Trade and Commerce** is not safely Concurrent as a whole (State within-State trade; Union inter-State/foreign).
- Famous Concurrent entries (raata set): criminal law; criminal procedure; marriage and divorce; adoption; succession; forests; education; population control and family planning; adulteration of foodstuffs; electricity; labour welfare; trade unions; industrial and labour disputes; economic and social planning; protection of wild animals and birds; price control; stamp duties (excluding court fees).
- Famous Union entries: defence, foreign affairs, currency, banking, census, Union taxation majors, inter-State trade.
- Famous State entries: police, public order, public health & sanitation, local government, agriculture, land.

> **Exam note:** Census = Union List (2019). Public health & sanitation = State List. Concurrent ≠ everything shared casually.

### Exam Facts (raata)

- 7th Schedule = 3 Lists.
- Art. 246 distributes powers.
- Residuary → Union (Art. 248).
- Concurrent conflict → Union prevails (general rule).
- Adulteration of goods / Succession → Concurrent (2024).
- Trade and Commerce (unqualified) ≠ automatic Concurrent.
- Public Health & Sanitation → State List.
- Census → Union List.

### PYQs — Seventh Schedule

1. **(UPPCS Prelims 2019, Q28)** Public Health and Sanitation — State List; Census — Union List (correct pairs in that question).
2. **(UPPCS Prelims 2024, Q143)** Concurrent list includes: 1. Trade and Commerce 2. Adulteration of Goods 3. Succession  
   → **D. 2 and 3** (not unqualified Trade and Commerce).
3. **(UPPCS Prelims 2022/2025 match)** Seventh Schedule = Union, State and Concurrent Lists.

| Example | Point illustrated |
|---------|-------------------|
| Education in Concurrent | Both Centre and States legislate |
| Police in State List | Classical State subject |

## 3.9 Ninth & Tenth Schedules

### Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Ninth Schedule** | List of Acts/Regulations placed for special protection from judicial challenge on FR grounds (origin: 1st Amendment, 1951) |
| **Tenth Schedule** | Anti-defection provisions — disqualification of MPs/MLAs on defection (52nd Amendment, 1985) |

### How It Works

- **Ninth Schedule** was created to protect agrarian reform laws from FR attack; later many Acts were added.
- After **I.R. Coelho (2007)** teaching, laws in Ninth Schedule enacted after 24 April 1973 can still be tested on **Basic Structure** — advanced but high-yield UPSC/UPPCS crossover.
- Never confuse Ninth with Fourth (RS seats) — 2023 direct trap.
- **Tenth Schedule** disqualifies legislators who voluntarily give up party membership or defy party whip in specified votes, with Speaker/Chairman deciding (Kihoto Hollohan awareness).
- 10th Schedule = anti-defection; 52nd Amendment origin; later 91st tweaks (strength limits) are Topic 16 neighbouring detail.
- Matching questions: Ninth = validation of Acts/Regulations; Tenth = defection disqualification.

> **Exam note:** **Ninth ≠ Rajya Sabha seats**. **Tenth = Anti-defection**.

### Exam Facts (raata)

- 9th Schedule added by **1st Amendment (1951)**.
- 9th = Acts/Regulations validation/protection list.
- 10th Schedule = Anti-defection (**52nd Amendment, 1985**).
- Coelho: post-1973 Ninth Schedule laws open to Basic Structure review.
- Speaker decides 10th Schedule disqualification (subject to judicial review).

### PYQs — 9th & 10th

1. **(UPPCS Prelims 2023, Q7)** Ninth — Allocation of seats in Council of States = **NOT** correct.
2. **(UPPCS Prelims 2019, Q28)** Anti-Defection — Tenth Schedule = correct.
3. **(UPPCS Prelims 2025, Q113)** Ninth = Validation of Acts; Tenth = Defection.

| Example | Point illustrated |
|---------|-------------------|

## 3.10 Eleventh & Twelfth Schedules

### Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Eleventh Schedule** | 29 subjects illustrating powers/authority of Panchayats (Art. 243G) |
| **Twelfth Schedule** | 18 subjects illustrating powers/authority of Municipalities (Art. 243W) |

### How It Works

- Both Schedules operationalise the 73rd/74th local government design.
- **11th Schedule = 29 subjects** (UPPCS 2019 direct number ask) — agriculture, land reform, minor irrigation, animal husbandry, fisheries, social forestry, small industry, rural housing, drinking water, poverty alleviation, education, health, women & child development, public distribution, and related rural subjects in the standard list.
- **12th Schedule = 18 subjects** — urban planning; regulation of land use and building construction; planning for economic and social development; roads and bridges; water supply for domestic/industrial/commercial purposes; public health, sanitation and solid waste management; fire services; urban forestry, protection of environment and promotion of ecological aspects; safeguarding interests of weaker sections including handicapped and mentally retarded; slum improvement and upgradation; urban poverty alleviation; provision of urban amenities and facilities such as parks, gardens, playgrounds; promotion of cultural, educational and aesthetic aspects; burials and burial grounds, cremations, cremation grounds and electric crematoriums; cattle pounds, prevention of cruelty to animals; vital statistics including registration of births and deaths; public amenities including street lighting, parking lots, bus stops and public conveniences; regulation of slaughterhouses and tanneries.
- Do not swap counts: **29 ≠ 18**.
- Presence of a subject in 11th/12th does not mean every State has devolved it equally — constitutional list vs actual devolution.

> **Exam note:** **11th = 29 (Panchayats)**; **12th = 18 (Municipalities)**.

### Exam Facts (raata)

- 11th Schedule → Panchayats → **29** items (73rd).
- 12th Schedule → Municipalities → **18** items (74th).
- Linked to Parts IX and IXA.
- 2019 asked 11th Schedule count = 29.
