### Key Takeaways

- **Early period (2018–20):** Average **12 questions** per paper.

- **2020 and 2024 dips** are anomalies — cross-subject env-adjacent questions (Economy reports, Geography protected areas) keep true environment ROI higher than tagged count alone.

> **Exam note:** 2025 reversed the historical pattern — **68% multi-format** vs 32% historically. Your Practice Zones must mirror 2-statement + A/R, not only single MCQs.

### UPPCS Favourite Question Types — Ranked

1. **2-statement correct?** — Scheme years, treaty purposes, pollution facts
2. **A/R** — SDGs, overgrazing→desertification, ecological concepts
3. **Match List-I / II** — SDG ↔ goal; org ↔ report; erosion ↔ landform
4. **NOT in UP / NOT in India** — Ramsar, sanctuaries, biosphere reserves
5. **NOT correctly matched** — Treaty ↔ wrong purpose; wrong HQ; wrong year
6. **Direct MCQ** — Movement identification; report publisher; single fact

## 45.4 Five Mega-Trends (2023–2025)

### Trend 1 — SDG Explosion

SDG-related questions surged from ~3 (2018–20) to **12+ (2023–25)**. UPPCS now treats SDGs as a **standalone high-weight cluster**, not a footnote under sustainable development.

**Must-know:** SDG 6 (water), 13 (climate), 14 (oceans/blue economy), 15 (land/biodiversity); India's VNR; SDG ↔ goal number matching.

**Files:** Topic 15 §15.1–15.3; Topic 44 §44.4–44.6.

---

### Trend 2 — Scheme Chronology Traps

Examiners systematically plant **wrong launch year** as statement 2:

| Scheme/Concept | Correct year | Common trap |
|--------------|--------------|-------------|
| **LiFE Mission** | Idea COP26 **2021**; launch **June 2022** | COP-25 Madrid 2019 |
| **MISHTI** | **2023–24 Union Budget** | Initiated 2019 |
| **DPAP** | **1973** | 1977, 1985, 1992 |
| **India Net Zero** | **2070** (COP26 2021) | 2050, 2030 |
| **GEF** | **1991** | Only developed donors |
| **National Green Hydrogen Mission** | **2023** | 2019, 2021 |
| **UNCCD** | **1994** | Rio 1992 (same year trap) |

### Trend 4 — Cross-Subject Environment Leakage

Environment concepts appear outside `Subject: Environment` — especially in **Economy** (reports, green finance), **Geography** (biosphere, climate zones), and **Current Affairs** (new schemes).

| Year | Q# | Subject | Env concept |

| 2024 | Q6 | Economy | Emissions Gap Report = **UNEP** |
| 2024 | Q35 | Environment | Kyoto = climate mitigation (Montreal trap) |

**Rule:** When revising Environment, also scan Economy/Geography PYQs for report ↔ org and treaty ↔ issue questions.

## 45.6 Year-Wise Snapshot

### 2018–2019 (Baseline era)
- Heavy ecology, pollution, biodiversity MCQs
- ~14 env Qs; mostly direct format
- International treaties appear as NOT-matched

### 2020 (Low year — 8 Qs)
- Do not benchmark weight from this paper
- Ramsar sites added to UP (Q48) — early UP wetland CA

### 2021–2022 (Upswing)
- Carbon credit → Kyoto (Q125)
- Wildlife sanctuaries UP geography
- SDG questions begin increasing
- 2022: 19 env Qs — format diversifying

### 2023 (Peak — 25 Qs)
- Maximum Environment & Ecology tagging
- SDG cluster expands heavily
- UP WLS district questions continue
- Environmental laws, population-environment appear

### 2024 (Dip — 9 tagged, but high leakage)
- Kyoto climate mitigation vs Montreal trap (Q35)
- Emissions Gap = UNEP (Q6, Economy subject)
- Treaty ↔ issue remains favourite

### 2025 (Model year — 19 Qs, format shift)
- Multi-statement dominance
- LiFE + MISHTI scheme year traps
- UP negative geography (Ramsar, ISFR, Biosphere)
- SDGs + current issues + ecology trifecta

## 45.10 Mock-Test Self-Audit Checklist

After every UPPCS mock, score yourself on these trend dimensions:

- [ ] Did I attempt all 15–20 env-linked Qs (including cross-subject)?
- [ ] Did I fall for any **scheme year trap** (LiFE, MISHTI, DPAP, GEF)?
- [ ] Did I confuse **Montreal vs Kyoto** for climate questions?
- [ ] Did I mark UP Ramsar/sanctuary/NP correctly on **negative** questions?
- [ ] Did I match **report ↔ organisation** correctly (UNEP, UNDP, FSI, CGWB)?
- [ ] Did I handle **2-statement** format by evaluating each stmt independently?
- [ ] Did I revise **SDG goal numbers** for matching questions?
- [ ] Did I know **2070** net zero and **1973** DPAP without hesitation?
