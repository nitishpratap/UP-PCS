# Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEFCC)

## Definition

The **Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEFCC)** is the **nodal ministry of the Government of India** responsible for **planning, promoting, coordinating, and overseeing environmental and forestry programmes and policies** — including **pollution control, wildlife conservation, forest management, climate change, and biodiversity**.

**Former name:** Ministry of Environment and Forests (MoEF) — renamed in **2014** to include Climate Change.

**Headquarters:** Indira Paryavaran Bhawan, New Delhi.

## Explanation

### Evolution of MoEFCC

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| **1985** | Ministry of Environment and Forests (MoEF) created |
| **1986** | Environment (Protection) Act enacted under MoEF |
| **1991** | Ozone Cell established under MoEF |
| **2006** | EIA Notification issued |
| **2014** | Renamed **MoEFCC** (Climate Change added) |
| **2019** | India submitted updated NDCs |
| **2021** | India announced Net Zero 2070 at COP26 |

### Key Functions of MoEFCC

|----------|--------|
| **Policy formulation** | National environmental and forest policies |
| **Environmental clearance** | Category A projects (EIA appraisal) |
| **Wildlife conservation** | Project Tiger, Elephant, Lion programmes |
| **Forest management** | Forest Conservation Act implementation |
| **Pollution control** | Oversees CPCB and SPCBs |
| **Climate change** | UNFCCC, Paris Agreement, NAPCC |
| **Biodiversity** | CBD implementation, NBA coordination |
| **International treaties** | UNFCCC, CBD, CITES, Ramsar, UNCCD |
| **Ozone protection** | Montreal Protocol implementation |

# Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB)

## Definition

The **Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB)** is a **statutory organisation under the MoEFCC** established in **1974** under the **Water (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act, 1974** — serving as the **apex body for pollution control** in India, coordinating activities of **State Pollution Control Boards (SPCBs)** and setting **national ambient air and water quality standards**.

**Headquarters:** Parivesh Bhawan, East Arjun Nagar, New Delhi.

## Explanation

### CPCB — Key Details

| Feature | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Established** | 1974 |
| **Legal basis** | Water Act 1974 (also Air Act 1981) |
| **Type** | Statutory body under MoEFCC |
| **HQ** | New Delhi |
| **Chairman** | Appointed by Central Government |
| **Member Secretary** | Chief executive officer |
| **Regional labs** | 7 zonal offices with laboratories |

### Functions of CPCB

| Function | Detail |
|----------|--------|
| **Standards setting** | NAAQS (air), water quality standards |
| **Monitoring** | National air and water quality monitoring |
| **Coordination** | Guide and coordinate SPCBs |
| **Research** | Pollution control technology research |
| **Advisory** | Advise Central Government on pollution |
| **EPR monitoring** | Extended Producer Responsibility compliance |
| **NAAQI** | National Air Quality Index (2014) |
| **Critically polluted areas** | Identify and monitor industrial clusters |
| **Noise standards** | Ambient noise level standards |
| **Hazardous waste** | Guidelines for hazardous waste management |

### CPCB Zonal Offices

### CPCB vs SPCB

| Feature | CPCB | SPCB |
|---------|------|------|
| **Level** | Central/national | State |
| **Established** | 1974 | 1974+ (state-wise) |
| **Role** | Standards, coordination, monitoring | Implementation, enforcement |
| **Consent** | No direct consent powers | Issues consent to establish/operate |
| **Legal basis** | Water Act + Air Act | Same acts (state level) |
| **UP example** | Sets standards for UP | UPPCB enforces in UP |

## Important Facts

- **Established 1974** — under Water Act 1974.
- **Apex pollution control body** — at national level.
- **7 zonal offices** — with laboratories across India.
- **NAAQI launched 2014** — 6-category air quality index.
- **Central zone (Bhopal)** — covers **Uttar Pradesh**.
- **UPPCB** — Uttar Pradesh Pollution Control Board (state counterpart).
- **14 of world's 20 most polluted cities** — in India (WHO/CPCB data).
- **NCAP 2019** — CPCB monitors 122 cities.
- **EPR framework** — CPCB monitors plastic, e-waste compliance.
- **Consent to Operate** — issued by SPCB, not CPCB directly.

# State Pollution Control Board (SPCB)

## Definition

The **State Pollution Control Board (SPCB)** is a **statutory body established in each state** under the **Water (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act, 1974** and **Air (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act, 1981** — responsible for **implementing pollution control laws, issuing consents, monitoring pollution, and enforcing standards** at the **state level**.

**UP counterpart:** **Uttar Pradesh Pollution Control Board (UPPCB)** — established for UP.

## Explanation

### SPCB — Key Details

| Feature | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Legal basis** | Water Act 1974 + Air Act 1981 |
| **Level** | State government |
| **Established** | 1974 onwards (state-wise) |
| **UPPCB** | Uttar Pradesh Pollution Control Board |
| **Chairman** | Appointed by state government |
| **Role** | Implementation and enforcement (not policy) |
| **Guided by** | CPCB standards and directions |

### Functions of SPCB

| Function | Detail |
|----------|--------|
| **Consent to Establish (CTE)** | Permission before setting up industry |
| **Consent to Operate (CTO)** | Permission to run industry (renewable) |
| **Monitoring** | State air and water quality monitoring |
| **Enforcement** | Closure, prosecution for violations |
| **Standards compliance** | Ensure industries meet CPCB norms |
| **Sewage treatment** | Monitor STPs and sewage discharge |
| **Hazardous waste** | Authorise hazardous waste handlers |
| **Biomedical waste** | Monitor hospital waste compliance |
| **Public complaints** | Address pollution complaints |
| **Data reporting** | Report to CPCB regularly |

### Consent Mechanism

```
INDUSTRY PROPOSES SETUP
        ↓
CONSENT TO ESTABLISH (CTE) — SPCB approval before construction
        ↓
INDUSTRY BUILDS AND COMMISSIONS
        ↓
CONSENT TO OPERATE (CTO) — SPCB approval to begin operations
        ↓
PERIODIC RENEWAL (every 1-5 years depending on category)
        ↓
VIOLATION → SHOW CAUSE NOTICE → CLOSURE ORDER → PROSECUTION
```

### Industry Categorisation by SPCB

| Category | Pollution Potential | Consent Validity | Examples |
|----------|-------------------|-----------------|----------|
| **Red** | High | 1-2 years | Tanneries, refineries, pharma |
| **Orange** | Medium | 3-5 years | Food processing, textiles |
| **Green** | Low | 5+ years | Assembly units, IT |
| **White** | Minimal | No CTO required | Small non-polluting units |

### UPPCB — Uttar Pradesh Pollution Control Board

| Feature | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Established** | 1975 (UP Water Pollution Prevention Board) |
| **HQ** | Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh |
| **Regional offices** | Multiple across UP |
| **Key cities monitored** | Lucknow, Kanpur, Ghaziabad, Noida, Varanasi, Agra |
| **Major challenges** | Ganga pollution (Kanpur), air quality (NCR), Singrauli |
| **Online portal** | UPPCB consent management system |
| **Ganga cities** | Kanpur, Varanasi, Allahabad monitoring |

### SPCB vs CPCB vs NGT

| Feature | SPCB | CPCB | NGT |
|---------|------|------|-----|
| **Level** | State | Central | National (judicial) |
| **Role** | Enforcement | Standards, coordination | Adjudication |
| **Consent powers** | Yes (CTE/CTO) | No | No |
| **Closure powers** | Yes | Advisory only | Order closure |
| **Established** | 1974+ | 1974 | 2010 |
| **Appeal against SPCB** | NGT | NGT | SC |

# National Green Tribunal (NGT)

## Definition

The **National Green Tribunal (NGT)** is a **specialised judicial body** established on **18 October 2010** under the **National Green Tribunal Act, 2010** for **effective and expeditious disposal of cases relating to environmental protection, conservation of forests, and other natural resources** — including **enforcement of environmental legal rights**.

**First chairperson:** Justice Lokeshwar Singh Panta.

**Inspired by:** Australia's Land and Environment Court; Rio Summit Principle 10.

## Explanation

### NGT — Key Details

| Feature | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Established** | 18 October 2010 |
| **Legal basis** | NGT Act, 2010 |
| **Type** | Specialised environmental tribunal |
| **Principal bench** | New Delhi |
| **Regional benches** | Bhopal, Pune, Kolkata, Chennai |
| **Chairperson** | Retired Supreme Court judge |
| **Jurisdiction** | Civil cases involving substantial environmental questions |
| **Appeal** | Supreme Court (within 90 days) |

### Powers of NGT

| Power | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Adjudication** | Hear environmental civil cases |
| **Relief and compensation** | Award damages for environmental harm |
| **Restitution** | Order restoration of damaged environment |
| **Interim orders** | Injunction, stay, closure orders |
| **Implementation** | Orders executable as decree of civil court |
| **Penalties** | Fine up to ₹25 crore; imprisonment up to 3 years |
| **Review** | Review own orders |
| **No CPC application** | Not bound by Code of Civil Procedure (expeditious) |

| EIA-related disputes | Specific WPA Schedule I species offences |
| Hazardous substances | — |
| Coastal Regulation Zone | — |
