# Habitat

## Definition

A **habitat** is the **natural environment** in which a particular species lives, finds food, shelter, and reproduces — the **physical "address"** of an organism.

**NCERT definition:** *"The place where an organism lives is called its habitat."*

**Habitat vs Niche:**
| Term | Analogy | Meaning |
|------|---------|---------|
| **Habitat** | Address | Physical place where organism lives |
| **Niche** | Profession | Functional role in ecosystem |

**Etymology:** Latin *habitare* = to dwell/inhabit.

## Explanation

### Types of Habitats

| Category | Habitat Types | Examples |
|----------|--------------|----------|
| **Terrestrial** | Forest, grassland, desert, tundra, mountain | Western Ghats forest, Thar Desert |
| **Aquatic — Freshwater** | Pond, lake, river, wetland, swamp | Chilika Lake, Ganga River |
| **Aquatic — Marine** | Ocean, sea, coral reef, estuary, mangrove | Gulf of Mannar, Sundarbans |
| **Arboreal** | Trees and canopy | Lion-tailed macaque habitat |
| **Fossorial** | Underground burrows | Earthworms, rodents, termites |
| **Aerial/Arboreal** | Air and sky | Birds, bats, insects |
| **Coastal/Intertidal** | Shoreline between tides | Crabs, molluscs, shorebirds |

### Major Habitat Types in India

| Habitat | Region | Key Species |
|---------|--------|-------------|
| **Tropical evergreen forest** | Western Ghats, NE India | Lion-tailed macaque, hornbills |
| **Tropical deciduous forest** | Central India, Terai | Tiger, deer, peacock |
| **Thorn forest/scrub** | Rajasthan, Gujarat | Great Indian Bustard, chinkara |
| **Desert** | Thar Desert | Desert fox, spiny-tailed lizard |
| **Grassland** | Deccan Plateau, Terai | Blackbuck, Indian wolf |
| **Wetland** | Chilika, Loktak, Keoladeo | Migratory birds, fishing cat |
| **Mangrove** | Sundarbans, Bhitarkanika | Royal Bengal Tiger, crocodile |
| **Coral reef** | Gulf of Mannar, Lakshadweep | Reef fish, sea turtles |
| **Alpine/Himalayan** | High Himalayas | Snow leopard, musk deer, yak |
| **Riverine** | Ganga, Brahmaputra | Gangetic Dolphin, gharial |

### Habitat Requirements of Species

| Requirement | Description | Example |
|-------------|-------------|---------|
| **Food** | Availability of prey/forage | Tiger needs deer populations |
| **Water** | Drinking and aquatic needs | Elephant needs daily water access |
| **Shelter** | Cover for rest, breeding, hiding | Birds need nesting trees |
| **Breeding sites** | Safe areas for reproduction | Turtles need sandy beaches |
| **Territory** | Space for feeding and mating | Tiger needs 50–100 sq km |
| **Climate** | Temperature and rainfall tolerance | Snow leopard in cold alpine zones |

### Habitat Fragmentation

- **Habitat fragmentation** = Large continuous habitat broken into smaller, isolated patches.
- **Causes:** Roads, dams, urbanisation, agriculture, mining.
- **Effects:** Species isolation, inbreeding, edge effects, population decline.
- **Solution:** Wildlife corridors (Kanha-Pench, Kaziranga-Karbi Anglong).

### Microhabitat vs Macrohabitat

| Type | Scale | Example |
|------|-------|---------|
| **Microhabitat** | Small, localised area within habitat | Tree bark (lichens), pond edge (frogs) |
| **Macrohabitat** | Large ecosystem/biome | Western Ghats forest, Thar Desert |

## Important Facts

- **Habitat loss** is the **#1 driver** of biodiversity loss globally (IPBES, 2019).
- **India has 10 biogeographic zones** with diverse habitats.
- **Critical habitats** — areas essential for survival of threatened species (WPA 1972 amendment).
- **Habitat destruction** affects **>85% of threatened species** (IUCN).
- **Edge effect** — boundary habitats have different conditions than interior.
- **Ecotone** — transition zone between two habitats; often higher biodiversity.
- **Endemic species** are restricted to specific habitats (e.g., Lion-tailed macaque — evergreen forest).

# Habitat Conservation

## Definition

**Habitat conservation** is the **protection, management, and restoration** of natural habitats to ensure the survival of species and maintenance of ecosystem functions — encompassing **in-situ conservation** of living spaces rather than individual species alone.

**Principle:** *"Protect the habitat, protect all species within it."*

## Explanation

### Approaches to Habitat Conservation

| Approach | Method | Examples |
|----------|--------|----------|
| **Protected Areas** | Legal protection of habitat | National Parks, Sanctuaries, Biosphere Reserves |
| **Habitat Restoration** | Rebuilding degraded habitats | Afforestation, wetland restoration |
| **Wildlife Corridors** | Connecting fragmented habitats | Kanha-Pench corridor |
| **Eco-Sensitive Zones (ESZ)** | Buffer zones around PAs | Western Ghats ESZ |
| **Community Conservation** | Local community management | Sacred groves, BMCs, JFM |
| **Landscape Conservation** | Large-scale habitat mosaic | Tiger Reserve network |
| **Marine Protected Areas** | Coastal/marine habitat protection | Gulf of Mannar MPA |
| **Ramsar Sites** | Wetland habitat protection | 85+ Indian Ramsar sites |

### Protected Area Categories (India — WPA 1972)

| Category | Habitat Protection Level | Human Activity |
|----------|------------------------|----------------|
| **National Park** | Highest — entire habitat protected | Strictly prohibited (except tourism) |
| **Wildlife Sanctuary** | High — habitat protected | Limited grazing, collection allowed |
| **Biosphere Reserve** | Zoned — core (strict), buffer, transition | Sustainable use in transition zone |
| **Conservation Reserve** | Community + State co-managed | Limited use with conservation |
| **Community Reserve** | Community-owned habitat protection | Community-managed |
| **Tiger Reserve** | Core + Buffer habitat model | Core inviolate; buffer regulated |
| **Ramsar Site** | Wetland habitat international protection | Wise use principle |

### Critical Habitat (WPA Amendment, 2006)

- **Critical habitat** = Specific areas within or outside PAs essential for survival of threatened species.
- Notified by **State Government** in consultation with **NBWL**.
- **Restrictions:** Activities detrimental to threatened species prohibited.
- Example: Critical habitat for Great Indian Bustard in Rajasthan grasslands.

### Habitat Restoration Techniques

| Technique | Application |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Afforestation/Reforestation** | Restoring forest habitats |
| **Wetland restoration** | Re-establishing hydrology, native vegetation |
| **Removal of invasives** | Lantana, Parthenium clearing |
| **Coral reef restoration** | Artificial reef structures, coral transplantation |
| **Grassland management** | Controlled burning, grazing management |
| **River rejuvenation** | Namami Gange — restoring riverine habitat |
| **Miyawaki method** | Rapid urban habitat creation |

### Wildlife Corridors in India

| Corridor | Connects | Species Benefiting |
|----------|----------|-------------------|
| **Kanha-Pench** | MP tiger habitats | Tiger, leopard, deer |
| **Kaziranga-Karbi Anglong** | Assam | Rhino, elephant, tiger |
| **Tirunelli-Kudrakote** | Kerala (Western Ghats) | Elephant, tiger |
| **Siju-Rewak** | Meghalaya | Elephant |
| **Rajaji-Corbett** | Uttarakhand | Elephant, tiger |

### Community-Based Habitat Conservation

| Method | Region | Feature |
|--------|--------|---------|
| **Sacred groves** | Meghalaya, Kerala, Maharashtra | Community-protected forest patches |
| **Joint Forest Management (JFM)** | Odisha, MP | Community-State forest co-management |
| **Biodiversity Management Committees** | Nationwide (BDA 2002) | Local habitat documentation and protection |
| **Van Panchayats** | Uttarakhand | Community forest governance |
| **Forest Rights Act (2006)** | Tribal areas | Community rights over forest habitats |

## Important Facts

- **India: 104 National Parks, 566 Sanctuaries, 18 Biosphere Reserves.**
- **54 Tiger Reserves** — ~75,000 sq km habitat protected.
- **85+ Ramsar sites** — wetland habitat conservation.
- **Critical Habitat** concept added to WPA in 2006.
- **UN Decade on Ecosystem Restoration (2021–2030)** — India pledged 26 Mha.
- **30×30 target (Kunming-Montreal GBF, 2022)** — protect 30% habitats by 2030.
- **Habitat conservation preferred over species-only approach** — umbrella species strategy.
- **Sacred groves** — ancient Indian tradition of habitat conservation.

# Flora and Fauna

## Definition

| Term | Definition | Origin |
|------|------------|--------|
| **Flora** | The **plant life** of a particular region, period, or environment | Roman goddess Flora (flowers) |
| **Fauna** | The **animal life** of a particular region, period, or environment | Roman goddess Fauna (forest spirits) |

Together, **flora and fauna** constitute the **biotic (living) component** of an ecosystem and represent the biodiversity of a region.

**In Indian context:** Flora and fauna are documented by **BSI (Botanical Survey of India)** and **ZSI (Zoological Survey of India)** respectively.

## Explanation
