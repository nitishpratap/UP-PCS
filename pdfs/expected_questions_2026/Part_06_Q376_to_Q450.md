### Question 376
**Subject:** Modern Indian History | **Topic:** Landmark Peasant Satyagrahas Chronology

Arrange the following peasant movements in chronological order:
1. Indigo Revolt (Neel Bidroha) in Bengal
2. Deccan Riots in Maharashtra
3. Champaran Satyagraha in Bihar
4. Bardoli Satyagraha in Gujarat

Select the correct answer from the codes given below:

**Options:**
A. 1 — 2 — 3 — 4  
B. 2 — 1 — 3 — 4  
C. 1 — 3 — 2 — 4  
D. 1 — 2 — 4 — 3  

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:**
- **Core Fact & Solution:**
  1. **Indigo Revolt (1859–1860):** Started in Nadia, Bengal, led by Digambar Biswas and Bishnu Biswas against oppressive European indigo planters.

- **आयोग का Trap / Ratta Point:** Mahatma Gandhi conferred the title "Sardar" on Vallabhbhai Patel on behalf of the women of Bardoli!

### Question 379
**Subject:** General Science (Biology) | **Topic:** Pineal & Thymus Gland Physiology

Consider the following statements regarding the human endocrine glands:
1. The Pineal gland secretes the hormone 'Melatonin', which regulates the 24-hour diurnal rhythm (sleep-wake cycle) and body temperature.
2. The Thymus gland secretes 'Thymosins', which play a major role in the differentiation and maturation of T-lymphocytes (cell-mediated immunity).
3. The Thymus gland is large in infants and children, but progressively degenerates and atrophies in old age, leading to weakened immune responses in the elderly.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

**Options:**
A. 1 and 2 only  
B. 2 and 3 only  
C. 1 and 3 only  
D. 1, 2 and 3  

**Answer:** D

**Explanation:**
- **Core Fact & Solution:**
  - All three statements are correct:
    - **Pineal Gland:** Located on the dorsal side of the forebrain; called the "third eye"; secretes **Melatonin** in darkness (inhibited by light); regulates the sleep-wake cycle, metabolism, skin pigmentation, and menstrual cycles.
    - **Thymus Gland:** Lobular gland located behind the sternum on the ventral side of the aorta; acts as the primary lymphoid organ where **T-lymphocytes mature and acquire immunological competence** ("Training School of T-cells").
    - **Age Atrophy:** Unlike other endocrine glands, the thymus shrinks steadily after puberty and is largely replaced by adipose tissue in old age, which explains the heightened susceptibility of senior citizens to infectious diseases.
- **आयोग का Trap / Ratta Point:** T-cells mature in the **Thymus**; B-cells mature in the **Bone Marrow**!

### Question 382
**Subject:** Indian Economy | **Topic:** Currency Devaluation vs Depreciation

Match List-I (Currency Adjustment) with List-II (Mechanism & Governing Regime) and select the correct answer:

**List-I (Term)**
A. Currency Devaluation  
B. Currency Depreciation  
C. Currency Appreciation  
D. J-Curve Effect  

**List-II (Mechanism & Regime)**
1. Fall in the exchange value of domestic currency determined by market forces of demand and supply in a floating exchange regime  
2. Deliberate official downward reduction in the value of domestic currency by the central bank under a fixed exchange rate regime  
3. Phenomenon where a currency depreciation worsens the trade balance initially before improving it in the medium term  
4. Increase in the external value of domestic currency due to market demand under a floating exchange regime  

**Options:**
A. A-2, B-1, C-4, D-3  
B. A-1, B-2, C-4, D-3  
C. A-2, B-4, C-1, D-3  
D. A-3, B-1, C-4, D-2  

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:**
- **Core Fact & Solution:**
  - **Devaluation:** Deliberate governmental reduction of currency value against foreign currencies under a **Fixed / Pegged Exchange Rate** (India devalued the rupee in 1949, 1966, and 1991).
  - **Depreciation:** Automatic reduction of currency value driven by market forces of demand and supply in a **Floating / Flexible Exchange Rate** system.
  - **J-Curve Effect:** Following currency depreciation, export volumes take time to expand while import prices rise immediately, so the trade deficit initially widens before reversing into surplus, tracing a 'J' curve.
- **आयोग का Trap / Ratta Point:** Devaluation is an OFFICIAL GOVT ACTION under a fixed regime; Depreciation is a MARKET DRIVEN PHENOMENON under a floating regime!
