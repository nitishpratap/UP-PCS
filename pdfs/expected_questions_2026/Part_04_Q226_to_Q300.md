### Question 226
**Subject:** Indian Polity | **Topic:** Right to Equality (Articles 14 to 18)

Match List-I (Article of the Constitution) with List-II (Specific Fundamental Right) and select the correct answer:

**List-I (Article)**
A. Article 14  
B. Article 15  
C. Article 16  
D. Article 17  

**List-II (Specific Right)**
1. Equality of opportunity in matters of public employment  
2. Equality before the law and equal protection of the laws  
3. Abolition of Untouchability and prohibition of its practice in any form  
4. Prohibition of discrimination on grounds only of religion, race, caste, sex, or place of birth  

**Options:**
A. A-2, B-4, C-1, D-3  
B. A-1, B-2, C-4, D-3  
C. A-2, B-1, C-4, D-3  
D. A-3, B-4, C-1, D-2  

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:**
- **Core Fact & Solution:**
  - **Article 14:** Two concepts: "Equality before law" (negative concept borrowed from British common law) and "Equal protection of laws" (positive concept borrowed from the American Constitution).
  - **Article 15:** Prohibits discrimination on five grounds ONLY: religion, race, caste, sex, place of birth.
  - **Article 16:** Equality of opportunity in public employment (grounds: religion, race, caste, sex, descent, place of birth, residence).
  - **Article 17:** Absolute right; abolishes Untouchability; enforced via Protection of Civil Rights Act, 1955.
  - **Article 18:** Abolishes titles (except military and academic distinctions).
- **आयोग का Trap / Ratta Point:** Articles available ONLY to Indian citizens: **Articles 15, 16, 19, 29, and 30**! All other Fundamental Rights (including Articles 14, 20, 21, 21A, 22, 25-28) apply to all persons (both citizens and foreigners).

### Question 229
**Subject:** General Science (Biology) | **Topic:** Endocrine Disorders in Humans

Match List-I (Endocrine Disorder) with List-II (Hormonal Cause) and select the correct answer:

**List-I (Disorder)**
A. Diabetes Mellitus  
B. Diabetes Insipidus  
C. Addison's Disease  
D. Cushing's Syndrome  

**List-II (Hormonal Cause)**
1. Hyposecretion of Antidiuretic Hormone (ADH / Vasopressin) from posterior pituitary  
2. Deficiency of Insulin secretion from beta-cells of pancreas (hyperglycemia)  
3. Hypersecretion of Cortisol (glucocorticoids) from adrenal cortex  
4. Hyposecretion of Corticoid hormones (cortisol & aldosterone) from adrenal cortex  

**Options:**
A. A-2, B-1, C-4, D-3  
B. A-1, B-2, C-4, D-3  
C. A-2, B-4, C-1, D-3  
D. A-3, B-1, C-4, D-2  

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:**
- **Core Fact & Solution:**
  - **Diabetes Mellitus:** Caused by insulin deficiency or receptor insensitivity; high blood glucose; glucose appears in urine (*Glycosuria*).
  - **Diabetes Insipidus:** Caused by ADH (Vasopressin) deficiency; distal tubules fail to reabsorb water; massive water loss in urine (*Polyuria*) without glucose!
  - **Addison's Disease:** Adrenal cortical insufficiency; bronze pigmentation of skin, low blood pressure, severe fatigue, hypoglycemia.
  - **Cushing's Syndrome:** Excess cortisol secretion; characterized by "Moon face", "Buffalo hump", obesity, and high blood pressure.
- **आयोग का Trap / Ratta Point:** Diabetes *Insipidus* involves WATER balance (ADH deficiency); urine is tasteless (*insipid*) with NO sugar. Diabetes *Mellitus* involves SUGAR balance (Insulin deficiency); urine is sweet (*mellitus* = honey)!

### Question 232

Consider the following statements regarding Treasury Bills (T-Bills) in India:
1. Treasury Bills are zero-coupon debt instruments issued by the Government of India to meet short-term liquidity mismatches.
2. They are issued at a discount to face value and redeemed at par on maturity.
3. Treasury Bills are currently issued in three tenors: 91-day, 182-day, and 364-day.
4. State Governments in India also issue their own Treasury Bills to raise short-term finance.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

**Options:**
A. 1, 2 and 3 only  
B. 1, 3 and 4 only  
C. 2, 3 and 4 only  
D. 1, 2, 3 and 4  

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:**
- **Core Fact & Solution:**
  - Statements 1, 2, and 3 are correct: T-Bills are short-term sovereign money market instruments auctioned by the RBI; issued at discount and redeemed at par (the difference is the interest yield). Active tenors are 91 days, 182 days, and 364 days.

- **आयोग का Trap / Ratta Point:** Massive UPPSC trap: Treasury Bills are issued EXCLUSIVELY by the Central Government, NEVER by State Governments!
