### Question 76
**Subject:** Indian Polity | **Topic:** Fundamental Rights & Judicial Review (Article 13)

Consider the following statements regarding Article 13 of the Indian Constitution:
1. Under Article 13(2), any law made by Parliament or State Legislatures that abridges or takes away Fundamental Rights is void to the extent of the contravention.
2. The 'Doctrine of Eclipse' and 'Doctrine of Severability' have evolved primarily through judicial interpretation of Article 13.
3. The Supreme Court in the Kesavananda Bharati case held that a Constitutional Amendment Act under Article 368 is considered ordinary "law" within the meaning of Article 13(2).

Which of the statements given above are correct?

**Options:**
A. 1 and 2 only  
B. 2 and 3 only  
C. 1 and 3 only  
D. 1, 2 and 3  

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:**
- **Core Fact & Solution:**
  - Statements 1 and 2 are correct: Article 13 provides the constitutional backbone for Judicial Review. Clause (2) mandates that the State shall not make any law which takes away or abridges Part III rights. The Supreme Court formulated the *Doctrine of Severability* (valid portions survive if invalid portions can be cleanly severed) and *Doctrine of Eclipse* (pre-constitutional laws inconsistent with FRs remain dormant/shadowed, not dead).
  - Statement 3 is incorrect: The Supreme Court in *Kesavananda Bharati (1973)* upheld the validity of clause (4) of Article 13 (inserted by 24th CAA 1971), affirming that a **Constitutional Amendment Act passed under Article 368 is NOT ordinary 'law' under Article 13(2)**. However, amendments can still be struck down if they violate the "Basic Structure".
- **आयोग का Trap / Ratta Point:** Remember: The term "Judicial Review" is nowhere explicitly mentioned in the text of the Constitution, but Article 13, 32, 136, 226, and 227 vest this power explicitly in the higher judiciary!

### Question 77
**Subject:** World Geography | **Topic:** World Deserts & Adjacent Cold Currents

Match List-I (Desert of the World) with List-II (Continent / Region & Influencing Cold Current) and select the correct answer:

**List-I (Desert)**
A. Atacama Desert  
B. Namib Desert  
C. Mojave Desert  
D. Great Victoria Desert  

**List-II (Region & Ocean Current)**
1. Southern Africa; Benguela Cold Current  
2. South America (Chile/Peru); Humboldt (Peru) Cold Current  
3. Australia; West Australian Cold Current  
4. North America (USA); California Cold Current  

**Options:**
A. A-2, B-1, C-4, D-3  
B. A-1, B-2, C-4, D-3  
C. A-2, B-4, C-1, D-3  
D. A-3, B-1, C-4, D-2  

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:**
- **Core Fact & Solution:**

### Question 81

**Options:**
A. 2  
B. 3  
C. 4  
D. 5  

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:**
- **Core Fact & Solution:**
  - Globally, Conservation International recognizes 36 Biodiversity Hotspots.
  - **4 Hotspots cover parts of India:**

### Question 82
**Subject:** Indian Economy | **Topic:** Budget Deficits & Fiscal Indicators

Match List-I (Type of Deficit) with List-II (Formula / Definition) and select the correct answer:

**List-I (Deficit)**
A. Fiscal Deficit  
B. Revenue Deficit  
C. Primary Deficit  
D. Effective Revenue Deficit  

**List-II (Formula / Definition)**
1. Fiscal Deficit minus Interest Payments  
2. Revenue Deficit minus Grants for Creation of Capital Assets  
3. Revenue Expenditure minus Revenue Receipts  
4. Total Expenditure minus Total Receipts excluding borrowings  

**Options:**
A. A-4, B-3, C-1, D-2  
B. A-3, B-4, C-1, D-2  
C. A-4, B-1, C-3, D-2  
D. A-2, B-3, C-1, D-4  

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:**
- **Core Fact & Solution:**
  - **Fiscal Deficit:** Total Expenditure - (Revenue Receipts + Non-debt Capital Receipts). Represents the total borrowing requirements of the government from all sources.
  - **Revenue Deficit:** Revenue Expenditure - Revenue Receipts. Indicates that the government is dissaving and using borrowings to finance routine current consumption.
  - **Primary Deficit:** Fiscal Deficit - Interest Payments. Indicates the borrowing requirements of the government excluding the interest liability on past accumulated debt.
  - **Effective Revenue Deficit:** Introduced in Union Budget 2011–12 (amended FRBM Act); excludes grants given to States/local bodies for the creation of capital assets from Revenue Deficit.
- **आयोग का Trap / Ratta Point:** If Primary Deficit is zero, it means: Fiscal Deficit = Interest Payments (i.e., the entire borrowing is being consumed just to pay interest on past loans!).

### Question 86
**Subject:** Indian Polity | **Topic:** Fundamental Duties (Article 51A)

Consider the following statements regarding the Fundamental Duties in the Indian Constitution:
1. Fundamental Duties were incorporated into Part IV-A of the Constitution upon the recommendation of the Swaran Singh Committee.
2. The 86th Constitutional Amendment Act, 2002 added the 11th Fundamental Duty regarding education of children between 6 and 14 years.
3. Fundamental Duties are directly enforceable by writs issued under Article 32.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

A. 1 and 2 only  
B. 2 and 3 only  
C. 1 only  
D. 1, 2 and 3  

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:**
- **Core Fact & Solution:**

### Question 89
**Subject:** General Science (Chemistry) | **Topic:** Polymers, Plastics & Synthetic Fibres

Match List-I (Polymer / Substance) with List-II (Monomer / Unique Application) and select the correct answer:

**List-I (Polymer)**
A. Teflon
