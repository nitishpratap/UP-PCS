### Question 6
**Subject:** Environment & Ecology | **Topic:** Aquatic Ecosystem & Eutrophication

Given below are two statements, one is labelled as Assertion (A) and the other as Reason (R):

**Assertion (A):** In a water body suffering from eutrophication, the Biochemical Oxygen Demand (BOD) increases sharply.  
**Reason (R):** Excessive proliferation of algae and phytoplankton increases the rate of decomposition, which rapidly consumes dissolved oxygen.

Select the correct answer from the codes given below:

**Options:**
A. Both (A) and (R) are true and (R) is the correct explanation of (A)  
B. Both (A) and (R) are true, but (R) is not the correct explanation of (A)  
C. (A) is true, but (R) is false  
D. (A) is false, but (R) is true  

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:**
- **Core Fact & Solution:**
  - Eutrophication occurs due to nutrient enrichment (especially nitrates and phosphates from agricultural runoff and domestic sewage).
  - This leads to algal blooms. When algae die, decomposers (aerobic bacteria) break down the organic matter and consume high quantities of Dissolved Oxygen (DO).
  - Therefore, Biochemical Oxygen Demand (BOD)—which measures the amount of oxygen required by aerobic microorganisms to decompose organic matter—surges dramatically, leading to hypoxia and death of aquatic fauna.
- **आयोग का Trap / Ratta Point:** Inverse relationship trap: High BOD means Low Dissolved Oxygen (DO) and HIGH organic pollution. UPPSC often flips this by asking: "Does high BOD mean clean water?" No, high BOD indicates heavily polluted water!

### Question 7
**Subject:** Indian Economy | **Topic:** Poverty Estimation Committees

Arrange the following committees associated with Poverty Estimation in India in chronological order:
1. Suresh Tendulkar Committee
2. Y.K. Alagh Committee
3. D.T. Lakdawala Committee
4. C. Rangarajan Committee

Select the correct answer from the codes given below:

**Options:**
A. 2 — 3 — 1 — 4  
B. 3 — 2 — 1 — 4  
C. 2 — 1 — 3 — 4  
D. 1 — 2 — 3 — 4  

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:**
- **Core Fact & Solution:**
  1. **Y.K. Alagh Committee (1979):** Defined official poverty line based on calorie intake (2400 kcal/day in rural areas and 2100 kcal/day in urban areas).
  2. **D.T. Lakdawala Committee (Task Force 1989, submitted 1993):** Recommended state-specific poverty lines using CPI-AL (rural) and CPI-IW (urban).
  3. **Suresh Tendulkar Committee (constituted 2005, submitted 2009):** Shifted away from calorie anchor to a uniform consumption basket including expenditure on health and education (Mixed Reference Period - MRP). Set poverty line at Rs 27/day rural, Rs 33/day urban (21.9% poverty in 2011-12).
  4. **C. Rangarajan Committee (constituted 2012, submitted 2014):** Modified Mixed Reference Period (MMRP), revised poverty line to Rs 32/day rural, Rs 47/day urban (29.5% poverty in 2011-12).
- **आयोग का Trap / Ratta Point:** Chronology order: **Alagh (1979) -> Lakdawala (1993) -> Tendulkar (2009) -> Rangarajan (2014)**. Tendulkar moved away from calorie-only to education & health consumption.

### Question 11
**Subject:** Indian Polity | **Topic:** Parliament & Rajya Sabha Powers

Given below are two statements, one is labelled as Assertion (A) and the other as Reason (R):

**Assertion (A):** The Rajya Sabha possesses exclusive constitutional powers not shared by the Lok Sabha to initiate legislation on certain matters.  
**Reason (R):** Under Article 249, the Rajya Sabha can authorize Parliament to make laws on a State List subject, and under Article 312, it can authorize the creation of one or more All-India Services.

Select the correct answer from the codes given below:

**Options:**
A. Both (A) and (R) are true and (R) is the correct explanation of (A)  
B. Both (A) and (R) are true, but (R) is not the correct explanation of (A)  
C. (A) is true, but (R) is false  
D. (A) is false, but (R) is true  

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:**
- **Core Fact & Solution:**
  - Assertion is true: As the council representing states in a federal setup, Rajya Sabha has special exclusive powers.
  - Reason is true: 
    1. **Article 249:** If Rajya Sabha passes a resolution supported by not less than two-thirds of members present and voting declaring that it is necessary in the national interest, Parliament can legislate on a State List subject (valid for 1 year).

### Question 14
**Subject:** General Science (Physics) | **Topic:** Optical Phenomena in Daily Life

Match List-I (Phenomenon) with List-II (Underlying Optical Principle) and select the correct answer:

**List-I (Phenomenon)**
A. Mirage in deserts & Sparkle of diamond  
B. Blue colour of clear sky  
C. Rainbow formation  
D. Twinkling of stars  

**List-II (Optical Principle)**
1. Atmospheric Refraction  
2. Dispersion, Refraction & Internal Reflection  
3. Total Internal Reflection  
4. Rayleigh Scattering of light  

**Options:**
A. A-3, B-4, C-2, D-1  
B. A-4, B-3, C-2, D-1  
C. A-3, B-1, C-2, D-4  
D. A-2, B-4, C-3, D-1  

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:**
- **Core Fact & Solution:**
  - **Total Internal Reflection (TIR):** Occurs when light travels from denser to rarer medium at an angle greater than the critical angle. Explains mirages, brilliance of diamonds, and light propagation through optical fibres and endoscopes.
  - **Scattering of Light:** Rayleigh's scattering law states scattering intensity is inversely proportional to $\lambda^4$. Shorter wavelengths (blue/violet) scatter much more than longer ones, giving the sky its blue color.
  - **Rainbow:** Composite optical phenomenon involving dispersion, refraction, and internal reflection within water droplets.
  - **Twinkling of Stars:** Caused by atmospheric refraction through layers of air with continuously varying refractive indices.
- **आयोग का Trap / Ratta Point:** In space or on the Moon (where there is no atmosphere), the sky appears black because there is no scattering of light!

### Question 16

Consider the following statements regarding the Wholesale Price Index (WPI) and Consumer Price Index (CPI) in India:
1. WPI is released by the Office of the Economic Adviser, Ministry of Commerce and Industry, whereas CPI is released by the National Statistical Office (NSO), MoSPI.
2. WPI accounts for the price changes of both goods and services, while CPI accounts only for goods.
3. In CPI (Combined), the 'Food and Beverages' group holds the highest weightage (~45.86%).

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

A. 1 and 3 only  
B. 1 and 2 only  
C. 2 and 3 only

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:**
- **Core Fact & Solution:**
