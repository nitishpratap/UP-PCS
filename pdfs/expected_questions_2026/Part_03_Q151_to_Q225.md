### Question 151
**Subject:** Modern Indian History | **Topic:** Lord Dalhousie & Doctrine of Lapse Chronology

Arrange the following Indian Princely States annexed by Lord Dalhousie under the 'Doctrine of Lapse' in chronological order:
1. Satara
2. Jhansi
3. Sambalpur
4. Nagpur

Select the correct answer from the codes given below:

**Options:**
A. 1 — 3 — 2 — 4  
B. 1 — 2 — 3 — 4  
C. 3 — 1 — 2 — 4  
D. 1 — 3 — 4 — 2  

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:**
- **Core Fact & Solution:**
  - Lord Dalhousie (1848–1856) applied the aggressive imperialist policy of **Doctrine of Lapse**, refusing to recognize adopted heirs to sovereign princely thrones:
    1. **Satara (1848):** First state annexed under the Doctrine.
    2. **Jaitpur (Bundelkhand) & Sambalpur (Odisha):** Annexed in **1849**.
    3. **Baghat (Himachal):** Annexed in **1850** (later restored by Lord Canning).
    4. **Udaipur (Chhattisgarh):** Annexed in **1852** (later restored).
    5. **Jhansi:** Annexed in **1853** (after Gangadhar Rao died without natural heir).
    6. **Nagpur:** Annexed in **1854** (Bhonsle state; royal jewels were publicly auctioned in Calcutta).
- **आयोग का Trap / Ratta Point:** Awadh was NOT annexed under the Doctrine of Lapse! Awadh was annexed in **February 1856 on charges of "misgovernance and maladministration"** based on James Outram's report!

### Question 154
**Subject:** General Science (Biology) | **Topic:** Genetics & Nucleic Acids (DNA vs RNA)

Consider the following statements regarding nucleic acids:
1. James Watson and Francis Crick proposed the Double Helix model of DNA in 1953 based on X-ray diffraction data of Rosalind Franklin.
2. In DNA, the purine Adenine pairs with Thymine via two hydrogen bonds ($A=T$), while Guanine pairs with Cytosine via three hydrogen bonds ($G \equiv C$).
3. In RNA, the pyrimidine base Thymine is replaced by Uracil.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

**Options:**
A. 1 and 2 only  
B. 2 and 3 only  
C. 1 and 3 only  
D. 1, 2 and 3  

**Answer:** D

**Explanation:**
- **Core Fact & Solution:**
  - All three statements are correct:
    - **Double Helix Model (1953):** Two antiparallel polynucleotide chains coiled around a central axis; Watson, Crick, and Maurice Wilkins shared the Nobel Prize in 1962.
    - **Base Pairing (Chargaff's Rule):** Purines = Pyrimidines ($A+G = T+C$). Adenine pairs with Thymine ($A=T$, two H-bonds); Guanine pairs with Cytosine ($G \equiv C$, three H-bonds).
    - **DNA vs RNA:** DNA contains 2-deoxyribose sugar and bases A, T, G, C. RNA contains ribose sugar and replaces **Thymine ($T$) with Uracil ($U$)**.
- **आयोग का Trap / Ratta Point:** Purines are double-ringed (**Adenine, Guanine**); Pyrimidines are single-ringed (**Cytosine, Thymine, Uracil**).

### Question 156
**Subject:** Environment & Ecology | **Topic:** Ecological Concepts and Principles

Match List-I (Ecological Concept) with List-II (Definition / Key Feature) and select the correct answer:

**List-I (Concept)**
A. Ecotone  
B. Ecological Niche  
C. Edge Effect  
D. Keystone Species  

**List-II (Definition)**
1. Functional role, resource utilization, and status of an organism in its ecosystem  
2. Zone of junction or transition between two or more diverse ecosystems (e.g. mangrove, marshland)  
3. Phenomenon where species diversity or population density is greater in the ecotone than in flanking communities  
4. Species whose presence and activity exerts disproportionately large influence on the entire community structure  

**Options:**
A. A-2, B-1, C-3, D-4  
B. A-1, B-2, C-3, D-4  
C. A-2, B-3, C-1, D-4  
D. A-3, B-1, C-4, D-2  

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:**
- **Core Fact & Solution:**
  - **Ecotone:** Transition zone between two biomes (e.g., mangrove between marine and terrestrial; grassland between forest and desert).
  - **Ecological Niche (Joseph Grinnell 1917):** "Address" is habitat, but "profession" is niche; unique functional role and trophic position occupied by a species. Two species cannot occupy the exact same niche indefinitely (Gause's Competitive Exclusion Principle).
  - **Edge Effect:** The heightened richness, density, and diversity of species in the ecotone boundary compared to adjacent communities.
  - **Keystone Species (Robert Paine 1969):** Species that plays a critical role in maintaining ecological balance (e.g., Tiger in forest, Sea Otter in kelp forest, Fig tree in tropical rainforest).
- **आयोग का Trap / Ratta Point:** Difference between Habitat and Niche: Many species can share the same Habitat, but NO two species can occupy the exact same Niche in the long run!

### Question 162
**Subject:** Medieval Indian History | **Topic:** Delhi Sultanate Fiscal Taxes

Match List-I (Fiscal Tax in Delhi Sultanate) with List-II (Nature / Levied Upon) and select the correct answer:

**List-I (Tax)**
A. Kharaj  
B. Jizya  
C. Zakat  
D. Khams  

**List-II (Nature)**
1. Poll tax levied on non-Muslim protected subjects (*Zimmis*) in lieu of military service  
2. Land revenue tax levied on agricultural produce (usually $\frac{1}{3}\text{rd}$ to $\frac{1}{2}$)  
3. State's share ($\frac{1}{5}\text{th}$) of the spoils of war and booty  
4. Religious tax of 2.5% levied exclusively on well-off Muslims for charitable purposes  

**Options:**
A. A-2, B-1, C-4, D-3  
B. A-1, B-2, C-4, D-3  
C. A-2, B-4, C-1, D-3  
D. A-3, B-1, C-4, D-2  

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:**
- **Core Fact & Solution:**
  - **Kharaj:** Agricultural land tax on cultivable lands (Alauddin Khalji raised Kharaj to 50% of gross produce based on measurement / *Masaht*).
  - **Jizya:** Capitation/poll tax paid by non-Muslims; Firoz Shah Tughlaq was the first Sultan to levy Jizya on Brahmins as a separate tax.

- **आयोग का Trap / Ratta Point:** Sharb was an irrigation tax levied by **Firoz Shah Tughlaq** at the rate of 10% of produce from lands irrigated by royal canals!

### Question 163
**Subject:** General Science (Biology) | **Topic:** Human Circulatory System & Heart Physiology

Consider the following statements regarding the human heart and blood vessels:
1. The Sinoatrial (SA) node located in the right atrium acts as the natural pacemaker of the heart.
2. Arteries always carry oxygenated blood away from the heart, with the sole exception of the Pulmonary Artery, which carries deoxygenated blood to the lungs.
3. In a standard Electrocardiogram (ECG), the P-wave represents the ventricular repolarization.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

**Options:**
A. 1 and 2 only  
B. 2 and 3 only  
C. 1 only  
D. 1, 2 and 3  

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:**
- **Core Fact & Solution:**
  - Statements 1 and 2 are correct: The SA node generates rhythmic action potentials (~72 times/minute), initiating cardiac contraction. Arteries carry blood away from the heart at high pressure; the Pulmonary Artery is the ONLY artery carrying deoxygenated blood (from right ventricle to lungs). The Pulmonary Vein is the ONLY vein carrying oxygenated blood (from lungs to left atrium).
  - Statement 3 is incorrect: In a standard ECG:
    - **P-wave:** Represents **Atrial Depolarization** (electrical excitation leading to contraction of both atria).
    - **QRS Complex:** Represents **Ventricular Depolarization** (contraction of ventricles).
    - **T-wave:** Represents **Ventricular Repolarization** (return of ventricles from excited to resting state).
- **आयोग का Trap / Ratta Point:** Tricuspid valve guards the right atrioventricular opening; Bicuspid (Mitral) valve guards the left atrioventricular opening!

    - (However, because Open Oceans cover ~71% of Earth's surface, their *total collective global productivity* is large).
- **आयोग का Trap / Ratta Point:** Coral reefs and tropical rainforests have the HIGHEST productivity per unit area; open oceans have LOW productivity per unit area!
