📘 Detailed Solution & High-Yield Analysis
  • Steering Committee was chaired by Dr. Rajendra Prasad (also Rules of Procedure Committee).

⚠️ आयोग का TRAP / RATTA POINT

UPPSC repeatedly traps candidates between the main Advisory Committee on Fundamental Rights & Minorities (headed by Sardar Patel) and its Sub-Committees (FR Sub-committee under J.B. Kripalani, Minorities Sub-committee under H.C. Mukherjee).

🌍 Geography 📌 Mountain Passes of India
Match
List-I (Pass)List-II (State / UT)
A Shipki La1 Sikkim
B Lipulekh2 Himachal Pradesh
C Nathu La3 Ladakh
D Zoji La4 Uttarakhand
A A-2, B-4, C-1, D-3
B A-2, B-1, C-4, D-3
C A-4, B-2, C-1, D-3
D A-3, B-4, C-1, D-2
✓ Correct Answer: Option A
📘 Detailed Solution & High-Yield Analysis
  • Shipki La: Located in Himachal Pradesh (Kinnaur district); the Sutlej river enters India through this pass from Tibet.
  • Lipulekh: Located at the tri-junction of Uttarakhand (Pithoragarh), India, Nepal, and China; vital for the Kailash Mansarovar pilgrimage route.
  • Nathu La: Located in Sikkim (East Sikkim); historic Silk Route branch connecting India with Tibet, reopened in 2006.
  • Zoji La: Located in the UT of Ladakh (connecting Srinagar with Kargil and Leh on Great Himalayas).
⚠️ आयोग का TRAP / RATTA POINT

Note that Zoji La is in Ladakh (post-2019 reorganization), not J&K UT! Banihal pass is in J&K (Pir Panjal range), whereas Zoji La connects Kashmir valley with Ladakh.

Q. 3
📜 Modern Indian History 📌 Gandhian Movements Chronology

Arrange the following events of Mahatma Gandhi's early political career in chronological order:

1 Ahmedabad Mill Strike
2 Champaran Satyagraha
3 Kheda Satyagraha
4 Rowlatt Satyagraha

Select the correct answer using the codes given below:

A 2 — 1 — 3 — 4
B 2 — 3 — 1 — 4
C 1 — 2 — 3 — 4
D 2 — 1 — 4 — 3
✓ Correct Answer: Option A
📘 Detailed Solution & High-Yield Analysis
  1. Champaran Satyagraha (April 1917): First Civil Disobedience in India against Tinkathia system in Bihar (invited by Raj Kumar Shukla).
  2. Ahmedabad Mill Strike (February–March 1918): First Hunger Strike by Gandhi over plague bonus dispute.
  3. Kheda Satyagraha (March 1918): First Non-Cooperation movement by Gandhi due to crop failure and revenue remission demand in Gujarat.
  4. Rowlatt Satyagraha (April 1919): First all-India mass protest against the Anarchical and Revolutionary Crimes Act (Rowlatt Act).
⚠️ आयोग का TRAP / RATTA POINT

Mnemonic: CAK (Champaran -> Ahmedabad -> Kheda). Ahmedabad Mill Strike preceded Kheda Satyagraha by a few weeks in early 1918!

Q. 4
🔬 General Science (Biology) 📌 Vitamins & Deficiency Diseases
Match
List-I (Vitamin)List-II (Disease / Feature)
A Vitamin B1 (Thiamine)1 Pellagra (4D syndrome)
B Vitamin B3 (Niacin)2 Beri-Beri
C Vitamin D (Calciferol)3 Pernicious Anemia & contains Cobalt
D Vitamin B12 (Cyanocobalamin)4 Rickets in children & Osteomalacia in adults
A A-2, B-1, C-4, D-3
B A-1, B-2, C-4, D-3
C A-2, B-4, C-1, D-3
D A-3, B-1, C-4, D-2
✓ Correct Answer: Option A
📘 Detailed Solution & High-Yield Analysis
  • Vitamin B1 (Thiamine): Deficiency causes Beri-Beri (affects cardiovascular and nervous systems; common in populations consuming polished rice).
  • Vitamin B3 (Niacin): Deficiency causes Pellagra, characterized by dermatitis, diarrhea, dementia, and death (4Ds).
  • Vitamin D (Calciferol): Synthesized in skin via sunlight; deficiency causes Rickets (bow legs) in children and Osteomalacia in adults.
  • Vitamin B12 (Cyanocobalamin): Contains the metallic element Cobalt; essential for RBC maturation; deficiency causes Pernicious Anemia.
⚠️ आयोग का TRAP / RATTA POINT

UPPSC frequently asks which vitamin contains Cobalt (Cyanocobalamin / B12). Remember also: Vitamin C is Ascorbic acid (heat-sensitive, lost on cooking), and Vitamin K (Phylloquinone) is needed for prothrombin synthesis in blood clotting.
