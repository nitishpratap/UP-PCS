## Content

### Context — soil as natural resource

- **Soil** = **dynamic natural body** — **mineral + organic matter + water + air + living organisms** — formed by **weathering + pedogenesis** over **centuries**.
- **India** has **diverse soils** linked to **parent rock, climate, vegetation, time, relief** — **ICAR classification** used in **agriculture planning**.
- **~58% livelihood** still **agriculture-dependent** — **soil health = national food security** — **Soil Health Card Scheme (2015)**.
- **South & South-East Asia:** **Deltaic alluvium (Ganges, Mekong, Irrawaddy)**, **volcanic soils (Indonesia)**, **lateritic tropics** — **India shares monsoon-soil patterns with region**.

### Soil profile — horizons explained

| Horizon | Features | Agricultural significance |
|---------|----------|---------------------------|
| **O** | **Organic litter, humus** | **Forest/mountain — nutrient cycling** |
| **A** | **Topsoil, dark, biologically active** | **Primary seedbed, fertility, tillage zone** |
| **E** | **Leached layer (optional)** | **Less common in Indian alluvial profiles** |
| **B** | **Accumulated clay, iron, aluminium** | **Subsoil moisture, root penetration limit if hardpan** |
| **C** | **Partially weathered parent material** | **Source of minerals, drainage base** |
| **R** | **Bedrock** | **Restricts deep-rooted crops if shallow profile** |

**Pedon / profile concept:** **Examining vertical section** reveals **why same rainfall yields different crops** — **precision agriculture basis**.

### Major soil types of India

| Soil | Region | Crops / traits |
|------|--------|----------------|
| **Alluvial** | **Indo-Gangetic, Brahmaputra, coastal deltas** | **Wheat, rice, sugarcane — most fertile, deep** |
| **Black (Regur)** | **Deccan trap (Maharashtra, MP, Gujarat)** | **Cotton, sorghum — high clay, self-ploughing, cracks** |
| **Red & Yellow** | **Low rainfall peninsular (TN, Karnataka, Odisha)** | **Millets, pulses — ferric oxide, lower N** |
| **Laterite** | **High rainfall (Western Ghats, NE, Odisha)** | **Plantation (tea, coffee, rubber) with manuring** |
| **Desert (Arid)** | **Rajasthan, Kutch** | **Drought-resistant crops, irrigation dependent** |
| **Saline-Alkaline (Usar)** | **Punjab, Haryana, UP, Gujarat coast** | **Reclamation via gypsum, drainage** |
| **Peaty/Forest** | **Kerala, WB deltas, Himalaya foothills** | **Organic rich, needs drainage in humid areas** |

### Why soil profile matters for agriculture (2023 PYQ)

**Agree — reasons:**

1. **Root zone depth** — **Shallow profiles (laterite, mountain)** limit **deep-rooted crops (cotton, trees)**.
2. **Nutrient availability** — **A horizon organic matter** supplies **N; B horizon holds ** **exchangeable cations (Ca, Mg, K)**.
3. **Texture & drainage** — **Clayey black soil** retains moisture but **waterlogs if profile poorly drained**; **sandy desert** **fast leaching**.
4. **pH profile** — **Acidic laterite needs lime; alkaline usar needs gypsum** — **nutrient lockout without correction**.
5. **Moisture retention** — **Profile structure (granular vs platy)** affects **drought resilience between monsoon showers**.
6. **Microbial activity** — **Topsoil biota** — **nitrogen fixation, organic decomposition** — **destroyed by erosion**.
7. **Erosion vulnerability** — **Thin A horizon on slopes (Shivalik, Western Ghats)** — **profile loss = permanent productivity loss**.

**Counter-nuance (critical agree):** **Modern inputs (fertilizer, irrigation)** can **mask poor profile temporarily** — **unsustainable without profile health** — **still agree profile is fundamental**.

### Soil degradation and conservation

- **Water erosion** — **Deforestation, overgrazing** — **Shivalik, Chambal ravines**.
- **Wind erosion** — **Thar desert margins**.
- **Salinization** — **Canal irrigation without drainage (Punjab, Haryana)**.
- **Chemical degradation** — **Over-fertilization, pesticide** — **soil health card aims correction**.
- **Conservation:** **Contour bunding, terracing (NE hills)**, **cover crops, agroforestry, organic farming, vermicompost**.

### Contemporary relevance

- **Soil Health Card Scheme** — **~14 crore cards** distributed — **NPK + micronutrient advisory**.
- **Natural Farming / Bharatiya Prakritik Krishi Paddhati** — **soil organic carbon focus**.
- **National Mission for Sustainable Agriculture (NMSA)** — **climate-resilient soil practices**.
- **PM-KUSUM, crop diversification** — **reduce soil exhaustion from ** **water-intensive monoculture**.
- **Desertification and Land Degradation Neutrality (SDG 15.3)** — **India UNCCD commitments**.

### Limits and balanced view

- **Soil profile varies within field** — **precision mapping (remote sensing) emerging**.
- **Green Revolution** boosted yields but **micronutrient depletion** in **Punjab, Haryana** — **profile management overdue**.
- **Not all laterite infertile** — **with organic inputs supports plantations**.

- **Root Zone Depth** — **Shallow profiles restrict deep-rooted crops; deep alluvial supports wheat-rice**.

- **pH & Nutrient Availability** — **Laterite acidity, usar alkalinity need correction for yields**.
- **Subsoil (B) Moisture Reserve** — **Critical between monsoon rains for rainfed crops**.
- **Erosion Susceptibility** — **Thin topsoil loss irreversible on slopes**.
- **Precision Inputs** — **Soil Health Card uses profile knowledge for ** **balanced fertilization**.
