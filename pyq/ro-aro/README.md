# UPPSC RO/ARO Question Bank (समीक्षा अधिकारी / सहायक समीक्षा अधिकारी)

Welcome to the dedicated **UPPSC RO/ARO (Review Officer / Assistant Review Officer)** Question Bank repository under `pyq/ro-aro/`.

This folder houses the complete official papers, topic-mapped questions, and verified answer keys with explanations for the UPPSC RO/ARO Preliminary Examinations.

---

## 📚 Examination Pattern & Structure

The UPPSC RO/ARO Preliminary Examination consists of **200 Questions** for **200 Marks** across two parts/papers:

| Paper | Subject | Questions | Marks | Duration | Negative Marking |
|:---|:---|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **Paper-I** | General Studies (सामान्य अध्ययन) | 140 | 140 | 120 min (2 Hours) | 1/3rd (0.33) |
| **Paper-II** | General Hindi (सामान्य हिन्दी - व्याकरण एवं शब्द ज्ञान) | 60 | 60 | 60 min (1 Hour) | 1/3rd (0.33) |
| **Total** | **Combined Prelims Merit Score** | **200** | **200** | **180 min (3 Hours)** | **1/3rd** |
