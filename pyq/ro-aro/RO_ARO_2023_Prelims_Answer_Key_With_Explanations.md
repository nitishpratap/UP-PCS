### Question 1
- **Subject:** Geography | **Topic:** World Geography | **Subtopic:** Physical Geography - Straits & Water Bodies
- **Question:** Which one of the following separates Tasmania from Australia?
- **Correct Answer:** **(B) Bass Strait**

> **Explanation:**
> Bass Strait separates Tasmania from the Australian mainland (state of Victoria). Great Australian Bight lies off the southern coast of mainland Australia. Magellan Strait is in southern South America, and Tasman Sea lies between Australia/Tasmania and New Zealand.

### Question 2
- **Subject:** Geography | **Topic:** Human Geography | **Subtopic:** World Population & Demographics
- **Question:** Consider the following statements with reference to world population:

I. Brazil has the highest population among the South American countries.
II. Asia continent has the highest growth rate of population.
III. Africa is the most densely populated continent of the world.

Which of the above statements is/are correct?
- **Correct Answer:** **(D) Only I**

> **Explanation:**
> Statement I is correct: Brazil is the most populous country in South America (~215 million). Statement II is incorrect: Africa has the highest population growth rate (~2.4% annually), while Asia's growth rate has slowed down significantly (~0.8%). Statement III is incorrect: Asia is the most densely populated continent (~150 people per sq km), not Africa.

### Question 9
- **Subject:** General Mental Ability | **Topic:** Reasoning & Aptitude | **Subtopic:** Time & Clock Calculations
- **Question:** Ashish leaves his house at 20 minutes to seven in the morning and reaches Kunal's house in 25 minutes. They finish their breakfast in another 15 minutes and leave for their office, which takes another 35 minutes to reach. At what time do they leave Kunal's house to reach their office?
- **Correct Answer:** **(B) 7:20 AM**

> **Explanation:**
> Ashish leaves home at 20 minutes to 7, which is 6:40 AM. He travels for 25 minutes and reaches Kunal's house at 6:40 + 25 min = 7:05 AM. They have breakfast for 15 minutes: 7:05 + 15 min = 7:20 AM. Therefore, they leave Kunal's house at 7:20 AM.

### Question 10
- **Subject:** General Mental Ability | **Topic:** Reasoning | **Subtopic:** Number Puzzles & Pattern Identification
- **Question:** Find the missing number:

Figure 1: Top numbers 17, 18; Result: 1225
Figure 2: Top numbers 13, 14; Result: 729
Figure 3: Top numbers 19, 4; Result: ?
- **Correct Answer:** **(C) 529**

> **Explanation:**
> Pattern: Sum of the two numbers squared. In Figure 1: (17 + 18)^2 = 35^2 = 1225. In Figure 2: (13 + 14)^2 = 27^2 = 729. In Figure 3: (19 + 4)^2 = 23^2 = 529.

### Question 13
- **Subject:** General Mental Ability | **Topic:** Reasoning | **Subtopic:** Blood Relations
- **Question:** Introducing Shyam, a lady said, "The father of his father-in-law is my father-in-law." How is Shyam related to the lady?
- **Correct Answer:** **(C) Son-in-law**

> **Explanation:**
> The father-in-law of Shyam's father-in-law's father is the lady's father-in-law. This means Shyam's father-in-law is the son of the lady's father-in-law (i.e., her husband). Thus, the lady's husband is Shyam's father-in-law, meaning the lady's daughter is Shyam's wife. Therefore, Shyam is the lady's son-in-law (दामाद).

### Question 61
- **Subject:** General Mental Ability | **Topic:** Reasoning - Ranking & Ordering | **Subtopic:** Position Interchanging in a Queue
- **Question:** In a queue of boys, A is seventh from the left and B is fourth from the right. When A and B interchange their positions, then A becomes fifteenth from the left. Find the position of B from the right.
- **Correct Answer:** **(B) 12th**

> **Explanation:**
> When A moves to B's position, his position is 15th from the left. But this was originally B's position, which was 4th from the right.
Total number of boys = (Position from left) + (Position from right) - 1 = 15 + 4 - 1 = 18.
Now, B sits at A's original position, which was 7th from the left.
Position of B from the right = (Total - Position from left) + 1 = (18 - 7) + 1 = 12th.

- **Question:** Find the value of x, if:

### Question 195
- **Subject:** General Hindi | **Topic:** विलोम शब्द | **Subtopic:** विलोम शब्द सुमेलन
- **Question:** सूची-I को सूची-II से सुमेलित कीजिए और नीचे दिए गए कूट का प्रयोग कर सही उत्तर चुनिए:

**सूची-I (शब्द)**
A. प्रफुल्ल
B. व्यास
C. कृत्रिम
D. प्रेम

**सूची-II (विलोम)**
i. घृणा
ii. समास
iii. म्लान
iv. प्राकृत

कूट:
(a) A-iii, B-iv, C-i, D-ii
(b) A-iv, B-ii, C-iii, D-i
(c) A-i, B-ii, C-iv, D-iii
(d) A-iii, B-ii, C-iv, D-i
- **Correct Answer:** **(D) A-iii, B-ii, C-iv, D-i**

> **Explanation:**
> प्रफुल्ल (खिला हुआ) का विलोम 'म्लान' (मुरझाया हुआ) (iii) होता है। व्यास का विलोम 'समास' (ii) होता है। कृत्रिम का विलोम 'प्राकृत' (या प्राकृतिक) (iv) होता है। प्रेम का विलोम 'घृणा' (i) होता है। कूट: A-iii, B-ii, C-iv, D-i।

### Question 197
- **Subject:** General Hindi | **Topic:** विशेष्य और विशेषण | **Subtopic:** प्रविशेषण पहचान
- **Question:** 'कश्मीरी सेब सिंदूरी लाल होता है।' वाक्य में 'सिंदूरी' शब्द है:
- **Correct Answer:** **(C) प्रविशेषण**

> **Explanation:**
> प्रस्तुत वाक्य में 'लाल' रंगबोधक गुणवाचक विशेषण है, और 'सिंदूरी' शब्द उस लाल रंग की विशेषता/छटा को प्रगट कर रहा है (सिंदूर जैसा लाल)। विशेषण की विशेषता बताने के कारण 'सिंदूरी' यहाँ प्रविशेषण है।

### Question 198
- **Subject:** General Hindi | **Topic:** विशेष्य और विशेषण | **Subtopic:** विशेष्य-विशेषण सुमेलन
- **Question:** सूची-I को सूची-II से सुमेलित कीजिए और नीचे दिए गए कूट का प्रयोग कर सही उत्तर चुनिए:

**सूची-I (विशेष्य)**
A. वायु
B. विवाद
C. विकास
D. विषाद

**सूची-II (विशेषण)**
i. विषण्ण
ii. विकसित
iii. विवादी
iv. वायव्य

कूट:
(a) A-ii, B-iv, C-i, D-iii
(b) A-i, B-ii, C-iii, D-iv
(c) A-iii, B-i, C-iv, D-ii
(d) A-iv, B-iii, C-ii, D-i
- **Correct Answer:** **(D) A-iv, B-iii, C-ii, D-i**

> **Explanation:**
> वायु का विशेषण 'वायव्य' (iv) होता है। विवाद का विशेषण 'विवादी' (iii) होता है। विकास का विशेषण 'विकसित' (ii) होता है। विषाद का विशेषण 'विषण्ण' (i) होता है। अतः सही कूट A-iv, B-iii, C-ii, D-i है।

### Question 200
